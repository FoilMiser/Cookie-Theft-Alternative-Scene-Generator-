What v0.50 changed

Fresh readers, who never saw the scoring key, describe every picture the way a test participant would, and separate scorers mark each scored item as mentioned, half-mentioned, missed or misread. The screen has now run on forty-seven releases, v0.4 to v0.50, and once more on the unchanged v0.9 pictures with fresh readers, to measure how much the readers themselves vary. v0.50 worked the list of the v0.49 screen. First on it was that grown figures were read as children: four of them were called a girl or a boy in 20 of their 89 pictures. Pictures of that screen were built again with four casts and read blind, and marks of age with a smaller head left one young name in 45 appearances, where the cast of v0.49 had 13. In this screen the four are called young in 3 of their 79 pictures. The children on the seesaw are set down by their own measured underside and hold a grab bar, and no reader has one above the plank. The lost balloon, its string ending by the child in the garden too, is read in full. 62 of the 64 templates pass the gate, where all 64 did in v0.49, and one item of the screen was misread. The game of catch fails: a ball drawn by the hands of a grown catcher was read as hers to throw. The square wheel fails on its one wheelbarrow, which has a hose on the ground behind the wheel. Neither drawing was changed in this release, and both head the next list. The set's overall score is 0.949, from 0.967: the clouds and a tree that some readers left out cost what they did before, and the rest of the fall is the two failures, the thief bird and the bread for the ducks. Nothing was mended, swapped or read again after the screen. With the 33 full marks that stand beside another reading halved, 52 of the 64 pass. Of the 19 pairs of a template and an angle an animal is drawn at, 18 pass. Probe pictures put every template in front of the readers at least three times, and every angle at least twice.

Combinatorics

How many different scenes the generator can make

Structures are template sets that fit a tier's plan and can reach its content band. Signatures are what the difficulty model sees (item types, chain lengths, scored setting entities); structures sharing a signature are candidate isomorphs. Totals multiply structures by cast assignments, expressions, facing, mirroring and optional features; positions are continuous and not counted.

Action library

actions, each with its own props and anchors

Lower-body postures combined with upper-body activities. Props are built for the pose and parented to the hand or head, so scenes can attach water, strings, leashes and ropes to the right point. These are the actions the templates use; more stay in the cast for future templates and are left out until one uses them. Earlier drawings that a refined pose replaced have been retired from the cast.
