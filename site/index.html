What v0.46 changed

Fresh readers, who never saw the scoring key, describe every picture the way a test participant would, and separate scorers mark each scored item as mentioned, half-mentioned, missed or misread. The screen has now run on forty-three releases, v0.4 to v0.46, and once more on the unchanged v0.9 pictures with fresh readers, to measure how much the readers themselves vary. v0.46 changes no scene and draws the whole cast again: an outer edge as heavy as the line the settings are drawn in, two flat tones where there were three soft ones, shorter necks, the safety vest's bands on the vest, glasses of plain wire and a smoother moustache. No reader of the 98 pictures named a neck, an outline or a mouth, and the woman in the yellow dress is a woman in 29 descriptions of 30. It is a weaker screen than v0.45's all the same. The set's overall score is 0.957, from 0.977, and four templates fall under the gate where v0.45 had none: the reading dog, the walker about to trip, the man sweeping with a fork and the stopped hose. Three fell on one misread picture each and the fourth on one reader's hedges. Nothing was mended, swapped or read again after the screen, and the four head the list for v0.47. 60 of the 64 templates pass, and 20 of the 22 pairs of a template and an angle an animal is drawn at. Probe pictures put every template in front of the readers at least three times, and every angle at least twice.

Combinatorics

How many different scenes the generator can make

Structures are template sets that fit a tier's plan and can reach its content band. Signatures are what the difficulty model sees (item types, chain lengths, scored setting entities); structures sharing a signature are candidate isomorphs. Totals multiply structures by cast assignments, expressions, facing, mirroring and optional features; positions are continuous and not counted.

Action library

actions, each with its own props and anchors

Lower-body postures combined with upper-body activities. Props are built for the pose and parented to the hand or head, so scenes can attach water, strings, leashes and ropes to the right point. These are the actions the templates use; more stay in the cast for future templates and are left out until one uses them. Earlier drawings that a refined pose replaced have been retired from the cast.
