What v0.51 changed

Fresh readers, who never saw the scoring key, describe every picture the way a test participant would, and separate scorers mark each scored item as mentioned, half-mentioned, missed or misread. The screen has now run on forty-eight releases, v0.4 to v0.51, and once more on the unchanged v0.9 pictures with fresh readers, to measure how much the readers themselves vary. v0.51 worked the list of the v0.50 screen. First on it were the two templates under the gate. In the game of catch a small girl had thrown to a grown woman, and the ball, drawn late on its arc by the hands of the woman, was read as hers to throw. The two pictures were built again in three versions and read blind: the girl was the thrower in 3 readings of 8 as screened, and in 7 of 8 with the ball drawn early on its path. In this screen the thrower is the thrower in all five pictures. The square wheel had failed on its one wheelbarrow, which had a hose on the ground behind the wheel; no hose lies by the barrow now, and it is read in full in eight pictures, four of them the barrow. The duck feeder holds a slice of bread, and the string of the lost balloon ends by no fence rail and over no pond; both are read in full. All 64 templates pass the gate, where 62 did in v0.50, and no item of the screen was misread. The set's overall score is 0.976, from 0.949: of its eight items under full marks, six are the clouds that a reader left out. Nothing was mended, swapped or read again after the screen. With the 44 full marks that stand beside another reading halved, 56 of the 64 pass, so a full gate is not the end of the reading. A wider moustache for the man in the red shirt was built and read in whole scenes and changed nothing; he is called a boy when he sweeps, which is next on the list. All 19 pairs of a template and an angle an animal is drawn at pass. Probe pictures put every template in front of the readers at least three times, and every angle at least twice.

Combinatorics

How many different scenes the generator can make

Structures are template sets that fit a tier's plan and can reach its content band. Signatures are what the difficulty model sees (item types, chain lengths, scored setting entities); structures sharing a signature are candidate isomorphs. Totals multiply structures by cast assignments, expressions, facing, mirroring and optional features; positions are continuous and not counted.

Action library

actions, each with its own props and anchors

Lower-body postures combined with upper-body activities. Props are built for the pose and parented to the hand or head, so scenes can attach water, strings, leashes and ropes to the right point. These are the actions the templates use; more stay in the cast for future templates and are left out until one uses them. Earlier drawings that a refined pose replaced have been retired from the cast.
