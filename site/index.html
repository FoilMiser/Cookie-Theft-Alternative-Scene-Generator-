Inspection and blind screen

What v0.19 fixed

Fresh readers, who never saw the scoring key, describe every picture the way a test participant would, and separate scorers mark each scored item as mentioned, half-mentioned, missed or misread. The screen has now run on sixteen releases, v0.4 to v0.19, and once more on the unchanged v0.9 pictures with fresh readers, to measure how much the readers themselves vary. v0.19 fixes the four templates that fell short of the v0.18 gate: a reader walking with a book in the wind, whose open pages read as a newspaper blowing; a cat on the fence too far from its bird to be stalking it; a frying pan beside a hose reel, taken for a hose nozzle; and a runner with no marks of speed. It also fixes the oddities the v0.18 readers reported: people kept clear of lamp posts, play equipment and the stall's legs, the begging dog clear of the eater, the child under a ball stuck in a tree pointing at it, the kicked ball at the toe, hose and sprinkler water drawn unbroken, and a curly-haired boy whose face now shows in profile. Each weak visual was first refined on its own: cut out of a picture with nothing else around it, read blind, redrawn and read again until it read as intended, then read blind in whole test scenes before going back into the set. Probe pictures put every template in front of the readers at least three times.

Combinatorics

How many different scenes the generator can make

Structures are template sets that fit a tier's plan and can reach its content band. Signatures are what the difficulty model sees (item types, chain lengths, scored setting entities); structures sharing a signature are candidate isomorphs. Totals multiply structures by cast assignments, expressions, facing, mirroring and optional features; positions are continuous and not counted.

Action library

actions, each with its own props and anchors

Lower-body postures combined with upper-body activities. Props are built for the pose and parented to the hand or head, so scenes can attach water, strings, leashes and ropes to the right point. These are the actions the templates use; more stay in the cast for future templates and are left out until one uses them. Earlier drawings that a refined pose replaced have been retired from the cast.
