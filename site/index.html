What v0.57 changed

Fresh readers, who never saw the scoring key, describe every picture the way a test participant would, and separate scorers mark each scored item as mentioned, half-mentioned, missed or misread. The screen has now run on fifty-four releases, v0.4 to v0.57, and once more on the unchanged v0.9 pictures with fresh readers, to measure how much the readers themselves vary. v0.56 ended with 63 of its 64 templates through the gate, and the brief for v0.57 was the list of its screen. Every new drawing and rule was put behind a switch and read blind in whole pictures before it went into scenes: 125 readings of pictures of v0.56 built again with the new drawings and of five new pictures, and 50 more in a second round. What was taken: the game of catch has a flat flight that keeps off the top of a fence, and a young catcher; the frying pan is hollow and dark inside, with an unused watering can on the grass behind its holder; the man on the heavy seesaw has no book and looks up at the child, on a seat with a back; the string of the kite in the tree lies on the grass; the figures at the pond stand lower in front of it; and in a park a blown cap is off the pond. What the readers did not take is not in the release: a pan that is pale inside was a grey watering can in four readings of ten. The release is like for like: the priors of the planner were left alone and every record of v0.56 was built again, so that 65 of its 126 pictures came back to the pixel and 17 more within 24 pixels, 41 were drawn again or moved with their own templates, three were replaced and ten probes were added. All 64 templates pass the gate, for the first time since v0.53. The four that v0.56 had under 1.00 read 1.00: the game of catch in six pictures, the frying pan in five, the heavy seesaw in five and the man who sits on nothing in three. Three are under 1.00: the square wheel at 0.967, where the small open wheel of the wheelbarrow, which this release did not change, lost two half marks in one garden, and two actions on one half mark each, where a scorer who now sees the names each figure may be called by marked a man called a boy. Where the man on the seesaw sits is not settled: nine bench readers of ten have him in front of his seat. Nothing was mended, swapped or read again after the screen. The overall score of the set is 0.971, for 0.975, and each of its eight items under full marks is the clouds or a tree. With the 57 full marks that the scorers flagged as standing beside another reading halved, 54 of the 64 pass. All 19 pairs of a template and an angle an animal is drawn at pass. Probe pictures put every template in front of the readers at least three times, and every angle at least twice.

Combinatorics

How many different scenes the generator can make

Structures are template sets that fit a tier's plan and can reach its content band. Signatures are what the difficulty model sees (item types, chain lengths, scored setting entities); structures sharing a signature are candidate isomorphs. Totals multiply structures by cast assignments, expressions, facing, mirroring and optional features; positions are continuous and not counted.

Action library

actions, each with its own props and anchors

Lower-body postures combined with upper-body activities. Props are built for the pose and parented to the hand or head, so scenes can attach water, strings, leashes and ropes to the right point. These are the actions the templates use; more stay in the cast for future templates and are left out until one uses them. Earlier drawings that a refined pose replaced have been retired from the cast.
