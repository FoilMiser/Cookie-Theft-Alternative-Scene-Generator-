Inspection and blind screen

What v0.42 fixed

Fresh readers, who never saw the scoring key, describe every picture the way a test participant would, and separate scorers mark each scored item as mentioned, half-mentioned, missed or misread. The screen has now run on thirty-nine releases, v0.4 to v0.42, and once more on the unchanged v0.9 pictures with fresh readers, to measure how much the readers themselves vary. v0.42 redraws the goat (and tethers it), both dogs, the begging and reading dogs' poses, the trousers' waist, the shoes and the woman in yellow's hair, and turns the songbirds and the duck to 45. None was misread and 63 of 64 templates pass, as does every angle an animal is drawn at; the trip falls, on one reader. The goat was out of place twice where it was seven times, and the begging dog knelt in none of its pictures; the woman in yellow was still "girl" in six of her fourteen. Probe pictures put every template in front of the readers at least three times, and every angle at least twice.

Combinatorics

How many different scenes the generator can make

Structures are template sets that fit a tier's plan and can reach its content band. Signatures are what the difficulty model sees (item types, chain lengths, scored setting entities); structures sharing a signature are candidate isomorphs. Totals multiply structures by cast assignments, expressions, facing, mirroring and optional features; positions are continuous and not counted.

Action library

actions, each with its own props and anchors

Lower-body postures combined with upper-body activities. Props are built for the pose and parented to the hand or head, so scenes can attach water, strings, leashes and ropes to the right point. These are the actions the templates use; more stay in the cast for future templates and are left out until one uses them. Earlier drawings that a refined pose replaced have been retired from the cast.
