Inspection and blind screen

What v0.39 fixed

Fresh readers, who never saw the scoring key, describe every picture the way a test participant would, and separate scorers mark each scored item as mentioned, half-mentioned, missed or misread. The screen has now run on thirty-six releases, v0.4 to v0.39, and once more on the unchanged v0.9 pictures with fresh readers, to measure how much the readers themselves vary. v0.38 passed all 64 templates, and its list began with autumn leaves swept under green trees. v0.39 redraws the cast in one round, benched in three: the seams where parts overlap, feet and ankles, the waist, the man in red as a grown man, the animals' legs, paws and builds. It turns the animals of eight templates from profile, stops the catch's ball short of the catcher's hands and draws the heaps for summer. None was misread and all 64 templates pass, as does every angle an animal is drawn at, though the scorers called 11 of their full marks hedged. With the cast redrawn no picture could be read twice unchanged; on 7 with the same seed and events in both releases, all 75 items scored the same. Probe pictures put every template in front of the readers at least three times, and every angle at least twice.

Combinatorics

How many different scenes the generator can make

Structures are template sets that fit a tier's plan and can reach its content band. Signatures are what the difficulty model sees (item types, chain lengths, scored setting entities); structures sharing a signature are candidate isomorphs. Totals multiply structures by cast assignments, expressions, facing, mirroring and optional features; positions are continuous and not counted.

Action library

actions, each with its own props and anchors

Lower-body postures combined with upper-body activities. Props are built for the pose and parented to the hand or head, so scenes can attach water, strings, leashes and ropes to the right point. These are the actions the templates use; more stay in the cast for future templates and are left out until one uses them. Earlier drawings that a refined pose replaced have been retired from the cast.
