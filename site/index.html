Inspection and blind screen

What v0.28 fixed

Fresh readers, who never saw the scoring key, describe every picture the way a test participant would, and separate scorers mark each scored item as mentioned, half-mentioned, missed or misread. The screen has now run on twenty-five releases, v0.4 to v0.28, and once more on the unchanged v0.9 pictures with fresh readers, to measure how much the readers themselves vary. v0.27 left one template short of the gate, a begging dog with no clear claim on anyone's food, and its readers remarked most on wide shadows and a fallen ice cream like a flying saucer. v0.28 moves the dog beside the eater, draws shadows from the figure's own feet, bursts the ice cream into a splat and puts the ball on a real branch, each drawing chosen on the isolation bench and read blind in whole test scenes before the set was rebuilt. The dog now reads in full and none of the items was misread, and readers' remarks fell by 12%, but three other templates fell under the gate, one of them perhaps because of the smaller shadows. Probe pictures put every template in front of the readers at least three times.

Combinatorics

How many different scenes the generator can make

Structures are template sets that fit a tier's plan and can reach its content band. Signatures are what the difficulty model sees (item types, chain lengths, scored setting entities); structures sharing a signature are candidate isomorphs. Totals multiply structures by cast assignments, expressions, facing, mirroring and optional features; positions are continuous and not counted.

Action library

actions, each with its own props and anchors

Lower-body postures combined with upper-body activities. Props are built for the pose and parented to the hand or head, so scenes can attach water, strings, leashes and ropes to the right point. These are the actions the templates use; more stay in the cast for future templates and are left out until one uses them. Earlier drawings that a refined pose replaced have been retired from the cast.
