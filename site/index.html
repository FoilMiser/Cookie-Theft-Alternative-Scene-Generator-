Inspection and blind screen

What v0.47 changed

Fresh readers, who never saw the scoring key, describe every picture the way a test participant would, and separate scorers mark each scored item as mentioned, half-mentioned, missed or misread. The screen has now run on forty-four releases, v0.4 to v0.47, and once more on the unchanged v0.9 pictures with fresh readers, to measure how much the readers themselves vary. v0.47 began from a study of one figure, the woman in the yellow dress, read as a drawing by three reviewers. Sloping shoulders, limbs that taper, a hand with a thumb and a shoe on a sole came of it for every person, and one whole eye in a side view for the animals. The weak actions of v0.46 were then drawn again on a blind bench, in five rounds, each read on its own before it went into a scene. It is a stronger screen than v0.46's, and one template fails again. The set's overall score is 0.991, from 0.957, and 63 of the 64 templates pass the gate, from 60. The reading dog, the man sweeping with a fork and the stopped hose, which failed then, are read in full in five pictures each. The walker about to trip fails the same way as before, on one picture in which the reader had him going the other way, and heads the list for v0.48. Nothing was mended, swapped or read again after the screen. The scorers were asked this time to list their judgement calls, and with the 41 full marks that stand beside another reading halved, 56 of the 64 pass. All 19 pairs of a template and an angle an animal is drawn at pass. Probe pictures put every template in front of the readers at least three times, and every angle at least twice.

Combinatorics

How many different scenes the generator can make

Structures are template sets that fit a tier's plan and can reach its content band. Signatures are what the difficulty model sees (item types, chain lengths, scored setting entities); structures sharing a signature are candidate isomorphs. Totals multiply structures by cast assignments, expressions, facing, mirroring and optional features; positions are continuous and not counted.

Action library

actions, each with its own props and anchors

Lower-body postures combined with upper-body activities. Props are built for the pose and parented to the hand or head, so scenes can attach water, strings, leashes and ropes to the right point. These are the actions the templates use; more stay in the cast for future templates and are left out until one uses them. Earlier drawings that a refined pose replaced have been retired from the cast.
