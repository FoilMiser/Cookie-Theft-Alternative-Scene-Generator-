Inspection and blind screen

What v0.24 fixed

Fresh readers, who never saw the scoring key, describe every picture the way a test participant would, and separate scorers mark each scored item as mentioned, half-mentioned, missed or misread. The screen has now run on twenty-one releases, v0.4 to v0.24, and once more on the unchanged v0.9 pictures with fresh readers, to measure how much the readers themselves vary. v0.23 reached the ceiling, every item at full marks, and left only oddities: a sleeping cat right behind a walking man's foot, a teenager no reader called a teenager, and figures at the back drawn as large as those in front. v0.24 redraws the teenager and the slide's arms, each chosen on the isolation bench from two or more drawings, and adds placement rules: sleeping animals clear of any walker's feet, and nobody in the last strip of ground before the horizon in the park and on the playground. The bench also ruled one out: raising the seesaw's riders read worse. Each change was read blind in whole test scenes first, then the set was rebuilt. Probe pictures put every template in front of the readers at least three times.

Combinatorics

How many different scenes the generator can make

Structures are template sets that fit a tier's plan and can reach its content band. Signatures are what the difficulty model sees (item types, chain lengths, scored setting entities); structures sharing a signature are candidate isomorphs. Totals multiply structures by cast assignments, expressions, facing, mirroring and optional features; positions are continuous and not counted.

Action library

actions, each with its own props and anchors

Lower-body postures combined with upper-body activities. Props are built for the pose and parented to the hand or head, so scenes can attach water, strings, leashes and ropes to the right point. These are the actions the templates use; more stay in the cast for future templates and are left out until one uses them. Earlier drawings that a refined pose replaced have been retired from the cast.
