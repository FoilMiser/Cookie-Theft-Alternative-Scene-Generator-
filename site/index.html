Inspection and blind screen

What v0.45 fixed

Fresh readers, who never saw the scoring key, describe every picture the way a test participant would, and separate scorers mark each scored item as mentioned, half-mentioned, missed or misread. The screen has now run on forty-two releases, v0.4 to v0.45, and once more on the unchanged v0.9 pictures with fresh readers, to measure how much the readers themselves vary. v0.45 draws the ice cream half as big again in a child's hand, gives the camera a strap, the painter a roller on a wire frame, the planter a trowel and the kite flier a reel, puts food on the picnic blanket and the swimming duck in the water, and lets the lost balloon's string hang well above the reaching hand. The ice cream, v0.44's one failure, reads in full in fourteen pictures. The reading dog's paper, read once as up in the air, was drawn onto its paws after the screen and read again, in full. All 64 templates pass, and 21 of the 22 pairs of a template and an angle an animal is drawn at: the thief bird in profile is just under, at 0.889. Probe pictures put every template in front of the readers at least three times, and every angle at least twice.

Combinatorics

How many different scenes the generator can make

Structures are template sets that fit a tier's plan and can reach its content band. Signatures are what the difficulty model sees (item types, chain lengths, scored setting entities); structures sharing a signature are candidate isomorphs. Totals multiply structures by cast assignments, expressions, facing, mirroring and optional features; positions are continuous and not counted.

Action library

actions, each with its own props and anchors

Lower-body postures combined with upper-body activities. Props are built for the pose and parented to the hand or head, so scenes can attach water, strings, leashes and ropes to the right point. These are the actions the templates use; more stay in the cast for future templates and are left out until one uses them. Earlier drawings that a refined pose replaced have been retired from the cast.
