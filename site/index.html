Inspection and blind screen

What v0.37 fixed

Fresh readers, who never saw the scoring key, describe every picture the way a test participant would, and separate scorers mark each scored item as mentioned, half-mentioned, missed or misread. The screen has now run on thirty-four releases, v0.4 to v0.37, and once more on the unchanged v0.9 pictures with fresh readers, to measure how much the readers themselves vary. v0.36 passed all 64 templates, and left a gate's knob behind a sweeper's head read as a bun a third time, a storm pointer standing on a dustpan, an empty washing line offered as what she points at, and a stall's leg standing on a crate. v0.37 changes no drawing: it keeps every figure's drawing off the knob, the sweepers' heaps and dustpan clear of others' feet, the clothesline out of a storm pointer's garden and the crate clear of the leg, each rule tried first on the picture that asked for it and read blind in whole scenes before the set was rebuilt. None was misread and 63 of 64 templates pass: the one failure, hanging the washing up, is on a picture identical to v0.36's, whose reader could not tell up from down. 75 pictures were read twice with nothing changed, and 775 of their 783 items scored the same. Probe pictures put every template in front of the readers at least three times.

Combinatorics

How many different scenes the generator can make

Structures are template sets that fit a tier's plan and can reach its content band. Signatures are what the difficulty model sees (item types, chain lengths, scored setting entities); structures sharing a signature are candidate isomorphs. Totals multiply structures by cast assignments, expressions, facing, mirroring and optional features; positions are continuous and not counted.

Action library

actions, each with its own props and anchors

Lower-body postures combined with upper-body activities. Props are built for the pose and parented to the hand or head, so scenes can attach water, strings, leashes and ropes to the right point. These are the actions the templates use; more stay in the cast for future templates and are left out until one uses them. Earlier drawings that a refined pose replaced have been retired from the cast.
