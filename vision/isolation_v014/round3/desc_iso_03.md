# iso_03

## What I see
A playground seesaw on green grass: a red plank on a dark gray triangular pivot, with a dark metal railing fence behind it and a brown post or tree trunk at the right edge. An older man with gray hair, a blue cap, a white shirt, a brown vest and dark trousers sits on the low left end, reading an open book and not looking at the other rider. A girl with dark hair in a bun, wearing a pink dress and yellow shoes, sits on the raised right end holding a black handle. Curved motion lines under her feet suggest her legs are dangling or kicking in the air. Because of the tilt, the man's end is on the ground and the girl is held up high while he reads.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl's expression is small and hard to read. It could be surprise or worry about being stuck up high, or just a neutral look.
- The motion lines under her feet could mean she is swinging her legs, or that the seesaw is bouncing or wobbling.
- Where the man meets the plank is hard to read. The left end of the plank seems to run through or behind his legs and lap rather than clearly under his seat, so he looks partly merged with the plank. His hand near the book may be holding the plank or the book's edge.
- The man's end has no visible handle (the girl's end does). It could be hidden behind him or just not drawn.
- A large dark circular outline is cut off at the top center-right. It could be a tree canopy, a sign, a ball or a wheel; I can't tell which.
- The man has an oval ground shadow, but the pivot and the girl's raised end have none. Her missing shadow is less expected, though it isn't very noticeable.
- The darker green band above the fence could be a hedge or more grass.
