# iso_12

## What I see
A red bicycle stands alone on plain green ground with a soft shadow under it. No one is riding it. It has a black saddle, curved dark handlebars, a red frame, and a chainring with a grey crank and pedal. Both wheels are square: dark square frames with spokes running out from the center hub, not round wheels. A bicycle is for riding, but with square wheels this one could not roll properly.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The square wheels are the main oddity. They look clearly deliberate, not like a drawing mistake, but they are the one thing that makes the object "wrong."
- The long grey bar going down and to the left from the crank to near the ground has two possible readings. It could be a pedal crank arm with a pedal at the end, or it could be a kickstand. It looks longer than a normal crank arm.
- There is a small grey piece to the right of the chainring. It could be the second pedal or part of the derailleur. I can't tell which.
- The bike seems to stand upright with nothing holding it, unless the grey bar is a kickstand.
- The parts of the frame near the rear wheel are a little crowded, and it is hard to see exactly where the frame tubes meet the square rear wheel.
