# iso_05

## What I see
A young person stands on a winding beige footpath in a green park. They have short brown hair and wear a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white sneakers. Both arms are stretched straight up with the hands open. Above them, a blue ball with a white curved stripe sits on a branch inside the leafy canopy of a large tree. The ball seems to be stuck there, and the person seems to be reaching toward it. A wooden park bench is on the grass at the left. Green hills and a little sky are in the background.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- **What the raised arms mean:** Reading 1, the person is reaching for or trying to knock down the ball stuck in the tree. Reading 2, they have just thrown the ball, or are waving or cheering. Their hands are far below the ball, so they could not actually reach it.
- **Where they are looking:** The head is turned mostly to the right and fairly level. It doesn't clearly tip up toward the ball, so the gaze could be read as looking sideways rather than up. Their small open mouth could mean surprise or concentration.
- **The ball:** Its stripe makes it look like a beach ball or a sports ball. It sits partly in front of the leaves, and the branch runs right along its underside, so it reads as resting on top of the branch. At a glance it could also look like it is floating in front of the tree.
- **The bench:** It is cut off at the left edge, so only part of it shows.
- **Proportions:** The legs are fairly long and thin compared with the torso, and the feet stand right at the edge where the path meets the grass. Nothing else looks clearly broken or overlapping.
