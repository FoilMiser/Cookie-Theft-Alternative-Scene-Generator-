# iso_14

## What I see
A girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes is flying a kite in a back garden. She stands on the lawn holding the string up with one hand and looking up at the kite. The kite is yellow and diamond-shaped, with a tail of red and blue bows, and it flies high in a blue sky with white clouds. Behind her is a tall wooden plank fence with green trees or a hedge above it. Also on the lawn are two terracotta pots with leafy green plants, a grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles, and a teal watering can cut off at the bottom-right edge.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- There is a dark green rounded shape just below the bottom of the fence near the middle. It could be a bush in front of the fence whose top is hidden, but that would mean the fence covers something in front of it. It could also be a bush behind the fence showing underneath, but the fence seems to reach the ground. Either way it looks like it is hanging from the bottom edge of the fence.
- The wheelbarrow looks odd. Its handles point up diagonally into the air instead of resting on the ground, and no one is holding them. It could also be read as a cart or a tipped-over barrow.
- At the far right edge, above the hedge, there is a small brown and red angled shape. It could be the corner of a house roof, a shed or a birdhouse. I can't tell which.
- The string and the bow tail both come off the kite's bottom point. The string is a long, gently curved line, which is plausible but slightly unusual for a taut kite string.
- One cloud is partly cut off at the top-left corner.
