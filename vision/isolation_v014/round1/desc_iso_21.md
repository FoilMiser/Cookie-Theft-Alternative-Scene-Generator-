# iso_21

## What I see
A sunny town square in front of a row of pastel shop buildings with striped awnings, under a blue sky with a sun, two clouds and white wind lines. In the centre an older woman with white hair, glasses, a teal cardigan and a blue-grey skirt stands with both arms raised high. She has a surprised, open-mouthed face and is reaching toward an orange hat (it looks like a cap or sun hat) that is flying through the air above and to the right of her. Wind lines and a few green and brown leaves blow past the hat. Around the square are a fruit stall with crates of green, yellow, orange and red fruit under a striped canopy, a cardboard box, a tall street lamp, blue and green rubbish bins, a potted plant, a red bicycle and a teal bicycle each parked in a grey U-shaped bike rack, a tree in a round planter, and two small round café tables with chairs.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- I am assuming the orange hat is the woman's because she has nothing on her head and is reaching for it, but nothing in the picture actually links it to her. At a glance its shape could also pass for a bird or a flying piece of fruit.
- Her raised arms can be read as reaching to catch the hat, or as a "hands up" or cheering pose. Her expression could be surprise or alarm.
- The white wind lines over the shop windows near the woman and over the tree canopy could be read as wind, or as streaks or reflections on the glass.
- Both bicycles sit inside the plane of their U-shaped racks rather than beside them. The rack loops overlap the bike frames, so each bike looks partly merged with its rack. The red bike also has its kickstand down while it is in the rack.
- The street lamp's head overlaps the upper-left window of the green building. It reads as standing in front of the building, but the overlap looks a little crowded.
- The fruit stall's canopy poles only show at the front, and the stall table has two visible legs.
- The woman's legs are long and thin compared with her body, though this fits the cartoon style. No objects are clearly floating apart from the hat and leaves, which are meant to be blowing in the wind.
