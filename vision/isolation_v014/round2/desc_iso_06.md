## What I see

At the top there are pale blue windows with teal and cream frames, and in front of them are two dark blue-gray planter boxes full of orange, pink, yellow and red flowers. In the middle, a teal bicycle is parked against a gray upside-down-U bike rack, so it seems to be locked or leaned there. A soft gray shadow lies on the ground under the bike. - The bike and the rack overlap in a confusing way. The rack's loop is behind the bike's frame, but its legs seem to pass through the wheel area. Near the inner edge of the right wheel there is a dark vertical shape that could be one rack leg, a kickstand, or a lock.
- It is hard to tell which end of the bike is the front. The dark part at the upper left could be the saddle or the handlebars, and the dark part near the top center-right could be the other one.
- The object in the bottom-right corner is too cut off to identify. It could be the leg of a chair or table, a post, or the edge of a sign.
