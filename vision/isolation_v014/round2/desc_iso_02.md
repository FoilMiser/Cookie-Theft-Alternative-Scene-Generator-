# iso_02

## What I see
In a grassy park, a man and a young girl are on a red seesaw with a grey triangular base. The bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers sits on the low right end with his feet on the ground, reading an open book and not looking at the girl. The girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes is up on the raised left end, holding a handle. Small motion lines under her feet suggest she is kicking or swinging her legs. Behind them are an empty wooden bench, a dark metal railing fence, a tree trunk at the left edge and a grey pole at the right edge.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl's motion lines can be read two ways. She could be kicking her legs impatiently or playfully because she is stuck in the air, or she could be bouncing and having fun.
- Her face is small, so her expression could be cheerful, or it could be waiting, puzzled or impatient.
- The man reading while on a seesaw reads as deliberately odd (an adult ignoring the game). It could also be read as him simply keeping her up there on purpose.
- The red bar seems to run behind or through the man's hips and lap rather than clearly under his seat. The end of the bar ends roughly at his back, so the overlap of his legs, book and bar is a little unclear.
- The grey pole on the right edge is cut off. It could be part of a swing frame, a lamp post or a sign post.
- The dark curved line at the top left could be the outline of a tree canopy or of some other round object outside the frame.
- The tilt of the seesaw (heavier adult down, lighter child up) looks physically consistent. Nothing appears to be floating.
