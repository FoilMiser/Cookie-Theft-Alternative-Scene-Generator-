- The grey hoop reads as a bike rack, but no lock or chain is visible. The bike could be parked against it or just standing in front of it. Its legs and arch overlap the frame, so it partly looks as if the rack passes through the bicycle and not beside it.
- At the bottom left of the frame there is a small dark shape. It could be a second pedal, a kickstand, or the foot of the rack.
