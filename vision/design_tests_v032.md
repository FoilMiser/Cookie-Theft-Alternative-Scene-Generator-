# v0.32 design tests

The new drawings and revision 30 were read blind in whole test scenes among other events: six pictures, six fresh readers, one picture each (`design_tests_v032/test_N.png`, `desc_test_N.md`; the prompts are `tpdb_work/dtp32/test_N.txt`, the reader template cut to one picture). `tools/cycle/design_tests.py` has the jobs. Each reader saw one picture and knew nothing of the others.

| Test | Picture | What it tests | What the reader said |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tier-3 playground | The girl stuck up high with the handle raised, the man reading on the low end | She "sits high up on the raised left end, holding the handle". The man as before: "his body overlaps the low end of the plank and his knees come up in front of it, so he might be sitting on the plank or just leaning or perched on its end" |
| 2 | Tier-3 playground | The bearded man on the low end, the girl, a box carried | The girl "holds its handle". Nothing on how the man sits. "a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks to the left carrying a taped cardboard box", and nothing floats |
| 3 | Tier-3 street | A ball stuck in a tree, a jump, a box carried | "He is not near the tree and does not seem to be looking at the ball." Of the jump to reach: "he is far from the tree and there is nothing above him, so (a) looks more likely", (a) being "jumping for joy or celebrating". The box: "a man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers carries a cardboard box" |
| 4 | Tier-3 garden | The hose watering under a clear sky | "sprays an arc of water onto a bed of colourful flowers", "a calm scene of garden chores". Nothing on the sky |
| 5 | Tier-3 park | A kite stuck in a tree, a jump, a box carried | The jump "could be for joy or cheering, or could be reacting to the fishing or the kite. It could also be reaching for something, but there is nothing above the child's hands." "walking to the right carrying a cardboard box" |
| 6 | Tier-3 garden | Watering with a can under a clear sky | "a stream of water is falling onto the flowers", "a calm, everyday scene". Nothing on the sky |

No reading called for a new rule. The girl reads as holding the handle in both seesaw pictures, neither jumper reads as reaching for the thing in the tree (both readers weigh it and set it aside), and no box floats. The man on the low end drew his remark in one of his two pictures, as the bench said he would.
