# v0.26 design tests

The new drawings and revision 24 were read blind in whole test scenes among other events: six pictures, three fresh readers, two pictures each in different settings (`design_tests_v026/test_N.png`, `desc_test_N.md`; `map.json` names the records). `tools/cycle/design_tests.py` lists what each forces. No new rule came out of them, so there was no second round.

## What this says

- **The older man.** Named an old or older man in a cap in both pictures, and his face drew a remark only on the seesaw, against ten of his 21 pictures in v0.25. The screen will count it.
- **The dog at the door.** Read at once as waiting for a walk, with open eyes.
- **The newspaper dogs.** Both read as dogs reading. The white one, "an odd white figure ... ghostly" in the v0.25 design tests, is now a "white dog with brown patches". Their legs still draw remarks ("its back legs splay forward").
- **The swept leaves.** Read in all three pictures. Twice a reader asked where they came from, as a tree is green; that is a small price for sweeping that reads, and goes on the list.
- **The lamp post.** No reader had a figure growing out of it or about to walk into it. The remarks now are that it "looks very tall" and stands alone on the grass.
- **The teenager.** A boy or a man in a red cap in every picture, and no head on the wrong way round.
