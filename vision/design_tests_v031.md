# v0.31 design tests

The new drawings and revision 29 were read blind in whole test scenes among other events: six pictures, six fresh readers, one picture each (`design_tests_v031/test_N.png`, `desc_test_N.md`). `tools/cycle/design_tests.py` has the jobs. Each reader saw one picture and knew nothing of the others.

| Test | Picture | What it tests | What the reader said |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tier-3 playground | A man on the seesaw's low end, a girl stuck up high | "Sitting on the low left end of the seesaw". Below: "It is unclear how the man is actually sitting", "the plank seems to pass behind or through his legs and lower body" |
| 2 | Tier-3 playground | Two children on the seesaw | "Sitting on the low left end of the seesaw", and nothing on how they sit on the plank. "The handles are hard to see" |
| 3 | Tier-3 garden | Washing being hung, the reel and the end of its hose off the bed | The reel "very close to the house step and flower bed, which is a little crowded, but no objects seem to pass through each other". And "he could be hanging the T-shirt up, or taking it down off the line", under a clear sky |
| 4 | Tier-3 garden | Storm clouds, no one at the washing, the reel | "Garden hose on a reel", "not in use". Nothing on the bed or the step |
| 5 | Tier-3 park | The bearded man's vest | "White shirt, brown vest". Nothing on the vest |
| 6 | Tier-3 playground | The bearded man on the seesaw, a castle | "He appears to sit somewhat in front of or across the beam", "it looks a little like the beam passes through or behind his legs". The castle: "possibly a small spade or toy tool", and "the knee and hands sit very tight against the castle" |

What this says:

- **The children are done, the man is not.** Two children on the plank drew nothing on how they sit, in a picture where the reader looked hard at everything else. Both men on the low end still drew the remark, in nearly v0.30's words. Both readers sat them on the low end, so nothing scored turns on it. It stays on the list as a matter of the pose, which puts a knee up and a shin across a rising plank.
- **The reel is off the bed and the step**, in two gardens.
- **The vest is gone as a remark**, in the one picture that shows it standing, and in test 6.
- **Taking the storm away did not settle the washing.** One reader offers "taking it down off the line" under a clear sky too. So the half mark v0.30 blamed on the sky may be partly the drawing, which shows a hand on a shirt on a line and no basket. The rule stays, as washing under storm clouds is still an odd thing to show, and the screen will say how the item reads.

No reading called for a new rule, and no scene was rebuilt.
