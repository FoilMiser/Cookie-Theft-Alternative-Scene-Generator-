## What I see
A backyard with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a paved path, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. Three dark grey storm clouds hang in a grey sky. A girl in a pink dress stands on the lawn, looking up toward the clouds with one arm stretched out to the side, palm out, as if checking for rain. Around her are a swing set with an empty red seat, a teal watering can, a grey wheelbarrow, a flower bed with colourful flowers, and a teal hose reel next to the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl's outstretched hand could be feeling for raindrops or pointing toward the wheelbarrow or watering can. No rain is drawn, so it is not clear whether it has started raining.
- Her face looks worried or unhappy, but the face is small and hard to read clearly.
- The wheelbarrow looks odd: its legs and handle seem tangled together under the tub, and the handle comes out at a strange angle on the left. It reads more like a jumble of parts than a clean wheelbarrow.
- The swing seat hangs on two thin ropes that are hard to see against the fence.
- The hose reel is partly behind the edge of the flower bed. It is not clear whether it is in front of or inside the bed.
