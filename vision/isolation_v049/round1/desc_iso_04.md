## What I see
This is a back garden under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds. A girl in a pink dress stands on the lawn, pointing up at the clouds with a worried or frowning face, as if she has noticed that rain is coming. Around her are a metal swing set with one red seat, a teal watering can, a grey wheelbarrow, a flower bed with many coloured flowers, and a teal garden hose on a reel. On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door. On the left is a paved path leading to a wooden gate in a tall wooden fence, with hedges and a tree behind.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The wheelbarrow looks oddly built: its wooden handle seems to run behind or through the grey tray, and the dark leg and wheel strut underneath look stubby and awkwardly joined, so it reads partly as a wheelbarrow and partly as a jumble of parts.
- The girl's expression could be worry about the storm, or simple curiosity while pointing at the sky.
- The hose reel sits half in front of the flower-bed edge; it is unclear whether it is on the grass or overlapping the soil.
- The swing frame's legs splay out at a steep angle, and the inner rear leg appears to cross close to the swing rope, which looks slightly tangled.
- This is a full scene, not a small cut-out.
