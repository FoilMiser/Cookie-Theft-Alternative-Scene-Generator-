# Review of pair_6: row a vs row b

Note: the image is small (about 190 px per head), so fine contour differences are judged at that size.

## 1. How the rows differ

Row a draws the lower face as a taper: the jaw narrows toward a softer, slightly pointed chin, and the necks are a little slimmer. Row b keeps the jaw wider further down and ends in a broader, squarer or rounder chin with slightly thicker necks. The difference is strongest on the men (front, turned, side and bearded) and slight on the women and the child.

## 2. Figure by figure (left to right)

1. **Woman, front: a.** Her tapered chin in a reads as softer and more feminine; b's wider lower face is fine but a little heavy for her.
2. **Woman, turned: no difference.** The chin and jaw contour are nearly the same at this size; neither reads better.
3. **Man, front: b.** The wider jaw and broader neck in b read as a grown man, while a's narrowing jaw makes him look younger and slightly egg-shaped.
4. **Man, turned: b.** b's fuller, squarer chin under the moustache gives a solid adult jaw; in a the jaw slopes to a small point and the face looks thin.
5. **Man, side: b.** In a the chin juts forward to a point with the moustache perched on the tip, so the profile looks beak-like; b's chin is blunter and the jaw-to-neck line is cleaner.
6. **Older woman: no difference.** Both rows give her a rounded lower face under the glasses that reads clearly as an older woman; the change is very slight.
7. **Bearded man: b.** b's broad, square jaw carries the beard and reads as a sturdy adult man; a's narrower jaw makes the beard look pasted onto a slimmer face.
8. **Child: no difference.** The round face, small features and short neck read as a child in both rows; I cannot see a meaningful change.

## 3. Faults

**Row a**
- Man, front: the jaw tapers too much, giving an egg-shaped face that reads younger than an adult.
- Man, turned: chin narrows to a small point; face looks thin.
- Man, side: chin juts forward to a point with the moustache on its tip; beak-like profile.
- Bearded man: jaw too narrow for the beard; less adult-looking.

**Row b**
- Woman, front: lower face slightly wide and heavy for a woman.
- Man, side: the hair edge behind the ear ends in a hard vertical line next to the jaw (also present in a, but more noticeable against b's wider jaw).
- Older woman: lower face slightly fuller and rounder, a touch heavy (minor).

## 4. Choice

**Row b, fairly sure.** For a friendly, clear style where grown-ups must read as grown-ups, b's broader jaws make the four men clearly adult and fix the pointed side profile, while the child stays clearly a child in both rows. Row a's only real advantage is the slightly softer front-view woman, which is a small gain against b's clearer men.

Counts: a = 1, b = 4, no difference = 3.
