# Review of pair_4.png (heads, row a vs row b)

## 1. How the rows differ

The two rows are very close. The clearest change is on the men: row a gives them a narrow, tapering V-shaped lower face that ends in a small pointed chin, and row b gives them a broader, squarer jaw with a fuller, rounder chin. On the women and the child the difference is small or not visible at this size.

## 2. Figure by figure (left to right)

1. **Woman, front**: no difference. Both rows show the same soft oval face and slim neck; I cannot see a change.
2. **Woman, turned**: no difference. Jaw, chin and neck look the same in both rows.
3. **Man, front**: b. Row a's face narrows to a pointed chin that looks elfin and a little young; row b's wider jaw reads clearly as a grown man.
4. **Man, turned**: b. In row b the jaw carries more weight below the ear and the head sits better on the neck; row a's jaw tapers to a thin point.
5. **Man, side**: b. Row a's chin is a small pointed hook under the moustache; row b's lower face is fuller and more adult, though it looks a little heavy.
6. **Older woman**: no difference. Row b's jaw may be very slightly wider, but at this size the two look the same.
7. **Bearded man**: b. In row b the beard sits on a broad jaw and the head looks solid; in row a the jaw narrows under the beard and the face looks pinched.
8. **Child**: no difference. The round head, wide cheeks and short neck look the same in both rows.

## 3. Faults

**Row a**
- Man, front: the V-shaped jaw and pointed chin make the face look narrow and boyish under adult features (moustache).
- Man, turned: the jaw tapers to a thin point and the line from the ear down to the collar makes the neck and jaw look like one long wedge.
- Man, side: the chin is a small pointed hook, weak for a grown man.
- Bearded man: the jaw narrows under the beard, so the lower face looks pinched.

**Row b**
- Man, side: the fuller lower face looks slightly heavy or blocky in profile.
- Man, turned: the broader jaw makes the neck look a little short.
- Child: no clear change from row a, so row b adds nothing to set the child apart from the adults (the child is still told apart mainly by the smaller face and the hair).

## 4. Choice

**Row b**, fairly sure. Its broader jaws make the men read clearly as grown men, while the women, older woman and child stay as friendly as in row a. Row a's pointed chins make the men look younger and less sturdy. The two rows are close, and four of eight figures show no visible difference, so this is not "sure".
