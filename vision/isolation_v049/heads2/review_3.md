# Review of pair_3.png (heads, row a vs row b)

Note: judged from the single image at its native size; the differences are small and some are near the limit of what this resolution shows.

## 1. How the rows differ

Row a draws the jaws broader and rounder: the adult men have wide, square-ish jaws that meet a fairly wide neck, and the chins sit higher. Row b tapers the jaw more toward the chin, so chins sit a little lower and narrower, and the turned and side heads get a cleaner, more continuous jaw line running back into the neck. On the woman from the front, the older woman and the child the two rows look almost the same.

## 2. Figure by figure (left to right)

1. **Woman, front** - no difference. Head, chin and neck read the same in both rows.
2. **Woman, turned** - a. Row a's chin is round and soft; row b's chin drops lower and comes to more of a point, which lengthens the face without making it clearer.
3. **Man, front** - a. Row a's broad, square-cornered jaw and wider neck read clearly as a grown man; row b's jaw narrows toward the chin and makes the face look slightly softer and younger.
4. **Man, turned** - b. Row a has an awkward step in the jaw line near the ear, while row b's jaw runs in one smooth line from chin to neck.
5. **Man, side** - b. Row a's profile is a rounded shape with a notch near the ear and little sense of chin; row b gives a distinct chin and a jaw line that slopes back to the neck, so it reads as a profile.
6. **Older woman** - no difference. The glasses, hair and rounded jaw look the same in both rows.
7. **Bearded man** - a. Row a's wide jaw and wide beard band look sturdy and adult; row b's tapered jaw squeezes the beard into a narrower block at the chin.
8. **Child** - no difference. The round face and small chin read as a child in both rows.

## 3. Faults

**Row a**
- Man, turned: step or notch in the jaw line just in front of the ear.
- Man, side: profile is a rounded mass with no clear chin, and the jaw-to-neck join is vague.

**Row b**
- Woman, turned: chin is longer and more pointed than the front view of the same woman, so the two views do not quite match.
- Man, front: jaw tapers toward the chin and softens the adult male look.
- Bearded man: narrowed jaw pinches the beard into a small block at the chin.
- Man, side: the jaw line continues down across the neck, which reads as a slightly stray line.

## 4. Choice

**Row a**, **toss-up**. For a friendly, clear style in which grown-ups must read as grown-ups, row a's broad jaws on the front-facing man and the bearded man do that job better, and its rounder chins are friendlier. The child reads as a child in both rows. Row b clearly draws the turned and side jaws better, though. The front views carry more weight, so a is ahead, but only just; the better result might be row a's front views with row b's turned and side jaw lines.
