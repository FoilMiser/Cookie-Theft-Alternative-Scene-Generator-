# v0.25 design tests

The new drawings were read blind in whole test scenes among other events: six pictures, three fresh readers, two pictures each in different settings (`design_tests_v025/test_N.png`, `desc_test_N.md`; `map.json` names the records). `tools/cycle/design_tests.py` lists what each forces. No new rule came out of them, so there was no second round.

| Test | Picture | What it tests | What the reader said |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tier-4 park | The older man's bag, the sandwich bird, the tan newspaper dog | "Carrying a yellow shopping bag"; "a blue bird ... with its beak at the food ... about to eat or take her lunch"; "white paws ... holding an open newspaper in its paws as if reading" |
| 2 | Tier-3 street | The bag on another man beside a box carrier, the sandwich bird | "Carrying a yellow shopping bag ... seems to be his shopping"; "pecking at or about to steal her lunch" |
| 3 | Tier-3 garden | The roller, the barrow's shadow | "A long-handled paint roller"; the barrow "empty ... ready to take the leaves away", nothing about its shadow |
| 4 | Tier-3 garden | The paw-printed fence, the roller, the barrow | "Pressing a long-handled roller or brush against the fence"; nothing about the barrow's shadow |
| 5 | Tier-4 street | The white newspaper dog, the bag, the sandwich bird | "Carrying a yellow shopping bag"; "pecking at or about to steal the sandwich". "An odd white figure, like a white dog or a person drawn all in white ... left uncoloured or ... a ghostly character" |
| 6 | Tier-3 garden | The square-wheeled barrow, the roller | "The wheelbarrow's wheel looks wrong because it is drawn as a square"; "a roller or wide brush on a long handle" |

## What this says

- **The bag.** Named a shopping bag in all three pictures with no hedge, on the older man too, whose bag failed the gate in v0.24.
- **The bird.** Read as a bird at the food in all three, none calling it oversized or a kettle.
- **The roller.** A roller in two and "a roller or brush" in the others, each against the fence.
- **The barrow's shadow.** No remark in any of the three pictures, and the square wheel still read.
- **The newspaper dog.** The tan dog read in full with its own paws on the paper. The white dog, small on pale paving in the busiest picture, read as "like a white dog or a person", a drawing that has not changed colour. One reader, so no rule; left for the screen to count.
