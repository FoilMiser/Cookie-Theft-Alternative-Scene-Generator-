# iso_24

## What I see
A white goat with a red collar and dark curved horns stands on a plain green background. It bends its neck and head steeply down toward a small tuft of dark green grass in front of it, as if grazing or about to eat the grass. Its short tail sticks up and it has a soft grey shadow under it. It has small dark blue-grey hooves and a pointed grey beard under its chin.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The muzzle is very close to the grass but does not clearly touch it. It could be eating or only sniffing or about to bite.
- The pointed grey shape under the chin reads as a goat beard. It could also be read as a tongue or a beak-like point, because it sticks out sharply to the lower left.
- There are small dark marks on top of the back just behind the collar. They could be a spot of dark fur, or a stray ear or horn piece that sits away from the head.
- The legs look awkward. The hind legs bend sharply under the body and the front legs angle back under the chest, so the stance looks cramped and somewhat unnatural. It is hard to tell which hoof belongs to which leg, and only about two hooves are clearly visible.
- The neck bends down very steeply and the head looks small for the long body.
