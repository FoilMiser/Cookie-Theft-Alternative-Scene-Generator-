# iso_23

## What I see
A white goat with dark grey, backward-curving horns and grey hooves stands on plain green grass. Its head is lowered to a small tuft of dark green grass on the left, and its mouth touches the grass, so it looks like it is grazing. It wears a red collar. A thin tan rope runs from the collar down to the ground and over to a short brown wooden stake, so the goat seems to be tethered. A soft grey shadow lies under the goat.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The rope looks stiff instead of slack. It runs as two straight segments, from the collar down to the ground and then across to the stake, with a sharp V-shaped bend where it meets the grass. It looks more like a rigid wire or stick than a loose rope. It also runs across the front of the goat's chest and front-leg area, so it might be passing in front of or through the near front leg.
- The thin tan line could be read two ways: as a tether rope, or as a long stick or tool leaning against the goat.
- The body is a large, rounded, high-backed mass, and the head and front legs look small next to it. The hind legs are sharply bent, so the goat looks a little crouched or hunched at the back.
- The small upright tuft at the rear is presumably the tail, but it could be mistaken for an ear or a horn stub.
- There is a faint green mark at the muzzle. It could be grass in the goat's mouth or just part of the grass tuft overlapping the face.
