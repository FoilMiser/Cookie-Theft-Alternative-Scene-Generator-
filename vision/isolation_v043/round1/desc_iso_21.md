# iso_21

## What I see
A sunny town street or plaza with a row of colourful two-storey shops with striped awnings, a blue sky, two clouds and a sun in the top right corner. In the centre a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is bent slightly forward, sweeping a small pile of brown leaves with a yellow broom. To her left a small girl in a pink dress is jumping with both arms raised and a big smile, and there is a shadow on the ground below her, so she reads as being in the air. Around them are a tree in a round planter, a tall black street lamp, a window box of flowers, a market stall with a teal-and-white awning and crates of red, orange, yellow and green fruit or vegetables, a closed wooden crate on the ground, a grey trash bin, and two potted plants at the bottom corners.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl's pose (arms up, eyes closed, smiling) could mean she is jumping for joy or cheering, or that she is about to jump into the leaf pile. It is unclear whether she is reacting to the woman or just playing.
- The leaves the woman is sweeping are a small dark brown clump. It could be read as dead leaves or as dirt or debris.
- The lone wooden crate at the right is closed and sits apart from the stall. It may be extra stock for the stall or just a box left on the pavement; its purpose is unclear.
- The trash bin is fairly small next to the people, though still believable.
- The street lamp stands well back on the pavement in front of the green building's awning and window. It looks fine, but it is very tall and thin.
- Nothing looks like it is floating except the jumping girl, and her shadow makes that look deliberate. I did not notice objects passing through each other or detached body parts.
