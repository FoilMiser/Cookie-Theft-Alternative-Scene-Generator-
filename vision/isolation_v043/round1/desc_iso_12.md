# iso_12

## What I see
A sunny town square paved with grey tiles, in front of a row of colourful two-storey shops with striped awnings. In the middle, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds a yellow broom with both hands and sweeps a small pile of brown leaves. To her left, a small girl in a pink dress jumps in the air with both arms raised and a happy face; her shadow is on the ground below her. Around the square: a tree in a round planter, a box of flowers, a black street lamp, a market stall with an awning and crates of red, orange, yellow and green fruit, a closed wooden crate, a grey rubbish bin, and two potted plants in the front corners. The sky has two clouds and a sun.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl is well above her shadow with both feet off the ground. It reads as a jump for joy, but the gap is large enough that she could look like she is floating. Her raised arms could mean cheering, waving, or reaching for something.
- The woman's head is turned left, so she could be watching the girl or looking down at the leaves she is sweeping.
- The brown pile at the broom could be read as dry leaves or as general dirt or rubbish.
- The fruit in the stall crates are plain coloured balls: red could be apples or tomatoes, orange could be oranges, yellow could be lemons, and green could be limes or green apples.
- The flower box sits on the pavement right at the base of a shop window. It could be a window box or a freestanding planter on the ground.
- The plain wooden crate on the right has no clear purpose. It could be a delivery or storage box, maybe for the fruit stall.
- The street lamp post is drawn in front of the green building's upper window and awning. This reads as overlap from depth and does not look like an error.
- Nothing looks clearly broken: limbs are attached normally, and the broom head meets the leaf pile at the woman's feet.
