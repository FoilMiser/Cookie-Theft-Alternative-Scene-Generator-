# iso_10

## What I see
A white cartoon goat with curved dark blue-grey horns and a red collar stands on plain green grass with its head lowered to a small tuft of dark green grass. It looks like it is grazing: a single green blade runs from the tuft up to its mouth. A thin tan line (a rope or tether) runs from the goat's chest/collar area down to the ground and then sideways to a short brown post stuck in the ground, so the goat seems to be tied to a stake. There is a soft darker-green shadow under the goat.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The tan line is drawn stiff and evenly curved, like a J-shaped hook or a walking-stick handle, not like a slack rope. It could be read as a rope tying the goat to the post, or as a separate rigid stick or cane leaning against the goat.
- The rope's top end is unclear. It seems to start at the chest just below the red collar rather than clearly tying onto the collar, and it runs down right next to and over the goat's front leg, so it partly merges with the leg outline.
- The brown post is small and stands slightly in front of the goat's legs. It could be a tether stake or a small stump.
- The green blade from the grass tuft to the mouth is thin and could be read as a blade being eaten or as a stray line.
- The goat's head is small and pushed far forward and down compared with its large body, but it reads well enough as a bending-down pose.
