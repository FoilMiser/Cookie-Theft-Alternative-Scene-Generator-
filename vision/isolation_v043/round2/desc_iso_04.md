# iso_04

## What I see
A tan-brown puppy with a darker brown ear and a red collar lies on its belly on green grass. One front paw rests on an open black-and-white newspaper lying flat on the ground, with headline bars and columns of text. In front of it, at the bottom, is a red dog bowl full of brown kibble. Behind it are a vertical brown post (its top is cut off by the frame) and terracotta flower pots, one only partly in view at the right edge.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- What the dog is doing with the newspaper can be read two ways. It may be "reading" or looking at the paper, or it may be pawing at it or about to tear it. Its eye and nose point forward and to the right, past the paper, not clearly down at it.
- The post could be a fence post, a sign post, a table leg or a clothesline pole. Too little of it shows to tell.
- The pots look nearly empty: only a thin green edge shows at the top, so it is unclear whether they hold plants or just soil.
- The dog's body looks a little squashed and short at the back. The hind end is a rounded lump with no clear hind legs or tail. Only one front leg is visible, stretched out stiffly onto the paper.
- The newspaper lies flat on bare grass, an odd place for one. The dog looks to be lying beside it, not on it.
