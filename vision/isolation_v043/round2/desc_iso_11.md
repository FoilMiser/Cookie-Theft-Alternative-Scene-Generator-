- The woman's visible arm seems to come out of the front of her chest instead of from the shoulder. It bends sharply up to her mouth, so the arm and shoulder join looks a little odd. Her other arm can't be seen.
- The woman's stride is very wide, and her feet point in opposite directions (back foot to the left, front foot to the right). It reads as walking but looks slightly stiff or splayed.
- I can't tell what the girl is reacting to. She could be jumping for joy, cheering, or dancing. Nothing near her seems to be the cause.
- The loose wooden crate on the right sits alone on the plaza, away from the stall. It may be a spare crate from the stall or just set dressing.
- The bistro chairs are drawn flat and side-on on either side of the table, so they look like cut-out silhouettes more than solid chairs.
- The litter bin and the crate have little or no shadow, which makes them look slightly pasted on.
