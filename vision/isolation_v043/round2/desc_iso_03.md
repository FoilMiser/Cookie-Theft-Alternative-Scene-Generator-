# iso_03

## What I see
A white goat with dark blue-grey curved horns and a red collar stands on plain green grass. It bends its head down toward a small tuft of dark green grass on the right, as if grazing or about to nibble it. A thin green line runs from its mouth down toward the tuft, which may be a blade of grass it is pulling. A tan rope or tether runs from its collar down to a short brown wooden stake in the ground in front of it. The goat seems to be tied to the stake.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The green line between the mouth and the grass could be a grass blade in its mouth, or a separate stalk standing up from the ground.
- The rope looks stiff and oddly placed. It drops straight down in front of or across the front leg, then bends sharply at ground level and runs flat to the stake, making an L-shape. It does not hang or curve the way a slack tether would, and it is hard to tell whether it passes in front of the leg or through it.
- The head is bent down and tucked against the chest, so the neck, head and beard area run together. The face is hard to read, and the head looks a little merged into the front of the body.
- The legs look stiff and thin, and the hind legs are angled a bit awkwardly under the body. The body looks long compared with the legs.
- The horns are dark blue or purple, which is an unusual colour for horns.
