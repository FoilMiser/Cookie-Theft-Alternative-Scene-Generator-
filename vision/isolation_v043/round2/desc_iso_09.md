## What I see
A light-brown cartoon dog with a dark-brown ear, a white muzzle, a black nose and a red collar is lying low on green grass. Its front legs reach forward and its paws rest on the near edge of an open newspaper lying flat on the grass. The paper is white with black headline bars and grey text lines. The dog's head is raised and it looks forward, to the right, rather than down at the paper. At the top right, a curved blue shape with a dark outline cuts into the frame.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- What the dog is doing with the paper: it could be lying on the grass reading the newspaper, which is how it is posed. It could also be pinning down or fetching the paper. Because its head is up and its eyes look past the paper, "reading" is not clear.
- The dog's pose could be read as lying down or as a low crouch, as if stalking or about to spring.
- The blue curved shape at the top could be a pond, a pool, or the edge of a blue mat or blanket. Not enough of it shows to tell.
- The front legs look quite long and stretched for the body. The front paws seem to go slightly over or into the paper's edge, so it is hard to tell whether they rest on top of it or pass through it.
- The paper is a flat, slightly tilted shape. It reads as lying on the grass, but it could also look like it hovers a little above the ground.
