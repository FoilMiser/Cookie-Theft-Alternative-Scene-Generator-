# iso_05

## What I see
A white goat with two curved grey-blue horns and a red collar stands on plain green grass. Its head is lowered toward a small tuft of dark green grass in front of it, so it looks like it is grazing or about to eat. A tan rope runs from the goat's collar or neck area down to a short brown wooden stake or peg in the ground, so the goat seems to be tethered. A soft darker-green shadow lies under the goat.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The brown stake is very short and stubby. It could be a tether peg or a small cut post or tree stump. The rope's end loops around its top.
- The rope seems to start near the chin or mouth rather than clearly at the red collar. It could be read as tied to the collar or as hanging from the goat's mouth. It also runs down in front of the front legs, which makes it a bit hard to see where the rope ends and the legs begin.
- The goat's mouth sits just above and beside the grass tuft rather than clearly in it, so it is not certain whether it is actually eating or just sniffing.
- The face is mostly hidden because the head is turned down and away, so the head reads mainly as horns plus a white blob.
- The hind legs are thin and bent at an odd angle (forward-leaning, almost crouched) compared with the straight front legs, so the back end looks a little awkward.
