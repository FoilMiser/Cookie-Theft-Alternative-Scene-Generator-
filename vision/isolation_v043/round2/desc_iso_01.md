## What I see
A sunny back garden: a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the left, with a tall wooden picket fence and green hedges and a tree behind. A woman with dark hair, in a yellow dress and red shoes, stands beside a round bed of red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers and pours water on them from a teal watering can. On the right, a child with dark skin and curly hair, in a yellow top and orange shorts, sits on the red seat of a dark grey swing set, smiling with eyes closed. Motion lines behind the child show the swing is moving. There is also a birdhouse on a tall pole, a wooden gate in the fence with a paved path leading from it toward the viewer, and a small grey wheelbarrow with a brown handle on the grass in the front.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The swing chains are thin and hard to follow. Both lean to one side, which fits a moving swing. But they seem to end at the child's hands and head rather than clearly at the seat ends. The right-hand chain runs very close to the child's head and could be read as passing behind or into it.
- The swing frame's legs splay out sideways in the picture, so it reads as two "A" shapes seen from the front. It is still clearly a swing set.
- The wheelbarrow is in the foreground but drawn small compared with the woman. It could be a child's toy wheelbarrow or a full-size one drawn too small. It has one wheel, one handle and a single back leg, so it looks a little thin or tipped, but it is recognisable.
- The woman's eyes look closed or looking down. Either way, she is clearly facing the flowers.
- Nothing seems to float. The woman, the swing frame, the birdhouse pole and the wheelbarrow all appear grounded, and the woman and wheelbarrow have small shadows.
