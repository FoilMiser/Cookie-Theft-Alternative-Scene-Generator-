# Description: test_5.png

## Free description

This is a cartoon of a park on a grey, overcast day. There are three dark storm clouds in the sky, and a yellow diamond-shaped kite flies in front of the leftmost cloud. In the front centre-right, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts holds the kite string with one arm raised and looks up at the kite. The string runs diagonally across the picture over the pond. To the left of the child, a girl in a pink dress sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket, and a picnic basket is next to the blanket. On the right, an older man in a flat cap, white shirt, brown vest and dark trousers stands next to a winding path. His mouth is open and he points up at the sky, maybe at the dark clouds as if warning that rain is coming, or maybe at the kite. A tan dog sits on the grass at his feet holding an open newspaper in its front paws as if reading it. A dog reading a newspaper is impossible and is the obvious oddity in the picture. The dark clouds suggest it may be about to rain on the picnic and the kite flying. The background has a pond with cattails, a tall black lamp post, a flower bed, trees, bushes, rolling green hills and a green trash can.
