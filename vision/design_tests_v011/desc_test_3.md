## 1. Free description

This is a cartoon of a paved town square or wide sidewalk in front of a row of four shops: pink, pale green, peach and cream. Each shop has a striped awning, and the sky above is overcast grey with three large dark storm clouds. At the front left, an elderly woman with white hair, glasses, a teal top and a grey-blue skirt has her mouth open and her arm raised, pointing up and to the right, maybe at the clouds. Behind her is an open-air produce stall with crates of green, yellow, orange and red fruit or vegetables, and a cardboard box sits on the ground to its left. Next to the stall, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers holds a camera to his face and is taking a photo of something to his right. In the middle, a young woman with a brown ponytail, a blue hoodie and dark trousers walks to the right carrying a yellow shopping bag. At the front centre, a brown dog sits on the ground holding an open newspaper in its front paws and appears to be reading it, which is impossible. Next to the dog, a red bicycle is parked in a grey bike rack. On the right are a tree in a round planter, a green trash bin, and an empty café table with two chairs, with potted plants in both front corners. With the dark clouds overhead it looks as if rain is about to start, but nobody has an umbrella, and the stall and café table are outdoors.

Her face is turned right, so a third reading is that she is reacting to the photographer or to something further right, such as the dog. Her open mouth could mean surprise or speech.

- **The dog reading a newspaper** is clearly impossible. It seems to be drawn as a deliberate oddity rather than a drawing error. The dog's pose is also odd, sitting upright like a person with its legs stretched forward.

- **The man's face** is dark with a dark beard. At this size his features are hard to make out because the camera covers most of his face.
- **Weather inconsistency.** Heavy dark storm clouds hang overhead, but nobody has an umbrella or seems worried, apart from possibly the pointing woman. The scene is otherwise calm and well lit. The shops' upper windows show no reflections or lights.
