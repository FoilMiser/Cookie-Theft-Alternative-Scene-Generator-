He is using a giant silver dinner fork, as big as a broom, to sweep dirt and pebbles toward a red dustpan, and little puffs of dust rise where the fork scrapes. Behind him near the fence, a young person in a blue hoodie looks up and points up and to the right, apparently at the ball in the tree. On the right, a grey-haired older woman with glasses waters a round bed of colourful flowers with a teal watering can, even though rain looks about to start.
