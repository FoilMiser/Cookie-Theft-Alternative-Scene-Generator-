## 1. Free description

This is a cartoon of a paved town square or pedestrian street lined with four colourful two-storey shops (pink, pale green, peach and cream). Each shop has a striped awning. The sky is grey and three heavy, dark storm clouds hang over the rooftops, so it looks like rain is about to start. On the left, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers holds a camera up to his face and points it to the right, as if taking a photo of the scene. In the middle, a young woman with a brown ponytail, a blue jacket and dark trousers walks to the right carrying a yellow shopping bag. To her right, an older woman with grey-white hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, looks up with her mouth open and points her finger at the sky or the dark clouds, as if warning that rain is coming. The impossible part is at the front: a tan dog with a red collar or bandana sits on the ground holding an open newspaper in its front paws and appears to be reading it. Next to the dog, a red bicycle is parked in a grey bike rack. Around the square there is an unattended fruit and vegetable stall, a street lamp, flower planters, a tree, rubbish bins, potted plants, and an empty café table with two chairs.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous

- What the man is photographing: his camera points right, so he could be photographing the dog reading the newspaper, the two women, or the street in general.
- What the older woman is pointing at: most likely the dark clouds or approaching rain. She could also be pointing at something in the upper windows or on a rooftop.
- The young woman: she could be simply walking past, or walking towards the older woman because she is looking at her or reacting to her pointing.
- The bicycle looks slightly odd. The wheels are solid black discs with no spokes and look oversized and very thick compared with the small red frame. The bike and rack overlap in a way that is a little hard to read, though overall it looks like a bike parked in a rack.
- The dog reading a newspaper is impossible. It looks like a deliberate absurdity, not a drawing error.
- The leftmost cloud sits low and slightly overlaps the green building's roofline, so it looks very close to the rooftop.
- The top of the street lamp overlaps the upper-left window of the green building. This is just the lamp standing in front of the building, but it can look like the lamp is attached to the window.
- The red item on the dog could be a collar, a bandana or a small bow.
- The green produce on the stall could be limes, apples or lettuce; the red could be apples or tomatoes.
