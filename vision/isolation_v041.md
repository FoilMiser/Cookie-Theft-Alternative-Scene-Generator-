# v0.41 isolation bench

Each visual was forced into a picture with little else and cut out, then read blind by fresh readers (the method of `isolation_v023.md`). There were two rounds:
- round 1: twenty-three readers of 45 cut-outs, two to a reader and never two takes of one thing (`isolation_v041/round1`);
- round 2: nine readers of 9 cut-outs, one each (`isolation_v041/round2`).

`map.json` in each says which cut-out is which case.

What was drawn is `flaws_v040.md`'s list. Three changes are in the cast, behind switches read when it is built:
- `TPDB_BEG41`: the begging dog (O2), its body leaning forward from the haunches with the head tipped up less (B), or between that and v0.40's (C);
- `TPDB_STALK41`: the stalking cat (O3), its paws apart with the near forepaw lifted ahead (B), or the far one just off the ground (C);
- `TPDB_MEI41`: the woman in yellow (O4), as tall as the men with a smaller head and a fuller bust and hips (B), and that with her dress below the knee (C).

Two changes are in placement revision 39:
- `templates.BOX41`: the floating box hanging level (L), where it was tilted (R2);
- `templates.SEASON41`: with a heap of dry leaves drawn, the setting's trees turning (O1). A has ochre and rust clusters over a green crown; B has the whole crown ochre, with rust clusters and two green ones left.

Each thing was read in v0.40's cast and scene, **O** (`BENCH_CAST=assets/cast/out040`, its library copied), against the new drawings: **N**, the cast with every switch at B; **C**, the cast with every switch at C; and the scene's L, A and B, on v0.40's cast. The first three letters of a case name the thing.

The goat's body (O5) was not benched. Four grazes were drawn at 45, 135 and 90 (`tpdb_work/bench41/look_goat1.png`), with the chest lower, the neck further forward and the forefeet further ahead, and the hunch at 45 and 135 was the same in every one: it is the body as built, its back short and its legs long, not the pose. Redrawing the goat's body means its bones and mesh, and every goat render read again at three angles, for one remark in the v0.40 screen; it is left on the list. The walking back foot (O6) drew nothing in the screen and was not benched.

Before the readers, the bench's own look caught three things:
- **Mei's first B barely differed from v0.40.** It was 1.02 tall with a head of 0.94. B was made taller and her head smaller, and C's bun, which stood on top of her head like a child's topknot, became a longer dress (`tpdb_work/bench41/look_mei_all.png`, `look_mei2_all.png`).
- **The leaning dogs placed nowhere at 45.** With the head tipped up 18 or 20 degrees, the dog's gaze passed under the eater's food, and the template placed on none of 40 street seeds. v0.40's dog placed on the first. Both were raised to 30 degrees, and both then placed.
- **A's turning trees looked like fruit.** Close up, A's clusters looked like oranges on a green tree, so B, the whole crown turned, was added before the readers saw either.

The trial poses were looked at first (`tpdb_work/bench41/look_beg1.png` to `look_beg3.png`, `look_cat1.png` to `look_cat3.png`).

**Round 1.**
- **The begging dog went to round 2.** Every beg, v0.40's included, had its forelegs held out level: "stiff rods" in two of v0.40's three and all three of B's. B's lean took nothing away, and C was no clearer.
- **The stalking cat: O.** All nine cut-outs had the legs bunched or tangled, whatever the spread of the paws. At 45 the four legs of a crouching cat overlap, and none of the two drawings parted them. The park seed at 45 also set the cat nose to beak with the bird in all three, "as if sniffing or meeting each other"; no v0.40 screen reader said so, and it stays a note.
- **The woman in yellow: O.** v0.40's was "a woman" in all four cut-outs. Neither B nor C did better, and C's was once "a teenage girl". The screen's "girl" is said of her in whole pictures, small among others, which the bench does not reproduce.
- **The floating box: O.** Level, it was still falling or thrown twice. In the park it read as a box standing far off on the ground, which is what the tilt was drawn to prevent (`draw.floating_crate`). The "falling" reading is not the tilt.
- **The season: O.** v0.40's heaps under green trees drew no remark on the bench. Both turnings drew fruit, and B drew the season the other way round, against the grass.
