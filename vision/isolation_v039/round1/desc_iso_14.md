# iso_14

## What I see
On green grass, a four-legged animal with a white head, thin white legs, curved dark blue-grey horns and a shaggy brown body bends its head right down to the ground. Its face is in a tuft of dark green grass, so it looks like it is grazing. It reads mostly as a goat, possibly a sheep or ram. Around it: a blue pond with a few cattails or reeds at the top left, a grey rock at the bottom centre, a wooden signpost with blank crosspieces on the right, and at the top right a green striped container (looks like a bin or basket) next to the dark base of a post (perhaps a lamp post).

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Which animal it is: a goat (horns, white legs) or a sheep or ram. The brown body could also be read as a llama-like shaggy coat.
- The brown body looks like a separate blanket, cloak or sack draped over a white animal rather than its own fur. It has an odd lumpy hump with a pointed top at the back, and it sits up high, so the animal looks top-heavy.
- The legs are thin white tubes with sharp bends, and they splay at slightly odd angles. It is hard to tell which leg is front and which is back, and the joints look rubbery.
- The head is low and partly hidden by the grass tuft. It almost looks detached, as if it were lying on the ground next to the grass rather than joined to the neck, because the neck joint is hard to see.
- The object at the top right could be the base of a lamp post, a bollard or a bench leg; it is cut off. The green striped thing could be a litter bin or a planter or basket.
- The signpost boards are blank, so it could also be a short fence section or a marker post.
