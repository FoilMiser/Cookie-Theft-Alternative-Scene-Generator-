## What I see
A man with brown hair and a moustache, wearing a red T-shirt, blue jeans and a dark belt, stands on a paved sidewalk sweeping with a long-handled broom with yellow bristles. Next to the bristles on the ground is a grey half-dome with dark speckles, which looks like a pile of swept-up dirt or dust. Behind him is a shop front with a large window, a teal door and a green awning at the top left. A wooden slatted bench stands to his left, and a green bush or tree is cut off at the right edge. At the bottom edge is a white oval with dark posts on each side, cut off.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The grey half-dome is very smooth and perfectly semicircular. It reads as a dirt pile, but it could also be a grey dustpan or a rock. If it is meant to be dirt, the shape looks a bit artificial.
- The white oval with dark uprights at the bottom could be the top of a round table, a stool seat, or the rim of a bin. Too little of it shows to tell.
- The man's lower hand sits at waist height and does not clearly hold the broom handle. The handle seems to pass beside it rather than through his grip.
- The broom head stands upright with its bristles fanned out sideways. It covers his front foot, so the bristles and foot overlap and it is hard to tell which is in front.
- A dark vertical shape at the left edge, near bench-back height, is cut off. It might be a post or part of another object.
- His face looks downward with a frowning brow, so he could be read as focused or as annoyed.
