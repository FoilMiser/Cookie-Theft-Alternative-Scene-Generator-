# iso_03

## What I see
A man with brown hair, a red short-sleeved shirt and blue jeans stands on green grass in front of a tall wooden plank fence, with green bushes behind the fence. He is reaching up with both hands to a clothesline strung between two wooden posts, apparently pinning a wet white T-shirt to the line with a clothespin; drops of water fall from the shirt. Further along the line, a pair of blue trousers hangs by its waistband from two clothespins, also dripping. On the grass at the lower left is a wooden laundry basket filled with pink, white and blue clothes, with a blue item hanging over its front edge and dripping, so this reads as hanging out washing to dry.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- It is not fully clear whether he is pinning the white shirt up or taking it down; the water drops suggest it has just been washed and is being hung.
- His two raised arms are thin and come together at the clothespin, so it is hard to tell which arm is which or exactly where each one joins the shoulder.
- His head looks large and his body long and narrow compared with the head, a cartoon proportion, but slightly stretched.
- The feet and dark shoes are small and bunched together, and I could not clearly tell which way each foot points.
- The blue shape at the bottom of the white shirt could be a water drop or a small blue item stuck to the shirt.
- The blue trousers look narrow and are drawn flat, so they could also be read as a single blue cloth or towel folded into a leg shape.
