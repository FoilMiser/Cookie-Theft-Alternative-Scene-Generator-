He holds a long wooden broom with yellow straw bristles and a red band in both hands and is sweeping a small pile of green and brown leaves. Behind him is a shopfront with large pale-blue windows and a teal door with a yellow knob, and a dark planter box of colourful flowers (orange, pink, yellow, red) at the left. - The broom head is in front of and slightly beside his feet, and the bristles touch the edge of the leaf pile, not the middle of it; it reads as sweeping, but the broom looks a little detached from the pile.
- The broom handle crosses in front of his body, so his lower arm and one leg are partly hidden, and it is hard to see how the lower hand joins the arm.
- The table and chair are cut off at the edge, so I cannot tell if they belong to a cafe or are just street furniture.
