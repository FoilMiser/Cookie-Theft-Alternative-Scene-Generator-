# iso_06

## What I see
An animal with a white head, two curved grey horns and white legs stands on green grass. It has its head lowered to a small tuft of grass and seems to be grazing. It reads as a goat. The top of its body is a dark-brown rounded mass. At the top left is a blue pond with two cattails or reeds sticking up. At the top centre is the base of a dark post, maybe a lamp post. On the right is the side of a wooden bench. At the bottom centre the top of a grey rounded object shows, maybe a rock or boulder.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The body looks odd. The brown part sits on top like a lumpy blob, sack or saddle blanket, and the white legs hang out from under it. It could be a brown-backed goat, or a white animal with something brown draped over or strapped onto its back.
- The legs look strange: long, white and bent at a knee-like angle, almost like human legs. The back legs bow forward. They don't join the brown body cleanly, so they look stuck on underneath.
- A thin dark line runs from the brown body toward the back of the head near the horns. It could be a strap, leash or rope, a mane line, or just an outline artefact.
- The neck is barely visible. The head seems attached straight to the front of the brown mass, low down.
- With the horns it might also be read as an ibex, antelope or ram rather than a goat.
- The grey shape at the bottom edge is cut off. It could be a rock, a stone dome or the top of another object.
