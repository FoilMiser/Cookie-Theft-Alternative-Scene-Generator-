# iso_17

## What I see
A four-legged animal stands on plain green grass with its head lowered to the ground, apparently grazing on a small tuft of grass that has a little pink/red flower at its base. The animal has a white head with two short, curved dark grey-blue horns, long thin white legs with dark hooves, and a brown upper body with a short brown tail sticking up at the back. It reads most like a goat (or possibly a sheep or antelope) eating grass. A soft grey shadow lies on the ground under it.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Species is unclear: the horns and upright tail suggest a goat, but the brown body on white legs could also read as a llama/alpaca or an antelope.
- The brown body looks like a lumpy sack or a blanket draped over the animal rather than a torso; it could be read as brown fur or as a separate cover on the animal's back.
- There is no clearly visible neck: the small white head seems to sit directly under the front of the brown mass, so the head looks oddly joined or tucked underneath the body.
- The head is small relative to the body and the legs are long and stick-like; the front legs angle forward toward the head, and I can only clearly count three legs (a fourth may be hidden).
- The grass tuft covers the face, so I cannot see eyes or mouth; the grass reads as either being eaten or as standing in front of the face.
- A small brown tuft near the front of the body (where a neck would be) is hard to interpret: maybe a mane or a fold of the brown covering.
