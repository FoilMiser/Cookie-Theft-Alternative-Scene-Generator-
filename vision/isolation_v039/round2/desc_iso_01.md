## What I see
A man with dark skin and short dark hair, wearing a green t-shirt, tan trousers with a dark belt and brown shoes, is walking a dog on grass. He holds a red leash in both hands at about waist height. The leash runs taut to the blue collar of a small white dog with brown spots and a brown floppy ear. The dog walks to the left ahead of him with its tail up. Behind them is a large blue pond or lake with a dark outline, cut off at the top of the frame. Both figures have soft shadows under them.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Which way the man faces: his eye and mouth sit on the left side of his head, so I read him as facing left and following the dog. Because the features are small, he could also be read as facing right with his hands and the leash held behind him, which would mean he and the dog are walking in opposite directions.
- His upper body leans back a little and his hands are held close together in front of his waist, so his pose looks slightly stiff, as if he is holding the dog back.
- The dog's head is large for its body, but that fits the cartoon style.
- Nothing looks like it is floating or passing through anything else. The leash meets the collar cleanly.
