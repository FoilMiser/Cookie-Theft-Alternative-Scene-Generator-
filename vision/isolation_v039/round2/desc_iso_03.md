# iso_03

## What I see
A four-legged animal with a brown body, thin brown legs, a white head and two grey horns curving backwards stands on green grass with its head lowered to a small tuft of grass. It reads as a goat (or goat-like antelope) grazing. Around it are a blue pond with two cattail reeds at the top left, the base of a dark lamp post at the top centre, the end of a wooden park bench at the right edge, and the top of a grey rounded rock or boulder at the bottom edge.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The species could be read two ways: a goat (white head, curved horns) or a deer/antelope (slender brown body, long thin legs). The sharp change from the white head to the brown body makes the head look almost like a separate piece stuck onto the front of the body.
- No neck is clearly visible. The head seems to come straight out from the underside of the front of the body, so the head-to-body join looks odd.
- The body is a lumpy, hunched shape, with a high rounded back and a bump at the rear (a tail, or the haunch). The legs are very thin and bent at awkward angles, and it is hard to count four distinct legs or tell front from back. It could even be read as facing away from the viewer at an angle.
- The grey shape at the bottom could be a rock, a stepping stone or the rim of a bowl or bin. Only its top is visible.
- The bench at the right is seen end-on and cut off at the edge, so it could also be taken for a fence or a rack.
