# iso_06

## What I see
A white dog with brown spots, a brown floppy ear and a blue collar or bandana is on plain green grass. It is in a "play bow": its front legs are stretched flat along the ground, its chest is low and its rear end is raised. Its tail sticks straight up, with curved motion lines beside it that suggest wagging. Just in front of its front paws is a yellow-green tennis ball, which the dog seems to be about to play with or pounce on. A soft shadow lies under the dog.

- The front legs are thin, long and very straight, almost like sticks pointing at the ball. The paws touch or overlap the ball's edge.
- A short dark line on the chest or belly, near where the front legs join, is unclear. It could be a spot, a fold or a stray line.
- The curved lines by the tail could be read as wagging or as sound lines.
- The head is large compared with the body, and the hind legs look somewhat thin and spindly. Nothing seems detached or floating.
