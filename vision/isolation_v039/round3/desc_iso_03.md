# iso_03

## What I see
A white four-legged animal, most likely a goat (it has dark curved horns), stands on plain green ground with its head lowered to a small tuft of dark green grass. It appears to be grazing or sniffing the grass. Its back is arched high, its rear end and short tail point up, and a soft grey shadow lies under it. Next to the grass there is a tiny pink dot on a thin green stem, which looks like a small flower.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Species: the horns say goat. But the long, strongly curved neck and tall, humped body could also read as a llama or alpaca with odd horns. The horns are dark blue-grey and look a bit like curled ribbons or ram horns.
- The head is very small for the body and sits partly behind the grass tuft, so I can't clearly see any eyes, mouth or ears. The pink dot by the head could be its nose or mouth, or a flower in the grass.
- The body shape looks strange: the back is a tall, hunched arch, the rear sticks up, and the neck bends sharply down and back toward the front legs. The whole animal looks folded or crouched instead of standing naturally.
- The legs are hard to sort out. There seem to be legs coming from the middle of the belly as well as front and back, and they look oddly joined to the body. It isn't clear which leg is front or back, or whether there are four. Some leg tops look like they start inside the body outline instead of from a shoulder or hip.
- A thin green stem with a pink tip pokes down below the grass, into or below the shadow. It may be a flower, or a grass blade sticking out under the ground line.
