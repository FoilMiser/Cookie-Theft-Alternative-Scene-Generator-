# iso_01

## What I see
A brown four-legged animal stands on a plain green background with its head lowered right down to the ground. It has two dark blue-grey curved horns, so it reads as a goat, or possibly an antelope or deer. Its muzzle is pushed into a small tuft of dark-outlined grass, so it seems to be grazing or sniffing the grass. A small white-and-pink spot sits in the grass at the muzzle, and a soft grey oval shadow lies under the animal.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Species: horned goat, or antelope/deer. The horns are short and stubby and stand straight up from the lowered head.
- The white-and-pink spot at the muzzle could be a small flower in the grass, or it could be the animal's nose or mouth showing through the blades.
- The grass tuft covers the lower part of the head, so the head looks partly sunk into the grass rather than resting in front of it.
- A green stem or blades hang below the tuft, past the shadow line. This makes the tuft look like it hangs or floats lower than the ground the animal stands on.
- The body shape is odd. The back arches into a tall hump near the rear, with a small upright tail at the top, and the whole body slopes steeply down toward the head. The rear looks much higher than the front.
- The legs are hard to read. I can clearly see only three dark hooves. One front leg comes off the chest at a sharp diagonal and looks oddly joined, and it is unclear which leg is which, or whether a fourth leg is hidden.
- The neck is barely visible. The head looks attached straight to the front of the body.
