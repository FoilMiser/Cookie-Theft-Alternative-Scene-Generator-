## What I see

It looks like it is grazing or sniffing the plant. The animal reads most like a goat or ibex, possibly a deer. Behind it at top left is a blue pond with two cattail or reed stalks at its edge. At the top right are a dark post base, which may be a lamp post, and a green slatted container that looks like a litter bin, both cut off by the top edge. On the right is a wooden signpost with crossbars but no visible lettering, and a large grey rock sits at the bottom centre.

- Body shape is odd: the hindquarters rise well above the shoulders, so the back looks hunched and humped. The pointed shape at the top right of the body could be a tail sticking up or just a bony haunch.
- The legs look a bit wobbly and bent. One hind leg seems to fold forward under the belly, and it is hard to tell how many legs there are or which belong to the front and which to the back.
- The outline is lumpy, as if the animal were shaggy or made of rough segments, and the head is fairly large and blends into the neck.
- The grass clump overlaps the front of the head. It could be read as the animal eating it, or as the muzzle passing into or behind the grass.
- The green container could be a litter bin or a planter or basket. The dark object next to it could be a lamp post base or a bollard.
- The signpost has blank boards with no text or arrows, so it is unclear what it points to.
