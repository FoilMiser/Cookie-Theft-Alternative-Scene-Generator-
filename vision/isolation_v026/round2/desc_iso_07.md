## What I see

An older man with grey hair and a grey-blue flat cap stands on green grass in front of a brown wooden plank fence. He wears a white long-sleeved shirt, a brown waistcoat, dark trousers and brown shoes, and he faces left. He holds a teal watering can with a long spout, and blue water drops stream from its rose in an arc down onto a bed of flowers. The flower bed is an oval of brown soil with tall red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers on green stems. He is watering the flowers.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- His hands are bunched close together at his chest, right against the top of the can, so it is hard to see exactly how he grips it (no clear handle in his grasp); the can seems to sit against his hands or belly rather than hang from a handle.
- The spout is very long and thin, and comes out of the lower part of the can almost horizontally, which looks a little stretched compared with the small can body.
- The water falls in a broad stream of separate dashes that passes in front of several flowers; it reads as water falling over them, though the lowest drops overlap the stems and the soil, so where it lands is not sharply defined.
- His legs are long and narrow and his stance leans slightly, with the feet close together, which looks a little stiff. The shoes overlap at the bottom, so it is a bit hard to separate one foot from the other.
- Nothing appears to be floating or detached otherwise.
