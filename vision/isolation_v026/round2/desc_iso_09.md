## What I see

An older man with grey hair, a grey-blue flat cap, a white long-sleeved shirt, a brown waistcoat, dark trousers and brown shoes stands on green grass in front of a wooden plank fence. He is holding a teal watering can out in front of him, tipped forward, with a long spout pointing down and to the left. Blue streaks of water fall from the spout's end onto a round bed of dark soil holding a row of colourful flowers (red, yellow, purple, pink and orange). He is watering the flowers.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The man's face is turned to the left and shown in a tight profile, so the features are hard to read at this size; the nose and mouth area are small and merge with the dark outline, and the head looks a little flattened against the cap.
- His arm and hand are hard to separate from the can: the white sleeve ends at the can's top, and I cannot clearly see how the hand grips it or whether there is a handle in use. The can could be read as attached directly to his forearm or as held by a hidden handle.
- The can looks small compared with the very long, thin spout, and the spout seems to run in a straight line from the can body like a rod. The water starts at its tip, though the stream looks like a wide fan of separate blue-and-white dashes rather than a continuous flow.
- The water stream passes in front of the flowers and over the orange and red flowers, and ends in drips near the soil at about the middle of the bed. It could be read as landing on the flowers or as falling past them to the soil.
- His legs are very long, thin and pressed close together, and the body looks stiff and narrow from the side; the two feet overlap so that they read almost as one shoe mass, turned to the left.
- The soft dark shadow under his feet sits slightly to the right of him, so he looks a touch separated from it, but he does not appear to be floating.
