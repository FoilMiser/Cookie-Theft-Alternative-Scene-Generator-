## What I see

On a plain green lawn, a tall man in a red shirt, blue trousers and a brown belt stands on the left with both arms stretched forward and his hands together, facing right. On the right, a small dark-skinned child with curly dark hair, a yellow top and orange shorts stands mid-stride with one arm extended forward towards the man, as though having just thrown something. A red ball with a white curved stripe hangs in the air near the man's hands, and a dashed curved line runs from the child's hand up to the ball, showing the ball's flight path. So the child seems to have thrown the ball and the man is about to catch it. Behind them is a dark metal railing fence along the top of the picture, a wooden bench cut off at the upper left, and an orange and yellow slatted object cut off at the right edge.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The man's hands are bunched together and held out in front of him, slightly below the ball's height and with a small gap to it. This could be read as him getting ready to catch, or as him having just thrown or pushed the ball; the dashed line favours the ball travelling from the child to him.
- The ball appears to be level with or above the top of the man's head and behind his hands relative to the line of flight, so it is not obvious that it will land in his hands; it might sail past or over them.
- The man's head looks tilted back and turned in a way that is a bit odd: the face is a narrow pale patch set into a large round brown shape, so the head can look like it is mostly hair with the face stuck on the side. He has very long, thin legs and a small torso, so his proportions look stretched.
- The man's feet overlap into a dark cluster and it is hard to see which foot is which.
- The child's outstretched hand ends in a thin pointed shape, more like a spike or a pointing finger than an open hand, and the other arm ends in a small lump bent behind the body. The child's hair is a dark, bumpy mass that wraps around the side of the head.
- The orange and yellow object at the right edge is cut off and floats at mid-height in the frame with no visible support; it could be the end of another bench, or a piece of play equipment, but I cannot tell.
- The bench at the upper left has its slats overlapping the fence posts, and only one leg is visible, so it looks partly merged with the fence.
