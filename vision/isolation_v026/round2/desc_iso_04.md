## What I see

A woman with dark hair tied back in a ponytail, wearing a purple short-sleeved top, a dark belt and teal trousers, stands on a pale paved path that runs between two green lawns. She holds a long wooden-handled broom or rake with both hands and looks down and to the side, and she appears to be sweeping. At the bottom of the handle is a yellow bristle head, and right beside it is a small pile of autumn leaves in red, orange, yellow and brown. Behind her at the top is a brick or wood-panel wall with a brown door or gate, and at the left edge there is part of a teal container that could be a bucket or bin for the leaves.

- There is a small dark shape at the far left edge, above the teal object, that is cut off and I cannot tell what it is.
- The structure at the top could be a brick wall, a wooden fence or a low building front. The brown shape with a diagonal line could be a door, a gate standing open, or a ramp.
- The handle passes in front of her body and down past her legs at a slant. It looks plausible, and the lower hand wraps around it, but the upper hand is close to the handle's top end, so the grip is a little high and tight to the chest.
- Her neck is hard to see and her head sits quite directly on the shoulders. Her legs are long and narrow, ending in small dark shoes that seem to merge into one dark shape, so the feet are hard to tell apart.
- Nothing is clearly floating. She and the leaves sit on a soft grey shadow on the path.
