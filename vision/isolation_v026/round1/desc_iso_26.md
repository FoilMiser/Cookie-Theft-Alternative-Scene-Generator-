## What I see

A white cartoon dog with brown patches on its ear and back sits on a pale paved street, wearing a blue collar or small neckerchief. It sits upright on its haunches, side-on, with its front paws raised to hold up an open newspaper that faces toward it, as if it is reading. Behind it at the upper left is a market stall with crates of orange fruit and red fruit on a wooden counter, and a row of shopfronts runs along the top edge. A thick dark pole stands on a base just behind the dog, and a dark grey ribbed bin sits in the foreground at the bottom.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The dog could be read as a dog doing a human activity on purpose (reading the paper), or as a dog simply holding or carrying a newspaper it has picked up.
- The newspaper appears to float a little: it is large and stands upright, with its lower edge at about the height of the dog's lower body, and only the paws at its right edge seem to hold it. It could also be read as a sign or board standing on the ground.
- The paws are overlapped by or merge with the edge of the paper, so it is hard to see exactly how they grip it.
- The blue item at the neck could be a collar, a scarf or a small bow; I could not tell which. There is also a small brown shape on the dog's back or chest below it that could be a marking or a tag.
- The dog's pose is odd: the body and hind legs bend sharply and the legs look thin and folded in a way that is a little hard to follow, so it could be sitting or half lying.
- The thick pole could be a lamp post or a post for the stall's awning, and a second dark pole at the top left stands next to the stall. The tops are cut off, so I could not tell what they hold up.
- The bin at the bottom is cut off at the lower edge of the cut-out, and could be a rubbish bin or a planter; I read it as a bin because it looks empty and ribbed.
