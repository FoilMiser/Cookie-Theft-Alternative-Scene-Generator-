He is sweeping, looking down toward the ground, and next to the broom head is a small grey speckled pile of dirt or gravel. Behind him at the top of the frame are shopfront windows and a dark planter box of red, yellow, pink and orange flowers. A red bicycle is at the lower right, cut off by the frame, with its front wheel near a grey metal hoop that looks like a bike rack.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- There is a dark mark around his mouth and chin. It could be a moustache or small beard, a dark open mouth, or a smudge; it looks a bit odd and I can't say which it is.
- The grey pile could be dirt, gravel or swept rubbish; it could also read as a small rock or mound, because it has a flat base and a clean triangular outline.
- The yellow shape at the bottom of the handle could be a broom head or a dustpan. It is angular and pointed rather than bristly, so it looks more like a dustpan or scraper than a broom, and it seems to sit partly behind or under the edge of the pile.
- His legs look long and narrow and come close together into a single column, with only one dark shoe clearly visible and a second barely apparent behind it, so his stance looks stiff and the far leg is hard to find.
- The arm positions look slightly awkward: the elbows and hands are bent around the handle in a way that looks a bit rubbery, and the head appears to sit forward on the shoulders with very little neck.
- The bicycle's front end overlaps the grey hoop, so it is unclear whether the bike is locked to a rack or just standing in front of it; a dark part below the wheel could be a kickstand or a pedal.
