## What I see

An older woman with grey hair in a bun, wearing a teal top, grey-blue skirt and brown shoes, stands on grass at the edge of a blue pond with one arm stretched out over the water. A white duck with an orange bill sits on the pond facing her, with white wake lines behind it. Small pale oval pieces are scattered on the water around the duck and in the air between the woman's hand and the pond, so she seems to be throwing food (bread or seed) to the duck. She holds a pale paper bag or packet against her chest with her other arm. Behind her are a dark lamp post or pole with a square base and a green slatted litter bin, plus two bulrushes at the pond edge and hedges and hills in the background.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The pale object at her chest could be a paper bag of food or a book/papers; its shape is blocky and it is hard to tell how she is holding it.
- The pale ovals could be bread pieces, seeds, or small stones or lily-type items on the water. Some sit on the water and one or two are over the grass or near the bulrushes, so they read as either thrown in mid-air or lying still.
- A small dark and brown stack at the far bottom-right edge is cut off; it could be part of a bench, a bag or another object, and I cannot tell which.
- Her face is turned so that a dark mark near her nose or mouth is hard to read; it could be glasses or a facial feature.
- Slightly strange: her very long, thin legs and small body proportions look stretched. The bulrush stems overlap a food piece near the pond's edge. The white lines by the duck look like wake marks but are sharply straight and detached from the duck, so they could also read as sticks lying on the water.
