## What I see

A tan-and-white cartoon dog with dark floppy ears and a red collar sits facing the viewer in front of a blue door in a cream house wall, on a grey doorstep. It holds a red leash in its mouth; the leash runs down its front and ends in a loop on the grass, as if the dog is asking to go for a walk. To the right, on the grass, is a dark teal garden hose coiled on a dark reel or stand, and beyond it is a soil flower bed with red, yellow and purple flowers. A wooden fence and a round green bush fill the upper right behind the house corner.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The dark teal object could be a garden hose reel, which is how I read it, or some kind of wheel or tyre on a stand. A short curved piece at its lower right could be the hose end or a handle, and it does not visibly connect to a tap or to anything else.
- The red line could be a leash held in the mouth, or could read as a long tongue hanging down, since it starts right at the mouth and is about the same colour as a tongue. The loop at the end on the grass favours a leash.
- The leash passes down the front of the body and over the collar area, so it is unclear whether it is also attached to the collar or only held in the mouth.
- The dog's body is very narrow and tube-like, with straight front legs that merge into the torso; the back legs are hard to separate from the front ones, and the white feet look like small blobs at the base.
- The bush appears to sit against or partly behind the house wall and fence, with no clear ground contact, because the wall corner cuts across it.
- The door is quite narrow and the dog is large compared with it, reaching a good way up the door, so the scale looks slightly off.
