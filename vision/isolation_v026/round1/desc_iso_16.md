## What I see

He is holding one arm out and down, and a line of small tan dots falls from his hand to the ground, so he seems to be scattering seed or crumbs. Two small birds, one red and one blue, are on the grass below with their heads down, pecking at the dots. In his other hand, held against his chest, there is a white rectangular object that looks like a paper bag or packet, presumably holding the feed.
