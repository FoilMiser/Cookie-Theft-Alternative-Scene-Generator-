## What I see
A man with short brown hair, a red T-shirt, a brown belt, blue trousers and dark shoes is standing on a pale paved pavement holding a long wooden-handled broom or brush with both hands. He is sweeping a pile of autumn leaves in red, orange, yellow and brown, and the yellow broom head rests at the edge of the pile. Behind him are shop windows and a dark blue planter box with red, yellow, pink and orange flowers at the top left. A red bicycle with a dark wheel is parked at the right edge in a grey metal hoop-shaped bike rack, cut off by the frame.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The yellow tool at the bottom of the handle could be a broom head seen at an angle or a yellow dustpan or shovel; it is a flat wedge shape with no visible bristles, so it could be read as either.
- The face has a dark shape near the mouth and chin that could be a mouth, a small beard or a goatee; I cannot tell which. The nose and eye are close together at the right side of the face.
- Both arms look somewhat short and bent sharply, and the hands look like round blobs placed on the handle; the upper hand sits very close to the chest.
- Only one shoe is clearly visible at the bottom left; the other foot appears hidden behind the broom head or the leaf pile, so the leg on that side seems to end in the leaves.
- The handle passes in front of the body and down across the leg, and it is hard to tell whether it is in front of or overlapping the leg.
- The leaves appear to sit in a tidy flat cluster overlapping the broom head and the foot, not obviously lying on the ground in depth.
- The bicycle frame and the rack overlap in a way that makes it hard to tell which parts belong to the bike and which to the rack.
