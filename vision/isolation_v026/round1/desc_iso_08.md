## What I see

An older, balding man with a patch of grey hair at the back of his head stands on grass at the edge of a blue pond, seen from behind and to one side. He wears a white long-sleeved shirt, a brown waistcoat, dark trousers and brown shoes, and he holds a long dark fishing rod in both hands, angled up and out over the water. A thin line curves down from the rod tip to a red-and-white float sitting in a small white ripple ring on the water. He is fishing. Bulrushes stand at the far right edge of the pond, a clump of pink, red, orange and purple flowers grows in a soil bed at the lower left, and green hills and hedges fill the background.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The small grey block on the rod near his hands could be a fishing reel, or it could be a separate object he is holding; it reads as a reel but is quite plain.
- The grey shape at the back of his head reads as hair, but it is flat and angular with a hard edge, so it could also be taken as a patch or a cap piece stuck onto the head.
- His head is hard to read for direction: the ear sits in the middle of the head's visible side and there is no visible face, so he could be facing away from the viewer or turned to the left. The small bump at the left of the head could be a nose or could be an ear.
- His arms look a little tangled at the chest. The two sleeves bunch together and it is not fully clear which hand belongs to which arm, and the forearms look short for the length of the rod.
- His body is quite narrow and his legs are long and thin and close together, with the feet overlapping, so the stance looks slightly stiff and tall compared with the head.
- The rod tip and line extend far over the water and the rod is very long and straight until the tip, where the line takes a marked curve that bows out rather than hanging straight down.
- The flowers are cut off by the lower left corner and the leftmost one is only partly visible.
