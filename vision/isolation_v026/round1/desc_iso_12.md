## What I see

A brown-skinned man in a green short-sleeved shirt, tan trousers, a dark belt and brown shoes stands on grass beside a blue pond. He holds a tan cardboard box or paper bag against his side with one arm and extends his other hand outward, and a line of small round grains or seeds falls from his fingers down to the ground. Two small birds, one red and one blue, stand at his feet pecking at the scattered seeds. He is feeding the birds. Bulrushes stand at the left end of the pond, and there is a grey rock at the lower left corner and part of a dark-edged green shape at the left edge.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- His head is the hardest part to read. It is a dark, rounded shape with tan and dark patches, and I cannot clearly find a face. It could be a dark cap or hat over a face with a beard, or it could be the back of a head with dark hair and a beard, turned away from the viewer; the small dark marks on the right could be an ear or facial features. It looks jumbled and rather like a mask or helmet.
- The box or bag could be a cardboard box or a paper sack of bird seed. It sits partly behind his arm and hip, and it is not clear how he is holding it, since the arm in front does not obviously grip it; it looks partly as if it is resting on or floating at his waist.
- The hand throwing the seed ends in a thin pointed shape, so it reads as a pointing finger as much as a hand dropping grains.
- The seeds fall in a neat, evenly spaced line that runs almost straight down and curves only slightly, more like a dotted line or a string of beads than a scattering, and they appear to hang in the air.
- The birds' tails stick up sharply and their heads are down; the red bird's head and body run together, and each bird looks like it could be either pecking or tipping forward. They have very thin legs.
- His legs are long and narrow and taper towards the ankles, and his feet overlap into a single dark shape, so the stance looks slightly unstable and he seems to lean backward.
- The green shape at the far left edge is cut off and I cannot tell what it is; it could be a bush or another small pond or bed.
