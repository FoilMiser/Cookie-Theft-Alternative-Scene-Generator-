## What I see

A brown and white dog with floppy ears and a red collar sits upright in front of a blue door in a cream-coloured house wall, on or just in front of a grey doorstep. The dog has a long red lead in its mouth; the lead hangs down its front and ends in a loop on the grass by its paws, so it seems to be holding its own lead as if waiting to go for a walk. To the right is a dark teal coiled garden hose on a reel or stand, and beyond it red, yellow and purple flowers grow in a soil bed, with a green bush and a wooden fence behind.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The red strip could be read as a lead held in the mouth or as an extra-long tongue hanging down, because it starts at the mouth and is the same kind of red as the collar. The looped end on the ground favours a lead.
- The lead does not clearly connect to the collar, so it could be loose in the mouth or attached to the collar with the join hidden.
- The dark teal object could be a hose reel or some other round device, such as a wheel or a spool; the short curved piece at its lower right could be a hose end or a handle.
- The dog looks very upright and narrow, with long straight front legs, so it could be read as sitting or as standing on its hind legs. The pose looks slightly stiff and human-like.
- The white patch on the dog's chest overlaps the red lead in a way that makes that area a little hard to read.
- The flower stems are thick and straight and the flowers are large compared with the dog and the door, which looks a little out of proportion.
