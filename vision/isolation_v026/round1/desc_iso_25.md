## What I see

A cartoon boy or young man stands on green grass in front of a dark metal railing fence. He wears a red baseball cap (brim pointing left), a blue hooded top, dark trousers and white shoes. He balances on his left-hand leg and swings the other leg forward and out to the left, with an orange ball with a white curved stripe and a dark outline right at the toe of the raised shoe. Two curved motion lines sit under the kicking leg, so he appears to be kicking the ball. His arms are held out to each side for balance.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The ball is drawn touching the toe at about shin height of the standing leg, with its own faint shadow on the grass below. It could be read as a ball being kicked at that moment, or as a ball balanced on the foot or stuck to the shoe.
- The ball's markings (a single white curve on orange) do not clearly match a particular sport; it could be a basketball, a generic play ball, or even a round fruit-like object.
- The motion lines sit below the leg and curve in an arc, which I read as the swing of the leg, though they could also be read as the path of the ball.
- The face is turned away or tilted so that very little of it is visible; the brown hair covers much of the head and there is a small dark mark near the cheek or ear that I could not identify (it could be an eye, an ear or part of the cap strap).
- The body looks somewhat oddly proportioned: the kicking leg is long and thin, and it joins the hip at a sharp angle. The hands are small pale blobs with little finger detail.
- There is a pale patch at the neck or chest under the hood that could be a shirt or skin.
