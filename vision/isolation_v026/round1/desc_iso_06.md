## What I see

An older man with grey hair and a moustache, wearing a blue-grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat, dark trousers and brown shoes, stands on grass beside a blue pond. He holds a long dark fishing rod in both hands, with a thin line curving from the rod tip down to a red and white float in the water, surrounded by a white ripple ring. He looks like he is fishing. There are two bulrushes at the pond edge behind him, hedges and hills in the background, and a flower bed with pink, red, orange and purple flowers in the bottom-left corner.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- There is a small grey shape at the rod near his lower hand; it is probably a reel, but it could also be read as a small box or a part of the handle.
- The man's face is turned away towards the water and the dark mark at the front of his face could be a moustache, a nose shadow or glasses; I cannot say exactly.
- Slightly strange: the line leaves the very tip of the rod and hangs in a long curve that bows outward to the left rather than straight down, which looks a little stiff. The bulrushes appear to stand in the pond right behind him and very close to his body. His legs are long and thin and his feet overlap so it is hard to separate the two shoes. His arms and hands bunch tightly where they hold the rod, so the join between arm, hand and rod is hard to separate.
