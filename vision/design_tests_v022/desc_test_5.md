## Unsure or ambiguous

- **The ladder**: it stands upright in the middle of the grass with nothing to lean on (no wall, tree or the lamp post next to it). That looks impossible or deliberately wrong. It is also not clear why a ladder is there at all. One reading is that it is a "something's wrong" element. Another is that it belongs to a worker who isn't shown, for example someone fixing the lamp. But it is not next to the lamp.
- **What the photographer is photographing**: the camera points right. It could be aimed at the jogger, who is running toward him, or at the older man and the birds, or simply at the park scenery. The jogger may be about to run into or across his shot.
- **The seed trail**: the dots could be seeds falling through the air from the man's hand, or a line of seeds already lying on the path. They run from the height of his hand down to the birds, which are a little in front of him. As falling seeds, they would be drifting sideways rather than dropping straight down, which looks slightly odd.
- **The object in the older man's other hand**: it looks like a small white paper bag or cup. It could also be a box or a book. It is hard to tell.
- **The jogger's back hand**: there is a small white shape at her rear hand, near her hip. It might just be her hand, or she might be holding something small, like a phone.
- **The older man's cap**: grey-blue, with the brim pointing forward/left. I could not tell whether he wears glasses.
- **Shadows**: the people, birds and ladder have small ground shadows, but the lamp post and trash can do not seem to have any. This is minor and probably just the drawing style.
- **Lamp post size and position**: it is very tall and thin and stands alone on open grass away from the path. That is a bit unusual but not impossible.
- **The photographer and the pond**: his upper body overlaps the pond in the picture, but his feet are on the grass in front of it. I read him as standing in front of the pond, not in the water.
