# v0.55 design tests (whole scenes)

v0.55 draws the lower body of every seated figure again and changes no placement rule (revision 49 stands), so its whole-scene test is the bench of `isolation_v055.md`: eighteen of v0.54's screened pictures that hold a seated figure, each built again from its own record with the release's cast, with the same templates and the same people in the same places, and each read both ways with the screen's own prompt by twelve fresh readers, six pictures a reader and no picture twice (`isolation_v055/bench`; the pictures are `blind/pic_NNN.png`, the readings `desc_pic_NNN.md`, and which picture is which is in `isolation_v055/bench_map.json`).

What the 36 readings of the new cast were read for:

- **That the figure sits.** All 36 have the seated figure sitting, on a bench, a swing or a seesaw, as all 36 of v0.54's pictures do.
- **What the sitter does.** A reader on a bench reads in 12 readings of 12, a sitter eats an ice cream or drinks in 8 of 8, each as in v0.54's pictures: "On the right, a man in a red shirt and blue trousers sits on a bench reading a red book."
- **The seat that is missing.** Both pictures of a figure that sits on nothing are read so by both their readers, as v0.54's are, and one reader says why the pose is a chair's: "- The woman in yellow is drawn sitting but there is nothing under her: she appears to be sitting on thin air. This could be intended as sitting on the grass, but her knees are bent at chair height and her feet are on the ground, so it looks like a missing chair."
- **The heavy seesaw.** The child is up in the air on the high end and the man sits reading on the low one in all four readings, as in v0.54's four: "On the left a red seesaw is tipped: a small girl in a pink outfit is stuck up on the high end, holding the handle, her feet dangling, looking unhappy with her hand to her mouth."
- **Anything new and odd about the legs.** A line of the unsure section names a sitter's legs, feet or seat in 6 readings of 36, for 10 of 36 of v0.54's pictures, and no reader of the new cast names a knee, a thigh or a shin as strange.

No rule was added after these readings, and no picture was built again for them.
