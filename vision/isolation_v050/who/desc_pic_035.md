# pic_035

## Free description
A windy, sunny day in a park with a pond, a curving path, a tall lamp post, trees and a flower bed. On the left a boy in a red shirt and blue trousers walks along the path holding a brown dog on a red lead; the dog is walking ahead to the left. Next to the path an old grey-haired woman with glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, holds a paper bag and scatters seeds on the ground for two small birds (one blue, one red) pecking at them. On the right by the pond a woman in a yellow dress reaches up with one arm and looks surprised, because a blue cap has been blown off and is flying away in the wind above the pond. Wind lines and loose leaves show the strong breeze. The cap seems about to land in the pond or blow further away.
