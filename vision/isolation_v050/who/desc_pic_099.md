## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills in the background. A woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers walks across the grass toward the left, eating a pink ice-cream cone. Behind her is a blue pond with reeds at both ends and a white duck swimming on it. A curving sandy path runs down the right side past an empty wooden bench and a large tree. A tall black lamp post and a green bin stand near the middle, a grey rock lies on the grass in front, and a bed of colourful flowers is at the front left. She is alone and seems to be enjoying a stroll.
