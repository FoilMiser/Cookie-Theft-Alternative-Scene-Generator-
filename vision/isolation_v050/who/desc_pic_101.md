## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back and a gate on the far right. On the right, on the paved path, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is holding a huge silver fork, like a giant dinner fork, and using it to push a little pile of dirt or grit towards a red dustpan, as if sweeping with it. A puff of dust rises from where the fork scrapes the ground. A grey cat is walking along the top of the back fence on the left. An orange cat is lying curled up on the lawn in the middle, apparently asleep. A round bed of colourful flowers is near the house, two potted plants stand by the fence, and a grey wheelbarrow and a green watering can sit on the grass at the front. Sweeping with a giant fork is the strange thing here; a broom would be expected.
