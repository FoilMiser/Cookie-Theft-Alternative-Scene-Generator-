## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, trees, a pond and a winding sand-coloured path. In the front centre, a woman in a yellow dress pushes a teal-and-navy pram; a baby with a pink polka-dot blanket lies asleep in it. On the pond behind her, a white duck is swimming. On the right, near the path, a man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers holds a paper bag and scatters seeds or crumbs on the ground. A blue bird and a red bird peck at the food at his feet. There is an empty wooden bench beside the path, a flower bed of colourful flowers at front left, a green bin, a grey rock, and a tall street lamp in the middle. Nothing looks impossible.
