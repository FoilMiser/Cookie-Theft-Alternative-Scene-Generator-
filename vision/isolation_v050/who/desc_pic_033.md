## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, a pond with reeds on the left, trees, bushes and a winding sandy path on the right. A man with short dark hair and a short beard, wearing a green T-shirt, beige trousers and brown shoes, is walking towards the left in front of the pond while eating a pink ice-cream cone. To the right, next to the path, a tall wooden ladder is standing upright and leaning slightly, but there is nothing for it to lean against — it seems to be propped up on thin air. A tall black lamppost stands in the middle with a green rubbish bin beside it. A grey rock sits on the grass at the front. No one is near the ladder and no one is climbing it.
