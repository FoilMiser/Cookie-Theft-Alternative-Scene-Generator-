# pic_030

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a gate in the fence at the far right. A woman in a yellow dress stands in the left-centre beside a round flower bed, holding a green watering can tipped towards the flowers. A thin blue stream curves up from the can's spout into the air and ends in a few droplets above the flowers. On the right, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers is sweeping a small pile of dry leaves on the paved path with a yellow broom. In the middle stands an empty metal swing frame with one red seat. Behind it, a washing line between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. A grey wheelbarrow sits empty on the lawn at the front. Nobody is talking to anybody; each adult is doing their own garden chore.
