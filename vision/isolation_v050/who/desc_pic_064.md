A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left and a wooden fence with a gate on the right. On the left, a woman in a yellow dress stands next to a flower bed holding a teal watering can; the water comes out in a long curve that rises up from the can and ends with droplets above the flowers, higher than the spout. On the far right a bearded man in a green shirt and beige trousers sweeps a pile of leaves on the paved path with a yellow broom. A grey wheelbarrow stands on the grass at the front. The rising water stream looks wrong.
