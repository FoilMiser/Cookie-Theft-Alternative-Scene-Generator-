## Free description
A sunny back garden seen the other way round: the cream house with blue door is on the right, the wooden fence runs across the back with a gate on the left, and a paved path runs down the left side. A dark-haired woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks right along the fence with a white, brown-spotted dog on a red lead. On the right, an old man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat kneels by the flower bed, using a small trowel to plant a pot of pink flowers. In the front left, a blue bird pecks at seeds on the grass while an orange cat crouches low just behind it, creeping towards it. The cat looks about to pounce on the bird, which does not seem to notice.
