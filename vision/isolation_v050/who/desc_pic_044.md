# pic_044

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate at the far left and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A woman with dark hair in a yellow dress stands next to a round bed of colourful flowers, holding a teal watering can out over the flowers. Strangely, the stream of water does not pour down onto the flowers: it rises from the spout in a long curved arc upward into the air, ending in a few drops above the woman's head height. That looks wrong or impossible, since water should fall downward. The rest of the garden is quiet: an empty grey wheelbarrow sits on the lawn at the front, two potted plants and a bush stand by the fence, and a paved path runs along the left side. No one else is present.
