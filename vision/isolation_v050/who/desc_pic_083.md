# pic_083

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, a gate on the right and a paved path down the right side. A woman with dark hair in a ponytail, in a purple top and teal trousers, stands by the flower bed holding a grey watering can tilted toward the flowers. Oddly, the stream of water from the can goes upward in a curve into the air, with drops at the top, rather than pouring down onto the flowers. On the right, an older man with grey hair and a grey cap, in a brown waistcoat and dark trousers, is sweeping a small pile of brown leaves with a yellow broom beside the path. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands in the front centre, and there are two potted plants and a bush by the fence. There are no animals.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water from the watering can flows upward into the air instead of down onto the flowers, which looks impossible. It could alternatively be read as a separate hose or sprinkler jet, but no hose is visible, so it reads as the can's water going the wrong way.
- The droplets at the top of the arc look like they are falling, so the stream might be meant as water arcing out and falling back, but it starts at the can and climbs, which is odd for a watering can.
