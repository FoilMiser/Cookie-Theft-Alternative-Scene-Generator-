## Free description
A sunny but windy park, with wind lines in the sky and leaves blowing about. There is a pond on the right, a tall lamp post in the middle and a winding path on the left. A woman in a yellow dress stands by the pond, reaching up after a blue cap that the wind has just blown off. The cap is flying off over the pond, so it may land in the water. On the path, an older woman with grey hair and glasses is scattering seed from a paper bag to two small birds, one blue and one red. Behind her, a man in a red T-shirt is walking a brown dog on a red lead along the path. There is a flower bed at the bottom right.
