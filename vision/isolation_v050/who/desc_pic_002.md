# pic_002

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a wooden gate at the far left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A woman with dark hair in a ponytail, wearing a yellow dress, stands in the middle right next to a round flower bed full of colourful flowers. She is holding a teal watering can tilted toward the flowers, so she is watering them. Oddly, the water appears as a blue arc rising up out of the can's spout and ending in drops in the air above the flowers, instead of pouring down onto them. A paved path runs from the gate down to the bottom left. Two potted plants and a round bush sit near the fence, and an empty grey wheelbarrow is on the lawn at the front. No one else is in the garden.

- Tree (behind fence, right) and sun and two clouds in the sky.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water stream curves upward from the spout like a fountain or hose rather than falling down; it could be read as a spray arc, but it looks physically wrong for a watering can.
- The blue line could also be read as a thin hose attached to the can.
- The woman could be read as an older girl or a young woman.
