A green watering can and a grey wheelbarrow stand unused on the grass at the front, and two potted plants and some bushes stand near the fence. The woman is looking down at her sweeping and does not seem to be looking at the dog. It may be that the dog is about to ask, or is hoping, to go for a walk.
