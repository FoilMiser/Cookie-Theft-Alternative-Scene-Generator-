# pic_026

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a paved path, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. At the back left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; a few planks are already blue. At her feet is a paint tin and a paint tray of blue paint. A white dog with brown spots is walking away along the path on the far left, and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray to the dog, so the dog has stepped in the paint and is tracking it across the grass and onto the path. In the centre front, a boy in a green T-shirt and beige trousers walks quickly (motion lines behind him) while reading an open book, not looking where he is going; a cardboard box sits on the grass right in front of him, so he is about to trip over it. On the right, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is watering a flower bed with a teal watering can. The water from the can is drawn as a stream that arcs upward above the can and then curves down, with droplets at the top, which looks odd for a watering can.
