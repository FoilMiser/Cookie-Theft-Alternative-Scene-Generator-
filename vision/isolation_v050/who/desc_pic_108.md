## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a long wooden fence across the back, a gate at the far right and a paved path running down the right side. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands by a flower bed near the house holding a grey watering can. Strangely, the water comes out of the can's spout and arcs upward and back, away from the flowers, with drops shooting into the air above, instead of pouring down onto the flowers. On the right, an older man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers sweeps a pile of leaves with a yellow broom beside the path. There are two potted plants and a bush in the middle, and an empty wheelbarrow at the front. The upward-flowing water looks wrong or impossible.
