## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path down the right side. A dark-haired woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands by the flower bed holding a grey watering can. The water coming out of the can does not pour down onto the flowers: it arcs upward and backward into the air, with drops flying off the top, which looks wrong. On the right, an old man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers sweeps a pile of brown leaves with a yellow broom beside the path. There is a bush, two potted plants and a wheelbarrow on the lawn. Nothing else is happening.
