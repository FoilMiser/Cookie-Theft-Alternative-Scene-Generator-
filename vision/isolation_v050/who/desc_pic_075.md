A busy, sunny back garden with a house and blue door on the left, a wooden fence, and a gate and path on the right. On the left a man in a red shirt sits on a bench reading a red book. In the middle a child in a yellow top and orange shorts holds a kite string; the purple kite is stuck in the tree behind the house. On the right a girl in a pink dress holds a purple and a blue balloon while a red balloon has escaped and floats up into the sky; she reaches up toward it.
