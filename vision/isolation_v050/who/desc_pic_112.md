## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate at the far left, a paved path running from the gate to the front, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A woman in a yellow dress stands on the right, next to a round bed of colourful flowers, holding a green watering can tipped towards the flowers. A thin blue stream rises from the can's spout in an upward curve and ends in a few droplets in the air, above and away from the flowers. Two potted plants and a bush stand by the fence on the left, and an empty grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the front. She is the only person; no animals are visible. The scene is calm, but the water going upward instead of falling onto the flowers looks wrong.
