# pic_102

## Free description
A windy, sunny day in a park with a curving path, a pond, a tall lamp post, a bin, a bench, trees and a flower bed. On the left a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks along the path eating a pink ice cream cone. On the right, by the pond, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans reaches up with a surprised face as a red cap is blown off and flies away over the pond. Wind lines and leaves show the strong breeze. The cap looks about to blow into the pond or further away. There is an empty bench in the middle and a grey rock at the front. Nothing impossible stands out.
