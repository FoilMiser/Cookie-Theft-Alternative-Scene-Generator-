# pic_011

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house, red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence and gate at the back, and a washing line with blue jeans and a yellow striped towel. At the front-left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. Beside her on the bench is a sandwich (or burger), and a small blue bird is perched next to it, pecking at it - apparently stealing her food while she isn't looking; crumbs are on the bench. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress sweeps a pile of leaves with a broom on the grass beside the path. A hose reel with a hose lies next to the bench, a grey wheelbarrow stands at the front-centre, and there is a flower bed and two potted plants.
