A man with dark skin, a short beard, a green T-shirt and khaki trousers walks across the grass in front of the pond, eating a pink ice-cream cone. Near the path on the right, a tall wooden ladder stands upright on the grass, but it is not leaning against anything: there is no wall or tree behind it. A grey rock sits in the foreground. Nobody else is in the picture. The free-standing ladder looks impossible; it should fall over.
