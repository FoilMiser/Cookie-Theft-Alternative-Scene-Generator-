## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, and a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right. A grey cat walks along the top of the fence near a tree. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands on a paved path, looking down. She is holding a huge silver fork, like an oversized dinner fork, and using it as if sweeping a small pile of grey dirt or gravel towards a red dustpan. Little dust puffs and specks show the pile being pushed. In the middle of the lawn a small orange cat is curled up asleep. A wheelbarrow and a teal watering can stand on the grass, and there is a bed of colourful flowers near the house and two potted plants by the fence. The odd thing is the tool: a giant table fork used in place of a broom or rake.
