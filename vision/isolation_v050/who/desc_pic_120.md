## Free description
A sunny town street with a row of colourful shops with striped awnings and a paved square in front. On the left, an older man in a flat cap, cream shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers walks a brown dog on a red lead; the dog is pulling ahead, near a fruit and vegetable stall with a striped canopy. In the middle stand a street lamp, an empty bench and a planter of flowers, and a teal bicycle is parked in a bike rack. At the bottom centre, a blue bird pecks at seeds on the ground while an orange cat creeps towards it in a crouch, as if about to pounce. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks to the right carrying a yellow shopping bag, next to a tree. There are potted plants in the front corners and a bin in the front left. The cat looks like it is about to catch the bird.
