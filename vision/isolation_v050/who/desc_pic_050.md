# pic_050

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence at the back with a gate in the left corner and a stone path on the left. At the back, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; a few planks are already blue. A paint tray and a paint tin with blue paint sit on the grass near her. A white dog with brown spots is walking on the path on the left, and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray to the dog, so the dog has stepped in the paint and is tracking it around. In the front centre, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers is walking quickly while reading an open book, with a cardboard box on the ground just ahead of him, so he looks about to trip over it. On the right, a boy or man in a red shirt and blue jeans is watering the flower bed with a teal watering can. Oddly, the water comes out of the watering can and curves upward into the air in a tall arc with drops at the top instead of pouring down onto the flowers.
