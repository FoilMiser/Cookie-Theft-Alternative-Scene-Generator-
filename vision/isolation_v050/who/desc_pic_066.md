## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence and gate at the back right, and a paved path on the right. In the front left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. On the bench beside her is a sandwich, and a small blue bird is perched on the bench pecking at the sandwich while she is not looking. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress stands by the path sweeping a small pile of dead leaves with a broom. In the middle of the garden is a washing line between two posts with a pair of blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel hanging on it. There is also a hose reel next to the bench, a flower bed, two potted plants near the fence, and an empty wheelbarrow at the front. It looks like the reading woman is about to lose her sandwich to the bird.
