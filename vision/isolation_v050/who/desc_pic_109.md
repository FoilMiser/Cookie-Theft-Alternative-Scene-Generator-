# pic_109

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence at the back, a gate on the right and a stone path in the right corner. The only person is a woman in a yellow dress standing on the path. She is sweeping a pile of grey dust or dirt towards a red dustpan, but instead of a broom she is using a huge silver fork, as big as a broom. This is clearly wrong and absurd: a giant fork is being used as a broom. Around the lawn are a hose reel with a teal hose, a grey wheelbarrow, a teal watering can, a flower bed, two potted plants and some bushes. Nothing else is happening.
