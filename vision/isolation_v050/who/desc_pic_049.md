# pic_049

## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, trees, a winding sandy path, a lamp post and a pond with bulrushes on the right. A dark-haired woman in a yellow dress and red shoes walks along (partly beside) the path on the left, licking a pink ice-cream cone. In the foreground centre, a white goat with small dark horns and a red collar has its head down eating a tuft of grass. A lead runs from the goat's collar down to a small wooden stake in the ground, so the goat is tethered. A green bin stands in the bottom-right corner. Nobody is interacting with anyone; it is a calm scene. The only slightly unusual thing is a goat tied up in a park.
