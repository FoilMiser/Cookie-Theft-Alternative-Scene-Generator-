## Free description
A sunny, windy park with green hills, a winding path on the left, a tall street lamp, a bin, a bench, a pond on the right and a flower bed. White wind lines streak across the sky and leaves are blowing off a tree. A man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands near the pond reaching up with one hand, looking shocked, as his red cap is blown off by the wind and flies up and away over the pond. On the left a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers walks along beside the path eating a pink ice cream cone, seemingly not noticing. There is a grey rock at the bottom centre. It looks like the cap might land in the pond.
