## Free description
A busy, sunny back garden with a house on the left, a fence, a gate and path on the right. On the left a man in a red T-shirt and jeans sits on a bench reading a red book. In the middle at the back, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts holds a kite string, but the purple kite is stuck in the tree beside the house. Next to the child, an older grey-haired man in a cap and brown waistcoat walks along carrying a cardboard box. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds the garden hose nozzle up, with the hose running from a reel by the house across the lawn. A tan dog with a red collar stands in the front on top of/over the hose, smiling. On the right a girl in a pink dress holds a blue and a purple balloon and reaches up towards a yellow balloon that is floating away into the sky.
