## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence and gate on the left and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A woman in a yellow dress with a dark ponytail stands on the paved path at the left, sweeping a small pile of dry leaves with a yellow broom. By the blue door on the right, a brown dog sits holding a yellow lead in its mouth, as if waiting to be taken for a walk. A teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow sit on the lawn at the front, and a round flower bed with colourful flowers is in the middle right. Two potted plants and two bushes are along the fence. The woman is looking down at her sweeping and has not noticed the dog.
