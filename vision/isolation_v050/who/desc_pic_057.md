# pic_057

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path running down the right side. A girl or young woman with a dark ponytail, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, stands by a round bed of colourful flowers holding a grey watering can. A stream of water comes from the watering can, but it arcs upwards and backwards over the flowers in a tall curve, with drops at the top, more like a fountain or hose than water pouring from a can. On the right, an old man with grey hair, a flat cap, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers is sweeping a small pile of brown leaves with a yellow broom on the edge of the path. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands at the front and two potted plants and a bush are near the fence. Everyone is calmly doing garden chores.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water from the watering can looks wrong: instead of flowing down out of the spout, it shoots up into a high arc (with drops at the top) and comes down at the far edge of the flower bed, like a hose jet. It could also be read as the water going from the flowers up into the can (reversed). Either way it looks physically odd for a watering can.
- The man's broom handle runs in front of his body and the leaf pile partly overlaps the path edge; it reads fine as sweeping.
