## Free description
A sunny back garden with the house on the left, fence and gate at the back-right, a paved path on the right, a flower bed, two potted plants and some bushes. The only person is a woman in a yellow dress on the right, by the path. She is holding a very large silver garden fork - or what looks like a giant dinner fork - and using it like a broom to sweep a pile of grey dust/dirt toward a red dustpan on the path. A little puff of dust is rising from the pile. This is odd: she is sweeping with a fork rather than a broom, so the dirt would just fall through the prongs. Around the garden lie a hose reel and hose, a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can, all unused.
