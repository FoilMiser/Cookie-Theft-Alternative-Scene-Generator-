## Free description
A back garden under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds overhead; there is a house with a blue door on the left, a wooden fence, a gate and path on the right, a flower bed and a swing set. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds a garden hose with its nozzle pointed upward, apparently about to water the plants, though no water is coming out. Next to her a man with a moustache in a red T-shirt and blue jeans points up at the dark clouds with a worried, surprised look, as if warning that rain is coming. On the swing set to the right, a small child in a yellow top and orange shorts swings and smiles. A white dog with brown spots stands in the front, near the hose, which runs from a hose reel by the house across the lawn. It seems odd to water the garden when rain is about to fall, which may be the point the man is making.
