A sunny back garden with a gate and path on the left and a house with a blue door on the right. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie has just thrown a red ball toward a woman in a purple top, who has her hands up ready to catch it; a dotted line shows the ball's path. On the left a child in a yellow top and orange shorts holds a blue and a red balloon, but a purple balloon has got away and is floating up into the sky; the child reaches up toward it, looking upset. In the front centre a brown dog sits up like a person, holding and reading a newspaper, which is impossible. There is an empty wheelbarrow, a teal watering can, and a flower bed by the house.
