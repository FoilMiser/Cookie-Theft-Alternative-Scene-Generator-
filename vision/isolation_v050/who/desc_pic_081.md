# pic_081

## Free description
A sunny, windy park with hills, a winding path, a lamp post, a pond and a flower bed; wind lines streak through the sky and leaves blow off a tree. At bottom left a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans walks a brown dog on a red lead along the path; the dog looks happy. Next to them an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and grey skirt holds a paper bag and scatters seeds on the ground, where a blue bird and a red bird are pecking. In the centre-right a woman in a yellow dress stands by the pond reaching up, looking startled, as her blue cap is blown off by the wind and flies over the pond. The cap seems likely to land in the water.
