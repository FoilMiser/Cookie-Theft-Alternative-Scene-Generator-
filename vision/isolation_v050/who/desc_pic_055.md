# pic_055

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A woman in a yellow dress stands beside a colourful flower bed near the house, holding a teal watering can. Instead of pouring down onto the flowers, the water comes out as a long curved stream that arcs upward and ends in droplets above her head, higher than the can. This looks wrong: water from a watering can should fall down, not shoot up. A grey wheelbarrow stands empty on the grass at the front, and two potted plants and a bush are by the fence. No one else is in the garden.
