# pic_059

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back and a gate on the right, with a paved path leading from the gate. A girl or young woman in a yellow dress stands at the edge of the path, looking down and sweeping a small pile of grey dirt toward a red dustpan. The odd thing is that she is not using a broom: she is pushing the dirt with a huge silver fork, as big as a broom. A little puff of dust is rising from the pile. Around the garden are a hose reel with a green hose, a grey wheelbarrow, a teal watering can, two potted plants, bushes, a tree and a round bed of colourful flowers. No one else is present.
