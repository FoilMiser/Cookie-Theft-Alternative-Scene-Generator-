## Free description
A back garden on a grey, cloudy day with dark storm clouds overhead; there is no sun. A house with a blue door is on the left, a fence across the back, a gate and path on the right. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds a hose nozzle in the middle, looking at it; a small white dog with brown spots stands on the hose near the reel. A child in a yellow top sits on a swing and is swinging (motion lines). On the right a man in a red shirt stands on the path pointing up at the sky with a worried face, as if warning that it is going to rain. An empty wheelbarrow is on the lawn. It seems rain is about to start.
