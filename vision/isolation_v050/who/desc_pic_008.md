# pic_008

## Free description
A back garden on a grey day, with three dark storm clouds overhead. On the left is a cream house with a blue door and a flower bed in front. In the middle, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds a garden hose up with its nozzle pointed at her own face. No water comes out of it. The hose runs back to a reel by the house, and a white dog with brown spots is standing on it near the joint. A child in a yellow top is swinging on a swing set, with motion lines behind the swing. On the right, a man in a red T-shirt stands on a paved path by the gate, pointing up at the dark clouds and looking worried, as if rain is about to start. An empty wheelbarrow sits on the grass.
