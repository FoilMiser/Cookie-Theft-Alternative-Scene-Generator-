# pic_040

## Free description
A sunny back garden with the house on the right, a fence and gate at the back-left and a paved path on the left. At the back, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence blue with a paint roller; a section of fence is already blue and a paint tin and tray sit on the grass beside her. A white dog with brown spots walks off toward the path on the left, leaving a trail of blue paw prints across the grass from the paint tray - it has stepped in the paint. In the centre-front, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers hurries along reading an open book, about to walk into a cardboard box on the ground in front of him. On the right, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans waters the flower bed with a teal watering can, with an arc of water and drops coming out.
