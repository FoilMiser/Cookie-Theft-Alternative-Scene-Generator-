## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, trees, a pond on the right, a winding path on the left and a tall street lamp in the middle. A woman in a yellow dress walks along the path towards the left, eating a pink ice-cream cone. In the front centre, a white goat with small dark horns and a red collar grazes on a tuft of grass. A tan rope/lead runs from its collar to a short wooden stake in the ground, so the goat is tethered. There is a green bin in the bottom right corner. Nothing looks clearly wrong, though a goat in a park is a little unusual.
