# pic_022

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence at the back, a gate in the right corner and a stone path on the right. At the front left, a woman or girl in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. Next to her on the bench is a sandwich, and a small blue bird is standing on the bench pecking at it, with crumbs around it. She is absorbed in her book and does not seem to notice the bird eating her food. On the right of the lawn, a woman in a yellow dress is sweeping a pile of dead leaves with a broom. A clothesline holds blue jeans and a yellow striped towel. A garden hose reel sits beside the bench, a grey wheelbarrow stands on the lawn at the front, and there are flowers and two potted plants. The bird stealing the sandwich is the notable event.
