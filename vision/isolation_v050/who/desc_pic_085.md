## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence with a gate at the back right, and a paved path down the right side. On the path at the front right, a woman with dark hair in a yellow dress is sweeping a little pile of grey dirt/dust towards a red dustpan, but instead of a broom she is using a huge silver fork, nearly as long as she is tall. A small puff of dust rises near the dustpan. This is clearly wrong: a giant dinner fork is the wrong tool for sweeping, and the dirt slips through the prongs. Around the garden are an empty wheelbarrow, a teal watering can, a hose reel with hose, a flower bed and two potted plants. No one else is in the garden.
