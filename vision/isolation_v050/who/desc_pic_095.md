# pic_095

## Free description
A sunny park with hills behind, a winding path on the left, a tall street lamp near the middle, a pond with bulrushes on the right, trees, bushes and a green litter bin in the bottom right corner. A woman with dark hair in a ponytail, in a yellow dress and red shoes, is walking along the path toward the left while eating a pink ice-cream cone. In the front centre, a white goat with small dark horns and a red collar has its head down, eating a tuft of grass. A rope runs from the goat's collar down to a short wooden stake in the ground, so the goat is tied up. The pond is empty, and no one else is around. Nothing looks clearly wrong.
