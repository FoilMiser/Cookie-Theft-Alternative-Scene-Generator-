# pic_004

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back and a gate at the far right. A woman in a yellow dress with a dark ponytail stands left of centre next to a round flower bed, holding a teal watering can and watering the flowers. As in a fountain, the water shows as a blue arc going up from the can with drops at the top, rather than falling. A washing line between two posts in the background holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. On the right, a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers sweeps a pile of dry leaves on the paved path with a yellow broom. An empty swing set with one red seat stands in the middle-right, and an empty grey wheelbarrow sits on the lawn at the front. Both adults are busy with garden chores and neither is looking at the other.
