## What I see
A girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes is walking or running across green grass. She holds a long stick in one raised hand, with a yellow oval on top of it. The oval reads as a balloon on a stick (or a lollipop). Along the top edge there is a row of dark grey posts, like the bottom of a fence or railing.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The yellow object could be a balloon on a stick or a large lollipop; the long thin stick and her held-out arm make "balloon" the more likely reading, but the stick looks rigid rather than like a string.
- Her hand holding the stick is drawn close to her chin/face, so it is a little unclear whether she is holding it out in front or up near her face.
- The posts at the top are cut off by the frame; one post at the top right is longer and thinner than the others and looks a bit out of place.
- Her shadow sits under her feet as she strides; nothing else seems to float.
