# iso_04

## What I see
An older man with grey hair, a blue-grey cap and a grey moustache stands in a garden, painting a wooden fence. He holds a long-handled paint roller with both hands and is rolling blue paint onto the fence; a few planks are already blue while the rest are still plain tan wood. A silver paint bucket with blue paint on its rim sits on the grass beside him. In the foreground there is a row of colourful flowers (yellow, orange, red, pink, purple), a green bush on the right, and a tree behind the fence.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The roller head is up near the top of the fence but slightly away from the painted area, and it is drawn on the fence surface with the handle angled oddly; it reads as rolling the fence, though it could look like it is hovering just in front of it.
- The painted region is a neat rectangle including the horizontal rails, more blue than the roller appears to have reached from where he stands; plausible as earlier work.
- His arms/hands are small and tucked close to the body; the hand-to-handle grip is a little hard to read, and his legs look quite long and thin relative to the torso.
- The bucket is fairly far from him (no tray), but that is not strange.
