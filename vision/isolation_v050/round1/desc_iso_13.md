## What I see
A man in a green shirt and beige trousers sits on the low left end of a red seesaw, reading an open book. A small girl in a pink outfit with yellow shoes sits on the raised right end, holding a handle with one hand and holding her other hand up to her mouth. The seesaw is a red beam on a grey triangular pivot, set on grass in front of a dark railing fence. The man's weight keeps his end down, so the girl is stuck up in the air.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl's hand-to-mouth pose could read as worry or fear (being stuck high up) or as surprise, or she could be calling out to the man. Her face looks uneasy rather than happy.
- The man is absorbed in his book and does not look at the girl. He may be ignoring her, or simply not noticing.
- The man's seat on the beam looks odd. The red beam seems to pass in front of or through his lower legs and shins rather than under his bottom, so his legs look tangled with the beam.
- The man is much larger than the girl, which fits an adult and a child, but his body looks quite bulky and crouched for the space.
- A green round shape at the top right edge is cut off; it may be a tree.
