## What I see
A girl in a pink dress stands on the grass in front of a wooden fence, holding the strings of a yellow and a blue balloon in one hand. Her other arm is raised toward a red balloon that is floating high up in the sky near a cloud, its string dangling below it. It reads as one balloon having escaped and the girl reaching after it.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The raised arm could be read as reaching for the lost balloon or as waving.
- The red balloon's string ends in mid-air well above her hand, so it is clearly detached; its end hangs in front of the bushes behind the fence.
- Her expression is small and hard to read (surprised vs. sad).
- The yellow balloon's string curves oddly and appears to reach her hand from a different direction from the blue one; both strings meet at the hand but are thin and hard to follow.
