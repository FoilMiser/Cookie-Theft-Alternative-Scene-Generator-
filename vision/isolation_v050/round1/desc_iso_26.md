# iso_26

## What I see
A girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes stands on grass in front of a wooden fence with green bushes or trees behind and blue sky above. She holds the strings of a yellow balloon and a blue balloon in her left hand. A red balloon has floated higher up, and she is reaching up toward it with her other arm, as if she has just let go of it or is trying to catch it.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The red balloon's string ends in mid-air well above her raised hand, so it reads as escaped/floating away; alternatively she might be about to grab it, but her hand does not reach it.
- Her raised hand is near the fence top but not touching the red string; the gap makes it unclear whether she is waving, pointing, or reaching.
- Her face looks calm or slightly surprised rather than clearly upset, so it is not obvious whether losing the balloon is meant to be sad.
- The yellow and blue balloon strings converge at her hand loosely; the yellow string curves in a slightly odd loop.
