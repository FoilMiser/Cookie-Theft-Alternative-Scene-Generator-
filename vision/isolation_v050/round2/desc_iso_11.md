# iso_11

## What I see
A girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes stands on grass in front of a wooden picket fence with green bushes behind it. She holds the strings of a red and a purple balloon in her outstretched left hand. A blue balloon floats higher up to the upper right with its string hanging loose, and she reaches up with her other arm and looks up toward it, as if it has just escaped and she is trying to catch it.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The blue balloon's string ends in mid-air, well away from her raised hand; this reads as a balloon that got away, but could also be read as a string that should connect to her hand and doesn't.
- Her raised hand is small and sits close to her head; it could read as reaching up or as waving/touching her head.
- The two held balloons' strings run to her hand at a long angle from far left; they look acceptable but the balloons are positioned against the fence rather than above her.
