## What I see
A white goat with dark, backward-curving ridged horns stands on green grass with its head lowered to the ground. It appears to be grazing: a few green leaf or grass blades hang from its mouth, a small pink tongue shows, and a small dark-green tuft of grass sits just in front of its muzzle. A soft dark shadow lies under the goat. In the top-right corner there is a blue area with a dark curved outline, which looks like the edge of a pond.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The blue shape at the top right is probably a pond or water edge, but because it is cut off it could also be read as a patch of sky.
- The green blades in the mouth do not clearly join the grass tuft; they look slightly separate from it, as if hanging or floating near the mouth rather than being pulled from the tuft.
- The neck is very short or hidden, so the head seems to come straight out of the front of a tall, humped body. The bend from back to head is abrupt.
- The legs are thin and clustered under the middle and rear of the body. The front legs sit back under the chest rather than under the lowered head, so the stance looks a little cramped. It is hard to tell which leg is which.
- There is a small pale blob under the chin that could be a beard or part of the muzzle.
- The ear sticks straight out sideways like a small flap, which looks slightly stiff.
