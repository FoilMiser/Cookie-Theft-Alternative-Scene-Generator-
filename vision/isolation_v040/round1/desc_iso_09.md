## What I see
A woman with dark hair pulled back, wearing a purple t-shirt, teal trousers and dark shoes, stands on a paved sidewalk in front of a shop. She has one arm stretched up toward an orange baseball cap that is flying through the air above her and to the left, and her mouth is open in a round "o" as if surprised. White wavy wind lines and a few small leaves (one brown, two green) streak across the air beside the cap, so the wind seems to have blown the cap away (presumably off her head). Behind her is a pale green building with a blue-grey door, a shop window and a green-and-white striped awning, plus a peach-coloured building on the right. In front, at left, is a dark blue planter box of orange, pink, yellow and red flowers, and at the bottom right part of a wooden slatted bench.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Wind direction is a little unclear: the wind lines trail off to the right of the cap, which could be read as wind blowing from the right and carrying the cap left (matches where the cap is), or as the lines simply sitting off to the side. The leaves are among the lines rather than clearly travelling with the cap.
- Her raised hand is clearly below the cap with a gap, so she could be grabbing at it as it flies off, or waving or reaching for something else. Nothing shows the cap was on her head; that is my inference.
- The raised hand is small, with spread-out, claw-like fingers that are hard to make out.
- Scale: her head reaches the height of the awning and she looks large next to the door behind her. That is probably just because she is nearer the viewer, but it could read as the awning being very low or her being very tall.
- The bench is cut off by the bottom edge, so only its backrest and seat slats show. Its position makes it hard to tell whether we see it from the front or the back.
- Her free arm hangs at her side with the hand near her hip; the trouser legs are fairly stiff and tube-like, but nothing looks detached or passes through anything else.
