- Only two hooves are clearly visible (one front, one back). The other legs seem hidden behind these or merged with them, so the animal can look two-legged at a glance. A rounded lump between the head and the front leg (near the neck/shoulder) could be the neck, a shoulder or an ear. It looks a little oddly joined.
- The rear end has a small upturned point, probably a tail, though it could read as a pointed hip.
- The wooden structure in the corner is too cropped to identify. It has no visible shadow or base, so it seems to sit or float on the grass without clear contact.
