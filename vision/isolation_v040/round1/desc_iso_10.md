## What I see

She appears to be sweeping, with the broom head next to a small mound of green leaves (or grass clippings) on the grass. Behind her is a tall wooden plank fence with pointed tops and greenery above it. A plain wooden post stands on the lawn at left, and two terracotta pots with green leafy plants sit at the left edge, one cut off. A beige paved path runs diagonally from the bottom of the picture up to a large brown wooden panel on the right, which has a round dark knob or hole and looks like a gate or door.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The pile is a smooth, perfect half-dome with a flat bottom, and it is green on green grass. It could read as a heap of leaves or grass cuttings, or as a solid green dome or upturned bowl.
- The broom bristles sit beside or just behind the pile rather than clearly pushing it, and their tips reach lower than her feet. That is probably because the broom is nearer the viewer, but the bristles could look as if they go below ground level.
- The brown panel on the upper right has a strong diagonal edge running from the top down to the right. It could be a gate or door seen at an angle (or partly open), or the side of a shed with a sloping roof. The round dark spot could be a knob, a keyhole or a knot-hole.
- The tall post at left goes above the fence and off the top of the picture without connecting to anything visible. It has a small green ring at its base, so it could be a fence post, a clothesline pole or a bare tree trunk.
- Her legs below the dress are quite thin. One red shoe is partly hidden behind the broom handle and bristles, so it is hard to tell how her feet are placed. Her grey shadow sits off to the right of her feet rather than directly under her.
- Her hands and arms on the broom handle look joined normally; nothing seems detached or floating.
