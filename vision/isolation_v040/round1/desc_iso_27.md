## What I see

She is sweeping: the yellow broom head rests on the ground right next to a small pile of brown, oval, leaf-like pieces, as if she is pushing them into a heap. Behind her is a wooden park bench (right), the base of a dark lamppost or pole (left), and the bottom edge of a shopfront or building with windows along the top of the frame. She faces left, looking down toward the broom and pile.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The pile on the ground: the pieces are brown and tan ovals with a centre line. They read as dry fallen leaves, but could also be read as seeds, nuts, beans, or small sausages or bread rolls. They are not clearly leaf-shaped (no stems or points), and there are no autumn reds or oranges.
- Her arms: both arms come forward to the broom handle. The arm holding the lower part of the handle seems to come from the front of her chest, under the puffed sleeve, rather than clearly from the shoulder. The joins look slightly stiff or tube-like.
- Her near foot (red shoe) is partly hidden behind the broom bristles and sits against the leaf pile, so it is hard to tell whether she is stepping on the pile or standing just behind it.
- The dark rectangle at the bottom of the pole on the left reads as a lamppost base, but it is cut off at the top, so it could be another kind of post.
- Only one bench leg is clearly visible. The bench looks fine, if slightly thin at the base.
