# iso_38

## What I see
An older man with grey hair, a grey cap, white long sleeves, a brown vest and dark trousers stands on a lawn at the left. He holds a hose nozzle out in front of him with both arms stretched forward, and a single blue water drop hangs at the nozzle. The dark teal hose runs from his hands down to the grass, across the bottom of the picture, and up to a round hose reel standing next to a cream house wall with a blue door on the right. A tan dog with dark floppy ears and a red collar stands in the middle, seen mostly from behind with its head turned to the left and its tail up. Behind them are a flower bed of mixed red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers, a round green bush, and a wooden fence.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Whether he is watering: only one drop shows at the nozzle, with no spray or stream, and the nozzle is not aimed at the flower bed, which is well off to the right. It could be read as watering (the hose just turned on) or as a hose that is only dripping while he holds it.
- His arms are held out stiffly and level at chest height, and his upper body faces the viewer while his arms point right. The pose looks a little mechanical.
- The hose runs under the dog's back feet, so the dog seems to be standing on it. That could be deliberate (the dog stepping on the hose) or just the two overlapping.
- The dog's body looks like it faces away from the viewer while its head is turned sideways to the left, so the way the head joins the body is slightly awkward.
- The hose reel sits on a small dark box against the house wall. It reads as a hose reel, but it could also be taken for a wall tap or a wheel.
