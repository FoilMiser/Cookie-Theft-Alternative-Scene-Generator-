# iso_35

## What I see
A white four-legged animal with a dark, backward-curving horn stands on green grass with its head lowered to a small tuft of grass. It looks like it is grazing or sniffing the grass. It reads most like a goat. Its body forms a high arch, and there is a soft shadow under it. In the top-right corner, cut off by the frame, is part of a brown wooden structure with upright posts and horizontal rails. It could be a fence, a bench or the back of a chair.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Species: the horn suggests a goat. The plain white body and round face could also read as a sheep or lamb. Only one horn is visible, so it could even pass for a unicorn-like creature.
- The head is a plain round white disc with one dot eye. It sits low beside the front leg, and I cannot clearly see a neck joining it to the body, so it looks detached or floating next to the leg.
- The body proportions look odd. The back is a tall hump, and the front of the body bends down sharply like an elbow near the shoulder. The legs look thin and bent at unusual angles, so the pose reads as hunched or contorted rather than a natural grazing stance.
- The grass tuft overlaps the muzzle. This fits eating, but it could also look as if the grass is passing through the head.
- The wooden structure is too cropped to identify with confidence (fence, bench or chair).
