- Her hands look small for her arms. Her legs are a bit uneven, with one straight and one slightly bent, which makes the pose look a little stiff, though not clearly wrong.
- The planter box sits in front of the lower part of the door, partly blocking it.
- The bench is cut off at the bottom edge, so I can't tell whether its legs reach the ground.
