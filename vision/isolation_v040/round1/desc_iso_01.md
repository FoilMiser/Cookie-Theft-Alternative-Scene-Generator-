# iso_01

## What I see
A woman with dark hair tied back, a purple short-sleeved T-shirt, teal trousers and dark shoes stands on grass in front of a blue pond with a few cattails, with green hills behind and a dark lamppost to the right. One arm is raised high toward an orange baseball cap flying through the air up and to the left. The other arm is bent across her waist with the hand on her stomach. White wind streaks come from the right, carrying the cap and a few brown and green leaves, so the wind seems to have blown her cap off. Her eyes are wide and her mouth is open, as if she is surprised, and she is looking toward the cap.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Her raised hand is well below the cap and the fingers are spread. She could be grabbing after it or just throwing up her hand in surprise. A third reading is that she has just tossed the cap herself, though the wind lines make "blown off" more likely.
- The hand on her stomach is small and blobby, and it sits low on the side of her torso nearest the viewer's left. It reads as clutching her middle, but it also looks a little stuck on, and it is not obvious which arm it belongs to until you follow the forearm back to the elbow on the right.
- At the far right edge, about halfway down, there are a few small dark rounded shapes and a tan/brown block that have been cut off by the crop. I cannot tell what they are. They could be part of a bench, a bag, or another figure's hand.
- The lamppost's square base sits at about the same height on the page as the far edge of the pond, so it reads as standing on the grass farther back. Its scale next to the woman is a bit hard to judge: it could be a short post nearby or a tall one in the distance.
- Nothing else looks clearly wrong. She overlaps the pond but is standing on the grass in front of it, and she casts a shadow under her feet.
