## What I see
An older man with grey hair, a grey beard, glasses and a grey-blue cap stands on a lawn. He wears a white shirt, a brown vest and dark trousers, and he holds a garden hose. One hand holds the nozzle up near his face and the other hand holds the hose lower down. A single blue water drop hangs just below the nozzle. He seems to be looking at or into the nozzle, as though checking why little or no water is coming out. The hose runs down from his hands, loops along the ground across the bottom of the picture and connects to a round hose reel at the right, next to a cream house wall with a blue door. A brown dog with a red collar stands on the hose with its back to us, its head turned to look over its shoulder and its tail raised. Behind them are a wooden fence, a green bush and a round bed of mixed red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The single drop could mean either of two things: the hose is barely dribbling, perhaps because the dog is standing on it, or water is just starting to come out. It does not read as a proper spray.
- Where the dog stands, the hose looks wider and flatter than elsewhere. It could be a bulge in the hose, a flattened or kinked section, or a small puddle under the dog's paws.
- The man's nozzle points roughly back toward his own face rather than at the flowers. This could be deliberate (he is peering into it) or just an awkward pose.
- I can't tell exactly how the dog's hind feet sit on the hose, so it is unclear whether a paw is really pressing on it or just next to it.
- The man's face is small and the grey around it blends together, so it is hard to separate the hair, beard and glasses.
- Nothing obviously floats or passes through anything else. The hose passes close beside the man's legs but seems to stay in front of them.
