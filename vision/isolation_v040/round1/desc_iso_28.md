# iso_28

## What I see
An older man with grey hair, wearing a grey cap, white shirt, brown vest and dark trousers, stands on a lawn holding a garden hose. One raised hand holds the nozzle up near his face, and the other hand holds the hose lower down. Only a single blue water drop comes from the yellow nozzle tip, and he seems to be looking at it. A white goat with small curved horns stands in the middle of the lawn facing him, and its back hooves stand on the hose where it lies across the grass. The hose runs from a hose reel mounted by the house wall at the right, along the ground under the goat, and up to the man. Behind them are a wooden fence, a round flowerbed full of colourful flowers (yellow, orange, purple, pink, red), a green bush, a cream house wall with a blue door, and part of a wooden bench at the far right edge.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The yellow fan shape at the nozzle tip could be the nozzle head itself (a yellow plastic sprayer) or a small spray or splash of water drawn in yellow. Only one blue drop is clearly water.
- Under the goat's back hooves the hose becomes a noticeably wider, darker teal stretch. This reads either as the hose squashed flat under the goat's hoof, or as a puddle of water on the ground. The change in thickness looks a bit odd.
- The nozzle points up and back toward the man's own face and shoulder, not toward the flowers or the goat. He could be checking why no water comes out, or the aim could just look awkward.
- The goat's head is a round shape on a fairly narrow neck, and it looks slightly loosely attached to the boxy body. Its front legs are thin, straight sticks, and its front hooves are hard to see where they meet the hose and grass line.
- The man's raised arm is long and straight and looks a little stretched. There is a small white tuft near his ear and jaw that could be a sideburn, a beard, or the arm of a pair of glasses.
- The hose reel sits on a dark box at the base of the house wall, just in front of or beside the flowerbed. It is unclear whether it is fixed to the wall or standing on the ground.
