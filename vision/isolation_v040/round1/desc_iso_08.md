## What I see

A few green strands run from its mouth to the tuft, as if it is chewing grass, and there is a small pink spot at its mouth. - The blue shape at the top right is cut off. It could be water (a pond) or some large blue object; it could even be a patch of sky, though the outline makes that less likely.
- The front legs slant back toward the middle of the body and nearly meet the hind legs, so all four legs bunch together under the belly. The stance looks cramped and a bit awkward.
- The body is hunched with a high, lumpy back. The short upright point at the rear left reads as a tail, but it could also be a hip bone sticking up.
- The head hangs below the body on a sharply bent neck; it seems attached, but the join between neck and head is not clearly drawn.
