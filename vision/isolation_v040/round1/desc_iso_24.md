# desc_iso_24

## What I see
This is a back-garden scene with a wooden fence, a green bush, a cream house wall with a blue door, a bed of colourful flowers (yellow, orange, purple, pink and red), and part of a wooden bench at the right edge. On the left, an older man in a grey cap, white long-sleeved shirt, brown vest, dark trousers and brown shoes holds a garden-hose nozzle up near his face with both hands. A single water drop hangs from the nozzle. The dark teal hose runs from his hands down to the ground, across the lawn and up to a hose reel by the house wall. A white goat with small grey horns stands in the middle on top of the hose, turning its head back to look at the man. It looks like the goat is standing on the hose and pinching off the water, while the man peers into the nozzle to see why only a drip comes out.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Under the goat's back legs, the hose widens into a flat, dark blob. It could be read as the hose squashed flat under the goat's hoof, or as a dark puddle of water on the grass.
- The man's arms are drawn as one white block reaching forward from about cheek height. The shoulder looks very high, and the sleeve partly covers the lower half of his face, so the arms look oddly joined to the body.
- He points the nozzle toward his own face. This can be read as looking into the nozzle or as about to drink or spray himself.
- The goat's neck is thick and its head is small and turned sharply back. The head looks slightly stuck on top of the body.
- The hose bends sharply upward from the ground to the man's hands and looks a bit stiff. Otherwise nothing seems to float or pass through anything else.
