# iso_13

## What I see
A white goat with two dark blue-grey horns stands on a plain green field. It has its head lowered right down to a small tuft of dark green grass in front of it, so it looks like it is grazing or sniffing the grass. A soft grey shadow sits under its feet. In the top-right corner there is the edge of a blue shape with a dark outline, which looks like the edge of a pond or a patch of water.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The body looks oddly shaped. It is short, tall and strongly hunched, with the rump and a small tail nub high up at the top-left, and the back curving steeply down to the head. The goat looks folded or curled up rather than standing level.
- There is no clear neck. The head seems tucked in under the front of the body, so at first glance it is hard to tell which end is the front. It could also be read as the animal bending over with its head between its front legs.
- The legs are long and thin compared with the small body, and it is hard to see which legs are front and which are back. They seem to join the body at odd points rather than at clear shoulders and hips.
- A thin dark line near the top of the neck or shoulder could be a collar or strap, or just a stray outline stroke.
- I could not clearly make out an eye or other face details. A white shape pointing right next to the horns is probably an ear.
- Single green blades below the grass tuft reach down past the shadow, as if the grass were sticking out below the ground line.
- Because of the horns I read it as a goat. Without them, the hunched white body could also pass for a sheep or lamb.
