# iso_02

## What I see
An older man with grey hair and a grey mustache walks to the left across grass in front of a blue pond with cattails at its right end. He wears a blue-grey flat cap, a white long-sleeved shirt, a brown vest, dark trousers and brown shoes. Down by his waist he holds a red leash, which runs in a straight, taut line to the blue collar of a white dog with brown spots and brown ears. The dog walks ahead of him to the left with its tail up. He is walking his dog.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- His hands are bunched together at the end of the leash, so I cannot clearly tell whether one hand or both are holding it. The hand shape is small and a little lumpy.
- The leash is a perfectly straight, stiff line, more like a rod than a rope, though it does read as a pulled-tight leash.
- The dog's head is fairly small next to its body. The leash meets it right where the brown ear overlaps the neck, so the clip point is partly hidden by the ear, but it does look attached to the collar.
- His legs are long and thin and his hips are narrow, so the join between the vest and the trousers looks slightly pinched. It is still believable as a walking stride.
- In the bottom-right corner there is a grey rounded shape cut off by the crop. It could be a rock or boulder, the top of a bin, or part of some other object. I cannot tell which.
- Nothing seems to be floating or passing through anything else. Both the man and the dog cast shadows on the grass, and the man's head overlapping the pond's outline reads as him standing in front of it.
