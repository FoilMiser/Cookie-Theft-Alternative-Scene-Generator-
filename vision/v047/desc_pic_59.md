# pic_59

## Free description
A sunny playground with a metal railing fence at the back, a tree on the left and a red-and-yellow slide on the right. A grey-haired old man in a cap and brown waistcoat is walking to the right while reading an open book, not looking where he goes. A closed cardboard box sits on the grass just behind him to his left. A child in a yellow shirt is sliding down the slide with one arm raised. In the front, a woman in a yellow dress is taking a photo with a camera pointed toward the right (toward the slide/sandpit). A girl in pink kneels in a sandpit building a sandcastle with a small flag, holding a red spade. A green bin, a grey rock and some bushes complete the scene.
