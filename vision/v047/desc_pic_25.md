# pic_25

## Free description
A sunny park playground with a railing fence and trees behind it. On the left a small girl in a pink dress stands holding a yellow balloon on a string next to a red-and-yellow slide. In the middle a grey cat creeps low towards a red bird that is pecking at seeds on the grass, as if about to pounce. Behind them, an old man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat sits on a bench reading a red book; a sandwich lies on the bench beside him and a small blue bird is pecking at it without him noticing. In the front centre a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and has a hand to their face as if crying; the pink ice cream has fallen on the ground and a brown dog with a red collar is licking it up. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue top holds a kite reel up, but the purple kite is stuck in the tree. A small white dog with brown spots crouches playfully by a tennis ball, tail wagging. An orange cat is curled up asleep near a sandpit that holds a red bucket and blue spade. A red bicycle stands by itself on the right.
