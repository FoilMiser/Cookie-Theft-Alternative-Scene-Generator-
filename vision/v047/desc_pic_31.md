# pic_31

## Free description
A sunny park with a pond, rolling hills, trees and a winding sandy path on the left. In the centre a man with short dark hair, a green shirt and beige trousers stands at the edge of the pond fishing with a long rod; the line hangs down to a red-and-white float in the water. On the right of the pond a white duck swims. On the left near the path a white goat with a red collar is grazing on a tuft of grass, tied by a rope to a small stake in the ground. There is an empty wooden bench, a tall black lamp post, a grey rock, a flower bed and a green litter bin at the bottom right. The scene is calm; nobody else is around.
