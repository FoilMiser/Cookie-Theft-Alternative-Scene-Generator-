# Picture 85

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate on the right, and a paved path leading to it. In the front, centre-left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is watering a flower bed, but he is pouring the water out of a frying pan rather than a watering can. At the back by the fence, a small girl in a pink dress holds the strings of a purple and a red balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other toward a blue balloon that has floated away high into the sky, its string dangling. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress walks with a white dog with brown spots on a red leash; the dog is walking toward the left ahead of her. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass in the front, and a wooden bench is in the bottom left corner. The odd things are the frying pan used for watering and the balloon the girl has lost.

## Inventory
- Boy (front, centre-left): red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers, white shoes; pouring water from a frying pan onto the flowers.
- Girl (back centre-right, by fence): small, dark hair, pink dress, yellow shoes; holding two balloons and reaching up toward a balloon that is flying away.
- Woman (right): dark hair, yellow dress, red shoes; walking a dog on a red leash.
- Dog (centre-right): white with brown spots and blue collar; walking on the leash ahead of the woman.
- Frying pan with water: stream of water pouring onto the flower bed.
- Flower bed (front left): round soil bed with red, yellow, orange, purple and pink flowers.
- Purple and red balloons (back centre-right): held by the girl.
- Blue balloon (sky, centre-right): floating away with string hanging down, not held by anyone.
- Wheelbarrow (front centre): empty grey wheelbarrow.
- Bench (front left corner): empty wooden bench.
- House (left): cream wall, red roof, blue door, one window.
- Fence and gate (back and right): wooden fence and closed gate; paved path leading to the gate.
- Tree behind the house, sun top right, two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The frying pan in place of a watering can looks deliberately wrong.
- The blue balloon is drifting with no one holding it; it reads as the girl having let it go, since she is reaching up toward it.
- The woman faces left following the dog, so they read as walking together toward the house.
