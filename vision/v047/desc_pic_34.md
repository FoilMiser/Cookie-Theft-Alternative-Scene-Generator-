# pic_34

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A small girl in a pink dress stands by the gate on the left holding the strings of a red and a yellow balloon, with her other arm reaching up; a purple balloon is floating away high in the sky above her, so she seems to have just let one go. In the middle a woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds a garden hose, but no water comes out of the nozzle. A brown dog with a red collar is standing on the hose further along, which would explain why the water has stopped. The hose leads to a hose reel near the flower bed on the right. There is a swing set with an empty red swing on the left-centre and a washing line with a yellow-and-red striped top and blue jeans hanging on it. Odd thing: a cardboard box is floating in the sky above the fence, tilted, for no apparent reason.
