# pic_61

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate at the left, a path down the left and a cream house with a blue door on the right. An old man in a cap and brown waistcoat is painting the fence blue with a paint roller; a few fence boards near the house are already blue. A paint tin and a paint tray with blue paint sit on the grass by his feet. A white-and-brown dog stands in the middle of the lawn with blue paint on its feet, and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray to the dog: it has clearly walked through the paint. The man has not noticed. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps leaves on the left near the path. There are two pot plants, a flower bed, a watering can and a wheelbarrow.
