## Free description
It's a sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left, a wooden fence, a gate and a paved path on the right. A child in a yellow top and orange shorts holds a red balloon and a yellow balloon. The child is reaching up with the other arm towards a blue balloon that has floated away high into the sky, trailing its string. An orange fish is sitting on a branch of the tree behind the house, which is impossible. In the front, a lawn sprinkler connected to a hose sprays arcs of water onto a cardboard box sitting on the grass. The box is getting wet and there's a puddle under it. There's also a flower bed by the house and two potted plants by the fence.
