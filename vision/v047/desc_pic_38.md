## Free description
A sunny back garden seen the other way round: cream house with red roof and blue door on the left, wooden fence across the back, gate and paved path on the right. An older man with grey hair, glasses and a flat cap, in a brown vest, is walking while reading an open book/paper, not looking where he goes; a cardboard box is on the grass just in front of him, so he looks about to trip over it. By the fence a small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a yellow and a purple balloon, reaching his other hand up, while a blue balloon floats away high in the sky above. On the right, on the path, an older grey-haired woman with glasses in a teal cardigan is sweeping with what looks like a giant fork instead of a broom, pushing a small pile of dust/dirt toward a red dustpan. A grey wheelbarrow and teal watering can sit on the lawn, and there is a flower bed by the house.
