## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a gate on the left, a paved path running down the left side, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. An older grey-haired woman with glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, stands at the right end of the fence holding a paint roller; the last few fence boards next to the house are already painted blue, so she seems to be painting the fence blue. A paint tin and a paint tray sit on the grass at her feet. A white dog with brown spots is walking across the lawn in the middle, and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray area to the dog, so the dog has stepped in the blue paint and is tracking it across the grass; its feet look blue. In the front right a woman with dark hair in a yellow dress sits on a wooden bench reading a red book, not noticing. There is a round flower bed with colourful flowers in front of the painter. Odd thing: an orange fish is sitting in the tree behind the fence, among the branches, which is impossible.
