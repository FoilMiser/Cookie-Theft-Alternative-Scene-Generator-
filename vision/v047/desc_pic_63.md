## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left, a wooden fence with a gate at the right, a paved path on the right and a washing line with blue jeans and a yellow striped towel. A woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench by the house drinking through a straw from a cup. In the middle, a girl in a purple top holds a garden hose and looks at the nozzle with a puzzled face; no water is coming out. The hose runs from a reel by the house across the lawn, and a brown dog is standing right on top of the hose, which would block the water. A small white-and-brown puppy is asleep curled up on the grass. On the right, a man in a green shirt walks down the path reading an open book and is about to walk into/trip over a cardboard box in front of him. A blue bird sits on top of the fence.
