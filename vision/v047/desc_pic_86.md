# Picture 86

## Free description
A back garden on a grey, overcast day with three dark storm clouds in the sky; there is no sun. On the right, an older man in a flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands at the edge of a paved path, pointing up toward the dark clouds with one hand. His face looks surprised or concerned, as if he is noticing that rain is coming. On the left is a cream house with a red roof and blue door, and a wooden fence and gate run along the back. In front of the house is a flower bed, and beside it a green hose reel with the hose lying on the grass, its yellow nozzle pointing out. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands in the front centre. No rain is falling yet, so it seems a storm is about to arrive.
