# pic_44

## Free description
A back garden under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds, suggesting rain is coming. An orange cat is walking along the top of the wooden fence in the middle. On the left, near the gate and path, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers points up at the cat (or the sky) with a surprised face. In the centre, an older man with grey hair, a flat cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers holds a hose with a yellow nozzle, apparently about to water, while the hose runs back across the lawn to a reel by the house on the right. A white dog with brown spots stands on the hose in front of the flower bed, smiling, which would squash the hose and stop the water. No water is coming out of the nozzle. It seems odd to be watering the garden when it looks about to rain.
