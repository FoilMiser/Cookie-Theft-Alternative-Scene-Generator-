# pic_41

## Free description
A sunny, breezy day in a park with a pond, rolling hills, trees and a winding path. On the left, in front of the pond, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers is reaching up with one arm toward a red cap that is flying through the air above him. White wind lines and a few small green leaves blow across from the left, so it looks like the wind has just blown his cap off and he is trying to grab it. His mouth is open as if surprised. On the right, beside the path, an older man with grey hair, a flat grey cap, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands eating a pink ice-cream cone. There is a tall black lamp post in the middle, a grey rock in the front, and a green bin in the bottom-left corner. The cap looks like it may land in the pond if he does not catch it.
