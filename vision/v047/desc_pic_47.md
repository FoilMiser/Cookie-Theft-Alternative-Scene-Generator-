## Free description
A sunny playground with a railing fence, a slide on the left, a swing set in the middle, a see-saw on the right, and a sandpit at the front. A boy in a yellow top and orange shorts slides down the slide, raising one arm. On the see-saw, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers sits on the low (left) end reading a book, while a small girl in a pink dress is stuck up on the high (right) end with a puzzled face, finger at her mouth. The man does not seem to notice the girl. The swing is empty. In the sandpit there is a red bucket and a blue spade but nobody is playing there. A green bin, a rock, a bush and a tree complete the scene.
