# pic_10

## Free description
A sunny back garden, this time with the cream red-roofed house on the right and a wooden gate on the left. On the left, on a pale paved path, a dark-haired girl or young woman in a yellow dress is sweeping a pile of dead leaves with a broom. On the right, an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, stands at a flower bed holding a grey watering can. A thin blue stream rises up out of the can into an arch over the flowers, with drops at the top, so the water seems to be shooting upwards. In the middle there is a washing line between two posts with a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. Two potted plants stand on the grass near the fence, an empty grey wheelbarrow is in the front, and a wooden bench stands by the house.

- Wheelbarrow, centre front: grey, empty.

- House, right: cream, red roof, window, blue door. Gate, left, in the wooden fence; tree behind fence on right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water stream goes up out of the can rather than pouring down; it could be read as water spraying upward (impossible for a watering can) or as a thin hose. It looks unintentionally odd.
- The figure sweeping could be a girl or a young woman; her size is close to an adult's.
