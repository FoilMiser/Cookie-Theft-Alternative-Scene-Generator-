# pic_16

## Free description
A sunny back garden with the cream red-roofed house on the left, a wooden fence along the back, a gate and path on the right, and an empty swing set. Three adults are doing garden jobs. On the left, a bearded man in a green shirt and beige trousers holds a teal watering can over the flower bed; the water comes out as a thin blue stream that rises upward in an arch with drops at the top rather than pouring down onto the flowers. In the middle back, a man with brown hair and a moustache in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a stick; a few boards are already blue and a paint tin stands on the grass behind him. In the front, a woman with dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers is digging with a shovel, lifting a clump of earth next to a mound of dirt. Nobody is on the swing.
