## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, a gate and paved path on the right. A woman with a dark ponytail, in a purple top and teal trousers, is in a sitting position next to the flower bed, drinking from a red cup with a straw — but there is no chair, bench or anything under her, so she seems to be sitting on thin air. On the right, a small child in a pink outfit is swinging on a red swing in a grey swing frame, with motion lines behind. In the front, a garden hose runs across the lawn to a sprinkler that is spraying arcs of water, and the spray is landing on a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which has a puddle under it and water dripping down its sides. So the box is getting soaked. Nobody seems to notice the wet box.
