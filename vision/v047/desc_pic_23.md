## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the left, a washing line in the middle, and a cream house with a blue door on the right. On the left, an older man with grey hair, a cap, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers walks along while reading an open book, and a cardboard box sits on the grass just ahead of him, so he may trip over it. In the middle, an older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, holds a grey watering can over the flower bed, but the stream of water goes upward in an arc from the spout, with drops at the top, instead of falling down onto the flowers. On the right, a boy in a green shirt sits on a bench reading a red book, and a blue bird is pecking at the sandwich on the bench beside him. A yellow striped top and blue jeans hang on the washing line. The man not watching where he walks and the bird taking the boy's sandwich both seem about to cause small mishaps.
