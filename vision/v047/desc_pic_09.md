# pic_09

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left. A man with brown hair and a moustache, in a red T-shirt and blue jeans, stands next to a round flower bed by the house. He holds a teal watering can over the flowers. Further right, on a pale paved path leading to a wooden gate in the fence, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and black trousers is sweeping a small pile of dead leaves with a long broom. In the middle of the lawn there is an empty swing set with one red seat, and in the front an empty grey wheelbarrow. A birdhouse on a tall pole stands near the gate. The odd part is the water from the watering can: a thin blue stream comes out of the can and curves upward and to the left, with drops at the top, as if the water were spraying up into the air instead of falling onto the flowers.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water stream goes up from the can and bends back over itself with drops at the very top. Two readings: (a) the water is spraying upward, which is not how a watering can works; (b) it is meant to be a thin hose or spray coming from the can. Either way it does not land on the flowers and looks unintentionally strange.
- It is not clear whether the man is watering on purpose or about to splash something; he looks calm.
