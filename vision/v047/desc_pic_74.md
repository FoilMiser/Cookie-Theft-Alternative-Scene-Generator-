## Free description
A busy, sunny town square with a row of colourful shops and a paved plaza. A little girl in a pink dress points up at a blue-and-white beach ball that is stuck in the branches of a tree. A man in a green shirt walks past carrying a blue shopping bag, and a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie has both arms raised in the air as if cheering or stretching. A woman in a purple top sits on a bench reading a red book, while a blue bird on the bench beside her pecks at her sandwich, which she has not noticed. A woman in a yellow dress sweeps a pile of leaves near a fruit-and-vegetable stall. In the front, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and covers his face as if crying, because his ice cream has fallen on the ground and a white spotted dog is licking it up. Two things look impossible: a brown dog wearing glasses sits upright reading a newspaper, and the red bicycle on the right has square wheels. A red bird flies overhead.
