# Picture 84

## Free description
A busy, sunny park with a pond, a path, a bench and lots going on at once. In the front centre-left, an older man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat is watering a flower bed, but he is pouring the water out of a frying pan instead of a watering can. On the far left, a brown dog wearing glasses is sitting up and reading a newspaper, which a dog could not really do. In the front centre, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is crying with a hand to his eye, holding an empty ice-cream cone; his pink ice cream has fallen on the ground and a white dog with brown spots is licking it up. Behind them by the pond, a girl in a pink dress is throwing bread to a white duck that is swimming toward the crumbs. At the back, a white goat tied to a stake is eating grass, and a blue bird flies in the sky. On the right, an older woman with grey hair and glasses is sweeping leaves on the path with a broom, and a boy in a red cap and blue jacket holds up a kite reel while his purple kite is caught in the tree, its string running into the branches. The bench is empty.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Using a frying pan to water flowers looks deliberately wrong/odd.
- A dog reading a newspaper with glasses is impossible/odd.
- The woman's tool looks like a broom with yellow bristles; it could also be read as a rake, but she seems to be sweeping.
- The kite reads as stuck in the tree, but could also be read as flying in front of the tree; the string goes into the branches, so stuck seems more likely.
- The girl by the pond might be holding a cup or a bag; I read it as a bread bag.
- The goat's rope goes to a small peg in the ground, so it is tethered.
