## Free description
A sunny park with a winding sandy path, a wooden bench, a lamp post, and a pond with reeds behind. A boy in a red baseball cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers sits on the bench eating a pink ice-cream cone with a big smile. A small white-and-brown dog sits up on its hind legs beside the bench, begging and looking up at the boy, clearly hoping for some ice cream. On the right, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers walks along in front of the pond, also holding a pink ice-cream cone up near his mouth. He is walking toward a flower bed and looks close to stepping into it. A green rubbish bin stands in the bottom-right corner.
