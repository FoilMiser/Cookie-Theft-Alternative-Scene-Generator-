## Free description
A sunny back garden with no people in it. The cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, a wooden fence runs across the back with a gate on the right, and a pale path leads to the gate. A grey cat is walking along the top of the fence on the left, in front of a tree. A small red bird is perched on top of the fence on the right. In the middle of the lawn a small light-brown puppy with a red collar is lying curled up asleep. A washing line between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel. Around the garden are a flower bed, a hose reel with a hose lying on the grass, a teal watering can, an empty grey wheelbarrow and two potted plants. The cat faces toward the bird, so it might be heading for it, though they are far apart.
