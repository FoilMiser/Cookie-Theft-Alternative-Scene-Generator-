# pic_53

## Free description
A sunny back garden beside a cream house with a red roof and blue door, with a wooden fence along the back and a gate at the right. A man with a moustache in a red T-shirt and blue trousers is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; about three fence boards to his right are already blue, while the rest is still plain wood. A small paint tin with blue paint sits on the grass near his feet. In the front-centre, an old woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal cardigan and grey skirt is raking up dry brown leaves into a small pile. A green watering can sits on the lawn to the right, near a paved path. A blue bird flies across the sky. There is a flower bed with colourful flowers in front of the house.
