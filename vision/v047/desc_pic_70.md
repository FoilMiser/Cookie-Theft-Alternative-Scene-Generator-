A sunny playground with a railing fence behind, a swing set, a red slide with a yellow chute, a sandpit and a tree. On the left, a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. Next to him is a teal bicycle whose wheels are square instead of round, which is impossible. In the sandpit on the right, a small child in pink is building a sandcastle with a small flag, holding a red spade. A small orange cat is curled up asleep on the grass at the far right near a grey rock. An empty bench and a green bin stand in the background; the swing is empty.
