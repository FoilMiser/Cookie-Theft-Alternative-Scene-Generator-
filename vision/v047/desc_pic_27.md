# pic_27

## Free description
A back garden: wooden fence, gate and paved path on the left, cream house with red roof and blue door on the right. A woman with dark hair in a ponytail, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, is painting the brown fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; a section of the fence beside her is already blue. On the grass by her feet are a paint tray with blue paint and a paint tin. A small white dog with brown spots is walking away towards the path, and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray to the dog, whose paws are blue: it has stepped in the paint. The woman has not noticed. There is a bird house on a tall pole, a teal watering can, an empty wheelbarrow, a flower bed, a bush, and a bench by the house.
