## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a wooden gate on the left, a paved path, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. An elderly woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal top and grey skirt stands in the right-centre of the garden watering a flower bed with a grey watering can. Coming out of the can's spout there is a thin blue arc of water that curves upward into the air, with droplets at the top, rather than pouring down onto the flowers. Behind her, a washing line between two posts holds a yellow-and-red striped top and a pair of blue jeans. Two potted plants sit on the grass near the fence, an empty wheelbarrow stands in the foreground, and a wooden bench is beside the house. No one else is present.
