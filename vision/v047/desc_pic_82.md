# Picture 82

## Free description
A sunny town street or square with a row of colourful two-storey shops with striped awnings, paved with grey tiles. In the centre-right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts has just thrown a red ball with his arm stretched out; a dashed line shows the ball arcing through the air toward a small girl in a pink dress on the right. The girl holds her arms out in front of her, ready to catch it. On the left, a woman in a yellow dress stands holding a camera up to her eye, taking a photo pointed toward the right, apparently toward the children playing catch. Behind her is a market stall with crates of green, yellow, orange and red fruit under a teal-and-white striped awning. There is a black lamp post, a tree in a round planter on the right, a green rubbish bin, and a potted plant in the front left corner. Nothing looks wrong; it reads as a game of catch being photographed.
