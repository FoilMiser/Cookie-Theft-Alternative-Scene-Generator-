## Free description
A busy, sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a gate at the back left, a paved path down the left side, and a cream house with a blue door on the right. On the path at the left, a woman in a yellow dress is sweeping a small pile of brown leaves with a broom. In front of her, a bearded man in a green shirt walks along while reading an open book, and a cardboard box sits on the grass just ahead of him, so he looks likely to trip over it. A woman in a purple top is painting the fence blue with a paint roller; part of the fence is already blue, and her paint pot and paint tray sit on the grass. A trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray to a white-and-brown dog, so the dog has stepped in the paint and is walking blue paint across the lawn. In the middle, a boy in a red cap and blue top holds out his hands to catch a red ball that a child in a yellow top and orange shorts has just thrown (a dotted line shows the ball's path). An older man in a cap and brown waistcoat is digging with a shovel beside a pile of soil. On the right, a man in a red shirt sits on a bench reading a red book while a blue bird pecks at the sandwich on the bench beside him. An orange cat is curled up asleep on the grass near a wheelbarrow, and a red bird flies in the sky. The wheelbarrow's wheel looks square, which is impossible for a wheel.
