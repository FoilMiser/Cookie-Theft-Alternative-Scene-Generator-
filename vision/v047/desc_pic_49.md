# pic_49

## Free description
A sunny back garden next to a cream house with a red roof and a blue door, and a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right. In the middle, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers is pegging washing on a clothes line strung between two posts: a pair of blue jeans and a white T-shirt are hanging there, both dripping water. A wicker laundry basket full of more clothes sits on the grass under the line. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress holds the end of a green garden hose and looks at the nozzle, puzzled, because no water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel at the front left across the lawn, and a white dog with brown spots is standing on the hose, which would explain why the water is blocked. Further right, a small brown dog wearing glasses sits up holding and reading a newspaper, which is impossible for a real dog. A flower bed with colourful flowers sits in front of the house and a paved path runs along the right edge to the gate.
