# pic_15

## Free description
A sunny back garden with the cream red-roofed house on the right and a wooden gate and paved path on the left. On the path at the front left, a small child with curly hair in a yellow top and orange shorts is flying an orange kite, holding the spool up; the kite flies high in the sky on a long string. Near the back fence, a man with brown hair and a moustache in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is raking a small pile of leaves. In the centre front, a woman with dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers is reading a red book in a sitting position, but there is nothing under her - no chair or bench - so she seems to be sitting in mid-air. A washing line holds a striped towel and jeans that hang at a slant, as if blowing in the wind. A teal watering can, a hose reel with hose, and a flower bed by the house are also on the lawn.
