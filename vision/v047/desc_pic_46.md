# pic_46

## Free description
A back garden under a grey sky with dark storm clouds. An older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, is painting the wooden fence blue with a roller on a long handle; a section of fence is already blue and a tin of blue paint sits on the grass behind her. A child in a yellow top and orange shorts stands in the middle of the lawn pointing up at the sky, probably at the clouds, as if warning that rain is coming. On the left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers kneels by the flower bed with a trowel, planting a pink flower from a pot. The coming rain would likely spoil the fresh paint. There is a wheelbarrow and a watering can in front and a path to a gate on the right.
