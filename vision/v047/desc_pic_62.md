A sunny park with a pond on the left, a lamp post in the middle, a sand path on the right and trees and bushes around. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands near the pond scattering seeds from a paper bag to two birds (one blue, one red) pecking on the ground. In the front middle, a little girl in a pink dress holds an empty ice-cream cone and is rubbing her eye, crying, because her pink ice cream has fallen on the ground; a brown dog is now licking the splat of ice cream. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks along the back carrying a yellow bag. On the right a white-and-brown dog plays with a yellow tennis ball on the path. A wooden bench sits by the path and a grey wheelbarrow is at the right edge; its wheel looks square, which is odd. A green bin is at bottom left.
