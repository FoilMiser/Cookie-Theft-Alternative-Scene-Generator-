# Picture 87

## Free description
A sunny playground surrounded by a dark metal railing fence, with the sun in the top left. On the left there is a red slide with a ladder, and in the front a sandpit containing a red bucket and blue spade, with a grey rock nearby. Near the slide, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a red and a blue balloon by their strings in one hand and reaches up with the other toward a purple balloon that is floating away into the sky. In the centre, a woman in a yellow dress stands with her hands on her hips wearing a metal bucket upside down on her head, which is strange. On the right, a man in a green shirt sits on the low end of a red seesaw reading a book, while a small girl in a pink dress sits up on the high end, held in the air, gripping the handle. A white dog with brown spots in the front is playing with a tennis ball, tail wagging, and a red bird sits on a branch of the tree on the right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The woman wearing a bucket on her head looks deliberately odd; her face looks fairly calm or slightly annoyed.
- The girl on the seesaw is held up because the heavier man is sitting on the other end not paying attention; she might be stuck and waiting for him, or just sitting there. Her hand near her mouth could mean she is calling out or thinking.
- The purple balloon has escaped; the boy reaching up reads as trying to get it back.
- The man is perched right at the low end of the seesaw, partly beside it rather than clearly on a seat, but it reads as sitting on it.
