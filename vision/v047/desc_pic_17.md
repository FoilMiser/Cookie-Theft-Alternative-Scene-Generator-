# pic_17

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a wooden gate at the back right. A man with a short beard, wearing a green shirt and beige trousers, stands near the house beside a round flower bed full of red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers. He is watering the flowers, but he is pouring the water out of a grey frying pan or saucepan held out by its handle, not a watering can. A stream of water arcs from the pan onto the flowers. On the grass there are a small bush and two potted plants by the fence, and a grey wheelbarrow at the front. A paved path runs from the gate down to the bottom right. Nothing else is happening; the main odd thing is that he is using a pan to water the plants.
