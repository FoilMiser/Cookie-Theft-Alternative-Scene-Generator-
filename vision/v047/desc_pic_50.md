# pic_50

## Free description
A windy day in a park with a winding sandy path, a lamp post, a pond on the right and green hills behind. White wind lines streak across the sky and over the pond, and leaves are blowing off the big tree on the left. On the far left, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers walks a white spotted dog on a red lead. In the middle, an older man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat holds a paper bag and scatters seeds onto the path for two small birds, one blue and one red, which are pecking at them. On the right, a man with a moustache in a red shirt and blue trousers stands by the pond with his arm stretched up and a surprised face, because his red cap has just blown off and is flying over the pond. It looks like the cap might land in the water.
