The new drawings and revision 26 were read blind in whole test scenes among other events: six pictures, six fresh readers, one picture each (`design_tests_v028/test_N.png`, `desc_test_N.md`). `tools/cycle/design_tests.py` has the jobs.

| 1 | Tier-4 park | The begging dog with a walker and a bag about, the ball on its branch, a runner | "Sits up on its hind legs right beside him, begging with its front paws raised and its nose pointed up at the ice cream". The ball "seems plausibly caught rather than floating" |

| 4 | Tier-3 street | The splat, the ball, the square wheel, a dog walked by | "Her pink ice cream has fallen and lies as a splat on the pavement with sprinkles around it"; "a blue-and-white striped ball that is stuck in the branches". | 5 | Tier-3 garden | The shadows of a sleeper, a digger, a waver, the hose | "Her shadow sits under her, so she looks grounded". The hose "looks a little stiff, as though the hose stands up by itself" |
| 6 | Tier-3 park | Closed eyes at a picnic, a runner and a pram | "Her eyes look closed or squinting". Her "shadow is only faintly visible", and "over one oval shadow" for the runner |

## What this says

- **The begging dog.** Whose food is not in doubt in either picture, and both readers offered "right beside". Each still allows that the dog may be doing something else. Nobody near it is named as its owner.
- **The shadows.** Six pictures and no oval called wide, large or offset. One "faintly visible" and one box's "slightly apart" are the new end of the same remark, and a much smaller one.
- **The splat.** Read as what it is in a whole picture. No hat and no saucer.
- **The ball.** Named as stuck in both pictures. Nobody remarks on the fork at the top of the trunk, or on a branch drawn on the crown.
- **The seesaw.** The story reads. The heavy rider's leg over the plank goes on being remarked on, and goes on the list again.
- **The beard.** Test 4's "mask" sent the bearded man back to the bench for a goatee (`isolation_v028.md`, round 3). These six pictures have the fuller beard, so the goatee is first read in a whole picture in the screen.
