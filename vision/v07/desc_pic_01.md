# Picture 01

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden, with a yellow sun in the top left, two white clouds, a tall wooden fence across the back with a wooden gate on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A woman with dark hair in a ponytail, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, stands in the middle of the lawn holding the nozzle of a garden hose in both hands at chest height. She is looking straight at the nozzle, and only a single drop of water is coming out of it. The hose runs from a hose reel beside the flowerbed at the right, along the grass, and up to her hands. A brown dog with a red collar is standing right on top of the hose, with its paws on it, and is looking to the left. It looks as if the dog is squashing the hose and stopping the water. That means the woman may get sprayed in the face if the dog steps off while she is peering into the nozzle. A red dog bowl of food sits on the grass near her feet. There are two potted plants near the fence, a round flowerbed full of bright flowers in front of the house, and a stone stepping-stone path running from the gate down the left side.
