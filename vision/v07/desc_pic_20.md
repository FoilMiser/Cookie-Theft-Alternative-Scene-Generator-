# Picture 20

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a gate and stepping-stone path on the left. A girl in a blue hoodie with a ponytail is painting the fence blue with a paintbrush, and a section of fence near the gate is already blue. A tray of blue paint sits on the grass a little way behind her. A white dog with brown spots has stepped in the paint and is walking away from the tray, leaving a trail of blue paw prints across the lawn. In the middle, a man in an orange safety vest and blue trousers stands with his hands on his hips wearing what looks like a blue bucket upside-down on his head, which is an odd thing to wear. On the right, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is watering a flower bed with a garden hose, which runs back to a hose reel. A teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow sit on the lawn at the bottom, and an empty wooden bench is in front of the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The thing on the orange-vest man's head. Reading 1: an upside-down blue bucket, possibly the paint bucket, worn as a joke or by mistake. Reading 2: a tall blue hat. There seems to be a bit of orange cap brim showing underneath, so it may be a bucket sitting on top of his cap.
- The hose loops down near the bearded man's legs before curving along the front of the flower bed. It's hard to tell whether it passes behind his legs or appears to go through them.
- The girl is painting with a brush, but her paint tray is several steps behind her, so she would have to walk back to reload the brush.
- The dog's paws don't clearly look blue, though the prints show it walked through the paint.
- The man in the orange vest is just standing, hands on hips. He could be supervising, watching the dog, or watching the man with the hose.
