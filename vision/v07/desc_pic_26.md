# Picture 26

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the sun in the top left and two white clouds. A wooden gate and stepping-stone path are on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the right. In the middle, a washing line strung between two wooden posts holds a yellow towel with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans, both pegged up. A bearded man with dark skin, wearing a green shirt and beige trousers, kneels on one knee at the flower bed on the right, holding a small pot with a pink flower, as if about to plant it among the other flowers. A black-and-white cat lies curled on the grass under the washing line, apparently resting or asleep. Two potted green plants stand on the grass at the left, and a teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow are at the bottom. Something impossible is happening: an orange fish is sitting in the tree behind the house, among the leaves.
