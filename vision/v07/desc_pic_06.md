# Picture 06

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence with a gate on the left, and a stepping-stone path down the left side. An older man with grey hair and a grey beard, wearing a blue-grey flat cap, a white long-sleeved shirt, a brown waistcoat, dark trousers and brown shoes, is standing at a washing line strung between two wooden poles in the middle of the garden. He is reaching up with both hands to peg a white T-shirt onto the line. A yellow garment with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans are already hanging on the line, and the man is standing partly in front of the striped garment. In the front left there is a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow with a wooden handle, sitting on the grass. A hose reel with the hose coiled up stands beside a round flowerbed of bright flowers in front of the house. Two potted plants stand near the gate. Nothing seems about to go wrong; it looks like an ordinary washing day.
