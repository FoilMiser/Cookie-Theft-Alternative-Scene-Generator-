# Picture 24

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden, with the sun in the top left, two white clouds, a wooden gate and stepping-stone path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A woman with a brown ponytail, wearing a blue hoodie and dark trousers, stands facing the wooden fence and is painting it blue with a paint roller on a long handle; three fence boards and the rails across them have already been painted blue. A grey paint tray with blue paint sits on the lawn behind her. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is walking away across the grass toward the left, and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray to the dog, showing it has stepped in the paint. The woman has her back to the dog and does not seem to have noticed. A red bowl of pet food sits on the grass at the bottom near the path, and there is a flower bed with colourful flowers by the house, a tree behind the house and a couple of bushes along the fence. It looks like the dog is about to spread blue paint further around the garden. One small inconsistency is that the dog's own feet still look white even though it is leaving blue prints.
