# Picture 02

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with nobody in it. The house, cream with a red roof and a blue door, is on the left, and a wooden gate is in the fence at the far right with a stepping-stone path leading from it toward the front right corner. The sun is in the top right and there are two white clouds. The strange thing is a brown cardboard box hanging in the air in front of the fence, in the middle of the picture. It is tilted at an angle, and there is a dark shadow on the grass some way below it, so it seems to be in mid-air rather than resting on anything. It looks like it is falling or tumbling, or floating, but there is no person or anything else nearby that could have thrown or dropped it. In the front of the garden there is a grey wheelbarrow with a wooden handle and a teal watering can, both just sitting on the grass. A round flowerbed full of bright flowers is in front of the house, and two potted plants stand near the gate.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The box can be read two ways: falling or tumbling down (perhaps tossed over the fence by someone out of sight), or impossibly floating in the air. The tilt suggests motion; nobody is there to explain it.
- It could also be taken as a box leaning against the fence, but the separate shadow on the grass well below it makes it look airborne.
- The box overlaps the fence boards and its bottom edge sits a little below the bottom of the fence, so it is in front of the fence, not stuck to it.
- Apart from the box, the scene looks like an ordinary empty garden.
