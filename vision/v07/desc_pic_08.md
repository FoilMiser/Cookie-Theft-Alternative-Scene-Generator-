# Picture 08

## Free description
This is a playground on a gloomy day: the sky is grey and three dark storm clouds hang overhead, but no rain is falling yet. On the left an old man in a flat cap and brown vest points up at the sky with his mouth open, as if he is saying it is about to rain. In the front centre an old woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses rakes a small pile of orange and green leaves on the grass. At the back on the right a bearded man in a green shirt raises one hand as if waving, and a woman in a yellow dress walks past him carrying a cardboard box. A child in a yellow top and orange shorts holds a kite string, but the purple kite is up in the tree on the right and looks stuck in the branches. In the front right two girls are on a seesaw: a small girl in a pink dress sits on the low end and a bigger girl in a blue hoodie sits on the raised end. The rest of the playground is empty: the slide, the single red swing and the sandbox have no one on them. Odd things: the bigger girl is on the end that is up while the smaller child is on the end that is down, which is the wrong way round for their weights. The kite is drawn overlapping the tree's leaves, so it reads as caught rather than flying.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The bearded man's raised hand could be a wave or he could be reaching or gesturing at something; it is not clear who he is waving at.
- The kite could be stuck in the tree or flying just in front of it. Because it overlaps the leaves, it looks stuck.
- The seesaw tilt looks physically wrong: the bigger girl is up and the smaller girl is down. Either the drawing is inconsistent, or the bigger girl is pushing herself up with her feet.
- The old man could be pointing at the clouds (rain coming) or at the kite; his finger points straight up rather than towards the kite.
- It is unclear whether the woman with the box is with the bearded man or just walking past him.
