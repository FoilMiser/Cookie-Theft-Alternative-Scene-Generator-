# Picture 16

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a gate and stepping-stone path on the right. An old man in a grey cap and brown vest is watering a round bed of colourful flowers, but he's doing it with a frying pan: he holds the pan out by its handle and a long arc of water pours out of it onto the flowers. That's odd and physically impossible, since a frying pan couldn't hold or pour that much water in a stream like a hose, and a proper teal watering can is sitting unused on the grass in the bottom right. An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a wooden bench in the bottom left, drinking from a white cup. A white cat sits on the doorstep by the blue door with a red leash coming from its mouth and lying in a loop on the grass, which is unusual for a cat. On the right there's an empty swing set with a red seat, and a grey wheelbarrow is parked near the bottom of the lawn. Nothing urgent seems about to happen. The main oddity is the frying-pan watering.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The leash and the cat. Reading 1: the cat is holding a leash in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. Reading 2: the leash is clipped to the cat's collar and is just lying on the ground with nobody holding it. The line seems to start at the mouth or chin.
- The dark round object the old man holds is most likely a frying pan. It could conceivably be read as a dark watering-can rose or a sieve, but it has a long straight handle like a pan.
- The water comes out in a long, continuous arc as if from a hose, which a pan couldn't produce.
- The woman's legs hang down in front of the bench, so she looks perched on its front edge rather than sitting back. Probably just the drawing style.
- I couldn't tell what is in the woman's cup, whether tea, coffee or water.
