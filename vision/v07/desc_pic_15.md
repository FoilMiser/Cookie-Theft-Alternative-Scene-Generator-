# Picture 15

## Free description

This is a busy, sunny town street with four shops along the back, a pedestrian plaza in front, and a fruit stall on the right. It looks windy: there are wind lines and green leaves blowing near the pink shop on the right, a red cap is flying through the air beside the lamppost, and a purple balloon with nobody holding it is drifting up by the pink building's upper window. A man in a red shirt with both arms up and his mouth open looks startled, possibly because his cap blew off. A bearded man in a green shirt reaches one hand up, as if trying to grab the cap or waving at it. In the bottom right a little girl in a pink dress has both arms up and her mouth open, maybe because her balloon got away. A woman in a yellow dress is reading a book as she walks, and a blue bucket sits right in front of her, so she looks about to walk into it or trip over it. An old man on a bench is eating a pink ice cream cone while a brown dog stands in front of him looking up at it, as if begging. In the front, an elderly woman is raking leaves, a man in an orange cap and orange safety vest is walking a white spotted dog on a red leash, and a girl in a blue hoodie walks along carrying a yellow shopping bag. Nobody is minding the fruit stall.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Whose cap is it? It's red, which matches the man in the red shirt, and he looks startled. But the bearded man in green is the one reaching toward it. Reading 1: the red-shirt man's cap blew off and the green-shirt man is trying to catch it. Reading 2: it's the green-shirt man's cap and he's reaching for his own hat.
- The bearded man's raised hand could be reaching for the cap or simply waving.
- The balloon is far from the little girl in pink (it's up near the top right building, she's at the bottom right). She may have lost it, or she may just be excited or reacting to something else, such as the wind.
- The red cap is drawn overlapping the top of the lamppost, so it's hard to tell whether it's in front of the post or caught on it.
- The man in red's raised arms could mean shock, trying to catch something, or enjoying the wind.
- The elderly woman's tool reads most naturally as a fan rake, but its narrow head could also be read as a litter picker.
- Whether the yellow-dress woman will actually hit the bucket isn't certain. It's just ahead of her and slightly to the side.
- The wind is shown only around the pink building. Nothing else in the scene, like the tree, people's clothes or the awnings, looks wind-blown.
