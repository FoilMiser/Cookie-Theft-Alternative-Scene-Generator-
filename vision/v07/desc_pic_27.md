# Picture 27

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the sun in the top right and two white clouds. A cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, and a wooden gate with a stepping-stone path is on the right. In the middle, an older woman with grey hair and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, reaches up to peg a white T-shirt onto a washing line that already holds blue jeans and a yellow striped towel. On the right, a child with dark skin and curly hair, in a yellow shirt and orange shorts, sits on a red swing on a metal swing frame, smiling with eyes closed. A birdhouse on a pole stands behind the fence. In the front there is a teal hose reel by the flower bed, an empty grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can. Something impossible is happening: an orange fish is sitting in the leaves of the tree behind the house.
