# Picture 28

## Free description
This is a sunny playground with a metal railing fence and hedges behind it, the sun in the top right and two clouds. In the front left, a red seesaw on a grey pivot is tipped up: a small girl with dark hair in a bun and a pink dress sits on the high end holding the handle, while a man in a red shirt and blue jeans sits on the low end with his feet on the ground. In the back centre is a swing frame with a single empty red swing. On the right is a slide with a red frame, a yellow-and-orange chute and a ladder, and a boy with dark skin and curly hair, wearing a yellow shirt and orange shorts, stands at the very top with both arms raised in the air, as if cheering, not holding on. An empty wooden bench and a green rubbish bin are in the background, a big tree is on the left and a bush on the right. In the front right there is a sandbox full of sand and a grey rock, and a red bucket and blue spade lie on the grass at the bottom centre, outside the sandbox. Nothing looks impossible, but the boy standing at the top of the slide with no hands on the rail looks a little risky.
