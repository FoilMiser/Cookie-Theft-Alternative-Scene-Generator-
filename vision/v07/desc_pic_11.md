# Picture 11

## Free description
This is a sunny town street with a paved square in front of a row of four colourful shops with striped awnings. On the left, a person in an orange cap and orange safety vest stands by a fruit and vegetable stall, holding a camera up to their face to take a photo. In the front left an old man in a flat cap and brown vest walks a white spotted dog on a red leash. In the middle a bearded man in a green shirt jogs or runs to the left past the shops. In the front centre a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an ice cream cone and looks down, because his scoop of pink ice cream has fallen on the ground and a brown dog has its head down eating it. Elsewhere there is a street lamp, a planter of flowers, a teal bicycle parked in a bike rack, a tree in a round planter, and an empty café table with two chairs at the bottom right. The scene is mostly ordinary; the main event is the dropped ice cream being eaten by the dog. Nothing looks impossible.
