# Picture 14

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden behind a house with a red roof and a blue door, with a bright sun at the top right and two white clouds. An old woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a wooden bench in front of the house reading a red book. A woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers stands by a flower bed watering it with a teal watering can. In the middle, between the posts of a clothesline hung with jeans and a striped towel, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts kicks an orange ball to the right. On the right are two potted plants, a wooden gate and a stepping-stone path, and at the front there is an empty wheelbarrow and a second teal watering can on the grass. The odd thing is the water from the woman's watering can: instead of pouring down onto the flowers, the stream curves back up into the air above the flowers like a fountain, with drops at the top, which water from a watering can could not do. The kicked ball may be heading towards the potted plants.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water stream looks physically wrong: it goes upward out of the flowers like a fountain instead of falling from the spout. Another reading is that it is just a stylised pour drawn oddly, but as drawn it rises above the flowers.
- There are two matching teal watering cans, one in the woman's hand and one on the grass. This is possible, but slightly odd.
- It is not clear where the ball is meant to go; it could hit the clothesline post or the potted plants, or just roll across the lawn.
- The boy appears to be standing between the clothesline posts, close to the fence. This is likely just perspective.
