# Picture 13

## Free description
This is a busy back garden behind a house on a gloomy day, with three dark storm clouds in a grey sky. A blue ball is stuck up in the tree behind the house, and a woman in a blue hoodie and a young man in an orange cap and safety vest are both pointing up towards it, the young man looking surprised. A man in a red shirt stands by the house with one arm raised, looking over at them with his mouth open. In the middle of the garden a little girl in a pink dress is jumping in the air with her arms up, and a white spotted dog lies on the grass nearby, rolling or sleeping, near a red bowl of dog food. A woman in a purple top is painting the fence blue with a brush, and part of the fence is already blue. A blue bird is perched on top of a clothesline post next to the hanging jeans and striped towel. On the right a boy in a yellow shirt reaches up with both arms while a purple balloon floats away high in the sky, as if he has just let go of it. The oddest thing is the wheelbarrow at the front: where its wheel should be there is a square shape, like a square wheel, which could not roll. With rain clouds overhead, the washing on the line and the wet paint may be about to get rained on.
