# Picture 22

## Free description
It's a sunny day in a back garden, with a bright sun in the top right corner and two white clouds. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue front door. A tall wooden picket fence runs across the back of the garden, with a wooden gate on the right and a beige stepping-stone path leading up to it. A child with dark skin and curly hair, wearing a yellow top and orange shorts, sits on a wooden bench in the front left and drinks from a white cup. In the centre-right a young man in a blue hoodie and dark trousers is raking leaves on the lawn; a small pile of brown and green leaves has gathered at the rake. An orange cat lies stretched out flat on the grass at the bottom centre, and a red bowl of pet food sits on the grass to its right. There is a round flower bed full of red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers in the middle-left, a tree behind the house and a bush beside it. Nothing looks clearly impossible; the only slightly odd thing is that there are fallen leaves to rake even though the only tree is fully green and far away from them.
