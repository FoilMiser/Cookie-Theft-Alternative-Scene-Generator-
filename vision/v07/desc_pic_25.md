# Picture 25

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny park with hills behind, a sun in the top left and two clouds. On the left, a girl with a brown ponytail in a blue top and dark trousers stands on a winding path holding a kite string and looking up: her purple kite is caught in the branches of the tree beside her. On a bench next to her, a woman with dark hair in a bun and a yellow dress is reading a red book; at the other end of the bench there is what looks like a big layered sandwich and a blue cap-shaped object. In the background a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks along carrying a yellow shopping bag, and a child with curly hair, a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands by a tall lamp post holding out a small white item toward the pond, where a white duck swims among small pieces of bread floating on the water. In the front centre, a white spotted dog with a blue collar stretches in a play-bow, a brown animal with big ears and a long tail (it looks like a monkey or a dog wearing a red bandana) sits on the grass reading an open book, and a white goat with small horns has its head down at the bottom. A wooden ladder rises out of the pond on its own with nothing to lean against, and on the right an older woman with grey hair and glasses carries a cardboard box past a flower bed. Several things look wrong or impossible: an animal reading a book, a goat loose in the park, and a ladder standing up by itself in the pond.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The ladder can be read two ways: standing upright by itself with nothing to support it (it has a shadow at its foot, which suggests it is upright), or lying flat on the ground with its far end reaching into the pond. Either way it is out of place.
- The brown reading animal could be a monkey (round ears, long tail) or a dog with floppy ears and a red bandana.
- The layered item on the bench looks like a giant sandwich, but could be a stack of books or folded cloth.
- The blue object at the end of the bench could be a cap lying on the bench, a cap in mid-air/falling, or part of the girl's other arm/hand; it overlaps her shoulder and ponytail, so it is hard to separate.
- The white thing in the child's hand could be bread for the duck, a cup, or a phone.
- The dog's tail looks very long and points straight up toward the child's feet, which looks a bit unnatural.
- The goat could be grazing or lowering its head to butt something.
- The walking woman in the background is drawn about as large as people in the front, so she looks a bit big for how far back she is.
