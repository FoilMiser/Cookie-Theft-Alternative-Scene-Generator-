# Picture 36

## Free description

This is a sunny day in a park, with a bright sun in the top left, two white clouds and green rolling hills and bushes in the background. A sandy path winds from the bottom left up past a big tree on the left. In the middle of the grass, near the path, a wooden ladder stands tilted at an angle but is not leaning against anything. To the right of a tall black lamp post, a girl in a pink dress is standing at the edge of a pond. She holds out something small and white, probably bread, towards the water. Small brown crumbs are scattered at the edge of the pond, and a white duck is swimming towards them, with speed lines behind it. In the front, a man in an orange cap and an orange high-visibility safety vest with reflective stripes is sitting on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket. He is leaning back on one arm with his eyes closed and a happy, relaxed face, and a wicker picnic basket sits in front of him. An empty wooden bench, a grey rock, a colourful flower bed and two green rubbish bins complete the scene. The odd things are the ladder standing up on its own in the open, and the man wearing a work safety vest while having a picnic.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The ladder is the strangest thing. It stands at a lean but its top does not touch the tree or anything else. It could be a deliberate "something is wrong" detail, or the ladder was meant to be leaning against the tree and was placed slightly too far away.
- The white object in the girl's hand is very small. It could be bread, a paper bag or a cup. The brown dots at the pond's edge could be breadcrumbs or small pebbles. Because the duck is heading towards them, I read them as bread.
- The man's closed eyes could mean he is dozing, sunbathing or laughing.
- A worker in a hi-vis vest picnicking is slightly out of place. He could be a park worker on his lunch break.
- The picnic basket is drawn over his lower legs, so it could be sitting on his shins or on the blanket just in front of them.
