# Picture 19

## Free description

This is a sunny town street with four colourful shops (cream, peach, green and pink) along the back, flower planters in front of them, and a paved plaza in the foreground. A red bird is flying over the roof of the peach-coloured shop. In the front centre, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice cream cone, and the pink scoop with sprinkles has fallen onto the pavement. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is eating the dropped ice cream. To the right, a red bicycle is parked in a grey bike rack. On the right, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is running or jogging toward the left, arms pumping. Behind her is a fruit stall with crates of produce and a cardboard box on the ground beside it, with nobody tending it. There's also a tree in a planter, a green trash can on the left, and potted plants in both bottom corners.
