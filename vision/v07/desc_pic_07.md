# Picture 07

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence with a gate on the left, and a stepping-stone path down the left side. A young woman or girl with a brown ponytail, wearing a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes, stands in the middle-right of the lawn beside the flowerbed. She is holding a dark grey frying pan out by its handle over the flowers, and a stream of water is pouring out of the pan onto them. So she is watering the flowers with a frying pan, which is an odd choice. A watering can and a hose reel are both right there in the garden and are not being used: the teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow sit in the front left, and the hose reel stands next to the flowerbed. A wooden bench stands on the grass in front of the house on the right. Behind the fence on the left, a small birdhouse with a red roof sits on top of a pole. Two potted plants stand near the gate.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The object in her hand reads most like a frying pan, but it could also be read as a large ladle, a saucepan or a scoop.
- The pan looks too shallow and small to hold the long, steady stream of water coming out of it, so the amount of water seems a bit unrealistic.
- The birdhouse post rises from behind the fence, so the birdhouse may be in the neighbour's garden rather than this one.
- Apart from watering with a pan, nothing is floating or physically impossible.
