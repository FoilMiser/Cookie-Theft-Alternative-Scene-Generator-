# Picture 03

## Free description
On a sunny day, a family is busy in a back garden with a cream house with a red roof on the left and a wooden fence and gate across the back. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts, with short dark curly hair, has just thrown a red ball. A dotted line shows its path rising from his hand toward the top of the fence. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands to the right with his hands held out together, ready to catch it, so the ball looks about to land in his hands. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping the stepping-stone path with a broom with a yellow head. In front of the blue door, a brown dog with a white chest sits holding a red leash in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. In the front middle, a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is in a play bow, with its front legs stretched down and its bottom up, looking to the right. A red bowl of dog food sits on the grass near the front. There is also a round flowerbed full of colourful flowers beside the house.

## Inventory
- Boy (centre-left, middle distance): dark curly hair, yellow shirt, orange shorts; arm stretched out, having just thrown the ball, stepping forward.
- Man (centre-right, middle distance): brown hair, red short-sleeved shirt, blue jeans, dark shoes; facing left with both hands together and out in front, waiting to catch.
- Woman (right, front): dark hair in a ponytail, purple top, teal trousers, dark shoes; holding a broom and sweeping the path, facing right.
- Brown dog (left, in front of the blue door): brown dog with white chest and paws, sitting, eyes closed or squinting happily, holding a red leash in its mouth; the leash trails onto the ground in a loop.
- White dog (centre, front): white dog with brown spots, brown ear, blue collar; front legs stretched down and rear up (play bow), looking right.
- Red ball (centre, near the top of the fence): red ball with a white stripe in mid-air, with a dotted line showing its path from the boy's hand.
- Broom (right): wooden handle with a yellow brush head on the path.
- Leash (left): red leash held in the brown dog's mouth.
- Dog bowl (front, centre-right): red bowl with brown dog food.
- Flowerbed (left, next to the house): round bed with red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers.
- Path, gate and fence (right and back): beige stepping-stone path leading to a brown wooden gate in the fence.
- House (left): cream walls, red roof, four-pane window, blue door with a step.
- Tree, hedges, sun, clouds: large tree behind the house, hedges behind the fence, a yellow sun top right, two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The yellow dot beside the brown dog's ear is the door handle behind it, but it could be mistaken for an ear tag or a tag on its collar.
- The white dog is looking right, toward the woman or the food bowl, rather than at the ball. Its play bow could mean it wants to join the ball game, or it could be stretching or eyeing the food.
- The brown dog's closed eyes could be read as happy and eager, or as sleepy.
- The ball's dotted path rises from the boy toward the fence top, so it is not entirely clear whether it will come down to the man or sail over him.
- Nothing looks impossible or floating apart from the ball, which is clearly meant to be in flight.
