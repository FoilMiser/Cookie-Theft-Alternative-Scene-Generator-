# Picture 33

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back with a gate on the right, a clothesline with blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel, and a flower bed. An orange kite with bow tails is flying high in the sky, and a little girl in a pink dress on the right is holding its string. Next to her, a woman in a blue jacket and black trousers with a brown ponytail has her arms out, and a red-and-white ball is flying through the air with a dotted line showing it has just been thrown from her direction towards the left. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts in the middle is holding his arms out as if ready to catch the ball. The ball is passing very close to the kite string and could hit it. In the centre, a man in a red shirt and jeans is in a sitting position drinking from a white mug, but there is no chair or anything under him: he is sitting on thin air, which is impossible. On the left, an older man in a blue flat cap and brown vest is sitting on a bench eating a pink ice cream cone, with a white dog with brown spots looking up at the ice cream. A black-and-white cat is walking along the top of the fence towards a birdhouse on a pole, and there is a teal hose reel beside the flower bed.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Man sitting on nothing: the man in red is clearly in a seated pose but there is no chair, stool or bench under him. This looks impossible, or like a chair has been left out of the drawing.
- Who threw the ball and where it is going: the dotted line starts near the woman's outstretched hands and the girl's head. Most likely the woman threw it to the boy, but it could also be read as the ball about to hit the kite string, or as having come off the girl's head.
- The cat walking along the fence towards the birdhouse could be read as going after birds, or just walking.
- The kite is drawn in front of the cloud, which is a normal cartoon way of showing it high up, but it does look as if it is right against the cloud.
- The girl's head is drawn mostly as a dark circle of hair, so it is hard to tell exactly which way she is facing; she seems to be looking up at the kite.
