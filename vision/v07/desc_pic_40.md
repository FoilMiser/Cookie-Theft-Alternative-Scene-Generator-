# Picture 40

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a sun in the top right, two clouds, and a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the left. A tall wooden fence runs across the back, with a closed wooden gate on the right and a birdhouse on a pole behind it. A stepping-stone path runs from the gate down to the front right. An older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses is standing at the fence, painting it blue with a long-handled brush or roller. She wears a teal top and a grey skirt. A section of boards in the middle of the fence is already bright blue. On the right, a man in an orange cap, an orange hi-vis vest and blue jeans is digging a hole with a spade. There is a mound of dirt beside the hole. In the middle front, a child with brown hair, a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes is drinking from a white cup. The child is in a sitting position with knees bent, but there is no chair, bench or anything underneath them. They seem to be sitting on thin air, which looks impossible. An empty wooden bench is in the bottom left, there is a colourful flower bed near the house, and a teal watering can stands in the front right.
