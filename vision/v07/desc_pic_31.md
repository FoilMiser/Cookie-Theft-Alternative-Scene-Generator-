# Picture 31

## Free description
This is a back garden on a gloomy day: the sky is grey and there are three dark storm clouds overhead, so it looks as if it is about to rain. On the left is a house with a red roof and a blue door, and a wooden fence runs along the back of the garden with a gate on the right. A young woman in a yellow dress is sitting on a wooden bench eating a pink ice cream cone, and a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is up on its hind legs beside her, looking at the ice cream as if begging for it. Next to them, a grey-haired older woman in glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt is watering a flower bed full of colourful flowers, with water dripping out. There is a clothesline in the middle with a pair of blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel hanging out to dry, even though rain is coming. A black-and-white cat is walking along the top of the fence, and on the right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is standing on the garden path, pointing up at the cat with a surprised look on her face. In the middle of the lawn a sprinkler is spraying arcs of water onto a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is getting wet. There is also a swing set with one red seat, nobody on it, and a teal watering can on the grass near the path. Several things don't make much sense together: watering the garden, running a sprinkler and leaving washing out when a storm is coming, and a cardboard box being soaked by the sprinkler.
