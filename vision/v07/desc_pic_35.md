# Picture 35

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back with a gate on the right, a clothesline with blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel, a birdhouse on a pole, and a flower bed. An orange kite with bow tails is flying high in front of a cloud, and its string runs down to a small girl in a pink dress standing by the corner of the house. In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress with short dark hair is holding the nozzle of a long green garden hose out in front of her and looking at it; only a single drop is coming out. The hose runs from a reel by the flower bed, loops along the bottom of the lawn, and back up to her hands. A white goat with small horns and a beard is standing right on top of the hose, which is probably why no water is coming out. If the goat steps off, the water could suddenly spray out, and the nozzle is pointing to the left. On the right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is happily sitting on a swing on a grey swing set. A teal watering can sits on the grass near the stepping-stone path. A goat in an ordinary back garden is unusual.

- Where the water would go: the nozzle points left, roughly towards the flower bed or the girl in the distance, so it could spray her if the water comes on suddenly.
- The girl is drawn very small and partly overlapping the edge of the house wall, while her feet are higher up the lawn than the house's base. She looks slightly as if she is in front of and behind the house at the same time, which is a small perspective oddity.
