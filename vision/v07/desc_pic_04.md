# Picture 04

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right, a wooden fence and gate on the left, and a stepping-stone path down the left side. In the middle of the garden there is a washing line strung between two wooden poles, with a yellow garment with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. On the right, a man wearing an orange cap, an orange high-visibility vest with reflective stripes, a grey shirt and blue jeans stands at the edge of the flowerbed. He holds a teal watering can down at his side, facing left toward the flowers. The water from the can looks wrong. Instead of pouring down onto the flowers, the stream leaves the spout low down and then curves upward into the air, ending in drops above the flowers, like a fountain going up. In the front left of the garden there is a second teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow with a wooden handle, sitting on the grass. Two potted plants stand near the gate.
