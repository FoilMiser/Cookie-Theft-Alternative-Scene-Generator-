# Picture 09

## Free description
This is a sunny, windy day in a park with a pond, a lamp post, a bench and a winding path. In the middle a bearded man in a green shirt throws both arms up as a gust of wind blows his orange cap off his head, along with a few leaves. On the path to the right a man in a red shirt walks a white spotted dog on a red leash towards a woman in a blue hoodie carrying a yellow shopping bag, and a black cat runs away under the dog. A red bird pecks at the ground on the path. In the front left an old woman with grey hair waters a colourful flower bed with a watering can, next to the pond. In the centre a sprinkler sprays water in arcs onto a cardboard box sitting on the grass, and water drops are landing on the box. A young person in an orange cap and orange safety vest walks along reading a red book without looking where he is going, with a red ball on the grass near his path, so he might trip over it or walk into the spray. The strangest thing is at the bottom right: a brown animal, which looks like a dog, sits up on the grass holding an open book or newspaper as if it is reading, which is impossible.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The brown animal could be a dog, a monkey, a meerkat or a kangaroo; it has a floppy ear and a long tail. Either way, an animal reading a book is impossible, and the drawing seems to show it on purpose.
- The young man is reading either a book or a phone in a red case; it looks most like a small red book.
- It is unclear whether the sprinkler is meant to be watering the box (odd) or the box just happens to be in the way.
- A cardboard box left on the grass in a park is out of place; it is not clear who it belongs to.
- The black cat could be running from the dog, or just running past.
- The woman in the hoodie and the man with the dog are heading towards each other; they may or may not know each other.
