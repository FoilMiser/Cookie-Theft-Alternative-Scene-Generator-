# Picture 39

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a sun in the top left, two clouds and a tall wooden fence. There is a closed wooden gate in the fence on the left, and a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the right. A sandy path with oval stepping stones runs from the gate down to the front left. An older man with grey hair and a grey beard is standing in the middle of the lawn watering a flower bed with a green garden hose. He wears a grey cap, a white shirt, a brown vest and dark trousers. Water arcs from the nozzle onto the colourful flowers. The hose runs from his hand across the grass to a hose reel next to the house. Behind him, a yellow towel with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans hang from a washing line strung between two wooden posts. On the left is a grey swing set with one red swing seat that nobody is using. In the front, a teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow sit on the grass. Nothing looks wrong or impossible. It is a peaceful gardening scene.
