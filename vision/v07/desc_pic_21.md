# Picture 21

## Free description

This is a sunny park with an oval pond on the left, a winding path on the right, green hills behind, and the sun in the top right. In the middle, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers stands by a tall black lamppost happily licking a pink ice cream cone, eyes closed and cheeks flushed. In the front centre, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty cone, because their pink ice cream scoop has fallen on the grass. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is eating the dropped ice cream right next to the child. On the path at the right, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is running toward the left, arms pumping, as if hurrying over or out jogging. An empty wooden bench stands near the lamppost. There's a flower bed and a green trash can at the bottom left, trees on both sides, and cattails at the pond's edges.
