# Picture 17

## Free description

This is a sunny park with an oval pond on the left, green hills and trees in the background, and a winding dirt path on the right. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands at the right edge of the pond fishing with a rod, and his line runs down to a red-and-white float bobbing in the water with a ripple around it. A white duck is swimming on the left side of the pond. Behind the man is a tall black lamppost with a blue bird perched on top. A yellow balloon with a dangling string, held by nobody, floats in the sky to the right of the lamppost. On the path at the right, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts has both arms up and mouth open, looking up as if they've just let go of the balloon and are trying to reach it. At the bottom there's a flower bed, a green trash can in the corner, and a grey wheelbarrow. The wheelbarrow looks wrong: instead of a round wheel it has a square, box-like wheel with an X across it, so it couldn't roll.
