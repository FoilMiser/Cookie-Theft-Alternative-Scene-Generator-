- The tilt is shallow, so it could be read as the seesaw in motion or simply resting off-level. The right child's legs are bent and reaching down to the ground, which might mean pushing off or just standing the seesaw down.
- The dark handles are hard to make out: they look like short black bars sticking out of or sitting behind the plank area near each child's hands, with no clear post connecting them to the plank. They seem slightly detached.
- Only the right child has a ground shadow; the left child and the stand have none. That may just be because the left child is off the ground, but the stand having no shadow looks inconsistent.
- The left child's legs look thin and short for the body, and the two shoes look stacked on top of each other rather than side by side. The right child's feet look similarly stacked.
- The right child's gender is not clear; could be a boy or a girl.
- The left child's hair is a round dark mass that wraps the face in a slightly helmet-like way, and the face is a narrow, squared-off strip in profile, which looks a little odd.
