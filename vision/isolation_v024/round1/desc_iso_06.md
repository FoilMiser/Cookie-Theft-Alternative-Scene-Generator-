He is kicking an orange ball with a white curved line on it: his front leg is extended forward and low to the left with the foot touching the ball, while he balances on his other leg. His arms are out to the sides for balance. Curved motion lines under the kicking leg show its swing. A row of dark fence posts runs along the top of the picture, and the bottom end of something yellow and orange with dark edges pokes in from the left side.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The yellow and orange shape at left is cut off by the picture edge. It looks like the lower end of a playground slide, but on its own it could also be a ramp or some kind of bent striped pole.
- The ball could be a football or soccer ball, or a small basketball; the single white curved band does not settle which. It seems small relative to the boy.
- The ball appears to be slightly in the air: its small shadow sits on the ground below and apart from it. This reads as the moment of the kick, though the ball could also look like it is floating.
- The shoe touches or slightly overlaps the ball, so it is not clear whether he has just struck it or is still pressing against it.
- The face is small and drawn in profile, with a dark mark at the front that is hard to read. It could be an eye, or the edge of the face or hair, and it looks a little odd next to the cap.
- The fence posts hang down from a bar at the very top of the picture and their lower ends stop in mid-grass with rounded tips, so they seem to float above the ground rather than being planted in it, though this may be the crop or the way the fence was drawn.
- The kicking leg looks long and quite straight compared with the body, and the waist where it joins the hips looks narrow, which makes the proportions slightly stretched.
