# pic_12

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, a gate and path on the right. In the middle a woman in a purple top is watering a flower bed, but she is pouring the water out of a frying pan instead of a watering can, which is odd. Behind her a woman in a yellow dress walks along while reading an open book, not looking where she is going, and there is a cardboard box right in front of her feet — she looks about to trip over it. On the left a small girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench drinking from a cup with a straw. An empty wheelbarrow stands on the lawn at the front. Nothing else is happening.
