## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, trees and a blue pond on the left. An old man in a flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands at the edge of the pond fishing with a long rod; his line drops into the water with a red-and-white float bobbing in it. A white duck swims on the pond not far from the float. On the right a woman in a yellow dress is walking along a winding sandy path, pushing a teal pram in which a baby sleeps under a pink blanket. There is an empty wooden bench in the middle, a tall black lamppost, a grey rock in front, a bed of colourful flowers at the front left and a green bin in the bottom left corner. Reeds grow at the pond's edges and bushes and trees stand around. Nothing looks dangerous; it is a calm scene.
