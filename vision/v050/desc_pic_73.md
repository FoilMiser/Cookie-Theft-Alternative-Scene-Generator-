A sunny playground surrounded by a dark metal railing fence, with a tree on the left and a red-and-yellow slide on the right. The seesaw is almost level, tipped very slightly down on the boy's side, and both children are holding on to the handles. In the centre front a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is walking towards the right while eating a pink ice-cream cone. The slide is empty. There is a green bin by the fence, a bush and a grey rock on the right.
