## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence and gate on the left, a paved path down the left side, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. Near the right end of the fence a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a short pole. Only the last strip of fence, right next to the house, has been painted blue so far. A small paint tin with blue paint stands on the grass behind him. The rest of the garden is quiet: two potted plants and a bush by the fence, a flower bed in front of the house, an empty wooden bench at the front right, and a teal watering can and an empty wheelbarrow at the front. Nothing looks wrong or impossible.
