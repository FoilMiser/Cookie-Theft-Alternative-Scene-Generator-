# pic_13

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a gate and stone path on the right. A single woman in a yellow dress stands near the fence in the middle with her hands on her hips, wearing an upside-down metal bucket on her head like a hat, which is odd. She seems to be looking around, perhaps searching for something. On the ground there is a hose reel with its hose and yellow nozzle lying on the grass, an empty wheelbarrow, and a teal watering can. There is also a flower bed, two potted plants and some bushes. No one else and no animals are in the scene.
