# Picture 01

## Free description
A sunny day in a fenced playground with a few white clouds and a yellow sun at top left. A man with a beard, green T-shirt and beige trousers is walking across the grass in the centre front, holding a pink ice-cream cone up to his mouth and eating it. On the right, two children are playing on a red seesaw: a girl in pink on the left end, which is up, and a child in a yellow top and orange shorts on the right end, which is down. On the left there is a red and yellow slide with nobody on it. A green litter bin stands against the fence, and a big tree is at the far right. Nothing looks dangerous, and nothing seems about to happen beyond the seesaw going up and down and the man finishing his ice cream.

- Man, centre front: bearded, dark skin, green T-shirt, beige trousers, brown shoes; walking to the left while eating a pink ice-cream cone.
- Girl, right middle: pink top, dark hair in a bun; sitting on the raised left end of the seesaw, holding the handle.
- Child, far right middle: yellow top, orange shorts, curly dark hair; sitting on the lowered right end of the seesaw, holding the handle.
- Seesaw (right): red plank on a grey triangular pivot, tilted down to the right; the two children are playing on it.
- Slide (left): red ladder frame with a yellow/orange chute; empty.
- Ice-cream cone: pink scoop in the man's hand.
- Green litter bin by the fence, centre-left.
- Grey rock, bottom left; green bush, left; large tree, far right; railing fence and hedges along the back.
