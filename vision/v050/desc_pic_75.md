## Free description
A sunny playground with a metal railing fence, a tree on the left and a red-and-yellow slide on the right. On the red seesaw at the left, a small girl in pink sits at the high end, stuck up in the air, while an older man with grey hair, a flat cap, a white shirt and a brown waistcoat sits on the low end reading an open book or newspaper. Because he is heavy and sitting still, the seesaw is fully tilted down on his side and the girl cannot come down. The girl looks surprised or upset and is holding the handle with one hand and pointing/gesturing with the other, as if calling to him. The old man seems not to notice; he is absorbed in his reading. On the right, a child in a yellow top is sliding down the slide with one arm raised. There is a green bin by the fence, a bush and a grey rock.
