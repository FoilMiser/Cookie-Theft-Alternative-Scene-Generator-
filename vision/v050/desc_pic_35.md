## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, trees, a tall black lamp post, a winding sandy path on the left and a blue pond with reeds on the right. A man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers sits on a wooden bench beside the path, absorbed in reading a red book. Next to him on the bench is a sandwich, and a blue bird has landed on the bench and is pecking at it; the man has not noticed, so the bird looks about to steal his lunch. In front of the pond, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands licking a pink ice-cream cone. A grey rock lies on the grass in the foreground and a green rubbish bin stands in the bottom right corner. Nothing looks impossible; the main story is the bird stealing the unattended sandwich.
