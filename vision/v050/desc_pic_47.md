# pic_47

## Free description
A back garden with a cream house on the left, a wooden fence and gate at the back, and a path on the right. A little girl in a pink dress stands in the middle holding the strings of a purple and a yellow balloon. A red balloon, with its string hanging loose, is floating up and away above her, and she is reaching up with one hand toward it, as if she has just let it go. On the right, an old man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat is sweeping a small pile of leaves with a yellow broom, not looking at her. There is a flower bed near the house, a birdhouse on a pole, two potted plants, an empty wheelbarrow and a teal watering can on the lawn.
