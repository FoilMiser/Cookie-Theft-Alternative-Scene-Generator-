## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a house on the left, a wooden fence across the back, a gate and a paved path on the right, and a metal swing set with one red swing in the middle. A young girl in a pink dress holds two balloons (red and blue) on strings in one hand, while reaching up with the other hand towards a purple balloon that has floated away above her, its string dangling out of reach. She looks up at it, seemingly upset or surprised. On the right, a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers is sweeping leaves on the path with a broom. A grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can are on the grass at the front. The swing is empty. The purple balloon seems about to float away for good.
