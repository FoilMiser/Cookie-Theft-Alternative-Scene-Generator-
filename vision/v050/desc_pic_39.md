# pic_39

## Free description
A sunny playground with a grassy area, a dark metal railing fence, a tree on the left, a bush and a green bin at the back. On a red seesaw, a little girl in a pink dress sits on the raised end, high up off the ground, holding the handle; she looks worried or upset, with her mouth open and one hand raised as if calling out. On the lowered end, a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers sits with his feet on the ground calmly reading an open book, keeping the seesaw pinned down so the girl is stuck in the air, and he seems not to notice her. On the right, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is sliding down a red-framed slide with a yellow/orange chute, one arm raised. A grey rock lies on the grass at the bottom right. The main oddity is the adult sitting on a seesaw reading while the child is stranded up high.
