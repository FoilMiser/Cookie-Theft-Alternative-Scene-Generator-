In the middle, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers kneels by the flowerbed digging a hole with a small trowel; a pot with a pink flower stands beside him, ready to be planted. Using a giant eating fork to sweep is clearly odd — it is far too big and the wrong tool. Around the garden there are a hose reel, a grey wheelbarrow, a teal watering can and two potted plants.
