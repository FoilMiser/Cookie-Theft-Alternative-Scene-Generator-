# pic_66

## Free description
A sunny town street with a row of colourful shops behind a tiled pavement. On the left a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is pointing up with a worried/frowning face at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of a tree. In the centre a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is sweeping a pile of dead leaves with a yellow broom. On the right a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts has just thrown a red ball: a dashed line shows it arcing over towards the left, and the ball is now near the bottom of the lamppost, heading toward a small girl in a pink dress who has her arms held out ready to catch. In the front centre is a teal bicycle whose wheels are square, which is impossible to ride. A red bird is perched on top of the lamppost and a blue bird is flying in the sky top left. There is also an unattended fruit stall on the right, a flower planter in the middle, a café table with two chairs, and potted plants in both front corners.
