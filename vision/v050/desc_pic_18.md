## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, a path down the right side, a clothesline in the middle and a metal swing set on the right. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a wooden bench in front of the house, reading a red book. A man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands in front of a flower bed, holding a green watering can and looking down at it. The water from the watering can does not pour down onto the flowers: it rises as a stream from the spout upwards and backwards over the flowers, with droplets at the top, which looks impossible. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue jacket stands on the path holding a kite handle, flying an orange diamond kite with a bow-tie tail high in the sky. A pair of blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel hang on the clothesline. The swing set has a single empty red swing, and an empty wheelbarrow is on the grass at the front.
