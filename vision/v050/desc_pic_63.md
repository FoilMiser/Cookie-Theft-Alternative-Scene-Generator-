# pic_63

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate at the far left, a paved path down the left side and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the front centre a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is flying a yellow diamond kite: he holds a red-and-white spool up in one hand and a long string runs up to the kite high in the sky, which has a tail with two small bows. To the right, behind him, a grey-haired older woman in a teal top and grey skirt is watering a round bed of colourful flowers with a grey watering can. The water from the can looks odd: it forms a long arc that rises up into the air above the flowers, with drops at the top, rather than pouring down onto them. A small red bird sits on top of the fence. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands on the lawn in front, and two potted plants and a bush stand near the fence. Nothing seems about to go wrong with the kite; it is flying well.
