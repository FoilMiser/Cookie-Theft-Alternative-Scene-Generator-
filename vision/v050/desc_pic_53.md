## Free description
A sunny park with a pond on the left, a tall black lamp post and green bin in the centre, a wooden bench and a winding path on the right, and trees and bushes. A girl in a pink dress with dark hair stands near the lamp post holding the string of a yellow balloon. At the front centre, a white goat with a red collar is eating a tuft of grass; it is tied by a rope to a short wooden stake in the ground. On the right, an orange fish sits on a branch of the big tree, which is strange. The scene is calm otherwise; no one is about to do anything dramatic.
