# pic_58

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a long wooden fence across the back and a gate on the right with a paved path leading to it. A man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is painting the fence blue with a long-handled paint roller; a section of the fence just to his right is already blue, and an open paint tin with blue paint stands on the grass behind him. In the front centre, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and grey skirt is raking up a small pile of brown leaves. A blue bird flies across the sky. A teal watering can sits on the grass at the front right, and there is a flower bed by the house. Nothing seems about to go wrong; it looks like ordinary garden chores.
