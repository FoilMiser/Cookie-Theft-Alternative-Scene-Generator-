## Free description
A sunny park with a pond, hills in the background, a street lamp, trees and a winding path. On the left a woman in a yellow dress kneels by a flower bed, using a trowel to plant a pink flower from a pot. In front of the pond a man in an orange cap and hi-vis orange vest walks along carrying a cardboard parcel, like a delivery worker. On the right a boy in a red cap holds up a kite spool, but his purple kite is caught in the branches of a tree. On the path at the right an older man in a cap and brown vest pushes a pram with a sleeping baby in it. Nothing looks impossible.
