## Free description
It is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a gate on the left and a house with a blue door on the right. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is painting the fence blue with a paint roller; a few fence boards near the tree are already blue. On the grass near him are a blue paint tray and a paint bucket. A small white dog with brown spots has clearly stepped in the paint tray: its paws are blue, and a trail of blue paw prints runs from the tray across the grass to where the dog now stands. The dog is walking left, toward the flower pots and the man, and will probably keep leaving blue prints. The boy is facing the fence and doesn't seem to have noticed. On the left, a man in a green shirt is sweeping a pile of dead leaves with a yellow broom beside the paved path. There is also a flower bed, a bench, a wheelbarrow, a watering can and two potted plants.
