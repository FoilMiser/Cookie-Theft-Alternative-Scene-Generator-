# Picture 06

## Free description
A town street of colourful shops under a grey sky with dark storm clouds, suggesting it is about to rain. A street worker in an orange cap and orange hi-vis vest is sweeping a pile of dust towards a red dustpan, but he is using a giant fork instead of a broom, which is absurd. Beside him a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is pointing up at a red bird perched on top of a street lamp. An old man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat is scattering seeds from a paper bag for a blue bird pecking on the pavement. At the front left a bearded man in a green T-shirt is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. In the centre front a child in a yellow top and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and seems to be crying, while a brown dog licks the dropped pink ice cream on the ground. There is a fruit stall on the left with no vendor, a teal bicycle locked to a rack, a tree, a green bin and a potted plant.
