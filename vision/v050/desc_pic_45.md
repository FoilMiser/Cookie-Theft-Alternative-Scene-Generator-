## Free description
A back garden on a sunny day with a cream house on the right and a wooden fence across the back. A man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller; a section of boards to his left is already blue. Behind him on the grass are a paint pot and a paint tray full of blue paint. A white dog with brown spots is walking away from the tray toward the flower bed, leaving a trail of blue paw prints, so it has stepped in the paint and has blue feet. The man has his back to the dog and doesn't seem to notice. The dog is heading straight for a colourful flower bed near the house. A hose reel with its hose lying on the ground, a watering can and an empty wheelbarrow are scattered on the lawn, and two potted plants stand near the fence.
