# Picture 04

## Free description
A busy, sunny playground with a railing fence, a slide on the left and a big tree on the right. A child in a yellow top and orange shorts stands in the centre holding an empty ice-cream cone and rubbing their eye as if crying; their pink ice cream has fallen on the grass and a brown dog is licking it up. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is holding a kite reel with the string going up to a purple kite caught in the tree. An old man sits on a bench reading a red book, and a blue bird is pecking at (or stealing) a sandwich lying on the bench next to him. A grey cat is creeping low towards a red bird that is pecking seeds on the grass. A girl in a pink dress holds a yellow balloon on the far left, and an orange cat sleeps on the grass. A white dog with brown spots is bowing playfully next to a tennis ball, wagging its tail. The red bicycle on the right has square wheels, which is impossible.

## Inventory
- Child, centre front: yellow top, orange shorts, curly dark hair; holding an empty cone and covering an eye, looking upset/crying.
- Brown dog, centre-right front: red collar; licking the fallen pink ice cream on the ground.
- Boy, right: red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers; holding a kite reel up, kite string running to the tree.
- Old man, right back: grey hair, flat cap, brown waistcoat; sitting on a bench reading a red book.
- Blue bird, on the bench beside the old man: pecking at the sandwich.
- Grey cat, centre back: crouched and stalking towards the red bird.
- Red bird, centre back: pecking at seeds on the grass.
- Girl, far left: pink dress; standing holding a yellow balloon on a string.
- Orange cat, left of centre: lying curled up asleep.
- White dog with brown spots, bottom right: front end down in a play bow next to a yellow tennis ball, tail wagging.
- Purple kite: stuck in the branches of the tree on the right.
- Sandwich on the bench, being eaten by the bird.
- Fallen pink ice cream with sprinkles on the grass.
- Red bicycle, right: standing on its own; its wheels are square.
- Sandbox with a red bucket and spade, bottom left; slide, left; grey rock, bottom left.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The bicycle has square wheels, which is impossible and looks deliberately wrong.
- The red bird may be eating scattered seed; the cat may be about to pounce on it.
- The bird on the bench: could be stealing the old man's sandwich while he is distracted by his book, or he may be sharing it.
- The crying child's gesture could also be wiping their eyes or face.
