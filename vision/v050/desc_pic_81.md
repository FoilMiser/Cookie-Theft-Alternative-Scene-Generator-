## Free description
A sunny back garden seen the other way round: the cream house with a blue door is on the right, the gate and paved path are on the left. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is watering a flower bed with a hose; the hose runs from a reel by the house. An older man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat sits on a bench by the house reading a red book. In the front centre, a brown dog wearing glasses sits up and holds an open newspaper as if reading it, which is impossible and funny. A swing set with one empty red swing stands on the left, and a wheelbarrow is on the grass in front.
