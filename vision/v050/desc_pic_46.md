# pic_46

## Free description
A back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left and a wooden fence and gate at the back. An old man in a grey cap, brown waistcoat and white shirt is watering a flower bed with a teal watering can; water sprinkles onto the flowers. On the right, near the path and gate, a woman in a yellow dress is digging with a shovel; there is a small heap of soil next to her and a scoop of soil on the shovel. Strangely, a cardboard box is floating in mid-air in the sky above the fence, with nothing holding it up and no one throwing it. Apart from that the scene is calm and sunny.
