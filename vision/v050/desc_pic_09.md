## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a stone path, and a cream house with a blue door on the right. On the left a man in a red shirt and blue trousers is sweeping a small pile of brown leaves with a yellow broom. In the middle an elderly grey-haired woman with glasses is holding a garden hose and peering closely at the nozzle, as if wondering why no water is coming out. The hose runs back across the lawn to a hose reel by the house, and a white dog with brown spots is standing right on the hose, where it has a swollen bulge just by the dog's feet. So the dog seems to be blocking the water, and it looks as if the water could burst out into the woman's face when the dog steps off. Behind them a clothesline holds a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans. There is also a flower bed, two potted plants, a bush and a wooden bench by the house.
