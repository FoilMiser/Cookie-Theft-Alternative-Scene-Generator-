# Picture 08

## Free description
A sunny park with rolling hills, a pond on the left, a tall lamp post in the middle and a winding sandy path on the right. A woman with dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers stands near the pond scattering seeds from a paper bag onto the grass. Two small birds, one red and one blue, are pecking at the seeds near her feet. On the right, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is walking towards the right while eating a pink ice-cream cone. There is a green litter bin in the bottom left corner, a grey rock at the front, and trees and bushes around. It is a calm scene and nothing looks wrong or about to go wrong.
