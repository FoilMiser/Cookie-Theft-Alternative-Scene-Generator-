# pic_43

## Free description
A busy back garden on a sunny day, with a cream house at the right and a wooden fence along the back. A woman in a yellow dress is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; a few boards are already blue. Beside her on the grass are a paint pot and a paint tray with blue paint. A small white dog with brown spots, on the path at the left, has blue paint on its paws and has left a trail of blue paw prints leading from the paint tray to where it stands, so it has clearly stepped in the paint. In the middle, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is flying an orange kite high in the sky, holding the string reel above his head. On the right, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is watering a flower bed with a hose that comes from a hose reel. An empty wheelbarrow sits in the front, and a birdhouse on a pole stands near the woman.
