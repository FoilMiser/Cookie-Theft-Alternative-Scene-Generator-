# pic_38

## Free description
A busy back garden under a grey, stormy sky with three dark clouds, so it looks like rain is coming. At the left by the gate, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sweeps leaves with a yellow broom, and next to her an older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, rakes leaves. An older man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat is digging with a spade, with a mound of soil beside him. A child in a yellow top and orange shorts stands near the fence pointing up at the dark sky. In the centre front, a man in a green T-shirt holds the nozzle of a long blue garden hose that runs back to a hose reel by the house, but no water is coming out; a white dog with brown spots is standing right on the hose, which may be what is blocking the water. Behind the flower bed a grey cat creeps towards a red bird pecking seeds. At the bottom left, an orange cat sleeps curled on the path while a brown dog bows playfully in front of a tennis ball. On a bench at the bottom right, a boy in a red cap reads a red book while a blue bird pecks at the sandwich beside him. Watering with a hose just before a storm is also a little ironic.
