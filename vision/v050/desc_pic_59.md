Behind him, a girl in a pink dress stands by the pond holding something out, possibly having thrown food to the duck. Further back, an older woman with grey hair sweeps leaves with a broom, and a white goat on a rope eats grass near her. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue top holds a kite reel, but his kite is caught in the tree.
