## Free description
A sunny playground with a fence and trees behind it, a few small clouds and a bright sun at top right. On the left, a small girl in a pink dress sits on the high end of a red seesaw, holding its handle, up in the air. At the low end of the seesaw an old man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat sits reading an open book, his feet on the ground, apparently paying no attention to the girl. Because he is sitting on the low end, the girl seems stuck up high; she looks towards him with her hand raised near her mouth, as if calling to him or complaining. In the centre is a swing frame with a single empty red swing seat, and on the right a red-and-yellow slide with nobody on it. At front right, a boy in a yellow shirt kneels in a sandpit, building a sandcastle with a little flag on top, using a small red spade. A green bin stands by the fence, and there is a grey rock near the sandpit.
