# Picture 05

## Free description
A sunny park with rolling hills, a pond on the left, a tall lamp post in the middle and a winding sandy path on the right. An old man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands at the edge of the pond fishing with a rod; his line ends in a red-and-white float bobbing in the water. A white goat with a red collar is grazing on a tuft of grass, tied by a rope to a small wooden stake in the ground. In the background, near the path, a wooden ladder stands upright on its own without leaning on anything, which looks odd or impossible. There is a flower bed at the bottom left, a green litter bin in the corner, a grey rock in the front, and several trees and bushes. Otherwise it is a calm scene.
