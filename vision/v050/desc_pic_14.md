# pic_14

## Free description
A sunny town square with coloured shops, striped awnings, a street lamp, a fruit stall, a bench and a tree. At the front centre is a teal bicycle with square wheels, which is impossible to ride. On the far right a man in a green shirt is sitting reading a red book, but there is no chair or bench under him — he is sitting on thin air. On the bench in the middle a woman in a purple top reads a red book while a blue bird pecks at the sandwich lying next to her on the bench, without her noticing. Behind her an old man in a cap and brown vest is dropping seeds from a bag for a red bird. A small child in a yellow top is crying while holding an empty ice-cream cone, and a brown dog is licking up the pink ice cream spilled on the ground. A blue-and-white beach ball is stuck in the tree, and a little girl in pink points at it. A boy in a red cap sweeps leaves, and on the left an elderly woman walks along carrying a yellow shopping bag.
