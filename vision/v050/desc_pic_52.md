# pic_52

## Free description
A windy, sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate at the left, a birdhouse on a pole, a washing line between two posts, a cream house with a blue door on the right, and a flower bed. A man with brown hair and a moustache, in a red T-shirt and blue jeans, stands on the paved path at the front left, looking startled and reaching up toward a blue cap that the wind is blowing away from him, along with some leaves. A grey cat walks along the top of the fence. On the washing line hang a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue trousers, blowing in the wind. Oddly, an orange fish sits in the tree on the right, on the end of a branch. More wind lines appear in the sky.
