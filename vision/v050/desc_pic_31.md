# pic_31

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house, red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence and gate at the back. At the front left, an old man in a grey cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. Next to him on the bench is a sandwich, and a blue bird is perched on the bench pecking at it, with crumbs falling — the man doesn't seem to notice the bird stealing his food. On the right, beside the paved path, a younger man in a red shirt and blue trousers is sweeping a pile of brown leaves with a yellow broom. A grey wheelbarrow sits empty in the centre, and there is a flowerbed and a hose reel near the house.
