# pic_50

## Free description
A windy, sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a birdhouse on a pole, a cream house with a blue door and red roof on the right, and a flower bed. White wind lines blow across the middle of the picture, and an orange cap is being blown away through the air, along with a few small leaves. An older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, stands in the centre with her mouth open in surprise, reaching up toward the flying cap - it seems the wind has just taken her hat. To her left, a young woman in a yellow dress sits (apparently on nothing) drinking from a red cup with a straw. At the back left, a girl in a purple top and green trousers walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead along the paved path near the gate. The wind is the main event; the cap is about to be lost or chased.
