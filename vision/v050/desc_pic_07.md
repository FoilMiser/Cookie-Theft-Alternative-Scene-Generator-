# Picture 07

## Free description
A sunny town street with colourful shops. An old man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat is walking quickly (motion lines behind him) while reading an open book, and a cardboard box lies on the pavement right in front of him, so he looks about to trip over it. On the left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping dust towards a red dustpan using a giant fork instead of a broom, which is absurd. On the right, a blue-and-white ball is stuck in the branches of a tree, and a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is pointing up at it. A girl in a pink dress holding a yellow balloon stands at the front. A red bird is flying in the sky at the top right. There is also a fruit stall, a lamp post, a flower box, a teal bicycle in a rack, a green bin and two potted plants.
