# Picture 02

## Free description
A sunny back garden enclosed by a tall wooden fence, with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is kneeling on the grass beside a flower bed, digging with a small trowel. Next to him is a terracotta pot with a pink tulip-like flower, as if he is about to plant it. A grey cat is walking along the top of the fence. A cardboard box is hanging in the sky above the fence, tilted, with nothing holding it up. That box is the odd thing: it looks as if it is floating or has been thrown and is falling. A wooden gate is in the fence on the left, with a paved path leading from it, and a wooden bench stands in front of the house.

## Inventory
- Boy, centre-right front: red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers, white shoes; kneeling and digging in the soil with a trowel by the flower bed.
- Cat, centre back: grey with white chest; walking along the top edge of the fence.
- Cardboard box, upper centre-left: brown box tilted in mid-air above the fence; floating or falling.
- Flower pot: small terracotta pot with a pink flower beside the boy, ready to be planted.
- Flower bed: red, yellow, orange, purple and pink flowers in brown soil, right of centre.
- Wooden gate in the fence at left; paved path running from the gate to the front left.
- House on the right with a blue door and window; bush beside it; wooden bench at bottom right; tree behind the fence at right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The box in the sky: it could be falling (dropped or thrown from somewhere out of view) or simply floating, which would be impossible. No one is near it to explain it.
- The boy could be digging a hole for the potted flower, or just weeding.
