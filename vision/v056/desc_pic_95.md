## Free description
A sunny, windy back garden, drawn mirror-image to the others: the house (red roof, blue door) is on the right, the gate and paved path on the left. White wind lines streak across the sky. A woman in a yellow dress stands on the lawn on the left, reaching up with one arm toward a red cap that is being blown away above her head, with a few green leaves flying with it; she looks surprised. On the washing line in the middle, a yellow striped towel and blue jeans are blown sideways, flapping out nearly horizontally in the wind. In the foreground are a teal watering can and a red wheelbarrow; a wooden bench stands at the front right next to the house and a flower bed. The hat is about to fly further away unless she catches it.
