# pic_50

## Free description
A windy, sunny day in a back garden with a cream house (red roof, blue door) on the left, a wooden fence, a swing set, a paved path and a gate on the right. White wind lines blow across the sky and leaves are being blown off the tree. A man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands in the middle of the lawn, reaching up with one arm, with a surprised face, as his blue cap is blown off his head into the air above him. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and grey skirt is kneeling by the flower bed, planting a small flower into a hole in the soil; a tray of seedlings sits in front of the bed. On the bench at the front left, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts sits reading a red book. The swing's red seat is tilted and pushed sideways, as if moved by the wind. A red wheelbarrow and a teal watering can stand on the grass at the front.
