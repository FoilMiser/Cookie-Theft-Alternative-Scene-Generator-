## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate at the left, a paved path down the left side and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. In the middle, a woman with dark hair, in a yellow dress and red shoes, rakes a small pile of dry leaves on the lawn with a long-handled rake while looking down at them. On the left stands a grey metal swing frame with one empty red swing seat, hanging still. At the front there is a red wheelbarrow and a teal watering can on the grass. At the front right, a hose reel sits on the lawn with a short hose ending in a yellow nozzle lying on the grass. There is also a flower bed with colourful flowers and a bush by the house. The scene is quiet and nothing seems about to happen.
