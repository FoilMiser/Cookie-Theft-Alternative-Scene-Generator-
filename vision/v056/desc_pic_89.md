## Free description
A busy, sunny town square in front of a row of colourful shops. On the left, a man in a red shirt walks away from a fruit stall carrying a yellow shopping bag, while a man in a green shirt by the shop scatters crumbs or seeds from a white paper bag and a red bird pecks at them on the ground. In the centre, an older woman with grey hair sits on a bench reading a red book, not noticing that a blue bird beside her on the bench is grabbing her sandwich. In front, a little girl in a pink dress holds an empty ice-cream cone with her hand to her mouth, looking upset, because her ice cream has fallen on the ground and a white-and-brown spotted dog is licking it up. Nearby, a brown dog wearing glasses sits up and reads a newspaper, which is impossible. A woman in a purple top sweeps leaves with a broom, and a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a beach ball stuck in a tree. On the far right, an old man in a cap and brown waistcoat pushes a red bicycle that has square wheels, which is also impossible.
