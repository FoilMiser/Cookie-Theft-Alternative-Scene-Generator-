## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate at the left, a paved path down the left side and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. On the left, a small girl with dark hair in a pink dress and yellow shoes sits on a red swing on a grey metal swing frame. The swing is pushed out to one side and motion lines behind it show that she is swinging back and forth. She is smiling. In the middle, an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, paints the fence pink with a paint roller on a pole. Three or four fence boards near her are already pink, and an open pot of pink paint stands on the grass behind her. At the front there is a red wheelbarrow, a teal watering can, and a hose reel with a short hose and yellow nozzle lying on the grass. There is also a flower bed and an empty wooden bench at the front right. Nothing looks dangerous.
