A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands at the right on a paved path, sweeping a small pile of brown leaves with a broom. The box is getting wet and looks about to be soaked, though the boy hasn't noticed. There is also a flower bed in front of the house, two potted plants, a birdhouse on a tall pole, bushes, a tree, a sun and two clouds.
