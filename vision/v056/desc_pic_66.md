# pic_66

## Free description
A sunny but windy park: white wind lines blow across the sky and over the pond, and a few leaves are blowing off the tree on the left. In front of the pond an old woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, reaches up with one arm towards her orange hat, which the wind has blown off and is flying away over the pond. She looks surprised. In the middle background, an old man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat stands by a lamp post scattering seeds from a paper bag, and a blue bird and a red bird are pecking up the seeds at his feet. On the left a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers is walking a white spotted dog on a red lead along the path. There is an empty wooden bench in the centre, a grey rock at the front, and a flower bed on the right. The hat looks like it may land in the pond.
