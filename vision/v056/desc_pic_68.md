## Free description
A sunny back garden with a fence, gate on the left, cream house with blue door on the right, two potted plants and a flower bed. On the path at the left, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans sweeps a small pile of leaves with a yellow broom. In the centre a girl in a purple top and teal trousers holds the end of a garden hose and is looking into the nozzle, which is pointed at her face. The hose runs from a hose reel by the house, and a brown dog is standing on the hose, squashing it, so no water comes out of the nozzle. There is a small leak/puddle by the reel. It looks like as soon as the dog steps off, the water will spray straight into the girl's face. The dog seems to be sniffing the flowers.
