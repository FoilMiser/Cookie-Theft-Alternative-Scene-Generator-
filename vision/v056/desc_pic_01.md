# pic_01

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate at the left, a paved path down the left side and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door at the right. In the middle of the lawn a child with curly dark hair, in a yellow top and orange shorts, holds a reel up and is flying a yellow diamond-shaped kite high in the sky. A long string runs from the reel to the kite, which has a short tail with two bows. At the front left, a lawn sprinkler connected to a long hose sprays arcs of water to the left, and the water lands on a cardboard box sitting on the path. The box is getting wet and there is a small puddle under it. The odd thing is that an orange goldfish is in the tree behind the fence, sitting among the branches as if it were a bird. There is a flower bed with colourful flowers, a wooden bench at the front right and two small bushes.
