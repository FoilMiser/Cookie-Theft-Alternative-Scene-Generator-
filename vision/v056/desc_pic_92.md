# pic_92

## Free description
A sunny playground with a metal railing fence at the back, a slide on the left, a swing frame in the middle, a sandpit in the front left and a tree on the right. On a red seesaw on the right, an old man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat sits on the low end reading a newspaper, while a little girl in a pink outfit is stuck up in the air on the high end, holding the handle with her legs dangling and looking down at him. Because the man is heavy and not paying attention, the girl cannot come down. The slide and swing are empty; the swing frame has only one seat. A red bucket and blue spade lie in the sandpit, there is a grey rock nearby, a green bin by the fence, and a blue bird perched on the fence top at the far left.
