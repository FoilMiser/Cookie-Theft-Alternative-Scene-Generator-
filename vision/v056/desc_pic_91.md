## Free description
A sunny, quiet town square in front of a row of colourful shops with striped awnings. On the far left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks leftward carrying a cardboard box in her arms, passing a fruit stall with nobody at it. In the front centre, an orange cat is crouched low and creeping forward, stalking a blue bird that is pecking at some seeds on the ground just in front of it; the cat looks about to pounce. A red bird sits on a branch in a tree on the right. The rest of the square is empty: an unused bench, a bicycle in a rack, a box of flowers, a street lamp, a green bin and a potted plant.
