# pic_65

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left, a fence across the back with a gate on the right and a stone path. A woman in a yellow dress stands at the fence painting it pink with a long-handled paint roller; a section of the fence is already bright pink. A pink paint tray and a paint tin sit on the grass behind her. A white dog with brown spots is walking away from the paint tray towards the flower bed, leaving a trail of pink paw prints, so it has clearly stepped in the paint. On the left an old woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a bench reading a red book. Beside her on the bench is a sandwich, and a blue bird is perched at the end of the bench pecking at / taking the sandwich without her noticing. On the right, a brown dog sits up on its haunches wearing glasses and holding a newspaper, as if reading it — which is impossible. A red wheelbarrow stands in the front and two potted plants are by the fence.
