## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence with a gate on the right, a paved path running to the gate, and a lawn. A man with a beard, green shirt and beige trousers stands by a round flower bed full of colourful flowers and is watering them, but instead of a watering can he is pouring water out of a frying pan. Water streams from the pan onto the flowers. A red wheelbarrow sits empty on the lawn in front, and two potted plants and a bush stand near the fence. No one else is there. The obvious oddity is that he is watering with a frying pan.
