# pic_21

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and path on the left, a swing set, and a house with a blue door on the right. A girl in a pink dress stands on the lawn holding the strings of a yellow and a blue balloon in one hand. A red balloon has got away and is floating up into the sky above her, its string dangling loose; she is reaching up toward it with her other hand and looks upset. It seems the red balloon is about to be lost for good. The swing set is empty, and a red wheelbarrow and a teal watering can sit on the grass. There is a flower bed in front of the house.
