## Free description
A sunny park with a pond on the left, a lamp post in the middle and a winding path on the right. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie walks across the front-left carrying a yellow shopping bag. Behind him, near the lamp post, a woman in a yellow dress stands with her hands on her hips wearing a metal bucket upside down on her head as a hat, which is strange. To the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers bends over scattering seed from a paper bag; a red bird and a blue bird are pecking at the seed on the grass in front of her. A wooden bench stands on the grass by the path. In the front centre a garden hose snakes across the bottom of the picture to a sprinkler, which is spraying arcs of water to the right, landing on a cardboard box sitting by the path; the box is getting wet and there is a small puddle under it. On the far right an older man with grey hair, a flat cap and a brown waistcoat walks along eating a pink ice-cream cone. A green bin stands by the lamp post.
