A sunny back garden with the cream house (red roof, blue door) on the right, a wooden gate and paved path on the left and a wooden fence along the back. On the left, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts is kicking an orange ball near the edge of the path. In the middle, a girl in a pink dress holds two balloons (yellow and purple) on strings, while a third, blue balloon has come loose and is floating up into the sky, trailing its string; she reaches up after it with an upset face. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is watering the flower bed with a dark grey watering can, water pouring out onto the flowers. An empty wooden bench stands in front of the house on the right and a red wheelbarrow stands on the grass at the front.
