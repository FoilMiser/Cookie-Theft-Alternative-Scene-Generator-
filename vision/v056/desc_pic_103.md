## Free description
This is a sunny park with rolling hills, a winding sandy path on the left, a wooden bench, a tall black lamp post and a pond with reeds on the right. In the front centre a white goat with small dark horns and a red collar is bending down eating a tuft of grass. The goat is tied with a rope from its collar to a short wooden stake in the ground. There are no people in the scene. A green rubbish bin stands in the bottom right corner. Nothing looks wrong apart from a goat being in a park, and it is tethered so it cannot wander off.
