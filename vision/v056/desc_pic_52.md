# pic_52

## Free description
A sunny back garden seen the other way round: the cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the right, a paved path and gate on the left, and a wooden fence along the back. An elderly man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is painting the fence pink with a paint roller on a long handle; a section of the fence is already bright pink. On the ground near him is a pink paint tray and an open pot of pink paint. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar walks away from the paint tray towards the left, leaving a trail of pink paw prints on the grass, and its paws are pink — it has clearly stepped in the paint. At the far left a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps a pile of leaves on the path with a yellow broom. A teal watering can and a red wheelbarrow stand on the grass at the front, and a flower bed sits in front of the house on the right.
