# pic_48

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back and a gate at the right end of a paved path. A woman with dark hair in a ponytail, wearing a yellow dress, stands by the flower bed on the left watering it. She holds a teal watering can, but a curved stream of water rises upward from the can's spout in a long arc, with droplets above the flowers — the water seems to come out of the can, go up high and fall back on the flowers. Behind her a washing line strung between two wooden posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes, pegged up. Two potted plants in terracotta pots stand on the grass near the fence on the right. An empty red wheelbarrow stands on the grass in the front centre-right.
