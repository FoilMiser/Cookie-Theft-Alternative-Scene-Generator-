# pic_114

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a wooden gate at the left, a paved path running down the left side, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. An older man with grey hair and a flat cap, in a brown waistcoat, is digging with a spade in the left-centre, next to a pile of dug-up earth. In the centre front a small child in a yellow top and orange shorts stands looking up, holding a string reel and flying an orange kite high in the sky. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is watering a round flower bed of colourful flowers. Water is pouring from the spout of what he holds. A red wheelbarrow sits empty in the front, and a wooden bench stands at the bottom right by the house. A tree shows above the fence near the house.

- The kite string runs from the reel to the kite in a long straight line passing in front of the fence and close past the man. It does not touch him, but it nearly does.
- The man's spade blade is in a dark hole beside the soil pile. This is fine, but the blade and the soil it holds are hard to separate.
