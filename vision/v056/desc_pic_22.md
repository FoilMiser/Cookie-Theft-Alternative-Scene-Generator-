A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a wooden gate at the far right. A man in a red shirt and blue trousers stands near the house, bending slightly over a round flower bed. He is watering the flowers, but instead of a watering can he is holding a dark frying pan by its handle, and water is pouring out of the pan onto the flowers. This looks wrong or silly: a frying pan is not a watering tool and could not hold a stream of water like that. On the right, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is on the paved path sweeping a small pile of dry leaves with a broom. In the middle is an empty swing set, and at the front an empty red wheelbarrow. A birdhouse on a tall pole stands near the gate.
