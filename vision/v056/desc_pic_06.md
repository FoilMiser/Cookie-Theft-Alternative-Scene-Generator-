## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills, trees, a round blue pond with bulrushes, a winding sand-coloured path on the right and a tall black street lamp in the middle. A white duck swims on the pond. In the front centre, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers sits leaning back, smiling with his eyes closed, on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket. Next to him is a wicker picnic basket with a baguette and a green bottle sticking out of it. Behind, to the right of the lamp, a woman with dark hair, in a purple top and teal trousers, holds a paper bag and scatters seeds or crumbs onto the grass. A red bird pecks at the seeds at her feet, so she is feeding the bird. There is an empty wooden bench by the path, a flower bed with colourful flowers at the front left, a grey rock at the front and a green litter bin in the bottom left corner. Nothing looks dangerous or impossible.
