On the left is a cream house with a red roof and blue door, with a flower bed in front of it; a wooden fence with a gate runs along the back and a paved path is on the right. A teal watering can stands on the grass at the front right. Something clearly impossible is in the tree behind the house: an orange fish is sitting on a branch of the tree, which looks like a deliberate "what's wrong with this picture" oddity.
