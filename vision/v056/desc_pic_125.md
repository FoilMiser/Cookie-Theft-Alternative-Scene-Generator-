## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence with a gate on the right, and a pale paved path. In the centre a woman with a dark ponytail in a yellow dress holds the nozzle of a long teal garden hose up near her face and peers into it, as if puzzled that no water is coming out. The hose runs back across the lawn to a hose reel by the house, and a small white dog with brown spots is standing on the hose with a paw on it, which would block the water. A little puddle/drip of water is leaking at the hose reel. This sets up the classic joke: when the dog steps off, the water will spray the woman in the face. Behind her a washing line between two posts holds blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel. There are also a flower bed, two potted plants, and a red wheelbarrow on the lawn in front.
