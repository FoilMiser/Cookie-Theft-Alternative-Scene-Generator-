## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate at far left, a paved path on the left, and a cream house with red roof and blue door on the right. At the front left, a woman in a yellow dress is sweeping a small pile of brown leaves on the path with a broom, looking down. On the right-centre, a small girl in a pink dress is holding a purple and a blue balloon on strings in one hand and reaching up with her other hand, looking worried or upset, toward a red balloon that has got away and is floating up above the fence; its string dangles free. So the girl has apparently just let go of the red balloon and it is drifting off. There are two potted plants and a bush in the middle, a flower bed by the house, and a teal watering can and red wheelbarrow in the foreground.
