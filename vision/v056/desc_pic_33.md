## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence and gate, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and green lawn. On the left an older man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat sweeps dirt on the paved path into a red dustpan, but he is using a large garden fork (like a giant dinner-fork) instead of a broom. In the centre-right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie kneels by a flower bed full of colourful flowers, planting a small seedling; a tray of seedlings sits at the edge of the bed. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress sits on a wooden bench reading a red book, and next to her on the bench a blue bird is pecking at/stealing her sandwich, which she has not noticed. A teal watering can, a red wheelbarrow and two potted plants sit on the lawn. The odd things are the man sweeping with a fork, and the bird about to make off with the sandwich.
