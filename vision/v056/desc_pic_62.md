# pic_62

## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, trees, a winding sandy path on the left and a round blue pond on the right. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands on the grass in front of the pond holding a fishing rod; his line runs down to a red-and-white float bobbing in the water. A white duck swims on the right side of the pond, not far from the float. In the middle of the grass a tall wooden ladder stands upright on its own, leaning against nothing — it just rises into the empty sky. There is a grey rock at the front, a green rubbish bin in the bottom-right corner, and bushes and cattails around the pond. Nobody is near the ladder and nothing is happening with it. The free-standing ladder is the odd thing in the picture: a ladder could not stand up like that without support.
