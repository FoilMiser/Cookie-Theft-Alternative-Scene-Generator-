# pic_82

## Free description
A sunny back garden with the house and blue door on the left, a wooden fence and gate behind, and a paved path on the right. A child in a yellow top and orange shorts stands in the middle of the lawn, holding a kite reel up with both hands. The string runs up to a yellow kite that is caught in the branches of the tree behind the house, so the kite seems stuck. To the right, an older man with grey hair, a grey cap, a white shirt and a brown waistcoat is painting the fence pink with a long-handled roller. A few planks are already pink, and an open tin of pink paint sits on the grass by his feet. A brown puppy lies asleep on the grass at the front. A wooden bench stands at the front left, and a teal watering can sits at the front right.
