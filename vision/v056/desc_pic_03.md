## Free description
This is the same kind of sunny back garden, with a wooden fence, a gate, a path on the left and a cream house with a blue door on the right. In the centre a woman with dark hair, in a yellow dress and red shoes, is bent forward digging in the lawn with a long-handled spade. The spade blade is in the ground and holds a clump of earth, and there is a pile of dug-up soil beside her on the left, so she is digging a hole. Behind her on the left stands a wheelbarrow with a red tray. Its wheel is drawn as a square, which a wheel could not be, so it could not roll. A teal watering can sits on the grass at the front left near the path. On the right there is a flower bed with colourful flowers, a wooden bench and a bush. No one else is in the picture.
