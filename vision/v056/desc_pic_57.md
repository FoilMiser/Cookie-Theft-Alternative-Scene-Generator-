## Free description
A sunny park with a round pond on the left, a lamp post in the middle and a winding path and big tree on the right. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands at the edge of the pond holding a brown paper bag, reaching out and throwing small pieces of food to a white duck on the water. The duck is pecking at the food bits floating in front of it. On the right a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench reading a red book. A grey rock lies on the grass in the front, and there is a flower bed and a green bin in the bottom left.
