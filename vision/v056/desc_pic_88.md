# pic_88

## Free description
A sunny town street with a row of colourful shops with striped awnings and a paved square in front. On the left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie looks up unhappily and points at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of a tree. In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress sits on a wooden bench drinking from a cup through a straw. In front of her, an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses is sweeping a pile of dirt toward a red dustpan, but she is using a giant fork instead of a broom, which is strange. A red bird sits on top of a street lamp. On the right, a little girl in a pink dress holds a yellow balloon on a string, standing near a fruit stall with no one at it. A bicycle is parked in a bike rack, and there is a small café table with two chairs, a bin, a wooden crate and potted plants around the square.
