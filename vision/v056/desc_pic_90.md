## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, and a gate and path on the right. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers rakes a small pile of leaves near the fence. In the front, a boy in a red shirt and blue trousers holds the nozzle of a garden hose up to his face and peers into it, looking puzzled, because no water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel by the house across the lawn, and a brown dog with a red collar is standing right on the hose, squashing it, which is presumably why the water has stopped. If the dog steps off, water may spray into the boy's face. At the hose reel there is a small puddle. On top of the fence, a grey cat creeps toward a blue bird perched further along, as if about to pounce. Two potted plants stand by the fence, and a flower bed is in front of the house.
