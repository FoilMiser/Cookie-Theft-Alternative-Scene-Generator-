## Free description
A sunny playground with a metal railing fence across the back, a tree on the left, a red slide on the right and a seesaw at the front left. An elderly man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat sits on the low (right) end of a red seesaw, reading a newspaper and not paying attention. A small girl in a pink outfit sits on the high (left) end of the seesaw, holding the handle, stuck up in the air with her feet dangling and looking a bit worried or unhappy. In the background, a bearded man in a green T-shirt and tan trousers walks along by the fence eating a pink ice-cream cone. A green litter bin stands near the fence, and a red-and-yellow slide with a ladder stands empty on the right. There is a grey rock on the grass at the front right. The scene suggests the girl cannot come down because the man is too heavy and absorbed in his paper.
