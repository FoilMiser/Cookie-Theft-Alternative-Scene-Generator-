# pic_18

## Free description
A sunny playground with a metal railing fence, a slide, a sandpit, a bench and a tree. On the left, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a kite spool up in the air; the kite string runs to a yellow kite that is stuck in the branches of the tree, so his kite is caught. A man in a red shirt sits on a bench reading a red book, not paying attention. In the middle, a brown dog with a red collar crouches playfully with a yellow-green tennis ball in front of it, tail wagging, as if wanting someone to throw it. On the right, a small girl in pink kneels in the sandpit building a sandcastle with a little flag, holding a red spade. There is also a red-and-yellow slide, a green bin and a grey rock.
