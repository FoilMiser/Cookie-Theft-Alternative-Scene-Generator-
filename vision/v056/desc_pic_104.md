## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a house on the left, a wooden fence and gate at the back, and a paved path on the right. In the middle a man in a green shirt and beige trousers is hanging washing on a clothesline strung between two wooden posts. Blue jeans and a white T-shirt are already pegged up and both are dripping water. A wicker laundry basket full of more clothes sits on the grass below the line. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress is sweeping a pile of brown leaves with a yellow broom, next to a birdhouse on a tall pole. Around the garden are a flower bed by the house, two potted plants, a red wheelbarrow and a teal watering can. Everything looks ordinary: two people doing chores on a sunny day.
