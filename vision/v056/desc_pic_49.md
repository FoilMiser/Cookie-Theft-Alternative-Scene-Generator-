## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house (red roof, blue door) on the left, a flower bed, a wooden fence along the back, a gate at the right and a paved path. A woman with dark hair in a yellow dress stands at the top of the path near the gate, sweeping a small pile of leaves with a yellow broom. In front of the swing set a lawn sprinkler, fed by a long dark-teal hose running across the bottom of the picture, is spraying arcs of water to the right. The water is landing on a cardboard parcel box sitting on the path, and there is a small blue puddle under the box, so the parcel is getting wet. The swing set in the middle has one red seat hanging still and empty. The woman does not seem to have noticed the parcel getting soaked.
