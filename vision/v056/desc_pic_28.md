# pic_28

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence and gate on the left, and a paved path from the gate. A man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is painting the brown fence pink with a long-handled paint roller; a section of the fence is already pink, and a pink paint tin sits on the grass by his feet. On the left, near the gate, a little girl in a pink dress holds strings to a blue and a purple balloon, while a red balloon floats away up into the sky with its string trailing; she is reaching up with her other hand and looks upset, as if she has just let it go. In front, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is waving (motion lines by his hand). On the right, a woman in a yellow dress is watering the flower bed by the house with a teal watering can.
