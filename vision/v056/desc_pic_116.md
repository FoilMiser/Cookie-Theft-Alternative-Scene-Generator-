## Free description
A sunny paved shopping street with a row of colourful shops with striped awnings, a street lamp, a fruit stall on the left and a tree in a planter on the right. In the centre a boy with dark skin, in a yellow top and orange shorts, has just thrown a red ball with one outstretched arm. A dashed line shows the ball's path through the air. To the right a smaller girl in a pink dress holds both hands out in front of her, ready to catch the ball. On the left a woman with long dark hair in a yellow dress is holding a camera up to her eye. She seems to be taking a photo, pointed roughly toward the children. A cardboard box sits on the ground at left and potted plants stand in both bottom corners.
