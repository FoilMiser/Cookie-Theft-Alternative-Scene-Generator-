## Free description
A sunny park with a round pond on the left, a tall lamp post in the middle, trees and a winding sand-coloured path on the right. A small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands at the edge of the pond holding a paper bag and tossing bread crumbs toward a white duck, which is swimming toward the crumbs floating on the water and pecking at them. Further back on the right, a girl in a pink dress runs along the grass holding the string of a yellow balloon. In the front right, a woman in a yellow dress pushes a teal-and-blue pram along the path; a baby sleeps inside under a pink spotted blanket. There is a green bin at bottom left and a grey rock at the bottom centre. Everything looks calm and ordinary; nothing seems about to go wrong.
