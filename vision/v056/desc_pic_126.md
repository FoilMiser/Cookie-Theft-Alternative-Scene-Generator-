## Free description
A sunny but windy day in a park, shown by white wind lines in the sky and across the pond. In the front right an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and a grey skirt, reaches up with one arm and has an open-mouthed, surprised look. Just above and to the left of her, over the pond, an orange cap is flying through the air along with a couple of leaves, so the wind has apparently just blown her cap off and it looks about to land in the pond. In the middle-left a boy with curly dark hair in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands near the path eating a pink ice-cream cone, not reacting to the cap. The rest of the park has a winding path on the left, a big tree, a tall black lamp post in the middle, a pond with bulrushes on the right, a small tree and bushes, and a green litter bin in the bottom right corner. Nothing looks impossible, apart from the cap flying a little ahead of the wind lines.
