## Free description
A sunny back garden with a gate and paved path on the left, a wooden fence along the back and a cream house with a blue door on the right. In the middle a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is pegging wet laundry onto a clothesline; a white T-shirt and blue jeans hang dripping, and a basket of more clothes sits below. To the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. In the front left a man in a green shirt and beige trousers digs a hole in the lawn with a shovel, with a pile of earth beside it. A flower bed is in front of the house.
