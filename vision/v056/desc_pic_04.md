## Free description
This is a busy sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a path down the left side and a cream house with a blue door on the right. On the left a man in a red shirt and jeans paints the fence pink with a paint roller on a pole. Two fence boards are already pink, and a pot of pink paint is at his feet. At the front left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps a pile of dry leaves on the path with a broom. In the centre, an older grey-haired woman with glasses holds the nozzle end of a green garden hose up near her face and looks at it. The hose runs back to a hose reel near the bench, but a brown dog is standing on the hose, so it seems to be blocking the water: no water comes out of her nozzle, and a little water is dripping at the reel. Behind her, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands with his hands on his hips with a white bucket upside down on his head, which looks silly. To the right, a small girl in a pink dress points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of a tree. A grey cat walks along the top of the fence, and a bluebird flies in the sky. At the front right, a woman in a yellow dress sits on the bench drinking from a cup with a straw.
