- The tiled background could be a floor seen from above or a tiled wall. The shadow under his feet suggests a floor, but the tile lines show little perspective.
- The planter box sits just below the window line. It could be mounted on the wall under the sill or standing on the floor further back; it does not clearly rest on a sill.
- The bottom-right object is probably a bicycle, but the thick gray curved tube above the red frame is unusual. It could be a mudguard, rack, handlebar or part of a walker or pushchair frame.
- The dark object at the bottom left is too cut off to identify (possibly a chair back, railing or stand).
- Nothing on the man looks detached or passing through anything. The bucket sits on his head with gray hair showing beneath it at the side, though it sits quite high and looks slightly perched.
