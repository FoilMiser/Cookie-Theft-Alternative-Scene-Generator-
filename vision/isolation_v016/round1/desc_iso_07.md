# iso_07

## What I see
A tall, narrow cut-out of the front of a cream-colored house with a red roof, a four-pane window, and a blue front door with a yellow knob and a gray doorstep. A brown-and-white dog (beagle-like, floppy brown ears, red collar, eyes closed in a happy squint) sits upright in front of the door, holding a red leash in its mouth. The leash runs down the front of the dog's body to the grass, where it ends in a loop near a teal round object with a dark base, which looks like a garden hose reel with a teal hose curling off to the left. To the left are a tree, a wooden picket fence, a green bush, and a bed of purple, yellow and red flowers in dark soil.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The red line could be read two ways: as a leash the dog is carrying, with its handle loop on the grass (as if asking for a walk), or as a red garden hose. Its loop end sits right next to the teal hose reel, so it can look connected to the reel.
- The teal object is most likely a hose reel. It could also be read as a round wheel or a drum on a stand. Its teal hose leaves the image at the left edge.
- The dog is very tall and narrow for a sitting dog, almost like it is standing on its hind legs. It overlaps the bottom of the door and the doorstep, so it is hard to tell whether it is on the step or on the grass in front of it.
- The dog's shadow is a large oval that reaches well out to the right of the dog instead of sitting directly under it.
- The flower bed and the hose are cut off at the left edge. Only part of the tree is visible behind the fence and the house.
