- The dark, bumpy, circular shapes on the head read as curly hair, but could also be a knitted hat or cap.
- The kicking foot sits right on the edge between grass and path, so it overlaps the path's outline.
- The head is large compared with the body, which is a normal cartoon style. Nothing looks detached or floating, apart from the ball being isolated as noted above.
