## What I see
An older man wears a grey flat cap, a white long-sleeved shirt, a brown vest, dark trousers and brown shoes. He stands on green grass in front of a wooden plank fence. He holds a dark grey round object on a long thin handle out in front of him. A blue stream of water arcs from it down onto a round bed of brown soil full of colourful flowers (yellow, orange, purple, red, pink), so he seems to be watering the flowers. A plain cream-coloured vertical panel at the far right edge looks like part of a house wall or a gatepost.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The thing he is watering with does not look like a normal watering can. It is a flat, dark oval on a straight stick handle, with no can body and no spout. It reads most easily as a frying pan or skillet, and could also be a large sprinkler head (rose) on a wand. The water comes straight out of the side of its rim, which looks odd for either reading.
- With no hose or can body, it is unclear where the water comes from.
- Only one arm is visible, and it reaches stiffly forward to the handle. The other arm cannot be seen.
- The grey shape at the back and side of his head could be grey hair or part of the cap. His face is small and hard to read.
- The cream panel on the right is cut off, so it could be a wall, a door frame or a post.
- His body leans back a little while his arm stretches far forward, which makes his posture look slightly stiff.
