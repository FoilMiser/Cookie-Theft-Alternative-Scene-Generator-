## What I see
A teal bicycle stands on a light gray tiled floor, with a soft gray shadow under it. It has a dark handlebar with a grip at the upper left, a small dark wedge-shaped saddle at the upper right, and a crank with two pedal arms hanging down in the middle. Both wheels are drawn as dark squares with spokes running from the corners to a center hub, not as circles. Nobody is riding it or touching it. At the left edge there is a cut-off dark object: a vertical post with a short horizontal bar sticking out. In the top-left corner there is part of another gray shadow and a dark curved shape.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The square wheels are the strangest thing. They could be a deliberate joke or "wrong" detail, or they could be a drawing error where round wheels came out as boxes. Either way, a bike with these wheels could not roll.
- The two pedal arms point down and outward like a pair of legs, and the pedals reach about as low as the bottoms of the wheels. At a glance the crank reads as a small stand or kickstand rather than pedals.
- The frame tubes run straight into the centers of the square wheels, so the wheels look like flat frames bolted onto the bike more than like wheels that turn.
- I can't tell what the dark object at the left edge is. It could be a chair or table leg, part of a bike rack, a coat stand, or a railing post.
- The shape in the top-left corner is too cut off to identify. It could be the bottom of some other object with its shadow.
