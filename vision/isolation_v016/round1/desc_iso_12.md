# iso_12

## What I see
A man with dark brown skin, a dark beard and dark hair stands in side profile facing right on green grass, in front of a tall wooden plank fence. He wears a green short-sleeved shirt, tan trousers and brown shoes. He holds a teal watering can out in front of him by its handle and is watering a flower bed. The bed is an oval of brown soil full of flowers in yellow, purple, orange, pink and red, with green stems and leaves. A pale-blue stream of water arcs out of the can's long spout and down into the middle of the flower bed, and a few small blue droplets fall among the flowers.

- The top of his head is a dark rounded shape with a lighter streak. It could be hair with a highlight, or a dark cap or helmet. The beard joins the hair, so the face area is small.
- Only one arm is visible (the one holding the can). The other arm is hidden or simply not drawn.
- His posture leans back slightly, with the feet set forward. The two shoes overlap and both point right, so the feet look a little bunched together or awkwardly placed.
