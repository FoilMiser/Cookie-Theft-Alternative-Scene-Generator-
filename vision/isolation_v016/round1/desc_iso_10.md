# iso_10

## What I see
A teal bicycle stands on its own against a plain green background, with a soft darker oval shadow underneath it. Nobody is riding it. It has a dark handlebar at the upper left, a dark seat at the upper right and a pedal/crank assembly in the middle at the bottom. Both wheels are square, not round. Each is a dark square frame with spokes running from a central hub to the edges.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The square wheels are the most striking thing. They look like a deliberate "something's wrong" joke, but a bicycle could not roll on them. Either this is the intended oddity or it is a drawing error.
- The two pedal crank arms both point downward and outward in an upside-down V, ending in dark pedals that rest on the ground like little feet or legs. On a real bike the crank arms point in opposite directions. They read either as pedals or as a pair of legs or a kickstand propping the bike up.
- The bike seems to stand upright on the pedals and the bottom edges of the square wheels. It is unclear whether the pedals are touching the ground or floating just above the shadow.
- The rear part of the frame (the chainstay/chain from the crank to the back wheel) joins the back wheel near its hub. The lines overlap the wheel frame, so it is hard to tell exactly where the frame ends and the wheel begins.
- The seat is a small, flat, dark wedge on a short post and could also be read as a rack. The handlebar is a single short dark grip pointing to the left.
