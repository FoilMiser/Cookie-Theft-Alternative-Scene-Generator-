## What I see
An older man with gray hair stands on green grass in front of a wooden plank fence. He wears a gray-blue flat cap, a white long-sleeved shirt, a brown vest, dark trousers and brown shoes. He holds a long, thin, straight handle out in front of him with one hand. At the end of the handle is a dark gray rounded container, and a stream of water pours from it in an arc onto a round bed of brown soil full of flowers in yellow, orange, purple, pink and red. He seems to be watering the flowers.

- Only one arm is visible; the other is hidden behind his body.
- His eyes are small and look closed, or nearly closed, in profile.
- His legs look long next to his upper body, and he leans back slightly.
