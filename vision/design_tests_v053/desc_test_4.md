# Description of test_4.png

## 1. Free description

This is a sunny day in a park, with green hills, trees, a pond, a winding sand-coloured path and a lamp post. A woman in a yellow dress stands at the front edge of the pond. She holds a brown paper bag in one hand and throws bread to a white duck, and several crumbs float on the water near the duck. A small girl in a pink dress stands at the far right on the grass, holding a kite reel up with both arms. Her long string runs up past a big tree to a yellow diamond kite with a bow tail, high in the sky. An empty wooden bench is in the middle right, next to the path. In front of the woman is a grey wheelbarrow, and its wheel is drawn as a square black frame instead of a round wheel, which looks wrong and means it could not roll. The wheelbarrow has no one with it. At the bottom left are a bed of brightly coloured flowers and a green rubbish bin. Nothing looks about to go dramatically wrong, apart from the duck being about to eat the bread.

- Where the woman stands: her body overlaps the front rim of the pond, and her feet are on the grass just below it. She is probably standing on the bank in front of the water, but she could also be read as standing partly in or over the pond.
- The kite string passes in front of the big tree's foliage and trunk, not behind or around them. It reads fine as the string being closer to the viewer, but it is slightly odd.
