# Description of test_1.png

## 1. Free description

It is a sunny day in a back garden. A tall wooden fence runs along the back, with a gate on the left and a house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. On the left, a small girl in a pink dress stands on a paved path and has just thrown a red ball. A dotted line runs from her hand up to the ball, showing where it went. In the middle, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds both arms out towards the ball, ready to catch it. Next to her, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts jumps in the air with both arms up, and his shadow is on the grass below him. Further back, by the fence, an older man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat rakes a small pile of leaves. On the right are a round flower bed of colourful flowers and a wooden bench. The ball looks about to reach the woman, or maybe the jumping boy.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous

- Who will catch the ball: the woman is reaching for it, but the boy is jumping with his arms up, so he could be trying to catch it too. The ball's path heads towards the woman.
- The boy's arms are straight up rather than towards the ball. He could be jumping to catch it, or just jumping for joy or cheering.
- The dotted line from the girl's hand ends at the ball rather than going ahead of it, so it shows where the ball has been, not where it will land.
- The girl's throwing hand is quite far from her body, so the arm looks a bit long and stiff.
- Nothing looks impossible. Everything sits on the ground except the boy, who is clearly jumping since he has a shadow, and the ball, which is clearly flying.
