## Free description
This is a sunny back garden seen from the other side: the cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the right, a gate is in the fence on the left, and a paved path runs down the left side. On the path at the front left a boy in a blue hoodie is using a giant silver dinner fork like a broom or rake to push a pile of dirt into a red dustpan, raising a little dust cloud. In the middle an older man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is holding the end of a garden hose up close to his face and peering into the nozzle, with a drop of water at its tip. The hose runs from a hose reel by the flower bed, along the ground, and a brown dog with a red collar is standing on the hose; the hose is swollen into a bulge on the reel side of where the dog is standing. So the dog is blocking the water, and it looks like the man is about to get sprayed in the face when the dog steps off. At the back a bearded man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers is walking to the right with a white dog with brown spots on a red leash. There is an empty swing set with a red seat on the left of the lawn and a red pet food bowl full of kibble on the grass at the front. The oversized fork is clearly odd, and the hose situation looks like an accident waiting to happen.
