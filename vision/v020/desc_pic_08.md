# Picture 08

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back with a gate on the right, and a stone path running down the right side. An orange cat is walking along the very top of the fence on the left, near the tree. In the front middle, a girl with a brown ponytail, blue hoodie and black trousers is kicking an orange ball, with swoosh lines showing her leg swinging. On the path at the right, an older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. In the middle there is a clothes line between two wooden posts with blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes pegged on it, and on the right a small red-roofed birdhouse on a tall pole. A teal watering can sits on the grass near the path, a hose reel stands by the flower bed, and there are two potted plants near the fence. The ball looks like it is about to fly off across the lawn.
