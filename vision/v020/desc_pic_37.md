# Picture 37

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the sun in the top left and two white clouds, and there are no people or animals in it. On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door, with a bed of colourful flowers in front of it. A wooden fence runs across the back with a gate on the left, and a paved path leads from the gate down to the bottom-left corner. On the lawn are two potted plants, a teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow. At the front a garden hose comes in from the bottom edge of the picture and connects to a sprinkler that sprays three arcs of water to the right. Instead of watering the flowers, the water lands on and around a cardboard box sitting on the grass just in front of the flower bed. The box has blue drips on its sides and sits in a small puddle, so it is getting soaked and whatever is inside may be ruined. The odd thing is that a cardboard box has been left out on the lawn right in the path of the sprinkler.
