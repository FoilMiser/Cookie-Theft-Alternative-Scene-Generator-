# Picture 35

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the sun in the top left and a couple of white clouds. In the middle a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers stands at a washing line between two wooden posts. She reaches up with both hands to peg up a white T-shirt, and a yellow shirt and a pair of blue jeans are already hanging on the line. On the left an orange cat walks along the pointed top of the wooden fence, just above the garden gate. A brown cardboard box is up in the sky above the fence, tilted at an angle, with nothing holding it up. It looks like it is flying or falling through the air, and it is not clear where it came from. On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door, with a bed of colourful flowers in front of it and a bush beside it. A paved path runs from the gate on the left down to the bottom-left corner. The flying box is the strange part of the picture. It could be about to fall into the garden, near the cat or the woman, who doesn't seem to have noticed it.
