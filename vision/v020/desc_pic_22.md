The man has his back to the dog and does not seem to have noticed. In the middle, a washing line between two posts holds a yellow-and-red striped shirt or towel and blue jeans. There is a flower bed and an empty bench by the house, a bush, and at the bottom a teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow. The mishap is the dog tracking blue paint through the garden.
