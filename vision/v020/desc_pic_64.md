# pic_64

## Free description
This is a quiet park on a sunny day with an oval pond on the left, a tall black lamp post in the middle, a winding path on the right, and trees and bushes around. A white duck is swimming on the pond. In the front centre, a woman with a long dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is pushing a teal pram toward the left; a baby is asleep inside under a pink polka-dot blanket. The strange thing is a tall wooden ladder standing straight up on the grass between the lamp post and the path, leaning against nothing at all - there is no wall, tree or post for it to rest on, so it could not really stand like that. Otherwise there is a green trash can at bottom left and a grey rock at the bottom centre. Nobody seems to be using the ladder or paying attention to it.
