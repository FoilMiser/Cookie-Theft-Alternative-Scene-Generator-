# Picture 26

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny day in a park with green hills in the background, a pond on the right, a winding footpath on the left, and a tall black lamp post in the middle. A small girl in a pink dress on the far left is tugging on a kite string. Her purple kite is caught in the branches of the big tree next to her, so it's stuck rather than flying. Further up the path, a brown dog with a red collar has its nose down in a pink ice-cream scoop that has fallen on the ground. A child in a yellow top and orange shorts stands just beyond it, holding an empty cone and rubbing one eye, as if crying over the lost ice cream. In the front left a worker in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest sweeps the path with a broom. An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a park bench reading a red book. Beside her on the bench, a blue bird is pecking at her sandwich, and she hasn't noticed. On the right a woman in a purple top kneels by a flower bed next to a potted pink flower, apparently about to plant it. A red bird flies across the sky, and there is a grey rock in the front centre and a green bin in the front right corner.
