## Free description
This is a back garden on a sunny day, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the left, and a paved path running down the left side. An older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt is standing in the middle holding a garden hose nozzle up and peering at it, because only a drop is coming out. The reason is a white goat standing right on top of the hose, which has swelled into a bulge on the reel side of the goat; it looks like the water might burst out when the goat steps off. On the left, a separate hose feeds a sprinkler that is spraying arcs of water onto a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is getting soaked and is standing in a puddle. Behind them, a clothesline between two posts holds a red-and-yellow striped towel and a pair of blue jeans, and a birdhouse sits on a tall pole. Most oddly, an orange fish is sitting in the branches of the tree behind the house, which is impossible. There is also a flower bed in front of the house, a hose reel, and a wooden bench at the bottom right.
