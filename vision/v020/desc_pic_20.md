## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate at the left end with a paved path leading to it, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the middle an older man with grey hair, a grey cap, a white shirt and a brown waistcoat is holding a watering can over a flower bed, but the stream of water arcs upwards into the air with drops at the top instead of pouring down on the flowers. Back on the left-centre, a little girl in a pink dress is jumping in the air with both arms up and a happy face. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a wooden bench drinking from a red cup with a straw. On the left there is a swing set with an empty red seat, and at the bottom there is an empty grey wheelbarrow. A tree stands behind the fence near the house. The main odd thing is the water going upwards out of the watering can.
