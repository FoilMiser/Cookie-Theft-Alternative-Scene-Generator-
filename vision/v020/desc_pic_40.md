On the far left a bearded man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers is in a sitting position drinking from a red cup with a straw, but there is no bench or chair under him, so he is sitting on thin air. An older man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat is sitting on a bench reading a red book, a girl in a pink dress is walking along the path holding a pink ice cream cone, and on the far right a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts is pointing up, looking worried, at a blue-and-white ball stuck in the branches of a big tree. Several things look wrong: the man sitting on nothing, the frying pan used for watering, and the sprinkler watering a box.
