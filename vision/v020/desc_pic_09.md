## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden picket fence across the back, a closed wooden gate at the far left, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. In the middle of the lawn there is one young person with shoulder-length brown hair, wearing a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes. They are in a sitting position, knees bent and feet on the grass, sipping a drink from a red cup through a yellow straw. The strange thing is that there is no chair, bench or anything else under them: they are sitting on thin air, as if on an invisible seat. A teal watering can sits on the grass near the path at the front left, and two potted plants stand near the fence. A round flower bed full of red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers is next to the house. Nobody else is around, and nothing else seems about to happen apart from the question of how the person is staying up.
