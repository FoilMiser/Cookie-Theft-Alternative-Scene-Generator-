# Picture 25

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden behind a house. The house is on the left. It is cream-coloured, with a red roof, a window, and a blue back door with a step. A tall wooden fence runs across the back of the garden, with a wooden gate on the right and a paved path leading from the gate toward the front right corner. A tree pokes up behind the house. A woman with a dark ponytail, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, stands in the middle of the lawn holding a garden hose. She is spraying a stream of water onto a round bed of colourful flowers on the left. The hose runs back across the grass to a hose reel near the house. Behind her a washing line strung between two wooden posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes, pegged up to dry. On the right there are two potted plants on the grass. Along the bottom there is an empty grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can. It looks like an ordinary, calm day of gardening and laundry, and nothing seems about to go wrong.
