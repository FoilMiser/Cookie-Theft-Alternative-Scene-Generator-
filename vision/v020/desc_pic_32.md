## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the sun in the top right, a few white clouds, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left. In the middle a man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands with both arms stretched out in front of him, about to catch a small red ball flying towards his hands. A dotted line shows the ball's path back to a child to the right. The child has dark curly hair, a yellow top and orange shorts and seems to have just thrown the ball. At the front a white dog with brown spots crouches in a "play bow", front legs stretched out, bottom up and tail wagging, with a yellow tennis ball by its front paws, as if it wants someone to throw the ball. On the left a brown-and-white dog (it looks like a boxer) sits by the blue back door holding a red lead in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. On the right a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers sweeps the paved path with a yellow broom and looks over towards the others. A red bowl of dog food sits on the grass near the path. Two potted plants stand on the lawn and a flower bed is in front of the house. A wooden fence runs across the back, with a gate on the right where the path begins. It looks like an ordinary happy family scene, and I noticed nothing clearly wrong.
