## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills behind, a winding sandy path on the left, a wooden bench, a tall black lamp post, and a blue pond with reeds on the right. An older woman with grey hair in a bun, wearing a teal top and a grey skirt, stands at the front edge of the pond scattering bird seed or crumbs from a white paper bag. A dotted trail of seeds falls to the grass, where a red bird is pecking them up. Next to her, a young person with a brown ponytail, blue hoodie and dark trousers is walking past carrying a yellow shopping bag. The strange thing is a brown cardboard box, tilted at an angle, that appears to be floating in the air up at the far end of the path, against the hills, with nothing holding it up. The bench is empty, and there is also a tree on the left, a smaller tree on the right, bushes, a bed of colourful flowers in the front right and a green rubbish bin in the bottom right corner. Apart from the floating box, the scene is calm.
