## Free description
This is a busy, sunny shopping street with a row of four coloured shop buildings (pink, pale green, peach and cream) with striped awnings, and a paved pavement in front. On the left a girl in a blue hoodie is carrying a cardboard box towards a fruit and vegetable stall, and in front of her a woman in a purple top is walking while reading an open book, heading straight towards a blue bucket on the ground that she seems about to trip over. In the middle a woman in a yellow dress is using a watering can by a planter of flowers, but the stream of water arcs upwards into the air instead of pouring down onto the flowers. Behind the bench, a workman in an orange cap and hi-vis vest is sweeping a pile of dirt towards a red dustpan using a giant dinner fork instead of a broom. On the right a tree has a blue ball stuck in its branches, and a little girl in a pink dress is pointing up at it. At the bottom an orange cat is crouching and creeping towards a blue bird that is pecking at the ground, as if about to pounce. A red bird flies across the sky, a boy in yellow holding a yellow balloon stands at the far right by a shop, and an older woman with grey hair carrying a yellow shopping bag walks along the front right. A green bicycle is locked in a bike rack, and there is a street lamp, an empty bench and potted plants. The impossible or wrong things are the giant fork used as a broom and the water spraying upwards.

- The giant fork: clearly a dinner-fork shape at giant size; could be read as an oversized rake, but the shape is a fork.
- The cat: could be about to pounce on the bird, or just walking; its low crouch suggests stalking.
- The reading woman: very likely about to trip on the bucket, but she has not reached it yet.
- The boy with the yellow balloon is small and far back; easy to miss. Gender of children read from clothing/hair only.
