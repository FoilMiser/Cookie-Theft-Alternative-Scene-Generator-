# Picture 03

## Free description
It is a sunny day on a paved town street lined with colourful two-storey shops: pink, pale green, peach and cream buildings with striped awnings. In the middle-left, a little girl in a pink dress is holding a purple and a blue balloon in one hand and reaching up with the other toward a yellow balloon that has got away from her; its string is dangling loose and nobody is holding it, and she looks upset. In the centre-right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is smiling and waving one hand, with little motion marks around it. On the far right, a woman with a long dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is walking to the right carrying a cardboard box. On the left there is an empty fruit and vegetable stall with crates of green, yellow, orange and red produce, and a cardboard box on the ground beside it. Around the street there are also a black street lamp, a wooden bench, a teal bicycle parked at a bike rack, a tree in a round planter, a planter of flowers under a shop window and a potted plant. The yellow balloon looks like it is about to float away out of reach.
