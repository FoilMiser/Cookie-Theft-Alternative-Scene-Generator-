## Free description
This is a busy back garden on a sunny day: a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path running down the right side. Behind the house, a blue ball is stuck in the branches of a tree, and a little girl in a pink dress is pointing up at it with her mouth open. In the middle, a woman in a blue top and black trousers has just thrown a red ball (a dotted line shows its arc) to a woman in a purple top and teal trousers, who has her hands up ready to catch it. In front of them, a person in a yellow dress is holding up a garden hose and staring at the nozzle, which is only dripping a single drop - because a white goat is standing right on the hose, and the hose has swelled up into a bulge behind the goat; it looks like the water could burst out when the goat moves. On the right, a smiling boy in a yellow shirt is swinging on a swing set, a white puppy is asleep on the grass in front of it, and a black-and-white cat is crouching low, stalking a small blue bird on the grass by the gate. Most oddly, a brown dog is sitting on the path at the bottom right, holding open a newspaper and reading it like a person, which is impossible. A red bird flies across the sky at the top.
