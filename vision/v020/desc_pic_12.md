## Free description
This is a busy, sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden picket fence across the back, a wooden gate at the back right, and a paved path running down the right side. A man with brown hair in a red shirt and blue jeans is walking to the right while reading an open book, and a red ball is lying on the grass just ahead of his feet, so he looks about to trip over it. Behind him, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence blue with a brush on a long handle; one section of the fence is already blue, and a paint tin sits by her feet. A blue bird is perched on top of the freshly painted blue section, and a red bird sits further along the fence top. A black-and-white cat is creeping along the top of the fence toward the red bird, as if about to pounce. Strangely, a tan dog is sitting up on the lawn holding an open newspaper in its front paws as if reading it, which a dog could not really do. A small white puppy is curled up asleep on the path at the bottom right, and a red bowl of dog food sits on the grass nearby. There is also a flower bed by the house, two potted plants near the gate, a bush and a tree.
