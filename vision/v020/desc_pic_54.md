# pic_54

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with no people or animals moving about in it. On the left is a house with a red roof, cream walls, a window and a blue door, and in front of it a round flower bed full of red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers, with a coiled teal garden hose on a reel beside it. A wooden fence runs across the back of the garden with a wooden gate on the right, and a pale paved path leads from the bottom right up to that gate. Near the fence there are two terracotta pots with green plants and a couple of round bushes. In the bottom centre is a grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles, and to its right a teal watering can sits on the grass. The odd thing is in the tree behind the house: an orange fish is sitting among the green leaves, as if it were a bird or a piece of fruit — fish do not live in trees, so this looks impossible. Otherwise everything is calm and nobody is using any of the tools.
