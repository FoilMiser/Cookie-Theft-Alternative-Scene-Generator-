## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a paved path running down the right side from a gate. In the front left-centre a man in an orange cap and an orange high-visibility safety vest is holding a teal watering can over a bed of colourful flowers. The water does not pour down from the can: a curved blue stream rises up from the spout into the air above the flowers, with drops flying off the top, as if the water is shooting upwards. In the middle at the back a woman with a brown ponytail, blue top and dark trousers is painting the fence blue with a roller on a long handle; one section of the fence is already blue and a paint bucket with blue paint sits on the grass by her feet. She is standing under a washing line strung between two wooden posts, and a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes hang on the line right in front of the freshly painted blue section. On the right a bearded man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers stands in front of the gate with one hand raised high and little motion lines around it, so he seems to be waving to someone. An empty wooden bench sits in the front left corner, an empty grey wheelbarrow sits in the front middle, and two potted plants stand on the grass on the right. The laundry hanging against wet paint looks like it could get paint on it, and the upward-flowing water looks wrong.
