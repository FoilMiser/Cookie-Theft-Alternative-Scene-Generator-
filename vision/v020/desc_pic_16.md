# pic_16

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden picket fence across the back, and a wooden gate and paved path on the right. An older man in a grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is watering a bed of colourful flowers with a teal watering can, with water streaming onto the flowers. At the back right, a woman with dark hair in a bun, a yellow dress and red shoes is painting the fence blue with a brush on a stick; a few boards next to the gate are already blue, and a paint tin and a tray of blue paint sit on the grass behind her. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has walked through the paint tray and is leaving a trail of blue paw prints across the lawn as it trots away to the left. In the front right, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is bent over on the path sweeping dirt into a red dustpan, but instead of a broom he is using a giant silver dinner fork, which is absurd; a little dust cloud rises from the pile. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands on the grass at the front centre, and a coiled garden hose on a reel sits by the flower bed, unused while the man waters by can.

- The man watering by can while a hose reel sits right next to him could be read as a detail or just set dressing; it is not impossible.
