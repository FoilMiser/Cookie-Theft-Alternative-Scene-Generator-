# Picture 01

## Free description
It is a sunny, windy day in a back garden, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left and a wooden fence across the back. In the middle of the lawn an older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, has both arms thrown up in the air because a red cap is being blown away from her by the wind, along with a few leaves. Behind her, a young man in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest is painting the fence blue with a long-handled brush; a few boards are already blue. His paint can and paint tray are on the grass by his feet, and there is a trail of blue paw prints leading from the paint tray across the grass to a white dog with brown spots on the path at the right, so the dog has clearly stepped in the paint and its paws are blue. In the front right, an older man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is reading a red book in a sitting position, but there is no chair, bench or anything under him: he is sitting on thin air, which is impossible. There is also a flower bed with colourful flowers by the house and a green garden hose reel. The dog looks like it is about to wander off down the path and spread blue paint further.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The seated man has no chair or bench at all; he could be read as floating or as a drawing with the chair missing. I see only a shadow on the grass beneath him.
- Whose cap it is: probably the woman's (she has no hat and is reaching up), but she could also just be throwing her hands up in surprise rather than grabbing for it.
- The painter's tool looks like a brush on a pole; it could also be read as a small roller.
- The small falling shapes near the cap look like leaves (one brown, two green); they could be something else small being blown along.
