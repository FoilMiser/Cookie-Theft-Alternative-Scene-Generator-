# pic_52

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a paved path running from the bottom left up to a wooden gate in the fence on the left. An older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, is digging a hole in the lawn with a shovel near the fence, and a small mound of dirt is piled beside the hole. In front of her is a round flower bed full of red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers. On the right, a younger woman (or girl) with short dark hair and a yellow dress is sitting on a wooden bench drinking from a red cup through a straw, looking towards the garden. On the left there is a metal swing set with a single red seat that is empty and hanging still, and a teal watering can sits on the grass in front of it. The strange thing is a cardboard box hanging in mid-air in the sky above the fence, tilted at an angle, with nobody near it who could have thrown it — it looks like it is flying or falling. Nobody seems to have noticed the box.
