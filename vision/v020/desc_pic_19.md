## Free description
This is a sunny shopping street with four coloured shop buildings (pink, pale green, peach and cream) with striped awnings and a paved pavement in front. In the middle a small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a yellow and a purple balloon by their strings in one hand, and is reaching up with his other hand towards a red balloon that has come loose and is floating away just above his fingers. On the left a young person with shoulder-length brown hair in a blue hoodie and dark trousers is jumping in the air with both arms up and a big happy, open-mouthed face, as if cheering or waving. On the right a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is running fast towards the centre (there are speed lines behind her), possibly to help catch the balloon. Around them are a fruit and vegetable stall with a wooden crate beside it, a street lamp, a planter of flowers, a green bicycle locked in a bike rack, a tree in a round planter, and a potted plant. Something is about to happen: the red balloon looks like it will drift away unless someone grabs it.
