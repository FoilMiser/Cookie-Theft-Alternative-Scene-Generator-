## Free description
This is a sunny playground surrounded by a dark metal railing fence, with a tree on the left, a swing set in the middle, a slide on the right and a sandpit at the front right. On the left, a little girl in a pink dress sits on the high end of a red seesaw, kicking her feet, while an older man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat sits on the low end reading a book, paying no attention to her, so she is stuck up in the air. Behind them, a young person with brown hair in a blue hoodie is leaping into the air with both arms raised, as if jumping for joy; a shadow on the ground beneath shows they are off the ground. In the centre, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is walking while reading an open book, and a cardboard box is right in front of her foot, so she seems about to trip over it. On the right, a child with dark curly hair, a yellow top and orange shorts is sliding down the slide. Next to the slide, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands with his hands on his hips wearing an upside-down metal bucket on his head like a hat, which is odd. A small brown-and-white puppy is asleep on the grass at the bottom left, the swing hangs empty, and the sandpit has a red bucket and blue spade but nobody in it.
