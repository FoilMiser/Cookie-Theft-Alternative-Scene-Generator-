# Picture 33

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park, with the sun in the top left, two white clouds, rolling green hills and bushes in the background. A sandy path winds from the bottom of the picture back towards the left. On the path an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, a teal top and a grey skirt bends forward and scatters seeds from a small white container. The seeds fall in a dotted line to two little birds on the ground, one blue and one red, which are pecking them up. In the middle a bearded man with dark hair, a green shirt and khaki trousers stands next to a tall black street lamp and holds a small white bag. He tosses brown pieces of bread through the air towards a pond on the right. A white duck with an orange beak swims across the pond towards the bread, with ripple lines behind it, and a few pieces of bread are already floating on the water. Reeds or cattails grow at both ends of the pond. At the front right a girl with dark hair, a pink top, pink shorts and yellow shoes sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket next to a wicker picnic basket, smiling with her eyes closed. There is a large tree on the left, a smaller tree on the right and several bushes. It is a calm, pleasant scene and nothing looks dangerous.
