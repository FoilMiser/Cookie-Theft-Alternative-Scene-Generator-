# Picture 30

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden, with the sun in the top left, two clouds, and a cream house with a red roof, window and blue door on the right. A tall wooden fence runs across the back, with a wooden gate on the left and a paved path leading from the gate to the front left. On the left a man in an orange cap, orange high-visibility vest and blue trousers stands facing the fence, painting it blue with a paint roller or brush on a short handle. One section of the fence is already painted bright blue, and the rest is still plain wood. A small paint tin sits on the grass by his feet. On the right a woman with dark hair in a bun, wearing a yellow dress and red shoes, kneels beside a flower bed of colourful flowers. She is reaching toward a pot with a pink flower in it, apparently about to plant it. In the front centre an orange cat lies curled up asleep on the grass. A teal watering can stands in the front left, and a bush and a tree stand by the house. The scene is calm. Nobody is in trouble, and the fence painting is half done.
