# Picture 36

## Free description
This is a back garden under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds overhead, so it looks like it is about to rain. On the left a young person with brown hair, a blue hoodie, black trousers and white trainers stands on the paved path near the gate. They look up with wide eyes and an open mouth and point up at the clouds, as if warning that rain is coming. In the middle an older man with a grey beard and a flat cap, a white shirt, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers reaches up to peg a white T-shirt onto the washing line, next to a yellow shirt and blue jeans already hanging there. He seems not to have noticed the storm, so the washing is likely to get wet. On the left of the lawn is a metal A-frame swing set with an empty red swing seat, and a teal watering can sits on the grass in front of it. At the front centre is an empty grey wheelbarrow, but its wheel looks square, or like a wheel inside a square frame, which looks strange. On the right is the cream house with the red roof and blue door, a bed of colourful flowers, and an empty wooden bench in the bottom-right corner. A wooden fence with a gate on the left runs across the back.
