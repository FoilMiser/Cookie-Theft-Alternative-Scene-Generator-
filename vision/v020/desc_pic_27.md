# Picture 27

## Free description
This is a sunny shopping street with a row of four colourful two-storey shops with striped awnings and a wide paved pavement in front. A blue bird flies in the top left. In the tree on the left there is an orange fish sitting in the leaves, which is impossible. A street sweeper in an orange cap and hi-vis vest sweeps on the far left. In front of the peach-coloured shop, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts holds a yellow and a purple balloon in one hand. The child reaches up with the other hand toward a blue balloon that has floated away and is drifting above the shop door with its string hanging loose. A woman in a yellow dress waters a planter box of flowers with a teal watering can. In the middle of the pavement, a brown dog is nosing a dropped pink ice cream, and a little girl in a pink dress stands beside it holding an empty cone and rubbing her eye as if crying. On the right a man in a red shirt walks along reading an open book instead of watching where he's going. A red ball is right in front of his feet, so he looks about to trip over it, and a lamp post is just beyond. Next to him a bearded man in a green shirt walks a white spotted dog on a red lead past a fruit and vegetable stall. A small café table with two chairs, a bench, a bin and a potted plant complete the scene.
