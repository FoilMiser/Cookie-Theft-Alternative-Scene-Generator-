# pic_49

## Free description
This is a windy, sunny day in a back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. White swirly wind lines are drawn in the sky and across the fence, and a few leaves are blowing about. On the left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands with both arms raised and her mouth open in surprise, looking up at an orange cap that the wind has blown off and is carrying away just above her reach. On the right, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is watering a round flower bed with a teal watering can, and water is pouring onto the flowers; he does not seem to notice the woman. A green hose reel sits on the ground at the edge of the flower bed. There is a small birdhouse on a tall wooden pole near the fence. Strangely, an orange fish is sitting in the leaves of the tree behind the house, which is impossible.
