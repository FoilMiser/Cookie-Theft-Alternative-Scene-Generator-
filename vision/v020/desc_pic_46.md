This is a sunny back garden enclosed by a tall wooden fence with a gate at the far left and a paved path leading from the gate to the front. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands in the middle of the lawn reaching up with both hands to peg a white T-shirt onto a clothesline strung between two wooden posts. Already hanging on the line are a yellow towel or cloth with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans, held on with wooden pegs. On the left there is a metal swing set with a single empty red swing seat. On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue front door, with a tree behind it and a bush beside it. In front of the house is a round flower bed full of colourful flowers. In the foreground, a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow sit on the grass. Nothing seems about to go wrong; it looks like an ordinary washing day, and nothing looks impossible.
