## Free description
This is a sunny park with rolling green hills, trees, a winding sandy path, and a blue pond with reeds/cattails at its edges. An older man with grey hair and a grey beard, wearing a blue cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers, is standing on the grass fishing; his rod reaches out over the pond and the line drops to a red-and-white float bobbing in the water with a little ripple around it. Behind him is a tall black old-fashioned lamppost. In the bottom right, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is running fast towards the left, with speed lines behind her and one arm stretched forward, passing along the edge of the pond. On the left side of the path, a wooden ladder is standing up on the grass at a slant, leaning against nothing at all — there is no wall, tree or building for it to rest on, so it should fall over. A grey rock sits at the bottom of the picture near the path. Nobody seems to be paying attention to the ladder.
