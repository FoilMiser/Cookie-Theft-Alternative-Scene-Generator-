# Review of share "set" (21 pictures)

Scope: I viewed all 21 pictures in list_set.txt whole at full size (1600 x 1000) before reading their records, then viewed every picture again reduced to 800 x 500 (stacked in pairs, crops_set/smallpair_*.png and *_small.png).
For every event I cropped the region of its elements from the record bounding boxes and viewed the crop enlarged 1.2 to 3.5 times (crops_set/<picture>_<tag>.png); every fault below was confirmed in such a crop.

## Findings, most severe first

1. **High - The sandwich-stealing bird is too small to be found.**
   Pictures: T4_playground_s512950 [1210,592,1252,630]; T4_street_s464887 [750,674,794,716].
   The thief bird is about 42 x 40 px, which is about 21 px wide at 800 x 500, and it sits half hidden behind the bench back next to an equally small sandwich.
   In s512950 the grandmother carrying a box walks right beside the bench, so the eye goes to her and to the reader rather than to the bird.
   Event: bird_steals ("A bird is pecking at a sandwich on the bench", and the inference rests on it).
   Seen in: 2 of 2.
   Fix: draw the thief bird and the sandwich at about 1.5 to 2 times their size, put them on the open seat with nothing behind them, and keep walkers at least one figure-width away from that bench.

2. **Medium - The stalking cat does not crouch low.**
   Pictures: T2_garden_s012 [866,900,1066,992]; T2_street_s032 [662,840,848,924]; T4_street_s171111_iso [734,900,934,992].
   In the stalk_paw pose the cat stands on fairly straight legs with its hips high and its belly well clear of the ground, so it reads as a cat walking or sneaking, not one crouched flat and ready to pounce.
   Event: cat_stalks_bird (scored "A cat is crouching low").
   Seen in: 3 of 3 (cat_01 and cat_02 alike).
   Fix: redo the pose with bent hind legs, belly and chest close to the ground, head low and level, and tail low.

3. **Medium - The pecking bird faces the cat that is stalking it.**

## Looked for and found fine

- Every seated figure I cropped sits on its seat: the bench drinkers, bench readers, the ice-cream eater and the picnicker on his blanket.
- Hands hold their props in every case I cropped except the mirrored spade: rakes, brooms, the giant fork, the roller, cameras, kite spools, leashes, the stroller handle, boxes, bags, watering cans and frying pans.
- All five kites caught in trees read as kites, the strings run from the spool to the kite, and the slack strings fit a stuck kite.
- All five balls in trees sit on a visible branch inside the crown, and the pointing child points at them in each picture.
- The watering water, the frying-pan water and the sprinkler arcs read as water and not as anything else.
- Figures stand on the ground with contact shadows; the jumping boy is clearly off the ground with his shadow below him.
- The same figures and props are drawn alike across pictures (kicker, thrower, catcher, sweepers, cone child, licking dogs, bikes, sandbox, slide).
- In the s3043 version of about_to_trip, the walking reader looks at her book and the box is clearly in her path.
- The goat tied to a stake and grazing, the dog bowing with a ball, the dogs sleeping, the cat sleeping, the cat on the fence, and the perched and flying birds all read at once.
- The pond, paths, fences, houses, shops and hills are drawn consistently, with nothing floating or misplaced.
