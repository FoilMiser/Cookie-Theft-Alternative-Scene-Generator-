# Review p2: 26 pictures (16 garden, 7 park, 2 playground, 1 street)

I viewed each of the 26 pictures whole at full size (1600 x 1000) before reading its record, then read each record and viewed crops of every event's elements at 1.6x to 5x (saved in tpdb_work/review56/crops_p2/).
I also viewed every picture reduced to 800 x 500 and zoomed parts of those reductions where the full-size reading was in doubt (floating box, ladder, square wheel, blocked hose, sprinkler, water upward, dropped ice cream).

## Findings, most severe first

1. **High: the floating box reads as a box thrown or tumbling through the air, not as a box hanging still.**
   Picture T3_garden_s25006, region [512,238,632,336], with the waving child at [240,644,354,852].
   The box is drawn tilted about 15 degrees with its underside and one side showing, the pose of a box caught mid-fall or mid-throw, and it has no shadow or anything else that says it is at rest in mid-air.
   The waving boy's raised hand points up toward the box, so a viewer can easily read the scene as "the boy threw a box over the fence" or "a box is falling", and then name nothing wrong.
   Event: floating_box (contradiction); the notice and explain items are at risk.
   Seen in: 1 of 1.
   Fix: draw the box level and upright, hovering over a spot on the lawn with a faint shadow on the grass directly below it, and place it away from any raised hand (for example over the empty lawn on the side away from the waver).

2. **Medium: the grandmother who "points up at the sky" points sideways at about 45 degrees and looks straight ahead, not up.**
   Pictures T3_garden_s23027 [1062,474,1176,732] and T3_garden_s23055 [928,676,1068,996].
   Her arm goes up and to the left at about 45 degrees, the pointing finger is a small pale nub, and her eyes look level to the side; in s23027 her gaze and arm aim at the painter and the freshly painted boards, so she reads as pointing at the painting.
   The teenager (leo) in T3_garden_s23059 does look up with raised eyes, which shows the gesture can work.
   Event: rain_coming (inference); the items "pointing up at the sky" and "It is about to rain" are at risk.
   Seen in: 2 of 3 (the rosa figure in both of its rain_coming pictures; the leo figure is fine).
   Fix: re-pose rosa's point_up with the arm nearer vertical, the head tilted back and the eyes turned up toward the clouds, and do not place her so her arm aims at another scored figure.

3. **Medium: the ladder that "leans on nothing" appears to lean on the distant hedge and hills.**
   Picture T3_park_s24030, region [378,438,484,704].
   The top of the ladder ends exactly at the skyline of the background hedge and hill, so at 800 x 500 it looks propped against the bushes behind it, which removes the contradiction.
   Event: ladder_nothing (contradiction).
   Seen in: 1 of 1.
   Fix: shorten the ladder or move it lower so its top stands clearly against open sky with a gap above the hedge line, or place it in the open lawn with sky behind its top.

4. **Medium: the square wheel of the wheelbarrow reads as a separate dark crate or window grille next to a wheelless barrow.**
   Picture T3_garden_s53703, region [470,684,552,764] within the barrow [308,670,552,770].
   The square wheel is drawn much larger than the round wheel of the same wheelbarrow in other pictures (about 80 px against about 45 px), filled dark grey with a spoked inner frame, and it sits beside the tray rather than under it, so at reduced size it looks like a black box or a framed grate standing next to the barrow.
   Event: square_wheel (contradiction).
   Seen in: 1 of 1.
   Fix: draw the square wheel at the normal wheel size and position, in the same colours as the normal tyre and hub, so that the only change from the familiar wheelbarrow is the shape.

5. **Medium: the frying pan used for watering reads as an ordinary saucepan, which is a plausible thing to water with.**
   Picture T3_garden_s24045, region [448,634,540,680] (pan) with water [402,660,456,800].
   The pan is grey, deep and held by a short handle; at 800 x 500 it looks like a saucepan, and pouring water from a saucepan onto flowers is something people really do, so a viewer may describe "a man watering flowers with a pot" without noticing anything wrong.
   Event: pan_watering (contradiction).
   Seen in: 1 of 1.
   Fix: make the object unmistakably a frying pan (shallow, wide, black, with a long handle) or swap it for an object that cannot hold water, such as a colander, a fork, or a tennis racket.

6. **Medium: the sleeping dog lies right across the sprinkler hose.**
   Picture T3_garden_s23016, region [440,902,564,970] with the hose [0,900,640,940].
   The dog is drawn lying on top of the hose that feeds the sprinkler; the same picture set uses "a dog standing on the hose blocks the water" as an inference (T3_garden_s23013), so a viewer may wonder whether the dog is meant to be blocking the water, while the sprinkler is in fact running.
   Event: dog_sleeping (event), sprinkler_package (inference).
   Seen in: 1 of 1 sprinkler picture.
   Fix: keep the sleeping dog clear of the hose (move the dog or route the hose behind or around it).

7. **Medium: the only duck is tiny and sits on the far rim of the pond, with the bank line cutting across its neck.**

   The duck is about 55 to 72 px wide (about 28 to 36 px at 800 x 500) and is placed against the far edge of the pond, so its head sticks up over the grass and the pond outline runs behind its neck; it is easy to miss and does not sit clearly in open water.
   In T3_park_s23066 the duck is the picture's only event, in an otherwise empty park.
   Event: ducks_swimming (event).
   Seen in: 2 of 2.
   Fix: place the duck in the middle of the pond, well inside the water, and draw it larger (or draw two or three ducks, as the template name says "ducks").

## Contradictions

- water_upward (T3_garden_s23048): the stream rises from the can and ends in drops above the flowers, which reads as water going up, but the stream leaves the spout sideways and is thin and pale over the flower colours, so some viewers may take it for a bent hose or a plant stake.
- fish_in_tree (T3_garden_s23051): an orange fish sits on a branch at the edge of the tree crown and reads at once as a fish where it should not be; it is small (84 px) but stands out against the dark green.
- pan_watering (T3_garden_s24045): it reads as watering with a saucepan, which a viewer may take as a normal if odd habit rather than something wrong (finding 5).
- floating_box (T3_garden_s25006): it reads as a box thrown or falling, possibly by the waving boy, rather than one hanging motionless (finding 1).
- dog_reading (T3_garden_s25023): the dog sits upright with glasses and holds an open newspaper in its paws; this is the clearest contradiction in the share and cannot be mistaken for a slip.
- square_wheel (T3_garden_s53703): the wheel is square, but it is so large and dark that it can be taken for a crate or grate standing beside a wheelless barrow (finding 4).
- ladder_nothing (T3_park_s24030): a free-standing slanted ladder is clear at full size, but its top meets the background hedge line and at reduced size it looks propped against the bushes (finding 3).
