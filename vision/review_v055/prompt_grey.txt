You are reviewing drawings for a picture-description test. Work only from this brief and the files it names.

BACKGROUND. The project in E:/Cognitive_Test_Scene_Creator generates cartoon scenes (1600 x 1000 pixels) that a person will be shown one at a time and asked to describe aloud, as in a clinical picture-description task. Each picture holds a few planned events: plain events (someone doing something), inferences (something the viewer must work out, such as a ball stuck in a tree), and deliberate contradictions (something impossible on purpose, such as a bicycle with square wheels or a dog reading a newspaper). The pictures are drawn in colour first, and a greyscale version is derived from each, because the test may be given in grey. A grey picture works when a first-time viewer still sees every event for what it is.

YOUR SHARE. The 21 grey pictures listed in E:/Cognitive_Test_Scene_Creator/tpdb_work/review56/list_grey.txt. Each line gives a grey picture's PNG (the colour picture is the same path without "_gray"), its record (a JSON file that lists the events under "templates", the scored statements under "items", and every drawn element with its bounding box under "elements"), and its events with their kind.

WHAT TO DO.
1. Look at every grey picture whole, at full size, before you read its record or see its colour version. Note what you take each thing to be, and what you cannot make out.
2. Then read its record and look closely at each event in grey: crop the region of its elements and view the crop enlarged two or three times (Python with Pillow is available; begin every Bash command with `cd /e/Cognitive_Test_Scene_Creator && source tools/env.sh`; save crops under tpdb_work/review56/crops_grey/). Also view the whole grey picture reduced to 800 x 500.
