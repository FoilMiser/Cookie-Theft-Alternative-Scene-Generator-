# Review p1: 26 pictures (16 garden, 8 park, 2 playground)

I looked at each picture whole at full size before reading its record, then read the record's templates, items and element boxes and viewed the region of every event cropped and enlarged 1.5 to 4 times (crops in tpdb_work/review56/crops_p1/).
I also viewed every picture reduced to 800 x 500, four to a sheet (sheet_small_0..6.png), to judge what reads at a normal viewing size.

## Findings, most severe first

1. High: the waiting dog's leash is only a short yellow ring and does not read as a leash.
   Pictures: T3_garden_s23001 [1410,700,1440,780]; T3_garden_s23010 [160,700,190,780].
   The dog has a small yellow loop hanging from its mouth, with no strap, clip or length of lead, so it looks like a ring toy or a rubber hoop rather than a leash; at 800 x 500 it is a yellow speck.
   Event: dog_wants_walk (items "dog ... with its leash in its mouth" and the inference "the dog wants to go for a walk" both depend on recognising the leash).
   Seen in: 2 of 2 pictures with the waiting dog.
   Fix: draw a proper leash in the mouth, a coloured strap of some length with a handle loop and a metal clip, with part of it trailing on the ground or step beside the dog.

2. High: the upward water from the watering can is drawn as a solid outlined pale tube and reads as a bent hose or pipe, not as water.
   Pictures: T3_garden_s23010 [422,568,516,712]; T3_garden_s25004 [388,658,490,816].
   The stream is a smooth, evenly thick tube with a dark outline running from the spout up and over, with three loose drops at the top; at 800 x 500 it looks like a pale hose looped up from the can, so a viewer may say "watering with a hose" and never notice that the water goes up; the ordinary watering in T3_garden_s23020 uses broken dashes and drops, which do read as water.
   Event: water_upward (contradiction notice and explain items).
   Seen in: 2 of 2 pictures with water_upward.
   Fix: draw the rising water in the same broken streaks and drops as normal pouring water, fanning upward from the spout, and drop the solid outline so it cannot read as a tube.

3. Medium: the paper bag held by the bird or duck feeder floats in front of the body and is held by no hand.
   Pictures: T3_park_s23041 [928,640,968,694]; T3_park_s40509 [596,740,636,798].
   In both pictures the brown bag hangs at waist or chest height between the arm and the torso while the visible hand is busy with bread or seed; no hand, fingers or arm are on the bag, so it looks pasted on.
   Events: ducks_bread (the man throwing bread) and feed_birds.
   Seen in: 2 of 2 pictures with the feed bag.
   Fix: put the bag in the far hand with fingers over its top edge, or tuck it under the near arm with the arm drawn over it.

4. Medium: the square wheel of the wheelbarrow is far too large and sits behind the tray, so it reads as a separate square frame or grate standing beside the barrow.
   Pictures: T3_garden_s51701 [962,854,1060,966]; T3_park_s51702 [1472,866,1570,966].
   The square wheel is about 95 px across, nearly as tall as the tray and about twice the diameter of the round wheel on the filler wheelbarrow in the other garden pictures; the whole wheelbarrow is also about 285 px long against about 180 px for the filler one; the tray overlaps the top of the square and only the axle arm joins them.
   Event: square_wheel (contradiction).
   Seen in: 2 of 2 pictures with square_wheel.
   Fix: keep the wheelbarrow at the filler size and draw the square wheel at the normal wheel size, in front of the tray side with the axle at its centre, so it reads as the wheel and its squareness is the only oddity.

5. Medium: the dog's foot is beside the hose bulge rather than clearly on top of it.
   Picture: T3_garden_s25021 [960,920,1030,955].

   Event: blocked_hose (items "the dog is standing on the hose" and the inference that the dog blocks the water).
   Seen in: 1 of 1.
   Fix: put a front paw squarely on the middle of the hose, draw the hose pinched flat under it, and swell the hose only on the reel side.

## Contradictions

- floating_box (T3_garden_s23001, T3_garden_s23020): reads at once as a cardboard box hanging in the sky with nothing under it; it could at most be taken for something thrown, and there is no thrower.
- water_upward (T3_garden_s23010, T3_garden_s25004): weak; the solid pale tube reads as a hose looped from the can, so the upward water may be missed (finding 2).
- sweep_fork (T3_garden_s23015, T3_garden_s23050): very clear; a giant silver table fork used as a broom over a dust pile, clearly deliberate.
- fish_in_tree (T3_garden_s23026): clear; an orange fish lying on a branch against the green canopy, the hooked trunk is the only distraction.
- sitting_on_air (T3_garden_s25021): clear; the woman sits drinking with bent knees and no seat under her, and the shadow is only under her feet; it could just possibly be taken for a squat.
- square_wheel (T3_garden_s51701, T3_park_s51702): noticeable but the oversized square behind the tray can be taken for a separate grate or frame (finding 4).
- ladder_nothing (T3_park_s25068): readable as a ladder standing alone, but its top touches the hedge line, so it can be taken as leaning on the bushes (finding 6).

## Looked for and found fine

- Feet on the ground: every standing figure has its feet on a shadow at ground level, and the jumping girl in T3_garden_s23026 has a detached shadow that makes the jump clear.
- Sitters: the bench readers in T3_park_s23041 and T3_garden_s23062, the girl in T3_park_s23701 and the seesaw riders all sit on their seats.
- Grips: the digger's spade, the fork sweeper's two hands, the broom sweepers, the paint rollers, the kite spools, the camera and the hose nozzle in T3_garden_s23043 are all held.
- Kite strings run unbroken from the kite to the spool in all three kite pictures.
- Faces: every face is visible and expressions fit their events (worried boy under dark clouds, startled man losing his cap, the calling girl on the seesaw, the dismayed balloon boy).
- The same figures, props and set pieces (watering can, wheelbarrow, house, fence, pond, bench, lamp post) are drawn alike across pictures, and the mirrored pictures are consistent.
- Inferences other than those in the findings read well: lost balloon, windy hat, rain coming, paint tracks, the sprinkler about to soak the package, the heavy seesaw end and the begging dog.
