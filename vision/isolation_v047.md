# v0.47 isolation bench

The brief for v0.47 was Sam's: "Go ahead and work on v0.47, addressing the flaws, and maybe focusing on one character, finding general anatomy flaws and defects and shortcomings, fix those up and make the character art cleaner and better, and apply that to the cast and animals, and refine weak actions too, to hopefully fix the screening". The flaws are the v0.47 list of `flaws_v046.md`: the four templates that failed v0.46's gate (R1 `dog_reading`, R2 `about_to_trip`, R3 `sweep_fork`, R4 `blocked_hose`), the two rows under them (R5 `bird_steals`; R6 `cat_stalks_bird`, `water_upward`, `lost_balloon`) and the oddities O1 to O7. The one character is the woman in the yellow dress (`person.mei`), whose neck was v0.46's brief. O3 (riders above their seat) was put on the bench and not changed, and O4 (figures over the pond) was not worked on; both are carried to `flaws_v047.md`.

This file has three parts: the study of v0.46's cast that found the faults of the drawing, the changes that came of the study and of the list, each behind a switch, and the five rounds of blind readings that judged them.

## The cast study

Before anything was changed, v0.46's cast was read as drawings. The woman in the yellow dress was rendered in 52 poses and angles (`tpdb_work/v047/study_mei.json`) and looked at on sheets of my own. Twenty-four of those figures were then put to three fresh reviewers, eight each, one figure to a picture on a plain ground, enlarged to 1160 px high (`isolation_v047/study_mei/r1`, `r2`, `r3`; `map_r1.txt` to `map_r3.txt` say which pose and angle each picture is): standing at five angles (seven figures), walking at three, running at two, sitting on a seat at two and on the ground, and nine actions (reaching up, sweeping, eating an ice cream, pointing, digging, walking with a box, waving, kneeling to plant and hanging washing). Each reviewer listed, figure by figure, whatever in the drawing looks wrong, awkward or unfinished, and closed with its top faults (`review_r1.md` to `review_r3.md`). Two more reviewers read the other nine people the same way, one figure of each (`study_cast/p1`, `p2`, `review_p1.md`, `review_p2.md`), and two read eight animal figures each: the two dogs, the two cats, the goat, a songbird and the duck (`study_cast/a1`, `a2`, `review_a1.md`, `review_a2.md`).

The faults named by two reviews or more are the cast's, not one figure's, and each became a switch (next section):
- shoulders: "Square coat-hanger shoulders", "a flat horizontal shelf right under the chin", "shoulder pads";
- arms and legs: "straight tubes of one width", "no elbows or wrists", "no knees or ankles", and of the trousers "The bottoms balloon over the shoes";
- hands: "mitten blobs with one curl and no thumb";
- shoes: "flat ovals or paddles with no toe, sole, heel or ankle", "a detached sole line";
- the skirt: "the skirt outline is torn where a leg exits", and seated "the thighs are missing";
- the face: the nose "an outlined ring", "a small ring stuck on the outline"; in a turned view "the far eye is clipped by the head outline", "the far eye sits right on the head outline and is half cut off";
- the animals: "A black crescent sits on the front edge of the head" (the eye, seen edge on in a side view), "Two eyes are drawn side by side on a head in side view", a coat's markings "each patch is a separately outlined blob that bulges beyond the body outline", legs as straight tubes on round feet, a white coat's grey far legs, the tail a straight stick.

## The bench

A case of the bench is one event forced alone on one seed in one setting (`vision/isolation_bench.py`): the picture is built whole at 1600 by 1000 and the event cut out of it. The first two letters of a case name the thing, the fourth the drawing, the number the seed. Every reader and reviewer was a fresh subagent on Opus that was given its prompt's text and nothing else, as every reader and scorer of this release is. There were five rounds:
- round 1: fifteen readers of 58 cut-outs, four to a reader (two readers had three), no reader with two takes of one thing (`isolation_v047/round1`, `tools/cycle/iso_round.py`). Eleven things, each in v0.46's drawing and in v0.47's on the same seeds: three seeds each of the dog on the hose (`hsy`), the fork sweeper (`sfy`), the reading dog (`rdy`), the walker about to trip (`tpy`), the bird at the sandwich (`bsy`), the stalking cat (`cby`) and the upward water (`wuy`), and two each of the lost balloon (`lby`), the fence painter (`pfy`), the seesaw (`ssy`) and the slide (`sly`). A reader writes "What I see" and "Unsure or ambiguous" for each cut-out. And four readers of eight whole tier-3 pictures in v0.47's drawing, two each, with the screen's whole-picture prompt (`whole1`; two each of the garden, the park, the street and the playground). And four figure reviewers of sixteen pairs, four each (`figs1`, `tpdb_work/v047/figpairs47.py`): one figure of the library as v0.46 rendered it and as v0.47 does, both enlarged by one factor on a plain ground, as "a" and "b" with the new drawing as "a" in half the pairs, with questions on the faults of each, the differences, the anatomy, the pose and which is better drawn;
- round 2: three readers of fifteen cut-outs, five each (`round2`): the three seeds of the hose, the fork, the reading dog, the sandwich bird and the stalking cat after round 1's changes. Two reviewers of four pairs, the new drawing against v0.46's (`figs2`: two pecking birds, two stalking cats), and one of two pairs, the new drawing against round 1's (`figs2r`: the reading dog, the dog on the hose);
- round 3: three readers of nine cut-outs, three each (`round3`): the reading dog, the sandwich bird and the stalking cat after round 2's changes. Two reviewers of four pairs against round 2's drawing (`figs3`: two pecking birds, the reading dog, a stalking cat) and two of four pairs, the pecking birds at two angles against v0.46's (`figs3o`);
- round 4: three readers of three cut-outs, one each (`round4`): the reading dog on its three seeds after round 3's change. One reviewer of one pair, the reading dog against round 3's (`figs4`);
- round 5, after the set was built: three readers of three cut-outs, one each (`round5`): the younger child's dropped ice cream, which a rule found late (`CONE47`) lets the generator draw. It has its own section at the end.

`map.json` in each folder says which picture is which case, and for a pair which variant it is, which of "a" and "b" is the newer drawing and which library the older one came from. The fourth letter O is v0.46: `BENCH_CAST=assets/cast/out046`, its library copied to `tpdb_work/v047/lib_O`, and every switch of revision 44 at O. N is v0.47's cast (`assets/cast/out047`, `assets/cast/lib047`) with every switch at the letter it then had; B or D in a case named for one rule is that rule's letter on the same cast. N was four casts in turn, three of them rebuilt in part (the runbook's "a bench cast rebuilt in part is two casts": what was rebuilt is named here):
- round 1: every family built with the cast's switches as listed below, `TPDB_READ47`, `TPDB_HOSE47` and `TPDB_PECK47` at B and `TPDB_STALK47` at O. Rosa had been built a second time before the round, for her glasses;
- round 2: the two dogs, the two cats and the two songbirds built again (`TPDB_READ47` C, `TPDB_HOSE47` D, `TPDB_PECK47` C, `TPDB_STALK47` B). Round 1's renders of those six families are kept in `tpdb_work/v047/r1_lib047_animals`;
- round 3: the same six built again (`TPDB_READ47` D, `TPDB_PECK47` D, `TPDB_STALK47` C). Round 2's renders are kept in `r2_lib047_animals`;
- round 4: the two dogs built again (`TPDB_READ47` E). Round 3's renders of them are kept in `r3_lib047_dogs`.

The ten people, the goat and the duck are round 1's files in every round and in the release.
