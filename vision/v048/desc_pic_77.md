# pic_77

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left (blue door, red roof), a tall wooden fence with a gate on the right, and a paved path. On the front left a woman in a yellow dress sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. In the middle a woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds a grey watering can over a flowerbed, but the water from the spout arcs upward and backward into the air (with drops at the top) instead of pouring down onto the flowers, which looks wrong. In the front centre-right a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a string reel up and flies an orange diamond kite with a bow tail high in the sky. An empty wheelbarrow sits on the grass in front, and two potted plants and a bush stand by the fence. A tree rises behind the house.
