## Free description
This is a sunny back garden next to a cream house with a red roof and a blue door, with a tall wooden fence and a garden gate at the right. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands by the fence on the right, painting it blue with a long-handled paint roller. A few fence boards next to him are already blue, while the rest of the fence is still plain wood. A small paint tin with blue paint sits on the grass by his feet. In the front centre an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt is raking up a small pile of brown leaves. A blue bird flies across the sky. A teal watering can sits on the lawn at the front right, near a paved path leading to the gate. A flower bed with colourful flowers is in front of the house.
