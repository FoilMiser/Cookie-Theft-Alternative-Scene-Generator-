# pic_86

## Free description
A sunny, windy back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. White wind lines blow across the garden, and leaves blow off the tree. An elderly woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt stands by the fence and reaches up toward an orange cap that is blowing away above her. A boy in a red cap, blue top and dark trousers kneels at the flowerbed in front, digging with a small trowel next to a pot with a pink flower, as if planting it. Strangely, an orange goldfish is sitting in the branches of the tree behind the house, which is impossible. A grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can stand on the lawn.
