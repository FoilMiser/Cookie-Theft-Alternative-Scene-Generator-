## Free description
A back garden under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds, so it looks like rain is coming. At the back left, a woman in a purple top sweeps leaves with a broom, an older woman with glasses and grey bun rakes leaves into a small pile, and an older man in a cap and brown waistcoat digs with a spade next to a mound of soil; a birdhouse on a pole stands between them. In the middle back, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts points up at the dark clouds. In the centre a man in a green shirt holds a garden hose nozzle and looks at it, but no water is coming out, because a white spotted dog is standing on the hose further along it. On the right, a grey cat is creeping toward a small red bird that is pecking at seeds on the grass. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sits on a bench reading a red book, while a blue bird beside him on the bench pecks at his sandwich without him noticing. At the front left, an orange cat is curled up asleep on the path and a brown dog crouches in a play bow, wagging its tail, next to a tennis ball.
