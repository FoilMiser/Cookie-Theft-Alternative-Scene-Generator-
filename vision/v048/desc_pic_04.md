## Free description
A sunny town square paved with grey tiles, in front of a row of colourful shops with striped awnings, a tree in a planter and a lamp post. On the left a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is scattering seeds from a paper bag to two small birds, one red and one blue, which are pecking at the ground. In front of him a man in a red shirt and blue trousers is sitting and reading a red book — but there is no bench or chair under him; he is sitting on thin air. In the centre an older man in a cap and brown waistcoat is walking quickly while reading an open book, and a cardboard box sits on the ground in front of him; he looks about to trip over it. At the back a worker in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest is watering a planter of flowers with a watering can. On the right a girl in a pink dress stands by a fruit stall holding an empty cone and rubbing her eye as if crying, because her pink ice cream has fallen on the ground, and a brown dog is licking up the spilled ice cream. A potted plant sits in the bottom right corner.
