# pic_28

## Free description
A sunny park with a dark railing fence, bushes behind it, a tree on the left and a red-and-yellow slide on the right. A man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers sits on the low end of a red seesaw, reading an open book and paying no attention. A small girl in a pink dress sits on the raised high end of the seesaw, stuck up in the air; she holds the handle and has her hand to her mouth, looking worried or as if calling out. Because the man is heavy and still, the girl cannot come down. A green rubbish bin stands by the fence in the background, and a grey rock lies on the grass at the front right. Nobody is using the slide.
