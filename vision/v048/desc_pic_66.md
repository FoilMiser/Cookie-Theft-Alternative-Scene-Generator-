# pic_66

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, a gate and paved path on the right. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is standing by a flower bed pouring water onto the flowers, but he is using a frying pan instead of a watering can, which looks odd. At the back right, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; a few fence boards near the gate are already blue. A blue paint tray and a paint tin sit on the grass near him. A white dog with brown spots is walking away from the paint tray and leaving a trail of blue paw prints on the grass, so it has stepped in the paint. In the front, a small brown puppy is lying asleep on the grass, and there is a wooden bench in the front left corner.
