A sunny back garden with the same cream house and blue door on the left, a wooden fence with a gate, and a paved path on the right. An older man in a cap and brown waistcoat is kneeling by the flower bed, digging with a small trowel next to a small pot holding a pink flower, as if about to plant it. By the house door, a brown dog with a red collar sits holding a yellow lead in its mouth, as if asking to be taken for a walk. In the tree behind the house, an orange fish sits on a branch, which is impossible — fish do not live in trees. A wheelbarrow and a teal watering can stand on the grass, and there are two potted plants and bushes near the fence.
