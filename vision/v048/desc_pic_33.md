# pic_33

## Free description
A sunny park with a curving sand-coloured path on the left, a pond on the right, a tall black lamp post and a green bin in the middle, and trees and bushes around the edges. An older man in a grey flat cap and brown waistcoat is raking a small pile of leaves next to the path. In front of him, a small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands on the path holding an empty ice-cream cone and rubbing his eye, as if he is crying. Just below him a brown dog with a red collar is licking up a pink puddle of melted ice cream on the path, so the boy seems to have dropped his ice cream and the dog is eating it. In the bottom centre a lawn sprinkler connected to a hose is spraying arcs of water, and the water is landing on a cardboard box that is getting wet and sitting in a small puddle. On the right a girl in a purple top and teal trousers sits drinking from a red cup with a straw, but there is nothing visible under her, so she seems to be sitting on thin air. Next to her a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead.
