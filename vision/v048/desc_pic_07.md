# pic_07

## Free description
A sunny back garden with the cream house and blue door on the left, a wooden fence with a gate, and a paved path on the right. A woman with dark hair in a yellow dress stands by the flower bed tipping a teal watering can, but the stream of water rises upward in an arc from the spout and droplets come off the top, instead of pouring down onto the flowers — water going up is impossible. On the right, on the path, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping leaves with a broom. In the middle a washing line strung between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes, pegged up. A grey wheelbarrow stands on the grass and there are two potted plants by the fence.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water could be read as a spray rising before falling, but it clearly leaves the spout going upward and no water lands on the flowers — it looks like water defying gravity.
