## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a tall wooden fence, a wooden gate on the left, a paved path, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the middle, by the fence, a girl in a pink dress is holding the strings of a yellow balloon and a red balloon in one hand. Her other arm is stretched up toward a blue balloon that has floated away above the fence, its string trailing down, out of reach. She looks worried or upset, so she seems to have just let the blue balloon go. On the right, a brown dog with a red collar sits beside the house door holding a yellow leash in its mouth, as if asking to be taken for a walk. In the front middle, a small white puppy with brown patches is lying on the grass asleep. There is a teal watering can on the lawn, two potted plants, a few bushes and a round flower bed full of colourful flowers.
