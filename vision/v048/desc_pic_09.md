# pic_09

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a stone path running down the right side to a gate. An older man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat is painting the fence blue with a paint roller; about three planks are already blue. A paint bucket and a paint tray of blue paint sit on the grass behind him. A line of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray to a white dog with brown spots, whose feet are blue, so the dog seems to have stepped in the paint and is walking away. In the front, a brown puppy wearing glasses is sitting up and reading a newspaper, which is odd for a dog. On the path at the right, a grey-haired older woman is walking along reading a book and not looking where she is going. A cardboard box sits on the path in front of her, so she looks likely to trip over it. There is also a bench, a flower bed and a birdhouse on a pole.
