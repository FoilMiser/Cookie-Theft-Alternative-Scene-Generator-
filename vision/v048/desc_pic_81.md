# pic_81

## Free description
A sunny, windy day in a park with a pond, a winding path, a bench and a lamppost. White wind lines streak across the sky and over the pond, and leaves are blowing off the tree on the right. Near the pond a woman in a yellow dress is reaching up with an open, surprised mouth as an orange cap flies away from her in the wind, with a few leaves tumbling along beside it. In the middle-right an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses and a teal cardigan bends over and sprinkles seeds from a paper bag for two small birds, one red and one blue, which are pecking at the ground. On the right, a girl in a purple top and teal trousers walks a brown dog on a red leash along the path. There is a flowerbed, a rubbish bin and a grey rock in the foreground. The cap looks about to be lost, perhaps landing in the pond.
