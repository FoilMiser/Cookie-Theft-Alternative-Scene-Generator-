# pic_78

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left, a wooden fence, a gate and a paved path on the right. In the middle an older man with grey hair, a grey cap and a brown waistcoat is in a sitting position drinking from a red cup with a straw, but there is no chair, bench or anything under him: he is sitting on thin air, which is impossible. Behind him a washing line between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel, pegged up. Further back a girl in a pink dress holds a yellow and a red balloon on strings and reaches up with her other hand toward a purple balloon that is floating away into the sky, so she seems to have just let it go. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands on the path flying a yellow diamond kite, holding the string reel up. In the front there is an empty wheelbarrow and a teal watering can, and a flowerbed sits in front of the house.
