## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence and gate on the left, a house with a blue door and red roof on the right, and a paved path down the left side. On the front left a woman in a yellow dress sweeps a small pile of dead leaves on the path with a broom. In the middle there is a metal swing set with one empty red swing. On the right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds a grey watering can over a flowerbed, but the stream of water rises upward from the spout into the air with drops at the top, instead of pouring down onto the flowers, which looks wrong. A birdhouse on a tall pole stands by the gate, with no bird visible. An empty wheelbarrow sits on the grass in the front, and an empty bench is by the house. There are no animals and no children.
