# pic_31

## Free description
A sunny, open park with rolling green hills, trees, bushes, a winding sandy path on the left and a tall street lamp in the middle. A man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers stands at the edge of a blue pond, holding a fishing rod with both hands. His line hangs down into the water, ending in a red-and-white float with a small ripple around it. Reeds grow at both ends of the pond. A grey rock sits near the front, and a green rubbish bin stands in the bottom right corner. He is the only person, and nothing else seems to be happening; he appears to be waiting for a fish to bite.
