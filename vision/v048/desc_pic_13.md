# pic_13

## Free description
A back garden under a grey sky with dark storm clouds. An older man in a cap and brown waistcoat is walking across the back carrying a cardboard box. In the middle front, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is holding the nozzle of a garden hose and looking at it, but no water is coming out. The reason seems to be that a white spotted dog is standing on the hose, squashing it, between the hose reel and the boy. A grey-haired older woman is waving her hand up in the air. A woman in a yellow dress is sweeping a pile of leaves with a broom. On the path at the right, a small child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is pointing up at the sky, perhaps at the storm clouds.
