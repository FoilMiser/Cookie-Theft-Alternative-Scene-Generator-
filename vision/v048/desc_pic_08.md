## Free description
A sunny park playground with a railing fence, a slide, a swing set with one empty red swing, a green litter bin, a bench and a tree. On the far left a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers is walking while carrying a cardboard box. On the bench an older man in a cap and brown waistcoat is eating a pink ice cream, and a small white dog is sitting up on its hind legs in front of him, begging for it. In the front centre a brown dog is crouched in a play bow next to a yellow tennis ball, wagging its tail, wanting to play. On the right a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is flying a yellow kite, holding the string on a reel; the kite is high in the sky and a red bird flies nearby. In the front there is a sandpit with a red bucket and spade, a grey rock, and a red bicycle whose wheels are square — which is impossible for riding.
