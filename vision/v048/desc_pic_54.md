# pic_54

## Free description
This is a sunny park with a winding sandy path on the left, a pond on the right, a lamp post, trees, bushes, a rock and a green bin. A man in a green shirt and beige trousers walks across the grass just beside the path, holding a pink ice-cream cone up to his mouth. At the pond's front edge a girl with a dark ponytail in a purple top and teal trousers holds a brown paper bag and tosses bits of bread towards the water. A white duck swims close to her, pecking at the crumbs floating on the water, with little ripple lines behind it. The duck looks like it is about to eat more crumbs. Nothing looks impossible.
