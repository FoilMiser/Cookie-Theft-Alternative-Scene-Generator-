On the left a woman in a yellow dress holds a garden hose nozzle and looks at it, but no water is coming out. A brown dog with a red collar is standing on the hose in the centre, which would explain why no water flows. On the right, an older man in a cap and brown waistcoat sits on a bench reading a red book, while a blue bird on the bench next to him pecks at his sandwich without him noticing. Two potted plants and a flower bed decorate the garden.
