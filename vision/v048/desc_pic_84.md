## Free description
A busy park on a sunny day, with a pond, a path, a bench and a lamppost, and several odd things happening. On the left, an elderly man in a cap and brown waistcoat is watering a flowerbed by pouring water out of a frying pan instead of a watering can. Next to him a brown dog wearing glasses sits up and reads a newspaper, which is impossible. On the pond a white duck swims toward some floating pieces of bread, with motion lines behind it. A small girl in a pink dress stands at the edge of the pond holding something small, perhaps the bread she has been throwing. In the centre front, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is crying and rubbing an eye while holding an empty cone; the pink ice cream has fallen on the ground, and a white dog with brown spots is licking it up. In the back, a white goat with a rope tied to a stake grazes on a tuft of grass, and an elderly woman with glasses sweeps a pile of leaves with a broom. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue top holds a kite reel, but his purple kite is stuck in the tree. A blue bird flies across the sky.
