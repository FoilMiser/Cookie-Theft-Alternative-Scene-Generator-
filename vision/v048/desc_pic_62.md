## Free description
This is a playground on a sunny day, with the sun at top right, a metal railing fence and hedges behind. On the left there is a red seesaw. A small girl in a pink dress with yellow shoes sits up high on the raised left end, with a hand at her mouth, looking a bit worried or shy. On the low right end, a man with dark skin in a green t-shirt and khaki trousers sits reading an open book, not looking at the girl; his weight is holding the seesaw down so the girl is stuck up in the air. In the front centre, a woman with long dark hair in a yellow dress stands eating a pink ice-cream cone. The swing set and slide in the background are empty. There is a green bin by the fence and a grey rock at the front right.
