# pic_02

## Free description
A sunny park with rolling hills, trees, bushes, a winding sandy path on the left, a tall black lamp post in the middle and a pond with reeds on the right. A girl with dark hair in a yellow dress and red shoes is walking up the path away from the viewer's front, eating or about to lick a pink ice-cream cone. In the front centre a white goat with small horns and a red collar is grazing on a tuft of grass; it is tied by a rope to a short stake in the ground. A green litter bin stands in the bottom right corner. Nothing dramatic seems about to happen; it is a calm scene. The girl's feet are right at the edge of the path, partly on the grass.
