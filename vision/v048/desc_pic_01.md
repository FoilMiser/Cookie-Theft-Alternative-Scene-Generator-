# pic_01

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a paved path, and a cream house with a blue door on the right. An older man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat stands at the back right painting the fence blue with a paint roller; a section of the fence beside him is already blue. On the ground near his feet are a paint tin and a paint tray with blue paint in it. A small white dog with brown spots is walking away from the paint tray, and a line of blue paw prints leads from the tray to the dog, whose paws are blue — it has clearly stepped in the paint and is tracking it across the lawn. The painter does not seem to have noticed. On the left a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping a small pile of leaves with a broom next to the path. There are two potted plants, a flower bed with colourful flowers, a watering can and a wheelbarrow on the lawn. The dog may be about to walk paint further around the garden.
