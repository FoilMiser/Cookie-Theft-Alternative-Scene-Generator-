# pic_76

## Free description
A sunny park with a pond on the right, a lamppost, a tree on the left and a sandy path. On the far left a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of the tree. An older man with grey hair, a grey cap and a brown waistcoat pushes a teal pram along the path; a baby with a pink blanket lies in the pram. In the middle a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie waves his hand high, with small motion marks beside it. On the right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers kneels by a flowerbed planting flowers with a trowel, with a pot of pink tulips next to her. In the front a garden sprinkler on a hose sprays arcs of water that land on a cardboard box, which is getting soaked and has a puddle under it. There is an empty bench and a green bin. Nothing looks physically impossible, though the box being sprayed seems to be an accident about to ruin it.
