## Free description
A sunny town street with a row of coloured shop buildings with striped awnings, a paved square, a street lamp, a bench and a tree. On the left a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands with his hand on his hip and a metal bucket upside down on his head, looking puzzled; beside him is a fruit-and-vegetable stall. Next to him a brown dog wearing glasses sits up on its hind legs reading a newspaper, which is impossible. An old woman in a teal top and grey skirt waters flowers in a window box with a watering can. Further back, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty cone and covers their face as if crying, while a white spotted dog licks the dropped pink ice cream off the ground. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie scatters seeds from a bag, and a blue bird pecks at them by his feet. A beach ball (blue and white) is stuck up in the tree, and a small girl in a pink dress points up at it, while a woman in a purple top sweeps leaves with a broom. In the front, a grey cat creeps towards a red bird that is pecking seeds on the ground.
