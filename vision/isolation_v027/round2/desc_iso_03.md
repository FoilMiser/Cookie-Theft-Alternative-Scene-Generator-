## What I see
An older man with grey hair, wearing a grey flat cap, a white long-sleeved shirt, a brown waistcoat, dark trousers and brown shoes, stands on green grass in front of a wooden fence. He is holding a teal watering can out in front of him at waist height and pouring water from its long spout. A stream of blue, dashed water lines arcs down over a round bed of brown soil planted with tall red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers. He is watering the flowers.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water stream is drawn as many short blue dashes that overlap the flower stems and blooms, so in the middle it is hard to tell which marks are water and which are flower parts. The water seems to pass in front of the flowers and end in droplets at the soil level.
- The can is held close against his body and his hands are bunched at its handle, so I cannot clearly separate the hands from the can or see exactly how he grips it.
- The can is quite small relative to him and its spout is long and thin; it reads clearly as a watering can, though the rose at the spout tip is only a small shape.
- His face is seen from the side with a large cheek and small features, and the head looks slightly tilted down and turned; his body is narrow and long-legged, with both feet close together and pointing the same way. The proportions are a little stretched but not broken.
