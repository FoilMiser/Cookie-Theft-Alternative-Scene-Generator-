# iso_12

## What I see
An older man with grey hair and a grey-blue flat cap stands on green grass in front of a tall wooden plank fence, with a rounded green bush at the left edge. He wears a white long-sleeved shirt, a dark brown vest or waistcoat, dark grey trousers and brown shoes. He is holding a teal watering can out in front of him at waist height and tilting it, and a stream of light blue water falls from its spout onto a round bed of brown soil. The bed holds a group of tall, brightly coloured flowers (red, yellow, purple, pink and orange) on green stems. He is watering the flowers.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The watering can's shape is hard to read: its body sits right against his hands, and a thin teal spout extends to the right and behind his legs' line before the water appears on the flower side. The water seems to start from the left of the can, near the orange and yellow flowers, so the spout and the water's starting point do not obviously line up. It could be read as water coming from the spout's rose or as water coming from somewhere near the can's body.
- The thin teal piece to the right of the can could be read as the spout or as a handle; it points away from the flowers, which looks odd if it is the spout.
- The water stream is drawn as a band of many small blue dashes that passes in front of the flowers and ends in droplets above the soil, among the stems, rather than clearly reaching the ground.
- The front of his vest has an irregular, jagged edge against the white shirt, which looks a little patchy, as if the shirt and vest shapes overlap unevenly. Only one arm is clearly visible, and the hands holding the can are small and hard to make out.
- His legs are very close together and slightly bowed, and his feet overlap, so the stance looks narrow, and it is hard to separate the two legs and shoes.
