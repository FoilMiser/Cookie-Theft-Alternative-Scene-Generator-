## What I see

An older woman with grey hair in a bun and round black glasses, wearing a teal long-sleeved top, a grey-blue skirt and brown shoes, is walking to the left on green grass. She holds an ice cream cone with a pink scoop up close to her mouth, as if about to lick or eat it. Behind her is an oval blue pond with a dark outline and two brown-topped reeds or cattails at its left end. A grey rock is at the lower left corner, and small dark green and dark shapes (cut off by the frame) appear at the upper left and right edges.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The shapes cut off at the edges are hard to name. The green one at the upper left could be a bush or tree, and the dark one at the right edge could be another rock or part of an object.
- The grey shape at the lower left is most likely a rock, but only part of it shows.
- The pond is a very flat, hard-outlined oval that looks like a sticker laid on the grass, and the reeds stand inside it near the edge rather than on the bank. It could be read as a pond seen from an odd angle or as a flat cut-out shape.
- The glasses sit toward the front edge of her face and stick out a little past the profile, so they look slightly detached from the head.
- A small white patch shows at her chest and waist beside the teal top. It could be a white shirt or scarf underneath, or a gap in the shape.
- Her skirt is a rather boxy, lumpy shape, and her thin legs seem to join it abruptly with no clear hip, so the lower body looks a little oddly attached.
