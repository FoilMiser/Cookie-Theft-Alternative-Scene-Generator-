## What I see

An older woman with grey hair in a bun and round glasses, wearing a teal long-sleeved top and a blue-grey skirt, stands on grass at the edge of a beige paved path. She holds a long-handled broom with both hands and is sweeping a small pile of autumn leaves (yellow, orange, red and brown) on the path. Behind her is a wooden slatted fence with a wooden gate or door at the top right. A terracotta pot with a green plant stands at the left, and a teal watering can is partly visible at the bottom left corner.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Her legs are hard to read: only one clear pale leg shows below the skirt, with one dark shoe, and the second leg is hidden by the broom handle and bristles or missing. She could be standing with her legs together or look one-legged.
- The dark round mark on the wooden panel at the top right could be a door knob or a knot/hole in the wood. The diagonal brace suggests a gate, but it could also be read as a shed door.
- The broom head overlaps her foot area, so the bristles seem to sit very close to or on top of her shoe.
- The leaves form a tidy heap with fairly simple, similar shapes. They could also be read as fruit or sweets at a glance, though the colours and the broom suggest leaves.
- Her face is drawn in a turned profile with the glasses pushed towards the side of the head, which makes the eye and glasses area look a little odd.
- Her arms are thin and the hand holding the lower part of the handle sits away from her body, which looks slightly stiff but not detached.
