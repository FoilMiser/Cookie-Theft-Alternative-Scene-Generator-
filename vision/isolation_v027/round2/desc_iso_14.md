## What I see
An older man with grey hair, wearing a grey flat cap, a white long-sleeved shirt, a brown waistcoat, dark trousers and brown shoes, stands on green grass by a wooden fence. He is holding a yellow hose nozzle out in front of him with one hand and spraying an arc of pale blue water down onto a round bed of red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers. A thick dark teal garden hose runs from the nozzle down past his body, along the grass and round to a hose reel on a dark stand at the lower left. At the left edge there is part of a cream house wall with a blue door and a grey step, and a bit of a wooden bench or similar at the bottom left.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The hose seems to run from behind or beside his hand and straight down in front of his waist, and there is a small teal segment between the nozzle and the loop that looks not quite connected to the main curve of the hose. It could be one continuous hose or two pieces that meet at his hand.
- The water arc is a thick solid band that ends among the flowers with a few droplets. It looks a bit like a solid curved blue tube or ribbon rather than loose water, and it passes in front of the flowers and an orange bloom.
- The round reel at the left could be read as a hose reel or as a wheel or a tyre on a box. The hose's join to it is a curled loop at the bottom, which looks slightly tangled.
- Only one forward arm clearly holds the nozzle; the hand and the nozzle are small and overlap, so the grip is hard to see.
- The wooden object at the bottom left is cut off by the frame, so I can only guess it is a bench.
