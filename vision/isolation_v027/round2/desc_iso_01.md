## What I see

An older man with grey hair, a blue-grey flat cap, a white long-sleeved shirt, a brown vest and dark trousers is walking to the left along a paved sidewalk. He holds a plain yellow shopping bag by its dark handles, hanging from the hand at his side. Behind him are the bottom edges of shop fronts (windows and a door) and a dark post with a square base at the upper left, probably a lamp post or sign pole. He has a soft oval shadow on the ground under his feet.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The bag is blank, so I cannot tell what is in it or which shop it came from.
- The dark post is cut off at the top, so it could be a lamp post or a sign pole.
- His torso looks oddly narrow and flat. The white sleeve and shirt read as one long vertical strip down the front, with the vest behind it, so it is hard to separate shirt, arm and vest.
- A thin white edge runs down the front of his chest and looks slightly separate from the body, as if the shirt were a flat panel. It could be read as a shirt front or as a stray piece of the shape.
- The arm seems to hang straight down from the shoulder with almost no visible join, and the hand sits close to the waist while the bag covers his thighs.
- The legs are heavily overlapped behind the bag, and the rear leg bends sharply at the knee, so the walking pose is a little hard to follow.
