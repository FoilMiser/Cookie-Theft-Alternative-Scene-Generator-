## What I see
A woman with dark hair tied back, wearing a purple T-shirt and teal trousers, stands on a paved sidewalk holding a long wooden-handled broom with both hands. She is looking down and sweeping a small pile of orange, red, yellow and brown autumn leaves with the broom's yellow bristles. Behind her is a building front with a teal door and a large window, and to the right a neighbouring shop with a green-and-white striped awning and another window. A teal bicycle is parked at the left against a grey rack or railing, and a dark planter box of red, yellow, pink and orange flowers sits on the pavement at the right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The bicycle is cut off at the left edge, so only the rear part is visible. The grey curved shape next to it could be a bike rack or a low railing running along the wall.
- The white section of the awning could be a blank shop sign or part of the awning itself.
- The broom head lies almost flat and appears to sit partly under or merged with the woman's feet and the leaf pile; her feet are hard to separate from the brown strip of the broom head, so it could read as her standing on the broom.
- The legs are very long and narrow compared with the upper body, and the trousers taper to almost nothing at the ankles, which looks slightly odd.
- The leaves sit on top of and just beside the bristles, so it could be either leaves being pushed or leaves resting on the broom.
- The grey band running along the base of the walls could be a kerb, a ledge or a step; it passes behind the bicycle and the planter.
