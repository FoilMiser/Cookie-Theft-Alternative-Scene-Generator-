## What I see

An older man with grey hair, wearing a blue-grey cap, white long-sleeved shirt, brown vest and dark trousers, stands on a lawn in front of a wooden fence. He is holding a garden hose by its yellow nozzle and watering a round bed of colourful flowers (red, yellow, purple, pink, orange). A light-blue arc of water leaves the nozzle and falls onto the flowers, with drops at its end. The dark teal hose runs along the grass from his hand back to a round hose reel on the left. To the left is the corner of a cream building with a blue door, and at the far bottom-left edge is a partly cut-off wooden post or small piece of brown structure.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The brown object at the bottom-left edge is cut off; it could be a fence post, a signpost, a stand or part of a railing. I cannot tell which.
- The hose rises vertically from the ground up to the man's hand in a narrow loop beside his leg, and the section just below his hand looks like a short thin stub joined to the thicker hose, so the connection between the hose and the nozzle looks slightly abrupt. The nozzle he holds points left while the hose comes up from below, so the join looks a bit odd.
- The water arc looks very thick and solid, like a pale blue ribbon or tube, rather than a spray. It could be read as a stream of water or as a second, lighter hose.
- Among the orange and yellow flowers near the top of the water arc, there is a small round shape with a darker outline that could be a flower centre or a small separate object such as an insect. It is hard to make out.
- His arm appears quite long and straight, and the hand holding the nozzle is small. His face is turned to the left in profile with the head tilted down.
- The door's threshold (grey step) sits on the grass and sticks out past the building's wall. The hose reel sits on the grass with no visible connection to the building or a tap.
