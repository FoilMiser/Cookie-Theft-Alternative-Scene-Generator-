## What I see

A person with short dark hair and a yellow knee-length dress with a thin brown belt and red shoes stands on green grass, in front of a blue pond edged with a dark outline and two cattails (bulrushes) at its far right. She holds a small cone with a round pink scoop up to her mouth with one hand, apparently eating or licking an ice cream; her eyes look closed and her cheek is pink as if enjoying it. Her other arm hangs at her side. A grey rock sits on the grass at the lower right.

- The pink round object on a small tan base reads as an ice cream cone, but it is so small and close to the face that it could also be a flower or a lollipop being smelled or tasted. Both eating and sniffing are possible readings.
- The expression could be enjoyment with eyes shut, or the closed eyes could suggest sleepiness; the marks around the eye and mouth are small and hard to read.
- The short hair could suit a woman, a girl or a boy in a dress; the age and gender are not fully clear.
- The body looks somewhat odd: the arm bent up to the mouth seems to overlap the shoulder and chest in a chunky way, the shoulders of the dress look boxy with angular sleeves, and the hanging arm's hand looks like a small rounded stub with a separate-looking wrist band or joint.
- The legs are long and thin relative to the rounded, barrel-like dress, and they stick straight out of the hem like separate sticks.
- The cattails appear to be standing at the very edge of the pond, overlapping its outline, with their stems seeming to start in the water and with no clear base.
- The rock at the lower right is cut off by the frame edge, so its full shape is unknown.
