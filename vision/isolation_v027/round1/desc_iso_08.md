# iso_08

## What I see
A girl or young woman in a pink dress and yellow shoes stands on a green lawn, looking up and holding a thin dark string in her raised hand. The string curves up across the yard to a purple diamond-shaped kite with small bow-like tail pieces, so she appears to be flying a kite. The kite is in front of a large leafy tree behind a tall wooden plank fence. On the left is a cream house with a red roof and a blue door, and in the middle of the lawn is a round dirt flower bed with red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers, with a green bush beside the house. At the bottom right edge, part of a grey object with a dark wheel is cut off; it looks like a wheelbarrow.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The kite overlaps the tree: some of the tree's lighter leaf clumps are drawn over the kite, while the kite's outline is over the darker tree. It could be read as the kite being tangled or caught in the tree, or as a kite flying freely in front of it. The layering looks a bit odd either way.
- The string sags in a deep curve near the girl and arrives at her hand almost horizontally, which is slack for a kite in flight. It also passes in front of the fence and tree in a long sweep.
- Her raised arm and hand are small and bunched near her face, so it is hard to see exactly how she grips the string; it ends at a small dark knob at her hand.
- The girl's body is quite narrow and her pose is seen from the side and slightly behind; her waist looks pinched, and the two legs are close together and thin.
- The grey wheeled object at the right edge is mostly cut off, so wheelbarrow is a best guess; it could be another kind of cart.
- The kite tail pieces are small and read as either little bows on a tail or two tiny butterfly-like shapes.
