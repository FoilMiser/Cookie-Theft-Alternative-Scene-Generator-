- The vertical post at the top could be a fence post, a washing-line post or a pole of some other kind, because it ends in a rounded tip above the grass and does not clearly reach the ground; it looks as if it is floating or hanging down from the top edge.
- The pale shape in the bottom-left corner is too cut off to identify; it could be the edge of a path.
- The arm holding the book looks odd: the forearm passes straight across the front of the book and the hand sticks out past its right edge as a small rounded lump, so the hand does not seem to grip the book. The other hand is not clearly visible, so the book seems to rest against his chest and arm rather than being clearly held.
- The neck and head join looks slightly off: the head sits tilted forward with a small separate-looking neck piece below the ear.
- The legs are long and thin for the body, and the waist narrows sharply at the belt, so the proportions look a little stretched.
