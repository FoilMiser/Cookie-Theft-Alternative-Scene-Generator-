## What I see

A backyard garden: a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a tall wooden plank fence across the middle, a large green tree behind the fence, and a round bed of colourful flowers on the lawn. A girl with dark hair, a pink dress and yellow shoes stands on the grass at the lower right, facing away from the viewer or to the left, with one arm raised, holding a long string. The string curves up across the lawn and fence to a purple diamond-shaped kite with small bow-shaped tail pieces, so she seems to be flying a kite. At the lower right edge, part of a grey object with a dark wheel is cut off; it looks like a wheelbarrow.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The kite overlaps the tree: its upper-left corner sits behind or among the leaf clumps, so it could be read either as a kite caught in the tree or as a kite flying in front of or beside it. Some leaf clumps are drawn on top of the kite while the rest of the kite lies over the foliage, so the depth order looks muddled.
- The string runs in a low slack curve, sagging down near her hand before rising, which looks loose for a kite in the air. It also passes in front of the fence and ends at the kite with no clear attachment point.
- The girl's body looks oddly joined: her pink dress appears as two separate blobs (a narrow upper torso and a lower skirt) with a pinched gap at the waist, and the torso is very thin.
- Her head direction is hard to read. The head is mostly a dark hair circle with a small round shape on it (could be a hair bun, an ear or a clip), and only a sliver of face shows, so she could be looking up at the kite or turned away.
- The grey object at the right edge is cut off, so wheelbarrow is a guess; it could also be some other wheeled cart.
