## What I see

A sunny back garden next to a cream house with a red roof and a blue door, enclosed by a wooden fence with a gate at the right. In the middle, a boy or young man in a red T-shirt and blue trousers stands holding a long rake and raking a small pile of leaves on the grass. Further back on the right, a second figure in a red cap, blue hooded top and dark trousers is sweeping a pile of orange and yellow leaves with a yellow-bristled broom, next to a paved path. Around them are a washing line with blue jeans and a yellow striped towel pegged to it, a raised flower bed with colourful flowers, a hose reel by the bed, two potted plants, and a teal watering can on the grass. There is a tree behind the fence corner, hedges beyond the fence, two clouds and a smiling-free yellow sun with orange rays.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The dark teal object by the flower bed reads as a coiled garden hose on a reel, but it could also be read as a wheel, or a speaker or machine on a small base. No hose runs from it to anything; it ends in a short curl.
- The rake head is small and grey and sits on top of the man's foot area, close to his shoe, so it is a bit hard to see where the rake ends and the shoe begins. The leaves under it are mostly greenish and brown and could be read as leaves or scattered small plants.
- The man's rake handle passes in front of his body and one arm in a way that makes it hard to tell exactly how both hands hold it.
- The second figure's broom sits at the very edge of the path and the leaf pile overlaps the path, so the leaves could be on the grass or the path. Their feet are mostly hidden behind the broom and leaves, with a white shoe just visible.
- The washing line posts stand on the grass but look very thin and tall, with the crossbars at the top; the line sags from one crossbar to the other. The left end of the line and the jeans' peg are close together, so the jeans look nearly attached to the post.
- The bush beside the house looks cut off by the house wall, sitting partly behind it, which looks a little abrupt.
- The door has a small grey step at the bottom, but no path leads to it; it opens directly onto the grass.
