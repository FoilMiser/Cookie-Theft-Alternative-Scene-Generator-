## What I see

A woman with dark hair tied back, wearing a purple T-shirt and teal trousers, stands on a paved pavement sweeping with a long wooden-handled broom that has a yellow bristle head. In front of her feet is a small pile of red, orange, yellow and brown leaves, which she is sweeping up. Behind her are shop fronts with a teal door and large windows, one with a green and white awning. A teal bicycle is parked at a metal rack on the left, and a planter box with red, yellow, pink and orange flowers stands on the right.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The pile of colourful shapes reads as autumn leaves, but it could also be read as scraps of litter; the pieces are quite bright and glossy.
- The broom head overlaps the pile and her foot area, so it is hard to tell if the bristles are touching the leaves or sitting in front of them. Only one of her feet is clearly visible, and the other seems hidden behind the broom and leaves.
- Her legs are very close together and look like one narrow column, which makes her stance slightly stiff.
- The bicycle is cut off at the left edge, and the frame of the rack blends into the bike frame, so it is hard to separate where the bike ends and the rack begins.
- Her hands grip the broom handle, but the top hand looks small and blob-like; the handle seems to pass in front of her body without a clear grip at the lower hand.
