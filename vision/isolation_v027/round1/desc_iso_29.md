He holds a long-handled broom or rake with a yellow fanned head in both hands and is sweeping a small pile of red, orange, yellow and brown autumn leaves. - The tool head overlaps the leaf pile, so it is hard to tell whether it is resting in front of the leaves or pushing into them. It is also close to the man's front foot.
- The legs are close together and hard to separate, so only one shoe is clearly visible; the second foot seems to be hidden behind the other leg or the broom.
- The dark round thing on the gate could be a knob or a keyhole or a knot in the wood. The strong diagonal line on the gate could be a brace or could read as the gate's edge or a shadow.
- A small dark and teal object is just visible at the bottom right corner, cut off by the edge, and I cannot tell what it is.
