# Picture 69

## Free description
This is a sunny park with a large blue pond with reeds, green hills behind, a winding sandy path on the right and a tall black lamp post in the middle. In the front, an old man in a blue-grey flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is walking to the left, pushing a teal pram. A baby lies asleep in the pram under a pink spotted blanket. On the grass near the bench, a small white and brown puppy is curled up asleep. Further back, a woman in a yellow dress is walking along carrying a cardboard box in front of her. On the path at the right, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands looking up with one arm raised, holding a kite string. The string goes up to a purple kite that is caught in the top of a large tree, with clumps of leaves over it, so the kite seems to be stuck. There is an empty wooden bench, a flower bed at the lower left, and a grey rock on the grass at the bottom. The pram is heading towards the flower bed and is already very close to it, so it may need to turn.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The kite could be read as flying behind the tree, or stuck in it. Leaf clumps overlap it, so I read it as stuck.
- The kite string is thin and runs close to the curved edge of the path, so part of it is hard to tell from the path outline.
- The woman's feet are on the grass beside the path, not on it, and she is quite large for how far back she seems to be. She could be walking on the grass or just off the path.
- The pram is almost touching the flower bed. It could be about to bump into it, or just be passing by.
- The boy's raised arm looks a bit stiff and his head is tilted far back, but it reads as looking up.
- The old man's head overlaps the pond behind him, which makes him look slightly like he is in front of the water, though his feet are on the grass.
