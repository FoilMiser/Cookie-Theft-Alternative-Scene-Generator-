# Picture 23

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with no people or ordinary animals in it. A cream house with a red roof and blue door stands on the left, a wooden fence runs across the back, and there is a gate in the far right corner with a paved path leading to it. The odd thing is in the tree behind the house: an orange goldfish is sitting on a curved brown branch among the green leaves, out of water, with its tail to the right. A fish in a tree is impossible in real life and this looks wrong for the scene. Everything else is ordinary: a flower bed with multicoloured flowers in front of the house, a pair of potted plants and bushes near the fence, and a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can left on the grass at the front. Nothing seems about to happen, since nobody is there; the garden looks like it was left mid-job or is simply waiting.

- No people are visible.

Objects:

- Flower bed (left of centre, front of the house): oval of brown soil with red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers.

- Bushes (one by the house, one near the pots): green background shapes.
- Wheelbarrow (bottom centre-right): grey tray, one black wheel, wooden handles; empty and unused.
- Watering can (bottom right of centre): teal can with a spout pointing left, standing on the grass, unused.
- Paved path (right): slabs from the bottom edge back to the gate.
- Fence and gate (back and back right): wooden fence and a darker wooden gate with a round knob.
- Sun (top right) and two clouds (top centre): sky.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The orange shape is most easily a goldfish, but it could also be read as a toy or ornament hung or placed in the tree. It has no visible string or stand, and it looks like it is simply lying on the branch.
- The branch and its small leaf tuft look drawn on top of the tree crown, more distinct and separate from the rest of the tree, so the branch itself looks a little pasted in.
- The fish's tail reaches right to the edge of the tree crown, so its back end almost hangs out against the sky.
