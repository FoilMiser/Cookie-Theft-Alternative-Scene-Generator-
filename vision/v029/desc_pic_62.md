# Picture 62

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path running to the lower right. A single person is in the picture: a woman or girl in a yellow dress and red shoes, with short dark hair. She is standing beside a round flower bed in front of the house, holding a teal watering can and tipping it towards the flowers. Several thin streams of water fall from the can's spout onto the flowers. She is looking down at what she is doing. Nothing seems about to happen and it is a calm, ordinary gardening scene. A grey wheelbarrow with a wooden handle stands empty on the lawn at the front, two pot plants stand on the grass near the fence, and there are a couple of bushes. I cannot see anything impossible in this picture.
