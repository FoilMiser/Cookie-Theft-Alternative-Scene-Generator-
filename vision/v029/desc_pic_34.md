# Picture 34

## Free description
This is a busy sunny park or playground on green grass with a dark railing fence and bushes behind it. In the middle, a boy in a yellow shirt holds a red and a yellow balloon in one hand and stretches his other arm up towards a purple balloon that has just escaped, its string just above his fingers. A man in a green shirt beside him is taking a photo with a camera, aiming up and to the left. Behind them on a bench, a man in a red shirt reads a red book while a blue bird beside him stands right next to his sandwich, so it may be about to steal it. In the front left, a woman in a yellow dress is running to the left with speed lines behind her, past an empty sandbox with a red bucket and blue spade in it. In the front middle stands a red bicycle with square wheels, which is impossible to ride. A white dog with brown patches bows down next to a tennis ball with its tail wagging, as if inviting play. On the right, a small girl in a pink dress holds a long string going up to a purple kite that is stuck in a tree, and at the far right a brown dog in a red bandana sits up holding an open newspaper as though reading it, also impossible. A red bird flies across the sky, and a slide stands empty at the back.

- The bicycle's square wheels are clearly wrong. They could be an intended impossibility or just an odd drawing, but they look deliberate. It stands up on its own, apparently on a small stand.
- The man's camera could be aimed at the balloons, at the boy, or at the red bird; it points left and upward, and there is no clear subject.
- The woman's running has no obvious reason; she could be chasing something off the left edge or just out jogging. She does not look at anything in the scene.
- The purple balloon could have just slipped from the boy's hand or he could just be reaching for one that came from elsewhere. The string seems to hang freely.
- The girl with the kite could be flying it or trying to free it; it looks caught because leaf clumps are drawn over it, and the string looks fairly slack near her hand.
- The blue bird could be simply perched near the sandwich or about to steal it.
- The brown dog's pose looks odd: long body, stuck-out hind legs, long tail, a bit like a kangaroo. A dog reading a newspaper is impossible.
