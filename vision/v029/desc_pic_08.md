# Picture 08

## Free description
This is a sunny playground on a large lawn, with dark metal railings and hedges along the back, a slide on the left, a swing frame in the middle and a sandpit at the front left. A white dog sits up on the grass next to the sandpit holding an open newspaper as if reading it, which is impossible. In the front centre a child with dark curly hair, a yellow top and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone with a hand up to their head, looking towards a pink ice cream with sprinkles splattered on the ground. A brown dog with a red collar has its nose down at the splat, probably licking or eating it. On the right, an elderly person with grey hair and glasses, a teal top and grey skirt sits on a wooden bench sipping from a red cup through a straw. At far right, beside a tree, a small girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes holds a long string that rises across the sky to a yellow kite with a bow tail, flying high in the centre. A blue bird flies at the top right. The slide, the swing and the sandpit are all empty, with a red bucket and blue spade left in the sand.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The elderly person could be a woman or a man; the skirt suggests a woman, but the short hair at this angle is not clear. The garment could also be read as shorts.
- The child's hand on the head could mean upset, or just scratching; the empty cone makes upset more likely.
- The brown dog could be eating the ice cream or only sniffing it.
- The girl flying the kite looks small next to the elderly person on the bench, and could be read as a young child or as simply further away.
- The kite string runs close past the edge of the tree and across the railings, so it is thin and easy to miss.
- The dog reading the newspaper is plainly impossible; the newspaper looks large and is held with its paws.
- The bucket has no obvious owner, and nobody is near the sandpit except the dog, so the bucket and spade seem simply abandoned.
