# Picture 27

## Free description

This is a sunny paved town square or shopping street, with a row of four coloured buildings across the back, with striped shop awnings below. In the centre-right, a boy or young man in a red cap, blue hoodie, black trousers and white shoes is walking to the left while reading an open book, looking down at the pages. Directly in his way on the ground, a little to his left, is a closed cardboard box, so he looks about to trip over it or bump into it because he is not watching where he is going. On the right, an old man with grey hair, a flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands holding a camera up to his eye and taking a picture toward the left. On the bench in the middle, a small girl in a pink dress is sitting and drinking from a cup with a straw. At the lower left, a person with short dark hair in a yellow outfit and red shoes walks to the right carrying a yellow shopping bag. There is a bicycle parked in a rack, a tree in a round planter, a tall lamp post, a planter of flowers and, at the back right, a market stall with crates of fruit. Apart from the box in the walker's path, nothing looks wrong or impossible.
