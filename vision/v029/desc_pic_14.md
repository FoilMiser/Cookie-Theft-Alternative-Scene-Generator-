# Picture 14

## Free description
This is a sunny, windy back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left and a wooden fence across the back. On the right, a bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers stands with both arms stretched up and his mouth open, looking startled. His blue cap has just been blown off his head and is flying through the air just out of reach above his hands. White wind lines sweep across the middle of the picture from the left towards him, and a few leaves are blowing along with them; more wind lines appear in the sky and leaves are being stripped from the tree. In the lower middle, a sprinkler fed by a hose is throwing three arcs of water to the right, and the longest arc lands on a closed cardboard box sitting in a puddle, which is getting wet. A small white puppy with brown patches is curled up asleep on the grass in front of the sprinkler, not bothered by any of this. It looks like the cap will fly away over the fence unless the man catches it, and the box will be soaked if no one moves it.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The man could be reaching for his flying cap, or throwing his arms up in surprise or alarm. He may also have thrown it himself, though his shocked face and the wind lines point to the wind having taken it.
- He might also be reacting to the wet box next to him, but he is looking towards the cap area rather than down at the box.
- The sprinkler's arcs curve evenly and are not bent by the wind, even though the wind seems strong enough to take a cap. The clouds are also calm. This looks a little inconsistent, but could just be how it is drawn.
- The puppy is close to the sprinkler but no water is on it, and it stays asleep.
- It is odd that the sun is out in a strong wind and the thick white wind lines are drawn across the fence as if they were objects, though that reads as a normal cartoon convention.
