# Picture 67

## Free description
This is a sunny but windy back garden, with a cream house and blue door on the right, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate on the left, and a paved path at the lower left. White wind lines streak across the sky and across the fence, with little leaves blowing around. On the left, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers stands on the path with both arms up, mouth open, reaching towards a red cap that is blowing away; the cap is up against the gate, just above his head, so the wind seems to have just taken it off him. In the middle, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands facing the fence, painting it with a long-handled roller; a section of the fence is already bright blue, and a paint tin and tray are on the ground beside her. A white dog with brown patches and a blue collar is walking away from the paint tray towards the house, leaving a trail of blue paw prints on the grass; its paws look blue, so it must have stepped in the paint, and the woman has not noticed. Odder still, in the tree by the house there is a large orange goldfish sitting among the leaves, right where a thick branch should have been, and at the end of that branch there is only a small tuft of leaves. A fish in a tree is impossible, and looks like a deliberate oddity. Leaves are also blowing off the tree. A watering can and a grey wheelbarrow stand at the front of the picture, there are two pot plants and a flower bed, and nobody is using them.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fish is the strangest thing. It could be sitting on the branch like a bird, or could be floating or flying past the tree. I can see no reason for it to be there; it may be meant as an impossible detail for the viewer to spot.
- The tree's branch looks unusual, as though cut out of the green leaf mass, with the fish and branch drawn over the foliage, and the small tuft at the end of the branch looks detached from the rest of the crown.
- The red cap looks a little as if it is pinned or stuck to the gate rather than in the air, because it overlaps the gate so closely. It could be read as hanging on the gate or being blown past it. The man has no hat on, and his raised arms and open mouth suggest it was his.
- The man could also be read as throwing his hands up in surprise, or as trying to grab the cap; his hands are below the cap and he does not quite reach it.
- The woman could be painting with a roller or a brush; the head looks like a blue rectangular block standing on the fence, and it seems to go up beyond the handle in a slightly odd way. The painted section reaches right up to the top of the fence pickets.
- The dog's body is drawn fairly stiffly, and it is not clear whether it is walking away slowly or standing still, but the paw prints indicate it has walked from the tray.
- Whether the woman knows about the dog is unclear: she is facing away towards the fence, so probably not.
