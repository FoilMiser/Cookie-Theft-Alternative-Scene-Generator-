# Picture 45

## Free description

This is a sunny town square or pedestrian street in front of a row of four colourful shops, and it is busy with people and animals. Several things look wrong or odd. In the front centre, a red bicycle lying or standing on the pavement has square wheels. On the right, a brown dog with a red collar is sitting up on the ground reading a newspaper held in its front paws. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands in the front right, looking up and pointing at a blue and white striped beach ball stuck in the branches of a tree. In the middle, a man in a green shirt sits on a bench reading a red book, and a blue bird on the bench beside him is pecking at or about to take his sandwich, which he has not noticed. On the left an older man in a flat cap, waistcoat and dark trousers walks along reading an open book, heading towards a cardboard box on the ground, so he seems about to trip over it. A woman in a yellow dress sweeps up a pile of autumn leaves with a broom near a fruit and vegetable stall. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands waving with one raised hand, and a man in a red shirt and blue trousers walks along eating a pink ice cream. A red bird perches on top of a street lamp.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The bicycle wheels are clearly square, which I read as an impossible object rather than a drawing error. The bike's position could be read as parked upright on its wheels, with no kickstand clearly holding it. There is a grey piece hanging at the bottom near the pedals that could be a kickstand or a pedal.
- A dog reading a newspaper could be a deliberate joke; it is not obviously chewing or playing with the paper, since it holds it open in front of its face.
- The older man may be about to trip over the box, or he may simply pass it; he is close but not touching it.
- The woman in purple could be waving to someone or reaching towards something; her raised hand has motion marks so waving seems more likely. I can't tell who she is waving to.
- The red bird's feet sit right at the lamp top and it overlaps a window of the building behind, so at first glance it looks as if it could be on the window; it reads as on the lamp.
- The ball seems wedged on a branch rather than floating, but it does sit against the green leaves with only a bit of branch under it.
- The woman in yellow's broom head and leaf pile overlap her feet slightly.
