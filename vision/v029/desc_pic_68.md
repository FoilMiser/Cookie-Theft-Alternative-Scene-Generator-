# Picture 68

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a long wooden fence across the back, and a concrete path running up to a wooden gate on the right. A man with dark skin in a green shirt and tan trousers is kneeling on one knee at a round flower bed near the house, reaching down among the flowers as if gardening or planting. A small potted pink plant stands at the front edge of the bed, next to his knee. In the middle of the lawn a small child in a yellow top and orange shorts stands waving one raised hand, with little curved lines beside the hand showing the movement. On the path at the right, a man in a red shirt and blue trousers is sweeping dirt along the ground, but he is using a giant silver dinner fork instead of a broom. The fork's prongs are pushing a small heap of dirt, with a puff of dust, towards a red dustpan lying at the edge of the path. This is clearly the odd thing in the picture: a fork as large as a person, used as a broom, and it would not sweep well because dirt would slip through the prongs. An empty wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the bottom centre.

- Right, on the path, front: man with brown hair, red shirt, blue trousers, belt; standing, looking down and to the left, holding a giant fork with both hands and sweeping dirt with it.

- Small dirt pile with dust puff and scrape marks (right, on path): the dirt being swept.
- Red dustpan (right, at the edge of path and grass): lying ready to catch the dirt, its handle pointing left over the grass.
- Flower bed (left-centre): round bed of brown soil with red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers on tall stems.
- Terracotta pot with pink plant (left-centre, front of the flower bed): seems to be waiting to be planted or just set down.
- Grey wheelbarrow with a wooden handle (bottom centre): empty and parked on the grass.
- House with blue door and window (left), tree behind it, small bush by the door; wooden fence and gate at the back; sun and two clouds in the sky: setting only.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The kneeling man's hands are hidden behind the flowers, so I cannot tell if he is planting, weeding or picking; he could also be about to take the potted plant, but his hands are not on it.
- The pot could be read as something he is about to plant, or just something standing there.
- The child's wave has no clear target. They could be waving at the kneeling man, at the man with the fork, or at the viewer.
- The giant fork looks like a deliberate absurdity rather than a drawing error, but it could be read as a strange rake. Its shape is exactly a dinner fork, not a garden fork.
- The man's arms and hands around the fork handle look slightly tangled and it is hard to see which hand is where.
- The dustpan is lying partly off the path on the grass, with the dirt heap right at its lip.
