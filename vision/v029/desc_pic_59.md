# Picture 59

## Free description

This is a cartoon of a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a paved path at the right leading to a wooden gate. On the right, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes stands on the path with his arm stretched out, having just thrown a red-and-white ball. A dashed line traces the ball's curved route through the air, and the ball is now flying left towards a man in a red T-shirt and blue trousers, who stands in the middle of the lawn with both arms held out and his hands together, ready to catch it. The man's eyes are wide and his mouth is open, so he looks either excited or a little startled. On the left, a woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers is digging with a shovel, lifting a load of earth out of a hole, with a heap of dirt beside her, just behind a flower bed. The strange thing is a closed cardboard box, tilted at an angle, hanging up in the sky above the washing line with nothing holding it up, and nobody is looking at it. A washing line with blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes runs between two posts in the middle, and a hose reel sits on the grass at the edge of the flower bed. The ball looks about to be caught; the box might be about to fall.
