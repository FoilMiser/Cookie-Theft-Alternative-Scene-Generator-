# Picture 33

## Free description
This is a back garden under a grey sky with three big dark storm clouds, though no rain is falling yet. A woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers stands on the grass with her mouth slightly open, looking up and pointing at the sky, apparently noticing the coming storm. On the path at the far left, an old man in a cap, white shirt and waistcoat is using an enormous silver fork as if it were a broom, pushing a small heap of dirt towards a red dustpan on the ground, with a puff of dust rising. A fork that size used for sweeping is absurd and looks impossible. A black cat with white paws walks along the top of the fence. Washing, a yellow striped top and blue jeans, is still hanging on the line, and it seems about to get rained on. A round flower bed and a house with a red roof and blue door are on the right, and an empty wheelbarrow stands in the foreground.

## Inventory
- Far left, on the path: old man with grey hair, blue-grey cap, white long-sleeved shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers, looking down and sweeping dirt with a huge fork.
- Left of centre, front: woman with long dark hair, purple top and teal trousers, standing, looking up, pointing up and to the right at the sky.
- Top of the fence, left of centre: black cat with white paws and chest, walking along the fence top.
- Giant fork: silver dinner fork, as tall as the man's chest, tines on the ground, used as a broom.
- Dirt heap and dust: small dark pile with a puff of grey dust and scrape marks on the ground.
- Dustpan: red with a thin handle, lying on the ground at the edge of the path, next to the heap.
- Storm clouds: three large dark grey-blue clouds in the top middle; grey sky, no sun.
- Washing line: two wooden posts with a sagging line, holding a yellow top with red stripes and blue jeans on pegs.
- Wheelbarrow: empty grey wheelbarrow, front centre.
- Flower bed: round bed of red, yellow, orange, purple and pink flowers, right, by the house.
- Bush: small green bush next to the house door.
- Gate: brown wooden gate at the top of the path, partly behind the old man.
- House: cream wall, blue door, window, red roof, right.
- Background: tree behind the fence, green bushes.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The woman could be pointing at the clouds, warning about rain, or at something else up there such as the tree or the cat. Her arm points up and to the right, away from the cat, and the clouds are the most obvious thing.
- The dustpan's thin red handle runs right up to the woman's hip and seems to pass behind her, so it could at first look as if she is holding it or it is connected to her.
- The fork is white-silver and overlaps the old man's white sleeves, so his arms and the fork handle are hard to tell apart. It could be read as an oversized fork or as a strange kind of garden tool, but the shape is clearly a table fork.
- There is a dark smudge near the old man's mouth that could be a moustache or just a shadow.
