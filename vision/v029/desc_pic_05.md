# Picture 05

## Free description
This is a sunny playground on a large lawn, with dark metal railings and a line of hedges along the back. One child is in the picture, a girl with dark hair, a pink dress and yellow shoes, who is sliding down a tall red-framed slide with yellow and orange rails on the left. She sits partway down the chute with her legs forward and both arms raised up, looking as if she is enjoying it. She is heading down and to the right and should reach the bottom of the slide in a moment. To the right of the slide stand a green bin and a grey-blue swing frame with one red seat, which hangs empty and still. At the front left is a wooden sandpit with a red bucket and a blue spade lying in the sand, and nobody is playing there. A large tree and a bush stand on the right. Nothing looks clearly wrong or impossible.
