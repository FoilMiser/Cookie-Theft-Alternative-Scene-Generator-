# Picture 10

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with nobody in it, neither people nor animals. On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a blue door and a window, with a tree behind it. A tall wooden fence runs across the back, with a closed wooden gate on the left and a paved path leading from the gate down to the front left corner. A garden hose comes in along the ground from the right edge, passes behind the legs of a wooden bench, and feeds a small sprinkler in the lower middle of the lawn. The sprinkler is running and throws three arcs of water to the left. The longest arc lands right on a closed cardboard box sitting on the grass, and the two shorter arcs fall on the lawn just in front of it. Water is dripping down the box and a puddle has formed under it, so the box and whatever is inside are getting soaked. It looks as though someone left a parcel on the lawn in the wrong place and it will be ruined if nobody moves it or turns the water off. Nothing else is happening: the flower bed, potted plants and birdhouse are simply there.
