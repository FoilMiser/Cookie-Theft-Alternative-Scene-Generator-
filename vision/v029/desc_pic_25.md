# Picture 25

## Free description

This is a sunny park with green hills in the distance, a winding sandy path on the left and a blue pond with bulrushes on the right. In the lower right, a child with dark curly or braided hair, yellow top and orange shorts is walking to the right, holding the string of a yellow balloon that floats above their hand. Slightly ahead, also walking to the right, is an old man with grey hair, a blue-grey flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers, eating a pink ice cream. The odd thing is on the left: a wooden ladder stands upright on the ground beside the path, tilted to the right and leaning against nothing at all, its top simply ending in mid-air in front of the bushes. A ladder could not stay up like that, and nobody is using it or standing near it. A tall black lamp post and a green bin stand near the centre, and there are trees on both sides, a few bushes and a grey rock at the bottom centre. The two walkers do not seem to have noticed the ladder. Nothing else seems about to happen.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The ladder could be read as an impossible free-standing ladder or as one that is meant to lean on something that is missing from the picture; there is no wall, tree or person it could be resting on. The tree on its left is too far away and it slants away from that tree anyway.
- The ladder's lower part overlaps the edge of the path, and the path outline shows through the rungs, which makes the bottom a little busy to read.
- The child and the old man could be together (for example a grandfather and grandchild out for a walk) or just two strangers going the same way; they are not touching or looking at each other.
- The child could be a boy or a girl.
- The old man's hand hides most of the cone, so the ice cream reads mainly as a pink ball held to his mouth.
- The old man's head and upper body overlap the pond's far edge, though his feet are clearly on the grass in front of it, so he is not in the water.
