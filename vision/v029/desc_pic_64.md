# Picture 64

## Free description
This is a sunny town square in front of a row of four shops, with several separate things going on. On the far left, a man in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest is sweeping a small heap of autumn-coloured leaves with a yellow-bristled broom. In the middle, a girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench eating an ice cream or something similar, holding it to her mouth, and a white dog with a brown patch and a blue collar sits up on its hind legs on the ground beside the bench, begging and looking up at her. To the right of her, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands with one arm raised high and the other holding three balloon strings; the balloons are purple, blue and red. The purple balloon is higher and further away from him, and his raised hand is reaching towards it, so it looks as if it has just slipped away from him. On the right, a man in a red cap and blue hoodie is walking while reading an open book, looking down at it, and directly in his path on the ground ahead of him is a cardboard box, so he appears about to trip over it. A red bird perches on top of the lamp post and a blue bird flies high up in the sky at the upper left. There is also a produce stall on the right with another box beside it, a tree in a planter on the left, a planter of flowers and a pot plant.
