# Picture 53

## Free description

This is a cartoon of a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a paved path at the lower left leading to a wooden gate in the fence. Three people are in the garden. At the back, an old woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, is painting the fence bright blue with a long-handled roller or brush; she has already done a wide section to her left, and a tin of blue paint stands on the grass behind her. On the right, an old man in a grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is watering a flower bed with a hose; the water arcs from the nozzle down into the flowers, and the hose runs back around the bed to a reel on the ground. In the front centre, a girl in a yellow dress with a belt and red shoes stands with her hands on her hips, and she has a silver metal bucket upside down on her head like a hat. The bucket on her head is the odd thing in the picture; nobody else seems to be reacting to it, and the two older people are just getting on with their jobs. A swing set with an empty red seat stands to her left. Nothing looks physically impossible, and nothing seems about to go wrong, though the girl seems to be looking off to the right as though at the man or the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The bucket on the girl's head could be a game or dressing up, or it could be meant as something silly and wrong; she looks calm about it either way.
- The girl could be a child or a small adult woman. Her dress and bare legs look childlike, but the proportions are not clear.
- The painting tool could be read as a paint roller or as a wide brush on a pole; the blue head is a flat rectangle.
- The top edge of the blue patch looks uneven: the left part follows the pointed tops of the pickets, while the right part has a flat top, as if the pickets were cut off or the paint just stopped lower down.
- The man's hands and the hose near the nozzle are a bit tangled-looking; the hose rises up beside him, and it is hard to see exactly how it joins the nozzle.
- The girl's bucket sits high on her head, almost as if resting on top rather than pulled down over it.
- Nothing is floating or clearly passing through anything else.
