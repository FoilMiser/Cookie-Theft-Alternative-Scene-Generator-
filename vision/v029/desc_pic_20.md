# Picture 20

## Free description

This is a sunny playground or park on grass, with a dark metal railing across the back and greenery behind it. In the lower left a small girl with dark hair in a pink dress is kneeling in a wooden-framed sandpit, building a sandcastle that has several towers and a tiny red flag on top. In the lower centre-right a boy or young man in a red cap, blue hoodie, black trousers and white shoes is kicking an orange ball with a white stripe; one leg is swung out to the right and the ball is right at his toe. Curved motion lines under him and a shadow on the ground show the movement. The ball looks like it is just about to fly off to the right, away from the girl. Behind them are a tall slide with a red frame, a dark ladder and an orange and yellow chute, nobody on it. There is also an empty bench, a green bin, a large tree on the far right, a bush on the left and a grey rock at the bottom left. Nobody seems to be interacting and nothing looks impossible.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The kicker could be an older boy or a young man.
- His head is turned down and to the right; the small grey mark near his nose and mouth area is hard to read as part of the face.
- The girl and the sandcastle overlap so that the castle hides her knees and hands; she could be behind the castle building it, or partly sitting on the castle. The first reading seems more likely.
- The ball is drawn just touching the toe, so it could be the moment of contact or a ball being trapped; the motion lines and swing suggest a kick.
- A second very faint dark smudge on the grass under the ball could be the ball's shadow.
