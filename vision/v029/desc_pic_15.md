# Picture 15

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills in the distance, a large blue pond with reeds on the left and centre, a tall lamppost in the middle, and a path winding up from the front towards the back on the right. In the lower left, an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, is kneeling on one knee at the edge of a flower bed. She has a small hand tool and a terracotta pot with a pink tulip-like flower, and appears to be planting or tending the flowers. In the lower centre, a white goat with a dark curved horn has its head down and is eating a small tuft of grass, near an empty bench. On the right, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands by a tree holding a paper bag and scattering crumbs or seeds, which fall in a dotted trail to the path. A red bird and a blue bird are on the path pecking at the food. Nobody is paying attention to anyone else and everything looks calm and ordinary, apart from a goat wandering loose in a park.

- The woman could be planting the pink flower into the bed, or taking it out of the bed and into the pot. The tool she holds is small and thin, and I could not make out whether it is a trowel, a dibber or a small fork.
- The pot overlaps the edge of the flower bed, so it could be standing on the soil or on the grass.
- The food could be bird seed or bread crumbs. The dots hang in a vertical trail in mid-air, which reads as falling.
