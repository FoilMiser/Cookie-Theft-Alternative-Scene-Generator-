# Picture 36

## Free description
This is a busy back garden on a sunny day, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence along the back, and a concrete path and gate on the right. A woman on a bench at the front left is absorbed in a red book, and she has not noticed that a small blue bird beside her is going for the sandwich sitting on the bench. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is holding a string that runs back to a purple kite stuck in the tree by the house, so he seems to be trying to tug it free. A man in a red T-shirt kneels and tends the flower bed, and an older woman with grey hair and glasses rakes leaves into a pile. An older man in a flat cap is painting the fence blue with a roller on a long pole, and part of the fence is already blue. His paint tray and bucket are on the grass, and a line of blue paw prints leads from the tray to a white spotted dog on the path, whose feet are blue, so the dog has clearly walked through the paint. An orange cat is creeping along the top of the fence towards a red bird perched there, so the bird may be about to be pounced on. A white goat with a curved horn has its head down at a tuft of grass in the middle of the garden, and a brown dog on the path is crouching playfully with a yellow-green tennis ball at its front paws. One thing looks impossible: the wheelbarrow at the front has a square wheel.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The wheelbarrow wheel is square with a cross inside. It could be a deliberate impossibility or a drawing glitch; either way, such a wheel could not roll.
- The kite could be read as stuck in the tree, with the boy trying to pull it out, or as still being flown and just passing in front of the tree. The leaves drawn over it make stuck more likely.
- The white animal looks like a goat because of the horn, but its body shape is close to the white dog's and its head is hard to read while lowered. It could be a lamb or kid.
- The blue bird could be standing on the bench or on the sandwich's edge. It could be stealing the food or just standing next to it.
- The kneeling man's hands are partly hidden by the flowers, so he could be weeding, planting or simply touching them.
- The cat could be walking casually or actively stalking the bird; its low crouch suggests stalking.
- The roller's pole seems quite short for the distance and the man's hands look a little awkward on it, but this is minor.
