# Picture 58

## Free description

This is a cartoon of a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a paved path at the right leading to a wooden gate. On the far left, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes is sitting on a wooden bench and drinking from a red cup through a yellow straw. On the far right, a man with dark skin and short black hair, wearing a green T-shirt, tan trousers and brown shoes, is standing on the path with a long-handled rake, pulling together a small pile of green and brown leaves at his feet. His head is turned to the left, away from the leaves and towards the rest of the garden, as though he is looking at something over there. In the front centre, an orange cat lies curled up on the grass with its eyes closed, apparently asleep. A green watering can stands on the grass near the man, a flower bed is near the house, and two potted plants sit by the fence. Nothing looks impossible and nothing seems about to happen; it is a calm scene of someone resting while someone else works.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Where the man is looking: his head is turned left, so he could be watching the boy, looking at the cat, or just glancing away from his raking. He is not looking at the leaves.
- Raking on a paved path instead of on the grass is a little unusual, though not strange in itself.
- The cat's pose is hard to read: its head is low and in front with the body curled behind, so it could be asleep curled up, or lying on its back. The drawing looks a bit jumbled.
- The rake head sits to the left of the leaf pile rather than behind it, so it is not obvious which way he is pulling.
- Nothing is floating or passing through anything else.
