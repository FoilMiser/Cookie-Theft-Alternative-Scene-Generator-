# Picture 65

## Free description
This is a sunny playground with a dark metal rail fence across the back and bushes beyond it. On the left is a red seesaw, and the two people on it are very mismatched. A small girl in a pink dress sits on the raised left end, up in the air with her legs dangling, her mouth open and little curved motion lines under her feet. A large man in a green shirt and beige trousers sits on the lowered right end with his legs crossed, calmly reading an open book, and does not seem to notice her. It looks as if his weight is keeping her stuck up high, and she may be calling out to him. On the right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is kneeling in a wooden sandpit, building a sandcastle with towers and a small red flag. Behind them are an empty swing set with one red seat, a green bin and a tall red and yellow slide with a ladder, none of which is in use. A tree stands at the far left, and there is a bush and a grey rock on the right. Nothing looks physically impossible, though the man's calm reading while the girl hangs up in the air is the odd part of the scene.
