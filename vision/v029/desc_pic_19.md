# Picture 19

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof on the left, a wooden fence across the back and a gate in the far right corner. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and a grey skirt, stands left of centre watering a flower bed with a hose. An arc of water comes from the yellow nozzle and falls among the red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers. The hose runs from a reel on the ground on the left, around the front of the flower bed and up to her hand. On the right, a boy or young man in a red T-shirt and blue trousers stands at the edge of a paved path, sweeping a small pile of coloured leaves with a yellow-bristled broom. The two people are not looking at or dealing with each other; each is doing their own chore. An empty wheelbarrow stands on the grass at the bottom middle and an empty bench sits at the bottom left. Nothing looks about to go wrong and I do not see anything clearly impossible.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The sweeper could be read as an older boy or a young man; his size and face do not settle it.
- The upper part of the hose rises quite stiffly next to the flower bed before it reaches the woman's hand, and it seems to tuck in behind her forearm or wrist; it could be a slightly odd drawing or just a raised hose.
- The fallen leaves are red, orange and yellow although every tree and bush in the picture is fully green, so where they came from is not obvious.
- The sweeper stands on the grass right at the path's edge while the broom head and leaves are on the path; one of his feet is hard to see behind the broom.
- The birdhouse pole has a small green patch at its foot and looks very tall and thin compared to the fence, but it does not look wrong as such.
