# Picture 66

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills in the background, a pond on the right, a winding pale path on the left, a lamp post and bin in the middle, and an empty bench. A single person is in the picture: a woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers, standing near the bottom centre of the picture with her back to the pond. She holds a small brown paper bag against her body with one hand and, with the other, is dropping or scattering small seeds or crumbs. A line of little dots falls from her fingers down to the ground in front of her. At her feet, two small birds, one red and one blue, are pecking at the seeds on the ground. She looks down at them. It is a calm scene and nothing seems about to happen, and nothing looks impossible, except perhaps that the falling seeds seem to hang in a long, slow line in the air.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The bag could be read as a paper bag of seed or as a small cardboard box; it is flat, plain and brown, and it looks oddly stuck to the front of her waist rather than clearly gripped.
- The line of dots could be seed or breadcrumbs falling, and it runs in a long curved line rather than straight down, which is a bit odd. The dots are small and easy to miss.
- The woman's legs are drawn in front of the pond's lower edge and her head overlaps it, so she might look at first as if she is standing at the water's edge, but her feet are clearly well forward of it, on the grass.
- The birds are small and sit at the very bottom edge of the picture, so they could be overlooked. They seem small relative to the woman but not impossibly so.
