# Picture 70

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, a gate and concrete path on the right, and a tall bird box on a pole. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands near the centre holding the nozzle of a garden hose up to his face with both hands, peering straight into the end of it. No water is coming out. The long teal hose runs from the nozzle down to the grass, along the ground, and back to a hose reel on the left near a bench. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is standing right on the hose, and just behind its paws the hose is swollen into a fat bulge. It looks as if the dog's weight has blocked the water, which is why nothing comes out. If the dog steps off, the water will probably shoot out of the nozzle into the boy's face. An empty wheelbarrow, two potted plants, a flower bed and an empty wooden bench are also in the garden.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The boy could be looking into the nozzle, or just squeezing the trigger or trying to fix it. His face is close to it and partly hidden by his raised arms, so I cannot see his expression clearly.
- The bulge could be read as water trapped behind the dog's foot, or as a flattened part of the hose. I read it as trapped water, which is the only reason given for the lack of spray.
- The reel does not visibly connect to a tap or the house, so the water source is not shown.
- The reel stands very close to both the bench and the flower bed and slightly overlaps them, which makes the left side look crowded.
- The dog does not appear to know it is standing on the hose.
- The boy's arms come out quite stiffly from his body and his head is twisted away from the viewer, which looks a bit awkward.
