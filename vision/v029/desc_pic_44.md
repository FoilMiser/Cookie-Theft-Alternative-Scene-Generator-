# Picture 44

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with no people in it. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door stands on the right, and a tall wooden plank fence with a wooden gate runs along the back, with a pale paved path leading from the gate down the left side. In the middle-left there is a grey-blue A-frame swing set with a red seat hanging empty and still. On the grass in the front centre a small orange cat is curled up or lying on its side with its eyes closed, apparently asleep. To the right of the cat there is a round flower bed with colourful flowers, and a teal hose reel sits on the ground at the edge of the bed. A teal watering can sits on the grass beside the path. Nothing much is happening and nothing seems about to happen; the scene is calm and nothing looks impossible. The only living thing is the sleeping cat.
