# Picture 51

## Free description

This is a sunny playground with a long dark metal railing fence at the back and trees and bushes behind it. On the left, a child with dark curly hair in a yellow top and orange shorts is sliding down a red and yellow slide with arms raised and stretched out. On the right, there is a red see-saw on a grey-blue triangular stand. An older man in a cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers sits on the low end, down near the ground, and he is calmly reading an open book. A small girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes sits high up on the other end, holding the handle, with her feet in the air and small motion marks under them, and her mouth is open as though surprised. The man's weight appears to keep her stuck in the air, and he does not seem to have noticed her. It looks as if the see-saw is not going to move while he sits and reads. There is a green bin by the fence, a bush on the left, a grey rock at the bottom left and a large tree on the right.
