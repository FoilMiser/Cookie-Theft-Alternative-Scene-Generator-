# Picture 41

## Free description
This is an empty park on a sunny day, with no people or animals anywhere. There is a large blue pond with bulrushes on the left, a tall black lamp post in the middle with a green litter bin beside it, and a winding sandy path on the right that leads back towards low green hills and bushes. There is a large tree on the right and a smaller one on the far left, plus a few bushes, a flower bed at the front left and a grey rock at the front centre. The one odd thing is a tall wooden ladder standing nearly upright beside the path with nothing to lean on. It is not touching the tree or the lamp post, so it looks as if it should fall over. Nothing seems to be happening or about to happen, apart from possibly the ladder toppling.
