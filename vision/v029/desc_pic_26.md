# Picture 26

## Free description

This is a sunny park with green hills in the distance, a winding sandy path on the left and a blue pond with bulrushes on the right. In the centre-right, a man with short dark hair and dark brown skin, wearing a green shirt and beige trousers, stands at the edge of the pond holding a brown paper bag and scattering crumbs from his other hand. A white duck on the water close to the shore is bending its head down to peck at the crumbs, some of which float on the water and some of which lie on the grass. In the lower right, a man with brown hair in a red T-shirt and blue trousers strides to the left, pushing a teal pram with a navy hood, in which a baby is sleeping under a pink polka-dot blanket. On the left, a woman with a long dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is running to the right across the grass, with speed lines behind her. The pram is heading toward the crumbs and the man feeding the duck, so it looks likely to pass close by him soon. An empty bench, a tall lamp post, a grey rock and a green bin are placed around the scene. The three adults are not interacting with one another. Nothing here looks impossible.
