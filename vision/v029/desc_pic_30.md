# Picture 30

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a paved path on the left leading to a wooden gate. A man in a red shirt and blue trousers stands facing the fence, holding a long-handled paint roller and painting it blue. About three planks are already blue, the last one only partly done, and a tin of blue paint sits on the grass near him. In front of him, an old man in a cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is standing by a round flower bed and pouring water onto the flowers from a frying pan held out by its handle, which is a very strange thing to water with. An orange cat is walking along the top of the fence. An empty wheelbarrow stands on the grass in the foreground, and there are two pot plants and a bush near the fence. Nobody seems to be taking any notice of each other, and nothing seems about to go wrong other than the frying pan being the wrong tool.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The frying pan could at first look like a large watering can head, a sieve or a shower head, but it reads as a frying pan. Using it to water flowers looks like an intended oddity or a mistake.
- The orange cat's feet land on the pointed tops of the fence planks, which looks a bit awkward. The cat seems large compared with the fence.
- The painter could be read as just finishing or just starting, since only a small patch is blue. He stands to the right of the painted area and quite far from the fence for the length of the pole.
- The paint tin could be read as belonging to either man because it sits between them, but its colour matches the roller.
