# Picture 35

## Free description
This is a back garden under a grey sky with three big dark storm clouds, though no rain is falling yet. The cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, a wooden fence runs along the back, and a paved path on the right leads to a gate. In the middle, a woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers is in a sitting position sipping a red cup through a yellow straw, but there is nothing under her, no chair at all, so she looks as if she is sitting on thin air, which is impossible. In front of her, an old woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, teal top and grey skirt is walking to the left carrying a cardboard box. On the path at the right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts looks up and points at the sky, apparently at the dark clouds. It looks like it is about to rain, and only the boy seems to have noticed. A teal watering can sits on the grass near the boy, and there is a round flower bed by the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The seated woman: she could be floating or levitating, or the seat could simply be missing from the drawing, for example a chair that was left out by mistake. Either way, there is nothing under her and only a small shadow at her feet.
- The old woman's hand seems to be on the front of the box rather than underneath it, so the box looks stuck to her rather than properly carried, and only one arm is clearly visible.
- The boy could be pointing at the clouds, or at a bird or something else up in the sky that I can't see; the clouds are the only obvious thing.
- The old woman's walking and the boy's pointing do not appear connected; she does not look up.
