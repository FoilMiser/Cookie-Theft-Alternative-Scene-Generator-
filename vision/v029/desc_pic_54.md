# Picture 54

## Free description

This is a cartoon of a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a paved path at the lower right leading to a wooden gate. In the centre, a man with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue trousers is standing and holding a green watering can out towards a flower bed. The strange thing is the water: it leaves the can's spout low down and curves upwards and to the left, ending in drops above the flowers, so it looks like the water is flowing up instead of falling down. On the far left, a girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes is sitting on a wooden bench reading a red book. In the front, to the right of centre, an orange cat is lying curled up on the grass with its eyes closed, apparently asleep, near a red bowl of pet food. Behind them a washing line between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes, a birdhouse stands on a tall pole, and two potted plants sit on the grass by the fence. Nobody seems bothered by anything, and all three look calm and busy with their own thing.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The water stream can be read two ways: water squirting upward out of the can, which is impossible for a watering can, or a stream that is really supposed to pour down but has been drawn with its direction reversed. Either way it does not look like normal pouring, since the highest point is furthest from the spout.
- The stream does not clearly connect to the spout end; it starts just at the tip and heads the opposite way to how the spout would pour.
- The cat's pose is hard to read: its head is low and to the front with the body curled behind, so it could be asleep curled up, or lying on its back or rolling over. The drawing looks a bit jumbled.
- The girl could be a child or a small adult; she looks small next to the man but the bench is in the foreground.
- The birdhouse pole has a small green blob at its base, perhaps a tuft of grass.
- The man is drawn with his body in a stiff upright pose and the can held fairly low, which makes the can look close to his hip rather than held out over the flowers.
