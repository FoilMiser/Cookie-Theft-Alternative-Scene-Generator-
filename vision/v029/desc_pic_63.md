# Picture 63

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden, seen with the cream house and blue door on the right, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate on the left, and a paved path running down to the lower left. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses stands on the lawn near the flower bed. She wears a teal top, a grey skirt and brown shoes. She is holding the nozzle of a long blue-green garden hose in both hands and spraying a curved stream of water onto the flowers. The hose runs from her hands down across the grass, loops around the front of the flower bed and connects to a hose reel on the right. It is a calm, ordinary gardening scene and nothing looks about to happen. A grey wheelbarrow stands empty at the front, two pot plants and a bush are behind her to the left, and an empty wooden bench is at the lower right near the house.
