# Picture 48

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with several people and animals. In the middle of the lawn, a girl in a pink dress and a woman in a purple top and teal trousers are playing catch: a red and white ball is in the air between them, with a dashed curved line showing its path from the girl's hand up towards the woman, whose hands are reaching out to catch it. In the front centre, a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is bowed down playfully over a yellow tennis ball on the grass, tail up and wagging. On the far left, by the blue house door, a brown dog with a red collar is sitting and holding a red lead in its mouth, as though asking to be taken for a walk. On the right, an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and a grey skirt, is sweeping a pile of autumn leaves on the path with a straw broom. A teal watering can stands on the grass near her. Nothing looks impossible; the ball in the air and the dogs' playful poses seem to be the point.

## Inventory

- Woman in purple: centre-left, long dark hair, purple top, teal trousers, dark shoes; standing with both arms stretched out, facing right, ready to catch the ball.
- White spotted dog: front centre, white with brown patches, blue collar; front down and rear up in a play bow, tail wagging, nose near a yellow tennis ball.
- Brown dog: far left, by the door, brown with white chest and red collar; sitting upright holding a red lead in its mouth.
- Older woman: right, on the path, grey hair in a bun, glasses, teal top, grey skirt, brown shoes; sweeping leaves with a broom.
- Red and white ball: centre, in the air in front of the woman's hands, with a dashed line behind it marking its flight.
- Tennis ball: front centre, yellow-green, on the grass in front of the white dog.
- Red lead: left, hanging from the brown dog's mouth to a loop lying on the ground.
- Leaf pile and broom: front right, orange, red and brown leaves on the path, being swept.
- Watering can: front right, teal; standing on the grass near the older woman.
- Flower bed: left of centre, round brown bed with red, yellow, orange, purple and pink flowers.
- House: left, cream with red roof, blue door, one window.
- Fence and gate: back, tall wooden fence with a closed gate at the right; a pale path leads down from it.
- Tree and bush: tree behind the house corner, small bush against the house wall.
- Sun and clouds: sun at top right, two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The ball's direction: the dashed line runs from the girl's raised hand, so I read her as the thrower and the woman as the catcher. It could be read the other way round, with the woman having thrown it and the girl catching it, but the woman's hands are in front of the ball, and her arms are out as if to receive.
- The white dog could be bowing to play with the tennis ball, or bending down to sniff or pick it up. The wiggly lines by its tail mean it is wagging.
- The brown dog could be read as waiting at the door for a walk, or just holding the lead. It is unclear whether it has any owner in the scene.
- The older woman's feet seem to overlap the broom and the leaves at the bottom; the bristles reach down past the leaf pile, slightly over the edge of the path, but it does not look wrong.
- The woman in purple's body is narrow and quite stiff. Nothing is floating except the ball, which is clearly meant to be in flight.
