# Picture 49

## Free description

This is a sunny park with a blue pond on the left, rolling green hills and trees behind, a winding path on the right and a tall street lamp near the centre. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands at the right edge of the pond holding a brown paper bag in one hand, stretching the other hand out over the water. A white duck is swimming on the pond, leaving white wake lines behind it, and a trail of small pale crumbs or seeds lies on the water in front of it. The duck is bending down towards the crumbs, so the boy seems to be feeding it bread. An empty wooden bench is on the right next to the path. There is a round flower bed and a green bin at the front left, a grey rock at the front centre, and bushes and reeds around the pond. Nothing looks wrong or impossible, and the scene is quiet. Only the boy and the duck are present.
