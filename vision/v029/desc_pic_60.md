# Picture 60

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path running down to the front right. A man in a red shirt and blue trousers stands in the middle of the lawn with both arms stretched out in front of him, looking up and to the right. A small red ball is in the air just beyond his hands, and a dotted line traces its path back to a small girl in a pink dress near the gate, so she seems to have just thrown it to him and he is about to catch it. In front of them, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is jumping off the ground with both arms up, smiling; his shadow is well below his feet, so he is clearly mid-jump. High in the sky, above the fence and well away from anyone, a large cardboard box hangs in the air at a tilt. Nothing is holding it up, and nobody is looking at it or reacting to it. That floating box is the thing that looks wrong or impossible, unless it is falling and about to land on someone. There is a flower bed in front of the house, a tree behind the house corner and a bush beside the door.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The box: it could be falling from somewhere (about to drop on the people) or simply floating impossibly. There is nowhere obvious it could have come from. No one has thrown it as far as I can tell, and it has no motion lines or dotted path.
- The boy could be jumping for joy, or jumping to try to intercept the ball, or even reaching for the box, but he is not looking directly at any of them and his hands are not near either object.
- The man's gaze: he seems to look towards the right where the ball is, though his head is tilted up, so he could conceivably be looking at something above. His hands are slightly short of the ball.
- The dotted line ends at the girl's hand, which reads as a throw, but she is very small compared with the man, so she could be read as a distant figure or simply a tiny child.
- The boy's shadow sits far below him on the grass, which fits a high jump, though it makes the jump look unusually high.
