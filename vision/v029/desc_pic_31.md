# Picture 31

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a paved path on the left. On the path, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is raking a small pile of leaves with a long rake, looking down at his work. In the middle-left, a boy in a yellow shirt is sitting on a swing in a dark metal swing frame, facing us with small motion lines beside him, so he is swinging. A small girl in a pink dress stands in the middle of the lawn holding a string and looking up, with one arm raised. Her string curves away up to a purple kite that is caught in the tree behind the fence, so she seems to be trying to get it down or is unaware it is stuck. A washing line between two wooden posts holds a yellow striped top and a pair of blue jeans. There is a round flower bed by the house, and an empty wheelbarrow and a teal watering can stand unused on the grass in the foreground.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl could be flying the kite and pulling it, or trying to free it from the tree. The kite does look caught because clumps of leaves are drawn over it, and the string is not tight.
- The boy's motion lines appear only on one side and the swing ropes hang nearly straight, so he could be swinging gently or be nearly still.
- The man's head sits forward and low on his body and looks large; his head faces left while his body seems to face right, which looks slightly awkward. He also looks very large compared with the girl, though that may be just because he is in front.
- The kite string passes in front of the fence and the tree, so it is easy to lose track of it where it crosses them.
