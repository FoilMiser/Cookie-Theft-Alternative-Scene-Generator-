# Picture 40

## Free description
This is a back garden on a windy but sunny day, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a long wooden fence, and a gate and path on the right. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands in the middle of the lawn with both arms stretched up and her mouth slightly open, looking surprised. Above her, a red cap is tumbling in the air with a few leaves, and white wind lines blow across the fence towards it, so the wind has just taken her hat and she is reaching for it. Further right, a woman with long dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers is reading a red book in a sitting pose, but there is no chair or bench under her, so she appears to be sitting on thin air. A man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers is walking to the right, holding a red lead attached to a white dog with brown spots, which is walking along the path ahead of him. Neither the reader nor the man seem to notice the woman's trouble. A teal watering can sits unused on the grass at the front, and there is a flower bed near the house.

- Woman, right-centre, middle distance: long dark hair, purple top, teal trousers, dark shoes. In a sitting position, reading a red book, with no seat beneath her.
- Man, right, middle distance: short dark hair, brown skin, green T-shirt, khaki trousers, brown shoes. Walking right, holding a dog lead.
- White dog with brown spots, right, on the path: blue collar. Walking to the right ahead of the man on a red lead.
- Red cap: in the air above the standing woman, in front of the fence, tumbling and apparently blown off.
- Leaves: a few small green and brown leaves blowing in the air just beneath the cap.
- White wind lines: three long wavy lines across the fence to the right of the cap, plus two small sets in the sky, showing a strong wind.
- Red book: held open by the seated woman.
- Red lead: running from the man's hands to the dog's collar.
- Teal watering can: on the grass, front right, by the edge of the path. Unattended.
- Flower bed: multicoloured flowers in a soil mound, left, by the house.
- Concrete path and wooden gate: far right.
- House, tree, fence, sun and clouds: background setting.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The reading woman has no visible seat. This could be a deliberate impossibility, or a missing object. It looks unintentionally strange either way, and her small shadow is on the ground below her.
- The red object in the air reads as a cap, but it is drawn in an odd shape, with the crown and brim looking almost like two separate pieces. It could be read as a red shell, fan or some other flying object.
- The standing woman could be trying to catch the cap, or simply throwing her hands up in surprise. It could also be that the cap belonged to someone else, since she has nothing on her head and nothing shows it was hers.
- The wind seems to affect only the cap and the leaves: the tree, flowers, the seated woman's hair and the book do not look disturbed, which is slightly inconsistent.
- It is not clear why the watering can is there or whose it is.
