# Picture 11

## Free description
This is a sunny playground on a lawn, with a dark metal railing fence across the back. In the lower right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is holding two balloons, a yellow one and a red one, by their strings in one hand. A third, purple balloon has got away from him and is rising up with its string trailing just above his fingertips. He is looking up at it, head tipped back, stretching his other arm up to try to catch it, and he looks like he is about to miss it. In the middle, a man in an orange cap, orange high-visibility vest and blue trousers is walking to the right carrying a cardboard box, looking like a delivery or work person, and he does not seem involved with the children. On the left, a girl in a pink dress is partway down a yellow and orange slide with her arms up in the air. At the front left is a sandbox with a red bucket and a blue spade in it, and nobody playing there. The bench at the back is empty and a large tree stands on the right.
