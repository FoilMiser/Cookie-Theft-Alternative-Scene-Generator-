# Picture 18

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills in the distance, a winding path on the left, a tall lamppost near the middle, and a blue pond with reeds on the right. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on a wooden bench eating a pink ice cream cone, with his eyes squeezed shut and a happy look. Right beside the bench, a small white dog with a brown patch and a blue neckerchief is sitting up on its hind legs with its front paws lifted, begging and looking up at the boy, clearly hoping for some of the ice cream. Further back, a man in a green shirt and tan trousers is walking across the grass near the lamppost, also eating a pink ice cream cone. On the right, a boy in a red cap, blue hooded top and dark trousers stands at the front edge of the pond holding a fishing rod. The line runs from the tip of the rod down to a red and white float in the water, with a small ripple around it, and no fish is showing. A green rubbish bin stands at the front right, near a flower bed. Everything looks calm and ordinary, and it seems the boy on the bench may soon drop or share his ice cream with the dog.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The boy's closed, arched eyes could mean he is enjoying the ice cream, is laughing, or is wincing from cold. I read it as enjoying it.
- The dog could be begging for the ice cream, or just sitting up to play. Its head and raised paws point towards the boy, so begging seems most likely. It sits very upright, and its body and front legs look a bit thin and awkward.
- The man and the boy have the same kind of pink ice cream, so they could be together, for example father and son, or it could be a coincidence. They are far apart and not looking at each other.
- The man is drawn fairly large for how far back he appears, and his head overlaps the hill line, so he could seem to be walking on the hill at first glance. His feet and shadow put him on the grass near the lamppost.
- The fishing line curves in a natural way and the float looks fine. There is no sign that a fish has been caught.
