# Picture 47

## Free description

This is a sunny town square in front of a row of four shops, with a few people and animals. In the front right, an orange cat is crouched and stalking towards a red bird that is standing on the pavement just ahead of it, so it looks as though the cat is about to pounce on the bird. In the front centre, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie walks along carrying a yellow shopping bag. In the middle, a child with braided or curly dark hair, in a yellow top and orange shorts, sits on a bench reading a red book. At the back right, a workman in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest with reflective stripes and blue trousers is standing by a street lamp and waving with one raised hand. A fruit stall with crates of red, orange, yellow and green produce stands at the far right, with nobody serving at it. Nothing looks impossible; the main tension is the cat and the bird, and the bird does not seem to have noticed.
