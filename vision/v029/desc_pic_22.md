# Picture 22

## Free description

This is a sunny playground on grass, with a dark metal railing across the back and greenery behind it. On the left, two small children are sitting on a red seesaw, facing each other and holding handles. The girl in the pink dress on the left end is a little higher, and the child in the yellow top and orange shorts on the right end is a little lower; neither end touches the ground and both children's feet dangle. To the right of the seesaw, a boy or young man in a red cap, blue hoodie, black trousers and white shoes stands by himself eating a pink ice cream, with his eyes squeezed shut as if enjoying it. A tall slide with a red frame, dark ladder and orange and yellow chute stands at the back right, empty. A large tree is on the far left, a bush on the far right and a grey rock near the bottom right. The children seem to be playing together while the ice cream eater takes no part. Nothing looks about to go wrong and nothing seems impossible.
