# Picture 50

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with several people and animals, and a couple of odd things going on. In the front centre, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, teal top and grey skirt is holding a garden hose up to her face and looking straight into its nozzle with both hands, apparently because no water is coming out. Right behind her, a white spotted dog with a blue collar is standing on the hose where it runs along the ground, and the hose bulges on the reel side of the dog, so the dog is blocking the water; if it steps off, she may get sprayed in the face. At the back, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the wooden fence blue with a long-handled brush, with a paint tin on the grass behind her. An orange cat is walking along the top of the fence, with the wet blue paint ahead of it, though it faces away from the painted section. On the right, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is sitting on a swing, smiling, with motion lines suggesting swinging. A wheelbarrow at the front has a square wheel. A bird box on a tall pole stands near the gate.

- Hose: front left to centre, teal; runs from the reel on the left, along the ground, under the dog, and up to the older woman's hands. A fat bulge sits between the reel and the dog.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The square wheel on the wheelbarrow looks like a deliberate impossibility (it could not roll), rather than a drawing error.
- The hose bulge: I read it as water backing up behind the dog's foot, but it could also be read as a loop of hose lying flat, or a thick section of hose.
- The older woman has a small yellow item at the nozzle, which could be a nozzle tip or a drop of water; a small pale blue drop or shape near her hands could be a trickle of water. Her face is partly hidden by her arm and hand.
- The cat may or may not be about to walk through the paint. It faces left, away from the blue section, so it seems it may be walking away from it rather than towards it, and I see no paw prints.
- The blue stroke at the top of the fence near the brush appears to stick up as a separate blue rectangle, and could be read as a freshly painted stroke or as a loose object hanging on the fence.
- The swing ropes run up to the top bar, but they tilt, and the child's seat is off-centre in the frame, which fits it being in motion. The child's legs hang straight down, which looks a bit stiff for swinging.
- The older woman's arms appear to reach out from just below her face with the head turned, which makes the neck and arms look a bit awkward.
