# Picture 21

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof on the right, a wooden fence across the back and a gate in the far left corner. On the left, a woman or older girl with a dark ponytail, purple T-shirt and teal trousers stands on a paved path and sweeps a small pile of coloured leaves with a yellow-bristled broom. In the middle at the back, a boy or man in a red T-shirt and blue trousers stands between two wooden posts, reaching up with both arms to peg a white T-shirt onto a washing line; a pair of blue jeans is already hanging next to him. Left of centre stands a tall dark swing frame with a single red seat, which is empty and hanging still. A teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow sit on the grass at the bottom centre, unused. A flower bed with multicoloured flowers lies on the right in front of the house, near the blue door. Both people are doing ordinary chores and are not interacting. Nothing looks about to go wrong, and I do not see anything clearly impossible.
