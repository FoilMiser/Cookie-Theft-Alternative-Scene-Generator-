# Picture 13

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right and a wooden fence across the back. At the far left, an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and a grey skirt, stands on the paved path sweeping. What she is sweeping with is the odd part: instead of a broom she is holding a huge silver dinner fork, as tall as her body, using it like a broom. The tines are dragging on the path and pushing a small pile of dirt, with a puff of dust rising, towards a red dustpan on the ground. It looks impossible or silly, because a fork would not sweep up dust very well, and a fork that size does not exist. In the middle of the lawn stands a swing set with one empty red seat, and a grey wheelbarrow with nothing in it is parked in front. There is nobody else in the picture. A flower bed sits in front of the house and a birdhouse on a tall pole stands near the gate.
