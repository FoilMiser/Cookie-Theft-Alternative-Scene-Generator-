# Picture 74

## Free description
This is a calm, sunny park with green hills in the distance, a winding sandy path on the left, a pond with reeds on the right, and a tall black lamp post near the middle. A man with dark skin and a short beard, wearing a green shirt and tan trousers, stands on the grass at the front right of centre, looking down. He holds a paper bag or small box against his body with one arm and, with the other hand, is dropping seeds or crumbs in a line down to the ground. Two small birds, one blue and one red, stand on the grass below his hand among the scattered seeds, pecking at them. A white duck with an orange beak is swimming on the pond behind him, facing left. There is an empty bench, a rubbish bin, a grey rock, and a flower bed just beside the birds. Nothing looks wrong or dangerous; it seems to be a quiet moment of a man feeding the birds.

- The man's head overlaps the pond, so at a glance it could seem he is closer to the water than he is; his feet are clearly on the grass in front.
- The duck is facing away from the man, so it may not be involved in the feeding at all, or could be about to come over.
