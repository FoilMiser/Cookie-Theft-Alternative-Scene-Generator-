# Picture 57

## Free description

This is a cartoon of a sunny park with green hills and bushes in the distance, a blue sky, a sun at the upper right and two white clouds. One person is in the picture: a woman or older girl with brown skin and long dark hair, wearing a purple top and teal trousers. She is standing on the grass at the right end of a blue pond, facing left, and holding a long dark fishing rod in both hands, with a reel near her hands. The rod reaches up and out over the water, and a thin line drops from its tip to a red-and-white float sitting in the pond with a small white ripple ring around it. She looks like she is waiting for a fish to bite; no fish can be seen. Around her the park is empty: an unused wooden bench by a winding path on the right, a tall black lamp post with a green rubbish bin, a grey rock at the front, a flower bed at the lower left and a few trees and bushes. Nothing looks wrong or impossible, and it is a quiet, ordinary scene.
