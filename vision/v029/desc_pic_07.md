# Picture 07

## Free description
This is a very busy sunny park with a pond on the left, a black lamp post right of centre, a winding path on the right and many things happening at once. At front left an older man in a cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat holds out a black frying pan and pours water from it onto a flower bed, which is an odd thing to water flowers with. Further left, a white dog sits up on the grass holding an open newspaper and appears to be reading it, which is impossible. On the pond a white duck goes after a trail of pale pieces on the water, and a girl in a pink dress at the pond's right edge holds a paper bag with her arm stretched out, as though she has been throwing them bread. In the front centre a child in a yellow top and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and clutches their head in dismay, because a pink ice cream with sprinkles lies splattered on the ground, and a brown dog with a red collar has its nose down at it. On the path an elderly woman with glasses, a teal top and grey skirt sweeps a pile of orange-brown leaves with a yellow broom. Behind her a white goat with small dark horns bends to eat a tuft of grass. At far right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie looks up and holds a string that leads to a purple kite stuck in the big tree. A blue bird flies across the sky near the lamp, and the wooden bench is empty.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The pale ovals on the pond could be bread pieces or small stones or lily pads; the girl's bag and arm make bread more likely. One or two sit very close to the bank.
- The two white lines near the duck could be a wake or motion lines.
- The pan could be a frying pan or a shallow saucepan; either way it is a strange thing to water flowers with.
- The child's hand on the head could mean upset, dismay or simple scratching; the empty cone makes upset more likely.
- The brown dog could be eating the ice cream or only sniffing it.
- The white animal is most likely a goat, but could be read as a sheep or a white dog with horns; a goat in a park is odd in itself.
- The big tree's crown has four small, lighter patches that look like small bushes pasted onto it, one partly covering the kite. They could be leaf clumps, but they look a bit unintentional.
- Fallen autumn leaves are being swept while the trees are fully green, which could be read as slightly out of place.
- The kite string runs across or behind the tree trunk, so it is hard to tell whether it passes in front or behind.
- The boy, the newspaper and the dog holding it sit close to the picture edges.
