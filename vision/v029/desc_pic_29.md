# Picture 29

## Free description
This is a sunny playground on a grass field with a dark metal railing fence and bushes behind it. On the right, an old man in a cap and a girl in a pink dress are on a red see-saw. The old man sits on the low end with his feet on the ground, reading an open book and paying no attention to the girl. The girl is stuck up in the air on the high end, holding the handle, with an open mouth that looks surprised or worried, and little motion lines under her dangling feet. On the left in the front, a boy in a yellow shirt kneels in a wooden sandbox and is building a sandcastle with a tiny red flag on top. A red and yellow slide with a ladder stands empty behind him, and there is a green litter bin by the fence. A big tree stands at the right edge and a grey rock sits on the grass near the sandbox. Nothing looks truly impossible, but the old man reading on a see-saw while the girl hangs in the air is a bit funny or unfair, and she may be unable to get down.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl's expression could be fear or surprise at being stuck up high, or she could just be saying something to the old man. The lines under her feet could mean kicking or that the see-saw has just moved.
- The old man could be deliberately holding the see-saw down, or he could simply have forgotten about her because of his book. He is on the see-saw but not "playing".
- It is an unusual pairing: an old man reading on a child's see-saw.
- The sandcastle overlaps the boy's legs, so it is hard to tell exactly how he is kneeling; part of his leg seems hidden behind the castle.
