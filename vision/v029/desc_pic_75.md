# Picture 75

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back, a gate and concrete path on the left, and a tall bird box on a pole. A woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers stands facing the fence, holding a long-handled paint roller and painting it bright blue. A few boards have already been painted blue in a neat block, and the rest of the fence is still plain brown. Behind her on the grass are a tin of blue paint and a paint tray with blue paint in it. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has clearly stepped in the tray, because its paws are blue and it has left a trail of blue paw prints across the grass from the tray to where it is now. The dog is walking away to the left, towards the concrete path, and is about to get paint on that too. The woman has her back to the dog and does not seem to have noticed. An empty wheelbarrow, a teal watering can, a flower bed and an empty bench are also in the garden.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The painting tool could be a paint roller or a wide brush on a long stick. The blue head is long and upright against the fence rather than horizontal, as a roller normally would be.
- The painted area has very straight edges, and its bottom and sides are complete while one top corner is not, so she seems to be finishing the last corner. It is odd that the block is so neat and sits in the middle of the fence, not starting from one end.
- The dog could be running away after making a mess, or just wandering. It looks calm and is not looking back.
- The paw print trail begins next to the tray, but no prints are shown inside the tray or clearly linking directly to it, though the nearest print is right at its edge.
- The woman's handle looks short for reaching and her hands sit high and close together, so her grip is a little hard to read.
