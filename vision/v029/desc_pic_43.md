# Picture 43

## Free description
This is a calm, sunny park with a pond on the right, a tall lamp post in the middle, a big tree on the left and a winding sandy path. A small girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes stands at the front left near the base of the tree, looking up and holding a string with both hands raised. The string runs a long way up across the path into the sky to a yellow diamond kite with a tail of small red and blue bows, so she is flying a kite. At the front right, a man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers is walking to the left, holding a red lead attached to a white dog with brown spots, which is walking ahead of him. The man and dog are quite large and close to the viewer, and they pass in front of the pond. There is an empty bench beside the path, a flower bed and a green litter bin at the front right. Everyone looks calm, and nothing seems to be going wrong or about to go wrong. Nothing looks impossible.
