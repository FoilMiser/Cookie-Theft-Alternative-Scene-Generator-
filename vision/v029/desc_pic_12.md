# Picture 12

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, and a wooden fence across the back. In the middle of the lawn, an older man with grey hair and a blue-grey cap is digging with a long-handled shovel, lifting soil out of a small hole next to a mound of dirt. Behind him, a pair of blue jeans and a striped yellow and red towel hang pegged on a washing line between two posts. On the right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands on the paved path with one arm raised, flying a yellow kite high in the sky on a long string, and looking up at it. The strangest thing is in the tree behind the house: a bright orange fish is sitting on a bare curved branch, which is impossible, since a fish belongs in water. Neither the man nor the boy is paying any attention to the fish. A wheelbarrow and a watering can stand unused at the front, and a coiled hose reel sits by the flower bed.
