# Picture 24

## Free description

This is a sunny playground on grass, with a dark metal railing across the back and greenery behind it. On the left, two small children are sitting on a red seesaw, facing each other and holding handles. The child in the yellow top and orange shorts on the left end is a little lower, and the girl in the pink dress on the right end is a little higher; neither end touches the ground and both children's feet dangle. In the centre at the back stands a tall dark swing frame with one red seat, which is empty and hanging still, and a tall slide with a red frame and orange and yellow chute stands at the right, also empty. In the lower right, a boy or young man in a red cap, blue hoodie, black trousers and white shoes stands by himself eating a pink ice cream with his eyes squeezed shut. A green bin stands between the swing and the slide, a grey rock lies at the bottom right and a large tree is on the far left. The two children seem to be playing together while the ice cream eater takes no part. Nothing looks about to go wrong and nothing seems impossible.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The ice cream eater's scrunched face with slanted closed eyes could be read as enjoyment or as a wince.
- His hand hides nearly all of the cone, so the pink scoop could at a glance read as a small ball or a sweet.
- The seesaw tilt is slight, so it could be mid-motion or nearly balanced.
- The child in yellow could be a boy or a girl, and the eater could be an older boy or a young man.
- The boy's head is level with the bottom of the slide chute and he looks close to it, so it is a little crowded, but he does not seem to be touching it.
