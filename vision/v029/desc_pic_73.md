# Picture 73

## Free description
This is a busy paved town street on a sunny day, with four coloured shop buildings and striped awnings along the back. At the lower left an elderly woman with a grey bun and dark round glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, is walking to the right with her nose in an open book. A cardboard box sits on the ground directly in front of her feet, so it looks like she is about to trip over it because she isn't looking where she is going. In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress is watering flowers in a planter by a shopfront with a teal watering can. An old man in a flat cap and waistcoat sits on a bench eating a pink ice cream cone, and a white dog with brown patches and a blue neckerchief is sitting up on its hind legs at the end of the bench, begging for it. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie holds up a camera and appears to be photographing that scene. A red bird is perched on top of the lamp post. There is also an unattended fruit stall with another cardboard box beside it, a bicycle parked in a metal rack, an empty café table with two chairs, a green bin and a street tree.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The boy's camera could be aimed at the old man and the dog, or at the woman with the watering can, or at the reading woman further left. All are in the direction he faces.
- The elderly woman could be read as about to trip over the box, or about to step past it, since it is slightly to one side of her front foot. Her eyes are hidden by the dark glasses, which could also be sunglasses, odd for reading.
- The dog could be begging for the ice cream or just sitting up to be friendly. The man does not look like he is responding to it.
- The dog's pose is a bit awkward, with its legs and body stretched and hard to read.
- The water seems to fall in front of the planter's face as well as onto the flowers, so some of it looks like it is missing the soil.
- The bird's feet are on the lamp top but it overlaps the building's window behind it, which makes it a little hard to separate from the background.
