# Picture 09

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate and pale path on the right. In the middle a girl or woman with dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes holds the end of a long blue-green hose out in front of her with both hands and bends her head towards it, as if peering into the nozzle, with a single blue drop of water at its tip. The hose runs back along the ground to a reel near the flower bed. A brown dog with a red collar stands on the lawn with its back paw right on the hose, and the hose swells into a fat bulge on the reel side of that paw, as if the water is blocked there. It looks as though, when the dog steps off, the water will burst out into the person's face. At far left by the door a small white dog with brown patches and a blue collar sits with a red lead in its mouth, the end of the lead lying in a loop on the grass. At the front right an elderly person with glasses, a teal top and a grey-blue skirt stands with hands on hips wearing an upside-down metal bucket on their head as if it were a hat, which is very odd. Neither the elderly person nor the dogs seem to be doing anything about the hose.

- Small white dog with brown patches, far left, beside the door: blue collar; sitting upright, holding a red lead in its mouth.
- Elderly person, right of centre, front: glasses, a little grey hair showing, teal long-sleeved top with a white strap down the front, grey-blue skirt, bare legs, brown shoes; standing facing forward with hands on hips and a bucket on the head.
- Hose and reel, left to centre: teal-blue hose running from a dark reel by the flower bed, along the grass, under the brown dog's back paw and up to the person's hands; a thick bulge sits just before the paw.

- Red lead, far left: one end in the white dog's mouth, the other a loop on the grass.

- House, far left: cream walls, red roof, window, blue door with a grey step.
- Tree, left, behind the house and fence: scenery.
- Wooden fence with a gate, back and right; paved path, front right: garden boundary and way in.
- Sun, top right, and two clouds, top centre: fine weather.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The person with the hose has her face mostly hidden by hair as she bends forward; she could be a girl or a young woman, and could be looking into the nozzle or just adjusting it. The drop at the tip makes peering in the likelier reading.
- Whether the brown dog knows it is standing on the hose is unclear; it looks away and seems unaware.
- The fat section of hose could be read as a bulge from trapped water, or as a thicker joint in the hose; its position right before the dog's paw makes trapped water likelier.
- The bucket on the head is plainly strange; it could be a joke, or the person might not be able to see well, though the glasses are visible just below it.
- The elderly person could be a woman or a man. The garment looks like a skirt but is quite round and bulgy, so it could also be read as baggy shorts.
- The elderly person's legs look stiff, straight and tube-like, slightly doll-like.
- The white dog holding the lead looks as if it wants a walk, but this is a guess, and nobody is attending to it.
- A short second stub of hose curls by the reel, which looks a little untidy but is not impossible.
