# Picture 28

## Free description
This is a sunny town square or paved shopping street with a row of four coloured shop buildings along the back. On the left, a boy in a yellow shirt is jumping with both arms up and a little girl in a pink dress is pointing up at a blue-and-white beach ball that is stuck in a tree. In the middle, a man in a green shirt sits on a bench reading a red book, and a small blue bird on the bench right beside him is next to his sandwich, so it looks about to take it while he isn't looking. Just right of him, an old woman with glasses is holding a watering can near a planter of flowers, but the stream of water curves away and upward into the air with drops at the top instead of landing on the flowers. Behind her, a worker in an orange cap and safety vest is sweeping a pile of autumn leaves with a broom. At the far right there is a fruit stall, and a person in a red cap and blue hoodie is walking past it with a yellow shopping bag. In the front right, a man in a red shirt is walking along reading an open book and is heading straight for a cardboard box on the ground, so he seems about to trip over it. In the front, a brown dog in a red bandana sits up holding an open newspaper as if reading it, which is impossible. A teal bicycle is parked in a rack at the back and a red bird flies overhead.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water: it could be read as squirting upward out of the can in a curve, or as a stream falling from somewhere in the air down to the can. Either way it does not go onto the flowers and looks physically wrong. The drops at the top seem to hang in the air.
- The jumping boy could be trying to reach the ball, or just cheering or laughing, since his eyes are closed and he is quite far from the tree. His shadow also sits low and to the right of him, near the potted plant.
- The blue bird could be simply perched near the sandwich or about to steal it.
- The person with the yellow bag could be a boy or a girl and could be just leaving the stall or simply passing.
- The dog's pose looks odd: long body, hind legs stuck out, long tail, a bit like a kangaroo. A dog reading a newspaper is impossible, though it may be intended as a joke.
- The sweeper stands right against the lamp post area and the shop front, so it takes a moment to separate him from the background.
