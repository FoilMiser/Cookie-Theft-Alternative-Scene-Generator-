# Review of pair_9: seated lower body, row a vs row b

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the thighs run almost level from a rounded seat to a squared knee, and the lower legs drop straight down as stiff, near-parallel columns, with the feet set right under the knees. In row b the thighs slope clearly downward from hip to knee, the legs are rounder and more tube-like, the knees sit lower and the shins angle slightly. The feet are bigger and set a little further forward. Row a reads as sitting on an ordinary chair. Row b reads more like perching on the front edge of a high seat, or sliding off it.

## 2. Per figure

1. **Man reading: a.** His level thighs and upright shins give a clear chair-sitting pose, while in b his thighs tilt down and he looks as if he is slipping forward.
2. **Woman with ice cream: a.** Her row a legs are a clean level-thigh sit, while in b the thighs slope and a skin-coloured lump (her lowered hand) shows awkwardly at the knee.
3. **Older man with drink: a.** His row a lap is level and stable, while in b the thighs droop steeply, a dark shape shows behind the knee, and the pose looks slumped for an older man.
4. **Teenager reading: no difference.** His thigh slope in b is mild and his legs read about as well in both rows, so neither is clearly better.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- All four: the lower legs are long, rigid columns, and the near and far shin nearly overlap, so each pair of legs reads as one thick block with a seam line down the middle.
- All four: the knees are squared corners rather than rounded bends, which looks stiff for a friendly cartoon.
- All four: the seat (buttock) is a round bulge sticking out behind, and it hangs in the air a little because the seat is not drawn.
- Man and woman: the dark shoes are small and flat next to the long shins, so the feet look tucked under rather than planted.
- Older man: a thin pale vertical line (strap or cane?) runs from his hip down past the thigh toward the shoe, and it is unclear what it is.
- Woman: her far arm hangs straight down behind the thigh, and a sliver of it shows at the hip, which reads as an odd extra shape.

### Row b
- All four: the thighs slope downward from hip to knee, so the figures look perched on a high stool edge or sliding off, not seated.
- All four: the legs are soft, uniform tubes with weak knee definition. At the small size the knees almost vanish and the legs look rubbery.
- Man, woman, older man: the feet are large and pushed forward, which adds to the slipping-forward look.
- Woman: a skin-coloured blob (her hand) sits at the knee and thigh joint and reads as a lump on the leg.
- Older man: a dark shape shows behind his knee, and the same pale vertical line runs down from his vest; his droopy thighs make him look slumped.
- Teenager: the thighs are fine, but the lower legs splay slightly and the big white shoes pull the eye.

## 4. Choice

**Row a**, fairly sure. Its level thighs and upright shins give the clearest "sitting on a seat" read at both sizes, even though its legs are stiffer and blockier than row b's. Softening row a's knees would get the best of both.
