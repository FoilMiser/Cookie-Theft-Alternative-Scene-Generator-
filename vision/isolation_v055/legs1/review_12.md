# Review of pair_12 (seated lower body)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the hips stay narrow and set under the torso. The thighs are short, slim and slope down toward the knee, and the lower legs angle back, so each figure looks as if it is half-squatting in the air with no clear seat. In row b the seat shows as a rounded bulge behind the hips, the thighs are long, thick and nearly level, and the knees make a clear right angle over straight vertical shins with the feet flat on the ground. At thumbnail size, row b reads as "sitting on a chair" and row a reads as "crouching" or "hovering".

## 2. Per figure

- **Man reading:** b. His level thighs and square knee make an obvious sitting pose, while in a the downward-sloping thighs and backward-angled shins look like a squat.
- **Woman with ice cream:** b. The seated shape in b is clear, while in a the thighs slope and a skin-coloured blob shows between her knees, which muddles the pose.
- **Older man with drink:** b. In b the thighs and knees clearly read as seated, while in a the thin legs sloping down and back look unsupported.
- **Teenager reading:** b. In a the thighs slope steeply, which almost reads as leaning against a wall, while b shows a clear chair-sitting pose (though his legs look heavy for his size).

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- All four figures: there is no seat or buttock volume, and the hips end flat under the torso, so nothing suggests they are resting on a surface.
- All four figures: the thighs slope down from hip to knee, which reads as a half-squat rather than sitting.
- All four figures: the lower legs angle back toward the body instead of dropping straight, so the feet end up roughly under the hips and the pose looks unbalanced.
- All four figures: the knees are soft, rounded bends with little definition, and both legs blend into one tube for much of their length.
- Man reading and older man: the thighs are short against the length of the lower legs.
- Woman with ice cream: a tan, skin-coloured shape shows between her knees, apparently her other hand hanging down, and it looks like a stray object.
- Older man: a thin white line (a strap or cane) runs from his chest down to his knees, and a small blob sits between his knees at its end. Its purpose is unclear.
- Teenager: the thighs slope most steeply of the four, so he looks as if he is sliding off the seat.
- Thumbnails: all four read as crouching or floating.

### Row b
- All four figures: the seat bulge at the back is large and balloon-like, sticking out beyond the back of the torso, which makes the hips look padded.
- All four figures: the thighs are very thick, and the near and far thighs merge into one mass separated only by a faint inner line.
- All four figures: the lower legs are long and wide, like stiff columns, with almost no shape at calf or ankle, and the trousers look oversized.
- All four figures: the knee is a sharp, boxy corner, and the far knee is barely shown.
- Woman with ice cream: her hips and thighs look much heavier than her slim upper body.
- Teenager: his lower body looks too large for his smaller torso and head, so he looks top-light.
- Older man: the same thin white line from chest to lap is present, which is less odd here but still unexplained.
- Feet are fine in all four (flat, side by side, at a sensible spot under the knees).

## 4. Choice

**Row b**, fairly sure. It reads unmistakably as sitting, even at picture size, which matters most for a clear cartoon. Its faults are proportion (heavy hips, thighs and shins) and could be slimmed down, while row a's fault is the pose itself.
