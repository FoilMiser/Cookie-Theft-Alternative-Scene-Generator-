# Review of pair_1.png: seated lower body, row a vs row b

## 1. How the rows differ

Row a draws a classic chair sit: a rounded seat that sticks out behind the torso, a flat thigh running level to a squared knee, and a near-vertical lower leg ending in two shoes (the far shoe peeks out behind the near one). Row b has no flat underside to the seat at all: the hip curves straight from the torso into a thigh that slopes down toward a rounded knee, and the lower leg ends in one larger shoe. In row b the figures look like they are perched on an edge or sliding off, not resting on a seat.

## 2. Per figure

- **Man reading (red shirt): a.** The level thigh and rounded seat in a read clearly as sitting on a chair, while in b the downward-sloping thigh and seat with no underside make him look as if he hovers or slides forward.
- **Bearded man (green shirt): a.** In a his hands rest on a knee that sits level with the hip, a calm seated pose; in b the thigh drops so steeply that the hands-on-knees pose looks like he is about to stand or is squatting.
- **Worker in safety vest: a.** Row a gives a solid seat and lap under the book; in b the curved, unsupported hip and sloped thigh give no lap, and at picture size the pose looks like riding or crouching.
- **Older man (cap, brown vest): a.** Row a's level thigh reads as sitting despite the heavy seat bulge; in b the thick dark waistband flares out at the back like a rim and the thigh slopes down, so he looks perched.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- All four: the seat is drawn as a rounded bulge that sticks out behind the back line of the torso, a bit like a cushion or padding; it is largest on the older man and the bearded man.
- All four: the top line of the thigh is almost dead flat and the knee is a hard square corner, which looks stiff and blocky.
- All four: the lower legs are long compared with the torso (knee to sole is about as long as shoulder to seat), so the figures look tall-legged.
- All four: the far shoe shows as a small dark shape behind the near shoe, which adds clutter at picture size, though it reads correctly.
- Man reading and worker: the shin narrows and slants slightly forward to the ankle, so the trouser bottom looks pinched above the shoe.
- Older man: the waistband is a thick dark band with a seam line that sits on top of the seat bulge and looks like a separate tube.

### Row b
- All four: there is no seat surface; the underside of the hip curves upward into the back, so nothing looks like it is resting on a chair.
- All four: the thigh slopes clearly downward from hip to knee, so the knee sits well below the hip and the pose reads as perching, leaning, or sliding off the edge.
- All four: the crotch is a small notch where the shirt meets the trouser, and the thigh looks thinner than the torso above it.
- All four: only one shoe is drawn and it is large and flat; the feet sit a little forward of the knee, adding to the sliding-forward look.
- Bearded man: the steep thigh plus hands on the knee looks like he is pushing himself up to stand.
- Older man: the dark waistband flares out behind his back like a rim or a tyre.
- At picture size, all four of row b look like they are squatting or riding something unseen, not sitting.

## 4. Choice

Row **a**. Its pose reads at once as "sitting on a chair", even at picture size, which matters most for a clear cartoon. Its faults (the seat bulge, the blocky knee) are matters of polish, while row b's fault (no seat, so the figure seems to hover or slide) is a fault in how the pose reads. **Fairly sure.**
