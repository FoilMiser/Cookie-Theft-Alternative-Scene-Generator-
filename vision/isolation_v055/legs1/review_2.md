# Review of pair_2 (seated lower body, rows a and b)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a every figure is compact and upright: the hips sit almost straight under the torso, the thighs are short or steep, and the lower legs drop more or less straight down, so the men look like they are squatting and the girl and child look like they are crouching mid-jump. In row b the hips are pushed back behind the torso and the thighs run forward. The men's legs make one long slope down to feet far in front, and the girl's and child's thighs are close to level with the lower legs hanging from the knee, so all four read as sitting.

## 2. Figure by figure

1. Bearded man reading: **b**. Row b reads as a man sitting low with his legs out in front, while row a reads as a squat with his feet tucked straight under his knees and nothing to sit on.
2. Older man reading: **b**. Same as the bearded man: row b reads as sitting, while in row a the dark seat looks like a separate lump hanging behind short, steep thighs, and the pose looks like a squat.
3. Girl, high end, legs hanging: **b**. In row b the seat sits back, the thigh is near level and the shins hang from a clear knee, while in row a the thigh slopes steeply down so she looks like she is standing in a crouch or hopping.
4. Child, high end, legs hanging: **b**. Row b has a clear seat behind the body, level thighs and hanging shins, while row a's steep thigh and folded legs make the child look like they are jumping rather than sitting.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Bearded man: the thigh is very short and points almost straight at the viewer, so the knee hides behind the hands and the lap barely shows.
- Bearded man: the lower legs are vertical with both feet flat right under the knees, which reads as a squat or crouch. The seat is only a small rounded curve in the air, with nothing under it.
- Bearded man: the two shoes are stacked one above the other and look like a double foot.
- Older man: the seat is a dark rounded blob that sticks out behind the vest and looks pasted on rather than part of the trousers.
- Older man: as with the bearded man, the thigh is short, the knee is hidden, and the lower legs and feet sit straight under the knees, so he squats instead of sitting.
- Girl: there is no seat. The hip sits directly under the torso and the dress runs down to a knee in front, so the thigh is steep rather than resting on anything.
- Girl: the lower legs fold back under her, with the feet together under the hips and pointing down, which reads as a jump or a crouch, not legs hanging from a seat.
- Child: same as the girl. The shorts drop straight down from the waist, the thigh is steep, the knees jut forward and the feet tuck under, so the child reads as hopping.
- Child: the bottom edge of the shorts is close to vertical, which makes the hip look very narrow from the side.

### Row b
- Bearded man: the seat is a very large bulging mass that spreads far back behind the torso. It looks like a sack or an oversized behind, and it is the heaviest shape in the figure.
- Bearded man: the leg runs as one nearly straight slope from the hip to the ankle with no visible bend at the knee. The knee is lost under the hands and book, so the leg looks stiff.
- Bearded man: the feet sit far out in front and very low, which makes the figure wide and bottom-heavy.
- Older man: the dark seat is also oversized and rounded. Together with the dark trousers it makes a large dark wedge.
- Older man: the leg is the same straight slope with no visible knee, and the two feet sit one behind the other at the far end.
- Girl: the seat bulge behind the dress is quite round and pushed far back, which is a little exaggerated.
- Girl: the feet hang at different heights and both point down at an angle, which is acceptable but slightly limp.
- Child: the shorts make a large boxy shape from the seat to the knee, and the line where the shirt meets the shorts runs nearly level across a wide hip.
- Child: the lower legs angle slightly back under the knee rather than hanging straight down, and the two shoes overlap.

### Other things that look wrong
- In both rows the girl and the child hold one hand up at the open mouth and the other as a fist at the chest. This looks like blowing or whistling and is odd for someone simply riding a seesaw.
- In both rows the men's books float with the hands under them, and in row a the books rest almost on the knees.

## 4. Choice

**Row b**, fairly sure. All four figures in row b clearly read as sitting, at both the large and the small size, while row a reads as squatting or jumping. Row b's oversized seat on the two men and their stiff, kneeless legs are its main weaknesses and should be tidied up.
