# Review of pair_4 (seated lower body, rows a and b)

## 1. How the rows differ

Row a keeps the lower body slim. On the front figures the knees sit just below a narrow shorts or skirt hem, and on the side figures the thigh slopes down from a small hip, so there is no flat seat. Row b makes the lower body much bigger. On the front figures the shorts and skirt widen into round knee shapes that come toward the viewer, and on the side figures the thigh lies close to level with a flat, rounded seat under it, so the figure clearly sits on something.

## 2. Figure by figure

1. Child on swing, front: **a**. Row a is neater and fits the torso, while in row b the orange shorts bulge wider than the shirt and look puffy, like a nappy.
2. Girl on swing, front: **no difference**. Row b shows bent knees a little more clearly, but its skirt is just as bulky; row a is tidier but looks less seated, so they come out even.
3. Child on slide, side: **b**. Row b has a level thigh, a flat seat and a clear bend at the knee, so the child reads as sitting on a slide; row a reads as leaping or falling backwards.
4. Girl on slide, side: **b**. Row b's dress sits on a flat, rounded seat with the thigh forward, while in row a the legs run almost straight from the hip and nothing shows that she is sitting.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Child on slide (side): there is no seat. The hip is a small wedge and the thigh drops steeply, so the body hangs in the air like a jump rather than resting on a slide.
- Child on slide (side): the knee bend is slight, so thigh and shin read as one long, nearly straight leg.
- Girl on slide (side): the legs come straight out of a narrow dress with almost no knee bend and no flat bottom, so she looks to be falling backwards.
- Girl on slide (side): the dress ends in a narrow, angled hem that cuts across the thigh, so the thigh hardly shows.
- Child on swing (front): the thighs are very short and the knees are small bumps under the hem, so at picture size the figure could pass for standing in short shorts.
- Girl on swing (front): the lower legs look long against very short thighs, which also weakens the seated read.

### Row b
- Child on swing (front): the shorts flare out wider than the torso and the knees are big orange circles, which looks puffy, like a nappy.
- Girl on swing (front): the skirt balloons out wider than the hips and the knees push against it, so it looks heavy.
- Child on slide (side): the shorts are a large, heavy block. The thigh is very thick and the seat is a deep rounded lump, which makes the lower body look too big for the small torso.
- Girl on slide (side): the dress turns into a large pink blob around the seat and thighs, so the seat and thighs do not read as separate parts.
- Both side figures: the thick thigh then narrows sharply into a thin shin, and the change in width at the knee is abrupt.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- Side figures: both feet are stacked on top of each other with the far foot showing only as a thin edge, so they look like one double-soled shoe.
- Side figures: the arms reach far up and forward with clasped hands, which looks more like diving than holding on or sliding.
- Front figures: the hands sit on the chest as fists rather than holding swing chains. Since the chains are not drawn, the pose looks odd.

## 4. Choice

**Row b**, fairly sure. It gives a clear, readable sit on the slide figures, where row a fails, even though the shorts and skirt are heavy and could be slimmed. On the front swing figures row a is a little neater, but the gap there is small.
