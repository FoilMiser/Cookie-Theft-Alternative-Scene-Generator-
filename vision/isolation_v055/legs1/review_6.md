# Review of pair_6 (seated lower body, rows a and b)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the hips are narrow and the underside of the seat slopes. The thighs are short stubs that end almost at the hem, and the long lower legs slant well forward, so the feet sit far out in front. The figures look as if they are leaning back or half squatting rather than sitting. In row b the hips are wider, the underside of the seat is flat and level, and a clear lap runs forward to rounded knees. The lower legs drop more steeply, with the feet closer to under the knees, so each figure reads as sitting on a seat.

## 2. Per figure

- Woman reading: **b**. Her flat seat and visible lap make her clearly seated, while in a she looks like she is sliding off or leaning back on nothing.
- Older woman with ice cream: **b**. The skirt in b sits on a level seat line with knees that read as knees, while in a the thin skirt and slanted shins make her look perched on an edge.
- Child with ice cream: **b**. In b the shorts sit flat with thighs coming forward, while in a the thighs are hidden and the legs angle out like a half-crouch.
- Girl with drink: **b**. Her whole body leans back strongly in a with the feet thrust far forward; b is more upright and grounded, though not perfect.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- All four: the thighs are barely visible. The knees sit right at the hem of the dress, skirt or shorts, so there is no readable lap.
- All four: the underside of the seat slopes up toward the knees instead of lying flat, so there is nothing that looks like sitting on a surface.
- All four: the lower legs are long and slant well forward, and the feet land far in front of the knees. The pose reads as reclining or bracing, not sitting.
- Woman reading: the knees are small bumps under the hem, and the shins look long and stiff, like poles.
- Older woman: the skirt is thin and the legs come out of it at an angle, so she looks perched on the very front edge of the seat.
- Child: the shorts are bunched high and the thighs are almost lost. At the small size the legs look like a crouch.
- Girl with drink: the torso and pelvis tilt far back, so the whole figure leans, and the shoes stick out far ahead. This is the weakest figure in row a.
- At picture size, all four look tipped back or half-standing.

### Row b
- All four: the seat is wide and boxy, with a hard flat bottom edge. It looks a little heavy, especially in the dresses.
- All four: the lower legs are long compared with the thighs and still slant somewhat forward, so the feet are a bit ahead of the knees.
- Woman reading: the dress puffs out into a big rounded hip shape, and the shins are long, tapered pillars.
- Older woman: the skirt is very wide and flat-bottomed, close to a block shape.
- Child: the orange shorts look bulky, the thighs are thick for a child, and the left shin has an odd bump at the knee.
- Girl with drink: the dress swells into a large round bulge at the hip. One thigh shows only as a short piece under the hem, and there is a small notch or kink in the hem outline near the knees.
- Other (both rows): the girl with the drink has a stray vertical line on her back where the arm meets the torso.

## 4. Choice

**Row b**, fairly sure. It reads clearly as seated figures at both the large size and picture size, which suits a friendly, clear cartoon style. Its faults are bulk and proportion; row a's fault is a pose that does not read as sitting at all.
