# Review of pair_8 (seated lower body, side view)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the figures sit as if on an ordinary chair. The seat is a big rounded mass with a flat underside, the thighs are level, the knees are at hand height, and the lower legs drop almost straight down to feet placed just in front of the knees. In row b the seat is a rounded bottom with no flat underside, set lower than the knees. The thighs slope up to the knees, and the lower legs angle well forward so the feet sit far in front of the knees. The b figures look as if they are leaning back on a low seat or sliding off it.

## 2. Figure by figure

1. Child in shorts, side: **a**. The level thigh, the knee above the foot and the flat seat line read at once as sitting on a chair. In b the low, rounded shorts and the forward-thrust shins make the child look as if it is tipping backward.
2. Girl in dress, side: **a**. The dress forms a clear flat lap and the shins come straight down. In b the lap slopes and the feet sit far out in front, so at picture size she looks half-reclined.
3. Woman reading, side: **a**. Her upright reading posture fits the level lap and the vertical shins in a. In b the slanted dress hem and the long diagonal shins clash with her upright torso.
4. Older woman in skirt, side: **a**. Despite the ballooning skirt, a reads as a proper seated pose. In b the skirt curves up to a pointed knee and the legs stretch forward, so she looks as if she is perched on the edge of something low.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- All four: the seat mass bulges a long way behind the back line, like a cushion stuck to the hips. This is worst on the child (orange shorts) and the older woman (skirt).
- Child: the shorts are puffy and much deeper than the thigh would need. The far lower leg shows only as a thin strip behind the near one.
- Girl: the lap is a large pink block, so her thigh looks short compared with the length of her lower leg.
- Woman reading: two separate rounded bumps sit at the knee, where both knees are drawn side by side, which looks slightly lumpy. The lower legs are very long next to the short visible thigh.
- Older woman: the knee shows as a separate round shape above the clasped hands and the skirt hem. The skirt balloons backward into a near-oval blob.
- All four: the shins are very long and the two shoes are staggered, which is acceptable but adds clutter at the small size.

### Row b
- All four: the hips sit lower than the knees, so the thighs slope upward. With no seat drawn and a rounded underside, the figures look as if they are tipping backward or floating.
- All four: the lower legs angle strongly forward and the feet land far in front of the knees, which reads as legs stretched out or sliding forward rather than sitting.
- Child: the shorts form a curved sausage that pinches in at the knee. The shoes stack into one and the far foot is almost hidden.
- Girl: the dress hem slopes and her knee comes to a narrow point under her hands. The pose looks like a low crouch at picture size.
- Woman reading: the diagonal dress hem and forward legs do not match her upright reading torso. There is a small stray dark tick on the yellow dress near the hip.
- Older woman: the skirt underside curves up into a pointed knee. The thin, straight thigh line and the long diagonal shins make her look slumped.

## 4. Choice

**Row a**, fairly sure. Its oversized seat bulge is a real fault, but row a reads clearly as "sitting on a chair" at both sizes. Row b's sloping thighs and forward feet make every figure look as if it is leaning back or slipping off its seat.
