# Review of pair_11 (seated lower body, rows a and b)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the seat of each figure is a flat, level underside, the thighs come toward the viewer, and the lower legs drop almost straight down, so the feet sit under or just in front of the knees. In row b the seat is rounder and hangs lower, the thighs are longer and slope down toward the knees, and the lower legs angle forward, so the feet end up well in front of the knees. Row b makes the figures look as if they are perched on an edge or sliding forward and leaning back. Row a looks like a plain, upright sit.

## 2. Per figure

1. Woman in a dress reading: **a**. Her knees sit level over upright shins with the feet under them, so she reads as sitting. In b her long, sloping thighs and forward feet make her look as if she is slipping off the seat.
2. Older woman in a skirt with an ice cream: **a**. Her skirt makes a clean, level lap with the knees together and the shoes under them. In b her lap tilts down and her feet push out ahead of her.
3. Child in shorts with an ice cream: **no difference**. Both rows read as a seated child. Row a is more upright but has a bulky shorts block. Row b slopes a little but looks natural for a small child whose feet do not reach the floor.
4. Girl in a dress with a drink: **a**. In b her torso leans far back and her legs stretch out forward, so she looks reclined or falling backward, not seated. In a she sits upright.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Woman reading: the seat is a hard, flat ledge that sticks out behind the hips. The dress over the thighs is lumpy, and the hem bulges at the knees.
- Woman reading: the thighs are short and stubby next to long shins, and the shins narrow sharply to small ankles.
- Older woman: the skirt underside is a flat shelf, and the seat bulges out behind the back. Her shins are thin and long compared with her thighs.
- Child: the orange shorts make a heavy block, and the seat sticks out well behind the torso. The knees are boxy.
- Girl with drink: the dress seat juts backward as a shelf. The thighs are very short (strongly foreshortened), and the shins are thin and stick-like.
- Girl with drink: even in row a the torso leans back a little and the back is very straight. The arm holding the cup looks stiff.
- In general, the knees sit very high and the lower legs look long, which makes the figures look a bit leggy.

### Row b
- All four: the thighs slope down to the knees, and the feet land well in front of the knees, so the figures look perched on an edge or sliding forward rather than seated.
- All four: the seat hangs as a round curve below the hips with no flat underside, so nothing tells you where the seat would be.
- Woman reading: the long thighs merge into the dress with a soft, saggy hem. Her shoes reach far forward, and her large feet stick out.
- Older woman: the skirt hem is uneven, and the lap tilts. The shins splay forward and look over-long.
- Child: the shorts and thighs blend into one soft shape, and the knees are poorly defined. The feet are a little far forward.
- Girl with drink: the worst of the set. The torso leans far back, the dress forms a long diagonal, and the legs stick out in front, so she looks like she is reclining or tipping over backward.
- At picture size, the row b figures read as slanted and leaning, especially the first and fourth.

## 4. Choice

**Row a**, fairly sure. Its poses are upright and easy to read as "sitting on a seat", which suits a friendly, clear cartoon style. Its faults are matters of shape (flat seat shelves, stubby thighs) rather than a wrong pose. Row b's forward-sliding, leaning-back posture is the bigger problem.
