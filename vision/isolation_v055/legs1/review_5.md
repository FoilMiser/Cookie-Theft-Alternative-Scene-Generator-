# Review of pair_5 (seated lower body, rows a and b)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the two men sit low with a big rounded seat at floor level and their legs run forward and down on a long slant to feet far in front. The girl and child bend at the hip and knee, so the thighs angle forward and the shins fold back under. In row b the men sit "chair style": a short level thigh, then the shin dropping straight down to feet under the knee. The girl and child are drawn more upright and straighter, with only a small bend at the hip.

## 2. Per figure

- **Bearded man (low end): b.** The level thigh and straight-down shin make the knee bend easy to read, while in a the legs look like one long straight slant with no visible knee.
- **Older man (low end): b.** Same reason: b shows a clear thigh, knee and shin, though its seat lump is ugly. In a the leg reads as stretched out rather than bent.
- **Girl (high end, legs hanging): a.** In a the thigh angles forward and the shins hang and tuck back, so she clearly reads as sitting. In b she is so upright that she looks like she is standing or hopping.
- **Child (high end, legs hanging): a.** In a the shorts show a forward thigh and a clear knee bend. In b the shorts hang straight down and the legs look like a jump, not a seated pose.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Bearded man: the seat is a large round bulge sitting on the floor and pushing out behind the back, like a cushion.
- Bearded man: the knee is hidden behind the hands and book, so thigh and shin merge into one long straight diagonal. The legs look stretched out, not bent.
- Bearded man: the two feet are stacked one above the other far forward. The upper foot floats with nothing under it.
- Older man: the same oversized round seat lump at floor level, here in dark trousers, so it reads as a heavy blob.
- Older man: there is no visible knee and the leg is one long slant. The upper foot floats above the lower one.
- Both men: at picture size they read as sitting on the ground hugging their knees, not as sitting on a seat.
- Girl: the dress bunches into a bulge behind the hip, so the seat pokes out backward a little.
- Girl: the shins fold back steeply, so the feet end up under the seat instead of hanging forward. They look tucked rather than dangling.
- Child: the shins also fold back under the body, and the bottom foot points down and back at an odd angle.

### Row b
- Bearded man: the seat is a tan lump that hangs below and behind the thigh, like a sagging pouch, and does not join the back cleanly.
- Bearded man: the thigh is short and the knee is hidden behind the hands, so the knee position is only implied.
- Bearded man: the two feet are stacked one above the other under the knee, and the upper foot floats.
- Older man: there is a dark blob under the vest at the back (the seat), like a saddlebag hanging off the hip. This is the worst seat in the image.
- Older man: the knee is hidden, and the shins are a stiff vertical block with stacked feet, the upper one floating.
- Girl: the torso and dress run almost straight down, and the thigh barely leaves the line of the body. There is almost no seat, and she reads as standing or leaping.
- Girl: the knees are bent forward and the feet are kicked back, so the pose looks like a jump, not legs hanging from a seat.
- Child: the shorts hang straight down, there is no forward thigh, and the knee bend comes from the shins kicking back. This looks like a hop, not sitting.
- Child and girl: at picture size neither one clearly reads as seated.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- Both children hold one hand up to the mouth with flat, oddly shaped fingers. The hand reads as a strange paddle in front of the face.

## 4. Choice

**Row a, toss-up.** Row b draws the men's knee bend more clearly, but its seat lumps are worse. It also makes both children look like they are standing or jumping instead of sitting with their legs hanging. Row a reads as "seated" for all four figures, even though the men look like they sit on the floor and their seat bulges are oversized.
