# Review of pair_10: seated lower body, row a vs row b

## 1. How the rows differ

In the front views, row a puts round, swollen cuffs (shorts) or lobes (skirt) around the knees, so the knees look like caps sitting in rings. Row b draws the shorts or skirt as a flatter band with the knees just below it. In the side views, row a has a flat seat and a nearly level thigh with a clear bend at the knee, so the figure reads as sitting. Row b has a rounded, sloping seat and a thigh that angles steeply down, so the figure leans back and looks as if it is jumping or falling instead of sitting.

## 2. Per figure

1. Child on swing, front: **b**. The shorts are a neat band with the knees just below, while row a's ballooning cuff rings around the knees look puffy and odd.
2. Girl on swing, front: **b** (slight). The skirt hem sits flatter over the knees, while row a's hem bulges out in two lobes at the sides of the knees.
3. Child on slide, side: **a**. The flat seat and nearly level thigh read clearly as sitting, while row b's sloped hips and steep thigh make the child look airborne.
4. Girl on slide, side: **a**. The long flat lower edge of the dress and the clear knee bend read as seated, while in row b the leg runs as one near-straight diagonal from hip to feet.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Child on swing (front): the shorts' leg openings swell into round rings around the knees, wider than the thighs, and the knees show as caps inside them. There is a hard, straight seat edge visible between the legs.
- Girl on swing (front): the skirt hem bulges into two rounded lobes over the knees. A hem or seat line shows through between the legs behind the knees.
- Both front figures: the lower legs are very long compared with the barely visible thighs. The feet hang far below and splay slightly outward.
- Child on slide (side): the shorts block is boxy and squared at the back of the seat. Otherwise it reads well.
- Girl on slide (side): the dress over the thigh is a long, heavy slab, and its front corner at the knee is sharp. The knee itself is hidden.

### Row b
- Child on swing (front): the shorts band is about as wide as the torso and quite blocky. The seat line between the legs still shows. The lower legs are long, as in row a.
- Girl on swing (front): the skirt is a bell shape with a narrow curved hem. A seat edge shows between the legs. The lower legs are long.
- Child on slide (side): the seat is rounded and slopes down with no flat base. The thigh drops at a steep angle, so the knee bend is weak. The whole body looks tilted back, as if leaping rather than sitting, and this is very clear at small size.
- Girl on slide (side): the hip curves down into the thigh, and the leg is close to one straight diagonal with little knee bend. A small hem flap sticks out at the knee. The figure looks as if it is reclining or sliding off, not seated, and the small view reads that way too.
- Both side figures: the torso appears to lean back more than in row a, because the hips move forward and down.

### Both rows
- The side feet are drawn as two stacked shoes that overlap almost completely. This is acceptable, but the far foot is barely readable at small size.

## 4. Choice

**Row a**, **fairly sure**. Row b's front views are slightly tidier, but its side views lose the sense of sitting, which is the bigger readability problem in a friendly, clear cartoon. Row a's puffy knee cuffs in the front views are a smaller, cosmetic fault.
