# Review of pair_7: seated lower body, row a vs row b

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the hips are small and set far back, the thighs are thin tubes that slope gently down to a low, rounded knee, and the back of the seat curves inward, so the figures look perched or half-squatting on nothing. In row b the seat is a large rounded buttock with a flat underside, the thighs run level to a clear knee, and the lower legs drop straight down to the feet, so each figure reads as sitting on an unseen chair. Row b's legs are also thicker and shorter, and the hands rest right on top of the knees.

## 2. Per figure

1. **Man reading (red shirt, blue jeans): b.** In a, the thin sloping thigh and the hollow under the hips make him look like he is leaning against a wall, while b gives him a level lap and a clear seat.
2. **Bearded man (green shirt, tan trousers): b.** In b his hands sit on a level knee and the seat is solid; in a the knee sags below the hands and the thigh looks like a narrow tube coming out from under the shirt.
3. **Worker in safety vest (blue trousers): b.** In a the trousers bend in a soft S from the vest down to the knee with no real seat; in b the seat, the level thigh and the upright shin make a clear sitting pose.
4. **Older man (brown vest, dark trousers): b.** In b the pose reads as seated even at picture size, though the dark seat is a big blob; in a he looks like he is hovering, and a dark waist ring sticks out behind his back.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- All four: the seat has almost no buttock. The back of the hips curves inward, so there is nothing to sit on and the figures look like they are hovering or squatting.
- All four: the thighs slope down from hip to knee instead of running level, and they are thin compared to the torso, so the lap is not a clear flat shape.
- All four: the knee is a soft rounded bend and sits lower than the hands, so the hands do not rest on it clearly (most visible on the bearded man and the older man).
- All four: the lower legs are long and slightly slanted, and the two legs mostly merge into one shape with only a faint second outline.
- Man reading and worker: the trousers start abruptly under the shirt or vest, like a narrow tube attached under the torso.
- Bearded man: the top of the thigh shows a doubled contour line near the hip.
- Older man: a dark belt or waist ring sticks out behind his back like a rim, separate from the body.
- At picture size, all four read as perched or floating rather than seated.

### Row b
- All four: the buttock is very large and balloon-like, bulging well behind the line of the back. On the man reading it sticks out past the torso, which looks heavy.
- All four: the thighs are very thick, which makes the trousers look padded.
- All four: the far lower leg shows as a thin strip behind the near shin, and the far shoe peeks out behind the near shoe. This is readable but a little cluttered.
- Older man: the dark trousers make the seat a big shapeless dark blob at small size, and the waist rim still sticks out slightly behind his back.
- Bearded man: the tan seat curves into a large rounded bulge at the back of the hip that looks swollen.

## 4. Choice

Row b, **sure**. It reads clearly as sitting at both sizes, with a level lap, a clear knee and upright shins. Its faults (a seat that is too big and thighs that are too thick) are about proportion, while row a's faults make the pose itself unclear.
