# Review of pair_3 (seated lower body, rows a and b)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a, the seat hangs as a rounded, sagging mass under the torso. The thighs are short and rise a little towards high knees, and long lower legs slant well forward to feet that sit almost on top of each other. The figures look as if they are sinking, leaning back, or hovering in a wall-sit. In row b, the seat has a flat bottom and the thighs are long and level, so the figure clearly rests on an unseen chair. The lower legs drop almost straight down, and the two feet are staggered, so both are visible.

## 2. Per figure

- **Child in shorts: b.** The level thigh and flat-bottomed shorts read as sitting at once, while row a's shorts sag and the long forward-slanting shin makes him look like he is sliding off.
- **Girl in dress: b.** Row b gives a clear right angle at hip and knee with two separate feet, while row a's dress sags into a low bowl and the feet are pushed far forward and stacked.
- **Woman reading: b.** Row a is the least bad of its row, but the knee still sits high above a low hip and the shin is too long; row b's level thigh is clearer, even though its seat bulges.
- **Older woman in skirt: b.** In row a the skirt sags under her like a hammock and the very long shin pushes the feet far out; row b reads as seated, though the skirt is puffed out.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- All four figures: the seat sags below the line of the thigh, as a rounded pouch, with no flat underside, so nothing suggests a seat surface.
- All four: the thighs are short and tilt up towards the knee, so the knees ride high, near chest/hand height.
- All four: the lower legs are much longer than the thighs and slant strongly forward, which puts the feet well in front of the knees, like a wall-sit or a reclined pose.
- All four: the two feet almost fully overlap (near foot drawn over far foot at the same spot), so they read as one thick shoe.
- Child: the orange shorts hang below and behind the torso like a bag, and the torso leans back, so he looks like he is tipping backwards.
- Girl: the pink dress drapes into a deep curve under the hips, and the bottom looks lower than the knees' support, so the pose is unclear at small size.
- Woman reading: the dress hem stops short and the thigh is barely visible; the knee joint is a high bump with a very long shin under it.
- Older woman: the dark skirt sags under her hips like a sling; her shins are the longest of all, and the feet are far forward of the body.
- Small versions: all four read as leaning back or floating rather than sitting.

### Row b
- All four: the seat (buttocks) bulges well behind the line of the back, which looks heavy and over-rounded.
- Child: the orange shorts look like a big inflated cushion, wider than the torso.
- Woman reading: a large rounded bulge at the back of the yellow dress makes the hip look swollen.
- Older woman: the blue-grey skirt balloons into a puffy oval, like a cushion or pouffe she is sitting on, rather than a skirt over a lap.
- All four: the near lower leg slants slightly forward and the near foot is pushed ahead of the far foot by a lot, which is slightly stiff, but acceptable.
- Girl: the dress hem at the knee is cut with a hard vertical edge, which looks a little boxy.
- Small versions: they read clearly as seated, and the bulging seats are less noticeable at that size.

## 4. Choice

**Row b**, fairly sure. The level thighs, flat seat and near-vertical shins read as "sitting on a chair" at both sizes, which row a does not manage. Row b's oversized, bulging seats are its main fault and should be slimmed.
