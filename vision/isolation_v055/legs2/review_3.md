# Review: pair_3 (seated lower body, row a vs row b)

## 1. How the rows differ

Row a draws a chair-sitting pose. The seat of the figure has a flat bottom edge, the thighs are level, the knees bend at about a right angle, and the shins drop almost straight down to feet placed under the knees. Row b gives each figure a rounded, sagging seat that hangs below the level of the knees. The thighs rise toward the knees, and the shins slope well forward, so the feet sit clearly in front of the knees. The figures look as if they are leaning back on a low, soft seat or sliding off it.

## 2. Per figure (left to right)

- **Child in shorts: a.** In row a the orange shorts sit flat and level, so the child reads at once as sitting on a chair. In row b the round, drooping shorts sit below the knees and the feet stick out in front, which reads as slumping or floating.
- **Girl in dress: a.** In row a the dress makes a level lap and the shins go straight down. In row b the bottom of the dress is a deep curve hanging below the knees, and the legs slant forward, so she looks like she is sinking.
- **Woman reading: a.** In row a the long level lap gives the book a clear base and the pose looks settled. In row b the dress bulges down into a rounded seat and the lower legs reach far forward, so she looks reclined and unsupported.
- **Older woman in skirt: a.** In row a the skirt has a firm flat bottom and the legs stand straight under the knees. In row b the skirt is a bowl shape hanging under the knees, and the slanted shins make her look as if she is sliding forward.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- All four: the seat of the figure ends in a hard, straight, horizontal bottom edge, which looks like a block or cushion rather than a body. It is stiff, though it would match a drawn chair seat.
- All four: the knees bend at a sharp right angle, and the shins are long straight posts, which looks a little wooden.
- Child: the shorts read as a separate rectangular block, with an extra orange band at the shirt hem that looks like a stacked layer. The thigh looks short and boxy.
- Girl: the dress lap is a squarish lump with a crease line through it. The knee is hidden under the hands, so the bend is hard to read.
- Woman reading: a long vertical line on the dress near the knee looks like a stray fold or a seam.
- Older woman: the legs are thin, pale tubes with lines drawn down their length. The knee shape is lost under the hands, and the shins look like stilts.
- All four: the two shoes are only slightly offset, so the far foot shows as a thin sliver under the near foot. This is acceptable but cramped.

### Row b
- All four: the seat of the figure is a rounded bulge that hangs below knee level, like a diaper or a beanbag shape. It would not rest on a flat chair seat.
- All four: the knees sit higher than the hips and the thighs slope upward. This reads as sinking or sitting on something very low that is not drawn.
- All four: the shins slant well forward, so the feet are far in front of the knees, which looks like sliding off the seat. At picture size the figures look as if they are leaning back on nothing.
- All four: the two shoes are stacked almost exactly on top of each other, so one foot is mostly hidden.
- Child: the orange shorts curve under as a balloon shape, and the leg comes out of them at an odd low point.
- Girl: the dress bottom is a deep, saggy curve, and the lap no longer has a clear top surface.
- Woman reading: the dress hangs down in a rounded mass, and the long forward lower legs make the figure look stretched.
- Older woman: the skirt is a bowl hanging under the thighs. A pale bulge at the knee is the only sign of the knee joint, and the legs slant like props.
- At picture size the whole row looks slightly reclined or floating compared with row a.

## 4. Choice

Row a. It reads clearly as "sitting on a chair" at both sizes and is easier to fit onto a drawn seat. Its faults are stiffness and boxiness, and they are minor next to row b's sagging seats and sliding-forward legs. How sure: fairly sure.
