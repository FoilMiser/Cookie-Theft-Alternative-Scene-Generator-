# Review of pair_2.png (seated lower body, rows a and b)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the thighs run nearly level from a rounded seat to the knee, the underside of the seat is a flat line as if resting on a chair, and long lower legs drop straight down to small, flat feet. In row b the thighs slope down from hip to knee so the knees sit lower than the hips, the seat has no flat underside, and the lower legs end in larger, blunter shoes. At picture size, row a reads as "sitting on a chair" and row b reads more as perched on, or sliding off, a high edge.

## 2. Per figure

- **Man reading:** a. His thighs are level and the seat sits on a clear flat line; in b the thighs tilt down and he looks as if he is slipping forward.
- **Woman with ice cream:** a. Row a gives a clean right angle at the knee; in b the thighs slope and the knees drop, so the pose looks less settled.
- **Older man with a drink:** a. Row b has the steepest thigh slope of the four and he looks perched on a stool edge; row a looks seated.
- **Teenager reading:** no difference. Row a has a large round lump at the seat, row b has a mild thigh slope, and at picture size both read about equally well.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Man, teenager (and to a lesser degree woman, older man): the seat bulges backward in a round, balloon-like lump behind the hips, larger than the body above suggests.
- All four: the lower legs are very long and straight relative to the thighs, so the figures look leggy and stiff.
- All four: the feet are small and flat compared with the thighs and hips.
- Woman: a small skin-coloured shape (a hand) hangs between the thighs near the knees and reads as an odd blob at picture size.
- Older man: the far knee is partly hidden and the near knee corner is sharp and boxy.

### Row b
- All four: the thighs slope down toward the knees, so the knees sit below the hips and the figures look as if they are sliding off the seat.
- All four: the seat has no flat underside, so nothing suggests a surface under them; they look perched on air.
- Older man: the slope is strongest here; he looks like he is on a high stool edge.
- All four: the shoes are oversized and blunt, and the ankles thick, which makes the feet the heaviest part of the lower body.
- Man, woman: the knee bend is soft and rounded, so the joint is less clear than in row a.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- Older man: a thin white line runs from his vest down past his hip to the knee area (perhaps a cane or a strap); it is unexplained and looks like a drawing error.
- Woman: her forearm holding the cone is drawn behind the upper arm with a long tan shape running down to the lap, which is hard to read.
- Teenager: the hood strings and pocket are fine, but the hidden far arm leaves the hoodie side looking bulky.

## 4. Choice

Row **a**, fairly sure. Its level thighs and flat seat line read clearly as sitting on a chair in a friendly cartoon style; its main faults (round seat bulge, long lower legs) are milder than row b's sliding-off look.
