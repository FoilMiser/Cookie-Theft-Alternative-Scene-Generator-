# Review of pair_11: seated lower body, row a vs row b

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the lower body narrows under the torso. Each thigh runs down at a steep slant from a thin hip to a pointed knee, and the underside rises toward the back, so no buttock or seat line is drawn and the figure looks like it is hanging or squatting in the air. In row b the pants make a full, rounded buttock with a flat underside, the thighs are level, the knees are squared off, and the lower legs drop straight down to the feet. That reads clearly as sitting on an unseen seat.

## 2. Per figure

1. Man reading (red shirt): **b**. The level thigh and rounded buttock give him a clear seat, while in row a the thin, slanting thigh makes him look like he is sliding off something.
2. Bearded man (green shirt): **b**. In row b his hands rest on knees that sit at the right height, while in row a the knee hangs below and in front of the hands and the hip is pinched to a point.
3. Worker in vest: **b**. In row a the vest bottom tapers into a narrow, slanting thigh that looks wasp-waisted and seatless, and row b fixes this with a solid hip and a level thigh.
4. Older man: **b**. In both rows the belt ring bulges out behind the back, but in row b it at least sits on a full seat, while in row a it floats above a thin, sagging thigh.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- All four: no buttock or flat seat underside. The bottom line climbs from the knee up to the back of the torso, so the figure seems to be held up by nothing (the hovering look is strongest in the small sizes).
- All four: the thighs slope steeply down from hip to knee, which reads as a half-squat or sliding forward rather than sitting.
- All four: the hip is pinched narrower than the torso above it, which gives a wasp-waist break where the shirt or vest meets the pants (worst on the worker and the red-shirt man).
- All four: the knees form a narrow, pointed bend that looks bony and kinked rather than rounded.
- Bearded man, older man: the knees are clearly lower than the resting hands, so the hands do not look like they rest on the knees.
- Older man: the dark belt or waistband sticks out behind the back like a separate ring or tyre, and the vest bottom overhangs it.
- All four: the two shoes are stacked one slightly above and behind the other, which looks like doubled soles rather than two feet side by side.

### Row b
- All four: the thighs and pants are thick and boxy, and the buttock is a large rounded block that looks a little heavy against the torso, mostly on the red-shirt man and the worker.
- All four: the lower legs flare slightly at the ankle (a bell-bottom look) and are long, so the feet sit a little low and far down.
- All four: the waist joint is a hard horizontal line with the pants slightly wider than the shirt at the back. It is clean but stiff.
- Older man: the belt ring still bulges out behind the back, the same as in row a.
- All four: the same stacked-shoe look as row a, with the far shoe raised and set behind the near one.

## 4. Choice

**Row b**, sure. It reads at once as a person sitting on a seat at both large and picture size. Row a's slanting, seatless legs look like hovering or squatting.
