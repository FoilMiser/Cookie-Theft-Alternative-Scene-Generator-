# Review of pair_12 (seated lower body, four figures)

## 1. How the rows differ

Row a sits each figure square: the underside of the seat is a flat line, the thighs run level, each knee makes a sharp right angle, the lower legs drop straight down, and both shoes show, one a little behind the other. Row b slopes the thighs down from hip to knee, rounds the knees, angles the lower legs a little forward, curves the underside of the seat upward at the back, and puts both feet into what reads as a single shoe. Without a seat drawn, row a reads as "sitting on a chair". Row b reads as perched, sliding forward, or sitting on air.

## 2. Per figure, left to right

1. Man reading: **a**. Level thighs and a flat seat line make him read as seated, while in b the blue seat bulges out behind his back and the down-sloping thigh makes him look as if he is slipping off.
2. Bearded man: **a**. In a his hands rest on top of a clear knee. In b the thigh drops away under his hands and his pose looks like a half squat.
3. Worker in vest: **a**. The level lap in a supports the book-reading pose, while in b the curved, lifted seat and sloping thigh make him look like he is leaning back on nothing.
4. Older man: **a**. In a his clasped hands sit on his knees. In b they float in front of the body above a thigh that slopes away, and the dark seat mass curls up behind like a cushion stuck to him.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- All four: the knee is a hard 90-degree corner and the lower leg is ruler-straight and vertical, so the legs look stiff and wooden.
- All four: the lower legs are long compared with the thighs, so the feet sit far below the knee and the figures look tall-legged for their seat height.
- All four: the trouser hems flare at the bottom, like bell-bottoms, which stands out at the large size.
- Man reading and worker: a crease line runs from the hip along the full thigh and turns down at the knee, which adds a tube-like look to the trousers.
- Older man: the waist area is a thick dark band with a dashed line that reads as a belt or inner tube rather than a waistband, and his seat looks bulky.
- Bearded man: the thigh and seat are wide and puffy compared with the slim lower leg.

### Row b
- All four: the underside of the seat curves up toward the back instead of sitting flat, so there is no sense of a surface under the figure; at the small size they look like they are floating.
- All four: the thighs slope down from hip to knee, which reads as slumping, sliding off a seat, or a half squat, not as relaxed sitting.
- All four: the two feet merge into one large shoe at the bottom, so you cannot tell there are two legs from the feet.
- Man reading: the seat of the trousers bulges out behind the line of the back, and a fold line at the waist cuts across the hip oddly.
- Bearded man and older man: the hands no longer rest on the knees; they hang in the air in front of the sloping thighs.
- Older man: the dark waist mass flares out behind like a curled cushion, worse than in a.
- Worker: the long sloping thigh plus the angled lower leg makes the leg look over-long and rubbery.
- Small versions: the figures look like they are hovering or falling backward. Row a's small versions read clearly as seated.

## 4. Choice

**Row a**, fairly sure. Its stiffness (square knees, straight shins, flared hems) is a style fault, but every figure clearly reads as sitting, with hands on knees or lap where they should be, at both sizes. Row b's softer knees are nicer in isolation, but the sloped thighs, upturned seat and merged feet make the pose unclear without a seat drawn.
