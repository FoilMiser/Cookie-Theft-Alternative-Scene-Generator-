# Review of pair_10: seated lower body, row a vs row b

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the lower body is folded tightly under the torso. The men's hips sit right above their feet, with the shins standing upright under the book, and the two children's legs hang almost straight down under the body. The result reads as crouching or squatting rather than sitting. In row b the hips are pushed back into a wide, rounded seat behind the torso. The men's legs then run on a long diagonal down and forward to the feet. The children's thighs angle forward to a knee in front of the body and the shins hang back down, so every figure reads as resting its weight on something.

## 2. Per figure

- **Bearded man (low end): b.** In row a his shins stand vertical under his knees and his feet sit right under his hips, so he looks like he is squatting. Row b gives him a clear seat behind him and legs reaching forward to the ground.
- **Older man (low end): b.** Same as the bearded man. In row a the dark seat blob floats at hip height above upright shins, a squat, while row b has a seat mass and a forward diagonal leg that read as sitting.
- **Girl (high end, legs hanging): b.** In row a her thighs go almost straight down and her knees poke forward a little, so she looks like she is jumping or standing with bent knees. In row b her hips are set back and her thighs angle forward to a knee, so she reads as perched with dangling legs.
- **Child (high end, legs hanging): b.** In row a the short shorts sit high and the legs drop knock-kneed below the body, like a standing crouch. In row b the shorts cover a forward thigh with a bent knee in front and the shin hanging below it, which reads as seated.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Bearded man: no horizontal thigh is visible; the seat is a small khaki bulge at the hip with the knee tucked up under the hands, so the pose reads as a squat.
- Bearded man: the shins are vertical and the feet are directly under the hips, with nothing in front of the body to suggest sitting.
- Bearded man: the two shoes are stacked one above the other and partly overlap, so the near foot hides the far one.
- Older man: the seat is a flattened dark blob stuck to the back of the hip, and it does not join cleanly onto the legs.
- Older man: he has the same vertical shins and feet under the hips, and reads as crouching.
- Older man: his shoes are stacked one above the other in the same way as the bearded man's.
- Girl: there is no seat; the dress falls straight to the knee, and the thighs point down rather than forward.
- Girl: the knees bend forward and the shins kick back, giving a jumping or crouched-standing look.
- Girl: the feet point down and backward (toes to the left, away from where she faces).
- Child: there is no seat; the shorts are short and sit high, and the thighs drop almost straight down.
- Child: the knees press together and the lower legs splay out, a knock-kneed look.
- Child: like the girl's, the feet point down and backward, away from the facing direction.
- At picture size, all four row-a figures look like they are squatting or hopping, not sitting.

### Row b
- Bearded man: the seat is a very large, flat-bottomed khaki slab sticking out well behind the torso, which looks heavy.
- Bearded man: from hip to ankle the leg is close to one straight diagonal with no clear knee bend, so the thigh and shin do not read as separate parts.
- Bearded man: his feet sit far forward of the body, so the overall pose leans toward sitting on the floor with legs stretched out.
- Older man: the dark seat slab is just as large and merges into the bottom of the vest, and a light shirt band shows between them.
- Older man: he has the same nearly straight diagonal leg, with no visible knee.
- Girl: the dress widens into a bulky hip and has a hem line crossing the thigh, so the shape is a bit lumpy.
- Girl: the thigh angles down at about 45 degrees rather than lying level, so she looks only half seated.
- Girl: the feet still point down and backward, the same fault as in row a.
- Child: a thin orange band shows between the yellow shirt and the shorts and looks like a stray strip.
- Child: the thighs slope down rather than lying level.
- Child: the feet point down and backward, the same fault as in row a.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows, outside the lower body)
- Both children hold one hand up at the mouth, with the fingers flat and pointing forward and the other fist below it. This reads oddly, like a whistle or a gasp.
- The men's far hand is mostly hidden behind the book. In row b a small part of it shows at the left page edge, which looks like a stray patch of skin.

## 4. Choice

**Row b, fairly sure.** Row b reads as sitting for all four figures, and row a reads as squatting or hopping, even at picture size. Row b's oversized seat slabs and the men's unbent legs are worth fixing, but they are smaller faults than a pose that does not read as sitting.
