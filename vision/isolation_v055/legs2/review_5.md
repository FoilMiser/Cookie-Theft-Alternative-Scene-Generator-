# Review of pair_5.png (seated lower body, rows a and b)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the hips are narrow and run straight down from the torso. The thighs are short and slope down toward the knees at a steep angle, and the shins are long and slant forward, so each figure looks perched or sliding forward rather than sitting. In row b there is a rounded seat behind the hips. The thighs are thicker and close to level, ending in a clear knee corner, with near-vertical shins under them, so each figure reads as sitting on something.

## 2. Per figure (left to right)

- **Man reading:** b. His level thighs and rounded seat clearly show sitting, while in row a his sloped thighs make him look like he is slipping off an unseen edge.
- **Woman with ice cream:** b. Her level teal thighs give a clear lap and knee, while in row a her thighs slope and her long shins make the legs look like stilts.
- **Older man with drink:** b. Under the belt the row b seat and level thighs look settled and weighty, while row a looks like he is half-standing and leaning back.
- **Teenager reading:** b. His thighs read as a lap even though his seat is bulky, while in row a his small sloped thighs and long thin shins make him look like he is hovering.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- All four: the thighs slope steeply down toward the knee, so the figures look like they are sliding off the seat or leaning on it, not sitting on it.
- All four: there is no seat mass. The hips are as narrow as the waist, with no buttock behind them for a seat to carry.
- All four: the thighs are short and the shins very long, so the lower legs look stretched and stilt-like, and most of all at the small size.
- All four: the knee is a soft, shallow bend with little sign of a joint.
- Man, woman: the far leg is set behind and slightly apart, so the gap between the legs shows background in a way that looks loose.
- Woman: a skin-coloured shape shows between her legs at knee height, where her hanging hand sits. It reads as an odd patch on the knee.
- Older man: his hand and the cane shaft also show between the legs at the knee, and they look tangled with the far leg.
- Teenager: the hoodie ends and the thin dark legs begin with almost no hip, which gives him a thin, stick-legged look.
- Feet: the shoes are acceptable and flat. Because they sit so far forward of the hips, the figure looks tipped back.

### Row b
- All four: the seat is a large rounded bulge behind the hips that looks puffy, as if padded. It is clearest on the teenager, whose black seat is a big blob below the hoodie.
- All four: the lower legs are straight, wide tubes that barely taper to the ankle, so the shins look stiff and heavy.
- All four: the shins are still long next to the thighs, so the knees sit high and the legs look a bit tall for the body.
- Woman: her teal thighs are very wide and blocky, heavier than her slim upper body.
- Teenager: the thighs are thick and boxy under a narrow hoodie, and the join between hoodie and trousers is abrupt.
- Man: the front shoe overhangs forward and looks slightly large for the straight shin.
- Feet: all are flat ovals pointing right, the same on every figure. That is fine, but the far foot is sometimes hidden behind or level with the near foot, which flattens the depth.
- Woman, older man: the hand or cane behind the legs still shows between the knees, as in row a, but it is less noticeable because the thighs are level.

## 4. Choice

Row b, fairly sure. Its level thighs and seat mass read as "sitting" at once, also at picture size, while row a looks like the figures are hovering or sliding. Row b's bulky seat and tube-like shins are faults, but they matter less than the posture problem in row a.
