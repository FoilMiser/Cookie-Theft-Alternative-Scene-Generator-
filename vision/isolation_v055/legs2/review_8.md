# Review of pair 8 (seated lower body, rows a and b)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the thighs run close to level from hip to knee, the bottom of the seat is a fairly flat line, and the knees make a clear corner above lower legs that drop down and a little forward. In row b the seat of the figure is rounder and sits lower, the thighs slope down toward the knees, and the lower legs angle further forward, so the feet end up further out in front. As a result the row b figures read as leaning back or sliding off the front of the seat, most strongly the woman reading and the girl with the drink.

## 2. Per figure

- **Woman reading (dress):** a. Her level thighs and clear knee corner read as sitting on a seat, while in b the sloping thighs and far-forward feet make her look perched and slipping forward.
- **Older woman with ice cream (skirt):** no difference. Both rows show a believable seated pose with near-vertical lower legs and feet under the knees, and b's slight slope in the thighs changes little.
- **Child with ice cream (shorts):** a. In a the shorts sit on a level lap with the knees clearly above the lower legs, while in b the thighs slope down and the lower legs lean well forward, so the child looks tipped back.
- **Girl with drink (dress):** a. In b her whole body reads as reclined, with sloping thighs and the feet pushed far forward as if she is sliding down, while a keeps her upright and seated.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Woman reading: the lower legs look long next to the short level thighs, and the feet sit wide apart and well forward of the knees.
- Woman reading: the dress makes a rounded bulge at the back of the seat, above a flat bottom line, which looks a little boxy.
- Older woman: the bottom of the skirt is a flat, hard line, as if she is sitting on an invisible plank.
- Child: the lower legs look long for a child, and the shoes are slightly splayed at different angles.
- Girl with drink: the back of the seat ends in a sharp, pointed corner at the left of the dress outline rather than a rounded bottom.
- General: in all four, the flat underside of the thighs looks cut off rather than resting on a seat, but with no seat drawn this is mild.

### Row b
- Woman reading: the thighs slope down to the knees and the feet are far in front, so she looks as if she is sliding off the seat. There is also an odd short crease line across the dress at the hip.
- Woman reading: the knees are softer and less defined than in a.
- Older woman: the thighs slope slightly, but this is otherwise acceptable. It is the best figure in row b.
- Child: the shorts are bulky and slope downward, and the lower legs angle strongly forward with the feet far out, so the child looks tipped back.
- Girl with drink: the torso appears reclined, the thighs slope steeply, and the feet are pushed far forward. She reads as half-lying or sliding down rather than sitting.
- General: the seat line is lower and rounder, which looks softer, but with no seat drawn the poses become hard to read as sitting at small size. In the small versions the b figures look like they are leaning back in mid-air.

## 4. Choice

Row **a**. It reads more clearly as "sitting on a seat" at both sizes, and that clarity suits a friendly cartoon style better than b's softer but slumped poses. How sure: **fairly sure**.
