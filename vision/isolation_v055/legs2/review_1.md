# Review of pair_1.png: seated lower body, row a vs row b

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the seat of each figure is narrow and the thighs are short, steep stubs that point mostly toward the viewer, so the knees sit close under the hips and most of the leg length is in long, thin lower legs that slant forward to the feet. In row b the seat is broader, the thighs run forward nearly level with a clear top line from hip to knee, and the lower legs drop more steeply from the knees, so the figure reads as resting on a seat rather than leaning back into the air. At picture size the row a figures look like they are half standing or tipping backward, while the row b figures read as sitting.

## 2. Figure by figure

1. Woman in a dress reading: **b**. In b the dress lies over a level thigh to the knee and the shins drop to the feet, while in a the thighs are hidden, foreshortened stubs and the long shins make her look perched on nothing.
2. Older woman in a skirt with ice cream: **b**. In b the skirt spreads over a wide seat and level thighs, while in a the skirt is a small block above long, straight, stilt-like shins.
3. Child in shorts with ice cream: **b**. In b the shorts cover a wide seat and level thighs with knees in front, while in a the knees are bunched up under the shorts and the thin shins stretch far forward.
4. Girl in a dress with a drink: **b**. In a she leans far back on a narrow seat with short thighs and long shins, as if sliding off, while in b the dress gathers on a seat and the thighs run forward to the knees.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- All four: the seat is narrow and has little weight, so nothing reads as resting on a seat; at picture size the figures look like they are leaning back or squatting in mid-air.
- All four: the thighs are very short and foreshortened, with the knees almost directly under or just ahead of the hips.
- All four: the lower legs are much longer than the thighs and thin, so the legs look like stilts; the feet are well forward of the knees.
- Woman reading: a bump sticks out at the back of the dress at hip level, and the knees show as two small round lumps under the hem with no thigh behind them.
- Older woman: the skirt is a small box with the knees poking out below it, and the shins are long, straight and parallel.
- Child: the knees are bunched together under the shorts and look knobbly; the shins splay forward and the feet are far out in front.
- Girl with drink: the whole body leans back strongly, the seat is the narrowest of the four, and she looks like she is sliding off or falling backward.

### Row b
- All four: the hips and seat are wide and heavy, close to bulky, especially the woman reading and the girl with the drink.
- All four: the lower legs still slant forward rather than dropping straight down, so the feet are ahead of the knees, though less than in a.
- Woman reading: the feet are set wide apart with the shins splaying outward.
- Older woman: the outer edge of the left shin (viewer's left) has a small kink or notch just below the knee.
- Child: a darker orange band shows between the shirt and the shorts, which looks like a separate strip rather than part of the shorts; the thighs are thick for a child.
- Girl with drink: the dress bunches into a large rounded mass at the hip, and she still leans back a little.

## 4. Choice

**Row b**, fairly sure. Its level thighs and broader seat make each figure read clearly as sitting, at both the large and the picture size, which suits a friendly, clear cartoon style. Its faults (heavy hips, a little forward slant in the shins) are smaller than row a's stilt-like legs and backward lean.
