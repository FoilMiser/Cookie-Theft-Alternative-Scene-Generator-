# pic_031

## Free description
A sunny playground with a dark metal railing fence at the back, a tree on the left and a bush on the right. On the left, a red seesaw is tilted: a small girl in a pink outfit sits high up on the raised end, holding the handle. An old man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat sits on the low end with his feet on the grass, reading a newspaper. Because he is sitting on the low end, the girl is stuck up in the air; she has a hand near her mouth and looks worried or as if she is calling to him, while he keeps reading and does not look at her. In the centre is a swing set with one empty red seat. On the right, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts slides down a yellow slide with one arm raised, as if waving or cheering. In the front right there is a sandpit with a red bucket and blue spade in it, and a grey rock beside it. A green bin stands near the swings.
