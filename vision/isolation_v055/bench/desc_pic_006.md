## Free description
A sunny day in a park with green hills, trees, a pond with bulrushes, a tall black lamp post, a green litter bin, a flower bed and a winding sandy path. At the edge of the pond a woman with a dark ponytail, in a purple top and teal trousers, is feeding a white duck. She holds a brown paper bag in one hand and a piece of bread in the other, and there are crumbs floating on the water. The duck is swimming towards the crumbs and pecking at them. On the right, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, sits on a wooden bench eating a pink ice cream cone. In front of the bench a small white dog with brown patches and a blue collar sits up on its hind legs, looking up at her. It seems to be begging for the ice cream. Nothing looks clearly wrong or impossible.
