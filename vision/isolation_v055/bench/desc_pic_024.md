## Free description
This is a sunny town square, with a row of colourful two-storey shops with striped awnings at the back. On the left is a fruit stall with crates of green, yellow, orange and red fruit under a striped canopy. In front of it, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers holds a paper bag and scatters seeds or crumbs. The seeds fall in a dotted trail down to two small birds, one red and one blue, which are pecking at them on the paving. In the middle a girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. On the right a teal bicycle is parked in a bike rack, and a tree grows from a round planter. Bottom right there is a small round café table with two black chairs and no one sitting at it. There are also a street lamp, flower planters, a green bin, a wooden crate and two potted plants. Nothing looks impossible. It is a peaceful street scene.
