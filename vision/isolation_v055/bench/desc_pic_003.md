## Free description
A sunny town square paved with grey stone, with a row of colourful two-storey shops along the back, each with a striped awning. In the front left, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers stands bent forward, holding a paper bag in one hand and scattering small seeds or crumbs from the other. The crumbs fall in a dotted trail to the ground, where two small birds, one red and one blue, are pecking at them. In the middle, a girl in a pink outfit sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. On the left there is a fruit and vegetable stall under a teal-and-white striped canopy, with crates of green, yellow, orange and red produce, but nobody is serving at it. A teal bicycle stands in a metal bike rack, a tree grows from a round planter on the right, and a small round café table with two black chairs stands empty at the front right. Nothing looks dangerous or about to go wrong; it is a calm scene of a man feeding birds.
