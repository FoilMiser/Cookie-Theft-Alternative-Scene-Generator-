A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back, a gate and paved path on the left, and a swing set with one red swing. In the middle a girl in a purple top and teal trousers holds the nozzle of a green garden hose and looks at it, but no water is coming out. The hose runs from a reel by the house, and a brown dog with a red collar is standing with its front paws on the hose near a joint, which seems to be blocking the water. At the front a small white dog with brown spots is crouched in a play bow next to a yellow-green tennis ball, wagging its tail and wanting to play. On the right a man in a red shirt and blue jeans sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. A washing line between two posts holds a yellow-and-red striped towel and blue jeans. There is a flower bed in front of the house. If the brown dog moves, water may suddenly spray at the girl.
