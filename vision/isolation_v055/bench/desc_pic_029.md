## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, trees, a winding sandy path on the left, a tall black lamp post, and a pond with bulrushes on the right. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers sits on a wooden bench eating a pink ice-cream cone with his eyes closed, enjoying it. A small white dog with brown ears and a blue collar sits up on its hind legs beside the bench, looking up at him and begging, apparently hoping for some of the ice cream. On the right a man in a green shirt and beige trousers walks along in front of the pond, also eating a pink ice-cream cone. There is a flower bed with colourful flowers near him and a green bin in the bottom-right corner. Nothing looks clearly impossible.
