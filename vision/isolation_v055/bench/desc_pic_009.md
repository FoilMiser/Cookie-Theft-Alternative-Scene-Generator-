## Free description
A sunny playground with a metal railing fence, a tree on the left and a bush on the right. On the left, a red seesaw is tipped right down at one end. An old man in a grey cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat sits on the low end, reading a book or newspaper and not paying attention. A little girl in a pink outfit is stuck up on the high end, holding the handle with one hand; she has her other hand at her mouth and her mouth open, as if she is calling out or surprised. Because the man is heavy and is just sitting there reading, the girl cannot come down; it looks like she wants him to notice her. In the middle there is a swing set with one empty red swing and a green rubbish bin. On the right, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts slides down a yellow and orange slide with one arm raised. In front on the right there is a sandpit with a red bucket and a blue spade in it, and a grey rock next to it.
