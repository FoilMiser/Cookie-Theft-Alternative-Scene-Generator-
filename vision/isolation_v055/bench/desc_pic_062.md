In the front left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a small wooden bench, absorbed in reading a red book. Beside her on the bench is a sandwich, and a small blue bird has landed on the bench and is pecking at it, with crumbs falling below; the woman does not seem to notice. Next to the bench is a hose reel with a hose and yellow nozzle lying on the grass. In the middle of the garden a washing line between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes, pegged up to dry. Further right are two potted plants, a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can. It looks like the bird is about to eat or carry off the woman's lunch.
