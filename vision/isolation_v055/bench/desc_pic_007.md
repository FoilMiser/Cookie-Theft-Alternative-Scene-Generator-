# pic_007

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house on the left, a wooden fence along the back, a gate on the right and a paved path down the right side. In the bottom-left corner an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses and a teal cardigan sits on a bench reading a red book. Next to her on the bench is a sandwich, and a small blue bird is standing on the bench pecking at the sandwich, which she does not seem to notice. In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress reaches up to hang wet laundry on a washing line: a pair of blue jeans and a white T-shirt, both dripping water, with a basket of more clothes at her feet. On the right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands on the path holding a red and a blue balloon; he reaches up with his other hand and looks up because a yellow balloon has escaped and is floating away up into the sky. A garden hose on a reel, a wheelbarrow and a watering can lie around the garden.
