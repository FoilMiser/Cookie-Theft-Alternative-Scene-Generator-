## Free description
This is a sunny back garden, with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a wooden fence with a gate at the back right, and a paved path on the right. Bottom left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a small wooden bench reading a red book. Next to her on the bench is a sandwich, and a blue bird is perched right beside it, apparently pecking at or about to steal it. The woman is absorbed in her book and doesn't seem to notice. A washing line strung between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes, pegged up to dry. There is also a flowerbed, a hose reel with a hose lying on the grass, a grey wheelbarrow, a teal watering can and two potted plants. The main thing happening is the bird going for the woman's sandwich.
