## Free description

Right beside the bench a brown dog with a red collar sits up on its hind legs, with its front paws raised, looking up at the girl. It looks like the dog is begging for her ice cream. On the right, in front of the pond, an older man with grey hair, a grey flat cap, a brown waistcoat over a white shirt and dark trousers stands eating a pink ice-cream cone as well. Nobody is interacting with the man; he is just standing and eating.
