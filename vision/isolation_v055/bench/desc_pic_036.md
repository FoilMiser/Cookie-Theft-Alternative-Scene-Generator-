## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house, red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. In the front left, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. A sandwich lies next to him on the bench, and a small blue bird is standing right by it, pecking at it, with crumbs around. The boy is absorbed in his book and doesn't seem to notice the bird stealing his food. In the middle, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers waters a flower bed with a hose that comes from a hose reel by the bench. On the right, a girl in a pink dress jumps up with both arms raised; she is in the air above her shadow and looks happy. Behind her stands a swing set with one empty swing, and a birdhouse on a pole stands by the gate.
