# pic_067

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left, a wooden fence with a gate on the right, and a stone path leading to the gate. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits at the left end of a wooden bench, absorbed in reading a red book. Beside her on the bench is a sandwich, and a blue bird has landed next to it and is pecking at it, leaving crumbs; the woman has not noticed. A hose reel with a hose and yellow nozzle lies on the grass next to the bench. In the middle a washing line between two posts holds a pair of jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. There is a flower bed with colourful flowers, two potted plants near the fence, a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can on the lawn. Nothing else is happening; there are no other people.
