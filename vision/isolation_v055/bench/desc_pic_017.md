# pic_017

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a paved path, a flowerbed and a cream house with a blue door on the right. In the centre front, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers holds the end of a garden hose and peers closely at the nozzle, as if wondering why no water is coming out. The hose runs from his hands across the lawn to a hose reel next to the house. A brown dog with a red collar is standing right on the hose, with a paw on it near a bulge in the hose, which would explain the water being blocked. On the left, a woman in a yellow dress drinks from a red cup through a straw; she is posed as if sitting, but there is no chair or seat under her. At the back, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead along the fence. It looks as if the man may soon get sprayed in the face if the dog steps off the hose.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The woman in yellow is drawn sitting but there is nothing under her: she appears to be sitting on thin air. This could be intended as sitting on the grass, but her knees are bent at chair height and her feet are on the ground, so it looks like a missing chair.
- The swelling in the hose where the dog stands could be read as the dog's paw pressing the hose flat (blocking the water) or as a joint/connector in the hose.
- The man could be examining a blocked nozzle, or about to accidentally spray himself; either way he seems puzzled.
