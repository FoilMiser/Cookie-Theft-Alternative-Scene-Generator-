## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and path on the left, and a house with a blue door on the right. In the middle a girl in a purple top and teal trousers holds a garden hose with the nozzle pointed up, but no water is coming out. The hose runs from a reel by the house across the lawn, and a brown dog with a red collar is standing on the hose with a front paw, right on a dark joint or bulge in the hose. That could be why no water is flowing: the dog seems to be blocking it. At the front, a white dog with brown spots is crouched in a play bow next to a yellow tennis ball, wagging its tail. On the right a man in a red shirt and blue jeans sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. There is an empty swing set on the left, and a clothesline in the background with a striped yellow towel and a pair of jeans hanging on it. A flowerbed with colourful flowers sits in front of the house.
