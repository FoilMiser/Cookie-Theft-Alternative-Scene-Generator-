This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a wooden fence and gate at the back, and a paved path down the right side. On the right, a smiling boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is swinging on a red swing hanging from a grey metal frame; motion lines show he is moving. In the front, a garden hose runs across the grass to a small sprinkler, which sprays arcs of water to the right. The water is landing right on a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is getting wet and has a puddle under it. On the hose, near the sprinkler, a brown puppy with a red collar lies asleep with its eyes closed, its body resting across the hose. A flowerbed is in front of the house.
