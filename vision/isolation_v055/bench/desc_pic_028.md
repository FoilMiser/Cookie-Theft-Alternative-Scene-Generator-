A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. In the front left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. A sandwich lies on the bench beside him, and a small blue bird is at the sandwich, pecking it, with crumbs falling. The boy is looking at his book and does not seem to notice that the bird is eating his sandwich. In the middle, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds a garden hose, and water sprays in an arc onto a bed of colourful flowers. The hose runs back to a hose reel next to the bench. On the right-centre, a girl in a pink dress jumps with both arms raised; she is off the ground with a shadow below her. Behind her is a swing set with one empty red seat, and a birdhouse on a tall pole stands near the gate.
