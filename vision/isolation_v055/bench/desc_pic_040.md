## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence and a gate with a paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the centre a man in a green shirt and beige trousers holds a garden hose and is looking down, puzzled, into the nozzle, as if no water is coming out. The hose runs from a reel near the house across the lawn, and a brown dog with a red collar is standing with its paw right on the hose, which seems to be squashing it and blocking the water. If the dog steps off, water may suddenly spray into the man's face. Behind them a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks along the fence with a white dog with brown spots on a red leash. On the left a girl in a yellow dress drinks through a straw from a red-and-white cup; she is in a sitting pose but there is no chair or bench under her, so she looks like she is sitting on thin air. There is a round flower bed in front of the house.
