## Free description
A sunny playground with a metal railing fence, a tree on the left, a swing set with one empty red swing, a red-and-yellow slide, a bin, a bush, a rock and a sandpit. On the left a red seesaw is tilted steeply. A small girl in pink sits on the raised end, holding the handle with her feet dangling in the air, and she looks worried or is calling out with her hand near her mouth. An older man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat sits on the low end reading a newspaper, so his weight keeps the girl stuck up high and he doesn't seem to notice her. At front right a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts kneels in the sandpit building a sandcastle with a little red flag on top, holding a red spade or bucket. The girl seems stuck until the man gets up.
