## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, trees, a winding sand path, a tall black lamp post and a pond with reeds. A girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench on the left of centre, eating a pink ice-cream cone. A brown dog with a red collar sits up on its back legs right next to the bench, facing her and looking up, as if begging for her ice cream. On the right, beside the pond, an older man with grey hair, a blue-grey flat cap, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands eating a pink ice-cream cone too. Nobody seems to be paying attention to the dog. A round bed of colourful flowers is next to the man, and a green rubbish bin stands in the bottom right corner. Nothing looks badly wrong; the main thing about to happen is the dog trying to get some ice cream.
