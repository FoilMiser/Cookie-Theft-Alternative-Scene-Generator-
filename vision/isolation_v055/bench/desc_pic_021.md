## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left, a flowerbed, a wooden fence with a gate on the right, and a paved path running to the gate. On the right, a smiling boy in a yellow shirt swings on a red swing hanging from a grey metal swing frame; little motion lines behind him show he is moving. In the front, a garden hose runs across the lawn to a small sprinkler, which is spraying arcs of water to the right. The water is landing on a cardboard box that sits in a puddle, so the box is getting soaked. A brown puppy with a red collar lies on the grass with its eyes closed, apparently asleep, right on or against the hose. Nobody seems to have noticed the box getting wet.
