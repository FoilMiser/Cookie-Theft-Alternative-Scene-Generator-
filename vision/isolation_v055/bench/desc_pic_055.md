# pic_055

## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, trees, bushes, a winding sandy path on the right, and a tall black lamp post in the middle with a green bin at its base. On the left there is a blue pond with reeds at both ends. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands at the edge of the pond holding a paper bag and tossing bits of bread onto the water. A white duck swims towards the bread and is pecking at it, leaving little ripple lines behind it. On the right, an old woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, sits on a wooden bench eating a pink ice-cream cone. A small white dog with brown spots sits up on its back legs in front of her, looking up at the ice cream as if begging for some. There is a flowerbed with colourful flowers at the front left.
