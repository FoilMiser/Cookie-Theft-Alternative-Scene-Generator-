## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back and a gate at the right. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses sits on a bench at the bottom left reading a red book; next to her on the bench is a sandwich, and a small blue bird is pecking at it without her noticing. In the middle a woman in a yellow dress is pegging wet laundry on a washing line between two posts: blue jeans and a white T-shirt, both dripping water; a basket of more clothes sits on the grass by her feet. On the right a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands on the paved path holding a red and a blue balloon, and he is looking up at a yellow balloon that has escaped and is floating away into the sky, its string trailing loose. There is also a birdhouse on a pole, a hose reel with a hose, a flower bed, a wheelbarrow and a watering can on the lawn. Two little mishaps are happening: the bird is stealing the grandmother's sandwich, and the boy has let go of a balloon.
