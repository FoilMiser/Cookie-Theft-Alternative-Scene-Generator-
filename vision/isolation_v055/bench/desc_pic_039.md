## Free description
A sunny fenced playground with a tree, a swing set, a slide and a sandpit. On the red see-saw on the left, a small girl in pink is stuck high up in the air on the raised end, holding the handle, with her hand near her mouth as if calling out or worried. An old man with grey hair, a cap and a brown waistcoat is sitting on the low end of the see-saw reading a newspaper, so his weight keeps her up there; he seems not to notice her. In the sandpit at the bottom right, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts kneels and builds a sandcastle with a little red flag on top, using a red spade. The swing set in the middle has one empty swing, and there is a green bin behind it. The slide on the right is empty. A grey rock sits beside the sandpit.
