# pic_065

## Free description
A sunny back garden beside a cream house with a red roof and blue door, closed in by a wooden fence with a gate at the right. In the bottom left an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses and a teal cardigan sits on a bench reading a red book. A sandwich lies on the bench beside her, and a small blue bird is standing on the bench pecking at it; she has not noticed, so the bird looks about to steal her sandwich. In the middle a woman with dark hair in a yellow dress is pegging washing on a line between two posts: a pair of jeans and a white T-shirt, both dripping water, with a basket of more clothes on the grass beside her. On the right a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands on the path holding the strings of a red and a blue balloon, looking up unhappily at a yellow balloon that has floated away above him with its string trailing. Next to that is a birdhouse on a tall post. On the grass are a hose reel with a hose, a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can.
