## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house with a blue door on the left, a wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the right, and an empty swing set. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds a garden hose and is spraying a stream of water onto a flowerbed in front of the house. The hose comes from a reel next to a bench at the front left. On that bench a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is reading a red book; next to him on the bench is a sandwich or burger, and a small blue bird is standing on the bench pecking at it. The boy does not seem to notice the bird taking his food. In the middle a girl in a pink dress is jumping with both arms raised; her shadow is on the ground below, so she is in mid-air. A birdhouse on a tall pole stands near the gate on the right.
