## Free description
A sunny playground with a tall railing fence across the back, a tree on the left, a swing set in the middle and a red slide on the right. On the left a red seesaw is tipped: a small girl in a pink outfit is stuck up on the high end, holding the handle, her feet dangling, looking unhappy with her hand to her mouth. On the low end an elderly man with grey hair and a blue cap, in a white shirt and brown waistcoat, is sitting reading a newspaper, apparently unaware that he is keeping the girl stuck in the air. On the slide at the right a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is sliding down, one arm raised as if waving or cheering. The swing set has a single empty red swing seat. A green bin stands near the swing set, and at the bottom right there is a sandbox with a red bucket and a blue spade, with a grey rock beside it. The odd thing is that a grown man is sitting on a seesaw reading a paper while the little girl is left hanging high up.
