# pic_058

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a gate and path on the left, a wooden fence at the back and a cream house with a blue door on the right. In the middle, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds a garden hose up and looks closely at the nozzle; no water is coming out. The reason seems to be a brown dog with a red collar standing right on the hose, near the flower bed, so the hose is squashed under its paw and the water is blocked. The dog is looking towards the flowers and doesn't notice. If the dog steps off, the water might suddenly spray into the woman's face. In the front, a small white dog with brown spots crouches in a play bow next to a yellow tennis ball, wagging its tail. On the right, a man in a red shirt and blue trousers sits on a bench reading a red book. A clothes line behind holds a yellow striped towel and a pair of blue jeans, and there is an empty swing on the left.
