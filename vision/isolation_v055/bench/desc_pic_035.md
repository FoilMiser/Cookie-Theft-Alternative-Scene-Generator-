## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence at the back, a gate and paved path on the right. A clothesline between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. In the middle, an old woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and grey skirt holds a garden hose, pointing the nozzle, but no water is coming out. A white dog with brown spots stands on the hose near where two lengths join, which seems to be stopping the water. Behind and to the right, an old man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is drinking from a red cup with a straw. He is in a sitting position but there is no chair, bench or anything under him; he seems to be sitting on thin air. On the right, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts jumps on the path with both arms raised, off the ground with a shadow beneath him. An empty bench, a hose reel and a flowerbed are on the left.
