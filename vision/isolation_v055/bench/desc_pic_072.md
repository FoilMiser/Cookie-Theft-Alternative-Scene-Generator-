A girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench beside the path, eating a pink ice-cream cone. Right next to the bench a brown dog with a red collar is sitting up on its hind legs, begging and looking up at her, apparently hoping for some of her ice cream. Behind him is a round flower bed with colourful flowers. A tall black street lamp stands in the middle of the grass, and there are trees, bushes and a green bin in the bottom right corner. It is a peaceful scene; the main tension is whether the dog will get the girl's ice cream.
