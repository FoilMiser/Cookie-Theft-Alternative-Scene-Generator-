## Free description
This is a sunny back garden, with a wooden fence along the back, a gate on the left, a paved path down the left side and a cream house with a blue door on the right. A woman in a yellow dress sits on the left drinking through a straw from a red cup, but there is no chair or bench under her and she seems to sit in mid-air. In the centre a man in a green shirt and beige trousers holds a garden hose and looks at its nozzle. The hose runs from a reel by the house, across the lawn and up to him. A brown dog in the front stands with a front paw on the hose, close to the reel, so the dog may be pinching it and stopping the water. That would explain why the man is peering at the nozzle, since no water is coming out. In the back a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. There is a flowerbed with colourful flowers in front of the house and a bush near the fence. The clearest oddity is the seated woman with no seat.
