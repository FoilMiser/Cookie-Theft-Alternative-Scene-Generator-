# pic_025

## Free description
A sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and a blue door, surrounded by a wooden fence with a gate on the right. In the middle a woman with dark hair in a yellow dress is hanging wet washing on a line between two posts: a pair of blue jeans and a white T-shirt, both dripping water, with a laundry basket full of clothes at her feet. On the left, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, in a teal cardigan, sits on a bench reading a red book. Next to her on the bench is a sandwich, and a small blue bird is standing on the bench pecking at it; she doesn't seem to have noticed. On the right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands on the paved path holding a red and a blue balloon on strings in one hand. He is reaching up and looking up at a yellow balloon that is floating away high in the air with its string dangling, so he seems to have just let it go and looks upset. There is also a bird house on a tall post, a hose reel with a hose lying on the grass, a wheelbarrow and a watering can in the foreground.
