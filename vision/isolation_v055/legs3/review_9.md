# Review of pair_9 (seated lower body, rows a and b)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the seat and thighs are small. In the front views the shorts or skirt is a thin band. In the side views the thighs slope down and forward from a rounded, hanging bottom, so the slide figures look like they are dropping or jumping rather than sitting. In row b the lap is bigger. The front views show a taller block of shorts or skirt that reads as thighs coming toward the viewer. The side views have a flat seat line and level thighs, which make a clear L-shaped sitting pose before the lower legs angle down to the feet.

## 2. Per figure

1. Child on swing, front: **b**, by a small margin. The taller shorts block reads as a lap with the knees at its front edge, while row a's thin orange band reads more like hips over very long standing legs.
2. Girl on swing, front: **b**. The skirt hem drapes over a lap and the knees peek out under it, so she looks seated; in row a the skirt ends in a narrow rounded hem and the legs hang down like a standing pose.
3. Child on slide, side: **b**. The flat-bottomed shorts and level thigh make a clear seat and knee bend; in row a the thigh slopes downward from a curved bottom, so there is no clear seat.
4. Girl on slide, side: **b**. The flat-bottomed dress and level thigh show her sitting; in row a the dress hangs at an angle and the pose reads as falling or leaping.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Child on swing (front): the shorts are a thin band with the knee bumps drawn inside it, so the thighs barely show. The lower legs are long and straight, which looks closer to standing than sitting.
- Girl on swing (front): the skirt is short and rounded and shows no lap. The legs drop straight from it with little sign of a knee bend.
- Child on slide (side): the seat is a rounded curve with no flat underside. The thigh slopes down and forward, so the knee is lower than the hip and not much of a bend is visible. The figure looks suspended in the air.
- Girl on slide (side): the dress slopes down to the knee with no flat seat line, and the thigh angles downward. The whole body reads as a backward leap, not sitting.
- Both slide figures: the shins have a doubled outline (two overlapping legs) that looks like a single thick leg with a stray line. The feet are stacked shoes that overlap a little awkwardly.

### Row b
- Child on swing (front): the shorts block is quite tall and boxy with a straight bottom edge, so the lap looks a bit stiff. The lower legs are still long, and the right foot sits higher than the left.
- Girl on swing (front): the wavy, folded skirt hem is slightly busy at picture size. The feet sit at different heights, as in row a.
- Child on slide (side): the shorts are a hard-cornered rectangle with a sharp front corner at the knee, which looks a little blocky. The same doubled shin outline appears as in row a.
- Girl on slide (side): the dress bottom is very flat and long, so the thigh section looks slab-like and a bit too long against the shins. The doubled shin outline and stacked shoes are the same as in row a.
- Both slide figures: the torso is upright while the arms reach far forward, so the pose is a little stiff. This is the same in both rows.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- Front figures: the hands sit on the shoulders or chest in a hunched pose. Nothing in the drawing shows they are holding swing chains, but the chains may be drawn separately.
- In the front views one foot is placed higher than the other, which looks unintended.

## 4. Choice

**Row b**, fairly sure. It reads clearly as sitting in all four figures, and that matters most in the slide figures, where row a looks like falling or jumping. Row b is a little blocky, but it is friendlier and clearer at picture size.
