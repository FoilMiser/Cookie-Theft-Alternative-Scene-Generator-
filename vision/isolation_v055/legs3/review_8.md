# Review of pair_8 (seated lower body, swing and slide)

## 1. How the rows differ

In the two front-view swing figures the rows are close. Row b's shorts and skirt end a little higher on the leg, so the visible lower legs are slightly longer, and the girl's skirt hangs as one smooth flared shape. In the two side-view slide figures the difference is clearer. Row a draws the hips and thighs as a hard-cornered box with a flat bottom and level thighs, like someone sitting upright on a chair. Row b rounds the seat, leans the torso back and tilts the thighs down a little, so the figure reads as sliding.

## 2. Per figure

- **Child on swing, front:** no difference. Shorts, knee bumps, straight lower legs and feet are almost the same; b's shorts are only slightly shorter.
- **Girl on swing, front:** b. Row b's skirt is one clean flared shape with the legs coming out under the hem. Row a has a fold line across the dress and knee outlines showing through the skirt, which looks busy.
- **Child on slide, side:** b. Row b's rounded seat and backward lean read as sliding. Row a's boxy shorts with a square back corner and level thighs read as sitting on a chair.
- **Girl on slide, side:** b. In row a the dress below the waist is a large stiff rectangle with a sharp corner at the seat. In row b the back curves into a rounded seat and the dress follows the thighs.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Child on swing (front): the kneecaps are drawn as round outlines inside the shorts, so the shorts look stretched over the knees. The thighs are foreshortened into a short band, so the lower legs look long and stilt-like.
- Girl on swing (front): a horizontal fold or seam crosses the dress above the skirt. The knee shapes push through the skirt hem as separate bumps. The skirt hem is uneven and puckered rather than falling cleanly.
- Child on slide (side): the seat is a sharp right-angle corner with a flat bottom. The thighs are perfectly level and long, then the shins drop steeply, like sitting on a chair edge rather than a slope. The shorts are a stiff box. The far leg is only a thin parallel line along the near shin.
- Girl on slide (side): the hips and thighs under the dress form one big rectangle with a hard back corner, so the lower body looks heavy and blocky. The thighs are level, and the upright torso does not match a slide. The two feet are stacked and slightly offset, which is acceptable but stiff.
- At picture size, both side figures read as an L-shaped chair pose.

### Row b
- Child on swing (front): the kneecap outlines still show inside the shorts (same as a), and the lower legs are long and straight with no bend shown.
- Girl on swing (front): the lower legs are long, thin and fully parallel, and there is no visible bend at the knee. The skirt is a little long and wide compared with the body.
- Child on slide (side): the seat is a large round bulge that makes the hips look a bit puffy. The shorts are short and very wide at the hem. The knee is a soft, unclear bend. The far leg is again just a parallel line.
- Girl on slide (side): there is a small notch or step at the back of the dress hem near the knee. The rounded seat bulges slightly. The torso leans back far enough that the body looks a little stretched between the shoulders and the hips.
- Other: in both rows the side-view feet are stacked one directly behind the other and point forward and down; they look fine.

## 4. Choice

Row b, fairly sure. The front figures are nearly the same, but row b's side figures read as sliding and its front girl is cleaner. Row a's side figures look like boxy chair-sitters.
