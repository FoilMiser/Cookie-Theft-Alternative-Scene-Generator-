# Review: pair_1 (seated lower body, rows a and b)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the lower body is boxy and stiff. The underside of the seat is a flat line, the thighs run almost level, the knee is a sharp corner, and the shins are long, thin, straight columns that drop straight down to small, flat shoes. In row b the lower body is rounder and chunkier. The seat bulges down at the back, the thighs slope down toward the knee, the knees are rounded, the shins are shorter, thicker and lean slightly forward, and the shoes are bigger and rounder.

## 2. Per figure

- **Man reading:** b. His thicker legs and rounded knees match the big head and chunky arms, while in a his shins look like long stilts under a short torso.
- **Woman with ice cream:** b. Her legs in b have soft, even thickness and a clear knee. In a they are long, straight tubes that make her look perched on a tall stool.
- **Older man with drink:** a. In b his thighs slope down steeply from a large bulge at the seat, so he looks like he is sliding forward. In a he reads as sitting squarely, even though his shins are long.
- **Teenager reading:** b. In a his legs are the thinnest and longest of the four, which makes his lower body look too tall for a teen. In b they are sturdier and shorter, which suits the figure better.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- All four: the shins are very long compared with the thighs and torso, so every figure looks like it sits on a high bar stool. This is worst for the teenager and the woman.
- All four: the knee is a hard, nearly square corner, and the top of the thigh is a straight flat line. It looks stiff, more like a mannequin than a cartoon.
- All four: the shins are straight, parallel tubes with no calf shape. The legs read as sticks at picture size.
- All four: the shoes are small and flat compared with the big heads. The man's and the woman's are dark ovals that barely read as feet.
- Man, woman: the far leg drops straight down behind the near leg, so the two shins look like a double column rather than two legs.
- Teenager: the thighs and shins are narrow compared with his bulky hoodie, so his top and bottom halves look mismatched.
- Woman: a skin-colored shape (her far hand or arm) sits in the gap under the thigh near the knee. It reads as a stray blob.
- Older man: a thin white line runs from his hand down past the hip to the knee, like a stick or cane drawn over the body. It is unclear what it is.

### Row b
- All four: the thighs slope down toward the knee, so the seat is no longer level. At picture size the figures look like they are slipping forward off the seat or perched on its edge.
- All four: the seat bulges down and back below the thigh line, a rounded sack shape that would sink into any flat seat under it.
- Older man: the slope and the seat bulge are strongest on him. His trousers look baggy and his lap looks tilted.
- Woman: the hip and seat bulge is large and the thigh is very thick near the hip, which makes her lap look swollen.
- Man, teenager: the shins lean forward a little, so the feet end up ahead of the knees. This adds to the "sliding off" look.
- Woman: the same skin-colored shape as in row a shows under the thigh near the knee.
- Older man: the same thin white line runs down from his hand to the knee.
- All four: the shoes are large and rounded, which is friendly, but the near shoe and the far shoe overlap heavily, so the two feet merge into one shape at picture size.

## 4. Choice

Row **b**, **fairly sure**. Its thicker, rounded legs and bigger shoes fit the big-head, chunky cartoon style and read as friendlier. Row a's stilt-like shins and square knees look stiff. The main thing to fix in b is the downward slope of the thighs and the bulge under the seat: a flatter thigh line would keep b's friendly shapes and give back the clear "sitting on a seat" look that a has.
