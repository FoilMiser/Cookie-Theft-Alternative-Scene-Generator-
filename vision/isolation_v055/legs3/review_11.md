# Review of pair_11 (seated lower body, rows a and b)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the thighs are fairly thin and slope down towards the knee, the seat of the trousers is a rounded curve with no flat underside, and the lower legs taper to narrow ankles, so the figures look perched or slightly sliding forward. In row b the thighs are thicker and close to horizontal with a flat underside and a squared back corner at the seat, the knees are squarer, and the lower legs are wide, straight, almost vertical trouser tubes that end in planted feet. Row b reads clearly as "sitting on something"; row a looks lighter and more rounded but less grounded.

## 2. Figure by figure

1. Man reading: **b**. His horizontal thighs and vertical shins make a clear right-angle sitting pose, whereas in a the down-sloping thighs make him look like he is slipping off the front of the seat.
2. Woman with ice cream: **b**. In b the thighs sit level and the pose is stable; in a the thighs droop and the rounded seat makes her look as though she floats above the seat.
3. Older man with drink: **b**. The level thighs and straight shins in b suit a heavier older figure; in a his thin, sloping thighs and narrow ankles look weak under the bulky vest.
4. Teenager reading: **b**. In a the thin, sloping legs look too slight under the big hoodie and he seems to hover; in b the thicker level thighs balance the top half.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- All four: thighs slope downward from hip to knee, so the figures look as if they are sliding forward off the seat rather than resting on it.
- All four: the seat of the trousers is one rounded curve with no flat underside, so nothing shows where the body meets the (undrawn) seat; at the small size they look like they are hovering.
- All four: lower legs narrow towards the ankle, giving thin, slightly wobbly-looking shins under large feet.
- All four: knees are soft rounded bumps, so the bend at the knee is less readable at small size.
- Teenager: the slim legs look undersized against the bulky hoodie and big head.
- Older man: thin legs under the wide vest make the lower body look weak.
- Woman: a patch of skin (her lower hand) shows between the knees, which at a glance reads like an odd shape at the knee.

### Row b
- All four: the back of the seat is a sharp, boxy corner and the thigh underside is a ruler-straight line, which looks stiff and a little wooden.
- All four: the lower legs are very wide straight tubes that flare slightly at the hem, so the trousers look like stiff stove-pipes; ankles are not seen.
- Woman: the hip and seat block is especially wide and square at the back, making her lower body look heavy compared with her slim top.
- Man and older man: the far shin slants outward while the near shin is vertical, so the legs splay slightly apart at the feet.
- Teenager: the near lower leg is long and straight, making the shins look a bit long for his size.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- Older man: a thin white line runs from his drinking hand down past his waist to his knee in both rows; it is not clear what it is (strap, stick, straw?) and reads as a stray line.
- Woman: her lower arm and hand disappear behind the thigh and reappear as a small skin patch near the knee in both rows.

## 4. Choice

**Row b**, fairly sure. Its level thighs and planted shins give a clear, readable sitting pose that holds up at the small picture size, which matters most for a friendly, clear cartoon style; its boxy seat corners and stiff trouser tubes are a smaller fault than row a's sliding, floating look and could be softened later.
