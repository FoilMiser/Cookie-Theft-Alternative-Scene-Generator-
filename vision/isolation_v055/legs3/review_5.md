# Review of pair_5 (seated lower body, rows a and b)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the seat is barely drawn: the two men look like they are squatting with near-vertical shins and feet straight under the knees, and the two children look like they are mid-jump, with the body tilted and the thighs angled down. In row b every figure has a large, flat-bottomed seat that clearly rests on something; the men's legs run forward in a long diagonal to feet well in front of them, and the children's thighs run forward almost level before the shins hang down.

## 2. Per figure

- **Bearded man (low end): b.** Row b's flat, rounded seat and forward legs read as sitting low down, while row a reads as a squat with the weight on the feet.
- **Older man (low end): b.** Row b shows a clear seat and outstretched legs; row a has a dark lump bunched under the vest and vertical shins that look like crouching.
- **Girl (high end, legs hanging): b.** Row b's dress has a flat seat and level thighs with the shins hanging, which reads as sitting on a raised edge; row a looks like she is jumping or falling.
- **Child (high end, legs hanging): b.** Row b's shorts sit on a flat seat with level thighs and dangling shins; row a's angled thighs and tucked shins look like a jump.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Bearded man: no real seat; the hips end in a short horizontal ledge, so he looks like he is squatting in the air.
- Bearded man: the shins drop almost vertically and the feet sit directly under the knees, stacked one above the other; the back shoe looks like it is floating behind the front shin.
- Bearded man: the knee is hidden behind the hands and book, so the bend of the leg is hard to read.
- Older man: a dark trouser lump sticks out to the left under the vest, like a cushion, not a seat.
- Older man: same vertical shins and stacked feet as the bearded man, so he reads as crouching, not sitting.
- Girl: the body tilts and the thighs slope down and forward, with no flat seat, so she looks airborne.
- Girl: the shins fold back under her and the feet point down and back, one behind the other, which is a jumping pose more than legs hanging from a seat.
- Child: the shorts have no flat underside; the thighs slope down to the knees and the shins tuck back, which reads as a hop.
- Child: the feet are bunched together under the body, one behind the other.

### Row b
- Bearded man: thigh and shin form one straight diagonal with no visible knee bend, so the legs look stiff, like ramps.
- Bearded man: the seat is very large and heavy compared with the torso.
- Older man: the same straight, unbent legs; the dark trousers merge into one big wedge with the seat.
- Older man: the seat blob is oversized and bulges well behind the back.
- Girl: the dress seat bulges behind her into a big rounded block; the hem and the knee are close together, so the thigh is short.
- Girl: the feet still point down and back, one behind the other, a little stiffly.
- Child: the shorts make a boxy, wide seat that sticks out behind the back.
- Child: the shins angle back under the knees instead of hanging straight down; the feet are crowded together.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- The girl and the child both hold one hand up at the mouth with the other fist raised at the chest, with an open "o" mouth; it reads as a startled or blowing gesture rather than holding on to a seesaw.
- At the small size, row a's men look like they are crouching and the children like they are jumping; row b holds up better as seated at that size.

## 4. Choice

**Row b**, fairly sure. It reads as sitting for all four figures, at both large and small size; its main weakness is the stiff, unbent legs and heavy seat on the two men.
