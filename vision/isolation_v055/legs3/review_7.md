# Review of pair_7: seated lower body, row a vs row b

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the two men sit low with a big rounded seat at the bottom. Their thighs run up on a long slant to knees near the hands, and their lower legs slant down and forward to feet well in front of the body. The two children have a short, fairly level thigh going forward from a flat seat, with the lower legs hanging down and back. In row b the men have a short thigh that comes straight forward and a lower leg that drops straight down, with the feet right under the knees, as if perched on a chair. The children's thighs and dresses or shorts slope steeply downward, so their bodies look longer and more upright.

## 2. Figure by figure (left to right)

1. Bearded man reading (low end): **a**. The low seat, raised knee and forward feet read at once as sitting low on something. Row b looks like squatting on an invisible stool, with a lumpy, short thigh.
2. Older man reading (low end): **a**. Same clear low-sitting shape as the bearded man. In row b the dark trouser seat is a squashed blob with a flap sticking out at the back, and the legs drop in one stiff column.
3. Girl on the high end, legs hanging: **a**. The flat seat edge and the forward thigh make her clearly sitting, with the lower legs hanging. In row b the long, steep dress and thigh make her look half-standing or sliding off.
4. Child on the high end, legs hanging: **a**. The shorts show a level thigh and a forward knee, so the pose reads as sitting. In row b the thighs slope down steeply and the knees are low, so the pose reads less as sitting and more as standing or dropping.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Bearded man: the knee is hidden behind the hands and book, so the bend at the knee is not seen. The leg reads as a long wedge, and the two legs merge into one wide trouser shape with only a faint line between them.
- Bearded man: the seat bulge is very large and round, close to sitting on the ground. The two shoes are stacked one directly in front of the other.
- Older man: the knee is hidden behind the hands in the same way. The dark trousers merge into one mass, so the near and far legs are hard to tell apart. The shoes are stacked.
- Both men: the lower legs are long, so the legs look a little long next to the torso.
- Girl: the feet point down and back (toes toward the left, behind her) like tiptoe. They read as dangling but a bit stiff. The far leg is only a thin sliver behind the near one.
- Child: the feet point down and back in the same way, and the far lower leg is mostly hidden. The shorts end in a sharp, angular hem at the knee.

### Row b
- Bearded man: the thigh is short and drawn as a rounded lump with an inner line, and does not read as a thigh. The lower legs drop straight down as one column, with the feet stacked under the knee. The pose looks like squatting on a chair-height seat, not like sitting on the low end of a seesaw.
- Older man: the trouser seat is a dark, squashed blob with an odd lip or flap sticking out at the back. The thigh is very short, and the lower legs are a stiff vertical column with the feet stacked under the knees.
- Both men: the book and hands sit right on top of the knees, so the knee area is crowded and unclear.
- Girl: there is no clear seat. The dress runs in one long diagonal from the waist to the knee, so she looks like she is leaning back or sliding, not sitting. The thighs are too steep, and the body looks stretched.
- Child: the shorts are long and baggy, and the thighs slope down steeply. The knees sit low, so the seat point is unclear, and the whole figure looks taller and less seated.
- Other: at picture size (the small figures), the row b men look like they are crouching, and the row b children look close to standing.

## 4. Choice

**Row a**, fairly sure. All four figures read more clearly as seated in row a, both large and at picture size. The faults it has (hidden knees, merged legs, stacked shoes) are smaller than row b's unclear seats and stiff, chair-like or standing-like legs.
