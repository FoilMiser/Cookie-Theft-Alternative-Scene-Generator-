# Review of pair_10: seated lower bodies, row a against row b

## 1. How the rows differ

Row a draws the seat of each figure as a flat, level shelf. The thighs come straight forward, the knees are clearly bent at about a right angle, and the lower legs drop down to feet that sit below the knees. Row b draws the seat and thighs as one slope running down from the hip to the knee. The knee angle is soft, and the lower legs and feet reach forward, so the figures look tilted back, half reclining or sliding off the front of the seat.

## 2. Per figure (left to right)

- **Woman reading (yellow dress): a.** In a, the hem sits level across the lap and the knees bend clearly, so she reads as sitting. In b, the dress slopes down to the knees and her feet reach forward, so she looks as if she is slipping off the seat.
- **Older woman with ice cream (teal cardigan, grey skirt): a.** In a, the skirt forms a level lap with clear knees under it. In b, the skirt tilts down toward the knees and the shins angle forward, which makes her lean back.
- **Child with ice cream (orange shorts): a.** In a, the knees are clearly bent with the feet below them, even though the knees are a bit knobbly. In b, the shorts run downhill as one long tube, the knee bend almost disappears, and the legs look like a straight diagonal.
- **Girl with drink (pink dress): a.** In b, her torso leans far back and her legs stretch out forward, so she looks as if she is falling off backward. In a, she sits upright on a level lap.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- All four: the seat of the figure is a hard, flat shelf with a squared front edge. It reads as a box under the clothes more than as a body.
- All four: the lower legs splay outward from the knees in a V, and the feet sit wider apart than the knees.
- All four: the shoes are drawn side-on and point outward, while the legs face the front, so the feet look twisted away from the legs.
- Woman reading: the lower legs are long compared with the short thighs, which gives her a stilt-like look.
- Older woman: the skirt is a stiff, boxy block that looks like a cushion strapped to her lap.
- Child: the knees are drawn as round knobs with extra lines, which looks lumpy at large size. The shorts have several crease lines that crowd the lap.
- Girl with drink: the legs spread in a wide V and the knee caps bulge under the hem.

### Row b
- All four: the seat and thighs make one downhill slope with no level lap, so the figures do not look supported by a seat.
- All four: the knee bend is shallow and hard to find, and the lower legs angle forward so the feet land in front of the knees. Together these give a reclining or sliding pose.
- All four: the feet still splay outward in side-on shoes, as in a.
- Woman reading: the dress hem tilts down toward the knees, and her body looks tipped back while she reads.
- Older woman: the skirt slants, and the shins are long and angled forward.
- Child: the shorts form a long diagonal tube, the knees are nearly lost, and the legs read as straight sticks.
- Girl with drink: her torso leans far back and her legs stretch out, which makes the clearest "falling off the seat" look in either row. At picture size she looks half lying down.

## 4. Choice

**Row a**, fairly sure. Its level laps and clear right-angle knees read as "sitting" at a glance, at both sizes. Row b's sloped laps make every figure look as if it is sliding or reclining. Row a's boxy seat edges and splayed feet are its weak points, but they are smaller faults than b's unclear pose.
