# Review of pair_2: seated lower body, row a vs row b

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the seat of each figure is a low, rounded U-shape. The thighs slope down from the hip to the knee with a sag under them, and the shins lean forward to the feet, so the figures look as if they are sinking into a sling or half-squatting with no seat under them. In row b the seat is a flat, squared bottom. The thighs run level from hip to knee, and the shins drop straight down to flat feet, which reads as an ordinary upright chair pose.

## 2. Per figure, left to right

1. Man reading (red shirt): **b**. Row b's level thigh and upright shin read clearly as sitting, while row a's sagging seat and downhill thigh make him look like he is slumping off an invisible seat.
2. Bearded man (green shirt): **b**. Row b gives a clean right angle at hip and knee, while row a's thigh slopes down and the shin angles forward, so he looks perched rather than seated.
3. Worker in safety vest: **b**. Row a draws a very small, sagging seat under the vest with a steeply sloping thigh, and row b's flat seat and level thigh carry the vest and the book naturally.
4. Older man (brown vest): **b**. Row a has a dark disc or ring sticking out at the waist and back like a tyre, plus the same sagging seat, while row b's lap is clean and level.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- All four: the seat hangs below the line of the thighs as a rounded U-shape, so the hip sits lower than it should and the figure looks like it is sinking into a hammock, not sitting on a seat.
- All four: the thighs slope down toward the knee and their underside is concave. Nothing looks flat enough to rest on a seat.
- All four: the shins lean forward, with the feet ahead of the knees, and they taper thin toward the ankle. Together with the backward-leaning torso, the pose looks unstable, like a wall-sit or a fall backward.
- All four: the knee is only a soft bend with no clear shape.
- Figure 1 (red shirt): the jeans narrow sharply at the seat and the waist sits pinched under the shirt.
- Figure 3 (worker): the seat is very small and pinched under the vest. The thigh drops steeply, so the lower body looks too small for the torso.
- Figure 4 (older man): a dark ring or flange juts out around the waist at the back and front, under the vest hem. It looks like a tyre or a stray belt shape and is clearly wrong.
- At picture size, the figures in row a read as floating or half-squatting, which would look odd once a bench is placed under them.

### Row b
- All four: the seat is very flat and boxy, with a hard horizontal bottom and one rounded back corner. It is clear but a little blocky.
- All four: the thighs are long and thick. Together with the long vertical shins, the legs look long for the torso, most of all in figures 1 and 3.
- All four: the knee is a square corner with no shaping, so the leg reads like a bent tube.
- Figures 1 and 3 (red shirt, worker): the trousers flare out slightly at the ankle, giving a bell-bottom look.
- Figures 1 and 2: the back of the seat sticks out slightly behind the line of the back, a small bump at the rear.
- Figure 4 (older man): the trouser waist under the vest is a plain dark band with little detail. This is minor, and much cleaner than row a.
- All four: the feet are simply placed side by side under the knees. They are fine, but a little stiff.

## 4. Choice

**Row b**, fairly sure. Its level-thigh, straight-shin pose reads as sitting at once, both large and at picture size, and it suits a friendly, clear cartoon style. Row a's sagging seats, sloping thighs and the stray ring on the older man look like errors. Row b's faults are mostly stiffness and slightly long legs, which are minor by comparison.
