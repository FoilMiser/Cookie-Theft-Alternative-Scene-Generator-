# Review of pair_5: seated lower body, row a vs row b

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the seat of the figure is a rounded curve that sags below and behind the waist, the thighs slope down steeply from hip to knee, and the shins lean forward to the feet, so each figure looks like it is half-squatting or sliding off something. In row b the seat of the figure has a flat underside, the thighs run level from hip to knee, the knee is a clear square corner and the shins drop straight down to flat feet, which reads as sitting on a chair.

## 2. Figure by figure

1. Man reading (red shirt): **b**. In b the level thighs and vertical shins read at once as sitting, while in a the drooping hips and sloping thighs make him look like he is sinking or squatting with nothing under him.
2. Bearded man (green shirt): **b**. In b he sits square with hands resting on his knees, while in a the thigh slopes down so far that the knee sits well below the hip and the pose looks like a wall squat.
3. Worker in safety vest: **b**. In a the hips hang down and back under the vest with a long downhill thigh, while in b the seat and thigh form a clear L with the shin.
4. Older man (brown vest): **b**. In a a dark ring sticks out around the waist at the back and the thighs sag, while in b he sits flat and steady, though his seat block is bulky.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- All four: the seat of the figure is not flat; it curves down and back from the waist, so there is no surface the figure could be resting on and the seat that is not drawn cannot be imagined.
- All four: the thighs slope steeply downhill to the knee, so the knee sits clearly lower than the hip; the pose reads as a squat or a slide, not a chair sit.
- All four: the knee is a soft rounded bend with no clear corner, so thigh and shin blur into one curve.
- All four: the shins lean forward from knee to foot, putting the feet ahead of the knees; together with the sloping thigh this tips the figure backward.
- Man reading and worker: the back of the hips bulges below the shirt or vest hem like a sagging seat of the trousers.
- Older man: a dark ring or band (belt or waistband) sticks out behind and below the vest, like a tire around the waist; it looks like a separate object.
- At picture size all four look like they are hovering or doing a wall squat.

### Row b
- All four: the thighs are long and very boxy, almost a straight rectangle, which is stiff for a friendly cartoon.
- All four: the seat of the figure is a rounded block that pushes out behind the line of the back, most of all on the worker and the older man.
- All four: the knee is a hard right angle; clear, but a little wooden.
- All four: the shins are long and dead vertical, so the figures look like they sit on a fairly tall seat; feet are set flat under the knees, which is correct.
- Older man: the thighs and seat are thick and heavy compared with his upper body; the waistband is not visible, so vest and trousers meet abruptly.
- Bearded man and man reading: small fold lines at the knee are fine at large size but vanish at picture size (no harm).

## 4. Choice

Row **b**, **sure**. At both the large and the picture size, row b clearly reads as four people sitting on a seat, while row a reads as people squatting or sliding with nothing under them. Row b's only weakness is stiffness, which is minor next to row a's posture problem.
