# Review of pair_1.png (seated lower body, row a vs row b)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the thighs slope steeply down from a small, rounded seat to knees that sit well below the hips, and the shins drop to feet that sit almost under the knees. The figures look perched on a front edge or sliding off, and at picture size they read more as half-standing or hovering than sitting. In row b the seat has a flat underside, the thighs run close to level, the knees make a clear near-right angle, and the shins go straight down to flat feet. The figures read plainly as sitting on a chair.

## 2. Figure by figure

- **Man reading:** b. His level thighs and square knee make an obvious sitting pose, where row a's sloping thighs make him look like he is sliding forward off the seat.
- **Woman with ice cream:** b. Row a gives her the steepest thigh slope and a skin-coloured bump at the far knee, while row b sits her squarely with a clean lap.
- **Older man with drink:** b. In row b the thighs give a stable seat under the vest, while row a's small rounded pelvis and dropped knees make him look like he is leaning on something rather than sitting.
- **Teenager reading:** b. His forward hunch over the book suits row b's level lap, while in row a the hunch plus sloping thighs makes him look like he is about to stand up or fall forward.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- All four: there is no flat seat line. The buttock is a small rounded bulge, so nothing shows where the body meets the seat, and the figures seem to float.
- All four: the thighs slope down at roughly 30 degrees, so the knees sit well below the hips. This reads as perching or sliding off, not sitting.
- All four: the knees are soft, loose curves with no clear bend, so the change from thigh to shin is vague, especially on the far leg.
- All four: the pelvis is narrow and pinched where it meets the shirt hem, so the hips look too small for the torsos.
- Man reading and teenager: the shins tilt slightly back under the knees, which adds to the half-standing look.
- Woman with ice cream: a skin-coloured lump (her lowered hand or arm) shows at the far knee and looks like a growth on the knee.
- Older man: a thin white vertical stick runs from the vest down to about the knee and simply stops. It reads as a stray line.
- At picture size, the long sloping legs make all four figures look tall and stilted.

### Row b
- All four: the back of the seat is a hard, boxy corner and the underside is a dead-straight line, so the buttocks look cut off and blocky.
- All four: the thighs are very thick and slab-like, and the near thigh is almost a rectangle.
- All four: the knees are sharp right-angle corners with no rounding, which looks stiff for a friendly cartoon style.
- All four: the shins are long, straight, untapered tubes, a little long for the thigh length, so the figures sit tall.
- All four: the waistband is a wavy line over a wide hip block, so the trousers look bunched at the waist.
- Woman with ice cream: a thin strip of her arm shows behind the thigh near the waist. It is less distracting than in row a but still reads as an odd sliver.
- Older man: the same white stick ends at the knee as in row a.
- Teenager: the near foot (white shoe) overlaps the far foot heavily, so the two feet merge into one white shape at small size.

## 4. Choice

Row b. It reads clearly as sitting at both sizes, and its faults (boxy seat corner, square knees, slab thighs) are matters of polish. Row a's faults make the pose itself unclear. How sure: fairly sure.
