# Review of pair_6 (seated lower body, four figures)

## 1. How the rows differ

Row a draws a boxy seated pose: a flat underside to the seat and thighs, thighs level and horizontal, a rounded right-angle knee, near-vertical shins and both shoes flat on one ground line. Row b draws a curved, swooping pose: the seat curves down and forward from under the torso, the thighs slope downward to a knee that sits lower than the hip, and the shins angle forward to the feet. As a result row b reads as sliding or hovering in the air, while row a reads as sitting on an invisible box or bench.

## 2. Per figure

1. Man reading (red shirt): **a**. The flat seat and level thighs make him clearly seated, while in b the trousers narrow under the shirt and slope away, so he looks like he is slipping off something.
2. Bearded man (green shirt): **a**. In a he sits squarely with his hands on level knees, while in b the thighs drop steeply and he looks like he is doing a wall-sit or floating.
3. Worker in vest: **a**. In a the seat and thighs give a stable base under the book, while in b the narrow, curved seat makes the lower body look thin and unsupported.
4. Older man (brown vest): **a**. Row b adds a dark band at the waist that sticks out behind his back like a ring or disc, and his legs slope down so that he seems to hang in the air. Row a is plain and stable.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- All four: the shapes are stiff and boxy, with a flat bottom line and an almost square knee corner, so the legs look like an L-shaped block rather than limbs.
- All four: the thighs are very thick and long compared with the shins, and the shins are straight tubes of nearly even width.
- All four: the two shoes are drawn as nearly identical stacked outlines, one just behind the other, which looks a little doubled.
- Figure 1 (red shirt): the bottom of the seat pushes out slightly behind the line of the back, giving a small shelf-like bump.
- Figure 4 (older man): a dark band at the trouser waist merges with the dark vest and dark trousers, so the hip area reads as one dark mass, and there is a small bump on the top line of the thigh near the hands.

### Row b
- All four: the seat curves inward under the torso, so there is no clear bottom. The figure does not look like it is resting on anything and reads as floating or sliding. This is clearest in the small, picture-size versions.
- All four: the thighs slope downward steeply, so the knee is well below the hip. Without a drawn seat, this looks like a wall-sit or falling forward rather than sitting.
- All four: the thighs are thin where they join the torso and widen toward the knee, which looks back to front.
- All four: the shins lean forward and the shoes are large and heavy compared with the thin upper leg. The far shoe is mostly hidden, so the feet read as one big shoe.
- Figure 4 (older man): a dark brown ring or band sticks out around the waist, especially behind the back, like a belt drawn as a disc.
- Figure 2 (bearded man): the hands rest on a knee that is lower than the hip, so the arms reach down and forward in an awkward way.
- Other: the upper bodies and heads in row b look slightly larger than in row a, which makes the thin, sloping legs look even weaker by comparison.

## 4. Choice

**Row a**, fairly sure. It is stiffer and boxier, but every figure clearly reads as seated at both sizes, and it would sit naturally on a bench or chair added later. Row b's curved seat and sloping thighs make the figures look like they are floating or sliding, and figure 4 has an odd waist ring.
