# Review of pair_3: seated lower body, row a vs row b

## 1. How the rows differ

Row a draws the seat as a flat bottom line with a squared-off back corner. The thighs run almost level, the knees are sharp corners, and the lower legs are long, thin, straight columns that end in small, flat shoes. Row b draws a rounded bottom. Its thighs slope gently downward to soft, rounded knees, and its lower legs are thicker and the same width all the way down, ending in larger, rounder shoes. Overall, row b looks softer and chunkier, while row a looks more angular and stiff.

## 2. Per figure (left to right)

1. **Man reading: b.** In row a his shins are much longer and thinner than his thighs, so they look like stilts under a chunky torso. Row b's thicker, rounded legs match his body.
2. **Woman with ice cream: b.** Row b's rounded seat and even leg width look natural. In row a her lower legs are long and narrow, and the knee is a hard corner.
3. **Older man with drink: b.** Row b gives him a believable rounded seat and knees. In row a his short level thighs meet very long straight shins, and the pose looks rigid.
4. **Teenager reading: a.** In row b his thighs slope down steeply and his rounded bottom hangs below the hip, so he looks like he is slumping or sliding off a high perch. Row a reads as plainly sitting.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- All four: the lower legs are too long compared with the thighs, so the figures look as if they sit on a very high seat with stilt-like shins.
- All four: the lower legs are thin and taper less than the chunky arms and torsos. The legs look weaker than the rest of the body.
- All four: the knees are sharp corners rather than rounded joints, which is stiff for this cartoon style.
- All four: the back of the seat is a hard square corner with a flat underside. It reads as a box shape rather than a body.
- Man, woman: the feet are small, flat shoes set almost straight under the knees. They look undersized next to the large heads and hands.
- Older man: the hip-to-thigh join is abrupt, and the thigh is very short before the knee bump.
- Woman: a thin skin-colored arm runs down behind the thigh toward the knee. It reads as an odd sliver between the legs.

### Row b
- Teenager: the thighs slope steeply downward and the rounded bottom bulges below the hip line, so he looks like he is slumping or perched on an edge.
- All four: the thighs slope down slightly, so the knees sit below the hip. At small size this makes the pose look a little like leaning or perching rather than resting square on a bench.
- All four: the lower legs are tube-like with almost no ankle narrowing, so the shoes join the legs bluntly.
- Woman: a skin-colored hand or arm is visible behind the thigh near the knee, where it looks like an extra shape tucked under the leg.
- Older man: the cream back arm runs down past the vest to the hip and blends into the seat area, so the outline there is muddled.
- Man, older man: the far shoe and near shoe sit at almost the same height and overlap. The feet look a little bunched.

## 4. Choice

**Row b, fairly sure.** Its rounded seat, softer knees and thicker legs match the round heads and chunky arms, and they look friendlier. Row a's long, thin, angular legs look stiff and stilt-like. The teenager in row b is the weak spot, because his steep thighs make him look like he is slumping, and that figure is better in row a.
