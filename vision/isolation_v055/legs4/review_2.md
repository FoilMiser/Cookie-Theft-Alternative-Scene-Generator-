# Review of pair_2: seated lower body, rows a and b

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the lap is shallow. The front figures show only a thin band of shorts or skirt above long straight legs, and the side figures have short thighs that drop steeply from the hip into very long shins, so they read as hanging or falling more than sitting. Row b gives the lap more depth. The front figures show a deeper band of shorts or skirt with rounded knee bumps, and the side figures have longer, flatter thighs with a clear seat underneath, so they read as sitting on something with their legs held out.

## 2. Per figure, left to right

1. Child on swing, front: **b**. The deeper shorts and rounded knee tops in b make the thighs look like they come toward the viewer, while a looks like a standing child in very short shorts.
2. Girl on swing, front: **b**. The fuller skirt over the lap with knee shapes under it reads as seated, while a's narrow hem over straight legs reads as standing. The gap between the rows is smaller here than for the boy.
3. Child on slide, side: **b**. The thigh in b is long and runs forward from a visible seat, while in a the thigh is a short stub and almost all of the leg length is in the shin.
4. Girl on slide, side: **b**. The dress in b sits on a flat seat and the thigh runs forward to the knee, while in a the body folds at the hip with the legs angled down, like a jump or a fall.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Child on swing (front): the shorts are a thin strip with no lap depth, so the thighs barely show. The legs run straight down from the hem with no knee mark, and the figure looks like it is standing. The left foot sits lower than the right.
- Girl on swing (front): there is the same lack of lap. The hem sits straight across with the legs hanging under it and no knees, and it reads as standing in a short dress. The left foot is again lower than the right.
- Child on slide (side): the thigh is very short and the shin very long, so the proportions look wrong. There is no flat seat under the hip, and the hip angle and downward-sloping legs make the child look like he is leaping or falling backward, not sliding. The two feet are stacked one on top of the other and look like one doubled shoe.
- Girl on slide (side): the thigh is short and the shin long, and the dress ends in a narrow, slanted edge with no seat under it. The pose reads as a backward fall. The feet are stacked and doubled.

### Row b
- Child on swing (front): the shorts block is wide and boxy, with a hard flat bottom edge. The shins are still long and quite straight below the knee bumps, and the left foot sits lower than the right.
- Girl on swing (front): a diagonal fold line runs across the skirt over the lap and looks a little like a stray seam. The skirt's bottom edge is a straight flat line, and the left foot is lower than the right.
- Child on slide (side): the seat of the shorts ends in a sharp square corner at the bottom left, which looks boxy and stiff. The shins are still long relative to the thigh, the legs are almost straight, and the stacked feet look doubled.
- Girl on slide (side): the bottom of the dress is a flat square block with a hard corner, like a box under her. The shin is long and the stacked feet look doubled.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- On the slide figures the arms reach far forward and up, which together with the torso leaning back makes the figures look a bit like they are mid-jump.
- At picture size the front swing figures in row a read as standing children. Row b reads as seated, but only just.

## 4. Choice

**Row b**, fairly sure. It reads as seated in all four figures, at both sizes. The squareness of its seat corners is the main thing to soften.
