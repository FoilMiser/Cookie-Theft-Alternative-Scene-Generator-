# v0.48 blind screen: what it found (the work list for v0.49)

The v0.48 screen read the set's 21 pictures and 66 probes with the prompts of v0.47 (`screen_prompts.md`), which now carry in the templates what v0.47 added when it sent them. There were eleven readers of eight pictures (the last of seven) and four scorers of 21 or 22. One template failed the legibility gate, the coming rain, on one inference a reader left unsaid, and nothing was redrawn, swapped or read again after the screen (below). No item of the 971 was misread. The oddity tally is by angle, as in v0.39. Keys, descriptions and scores are in `v048/`.

v0.48 worked Sam's brief: "go for v0.48 now. continue refining the character art, focusing on a couple of individual characters first in blender and really making them look good, then also fixing things like the quality of the animals and their limbs. I am not a fan of the squashed oval heads, and the faces need fixing. feel free to update the art style to make it easier to make the characters look better, and make sure to check for quality as you are working. I have blender installed so feel free to open that and do work there." The work is in the cast (builder 0.48.0) and in three placement rules (revision 45), each change behind a switch, and each was judged blind before it went into scenes (`isolation_v048.md`, `design_tests_v048.md`):
- **a study of two figures**, the woman in the yellow dress and the man in the red shirt, built alone in Blender one change at a time; what came of it is on every person: an egg head with a jaw and a chin, a smaller and lower face with a plain line for a mouth, hair cut along a curve with a parting, a face of its own for a side view, one flat tone with no shadow tone, a tee to the hips, feet turned out;

- **v0.47's list**: the walker about to trip (R1) and which way a side-view figure goes (O1), by the side view's face, two motion lines and a nearer obstacle; the figures over the pond (O2), by a rule. R2 to R6, O3 and O5 to O9 were not worked.

In the template's other two pictures the readers went on to the rain: "A back garden under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds, so it looks like rain is coming." (19). One unsaid inference in three pictures is 0.889; with five pictures it would have been 0.933. It heads the list for v0.49, with five pictures, because a template that passes or fails on whether one reader finishes a thought is drawn too quietly: the sky and a pointing hand, and nothing a person does about the rain.

**Why no picture was swapped or read again.** A probe swapped for one that looks better to the builder is selection, and a second reading of an unchanged picture is a second roll of the dice; either would raise the count without a change in what a patient sees. The numbers above are the first and only count.

**What it means for the release.** v0.48's screen is the cleanest of the five on the set: 0.993 over all items, no misread anywhere in 971 items, 2 logged oddities in the set for 7. The template the release was aimed at, the walker about to trip, reads in full in five pictures after failing twice, and the redrawn animals are named without fail. Three things stand against that and are not to be read past. The gate count did not move, 63 of 64, with a different template under it. The new, finer faces cost two readings the old ones had: no moustache or beard is named any more, and four grown figures are called a girl or a boy in 11 of their 81 pictures, where v0.47's count for the same four by the same rule was 6 of 80.
