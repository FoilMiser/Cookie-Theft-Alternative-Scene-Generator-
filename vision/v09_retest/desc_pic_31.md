# Picture 31: description

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate on the left, a house with a blue door on the right, and a sun in the top left. A woman in a blue hoodie with a brown ponytail is painting the fence blue with a brush. The section next to the gate is already painted, and a paint can and a paint tray sit on the grass at her feet. A trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the grass to a white dog with brown spots, whose paws are blue. It has clearly stepped in the paint and is walking away. In the front on the left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping a pile of dirt or rubbish on the path. Strangely, she is using a giant silver fork instead of a broom, and a little dust cloud rises from the pile. A clothesline between two posts holds a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans. On the right, a brown-and-white dog sits on the doorstep of the house holding a red lead in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. There are also a flower bed full of colourful flowers, a wooden bench, a teal watering can, an empty wheelbarrow, a bush and a tree behind the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The sweeping tool really does look like an oversized dinner fork. It could possibly be read as a strange metal rake, but its shape is a fork.
- I can't be sure whether the debris pile is dirt, leaves or litter. It has dark bits and a white scrap in it.
- The paintbrush has a fairly long handle, which could be a roller or a long brush.
- The brown dog may just be holding the lead rather than asking for a walk, but the pose strongly suggests it wants to go out.
