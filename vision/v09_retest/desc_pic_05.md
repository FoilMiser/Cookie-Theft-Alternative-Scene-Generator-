# Picture 05

## Free description

This is a sunny town square or high street paved with grey slabs, in front of a row of colourful two-storey shops with striped awnings. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes sits on a wooden bench licking a pink ice-cream cone with her eyes closed. A brown dog with a red collar is stretching up beside her with its nose toward her, as if it is begging for or about to grab the ice cream. Behind them, a woman with a ponytail in a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping the pavement with a broom next to a black lamp post. At the bottom centre, a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is sitting up holding an open newspaper and appears to be reading it, which is impossible for a real dog. An orange cat is lying stretched out on the pavement at the bottom right, apparently asleep. A red bird is flying near the rooftops at the top left. There are also two bicycles in bike racks (a teal one behind the bench and a red one at the front), a fruit stall with a striped canopy on the right, a cardboard box, a tree, a green bin, two small café tables with chairs at the front left, and a potted plant in the bottom-right corner.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The brown dog could be begging, sniffing, or about to snatch the ice cream; it could also be stretching or jumping up. The woman, with eyes closed, does not seem to notice.
- The orange cat could be sleeping or simply lying down; its eyes look closed.
- The white dog reading a newspaper is impossible; it may be a deliberate cartoon joke.
- The brown dog overlaps the front of the bench and the woman's legs slightly, so it is a little hard to tell exactly where its front paws are.
