# Picture 04

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate on the left, a sandy path with stepping stones on the bottom left, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. A girl with a brown ponytail, a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes stands in the middle of the lawn holding the end of a green garden hose up in both hands and peering into the nozzle, as if wondering why no water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel next to the flower bed by the house, across the grass to her. A white goat with small horns is standing right on top of the hose, which would block the water. It looks like if the goat steps off, the water will suddenly spray into the girl's face. Near the fence on the left are two potted plants, and a teal watering can sits on the grass at the bottom left. Behind the fence there is a birdhouse on a pole, and on the right are a flower bed, an empty wooden bench, a tree and a bush.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Whether the goat is deliberately standing on the hose or just happens to be there is unclear, but its hooves are on the hose.
- There may be a tiny drop or highlight at the nozzle; it is too small to say whether any water is coming out.
- The girl could be looking into the nozzle because it has stopped, or could be about to point it somewhere; her posture (holding it close to her face) suggests she is checking it.
- A goat in a back garden is unusual but not impossible. Nothing looks unintentionally strange.
