# Picture 01

## Free description

This is a sunny day in a park, with a sun in the top-left corner, two white clouds and rolling green hills in the background. In the middle, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers stands beside a tall black lamp post. She holds a small white bag or cup in one hand and is tossing bits of food (three small brown crumbs are in the air) toward a pond on the right. A white duck with an orange beak is swimming across the pond toward her, leaving ripple lines behind it, so it looks like she is feeding the duck. In the front, a girl in a pink dress with yellow shoes sits at the edge of a red-and-white checked picnic blanket, with a wicker picnic basket on the grass next to her. On the left there is a winding sandy path, a large tree, a small bush, a wooden park bench and a green rubbish bin. On the right there is a bed of colourful flowers and, beside it, a wooden ladder. The odd thing is the ladder: it stands upright at a slant in the open grass with nothing behind it to lean on, which would be impossible in real life. A grey rock sits in the grass at the bottom centre.
