# Picture 18

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence and gate at the back, a stepping-stone path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A family is playing. On the left a small girl in a pink outfit sits on a swing on a metal swing set, smiling with her eyes closed. In the middle a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands with his hands held out, ready to catch a red ball that is flying through the air toward him; a dotted line shows the ball came from a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts on the right, who has his arm stretched out as if he just threw it. Behind them, near the fence, a girl with a brown ponytail, blue top and black trousers holds a string up to a purple kite with a bow tail, which is right in front of the tree behind the fence. At the bottom, a black-and-white cat lies on the grass, probably asleep, next to a red bowl of pet food. A flower bed and a bush are near the house. It looks like a happy, ordinary afternoon; the main question is whether the kite is flying or caught in the tree.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The kite: it is drawn overlapping the tree's leaves with its tail hanging over them. It could be flying in the sky in front of the tree, or it could be stuck/tangled in the tree.
- The ball: the dotted line connects the boy's hand to the ball, so most likely the boy threw it to the man. It could also be read the other way - the man threw it and the boy is reaching to catch it - but the man's open hands suggest he is the catcher.
- The ball is quite high and slightly past the man's hands, so it might go over his head.
- The cat: probably sleeping; it could also be lying down by its food bowl resting or stretching.
- The girl with the kite is standing very close to the fence; her head is above the fence top, which is fine but makes her look a bit like she is standing on it at first glance (her shadow shows she is on the grass).
