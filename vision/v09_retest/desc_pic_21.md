# Picture 21

## Free description

This is a sunny park with green hills and trees in the background, a winding path on the left and a pond on the right. At the front left a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is sweeping the path with a broom that has a yellow brush. In the middle a girl in a pink dress stands next to the lamp post flying an orange kite, whose long string goes up to the kite high in the sky in front of a cloud. Below her, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice cream cone and looks unhappy, because at his feet a white spotted dog with a blue collar is eating a pink ice cream that has fallen onto the grass. On the right a young woman with a brown ponytail, blue hoodie and black trousers is walking past the pond carrying a cardboard box. There is an empty bench, a green rubbish bin by the lamp post and another in the bottom right corner. Nothing else looks wrong; the main mishap is the dropped ice cream being eaten by the dog.

- The kite overlaps a cloud. It reads as the kite flying in front of the cloud, but it could look as if it is stuck on/in the cloud.
- The boy: most likely he dropped his ice cream and the dog is eating it; he could also be holding a cone he hasn't been given ice cream in yet, but his expression and the dog suggest he dropped it.
