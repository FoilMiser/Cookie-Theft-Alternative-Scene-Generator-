# Picture 35: description

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence and gate at the back, a path with stepping stones on the left, a house with a blue door on the right, and a sun in the top left. A man in a red top and blue jeans stands at the clothesline pegging up a white T-shirt next to a striped towel and a pair of jeans that are already hanging. An orange cat is walking along the top of the fence near the tree. In the front right, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is watering the flower bed. Strangely, she is pouring the water out of a frying pan instead of a watering can. A teal watering can sits unused on the grass in the front left, and a hose reel is right beside the flower bed. On the left is a grey A-frame swing set with one empty red swing. There is also an empty wheelbarrow on the grass in the front centre.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The object the woman is pouring from looks like a frying pan or skillet. It might also be read as a large ladle or a shallow saucepan, but either way it is a kitchen pan, not a watering tool. That is odd, because a watering can and a hose reel are both right there.
- The man faces the line with his back half-turned, so it is hard to see his face clearly.
- A cat walking on the fence is ordinary, but it is easy to miss up in the top-right area against the tree.
