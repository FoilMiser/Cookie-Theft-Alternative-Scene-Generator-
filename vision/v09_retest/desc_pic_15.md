# Picture 15

## Free description

This is a sunny town street or shopping plaza with a row of four colourful two-storey shops behind a paved square. Lots of people and animals are doing different things. On the left a boy in a yellow shirt stands with both arms up, looking toward a tree that has a blue ball stuck in its leaves, while a tan dog lies asleep on the pavement behind him. In the middle an older woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a bench reading a red book. Behind her a young person in a blue hoodie jumps in the air with arms raised, looking happy. On the right an older man in a cap and vest holds a watering can, but the stream of water goes up from the can to the shop awning instead of falling down onto anything. In front a bearded man in a green shirt is in a sitting position drinking from a cup, but there is no chair or bench under him. Near his feet a black-and-white cat creeps toward a red bird that is pecking at the ground, so the cat may be about to pounce. At the front right a spotted dog is eating a pink ice cream that has fallen on the ground, and a little girl in a pink dress next to it holds a cone that looks empty, so it is probably her ice cream. There is also a fruit stall with nobody at it, a bike parked in a rack, a flower planter, a street lamp, an empty café table and two potted plants.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Watering can water: read one way, water is flowing upward out of the can to the awning, which is impossible. Read another way, water is dripping off the awning down into the can. Either way, nothing is actually being watered; the flower planter is to the left of the lamp post, not under the stream.
- The man in the green shirt is clearly posed as if seated on a chair, but there is no seat - he looks like he is sitting on air.
- The boy with raised arms: he could be reaching or calling for the ball stuck in the tree, or he could just be cheering/stretching. His face points toward the tree.
- The cat: could be stalking/about to pounce on the red bird, or just walking past it.
- The girl's cone: looks empty, which suggests the ice cream the dog is eating was hers, but she does not look especially upset; she seems to be just holding/looking at the cone.
- The spotted dog's head is drawn as a white round shape sitting on top of the pink ice cream, so it is a little hard to tell where the head ends and the ice cream begins; it could even be read as a white scoop on a pink scoop.
- The jumping person: could be jumping for joy, dancing, or reacting to something; nothing obvious nearby explains it.
- The tan dog at the far left is small and partly squashed in shape; most likely lying asleep, but could also be read as lying stretched out on its back/side.
