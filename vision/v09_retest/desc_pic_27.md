# Picture 27

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden, with the sun in the top right and two white clouds. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, and a wooden picket fence with a gate on the right runs across the back. At the front left a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. Next to him a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is watering the flower bed by pouring water out of what looks like a dark grey frying pan, while an actual teal watering can sits unused on the grass at the front right. In the middle a clothesline strung between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel, and a black-and-white cat is walking along the line like a tightrope. Below it a little girl in a pink dress holds a blue and a yellow balloon on strings and reaches up toward a red balloon that has slipped away and is floating up. At the front centre an orange cat is crouched low, stalking a blue bird that is pecking at the grass, and looks about to pounce. On the right there is an empty swing set, and a white dog with brown patches and a blue collar is lying stretched out on the path. The odd things are the frying pan being used to water the plants and the cat balancing on the washing line.
