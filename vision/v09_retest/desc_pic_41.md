# Picture 41

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny park playground with a yellow sun in the top right, two clouds, a railing fence and hedge across the back, and a tree on the far left. A young woman with a brown ponytail and a blue top is flying a yellow diamond-shaped kite high in the sky, holding the string up with one hand. On a bench at the left a bearded man in a green shirt is reading a red book, and while he isn't looking a blue bird is going for a huge sandwich lying on the bench next to him; the sandwich is oddly large. Behind the kite-flyer, a brown dog with a red collar has its head down at something pink on the ground, maybe a dropped ice cream or a cake, while a small girl in a pink dress stands nearby with a cone or something else held to her mouth. A woman in a yellow dress is taking a photo with a camera, pointed toward the right. On the right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a yellow balloon on a string in front of a slide, and at the far right an old man in a blue cap and brown vest waves one raised hand, with little lines showing the movement or a call. In the foreground an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses walks along reading an open book, heading straight for a cardboard box on the grass that she looks about to trip over. Near the bottom left, a white animal with brown patches and a blue collar sits on the grass holding up and "reading" a newspaper, which is impossible. In the bottom right an orange cat lies on the grass next to a sandbox with a red bucket and a blue spade. An empty red seesaw stands in the left foreground.
