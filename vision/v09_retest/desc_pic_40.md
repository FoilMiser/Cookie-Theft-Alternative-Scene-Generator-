# Picture 40

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a yellow sun in the top right, two white clouds, a tall wooden picket fence with bushes behind it, and a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the left. A tree stands behind the house and a birdhouse on a pole rises behind the fence. In front of the house, a woman with a brown ponytail, a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes is watering a colourful flower bed with a garden hose; the water arcs from the nozzle onto the flowers, and the hose runs across the grass from a teal hose reel at the bottom left. In the centre foreground, a woman with short dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes is digging a hole in the lawn with a spade, with a mound of dirt piled beside it. Behind them a clothesline between two wooden posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. On the right, a man with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans is walking toward the left, carrying a cardboard box in both hands. Next to him is a grey metal swing set with one red swing seat hanging empty. A wooden gate on the far right opens onto a beige path with stepping stones running down to the bottom right, and a teal watering can sits on the grass near the path. Everyone is busy with garden chores, and nothing looks dangerous or impossible.
