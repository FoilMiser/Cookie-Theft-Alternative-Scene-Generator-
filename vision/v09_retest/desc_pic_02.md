# Picture 02

## Free description

This is a sunny park with the sun in the top-right corner, two clouds, green hills, a pond on the left and a winding sandy path on the right. In the middle distance, a girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes is kicking an orange-and-white ball with one leg stretched out, and the ball is in the air just off her foot. In the middle, a tan dog with a long brown ear and a red collar or bow is sitting upright on its haunches holding open a newspaper in its front paws, as if reading it, which is impossible for a real dog. At the bottom centre, a black cat with white paws is creeping low across the grass toward a red bird (like a cardinal) that is pecking at the ground with its back to the cat, so the cat looks like it is about to pounce on the bird. In the bottom-right corner, a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is lying on its belly on the path with its head raised. A blue bird is perched in the large tree on the right. There is also a tall black lamp post in the centre, a flower bed and a green rubbish bin at the bottom left, and bushes on both sides.
