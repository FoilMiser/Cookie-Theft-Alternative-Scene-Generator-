# Picture 20

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence and gate, a stepping-stone path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the middle a girl with a brown ponytail, blue hoodie and black trousers is holding the nozzle of a long green garden hose and looking at it, but only a single drop of water is coming out. The reason seems to be that a white goat with small horns is standing right on top of the hose, which runs across the grass from a hose reel by the house. On the left, a separate hose runs to a sprinkler on the path, and the sprinkler is spraying water onto a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is getting wet. Something impossible is in the tree behind the fence: an orange goldfish is sitting in its leaves. There is also a teal watering can on the grass, a flower bed, and an empty wooden bench by the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The goldfish in the tree is clearly impossible/out of place.
- The goat on the hose: I read it as the goat squashing the hose so only a drip comes out of the girl's nozzle. Another reading is that the hose just isn't turned on yet and the goat happens to be standing there.
- The sprinkler water lands on the cardboard box - it could be an accident (the sprinkler is soaking something that shouldn't get wet) or the box could just happen to be in the way.
- Which hose feeds which: the girl's hose appears to come from the reel by the house, and the sprinkler's hose goes off the left edge of the picture, so they look like two separate hoses; it is possible they are meant to be connected, but I can't see a link.
- Why a goat is in a back garden is a little odd but not impossible.
