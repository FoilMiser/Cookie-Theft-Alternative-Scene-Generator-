# Picture 29: description

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park, with green hills and trees in the background, a sun in the top right and two white clouds. On the left-centre there is an oval pond with cattails (bulrushes) at both ends, and a white duck swimming in it. A woman with a dark ponytail, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, stands on the grass to the right of the pond. She holds a fishing rod out over the water, and her line ends in a red-and-white float with a ripple around it. The duck is right next to the float and faces it, so it looks as if the duck might peck at the float or get caught on the line. In the front on the left, an older man with a grey moustache stands with his hands on his hips. He wears a white shirt, a brown vest, suspenders and dark trousers, and he has a blue bucket on his head as if it were a hat, which is the odd thing in the picture. Behind the woman are a tall black lamp post and a green bin. On the right there are an empty wooden bench, a winding sandy path, a large tree and some bushes. A grey rock sits on the grass at the bottom centre, and another green bin is cut off at the bottom-left corner.
