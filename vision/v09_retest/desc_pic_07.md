# Picture 07

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden seen the other way round: a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the left, a tall wooden fence runs across the back, a wooden gate is on the right, and a sandy path with stepping stones runs down the right side. A woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers stands beside a bed of colourful flowers holding a teal watering can. The odd thing is the water: instead of pouring down onto the flowers, the stream comes out of the spout, curves and rises upward, with drops flying up into the air above the flowers. That is impossible, since water should fall down. Behind her, a washing line strung between two wooden posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes, held by clothes pegs. Two potted plants stand near the fence on the right, and a grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the bottom. There is a tree and a bush beside the house, and a sun in the top right with two clouds.

## Inventory

- Woman, centre-left, front: dark hair in a ponytail, purple top, teal trousers, dark shoes. Standing facing left and holding a watering can over the flower bed.
- Watering can: teal watering can held by the woman at waist height.
- Water stream: blue stream that leaves the spout, curves round and goes upward, ending in drops rising above the flowers.
- Flower bed: soil mound with red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers, left, in front of the house.
- Washing line: rope between two wooden posts, centre, sagging slightly.
- Jeans: blue jeans pegged on the washing line.
- Towel: yellow towel with red stripes pegged on the washing line.
- Potted plants: two terracotta pots with green plants near the fence, right-centre.
- Wheelbarrow: grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles, bottom centre, empty.
- House: cream house with red roof, window and blue door with step, left.
- Fence and gate: tall wooden picket fence across the back; wooden gate on the far right.
- Path: sandy path with oval stepping stones, right.
- Tree and bush: tree behind the house, round bush beside it.
- Sky: sun top right, two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The water could be read two ways: (a) water spraying upward out of the can, against gravity; or (b) water falling from above down toward the can. The drops at the top point upward and the stream connects to the spout, so (a) seems more likely, and either way it looks impossible.
- The woman does not seem to react to the strange water; she looks at the flowers.
- Nothing else looks unintentionally strange.
