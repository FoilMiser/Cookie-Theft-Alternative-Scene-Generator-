# Picture 37

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park with a pond, rolling green hills and bushes in the background, a yellow sun in the top right and two white clouds. On the left-centre there is an oval blue pond with brown cattail reeds at both ends. An elderly man with grey hair, a blue cap, a white shirt, a brown vest and dark trousers stands on the grass at the right edge of the pond, fishing. His rod reaches out over the water and the line drops to a red-and-white float bobbing in a small ripple ring. Behind him stands a tall black lamp post. A sandy path winds from the back right down to the bottom of the picture, with an empty wooden bench beside it. On the path a woman with short dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes is scattering seeds from a small white bag or cup. Two birds, one red and one blue, are pecking up the seeds on the grass beside her. In the front left a woman with a brown ponytail, a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes kneels on one knee next to a colourful flower bed, working near a terracotta pot of pink tulip-like flowers with a small hand tool, as if she is about to plant them. Nothing looks dangerous or impossible; it is a calm scene. There are trees on the far left and far right.
