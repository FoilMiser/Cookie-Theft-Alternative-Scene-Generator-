# Picture 23

## Free description
This is a playground in a park on a gloomy day: the sky is grey and three dark storm clouds hang overhead, so it looks as if it might rain soon. On the left a purple kite is caught in the top of a tree, and a small dark-skinned child in a yellow top and orange shorts stands below holding the kite string up, apparently trying to pull it free. Next to them is a red seesaw: a small girl in a pink dress sits on the raised left end, while a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers sits on the low right end reading an open book instead of paying attention to her. Behind them a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a park bench reading a red book, and a person in a blue hoodie stands beside the bench pointing up at the sky, looking at the dark clouds. In the centre a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans holds a camera to his face and is taking a photo toward the left, toward the seesaw and kite. Behind him is an empty swing set, a green rubbish bin and, to the right, a red and yellow slide with a ladder. At the front right is a sandpit with a red bucket and a blue spade but nobody playing in it, and at the far right a woman in a yellow dress is licking a pink ice-cream cone. The odd things are the kite stuck in the tree and people carrying on (eating ice cream, reading, taking photos) while a storm looks to be coming.
