## Free description
This is a busy, sunny, windy back garden behind a house with a red roof and a blue door, enclosed by a wooden fence with a gate on the right. In the front left a man in a blue hoodie sits on a bench, happily eating a pink ice-cream cone with his eyes closed. A brown dog with a red collar is stretching up towards him, clearly begging for or about to grab the ice cream. Near the house a girl in a pink dress is flying an orange diamond kite with a bow tail, which is high up next to a cloud. In the middle a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks under a clothesline (blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel hanging on it) looking at an open book or a white sheet in her hands, with a blue bucket on the grass nearby. On the right a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on a swing, smiling. By the gate a bearded man in a green shirt has both arms up and looks surprised, because the wind (shown by white lines and blowing leaves) has blown his blue cap off into the air. In the middle of the lawn an orange cat is creeping or running towards a red bird pecking at the ground, so the cat seems about to pounce. In the front centre there is a grey wheelbarrow whose wheel is drawn as a square with an X in it, which looks wrong. In the lower right a white dog with brown spots is in a play-bow pose on the path near a red food bowl. A small blue bird is perched on the fence by the tree on the left.
