# Picture 39

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a yellow sun in the top left, two white clouds, a tall wooden picket fence across the back with bushes and a tree behind it, and a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue front door on the right. On the left a man with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller or brush; a few boards next to him are already painted blue, and a small grey paint tin with blue paint sits on the grass by his feet. In the centre, a clothesline is strung between two wooden posts with a white T-shirt, a yellow towel with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. A man with short dark hair and a beard, wearing a green shirt and khaki trousers, stands under the line reaching up to peg the washing. There is a wooden gate in the fence at the far left, and a beige path with stepping stones runs from it down to the bottom-left corner. In front of the house there is a bed of colourful flowers and a wooden bench at the bottom right. On the grass in the front lie a teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles. One odd thing is a tan box or crate on the right, sitting at a tilt between the fence and the house corner; its far end disappears behind the house wall, as if it were leaning or falling or sticking out of the wall. Otherwise the scene is a calm day of chores.
