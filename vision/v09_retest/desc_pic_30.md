# Picture 30: description

## Free description
This is a sunny town square or high street with a row of colourful two-storey shops (cream, peach, pale green and pink) with striped awnings. A red bird with its wings raised is perched on the roof edge between the cream building and the peach building. In the centre, an older man in a flat cap, white shirt, brown vest and dark trousers sits on a bench eating a pink ice cream cone. A brown-and-white dog with a red collar stretches up towards him with its head near his knees, as if it is after the ice cream. To the left, an older woman with grey hair and glasses stands with her hands on her hips. She has a blue bucket on her head like a hat, which looks odd. In the front on the right of centre, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is walking while reading an open book. She is heading towards a red-and-white ball lying on the pavement, so she looks about to trip over it. Further right, a woman in a blue hoodie carrying a yellow shopping bag walks past a fruit stall with crates of red, orange, yellow and green fruit. An orange cat lies asleep on the pavement in the bottom-right corner. There are also a tree in a planter, a café table with two chairs, bins, a lamp post, a cardboard box and a potted plant.
