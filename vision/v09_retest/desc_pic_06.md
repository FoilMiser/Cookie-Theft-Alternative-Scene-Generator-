# Picture 06

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate on the left, a sandy path with stepping stones on the bottom left, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. A man in an orange cap, an orange high-visibility vest with reflective stripes and blue trousers or overalls is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller. A section of fence to his left is already painted blue, and the rest is still plain wood. On the ground beside him is a paint can and a paint tray with blue paint in it. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has stepped in the paint tray and is walking away to the right, leaving a trail of blue paw prints across the grass; its feet are blue. The man is facing the fence and does not seem to have noticed. Around the garden there is a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow at the front, a flower bed, a hose reel and an empty wooden bench near the house, and a tree and bush behind it.
