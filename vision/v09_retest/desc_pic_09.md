## Free description
This is a busy, sunny park with a winding path, a pond, a lamp post, trees and a flower bed. On the far left a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands with both arms raised, looking up unhappily at a blue ball stuck in the leaves of a tree. Next to the path a park worker in an orange cap and an orange high-visibility vest is raking a small pile of leaves. Further back, a girl in a pink dress is looking at her hand, maybe at an empty ice-cream cone, while a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar sniffs or eats something pink on the ground, probably her dropped ice cream. An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a bench reading a red book. She doesn't notice that a blue bird is pecking at the big sandwich lying on the bench beside her. In the front centre a brown dog with a red collar holds an open newspaper in its front paws as if reading it, which is impossible. To the right, a man in a red shirt and jeans is digging a hole with a shovel next to a pile of dirt, and a bearded man in a green shirt is watering a bed of colourful flowers with a teal watering can. An orange cat is lying flat on the grass near the lamp post, and a red bird is flying in the sky in front of a cloud.
