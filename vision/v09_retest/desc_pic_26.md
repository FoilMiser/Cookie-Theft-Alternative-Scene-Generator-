# Picture 26

## Free description
It is a sunny but windy day in a back garden, with the sun in the top right and two white clouds. A cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the left, and a wooden picket fence with a gate on the right runs across the back. In the middle of the lawn a dark-skinned, bearded man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers stands with both arms raised and a startled look, reaching for his orange cap, which a gust of wind (shown by white wavy lines and a few blowing leaves) has blown off and is carrying away over the fence to the right. At the blue door a brown-and-white dog sits on the step holding its red lead in its mouth, as if asking to be taken for a walk. In front of the house there is an empty wooden bench and a flower bed full of colourful flowers. On the right two terracotta pots with green plants stand on the grass, a birdhouse on a pole pokes up behind the fence, and a path with stepping stones leads from the gate to the front. At the front right is a grey wheelbarrow whose wheel is a square instead of a circle, which is impossible for a wheel. The cap blowing away looks like what is about to happen next: the man may not catch it.
