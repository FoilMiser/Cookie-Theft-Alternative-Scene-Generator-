# Picture 16

## Free description

This is a sunny park with rolling green hills, hedges and trees in the background, a winding sand-coloured path on the left, and a pond on the right. There is only one person: an older man with a grey beard, a blue cap, a white shirt and a brown vest, sitting on the edge of a red-and-white checked picnic blanket in front of the pond. He is smiling with his eyes closed, one hand resting on the blanket, with a brown picnic basket beside his legs. A single white duck is swimming on the pond. The strange thing is a cardboard box hanging in mid-air, tilted, above the grass near a green rubbish bin and the lamp post, with its shadow on the grass below it, as if it is floating or falling. Nobody is near the box who could have thrown it. Otherwise the scene is calm: a flower bed with colourful flowers is on the right, a grey rock is in front, and another rubbish bin is at the bottom right corner.
