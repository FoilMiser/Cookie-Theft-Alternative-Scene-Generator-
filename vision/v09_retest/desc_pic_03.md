# Picture 03

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate on the left, a sandy path with stepping stones on the bottom left, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. On the left stands a grey metal swing set with one empty red swing seat hanging still. In the middle, a child with dark hair, a yellow top and orange shorts is jumping in the air with both arms raised, and her shadow is on the grass below her. At the front, a small girl in a pink dress holds the strings of a blue and a yellow balloon in one hand and reaches up with her other arm toward a purple balloon that has got away and is floating up and to the left, with its string hanging loose. In the centre-right, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is watering a bed of colourful flowers with a teal watering can, and water is pouring onto the flowers. Next to the flower bed, against the house, is a green garden-hose reel. On the far right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. A tree and a bush are behind and beside the house, and the sky has a sun at the top left and two clouds.

- Small girl, front centre: dark hair, pink dress, yellow shoes. Holding two balloon strings in one hand and reaching up with the other arm toward the escaping purple balloon.
- Woman watering, centre-right: dark short hair, yellow dress, red shoes. Pouring water from a watering can onto the flowers.
- Woman reading, front right, on the bench: dark hair, purple top, teal trousers, dark shoes. Sitting and reading a red book.
- Purple balloon: floating up and to the left, away from the small girl, with its string loose (no one holding it).
- Blue and yellow balloons: held by their strings in the small girl's hand.

- Swing set: grey metal A-frame swing set, left, with one red seat hanging empty.
- Bench: wooden bench, front right, occupied by the reading woman.
- Fence and gate: tall wooden picket fence across the back; wooden gate on the far left.
- Path: sandy path with oval stepping stones, bottom left.
- House: cream house with red roof, a window and a blue door with a step, right.
- Tree and bush: tree behind the house, round bush beside the house.
- Sky: sun top left, two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The jumping child could be jumping for joy, or jumping to try to catch the escaping balloon; she is to the right of the balloon and not reaching straight for it. She also appears fairly high above her shadow, which could be read as a big jump or as slightly floating.
- The small girl could be reaching after the purple balloon she let go of, or waving/pointing at it.
- The water from the watering can is drawn as a chain of blue segments with drops at the end; it reads as water pouring onto the flowers.
- Nothing else looks impossible or unintentionally strange.
