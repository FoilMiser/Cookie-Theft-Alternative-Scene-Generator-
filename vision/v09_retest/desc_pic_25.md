# Picture 25

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny park with green hills, a pond on the left, a lamp post in the middle and a winding path on the right. At the front left a little girl in a pink dress has just thrown a red-and-white ball; a dotted line shows it flying up over the edge of the pond, and a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands in front of the pond with both arms stretched out toward it as if to catch it. Next to him a wooden ladder stands straight up on the grass by the pond with nothing to lean against, which looks impossible. In the front centre a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers walks along reading an open book, heading toward a red bucket of water on the grass that he might trip over. In the background a small child in a yellow top and orange shorts walks with a yellow balloon, a woman in a yellow dress walks a white spotted dog on a red lead, and an older grey-haired woman with glasses walks down the path eating a pink ice-cream cone. On the right a woman in a purple top sits on a bench reading a red book while a blue bird beside her on the bench pecks at her big sandwich. At the far right a person in a blue hoodie points up at a blue ball stuck in the branches of a tree. There is also a flower bed and a rubbish bin at the front left.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The free-standing ladder is the clearest impossible-looking thing: it stands upright by the pond without leaning on anything. It could also be read as standing in the edge of the water, but its feet appear to be on the grass in front of the pond.
- The man in red could be getting ready to catch the ball, or could be clapping, or even about to dive into the pond; his hands are together and held out toward the ball.
- The ball's dotted path goes up over the pond, so it might land in the water instead of in the man's hands.
- The reading man is walking toward the red bucket and may be about to trip over or kick it; he could equally walk past it.
- The bird on the bench could be eating the sandwich or just standing next to it; its head is down at the sandwich, so I read it as pecking/stealing.
- The person in the blue hoodie could be a man or a woman.
- The thing the older woman carries could be a handbag strap; hard to make out.
