# Picture 36

## Free description
This is a sunny playground in a park, with a yellow sun in the top left, two white clouds, a row of green bushes and a dark railing fence across the back. On the left is a red-framed slide with a dark blue ladder and an orange-and-yellow chute. A boy with curly black hair, a yellow shirt and orange shorts has come down the slide and is sitting on the ground at its bottom end with his legs stretched out. On the right is a red seesaw on a grey triangular stand. An elderly man with grey hair, a blue cap, a brown vest and dark trousers is sitting on the low, front end of the seesaw with his legs crossed, reading an open book or newspaper. Because he is heavy and sitting still, a small girl in a pink dress with dark hair in a bun is stuck up in the air on the high end, holding the handle with her yellow shoes dangling. He doesn't seem to notice her; he looks absorbed in his reading. It looks wrong for an adult to sit on a children's seesaw and read while a child is left stranded at the top. In the front left there is an empty sandbox with a red bucket and a blue spade in it, and a grey rock beside it. A big tree stands on the far right and a bush pokes in at the far left edge.
