# Picture 33: description

## Free description
This is a back garden under a grey, stormy sky with three dark storm clouds, so it looks as if it is about to rain. There is a wooden fence and gate at the back, a path with stepping stones on the left, and a house with a blue door on the right. A small girl in a pink dress on the left has thrown a red-and-white ball high over towards a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers, who holds out his hands to catch it. In the middle, an older man in a flat cap, white shirt, brown vest and dark trousers looks up and points or reaches up with one arm near a clothesline that holds a striped towel and jeans. An older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses is watering the flower bed with a garden hose, even though rain seems to be coming. In the front, a lawn sprinkler connected to a hose is spraying water onto a cardboard box, which is getting wet. Very oddly, there is an orange goldfish in the leaves of the tree behind the house, which is impossible. In the front centre-right, a white goat with small horns has its head down, eating a tuft of grass. There are also a wooden bench, a hose reel and the flower bed with colourful flowers.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The goldfish in the tree is plainly impossible, though it could perhaps be read as a fish-shaped decoration hung in the tree.
- The older man's raised arm could mean he is pointing at the storm clouds or rain, pointing at the fish in the tree, or reaching to take the washing off the line before it rains.
- Watering the flowers with a hose and running a sprinkler while a storm is about to break seems unnecessary or odd.
- The sprinkler watering a cardboard box seems wrong. The box will get soggy, and it is unclear why the box is there.
- A goat grazing in a small back garden is unusual. Its body pose looks a little awkward or contorted, but it is clearly eating grass.
- The hose to the sprinkler lies across the path, so someone could trip on it.
