# Picture 24

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden, with the sun in the top left and two white clouds. A cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the right, with a flower bed of colourful flowers in front of it and a tree behind it. A wooden picket fence runs across the back, with a gate on the left. At the front left a dark-skinned, bearded man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers stands on the paved path pushing a gigantic silver fork, shaped exactly like a kitchen dinner fork but as tall as he is, into the path, kicking up a grey cloud of dust and a little pile of broken rubble beside a stepping stone. At the back an older man with a grey beard, a cap, a white shirt and a brown waistcoat is painting the fence blue with a long-handled brush; a section of the fence has already been painted blue, and a tin of blue paint stands at his feet. In the middle of the lawn a white goat with small horns is bending down to eat a tuft of grass. An empty swing set stands on the left side of the lawn, and a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow lie on the grass at the front. The thing that looks clearly wrong or impossible is the giant table fork being used as a garden tool.
