# pic_62

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a wooden gate and paved path on the right. A woman with a dark ponytail in a purple top and teal trousers is painting one section of the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; a paint tin is on the grass behind her. In the front middle, a garden sprinkler attached to a hose is spraying arcs of water, but the water is landing on a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is wet, dripping, and standing in a puddle, rather than on the flower bed. On the path at the right, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, is sweeping a small pile of dirt toward a red dustpan, but she is using what looks like a giant silver dinner fork instead of a broom. A little puff of dust rises from the dirt. Two potted plants stand by the fence, and a colourful flower bed is in front of the house. The sun is at the top right with two white clouds.
