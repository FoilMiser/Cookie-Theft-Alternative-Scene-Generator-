## Free description
A sunny back garden with the house (red roof, blue door) on the right, a flower bed and bush in front of it, and a washing line in the middle with a yellow-and-red striped top and blue jeans hanging still. On the left, on the path by the wooden gate, an old man with grey hair and a moustache, wearing a grey flat cap, a white shirt, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers, is sweeping a pile of autumn leaves with a broom. In the centre, a man with dark skin in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers holds the nozzle of a garden hose out in front of him with both hands and peers at it. Only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel by the house, along the ground in a long loop, and back up to his hands. A white goat with small dark horns stands on the grass between the man and the flower bed, looking towards the man. One of the goat's back hooves seems to be standing on the hose, which looks squashed there. That would explain why no water comes out. The water might suddenly spray the man in the face when the goat steps off. Two potted plants stand near the fence.
