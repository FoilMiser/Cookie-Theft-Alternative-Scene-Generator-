## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, a gate on the right and a paved path down the right side. In the middle back an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a stick; a section of about three fence panels has already been painted blue. At her feet on the grass there is a paint tin and a paint tray with blue paint in it. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is on the path to the right, and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the grass to the dog, whose paws are blue, so the dog has clearly stepped in the paint tray and is walking paint around. The dog is looking back towards the woman, and she is busy painting and does not seem to have noticed yet. In the foreground there is a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can, and on the left a colourful flower bed, a bush and a tree by the house. The sun is at top right with two clouds.
