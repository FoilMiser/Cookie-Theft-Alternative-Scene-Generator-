## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence and gate across the back, a house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a paved path on the left, and a sun and two clouds in the sky. At the back a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a handle; a section of fence boards behind the swing set has already been painted blue, and an open tin of blue paint stands on the grass by his feet. In the front a young woman (or older girl) with a dark ponytail, in a purple top and teal trousers, is digging a hole in the lawn with a spade, with a pile of brown soil heaped beside it. On the left there is an empty swing set, a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can on the grass. On the right there is a flowerbed of colourful flowers and a green bush beside the house. The odd, impossible thing is an orange goldfish sitting in the branches of the tree behind the fence, out of water.
