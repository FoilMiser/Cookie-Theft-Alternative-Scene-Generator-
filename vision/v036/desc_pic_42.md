## Free description
A sunny back garden with the house (red roof, blue door) on the left, a flower bed and bush in front of it, and a wooden picket fence across the back. A boy in a red cap, a blue hoodie and dark trousers is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle. A section of a few fence boards to his left is already blue. Behind him on the grass are a paint tin and a paint tray with blue paint in it. A trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the grass to a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar, which is walking away to the right with blue paint on its paws. The dog has clearly stepped in the paint tray and is spreading paint prints, and the boy has not noticed. On the right, on the path, a man with dark skin in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers is sweeping a pile of autumn leaves with a broom. A birdhouse on a tall pole, two potted plants, a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can are also in the garden.
