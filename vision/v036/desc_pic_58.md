## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, stands in the middle holding the nozzle of a green garden hose up in front of her face, as if peering into it to see why nothing is coming out; there is just a small drop at the nozzle. The hose runs from a hose reel next to the house, along the ground, and loops round to her. A small white goat with short horns and a little beard is standing right on top of the hose, between her and the reel, which would block the water. The hose just past the goat's back feet looks swollen or thicker, as if water is building up behind the blockage. It looks as if, when the goat steps off, the water may suddenly spray into the woman's face. A flower bed with colourful flowers is near the house, two potted plants stand by the fence on the left, and there is a tree behind the fence on the right.
