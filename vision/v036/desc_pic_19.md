# pic_19

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate on the far left, a paved path down the left side and a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the right. A woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers stands next to a colourful flower bed holding a teal watering can tipped towards the flowers. The odd thing is the water: instead of pouring down onto the flowers, the stream comes out of the spout and curves upwards into the air, with water drops at its top end flying up, which is impossible because water should fall down. On the left there is a grey swing frame with one empty red swing and a birdhouse with a red roof on a tall pole near the gate. In the foreground there is an empty grey wheelbarrow, and an empty wooden bench stands at the front right by the house. A tree shows above the fence on the right, and the sun is at top left with two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
