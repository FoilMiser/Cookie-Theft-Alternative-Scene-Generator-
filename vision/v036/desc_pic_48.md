## Free description
A sunny paved town square in front of a row of four coloured shop buildings (cream, peach, pale green, pink) with striped awnings. On the left, a street worker in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest sweeps a pile of autumn leaves with a yellow broom, next to a tree in a round planter. In the centre, a girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench, eating a small pink treat. A white dog with brown ears sits up on its hind legs in front of her, apparently begging for her food. Further back, a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts holds the strings of a blue and a red balloon in one hand. He stretches his other arm up toward a purple balloon floating just above him, which seems to have got loose. A red bird perches on top of a street lamp next to him, and a blue bird flies in the sky at the top left. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie walks to the left with his eyes on an open book. A cardboard box sits on the ground right in his path, so he looks about to trip over it. Behind him is a market stall with crates of red, orange, yellow and green fruit, but nobody is selling.
