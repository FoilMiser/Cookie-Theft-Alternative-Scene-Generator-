## Scored 1 but with caveats (not penalised, worth checking in the pictures)

The accepted phrasings were enough for full credit, but part of the target was missing, hedged or attached to the wrong kind of person:

- **Part of the target not conveyed**
  - pic_01 i01 (woman holding a hose, looking into the nozzle): she holds the nozzle "out in front", pointed to the right. She is not seen looking into it or puzzled, and she is called a girl.
  - pic_21 i02 and pic_23 i02 (bird pecking at a sandwich): the bird is "standing right next to the sandwich, facing it", not pecking.
  - pic_13 i02 (child upset, reaching for a balloon): the child is read as surprised, not upset.
- **Inference stated with a hedge, and the unsure section gives an alternative**
  - pic_01 i04: dog blocking the water ("may be blocking"; or the bulge is just a hose joint).
  - pic_02 i03: dog begging, or just being friendly.
  - pic_21 i03 and pic_23 i03: bird about to steal the sandwich ("as if about to take it"; or "may just be standing there"). In neither picture is the reader's not noticing mentioned.
  - pic_02 i07, pic_21 i12, pic_23 i09 and pic_24 i04: the walker "might" trip over the box, or might pass beside it.
- **Wrong kind of person (action credited)**
  - pic_01 i11 and pic_10 i01: the grandmother in the teal shirt and grey shorts is read as an older man. The grandmother in a bun and skirt is always read as an elderly woman.
  - pic_12 i01: the man hanging laundry is called a boy. The same man is read as a man in pic_01, pic_09, pic_22 and pic_23.
  - pic_01 i01: the woman in the yellow dress is called a girl. She is read as a woman in pic_02, pic_23 and pic_24.
