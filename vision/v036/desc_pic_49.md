# pic_49

## Free description
A sunny playground with a dark metal fence and a hedge across the back. On the far left, a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts holds a red and a yellow balloon on strings. He reaches up with his other arm toward a blue balloon floating just above and to the left of him, near a tree trunk. The blue balloon seems to have got away, and his face looks unhappy. A blue bird perches on a branch of the tree above him. An old man in a white shirt and brown waistcoat sits on a wooden bench, drinking from a red cup through a straw, with his eyes closed. In the middle is a swing set with one empty red seat, and a green bin stands beside it. To the right is a red and yellow slide, and beyond it a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie flies an orange kite, which is high in the sky. In the front centre, a red bird stands on the grass while an orange cat crouches low right behind it, about to pounce. An empty sandpit with a grey rock beside it is at the front right.
