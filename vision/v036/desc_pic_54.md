## Free description
A sunny back garden. A cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the left, and a wooden fence with a gate in the right-hand corner runs across the back. A paved path leads from the gate down to the front right. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers and a little girl in a pink dress are playing catch with a red-and-white ball. The ball is in the air just in front of the woman's outstretched hands, with a dashed line showing its curved path from the girl. The girl's arm is still stretched out, so it looks as though she has just thrown it and the woman is about to catch it. Next to the blue door, a brown dog sits holding a red lead in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. In the front centre, a white dog with brown spots crouches in a playful bow over a yellow-green tennis ball, wagging its tail (motion marks). On the path at the right, an old woman with grey hair and glasses sweeps a pile of autumn leaves with a yellow broom. A teal watering can stands on the grass, and a bed of colourful flowers is in the left-centre.
