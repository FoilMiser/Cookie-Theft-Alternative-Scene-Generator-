# pic_73

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate at the far left, a paved path on the left and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the left, by the path, a woman with dark hair in a bun, wearing a green top and beige trousers, is sweeping dirt into a red dustpan. Strangely, she is using a giant silver fork as if it were a broom, which is wrong: a fork is not a sweeping tool and is far too big. In the middle back, a man or boy in a red shirt and blue jeans is painting the fence blue with a long-handled paint roller. One section of the fence is already blue, and a paint tin sits on the grass behind him. On the right, a woman with a dark ponytail in a purple top and teal trousers is watering the flower bed with a garden hose that runs from a hose reel by the house. An empty wheelbarrow stands at the front, and two potted plants sit by the fence.
