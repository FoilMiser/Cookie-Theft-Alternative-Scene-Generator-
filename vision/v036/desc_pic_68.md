## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back and a wooden gate on the right. A paved path runs from the gate to the front right. A woman with dark hair in a ponytail, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, stands by a round flower bed next to the house. She is watering the flowers with a teal watering can, and you can see the water pouring out. Near the bottom centre, an orange cat lies curled up on the grass, apparently asleep with its eyes closed. A red bowl of cat food sits on the grass nearby. The strange thing is an orange fish perched in the tree behind the house, sitting on a branch where a bird would normally be. A fish in a tree is impossible in real life.
