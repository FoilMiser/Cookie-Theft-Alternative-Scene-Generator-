## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence along the back, a house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a tree behind the fence and a sun and two clouds in a blue sky. On the left a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is raking leaves on a paved path, and a brown-and-white puppy lies asleep on the path below him. In the middle there is an empty swing set, and beside it an older man with grey hair and glasses stands with his hands on his hips, wearing an upside-down metal bucket on his head like a hat. A girl in a yellow dress holds a garden hose nozzle out in front of her, and a drop of water is coming out of it. A white dog with brown spots is standing on the hose, and the hose is bulged just next to its back paws, so the dog may be blocking the water. At the back a small girl in pink holds a kite string, but her purple kite is caught in the tree. An orange cat is walking along the top of the fence. On the right a man in a red shirt sits on a bench reading a red book, and a blue bird has landed beside him and is pecking at the sandwich he left on the bench; he does not seem to notice. In the front a grey wheelbarrow has a square wheel, which could not roll properly.
