## Free description
A sunny back garden. A cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the right, and a wooden fence with a gate in the left-hand corner runs across the back. A paved path runs from the gate down to the front left. On the path at the left, an old man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat sweeps a pile of autumn leaves with a yellow broom. Two potted plants stand on the grass behind him. In the right-centre, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie holds a hose and sprays water onto a bed of colourful flowers. The hose runs from his hand around the flower bed to a hose reel on the right. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands on the grass at the front centre, and an empty wooden bench is at the front right next to the house. Both people are busy with garden jobs, and nothing seems about to go wrong.
