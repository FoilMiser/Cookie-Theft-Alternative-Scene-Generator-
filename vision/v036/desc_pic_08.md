# pic_08

## Free description
This is a sunny but windy back garden with a house (cream walls, red roof, blue door) on the left, a wooden fence and gate across the back, a paved path on the right, a tree behind the house, and a sun and two clouds in the sky with white wind lines. In the middle an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, stands with both arms raised and her mouth open in surprise. Just above and to the left of her a blue hat is flying through the air, along with a few small leaves, and white wind streaks run past it, so the wind seems to have just blown her hat off and she is reaching or throwing up her hands after it. On the left there is a round flowerbed of colourful flowers, in the background two potted plants stand on the grass, and a teal watering can sits near the path. Nobody else is in the garden.
