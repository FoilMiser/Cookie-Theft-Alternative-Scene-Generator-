## Free description
A sunny back garden. A tall wooden fence runs along the back, with a gate on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the right. Lots of people and animals are busy. On the left a woman in a yellow dress sweeps leaves on the paved path with a broom. Next to her a brown puppy stands on its hind legs holding an open newspaper as if it is reading it. In the middle a bearded man in a red shirt and blue trousers walks along reading an open book, and a cardboard box sits on the grass just ahead of him, so he looks about to trip over it. Near the fence an older woman with glasses is raking leaves. Beside her a boy in a yellow shirt points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of a tree behind the fence. A grey cat walks along the top of the fence. On the right a man in a green shirt sits on a bench reading a red book. A blue bird stands on the bench next to his sandwich and looks about to take it. An orange cat sleeps curled up on the lawn at the front. The wheelbarrow in the middle looks odd: its wheel seems to be a dark square instead of a circle.
