## Free description
A sunny town street with a row of four coloured shop buildings with striped awnings, a street lamp, and a paved pavement in front. On the left a tree has a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in its branches, and a small girl in a pink dress stands below it pointing up at the ball, as if it is stuck and she wants it back. In the middle a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans sits on a wooden bench eating a pink ice-cream cone with his eyes closed, while a small white dog with brown spots sits up on its hind legs in front of him, begging, apparently for the ice cream. Behind him a man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers waters a box planter of flowers with a teal watering can. On the right a woman in a yellow dress stands by a fruit stall, also eating a pink ice-cream cone. In the right foreground an elderly man in a grey flat cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is walking while reading an open book, and a cardboard box sits on the ground right in front of him, so he looks about to bump into or trip over it. A bicycle is parked in a bike rack, and there is a cafe table with two chairs in the bottom-left corner.
