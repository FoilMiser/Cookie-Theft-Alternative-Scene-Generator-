# Picture 46

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills, trees, a winding sandy path on the left, a tall black street lamp and a blue pond on the right. A man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers stands on the grass near the path holding a brown paper bag and sprinkling seeds or crumbs onto the ground. Two small birds, one blue and one red, are at his feet pecking at the seeds as they fall. In the front centre, a white goat with curved horns and a red collar is grazing on a tuft of grass; it is tied by a rope to a short stake in the ground. A white duck swims on the pond. In the foreground right there is a flower bed with colourful flowers and a green rubbish bin in the corner. The scene is calm: a man feeding birds and a tethered goat eating grass.
