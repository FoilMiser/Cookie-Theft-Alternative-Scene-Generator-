# Picture 76

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with no people or ordinary animals in it. A cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the left, and a tall wooden fence runs across the back of the garden with a wooden gate at the far right. A pale paved path leads from the front right corner up to the gate. In the garden there is a flower bed of mixed colourful flowers, a wooden bench at the front left, two potted plants, some round bushes, a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can. A small red-roofed birdhouse sits on top of a tall pole in front of the fence. The strangest thing is that an orange fish is sitting up in the tree behind the house, resting on a branch as if it were a bird. A fish in a tree is impossible, and nothing else in the picture explains how it got there. Otherwise the garden looks calm and tidy, and nothing seems about to happen.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The branch it sits on curves up from the tree trunk in an odd way, as if it was drawn just to hold the fish.
- The birdhouse pole seems to stand on the grass in front of the fence; it is not clear if it is fixed to the fence.
- Nothing else looks wrong to me; there are no people.
