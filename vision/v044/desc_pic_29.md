## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence along the back, a wooden gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof, window and blue door on the right. A grey-and-white cat is walking along the top of the fence, heading right, just past a little red-roofed birdhouse on a tall pole. In the middle there is a washing line strung between two wooden posts, with a yellow towel with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. In the front, a garden sprinkler attached to a hose (coming in from the right) is shooting three arcs of water to the left. One of the arcs is landing right on a cardboard box sitting on the lawn, which is standing in a puddle and dripping, so the box is getting soaked. To the left of that, a white dog with brown patches is crouched in a playful "play bow" next to a tennis ball, wagging its tail. There are two potted plants near the fence, a flower bed in front of the house, a bush, a tree behind the house, two clouds and a sun. No people are in the picture.
