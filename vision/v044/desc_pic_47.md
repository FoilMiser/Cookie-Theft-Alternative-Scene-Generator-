# Picture 47

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden beside a cream house with a red roof and blue door, with a wooden fence along the back, a gate on the right and a paved path leading to it. On the right side of the fence, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; a few boards next to him are already painted bright blue while the rest of the fence is still plain brown wood. An open paint can with blue paint sits on the grass behind him. In the foreground, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and grey skirt is raking a small pile of brown leaves. A teal watering can stands on the grass near the path at the bottom right. A blue bird is flying across the sky, and there is a flower bed with colourful flowers in front of the house. It is a quiet scene of two people doing garden chores.
