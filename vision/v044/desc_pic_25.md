## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house on the left (red roof, blue door, one window), a tall wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate on the right, and a paved path running from the gate down the right-hand side. In the middle, an older man in a grey flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers holds a garden hose nozzle up in front of his face and seems to be peering at it. Only a single drop of water is coming out of the nozzle. The hose runs from a hose reel by the house corner, along the ground, to him. A tan dog with a red collar is standing on the hose, and just behind the dog's paws the hose looks swollen or bulged, as if the water is blocked there. So the dog seems to be stopping the water. It looks like water could suddenly spray into the man's face once the dog steps off. On the right, near the fence, a younger man in a red shirt and blue trousers is sweeping a small pile of dead leaves with a yellow broom. There is a flower bed in front of the house, a small bush, a tree behind the house, two clouds and a sun.
