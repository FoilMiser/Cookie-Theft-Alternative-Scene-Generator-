## Free description
It's a sunny day in a back garden with a house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a wooden fence and gate across the back, and a path at the right. A boy in a red cap and a blue hoodie stands at the fence painting it blue with a roller on a long handle. A few boards are already blue, and a paint tin sits on the grass behind him. In the foreground a man in a red T-shirt and blue trousers holds the nozzle of a green garden hose up to his face and peers into it, as only a drop of water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel at the left across the grass, and a white dog with brown spots is standing right on top of it, squashing it and blocking the water. It looks as if, when the dog steps off, the water will spray straight into the man's face. Oddly, a cardboard box hangs in the sky at the top right near the sun with nothing holding it up. A metal swing set with an empty red swing stands on the right.
