# pic_17

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate in the fence on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. On the paved path at the front left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is pushing a small pile of dirt and pebbles along the ground. He is not using a broom. He is using a huge silver fork that looks like an oversized dinner fork, and a little puff of dust rises from the pile. A red dustpan lies on the ground just in front of the pile, so he seems to be sweeping the dirt into it. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is watering a round flower bed of colourful flowers with a grey watering can, and you can see the water pouring out. A small blue bird sits on top of the fence in the middle. A metal swing set with an empty red seat stands on the lawn on the left, and an empty grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the front centre. The odd thing is that the boy is cleaning the path with a giant table fork instead of a broom.
