# pic_27

## Free description
A sunny playground with a railing fence at the back, a tree on the left, a swing set in the middle with one empty swing, a slide on the right, a green bin by the fence, a bush and a grey rock. On the left, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers is sitting on the low end of a red seesaw, reading an open book. Because he is sitting on it, the other end is stuck high up in the air, and a small girl in a pink dress is perched up on that high end, legs dangling. She has her mouth open and her hand out towards him, as if she is calling to him or complaining, but he is looking at his book and not at her. So the girl seems stuck in the air while the man ignores her. On the right, in a sandpit, a child in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts is kneeling and digging with a small red spade next to a sandcastle that has a little red flag on top. The swing and slide are not being used.
