# Picture 79

## Free description
This is a sunny playground with a grey metal railing fence along the back, a tree on the left, a bush on the right and a green bin by the fence. On the left there is a red seesaw. A small girl in a pink dress sits on the high (left) end and an old man with grey hair, a cap and a brown waistcoat sits on the low (right) end. The old man is not playing: he is reading an open book while sitting on the seesaw, and because he is heavier he keeps the girl stuck up in the air. The girl has her mouth open and one hand raised, as if she is calling out or complaining to him. On the right, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is sliding down a big red-and-orange slide with arms raised, looking happy. In the front right there is a sandpit with a red bucket and a blue spade, and a grey rock beside it.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl could be calling out to the old man to make him let her down or play properly, or she could just be chatting or cheering. Her face looks surprised or upset rather than happy.
- The old man reading on a seesaw is odd behaviour, but it reads as the joke of the picture rather than a drawing mistake.
- The child on the slide could be a boy or a girl.
- The old man's legs are drawn a bit awkwardly. His lower legs come down in front of the seesaw plank, so it is slightly unclear how he is sitting on it. It still reads as sitting with his feet on the ground.
