## Free description
This is a back garden behind a house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back and a wooden gate on the right, but the sky is grey and three dark storm clouds hang overhead. In the centre an older man in a cap and brown vest is holding the end of a garden hose up close to his face and peering into the nozzle, where only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose runs back along the ground to a hose reel by the house, and a white-and-brown spotted dog is standing right on top of the hose, which seems to be why the water is not flowing; it looks as though the water will burst out into the man's face as soon as the dog steps off. Behind him on the right, a man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers is in a sitting position drinking from a red cup with a straw - but there is no chair or seat under him, so he appears to be sitting on thin air. On the far right a woman in a purple top stands on the paved path near the gate, pointing up at the dark clouds as if warning that rain is coming. There is a flower bed and a bush in front of the house. The man watering the garden just before a storm also seems a bit pointless.
