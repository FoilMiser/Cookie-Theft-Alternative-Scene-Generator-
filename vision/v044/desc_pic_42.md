# Picture 42

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden beside a cream house with a red roof and blue door, with a wooden fence and gate at the back and a paved path on the right. In the middle, an old man in a grey cap and brown vest holds a green garden hose and is peering straight into the nozzle, which shows only a single drop of water. The hose runs from a hose reel at the front left along the grass, and a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is standing right on top of the hose, looking pleased with itself. Just behind the dog (between the dog and the reel) the hose looks swollen or bulging, as if water is building up behind the spot the dog is blocking. It looks as if, as soon as the dog steps off, water will spray into the old man's face. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sweeps a small pile of dry leaves on the path with a yellow broom. There is an empty swing set with a red seat in the right-centre and an empty grey wheelbarrow on the grass in front. A flower bed with colourful flowers sits in front of the house.

- Front centre-left: white dog with brown spots, blue collar, standing on the hose, eyes closed/smiling, tail up.
- Right, on the path: woman with dark hair in a ponytail, purple top, teal trousers, sweeping leaves with a broom.
- Hose reel (teal) at front left, with the hose running along the ground under the dog to the old man.
- Hose nozzle in the man's hand with one blue water drop coming out — the water seems blocked.
- Bulge/swelling in the hose just to the left of the dog — water pressure building up.
- Swing set (grey metal frame) with a single red swing seat, nobody on it.
- Grey wheelbarrow, empty, on the grass at front centre.
- Pile of brown leaves and a yellow broom on the path.
- Flower bed with red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers in front of the house.
- House with red roof, window, blue door; tree and bush beside it; wooden fence with gate; paved path; sun and two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The swollen section of hose next to the dog could be a bulge of trapped water (the gag reading) or a flat puddle/leak on the grass.
- The dog may be standing on the hose on purpose (playful) or just happen to be there.
- The old man could be checking why no water is coming out, or he might be about to drink from the hose.
- The wheelbarrow and swing set are unused props; nothing seems wrong with them.
- Nothing appears to be floating or passing through anyone.
