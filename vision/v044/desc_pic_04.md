# pic_04

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence along the back, a gate on the left and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the left path a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping dirt into a red dustpan. Strangely, she is using a giant silver dinner fork instead of a broom. Behind her a little girl in a pink dress swings happily on a swing set, with motion lines showing she is moving. A washing line between two posts holds a yellow striped towel and a pair of blue jeans. In the middle a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers holds up the nozzle of a garden hose close to his face and peers into it; only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose runs across the lawn from a hose reel by the house, and a brown dog with a red collar is standing right on the hose. It looks as if the dog is blocking the water, and when it steps off, the water may spray into the man's face. A flower bed and a bush are near the house.
