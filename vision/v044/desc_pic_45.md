# Picture 45

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny back garden beside a cream house with a red roof and blue door, with a wooden fence, a birdhouse on a pole and a gate leading to a paved path on the right. A blue-and-white beach ball is stuck in the branches of the tree behind the house, and a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands near the fence pointing up at it. On top of the fence, a grey cat is creeping along towards a red bird perched further along the fence, as if stalking it. In front of the house, an elderly woman with glasses sits on a bench reading a red book; a sandwich lies beside her on the bench, and a small blue bird is standing right next to it, apparently about to peck at or steal the sandwich while she isn't looking. In the middle, a woman in a purple top walks along while reading an open book, heading straight towards a blue bucket on the grass that she may trip over. Behind her, an old man in a grey cap carries a cardboard box. On the right, a brown dog with a red collar crouches playfully by a tennis ball, wagging its tail. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie rakes leaves in the front centre, and a little girl in a pink dress jumps in the air with her arms up on the path, looking delighted. A hose lies unused on the ground by a hose reel and an empty grey wheelbarrow stands at the front.
