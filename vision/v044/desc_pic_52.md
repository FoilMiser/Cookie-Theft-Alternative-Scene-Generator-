# pic_52

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence and gate on the left, a paved path leading from the gate, a tree behind the fence, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the left a small girl in a pink dress holds a yellow and a blue balloon by their strings in one hand, while a red balloon floats away above her; she is reaching up with her other hand toward its dangling string, as if she has just let it go and is trying to catch it. In the middle a woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds her arms out in front of her, ready to catch a red ball. The ball is in the air just in front of her hands, with a dashed line showing it was thrown by a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts who is running toward the right with his throwing arm out. To the right, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is watering the flower bed by pouring water out of a grey pan-like object with a handle, which is an unusual thing to water with. An empty wheelbarrow stands in the front centre and a wooden bench stands in front of the house.
