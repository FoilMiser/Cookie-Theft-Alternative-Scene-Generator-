# Picture 82

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny park with many people and animals doing different things at once. On the left an old man in a cap and brown waistcoat is watering a flower bed by pouring water out of a frying pan, which is an odd tool for watering. Behind him a white duck swims across the pond towards little pieces of bread floating on the water, and a girl in a pink dress at the edge of the pond seems to be throwing bread to it. In the middle front a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and rubs his eye as if crying, while a white dog with brown patches and a blue collar licks his dropped pink ice cream off the ground. In the background an elderly woman with glasses sweeps a pile of leaves with a broom, next to a white goat with a red collar that is tied to a stake and eating grass. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie pulls on a string whose purple kite is stuck in the top of a tree. At the bottom right a brown dog with a red neckerchief sits up on its back legs reading a newspaper, which is impossible. A blue bird flies across the sky, and there is an empty bench, a lamp post, a path and a green bin.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The frying pan used as a watering can is odd. It could be a saucepan or a ladle, but it looks like a frying pan with a long handle. Water pouring out of a shallow pan in a steady stream is unlikely.
- The dog reading a newspaper is impossible, and it looks like a deliberate oddity.
- I can't tell exactly what the girl is holding. It could be a bag of bread, a slice of bread or a small box. Feeding the duck is my best guess because of the floating bread.
- The kite string runs from the boy's hand up into the tree, so the kite seems stuck rather than flying. It could also be read as him tugging it free.
- A goat tied up in a public park is unusual but not impossible.
- The scene is crowded, with several separate little stories going on at once, and none of the people interact with each other.
