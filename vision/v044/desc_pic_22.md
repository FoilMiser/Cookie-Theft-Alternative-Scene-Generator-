# pic_22

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. In the middle, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds the nozzle of a garden hose up close to her face and peers into it. Only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel by the house, across the lawn, to her hands. A brown dog with a red collar is standing on the hose partway along, and just on the reel side of the dog's paw the hose bulges, as if water is being blocked. It looks like the dog has cut off the water, and when it steps off, the woman will get sprayed in the face. On the path at the front left, an older man in a grey cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is sweeping a small pile of dead leaves with a yellow broom. A metal swing set with an empty red seat stands on the left, and a tall pole with a red-roofed birdhouse stands by the gate. A flower bed of colourful flowers sits in front of the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- I read the dog standing on the hose and the bulge as the water being blocked. A viewer might not notice the dog's paw is on the hose, though, and could read the woman as simply checking the nozzle or drinking from it.
- The bulge in the hose is a cartoon convention (water pressure building up). Literally, it is just a thicker section of hose.
- The woman's hose is drawn partly in front of her leg and runs down to the ground. It does not look like it passes through her, but it is close.
