# pic_65

## Free description
It's a sunny day in a back garden, with a house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a wooden fence and gate across the back, and a path at the right. An older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, waters the flower bed with a grey watering can. Strangely, the stream of water arcs up into the air from the spout instead of pouring down onto the flowers, with drops flying above it. At the back an older man in a cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat paints the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle. Part of the fence is already blue. A paint tin and a paint tray of blue paint sit on the grass at his feet. A trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the grass to a white dog with brown spots on the path. The dog's paws are blue, so it has clearly stepped in the paint and is walking off happily, spreading paint as it goes. At the far left a brown dog sits by the house door holding its lead in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands on the grass at the front.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water stream is the main oddity. It rises from the spout up and to the left like a fountain, with drops at the top, and doesn't fall onto the flowers. This could be read as the watering can spraying upward, which is impossible for a normal can. Its lower end also sits right among the flowers, so it could be read as a jet of water spurting up out of the flower bed.
- The man may not have noticed the dog walking paint across the garden, because he is facing the fence.
- The brown dog with the lead is probably asking for a walk, but it could just be carrying the lead about.
- Nothing else looks physically impossible.
