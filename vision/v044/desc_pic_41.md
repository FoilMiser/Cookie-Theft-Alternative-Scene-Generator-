# Picture 41

## Free description
This is a back garden beside a cream house with a red roof, a blue front door and a window, under a grey, overcast sky. Three big dark storm clouds hang over the garden, so it looks as if it is about to rain. A wooden fence runs across the back with a gate on the right, and a pale stone path leads up to the gate. On the left, in front of the house, an old man in a grey cap sits on a wooden bench reading a red book, apparently unaware of the weather. In the middle, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans kneels at a flower bed full of colourful flowers, reaching into the flowers; a small pot with a pink flower sits at the front edge of the bed, as if he is about to plant it. On the right, a man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers stands looking up with wide eyes and points up at the dark clouds, as though warning the others that rain is coming. A birdhouse on a tall pole stands in front of the fence. The overall story seems to be: rain is about to start while people are busy gardening and reading outside.
