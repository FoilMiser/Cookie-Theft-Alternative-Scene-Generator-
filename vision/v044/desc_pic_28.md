## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills and hedges in the background, a winding sandy path on the left, a tall tree on the left and a smaller tree on the right, several bushes, a tall black lamp post in the middle, and an oval blue pond on the right with bulrushes at each end. A woman in a yellow dress with long dark hair is standing on the grass at the near left side of the pond, fishing. She holds a long fishing rod in both hands. The rod reaches out over the pond, and the line drops down to a red-and-white float sitting in the water with a small ripple ring around it. She is looking out towards the float. Nothing dramatic seems to be happening; she seems to be waiting for a bite. There is a grey rock near the bottom of the picture and a green rubbish bin in the bottom-right corner. There are no other people or animals.
