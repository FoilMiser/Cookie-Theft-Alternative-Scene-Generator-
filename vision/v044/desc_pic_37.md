## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a closed gate at the far left, a paved path down the left side, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller, toward the right end of the fence. A section of about three panels to her right is already blue. Her paint bucket and paint tray sit on the grass just to her left. A white dog with brown patches and a blue collar has blue paws. A trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray leftward to the dog, which is walking left across the lawn with its head turned toward the viewer. So the dog has evidently stepped in the paint tray. In the front left, an older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, is sweeping a small pile of brown leaves on the path with a broom. The dog is heading in her direction. A teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow sit on the grass in the front, and a flower bed is on the right by the house.
