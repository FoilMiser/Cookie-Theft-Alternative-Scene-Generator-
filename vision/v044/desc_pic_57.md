# pic_57

## Free description
This is a busy sunny back garden behind a house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, with a tall wooden fence across the back and a wooden gate at the right end of the fence. A woman in a purple top is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller; the section of fence nearest the house is already blue, and a paint tin and a paint tray with blue paint sit on the grass beside her. A white-and-brown spotted dog is walking away from the paint tray to the right, and a trail of blue paw prints runs across the grass from the tray to the dog, whose feet look blue - it seems to have stepped in the paint. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands in the middle flying a yellow kite, whose string runs up past the fence to the kite high in the sky. An orange cat is walking along the top of the fence, close to where the kite string passes. On the right, an older man in a cap and brown vest stands on a paved path holding a huge silver fork that looks like an oversized dinner fork, using it to scrape or sweep a pile of dirt toward a red dustpan, with a puff of dust rising. In the front left a garden sprinkler attached to a hose is spraying arcs of water onto a cardboard box sitting in a puddle, so the box is getting soaked. There is also an empty wheelbarrow at the front, two potted plants by the fence, a flower bed by the house, and a tree behind the house.
