## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a long wooden fence across the back, a gate on the right and a paved path running down the right side. In front of the house a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers sits on a bench reading a red book. Next to him is a hose reel with a hose lying on the grass, ending in a yellow nozzle. A brown puppy with a red collar is curled up asleep in the middle of the lawn. Around the garden are a grey wheelbarrow, a teal watering can, two potted plants and a round flower bed. The strange thing is the tree behind the house: an orange goldfish is sitting in its branches, which is impossible. Apart from that the scene is calm and nobody is doing much.
