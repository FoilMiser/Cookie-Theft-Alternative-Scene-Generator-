# Picture 44

## Free description
This is a sunny park with hills, trees, a tall black street lamp, a winding path on the right and a pond on the left. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and grey skirt stands in front of the pond fishing with a long rod; her line drops into the water where a red-and-white float (bobber) is bobbing. A white duck is swimming in the pond on the far left. In the middle distance, a child with dark skin and braided/twisted hair, in a yellow shirt and orange shorts, holds a blue and a yellow balloon in one hand. A purple balloon floats higher up, and its string ends just above the child's other, raised hand, so the child seems either to be holding it by the very tip of the string or to have just let go and be reaching for it as it drifts away. A blue bird sits on a branch of the big tree on the right. In the foreground there is a grey wheelbarrow whose wheel looks oddly square, a flower bed with colourful flowers, and a green rubbish bin in the bottom-left corner.

- Pond, far left: white duck swimming.
- Centre-right, middle distance: child with dark skin and braided hair, yellow T-shirt, orange shorts, holding blue and yellow balloons in the right hand and raising the left hand towards a purple balloon above, looking up at it.

- Blue and yellow balloons: held together by their strings in the child's lowered hand.
- Right: blue bird perched on a branch in the large tree.
- Front centre: grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles pointing left; its wheel is drawn as a square black frame.
- Front left: flower bed with red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers.
- Bottom-left corner: green rubbish bin.
- Centre: tall black street lamp.
- Right: winding sandy path; trees, bushes, cattail reeds at the pond, sun top right, two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Purple balloon: two readings — (a) the child is holding the string by its very end, or (b) the child has just let go and the balloon is floating away while the child reaches up for it. The string's end has a small curl that sits just above the hand rather than clearly in it.
- The wheelbarrow wheel looks like a square/box frame with a square hub rather than a round wheel; this looks unintentionally strange (or it is a wheel seen from an odd angle).
- The woman's body overlaps the front of the pond, but her feet are on the grass, so she reads as standing at the edge.
- The fishing line is very thin and slightly curved; it clearly reaches the float.
