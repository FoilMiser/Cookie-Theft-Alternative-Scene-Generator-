# Picture 81

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence with a gate on the right, and a pale paved path leading to the gate. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and round glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, stands in the middle of the lawn reaching up with one arm. A red hat (it looks like a cap with a brim) is flying through the air above and to the left of her, and a couple of small leaves are flying with it. White swirly wind lines are drawn next to the hat and in the sky. So a gust of wind has just blown her hat off, and she is reaching for it with her mouth open in surprise. The hat looks like it will keep blowing away out of her reach. The rest of the garden has a flower bed, a coiled hose on a reel, two potted plants, a bush, a wheelbarrow and a watering can.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The hat looks more like a red cap than a typical old lady's sun hat, so it is a little unclear what kind of hat it is. It could even be read as a red bowl or lid, but the wind lines make "hat blown off" the obvious reading.
- The wind lines trail off to the right of the hat, which suggests the hat is moving left, away from her hand. That direction is a bit hard to read.
- Only the hat and a few leaves are blowing. The flowers, tree and plants are perfectly still, which is slightly inconsistent with a gust strong enough to take her hat.
- There is no bench in this version of the garden, but nothing looks impossible.
