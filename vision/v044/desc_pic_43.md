# Picture 43

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green rolling hills in the background, a sun in the top left and a couple of white clouds. A winding sandy path runs from the bottom left into the distance, with a big tree and an empty wooden park bench beside it. A tall black street lamp stands in the middle of the grass. On the right there is a blue pond with reeds (cattails) at both ends. A man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers stands at the front edge of the pond holding a brown paper bag and tossing pieces of bread or crumbs onto the water. A white duck in the water is swimming quickly towards him (it has little motion lines behind it) and is pecking at one of the crumbs. Several pale crumbs are floating on the water around the duck. In the foreground there is a grey rock, a flower bed with colourful flowers on the right, and a green rubbish bin in the bottom-right corner. It is a calm, simple scene of a man feeding a duck.
