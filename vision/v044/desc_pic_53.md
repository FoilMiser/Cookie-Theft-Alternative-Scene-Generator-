# pic_53

## Free description
A sunny park or playground behind a dark metal railing fence, with green bushes beyond it. On the left a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes stands looking up and holding a string; the string runs up to a purple kite that is caught in the leaves of the tree on the left, so his kite is stuck in the tree. Behind him there is an empty wooden bench and, further back in the middle, a green rubbish bin. On the right a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is sliding down a tall red-framed slide with an orange chute, arms raised happily. In the front right a girl in a pink dress kneels in a wooden sandpit, building a sandcastle with a little red flag on top while holding a small red spade. A grey rock sits just to the right of the sandpit, and there is a bush at the far right.
