# v0.53 blind screen: what it found (the work list for v0.54)

The v0.53 screen read the set's 21 pictures and 78 probes with the prompts of v0.52 (`screen_prompts.md`). There were thirteen readers, eight of eight pictures and five of seven, and four scorers of 24 or 25. The legibility gate passed all 64 templates, where it had passed 63 in v0.52, 64 in v0.51 and 62 in v0.50. No item of the 1,102 was misread, and nothing was redrawn, swapped or read again after the screen (below). Keys, descriptions and scores are in `v053/`.

v0.53 worked the list of `flaws_v052.md`. The cast is v0.52's (builder 0.52.0); the placement rules are revision 49:
- **the wheelbarrow's square wheel is larger** (`draw.WHEEL53`): 34 units from hub to side for 25, the barrow raised with it. Chosen in whole pictures: v0.52's own barrows built again with it and read beside v0.52's by nine readers (`isolation_v053.md`);
- **a child jumping for joy is never planned into a picture with a game of catch** (`templates.JUMP53`), after a gap of 150 units between them read no better in two design tests (`design_tests_v053.md`);
- benched in whole scenes and not taken: the woman in purple with a fuller figure and a cardigan, or a longer skirt (O1). Readers called her a woman in every picture of every arm, v0.52's among them, so the bench had nothing to choose;
- looked at and left, each for a reason found in the code or by measuring: the floating box (R3), the thief bird (R4), the heavy seesaw's man (O2), the pond's edge (O3).

**Nineteen of the set's 21 pictures are v0.52's to the pixel.** The twelve cells of tiers 1 to 3, the tier-4 park and street and the five isomorphs placed on v0.52's seeds and came back identical. The tier-4 garden is new: v0.52's held a jump beside a game of catch, and the cell was built from v0.46's set on v0.46's seed. The tier-4 playground is new: v0.52's base did not place under revision 49, and a later base did. Of the 78 probes five are v0.52's to the pixel; the probe pass composes its groups from what the set already shows, and two changed cells changed most of them.
