# v0.38 isolation bench

Each visual was forced into a picture with little else and cut out, then read blind by fresh readers (the method of `isolation_v023.md`). Two rounds, every cut-out to its own reader, since all are takes of one template: round 1, nine readers and 9 cut-outs, the washing hung out drawn three ways (`isolation_v038/round1`); round 2, six readers and 6 cut-outs, two more ways (`isolation_v038/round2`). `map.json` in each says which cut-out is which case. The drawings are one switch, `templates.BASKET38`, drawn in SVG by the template (`HangLaundry.basket_at`, `draw.laundry_basket`), so the cast is v0.37's (builder 0.38.0). Each case's three cut-outs are three garden seeds (38111 to 38113) with `hang_laundry` forced alone; the three seeds give a man in green facing right, and a man in red facing left and facing right.

## The washing hung out (`BASKET38`)

`hang_laundry`, v0.37's one failure: "I can't tell whether the boy is hanging the T-shirt up or taking it down" (v0.37, picture 12, on a picture identical to v0.36's), as in v0.30 and v0.31. The template has one scored item a picture, so one half mark takes it under the gate. Readers of earlier screens had said what was missing: "there is no laundry basket to show which", "There is no washing basket visible, so I can't tell where the washing came from" (`v023` to `v035`). The garden has had a laundry basket since v0.4, a filler by the line that no picture of the v0.37 set or probes drew; the readers who saw it named it at once ("Laundry basket with blue, white and pink clothes"). The basket here is that drawing, at 0.9 of the hanger's scale, set on the grass beside her feet: measured from her drawing's feet, on the side she faces, under the shirt, or else behind her heels, between the posts, on the lawn, clear of every scored thing and of others' feet, and kept as a thing on the ground.

**Round 1.** The basket alone did little: a full basket says washing, not which way it is going. The drops said "wet", and wet washing goes up; all three readers of W said so. But the bundles were balls or cushions to five of six readers of a basket, so round 2 drew them as cloth, and tried the drops without a basket, to see what the basket adds for the room it takes.

**Chosen: C.** With no basket and no drops, three readers of three were unsure which way the shirt went, and nothing in the picture said; with the drops, all nine readers of W, C and D leaned to hanging out, two of them with no doubt left. The drops carry the reading, and the basket gives it a second cue: the one reader of round 2 who raised no doubt read the basket as "clothes still to be hung", and the drops alone were once taken for rain or beads. The cloth took the bundles from balls or cushions (three of three in W) to one "hat or a ball", and the sleeve was seen by all three. The basket costs nothing in placement: on the same twelve seeds each (`tpdb_work/bcost38.py`), the washing alone, with a planter, and with a digger and a dog placed 12 of 12 with it and without it (and with a watering can 0 of 12 either way, a limit older than the basket). The basket's slats keep a crate as a second reading, as they did for the filler; with clothes in it, every reader called it a laundry basket first.

After the design tests the rest of the washing drips too, the jeans on the line and the sleeve over the basket's rim: with the shirt alone wet, the basket's clothes read dry and "about to get wet" under it (`design_tests_v038.md`).

Left alone: the hanger's arms crossing before his face, his long legs and his twisted stance, which every reader of all five drawings mentioned, as the readers of v0.29's bench did, and which cost no marks in the screens.
