# iso_07

## Free description
A sunny day in a park with green hills, trees, a winding sand-coloured path, a lamp post, a wooden bench and an oval pond. In the middle, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands near the lamp post holding a brown paper bag and reaching his other hand out toward the pond, apparently tossing bread or seeds to a white duck. The duck is swimming on the pond toward a few small pale crumbs floating on the water. In the front centre, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket next to a wicker basket holding a baguette and a green bottle. To the right, an older man with grey hair, a moustache and a blue cap kneels by a flower bed, digging with a trowel next to many colourful flowers and a pot with a pink tulip-like flower. On the far left, a white goat with a red collar grazes on a tuft of grass; it is tied by a rope to a small stake in the ground. Nothing looks clearly impossible, though the boy is standing quite far from the pond while the crumbs land in the water.
