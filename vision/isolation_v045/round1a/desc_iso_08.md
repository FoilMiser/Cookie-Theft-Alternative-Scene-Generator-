An older grey-haired woman with glasses sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket by the pond, with a wicker basket holding a baguette and a bottle, and a plate with a watermelon slice; she looks to be laughing or smiling with her eyes shut. In front of her a girl in a pink dress runs across the grass holding a pink ice-cream cone. The blue balloon's string dangles well above his hand, so it seems he has just let go of it and can't reach it. A white duck swims on the pond, there is an empty wooden bench beside the path, a tall black lamp post in the middle, a flower bed with colourful flowers at front left, and a green rubbish bin in the bottom-left corner. Nothing looks clearly impossible, apart from the escaping balloon being the main "event".
