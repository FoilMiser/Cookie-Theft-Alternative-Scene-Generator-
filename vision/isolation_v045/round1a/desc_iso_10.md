## Free description
A sunny back garden next to a cream house with a red roof, a blue front door and a window on the left. An older man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown vest is kneeling by a flower bed, digging with a small trowel; a pot with a pink flower stands at the edge of the bed, apparently waiting to be planted. Behind him, a grey-haired woman with glasses in a teal top and grey skirt is painting the wooden fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; a section of the fence is already blue and a paint tin sits on the grass behind her. On the right, a younger man in a green T-shirt and tan trousers walks towards the left carrying a cardboard box. In the front centre there is an empty grey wheelbarrow, and a paved path runs from the bottom right to a wooden gate in the fence. A birdhouse sits on top of a tall pole near the gate. Nothing looks dramatically wrong; the man carrying the box seems to be walking past the wheelbarrow rather than using it.
