## Free description
It is a sunny day in a park with green hills, a pond on the left, a tall black lamp post with a green rubbish bin in the centre, and a sand-coloured path curving up on the right. A white duck is swimming on the pond. In the front left-centre a child with dark curly hair, a yellow top and orange shorts is sitting on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket next to a wicker basket holding a green bottle and a long loaf of bread. In the middle a white goat with small horns and a red collar is bending down to eat a tuft of grass; its collar is tied by a rope to a short stake in the ground. On the right, a man in a red shirt and blue trousers is standing on the path holding a camera up to his eye, taking a photo pointed to the left, towards the goat and the picnic. A grey rock sits in the front near the bottom. Nothing seems about to go wrong; the goat is tethered, though it is quite close to the picnic.
