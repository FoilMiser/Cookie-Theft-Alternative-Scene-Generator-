# iso_05

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a swing set, a clothesline and a flower bed. An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses walks up a paved path on the left carrying a cardboard box. In the middle, an elderly man in a cap and brown waistcoat holds the nozzle of a green garden hose up near his face and is looking into it; only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose runs back across the lawn to a hose reel by the house, and a brown dog with a red collar is standing right on the hose, which looks squashed under its feet. So the dog seems to be blocking the water, and the man may get sprayed in the face when the dog steps off. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sits on a bench reading a red book, and a small blue bird on the bench beside him is pecking at his sandwich without him noticing. A yellow striped towel and a pair of jeans hang on the clothesline, and an empty wheelbarrow sits at the front. The swing set is empty.
