A small girl in a pink dress stands near the path holding the strings of two balloons, a red one and a purple one, in one hand. A third balloon, a blue one, is floating up and away above her with its string dangling free. She is reaching up toward it with her other arm, so it looks like she has just let it go and can't reach it. In the front, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers walks along holding up a pink ice-cream cone. To his right, a woman in a yellow dress stands in front of the pond holding a camera up to her eye. The camera points left, toward the man and the girl, so she seems to be taking a photo of them. There is a green rubbish bin in the bottom-right corner. Nothing looks impossible; the main event is the escaping blue balloon.
