## Free description
A sunny playground with green grass, a long dark railing fence across the back, a red-framed slide on the left, a green rubbish bin, a bush, a grey rock and a tree on the right. In the centre-left background, a small boy in a yellow top and orange shorts has just thrown a red-and-white ball; a dotted line shows it arcing through the air toward a woman. The woman, in a purple top and teal trousers, stands with her hands up in front of her ready to catch the ball. In the front centre, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers jumps in the air with both arms raised and a big grin, his feet off the ground above his shadow. On the right, a small girl in a pink dress holds a reel/spool with a string that runs up to a purple kite, which is caught in the branches of the tree. It looks like the kite is stuck in the tree rather than flying. The ball seems about to reach the woman's hands.
