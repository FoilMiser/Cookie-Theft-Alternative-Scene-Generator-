A small girl in a pink dress stands near the path holding balloons. In one hand she holds the strings of a yellow and a red balloon. Her other arm is stretched up toward a purple balloon that is higher up. To his right, a woman in a yellow dress holds a camera up to her eye and points it to the left, toward the man and the scene. There is a green rubbish bin in the bottom right corner. Nothing looks impossible apart from the purple balloon's string being loose.
