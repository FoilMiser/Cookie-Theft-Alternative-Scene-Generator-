## Free description
This is a sunny town street or pedestrian square with a row of colourful two-storey shops along the back, each with a striped awning. In the middle, an older man with a grey moustache, a blue cap, a white shirt and a brown waistcoat walks along eating a pink ice-cream cone. In front of him, a woman with dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers walks to the left carrying a cardboard box in both arms. On the right, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers stands in front of a fruit and vegetable stall and holds a camera to his eye, pointing it to the left toward the other people. Behind the people there is a teal bicycle parked in a metal rack, two planters full of flowers, and a tall black lamp post. In the foreground there is a small round café table with two chairs, a tree in a round planter, potted plants in both bottom corners, a green rubbish bin and a wooden crate. Nothing seems about to go wrong, and nothing looks impossible.
