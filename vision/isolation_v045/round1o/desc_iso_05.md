# iso_05

## Free description
A sunny day in a grassy playground enclosed by a dark railing fence, with bushes behind it. On the left is a slide with a red frame, a dark ladder and a long yellow-and-orange chute. In the middle-back a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts has just thrown a red ball, shown by a dashed arc, towards a woman in a purple top and teal trousers who holds her hands out in front of her ready to catch it. In the front centre a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and black trousers jumps in the air with both arms raised, looking excited; his shadow is on the ground below him. On the right a small girl in a pink dress holds a string attached to a purple kite, but the kite is stuck in the leaves of a tree. There is a green rubbish bin by the fence and a grey rock in the bottom-left corner. The ball looks about to reach the woman, and the girl's kite looks caught and unable to fly.

## Inventory
- Boy in yellow shirt (centre-left, back): dark skin, yellow shirt, orange shorts; running/stepping forward, arm extended after throwing the ball.
- Woman (centre-right, back): long dark hair, purple top, teal trousers; standing with hands together in front, looking at the ball, ready to catch.
- Boy in red cap (centre-front): red cap, blue hoodie, black trousers, white shoes; jumping with both arms up, smiling, feet off the ground (shadow beneath).
- Girl (right, mid-ground): dark hair, pink dress, yellow shoes; holding a kite string up in one hand, looking up toward the tree.
- Red ball (centre, in the air): mid-flight between the boy in yellow and the woman, dashed line showing its path.
- Purple kite (far right): caught in the tree canopy, small bow-tie tail pieces hanging down; its string runs down to the girl.
- Slide (left): red frame, dark ladder, yellow/orange slide; nobody on it.
- Rubbish bin (centre-left, by the fence): green bin.
- Rock (bottom-left): grey stone.
- Fence (across the back): dark metal railing.
- Tree (right), bush (left), sun, two clouds, hedges behind the fence.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The kite: it most plausibly is tangled/stuck in the tree; it could also be read as flying just behind the tree, partly hidden by leaves, though the leaves overlap it.
- The boy in the red cap: could be jumping for joy, or jumping/reaching to catch something, but nothing is coming towards him; his raised hands could also be read as cheering.
- The woman's hands are clasped in front of her chest, which could be read as preparing to catch the ball or as clapping.
- The ball's dashed arc starts at the yellow-shirted boy's hand but the ball is drawn high, near the woman's head height above the fence line, so it could be read as about to go over her.
- The slide's ladder is drawn slightly slanted and separate from the frame; minor drawing oddity. No objects pass through people.
