## Free description
A sunny day in a park with green hills, trees, a winding sand-coloured path, a lamp post, a green litter bin, a wooden bench and a pond. On the left a white goat with a red collar is on a long lead that is tied to a small stake in the ground, and it is bending down to eat a tuft of grass. In the middle a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands near the lamp post holding a brown paper bag and reaching out his other hand toward the pond, as if he is throwing bread. In the pond a white duck swims toward a trail of small bread crumbs floating on the water, leaving ripple lines behind it. In the front, a girl in a yellow top and orange shorts sits smiling on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket with a wicker basket beside it. On the right an elderly grey-haired woman with glasses kneels by a flower bed full of colourful flowers, putting a pink tulip in a clay pot into the ground (or lifting it). Nothing looks clearly impossible; it is a calm, ordinary scene, with the duck about to eat the crumbs.
