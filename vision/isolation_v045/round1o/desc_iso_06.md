## Free description
A sunny back garden next to a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, enclosed by a wooden fence with a gate on the right. An elderly woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal top and grey skirt stands at the fence painting it blue with a paint roller on a stick; a section of the fence is already blue and a paint tin sits on the grass behind her. An older man with a grey moustache, blue cap, brown waistcoat and white shirt kneels by a flower bed on the left, tending the colourful flowers, with a small pot of pink flowers beside him. In the front centre, a younger man in a green shirt and beige trousers walks along carrying a cardboard box. On the right there is a birdhouse on a tall pole, two potted plants, a teal watering can on the grass and a paved path leading to the gate. Nothing looks dangerous or about to go wrong; everyone seems busy with garden jobs.
