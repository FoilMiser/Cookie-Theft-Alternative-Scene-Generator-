# iso_04

## Free description
A sunny day in a park with green hills, trees, a small blue pond and a winding sand-coloured path. An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket with a wicker basket beside her, looking happy. On the pond a white duck is swimming, with bulrushes at both ends of the pond. In the middle-right a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a red and a purple balloon on strings in one hand and stretches his other arm up toward a yellow balloon that is floating away above him, its string dangling loose; he looks upset, as if he has just let it go. Further down the path a girl in a pink dress walks or skips towards the viewer, holding a small pink ice-cream cone near her mouth. There is an empty wooden bench beside the path, a tall black lamp post in the middle, a flower bed with colourful flowers at front left, and a green rubbish bin in the bottom-left corner. The yellow balloon seems about to drift out of reach.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The yellow balloon: most likely it has just escaped from the boy (he reaches for it and looks upset), but it could also be read as him letting it go on purpose; the string ends in a little curl well above his hand.
- The girl's ice cream is very small and could be read as a lollipop or a pink sweet rather than an ice-cream cone.
- The girl's pose could be walking, skipping or running.
- The boy appears to stand on the grass just behind the bench; his feet sit a little above the bench's top rail level, so depth is slightly unclear (he is further back, not standing on the bench).
- Nothing obviously impossible otherwise; the lamp post standing alone in the grass is a little unusual but plausible.
