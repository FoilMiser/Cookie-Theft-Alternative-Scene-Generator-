## Free description
A sunny day in a park with a pond on the right, a lamp post, trees, an empty wooden bench and a winding path on the left. In the front centre, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is kicking an orange ball with his right foot; motion lines under his foot show the kick. On the right, in front of the pond, a child with dark skin in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a kite reel up with both hands. A long string runs from the reel up to an orange diamond-shaped kite with a bow-tied tail flying high in the sky near the clouds. The ball looks like it is about to fly off to the left. No one else is in the park.
