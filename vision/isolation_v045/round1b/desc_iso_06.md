# iso_06

## Free description
A sunny day in a park with a pond, a lamp post, trees, a bench, a winding path and a flower bed. On the right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a red and a purple balloon in one hand while stretching his other arm up toward a blue balloon that has escaped and is floating away above him; he looks worried or upset. In the middle front, an older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket next to a wicker basket holding a baguette and a bottle; a plate with a slice of watermelon sits on the blanket. A girl in a pink dress walks or runs across the front of the scene holding a pink ice-cream cone, just past the picnic blanket. A white duck swims on the pond. The blue balloon seems about to be lost for good, as its string end is already above the boy's reach. The wooden bench is empty.
