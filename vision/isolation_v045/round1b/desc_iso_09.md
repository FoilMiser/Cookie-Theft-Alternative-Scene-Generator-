## Free description
A sunny day in a park with green hills, a winding sand-coloured path, an empty wooden bench, a tall black lamp post and a blue pond. At the left front a white goat with a red collar is eating a tuft of grass; it is tied by a rope to a small stake in the ground. In the middle, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands near the pond holding a brown paper bag and reaching out his hand, as if tossing bread to the water. On the pond a white duck swims towards several small bread pieces floating on the water, leaving a wake behind it. In front of the pond a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket next to a wicker basket holding a baguette and a green bottle. At the right front an older man with grey hair, a moustache, a blue-grey cap and a brown vest kneels and digs with a small trowel in a flower bed full of colourful flowers, with a pot of pink tulips beside him, so he seems to be planting. Nothing looks clearly impossible; the scene is calm and everyday.
