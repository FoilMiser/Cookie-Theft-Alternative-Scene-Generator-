## Free description
It is a sunny day in a park with green hills, trees, a winding sand-coloured path, a tall black lamp post and a blue pond with bulrushes. In the middle-left, a small girl in a pink dress is holding the strings of two balloons, a red one and a purple one, in one hand. A third balloon, a blue one, has come loose. It floats higher up to her left with its string hanging free, and she is reaching up towards it with her other hand. It looks as if the blue balloon is about to float away. In front of the pond, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers is walking along holding a pink ice-cream cone. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress holds a camera up to her eye and points it left, towards the man and the girl. A green rubbish bin stands in the bottom-right corner. Nothing looks clearly impossible, though the escaping balloon is the main thing that seems about to happen.
