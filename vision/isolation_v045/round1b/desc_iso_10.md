## Free description
It is a sunny day in a grassy park with rolling green hills, a few clouds and a bright sun in the top right. On the left there is an oval pond with a white duck swimming on it and cattails at both ends. In the front centre-left a girl in a yellow top and orange shorts sits at the edge of a red-and-white checked picnic blanket, leaning back on her hands, next to a wicker picnic basket with a green bottle and a baguette sticking out. In the centre a white goat with a red collar has its head down eating a tuft of grass; a rope runs from its collar to a small wooden stake in the ground, so it is tied up. On the right a man in a red shirt and blue trousers stands on a winding sandy path holding a camera up to his eye and pointing it to the left, toward the goat and the picnic. A tall black lamp post and a green trash bin stand in the middle background, and a grey rock sits in the foreground. Nothing seems about to happen beyond the man taking a photo; nothing looks clearly impossible.
