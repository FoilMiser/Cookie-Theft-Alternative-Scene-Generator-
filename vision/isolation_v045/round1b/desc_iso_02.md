## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a gate on the left, a swing set, a washing line and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. On the left, a grey-haired older woman with glasses and a teal top is walking along a paved path carrying a cardboard box. In the middle, an older man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat holds a garden hose. The nozzle is pointed up near his face, with a drop or two of water coming out. The hose runs across the lawn from a hose reel near the house. A brown-and-white dog with a red collar stands on the lawn right over the hose, looking left. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. A sandwich sits on the bench beside him, and a small blue bird on the bench is right next to the sandwich, as if about to peck at it or steal it. A yellow striped top and a pair of blue jeans hang on the washing line, there is an empty grey wheelbarrow at the front, and a bed of colourful flowers sits in front of the house.
