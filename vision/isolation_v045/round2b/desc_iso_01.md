# iso_01

## Free description
A sunny back garden next to a cream-coloured house with a red roof and a blue front door on the left. A tall wooden fence runs across the back, with trees and hedges behind it and a wooden gate at the right end. An elderly man in a cap and brown vest kneels on the grass at the left, digging in a flower bed with a small trowel; a pot of pink flowers stands beside him, apparently waiting to be planted. Behind him in the middle, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses is painting the fence with a paint roller on a stick; one section of the fence is already painted bright blue, and a small paint tin sits on the grass behind her. On the right, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers walks across the lawn carrying a cardboard box, coming from the direction of the paved path and the gate. A birdhouse on a tall pole stands near the fence, and an empty grey wheelbarrow sits in the front of the lawn. Nothing looks dangerous; everyone is busy with garden chores. One slightly odd thing is that the man is carrying the box by hand while the wheelbarrow stands empty nearby.
