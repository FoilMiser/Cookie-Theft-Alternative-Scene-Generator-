## Free description
A sunny day in a park with a dark railing fence across the back, a red climbing frame with a yellow/orange slide on the left, a small bush, a grey rock, and a big tree on the right. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands near the fence, left of centre, with both arms held out in front of her at chest height. A red ball hangs in the air just in front of her hands, and a dashed curved line runs from the ball back to a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts who is running in the middle distance with one arm stretched forward, so it looks as if the child has just thrown the ball to the woman and she is about to catch it. In the foreground centre, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and black trousers is jumping in the air with both arms raised high and a happy face; his shadow is on the grass well below his feet. On the right a small girl in a pink dress holds a kite reel up, and the string leads up to a purple kite that is caught in the leaves of the tree. The kite appears stuck in the tree, and the girl looks up at it. Nothing looks impossible, though the boy's raised arms suggest he wants something even though nothing is above him.
