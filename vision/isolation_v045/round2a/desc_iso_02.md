# iso_02

## Free description
A sunny park scene: railing fence across the back, a red climbing frame and yellow slide on the left, a bush, a grey rock, and a tree on the right. Left of centre, near the bottom of the slide, a little girl in a pink dress holds the strings of a purple and a blue balloon in one hand while her other arm reaches up toward a yellow balloon that is floating away above her; its string ends just out of her reach. In the centre a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and covers their face with a hand, apparently crying. On the grass in front of this child is a splat of pink ice cream with sprinkles, and a brown dog with a red collar or bandana has its nose in it, licking it up. On the right, in the foreground, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt holds a camera to her face and is taking a photo pointed toward the left side of the scene. The yellow balloon looks about to be lost into the sky.
