# pic_014

## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, trees, a winding sandy path, a lamp post and a pond. On a bench beside the path, an elderly man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat and white shirt sits reading a red book. Next to him on the bench, a blue bird is pulling at his sandwich, lifting the top of the bread off; the man does not seem to notice. On the right, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands at the edge of the pond holding a paper bag and tossing bits of bread to a white duck in the water. In the front left is a red wheelbarrow whose wheel is drawn as a square, which looks wrong. There is also a green bin, a grey rock, a flower bed with colourful flowers, and bulrushes at the pond's edge.
