## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left, a wooden fence with a gate on the right, a clothesline with blue jeans and a yellow striped towel, a flower bed and a paved path. An elderly woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal top and grey skirt holds a garden hose and is looking into its nozzle, which is held up near her face. The hose runs from a reel by the house across the grass, and a white dog with brown spots is standing on the hose, which looks pinched/bulged under its feet, so the water seems blocked; if the dog steps off, the water may spray into her face. An old man in a cap and brown waistcoat is drinking through a straw from a red cup in a seated pose, but there is no chair or anything under him: he is sitting on thin air. On the right a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts jumps with both arms up on the path, with his shadow beneath him. An empty wooden bench stands at the bottom left.
