# pic_030

## Free description
A sunny town square in front of a row of colourful shops with striped awnings, paved with grey tiles. On the left a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is walking a teal bicycle, but the bicycle's wheels are square, which is impossible to ride. Behind him is a fruit stall with crates of green, yellow, orange and red fruit. In the centre a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie holds a small brown bag and is sprinkling seeds onto the ground, where a red bird is pecking at them. Behind him an older woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a bench reading a red book; beside her a blue bird is perched on the bench grabbing at her sandwich, lifting the top slice of bread. On the right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sweeps a pile of brown leaves with a yellow broom. There is also a tree in a round planter, a lamp post, a green bin and a potted plant in the corner.
