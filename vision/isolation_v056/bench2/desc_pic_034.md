## Free description
A sunny playground in a park, with a tall dark fence across the back, a slide on the left, a sandbox at the front and a tree and bench on the right. In the middle at the back, a small girl in a pink dress holds a blue and a yellow balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other arm toward a red balloon that has floated away high above her; it looks like she has just lost it. Near her, a woman in a yellow dress stands holding a camera up to her eye and taking a photo, facing left toward the girl. In the front centre, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and has his hand to his face, looking upset; his pink ice cream has fallen on the ground in a splat. A brown dog with a red collar is licking up the dropped ice cream. On the right, a grey-haired grandmother with glasses pushes a teal pram with a baby lying inside under a pink blanket. The sandbox has a red bucket and blue spade in it but nobody is playing there, and the slide and bench are empty.
