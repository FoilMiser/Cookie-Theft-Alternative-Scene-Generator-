# pic_025

## Free description
A sunny shopping street with a row of colourful shop buildings with striped awnings, a paved square in front, a lamp post, a tree and a fruit stall. On the left a man in a red shirt and blue trousers stands holding a green bicycle by the handlebars; the bicycle's wheels are square instead of round, which is impossible for riding. In the centre a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie holds a paper bag and scatters seeds onto the ground, where a red bird is pecking them up. Behind him an older woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a bench reading a red book; on the bench beside her is a sandwich, and a blue bird is leaning over it with its beak at the sandwich, apparently stealing it while she reads. On the right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping a pile of brown leaves with a broom. There is a green bin, a potted plant in the bottom right corner and a fruit stall with crates of fruit on the left.
