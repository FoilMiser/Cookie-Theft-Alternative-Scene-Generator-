## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, trees, a winding sandy path on the left, a tall street lamp in the middle and an oval pond on the right. An older man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat sits on a wooden bench beside the path, reading a red book. On the bench next to him lies a sandwich, and a blue bird is perched at the end of the bench pecking at it, apparently without the man noticing. By the pond, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers holds a white paper bag and tosses crumbs toward a white duck, which is swimming over to them with its head down; some crumbs lie on the pond edge and grass. In the front centre there is a red wheelbarrow with nobody using it. At the front right is a flower bed with colourful flowers, and in the corner a green rubbish bin. The wheelbarrow's wheel looks odd — it is drawn as a square box rather than a round wheel.
