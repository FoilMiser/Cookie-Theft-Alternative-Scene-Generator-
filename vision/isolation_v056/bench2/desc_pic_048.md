# pic_048

## Free description
A sunny back garden next to a cream house with a red roof and blue door, surrounded by a tall wooden fence with a gate on the left. A man in a red T-shirt is painting the fence pink with a paint roller; a few fence boards near the gate are already pink and a pink paint tin sits on the grass by his feet. On the far left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps leaves on the path with a broom. In the middle, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses holds a garden hose, which runs back to a hose reel near the house; a brown dog is standing on the hose. A man in a green shirt stands in the centre with a white bucket upside down on his head, which looks odd. Near the house, a small girl in a pink dress points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the tree. A grey cat walks along the top of the fence and a blue bird flies in the sky. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench drinking through a straw from a red cup, and there is a flower bed with colourful flowers and an empty red wheelbarrow.
