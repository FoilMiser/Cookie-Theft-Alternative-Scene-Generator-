# pic_001

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and a paved path on the left, a swing set, a washing line and a house with a blue door on the right. In the middle a girl in a purple top and teal trousers holds up the end of a long dark-teal garden hose. She has a small yellow nozzle at the end and seems to be looking straight into it, holding it close to her face. The hose runs along the grass to a hose reel by the house, and a brown dog with a red collar is standing on the hose near the reel, as if pinching it shut. So it looks as if no water is coming out, and if the dog steps off, the girl may get sprayed in the face. At the front a white dog with brown spots crouches playfully over a yellow-green tennis ball, wagging its tail. On the right a man in a red shirt and blue jeans sits on a wooden bench reading a red book, not paying attention. The washing line holds a yellow striped towel and a pair of blue jeans; the swing hangs empty.
