# pic_026

## Free description
A sunny town square paved with grey tiles, in front of a row of colourful shops with striped awnings. At the front left a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is walking a green bicycle whose wheels are square, which is impossible to ride. Next to a fruit stall, in the middle, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie scatters seeds from a paper bag, and a small red bird pecks at the seeds on the ground. On a bench behind him an older woman with grey hair and glasses is reading a red book, while a blue bird on the other end of the bench pecks at her sandwich; she has not noticed and seems about to lose her lunch. On the right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sweeps a pile of dead leaves with a broom. There is also a black street lamp, a tree in a round planter, a green bin and a potted plant in the corner.
