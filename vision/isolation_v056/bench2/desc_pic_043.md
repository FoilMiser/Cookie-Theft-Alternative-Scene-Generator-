# pic_043

## Free description
A sunny playground with a metal fence, a slide on the left, a sandbox at the front left, a bench and tree on the right. A small girl in a pink dress holds a blue and a yellow balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other toward a red balloon that has floated away high into the sky; she has just lost it. A woman in a yellow dress stands nearby taking a photo with a camera, pointed to the left. At the front a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and rubs his eye as if crying, because his pink ice cream has fallen on the ground, where a brown dog is now licking it up. On the right an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses pushes a teal pram with a sleeping baby in it. The sandbox has a red bucket and blue spade, with a grey rock beside it.
