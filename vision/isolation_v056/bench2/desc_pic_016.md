# pic_016

## Free description
A sunny town street with a row of coloured shops and a paved square in front of them. On the left, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands holding a teal bicycle by its handlebars, in front of a fruit stall. His bicycle has square wheels, which is impossible and clearly wrong. In the centre, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie bends over and sprinkles seeds from a brown paper bag. A small red bird on the ground pecks at the seeds he has dropped. Behind him, an old woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a bench reading a red book. A blue bird on the bench next to her grabs her sandwich with its beak, lifting the top of the bread, and she hasn't noticed. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sweeps a pile of brown leaves with a broom. There is a tree in a round planter, a green bin and a potted plant in the bottom right corner.
