## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, trees, a winding sandy path on the left, a tall lamp post in the middle and a pond on the right. An older man with grey hair, a grey flat cap and a brown waistcoat sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. Next to him on the bench is a sandwich, and a blue bird is perched there pecking at it, apparently stealing his food while he reads. At the pond, a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers holds a white paper bag and tosses bread crumbs towards a white duck swimming at the edge, which is eating them. In the front centre there is a red wheelbarrow standing on its own with nobody using it. Its wheel looks square rather than round, which seems wrong. Bottom right there is a flower bed with colourful flowers and a green litter bin.
