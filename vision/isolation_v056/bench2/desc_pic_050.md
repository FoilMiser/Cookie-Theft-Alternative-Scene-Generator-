# pic_050

## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, trees, a winding sandy path and a pond. On the left, an elderly man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat sits on a bench by the path reading a red book. A blue bird stands on the other end of the bench, pecking at an open sandwich, while the man reads without noticing. In the middle-right, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers holds a white paper bag and tosses bread or crumbs toward a white duck swimming in the blue pond. In the front left is an empty red wheelbarrow, and in the front right a flower bed with colourful flowers. There is also a lamp post, a green bin, a grey rock, bushes, and reeds at the edges of the pond.
