## Free description
A sunny town square or pedestrian street lined with colourful two-storey shops (pink, pale green, peach and cream) with striped awnings. On the left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie bends over, scattering seeds or crumbs from a small paper bag for a red bird pecking on the ground at his feet. Behind him stands a fruit and vegetable stall with crates of green, yellow, orange and red produce. In the centre, a boy in a red T-shirt and blue trousers stands holding a red bicycle by the handlebar, looking down at it; the bicycle's wheels are drawn as square black frames instead of round wheels, which looks impossible. Behind him an old woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a bench reading a red book, while at the other end of the same bench a blue bird is pecking at (stealing from) her sandwich, which she does not seem to notice. On the right, a girl in a purple top and green trousers sweeps a pile of brown leaves with a broom. Further right are a tree in a round planter, a green bin and a potted plant.
