Her line and red-and-white float are in the water, with a little ripple around the float. In the front, an old man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. The dog walks ahead on the path. There is a street lamp in the centre, a grey rock at the front, and a green bin in the bottom left corner. Nothing looks clearly impossible.
