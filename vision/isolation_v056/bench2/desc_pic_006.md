# pic_006

## Free description
A sunny town street with a row of colourful shops behind a wide paved pavement. On the left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie bends over and scatters seeds from a small paper bag onto the ground, where a red bird is pecking at them. In the middle, a boy in a red shirt and blue trousers stands holding the handlebars of a red bicycle, looking down at it; the bicycle's wheels are drawn as squares, which is impossible for a working bike. Behind him, an elderly grey-haired woman in a green cardigan sits on a bench reading a red book, and right beside her on the bench a blue bird is pecking at her sandwich, which she does not seem to notice. On the right, a girl in a purple top and teal trousers sweeps a pile of brown leaves with a broom. There is a fruit and vegetable stall at the left, a street lamp, a tree in a round planter, a green bin and a potted plant.
