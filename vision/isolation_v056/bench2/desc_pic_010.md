# pic_010

## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, trees, a winding sandy path, a lamppost and a pond. An old man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers sits on a wooden bench beside the path reading a red book. Next to him on the bench is a sandwich, and a blue bird is pecking at it; he does not seem to notice, so the bird may be about to take his lunch. On the right a man in a green shirt and beige trousers stands at the edge of the pond holding a white paper bag and tossing bread pieces to a white duck, which swims toward the bread. In the bottom left there is a red wheelbarrow whose wheel is square, which would not roll. There is also a flower bed with colourful flowers at the bottom right, a grey rock at the bottom centre and a green bin in the corner.
