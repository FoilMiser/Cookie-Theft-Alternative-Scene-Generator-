## Free description
A sunny town square paved with grey tiles in front of a row of colourful two-storey shops with striped awnings. At the front left, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands holding a teal bicycle by the handlebar, but the bicycle's wheels are square, which is impossible to ride. Behind him is a fruit stall with boxes of green, yellow, orange and red fruit under a striped canopy. In the centre, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie bends over, scattering seeds from a brown paper bag for a red bird that is pecking at them on the ground. Behind him an older woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a bench reading a red book, while a blue bird at the end of the bench pecks at a sandwich next to her without her noticing. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sweeps a pile of brown leaves with a yellow broom. A tree in a round planter, a green bin, a black lamp post and a potted plant complete the scene.
