# pic_031

## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, trees, a path winding from the front up to the back, a lamp post and a pond. On the left, an older man with grey hair, a grey flat cap and a brown vest sits on a wooden bench beside the path, reading a red book. On the bench next to him lies an open sandwich, and a blue bird stands at the end of the bench with its beak right at the sandwich, apparently eating it while the man reads. In the middle right, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers holding a paper bag is throwing bread pieces toward a white duck at the edge of the pond; crumbs are falling between them and the duck is swimming toward them. In the front left there is a red wheelbarrow, empty. On the right front there is a flower bed with colourful flowers. A grey rock sits in the front centre and a green bin stands near the lamp post.
