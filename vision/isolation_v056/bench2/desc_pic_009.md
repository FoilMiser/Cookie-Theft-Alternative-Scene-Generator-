# pic_009

## Free description
A sunny town street with a row of coloured shop buildings with striped awnings, a lamppost, a tree and paved ground. On the left a boy in a red shirt and blue trousers stands holding a green bicycle, but the bicycle's wheels are square, which is impossible for riding. In the centre a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie bends over, holding a brown paper bag and scattering seeds; a small red bird on the ground is pecking at the seeds. Behind him an elderly grey-haired woman in a teal cardigan sits on a bench reading a red book, and next to her on the bench is a sandwich that a blue bird is pecking at; she does not seem to notice, so the bird looks about to eat or take her sandwich. On the right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sweeps a pile of brown leaves with a yellow broom. Behind the boy on the left is a fruit stall with boxes of fruit under a small striped canopy. The square bicycle wheels are the one clearly wrong thing.
