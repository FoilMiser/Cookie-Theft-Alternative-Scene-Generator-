On the path to the right a small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is running along holding the string of a red balloon. In the front, an elderly man in a cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers strides along holding a red leash attached to a white dog with brown spots, which is walking ahead of him on the path. Nothing seems about to go badly wrong; the float in the water might mean a fish is biting. There is a grey rock and a green bin in the foreground.
