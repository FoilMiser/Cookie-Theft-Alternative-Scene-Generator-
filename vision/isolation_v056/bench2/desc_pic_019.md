## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a tree on the right and a cream house with a red roof and blue door at the far right. On the left a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps a pile of dead leaves on a paved path, and behind him a man in a red shirt is painting the fence pink with a paint roller; a few planks are already pink and a tin of pink paint sits at his feet. In the middle an older woman with grey hair and glasses holds the nozzle of a garden hose up near her face, looking into it as if puzzled that no water is coming out. The reason seems to be the brown dog standing right on the hose, pinching it, between her and the hose reel by the house; it looks as if she is about to get sprayed in the face when the dog steps off. Behind her a man in a green shirt stands with his hands on his hips wearing an upturned metal bucket on his head, which is odd. Near the house a small girl in a pink dress points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of the tree. A grey cat walks along the top of the fence, a blue bird flies in the sky, and a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench at the bottom right drinking through a straw. There is also a red wheelbarrow and a flower bed in the garden.
