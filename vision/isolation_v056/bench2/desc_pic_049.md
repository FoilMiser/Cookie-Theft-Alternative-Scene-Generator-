# pic_049

## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, trees, a winding sandy path, a lamppost, a blue pond with reeds and a flower bed. On the left, an old man with grey hair, a cap and a brown waistcoat sits on a bench beside the path reading a red book. Next to him on the bench is a sandwich, and a blue bird is perched on the bench with its beak at the sandwich, apparently eating or stealing it while he reads. In the centre, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers stands at the edge of the pond holding a paper bag and throwing bits of bread toward a white duck swimming in the pond. Some crumbs land on the water and some on the pond edge. In the front centre is a red wheelbarrow with a square wheel, which looks wrong or impossible. On the right is a bed of colourful flowers and a green bin in the corner. The main events are the bird taking the man's sandwich and the man feeding the duck; the square wheelbarrow wheel is the odd thing.
