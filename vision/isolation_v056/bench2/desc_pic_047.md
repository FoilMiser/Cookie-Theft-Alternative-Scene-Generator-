## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, trees and a winding sandy path. On a wooden bench by the path an older man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is reading a red book, while a blue bird beside him on the bench pecks at his sandwich without him noticing. On the right a man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers stands at the edge of a pond holding a paper bag and tossing pieces of bread to a white duck swimming in the water. A few bread pieces are also lying on the ground at the pond's edge. In the front left there is an empty red wheelbarrow, and around the park are a tall street lamp, a grey rock, a flower bed and a green bin. Nothing dramatic is about to happen apart from the bird helping itself to the reader's sandwich.
