# pic_037

## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, trees, a winding sandy path on the left and a pond on the right. On a wooden bench beside the path, an elderly man in a grey cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers sits reading a red book. Next to him on the bench is a sandwich, and a small blue bird is pecking at it, apparently taking his lunch without him noticing. At the pond a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers holds a white paper bag and reaches out over the water, dropping pieces of bread to a white duck that is swimming up and eating them; a few crumbs float on the water and lie on the pond's edge. In the bottom left there is a red wheelbarrow, whose wheel is drawn as a square, which would not roll. A flower bed with colourful flowers sits in the bottom right, along with a green bin, and there is a grey rock at the bottom centre and a tall street lamp in the middle.
