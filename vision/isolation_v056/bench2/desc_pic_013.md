# pic_013

## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, trees, a winding sandy path, a tall lamp post and a pond on the right. An old man in a grey flat cap and brown waistcoat sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. Next to him on the bench is a sandwich, and a blue bird is standing beside it pecking at the sandwich while he is not looking, so it looks like the bird is about to steal his lunch. By the pond, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers holds a paper bag and is throwing bread crumbs to a white duck swimming at the edge of the water. A red wheelbarrow stands in the front left, a grey rock in the front centre, a flower bed in the front right and a green bin in the bottom right corner. Nothing else seems to be happening. One odd detail: the wheelbarrow's wheel looks square with crossed spokes rather than round.
