## Free description
A sunny park with a pond on the right, a winding sand path on the left, hills, trees and a street lamp. On the left, an old man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. A blue bird on the bench beside him pecks at his sandwich, and he hasn't noticed. In the centre, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers stands by the pond holding a white paper bag. He reaches out over the water, tossing bread crumbs to a white duck. The duck swims toward him, with wake lines behind it, and some crumbs are on the water and the pond edge. In the front centre there is a red wheelbarrow with nobody using it. Its wheel looks square, which would be impossible. On the right there is a bed of colourful flowers (purple, orange, red, pink, yellow), and a green bin sits in the bottom right corner.
