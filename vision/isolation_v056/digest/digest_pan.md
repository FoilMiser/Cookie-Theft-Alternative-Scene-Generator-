## What differs between the arms

Every reading in every arm notices the pan and calls it the wrong thing to water with. In O, 2 of 3 readings settle on frying pan and 1 settles on cooking pot or saucepan; one of the frying-pan readings also offers saucepan. In B, all 6 readings settle on frying pan, and 2 of them still offer saucepan (one also ladle) as a second reading. Both F readings settle on frying pan with no other name.
