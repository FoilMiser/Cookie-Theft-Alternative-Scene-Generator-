## What differs between the arms

Every reading in every arm says plainly that the person hangs or pegs up washing: 3 of 3 in O, 6 of 6 in B, 2 of 2 in C and 4 of 4 in F. "Arms raised", "both arms raised" or "arms up" is written in 2 of 3 O readings, 2 of 6 B readings, 1 of 2 C readings and 0 of 4 F readings; no reading in any arm says the hands are together or apart. No reading says a peg is held in the hand; the only peg remarks are "hanging laundry with pegs" (B) and "reaching for the next peg" (F). Doubt about what the hands are doing is recorded once in O (pegging on or taking down), once in B (whether the towel is being hung) and once in F (pegging or reaching for the next peg).
