## What differs between the arms

Every reading in every arm says plainly that the person feeds the birds: 4 of 4 in O, 7 of 7 in B and 4 of 4 in F. All 15 readings call it a "paper bag" somewhere in their lines; no reading in any arm calls it a box or says it floats. In O, 1 of 4 readings says the bag is held ("in the woman's hand"); the other 3 say only that the seed comes "from a paper bag". In B, 5 of 7 say the bag is held, and 2 of those say the seed is sprinkled from the woman's "other hand"; in F, 3 of 4 say the bag is held. One B reading raises, then sets aside, accidental spilling from the bag.
