No O, B or C reading links the box to a hand or a wave. Six of them (O 2, B 2, C 2: pic_091, pic_098, pic_014, pic_094, pic_199, pic_076) and the B reading pic_127 ("nobody throwing it") say instead that no one is there to have thrown it.

## What differs between the arms

All 10 readings in every arm notice the floating box, and all 10 offer thrown, falling or blown beside it, so no arm has a plain (a) reading. The arms differ in what the readers tie the box to. In O, B and C, 7 of 8 readings say no one is near enough to have thrown it. In F, both readings tie it to the boy's raised arms (thrown by him or about to be caught) and also say it is far above him.
