The "other" column holds readings that say the water goes up and is wrong but offer a second reading that is not a hose, fountain or spray (a downward pour drawn awkwardly, a splash, water falling back, the person splashing). They fit neither (a) nor (b) as written. The two F readings are counted in (b) although neither calls the upward water impossible: both mention drops going up and offer a sprinkler or a hose.

## What differs between the arms

Arm O has the only plain reading (1 of 3); its other 2 readings call the water impossible but offer a hose or a fountain beside it. In arm B, 5 of 6 readings remark on the water's path, but none gives the plain reading: 2 offer a spray, 2 offer a different second reading, 1 calls it only "a little odd" and still reads it as watering, and 1 does not notice it. Both C readings call the water wrong and both offer a second reading that is not a hose, fountain or spray. Both F readings describe ordinary watering in the free description and mention the rising drops only in the unsure list, with a sprinkler or a hose offered; neither calls the rising water impossible.
