## What differs

Arm B has the most readings giving the man's weight as why the child is stuck, 4 of 6 plus 1 with another reading beside it, against 3 of 6 in arm C, 1 of 3 in arm O and none plainly in arm F (1 of 2 with doubt). Every reading in every arm says or implies the child is up in the air; the (c) readings, 2 in O, 1 in B, 3 in C and 1 in F, say she is stuck but give his ignoring her or no reason instead of his weight. The child's hand or arm is read as a call or point toward the man in 2 B readings, and as a hand at the mouth (worried, scared, surprised or giggling) in all 6 C readings and 2 of 3 O readings. Doubt about where the man sits appears in 2 O readings and 1 B reading, none in C or F; the stops are remarked on 3 times (2 B, 1 C), as blocks or bumpers, and the seat backs never.
