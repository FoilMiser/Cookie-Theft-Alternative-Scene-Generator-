## What differs

No reading in any arm read the kite as flying; all 14 called it caught or stuck. In arm O 1 of 4 readings (pic_035) gave no other reading, while 3 offered flying in front, near or behind. In arm B all 8 and in arm F both readings offered flying in front, behind, near or low next to the tree beside caught. Strings were remarked on in all arms as running from the child to the kite or the tree, and 2 readings (pic_142 in O, pic_019 in F) called the string sagging, drooping or slack.
