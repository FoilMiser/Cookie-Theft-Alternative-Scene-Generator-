## What differs between the arms

Neither arm draws a string, tether or yo-yo reading (0 of 4 in O, 0 of 8 in B). Doubt about the thrower or the ball's direction appears in 1 of 4 O readings and in 6 of 8 B readings. Every reading in both arms still names the intended thrower as the first or most likely one; the doubts are offered as second readings ("less likely", "conceivably", "might instead be waving"). In O, three readers say the trail starts at the thrower's hand and take that as settling the throw. In B, two readings (pic_119, pic_214) still describe the line as running from the boy's hand.
