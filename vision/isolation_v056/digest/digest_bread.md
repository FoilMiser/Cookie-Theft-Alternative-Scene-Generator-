## What differs between the arms

All 4 O readings and all 8 B readings say plainly that the person feeds or throws bread to the duck; the 2 F readings say so too but each adds a doubt that the bird may be standing by or stealing a "sandwich", so both are counted (b). All 14 readings say there is a paper bag in the person's hand. In O, 2 of 4 readings mention a slice held in the hand ("holds a slice of bread", "a slice of toast"); none of the 8 B or 2 F readings mention a slice. 2 of 8 B readings call the food "bread or seeds" / "bread or seed", and 2 B readings note that the reaching arm or hand overlaps the pond rim; no O or F reading says either. No reading in any arm says the pieces are in the air: every reading that places them says they are on the water or the bank.
