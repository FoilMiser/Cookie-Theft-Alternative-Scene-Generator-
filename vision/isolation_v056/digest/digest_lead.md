## What differs

In arm O all 4 readings called the thing a lead and read a walk, 1 plainly and 3 with doubt (play, a toy, just holding). In arm B 6 of 8 readings called it a yellow garden hose, and the other 2 called it a lead but raised the hose or rope reading themselves. In arm C 8 of 10 readings called it a lead or leash and 2 called it a red garden hose; of the 8, 1 read the walk plainly, 5 with doubt and 2 did not mention a walk. Both arm F readings called it a yellow garden hose. Across arms B and C, 5 readings that called it a lead still offered a hose as another reading in their unsure lines; no arm O reading mentioned a hose.
