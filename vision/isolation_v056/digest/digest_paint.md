## What differs between the arms

All 18 readings say plainly that the person paints the fence, and none remarks on how far the painter stands from it. Roller remarks come in 2 of 4 O readings, 4 of 8 B readings, 1 of 2 C readings and 0 of 4 F readings. Where O readers comment, it is the roller looking detached or held away from or above the fence. Among B readers, 2 of 4 say much the same (detached or floating, doubled), and the other 2 say it looks like a spray can or brush or is held at an odd angle. Every reading of a picture with prints (O 1, B 2, C 2, F 2) says the dog walked through the paint. O readers name the container a paint tin (one also a paint tray). B, C and F readers use tin, pot and can for the paint, and tray (C: roller tray) where there are prints.
