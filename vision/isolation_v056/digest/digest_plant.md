## What differs between the arms

Plain planting readings are 2 of 4 in O, 5 of 8 in B and 2 of 2 in F. All the remaining readings (O 2, B 3) offer planting together with something else. In O the second reading is digging or weeding with the trowel. In B it is pulling a plant out or picking one, and two of those three readers say the plant in the hand is small or blends into the bed. All 4 O readings see the pot and say it is waiting to be planted; all 10 B and F readings see the seedling tray. Posture: 3 of 4 O readings say "on one knee" and 2 of 4 mention a stretched back leg (one of them calls the pose "a bit stretched"); 2 of 8 B readings call the kneeling pose stretched or awkward; no reading in any arm says lunge, crouch or all fours.
