## What differs

All 18 readings linked the box to the water and said it was getting wet or soaked. In arm O all 4 called it a cardboard box; 1 raised a delivered parcel only as one of two options, and 3 doubted whether the wetting was an accident or meant. In arm B 1 of 8 treated it plainly as a delivered parcel, 5 offered parcel as one of two readings, and 2 called it a cardboard box without saying parcel; 6 of the 8 arm B readings mentioned the label, against none in arm O. In arm F 1 of 2 called it a parcel plainly and 1 only as a possibility; in arm C 2 of 4 did so plainly.
