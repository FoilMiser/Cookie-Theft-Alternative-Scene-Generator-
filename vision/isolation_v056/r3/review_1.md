# Review of pair_1.png (rows a and b)

## 1. How the rows differ

The figures are the same in both rows: same clothes, faces, colours and held objects. The difference is the walk. Row a takes short steps with bent knees, so the figures look crouched or tiptoeing. Row b takes a longer, open stride with straighter legs, and the figures look upright and taller. Heads in row b also look a little larger against the body.

## 2. Figure by figure

**Woman in purple top with bag**
- Row a: short step, knees together. The bag hangs in front of both legs and hides most of them.
- Row b: longer stride, feet well apart. The bag hangs between the legs and both legs show on either side of it.
- Better: **b**. The legs read clearly as walking instead of being hidden behind the bag.

**Older woman in cardigan with ice cream**
- Row a: licking the cone, hunched forward. Knees are bent and the front knee crosses in, so the step looks unsteady.
- Row b: licking the cone, upright. Long step with straight legs and both feet planted.
- Better: **b**. The step reads as a calm walk instead of a stumble or knock-kneed shuffle.

**Man in red shirt holding a lead**
- Row a: bent knees and a short, crouched step, with both fists held together in front of the waist.
- Row b: long, straight-legged stride, with the same fists held together in front.
- Better: **b**. The stride is natural, although neither row actually shows a lead (see faults).

**Child in yellow top with ice cream**
- Row a: knees deeply bent and body crouched, as if sneaking or tiptoeing. The cone is held up at the nose.
- Row b: big open stride with a forward lean, like a brisk walk or trot. The cone is held at the mouth.
- Better: **b**. The crouch in row a looks furtive. Row b looks like a cheerful child on the move.

## 3. Faults

**Row a**
- Woman: the bag covers almost both legs, and at the small size the lower body is a yellow block with feet poking out.
- Woman: the hand of the far arm sticks out at the back of the hip as a small detached-looking lump (row b has this too).
- Older woman: the bent, inward-crossing front knee looks awkward and unsteady.
- Man: crouched, bent-knee step looks like sneaking or bracing, not walking.
- Man: no lead is drawn. The two hands meet in front as big, bone-shaped fists holding nothing, which is unclear at both sizes.
- Child: the deep crouch reads as sneaking. At the small size the child looks squat and hunched.
- Child: the far hand shows as a small lump at the front of the shorts (row b has this too).

**Row b**
- Woman: the far-arm hand still sticks out at the back of the hip.
- Woman: the very wide stride is a little stiff, like a scissor step.
- Man: still no lead drawn, so the bone-shaped fists held together in front are unexplained. At the small size they read as a blob in front of the belly.
- Child: the head is very large against the body, and the stride is so long it borders on a lunge.
- Child: the far hand still shows as a lump at the front of the shorts.
- Older woman: the long straight-legged stride is energetic for an older figure, but it reads fine.

## 4. Choice

**Row b**, fairly sure. Its walks are open, upright and easy to read at both sizes. Row a's bent-knee crouch makes several figures look sneaky or unsteady. The missing lead on the man is a fault in both rows and does not affect the choice.
