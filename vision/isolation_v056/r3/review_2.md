# Review of pair_2.png

## 1. How the rows differ

Row a draws the figures leaning forward with long, wide strides and knees clearly bent, so they look like they are hurrying or creeping. Row b draws them more upright, with shorter strides and slightly larger heads, so they look like they are walking calmly. Clothes, colours, faces and held objects are almost the same in both rows.

## 2. Figure by figure

**Woman in purple top with bag**
- Row a: walking with a long stride, bag hanging from her right hand beside her legs, both legs clearly visible either side of the bag.
- Row b: walking with a shorter stride, bag hanging in front of her legs and covering most of them.
- Better: a. Her legs and stride read clearly around the bag, while in row b the bag hides the legs and the walk is harder to see, mainly at the small size.

**Older woman in cardigan with ice cream**
- Row a: wide walking stride, leaning forward, ice cream raised to her mouth.
- Row b: shorter stride, more upright, ice cream raised to her mouth, skirt a little longer.
- Better: b. The calmer upright walk suits an older woman, and the very wide stride in row a looks strained for her.

**Man in red shirt holding a lead**
- Row a: crouched forward with bent knees and a long stride, both hands pushed out in front at waist height, holding nothing visible.
- Row b: more upright with a straighter stride, both hands out in front at waist height, holding nothing visible.
- Better: b. He looks like an ordinary walking man, while in row a the hunched crouch looks sneaky or odd.

**Child in yellow top with ice cream**
- Row a: leaning forward in a running or striding pose, ice cream held up at his mouth.
- Row b: more upright striding pose, larger head, ice cream held up at his mouth.
- Better: no difference. Both read clearly as a child walking and eating ice cream; row a looks livelier and row b calmer, and neither is clearly better.

## 3. Faults

**Row a**
- Man in red shirt: no lead is drawn; his hands are held out empty, so it is unclear what he is doing. The bent-knee crouch makes him look hunched or sneaking.
- Man in red shirt: the two hands merge into one blob in front of him, hard to read at the small size.
- Older woman: the stride is very wide and the body leans forward, which looks strained for an older figure.
- Child: strong forward lean makes him look like he is rushing; at the small size the ice cream and face merge.
- All figures: the overall forward lean gives the row a slightly hurried, uneasy feel.

**Row b**
- Man in red shirt: no lead is drawn here either; the empty hands held out in front are unclear at both sizes.
- Man in red shirt: hands again merge into one blob.
- Woman with bag: the bag covers most of her legs, so the walk is hard to see at the small size.
- Older woman: knees come close together and the legs look slightly knock-kneed under the skirt.
- Child: the head is large compared with the body, and the ice cream overlaps the mouth so the face is partly hidden; at the small size the cone and face merge.

## 4. Choice

Row b, for its calmer, upright and friendlier poses, especially for the man and the older woman. Fairly sure.
