# Review of pair_5 (rows a and b)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a each figure has a tan cardboard box in front of the chest or belly, and one arm passes over or beside the box and reaches out past it, so no hand clearly holds the box. In row b each figure holds a paper bag by its top in a hand hanging down at the hip, and the other arm reaches forward on its own. Faces, clothes, legs and feet are the same in both rows.

## 2. Figure by figure

**Woman in purple top**
- a: standing, a box at chest height in front of her, one arm reaching straight forward over the top of the box with the hand open.
- b: standing, a brown paper bag hanging from one hand at her hip, the other arm reaching straight forward with the hand open.
- Better: b. The bag is plainly gripped by a hand, while in a the box seems to hang in the air with the arm passing over it.

**Older man in cap**
- a: standing slightly stooped, a box at belly height in front of him, the cream-sleeved arm reaching forward across the top of the box.
- b: standing slightly stooped, a brown paper bag hanging from one hand at his hip, the other cream-sleeved arm reaching forward.
- Better: b. He is clearly carrying the bag; in a the box sits against his belly with no hand under or around it.

**Bearded man in green**
- a: leaning forward, a box at waist height in front of him, one arm reaching down and forward past the box with fingers pointing.
- b: leaning forward, a white bag held by its handles in one low hand, the other arm reaching forward with the hand open.
- Better: b. The held bag reads as carried; in a the arm slides past the box and the box looks stuck to his front.

**Woman in yellow dress**
- a: leaning forward, a box at chest height in front of her, one arm reaching down and forward past the box.
- b: leaning forward, both arms forward and low, one hand gripping the top of a brown bag, the other hand open just beyond it.
- Better: b. One hand visibly holds the bag; in a the box floats in front of the dress with no hand on it.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- All four: no hand holds the box. The arm crosses over or past it, so the box looks pinned or floating in front of the body.
- All four: the box has a doubled outline at the top and right side, so it reads as two stacked or offset boxes, or a box with a lid shifted.
- Woman in purple, woman in yellow: the tan box is close in colour to the skin of the arm crossing it, so at the small size box and arm blur into one lump.
- Bearded man, woman in yellow: the reaching arm points down and forward with stiff fingers; it looks like pointing at the ground rather than doing anything with the box.
- Older man: at the small size the box almost merges with his brown vest.
- All four: the reaching arm has no clear purpose; with the box not held, the whole pose is hard to read.

### Row b
- Older man: the arm holding the bag comes out from behind his waist at an odd bend, with a short stretch of cream sleeve showing below the vest; the shoulder-to-hand line is hard to follow.
- Bearded man: the white bag is pale against the light background; at the small size it is faint and reads mostly as an outline.
- Woman in yellow: the two hands are close together near the bag, so at the small size it is unclear which hand holds the bag and what the open hand is doing.
- Woman in purple, older man: the gripping hand covers most of the bag top, so the handle/fold is hard to make out; small size still reads as a bag, but just barely as "held by the top".
- All four: the forward-reaching arm is long and straight with an open hand; the gesture (offering, reaching, pointing) is not clear.

## 4. Choice

Row b, for a friendly, clear cartoon style: each figure is visibly carrying something, and the held object reads at the small size. Row a's floating, ungripped boxes are the bigger fault. How sure: fairly sure.
