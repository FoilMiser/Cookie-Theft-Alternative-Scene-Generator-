# Review of pair 13 (grey cat, ginger cat)

## 1. How the rows differ

Both rows show the two cats in the same low, side-on stalking pose, with the head pushed forward and the tail held out straight behind. In row a the legs are short and bunched under the body, so the cat looks squat and crouched with its paws close together. In row b the body sits higher and the legs are longer and spread wider: the near front leg reaches far forward and the near hind leg stretches back, so the stride reads clearly as a creeping walk.

## 2. Figure by figure

**Grey cat (left)**
- Row a: crouched low, legs folded short under the body, paws close together, head forward, tail straight back.
- Row b: low creeping stride, front leg reaching well forward, hind leg pushed back, head forward, tail straight back.
- Better: **b**. The longer, spread legs make the stalking walk easy to read, while row a's folded stubby legs look cramped and awkward.

**Ginger cat (right)**
- Row a: same squat crouch as the grey cat, short folded legs, paws bunched, head forward.
- Row b: same long creeping stride as the grey cat, front paw reaching forward, hind leg back.
- Better: **b**. Same reason: the stride is clear and the darker far legs separate cleanly from the near legs, which they do less well in row a's tight bunch.

## 3. Faults

**Row a**
- Grey cat: the legs are very short and folded, so the cat looks squashed and the pose is hard to read as walking or stalking.
- Grey cat: small stray dark marks on the hind haunch and the front leg look like smudges or stray lines.
- Grey cat: the hind leg bends forward at a sharp knee-like angle under the belly, which looks odd for a cat.
- Ginger cat: same stubby, folded legs and squashed look as the grey cat.
- Ginger cat: same stray dark marks on the haunch and leg.
- Both cats, small size: the four paws merge into a cluster of blobs under the body, so the legs are hard to count or tell apart.

**Row b**
- Grey cat: the forward front paw is long and flat, almost like a slipper, and the foreleg is very long, which looks a little stretched.
- Grey cat: the white chest patch runs into a thin pale strip along the top of the front leg, which looks like a gap in the drawing.
- Ginger cat: the same long flat front paw and stretched foreleg.
- Ginger cat: the pale chest patch is cream on orange and low in contrast, so it reads as a smudge.
- Both cats: the body is long and the back slopes down sharply to the head, so the cat looks a little elongated.
- Both cats, small size: still readable, though the reaching paw is thin.

## 4. Choice

I would choose **row b** for a friendly, clear cartoon style. The spread legs give a clear creeping-walk silhouette at both the large and the small size, and row a's bunched legs and stray marks are the bigger problems. How sure: **fairly sure**.
