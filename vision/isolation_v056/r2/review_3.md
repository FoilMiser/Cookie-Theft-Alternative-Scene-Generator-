# Review of pair_3 (r2)

## 1. How the rows differ

The heads, arms, held objects and lower legs are almost the same in both rows; the difference is the lap. In row a the thighs are short and drop steeply from the hip, so the knees sit close under the body and the figures look perched on an edge or half-standing. In row b the thighs run longer and more level toward the viewer, with the skirt or dress carried out over them to the knee, so the figures read more plainly as sitting on a seat.

## 2. Figure by figure

1. **Woman in yellow dress (book)**
   - Row a: seated, reading an open red book held at chest height, head tipped down; short lap, knees just under the hips.
   - Row b: same reading pose, book slightly lower; a longer, flatter lap with the dress reaching the knees.
   - Better: **b**. The longer lap makes the seated pose clear, where row a's short thighs look like she is leaning on a stool.

2. **Older woman in skirt (ice cream)**
   - Row a: seated, licking a pink ice-cream cone held to her mouth; short grey skirt lap, knees tucked under her.
   - Row b: same cone-licking pose; the grey skirt makes a deeper lap with the knees further forward.
   - Better: **b**. The skirt lap reads as a real seat in row b, while in row a the skirt looks like a short cushion with legs hanging straight down.

3. **Girl in pink dress (drink with straw)**
   - Row a: seated, sipping from a red cup through a straw, eyes closed in a smile; very short lap, pink hem at the knees.
   - Row b: same sipping pose; a longer pink lap with the knees further out.
   - Better: **b**. Row a's lap is so short that she looks like she is standing with bent knees; row b is clearer, though its lap is a little boxy.

4. **Woman in yellow dress (drink with straw)**
   - Row a: seated, sipping from a large red cup through a straw held in both hands; short lap.
   - Row b: same sipping pose; longer, level lap with the dress over the thighs.
   - Better: **b**. Same reason as figure 1: the seated pose is clearer and the body looks better balanced.

## 3. Faults

**Row a**
- Woman in yellow (book): the thighs are very short and steep, so she looks perched or half-standing rather than seated.
- Older woman: the skirt lap is a thin slab with the shins dropping straight down; at the small size she looks like she is standing.
- Girl in pink: almost no lap. The body sits straight on top of the bent legs, which looks odd for a seated child. At the small size she is tiny and her cup and straw are hard to make out.
- Woman in yellow (drink): the lap is short, as in figure 1. The hip line under the dress shows a hard, boxy edge.
- All four: the bottom of the dress is a flat, square shelf that looks cut off, because there is no seat under it.

**Row b**
- Woman in yellow (book): the lap is wide and boxy, a square block with rounded corners, and the dress looks a bit stiff.
- Older woman: the skirt lap is large and slab-like, and its front edge is sharp, which looks a little heavy.
- Girl in pink: the lap is long for a child's body, and the pink thigh block looks chunky next to her thin arms. At the small size she is still very small and her cup is hard to read.
- Woman in yellow (drink): the lap is deep and blocky, and the flat underside still looks cut off where a seat would be.
- All four: the flat-bottomed lap still shows clearly with no seat drawn, though less badly than in row a.

## 4. Choice

**Row b**, fairly sure. Its longer, level laps make every figure read clearly as seated, at both sizes, which suits a friendly, clear cartoon style. Row a's short thighs make the figures look perched or half-standing. Row b's laps are a little blocky, but that is the smaller fault.
