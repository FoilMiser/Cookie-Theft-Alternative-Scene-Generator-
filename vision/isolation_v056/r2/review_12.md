# Review of pair_12

## 1. How the rows differ

All eight figures stand and point up with the right arm. In row a the arm goes up almost straight, with a thin pointing hand high above the head, so it reads partly as "hand up". In row b the arm leans out at a slant toward the upper right, the pointing hand is larger, and the bodies are drawn wider and heavier, so it reads more clearly as pointing at something up and to the side.

## 2. Figure by figure

**Older woman in a cardigan**
- Row a: standing, arm raised nearly straight up, thin finger pointing, head tipped back looking up.
- Row b: standing, arm slanted up and out to the right, larger hand pointing, looking up to the right.
- Better: b. The slanted arm and bigger hand make the pointing gesture clear, where row a's nearly upright arm with a small hand looks more like asking to speak.

**Woman in a purple top**
- Row a: standing, very long arm straight up, narrow sleeve band on the shoulder, looking up.
- Row b: standing, arm slanted up and right, puffed short sleeve, looking up toward the hand.
- Better: b. The sleeve and shoulder join look natural in row b, while row a's thin sleeve on a long upright arm looks stretched.

**Girl in a pink dress**
- Row a: standing, large head tipped back, arm raised steeply up past the side of the head.
- Row b: standing, large head, arm raised at a slightly wider slant, pointing up and right.
- Better: no difference. Both draw her almost the same, with the same big head and the arm starting close to the neck; the small versions are equally readable.

**Boy in a red cap**
- Row a: standing in a hoodie, arm raised fairly straight up, thin pointing hand, looking up.
- Row b: standing in a hoodie, arm slanted up and right, bigger hand, looking up to the right.
- Better: b. The slanted arm reads as a clear point at both sizes, and the hand stays visible in the small version.

## 3. Faults

**Row a**
- Older woman: raised hand is small and thin; at the small size the finger is barely visible and the gesture looks like a raised hand rather than a point.
- Woman in purple: raised arm looks overly long, and the sleeve is a thin band on the shoulder that looks odd.
- Girl: arm appears to rise from behind her neck/head; her head is very large and tipped back, so the face points mostly up.
- Boy: hand at the top is small and thin; the arm is nearly vertical so the direction of the point is unclear.
- All: feet splayed outward in the same stance, a little stiff.

**Row b**
- Older woman: torso is broad and boxy, which makes her look heavy.
- Woman in purple: raised shoulder sits high and close to the chin.
- Girl: arm still starts close to the neck; the dress is a long straight tube; head very large.
- Boy: raised arm is long and starts up at the hood, so the shoulder is hard to see.
- All: pointing hands are big and blunt, almost mitten-like at the large size; bodies look a bit stocky.

## 4. Choice

Row b, fairly sure. Its slanted arms and larger hands make the pointing gesture read clearly at the small picture size, and the heavier, rounder build suits a friendly cartoon style; row a's near-vertical arms are more ambiguous.
