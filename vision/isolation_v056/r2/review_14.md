# Review of pair_14

## 1. How the rows differ

Both rows show the same four figures standing with one arm raised, pointing upward. In row a the arm rises at a slant and the hand is a small fist with one finger up. In row b the arm goes up nearer to straight, the figures are drawn a little taller, and each pointing finger has an oval drawn on it (a knuckle or nail line). At picture size the two rows look almost the same.

## 2. Figure by figure

**Older woman in a cardigan**
- Row a: standing, right arm raised at a slant, pointing up, looking up.
- Row b: standing, right arm raised more steeply, pointing up, looking up.
- Better: a. The fist-and-finger hand reads cleanly, while row b's finger carries an oval that looks like a hole or ring.

**Woman in a purple top**
- Row a: standing, arm raised at a slant, pointing up; the purple sleeve bunches into a bump behind the shoulder.
- Row b: standing, arm raised more steeply, pointing up; the sleeve sits on the upper arm.
- Better: b. The shoulder and sleeve join looks more natural in b.

**Girl in a pink dress**
- Row a: standing, arm raised, pointing up, head tipped back.
- Row b: the same pose, a little taller, arm more upright.
- Better: no difference. Both have the same oversized head, and the small versions match.

**Boy in a red cap**
- Row a: standing in a hoodie, arm raised at a slant, pointing up, looking up.
- Row b: the same pose, arm more upright, with an oval drawn on the finger.
- Better: a. The hand is cleaner and nothing else changes.

## 3. Faults

**Row a**
- Woman in purple: the sleeve forms an odd bump behind the shoulder, and the arm seems to come out of the top of it.
- Girl: the head is very large, almost as wide as her torso, and it crowds the raised arm.
- All: the raised arm is short next to the body, so the pointing reads a little stiff.
- All, small size: the hand is only a dot of a finger, but the pointing gesture still reads.

**Row b**
- All four: the oval on the pointing finger looks like a hole or ring at the large size.
- Girl: the same oversized head as in row a; the arm seems to sprout from the side of the head.
- Older woman and boy: the arm is so upright that it nearly touches the side of the head.
- All, small size: no real difference from row a; the finger oval disappears.

## 4. Choice

Row a, for its cleaner pointing hands. This is a toss-up: the rows are nearly the same, and at picture size you cannot tell them apart.
