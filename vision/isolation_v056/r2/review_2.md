# Review of pair_2.png (rows a and b)

## 1. How the rows differ

The only clear difference is the front-view girl (figure 1). In row a her fists are pulled in to the tops of her shoulders, and her head sits straight on the dress with no neck. In row b her arms are raised out to the sides with fists up in a cheering pose, and a short neck shows. The other three figures look almost the same in both rows. The only other change is small: the older woman's cone sits a little lower in row b.

## 2. Figure by figure

**Figure 1: girl in pink dress, seated, front**
- Row a: sitting with both fists bunched against the tops of her shoulders, elbows tucked in; smiling.
- Row b: sitting with both arms lifted out to the sides, elbows bent, fists up; smiling.
- Better: **b**. Row b's gesture reads at once as happy or cheering, even at the small size. Row a's hands-at-shoulders pose is cramped and unclear, as if she is clutching straps or shrugging.

**Figure 2: same girl, seated, side view**
- Row a: sitting upright with her hands clasped over her knees and her feet flat.
- Row b: the same pose, hands clasped over her knees.
- Better: **no difference**. The two drawings are the same apart from very small shifts in outline.

**Figure 3: woman in yellow dress, seated, front**
- Row a: holding an open red book in front of her chest with both hands, elbows spread wide, looking down at it.
- Row b: the same, reading the open red book.
- Better: **no difference**. The pose, the book and the proportions match.

**Figure 4: older woman in skirt, seated, front**
- Row a: holding a pink ice-cream cone up to her mouth with one hand. Her other arm hangs straight down at her side.
- Row b: the same, with the cone held slightly lower so the scoop sits at her mouth and chin.
- Better: **no difference**. The cone is a touch lower in b and covers a little less of her face, but the change is too small to matter.

## 3. Faults

**Row a**
- Figure 1: her fists at her shoulders are hard to read. They could be a shrug, a hug or holding straps, and at the small size the arms merge into the body.
- Figure 1: she has no neck, so the large head looks pressed straight onto the dress.
- Figure 4: the ice-cream scoop covers her mouth and nose and touches her glasses, so the face is partly hidden.
- Figure 4: her hanging arm is very long, and the hand ends beside the seat below the skirt line. At the small size it looks like a stray shape.
- Figure 3: her elbows splay very wide and the arms look bulky. The book hides the whole chest, though the action is still clear.

**Row b**
- Figure 1: her raised arms are thin and the fists are small. At the small size the hands are only just visible, though the pose still reads.
- Figure 1: the cheering pose is energetic, which may not suit a quiet seated scene, depending on context.
- Figure 4: the same long hanging arm, with the hand below the skirt line, as in row a.
- Figure 4: the scoop still covers her mouth, though a little less than in row a.
- Figure 3: the same wide, bulky elbows as in row a.

## 4. Choice

**Row b**, fairly sure. The other three figures are a wash. Row b's front-view girl has an open, readable, friendly gesture and a visible neck. Row a's version looks cramped and unclear at both sizes.
