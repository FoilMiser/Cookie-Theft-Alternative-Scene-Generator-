# Review of pair_10

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a each figure is in a low lunge with one knee down and the other foot flat. The body leans far forward and both hands push a garden trowel point-down. In row b each figure kneels on both knees, with the shins flat behind and the body more upright. One hand holds out a small seedling (a pink bud and two leaves) with a brown root ball hanging below the hand. Row b also draws the figures a little larger and wider.

## 2. Figure by figure

**Woman in a purple top**
- Row a: in a lunge with the back knee down, leaning steeply and pushing a trowel down with both hands.
- Row b: kneeling on both knees, leaning a little and holding a seedling with its root ball out in front.
- Better: b. The kneeling pose is calmer and easier to read, and the seedling says "gardening" more clearly than a small grey triangle.

**Man in a red shirt**
- Row a: the same lunge and steep lean, pushing a trowel down with both hands.
- Row b: kneeling on both knees and holding out a seedling with its root ball.
- Better: b. At the small size the trowel nearly disappears, but the pink and green plant still stands out.

**Older man in a cap**
- Row a: in a lunge with his cream sleeves stretched long and straight down to the trowel.
- Row b: kneeling upright and holding a seedling out with one bent arm.
- Better: no difference. Row a's long arm looks strained, and in row b the root ball floating under his hand looks just as odd. Overall the two read about equally well.

**Boy in a red cap**
- Row a: in a lunge in his blue hoodie, leaning hard and pushing a trowel down.
- Row b: kneeling on both knees and holding out a seedling.
- Better: b. Row a's hard lean bunches up the hoodie and makes the waist messy, while row b's more upright body is tidier.

## 3. Faults

**Row a**
- All four: the trowel is a small grey triangle on a short stick. At the small size it reads as a cone or an ice-cream cone more than a tool.
- All four: there is a small red fleck at the wrist or hand that looks like a stray mark or a cut.
- All four: the two hands overlap into one lumpy fist on the trowel handle.
- All four: the lean is very steep and the arms reach far down, so the pose looks strained rather than relaxed.
- Woman and man: the hip and thigh form a large curved lump, so the lunge reads partly as a squat.
- Older man: his sleeve arm is very long and straight, like a stick, and the back of his vest bulges.
- Boy: there are loose lines and straps at the hoodie's waist, and the hood forms a bump behind his neck. These are busy and hard to make out at the small size.

**Row b**
- All four: the seedling is held out in the air with the root ball dangling under the hand. It looks like holding a lollipop or a balloon on a string rather than planting.
- All four: the two shoes stack at the far left and the back one sits slightly above the other, so the far foot looks like it floats.
- All four: there is a small triangular gap between the legs at the knee that looks like a hole in the trousers.
- Woman: her trousers are very wide and her thigh is long, so her legs look heavy next to her small top.
- Older man: his vest bulges at the back, the same as in row a.
- Boy: the hood bump behind his neck is a bit large.
- At the small size the brown root ball becomes a dark dot that could be read as a ball.

## 4. Choice

I would choose row b. Its kneeling pose is calmer, and its colourful seedling makes the action clear and friendly even at the small size. I am fairly sure.
