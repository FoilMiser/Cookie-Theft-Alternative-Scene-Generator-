# Review of pair_4

## 1. How the rows differ

Both rows use the same bodies, heads, clothes and forward-reaching arm. In row a, each figure carries a paper bag that hangs from the near hand at hip height, and the hand visibly grips the top of the bag. In row b, the near arm is gone. A tan box (drawn as two offset outlines) sits in front of the belly with no hand holding it, and the far arm reaches forward over or past it.

## 2. Per figure

**Woman in purple top**
- Row a: stands, reaching one open hand forward, other hand holding a brown paper bag by her side.
- Row b: stands, reaching one hand forward over a tan box that sits at her waist.
- Better: **a**. The bag is plainly gripped by a hand, but in row b the box floats against her stomach with nothing holding it.

**Older man in cap**
- Row a: stands slightly stooped, white-sleeved arm reaching forward, other hand gripping a brown bag at his hip.
- Row b: stands, white-sleeved arm reaching forward across a tan box held against his vest.
- Better: **a**. Both arms read clearly in row a, while row b's box looks pasted onto the vest.

**Bearded man in green**
- Row a: leans forward, one hand reaching down and forward, other hand holding a white bag by its top.
- Row b: leans forward, one arm reaching down and forward with flat fingers, a tan box at his hip.
- Better: **a**. Row a shows a clear carry, but in row b the arm sweeps across a floating box and the action is unclear.

**Woman in yellow dress**
- Row a: bends forward, one hand palm up reaching out, other hand holding a brown bag in front of her legs.
- Row b: bends forward, one arm reaching out past a tan box that floats at her hip.
- Better: **a**. Row a's two hands each have a clear job, while row b's box has no visible support and its colour runs into her skin.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Woman in purple: the bag hangs a little away from her body, so her lower arm angles out stiffly.
- Older man: the hand holding the bag comes out from under the vest at an awkward angle. The arm is partly hidden, so the arm-to-bag link is a little hard to follow.
- Bearded man in green: the white bag is pale against the pale background, and at small size it reads as a white blank shape more than a bag.
- Woman in yellow: the bag sits low in front of her dress and legs, so at small size it partly merges with the leg area.
- All: each bag is drawn as a plain trapezoid. It reads as a bag but has little detail.

### Row b
- All four: nothing holds the box. No hand or arm is under or around it, so it seems glued to the body or floating.
- All four: the box has a doubled, offset outline that looks like a drawing error (a ghosted second box) rather than a stack.
- Woman in purple and woman in yellow: the tan box is close in colour to the skin of the forearm crossing it. At small size, arm and box blend into one blob.
- Bearded man in green: the arm crosses in front of the box and ends in pointed, flat fingers. It looks like pointing rather than carrying or reaching, which makes the action unclear.
- Older man: the box sits over the vest and belt line, and the layering (sleeve over box over vest) is muddled at small size.
- At small size, it is hard to tell in all four what the figure is doing with the box.

## 4. Choice

**Row a**, fairly sure. Its bags are held by visible hands, and each figure's two arms have readable jobs. Row b's unsupported, double-outlined boxes look like mistakes and are hard to read at picture size.
