# Review of pair_1

## 1. How the rows differ

Bodies, heads, legs and the forward reaching arm are almost the same in both rows. In row a the far hand grips the neck of a tied sack that sits on top of a box hanging at hip height, so a brown jug-like shape stacks on the box. In row b there is no sack: a plain box is held higher, in front of the chest or waist, partly behind the forward arm, and the far hand is not seen.

## 2. Figure by figure

**Woman in purple top**
- Row a: stands upright; far hand on the neck of a sack resting on a box at her hip; near arm held straight forward at shoulder height, hand open.
- Row b: stands upright; box at waist or chest height in front of her body; near arm held straight forward over the top of the box, hand open.
- Better: **b**. It reads simply as "carrying a box and reaching out", where in a the sack-on-box stack looks like a vase and is hard to read.

**Older man in cap**
- Row a: stands upright; far hand on a sack on a box at his hip, close against the trousers; near arm straight forward, hand open.
- Row b: stands upright; box in front of his waist, under the forward arm; near arm straight forward, hand open.
- Better: **b**. In a the sack and box merge with his hip and cuff into a brown clump against the dark trousers; in b the box stands out cleanly.

**Bearded man in green**
- Row a: bent forward; far hand on a sack on a box at his hip; near arm reaching down and forward, fingers spread as if scattering.
- Row b: bent forward; box in front of his chest; near arm stiff and straight, angled down and forward, as if pointing.
- Better: **a**. The spread, low hand in a reads as a real action (scattering or giving out), while in b the stiff arm points past a box that seems to float.

**Woman in yellow dress**
- Row a: bent forward; far hand on a sack on a box at her hip, the box partly behind the edge of the dress; near arm forward and down, hand cupped palm up.
- Row b: bent forward; box in front of her chest; near arm straight down and forward, fingers pointing.
- Better: **b**. In a the box and sack crowd into the dress outline and the box is half hidden, while in b the box is clear of the dress.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- All four: the tied sack sitting on the box reads as a jug or vase on a crate; it is not clear what the object is.
- All four: the box hangs at the hip with nothing holding it except a grip on the sack neck, so its weight looks unsupported.
- All four, small size: the sack and box shrink to a muddy brown blob at the hip; the action is hard to make out.
- Older man: the sack, box and shirt cuff run together against the dark trousers.
- Woman in yellow dress: the box is half hidden behind the dress and its outline clashes with the skirt line.
- Woman in purple and older man: the forward arm reaching at shoulder height has no clear link to the sack the other hand holds.

### Row b
- All four: no hand is seen under or around the box, so it looks pinned to the body or floating.
- All four: the box sits behind the forward arm, so its top edge is cut by the arm and the box looks flat.
- Bearded man in green and woman in yellow dress: the arm is very stiff and straight with pointed fingers, reading as pointing rather than handing over.
- Bearded man in green and woman in yellow dress: the box floats in front of the chest away from the body while they bend, which looks odd.
- Small size: still readable as "person with a box", but the box is quite small and pale against the shirts for the purple woman and the yellow woman.

## 4. Choice

Row **b**, **fairly sure**. It is simpler and reads at the small size as a person carrying a box and reaching out; row a's sack-on-box stack is the bigger clarity problem, though row b should show a hand under the box.
