# Review of pair_30

## 1. How the rows differ

The upper bodies, hands and held objects are nearly the same in both rows. The difference is in the lap. Row a draws a short hem with the thighs angled back toward a seat, and the knees bend clearly. Row b draws a deep, wide, flat-bottomed block of skirt or dress with the lower legs coming straight out of it, so the lap reads as a box. Row b's heads and torsos also look a little larger.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Figure 2 (older woman): a small pale shape between the knees under the skirt is a little unclear.
- Figure 3 (girl): her near arm crosses the body with heavy outlines, so the arm and torso merge somewhat.
- Figures 1 and 4 (woman in yellow): the hem edge and the thigh line make a slightly busy area at the lap, though it still reads.
- At the small size, figure 3 (girl) is very small, and her face and cup are barely legible.

### Row b
- All four figures: the lap is a deep rectangular block with a flat bottom, so the figures look as if they are sitting in boxes, and the knee bend is hard to see.
- Figure 3 (girl): the legs look long and stiff below the block, which makes her look less like a child.
- Figure 2 (older woman): the skirt is oversized and heavy compared with her torso.
- At the small size the lap block reads as a seat or crate under the figure. Figure 3 (girl) is again hard to make out.

## 4. Choice

**Row a**, fairly sure. In row a the poses look relaxed and clearly seated. Row b's boxy laps look stiff and less friendly.
