# Review of pair 8

## 1. How the rows differ

I can find no clear difference between the two rows. They match in pose, proportions, colours, faces, books and line weight, at both the large and the small size. Any differences, such as the exact thickness of a leg or the length of a shirt on the walking figures, are too small for me to see with confidence.

Note: the brief says all four figures are seated. In both rows only figures 1 and 2 are seated. Figures 3 and 4 are drawn mid-stride, walking.

## 2. Figure by figure

1. **Man in red shirt (seated)**
   - Row a: sits upright with knees bent and feet flat, holding a red book open at chest height in both hands, eyes down on it.
   - Row b: the same.
   - Better: no difference. The two drawings look identical at both sizes.

2. **Bearded man in green (seated)**
   - Row a: sits upright, holding a red book open at chest height with both hands, looking down at it.
   - Row b: the same.
   - Better: no difference. I can see no change in pose, hands or face.

3. **Man in red shirt (again)**
   - Row a: walks in a long stride, leaning slightly forward. Both arms reach out to hold a large open book with white, lined pages, and he looks at it.
   - Row b: the same walking stride, with the book held out at arm's length.
   - Better: no difference. The stride, arm reach and book match.

4. **Bearded man in green (again)**
   - Row a: walks in a long stride, holding a large open book at arm's length and looking at it.
   - Row b: the same.
   - Better: no difference. Same pose, same book, same reading at small size.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Figures 3 and 4: drawn walking, not seated, so they do not match the seated figure they are meant to be. They look like someone reading while walking along.
- Figure 1: the red book sits on the red shirt. At small size the book almost disappears into the shirt, and the reading action is hard to see.
- Figures 1 and 2: the near forearm runs across the book's lower edge, so it is hard to tell which hand holds which cover.
- Figures 3 and 4: the arms are very long and straight, with the book held far out from the body. This looks stiff, and the forward reach is slightly awkward.
- Figures 1 and 2: the seated pose has nothing under it (as the brief says), so at small size the figures look like they are hovering. This is expected.

### Row b
- The same faults as row a, since the drawings look the same:
- Figures 3 and 4: walking, not seated.
- Figure 1: the red book is lost against the red shirt at small size.
- Figures 1 and 2: the forearm crossing the book makes the grip unclear.
- Figures 3 and 4: stiff, over-long arms holding the book far out.

## 4. Choice

For a friendly, clear cartoon style either row works equally well. I pick **a**, but only as a toss-up, because I cannot see a real difference between the rows.
