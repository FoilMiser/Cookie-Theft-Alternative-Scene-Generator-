# Review of pair_22

## 1. How the rows differ

The clear difference is the front view of the seated girl. In row a her fists are pulled in against her upper chest and her shoulders are boxy and raised. In row b her arms are spread out with bent elbows and fists up beside her head, in a "hooray" or muscle-flex pose. The other three figures look almost the same in both rows. Row b may show a little more neck and torso, but the difference is very small.

## 2. Figure by figure

1. **Girl in pink dress, seated, front.**
   - Row a: sits with both fists held against her upper chest; elbows tucked in; smiling with eyes closed.
   - Row b: sits with arms spread wide, elbows bent and fists raised at shoulder height; smiling with eyes closed.
   - Better: **b**. The raised arms stand clear of the body and read as a cheerful gesture at both sizes, while row a's fists merge into the pink dress and the pose looks tense or cramped.

2. **Same girl, seated, side.**
   - Row a: sits upright with her hands clasped on her knees, looking ahead.
   - Row b: the same pose: upright, hands on her knees.
   - Better: **no difference**. I cannot see any real difference between the two drawings.

3. **Woman in yellow dress, seated, front.**
   - Row a: holds an open red book in both hands at her chest and looks down at it.
   - Row b: the same pose with the open red book; her head may sit a hair higher.
   - Better: **no difference**. Both read the same, and any change is too small to matter.

4. **Older woman in skirt, seated, front.**
   - Row a: holds a pink ice-cream cone to her mouth with her right hand; her left arm hangs straight down by her side.
   - Row b: the same pose with the cone at her mouth and the other arm hanging.
   - Better: **no difference**. The two drawings are almost identical.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Girl, front: her fists press into the chest and merge with the pink dress, so it is hard to tell what the hands are doing, especially at the small size.
- Girl, front: her shoulders are square and hunched up close to the big head, so she has almost no neck and looks stiff.
- Girl, side: the seat of the dress is a hard, flat rectangle, so the bottom looks boxy.
- Woman in yellow: her forearms are very wide and her elbows stick out a lot, so the arms look heavy. Her head is tipped forward with the chin close to the neckline.
- Older woman: the hanging arm is very long, with the hand below the seat beside the knee. The cone covers her mouth and nose. At the small size the cone and face blur into one pink blob.

### Row b
- Girl, front: the raised-fist "flex" pose makes the figure much wider. It is a strong, specific gesture for a girl who is just sitting, and it could read as cheering or showing off rather than a neutral pose.
- Girl, front: her hands are small mitten shapes at the ends of thin arms, so they are a little crude at the large size.
- Girl, side: same boxy dress seat as in row a.
- Woman in yellow: same heavy forearms and wide elbows as in row a.
- Older woman: same over-long hanging arm, and the same cone hiding her face at the small size.

## 4. Choice

**Row b**, fairly sure. The only figure that really differs is the front view of the girl. In row b her arms are separate from her body and her gesture is easy to read, even at picture size. Row a's fists against the chest are unclear and look cramped. The other three figures are a tie.
