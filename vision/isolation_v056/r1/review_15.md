# Review of pair_15

## 1. How the rows differ

Heads, hands and held items (book, ice cream cone, two cups with straws) are almost the same in both rows. The difference is the lap. In row a the skirt or dress hem ends at a clean line above the knees, and the thighs and knees come forward clearly below it. In row b the hem drapes over the knees, the knees show as bumps under the cloth, and small slivers of leg or cloth show at the hem edge, so the lap reads as more crumpled.

## 2. Per figure

**1. Woman in yellow dress, seated (book)**
- Row a: seated, looking down at an open red book held in both hands; hem above knees, knees clear.
- Row b: same pose, reading the same book; hem lies over the knees, a small extra sliver of leg shows under the hem on the left.
- Better: **a**. The lap and knees read cleanly and nothing stray sits at the hem.

**2. Older woman in skirt, seated (ice cream)**
- Row a: seated, licking a pink ice cream cone held at her mouth; grey skirt ends above clear knees.
- Row b: same, cone held a little higher; skirt hem crosses the knees, with a small grey wedge showing under the right knee.
- Better: **a**. The skirt edge and knees are tidy, while b has a small odd wedge at the knee.

**3. Girl in pink dress, seated (cup)**
- Row a: seated, sipping through a straw from a red cup held in one hand; hem above the knees, legs bend clearly.
- Row b: same sip; hem falls lower and runs over the knees, so the thighs are mostly hidden and the legs look longer.
- Better: **a**. The bend at the knee is easier to see, which makes "seated" read faster at the small size.

**4. Woman in yellow dress again, seated (cup)**
- Row a: seated, drinking through a straw from a large red cup held at her chest; clean hem, clear knees.
- Row b: same; hem over the knees, a small loose shape shows between the hem and the right thigh.
- Better: **a**. The same lap issue as figure 1, with a stray piece at the hem in b.

## 3. Faults

**Row a**
- Figure 1: the hem is a very straight block edge, so the lap looks a little like a box.
- Figure 2: the skirt and seat area form a stiff flat shelf; it looks slightly wooden.
- Figure 3: the head is very large for the body, which is fine for a child but makes the cup arm look short; at the small size the face details are hard to make out.
- Figure 4: the cup is large and covers much of the chest and hand; the hand holding it is hard to read at the small size.

**Row b**
- Figure 1: a stray sliver of leg or cloth at the hem under the left knee; the knees show as lumps under the dress.
- Figure 2: a small grey wedge under the right knee looks like a drawing error; the skirt hem is uneven over the knees.
- Figure 3: the thighs are almost hidden, so the legs look long and straight, and the seated pose is weaker, especially small.
- Figure 4: a loose shape between the hem and the right thigh; the lap looks crumpled.
- All: at the small size the hem detail turns into muddle around the knees, where row a shows a clear bend.

## 4. Choice

Row **a**, fairly sure. It reads as cleaner and friendlier, with a clear seated bend at the knee. Row b's draped hem adds realism but leaves small stray shapes at the knees that look like mistakes.
