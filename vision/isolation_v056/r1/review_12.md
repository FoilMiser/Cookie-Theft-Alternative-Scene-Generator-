# Review of pair_12

## 1. How the rows differ

Every figure in both rows stands and points upward with the right arm. In row a the arm goes up and out on a diagonal toward the upper right, and the head turns that way. In row b the arm goes almost straight up beside the head, the finger is above the crown, and the head tips further back.

## 2. Figure by figure

**Older woman in a cardigan**
- Row a: standing, pointing up and to the right on a diagonal, looking up-right.
- Row b: standing, arm raised almost vertically, finger above her head, head tipped back.
- Better: **a**. The diagonal arm clearly points *at* something, while the vertical arm in b looks more like raising a hand to ask a question.

**Woman in a purple top**
- Row a: standing, pointing up-right on a long diagonal, looking up.
- Row b: standing, arm straight up beside the head, looking up.
- Better: **a**. The diagonal points in a readable direction and leaves the face and hair uncrowded. In b the upright arm presses against the side of her head.

**Girl in a pink dress**
- Row a: standing, pointing up-right, arm rising across the side of her face, looking up.
- Row b: standing, arm straight up against her cheek, head tipped well back, looking up.
- Better: **no difference**. In both rows the big head and the raised arm crowd each other. In a the hand sits near her eye, and in b the arm hugs her cheek.

**Boy in a red cap**
- Row a: standing, pointing up-right on a straight diagonal, looking up-right.
- Row b: standing, arm almost vertical beside the cap, head tipped back.
- Better: **a**. The pointing direction is clear at both sizes. In b it reads more like a raised hand, and the arm runs close to the cap brim.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Woman in purple top: the purple sleeve on the raised arm looks like a loose lump at the shoulder rather than a sleeve.
- Girl in pink dress: the raised arm passes close in front of the side of her face, and the hand nearly touches the eye area. It looks crowded at the large size.
- Girl in pink dress: the head is very large for the body. At the small size the figure reads as top-heavy, though it is still recognisable.
- Older woman: the face is turned so far that the glasses sit at the edge of the head. This is fine large but slightly blurry small.

### Row b
- All four: the near-vertical arm with one finger up reads as "raising a hand" or "I have a question" more than "pointing at something". This is most noticeable at the small size.
- Girl in pink dress: the arm is pressed against her cheek and the hand rises right beside the hair bun, so the head and arm merge into one shape.
- Woman in purple top: the same loose-lump sleeve at the shoulder, and the arm hugs the hair.
- Boy in red cap: the raised arm runs close to the cap brim, and at the small size the cap, hand and arm bunch together.
- Older woman: the head is tipped back so far that the glasses and face are foreshortened and harder to read at the small size.

## 4. Choice

**Row a**, fairly sure. Its diagonal arms make a clear, friendly pointing gesture with an obvious direction, and the arms stay farther from the heads. Row b's upright arms are ambiguous and crowd the faces.
