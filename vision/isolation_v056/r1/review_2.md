# Review of pair_2

## 1. How the rows differ

The two rows are close to identical: same poses, same books, same colours, same placement of hands and feet. I could not find a clear difference at either size. At most, row b's bodies look a touch fuller through the shirt and trousers. That could be my eye and not the drawing.

## 2. Figure by figure

**Figure 1: man in red shirt**
- Row a: seated with knees bent, reading an open red book held at chest height with both hands. Looks down at the book.
- Row b: the same. Seated, reading a red book at chest height.
- Better: no difference. The poses, hands and book look the same at both sizes.

**Figure 2: bearded man in green**
- Row a: seated, reading an open red book held at chest height with both hands. Eyes down on the book.
- Row b: the same pose and book.
- Better: no difference. I can see no change in pose, proportion or readability.

**Figure 3: man in red shirt again**
- Row a: walking or striding to the right, not seated, with both arms out holding a large open book (cream pages, red cover). Head turned toward the book.
- Row b: the same walking pose with the open book held out.
- Better: no difference. Both draw the same stride and grip, and the small versions read the same.

**Figure 4: bearded man in green again**
- Row a: walking or striding to the right with a large open book held out in both arms. Head pushed forward.
- Row b: the same walking pose and book.
- Better: no difference. The poses match, and neither is clearer at small size.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Figures 3 and 4: both are drawn walking with a long stride, not seated, which does not match "seated". They read as reading while walking.
- Figures 3 and 4: the near arm reaches stiffly straight out to the far edge of the book, and the far hand is hidden. The grip looks awkward, as if the book is pushed away instead of held to read.
- Figure 4: the head juts forward on a long, slanted neck, and the stance looks hunched.
- Figure 1: the near forearm lies flat across the stomach under the book, and the arm reads as a stiff tube. The far hand is a blob at the book's corner.
- Figures 1 and 2: at small size the red book merges into the red shirt (figure 1) and becomes a flat red block (both). It is hard to tell it is a book.
- Figures 1 and 2: with no seat drawn they float in a sitting pose. That is expected here, but the thighs are long and level, so the pose looks stiff.

### Row b
- Figures 3 and 4: both are drawn walking, not seated, the same mismatch as row a.
- Figures 3 and 4: the same stiff, straight near arm and hidden far hand on the book.
- Figure 4: the same forward-jutting head and long neck.
- Figure 1: the same stiff forearm across the stomach. At small size the red book is lost against the red shirt.
- Figure 2: at small size the red book is only a red block.

## 4. Choice

For a friendly, clear cartoon style I would pick **row b**, but only by a hair, for its slightly fuller body shapes. It is a **toss-up**: I can see no real difference between the rows. The faults that matter (figures 3 and 4 walking instead of seated, stiff book-holding arms, a red book on a red shirt) are the same in both rows.
