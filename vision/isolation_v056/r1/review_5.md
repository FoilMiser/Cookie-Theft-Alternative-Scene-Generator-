# Review of pair_5

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a, all four figures stretch both arms nearly straight up. The hands are rounded mitten shapes, a little apart, and the nearer hand grips a thin brown object whose top is cut off by the frame edge. In row b, the arms point up and forward at a steeper diagonal and the two hands meet palm to palm with outlined fingertips. Nothing is held in row b. The bodies and legs are almost the same in both rows, though the legs in row a look very slightly bent and leaned back.

## 2. Figure by figure

**Woman in yellow dress**
- a: both arms straight up, one fist gripping a small brown handle above her head.
- b: both arms angled up and forward, palms pressed together, empty-handed.
- Better: **a**. It reads as reaching up and holding something. Row b's joined hands look like a dive or a prayer stretch, with no clear purpose.

**Man in red shirt**
- a: arms straight up, one fist gripping a brown handle, the other hand open beside it.
- b: arms angled forward and up, hands together at the fingertips, nothing held.
- Better: **a**. Same reason: the grip on an object makes the action readable, and the mitten hands stay clean at small size.

**Woman in purple top**
- a: arms straight up, one fist holding a brown handle.
- b: arms angled forward and up, palms together, empty.
- Better: **a**. The pose is clearer, and row b's fingertip lines turn to scribble at the small size.

**Older man in cap**
- a: arms up in white sleeves, one fist holding a brown handle. The near arm's sleeve seems to start from a white lump on his chest.
- b: arms up and forward in white sleeves, hands together. He has the same white lump on his chest where the near arm starts.
- Better: **no difference**. Both rows have the same odd near arm that seems to grow from the chest. Row a's held object is slightly clearer, but row b's hands are equally hidden in the bright sleeves.

## 3. Faults

**Row a**
- All four: the held brown object is cut off at the top edge of the frame, so you cannot tell what it is (only a stub shows). At the small size it is just a brown speck.
- All four: the near upper arm crosses in front of the chin and mouth, partly hiding the face.
- All four: the legs look slightly bent and the body slightly leaned back, which gives a stiff, uneasy stance.
- Older man: the near white sleeve starts from a white blob on the chest instead of the shoulder, so the arm looks detached.
- Older man: one forearm is skin-coloured and the other white-sleeved, which reads as a mismatch.

**Row b**
- All four: the palms are pressed together with thin outlined fingertips. At the small size these turn to fuzzy scribbles at the tips.
- All four: the pose (arms forward and up, hands joined) is unclear. It could be diving, stretching or praying, and there is no object to explain it.
- All four: the near upper arm again crosses the chin and mouth.
- Older man: the same white blob on the chest where the near sleeve starts, so the arm looks attached to the chest.

## 4. Choice

**Row a**, fairly sure. Its mitten hands are simpler and stay readable at picture size, and the grip shows what the figure is doing. The held object needs to fit inside the frame, though.
