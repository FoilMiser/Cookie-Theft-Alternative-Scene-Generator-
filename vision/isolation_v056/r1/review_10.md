# Review of pair_10 (grey cat, ginger cat)

## 1. How the rows differ

Row a draws both cats flattened into a low crouch: the belly almost touches the ground and the legs fold up under the body. Row b raises the body on legs you can see. Each cat walks forward in a low prowl, with the hind legs planted, one front leg set down and the near front leg reaching far ahead. The heads, faces, ears, tails and colours are the same in both rows.

## 2. Figure by figure

**Grey cat (left)**
- Row a: crouched flat, body close to the ground, hind legs bunched under the haunch, front leg stretched along the ground.
- Row b: prowling forward on four visible legs, back sloping down to the head, near front paw reaching far ahead.
- Better: **b**. Row b shows four legs and a clear walking pose, while row a's folded legs read as lumps under a long flat body.

**Ginger cat (right)**
- Row a: the same flat crouch as the grey cat, legs folded under, front paw stretched forward.
- Row b: the same prowling walk, with the far legs drawn in a darker orange so near and far legs separate.
- Better: **b**. The darker far legs make all four legs easy to count, and the pose reads as a cat walking.

## 3. Faults

**Row a**
- Grey cat: the hind leg is a rounded blob with small stray crease marks, and you cannot tell which part is thigh and which is foot.
- Grey cat: the body is very long and flat, almost like a sausage, and the front leg barely separates from the chest.
- Ginger cat: the same blobby folded hind leg and stray crease marks. The far legs are only small stubs that peek out.
- Both: at the small size the legs merge into one dark lump under the body. The cats look like they are lying down or sliding rather than doing something clear.

**Row b**
- Grey cat: the far legs are the same grey as the near legs, so at the small size the four legs tangle a little.
- Grey cat: the reaching front leg is very long and bends smoothly with no clear elbow, so it looks a bit rubbery. The paw at its end is large and flat.
- Ginger cat: the same over-long, rubbery reaching front leg.
- Both: the hind legs are tall and straight-backed, which looks a little stiff. The pale chest patch sits as a sharp wedge between the front legs.

## 4. Choice

**Row b, fairly sure.** Its cats stand on readable legs in a clear prowling walk that stays legible at the small size. Row a's flat crouch turns the legs into shapeless lumps.
