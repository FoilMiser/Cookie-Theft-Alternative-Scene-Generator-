# Review of pair_9

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a every figure hangs its head far forward and presses a hand against its face, so the face is mostly hidden and only one eye and a nose/mouth smudge show. In row b the heads are more upright and turned toward the viewer: both eyes, eyebrows and a frowning mouth are visible, and the free hand sits at the mouth or chin instead of over the eye. The cone hand, clothes, legs and feet are almost the same in both rows. In row b the cone is a little bigger and shows a light inside where row a shows a dark brown inside.

## 2. Figure by figure

**Child in yellow top**
- Row a: head bowed low, hand pressed over the eye and cheek, holding out an empty cone (dark inside) with a pink blob at the rim.
- Row b: head slightly tilted, sad eyes and a down-turned mouth, fingers at the mouth, holding out an empty cone with a pink blob at the rim.
- Better: b. The sad face reads at once, while in row a the face is a jumble of hand, nose and mouth under the hair.

**Girl in pink dress**
- Row a: head bowed and turned away, hand over the eye, a long nose-like shape and a brown smudge show under the hair; she holds out the cone.
- Row b: head tilted, worried eyes and brows, frowning mouth with a brown smear, hand at the mouth; she holds out the cone.
- Better: b. In row a her face looks like a long hooked profile and the mood is hard to guess; in row b it is plainly sad.

**Boy in red cap**
- Row a: head bowed under the cap, hand on the forehead below the brim, one closed-looking eye, cone held out to the side.
- Row b: head turned toward the viewer, worried eyes, fingers at the mouth as if biting a nail, brown smear by the mouth, cone held out.
- Better: b. Row b shows a clear worried face; row a reads more as a headache or tiredness than as upset.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- All three: the face is almost fully hidden by the hand and the bowed head; at the small size there is no readable face at all, only hair and a hand.
- All three: the cone's dark brown inside reads as a hole or a cup of chocolate more than an empty cone; the pink blob is small and unclear.
- Child in yellow top: the brown nose/mouth/hand area runs together into one shape; the bowed head looks too heavy for the body.
- Girl in pink dress: the visible face part looks like a long hooked nose with a smudge under it, an odd profile.
- Boy in red cap: the hand on the forehead under the brim reads as headache or shading the eyes, not sadness; the head looks pushed down into the hood.

### Row b
- Child in yellow top and girl in pink dress: the hand at the mouth is a small rounded blob with lines, hard to read as fingers at the large size.
- Girl in pink dress: a small pink-brown mark on the arm near the shoulder looks like a stray patch or a stain.
- Child in yellow top: a brown mark at the elbow of the raised arm looks like a stray patch.
- Girl in pink dress and boy in red cap: the brown smear by the mouth can read as a moustache, especially on the boy.
- Boy in red cap: the hoodie collar and raised arm crowd around the chin, so the neck and shoulder are muddled.
- All three, small size: the faces are tiny, but eyes and frown still read. The cone is the clearest part.

## 4. Choice

Row b. It shows open, readable sad and worried faces that suit a friendly, clear cartoon style, and it still reads at the small size. Fairly sure.
