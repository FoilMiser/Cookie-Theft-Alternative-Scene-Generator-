# Review of pair_27

## 1. How the rows differ

Both rows show the same three figures standing, each holding an ice-cream cone tipped down in the hand nearest the viewer's left, with a pink blob inside the cone. In row a each figure faces the viewer with a worried face and one hand raised to the mouth. In row b each figure bows the head and covers the eyes with the raised hand, so the face is mostly hidden; the cone's inside is dark brown rather than light tan.

## 2. Figure by figure

**Child in yellow top (left)**
- Row a: stands facing us, frowning, hand to mouth, holding a tipped empty cone out to the side.
- Row b: head bowed, hand over eyes as if crying, holding a tipped cone with a dark inside.
- Better: **a**. The frowning face reads clearly as upset and stays friendly, while in b the face is lost behind the hand and a brown smudge under the hand is hard to read.

**Girl in pink dress (middle)**
- Row a: stands facing us, worried eyebrows, hand to mouth, brown mark at the mouth, holding a tipped cone.
- Row b: head bowed, hand pressed over eyes and nose, brown mark under the hand, holding a tipped cone.
- Better: **a**. Her worried eyes and brows are visible and easy to read, while in b the oversized bowed head with the round bun on top looks odd and the face is hidden.

**Boy in red cap (right)**
- Row a: stands facing us, hand curled at chin, small mouth that looks almost like a smile, brown mark under the nose, holding a tipped cone.
- Row b: head bowed under the cap, arm raised with hand over eyes, holding a tipped cone.
- Better: **b**. The covered-eyes gesture reads clearly as upset, while in a the near-smiling mouth and moustache-like brown mark make his feeling unclear.

## 3. Faults

**Row a**
- All three: the pink blob inside the cone looks like a thumb or fingertip rather than ice cream, so it is unclear whether the cone is empty or what was lost.
- All three: the arm holding the cone is foreshortened toward the viewer and the hand is hidden behind the cone, so at small size the cone seems to float beside the body.
- Girl and boy: a brown mark at the mouth reads like a moustache, not like a smear of ice cream.
- Boy: mouth looks like a slight smile, which clashes with the worried hand-to-chin pose.
- Child: hand at the mouth covers part of the face; at small size the frown is barely visible.
- Small size: the faces become tiny and the sad expressions are hard to make out.

**Row b**
- All three: the face is almost fully hidden by the hand and bowed head, losing the eyes and expression that make the style friendly.
- All three: the dark brown inside of the cone reads as a hole or as chocolate; with the pink blob it is unclear what is in the cone.
- All three: the cone hand is again hidden behind the cone, so the cone seems to float at small size.
- Child and girl: a brown smudge under the hand looks like a moustache or a dirty mark.
- Girl: the head looks oversized when bowed, and the round hair bun on top reads as a flat circle stuck on the scalp.
- Boy: the arm across the face is long and the cap is tipped far forward, making the head shape crowded.
- Small size: the gesture reads as crying, but the figures look more distressed than friendly.

## 4. Choice

Row **a**, fairly sure. Its visible faces suit a friendly, clear cartoon style better, though the boy reads better in row b and both rows share the unclear cone contents.
