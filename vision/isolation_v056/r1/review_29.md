# Review of pair_29

## 1. How the rows differ

The two children differ the most. In row a each child holds one arm straight out in front at shoulder height with a flat hand, and the other arm is bent and mostly hidden. In row b both of the child's hands are drawn up at chest and chin height: one is a loose fist and the other is a flat hand just in front of the mouth. The two men reading books are almost the same in both rows. Row b draws them a little larger, and the bearded man sits a little more upright.

## 2. Figure by figure

**Girl in pink dress (raised end)**
- Row a: sitting with legs hanging and toes pointing down; one arm stretched straight forward, flat hand, like pointing or reaching.
- Row b: same legs; both hands drawn up near the chest and chin, one fist and one flat hand under the mouth.
- Better: b. Hands held close in front of the body read more like gripping a seesaw handle than a stiff, pointing arm does, though the hand by the mouth is a little odd.

**Child in yellow top (raised end)**
- Row a: same pose as the girl; one arm straight out with a flat hand, other arm tucked; open mouth.
- Row b: same pose as the girl in b; fist at the chest, flat hand in front of the mouth, open mouth.
- Better: b. Same reason as the girl: the hands-in pose looks like holding on, while the straight arm looks like a sleepwalker or someone pointing.

**Older man in cap (low end)**
- Row a: sitting with knees up and feet forward, holding an open book in both hands and reading it.
- Row b: the same pose, drawn very slightly larger.
- Better: no difference. The two drawings are the same apart from the size.

**Bearded man in green (low end)**
- Row a: sitting with knees up, holding an open book up and reading; his torso leans forward and looks a little bent at the hip.
- Row b: the same pose, with the torso a little more upright and the book held a little lower.
- Better: no difference. The change is small, and both drawings read the same way at the small size.

## 3. Faults

**Row a**
- Girl in pink: the straight arm with a flat hand reads as pointing at something or reaching like a sleepwalker, not as holding on. At the small size it looks like a pointing gesture.
- Girl in pink: the other arm is mostly hidden behind the body, so only one hand can be made out.
- Child in yellow: the same straight-arm pointing look, and only one hand can be seen.
- Older man in cap: he is reading a book while seated on a seesaw, which is an unclear action for a seesaw (the same in row b).
- Bearded man in green: the torso looks slightly bent at the hip, and he is also reading a book on a seesaw.

**Row b**
- Girl in pink: the flat hand right in front of the mouth reads as covering the mouth, gasping or being shy, especially together with the open "o" mouth. At the small size the hands blur together near the face.
- Child in yellow: the same hand-at-mouth look; at the small size it looks like hands up to the face.
- Older man in cap: he is reading a book on a seesaw, which is unclear as a seesaw action.
- Bearded man in green: the same book-reading point. Otherwise no problems.

## 4. Choice

Row b. The children's hands held close in front of the body look more like riding and holding on, and they look friendlier than the stiff, straight pointing arm in row a. The hand in front of the mouth keeps it from being a clear win. Confidence: fairly sure.
