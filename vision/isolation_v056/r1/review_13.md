# Review of pair_13

## 1. How the rows differ

Only the arms of the two children change. In row a each child holds one forearm flat across the face at mouth height, with the other fist at the chest. In row b each child stretches one arm straight forward at shoulder height, with the other fist low by the hip. The two seated men, reading an open book with knees drawn up, look almost the same in both rows. The men in row b sit a little more upright, with the thigh closer to level.

## 2. Figure by figure

**Girl in pink dress (raised end)**
- Row a: sits in the air with legs hanging, toes pointing down. One forearm is held flat just under the nose, with the hand at the open mouth. The other fist is at the chest.
- Row b: same seated, hanging-leg pose. One arm is held straight out in front at shoulder height, with an open, pointed hand. The other fist is down by the hip.
- Better: **b**. A straight-out arm reads as holding on or reaching forward, while the arm across the face looks like covering a yawn, shouting or blowing, and hides part of the face.

**Child in yellow top (raised end)**
- Row a: same as the girl. One forearm is flat at the mouth, the other fist is at the chest, and the legs hang with blue shoes pointing down.
- Row b: same as the girl. One arm is straight forward, the other fist is at the hip, and the legs hang.
- Better: **b**. For the same reason, the outline is open and easy to read, and at the small size the arm still shows as a clear line rather than a lump at the face.

**Older man in cap (low end)**
- Row a: sits low with knees up and feet planted far forward, reading an open book held in front of the face.
- Row b: the same, with the thigh a little more level and the back a little straighter.
- Better: **no difference**. The change is tiny and both read the same, large and small.

**Bearded man in green (low end)**
- Row a: sits low with knees up, reading an open book held above the knee.
- Row b: the same, with a slightly more level thigh.
- Better: **no difference**. The two are nearly identical at both sizes.

## 3. Faults

**Row a**
- Girl in pink: the forearm across the mouth with the open mouth reads as yawning, shouting or blowing, not riding a seesaw. The arm covers the lower face.
- Girl in pink: at the small size the raised hand merges with the face into a blob, so you cannot tell what the arm is doing.
- Child in yellow: the same arm-at-mouth fault as the girl, and the same blob at the small size.
- Both children: the fist at the chest plus the arm at the face make a cramped, huddled outline, almost like a boxing guard.
- Both children: the feet point steeply down and backward, which looks like falling or dangling rather than sitting. The same is true in row b.
- Older man in cap: he reads a book while on a seesaw, which is an odd activity for the scene. The near hand is hidden behind the book, and the knee-up pose with feet far out reads as sitting on the ground. The same is true in row b.
- Bearded man in green: the same book and ground-sitting look. The far arm comes from behind the book with an unclear join at the shoulder.

**Row b**
- Girl in pink: the outstretched arm is long and thin, with a pointed, blade-like hand, so it looks a little stiff, like pointing. The second fist by the hip is partly hidden and hard to read.
- Child in yellow: the same long, stiff arm and half-hidden fist at the hip.
- Both children: the feet point steeply down, which looks like dangling.
- Both children: the open mouth with no clear expression looks startled rather than happy.
- Older man in cap: the same book-reading-on-a-seesaw oddity, hidden near hand, and ground-sitting look as in row a.
- Bearded man in green: the same as row a. The book covers the hands and chest, so at the small size the upper body is mostly book.

## 4. Choice

**Row b**, fairly sure. The children's straight-forward arms give a clear, open outline that reads as holding on, and it stays readable at the small size. Row a's arm-at-mouth pose is confusing and hides the face. The men are equal in both rows.
