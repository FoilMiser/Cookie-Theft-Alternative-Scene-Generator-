# Review of pair_4 (rows a and b)

## 1. How the rows differ

The main difference is the front-facing seated girl. In row a her arms are raised and bent outward with fists up at shoulder height. In row b her fists are held together in front of her chest. The other three figures are close to identical. Row b's seated adults have slightly longer, bulkier torsos and wider, boxier hips and skirts, and row b's side-view girl has a slightly more upright back.

## 2. Figure by figure

**Figure 1: girl in pink dress, seated, front**
- Row a: sitting with knees apart, both arms bent up and out with fists at shoulder height (a cheer or flex), smiling with eyes closed.
- Row b: sitting with knees apart, both fists held up in front of her chest with elbows tucked in, smiling with eyes closed.
- Better: **a**. The raised arms make an open, readable silhouette that still reads as a cheer at the small size. Row b's fists sit on top of the dress and merge into the body.

**Figure 2: same girl, seated, side**
- Row a: sitting upright in profile with both hands resting on her knees and feet flat.
- Row b: the same pose with a slightly straighter, more vertical back.
- Better: **no difference**. The pose and the outline are nearly the same, and both read the same way at the small size.

**Figure 3: woman in yellow dress, seated, front**
- Row a: sitting with knees together, holding an open red book at her chest with both hands and looking down at it.
- Row b: the same pose, with a slightly fuller torso and a wider, squarer skirt at the hips.
- Better: **no difference**. Both read clearly as reading a book. Row b's boxier hips and row a's narrower waist trade off evenly.

**Figure 4: older woman in skirt, seated, front**
- Row a: sitting and holding a pink ice-cream cone up to her mouth with one hand, other arm hanging straight down at her side.
- Row b: the same pose with a slightly longer, bulkier cardigan and a wider skirt.
- Better: **no difference**. The action is equally clear in both, and both share the same faults.

## 3. Faults

**Row a**
- Figure 1: the arms come straight out from the shoulders with oversized round fists, so it looks a little stiff, like a mechanical "flex".
- Figure 3: the waist is pinched and the hips are narrow, and the shins look very long next to the short torso.
- Figure 4: the ice cream covers her mouth and nose, so her face is hard to make out at the small size.
- Figure 4: the hanging arm is long, and its hand dangles beside the seat below hip level. That looks odd when no seat is drawn.
- Figure 4: her cardigan is short, so her torso looks cramped between the head and the lap.

**Row b**
- Figure 1: the shoulders are flat and square, so the top of the dress looks like a box.
- Figure 1: the fists overlap the chest and the forearms cross the dress, so at the small size the arms blur into the body and the gesture is unclear.
- Figure 2: the skirt and seat make a hard rectangular block with a sharp back corner, slightly more than in row a.
- Figure 3: the skirt flares into a wide, square block at the hips, which looks stiff.
- Figure 4: the ice cream still covers her mouth and nose, and the long hanging arm with its dangling hand is the same as in row a.
- Figure 4: the body is bulky and wide at the hips, and the cardigan and skirt read as one heavy block.

## 4. Choice

I would choose **row a** for a friendly, clear cartoon style, and I am **fairly sure**. Only figure 1 really differs. There, row a's open, raised-arm cheer reads clearly at both sizes, while row b's hands-at-chest pose crowds into the body. The other three figures are a wash.
