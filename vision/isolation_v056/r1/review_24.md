# Review of pair_24

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a every figure has a plain brown box (or carton) sitting at waist or chest height in front of the body, with no hand visibly holding it, and the near arm reaches forward. In row b the near arm still reaches forward, but the far arm hangs down and the hand grips the gathered neck of a brown paper sack held low at the hip. The bodies, faces, clothes and stance are the same in both rows.

## 2. Figure by figure

**Woman in purple top**
- Row a: standing upright, box at belly height in front of her, near arm straight out with the hand open.
- Row b: standing upright, near arm straight out, far hand holding a paper sack by its top at hip level.
- Better: b. Her hand is seen gripping the sack, so it is clear she is carrying it; in a the box floats with no hand on it.

**Older man in cap**
- Row a: slight stoop, box at waist in front of the vest, near arm (cream sleeve) out level with an open hand.
- Row b: slight stoop, near arm out level, far hand at the hip holding a sack by its neck, cream cuff visible.
- Better: b. The cuff and hand on the sack show the hold; in a the box looks pasted onto the vest.

**Bearded man in green**
- Row a: leaning forward, box at waist, near arm angled down and forward with a flat, pointing hand.
- Row b: leaning forward, near arm angled down with a cupped hand, far hand holding a sack low by the hip.
- Better: b. The cupped reaching hand and the gripped sack read as an action (offering or reaching); the flat pointing hand and floating box in a read less clearly.

**Woman in yellow dress**
- Row a: bent forward, box at chest height in front of the dress, near arm angled down with a flat hand.
- Row b: bent forward, near arm angled down with a cupped hand, sack at hip level partly overlapping the skirt edge, holding hand mostly hidden.
- Better: a. In b the sack merges with the skirt and the grip is hard to see, so the box at the chest in a is the clearer of the two.

## 3. Faults

**Row a**
- All four figures: the box has no visible hand holding it; it floats in front of the body or looks stuck on.
- All four figures: the box is drawn as two stacked flat shapes with a jagged top, which reads oddly (half box, half bag).
- Bearded man in green and woman in yellow dress: the near hand is a flat, pointed shape that looks like pointing rather than holding or offering.
- Older man in cap: box overlaps the vest and belt so it looks glued to his stomach.
- Small size: the box is a plain tan block with nothing to show what it is or that it is carried.

**Row b**
- All four figures: the sack's gathered top and the hand on it make a vase or jar shape; at small size it can read as a pot or bottle rather than a bag.
- Woman in yellow dress: the sack overlaps the skirt edge and her holding hand is mostly hidden, so it is unclear who or what holds it; the sack and dress blur together at small size.
- Bearded man in green: the sack sits against his thigh in the same tan as his trousers, so it is low in contrast at small size.
- Woman in purple top and older man in cap: the outstretched near arm has nothing to do; with the sack held at the side the reaching arm looks unexplained.

## 4. Choice

Row b, fairly sure. It shows each figure actually gripping what they carry, which is clearer and friendlier, though the sack shape and the yellow-dress overlap need tidying.
