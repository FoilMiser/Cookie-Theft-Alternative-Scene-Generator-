# pic_115

## Free description
A playground on a sunny day, fenced in with railings, with a slide, a seesaw, a tree and a bin. On the left a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is sliding down the red-and-yellow slide with one arm raised, looking happy. On the right there is a seesaw: an older man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat sits on the low end reading a newspaper, while a small girl in a pink outfit sits on the high end, stuck up in the air with her feet dangling. Because the heavy man is just sitting and reading, the seesaw is not moving and the girl is left hanging; she looks surprised or unhappy. It is odd to see a grown man reading the paper on a seesaw instead of playing.
