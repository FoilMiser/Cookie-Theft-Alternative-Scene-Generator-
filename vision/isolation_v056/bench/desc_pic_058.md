# pic_058

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path running down the right side. In the middle a woman in a yellow dress is hanging wet laundry on a clothesline strung between two posts: blue jeans and a white T-shirt are already pegged up and dripping water, and a basket of more clothes sits on the grass below. In the front left an old grey-haired woman in a teal cardigan sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. Right beside her on the bench a blue bird is pecking at a sandwich she has left there, and she does not seem to have noticed. On the path at the right a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds the strings of a red and a yellow balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other; a purple balloon has got away and is floating up into the sky, and he looks upset. A birdhouse on a tall pole stands by the fence, a flower bed is in front of the house, and a teal watering can sits on the lawn. The sandwich is about to be eaten by the bird, and the purple balloon is about to be lost for good.
