# pic_065

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a wooden fence with a gate on the right, and a paved path. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands in the middle holding a yellow and a blue balloon in one hand while reaching up with the other towards a red balloon that is floating away above him; its string ends in mid-air. A girl in a pink dress on the right is jumping in the air with both arms raised, looking happy. In the tree behind the house, a goldfish (an orange fish) is sitting on a branch, which is impossible - fish don't live in trees. There is a flowerbed, a wooden bench at front left, a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can in the front.
