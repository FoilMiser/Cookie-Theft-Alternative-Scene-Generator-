## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a gate on the left and a cream house with a blue door on the right. A man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers stands at the fence painting it pink with a long-handled paint roller; a section of fence on the left is already pink. On the ground next to him are an open pink paint tin and a paint tray with a roller. A white dog with brown spots is walking away from the paint tray toward the right, leaving a trail of pink paw prints on the grass — it looks like the dog stepped in the paint. On the left, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, sweeps dead leaves on a paved path with a yellow broom. In the foreground are a teal watering can and an empty red wheelbarrow, and on the right a hose reel with a green hose lies near a round flower bed. The dog's pink footprints are the funny "something went wrong" detail.
