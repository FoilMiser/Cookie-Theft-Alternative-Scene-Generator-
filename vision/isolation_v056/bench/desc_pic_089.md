## Free description
A back garden under a grey, overcast sky with three dark storm clouds. A house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, with a flower bed and a wooden bench in front of it; a wooden fence, a gate and a birdhouse on a pole are at the back right. In the centre a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands pointing up at the dark clouds with a worried or puzzled look, as if saying it's going to rain. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping a pile of leaves on the path with a yellow broom, looking down at her work and not at the sky. A swing set with one empty swing stands in the middle, and a teal watering can sits on the grass. It looks like rain is about to start.
