# pic_018

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence with a gate on the right, and a stone path. A boy in a yellow top and orange shorts holds a yellow and a blue balloon in one hand while reaching up with the other, looking up at a red balloon that has escaped and is floating away above the fence with its string trailing. A girl in a pink dress is jumping in the air with both arms raised; her shadow is on the ground below her. Strangely, an orange goldfish is sitting on a branch of the tree behind the house, which is impossible. There is a flower bed of colourful flowers, a wooden bench, a red wheelbarrow and a teal watering can on the lawn.
