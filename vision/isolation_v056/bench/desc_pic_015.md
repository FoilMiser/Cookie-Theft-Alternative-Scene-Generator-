# pic_015

## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, trees, a pond on the left and a winding sandy path on the right. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands near the front, right of centre, holding up a reel and flying an orange diamond kite with a bow tail high in the sky. The kite string runs from his reel up past a tall black lamp post. On the right, a garden sprinkler fed by a hose is spraying arcs of water, and the water lands on a cardboard box sitting on the edge of the path; the box has drips on it and a small puddle underneath, so it is getting soaked. In the front, a red wheelbarrow stands in the middle of the grass, and on the left there is a bed of colourful flowers and a green rubbish bin. The wheelbarrow's wheel looks like a dark square rather than a round wheel, which seems odd.
