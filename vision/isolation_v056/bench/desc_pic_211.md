# pic_211

## Free description
A sunny playground with a dark metal railing fence across the back, a tree on the right and a bush on the left. On the right front, a seesaw is tipped down on the left: an old man in a grey cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat sits on the low end reading a newspaper, while a small girl in pink sits high up on the raised right end, gripping the handle with one hand and holding the other to her mouth, looking worried or surprised. She seems stuck in the air because the man is heavier and absorbed in his paper. On the left, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts slides down a red-and-yellow slide with an arm stretched out, looking cheerful. A green rubbish bin stands by the fence and a grey rock lies in the front left corner.
