## Free description
A busy, sunny back garden with a cream house on the right, a wooden fence at the back, a gate at the back left and a paved path on the left. On the left path a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers is walking while reading an open book, and there is a cardboard box on the ground right in front of him, so he looks about to trip over it. Behind him a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. In the middle an old woman in a teal cardigan is watering with a hose, but the hose is leaking at the reel end (a little puddle) and a brown dog is standing on the hose, which may be blocking the water. At the back right a little girl in a pink dress is reaching up toward a blue-and-white beach ball that is stuck in the tree. On the far right a man in a red T-shirt sits on a bench reading a red book. There is also a red wheelbarrow and a flower bed.
