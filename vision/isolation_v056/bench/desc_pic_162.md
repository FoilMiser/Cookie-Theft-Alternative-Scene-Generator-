A girl in a pink dress points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of a tree. On the far right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps a pile of leaves with a broom, and an older woman with glasses and a yellow shopping bag walks past at the back right. A red bicycle stands in the front centre, but it has square wheels, which is impossible.
