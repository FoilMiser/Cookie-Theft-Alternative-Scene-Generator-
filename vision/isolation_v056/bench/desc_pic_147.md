## Free description
A sunny town square paved with grey tiles, in front of a row of colourful shop buildings with striped awnings. On the left a woman in a yellow dress holds a camera up to her eye with both hands, with its red strap around her neck, taking a photo. In the middle a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts has just thrown a red ball, which is in the air (a dotted line shows its path) heading towards a small girl in a pink dress on the right. The girl stands with her arms held out ready to catch it. Behind them there is a fruit stall with crates of green, yellow, orange and red fruit under a striped canopy, a black street lamp, a tree in a round planter and a green bin. A potted plant stands in the bottom left corner. The girl is about to catch (or miss) the ball.
