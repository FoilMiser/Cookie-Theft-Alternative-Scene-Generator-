The house is on the right (red roof, blue door), a wooden fence with a gate runs along the back, and a paved path goes down the left side. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands on the grass in front, a flower bed sits in front of the house, and a coiled garden hose on a reel lies on the grass at the right. Two potted plants and a couple of bushes are by the fence. It looks like it is about to rain.
