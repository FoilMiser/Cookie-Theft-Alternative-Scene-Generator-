## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house on the left, a wooden fence at the back, a gate at the back right and a paved path on the right. In the middle, under a washing line with blue jeans and a yellow striped towel on it, an old man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is raking up a small pile of leaves. In the front, a garden sprinkler attached to a long hose is spraying arcs of water, and the water is landing on a cardboard box standing on the grass, which is getting wet and sitting in a puddle. Next to the box is a grey wheelbarrow, and between them there is a strange black square frame with a cross inside it that looks like a square wheel. There are two potted plants by the fence and a flower bed by the house. There are no animals.
