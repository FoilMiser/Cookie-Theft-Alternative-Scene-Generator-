# pic_210

## Free description
A busy, sunny park with a pond, a lamp post, hills, trees, a bench and a winding sand path. On the left, an older man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat waters a flower bed by pouring water out of a frying pan. At the pond, a white duck swims towards some crumbs that a girl in a pink dress is tossing from a paper bag. In the middle, a brown dog sits upright holding and reading a newspaper, which is impossible. Behind, a white goat tied to a stake is grazing, and a grey-haired grandmother in a teal cardigan sweeps a pile of leaves with a broom. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie holds a kite spool, but his yellow kite is caught in the big tree. On the path, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and is crying or upset, because the pink ice cream has fallen on the path and a white spotted dog is licking it up. A blue bird flies in the sky, and there is a grey rock and a green bin in the front.
