# pic_020

## Free description
A busy sunny park with a pond on the left, a lamp post in the middle, a bench and a winding path on the right, and hills behind. An older man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat at front left is pouring water onto a flower bed, but he is using a frying pan, not a watering can, which looks odd. A girl in a pink dress near the pond is throwing bread pieces to a white duck swimming in the pond. A brown dog sits up near the centre holding a newspaper as if reading it, which is impossible/silly. Behind, a white goat tied to a short stake is eating grass, and an elderly woman with grey hair is sweeping leaves with a broom near the path. On the path, a white spotted dog licks a pink ice cream that has fallen on the ground, while a boy in a yellow shirt beside it holds an empty cone and rubs his eye as if crying. On the right a boy in a blue hoodie and red cap holds a reel, flying a yellow kite that is caught in the branches of a tree. A blue bird flies in the sky, and there is a bin at bottom left and a grey rock at the bottom.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Frying pan used for watering: clearly odd; could be intended as a mistake to spot.
- Dog reading a newspaper: impossible; reads as a deliberate absurdity.
- Kite: either stuck in the tree, or just flying in front of it; the string and leaves overlapping suggests it is caught.
- The goat's tether is short and the stake is tiny; the goat seems loosely tied.
- The man's water stream flows from the pan in a smooth arc as if from a spout, which is unlike how a pan would pour.
