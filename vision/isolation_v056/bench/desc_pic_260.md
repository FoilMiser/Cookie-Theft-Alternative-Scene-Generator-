# pic_260

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house, a blue door, a wooden fence, a gate and a paved path. In the centre a woman in a yellow dress is reaching up to peg washing on a line; the blue jeans and white T-shirt already hanging are dripping water, and a basket of more laundry sits at her feet. At the bottom left an older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan, sits on a bench reading a red book, while a blue bird on the bench beside her is pecking at her sandwich without her noticing. On the right a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts holds the strings of a red and a yellow balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other towards a purple balloon that has escaped and is floating away into the sky; he looks upset. There is also a birdhouse on a tall pole, a teal watering can on the grass and a flower bed.
