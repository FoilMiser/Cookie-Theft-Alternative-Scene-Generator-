# pic_257

## Free description
A sunny playground with a railing fence, a tree, swings and a slide. On the left is a red seesaw: a small girl in pink sits on the raised high end, holding the handle and putting a hand to her mouth, looking worried or stuck. An old man in a cap and brown waistcoat sits on the low end, which is resting on the ground, and he is reading a newspaper, apparently ignoring her - so she is stuck up in the air. On the right, a boy in a yellow shirt kneels in a sandbox building a sandcastle with a small red flag on top, holding a red spade. The swing set in the middle has one empty red swing. There is also a green bin by the fence, a bush and a grey rock.
