## Free description
A sunny playground with a dark railing fence across the back. On the left is a red seesaw: a small girl in pink sits on the high end, raised up in the air, holding the handle with her hand to her mouth and looking worried. An old man in a grey flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat sits on the low end on the ground, reading a newspaper, seemingly unaware of the girl, so she is stuck up in the air. In the centre is a swing frame with one empty red swing. On the right are an empty slide and a green litter bin. In the front right a boy with dark curly hair in a yellow shirt and orange shorts kneels in a sandpit building a sandcastle topped with a small red flag, holding a red spade. A tree stands at the far left and a grey rock and a bush on the right.
