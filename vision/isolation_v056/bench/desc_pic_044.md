# pic_044

## Free description
A back garden under a grey sky with dark storm clouds overhead. A house with a blue door is on the left, a wooden fence runs across the back, a gate and paved path are on the right, and a birdhouse on a pole stands in the middle-right. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence blue with a paint roller; about three planks are already blue, and a paint tin sits on the grass by her feet. An older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, stands to the right pointing up at the sky, apparently warning that rain is coming. The dark clouds suggest it is about to rain, which would spoil the fresh paint. A small white puppy is curled up asleep on the grass in front. A wheelbarrow and a watering can sit on the lawn, and there is a flower bed by the house.
