A back garden on a gloomy day: the sky is grey and three dark storm clouds hang overhead, so it looks like it is about to rain. He does not seem to have noticed the clouds, and rain would spoil the fresh paint. A small white dog with brown ears lies curled up asleep on the lawn in the front. A red wheelbarrow stands in the front, and a flower bed sits in front of the cream house with the blue door on the right; a gate and path are on the left.
