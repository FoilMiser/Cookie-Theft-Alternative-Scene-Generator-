# pic_265

## Free description
A sunny park with a pond, rolling hills, trees, a lamppost, a bench and a winding path. On the left, a woman in a yellow dress waters a flowerbed by pouring water from a frying pan, which is an odd thing to use. Next to the pond, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers holds a paper bag and sprinkles seeds onto the ground for a red bird that is pecking at them. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts runs through the middle holding a pink ice-cream cone, heading right towards the bird and the seeds - he might step on or scare the bird. In the background a girl in a pink dress holds a red and a purple balloon and reaches up after a blue balloon floating away into the sky. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks along the path carrying a yellow shopping bag. The bench is empty, and there is a green bin at bottom left.
