## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the left, a swing set, a washing line, a flower bed and a cream house with a blue door on the right. In the middle, a woman with dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers is holding a garden hose up and peering into the yellow nozzle, as if wondering why no water is coming out. The hose runs across the lawn to a hose reel by the house, and a brown dog with a red collar is standing with a paw right on the hose, which would block the water. There is a small drip/puddle of water at the hose reel. It looks as if, when the dog steps off, the water will spray straight into the woman's face. On the right, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans sits on a wooden bench reading a red book, not paying attention. In the front, a small white puppy with brown spots is crouched in a playful bow next to a yellow-green tennis ball, wagging its tail. A yellow striped towel and a pair of blue jeans hang on the washing line, and the swing is empty.
