# pic_184

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left, a wooden fence, a gate and a path on the right. In the centre a woman in a yellow dress is pegging wet laundry on a washing line between two posts: blue jeans and a white T-shirt are hanging and dripping water, and a basket of more clothes sits on the grass beside her. On the left, an old woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a bench reading a red book; next to her on the bench a blue bird is pecking at her sandwich, which she has not noticed. On the right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands on the path holding the strings of a red and a yellow balloon in one hand while reaching up with the other; a purple balloon has got away and is floating up into the sky. There is a bird house on a pole, a flower bed and a teal watering can on the grass.
