A cardboard box is on the grass just in front of and slightly to the left of his path, so he may trip over it. On the right an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, teal cardigan and grey skirt digs with a spade, with a mound of dirt beside the hole. Nothing looks physically impossible.
