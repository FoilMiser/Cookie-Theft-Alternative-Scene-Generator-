# pic_224

## Free description
A sunny but windy back garden (wind lines in the sky and over the fence). A man in a green shirt stands in the middle reaching up with a surprised look as his red cap blows away in the wind along with a few leaves. On the right by the house's blue door, a brown dog sits holding its red leash in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. A small white puppy lies asleep (curled up) on the grass in front. There is a flower bed, two potted plants, a bush, a teal watering can, a red wheelbarrow, a path and a gate.
