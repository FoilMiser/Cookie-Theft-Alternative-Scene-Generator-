## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, a gate and paved path on the right, a swing set and a birdhouse on a pole. By the house door at the far left, a brown dog with a red collar is sitting up holding the end of a yellow garden hose in its mouth, as if it has picked up or is chewing the hose. In front of a flower bed, a man (or boy) with brown hair in a red shirt and blue jeans is holding a teal watering can, and water comes out of the spout; the water stream arcs upward and back over the flowers rather than falling straight down. In the front centre, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and grey skirt is digging with a spade, with a pile of earth beside her. A red wheelbarrow stands at the front right and the swing set has a single empty red swing.
