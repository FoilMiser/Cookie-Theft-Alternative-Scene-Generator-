# pic_176

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a gate and paved path on the left, a wooden fence, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers is painting the fence pink with a roller on a long pole; a few planks on the left are already pink, and a paint tin and a paint tray sit on the grass behind him. A small white dog with brown spots is trotting away across the lawn with pink feet, leaving a line of pink paw prints from the paint tray, so it has obviously walked through the paint. The man has his back to the dog and has not noticed. On the left path an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses, in a green cardigan, is sweeping a small pile of leaves with a broom. A teal watering can, a red wheelbarrow, a hose reel with hose and a flower bed are spread around the garden.
