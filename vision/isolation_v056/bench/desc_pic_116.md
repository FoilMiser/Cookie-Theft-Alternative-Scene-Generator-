## Free description
A sunny playground with a black metal railing fence, a tree on the left, a swing set in the centre, a slide on the right, a sandpit with a bucket and spade in the front right, a green bin, a bush and a grey rock. On the left, a little girl in a pink dress sits on the raised end of a red seesaw, holding the handle and looking toward the other end. Because he is heavy and sitting still, the girl is stuck up in the air. On the right, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is sliding down the slide with one arm raised. The swing hangs empty. Nothing else is happening.
