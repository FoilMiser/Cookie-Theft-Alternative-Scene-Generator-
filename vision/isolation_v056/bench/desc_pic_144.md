# pic_144

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house (red roof, blue door) on the left, a wooden fence and gate at the back, a paved path on the right, and a washing line strung between two wooden posts. In the centre-back, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is reaching up to peg laundry on the line; hanging there are blue jeans, a yellow-and-red striped towel, and a white T-shirt that is dripping water. A laundry basket full of clothes sits on the grass under the line. In the left-front, a woman in a yellow dress is holding a teal watering can by a flower bed, but a long curved blue stream rises up from the spout into the air above the flowers and ends in drops, so the water seems to shoot upward rather than pour down. On the right, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts stands on the path holding a reel and flying a yellow kite high in the sky on a long string. An empty wooden bench is in the front left and an empty grey wheelbarrow in the front centre.
