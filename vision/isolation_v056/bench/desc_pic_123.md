## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, trees, a pond on the left and a winding sandy path on the right. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands at the edge of the pond holding a paper bag and tossing a piece of bread to a white duck, which is swimming towards the bread pieces floating in the water. On the right, a grey-haired older woman with glasses sits on a wooden bench eating a pink ice-cream cone. A small white dog with brown spots sits up on its hind legs in front of her, looking up at the ice cream as if begging. Between them stand a tall black street lamp and a green bin. A bed of colourful flowers is at the front left, and reeds grow at the edges of the pond.
