## Free description
A sunny back garden beside a house with a blue door, a wooden fence, a gate and a paved path. An older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, kneels at a flower bed planting flowers; she holds a plant at a small hole and there is a tray of pink seedlings next to her. On top of the fence an orange cat is creeping along in a crouch towards a red bird perched on the fence, as if about to pounce on it; the bird does not seem to have noticed. On the right is an empty swing set. A sprinkler at the end of a hose that runs across the bottom of the lawn is spraying arcs of water, and the water is falling on a cardboard box sitting by the path, which is getting wet and has a puddle under it.
