# pic_152

## Free description
A back garden on a grey, overcast day with three dark storm clouds overhead, so rain seems about to start. A girl in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence pink with a paint roller on a long pole; a few fence boards are already pink and a pink paint tin stands by her feet. To the right an old woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, points up at the sky, as if warning that it is going to rain (which would spoil the wet paint). A small white puppy is lying curled up asleep on the lawn in front. A red wheelbarrow and a teal watering can stand at the front, and there is a birdhouse on a pole, a flower bed, a house with a blue door on the left and a gate and path on the right.
