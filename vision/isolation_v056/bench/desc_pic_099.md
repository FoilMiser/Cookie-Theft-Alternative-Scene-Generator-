## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house (red roof, blue door) on the left, a wooden fence and gate at the back, a paved path on the right, a swing set and a bird house on a pole. On the far left, by the door, a brown dog with a red collar is sitting up and holding the yellow garden hose in its mouth, with the rest of the hose coiled on the ground. In the left-centre, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is holding a teal watering can over a flower bed. The water from the can does not fall down onto the flowers: it shoots up and backwards in an arc, rising above the flowers and ending in drops in the air, which looks impossible. In the front centre, an old woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and grey skirt is digging with a spade; a pile of brown soil is beside her and her spade holds a scoop of soil. A red wheelbarrow, empty, stands in front of the swing set. The swing hangs still and empty.
