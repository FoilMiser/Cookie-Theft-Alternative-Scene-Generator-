## Free description

An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, stands in the middle of the lawn pointing up at the dark clouds with a worried look. It looks like it is about to rain. A teal watering can sits on the grass just to her right. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door, with a flowerbed of colourful flowers in front of it. Behind is a tall wooden fence with a gate on the right, and a paved path leads from the gate towards the front. There is a birdhouse on a tall pole and two potted plants by the fence. Nothing looks clearly impossible; the point seems to be that rain is coming, so the watering can may not be needed.
