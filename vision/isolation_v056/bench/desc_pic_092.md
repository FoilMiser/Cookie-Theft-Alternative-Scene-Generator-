## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house (blue door) on the left, a wooden fence with a gate on the right, a stone path, a flower bed and a bench. In the centre, a boy in a yellow t-shirt and orange shorts holds the strings of a yellow and a blue balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other toward a red balloon that is floating away above him; its string hangs loose, so he seems to have let it go. He looks up at it, worried. To the right, a girl in a pink dress is jumping in the air with both arms raised, smiling (her shadow is below her feet). Oddly, an orange fish is sitting in the branches of the tree behind the house. A red wheelbarrow and a teal watering can are on the grass at the front.
