## Free description
A sunny day in a park with a pond on the left, rolling green hills, trees and a winding sandy path on the right. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands at the edge of the pond holding a brown paper bag and throwing bits of bread or seed into the water. A white duck swims toward the food, with little ripple lines behind it. On the right, an older grey-haired woman in glasses and a teal cardigan sits on a wooden bench eating a pink ice-cream cone. A small white-and-brown puppy sits up on its hind legs in front of her, begging, apparently for the ice cream. A tall black lamp post and a green bin stand in the middle, and a bed of colourful flowers is in the front left. Nothing looks clearly wrong; it reads as a calm park scene where the dog may be hoping to get the ice cream.
