## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house (red roof, blue door) on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. In the middle, an old man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is raking up a small pile of leaves under a washing line. On the line hang a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes, held by pegs. At the front right, a sprinkler connected to a long hose across the lawn is spraying arcs of water onto a cardboard box sitting on the path, which is getting wet and has a puddle under it. A red wheelbarrow stands empty in the front centre, and a flower bed is in front of the house. Nobody seems to notice the box getting soaked.
