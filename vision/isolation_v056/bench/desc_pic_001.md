# pic_001

## Free description
A sunny day in a fenced park playground with a red-and-yellow slide on the left, a sandpit in front, a bench and a tree on the right. A small girl in a pink dress stands in the middle holding a red and a purple balloon, reaching up with her other hand towards a yellow balloon that is floating away high in the sky - she seems to have let it go. A grey-haired old woman in a teal top walks to the right carrying a cardboard box. An older man in a flat cap sits on the bench reading a red book; beside him on the bench is a sandwich, and a blue bird is pecking at / standing at the sandwich, apparently about to steal it. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the tree branches. In the front, a boy in a yellow shirt holds an empty ice-cream cone and rubs his eye as if crying, while a brown dog licks up the pink ice cream that has fallen on the grass. A white spotted dog in a play bow is about to grab a tennis ball near the slide. A red bike leans against a post in the front right, and a small red bird sits on the fence at the far left.
