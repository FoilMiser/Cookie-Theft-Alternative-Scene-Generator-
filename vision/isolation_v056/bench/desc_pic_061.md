# pic_061

## Free description
A sunny day in a park with a pond, a lamp post, a bench, a winding path and trees. In front on the left, an older man in a cap and brown vest is pouring water onto a flower bed, but he is pouring it out of a frying pan rather than a watering can. Beside the flower bed, a brown dog wearing glasses sits up on its hind legs reading a newspaper. On the pond a white duck swims towards a trail of bread crumbs, and a girl in a pink dress at the pond's edge holds a slice of bread, feeding it. In the middle front, a girl in a yellow top holds an empty ice-cream cone and rubs her eye as if crying; her pink ice cream has fallen on the ground and a white spotted dog is licking it up. Behind, an older woman with glasses sweeps leaves into a pile with a broom, and a white goat tied to a stake grazes nearby. On the right, a boy in a red cap holds a kite string, but his purple kite is caught in the tree. A blue bird flies in the sky.
