In the middle a man in a green shirt and beige trousers is holding a garden hose nozzle up to his face and looking into it, as if puzzled that no water is coming out. When the dog moves, the water may spray in the man's face. On the left a woman in a yellow dress sits drinking through a straw from a red cup — but there is no chair or bench under her, so she is sitting on thin air. In the back a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks a white spotted dog on a red lead.
