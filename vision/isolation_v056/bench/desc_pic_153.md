## Free description
A sunny town street with shops, a fruit stall, a lamp post, a tree and a small café table with two chairs. On the left a man in a green shirt and beige trousers is walking with a red bicycle, holding its handlebars; the bicycle has square wheels, which is impossible. In the middle a boy in a red shirt and blue trousers scatters seeds from a paper bag for a red bird pecking on the ground. On the bench, a blue bird is pulling at or pecking a sandwich, while an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses sits on the other end reading a red book, apparently not noticing. On the right an old man in a cap and brown waistcoat sweeps a pile of leaves with a broom. The café table at the right is empty.
