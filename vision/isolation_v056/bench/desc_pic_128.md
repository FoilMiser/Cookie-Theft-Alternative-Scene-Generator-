## Free description
A sunny, empty back garden beside a cream house with a red roof and blue door. There are no people or animals in the picture. A long dark-teal garden hose runs across the front of the lawn from the left to a small lawn sprinkler near the right. The sprinkler is spraying arcs of water to the right, and the spray is landing on a cardboard parcel box that has been left at the edge of the paved path. Water is dripping on the box and a small puddle is forming under it, so the parcel is getting soaked. The rest of the garden is ordinary: flower bed, two bushes, a tree, a wooden fence and a gate at the right end of the path.
