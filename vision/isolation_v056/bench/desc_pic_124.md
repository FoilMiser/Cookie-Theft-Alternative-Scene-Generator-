In the front left, a woman with dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers is watering a bed of colourful flowers with a grey watering can; water is pouring onto the flowers. Next to her, a man with a beard in a green shirt and beige trousers is reclining on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket beside a picnic basket holding a bottle and a baguette. On the right, a small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds up a kite reel; the kite string runs up and to the right, and the yellow kite is caught in the branches of the big tree at the right. The boy is looking up, so it seems his kite is stuck in the tree. Nobody else is around.
