## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a gate in the fence at the right with a paved path leading to it. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and grey skirt stands on the lawn in the centre-left, holding a grey watering can and watering a round flower bed of red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers. A stream of blue water arcs from the can over the flowers, with a few drops in the air above them. Behind her a washing line between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes, pegged up to dry. A wooden bench sits empty in the front left, a red wheelbarrow stands empty in the front centre-right, and two potted plants and a birdhouse on a tall pole are by the fence on the right. Nothing seems about to go wrong; it is a calm gardening scene.
