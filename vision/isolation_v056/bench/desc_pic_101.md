# pic_101

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate on the right and a paved path leading to it. A woman with dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a wooden bench in front of the house, absorbed in reading a red book. At the other end of the bench a blue bird is perched next to a large sandwich and has its beak on it, as if it is stealing or pecking at her lunch while she isn't looking. Behind her a clothesline strung between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow striped towel. There is a flower bed with colourful flowers in the middle, two potted plants near the fence, a red wheelbarrow and a teal watering can on the lawn at the front right. Nothing else seems to be happening; the main event is the bird going for the sandwich.

- Clothesline (centre back): two wooden posts with a line; blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes pegged on it.
- Flower bed (centre left): soil mound with multicoloured flowers.

- Red wheelbarrow (front centre-right): empty, round black wheel.
- Teal watering can (front right).
- House with blue door and window (left); wooden gate (right); paved path (right); tree and bush behind fence/house; sun and clouds.
