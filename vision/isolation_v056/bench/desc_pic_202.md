# pic_202

## Free description
A sunny back garden with the house on the right, a fence along the back and a path on the left leading to a gate. A man in a red shirt holds a garden hose and looks at its nozzle, which has only a few drops coming out. The hose runs from a reel by the house, across the lawn, and a white spotted puppy is standing on the hose, which is why no water is coming out; there is a small puddle at the hose reel. On the left a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is jumping on the path with both arms up, looking happy. A cardboard box is floating high in the sky above the fence, with nothing holding it.
