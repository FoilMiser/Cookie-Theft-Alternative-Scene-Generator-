## Free description
A sunny playground with a metal railing fence, a tree, a swing set, a slide and a sandpit. On the left is a red seesaw: a small girl in a pink outfit sits on the high end, up in the air, holding the handle with her legs dangling. An older man with grey hair and a flat cap is sitting on the low end, which rests on the ground, reading an open newspaper and not looking at the girl. Because he is heavy and not pushing up, the girl seems stuck at the top; her mouth is open and her arm is stretched forward as if she is calling out or complaining to him. On the right, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is kneeling in a sandpit, building a sandcastle with a small red spade; the castle has a little red flag on top. The swing set in the middle has one empty swing, and the slide on the right is empty. A green bin stands by the fence and a grey rock lies near the sandpit.
