# pic_097

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house on the left, a long wooden fence across the back, a gate and a stone path on the right. A woman in a purple top is painting the fence pink with a paint roller on a long handle; a section of the fence is already pink, and a paint tin and a roller tray with pink paint sit on the grass by her feet. Nearby a man in a red shirt is sweeping a pile of dry leaves with a broom. A white dog with brown spots is walking on the path with pink paws, and a trail of pink paw prints leads from the paint tray to the dog, so it has stepped in the paint. By the blue door, a brown dog sits holding a red lead in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. A girl in a pink dress waves, and a man in a green shirt walks across the front carrying a cardboard box.
