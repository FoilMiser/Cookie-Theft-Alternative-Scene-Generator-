# pic_249

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the right, a swing set, a clothesline and a fence with a gate. In the middle, a woman in a purple top holds a garden hose and looks closely into the nozzle, as if wondering why no water comes out. The hose runs back to a hose reel by the house, and a brown dog is standing on the hose, which would block the water; it looks like she may get sprayed in the face when the dog steps off. Water drips into a small puddle at the reel. In front, a white spotted puppy plays with a tennis ball, bowing down with its tail wagging. On the right, a man in a red shirt sits on a bench reading a red book. A yellow towel and blue jeans hang on the clothesline, and the swing is empty.
