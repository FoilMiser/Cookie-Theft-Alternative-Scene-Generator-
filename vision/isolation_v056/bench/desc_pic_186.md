## Free description
A sunny town square with coloured shops and awnings behind. On the left a man in a green shirt walks with a red bicycle whose wheels are square instead of round, which is impossible to ride. In the centre a man in a red shirt holds a paper bag and scatters seeds onto the ground, where a red bird is pecking them up. On a bench, an older woman in glasses and a teal cardigan is reading a red book, and does not notice a blue bird beside her pecking at / taking her sandwich. On the right an older man in a cap and waistcoat sweeps a pile of leaves with a broom. There is also a fruit stall, a lamp post, a bin, a tree, and a small café table with two chairs.
