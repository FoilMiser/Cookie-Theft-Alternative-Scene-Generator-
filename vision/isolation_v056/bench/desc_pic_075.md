# pic_075

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house (red roof, blue door) on the left, a wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the right, and a bird box on a tall pole. In the centre a woman in a yellow dress is hanging washing on a clothesline between two posts: a pair of blue jeans and a white T-shirt, both dripping water; a basket of more clothes sits on the grass beside her. In the bottom-left an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses, in a green cardigan, sits on a bench reading a red book; next to her on the bench is a sandwich and a blue bird is pecking at it, apparently without her noticing. On the right, on the path, a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts holds a red and a blue balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other toward a yellow balloon that is floating away high in the sky, its string dangling. A flower bed, a hose reel with hose, a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can are around the garden.
