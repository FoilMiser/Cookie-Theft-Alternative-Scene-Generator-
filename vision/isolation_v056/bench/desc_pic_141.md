## Free description
A sunny playground with a dark railing fence, a slide on the left, a tree on the right, a green bin and a grey rock. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is sliding down the slide with his arms thrown up happily. On the right is a red seesaw: an older man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers sits on the low end reading a newspaper, completely absorbed, while a small girl in a pink dress is stuck up on the high end. The girl holds her hand to her mouth and looks worried, her feet dangling well off the ground. The man's weight keeps his end on the ground, so the girl cannot come down, and he does not seem to notice her. The scene is otherwise quiet with lots of empty grass.
