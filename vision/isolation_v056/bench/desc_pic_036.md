## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a paved path running down the left side and a house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the front left, an elderly grey-haired woman with glasses, a teal cardigan and grey skirt is digging with a shovel, and a small pile of dug-up earth sits beside her. On the right, by the house door, a brown dog sits up holding a yellow garden hose in its mouth, as if it has picked it up or is pulling it away. A washing line between two posts in the middle holds a yellow striped towel and a pair of blue jeans. Behind the left side is a birdhouse on a tall pole, and there is a round flower bed with colourful flowers near the house. Strangely, a closed cardboard box is up in the sky above the washing line, tilted as though it is flying or falling, with nothing holding it.
