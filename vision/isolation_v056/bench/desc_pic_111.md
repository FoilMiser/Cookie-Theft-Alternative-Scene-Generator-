## Free description
A sunny back garden with a gate and path on the left, a wooden fence along the back and a cream house with a blue door on the right. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps a small pile of leaves on the path with a broom. A man in a red shirt is painting the fence pink with a paint roller; a few fence boards are already pink and a pot of pink paint stands at his feet. A red wheelbarrow sits empty on the lawn. An older woman with grey hair holds the nozzle of a garden hose and looks at it, puzzled, because no water is coming out: a brown dog is standing on the hose, and the hose reel by the house is leaking a puddle. Behind her a man in a green shirt stands with a metal bucket upside down on his head, hands on his hips, which looks silly or strange. A small girl in a pink dress points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the tree, a grey cat walks along the top of the fence, and a blue bird flies in the sky. On the right a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench drinking through a straw from a red cup.
