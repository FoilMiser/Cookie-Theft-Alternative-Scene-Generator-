# pic_197

## Free description
A sunny but windy back garden: white wind lines streak across the sky and across the middle of the picture. The house is on the right (red roof, blue door), a wooden fence and gate are on the left, and a paved path runs down the left side. In the centre a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers is reaching up with one hand, looking surprised, as his red cap is blown off his head by the wind; a few leaves are blowing along with it. By the house door a brown dog sits holding a red garden hose in its mouth. In the front right a small white puppy with brown ears lies curled up asleep on the grass. A red wheelbarrow and a teal watering can stand on the lawn at the front, and there are two potted plants and a bush by the fence and a flower bed by the house. The man is about to lose, or grab, his cap.
