# pic_225

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house (red roof, blue door) on the left, a wooden fence along the back and a gate with a paved path on the right. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence pink with a paint roller; a section of fence is already pink, and a pot of pink paint and a paint tray sit on the grass by her feet. A white spotted puppy on the path at the right has pink paws and has left a trail of pink paw prints across the grass from the paint tray, so it seems to have stepped in the paint. Nearby, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans sweeps leaves into a pile with a broom. At the front, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers walks carrying a cardboard box, and a girl in a pink dress stands by the flower bed waving. On the far left, a brown dog by the front door holds a yellow dog lead in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk.
