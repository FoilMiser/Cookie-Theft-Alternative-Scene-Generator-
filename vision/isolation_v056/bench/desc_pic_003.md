## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a tree behind the fence. On the paved path at the left, a man in a green shirt walks along reading an open book, and a cardboard box sits on the path just in front of his feet, so he looks about to trip over it. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie walks a white spotted dog on a red lead across the lawn. In the middle, a grey-haired older woman holds the nozzle of a garden hose up and peers into it; the hose runs across the grass to a hose reel by the house, and a brown dog is standing on the hose, apparently blocking the water, while a small puddle drips at the reel. A girl in a pink dress near the fence reaches up towards a blue-and-white beach ball that is stuck in the branches of the tree. On the right, a man in a red shirt sits on a bench reading a red book. A red wheelbarrow stands empty in the foreground, and there is a flower bed near the house.
