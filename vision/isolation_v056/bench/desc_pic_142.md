# pic_142

## Free description
A sunny park with hills, a pond with reeds, a street lamp, trees and a winding sandy path. On the left a woman in a purple top and teal trousers waters a bed of colourful flowers with a grey watering can. In the centre a man in a green shirt and beige trousers is reclining on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket, beside a wicker picnic basket with a bottle and a baguette sticking out. On the right a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds up a kite reel, but his purple kite is caught in the branches of a large tree on the right; the string runs from his hand to the tree. The boy is looking up at the stuck kite. Nothing else looks odd.
