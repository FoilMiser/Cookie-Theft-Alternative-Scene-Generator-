# pic_076

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a gate on the left, a paved path running down the left side, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the front-left, a grey-haired grandmother in a teal cardigan and glasses is digging with a shovel, with a pile of dirt next to her. In the middle, a clothesline between two posts holds a yellow striped shirt and a pair of blue jeans. Above the clothesline, a cardboard box is floating in the sky with nothing holding it up, which looks impossible. On the right, by the blue door, a brown dog sits holding a red leash (with a metal clip) in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. There is a flower bed of mixed flowers near the house, a birdhouse on a pole near the gate, and a tree behind the fence.
