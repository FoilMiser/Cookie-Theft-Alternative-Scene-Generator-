## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a gate on the left and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the paved path at the left, an older man with grey hair, a flat cap, a brown vest and white shirt is sweeping a small pile of dead leaves with a yellow broom. In the middle of the lawn a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is jumping in the air with both arms up and a big open-mouthed smile, as if cheering; his shadow is on the grass below his feet. At the front right, a grey cat is crouched low, creeping towards a red bird that is pecking at seeds on the grass, so the cat looks about to pounce on the bird. There are two potted plants by the fence, a teal watering can on the grass, and a flower bed in front of the house.
