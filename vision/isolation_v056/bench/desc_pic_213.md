## Free description

On the far left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping a pile of brown leaves on the path with a broom. Next to him, a man in a red T-shirt and jeans is painting the fence pink with a paint roller; a few planks are already pink and a pink paint tin is at his feet. In the centre, an old woman with glasses and a grey bun is holding the nozzle of a green garden hose and peering into it, because no water comes out: a brown dog is standing on the hose, which blocks the water, while water leaks out near the hose reel by the house. Behind her, a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers stands with his hands on his hips with a white bucket upside down on his head. On the right by the fence, a little girl in a pink dress points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the tree. A grey cat walks along the top of the fence, a blue bird flies in the sky, and on the far right a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench drinking from a cup with a straw. A red wheelbarrow stands near the fence, with a black box-shaped thing at its front.
