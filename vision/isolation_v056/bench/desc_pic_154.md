# pic_154

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left, a fence, a swing set and a path on the right. A man in a red shirt is watering the flower bed with a green watering can, but the water spout is shooting upward and arcing back rather than pouring down onto the flowers. In front, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and grey skirt is digging a hole in the lawn with a spade, with a pile of soil beside her. A red wheelbarrow stands nearby. On the left a brown dog sits by the blue door holding a red lead in its mouth. The swing set is empty.
