# pic_002

## Free description
A sunny park with a round pond on the left, rolling green hills and trees behind, and a sandy path curving down the right side. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands at the edge of the pond holding a brown paper bag and reaching out to feed a white duck; small pieces of bread are floating on the water in front of the duck, which is swimming towards them. Behind the bench, on the grass, a white goat with a red collar is tied by a rope to a small wooden stake and is bending down to eat a tuft of grass. A boy in a yellow top and orange shorts stands on the path holding the string of a red balloon. A wooden bench, a tall black lamp post, a green rubbish bin and a grey rock complete the scene. Nothing looks dramatic; the main oddity is a goat tethered in the middle of a town park.
