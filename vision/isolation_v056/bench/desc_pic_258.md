# pic_258

## Free description
A sunny park with a pond, a lamp post, a bench, a winding path and trees. At the front left a woman in a yellow dress is watering a flower bed by pouring water out of a frying pan, an odd thing to water flowers with. Next to her a man in a green shirt scatters seeds or crumbs from a paper bag onto the grass, where a red bird is pecking them up. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts runs along holding a pink ice-cream cone. Further back, a girl in a pink dress holds a red and a purple balloon and reaches up toward a yellow balloon floating away into the sky. On the right a woman in a purple top and green trousers walks along the path carrying a yellow shopping bag. There is an empty bench, a green rubbish bin at bottom left, and reeds at the pond edge.
