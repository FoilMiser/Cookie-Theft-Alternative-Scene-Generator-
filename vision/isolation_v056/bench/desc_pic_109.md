## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left, a wooden fence across the back and a path on the right leading to a gate. Part of the fence has been painted bright pink; a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence with a pink paint roller on a long handle, and a paint tin and roller tray sit on the grass at her feet. To her right a man in a red shirt sweeps a small pile of leaves with a broom. A small white spotted puppy with pink paws is on the path, and a trail of pink paw prints leads from the paint tray to the puppy, so it has evidently stepped in the paint. On the left a brown dog sits by the blue door holding a red lead in its mouth, as if wanting a walk. In the centre a man in a green shirt walks carrying a cardboard box, and a little girl in a pink dress waves.
