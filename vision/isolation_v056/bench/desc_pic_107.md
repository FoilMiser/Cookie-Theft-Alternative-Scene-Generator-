# pic_107

## Free description
A busy park playground on a sunny day, with a railing fence along the back, a slide on the left, a sandpit at the front and a tree on the right. A little girl in a pink dress holds a red and a purple balloon and reaches up as a yellow balloon floats away high above her. An older woman with grey hair walks across carrying a cardboard box. An older man in a cap and brown waistcoat sits on a bench reading a red book while a blue bird beside him pecks at his sandwich on the bench. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of the tree. In front, a boy in a yellow shirt holds an empty ice-cream cone and rubs his eyes, crying, because his pink ice cream has fallen on the ground and a brown dog is licking it up. To the left, a white dog with brown spots crouches playfully in front of a tennis ball. A red bicycle stands on the grass, a red bird sits on the fence, and there is a bucket and spade in the sandpit and a grey rock.
