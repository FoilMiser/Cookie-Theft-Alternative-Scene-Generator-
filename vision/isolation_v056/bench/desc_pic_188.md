A sunny playground with a fence, a tree, a swing set, a slide and a sandbox. On the left, an old man in a cap and brown waistcoat sits on the low end of a red seesaw reading a newspaper, while a small girl in pink is stuck up in the air on the high end, holding the handle and looking toward him with an open mouth as if calling out or complaining. On the right, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts slides down the slide with one arm raised, looking happy. In the front right there is a sandbox with a red bucket and blue spade, and a grey rock. There is a green bin by the fence.
