# pic_034

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left and a wooden fence behind. A woman in a yellow dress stands between two wooden posts, reaching up to hang washing on a clothesline. Already on the line are a pair of blue jeans and a white T-shirt, both pegged up and dripping water, so they are freshly washed. A wicker laundry basket full of more clothes sits on the grass by her feet. In the front there is a red wheelbarrow and a teal watering can. The garden also has a flowerbed, two potted plants, a birdhouse on a pole, a gate and a paved path. It is a calm, ordinary scene; nothing looks wrong.
