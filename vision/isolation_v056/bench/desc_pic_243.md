# pic_243

## Free description
A sunny park with hills and trees, a pond on the left, a tall black lamp post in the middle and a winding sandy path on the right. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands at the edge of the pond holding a brown paper bag and throwing bread crumbs to a white duck, which is swimming toward the crumbs and pecking at them. Behind, on the grass, a white goat with a red collar is tied by a rope to a small wooden stake and is bending down eating a tuft of grass. On the path on the right, a small boy in a yellow top and orange shorts stands holding a red balloon on a string. There is an empty wooden bench beside the path, a grey rock in the foreground, and a green bin in the bottom-left corner. Nothing looks clearly wrong, though a goat in a park is a little unusual.
