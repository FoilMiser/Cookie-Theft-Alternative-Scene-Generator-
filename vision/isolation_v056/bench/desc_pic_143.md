# pic_143

## Free description
A sunny playground with a dark metal railing fence across the back, a tree on the left and a bush on the right. On the left front, a seesaw is tipped down on the right: an old man in a grey cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat sits on the low end reading a newspaper, while a small girl in pink sits high up on the raised left end, holding the handle with one hand and the other hand to her mouth, looking worried or surprised. She seems stuck up in the air because the man is heavier and isn't paying attention. In the middle stands a swing frame with a single empty red swing seat. On the right, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts slides down a red-and-yellow slide with one arm raised, looking happy. At the front right there is a sandbox with a red bucket and blue spade in it, and a grey rock next to it; a green rubbish bin stands by the fence.
