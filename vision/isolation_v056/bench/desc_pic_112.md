## Free description
Three children play in a sunny park with a pond, a lamp post, trees and a sandy path. On the left a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is kicking an orange ball with motion lines under his foot. In the middle a girl in a yellow top and orange shorts has just thrown a red ball, which is flying through the air (dashed line behind it) towards a girl in a pink dress on the right, who holds out her arms to catch it. Between the middle girl and the pink girl an orange cat lies curled up asleep on the grass. On the far right a lawn sprinkler fed by a hose is spraying arcs of water, and the water is landing on a cardboard box sitting on the path, which is wet and dripping with a small puddle under it. Nobody seems to notice the box getting soaked.
