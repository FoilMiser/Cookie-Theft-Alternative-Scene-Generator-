# pic_080

## Free description
A busy sunny park with a dark railing fence across the back. In the centre a small girl in a pink dress holds a red and a purple balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other toward a yellow balloon that is floating away into the sky, its string dangling. In the front centre a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and rubs his eye as if crying, while a brown dog at his feet licks up a pink ice-cream scoop that has fallen on the ground. To the left a white dog with brown spots crouches playfully with its tail wagging next to a tennis ball. An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses walks to the right carrying a cardboard box. An old man in a flat cap sits on a bench reading a red book, while a blue bird beside him on the bench pecks at his sandwich. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of a tree. A red bicycle stands upright on its kickstand at the front right, a small red bird sits on the fence at the far left, and an empty slide, a sandpit with a bucket and spade, and a grey rock are on the left.
