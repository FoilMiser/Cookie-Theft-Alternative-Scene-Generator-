# pic_014

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house on the right (red roof, blue door), a wooden fence along the back, a gate in the back left corner and a paved path running from the gate to the front left. An old woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and grey skirt is digging with a spade in the front left of the lawn, with a pile of earth next to her hole. On the right, by the blue door, a brown dog sits holding a yellow lead in its mouth, the lead lying on the doorstep, as if waiting to be taken for a walk. In the middle there is a washing line between two posts with a yellow striped towel and blue jeans pegged on it. Oddly, a cardboard box is hanging in mid-air in the sky above the washing line, tilted, with nothing holding it up. There is also a flower bed by the house and a birdhouse on a pole on the left.
