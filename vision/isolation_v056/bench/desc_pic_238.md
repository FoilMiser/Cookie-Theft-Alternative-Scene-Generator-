## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a gate and paving path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A woman in a yellow dress walks across the lawn carrying a cardboard box in both arms, heading towards a red wheelbarrow, perhaps to put the box in it. A man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands by a flower bed near the house, watering the flowers with a teal watering can; a stream of water pours onto them. A wooden bench sits empty by the house. The wheelbarrow looks odd: where its front wheel should be there is a dark square box shape, as if a black box is sitting against it or the wheel has been drawn as a square.
