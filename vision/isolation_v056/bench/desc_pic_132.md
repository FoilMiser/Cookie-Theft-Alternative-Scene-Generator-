## Free description
A sunny playground surrounded by a dark metal railing fence, with a tree on the left, a bench, a red slide on the right and a sandpit at the front right. In the middle back, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie has just thrown a red ball, which is in the air (with a dotted trail) heading towards a woman in a yellow dress, who has her hands up ready to catch it. In the front left, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a kite reel up; the string goes to a purple kite that is caught in the leaves of the tree on the left. In the centre a white dog with brown spots is in a playful bow with a yellow tennis ball by its front paws, wagging its tail. In the sandpit at the front right, a small girl in pink is kneeling and building a sandcastle with a little flag on top, holding a small red spade. A grey rock sits beside the sandpit.
