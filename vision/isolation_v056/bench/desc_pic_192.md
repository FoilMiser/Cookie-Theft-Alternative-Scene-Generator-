A sunny back garden with a house on the left (red roof, blue door), a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path down the right side. A flower bed is in front of the house and a teal watering can stands on the lawn near the path. The pink fence stands out oddly against the rest of the plain wooden fence.
