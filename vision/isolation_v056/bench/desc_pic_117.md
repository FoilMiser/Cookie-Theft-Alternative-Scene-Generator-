# pic_117

## Free description
A sunny but windy back garden: white wind lines streak across the sky and across the middle of the picture. A man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands in the centre of the lawn reaching up with one arm as his red cap is blown off his head by the wind, along with a few leaves; he looks surprised. On the right, beside the house's blue door, a brown dog with a red collar is sitting up holding a coiled yellow garden hose in its mouth, as if it has taken or is fetching the hose. A small white puppy with brown ears lies asleep on the grass in the front right. In the foreground there is a teal watering can and an empty red wheelbarrow, and two potted plants sit near the fence on the left. There is also a flower bed, a tree, a gate and a paved path.
