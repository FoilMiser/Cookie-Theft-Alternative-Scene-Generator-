# pic_235

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left and a cream house with a blue door on the right. A man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands in the middle holding a green garden hose with both hands and looking down at the nozzle with a puzzled or frowning face — no water is coming out (only a tiny drip). The hose runs along the ground from a hose reel by the house, and a white dog with brown spots is standing right on the hose, which seems to be squashing it and blocking the water; a small puddle is leaking at the reel end. On the left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands on the path with both arms raised high, smiling, as if he has just thrown something or is about to catch something. A cardboard box is floating/flying in the sky above the fence, tilted, with no visible reason for being up there. There is a flower bed in front of the house.
