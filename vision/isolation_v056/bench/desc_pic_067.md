## Free description
A sunny back garden seen with the house on the right (cream walls, red roof, blue door) and a wooden fence and gate on the left, with a paved path running down the left side. In the centre a woman in a yellow dress is pegging washing on a clothesline: a white T-shirt and blue jeans hang up, both dripping water, and a basket of clothes sits on the grass beneath. In the front right an older man in a grey cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is watering a flower bed with a teal watering can; water arcs out of the spout over the flowers with drops above it. Beside the blue door a brown dog is sitting up holding a yellow garden hose in its mouth, apparently chewing or carrying it off. A red wheelbarrow stands empty on the lawn at the front, and two potted plants sit near the fence on the left. Nothing dramatic is happening; the dog with the hose is the one bit of mischief.
