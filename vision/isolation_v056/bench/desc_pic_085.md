# pic_085

## Free description
A sunny park with a pond on the left, a lamp post and bin in the centre, a curving path and a large tree on the right, and a flower bed at the front left. A girl in a purple top and teal trousers stands at the edge of the pond holding a paper bag and tossing bread or seeds to a white duck swimming in the water; small pieces float near the duck. On the right, an older grey-haired woman with glasses, in a teal cardigan, sits on a wooden bench eating a pink ice-cream cone. In front of the bench, a small white-and-brown spotted puppy sits up on its hind legs, begging and looking up at the woman's ice cream. It looks as if the puppy hopes to be given some of the ice cream.
