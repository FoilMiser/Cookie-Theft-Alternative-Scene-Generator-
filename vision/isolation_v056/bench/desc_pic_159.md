## Free description
A busy, sunny playground fenced with dark railings, with a slide on the left, a sandpit at the front, a bench and a tree on the right. In the front centre, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts has dropped his pink ice cream on the ground; he holds the empty cone and rubs his eye as if crying, while a brown dog licks up the melted ice cream at his feet. A spotty white dog by the slide crouches playfully next to a tennis ball, wagging its tail. In the middle, a girl in a pink dress holds a red and a purple balloon and reaches up towards a yellow balloon that has floated away into the sky. An old woman with grey hair and glasses walks to the right carrying a cardboard box. An old man in a flat cap sits on the bench reading a red book; next to him on the bench is a sandwich, and a blue bird is pecking at it. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the tree branches. A red bicycle leans against a post at the front right, and a red bird sits on the fence at the far left.
