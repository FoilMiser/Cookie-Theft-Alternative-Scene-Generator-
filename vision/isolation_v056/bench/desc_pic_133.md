## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house (blue door) on the left, a wooden fence with a gate on the right and a stone path. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence blue with a roller on a long pole; a section of the fence is already blue, and a paint tin and paint tray sit on the grass. A white dog with brown spots has walked through the paint: its paws are blue and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the grass to it on the path. On the right, a man in a red shirt and blue trousers is sweeping leaves on the path with a broom. In the front centre, a bearded man in a green shirt carries a cardboard box, walking toward the viewer, and a girl in a pink dress waves at him. On the far left, a brown dog sits by the door holding its lead in its mouth, as if asking for a walk.
