## Free description
A sunny back garden seen with the cream house (red roof, blue door) on the left, a long wooden fence across the back, a gate and a paved path on the right, a birdhouse on a pole and a metal swing set. By the house door on the left, a brown dog sits holding a red leash in its mouth. In front of the flower bed, a man in a red shirt and blue trousers holds a watering can, but the water is spraying upward and backward out of the spout into the air instead of falling onto the flowers. In the front centre, a grey-haired grandmother in a teal cardigan is digging with a shovel beside a pile of dirt. A red wheelbarrow stands empty to her right. The swing set has one red swing hanging still, with nobody on it.
