A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a long wooden fence with a gate on the right, and a paved path running from the gate to the front right. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands by a round flowerbed and waters the colourful flowers with a teal watering can. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers rakes a small pile of brown leaves on the grass near the path. Up in the sky above the fence, a brown cardboard box is hanging in mid-air, tilted, with nothing holding it up, which is impossible. Otherwise it is a quiet, ordinary gardening scene.
