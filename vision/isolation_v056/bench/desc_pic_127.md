## Free description
A sunny back garden with the cream house and blue door on the left, a wooden fence and gate, and a paved path on the right. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands at the flower bed, watering the flowers with a teal watering can; water streams onto them. On the right, near the fence, a woman with dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers rakes a small pile of leaves on the grass. Strangely, a cardboard box is hanging in mid-air in the sky above the fence, tilted, with nothing holding it up and nobody throwing it. Neither person is looking at the box. The floating box is the obviously impossible thing in the picture.
