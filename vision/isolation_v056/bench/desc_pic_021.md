# pic_021

## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, a pond with bulrushes on the left, and a winding sandy path on the right. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands near the front, right of centre, flying an orange diamond kite. He holds a red reel up in one hand and the kite string runs high into the sky, where the kite has a tail with little bows. On the right, beside the path, a garden sprinkler fed by a dark hose is spraying arcs of water, and some of the water is landing on a cardboard box sitting on the path edge; there is a small puddle under the box, so the box is getting wet. A red wheelbarrow stands empty in the front centre, a flower bed with colourful flowers is front left, and a green bin is in the bottom-left corner. A tall black lamppost stands in the middle of the park. Nobody seems to have noticed the box getting soaked.
