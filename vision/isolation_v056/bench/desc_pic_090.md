## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a wooden gate on the right, and a paved path leading to the gate. An old man with grey hair, a blue-grey flat cap, a white long-sleeved shirt, a brown vest/waistcoat and dark trousers stands in the left-centre beside a round flower bed. He is holding out a grey cooking pot or saucepan (with a handle, like a kitchen pan) and tipping it so a stream of water pours onto the flowers. He seems to be watering the flowers with a kitchen saucepan instead of a watering can, which looks unusual. Two pots of green plants stand near the fence, there is a green bush, and an empty grey wheelbarrow sits in the front centre. No one else is in the garden.
