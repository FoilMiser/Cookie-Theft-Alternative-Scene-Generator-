# pic_049

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a paved path running to a gate on the right. In front of the house an older woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a bench reading a red book. At the other end of the same bench a blue bird is pecking at, or pulling at, a sandwich, apparently taking her food while she reads. In the middle a woman in a yellow dress is pegging washing on a line between two posts: a pair of blue jeans and a white T-shirt, both dripping water, with a basket of laundry at her feet. On the right a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands on the path holding a red and a yellow balloon in one hand and reaching up with the other toward a purple balloon that is floating away above him; he looks upset. A teal watering can sits on the lawn, and there is a flower bed, a tree, a bush and a birdhouse on a pole.
