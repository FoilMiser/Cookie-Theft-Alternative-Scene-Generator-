On the left, an old man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat sits on the low end of a red seesaw, reading a newspaper and not paying attention. On the slide at the right, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts slides down with one arm raised, looking cheerful. The swing set has a single empty swing. A green bin stands by the fence, and a bucket and spade lie in the sandpit, with a grey rock on the grass beside it. The funny/odd point is that the man's weight keeps the girl stranded high up while he reads.
