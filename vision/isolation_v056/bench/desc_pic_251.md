A child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts slides down the slide with one arm raised happily. On the seesaw, an older man in a cap and brown vest sits on the low end, which rests on the ground, and reads a newspaper or book, paying no attention. Because the man is much heavier and isn't moving, she can't get down. A green bin stands near the fence, a tree is on the right, and there are a bush and a grey rock on the left.
