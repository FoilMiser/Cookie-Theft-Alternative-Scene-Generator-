# pic_019

## Free description
A sunny day in a park with a pond, rolling hills, trees, a tall black lamp post and a winding sand path on the right. On the left, a man in an orange cap and orange-and-white striped safety vest is watering a flower bed, but he is pouring the water out of a frying pan instead of a watering can. In the centre, a grey-haired grandmother in a teal cardigan and glasses sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket with a wicker basket (a baguette and a bottle sticking out) and a plate of watermelon slices. Further back, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts holds a kite spool up high; the string runs to a yellow kite that is caught in the branches of the big tree on the right. On the path at the right, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers pushes a teal-and-blue pram with a sleeping baby under a pink blanket. A green rubbish bin stands in the bottom-left corner. The odd things are the frying pan used for watering and the kite stuck in the tree.

- The kite could be read as stuck in the tree, or as just flying behind the tree; the branch overlap and the drooping string suggest it is caught.
