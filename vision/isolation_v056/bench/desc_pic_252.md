# pic_252

## Free description
A busy, sunny playground with a metal railing fence, a red and yellow slide on the left, a sandpit at the front left, a bench and a big tree on the right. In the front centre a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and rubs his eye as if crying, because his pink ice cream has fallen on the ground, where a brown dog is now licking it up. Behind them a girl in a pink dress holds a red and a purple balloon and reaches up toward a yellow balloon that is floating away high in the sky. A white dog with brown spots crouches in a play bow next to a tennis ball near the sandpit, which has a red bucket and spade in it. An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses walks along carrying a cardboard box. On the bench an older man in a flat cap reads a red book while a blue bird pecks at the sandwich lying beside him. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of the tree. A red bicycle stands beside a short wooden post in the front right, and a small red bird is perched on the fence at the far left.
