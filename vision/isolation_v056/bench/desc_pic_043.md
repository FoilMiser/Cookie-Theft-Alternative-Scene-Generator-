## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a paved path, a swing set, a washing line, and a house with a blue door on the right. A girl in a purple top and teal trousers stands in the middle holding the end of a green garden hose and looking into the nozzle, but no water is coming out. Following the hose back, a brown dog is standing on it, which pinches it, and further along the hose is a reel by the house with a small puddle leaking near it. So the dog is likely blocking the water, and when it steps off the water may squirt into the girl's face. At the front a small white puppy with brown spots plays with a yellow tennis ball, wagging its tail. On the right a man in a red shirt sits on a bench reading a red book. A yellow striped towel/top and blue jeans hang on the washing line; there is a flower bed by the house.
