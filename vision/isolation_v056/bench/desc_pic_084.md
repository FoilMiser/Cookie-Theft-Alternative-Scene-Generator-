## Free description
A back garden on a grey, overcast day with three dark storm clouds overhead. A man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands near the fence on the right painting it bright pink with a paint roller on a long handle; a few fence boards are already pink and a pot of pink paint sits on the grass by his feet. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands on the left on the grass beside a path, pointing up at the dark clouds with a worried look, as if warning that rain is coming. The implication is that it is about to rain on the freshly painted fence. A small white puppy with brown ears lies curled up asleep on the grass in the front. A red wheelbarrow sits empty in the front centre. There is a house with a blue door on the right, a flower bed, bushes and a wooden gate on the left.
