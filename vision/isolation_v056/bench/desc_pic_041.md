## Free description
It is a sunny day in a park with rolling green hills, trees and a round blue pond on the left. A woman with dark hair in a yellow dress stands on the grass at the edge of the pond, holding a long fishing rod out over the water. Her line drops into the pond and ends in a red-and-white float bobbing near a white duck that is swimming on the left side of the pond. Nobody else is in the scene. A tall black lamp post and a green litter bin stand in the middle background. On the right, a wooden ladder stands upright on the grass beside a winding sandy path, with nothing for it to lean against, which looks odd. In the foreground there is a bed of colourful flowers on the left and a grey rock at the bottom centre.
