A small girl in a pink dress points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of a tree. On the right an older grey-haired woman in a teal top walks away carrying a yellow shopping bag, and a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps a pile of leaves with a broom. A red bicycle stands in the front centre leaning on a post, but its wheels are drawn as squares, which is impossible.
