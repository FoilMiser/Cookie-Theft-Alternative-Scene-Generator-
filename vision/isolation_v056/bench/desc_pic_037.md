# pic_037

## Free description
A sunny park scene with a pond on the left, a lamp post in the middle, a winding path on the right and green hills behind. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands at the edge of the pond holding a brown paper bag and tossing bits of bread to a white duck. The duck is swimming towards her and pecking at bread pieces floating on the water, leaving little wake lines behind it. On the right, a white goat with a red collar is grazing on a tuft of grass; its rope runs to a short wooden stake in the ground, so it is tethered. A boy in a yellow top and orange shorts stands on the path holding the string of a red balloon. An empty wooden bench sits next to the path. Nothing looks clearly wrong; the scene is calm.
