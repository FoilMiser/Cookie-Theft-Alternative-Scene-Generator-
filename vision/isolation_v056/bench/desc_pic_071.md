# pic_071

## Free description
A sunny day in a park with a winding sandy path, a pond, trees, a lamp post and a bin. On the left an older man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. Next to him on the bench is an open sandwich, and a blue bird is standing right by it with its beak at the sandwich, apparently pecking at or stealing his food while he is not looking. On the right a man in a green shirt and beige trousers stands at the edge of the pond holding a paper bag and tossing bread crumbs to a white duck in the water, which is eating them. In the bottom-left corner is an empty red wheelbarrow. There is a grey rock at the front and a flower bed with colourful flowers at the bottom right. The main "about to happen" moment is the bird making off with the reading man's sandwich.
