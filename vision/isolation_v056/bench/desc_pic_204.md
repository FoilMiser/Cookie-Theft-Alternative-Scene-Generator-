# pic_204

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left, a wooden fence and a gate on the right. An old man with grey hair, a blue-grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands beside the flowerbed. He is watering the flowers, but instead of a watering can he is pouring water out of a frying pan, which is strange. A stream of water arcs from the pan onto the flowers. A red wheelbarrow stands empty on the lawn at the front, and there are a bush and two potted plants by the fence. No one else is present.

## Inventory
- Old man in cap and brown waistcoat (left-centre, middle): holding a frying pan out and pouring water onto the flowers.
- Frying pan (held by man): being used like a watering can, water pouring out.
- Flowerbed (left): colourful flowers receiving the water.
- Red wheelbarrow (centre, front): empty.
- Bush and two potted plants (right, back), house (left), gate and path (right), sun and clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The frying pan is the wrong tool for watering; it seems to be the deliberate oddity. It could also be read as a dark saucepan or ladle, but the shape is a frying pan.
- The amount of water coming from a shallow pan looks more than it could hold.
