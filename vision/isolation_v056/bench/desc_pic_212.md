# pic_212

## Free description
A busy, sunny playground with a railing fence at the back, a slide on the left and a tree on the right. A yellow balloon is floating away high in the sky, and a girl in a pink dress holding red and purple balloons reaches up toward it, so it seems to be hers. An older woman in a teal top walks along carrying a cardboard box. An older man in a cap sits on a bench reading a red book while a blue bird pecks at a sandwich beside him. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the tree. In the front, a brown dog eats a pink ice cream that has fallen on the ground while a boy in yellow holding an empty cone cries. A white spotted dog crouches playfully by a tennis ball, a sandpit holds a bucket and spade, and a red bicycle on the right has square wheels, which is impossible.
