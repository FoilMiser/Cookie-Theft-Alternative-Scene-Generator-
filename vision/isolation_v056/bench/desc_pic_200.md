# pic_200

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house (red roof, blue door) on the left, a wooden fence across the back, a gate on the right and a paved path on the right side. In the front left-centre, a woman in a yellow dress holds a teal watering can over the round flower bed, watering the flowers. In the middle, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is pegging a wet white T-shirt on the washing line, which drips water; jeans and a striped towel already hang there and a laundry basket full of clothes sits on the grass by his feet. On the right, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts stands on the path flying a yellow kite high in the sky. An empty bench is at the front left, and there are two potted plants and a red wheelbarrow on the lawn. The water from the watering can looks odd: it spreads out in a wide stream and drops seem to spurt upward above the flowers rather than falling down.
