# pic_078

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house on the left, a wooden fence across the back, a gate and paved path on the right. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence pink with a roller on a long pole; a few planks are already pink, and a paint tin and a paint tray with pink paint sit on the grass by her feet. Next to her a man in a red T-shirt and jeans is sweeping a pile of leaves with a broom. A small white dog with brown spots is walking on the path on the right; its paws are pink and a trail of pink paw prints leads from the paint tray across the grass to it, so it has clearly stepped in the paint. On the far left by the blue door a brown dog sits holding a yellow garden hose in its mouth, apparently chewing it. In the middle front, a man in a green T-shirt carries a cardboard box across the lawn, and a little girl in a pink dress is waving with one hand raised. A flower bed is next to the house.
