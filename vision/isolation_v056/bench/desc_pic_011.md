# pic_011

## Free description
A busy, sunny playground in a park with a railing fence behind. A small girl in a pink dress holds a red and a purple balloon and reaches up towards a yellow balloon that has floated away into the sky with its string trailing - she seems to have let it go. In the front centre, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and covers his face as if crying, because his ice cream has fallen on the ground and a brown dog is licking it up. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in a tree. An old man in a cap sits on a bench reading a red book, while a blue bird on the bench pecks at his sandwich behind him without him noticing. An elderly woman with glasses walks across carrying a cardboard box. A white spotted dog plays with a tennis ball near the slide. There is also a red bike, a sandbox with a bucket and spade, a red bird on the fence and a rock.
