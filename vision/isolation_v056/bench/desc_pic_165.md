A busy, sunny park with a slide, a sandpit, a bench and a tree behind a railing fence. A girl in a pink dress holds a red and a purple balloon and reaches up toward a yellow balloon floating away into the sky. An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses walks along carrying a cardboard box. An older man in a cap and brown waistcoat sits on the bench reading a red book, while a blue bird beside him pecks at his sandwich on the bench without him noticing. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the tree. At the front, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is crying, holding an empty ice-cream cone, while a brown dog licks the dropped pink ice cream off the ground. Nearby a white spotted dog play-bows next to a tennis ball, and a red bicycle with square wheels stands against a post.
