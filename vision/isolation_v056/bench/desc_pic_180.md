## Free description
A sunny park with a round pond on the left, a lamp post and a green bin in the middle, a winding sandy path and a big tree on the right, and hills behind. A woman with dark hair in a yellow dress stands at the edge of the pond fishing with a long rod; her line and red-and-white float are in the water. A white duck is swimming in the pond near the float. On the right, a wooden ladder stands upright on the grass beside the path, leaning against nothing — it is standing on its own in the open, which looks impossible or at least very odd. A flower bed is in the front left and a grey rock in the front centre. No one else is around.
