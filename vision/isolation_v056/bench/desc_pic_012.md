# pic_012

## Free description
A back garden beside a cream house with a red roof and blue door, under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds. In the middle of the garden a girl or young woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the wooden fence pink with a long-handled paint roller; three fence boards are already bright pink and the rest are still plain brown. A tin of pink paint stands on the grass by her feet. To her right an older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, points up at the sky, as if warning that rain is coming and the fresh paint will get wet. A small white puppy with brown ears lies asleep on the grass at the front. A red wheelbarrow and a teal watering can sit on the lawn near the front, and a paved path runs to a wooden gate on the right. A birdhouse on a tall pole stands behind the older woman. Rain seems about to start.
