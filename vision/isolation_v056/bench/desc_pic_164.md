# pic_164

## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, trees, a lamp post, a winding sandy path on the left and a round pond on the right. An older man in a grey flat cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands by the pond holding a paper bag and throwing pieces of bread to a white duck swimming in the pond; bread bits float on the water near the duck. On the left a white goat with a red collar is tied by a rope to a small wooden stake and is eating a tuft of grass beside the path. At the front right a girl in a pink dress walks along licking a pink ice-cream cone. A grey rock sits on the grass at the front and a green rubbish bin stands in the bottom right corner. It looks like a calm, ordinary day in the park.
