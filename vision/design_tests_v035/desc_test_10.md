# desc_test_10

## 1. Free description

It is a sunny, windy day in a back garden. On the left is a cream-coloured house with a red roof, a window and a blue door, and a wooden fence runs across the back with a gate on the right and green bushes and a tree behind it. In the middle, a bearded man in a green shirt and beige trousers has both arms raised high and his mouth open in surprise. He is looking up at a red baseball cap flying through the air just above and to the left of him. White wind lines and a few small leaves stream past the cap, so the wind seems to have just blown the cap off his head, and he is grabbing for it. It looks like the cap might sail past his hands, towards the flower bed or the house. On the right, a smiling boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on a red swing on a dark metal swing frame, with his eyes closed. The swing is tilted to the left, and short motion lines behind him show that he is swinging. In the foreground there is an empty grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can, and an orange cat lies curled up asleep on the stone path that leads to the gate. There is a flower bed with colourful flowers to the left of the man. Nothing looks clearly impossible.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous

- The cap and the man's arms can be read two ways: (a) the wind has just blown his cap off and he is grabbing for it, which fits his surprised face and the wind lines, or (b) he has just thrown the cap up into the air. Reading (a) seems more likely.
- At first glance the red cap could also pass for a red bird or a frisbee. The brim and crown shape show it is a cap.
- I could not tell what the small things next to the cap are. They look like leaves (two green, one brown), but they could be seeds or bits of debris.
- The cat is small and its pose is hard to read. It is most likely curled up asleep, but it could also be lying on its side or back. There is a small white patch next to it, which may be a white paw or the tip of its tail.
- The boy's closed eyes could mean he is enjoying the swing or that he is laughing. Either way he looks happy, and he seems not to have noticed the man's cap.
- Nothing seems to be floating or passing through anything apart from the cap, which is flying on purpose because of the wind. The wheelbarrow and watering can just stand on the grass and do not seem to be part of the action.
