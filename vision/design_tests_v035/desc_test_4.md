## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a stone path running from the gate down to the bottom right corner. In the middle of the lawn a bearded man in a green T-shirt and tan trousers stands with both arms stretched up. His eyes are wide and he looks surprised. Just above and to the left of his hands, an orange cap is flying through the air. White wind lines blow from the right towards the cap, and a couple of small green leaves are blowing with it. It looks as if the wind has just blown his cap off his head and he is reaching up to catch it, but the cap is already out of reach and may carry on to the left towards the tree and the house. On the right a smiling child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on a red swing on a dark metal swing set, eyes closed, with motion lines showing the swing moving. In the front an orange cat is curled up asleep on the grass, near a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can, and on the left there is a round bed of colourful flowers. The only slightly odd thing is that the wind is strong enough to take the man's cap, yet the tree, the bushes and the flowers stand perfectly still.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The cap: most likely the wind has just blown it off and the man is trying to grab it. Another reading is that he has just tossed it up himself (in celebration or play) and the wind is carrying it. Given the wind lines and his surprised face, the "blown off" reading seems stronger.
- I can't tell whether the man is looking at the cap or straight out of the picture. His eyes look wide and forward rather than clearly turned up towards the cap.
- The wind seems to affect only the cap and a few leaves. The tree, bushes, flowers, the man's clothes and the child's hair don't move at all, which is a little inconsistent with a gust strong enough to take a hat.
- The cap seems to float a fair way from his hands with nothing obviously holding it up. That fits being blown, but it could also look like it is hovering.
- The child could be a boy or a girl. The short hair and yellow shirt make me read it as a boy, but I'm not certain.
- The swing's motion lines are on the left only, so it's not clear whether the child is swinging forwards or backwards.
- Nothing looks like it is passing through anything else. The cat, wheelbarrow and watering can are separate from each other, and the child sits within the swing frame without overlapping its legs.
