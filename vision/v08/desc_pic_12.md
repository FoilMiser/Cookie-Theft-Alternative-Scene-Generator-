# Picture 12

## Free description

This is a busy sunny day in a park with green hills, a pond with cattails on the right, and a winding path on the left. A girl in a pink dress is pulling on the string of a purple kite that is stuck in the branches of a tree. Further up the path, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands eating a pink ice cream with his eyes closed. Next to him, a man in a blue hoodie is digging a hole in the grass with a long-handled spade, with a pile of dirt beside the hole. In the middle stands a tall black lamp post with a blue bird perched on top, and a purple balloon is floating away just beside it. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts reaches up for the balloon, but it is already out of his reach. In front, a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench eating a pink ice cream, and a white dog with brown spots is rearing up beside her, apparently hoping for some. A brown dog in the front is crouched in a play bow on the path. In the middle of the lawn a black-and-white cat is creeping toward a red bird pecking at the grass, as if about to pounce. On the right, an elderly woman with grey hair is pouring water from a watering can, but the stream of water arcs upward into the air toward the pond instead of falling onto the flowers below. That rising water looks impossible. There is also a grey rock and a green trash bin.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The water from the watering can goes up into the air instead of down, which looks impossible. It could alternatively be read as water rising from the pond into the can, but it clearly connects to the can's spout.
- The digging tool: the bottom is a spade blade in the hole, but near the man's hands at the top of the handle there is a small pronged shape. It could be a fork head (making it look like a pitchfork) or just his hand/a D-shaped handle grip. So the tool reads as a spade, possibly with a fork-like top.
- The white dog could be standing up begging for the woman's ice cream or jumping up at her. It might also be read as about to jump onto the bench.
- The brown dog's pose could be a play bow, a stretch, or sniffing the ground.
- The balloon could belong to the boy, who has just let go of it, or it could simply be floating past and he is trying to catch it.
- The cat is clearly stalking the red bird. It looks about to pounce, but it has not happened yet.
