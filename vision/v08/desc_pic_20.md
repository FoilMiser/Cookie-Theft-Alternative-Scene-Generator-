# Picture 20

## Free description
A sunny park with a sun in the top right, two clouds, green hills and hedges at the back. On the left there is an oval pond with bulrushes, a small tree and bushes, and a flower bed of red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers in the front left. In the middle a woman with long dark hair, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, stands by a tall black lamp post happily eating a pink ice-cream cone with her eyes closed and rosy cheeks. In the front centre a small boy with dark skin and curly black hair, wearing a yellow shirt and orange shorts, holds an empty cone. His pink scoop with sprinkles has fallen to the ground at the bottom of the picture, and a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has its head down at it, licking it up. Next to the boy a brown-and-white dog with floppy ears and a red collar crouches in a playful "bow" (front down, rear up), tongue out, facing the boy. On the right a man with dark skin, a beard and short dark hair, wearing a green shirt and khaki trousers, is running towards the left along and across a winding path. Behind him is an empty wooden bench and a large tree, and a green rubbish bin is partly visible in the bottom-left corner. The contrast between the woman enjoying her ice cream and the boy who has lost his stands out; nothing looks physically impossible.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The brown dog's intention: it could be inviting the boy to play, or begging for or eyeing the boy's empty cone.
- The running man could be out jogging, chasing one of the dogs, or hurrying towards the boy; nothing makes it clear.
- It is not clear who the dogs belong to, or whether the woman and the boy are together.
- The fallen ice cream and the white dog's head are partly cut off by the bottom edge of the picture, so the puddle is only partly visible.
- The woman stands directly in front of the lamp post, so her body overlaps it; she reads as standing in front of it, not touching it.
