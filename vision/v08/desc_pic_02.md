# Picture 02

## Free description

This is the same kind of park on a bright sunny day, with a yellow sun at the top left and two small white clouds. An older man in a blue-grey flat cap, white long-sleeved shirt, brown vest and dark trousers sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket. His eyes are closed and he is smiling with rosy cheeks, looking relaxed and content. A brown picnic basket sits beside him. A white duck swims on the blue pond behind him. To the left of the winding sandy path, a wooden ladder stands upright on the grass by itself. It is leaning slightly but there is nothing for it to lean against, which looks impossible. Apart from the man and the duck, the park is empty: there is a lamp post with nothing on it, two trash bins, a flower bed, a grey rock, trees and bushes. Nothing much seems to be about to happen, apart from the ladder, which looks like it should fall over.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The ladder stands upright and tilted with nothing holding it up: no wall, tree or person. It even casts a small shadow at its base, so it appears to be balancing on its own, which is impossible.
- The man's closed eyes could mean he is enjoying the sun with his eyes shut, or that he is dozing off.
- The man sits at the very end of the blanket with his legs stretched off it toward the basket. That looks slightly awkward but not wrong.
- Why a ladder is in the park at all is unclear: no one is using it, and there is nothing nearby to climb.
