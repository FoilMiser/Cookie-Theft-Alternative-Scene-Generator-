## Free description

This is a sunny back garden behind a house with a red roof, enclosed by a tall wooden fence with a gate on the right. A small child with dark skin, curly hair, a yellow top and orange shorts has just thrown a red ball. A dotted line shows the ball's path rising from the child's hand up into the air. A man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands with his arms stretched out and hands together, ready to catch it. At the blue back door of the house, a brown dog sits with its red leash in its mouth and its eyes shut happily, as if asking to go for a walk. In the middle front, a white dog with brown spots crouches in a play bow, front low and tail up, near a red food bowl full of kibble on the grass. On the right, a woman with dark hair in a ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers sweeps with a yellow broom beside a stone path. There is a colourful flower bed by the house, two potted plants by the fence, and a tree behind the house. Nothing looks clearly impossible. It is a cheerful, busy garden scene, and the ball is about to be caught.
