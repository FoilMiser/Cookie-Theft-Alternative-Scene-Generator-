# Picture 04

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a house with a red roof and blue door on the right and a tall wooden fence behind. An elderly woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal top and grey skirt reaches up to peg a white T-shirt onto a clothesline. A yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans are already hanging there. A man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller or brush, and a section of the fence near the house is already blue. At the front right, a girl with a brown ponytail in a blue hoodie is watering the flower bed by pouring water from a dark frying pan. That is the strangest thing in the picture, because a teal watering can sits unused on the grass at the bottom left. On the left there is a metal swing set with one empty red swing, a stone path leading to a wooden gate, and a grey wheelbarrow lying on the grass. Everyone is busy with chores.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The girl's watering tool looks like a frying pan or skillet: round, dark, with a handle. Another reading is a dark saucepan. Either way it is not a watering can, even though there is a watering can nearby.
- The man's tool could be a paint roller on a pole or a large brush. The end is blue and lumpy. There is no paint tin visible anywhere.
- The water pouring from the pan is drawn as a light-blue stream with drops falling onto the edge of the flower bed. That is fine, but it is unclear how a pan would hold that much water.
- The empty swing and the unused wheelbarrow are just sitting there with nobody using them.
