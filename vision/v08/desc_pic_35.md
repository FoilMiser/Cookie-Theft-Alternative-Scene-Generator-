# Picture 35

## Free description
This is a sunny playground in a park, with a low metal railing fence and hedges along the back. On the left, a girl in a pink dress stands at the very top of a tall slide with both arms raised and a big happy smile, eyes closed, as if celebrating before she slides down. The slide has a red frame, a dark grey ladder on its left side, and a yellow-and-orange chute sloping down to the right. On the right, two people are on a red seesaw: an older girl or young woman with a brown ponytail, blue hoodie and black trousers sits on the left end, and a smaller child with dark curly hair, a yellow top and orange shorts sits on the right end holding the handle. The seesaw is nearly level, tipped slightly down on the bigger person's side; her feet are on the ground while the small child's legs dangle in the air, and the two are facing each other. At the front there is a sandbox with a red bucket and a blue spade in it, and a grey rock on the grass beside it. A green rubbish bin stands by the fence, and a big tree is on the far right. Nothing looks clearly impossible, though standing up with arms raised at the top of a slide is a little unsafe.
