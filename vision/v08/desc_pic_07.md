# Picture 07

## Free description

This is a very busy, sunny, windy back garden with a house on the right, a tall wooden fence, a gate on the left and a stone path. Wind lines blow leaves across the top left, and an orange hat is flying through the air. An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses throws both arms up in alarm, apparently because it was her hat. At the far left by the gate, a small child in a yellow top and orange shorts reaches up toward a red balloon floating above. In the centre, a bearded man in a green shirt holds his hands out to catch a red ball that a woman in a blue hoodie on the right has just thrown (a dotted line shows its path). A woman in a purple top walks while looking at a phone, and a second red ball is lying on the grass just ahead of her, so she might trip over it. A man in a red T-shirt carries a cardboard box toward the path. On the lawn, a white goat nibbles grass, and a white dog with brown spots sits holding an open book as if reading it, which is impossible. An orange fish is sitting in the leaves of the tree behind the house, which is also impossible. At the bottom right, a girl in a pink dress sits on a bench reading a red book.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The balloon: the string seems to end near the child's raised hand. It could be read as the child holding the balloon, or as the child having just let go and reaching up to grab it back. The child's pose (both arms up) fits either reading.
- The hat: it is not certain that the hat belongs to the elderly woman, but she is the one reacting to it, with arms up and a shocked face.
- The woman with the phone: the ball on the grass is to the left of her and in front of her, but it is not certain she is walking toward it. She looks like she is walking forward and to the left, so she may be about to trip over it.
- A fish in a tree and a dog reading a book are clearly impossible.
- The bearded man's arms are out in front, which most likely means catching. They could also be read as gesturing.
- There are two red balls, one in the air and one on the ground. They look identical, which could be intentional or confusing.
