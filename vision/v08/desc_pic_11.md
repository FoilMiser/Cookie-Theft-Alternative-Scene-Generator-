# Picture 11

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a house (red roof, blue door) on the right, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate on the left, and a stepping-stone path leading to the gate. A black-and-white cat is walking along the top of the fence. In the middle, laundry hangs on a clothesline between two wooden posts: a yellow shirt or towel with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans. Below the clothesline stands an older man in a white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers with his hands on his hips, and he is wearing an upside-down blue bucket on his head like a hat, which is very odd. On the left, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers holds the nozzle of a garden hose with both hands, but only a single drop of water is dripping out. The reason seems to be the white goat standing in the front middle of the lawn: it is standing right on top of the hose, squashing it so the water can't get through. The hose runs from a reel by the flower bed near the house, across the lawn under the goat, and up to the man. The nozzle is pointed toward the man with the bucket, so if the goat steps off, the water might spray out suddenly. A flower bed with colourful flowers sits by the house. The odd things are the bucket worn as a hat and the goat blocking the hose.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The blue thing on the older man's head is most likely an upside-down bucket (it has a bucket shape with a rim), but it could also be read as a tall blue hat.
- The man with the hose could be peering at the nozzle wondering why no water comes out, or he could be aiming it at the man with the bucket. It points roughly toward him.
- It is not clear whether the goat is standing on the hose deliberately or by accident. It may simply be looking at the man.
- The older man's expression is hard to read. He could be amused, impatient or waiting.
