On the left there is a fruit and vegetable stall with crates of green, yellow, orange and red produce under a striped canopy, and a cardboard box on the ground beside it. There is also a black street lamp, two flower planters, a bicycle parked in a bike rack, a tree in a round planter, a small café table with two chairs, and a potted plant. Nothing looks impossible. The main mishap is the dropped ice cream being eaten by the dog.
