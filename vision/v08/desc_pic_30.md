## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate on the left, a birdhouse on a post and a big tree peeking over the fence. A house with a red roof, cream walls, a window and a blue door is on the right, and a pale path with stepping stones runs from the gate down the left side. In the middle-left an older man in a blue cap, brown vest, white shirt and dark trousers is raking a small pile of green and brown leaves. Behind him a clothesline between two wooden posts has a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. In the middle-right a bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers holds a teal garden hose and sprays water onto a bed of colourful flowers. The hose loops across the lawn to a hose reel at the front right. At the front left a little girl in a pink dress kicks an orange ball across the grass. Everyone seems busy with ordinary chores or play, and nothing obviously impossible stands out.
