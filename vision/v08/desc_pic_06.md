# Picture 06

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a house on the right, a tall wooden fence, a gate on the left and a stone path. A young man or boy in a red T-shirt and blue jeans holds the nozzle of a garden hose and peers into it, puzzled, because only a single drop of water is coming out. The reason is that a white goat is standing right on top of the hose in the middle of the lawn, squashing it. The hose runs from a hose reel next to the house, across the grass and under the goat's hooves, up to the man. On the left, near the path, a small sprinkler is spraying arcs of water, but they land on a cardboard box sitting on the grass instead of on the lawn or flowers, and no hose appears to connect to the sprinkler. Another cardboard box sits tilted against the fence near the house, looking as if it is tipping over or stuck there. A flower bed with colourful flowers sits in front of the house. It seems likely that the man will soon spray himself in the face if the goat steps off the hose.

- Garden hose: dark teal, running from the hose reel by the house along the ground, under the goat, and up to the man's hands.
- Hose nozzle: in the man's hands, dripping a single drop.
- Hose reel: right, next to the flower bed and the house.
- Sprinkler: left, on the grass next to the stone path, spraying blue dashed arcs of water.
- Cardboard box near the sprinkler: centre-left, on the grass, getting wet (water drops on top).
- Cardboard box by the fence: right, tilted at an angle against the fence near the house.
- Flower bed: right, in front of the house, with colourful flowers.
- House: right, with a red roof, a window and a blue door with a grey step.
- Wooden fence and gate: across the back; the gate is on the left.
- Stone path: left, with stepping-stone spots.
- Tree: behind the house.
- Sun and clouds: the sun is at the top left, with two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The sprinkler has no visible hose or pipe connected to it, yet it is spraying water. It might be fed from underground, or it is a drawing oddity.
- The box by the fence is tilted at an odd angle. It could be resting on the ground and leaning against the fence, or it could look as if it is floating or stuck on the fence partway up. Its bottom corner is at about the base of the fence, so it is probably resting there, but the angle makes it look unstable.
- The sprinkler watering a cardboard box looks like a mistake by the gardener (it is aimed wrongly) rather than something impossible.
- There is a small grey shadow-shape behind the flower bed, near the hose reel, that does not seem to belong to anything.
- I could not tell whether the man is a teenage boy or a young man.
