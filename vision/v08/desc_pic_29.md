# Picture 29

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny day in a park with a pond, a winding path, a big tree on the left and hills behind. On the left a girl with a brown ponytail and a blue top stands on the path holding a kite string; her purple kite is up against the leaves of the big tree, so it may be stuck in the tree. Behind her a woman in a yellow dress walks a brown-and-white dog on a red lead. In the middle a bearded man in a green shirt stands by a lamp post throwing crumbs to a white duck swimming toward him in the pond, and a blue bird flies near the top of the lamp post. In front of the pond an older woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a red-and-white picnic blanket with a basket, and a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands beside her holding a yellow balloon. On the right an older man in a cap and brown vest waters a flower bed with a teal watering can. At the very front an orange cat creeps low toward a red bird pecking at the ground, and it looks like the cat is about to pounce. One thing looks wrong: the grey wheelbarrow beside the bench has a square wheel with an X on it instead of a round one.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The kite could be (a) caught in the big tree, since it is drawn on top of the leaves with its tail hanging over them, or (b) flying low in front of the tree. The girl reaching up could be flying it or trying to tug it free.
- The wheelbarrow's wheel is square with an X, which looks impossible for a wheel. It might instead be a small crate or box sitting where the wheel should be, but it is placed exactly where a wheel goes. The wheelbarrow also shows only one handle.
- I couldn't tell for sure whether the woman with the dog is facing and walking left, behind the dog, or is being pulled. She seems to be walking left.
- The older woman and the boy may be together (grandmother and grandson), but nothing confirms it.
