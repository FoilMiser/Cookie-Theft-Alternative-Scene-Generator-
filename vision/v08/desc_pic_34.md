# Picture 34

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a house on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a gate and stone path on the right. In the middle an elderly woman with grey hair, round glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt stands with both arms thrown up in the air and her mouth open in an "O", as if startled. Just above and to her right, an orange cap is flying through the air near a washing line, and white wind lines and a few leaves blowing from the left show a strong gust of wind. It looks like the wind has just blown the cap away (either off her head or off the line) and she is reaching or grabbing for it. On the washing line between two posts hang a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes, held by clothes pegs. Off to the right, a white goat with small horns has its head down and is eating a tuft of grass on the lawn. Oddly, there is an orange fish sitting in the leaves of the tree behind the house, which is impossible. In the front left corner there is an empty wooden bench, and next to the house there is a bed of colourful flowers.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fish in the tree is impossible (fish do not sit in trees); it does not look like a kite or a toy, it looks like an ordinary fish drawing placed in the leaves.
- It is not clear where the cap came from: it could have blown off the woman's head, or off the washing line (there is a gap on the line to the right of the towel). Both readings fit.
- The woman's raised arms could be read as reaching to catch the cap, or as throwing her arms up in surprise/alarm; her hands are close to, but not touching, the cap.
- A goat loose in a home garden is unusual; it could be a pet or an animal that has wandered in. It might be read as a sheep at a quick glance, but the small horns and shape suggest a goat.
- The goat is drawn quite plainly (all white, simple outline), but nothing about it looks physically wrong.
