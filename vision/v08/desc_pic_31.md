# Picture 31

## Free description
This is a sunny town street or pedestrian square with a row of colourful two-storey shops (pink, pale green, peach and cream) with striped awnings, plus a fruit stall, a lamp post and a tree in a planter. On the left a woman in a yellow dress pushes a teal pram with a baby in it past the fruit stall. In the front a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks along reading a red book instead of looking where she is going. A blue bucket and a white dog with brown spots lying flat on the pavement are just ahead of her, so she looks likely to trip over one of them. In the middle a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts has thrown a red ball high into the air, shown by a dotted line, and a woman in a blue top with a ponytail holds her hands up ready to catch it. An older woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a bench reading a red book. On the right a bearded man in a green shirt is sweeping a small pile of litter and dust using a giant silver fork instead of a broom, which looks absurd. There are also rubbish bins on the left and right.

- I couldn't tell whether the dog is asleep, resting or hurt; it is lying flat on its side with its eyes seemingly closed.
