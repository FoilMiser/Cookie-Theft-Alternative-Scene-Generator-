# Picture 13

## Free description

This is a sunny day at a park playground with a dark railing fence and green bushes behind. A girl with a ponytail in a blue top and black trousers is flying an orange kite that is high up in the sky near a cloud. Beside her, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is waving with one hand raised. Behind them, a man in an orange cap and an orange high-visibility safety vest is walking while reading or writing in a small red book. On the far left, near the red slide, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts reaches up with both arms toward a yellow balloon that is floating away above him, just out of reach. On the right, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a bench holding something small and red to her mouth, and a brown dog with a red collar stands beside her looking up at her. In the front, an older man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is reading a red book while sitting, but there is no chair or bench under him at all. He is sitting on thin air, which is impossible. Around him, an orange cat is running toward a blue bird that is pecking at the grass, a black-and-white cat is lying asleep on the grass, and a white dog with brown spots is lying down in the front right. The seesaw on the right is empty, and there is a sandbox with a red bucket and blue spade, a grey rock, and a cardboard box on the grass.
