# Picture 28

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park, with a bright sun in the top left, two white clouds, and green rolling hills and a line of trees in the background. The only person is an older man standing in the middle of the picture beside a tall black lamp post. He has grey hair and wears a blue cap, a brown vest over a white long-sleeved shirt, and dark trousers. He holds a small white cup or bag in one hand and reaches the other arm out toward a pond on the right, tossing little brown crumbs or seeds into the air. A white duck in the pond is swimming toward him, with ripple lines behind it, so it looks like he is feeding the duck. A sandy path winds from the back of the park down to the front left, past a big tree and an empty wooden bench. The odd thing is a cardboard box hanging in mid-air near the top of the path, tilted at an angle, with its shadow on the grass well below it, as if it is floating or falling. The front of the scene also has a grey rock, a bed of colourful flowers and green rubbish bins.
