# Picture 05

## Free description

This is a sunny town square or pedestrian street lined with colourful shop buildings that have striped awnings. On the left, an old man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown vest waves with one raised hand; little lines show the hand is moving. Next to him, a blue ball is stuck in the leaves of a small tree in a planter, and a boy with dark skin, curly hair, a yellow shirt and orange shorts points up at it. Nearby, a woman with a brown ponytail in a blue hoodie is taking a photo with a camera, and a teal bicycle is parked in a bike rack behind her. In the centre, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a bench reading a red book. A big sandwich lies on the bench beside her, and a blue bird has landed on the bench next to it, looking like it is about to steal it. A woman in a purple top sweeps with a yellow broom near a lamp post, and a bearded man in a green shirt carrying a yellow bag walks past a fruit stall with crates of red, orange, yellow and green fruit. Several things are impossible. A red bicycle parked in front of the stall seems to have square grey wheels. A brown dog sits on the pavement holding an open book or newspaper and reading it. At the bottom, a black-and-white cat is creeping toward a red bird pecking at the ground, so it looks like the cat is about to pounce.
