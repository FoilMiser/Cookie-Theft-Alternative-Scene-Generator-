# Picture 16

## Free description
A sunny park scene with a pond, a sun in the top right, two white clouds, green rolling hills and hedges in the background. In the middle-left there is an oval blue pond with bulrushes (cattails) at both ends and a white duck with an orange beak swimming near its left side. In the front, a man with brown hair, wearing a red T-shirt and blue jeans, holds a green watering can. The stream of water coming from the spout does not fall down onto the flower bed below him. Instead it curves up and away to the left and ends in drops over the pond, as if the water were flowing uphill. The flower bed with red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers is at the lower left and is not being watered. To the right of the pond a young woman with a brown ponytail, a blue hoodie and dark trousers stands fishing; her rod reaches out over the pond and the line drops to a red-and-white float bobbing on the water with ripples around it. Behind her are a tall black lamp post and a green rubbish bin. On the right there is an empty wooden bench, a winding sandy footpath leading into the distance, and a large tree. A grey rock sits in the front centre, and another green bin is partly visible in the bottom-left corner. The upward-flowing water is the clearly impossible thing in the picture.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Water stream: it rises from the can's spout up to the pond. It can be read two ways: the man is pouring water up into the pond, against gravity, or water is somehow being drawn up out of the pond into the can. Either way it is physically wrong, and it misses the flowers, which are below the spout.
- The drops over the pond near the duck could be the end of the odd stream or splashes made by the duck.
- The man's gaze: his head is turned left and slightly down, towards the can/stream area rather than towards the flowers.
- The fisher's age: she could be a teenage girl or a young woman.
- The fishing woman stands a little back from the water's edge on the grass, which is plausible but means the rod has to stretch quite far over the pond.
