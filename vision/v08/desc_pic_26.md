## Free description
This is a back garden on a sunny day, with a tall wooden fence across the back, trees above it, a sun in the top left, two white clouds, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the left a little girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes sits on the red seat of a dark grey A-frame swing set. In the middle a man with brown hair, a red shirt and blue jeans stands on tiptoe with his arms raised, pegging a white T-shirt onto a washing line strung between two wooden posts. A yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans already hang on the line next to it. On the right a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is watering the flower bed by pouring water out of a frying pan, which is clearly the strange part. A proper teal watering can lies unused on the grass at the front, and a hose reel stands unused right beside the flower bed. Behind the fence, above the swing set, there is a small birdhouse with a red roof on a pole. The garden also has a wooden gate on the left, a pale stepping-stone path, and a grey wheelbarrow at the front.
