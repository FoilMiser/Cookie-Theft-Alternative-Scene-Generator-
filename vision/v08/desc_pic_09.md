# Picture 09

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden behind a house with a red roof and a blue door on the left. Across the back runs a tall wooden fence, and one section of it, near the middle-right, has been painted bright blue. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands in front of the fence holding a long-handled brush with blue paint on it. He is painting the fence, though his head is tipped back and he seems to be looking upward. Behind him on the grass is a tray of blue paint, and a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has clearly stepped in it: a trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray to the dog, and its paws are blue. The dog is walking left toward a bed of colourful flowers and the house, so it looks about to track paint further or walk into the flowers. On the right, a little girl in a pink dress sits on a swing on a dark metal swing set. In the front of the garden a lawn sprinkler is spraying arcs of water, and the water is landing on a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is getting wet. A green watering can, a hose reel, a wooden bench and a stepping-stone path to a wooden gate complete the scene. The odd things are the dog leaving painted paw prints, the box being soaked by the sprinkler, and the sprinkler not seeming to be connected to any hose.
