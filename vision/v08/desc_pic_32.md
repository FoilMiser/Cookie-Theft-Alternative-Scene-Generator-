# Picture 32

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a gate on the right and a stepping-stone path running down the right side. A boy in a yellow top and orange shorts stands near the fence on the left flying a yellow kite high in the sky. On a bench at the front left a man in a blue hoodie happily eats a pink ice-cream cone while a white spotted dog stands beside him looking up, hoping for some. In the centre a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is drinking from a white cup while sitting in a chair pose, but there is no chair or seat under him, which is impossible. To his right a bearded man in a green shirt holds his hands up to catch a red ball that a little girl in a pink dress on the path has thrown to him, shown by a dotted line. A clothesline holds blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel, and a black cat walks along the top of the fence on the right near a birdhouse. There is also a flower bed and a hose reel near the house.

- The man on the bench may have his eyes closed in enjoyment or may be squinting; I couldn't tell which.
