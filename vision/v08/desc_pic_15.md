# Picture 15

## Free description
This is a sunny day at a park playground. There is a bright sun in the top left and two small white clouds, with a row of green hedges and a long dark metal railing fence running across the back. On the left stands a playground slide with a red frame, a grey-blue ladder, a small blue platform at the top and a long yellow-and-orange chute sloping down to the right; nobody is on it. In the front left there is a wooden sandbox where a little girl with dark hair, wearing a pink dress, kneels and builds a sandcastle that has a small red flag on top. In the front centre-right a young woman (or teenage girl) with a brown ponytail, a blue hoodie and dark trousers stands on one leg and swings her other leg forward to kick an orange ball that sits on the grass right at her foot, so the ball looks about to be kicked away. Her head is turned to the left and her arms are not swinging; one hand seems to be tucked behind her back. Further back there is an empty wooden bench, a green rubbish bin by the fence, a big tree on the far right and some round bushes. A grey rock sits on the grass just left of the sandbox. Nothing looks clearly impossible, though the kicker's arms and posture look a little stiff for someone kicking a ball.
