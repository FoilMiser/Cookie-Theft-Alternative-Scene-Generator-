## Free description
This is a back garden on a sunny day with a tall wooden fence across the back, trees above it, a sun in the top left and two white clouds. On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door. A man in an orange cap, an orange high-visibility vest with white reflective stripes, a grey shirt and blue trousers stands in front of the fence, painting it. He holds a paintbrush with blue paint on the tip. A section of the fence about three boards wide, just to his left, has already been painted bright blue from top to bottom, while the rest of the fence is still plain wood. Oddly, I can't see a paint tin or tray anywhere near him. On the left are a wooden gate in the fence, a pale stepping-stone path, two potted plants, and a small bush at the bottom of the fence. In the front are a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow. On the right, near the house, are a round bed of colourful flowers, a hose reel and a wooden bench. Nobody else is in the garden.
