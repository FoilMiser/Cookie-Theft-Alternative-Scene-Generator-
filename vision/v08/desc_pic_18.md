## Free description
A sunny town street with a paved pedestrian square in front of a row of four two-storey shops: a cream building with a red-and-white striped awning, a peach building with a teal door, a pale green building with a green-and-white awning and a grey door, and a pink building with an orange-and-white awning and a purple door. The sun is in the top right and there are two clouds. A red bird with a yellow beak flies with its wings raised, right at the roofline of the peach building. In the front centre a small boy with dark skin and curly black hair, wearing a yellow shirt and orange shorts, holds an empty ice-cream cone and looks dismayed. His pink scoop with sprinkles lies melting on the paving, and a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is bent over it, licking it up. To the right a red bicycle stands in a grey metal bike rack. On the right a woman with a dark ponytail, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, is running or jogging towards the left, in the direction of the boy and the bike. Behind her stands a fruit and vegetable stall with a teal-and-white awning and crates of red, orange, yellow and green produce, with a cardboard box on the ground beside it. Other things on the square: a tree in a round planter on the left, a green rubbish bin, three flower boxes in front of the shops, a black lamp post, and potted plants in the bottom-left and bottom-right corners. Nothing is clearly impossible.
