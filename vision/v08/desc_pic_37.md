# Picture 37

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny town street or shopping square paved with grey tiles, in front of a row of four coloured shop buildings with striped awnings. On the far left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping with an ordinary broom. Next to her, an elderly man with a grey moustache, blue cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is walking along reading a red book, and a cardboard box sits on the ground right in his path, so he looks about to trip over it. In the middle, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers sits on a bench drinking from a white cup, and behind him a woman with a brown ponytail and blue hoodie is pouring water from a teal watering can onto a planter of flowers. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes walks past a market stall with crates of red, orange, yellow and green fruit and vegetables, carrying a yellow bag. In the front right, an elderly woman with a grey bun, glasses, teal top and grey skirt is sweeping a pile of dust and litter with a giant silver fork instead of a broom, which is absurd. A red bicycle is parked in a bike rack at the front centre, and there is a lamp post, a tree in a round planter, a second cardboard box by the stall, and a potted plant in the bottom right corner.
