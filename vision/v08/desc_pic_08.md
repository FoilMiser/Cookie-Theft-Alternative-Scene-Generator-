# Picture 08

## Free description

This is a sunny day at a playground in a park. A row of green bushes and a dark railing fence run across the back. On the left, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands right on top of a red-framed slide with both arms raised high and a big grin, as if he is cheering or about to come down the slide. In the middle is a swing set with one red swing seat, and nobody is on it. On the right, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. A large sandwich sits on the bench beside him, and a blue bird has landed right next to it and is pecking at it. The man is looking at his book and does not seem to notice, so the bird looks about to steal or eat his sandwich. In front of him, two girls are on a red seesaw: a bigger girl with a brown ponytail, a blue hoodie and black trousers sits on the low left end with her feet on the ground, and a small girl in a pink dress sits on the high right end with her feet dangling. In the front left there is a sandbox with a red bucket and blue spade in it, and a grey rock beside it. Nothing looks clearly impossible. The main things that seem wrong are the bird going for the man's lunch and the boy standing on top of the slide.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The food on the bench could be a large sandwich or a big burger/sub. It has a bun-like top with red and green layers.
- The bird could already be eating the sandwich, or only be landing beside it about to take a bite. Either way, the man has not noticed.
- The boy on the slide could be cheering or showing off, or getting ready to slide down. He is standing upright on the very top, which looks a bit unsafe.
- The girl in the blue hoodie looks like she is sitting on the very end of the seesaw. Her body sits slightly in front of the beam and her shadow is a little to the left, so at a glance she could look as if she is perching just in front of the seat rather than on it.
- The swing has only one seat and is empty. It is not clear whether anyone has just left it.
