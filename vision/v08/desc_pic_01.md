# Picture 01

## Free description

This is a park on a grey, overcast day. Three big dark storm clouds hang in the sky, but no rain is falling yet. A small red bird is perched on top of a tall black lamp post. At the back left, an elderly woman with grey hair, glasses and a teal top walks on the grass beside a winding sandy path, carrying a yellow bag. In the middle, a girl with a brown ponytail in a blue hoodie is fishing. Her line runs into a round blue pond, where a red-and-white float bobs on the water. In front of the pond, a man in a brown vest, white shirt and dark trousers stands with his hands on his hips. On his head is a blue bucket, worn upside down like a hat, which is the oddest thing in the scene. On the right, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans points up toward the sky with a surprised, open-mouthed face, as if he has noticed the clouds or the bird, and it looks like it might be about to rain. At the bottom of the picture, a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar lies stretched flat on the grass next to a grey rock, apparently asleep. The wooden park bench is empty.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The bucket on the man's head: it is clearly a blue bucket, worn upside down. It could be meant as a joke or a makeshift rain hat, but either way it is not a normal hat.
- What the pointing man is pointing at: it could be the dark clouds, which suggest rain is coming, or the red bird on the lamp post. Both are up and to his left.
- The dog: it could be sleeping, or it could be rolling on its back. The pose is flat and sprawled, and I could not clearly make out which way its legs are facing.
- The man with the bucket seems to have a white or grey patch beside his ear, which could be a sideburn, a beard or hair. It was hard to tell.
- The sky is dark and stormy, but everyone else is calmly carrying on, and nothing shows rain actually falling.
