# Picture 14

## Free description

This is a park playground on a grey, gloomy day: there is no sun, the sky is grey, and three dark storm clouds hang overhead, so it looks like it may rain soon. On the left, a purple kite is stuck in the top of a tree, and a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands with his back to us holding its string, trying to pull it free. In front of him, a small girl in a pink dress sits on the high end of a red seesaw, and a man with a beard in a green shirt and khaki trousers sits on the low end. An older man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat points up at the dark clouds with his mouth open, as if warning everyone about the coming rain. In the middle is a swing set with one empty red swing, a green trash can, and a red slide on the right. On the far right, a young person in a blue hoodie and black trousers is leaping into the air with both arms up and a big smile, with their shadow on the ground below them. In the front centre, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is eating a pink ice cream with her eyes closed, which seems a little out of place on such a grey day. A black-and-white cat is lying asleep on the grass, and there is a sandbox with a red bucket and blue spade and a grey rock in the front right. Nothing is clearly impossible. The odd notes are the kite caught in the tree and people carrying on happily under storm clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The older man could be pointing at the storm clouds (most likely, since he points up and right where the clouds are) or pointing out something else in the sky. He is not pointing toward the kite, which is to the left.
- The person in blue could be jumping for joy, or could have just jumped off the slide or its ladder. Their shadow is separate from their feet, which reads as being in mid-air, though at a glance they could seem to be floating.
- The person in blue could be a teenage boy or girl. It is hard to tell.
- It is not clear whether the boy is flying the kite and it got stuck, or is trying to pull an already-stuck kite down.
- Eating ice cream and playing cheerfully under dark storm clouds is a bit odd but not impossible.
