# Picture 39

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a gate and stone path on the left, and a house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A woman with a brown ponytail, blue hoodie and black trousers stands facing the fence, painting it blue with a paintbrush; a section of about three to four fence boards to her left is already blue. Behind her on the grass is a flat tray of blue paint. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has stepped in the paint tray: its feet are blue and it has left a trail of blue paw prints across the grass as it walks away to the right, toward the flower bed and the house. The woman has her back to the dog and does not seem to have noticed. It looks as if the dog may be about to track blue paint into the flowers or the house. A red dog bowl with food sits on the grass at the front, there is a birdhouse on a pole behind the fence, and an empty wooden bench stands in the front right corner.
