# Description of test_5.png

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a concrete path running from a gate at the back right down to the bottom of the picture. In the middle at the back, a boy in a red shirt and blue trousers stands between two clothesline posts, reaching up with both hands to peg a white T-shirt on the line, next to a pair of blue jeans that is already hanging. In the foreground, a boy in a red cap and blue hooded top holds a garden hose up in front of him with both hands and seems to be looking at the yellow nozzle, which has only a single small blue drop at its tip and no spray of water coming out. The hose runs from a reel by the flower bed along the ground, and a white dog with brown spots is standing right on top of it; just behind the dog's back foot the hose swells into a flattened bulge, so the dog seems to be blocking the water. If the dog steps off, the water might suddenly burst out, possibly at the boy, who is holding the nozzle close to himself. At the right, a man in a green shirt and tan trousers has come along by the gate, walking to the left while reading an open book held in front of him, not looking where he is going. A closed cardboard box sits on the grass just ahead of him at the edge of the path, so he seems about to walk into it or trip over it. An orange cat lies curled on the grass with its eyes closed, apparently asleep, and a red bowl of pet food sits on the grass near the bottom of the picture. Nothing looks impossible, but two small accidents seem about to happen: the water from the hose and the man reaching the box.
