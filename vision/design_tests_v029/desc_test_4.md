## Free description

This is a cartoon of a sunny day in a park, with a bright sun at top left, two white clouds, green hills in the distance, a tall black lamp post, a winding sand-coloured path and a blue pond on the right. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses sits on a wooden bench in the centre-left, reading a red book. A man in a green shirt and tan trousers stands at the near edge of the pond, holding a brown paper bag and reaching out his hand towards a white duck. The duck is in the water with its head down, pecking at pale crumbs that are scattered on the water; several more crumbs lie on the grass beyond the pond's edge, as if they have been dropped or thrown. A small brown and white puppy with a red collar is curled up asleep on the grass at the front, with its eyes closed. The odd thing in the picture is an orange fish sitting in the tree on the left, resting on a bare branch as if it were a bird, which is impossible since fish live in water and there is a pond right there. Otherwise, the scene looks calm and nothing seems about to happen beyond the duck carrying on eating. There is also a flower bed at front right and a green rubbish bin in the bottom right corner.
