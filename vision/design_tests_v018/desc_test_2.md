## Free description

In the middle distance two children are playing catch in front of the fence. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts has his arm out toward a red-and-white ball. A dashed curved line runs from his hand to the ball, and the ball is near a smaller girl in a pink dress, who is reaching out with both arms to catch it. In the front centre, a young woman or older girl with a ponytail, in a purple top and teal trousers, is walking to the right while reading an open book held out in front of her. She is looking at the book and not at the ground, and a closed cardboard box sits right in front of her leading foot, so she looks about to trip over it. On the right, a man in a grey-blue flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat with braces and dark trousers stands on a paved path in front of the gate, waving one raised hand (motion lines show the waving). An orange cat is curled up asleep on the grass near the path in the front right. There is also a colourful flower bed in front of the house and two potted plants on the lawn. The sky has two white clouds and a bright sun in the top right corner.
