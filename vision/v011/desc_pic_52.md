## Free description
This is a sunny back garden: a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate at the right, and trees behind the fence. A woman with dark hair in a ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers stands beside the flower bed on the left. She is watering the flowers with a teal watering can, and a stream of water arcs from the spout onto the blooms. In the centre right, a small child with curly dark hair, a yellow shirt and orange shorts looks up and holds the string of a yellow diamond-shaped kite. The kite flies high near the clouds and has a tail of little bows. A black-and-white cat is walking along the top of the fence. A red pet bowl full of kibble sits on the grass at the front, perhaps the cat's food. Two potted plants stand near the fence, and a paved path leads from the gate to the front right. Everything looks normal and peaceful; nothing seems about to go wrong.
