# Picture 08

## Free description

This is a sunny day on a town street lined with colourful two-storey shops (pink, pale green, peach and cream buildings with striped awnings). On the left a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is pushing a teal pram with a blue hood, and a baby is lying inside it. Behind him is an unattended fruit and vegetable stall with crates of green, yellow, orange and red produce, and there is a cardboard box beside it. In the middle a little girl in a pink dress holds a red and a purple balloon in one hand. She is reaching up with her other arm toward a blue balloon that has got loose and is floating up and away. She looks upset or worried. To her right a person with short brown hair in a blue hoodie and dark trousers is eating a pink ice-cream cone with their eyes closed, looking very happy and unaware of the girl. On the far right, next to a tree in a round planter, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is jumping in the air with both arms up and looks delighted. His shadow is on the pavement below him. Nothing looks clearly impossible. The main "event" is the blue balloon escaping.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The person eating ice cream could be a man or a woman (short brown hair, hoodie). Their eyes are closed, which could mean they are enjoying the ice cream or just blinking.
- The girl's face reads as upset or worried, but it is small, so it could also be just concentrating as she reaches.
- The jumping boy is clearly off the ground (his shadow is separate and below him). This looks intentional rather than like floating.
- The produce on the stall is only coloured blobs: green could be limes or apples, yellow lemons, orange oranges, red tomatoes or apples.
- The beige shape in the pram could be a blanket or a pillow.
