# Picture 40

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the cream, red-roofed house on the right side this time, a wooden fence across the back, a gate at the far left and a paved path running down the left side. On the left, a small smiling child in a pink outfit is sitting on a red swing, with little motion lines beside her showing she is swinging. In the middle, a young woman or girl with a brown ponytail, blue top and dark trousers is holding a kite string up, but her purple kite is stuck in the tree behind the house, tangled among the leaves. To the right of centre, an older man with grey hair, a cap, a brown vest and white shirt is watering the flower bed with a teal watering can. Something is wrong with the water: it comes out of the spout and arcs upward into the air, with drops at the top, instead of pouring down onto the flowers. Beside the flower bed is a hose reel with a coiled hose that is not being used. A wooden bench stands empty at the front right, and a grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the bottom centre. The flower bed has red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers, and the sky is blue with two clouds and the sun in the top left.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water from the watering can rises upward rather than falling; read literally, it is going against gravity. It could perhaps be meant as water being flung out, but as drawn the arc curves up and away from the flowers, so it looks wrong.
- The purple kite has leaf clumps drawn over it, so it reads as stuck in the tree; an alternative reading is that it is flying just in front of the tree, but its position among the leaves makes "stuck" more likely.
- The girl with the kite may be trying to pull it free or simply holding the string; it's not clear which.
- The child on the swing has her eyes closed and is smiling - she seems happy; nobody appears to be pushing her.
- The hose reel is right next to the man but he is using a watering can instead; not impossible, just noticeable.
