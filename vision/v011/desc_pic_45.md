# Picture 45

## Free description
This is a sunny park with a grassy field, a dark metal railing along the back, trees and hedges behind it, a slide on the left and a large tree on the right. In the middle background a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts has just thrown a red ball; a dotted line shows its path from his hand up into the air. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands to his right with her arms held out toward the ball, as if getting ready to catch it. In the front centre a little girl in a pink dress holds the strings of a yellow and a red balloon in one hand and reaches up with her other hand toward a purple balloon that has floated away from her, its string hanging loose just out of reach. A young man in a blue hoodie and black trousers on the right is waving one raised hand, with little motion lines beside it. At the front right an older man in a grey cap, brown vest and white shirt is happily eating a pink ice-cream cone with his eyes closed and a smile. The purple balloon looks about to drift away for good unless someone catches it. There is a green rubbish bin by the railing and a grey rock on the grass at bottom left.
