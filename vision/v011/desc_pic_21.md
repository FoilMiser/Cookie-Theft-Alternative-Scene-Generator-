# Picture 21

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and the sun in the top right with two clouds. In the middle front an elderly woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt is standing with her hands on her hips, wearing an upside-down blue bucket on her head like a hat, which looks silly. Behind her, by the fence, an older man with a grey beard, a grey cap, a white shirt, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers is painting the fence blue with a long-handled brush or roller; several boards are already blue and a paint can is on the grass at his feet. On the right a man with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans has both arms up and his mouth open in surprise, as a gust of wind (white wind lines) blows his blue cap off his head along with some leaves; the cap is flying through the air above him toward the left. Oddly, the clothes on the clothesline (blue jeans and a yellow striped towel) are hanging perfectly still even though it is windy. There is also an empty wooden bench in the front left, a flowerbed of colourful flowers, a grey wheelbarrow in the front, and a paved path leading to the gate.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The thing on the woman's head: Reading 1: it is a paint bucket (it matches the blue paint and is bucket-shaped) worn upside-down as a hat, which is absurd. Reading 2: it is a tall blue hat (like a bucket hat or fez). Her hands-on-hips pose could mean she is cross or simply standing and watching.
- The flying cap: it is blue, while the man in red is bareheaded, so it is most likely his cap blowing off; but it could also belong to someone else, and he is just trying to catch it.
- The wind: the cap and leaves are being blown hard, but the laundry on the line and the flowers are not moving at all, which is inconsistent.
- The older man's tool could be a paintbrush or a small paint roller.
