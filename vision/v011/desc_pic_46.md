# Picture 46

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a wooden gate at the back left with a paved path running down to the front left. A young boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on a red swing in a grey swing set on the left, holding the chains and smiling; little motion lines next to the swing show he is swinging. In the middle is a clothesline with a yellow red-striped towel and a pair of blue jeans hanging from it. On the right, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers holds a teal watering can over the flower bed in front of the house. Strangely, the water from the watering can does not pour down onto the flowers: it shoots upward in a curved arc from the spout, with drops flying above it, like a fountain, which is impossible for a watering can. A black-and-white cat is walking along the top of the fence near the tree behind the house. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits on the lawn at the front.

## Inventory
- Boy, left, middle ground: dark curly hair, yellow shirt, orange shorts; sitting on the red swing seat, holding both chains, smiling, swinging (motion lines).
- Woman, right of centre, front: dark hair in a ponytail, purple top, teal trousers, dark shoes; holding a teal watering can toward the flower bed.
- Cat, right, back: black with white chest and paws; walking along the top of the fence in front of the tree.
- Watering can: teal, in the woman's hands; water comes out of the spout and arcs upward, ending in drops in the air above the flowers.
- Flower bed: right, in front of the house; red, yellow, orange, purple and pink flowers in brown soil.
- Swing set: left; dark grey A-frame with one red seat (occupied by the boy).
- Clothesline: centre-back; two wooden posts, yellow towel with red stripes and blue jeans pegged on the line.
- Wheelbarrow: front, centre-left; grey metal tray, wooden handles, one wheel; empty, resting on the grass.
- House: right; cream walls, red roof, four-pane window, blue door with a step.
- Path: left side; pale paving slabs from the gate down to the bottom left.
- Gate: back left; wooden gate with a round handle.
- Fence: wooden fence across the back; hedge/trees behind; tree behind the house on the right.
- Sun: top left; two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water arc rising from the watering can is the clearest oddity: it can be read as water flowing upward out of the can (impossible), or, less likely, as a separate jet/sprinkler in the flower bed that just happens to line up with the spout.
- The swing has motion lines, but the swing and chains are drawn hanging straight down, so it is unclear whether the boy is moving a lot or just gently.
- The cat could be read as walking along the fence top or just standing there; its legs are on the top edge of the fence.
