# Picture 19

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and the sun in the top right with two clouds. An older man with a grey beard, a grey cap, a white shirt, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers is standing next to a flowerbed and pouring water onto the flowers out of a frying pan, which is a strange thing to water flowers with. Behind him, near the fence, a girl or young woman with a brown ponytail, a blue top and dark trousers is holding a kite string up with both hands. Her purple diamond-shaped kite is up in the big tree behind the house, with leaves drawn over it, so it looks like the kite is stuck in the tree. In the front, a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is crouched in a play bow, facing left. A red bowl of dog food sits on the grass at the bottom right. There are also two potted plants by the fence, a bush peeking over the fence, and a paved path leading to the gate on the right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The pan: it looks like a frying pan (or possibly a saucepan); using it to water flowers is odd. It is not clear where the water is coming from, since the pan looks too shallow to hold that much.
- The kite: Reading 1: it is stuck/tangled in the tree (leaves are drawn on top of it, and the girl seems to be pulling the string). Reading 2: it is flying high just behind the tree and only appears to overlap it. I lean toward it being stuck.
- A few small dark-green curly shapes sit on top of the kite; they look like tree leaves/branches drawn over it, but they are a slightly different style from the rest of the tree.
- The dog's play bow could be aimed at the man, or it could just be stretching.
