# Picture 06

## Free description
This is a sunny town street with a row of colourful shops with striped awnings, a fruit and vegetable stall, a lamp post, a flower planter, a bicycle in a rack and a tree in a planter. On the left a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is taking a photo with a camera, aiming toward the right. Next to her an older woman with glasses and a teal cardigan is walking a brown dog on a red lead. In the middle a small child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is holding a purple and a blue balloon in one hand and reaching up with the other toward a yellow balloon that has floated away out of reach. The yellow balloon's string is dangling loose in the air, not attached to anything. On the right a woman with long dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers is running toward the left, toward the child, with her arm reaching out. It looks like the child has just let go of the yellow balloon and it is about to float away, and the running woman may be trying to catch it or reach the child. A teal bicycle is parked in a bike rack in front of the peach building.
