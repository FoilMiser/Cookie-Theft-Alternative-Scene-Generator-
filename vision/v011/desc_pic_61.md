# Picture 61

## Free description
This is a sunny park with the sun in the upper right, two white clouds, and green rolling hills and hedges in the background. On the left is a pond with a white duck swimming and reeds or cattails at each end, and a young woman with a brown ponytail in a blue hoodie and dark trousers stands at the right edge of the pond fishing with a rod; her line runs down to a red-and-white float bobbing in the water with a ripple around it. A tall black lamppost stands in the middle with a green trash bin next to it, and a sandy path winds from the bottom of the picture toward the back right. Beside the path, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, is reading a red book in a sitting position, but there is no bench or seat under her. At the bottom right, a girl in a pink dress is kicking an orange ball that sits on the path near her foot. At the bottom centre, a black-and-white cat is crouched low, creeping toward a red bird that is pecking at the ground, as if the cat is about to pounce. There is also a flower bed bottom left, a second trash bin in the bottom-left corner, a small tree on the left, a large tree on the right, and bushes on both sides.
