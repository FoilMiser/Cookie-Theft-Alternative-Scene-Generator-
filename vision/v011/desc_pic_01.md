# Picture 01

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a gate in the fence on the right with a paved path leading from it toward the front. A man in an orange cap and an orange high-visibility vest is standing in the middle of the lawn holding the end of a green garden hose out in front of him with both hands and looking at the nozzle. Only a single drop of water is coming out of the nozzle, so the hose seems not to be working. The hose runs from a hose reel at the lower left, across the grass, and right under the feet of a brown dog with a red collar, so the dog appears to be standing on the hose and blocking the water. The dog is looking over toward the man. Behind the man there is a metal swing set with one empty red swing hanging still. There is a colourful flower bed next to the house and a teal watering can sitting on the grass near the path. A birdhouse on a pole stands behind the fence, and a tree and bushes show over and beside the house. It looks like the man is puzzled about the water and may soon find that the dog is stepping on the hose.
