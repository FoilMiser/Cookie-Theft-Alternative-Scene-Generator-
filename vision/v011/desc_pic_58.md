# Picture 58

## Free description
This is a back garden on a grey, overcast day, with three dark storm clouds gathering in the sky. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door, with a flower bed and a coiled garden hose in front of it. A young woman with a brown ponytail, wearing a blue hoodie and dark trousers, is painting the tall wooden fence blue with a brush; a few panels on the left are already blue, and a paint can and paint tray sit on the grass by her feet. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has walked through the paint: there is a trail of blue paw prints leading from the paint tray to the dog, whose paws are blue, and the dog is trotting away to the right with its tongue out. In the front right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is in a sitting position drinking from a white mug, but there is no chair or seat under her at all. On the far right, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands on a paved path, pointing up at the dark clouds with a surprised or worried face. Near the man are two potted plants, and at the right end of the fence is a wooden gate. It looks like rain is about to start while the fence paint is still wet, and the dog is spreading paint across the lawn.
