# Picture 41

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the cream, red-roofed house on the right, a wooden fence across the back, a gate at the far left and a paved path running down the left side. In the centre, a bearded man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers is standing on the grass holding a garden hose and spraying water onto the flower bed to his right. The hose runs from his hand down across the lawn in a long curve to a hose reel at the right edge of the flower bed. The water comes out of the nozzle in an arc and falls down toward the front of the flower bed, with drops landing near the flowers. Behind him is a clothesline strung between two wooden posts, with a yellow item with red stripes (a towel or shirt) and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. On the left, two terracotta pots with leafy green plants stand on the grass, and at the bottom left there is a teal watering can. A grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the bottom centre. The sky is blue with two clouds and the sun in the top left, a tree stands behind the house, and a bush is next to it. Nothing looks wrong or impossible; it is an ordinary watering scene.
