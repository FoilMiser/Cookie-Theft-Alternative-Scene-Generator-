# Picture 05

## Free description
This is the same kind of sunny back garden as a house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back and a gate on the right with a path. A bearded man in a green shirt is standing by the flower bed watering the flowers by pouring water out of a frying pan, which is a very odd thing to use instead of a watering can. Behind him there is a washing line between two posts with a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes pegged to it. A man in an orange cap and orange high-vis vest is painting the fence blue with a long-handled brush, and part of the fence is already blue; a paint tin sits at his feet. A blue bird is perched on top of the fence near the painted section. In the middle of the lawn, a sprinkler attached to a hose is spraying water onto a cardboard box instead of the grass or flowers, and the box has water drops on it. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is lying on the grass right next to the sprinkler. On the right, a boy in a red shirt and blue jeans is walking along the path reading an open book, and there is a red ball on the grass just in front of him, so he may trip over it. The sun is shining at the top right.
