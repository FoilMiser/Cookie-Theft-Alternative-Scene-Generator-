# Picture 43

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a wooden gate at the back right with a paved path running from it toward the front right corner. A man in an orange cap, an orange high-visibility safety vest with silver reflective stripes and blue jeans stands in the middle-right of the lawn. He holds the end of a green garden hose up in front of his face and looks closely into the yellow nozzle. Only a single small drop of water is coming out of it. The hose runs from a hose reel by the flower bed on the left, along the grass, and up to his hands. A brown-and-white dog with a red collar stands on the lawn between the reel and the man, and its feet appear to be standing on the hose, which would block the water. The dog has its tongue out and is looking toward the man. It looks as if, when the dog steps off the hose, the water will spray straight into the man's face. Behind them is a clothesline with blue jeans and a yellow red-striped towel pegged on it, and on the right is a swing set with one empty red swing. A red dog bowl with food sits on the grass near the path.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The dog's feet look like they are on the hose, pinching it; it could also be read as the dog standing just behind the hose with the hose passing in front of its feet.
- The man could be checking why no water is coming out, or simply examining the nozzle; the single drop suggests the water flow is blocked.
- His orange hi-vis vest and cap look like work/safety clothing, which is a bit unusual for watering a home garden.
- The man stands in front of the right clothesline post, so the post appears to go behind his body.
