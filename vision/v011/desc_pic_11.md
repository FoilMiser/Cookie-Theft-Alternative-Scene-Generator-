# Picture 11

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden. A cream house with a red roof and a blue front door is on the left, with a tall wooden fence running across the back and a wooden gate on the right. A grey-haired elderly woman in a teal top and grey skirt is painting the fence blue with a paintbrush. One section in the middle of the fence is already painted blue, and a small tin of blue paint sits on the grass by her feet. In front of the house there is a round flower bed full of colourful flowers. In the centre, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers has both arms raised and looks surprised, because a gust of wind (shown by white lines) has blown his red cap off his head along with a few leaves. On the right, on a paved path, an older man in a blue cap, white shirt and brown vest is using a gigantic dinner fork as if it were a broom or rake, pushing a pile of dirt and pebbles toward a red dustpan, with a little cloud of dust rising. The giant fork is the strangest thing in the picture. It is a kitchen utensil many times normal size being used as a garden tool.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The giant fork: it could be read as an odd joke tool (a huge dining fork) or, less literally, as a garden fork drawn oddly. Its shape is clearly a dinner fork, though, not a garden pitchfork, and it is far too big.
- The man with the fork could be sweeping the dirt into the dustpan, or raking or scattering it. The dust cloud suggests he is pushing it along.
- The woman may have painted the blue section herself or be just starting on it. Painting one middle section of a brown fence blue is a little odd.
- The wind lines extend to the right of the cap, so the cap seems to be blowing toward the left, toward the woman painting.
- The woman's paintbrush handle is long and thin. It could be a normal brush or a small roller-type stick.
