# Picture 02

## Free description
This is a sunny playground in a park, with a railing fence and hedges at the back and lots of people doing different things. On the left an old man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat is waving with one raised hand, and next to him a child in a yellow top is going down a yellow slide. A bearded man in a green shirt is holding a camera up to his face and taking a photo, pointed toward the left side of the picture. In the front left, a brown dog is sitting up beside a sandbox holding an open newspaper in its paws as if reading it, which is impossible; the sandbox has a red bucket and blue spade in it and there is a grey rock nearby. In the centre front a boy in a red shirt is walking while reading an open book, and a blue bucket is on the ground just in front of him, so he looks about to trip over it. On the right an older woman with glasses sits on a bench eating a pink ice cream while a white dog with brown spots sits beside her looking up, probably hoping for some. A blue-and-white ball is stuck up in the tree on the right, and a small girl in a pink dress is pointing up at it. In the front right a girl with a ponytail in a blue hoodie is kicking an orange ball, near an empty red seesaw. There is also an empty swing in the middle and a blue bird sitting on the fence.
