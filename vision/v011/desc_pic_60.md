# Picture 60

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the sun in the upper right and two white clouds. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door, with a small bush beside it and a flower bed of colourful flowers in front. Along the back runs a tall wooden fence with a wooden gate at the right end, and a paved path runs from the gate down to the bottom right. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt stands near the fence digging with a shovel; there is a hole and a small mound of dirt next to her. In the front right, a young woman with a dark ponytail in a purple top and teal trousers walks a brown-and-white dog on a red leash, heading to the left. A red dog bowl with food sits on the grass at the bottom. Strangely, a cardboard box is hanging in mid-air in the middle of the garden, tilted, in front of the fence, with its shadow on the grass well below it, as if it is floating or falling.
