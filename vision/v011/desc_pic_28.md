A blue ball is stuck up in the tree behind the house, and a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands by the fence pointing up at it. Only a single drop of water is coming out, and he is looking at the nozzle as if puzzled. The hose runs from a hose reel next to the flower bed, along the ground, and up to the man. A white goat with small horns and a little beard is standing on the grass right where the hose runs, apparently standing on it, which would explain why no water is coming out. A grey wheelbarrow sits on the path at the right. Its wheel looks like a square instead of a circle, which is impossible. An empty wooden bench is in the front left, and there are two potted plants near the fence.

Reading 2 is that the hose just passes behind or near its feet and the water is blocked for another reason. I lean to reading 1, but the overlap is small.
