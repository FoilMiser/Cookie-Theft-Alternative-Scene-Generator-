# Picture 07

## Free description
This is a busy sunny back garden with a house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back and a gate and path on the right. A boy in a red shirt is painting the fence blue with a long-handled brush, and the left part of the fence is already blue; a paint tin and a paint tray are on the grass behind him. A white dog with brown spots has stepped in the blue paint and is leaving a trail of blue paw prints across the grass from the paint tray. An old man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat is walking along carrying a cardboard box. On the right a little girl in a pink dress on the path has thrown a red-and-white ball, shown by a dotted line, toward a woman in a purple top and teal trousers who has her hands out ready to catch it. A child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is jumping with both arms up, looking happy. In the front middle, a person in a blue hoodie is sitting drinking from a mug, but there is no chair or seat under them, so they are sitting on thin air, which is impossible. At the front left a bearded man in a green shirt is sitting on a bench eating a pink ice cream while a brown dog leans up toward him, probably begging. On top of the fence, an orange cat is crouching and creeping toward a blue bird perched further along the fence, as if about to pounce, and a red bird is flying in the sky. There is also a flower bed, a hose reel and an empty wheelbarrow on the lawn.
