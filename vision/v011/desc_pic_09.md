# Picture 09

## Free description

This is a busy, sunny town street with colourful shops (cream, peach, pale green and pink buildings with striped awnings). Several small mishaps are happening at once. On the left a bearded man in a green shirt is sweeping the pavement with a broom beside a tree, and a red bird is perched in the tree's leaves. A little girl in a pink dress walks past holding a yellow balloon. At the back, a man in a blue shirt has his arms up in surprise because the wind has blown his orange cap off his head, and it is flying away with a few leaves. Next to him a teal bicycle is parked in a bike rack. In the centre an old man in a cap and brown vest sits on a bench reading a red book. Beside him on the bench is a sandwich, and a blue bird is pecking at it or stealing it without him noticing. At the back right a man in a red shirt and jeans is walking along with his nose in an open newspaper or book, heading toward a red bucket on the ground by the lamp post, so he may trip over it. In front of the fruit stall, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands with her hands on her hips with a blue bucket upside down on her head like a hat, which is odd. In the front right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is crying and rubbing his eye, holding an empty cone, because his pink ice cream has fallen on the ground. A brown dog with a red collar is sniffing or licking the dropped ice cream.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The blue bucket on the woman's head: either she is wearing it as a joke hat, or it is a strange blue hat shaped like a bucket. Either way it looks out of place.
- The red bucket by the lamp post appears to have something dark inside (water?). The man reading may be about to trip over it or walk into the lamp post, but he may also walk past it.
- What the man in red is reading could be a newspaper, a map or a large book.
- The blue bird might be eating the sandwich or about to fly off with it.
- The red bird sits on the leafy surface of the tree rather than on a visible branch. This is a little odd but normal for a cartoon.
- The boy's gesture could be rubbing his eyes while crying or wiping his face. His sad expression and the dropped scoop suggest crying.
- The woman with the bucket is standing in front of the stall, so she could be the stallholder or a customer.
