# Picture 12

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate and a paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. A person with brown hair in a ponytail, wearing a blue long-sleeved top and dark trousers, is flying a yellow diamond-shaped kite. Its long string goes up over the fence to the kite, which is high in the sky right next to a cloud and has a tail with little bows. In the front centre, a man in a red t-shirt and blue jeans stands with his hands on his hips with a blue bucket upside down on his head, which is clearly odd. On the right an elderly grey-haired woman in a teal top and grey skirt is watering a round bed of colourful flowers with a teal watering can, and water is pouring onto the flowers. Two potted plants stand on the grass near the fence. Everyone seems calm. The only strange thing is the bucket being worn as a hat.
