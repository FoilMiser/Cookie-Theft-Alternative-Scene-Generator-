# Picture 04

## Free description
This is a town street or square with a row of colourful shop buildings with striped awnings, under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds, so it looks like it might rain soon. An older woman with glasses and a teal cardigan is standing in the middle and pointing up at the sky. Next to her, a person in a blue hoodie is sitting on a bench eating a pink ice cream cone, and a brown dog with a red bandana is stretching toward them as if begging for the ice cream. In front of them, a white dog with brown spots is sitting on the pavement holding an open newspaper in its paws as if reading it, which is impossible. A bearded man in a green shirt is standing by a lamp post and waving with one raised hand. On the right there is a fruit and vegetable stall with crates of produce, and an old man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat is walking away from it carrying a yellow shopping bag. A red bird is flying in the top left corner. There is also a tree in a planter, a green bin, a small café table with two chairs, a flower planter box, a red bicycle in a bike rack, a cardboard box and a potted plant. Despite the dark clouds, no one has an umbrella.
