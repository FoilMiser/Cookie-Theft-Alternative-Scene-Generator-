# Picture 15

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park, with a bright sun in the top right, two white clouds, rolling green hills and a line of trees in the background. On the left side there is an oval blue pond with a few cattails/reeds at its edges. In the front left a woman with short black hair, a yellow dress and red shoes is walking to the left, pushing a teal pram with a blue hood; a baby wrapped in a cream blanket is lying in the pram. In the middle of the picture, just in front of the pond, a bearded man with dark hair, a green shirt and tan trousers is running (or striding quickly) to the left with one arm reaching forward, in the same direction as the woman. Just to the right of the man a tall wooden ladder is standing up on the grass, tilted at an angle, but there is nothing for it to lean against, so it looks like it is standing by itself, which is impossible. Behind the ladder there is a tall black lamp post with a green rubbish bin beside it. On the right there is an empty wooden park bench, a sandy path winding off into the distance, a large tree and some bushes. Nothing dramatic seems about to happen, but the free-standing ladder looks like it should fall over.
