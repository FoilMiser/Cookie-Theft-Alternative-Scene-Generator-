# Picture 37

## Free description
This is a playground in a park on a gloomy day: the sky is grey and three big dark storm clouds hang overhead, so it looks like it might rain soon. On the left, a small girl in a pink dress with dark hair in a bun sits on the raised end of a red seesaw, holding the handle with her feet dangling. On the low end of the same seesaw, which is down near the ground, an older man with grey hair and a cap, wearing a brown vest and white shirt, sits reading an open book or newspaper rather than paying attention to the girl. In the middle background, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands with her arm raised, pointing up and to the right toward the dark clouds, with her mouth open as if saying something. In front, an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, is pushing a teal pram with a navy hood; a baby's head is visible inside. Behind them are an empty swing set with one red swing, a green rubbish bin, an empty wooden bench, and a red-and-orange slide with a ladder on the right. At the front right is a sandbox with a red bucket and blue spade in it, a grey rock beside it, and a black-and-white cat lying on the grass just behind the sandbox. A blue bird is flying in the upper left. Something is clearly wrong: there is an orange goldfish sitting in the leaves of the tree on the far left, which is impossible.
