# Picture 13

## Free description

This is a quiet, sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate and a paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. The only person is a woman with dark hair in a ponytail, wearing a purple top and teal trousers. She is kneeling on one knee on the lawn beside a round flower bed of colourful flowers. She is reaching into the flowers and seems to be gardening. A small terracotta pot with a pink flower sits on the edge of the bed in front of her, as if she is about to plant it. Two potted green plants stand on the grass near the fence. In the front, a teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow are left on the grass. Everything looks calm and ordinary. Nothing seems about to go wrong.
