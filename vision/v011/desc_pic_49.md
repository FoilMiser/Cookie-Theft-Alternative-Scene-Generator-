# Picture 49

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a wooden gate at the back left with a paved path running down to the front left. In the middle, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is standing at the clothesline with both arms raised, pegging a white T-shirt onto the line; a yellow towel and blue jeans are already hanging there. In front of the house, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is watering the flower bed, but she is pouring the water out of a black frying pan instead of a watering can. Oddly, a teal watering can is sitting unused on the grass at the front left, and a hose reel is right next to the flower bed. On the far right a man in a blue hoodie sits on a wooden bench in front of the house, drinking from a white mug. Two potted plants stand on the lawn near the gate, and an empty grey wheelbarrow is on the grass at the front.
