# Picture 38

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate at the far right and a paved path running down the right side. In the front left, a woman with a short black bob in a yellow dress and red shoes sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. In the centre, a young man or teenager in an orange cap, an orange high-visibility vest with reflective stripes and blue jeans holds a teal watering can over the flower bed. Something is wrong with the water: instead of falling down onto the flowers, it shoots out of the spout in an arc that goes upward into the air, with drops at the top of the arc. Behind him near the fence, a small girl in a pink dress with dark hair in a bun stands pointing up and to the left. Up in the tree behind the house there is a blue ball stuck in the leaves, which seems to be what she is pointing at. On the right is a tall swing set with a single empty red swing, and in the lower middle a grey wheelbarrow stands on the grass. The flower bed is full of red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers. The sky is blue with two clouds and a bright sun in the top right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water from the watering can rises upward instead of falling; read literally it is going up, against gravity. Another possible reading is that it is meant to be a spray being flung outward, but as drawn the stream curves up and away from the flowers, so it looks wrong/impossible.
- The girl is pointing up-left, most likely at the ball in the tree; she could also be pointing at the sky or something over the fence, but the ball is in the direction she points.
- The young man's outfit (hi-vis vest, cap) could mean he is a worker/gardener, or it could just be his clothing.
- The blue ball's stripe makes it look like a beach ball or a play ball; it could be read either way.
- The girl is drawn quite small and right against the fence, so she reads as far back in the garden.
