# Picture 14

## Free description

This is a sunny park with green hills in the background, a blue pond with bulrushes, a winding sandy path, a lamp post and a rubbish bin. On the far left a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is walking away to the left, carrying a yellow shopping bag. By the pond a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is walking along eating a pink ice-cream cone. In front of the flower bed area, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a red and a yellow balloon in one hand. He is reaching up with the other hand toward a purple balloon that has escaped and is floating away. On the right a little girl in a pink dress sits on a bench eating a small pink ice cream. A brown dog with a red neckerchief sits on the path next to the bench, looking up at her as if begging. Further back, a person with a brown ponytail in a blue top and dark trousers is kicking an orange ball. Something clearly impossible is in the big tree on the right: an orange fish is sitting in the leaves, where a bird might normally be.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The fish in the tree is clearly out of place and impossible. It is drawn as an ordinary orange fish (like a goldfish) sitting in the leaves.
- The ball kicker could be a woman, girl or long-haired boy. The ponytail suggests female.
- The ball is orange with a white band, so it could be a football, a beach ball or a basketball-like ball.
- The dog could be begging for the girl's ice cream or just sitting next to her. It is looking up at her, so begging seems likely.
- The woman in yellow is seen mostly from the side and back, and her face is hard to see. She may be leaving the park.
- The man eating ice cream is drawn overlapping the pond, but his feet are on the grass in front of it, so he is walking in front of the pond, not in it.
