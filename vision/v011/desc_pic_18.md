# Picture 18

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden seen from the other side: a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the left, a tall wooden fence runs across the back with a gate on the far right, and the sun is in the top right with two clouds. In the front left a young man or teenage boy with brown hair, a blue hoodie and dark trousers is sitting on a wooden bench, absorbed in reading a red book. Next to him on the bench is a big sandwich with lettuce and tomato, and a blue bird has landed at the end of the bench right next to it, so the bird looks like it is about to steal or peck at his food while he isn't looking. Behind him, by the blue door, a brown dog with a white muzzle and chest is sitting up holding a red lead/leash in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk; the lead trails down onto the grass. In the middle front a white goat with small horns is bent over eating a tuft of grass, which is unusual for a back garden. A clothesline in the middle holds blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. There is a flowerbed of colourful flowers, two potted plants by the fence, a teal watering can on the lawn, and a paved path leading to the gate on the right.
