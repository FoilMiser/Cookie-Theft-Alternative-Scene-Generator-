# Picture 17

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a sun in the top left, two clouds, a tall brown wooden fence with a gate at the far left, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt is standing by the fence near the gate, painting the fence blue with a long-handled brush; a few boards next to the gate are already blue. At her feet there is a can of blue paint and a paint tray full of blue paint. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has walked through the paint: a trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the lawn to the dog, whose paws are blue. The woman has her back to this and does not seem to have noticed. In the front middle a brown dog with a red collar is lying flat on its belly with its head down on its paws, maybe asleep or sniffing the grass, and a red bowl of dog food sits on the grass to the front left. A clothesline in the middle holds a yellow towel with red stripes and blue jeans. On the right, a cardboard box is tilted at an angle near the bottom of the fence, as if it is falling, tumbling or floating. There is a flowerbed of colourful flowers in front of the house and a paved path on the left.
