# Picture 57

## Free description
This is a sunny playground with the sun in the upper right, two white clouds, a grey metal railing fence across the back and a row of green hedges behind it. On the left side a red seesaw is tipped steeply: a small girl in a pink dress sits at the high end up in the air, holding the handle, while a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers sits at the low end with his end resting near the ground. The man is reading an open book and does not seem to be paying any attention to the girl, who looks stuck up in the air because he is so much heavier. On the right there is a slide with a red frame, blue platform and grey ladder, and a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is partway down the yellow and orange chute with both arms raised up by the head. Near the fence in the middle is a green trash bin. There is a tree on the far left, a bush on the far right edge, and a grey rock in the bottom right. The odd thing in the scene is the grown man sitting on the seesaw reading a book, leaving the little girl hanging in the air on the other end.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The child on the slide has both arms raised to the head: this could be cheering "hands up" while sliding, or holding/covering the head or face.
- It is unclear whether the man knows the girl is on the other end; he looks fully absorbed in his book. The girl's expression is hard to read (could be waiting, could be unhappy).
- The man's legs and the seesaw plank overlap; he reads as sitting on the end of the plank, but the plank could also be read as passing in front of or through his legs.
- Nothing is floating or physically impossible; the strange part is the situation (an adult reading on a seesaw with a small child stuck up high).
