# Picture 03

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green rolling hills, trees and bushes, a winding path on the left, a tall lamp post, and a blue pond on the right. An old man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat stands at the edge of the pond holding a white bag and tossing bits of bread toward the water. A white duck is swimming in the pond toward the bread crumbs, leaving little wake lines behind it. In the front, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is holding an empty ice-cream cone and rubbing their eyes as if crying, because their pink ice-cream scoop with sprinkles has fallen on the ground and is melting. A brown dog with a red collar is standing on the path with its nose right down at the fallen ice cream, sniffing or licking it. To the right, a person in a blue hoodie is sitting on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket with a wicker picnic basket, eyes closed and smiling, as if relaxing in the sun. There are two green rubbish bins, one by the lamp post and one cut off at the bottom right corner. Reeds grow at both ends of the pond.
