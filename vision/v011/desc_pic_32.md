## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a wooden gate on the right with a paved path running from it to the bottom-right corner. In the centre a girl with dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes is standing with her back mostly to us, holding a garden hose and spraying an arc of water onto a bed of colourful flowers in front of the house. The hose runs along the grass from a teal hose reel at the front left of the flower bed to her hands. Behind her, under a washing line with blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel pegged on it, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt is raking up a small pile of leaves. In the front of the picture a white goat with small curved horns is bending its head down to eat a tuft of grass. Two potted plants stand by the gate and a teal watering can sits on the grass at the front right. The goat is the surprising thing: it seems unusual to have a goat loose in a small family garden, but nothing looks physically impossible.
