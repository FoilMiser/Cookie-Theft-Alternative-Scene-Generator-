# Picture 20

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park, with the sun in the top left, two clouds, green hills and a line of trees in the background. A big tree stands on the left and a sandy path winds from the bottom left toward the hills, with an empty wooden bench beside it. Near the middle, next to a tall black lamp post and a green rubbish bin, a girl or young woman with a brown ponytail, a blue hoodie and dark trousers is standing and fishing with a long rod. Her line goes all the way over to an oval pond on the right, where a red-and-white float bobs in the water with a ripple around it. In the front right an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt is walking to the right along the front of the pond, carrying a yellow bag. In the front middle there is a grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles; where its wheel should be there is a dark square object with a circle in the middle and lines from the corners, which looks like a square wheel. There is a second green rubbish bin at the bottom right corner, cut off by the edge, plus some bushes and a small tree on the right.

- Elderly woman (front right, in front of the pond): grey hair in a bun, glasses, teal top, grey skirt, brown shoes; walking to the right, carrying a yellow bag in her hand.
- Fishing rod and line (centre to right): long dark rod held by the girl; thin line from its tip down to a red-and-white float in the pond.
- Float/bobber (in the pond, right of centre): small red-and-white float with a white ripple ring around it.
- Pond (right): oval blue pond with cattails at both ends.
- Wheelbarrow (front centre): grey tray, wooden handles, a short leg, and a dark square object at the front where a wheel would be.
- Square object (front centre, at the wheelbarrow's front): dark grey square with a circle in the middle and diagonal lines to the corners.
- Yellow bag (front right): yellow rectangular bag/carrier held by the elderly woman.
- Lamp post (centre left): tall black lamp post with a white lamp on top.
- Rubbish bins: one green slatted bin beside the lamp post, and another at the bottom right edge, partly cut off.
- Bench (left of centre): empty brown wooden bench next to the path.
- Path (left): sandy path winding from the bottom of the picture back to the hills.
- Trees and bushes: large tree on the left, smaller tree on the right, round bushes at both edges.
- Sun and clouds (sky): sun in the top left, two white clouds near the top middle/right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The square object on the wheelbarrow: Reading 1: it is the wheelbarrow's wheel but drawn square (with a hub in the middle and spokes to the corners), which would be an impossible/useless wheel. Reading 2: it is a separate box, crate, or small safe sitting in front of the wheelbarrow, and the real wheel is hidden behind it.
- The yellow item the elderly woman carries could be a bag, a book or a folder.
- The girl is standing a fair distance back from the pond while fishing; the rod and line are long enough to reach, but it looks a little unusual.
- The elderly woman is walking very close to the edge of the pond; she is drawn in front of it, so she is probably on the grass in front, but if read differently she could look like she is about to step into the water.
