# Picture 46

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence along the back and a gate and paved path on the left. An older woman with a grey bun and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, stands at a washing line and reaches up to peg out clothes; a white T-shirt and a pair of blue jeans are already hanging on the line. In the front a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes is kicking an orange ball with a white band, swinging his leg out to the left, with curved movement lines by his foot. The ball is just off his toe and heading to the left, towards the path. To the right, an old man in a cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers kneels on one knee by a bed of colourful flowers, handling a terracotta pot with a pink plant, as if planting or tending it. An empty swing set with a red seat stands on the left of the lawn, and a teal hose reel sits next to the flower bed. A birdhouse on a tall post stands near the gate. Everyone seems absorbed in their own activity, and nothing looks clearly impossible.
