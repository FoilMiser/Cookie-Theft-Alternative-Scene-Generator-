# Picture 36

## Free description

This is a sunny park with a blue pond on the right, hills, trees, a tall lamp post and a winding sandy path on the left. The only person is a woman with short dark hair in a yellow dress and red shoes, who is walking to the right across the grass in the foreground. She holds a small ice-cream cone with a pink scoop up in front of her face and appears to be about to lick it or is looking at it. She seems content and absorbed in it and is not paying attention to anything else. The pond is directly behind her from our viewpoint, with bulrushes at each end and nothing on the water. A green bin stands by the lamp post at the back and another stands in the bottom right corner, and a grey rock sits on the grass beside the path. There are no benches, animals or other people. Nothing seems about to happen, and nothing looks clearly impossible, although her dress looks a little odd.
