# Picture 21

## Free description
This is a busy sunny day in a park, with a pond on the left, a winding path on the right, and hills behind. A man in a red shirt and blue trousers stands by the pond with a paper bag, reaching out to feed a white duck, with crumbs scattered on the water. On a bench in the centre, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is reading a red book and has not noticed that a blue bird on the bench beside him is pecking at his sandwich. Just left of the bench, a white goat has its head down, eating a tuft of grass and small flowers. At the right front, an older man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat is also reading a red book in a sitting pose, but there is no bench or seat under him at all, so he is sitting on thin air, which looks impossible. On the far right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts points up at a blue ball stuck on a branch in the large tree. At the bottom left, a girl in a pink dress with an ice cream runs or walks away to the left, near a sprinkler whose water arcs across the grass and lands on a wooden crate standing in a puddle. A small brown and white dog is asleep on the grass at the front, and an elderly woman with a yellow shopping bag walks along the top of the path in the distance.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The older man could be read as sitting on an invisible bench, or as leaning or crouching, but his bent legs and knees make it look like a seat that is missing.
- The crate could be read as simply being in the way of the sprinkler water, or as something being deliberately soaked. It might also be read as a box full of water, since blue streaks run down its sides. I cannot tell what is in it.
- The girl may be running away to avoid getting wet, or simply walking off with her ice cream; she does not look back at the sprinkler.
- The goat is drawn a little strangely, and at first glance it could be a white dog or lamb; only the single dark curved horn suggests a goat.
- The water arcs mostly land short on the grass, and only the longest reaches the crate. The ends of the arcs look like small splashes that are hard to make out.
- The elderly woman's feet are on the grass or the very top of the path; it is hard to say which, and she is drawn large for how far back she seems to be.
