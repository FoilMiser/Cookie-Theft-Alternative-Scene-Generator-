# Picture 49

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back, and a gate at the far left with a paved path running down from it. A little girl in a pink outfit sits on a red seat on a metal swing set, on the left of centre, laughing with her eyes squeezed shut, and short lines beside the seat suggest she is swinging. In the middle, a woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers stands with both hands held up in front of her, facing right. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers faces her with one arm stretched out, and a dashed line curves from his hand to a red ball in the air, just in front of the woman's hands. So the boy seems to have just thrown the ball and the woman is about to catch it. At the front there is a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow, and the odd thing is that the wheelbarrow's wheel is square, like a window frame, instead of round. A wheelbarrow could not roll on that. A flower bed and a bush are near the house.

## Inventory

- Little girl, left of centre: brown skin, hair in bunches, pink outfit, yellow shoes; sitting on the swing, laughing, swinging.
- Woman, centre: long dark hair, purple top, teal trousers, dark shoes; standing with hands up, about to catch the ball.
- Boy, right of centre: red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers, white shoes; in a throwing pose, one arm stretched towards the ball, the other low.
- Ball: red with a white stripe, in the air between the woman and the boy, close to the woman's hands; has just been thrown.
- Dashed line: from the boy's hand up to the ball; shows the ball's path.
- Swing set: tall blue-grey A-frame with a red seat on two ropes, left of centre.
- Wheelbarrow: grey tray with wooden handles, front centre, with a square black-framed wheel at the front; not being used.
- Watering can: teal, on the grass at front left beside the path; not being used.
- Flower bed: oval of soil with red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers, right, in front of the house.
- Bush: round and green, next to the house.
- House: cream walls, red roof, blue door with step, one window, right side.
- Fence and gate: wooden fence along the back, with a darker gate at the left.
- Path: pale paving slabs, left side.
- Tree and hedges: a tree behind the fence near the house, dark green bushes along the back.
- Sun and clouds: sun top left, two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The wheel: most likely a square wheel, which is impossible for rolling. It has a cross or lattice inside, with green showing through, so at first glance it could also be read as a separate small crate or a grid object sitting behind the wheelbarrow, but it is joined to the barrow's axle.
- Who threw the ball: the dashed line leads back to the boy, so he threw it to the woman. Without the line it could equally be that she has just thrown it to him.
- The little girl is laughing with her eyes closed, so it could be delight or squinting. Nobody is pushing her.
- The swing ropes seem to come together behind her head and then spread out to the corners of the seat, which looks a bit odd, as though they pass behind her.
- The woman's hands are clasped together rather than open, so she could look like she is clapping or praying as well as getting ready to catch.
