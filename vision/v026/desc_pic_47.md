# Picture 47

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a long wooden fence across the back, and a gate at the far right with a paved path leading from it towards the viewer. In the front left, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers sits on a wooden bench drinking from a red cup through a yellow straw. On the right, a bearded man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers stands on the path holding a long rake, with a small pile of green and brown leaves at the rake's teeth. He is looking off to the right rather than down at the leaves. An orange cat lies curled up on the grass in the middle at the front, with its eyes closed, apparently asleep. A flower bed with mixed colourful flowers is on the lawn near the house, two potted plants stand on the grass in front of the fence, and a teal watering can sits on the grass beside the path, not being used. Nobody is interacting with anyone else; each is doing their own thing. Nothing seems about to happen and nothing looks clearly wrong or impossible.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The cat could be asleep or just lying contentedly; its head is turned at an awkward angle, and the body is hard to read at that size, so it is partly a blob of orange with a face.
- The man's gaze: he faces right, away from the leaves, so he could be looking at something out of the picture, or the head may simply be drawn in profile while he works.
- The rake handle crosses in front of his body and leg; it reads as him holding it in front of him, not as passing through him.
- The boy sits right at the front of the bench and his feet barely reach the ground, which looks slightly stiff but not impossible.
- The watering can and pots seem placed on the lawn with no clear purpose.
