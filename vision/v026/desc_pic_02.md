# Picture 02

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left and a wooden fence across the back. In the middle foreground a woman in a yellow top and yellow skirt with red shoes is watering a round flower bed, but she is pouring the water out of a frying pan instead of a watering can, which looks odd. Water is streaming from the pan onto the flowers. Behind her, near the fence, a girl in a yellow top and orange shorts is standing with her back partly to us and holding a string up, flying a purple kite. The kite has gone into the big tree behind the house's corner and looks caught in the leaves, with the string going slackly down to the girl in a curve, so it seems to be stuck rather than flying. Next to the house door, a brown and white dog is sitting upright holding its own red lead in its mouth, as if it is waiting to be taken for a walk. On the right there is an empty swing set with a red seat, a tall bird box on a pole, a gate in the fence, a paved path, and a wheelbarrow on the grass at the front. No one is using the swing or the wheelbarrow.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The frying pan: it could be a deliberate silly mistake (using a pan instead of a watering can), or it could be read as a saucepan used because nothing else was handy. Either way, the water is coming out of it, and I cannot see where it got the water from.
- The kite: it could be caught in the tree, or it could just be flying in front of the tree. The leaf clusters overlap it, and the slack, drooping string suggests it is stuck, but it could be read as flying low.
- The dog: it could be asking for a walk by holding its lead, or simply chewing on it.
- The woman's face and body: her head looks turned away from the pan with her face in profile, so she may not be looking directly at what she is doing.
- The lighter leaf clumps on the tree only appear around the kite and look slightly like an added patch rather than part of the rest of the tree.
- The girl seems very thin and small compared with the woman; this may just be because she is further away.
