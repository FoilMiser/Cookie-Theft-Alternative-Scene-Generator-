# Picture 61

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and a blue door, enclosed by a tall wooden fence with a gate on the right. On the right, a man with brown hair, a red shirt and blue trousers is standing at the edge of a paved path, raking a small pile of green and brown leaves. In the middle of the lawn, a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has its front end down and its tail up, right beside a yellow-green tennis ball, as if it is playing with or about to grab the ball. A clothesline strung between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. Something odd is happening in the sky: a closed cardboard box is hanging in mid-air above the clothesline, tilted, with nothing holding it up. It may be falling, and if it comes straight down it would land on or near the washing line. Nobody in the picture is looking at the box or reacting to it. Near the house there is a flower bed with colourful flowers and a green hose reel, and two potted plants stand on the grass in front of the fence. A red bowl of dog food sits at the bottom of the lawn.
