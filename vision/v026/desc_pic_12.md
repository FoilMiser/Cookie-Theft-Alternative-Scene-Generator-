# Picture 12

## Free description

This is a sunny playground with a metal railing along the back, a slide on the left, an empty swing in the middle, a seesaw on the right, and a sandpit at the front left. A child in a yellow top and orange shorts is sliding down the slide with both arms up. A man in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest with blue trousers stands near the ladder of the slide, waving one hand high in the air. On the seesaw, an older man with a cap, brown waistcoat and white shirt sits on the low end with his feet on the ground, reading a book. At the raised end, a small girl in a pink dress is stuck up in the air with her legs dangling and kicking, and he does not seem to notice. In the front centre is a teal bicycle with square wheels, which is impossible to ride normally and is the clearest wrong thing in the picture. A small white and brown puppy is curled up asleep on the grass at the front, and a blue bird perches on the railing. The sandpit has a red bucket and blue spade but nobody playing in it.
