# Picture 63

## Free description

This is a sunny park with green hills and a line of bushes in the background, a winding sand-coloured path on the left and a blue pond on the right. There is just one person in the picture: a woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers is standing on the grass just in front of the pond, eating a pink ice cream cone with her eyes closed, looking contented. The odd thing in the picture is a wooden ladder standing on the grass to the left of centre. It stands up at a slant with nothing at all to lean against, so it looks like it should fall over. It is close to a tall black lamp post, but it doesn't touch the post, and it is leaning away from it. Nobody is on the ladder or near it, and the woman isn't looking at it. Otherwise there are trees, bushes, reeds at the edges of the pond, a grey rock near the bottom and a green rubbish bin in the bottom right corner. Nothing else seems to be about to happen, other than the ladder possibly toppling.

- Woman, centre-right, mid-foreground, in front of the left part of the pond: tan skin, long dark hair, purple short-sleeved top, teal trousers, dark shoes. Standing still, holding up a pink ice cream cone and eating or licking it, eyes closed, smiling.

Objects:
- Ladder: left of centre, wooden with dark rungs, standing at an angle on the grass with its top in open air, not leaning on anything. Seems impossible or about to fall.
- Lamp post: centre, tall, black, standing on the grass, with its lamp at the top.
- Pink ice cream cone: held by the woman.
- Pond: right, large blue oval with reeds (bulrushes) at both ends.
- Path: left, winding sand-coloured path from the back down to the bottom edge.
- Large tree: far left. Smaller tree: far right, behind the pond.
- Bushes: small bush bottom left area, another at the right end of the pond.
- Grey rock: bottom centre, on the grass.
- Green rubbish bin: bottom right corner.
- Hills and bushes: background, across the width of the picture.
- Sun, top left; two white clouds, top centre and top right.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The ladder: it could be read as an impossible, free-standing ladder that defies gravity, or as a ladder at the moment of falling over. A third possible reading is that it is meant to be leaning on the lamp post but is drawn too far from it; however, there is a clear gap between them and the ladder's top tilts away, so it does not look like it is leaning on the post. There is a small shadow at its base.
- The woman's face is partly hidden by her cone and the eyes are drawn as closed curves, so she could be smelling or tasting the ice cream rather than licking it.
- She stands overlapping the pond visually, so she could look as if she is standing in the water at first glance, but her feet are on the grass in front of it.
- Her lower arm hangs down with a small, slightly awkward hand.
