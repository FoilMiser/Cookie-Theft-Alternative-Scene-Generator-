# Picture 25

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a cream house with a red roof on the right, and a paved path on the left. An old woman with grey hair in a bun is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller, and about three planks' worth of the fence is already blue; a paint can and a paint tray sit on the grass behind her. A spotted white dog has clearly walked through the paint tray, because a trail of blue paw prints runs across the grass from the tray to the dog, and its paws look blue. A brown dog with a red neckerchief is sitting on the grass holding up a newspaper and reading it like a person, which is impossible. A young girl in a pink dress stands reaching one arm up towards a red balloon that has got away and is floating up by the fence, while she still holds a yellow and a purple balloon. A black-and-white cat is creeping along the top of the fence towards a red bird perched further along, so the cat looks about to pounce on it. An orange fish is sitting in the middle of the tree, which is impossible, since fish do not live in trees. A man with a beard sits on a bench at the bottom right reading a red book, and an orange cat is curled up asleep on the path. A blue bird flies overhead, and a birdhouse on a pole stands by the fence.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The red balloon's string: it hangs in a curve and ends in mid-air near the fence well above the girl's raised hand, so it could read as a balloon that has just escaped from her, or as one floating independently. The girl's raised hand does not actually reach it.
- The girl's raised hand may be reaching for the red balloon, or she may simply be waving.
- The white dog's paws look blue on the same paint colour as the prints, but they are small; the paw prints stop just short of the dog and start a little short of the tray, with a small gap.
- The cat on the fence: its front feet look slightly tangled with white paws hanging over the fence; it could read as crawling or as clinging on.
- The brown dog's pose is awkward, with a long body and legs splayed; it may look like a person-shaped dog rather than a natural sitting dog.
- The man sits at the edge of the picture and his feet look slightly bunched together; the bench is close to the door step but does not overlap it much.
- The roller's head is held upright at the very top of the fence and overlaps the top edge.
