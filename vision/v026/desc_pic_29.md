# Picture 29

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden seen with the cream house and red roof on the left and a paved path on the right. A woman with short dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes stands at the wooden fence with her back mostly to us, painting it blue with a long-handled roller. About three planks of the fence are already blue, and the roller is at the top of the next plank she is working on. A small paint can stands on the grass right beside her feet. Nobody else is in the picture. In the foreground there is a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can, and two potted plants stand near the fence on the right. A flower bed sits in front of the house. Everything looks normal and calm, and nothing seems about to go wrong.

- Grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles, centre-front, on the grass: parked, empty.
- Teal watering can, right-front, on the grass near the path: standing unused.
- Two terracotta pots with green plants, right of centre, near the fence: decorative.
- Flower bed with mixed colourful flowers, left, in front of the house.
- Two small bushes, one by the house and one near the pots.
- Wooden gate in the fence at far right, with a paved path running down from it.
- House with blue door and window, far left; tree behind the fence on the left.
- Sun at top right and two clouds in the sky.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The blue-painted planks and the part being painted are on the left of the woman, and the unpainted top of the plank closest to the roller is still plain; so she is painting towards the right.
- The roller's head sits against the very top of the fence and looks slightly raised above the plank; it could look like it is floating just above the top, although the pole connects it to her hands.
- The woman's face is turned away from us and only part of it shows, so her expression is hard to make out; she seems to be facing the fence and looking up.
- There is no tray in this picture, only a can, so it is not obvious how she loads the roller.
- Nothing seems strange otherwise.
