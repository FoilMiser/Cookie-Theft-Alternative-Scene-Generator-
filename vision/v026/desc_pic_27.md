## Free description

This is a sunny paved town square in front of a row of colourful shops with striped awnings. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is walking across the middle of the square reading an open book, not looking where he is going, and a red bucket stands on the ground in front of him and to the left, though he appears to be heading away from it rather than straight into it. A small girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench drinking from a cup with a straw. An old man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat stands near a lamp post holding a camera up to his eye and taking a photo, pointing it to the left. A woman in a yellow dress with short dark hair is walking to the right at the front, carrying a yellow shopping bag. Behind the old man is a market stall with crates of fruit. A bicycle is locked in a rack, and there is a small cafe table with two chairs at the bottom left. Nothing looks clearly wrong or impossible, though the boy may be about to bump into something because he is not watching his step.
