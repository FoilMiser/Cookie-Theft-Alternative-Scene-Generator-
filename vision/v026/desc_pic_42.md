# Picture 42

## Free description
This is a sunny park with rolling green hills in the background, trees, bushes, a winding sandy path on the left, a tall black lamp post in the middle and a blue pond on the right. Just behind him, an older woman with a grey bun and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, walks to the right pushing a teal and navy pram. A baby lies asleep in the pram under a pink spotted blanket. To the right, an old man in a cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers stands at the edge of the pond, holding a long fishing rod, with the line running down to a red and white float that has a ripple around it. The runner and the woman are heading in opposite directions and do not seem to be paying attention to each other. Nothing looks clearly wrong or impossible; everyone seems to be doing an ordinary park activity. A grey rock and a green bin sit on the grass at the front.
