# Picture 11

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. There is one person in the picture: a boy or young man in a red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes. He is standing beside the flower bed near the house, holding a dark pan by its handle out over the flowers. Water is pouring out of the pan in a thin stream, with drips landing around the edge of the bed. So he is watering the flowers with what looks like a frying pan or cooking pan rather than a watering can, which is the odd thing in this picture. Behind him, jeans and a yellow striped towel hang on a washing line. There are two potted plants by the fence and an empty wheelbarrow in the foreground. Nothing else is happening, and there is no one else around.
