He is heading toward a cardboard box lying on the ground in the foreground, so he might trip over it, although he seems to be moving off to one side of it. There is a fruit and vegetable stall with a striped canopy behind him, and a second box sits next to the stall. Overall it looks like a normal busy day with a few little problems going on.
