# Picture 50

## Free description

This is a sunny park with a pond on the left, a winding path on the right, green hills behind, and a tall black lamp post right of centre. In the front centre, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers looks down, holding a paper bag and letting seeds fall from his hand to a red bird and a blue bird pecking on the grass. On the right, a bearded man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers sits on a wooden bench holding up a pink ice-cream cone and looking at it. A brown dog with a red neckerchief sits up on its hind legs on the grass beside him, front paws raised, nose pointing up, as if begging. It looks as if the dog wants the ice cream, and it may be about to get it or to snatch it, though the man faces the other way. At the back near the lamp post, a small girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes stands holding the string of a yellow balloon. There is a flower bed at the front left, two green bins, and bulrushes at each side of the pond. Nothing looks clearly impossible, though the dog's upright pose is rather human-like.

## Inventory

- Boy, front centre: red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers, white shoes; standing, head bent down, feeding the birds from a paper bag.
- Red bird, front centre: on the grass by the boy's feet; pecking at seeds.
- Blue bird, front centre: right next to the red bird; pecking at seeds.
- Man, right: dark skin, black beard, green T-shirt, beige trousers, brown shoes; sitting on the bench, holding up an ice-cream cone and looking at it.
- Dog, right of centre: tan or brown with darker ears, white paws, red neckerchief; sitting up on its hind legs beside the bench, begging towards the man.
- Girl, centre back: brown skin, dark hair tied back, pink dress, yellow shoes; standing and holding a balloon string, looking left.
- Ice cream: pink scoop in a cone, in the man's raised hand.
- Balloon: yellow, on a string, in the air above the girl; held by her.
- Seeds: small dots falling from the boy's hand to the ground; food for the birds.
- Paper bag: brown, held at the boy's waist; holds the seeds.
- Bench: wooden, right; the man sits on it.
- Lamp post: tall, black, right of centre back; not lit.
- Bins: two green bins, one next to the lamp post, one at bottom left.
- Pond: large, blue, left side, with bulrushes at both ends; nothing is on it.
- Flower bed: oval of soil with mixed flowers, front left.
- Trees and bushes: a large tree on the right, a small tree on the left, and round bushes at each side.
- Path: pale, winding from the back down to the front right.
- Sun and clouds: sun top right, two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The dog could be begging for the ice cream, or simply sitting up looking at the man; the man does not look at the dog, and the cone is held on his far side from it, so the link between them is only suggested.
- One of the dog's paws reaches near a bench leg, and could read as it touching or leaning on the bench.
- The boy's head is tilted down so the face is largely hidden by his cap and hair.
- The girl's expression and what she is looking at are hard to judge; she stands alone and nothing obvious is happening with her balloon.
- The boy's upper body overlaps the pond behind him, which at a glance could make him seem to be standing at or in the water's edge, but his feet are clearly on the grass in front.
