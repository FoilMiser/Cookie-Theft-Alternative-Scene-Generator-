# Picture 45

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with no people or animals in it. A cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, a wooden fence runs along the back, and a gate and paved path are on the right. There is a flower bed, two bushes and two potted plants. A dark green hose comes in from the left edge of the picture and runs across the grass to a small garden sprinkler at the front of the lawn. The sprinkler is on, sending three thin arcs of water to the right. The two shorter arcs land on the grass, and the longest one lands on a closed cardboard box sitting on the lawn beside the path. Water is running down the sides of the box and there is a small puddle underneath it, so the box is getting soaked. Nobody is around to move it; it looks like a parcel that has been left out and is being ruined. Nothing looks impossible.
