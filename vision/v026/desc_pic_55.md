# Picture 55

## Free description

A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, a gate at the back right and a paved path down the right side. A bearded man in a green shirt and beige trousers sits on a wooden bench at the far left, sipping through a straw from a red and white cup. A woman with dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers, right of centre, is posed exactly as if sitting, with her knees bent and a similar drink at her lips, but there is no chair or bench under her, so she appears to be sitting on thin air. A third person, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes, is walking across the back of the lawn, carrying a cardboard box with a white label against her body. A teal watering can sits on the grass near the path and a hose reel stands by the flower bed. It looks as if the seated woman has nothing holding her up, which seems impossible. Nobody seems to be paying attention to anyone else.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The woman in purple could be read as sitting on an invisible chair (impossible or a deliberate oddity), or as a person crouching or squatting, but her pose matches the seated man and she has no visible support, so it looks like sitting on nothing.
- The box in the yellow-dressed woman's arms looks flat and semi-transparent where the fence lines meet it, and her forearm sits across its front, so it is slightly hard to tell how she is holding it. Could be read as carrying a box or as the box being stuck to the fence behind her.
- The woman in yellow's feet are on the lawn but her upper body overlaps the fence, which is normal perspective but makes her look quite tall compared with the fence.
