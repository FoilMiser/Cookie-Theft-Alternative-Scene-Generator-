# Picture 54

## Free description

A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path running down the right side. A woman with dark hair in a ponytail, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, stands right of centre holding up the end of a garden hose in both hands and peering closely into its nozzle. A single drop of water is hanging at the nozzle, but no water is flowing. A white goat with small curved horns stands on the lawn just behind the hose, with its hind feet at or on the hose. To the goat's left there is a large bulge in the hose lying on the grass, so it looks as if the water is trapped behind the point where the goat is standing. It seems the goat is standing on the hose and blocking the water, and the woman has not noticed and is looking down the nozzle to see why nothing is coming out. It looks as if she is about to get sprayed in the face if the goat steps off. The hose runs from a reel near the flower bed in front of the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Whether the goat is actually standing on the hose or the hose just passes behind or under its feet; the lower line of the hose meets its hind hooves, which reads as standing on it, but the front feet are unclear. Its front foot appears to sit near the hose too.
- The bulge could be read as water backed up behind the goat (most likely) or as a separate object lying in the hose's path, such as a dark blue pad or fat fitting.
- Her hold on the nozzle: she may be squeezing or fiddling with it, or just looking into it. The drop could be read as the last trickle or as a tiny blue shape in her hand.
- The goat's rear leg outline and the body look a little stiff and blocky, with a faint pale shape under its belly that I could not identify.
