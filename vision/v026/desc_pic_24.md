# Picture 24

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park, with a winding path on the left, a pond on the right and hills behind. At the front left, a brown dog is sitting up on its haunches like a person, holding an open newspaper in its front paws and apparently reading it, which is impossible and looks absurd. A woman with a long dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers walks to the right near the top of the path, carrying a yellow shopping bag. A small girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench in the centre, reading a red book. To the right of her, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a blue and a purple balloon by their strings in one hand, and reaches up with the other towards a red balloon that has got away, its string hanging loose; he is frowning and looks up at it. A white duck is swimming on the pond with no one near it. Nobody seems to notice the dog. A flower bed and a green litter bin are at the lower right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The dog could be read as really reading the paper, or as merely holding or playing with it. Its head faces right towards the paper, which favours reading, though its eyes do not obviously look down at the page.
- The red balloon could have just slipped from the boy's hand or could be someone else's that he is trying to catch. Since he has two others and the string hangs free, slipped seems likeliest.
- The woman seems to walk on the grass beside the path rather than on it, which may or may not be intended.
- The girl on the bench looks quite small compared with the boy and the woman, so it could be read as a younger child or just a size difference.
- The red balloon floats no higher than the lamp post's middle and looks as if it is hanging quite still rather than rising, but it is out of the boy's reach.
