# Picture 37

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence and gate along the back, a concrete path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the left an older man in a flat blue-grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is raking autumn-coloured leaves into a small pile with a long yellow-headed rake. On the right a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands with his back mostly to the man and pours water from a teal watering can over a bed of colourful flowers, with the water visibly falling. The strange thing is a cardboard box hanging in the sky above the fence in the middle of the picture, tilted at an angle, with nothing holding it up. It might be falling, or may have been thrown, but nobody is looking at it or reacting. A wooden bench stands empty at the bottom right. Both people seem calmly busy with their own garden chores and neither is paying attention to the other.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The box could be falling down, thrown over the fence, blown by wind, or simply floating impossibly. It has no motion lines and casts no shadow on the ground, and no one is near it or looks up. It could also look to some viewers like a deliberate "impossible" thing, or like a mistake in the drawing.
- The older man's head is turned to the right. He could be looking at the boy or just looking down at his raking; his gaze is not at the box.
- His shadow falls to the left, over the edge of the path. The sun is also on the left, so the shadow direction looks slightly wrong, though only slightly.
- His feet are very near the path edge; he seems to be half on the grass and half at the path, but this looks okay.
- The water lines fall through and in front of the flowers. They look fine but overlap with the flower stems so that it is a little hard to separate them.
- The watering can looks small against the boy, and the spout seems short.
