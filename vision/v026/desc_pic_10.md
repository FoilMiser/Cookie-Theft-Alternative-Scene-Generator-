# Picture 10

## Free description

This is a sunny park with a blue pond on the left, green hills behind, a tall black lamp post in the middle, a wooden bench, trees and a winding path on the right. At the front left, an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, kneels on the grass next to a flower bed. A pot with a pink flower stands by her hand, so she seems to be planting or tending it. In the centre front, a white goat with a dark curved horn has its head down and is eating a small tuft of grass. On the right, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands at the edge of the path holding a brown paper bag and sprinkling seeds or crumbs from his other hand. A line of small dots falls from his hand to the ground. Two small birds, one red and one blue, are pecking at the seeds on the path. Everyone is calmly doing their own thing, and nobody is interacting with anyone else except the man with the birds. The only slightly unusual thing is a goat loose in a park, and nothing looks about to go wrong.
