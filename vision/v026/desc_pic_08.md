# Picture 08

## Free description

This is a sunny, quiet back garden with a wooden fence, a house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a gate and paved path on the left. There is one person, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey-blue skirt, standing by a round flower bed. She is watering the flowers with a garden hose held in one hand; the dark blue-green hose runs from a reel on the ground near the house, along the front of the bed and up to the nozzle in her hand. A stream of water arcs out of the nozzle and falls onto the flowers, with a few drops splashing at the bottom. Two potted plants and a bush stand further back on the grass, and an empty wheelbarrow is parked in the foreground. Everything seems ordinary and there is no sign of anything about to go wrong. The hose looks a little stiff and curves up in front of her in a slightly unnatural way, but otherwise nothing is odd.

- The water stream and the hose run close together and have nearly the same curve, so at first glance the pale blue stream looks like a second hose or a stiff band rather than water. It also seems to go in front of the flowers in the back part of the bed.
- The hose passes in front of the flower bed and overlaps the soil, so it could be lying on the bed or on the grass in front of it.
- The reel is drawn with the hose looped around its base, and it is sitting on the edge of the bed, so I cannot tell whether it is fixed to anything or free-standing.
