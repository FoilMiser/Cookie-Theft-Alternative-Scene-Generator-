# Picture 19

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a long wooden fence across the back, and a gate and paved path on the left. A small girl in a pink dress stands in the middle front, holding a purple balloon and a blue balloon by their strings in one hand. With her other hand she reaches up towards a red balloon that has got away from her, its string dangling free as it floats up and to the left; she looks up at it and appears worried. Near the door on the right, a brown and white dog sits with a red lead held in its mouth, the loop of the lead lying on the grass, as though it is asking to be taken for a walk. An orange goldfish appears in the tree on the upper right, in front of the leaves, which is plainly impossible and looks wrong. A swing set stands empty on the left, a teal watering can sits on the lawn near the path, and a flower bed lies to the right. Nobody seems to notice the fish.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fish could be read as a real fish floating in the air in front of the tree, or a fish stuck in the tree. Either way it is impossible, and I cannot tell whether it is meant to be a live fish or some kind of toy or ornament.
- The red balloon might have just slipped from the girl's hand, or she might be reaching for one that came from elsewhere. Since she holds two others and its string hangs free, slipped from her hand seems likeliest.
- The dog's front overlaps the step and the door, and it sits very close to the house. It does not look wrong, just tight.
- The dog appears to have something at its neck that blends with the lead, so I am unsure if the lead is attached to a collar or just held in the mouth.
