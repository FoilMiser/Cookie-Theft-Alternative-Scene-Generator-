# Picture 60

## Free description

A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back, a gate at the back left and a paved path down the left side. A small girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench at the lower right, in front of the house, holding a pink ice cream up to her mouth. A brown dog with a red collar is sitting up on its hind legs right in front of her, begging, with its front paws raised and its face pointing up at the ice cream or at her. The girl does not seem to be looking at the dog, or may be looking down at it. It looks as if the dog hopes she will give it some or that it might grab the ice cream. Washing hangs on a line between two posts in the centre: a yellow towel or shirt with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans. A wheelbarrow, a teal watering can and two potted plants sit on the lawn, and a birdhouse on a tall pole stands near the fence on the left. Nobody else is in the garden, and nothing looks wrong apart from the dog's human-like begging pose.
