# Picture 44

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back and a gate and paved path on the right. A woman with a dark ponytail, in a purple top and teal trousers, stands by a colourful flower bed, holding a hose nozzle and spraying an arc of water down onto the flowers. The hose runs along the grass from a teal hose reel near the flower bed. In the middle of the lawn a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes is jumping in the air with both arms raised and a big grin, as if cheering; his shadow is on the ground below his feet. An obviously odd thing is that a large orange goldfish is in the tree behind the house, sitting among the leaves far from any water. Neither person seems to notice the fish. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands at the front of the lawn and two potted plants are near the fence. There is no water other than the hose, no bowl or pond the fish could have come from.

- Hose and reel (left, front): teal reel on a dark stand, thick hose curving across the grass up towards the woman.
- Hose nozzle and water stream (left of centre): yellow nozzle in her hand, pale blue arc of water falling into the flower bed with drops at the bottom.
- Flower bed (left of centre): round bed of dark soil with red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers.
- Two terracotta pots with green plants (right of centre, back): on the grass near the fence.
- Wheelbarrow (centre-right, front): grey with wooden handles, empty.
- House, fence, gate, path, tree, sun, two clouds: setting.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The goldfish in the tree: it looks impossible. It could be read as a real fish that has somehow ended up in the tree or is floating in front of it, or as a toy, decoration or kite caught in the branches. Nothing in the picture explains it, and it is drawn just like a real fish with a slightly plain, flat look.
- The boy: he could be jumping for joy or playing, or celebrating something; he does not seem to be looking at the fish, so it is not clear what he is excited about. He could also be read as exercising (a star jump).
- The hose rises stiffly from the ground to about her waist and seems to end behind her body, with the nozzle then in her hand. The middle stretch looks a bit unnatural, like a stiff loop standing in the air.
- The hose reel slightly overlaps the edge of the flower bed.
