A small girl in a pink dress is kneeling at the back corner of the sandpit, building a sandcastle with three towers and a small red flag on top. In the middle, a boy or young man in a red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes is kicking an orange ball, with curved motion lines showing the swing of his leg. He is facing right and the ball is right at his outstretched foot, moving away from the sandcastle. The two people are not paying attention to each other. The slide and bench are empty. The ball is heading off to the right, so the sandcastle does not seem to be in danger. Nothing looks clearly impossible, although the girl and castle sit strangely on the rim of the sandpit rather than in the middle of the sand.
