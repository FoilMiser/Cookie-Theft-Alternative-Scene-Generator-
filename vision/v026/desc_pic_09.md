# Picture 09

## Free description

This is a back garden under a grey sky with three big dark storm clouds overhead. There is a wooden fence with a gate on the left, a paved path, a washing line in the middle, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the path at the left, a woman in a yellow dress looks up with her mouth open and points at the sky or clouds. Beside her, a small child in pink sits on a red swing seat, smiling, with small motion lines suggesting the swing is moving. In the centre, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans holds the end of a garden hose up in front of his face with both hands and looks at the nozzle, from which only a single drop is coming. The hose runs back across the grass to a reel on the right, and a brown-and-white dog is standing on it. The hose has a fat, swollen section on the reel side of the dog, while the part between the dog and the man looks thin. It looks as if the dog is blocking the water, so the man is peering into the nozzle to see why nothing comes out, and he could get sprayed in the face if the dog steps off. Rain also seems about to fall, with clothes still out on the line.

## Inventory

- Woman, left, front-middle on the path: dark hair in a bun or short style, yellow dress, red shoes; looking up with her mouth open and pointing one arm at the sky.
- Small child, left-centre, under the swing frame: dark hair, pink outfit, sitting on a red swing seat with feet dangling; smiling, eyes squeezed shut, apparently swinging.
- Man, centre: brown hair, red T-shirt, blue jeans, dark shoes; standing and holding the hose nozzle up near his face, looking at it.
- Dog, centre-right, front: tan and white with a red collar and darker ears; standing on the hose, facing left toward the man.
- Dark clouds, top centre (three): storm clouds; suggest rain is coming.
- Swing set, left-centre: dark blue-grey A-frame with one red seat; in use by the child.
- Washing line, centre-back: two wooden posts and a line holding a yellow towel or top with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans, pegged up to dry.
- Garden hose and reel, centre to right: teal hose coming from a reel on the right, running along the grass under the dog, then curving up to the man's hands; a swollen bulge sits just to the right of the dog.
- Water drop, centre: one drop at the nozzle by the man's hands.
- Wheelbarrow, front centre: grey with a wooden handle; empty and unattended.
- Flower bed, right: round bed of soil with red, yellow, orange, purple and pink flowers.
- Gate, far left at the back: brown wooden gate at the end of the path, closed.
- House, right: cream walls, red roof, blue door, one window.
- Tree, back right: behind the fence, beside the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The woman could be pointing out the coming rain (perhaps warning about the washing), or she could just be pointing at something in the sky. She could be calling to the man, but she is not looking at him.
- The man could be puzzled that no water is coming out, or he could be trying to adjust the nozzle. His face is turned mostly to the side, so his expression is hard to read.
- The bulge could be read as water backed up behind the dog's paw, or as a separate fat piece of hose or a connector. It starts right about where the dog's hind paw is.
- It is hard to tell exactly which of the dog's paws is on the hose; the front paw is also very close to it.
- The hose rises almost straight up from the grass to the man's hands in a stiff curve, some way in front of his body, which looks slightly unnatural.
- The man's legs and feet are narrow and overlap, so his stance looks a bit odd.
- The child's swing ropes pass behind her and seem to meet near her body in a small V, which looks slightly strange. The motion lines are on one side only and are faint.
