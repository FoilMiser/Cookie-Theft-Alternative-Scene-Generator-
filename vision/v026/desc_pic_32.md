# Picture 32

## Free description

This is a busy, windy day in a paved town square in front of a row of colourful shops. White wind lines blow across the sky, and leaves are flying through the air. A woman in a yellow dress stands with both arms up reaching for a red cap that the wind has blown high up by a window, looking surprised. A small girl in a pink dress on the left is jumping in the air with her arms up, looking happy. An old woman sits on a bench reading a red book, with a sandwich beside her, and a blue bird is pecking at or stealing the sandwich without her noticing. A woman in a purple top is watering flowers in a planter with a teal watering can, and a street sweeper in an orange cap and vest is sweeping up a pile of autumn leaves near the lamp post. A man with a beard stands on the right with his hands on his hips, wearing a big metal bucket on his head as a hat, which looks silly. At the front, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is holding an empty ice cream cone and holding a hand to his or her face, looking upset, because a pink ice cream has fallen on the ground, and a brown dog with a red collar is bending down to eat the melted puddle. A white dog with a blue collar sits on the ground holding up a newspaper and reading it like a person, which is impossible. A red bird flies overhead.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The red cap: nobody in the picture is obviously missing it for certain. The woman in yellow reaches towards it, but she also could be throwing her hands up or waving, and the small girl is also raising her arms. The cap could belong to the woman or to someone else.
- The small girl's shadow is well below her feet on the ground, which reads as a jump. Could also look like she is floating.
- The woman in yellow overlaps the shop door and the window; she could look like she is standing in front of the door, though her feet are on the paving.
- The blue bird stands on the bench and on or near the sandwich; it could be eating it or just sitting by it. The sandwich looks oversized compared with the bird.
- The man's bucket hides most of the top of his head; it could read as a chef's hat or lampshade, but looks most like a metal bucket. It also overlaps the market stall's canopy behind him.
- The bird in the sky is close to the sun but does not overlap it.
- The man looks very close to the stall and his body hides much of the fruit; his hands on hips look a little awkward.
- The child's hand to the face could be crying or scratching the head; the other hand holds the cone downward, which is why the ice cream may have fallen.
- The woman in purple's water spray falls on the flowers in the planter but seems to fall very straight and rigid, like a fan of blue drops.
- The broom's head and leaves overlap at the sweeper's feet; the leaves look like a colourful heap with the bristles passing into it.
