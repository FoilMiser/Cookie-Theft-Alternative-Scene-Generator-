# Picture 52

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back, and a gate at the far left with a paved path running down from it. The only person is an old man with grey hair and a blue-grey cap, wearing a white shirt, a brown waistcoat, dark trousers and brown shoes, standing on the grass beside a flower bed. He is holding a teal watering can tilted towards the flowers. The strange thing is that the water does not fall from the spout: it curves upwards into the air in a bright stream, ending in a few drops above the flowers, which looks impossible. None of the water reaches the flowers. A grey wheelbarrow stands empty on the grass at the front, and two potted plants, a bush and a bird house on a tall post are on the left. Nothing else is going on, and there are no animals.

## Inventory

- Old man, right of centre: brown skin, grey hair, blue-grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat, dark trousers, brown shoes; standing, watering with the can, looking towards the water.
- Watering can: teal, held at waist height by the man, spout pointing right and slightly down.
- Water stream: blue-white ribbon from the spout curving up and to the right, ending in the air.
- Water drops: three small drops at the top of the stream, in the air.
- Flower bed: oval of soil with mixed coloured flowers, right, in front of the house; directly below the water but not getting wet.
- Wheelbarrow: grey tray, wooden handles, round black wheel, front centre; empty, not being used.
- Two potted plants: terracotta pots with green leaves, on the grass, left of centre.
- Bird house: small, wooden with a red roof and round hole, on a tall post, left of centre; no bird visible.
- Bush: round and green, left of centre, in front of the fence.
- House: cream walls, red roof, blue door with step, one window, right side.
- Fence and gate: wooden fence along the back, with a darker gate at the left.
- Path: pale paving slabs, left side.
- Tree and hedges: a tree behind the fence near the house, dark green bushes along the back.
- Sun and clouds: sun top left, two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The water: reading one, it is flowing upwards out of the can against gravity, which is impossible. Reading two, it has been flung up in an arc and is about to fall on the flowers, but the can is tilted down and there is no falling part of the stream, so the first reading seems more likely.
- Whether the man has noticed: his face is turned towards the stream, but his expression is calm, so he does not seem surprised.
- The can is held quite low and close to his body, and his hands overlap its handle, so the grip is a little hard to make out.
