# Picture 14

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the left, a washing line in the middle, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the left, a boy or young man in a red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes is kicking an orange ball toward the path, with curved lines showing his leg swinging. Toward the back in the middle, a tan dog with a red collar is bent forward with its bottom and tail up and its front paw on a small yellow-green tennis ball, its tail wagging as if it wants to play. On the right, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers stands by the flower bed holding a teal watering can. The strange thing is the water: instead of falling onto the flowers, it shoots from the spout in a long curve going upward, ending high above the flowers with a few drops at the top. That is the wrong direction for water from a watering can and looks impossible. The woman looks down at the can and does not seem to react. Nobody is looking at anyone else, and the ball being kicked is going away from the others.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The water could be read as going impossibly upward out of the spout, or as a stream coming down from above into the can. The drops at the top and the thin end at the spout make it hard to be certain, but it looks as if it leaves the spout and rises. The stream also looks white-edged and thick, almost like a solid tube.
- The dog could be playing with the tennis ball, bowing to invite play, or stretching. It may want the boy's orange ball too, but it is looking at its own ball or to the right.
- The boy's foot and the ball overlap, so he could be mid-kick or about to kick. The ball sits right at the border of grass and path and appears to hang slightly above the ground.
- The woman holds the can low beside her body, and her arm and the can overlap the flowers, so it is hard to see exactly how she grips it.
