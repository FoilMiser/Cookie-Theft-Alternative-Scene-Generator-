# Picture 67

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and a blue door, with a tall wooden fence and a gate on the right. An older woman with grey hair in a bun, wearing a teal top and a grey skirt, is standing at the clothesline in the middle of the lawn, stretching both arms up to peg a white T-shirt onto the line next to a pair of blue jeans that is already hanging there. She is the only person in the picture and there are no animals. Around the garden there is an empty wooden bench at the bottom left, a hose reel, a flower bed full of colourful flowers, two potted plants by the fence, a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can lying or standing on the grass near the bottom. A paved path runs from the gate down the right side. Everything looks calm and ordinary; it looks like an everyday scene of someone hanging out washing. Nothing seems to be about to happen, and I don't see anything that looks impossible.
