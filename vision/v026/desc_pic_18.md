# Picture 18

## Free description
This is a back garden under a grey, overcast sky with three dark storm clouds overhead, so it looks as though it is about to rain. A house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, a wooden fence runs across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path leads to that gate. A woman in a yellow dress stands by the flower bed and is watering the flowers with a garden hose, the water arcing from the nozzle down onto the bed. A bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands in the middle right, looking up and pointing at the dark clouds with his mouth open, as if warning that rain is coming. At the front, a boy in a red shirt and blue trousers is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red lead, both heading left. A swing set stands empty on the right. The slightly odd or funny point is that the woman is watering the garden just as it seems about to rain, and she does not appear to have noticed. Nothing physically impossible stands out.
