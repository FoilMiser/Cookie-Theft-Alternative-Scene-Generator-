# Picture 05

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a gate and paved path on the left. In the left foreground a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is standing on the path and sweeping up a small pile of dirt, but he is using a huge dinner fork instead of a broom, which is obviously wrong and funny. There is a little puff of dust and bits of dirt flying, and a red dustpan is lying next to the pile. In the centre an elderly woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal top and grey-blue skirt is digging a hole in the lawn with a long-handled spade; the spade has a clump of soil on it and there is a small mound of earth next to the hole. An orange cat is walking along the very top of the back fence, balancing on the pointed boards. Two potted plants stand on the grass near the back, a round flower bed sits by the house, and a hose reel is on the ground at the edge of the bed. Nobody seems to be interacting with anybody else. The fork looks far too big to be real, about as long as the boy's legs.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The giant fork: it could be meant as a real oversized fork being used as a broom by mistake, or it could be read as a garden fork or rake. The shape, though, is a smooth silver dinner fork rather than a garden tool.
- The dust and dirt: it looks like he might be spreading it about rather than collecting it, since dust is puffing up and bits are flying, but it could be read as him sweeping it towards the dustpan.
- The dustpan is lying on its own with the handle pointing away from him, so it is on the far side of the pile and nobody is holding it.
- The woman's spade: the blade seems to be going into the hole with the clump on top of it. I cannot tell if she is pushing in or lifting out.
- The mound by the hole looks like a neat flat-bottomed brown dome, slightly separate from the hole and sitting on top of the grass, rather than being loose soil.
- The cat seems to walk on the tips of the fence points, with its white paws touching individual pickets, which is unlikely but cartoon-plausible. Its hind leg and tail are thin and slightly stiff.
- The hose reel sits partly overlapping the edge of the flower bed. The hose does not lead anywhere, so it just looks stored.
