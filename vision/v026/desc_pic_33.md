# Picture 33

## Free description

It is a sunny day in a fenced playground with grass, a swing set, a slide and a sandpit. On the left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands by a tree and points up at a blue ball with a white stripe that is stuck on a branch in the tree, while a red bird flies just above the treetop. Behind him a bearded man in a green shirt sits on a bench reading a red book and does not look up, while a small blue bird on the bench beside him is right up against his sandwich and seems about to peck at it or steal it. In the middle an elderly grey-haired woman in a teal top and grey skirt stands holding binoculars or a camera to her eyes, aimed to the left and slightly upward. At the front a small child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and has a hand to one eye as if crying, because a pink scoop has fallen and melted on the ground, and a brown dog with a red neckerchief has its nose down right next to it. To the right a toddler in pink kneels in a sandpit working on a sandcastle with a little red flag on top. At the far right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands with her hands on her hips, wearing a bucket upside down on her head, which looks silly. The red bicycle parked at the front has square wheels, so it could not roll properly; this looks plainly wrong or impossible. An orange cat lies curled up with its eyes closed on the grass at the bottom left. The swing and the slide are both empty.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The device the elderly woman holds could be binoculars or a video or photo camera. I also cannot tell what she is aiming at: the tree, the ball, the bird, or something beyond the picture.
- The boy's pointing finger ends beside the tree trunk, below the ball. He could be pointing at the ball or at the red bird, as both are in about the same direction. The ball seems the likelier target.
- The blue bird could be stealing the sandwich or merely standing next to it. The contact between its head and the food looks close but I cannot see it actually taking a bite.
- The child could be crying or just rubbing an eye. The empty cone and the fallen scoop suggest crying.
- The dog could be sniffing or starting to eat the ice cream; its nose stops just short of it.
- The bucket could be worn on purpose as a joke or sun hat, or it could have landed on her; nothing else in the scene explains it and her pose looks annoyed or unbothered rather than playful. Her face seems turned toward the right of the picture, away from the playground, though this is hard to judge.
- The square wheels look like a deliberately impossible thing, but could be mistaken for a drawing glitch. The wheels have spoke-like lines inside square frames.
- The cat is lying twisted with its head low and could be read as sleeping or, less likely, hurt.
- The ball and its branch look a bit pasted onto the tree, with little lighter leaf clumps around it, and the ball is large compared with the branch holding it.
- Nothing is clearly passing through anything else, although the blue bird, sandwich and bench are crowded together and hard to separate at a glance.
