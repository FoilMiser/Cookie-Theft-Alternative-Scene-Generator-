# Picture 66

## Free description

This is a windy day in a sunny park, with hills and bushes in the background, a winding path on the left and a blue pond on the right. White wind lines are drawn in the sky. At the back on the path, an older woman with grey hair and glasses, wearing a teal jacket and a grey skirt, has both arms thrown up and her mouth open, looking surprised, because a red cap is flying away above her along with a few small leaves carried on the wind. In the foreground on the left, a man with brown hair, a red shirt and blue trousers is walking along the path pushing a teal pram, in which a baby is sleeping under a pink spotted blanket. The man and the pram are heading left, and the pram is partly off the path on the grass. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands on the grass at the edge of the pond, fishing with a long rod, and her red float is sitting on the water with a ripple around it. No one else is reacting to the wind or the flying cap, and the woman's arms suggest she might be trying to catch her cap as it blows away. An empty wooden bench, a green bin, a lamp post, a rock and another bin are scattered around the park.
