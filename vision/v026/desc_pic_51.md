# Picture 51

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a gate at the far right with a paved path running down from it. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands in the middle looking up, flying a yellow kite on a long line, and the kite with two little bows on its tail is high in the sky. Next to him, a man with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller, and a section of about three boards is already blue, with a paint tin on the grass near his feet. At the front, a sprinkler on the end of a green hose shoots three arcs of water to the right. Two of the arcs land on the grass and the third is hitting a cardboard box, which stands in a blue puddle with water running down its sides. That is odd: the sprinkler is soaking a box, while the flower bed just to its left gets no water at all. The box will presumably get ruined. There is also a bird house on a tall post, two potted plants, and a bush near the house, and there are no animals in the picture.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Whether the sprinkler is meant to be aimed at the box or the box has just been left in the way: it could be an accident waiting to ruin the box, or just careless placement. Either way the water is not doing anything useful.
- The man stands side-on to the fence, facing right along it, and reaches sideways with the roller, rather than facing the fence; it looks slightly awkward.
- The roller head could also be a wide brush. It stands a little higher than the top of the fence.
- The kite line passes behind or just above the man's head area and across the hedge; it does not seem to touch him but it comes fairly close.
- The two arcs landing short end in small marks on the grass that could be splashes or small sprouting tufts.
