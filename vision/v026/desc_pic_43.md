# Picture 43

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence along the back and a gate and paved path on the left. A woman with dark hair in a yellow dress and red shoes stands at the fence with a long-handled brush, painting it blue; three boards are already blue, and a tin of blue paint is on the grass by her feet. An orange cat is walking along the top of the fence right above the freshly painted section, so it looks as if it could get paint on its paws. To the right a man with brown hair in a red T-shirt and blue trousers holds a teal watering can over towards a bed of colourful flowers. Something is clearly wrong with the water: instead of pouring down onto the flowers, it comes out of the spout and curves upward into the air, with drops at the top. A grey wheelbarrow, empty, stands on the lawn at the front, and an empty wooden bench is on the right. Two potted plants and a tall post with a birdhouse are near the left of the fence. Neither person seems to notice the cat or the odd water.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water going upward from the watering can looks impossible. Reading one: the water is defying gravity. Reading two (weaker): the stream is coming from somewhere else and falling into the can, but the drops at the top and the join at the spout suggest it comes out of the can.
- The cat: it could be about to step in wet paint or may already be walking on it; the boards under its feet are blue. Its paws are white rather than blue, so it has apparently not picked up any paint yet (or those are just its markings).
- It is not clear whether the woman is using a brush or a roller on a stick; the blue block at the end of the handle could be either.
- The cat is close enough that the brush might touch it soon, but she is painting a little to the left of it.
