# Picture 15

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. Three people are working. An older man with a grey-blue cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers stands near the fence digging with a spade, with a dark hole and a mound of soil next to him. An older woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal top and grey skirt kneels beside the flower bed by the house with a pot holding a pink flower, and seems to be planting. On the path at the right, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is sweeping a small pile of dirt toward a red dustpan, but he is using a giant silver fork as if it were a broom. The fork is shaped like a dinner fork but huge, and using it to sweep is the odd thing in the picture. A little cloud of dust rises from the pile. There are also two potted plants on the grass and an empty wheelbarrow at the front. Nobody is talking or looking at anyone else; each person is busy with their own job.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The fork could be read as a giant dinner fork, or as an oddly drawn garden fork or rake. Its shape and shiny colour look like cutlery. The man could also be read as raking.
- The grey shape at the top of the fork handle, by his hands, looks like a big rounded grip and is a bit hard to read; it partly merges with his hands and shirt.
- The older woman's foot is partly hidden behind the pot and flower bed, and her arm disappears in front of her, so I cannot tell if she is holding the pot or working in the soil.
- The older man is very close to the fence and seems to be pressing the spade while standing just next to the hole; he could be digging or filling the hole in.
- The dark shadow under the man on the right extends off the edge of the picture.
