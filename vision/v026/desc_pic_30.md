# Picture 30

## Free description

This is a sunny park with a pond, a curving path, rolling hills and trees. An old man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat walks to the right pushing a teal pram in which a baby is sleeping under a pink spotted blanket. A workman in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest stands near a tall lamp post digging with a shovel, and there is a low mound of earth beside the hole. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands on the path with his arm raised holding a string, and his purple kite is stuck high up in the tree on the right, so it has got caught in the branches. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes walks at the bottom right carrying a cardboard box. A wooden bench stands empty next to the path. A flower bed and a green bin are at the bottom left. Nobody is in the pond. It looks like the boy is trying to tug his kite free from the tree.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The kite: the string goes from the boy's hand into the tree, and the kite is partly hidden by leaves, so it is stuck in the tree. It could also be read as the boy trying to fly the kite past the tree, but the leaves over it make "stuck" the likelier reading.
- There are two extra green leaf clumps drawn over the kite that are outlined separately on top of the tree; these look a bit pasted on.
- The kite's string looks loose and curved instead of pulled tight, so he might not actually be tugging at it.
- The workman's head and the lamp post are close, and the lamp post stands just to the left of him; he seems to be digging right beside it. His digging spot is flat, and the mound looks like a thin brown arch rather than a pile of earth.
- The old man's body overlaps the pond behind him; he looks like he is walking in front of it, and could be read as very close to the water, though his feet are on the grass well in front of it.
- The boy stands on the edge of the path and his head overlaps the path's edge; the bench is right behind him and could look like he is touching it.
- The woman's box seems to float slightly ahead of her, held only by her hands.
