# Picture 17

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park, with a winding sand-coloured path on the left, a pond on the right, and green hills in the distance. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun stands at the front left by the path, holding a camera up to her eye and taking a photo towards the right of the scene. In the middle right, a man in a red shirt and blue trousers stands at the edge of the pond holding a brown paper bag in one hand and stretching out the other towards a white duck. Small pale crumbs are scattered on the water around the duck, so he appears to be feeding it, and the duck is swimming towards the crumbs with white wake lines behind it. A tall wooden ladder stands on the grass near the centre, tilted to the right, but its top rests against nothing at all, stopping in mid-air just short of a tall black lamp post. That unsupported ladder is the thing that looks wrong, because it ought to fall over. Nothing else dramatic seems about to happen; the scene is calm otherwise.
