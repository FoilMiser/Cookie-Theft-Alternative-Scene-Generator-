# Picture 40

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence along the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. In the middle of the lawn a woman with a long dark ponytail, in a purple top and teal trousers, stands with both hands out in front of her. A red ball with a white stripe is in the air just in front of her hands, and a dotted line curves from the ball down to a small girl in a pink dress, who has one arm stretched towards the woman. It looks as though the girl has just tossed the ball and the woman is about to catch it. In front of the blue door a brown dog sits holding a red lead in its mouth, with the end of the lead looped on the ground, as if it is asking to go for a walk. In the front of the lawn a white dog with brown spots is bowing down with its back end up and tail wagging, nose close to a yellow-green tennis ball. On the right an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses is sweeping a pile of red, orange and brown leaves with a long broom at the edge of the path. A teal watering can stands unused on the grass near her. There is a bed of colourful flowers near the house. Nothing looks clearly impossible, though nobody is paying attention to the dog with the lead.

- Small girl (centre, middle distance, near the fence): dark hair in a bun, pink dress, yellow shoes; arm stretched out towards the woman, stepping forward, seems to have thrown the ball.
- Older woman (right, front, on the edge of the path): grey bun, glasses, teal cardigan, grey skirt; sweeping leaves with a long-handled broom.
- Brown dog (far left, front of the blue door): brown and white, red collar; sitting upright holding its red lead in its mouth.
- White spotted dog (centre, front): white with brown patches, blue collar; in a play bow, tail up and wagging, nose by the tennis ball.
- Red ball with white stripe (centre-left, in the air by the woman's hands): being thrown or caught; dotted line shows its path from the girl.
- Yellow-green tennis ball (centre, front, on the grass): right at the spotted dog's nose, seemingly the dog's toy.
- Red lead (far left): held in the brown dog's mouth, lying in a loop on the ground.
- Pile of autumn leaves (right, front): red, orange and brown, being swept together by the broom.
- Teal watering can (right of centre, front): standing on the grass, not being used.
- Flower bed (left of centre): round bed of dark soil with red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers.
- Long broom with yellow bristles (right): in the older woman's hands, its head in the leaf pile.
- House, fence, gate, path, tree, bush, sun, two clouds: background setting.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Direction of the ball: most likely the girl threw it to the woman (the dotted line trails back to the girl), but it could also be read as the woman having just thrown it to the girl, whose arm is out to catch it.
- The ball is slightly above and beyond the woman's hands rather than in them, so she has not quite got it yet.
- The brown dog with the lead: could be simply carrying it, or waiting and asking for a walk. There is no person holding the lead.
- The pile of autumn leaves does not match the tree, which is fully green with no fallen-leaf look, though this is only mildly odd.
- The spotted dog could be sniffing or nudging the ball, or getting ready to pounce on it.
- Nothing is clearly floating or passing through anyone, apart from the ball being in mid-air as part of the throw.
