# Picture 16

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A single woman stands in the middle of the lawn with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers. She has a silver-white bucket upside down on her head, like a hat, and it covers the top of her head down to her eyebrows. She has her hands on her hips and is looking off to the right, and she does not look bothered by the bucket. Wearing a bucket as a hat is the obvious odd thing here. Around her are a teal watering can and an empty wheelbarrow on the grass at the front, two potted plants, two bushes, a flower bed, a bench by the house, and a birdhouse on a tall pole by the fence. Nothing else is happening, there are no animals, and nothing appears about to happen.
