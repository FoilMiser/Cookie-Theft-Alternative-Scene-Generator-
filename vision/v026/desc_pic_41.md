# Picture 41

## Free description
This is a sunny playground on a grass field with a metal railing fence along the back. On the left is a red seesaw on a grey triangular support. An old man in a cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers sits on the low end with his feet on the ground, reading an open book. A little girl in a pink dress sits on the high end, up in the air, holding the handle with her feet dangling and small curved lines below them. The man's weight is keeping her stuck up high, and because he is reading he does not seem to notice her. In the middle is a large swing frame with one empty red seat, and behind it a green bin. On the right is a tall red slide frame with a yellow and orange slide and a ladder, with nobody on it. In the front right a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts kneels in a wooden sandpit, building a sandcastle with a small red flag on top. A grey rock lies on the grass next to the sandpit. Nothing else looks impossible; the odd part is the seesaw being stuck with the child high up while the adult reads.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl could be stuck and waiting for the man to notice, or she could be happily sitting up there; her face does not show clear distress. The curved lines under her feet could mean she is swinging or kicking her legs, or that the seesaw has just moved.
- The man might be sitting on the seesaw on purpose, or simply using it as a seat without realising there is a child on the other end.
- His open book seems to show its printed lines towards us rather than towards him, which looks a little strange.
- His white sleeve looks rather thick and smooth, almost like a bandaged arm, but it is probably just a shirt sleeve.
- The boy is partly hidden by the sandcastle, so it is hard to see his hands and what exactly he is doing with them.
