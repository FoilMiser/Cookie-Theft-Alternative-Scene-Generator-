# Picture 01

## Free description

This is a sunny day in a playground or park with a dark metal railing fence running across the back and a line of green bushes behind it. On the left, a small girl in a pink dress and a large bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers are on a red seesaw. The man is sitting on the low end at the front, reading an open book and paying no attention to the seesaw, and his weight has pushed his end right down to the ground. The little girl is stuck high up at the other end, her feet dangling and kicking in the air, with small motion lines under her feet, and she is looking across towards him. She seems unable to get down or make the seesaw move because he is so much heavier and is not noticing her. On the right, a girl in a yellow top and orange shorts is coming down a red-framed, yellow and orange slide with both arms thrown up in the air, apparently enjoying herself. She is about halfway down and will reach the bottom soon. There is nothing obviously impossible here, but the seesaw is very unbalanced and the man sitting on a child's seesaw reading a book is a funny, odd thing to do.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The girl on the seesaw: she could be read as stranded and frustrated because the man is too heavy, or as just happily bouncing and kicking her legs. Her face looks fairly neutral, so I cannot tell which.
- The man: he could be an adult using the seesaw as a seat without realising there is a child on the other end, or he could be deliberately playing with her while reading. He does not appear to be looking at her at all.
- The girl's seat seems to sit on the very end of the plank and she looks slightly perched rather than firmly seated; the handle is just in front of her.
- The man's knees are bent and his feet are on the ground, in front of and overlapping the low end of the plank, so the plank seems to run behind or through his legs a little; it reads as him straddling it, but the overlap is slightly hard to make out.
- The girl on the slide appears to be sitting on the chute with her feet ahead of her, but her body overlaps the side rail, so she could be read as on top of the chute or slightly beside it.
