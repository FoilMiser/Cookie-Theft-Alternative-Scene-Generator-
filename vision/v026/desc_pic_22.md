# Picture 22

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. An elderly woman with grey hair stands in the middle, reaching up with both hands to hang washing on a clothes line strung between two wooden posts. Blue jeans, a yellow and red striped towel and a white T-shirt are already on the line, held with pegs. On the paved path at the lower right, a small girl in a pink dress holds a string and looks up at a yellow kite with a bow tail flying high in the sky. A black cat with white paws walks along the top of the fence at the left, in front of the tree. Nothing looks about to go wrong in any obvious way, though the kite string runs a long way across the garden. A grey wheelbarrow, a teal watering can and two potted plants stand around the lawn unused, and a flower bed and hose reel are at the left. Everything looks plausible; I do not see anything impossible.

- The woman's feet stand further back on the lawn than the bases of the clothes posts, yet her body overlaps the washing as if she is in front of it, so the depth looks slightly off.
