# Picture 59

## Free description

A busy, sunny, windy park with a pond, a winding path, a lamp post, trees and wind lines in the sky. An older man in a blue cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers stands at lower left, holding out a frying pan and pouring a stream of water from it onto a flower bed, which is an odd thing to water flowers with. Next to him a small girl in a pink dress is crying, holding an empty ice-cream cone, and her pink ice-cream scoop has fallen and lies melted on the ground at her feet. A brown dog with a red collar is sniffing or licking at the fallen ice cream right next to her. On a bench at the right, a woman in a yellow dress is reading a red book, not noticing that a blue bird has landed beside her and is pecking at her sandwich on the bench. Behind her on the path an older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, throws her arms up in alarm because the wind has blown her red hat off her head; the hat is flying away and small seeds or leaves are in the air with it. At lower right, a white dog with a blue scarf is sitting upright on the grass holding and reading an open newspaper as if it were a person, which is impossible. An orange cat is asleep curled up at the edge of the path, a white duck swims on the pond, a white goat grazes behind the lamp post, and a red bird flies in the sky.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The frying pan with water could be read as a silly joke (using a pan as a watering can) or as a mistake in the picture. The pan seems to hold only water, and the water falls in a thick arc that looks a little like it comes from the rim.
- The small green and brown bits near the hat could be leaves, seeds, or something falling out of the hat, such as sweets. Their cause is unclear.
- The older woman's raised arms could mean she is reaching to catch her hat or expressing shock; her hat is a cap with a peak, and it could be read as blown off by the wind.
- The girl may be crying over the fallen ice cream or may be rubbing her eye. The dog may be eating the ice cream or sniffing at it; it also could have caused the drop.
- The woman on the bench may be aware of the bird or not; she appears absorbed in her book.
- The item beside the sandwich is unclear, and the blue bird's position, overlapping the sandwich and the bench's right side, makes it hard to see what it is doing exactly.
- The white dog holding a newspaper is clearly impossible for a real dog, so it could be a deliberate oddity. The dog's body is very twisted and its legs are thin and awkward.
- The orange cat's pose is hard to read: curled with closed eyes, it could be sleeping, or could possibly be read as hurt or fallen, as it lies right at the path's edge.
- The goat is close to the lamp post, with its rear overlapping the post slightly in depth, but it does not appear to pass through.
