# Picture 58

## Free description

A sunny playground with a metal railing fence at the back, a large tree on the left, a swing set in the centre, and a red-framed slide on the right. Two children are sitting on a red seesaw at the left of the picture: a small girl in a pink dress on the left end and a child with curly dark hair in a yellow top and orange shorts on the right end. The seesaw is nearly level, tipped very slightly with the left end higher, even though the left child looks smaller and lighter, and neither child seems to be pushing up. A bearded man in a green shirt and beige trousers walks across on the far right, holding a pink ice-cream cone up near his face and looking at it, facing left. The swing and slide are empty. A dark green bin stands near the fence and a grey rock sits on the grass at the lower right. It is a calm, ordinary scene, and nothing seems about to go wrong, though the man is walking towards the slide area with his eyes on his ice cream.
