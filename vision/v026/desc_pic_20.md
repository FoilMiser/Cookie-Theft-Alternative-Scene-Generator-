# Picture 20

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. A woman in a yellow dress stands in the middle, holding the end of a garden hose up in front of her face with both hands and looking into the nozzle, with only a single drop of water coming out. A brown dog is standing on the hose as it runs along the ground, and the hose bulges up just behind the dog, so the dog seems to be blocking the water. It looks as if the water may burst out into the woman's face if the dog steps off. On the right, a smiling boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is swinging on a swing, with motion lines behind him. A cardboard box hangs in the sky in the upper right, tilted, with nothing holding it up, which looks impossible or as if it is falling. A grey wheelbarrow stands empty on the lawn at the front, and a flower bed is at the left.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The bulge in the hose could be read as water building up behind the dog's foot, or as a flat puddle or a thicker piece of hose on the ground; it looks dark teal and spreads out a little.
- It is not quite clear which of the dog's feet is on the hose, as the hose passes right underneath its back and front paws.
- The woman's face is mostly turned away, so I cannot read her expression, though her pose suggests puzzlement at why no water is coming out.
- The motion lines by the boy are thin and could be missed, but the slanted swing ropes support the idea that he is swinging.
