# Picture 38

## Free description

This is a back garden under a grey overcast sky with three big dark storm clouds and no sun. A wooden fence and gate run along the back, a concrete path runs down the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door stands on the right. In the middle of the foreground a man with brown hair, a red T-shirt, blue jeans and dark shoes stands with his head tilted back, looking up and pointing one finger at the clouds. His wide eyes and small mouth make him look concerned or surprised, as though he has noticed that rain is coming. No rain is falling yet, but it looks as if it might start soon. A teal watering can sits on the grass on his left near the path, and two terracotta pots with green plants stand near the fence behind it. A bed of colourful flowers grows on the right, and a wooden bench stands empty at the bottom right. He is the only person, and there are no animals. Nothing looks impossible.
