# Picture 48

## Free description

This is a sunny park with a winding path on the left, a large pond on the right, rolling green hills behind, and a tall black lamp post in the middle. On the left, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes holds a paper bag and sprinkles seeds from her hand onto the grass, where a blue bird and a red bird are pecking at them. In the middle, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands at the edge of the pond holding a paper bag, tossing crumbs toward the water. A white duck on the pond faces left towards the crumbs, which float near it and also lie by the bank, and white lines behind it show it is moving through the water. At the front right, a child with short curly hair, a yellow top and orange shorts sits on a red and white checked picnic blanket next to a wicker basket, with eyes closed and a contented look. An empty wooden bench stands by the path, a grey rock sits at the front, a flower bed is at the bottom right, and there are two green bins. The two feeders are doing the same kind of thing separately, one for land birds and one for the duck. It looks like the duck is about to eat the crumbs. Nothing looks clearly impossible.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The child on the blanket has closed eyes and blushing cheeks: could be asleep, or could be happily sunbathing or enjoying the picnic. The child could also be a boy or a girl.
- The woman's face is tilted so far down that it is mostly hidden by her hair; hard to tell her expression.
- The paper bags are tucked against the bodies and the arms overlap them, so it is not fully clear how each is being held.
- Some crumbs seem to hang in mid-air and others are on the bank, but that reads as them being thrown rather than a mistake.
- The white lines behind the duck could be a wake, or stray marks; they look quite stiff and straight for water.
