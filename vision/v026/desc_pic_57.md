# Picture 57

## Free description

A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back, a gate at the back left and a paved path down the left side. A small boy with curly dark hair in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands near the house, pointing up at a tree where a blue and white ball is stuck on a branch among the leaves. A bearded man in a green shirt and beige trousers walks across the lawn towards the right, reading an open book as he goes, not looking up. A man with brown hair in a red shirt and blue jeans walks up the path on the left carrying a cardboard box, facing left towards the gate. In the foreground a black cat with white paws is crouched low and stretched out, right behind a small red bird that is pecking at the ground, and seems about to pounce. A blue bird flies overhead. An empty swing set stands left of centre, and a red bucket, a teal watering can and a hose reel are scattered around the garden. The boy seems to want someone to help him get his ball down, but neither man is looking at him.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The man with the book may simply be strolling, or may be about to walk into something; he does not seem to be heading straight for anything specific. There is a red bucket ahead of him and to the right, so he could trip on it, but it lies slightly off his line.
- The bird's head-down pose with tail up could be read as pecking or as tumbling over.
- The man in red's direction is a little ambiguous: his head faces left towards the gate, but his stride looks as though he is walking down or across the path. He could be arriving or leaving, and could be a delivery person.
- The ball sits on the branch with leaf clusters around it, and it could be read as stuck in the tree or balanced on a branch.
- The boy's pointing hand is well below the ball and could also be read as pointing at the tree generally.
