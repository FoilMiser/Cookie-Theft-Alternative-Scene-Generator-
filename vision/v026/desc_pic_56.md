# Picture 56

## Free description

A sunny playground with a metal railing fence at the back, a large tree on the left, and low hedges behind the fence. A small girl in a pink dress, towards the back centre, is holding the string of a yellow diamond kite that is flying high in the sky with bows on its tail. An older child with curly dark hair, in a yellow top and orange shorts, is sliding down a red-framed orange and yellow slide on the right, arms raised in front of them. In the foreground, a black cat with white paws is crouched low and stretched out, sneaking up on a small red bird that is pecking at the ground just in front of its nose. It looks as if the cat is about to pounce on the bird, which does not seem to have noticed. There is an empty red seesaw at the left, tilted up to the right with nobody on it. A sandpit at the lower right holds a red bucket and a blue spade, and a grey rock sits beside it.
