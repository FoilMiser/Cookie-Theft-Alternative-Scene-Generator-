# Picture 03

## Free description

This is a busy sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a gate and paved path on the left. At the back left a woman with long dark hair, a purple top and green trousers is painting the fence blue with a roller, and part of the fence is already blue. A paint tin and a paint tray are on the grass beside her. A white dog with brown patches and a blue collar has clearly stepped in the paint tray, because a trail of blue paw prints leads from the tray across the grass to the dog, and its feet are blue. Nobody seems to have noticed the dog yet. In the middle at the back, a girl in pink and a girl in yellow and orange are throwing a red and white ball to each other; a dotted line shows the ball's path through the air from the pink girl towards the other girl, and the ball is about to reach her. On the right a bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers is watering the flower bed with a green watering can. In the left foreground a man or boy in a red shirt and blue trousers sits at the edge of the path drinking through a straw from a red cup, and at the front a brown puppy is curled up asleep on the grass next to a red bowl of dog food. A red bird is perched on top of the fence.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The seated person: there is nothing visible to sit on. He seems to be sitting in mid-air or on the edge of the path, with his body overlapping the path line and his legs on the grass. It could be read as sitting on a kerb, or as floating with no seat.
- His age: he could be a teenage boy or a young man; the face looks childlike, but the body is adult-sized.
- The dotted arc looks as if it starts at the pink girl's hand and peaks near the ball, so the ball seems to be heading to the girl in yellow. It could also be read as the ball having been thrown from the other way, but the line only joins up with the pink girl.
- The woman's roller head looks as if it is standing straight up against the fence and is partly above the top of the boards, with the blue patch going right up to the pickets and slightly past where she is reaching.
- The dog's paw prints start just beyond the tray and look evenly spaced, like a pattern. They are clear, but the first group is a small distance from the tray.
- The puppy could be sleeping or just lying down with its eyes shut; it looks asleep. It is not clear whose food the bowl is, the puppy's or the bigger dog's.
- The water from the watering can falls as a thick band of drops, over the flowers rather than beside them, and looks a bit like a beaded curtain.
