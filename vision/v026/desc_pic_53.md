# Picture 53

## Free description

This is a sunny shopping street with a row of four coloured buildings with striped awnings along the back and a paved square in front. The strangest thing is at the front centre: a brown dog with a red neckerchief sits on the ground holding an open newspaper in its front paws and appears to be reading it. On the right, a blue ball with a white stripe is stuck on a branch in a tree, and a small girl in a pink dress stands nearby pointing up at it, so she seems to want it back. In the middle, a woman with dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers stands with one arm raised, waving, with small lines by her hand showing the movement. A boy with short curly hair, a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts stands near her holding a yellow balloon on a string. A blue bird is perched on the top of a tall black lamp post at the left. On the left there is a fruit and vegetable stall with crates under a striped canopy, with no one serving at it, and a cardboard box on the ground beside it. At the front are a red bicycle in a metal rack, a bin, a small white cafe table with two black chairs, and two potted plants at the corners.

- The woman: she could be waving hello to someone, to the boy or to someone out of view on the left, or trying to get attention. Her gaze does not clearly connect to anyone in the picture.
- The girl: she could be pointing out the ball to someone, or asking for help, but she is not obviously connected to the woman or boy. It is not clear whose ball it is.
- The bird stands in front of a window of the green building, so at a glance it could look as if it is on the window, but it is on the lamp.
- The bicycle wheels appear solid black with no visible spokes, and the bike overlaps its rack, so it is hard to tell if it is locked or just leaning there.
