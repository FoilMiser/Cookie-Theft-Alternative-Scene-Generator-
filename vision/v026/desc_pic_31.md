# Picture 31

## Free description

This is a sunny park with no people or animals in it at all. A curving path runs from the bottom up to a large tree on the left, and a blue pond with reeds lies on the right. A tall black lamp post stands in the middle of the grass, and right next to it a long wooden ladder stands upright on the grass, but it is not leaning against the lamp post or anything else. That looks odd, since a ladder standing up by itself like that should fall over. Everything else looks normal: a flower bed and a green bin at the bottom right, a grey rock near the bottom centre, a small tree and bushes around the pond, and rolling green hills behind. Two clouds and the sun are in the sky. It looks as though the ladder might topple, or that someone has just left it there.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The ladder could be read two ways: as leaning against the lamp post (its top is close to the lamp post's level, and it tilts in the opposite direction from it) or, more literally, as standing on its own with nothing supporting it. Its top leans away to the left, away from the lamp post, so it does not touch the post. This looks physically impossible.
- The ladder's top reaches just above the horizon of bushes, so it could look like it is resting on the hills in the background, but it is not connected to them.
- There is a small shadow under the ladder's foot, so it seems to be standing on the ground rather than floating.
- It is a very empty picture, and it could be read as a scene where nothing is happening, with the ladder as the only odd thing.
