# Picture 39

## Free description

This is a sunny paved shopping street with a row of four pastel-coloured shop buildings with striped awnings along the back. On the left an elderly grey-haired woman in a teal top and grey skirt walks to the left, leading a small white dog with brown spots on a red lead, and the dog has its tongue out. In the middle a woman with a long dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers stands and waves one raised hand, with small curved marks beside the hand showing the waving, and she faces to the left. On the right of centre a brown dog with a red neckerchief has its head down at a pink ice cream that has fallen and melted on the ground. Beside the dog a small girl in a pink dress holds an empty cone in one hand and has the other hand to her eye as if crying. It looks as though the dog is about to eat the ice cream. At the far left is a fruit stall with crates of green, yellow, orange and red fruit under a striped canopy, with no one serving at it and a cardboard box on the ground beside it. An empty bench, a teal bicycle at a metal bike rack, a tree in a round planter, a green bin, a lamp post and a potted plant complete the street. Nothing looks impossible.
