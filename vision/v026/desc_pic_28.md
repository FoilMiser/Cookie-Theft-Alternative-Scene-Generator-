# Picture 28

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a paved path on the left. The only person is a woman with a dark ponytail, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, standing on the path at the far left and looking down. She is holding a giant silver dinner fork with both hands, as if it were a broom, and is using it to sweep a little pile of dirt and dust towards a red dustpan on the path. This looks wrong, because a fork is not a sweeping tool and it is far too big. Puffs of dust rise from the pile. In the middle of the lawn stands a swing set with a red seat that is empty and still, and a wheelbarrow stands in the foreground. A coiled hose sits beside the flower bed by the house. Nobody else is in the garden.
