# Picture 04

## Free description

This is a calm, sunny park with rolling green hills and a line of bushes in the distance. There is only one person in the picture, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top, a grey-blue skirt and brown shoes. She is standing on the grass at the near edge of a blue pond and holding a long fishing rod with both hands. The line goes down from the tip of the rod to a red and white float in the water, which has a small ring of ripples around it. She seems to be fishing and waiting for a bite, and nothing has been caught yet. Around her are ordinary park things: a winding sandy path, an empty wooden bench, a tall black lamp post, trees, bushes, rubbish bins, a flower bed and a rock. Nothing looks clearly wrong, although fishing in such a small park pond is slightly unusual.
