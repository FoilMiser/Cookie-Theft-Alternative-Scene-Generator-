# Description of test_9.png

## 1. Free description

This is a sunny day in a park, with a yellow sun at top left, two white clouds, green rolling hills and a line of bushy trees in the background. On the left, a young girl in a pink dress stands on a winding sandy path and holds up the string of a purple kite, but the kite is stuck in the leaves of a big tree. Its tail of red and blue bows hangs down, and the string droops loosely down to her hand. In the middle, an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses (teal top, grey-blue skirt) holds a small white bag and throws brown crumbs toward a pond. A white duck on the pond swims toward the crumbs, with wake lines behind it, and seems to have a crumb at its beak. On the right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts has just thrown a red ball: a dotted line goes from his hand up to the ball, which is in the air above the pond. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers, standing in front of the pond, holds her cupped hands out toward him, as if ready to catch it. In the foreground, an orange cat crouches low and creeps up behind a blue bird that is pecking at the ground with its back to the cat, so the cat looks about to pounce. In the bottom-left corner, a small white puppy with brown ears lies curled up asleep on the grass. There is also an empty wooden bench, a tall black lamp post with a green rubbish bin by its base, a grey rock, a bush on the far left and a smaller tree on the far right. The main things going wrong or about to happen are the kite caught in the tree, the cat about to pounce on the bird, and the ball that may be caught by the woman or may drop into the pond.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous

- **Ball direction**: The dashed line starts at the boy's hand and rises to the ball. Most likely he threw it toward the woman, who is waiting to catch it. It could also be read as the ball arcing toward him, or as the ball about to fall into the pond, since it is right over the water.
- **Woman's cupped hands**: These most likely mean she is ready to catch the ball. They could also be read as clapping or as holding something small, because her hands are at chest height while the ball is higher up and further right.
- **Woman's position relative to the pond**: Her upper body is drawn against the water, and the pond's near edge lines up almost exactly with her waist/belt. At a glance she can look as if she is standing in the pond. Her feet are clearly on the grass in front of it.
- **What the old woman is holding**: The white item in her hand looks like a bag of bread (brown at the top), but it could also be read as a cup or carton. There is also a white vertical strip at her chest behind her arm. It could be a folded newspaper or paper tucked under her arm, a bag strap, or part of the same bag.
- **Duck and crumb**: It is hard to tell whether the duck is already eating a crumb or the crumb is just floating right in front of its beak. The duck also sits very high on the water with a visible shadow and a little orange foot sticking out at the back, so it looks a bit like a floating toy or bath duck rather than a swimming duck.
- **Kite string over the trunk**: The string is drawn crossing over the top of the tree trunk (in front of it) instead of going through branches. This looks slightly unnatural. Its loose sag also suggests the kite is snagged rather than being flown.
- **Girl's face**: Her face is mostly hidden. She shows mostly dark hair and only a sliver of face on the left side, so it's hard to tell whether she is looking up at the kite or turned away from the viewer.
- **Blue bird posture**: Its head is lower than its body and its tail sticks up steeply. It reads as pecking at the ground, but it could look as if it is tipping forward.
- **Cat**: Its crouched, stretched-out pose reads as stalking/creeping. It could also be read as about to leap right now.
- **Crumb in mid-air**: One crumb seems to hang in the air over the grass between the old woman's hand and the pond. This makes sense as a crumb mid-throw, but it could be read as floating.
