## Free description

This is a sunny day on a paved town square. Behind it is a row of four two-storey shop buildings in pink, pale green, peach and cream, with a bright sun in the top-left corner and two small white clouds. In the middle of the square a bearded man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers has both arms raised above his head and his mouth open in surprise. Just above him to his left, an orange baseball cap is flying through the air. White wind lines and a few green and brown leaves are blowing across the scene from the tree on the right towards him, so the wind seems to have just blown his cap off and he is grabbing for it, though it is already past his hands. On the left, an elderly grey-haired woman with glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, is walking to the right pushing a teal pram with a baby tucked inside. Behind her is a fruit stall with crates of green, yellow, orange and red fruit, and there is a cardboard box on the ground beside it. In the centre there is an empty wooden bench and a tall black street lamp. A teal bicycle is locked to a bike rack in front of the peach building. In the right foreground stands a second teal bicycle, and its wheels are square, which is impossible. An orange cat is curled up asleep on the ground right beside that bicycle. A leafy tree in a round planter, a green litter bin and two potted plants in the bottom corners complete the scene.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- **Square wheels:** the front bicycle has square wheels. This is clearly impossible and reads as a deliberate "what's wrong" element. Both readings are open: it is the intended absurdity, or a drawing quirk. The square, spoked frames look intentional.
- **The man's pose:** most likely he is reaching for his cap after the wind blew it off. But his hands point straight up while the cap is above and to the left, out of reach, so his pose could also be read as cheering, stretching or throwing his arms up in alarm. Nothing on his head confirms that the cap is his, though it is the obvious reading.
- **Wind direction:** the leaves and lines suggest the wind blows from the tree (right) towards the cap (left). The wind lines start near the cap and stretch rightward, though, so a viewer could read it the other way round. The tree canopy and the woman's hair and skirt are not bent or moving, which is a bit inconsistent with a strong gust.
- **The cat and the bike:** the cat is asleep very close to the base of the free-standing square-wheeled bike. This could be read as "the bike might topple onto the cat," or the cat could simply be lying beside it.
- **Free-standing bike:** the square-wheeled bicycle stands upright with no kickstand or rider visible, which is a little odd, although square wheels would in fact let it stand.
- **Baby:** only a small face is visible in the pram, so I can't tell whether the baby is asleep or awake.
- **Shadows:** people, bikes, the cat and the box have ground shadows, but the bench, lamp post, bin and pots do not. This is a minor, probably unintentional inconsistency.
- **Fruit stall:** no stallholder is visible, so it is unclear whether the stall is open or unattended.
- **Small items in the air:** the green and brown flecks near the man and the peach building's door are leaves, as far as I can tell. At this size they could also be read as bits of litter or fruit.
- **Lamp overlap:** the street lamp's lantern overlaps a window of the green building. This is just depth layering, the lamp standing in front of the building, not an error.
