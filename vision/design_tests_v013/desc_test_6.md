# Picture description: test_6

## Free description

It's a sunny day in a park with green hills, a few clouds, a winding dirt path, a wooden bench, a tall black lamp post and a blue pond with reeds. On the left, a little girl in a pink dress stands next to the bench and holds up a kite string with both hands. Her purple kite is stuck in the leaves of a big tree, so the string sags across the path instead of the kite flying. In the middle, an older woman with grey hair and glasses holds a small white bag and tosses bread crumbs toward the pond. A white duck swims toward the crumbs (it leaves wake lines behind it), and some crumbs are still in the air while others float on the water. To the right of her, a woman with a long dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers stands in front of the pond with both arms stretched out and her hands together. She is facing a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts on the far right. A red ball is high in the air over the pond, and a dotted line runs from the boy's outstretched hand up to it, so it looks like the boy has just thrown it to her and she is getting ready to catch it. In the front, an orange cat crouches low and creeps toward a blue bird that is pecking at the ground, and it looks as if the cat is about to pounce. A small white puppy is curled up asleep at the edge of the path near the big tree, and there is a green trash can in the bottom-right corner.
