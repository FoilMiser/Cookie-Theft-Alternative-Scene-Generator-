A wooden fence runs across the back, and there is a gate in it on the right. She is reaching up to peg washing onto it: blue jeans, a yellow towel with red stripes, and a white T-shirt. In the front right, an elderly man with a grey beard, a blue cap and a brown vest holds a garden hose nozzle right up to his face and peers into it. Only a single drop of water is coming out. It looks as though the dog is blocking the water, and the man is about to get sprayed in the face when the dog steps off. An orange cat is lying on the grass near the man's feet, right beside the hose. Something is clearly wrong in the sky: a cardboard box is floating in mid-air under the sun, with nothing holding it up.
