# Picture description: test_7

## Free description

It is a sunny day in a back garden with green grass, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left. In the middle, a grey-haired older woman in a teal top and grey skirt is reaching up to peg a white T-shirt onto a clothesline strung between two wooden posts; blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes are already hanging on it. On the right, an older man with a grey beard, blue cap, white shirt and brown vest is holding the end of a green-blue garden hose up close to his face and peering into the nozzle, and only a single drop of water is coming out. The reason seems to be the white dog with brown spots in the middle of the lawn: it is standing on the hose, and the hose is swollen into a big bulge just behind the dog, as if water is backed up there. It looks as if something is about to happen: if the dog steps off the hose, the trapped water will probably burst out and spray the man right in the face. In the front of the picture an orange cat is curled up asleep on the grass, and over to the right is a red bowl full of pet food that nobody is eating. There is a flower bed of colourful flowers next to the house, with the hose reel beside it, and a paved path leads from a gate in the fence down to the bottom right corner. One thing looks wrong: a closed cardboard box is hanging in the sky just below the sun, tilted, with nothing holding it up, and nobody in the picture is reacting to it.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The cardboard box in the sky: it could be read as falling (about to drop into the garden near the gate) or simply floating. Either way there is no visible reason for it to be there (no plane, no one throwing it), and no one is looking at it. It may be a deliberate "impossible" detail or an out-of-place drawing error.
- Which way the nozzle is pointing: the man holds it up near his face and appears to be peering into the end, but it is hard to tell whether the opening points straight at his face or off to the side.
- The man's arms: his white sleeves stick straight out flat and there is a long pointed white strip running back from his arms toward his chest. It could be his arms/sleeves drawn oddly, or a white cloth or towel draped over his arms. The arms look flat and slightly detached from his shoulders.
- The woman: there is a small white strip at her side/hip. It could be part of her blouse, an apron string, or a peg bag. Her legs are very thin and her feet small, but that seems to be just the drawing style.
- The dog's position: it reads as standing on the hose, which is what makes the "blocked hose" story. But its feet are only just on the hose line, so it could also be read as standing right next to the hose.
- The bulge in the hose: I read it as trapped water building up behind the dog. Another reading is that it is a fitting or connector on the hose, but its shape (a smooth swelling with a highlight) suggests pressure.
- The cat and the food bowl are far apart and nobody is paying attention to either. It is unclear whether the bowl belongs to the cat or the dog.
- Nothing else looks like it is passing through anything else. The woman stands in front of the clothesline and the hose goes under the dog's feet, so the layering looks consistent.
