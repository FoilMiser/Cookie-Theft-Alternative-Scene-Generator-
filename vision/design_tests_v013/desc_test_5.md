# Description of test_5.png

## 1. Free description

It is a sunny day on a paved town sidewalk in front of a row of four colourful two-storey shops (pink, pale green, peach and cream), with a bright sun in the top-left corner and two small white clouds. In the middle of the picture a man with dark skin and a beard, wearing a green shirt and khaki trousers, stands with both arms stretched up and his mouth open in surprise, looking up at his blue cap, which is flying away up and to the right above his head. White wind lines blow from the left across the scene toward the cap, and a few small green and brown leaves are being blown along with it, so the wind seems to have just blown his cap off and he is reaching for it. On the left, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt is walking toward the left, pushing a teal pram with a baby lying in it, past a fruit and vegetable stall with a teal-and-white striped canopy and crates of green, yellow, orange and red produce. A cardboard box sits on the ground at the far left. Behind the man is an empty wooden bench and a black street lamp. On the right there is a teal bicycle locked in a grey bike rack by the peach shop, a tree in a round planter, and a green rubbish bin. In the lower right there is a second teal bicycle whose wheels are square instead of round, which is impossible, as a bike like that could not roll. A small orange cat lies curled up asleep on the pavement in the lower left-centre, and there are potted plants in the bottom-left and bottom-right corners.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous

- The man and the cap: the most likely reading is that the wind blew his cap off and he is reaching up in surprise to catch it (wind lines and leaves support this). Another possible reading is that he tossed his cap into the air himself, for example cheering, and is about to catch it; his open-mouthed expression fits surprise better than celebration.
- Square bicycle wheels (lower right): this looks like a deliberate "impossible" detail, a bike with square wheels. A less likely reading is that the bike is standing in a square floor-type bike stand, but the spokes and hubs are drawn inside the squares, so they read as the wheels themselves.
- The wind only seems to affect the cap and the few leaves. The tree, the awnings, the stall canopy, and the woman's hair and skirt show no sign of wind, which is slightly inconsistent.
- The woman's direction: she faces and walks to the left, pushing the pram ahead of her toward the stall and the cardboard box. It is unclear whether she is shopping at the stall or just walking past.
- The pram overlaps the front of the fruit stall's table and its leg, so the pram looks as if it is partly inside or under the stall rather than clearly in front of it.
- The baby is hard to make out: only a small tan circle (the head) under the hood and a cream oval (blanket) are visible. It could also be read as a doll or just bedding.
- The produce in the crates is too small to identify exactly: green, yellow, orange and red round items (could be limes/apples, lemons, oranges, apples/tomatoes).
- The strip hanging down the front of the woman's top could be a bag strap, a scarf, or a lanyard/necklace.
- The cat is very small compared with the people, and it is unclear whether it is asleep or just lying down.
- Bicycle 1 overlaps the bike rack, so it is a little hard to tell whether it is locked to the rack or simply standing in front of it.
