# v0.47 blind screen: what it found (the work list for v0.48)

The v0.47 screen read the set's 21 pictures and 66 probes with the prompts of v0.39 (`screen_prompts.md`): the readers' unchanged, the scorers' with three additions that are set out under "Slips in the blind rule" below. There were eleven readers of eight pictures (the last of seven) and four scorers of 21 or 22. One template failed the legibility gate, the walker about to trip, and nothing was redrawn, swapped or read again after the screen (below). The oddity tally is by angle, as in v0.39. Keys, descriptions and scores are in `v047/`.

v0.47 worked Sam's brief: "Go ahead and work on v0.47, addressing the flaws, and maybe focusing on one character, finding general anatomy flaws and defects and shortcomings, fix those up and make the character art cleaner and better, and apply that to the cast and animals, and refine weak actions too, to hopefully fix the screening". The work is in the cast (builder 0.47.0) and in the placement rules (revision 44), each change behind a switch, and each was read blind on a bench of five rounds before it went into scenes (`isolation_v047.md`):
- **a study of one figure**, the woman in the yellow dress, in 52 poses and angles, read by fresh reviewers with the other nine people and the animals beside her; what two reviews or more named was mended on every person: sloped shoulders, limbs that taper with an elbow, a knee and an ankle, a hand with a thumb, a shoe with a toe and a sole, a skirt that turns with the thighs, a nose with no ring round it, a fuller head, and a side view whose head turns 48 degrees toward the viewer;
- **the clothes and the animals**: the cardigan, the vest and the glasses; the eyes of the dogs and cats further round the head, a coat's markings as patches of the body's own surface, shorter paws and tails;
- **the weak actions**: the walk, the run and the sitting on the ground; a reading dog of its own, sitting up with the paper in both forepaws; the dog standing square on the hose; the pecking bird tipped over; the stalking cat's legs apart;
- **v0.46's list**, R1 to R6 and O1 to O7, by thirteen placement rules and the moustache and shading of the cast. O3 (the riders above their seat) was put on the bench and left, and O4 (the figures over the pond) was not worked.

## After the screen

The one failure stands. Four earlier releases shipped with their gate failures listed (v0.42 one, v0.43 two, v0.44 one, v0.46 four); v0.45 fixed its one and read three pictures again.

**`about_to_trip` (59).** The older man reading as he walks faces left: his face, the peak of his cap and the open book all point at the box by his leading foot, and his trailing leg stretches out to the right. The reader took the stride to go right and so put the box "just behind him". He is the smallest of the screen's five walkers, 0.41 of full size where the others are 0.46 to 0.51, and the picture is a set isomorph (`T2_playground_s13109_iso`). `TRIP47` did what it was made for: nothing upright stands near him, and the reader named no second hazard, as v0.46's had. The walk's new feet, the trailing foot on its toe and the leading foot flat, did not carry the direction at that size. The other four pictures read: "a cardboard box sits on the grass just ahead of him, so he may trip over it" (23); "Cardboard box: on the path directly in front of the man; he is about to trip on it." (63). Three of the four add the other reading: "The man may walk past the box rather than into it; he faces toward it, so tripping seems likely." (23); "The old man may be walking past the box rather than into it; its position suggests he will trip." (38); "unclear whether he is about to walk into the box or past it" (19). It is the second release running in which this template fails on a walker read as going the other way, and the mend this release made was aimed at that. It heads the next list, with a bench of its own.

**Why no picture was swapped or read again.** A probe or an isomorph swapped for one that looks better to the builder is selection, and a second reading of an unchanged picture is a second roll of the dice; either would raise the count without a change in what a patient sees. The numbers above are the first and only count.

**What it means for the release.** v0.47's screen is the strongest on the set since v0.44's: 0.991 over all items for v0.46's 0.957, one misread for three across the screen, 63 of 64 for 60 of 64. Three of v0.46's four failing templates and all four under them read 1.00 or near it in five pictures each. Three things stand against that and are not to be read past: the one failure is the same template failing the same way; the logged oddities rose, a new kind among them (which way a figure faces); and many full marks carry a hedge, 56 of 64 on the strict count.
