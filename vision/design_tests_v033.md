# v0.33 design tests

The bench's choices and revision 31 were read blind in whole test scenes among other events: six pictures, six fresh readers, one picture each (`design_tests_v033/test_N.png`, `desc_test_N.md`; the prompts are `tpdb_work/dtp33/test_N.txt`, the reader template cut to one picture). `tools/cycle/design_tests.py` has the jobs. Each reader saw one picture and knew nothing of the others.

| Test | Picture | What it tests | What the reader said |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tier-3 playground | The girl stuck up high with no kick arcs, the man reading on the low end | "Because the heavy adult is down and she is stuck up high, it looks like she can't get down". "Nothing appears to be floating or impossible." The man as before: "the reading man's legs and the seesaw board seem to overlap a little" |
| 2 | Tier-3 playground | The same, with the slide and a box carried | "A small girl in a pink dress is stuck up in the air on the high end, holding the handle". "Nothing else looks unintentionally strange: nothing is floating and nothing passes through people." |
| 3 | Tier-3 garden | The storm pointer, a cat on the fence and paint tracks: v0.32's tier-3 garden's set | Pointing "as if warning that rain is coming, which would spoil the wet paint". Weighed: "most likely the storm clouds ... He could also be pointing at the cat on the fence, which is up and to his left. His gaze and arm point more towards the sky." The cat is about 400 units from his hand |
| 4 | Tier-3 garden | The storm pointer, a cat on the fence, a bird perched, a jump | "pointing up and to the right with one arm, apparently at the storm clouds". The bird, a little over 100 units from her fingertip and behind the line of her arm, is not named as what she points at. "She could also be pointing towards the cat on the fence far to the right, or at the sky in general. I saw nothing in the air where she points." |
| 5 | Tier-3 street | The storm pointer, a bird on the lamp, a photo | "The man is pointing up and to the left, away from the bird, which is above him to his right, so he seems to be pointing at the dark clouds or the sky." |
| 6 | Tier-3 garden | The stopped hose, as it was | "The goat seems to be standing on the hose, which could be squashing it and stopping the water, so the girl looks puzzled that nothing is coming out." Hedged after: "Its feet and the hose line overlap, so I can't tell for sure." |

No reading called for a new rule. Without the arcs the girl reads as stuck in both pictures, and neither reader says she floats. Every storm pointer reads first as pointing at the clouds, and nothing close by the fingertip is taken for what she points at. Twice a reader offers a cat far along the fence as a second reading, as in earlier screens, where the rule of revision 6 keeps the line to the clouds clear and a thing well away from it is still in the arm's general direction. The stopped hose reads as the gag, first, with the foot's contact hedged after, as on the bench.
