## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden picket fence, a wooden gate on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. A boy is flying a kite. He wears a red cap, a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes. He stands on the lawn just right of centre, looking up and holding the kite string up with both hands. The orange diamond-shaped kite is high in the sky at the top left, near the sun, and its tail has two small bows. Its string runs in a long straight line down to the boy's hands. To the left there is a grey A-frame swing set with one red seat, which hangs empty and still. A teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow sit on the grass at the front. A paved path runs along the left edge from the gate. A round bed of colourful flowers is to the right, near the house, and a tree and bushes are behind the fence. Nothing seems wrong or about to go wrong. It is a calm scene of a child flying a kite alone.
