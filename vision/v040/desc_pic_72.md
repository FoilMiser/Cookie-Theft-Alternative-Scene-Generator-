## Free description
A sunny back garden with the cream house (red roof, blue door, one window) on the left and a wooden gate with a paved path on the right, in front of a wooden picket fence. A woman with dark hair in a ponytail, wearing a yellow dress and red shoes, stands just to the right of a flower bed holding a teal watering can tilted toward the flowers. The water coming from the can looks wrong: instead of pouring down onto the flowers, a pale blue stream rises up from the spout in a curve and ends in the air above the flowers, with drops flying upward at the top, as if the water were flowing uphill. Behind her, a washing line between two posts holds blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. On the right there are two potted plants on the lawn, and at the front there is an empty grey wheelbarrow. A tree stands behind the house and a bush next to it. Nobody else is in the garden.
