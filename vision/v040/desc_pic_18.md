## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence and a garden gate on the left. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door stands on the right. On the paved path at the far left, a girl or woman in a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping a small pile of brown dead leaves with a yellow broom. In the middle, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie holds the end of a garden hose up near his face and stares into the nozzle, but only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose runs back to a hose reel by the house, and a white dog with brown spots is standing right on top of the hose. The dog is probably blocking the water. If the dog steps off, the water may suddenly spray the boy in the face. Behind them are an empty swing set with one red seat and a washing line with a striped yellow towel and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. An empty wheelbarrow sits at the front, and a flower bed grows next to the house.
