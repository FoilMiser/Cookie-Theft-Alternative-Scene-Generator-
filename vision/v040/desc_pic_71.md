## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden picket fence, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a gate with a paved path on the left. At the front left a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is sweeping a small pile of brown dry leaves on the path with a yellow broom. Behind him is a bird house on a tall pole, and next to it an empty swing set with a red seat hanging still. In the centre a man with dark skin and short hair, in a green shirt and khaki trousers, is holding a garden hose nozzle out in front of him and looking at it; only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose loops across the lawn to a hose reel on the right, and a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is standing on top of the hose, which seems to be stopping the water. A washing line in the back holds a yellow red-striped towel and blue jeans, and there is a flower bed in front of the house. One odd thing is that the leaves being swept are brown autumn leaves, while all the trees and hedges are fully green and it looks like a bright summer day.
