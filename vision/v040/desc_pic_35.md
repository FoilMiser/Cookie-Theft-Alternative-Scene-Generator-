# Picture 35

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden seen the other way round: the house with the red roof and blue door is on the left, the gate is at the right end of the fence, and the paved path runs down the right side. A woman with long dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers stands in the middle with both arms stretched out in front of her. A red-and-white ball hangs in the air just beyond her hands. A dashed line arcs from the ball down to a boy on the right, so the ball seems to have been thrown between them. The boy has braided hair and wears a yellow top and orange shorts, and he is stepping forward with one arm out, as if he has just thrown it. At the front centre, a white dog with brown spots is crouched in a playful bow over a yellow-green tennis ball, wagging its tail (motion marks by the tail). On the left, a brown dog with a red collar sits beside the blue door holding a red lead in its mouth, as if it wants a walk. On the path at the right, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt is sweeping a pile of brown leaves with a broom. There is also a flower bed by the house, a bush and a teal watering can.

## Inventory
- Woman (centre, mid-ground): long dark hair, purple top, teal trousers. Both arms are stretched forward with hands together, ready to catch the ball or just having thrown it.
- Boy (centre right, mid-ground): braided hair, yellow top, orange shorts. He is stepping forward with an arm out and seems to have just thrown the ball.
- White spotted dog (front centre): white with brown spots and a blue collar. It is in a play bow with its nose at a tennis ball and its tail wagging.
- Brown dog (left, by the house door): brown with a red collar, sitting and holding a red lead in its mouth that trails on the ground.
- Elderly woman (front right, on the path): grey hair in a bun, glasses, teal top, grey skirt. She is sweeping a pile of leaves with a yellow broom.
- Red-and-white ball: in the air near the woman's hands, with a dashed path line linking it back to the boy.
- Tennis ball: yellow-green, on the grass in front of the white dog's nose.
- Red lead/leash: in the brown dog's mouth, looped on the ground.
- Broom and leaf pile: on the path at the elderly woman's feet.
- Watering can: teal, on the grass near the path, not in use.
- Flower bed: colourful flowers in front of the house.
- Gate: wooden gate at the right end of the fence, closed.
- House on the left, a tree behind the house, a bush, the sun at the top right and two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The direction of the throw could be read two ways: (a) the boy threw the ball and the woman is about to catch it, which fits the dashed line starting at the boy's hand; or (b) the woman just threw it towards the boy. Her outstretched hands fit either reading.
- The ball is drawn at about the height of the top of the fence, a little above and in front of her hands, so it could also look as if it is touching or in front of the fence.
- The white dog has a ball of its own and is ignoring the game of catch. The brown dog with its lead again seems to want a walk.
- The brown leaves on the path are slightly odd when the trees and flowers are all green and blooming.
