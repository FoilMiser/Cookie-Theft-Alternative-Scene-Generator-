# pic_68

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden picket fence, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a gate and paved path on the left. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; a strip of about three fence boards is already blue from top to bottom, and an open paint tin sits on the grass beside her. On top of the fence a grey cat is crouched and creeping toward a blue bird perched further along, as if about to pounce, and a red bird is flying near the sun at the upper left. In the middle an older man with grey hair, a grey cap, a brown waistcoat and white shirt is holding a garden hose nozzle up near his face and peering at it; only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose loops across the lawn to a hose reel on the right, and a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is standing right on top of the hose, which would explain why no water is coming through. In the front left a girl in a pink dress holds a blue and a purple balloon by their strings while a yellow balloon floats away above her, and she is reaching up after it. There is also a flower bed in front of the house.
