## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills, trees, a winding sandy path and a pond on the right. An elderly man with grey hair, a moustache and a flat cap is pushing a teal pram along the path; a baby lies asleep in it under a pink spotted blanket. Behind them are an empty wooden bench, a green bin and a tall street lamp. By the pond a man in a green shirt holds a paper bag and is tossing bread crumbs onto the water, and a white duck is swimming over to eat them. In the front a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands holding a yellow balloon on a string. Everything looks calm and normal; nothing seems about to go wrong.
