# Picture 34

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate at the far left, a paved path in the front left, and a house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. A woman with long dark hair in a yellow dress and red shoes stands near the fence in the middle, painting it blue with a paint roller. About three planks of the fence have already been painted bright blue. Behind her on the grass are a paint tray with blue paint in it and a small paint tin. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is walking away to the left towards the path. It has left a trail of blue paw prints from the paint tray, and its paws look blue, so it seems to have stepped in the paint. The woman has her back to the dog and does not seem to have noticed. There is also a flower bed, a wooden bench at the front right, a teal watering can, an empty wheelbarrow and a bush by the house.
