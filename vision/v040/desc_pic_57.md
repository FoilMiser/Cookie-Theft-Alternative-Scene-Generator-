# pic_57

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate at the far left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses is raking a small pile of leaves on the path at the left, and a brown puppy is curled up asleep further down the same path. In the middle a man in a green shirt stands with his hands on his hips next to an empty swing set, and he is wearing a white bucket upside down on his head like a hat. Far back by the fence a small girl in a pink dress holds a kite string; the purple kite is up at the top of a tree behind the fence and looks caught in the leaves. A grey cat is walking along the top of the fence. A woman in a yellow dress holds the nozzle of a garden hose and looks at it, but only a drip or two comes out, and a white spotted dog is standing right on the hose. On the right a man in a red shirt sits on a bench reading a red book, and a blue bird has landed beside him and is pecking at his sandwich, which he has not noticed. At the front there is a wheelbarrow whose wheel is drawn square, which could not roll.
