# pic_25

## Free description
A sunny park with a pond, a winding path, a lamp post, a bench and trees. On the left, a white spotted dog sits up on its back legs holding an open newspaper as if reading it. Next to it, an old man in a cap and brown vest is watering a flower bed, but he is pouring the water out of a frying pan instead of a watering can. A white duck swims on the pond, pecking at bread crumbs on the water, and a girl in a pink dress stands at the edge of the pond holding a paper bag, apparently feeding it. In the middle front, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and has a hand up to one eye as if crying. Their pink ice cream has fallen on the ground, and a brown dog is licking it up. On the path, a grey-haired old woman with glasses is sweeping a pile of leaves with a broom, and a white goat is grazing on the grass behind her. On the right, a boy in a red cap is holding a kite string, but his purple kite is stuck in the branches of a tree. A blue bird flies across the sky. The dog reading a newspaper and the man watering with a frying pan look wrong or impossible.
