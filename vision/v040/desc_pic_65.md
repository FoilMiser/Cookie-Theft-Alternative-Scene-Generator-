## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden picket fence across the back, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a wooden gate with a paved path on the left. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans, with brown hair and a moustache, is digging in the lawn with a long-handled shovel. The shovel blade has a scoop of soil on it, there is a dark hole under it, and a small mound of dug-up earth sits beside him. On top of the fence a grey cat is crouched low and creeping toward a blue bird perched a little further along the fence, and the bird faces the cat. The cat looks as if it is about to pounce on the bird. In the front of the lawn a garden sprinkler, connected to a long green-blue hose that runs off to the right, is spraying arcs of water to the left. The water is landing on a cardboard box left on the grass, which is sitting in a puddle and has water running down its sides, so it is getting soaked. There is a flower bed with mixed bright flowers in front of the house. Nobody seems to have noticed the cat or the wet box.
