# pic_04

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden picket fence, a gate on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. A man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands at the fence on the left-centre. He is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle. A section of fence to his left, a few planks wide, is already painted blue. On the grass by his feet there is an open tin of blue paint and a tray of blue paint. A trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the lawn to a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar. The dog's paws are blue, so it has clearly stepped in the paint tray. It is walking off to the right looking pleased, and will keep leaving prints. The man has his back to the dog and does not seem to have noticed. In the front left, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, is sweeping a small pile of brown leaves with a yellow broom at the edge of the path. A watering can, an empty wheelbarrow, and a hose on a reel also lie on the grass, and there is a flower bed by the house.
