# Picture 47

## Free description
This is a paved town square or shopping street on a sunny but windy day, with a row of colourful shop buildings (pink, green, peach and cream) with striped awnings along the back. White wind lines blow across the sky and along the awnings, and small leaves are flying through the air, some from the tree on the right. An orange cap is flying away in the wind high up at the top left, near the pink shop's awning. Below it, a man with dark skin and a beard, in a green shirt and beige trousers, stands in front of a fruit and vegetable stall with one arm raised towards the cap and a surprised, open-mouthed face, as if his cap has just blown off his head. In the centre, an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and a grey skirt, sits calmly on a bench reading a red book. At the front there is a red bicycle locked to a bike rack, a litter bin, a potted plant and two small café tables with chairs. A tall black lamppost stands near the stall.
