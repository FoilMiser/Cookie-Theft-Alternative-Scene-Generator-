# Picture 48

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path running to the gate. A tan-and-white dog with a red collar sits on the grass right next to the blue door, holding the end of its own red lead in its mouth, with the rest of the lead coiled on the ground; it looks as if it is asking to be taken for a walk. On the right, an older man with grey hair, a moustache and a blue-grey flat cap, wearing a white shirt, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers, is sweeping a pile of dead leaves on the path with a yellow broom, with his back half turned to the dog. A clothes line between two wooden posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. In the middle of the lawn stands a swing set with an empty red seat. A wheelbarrow and a teal watering can are at the front, and a flower bed is next to the house. Nothing looks impossible; the main point of interest is the dog waiting with its lead.
