## Free description
This is a back garden under a grey, overcast sky with three dark storm clouds overhead. It looks as if it is about to rain. On the paved path at the left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers points up at the dark clouds with wide eyes and a round "oh" mouth, as if warning that rain is coming. In the middle of the lawn, a girl in a yellow dress and red shoes sits reading a red book, but there is no chair, bench or anything under her. She is in a sitting position in mid-air, which looks impossible. By the blue door of the house on the right, a brown dog with a red collar sits holding its red lead in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. A garden hose on a reel lies on the grass near a colourful flower bed. Two potted plants stand by the fence.
