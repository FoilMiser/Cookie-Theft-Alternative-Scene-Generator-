## Free description
This is a sunny town square in front of a row of colourful shops with striped awnings, with a red bird flying in the sky at the top right. On the left a man in a green shirt walks away from a fruit stall carrying a yellow shopping bag. In front of him a woman in a yellow dress walks along reading an open book, heading toward a cardboard box sitting on the pavement ahead of her, so she may trip over it; a little girl in a pink dress skips beside her with her arms up, laughing. A woman in a purple top waters a planter of flowers with a watering can, but the stream of water curves upward into the air instead of falling onto the flowers. In the middle an elderly woman with glasses sits on a bench reading a red book, and a blue bird beside her is pecking at a sandwich on the bench. Next to her a worker in an orange cap and orange safety vest is sweeping leaves with a broom. A brown dog sits upright holding an open newspaper as if reading it, which is impossible. On the right a blue-and-white ball is stuck in a tree, and a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at it; a small café table with two chairs, and potted plants in the corners, complete the scene.
