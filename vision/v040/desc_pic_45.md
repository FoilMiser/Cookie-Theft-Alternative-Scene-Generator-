# Picture 45

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a house with a red roof and a blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back, a gate and a paved path on the left. In the middle, a man with dark skin, wearing a green shirt and beige trousers, is watering the flower bed, but instead of a watering can he is using a black frying pan: a stream of water pours out of the edge of the pan onto the flowers. That is the odd thing in the picture, since a frying pan is not a watering tool and could not really hold or pour water like that. At the front centre, a boy in a red cap, a blue hoodie and dark trousers is walking along carrying a cardboard box in both hands. On the right, an older man with grey hair, a moustache and a flat cap sits on a wooden bench drinking from a red cup through a straw. Two potted plants and a bush stand near the fence. Nobody seems to notice the frying pan.

- Front centre: boy with a red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes; walking and carrying a cardboard box, facing left.
- Right, on a bench: older man with grey hair, moustache, blue-grey flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers; sitting and drinking from a red cup with a straw.
- Frying pan: black pan held out by its handle over the flowers, with water pouring from it like from a watering can.

- Cardboard box: closed brown box with a small white label or tape, held in front of the boy.
- Red cup with straw: drink in the older man's hand.
- Flower bed (centre right): round bed of mixed coloured flowers being watered.
- Bench (right): wooden bench in front of the house.
- Two potted plants and a round bush (centre left, by the fence).
- House with a blue door (right), gate and path (left), tree, sun in the top left, two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The frying pan: it could be read as a deliberate silly mistake (using the wrong tool) or as a strangely drawn watering can, but its shape is clearly a flat round frying pan with a long handle.
- The boy's direction: his cap brim and face point left, so he seems to be walking left (towards the gate side), though his legs could also suggest walking towards the viewer.
- What is in the box, and whether it is a delivery, cannot be told.
