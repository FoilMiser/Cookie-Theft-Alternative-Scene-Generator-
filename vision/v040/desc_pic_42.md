# Picture 42

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path leading to the gate. A girl with dark skin and braided hair, wearing a yellow top and orange shorts, sits on a wooden bench at the front left reading a red book. Next to her is a round flower bed full of red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers. In the middle of the lawn an orange cat is curled up, apparently asleep, and a red pet bowl full of food sits on the grass at the front. Two potted plants stand near the fence. The odd thing is a closed cardboard box hanging high in the sky above the fence, tilted as if it is tumbling. Nobody is near it who could have thrown it, so it seems to be flying or falling into the garden from over the fence. No one in the garden has noticed it.

- House (left): cream wall, red roof, window, blue door with a step.
- Fence and gate: wooden fence across the back with a brown gate on the right; a paved path runs from the front right to the gate.
- Tree behind the house, a bush by the house wall, sun in the top right, two clouds.

- Whether the box will land on anything: it is roughly above the middle of the garden, not obviously over the cat or the girl.
- The cat is small; it looks curled up asleep, but it could also be crouching or eating something on the grass.
