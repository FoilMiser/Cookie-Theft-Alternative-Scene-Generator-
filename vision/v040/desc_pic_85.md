## Free description
This is a busy, sunny back garden with the cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a fence along the back, a gate and path on the right, a swing set and a birdhouse on a pole. At the front left an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a wooden bench reading a red book, and a blue bird is perched on the bench next to her, pecking at her sandwich while she isn't looking. A man in a green shirt is watering the flowerbed with a teal watering can. In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress is walking along while reading an open book, and a cardboard box lies on the grass just ahead of her, so she may be about to trip over it. At the back a girl in a pink dress holds a kite string, but her purple kite is caught in the tree behind the house. A grey cat is walking along the top of the fence. On the right a brown dog sits on the path upright like a person, holding and reading a newspaper, which is impossible, and an orange cat is curled up asleep further down the path. A white puppy with brown spots is asleep on the grass, the swing hangs empty, and the wheelbarrow at the front has a square wheel, which would not roll.
