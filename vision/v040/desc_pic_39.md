# Picture 39

## Free description
This is a back garden under a grey, overcast sky with three large dark storm clouds and no sun. The house (red roof, blue door) is on the left, a gate is at the right end of the fence and a paved path runs down the right side. The only person is a girl with dark hair in a bun, a pink dress and yellow shoes, standing on the path at the front right. She is looking up and pointing at the sky with her mouth open, as if she has just noticed the storm coming or felt the first drop of rain. No rain is visible yet. An empty wooden bench is at the front left, a wheelbarrow and a teal watering can are on the lawn at the front, and there is a flower bed by the house. Two potted plants and a red-roofed birdhouse on a tall pole stand near the back fence. It looks as if rain is about to start. Nothing looks impossible.
