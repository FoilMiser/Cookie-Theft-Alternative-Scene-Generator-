# pic_86

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a fence along the back, and a gate and path on the right. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, holds a green garden hose and is peering right into the nozzle, which is pointed at her own face; only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose comes from a hose reel next to an empty bench on the left and loops across the lawn. A white dog with brown spots is standing right on the hose, and just before the dog's feet the hose is swollen into a fat bulge, as if the water is blocked there. So the dog is stopping the water, the woman is looking into the hose to see why, and when the dog steps off she is likely to get sprayed in the face. Behind them a washing line between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow striped towel. There is also a birdhouse on a pole, two potted plants, a flowerbed and an empty grey wheelbarrow.
