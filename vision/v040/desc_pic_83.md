## Free description
This is a sunny back garden seen the other way round: the cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the right, and the gate and paved path are on the left. On the path at the front left a small girl in a pink dress holds a kite string up with one hand, and the string runs high into the sky to an orange diamond kite with a bow-tied tail. In the middle a man in a green shirt and tan trousers is drinking from a red cup through a straw. He is in a sitting position with his knees bent, but there is no chair, bench or anything else under him, so he seems to be sitting on thin air, which is impossible. Behind him an older man with grey hair and a moustache, wearing a blue-grey cap, a brown waistcoat, a white shirt and dark trousers, is raking a small pile of leaves on the grass. There are two potted plants by the fence, a teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow on the front lawn, and a flowerbed in front of the house. The main thing that looks wrong is the man sitting on nothing.
