# Picture 46

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path leading to the gate. In the middle, a man with dark skin, wearing a green shirt and beige trousers, is reaching up to hang washing on a clothes line between two wooden posts. A pair of blue jeans and a white T-shirt are pegged on the line and dripping water, and a basket of more clothes is on the grass beneath. On the left, a boy in a red cap and a blue hoodie sits on a wooden bench drinking from a red cup through a straw. On the right, on the path, a man with brown hair and a moustache, in a red T-shirt and blue jeans, is using a huge silver fork, shaped like a giant dinner fork, to scrape dirt and gravel into a red dustpan, raising a little cloud of dust. The giant kitchen fork used as a garden tool is the strange part of the picture. A wheelbarrow, two potted plants and a flower bed complete the scene.
