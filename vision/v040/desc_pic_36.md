# Picture 36

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the house (red roof, blue door) on the left, a gate at the right end of the fence and a paved path down the right side. A woman with long dark hair in a yellow dress and red shoes stands next to the flower bed by the house, watering the flowers with a garden hose. A stream of water arcs from the nozzle into the flowers with drops falling. The hose runs from a hose reel on the ground at the front left, across the grass, and up to her hand. At the bottom right on the path, a white dog with brown spots is crouched in a playful bow with a yellow-green tennis ball, wagging its tail. The strange thing in this picture is a cardboard box floating, tilted, high in the sky above the fence, centre right, with nobody near it. It could be falling, or it could have been thrown over the fence from next door, but nothing shows how it got there. There is also a bush by the house.

## Inventory
- Woman (centre left, mid-ground, beside the flower bed): long dark hair, yellow dress, red shoes. She is holding a hose nozzle and spraying water onto the flowers.
- Dog (bottom right, on the path): white with brown spots and a blue collar. It is in a play bow over a tennis ball, with its tail wagging.
- Cardboard box: brown box in mid-air above the fence, centre right, tilted as if tumbling. It is not held or supported by anything.
- Garden hose and reel: dark teal hose on a round reel at the front left, running across the lawn to the woman's hand.
- Water spray: a light-blue arc of water with drops, going from the nozzle into the flower bed.
- Tennis ball: yellow-green, on the path in front of the dog.
- Flower bed: colourful flowers in front of the house, being watered.
- Gate: wooden gate at the right end of the fence, closed.
- House on the left, a tree behind it, a bush, the sun at the top right and two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The floating box is the clear oddity. It could be (a) falling or thrown in from over the fence, or (b) just impossibly hanging in the sky. There are no motion lines, so I cannot tell if it is moving, and nobody is looking at it.
- The water arc comes out of the nozzle and curves down into the flowers. It is drawn quite thin and pale, so it could be missed at a glance.
- The hose loops up from the ground to her hand. Its path near the flower bed is a little tangled-looking, but it reads as one hose.
- The woman faces the flowers and has her back to the dog and the box, so she does not seem to notice either.
