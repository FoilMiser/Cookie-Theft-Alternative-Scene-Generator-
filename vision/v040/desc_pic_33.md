# Picture 33

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with blue sky, a bright sun in the top left and two small white clouds. A tall wooden fence runs across the back, with a wooden gate at the far left and a paved path running from the gate down to the front left corner. A house with a red roof, a window and a blue front door is on the right, and a tree sticks up behind the fence next to it. On the path at the front left, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is sweeping a small pile of brown fallen leaves with a yellow broom. On the right, a brown dog with a red collar sits next to the blue door holding a red lead in its mouth. The lead trails on the ground, so the dog seems to be asking to go for a walk. There is a flower bed of colourful flowers, two potted plants by the fence, a couple of bushes, a teal watering can and an empty wheelbarrow on the lawn. Nothing looks impossible. The only slightly odd thing is the pile of brown leaves when every tree and plant in the garden is green and in flower.
