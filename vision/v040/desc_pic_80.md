## Free description
A windy day in a sunny park: white wind lines streak through the sky and across the middle of the scene, and a few leaves are blowing about. There is a pond with bulrushes on the left, a tall black lamp post in the middle, a wooden bench, a winding path on the right, trees on both sides, a flower bed and a green bin at the front left, and a grey rock at the front centre. A woman with long dark hair, wearing a yellow dress and red shoes, stands in front of the pond with her mouth open in surprise, reaching up with one arm towards an orange cap that is flying through the air above her, carried off by the wind along with some leaves. On the right, a man with brown hair and a moustache, wearing a red shirt and blue jeans, is running fast (speed lines behind him) towards the right, away from the woman and the cap, with one arm stretched forward. Near the big tree on the right, more leaves are being blown off. The cap looks as if it has just been blown away and the woman is trying to grab it.
