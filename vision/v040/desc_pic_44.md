# Picture 44

## Free description
This is the same kind of sunny back garden, with a house with a blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back, a gate on the left and a paved path. An older man with grey hair, a moustache and a flat cap, wearing a white shirt, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers, is painting the fence blue with a paint roller; three fence boards next to him are already blue. On the grass at his feet are a tray of blue paint and a paint tin. Just to his left, a younger man with brown hair and a moustache, in a red T-shirt and blue jeans, is sweeping a small pile of dead leaves with a yellow broom. A white dog with brown spots and a brown ear is on the path at the front left, looking happy, and its paws are blue. A trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray to the dog, so the dog has clearly stepped in the paint and is walking away, about to spread blue paw prints along the path. Neither man has noticed the dog. A watering can and a wheelbarrow are at the front, and a flower bed is next to the house.

- Watering can (front left) and wheelbarrow (front centre): garden tools, not in use.
- Flower bed, house with a blue door, tree, bush, sun in the top left, two clouds.
