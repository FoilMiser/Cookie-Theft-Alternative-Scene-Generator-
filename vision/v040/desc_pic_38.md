# Picture 38

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the house (red roof, blue door) on the left, a gate at the right end of the fence and a paved path down the right side. In the middle there is a washing line between two posts, with a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes pegged on it. A tall pole with a small red-roofed birdhouse stands further right. In the centre, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt is holding the end of a garden hose up in front of her face, peering into the nozzle as if no water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel at the front left, across the lawn, and up to her. A white goat with small horns is standing on the hose between the reel and the woman, looking at her. The hose under its feet looks squashed, so the goat seems to be blocking the water. On the right, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is sweeping a pile of brown leaves with a broom near the path. There is also a flower bed by the house, two potted plants and a bush.

- Goat (centre left, front): white, with small curved horns. It is standing on the garden hose and looking towards the woman.
- Man (right, back, near the path): green shirt, khaki trousers. He is sweeping a pile of brown leaves with a yellow broom.
- Garden hose and reel: dark teal hose on a reel at the front left. It runs along the grass under the goat's feet and up to the woman's hand. Under the goat it looks squashed or bulging.
- Hose nozzle: yellow, in the woman's hand, with no water coming out.
- Washing line: two wooden posts with a line between them, holding blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel on pegs.
- Birdhouse: small red-roofed birdhouse on top of a tall wooden pole, right of centre.

- Two potted plants: terracotta pots near the back right.

- Gate: wooden gate at the right end of the fence, closed.
- House on the left, a tree behind it, a bush, the sun at the top right and two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The goat-on-the-hose reading depends on a dark squashed patch of hose near the goat's feet. It is fairly clear, but the goat's legs and the hose overlap, so it could also look as if the goat's hooves are simply next to the hose.
- The woman holds the nozzle close to her face. It could be read as checking why no water comes out, or as about to drink from or spray herself.
- A goat in a suburban back garden is unusual but not impossible.
- The goat's body is drawn somewhat from behind with its head turned, which makes its pose a little awkward to read.
- The brown leaves are slightly odd when the trees are green and flowers are blooming.
