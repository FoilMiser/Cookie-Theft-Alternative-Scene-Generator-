# pic_63

## Free description
This is a sunny town street in front of a row of colourful shops with striped awnings, a fruit stall on the left and a tree in a planter on the right. In the middle a little girl in a pink dress is holding a blue and a purple balloon on strings in one hand, while her yellow balloon has slipped away and is floating up out of reach. She is reaching up toward it with her other hand and looks upset. On the left a man in a green shirt walks along carrying a yellow shopping bag. On the right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks along carrying a cardboard box. There is an empty bench in the middle, a green bicycle parked in a bike rack behind it, a small box on the ground at the left, a street lamp, a green bin and potted plants in the bottom corners.
