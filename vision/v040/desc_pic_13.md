## Free description
It is a sunny day in a park with green hills in the distance. On the left there is an oval blue pond with cattails, and a white duck is swimming on its left side. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie, black trousers and white trainers stands on the grass at the front edge of the pond, holding a fishing rod with both hands. His line goes down into the water, where a red-and-white float sits in a small ripple, so he is fishing and waiting for a bite. In the centre there is a tall black lamp post with a green litter bin beside it, and a winding sandy path runs from the bottom right into the distance past a big tree on the right. Next to the far end of the path, a wooden ladder stands upright on the grass, leaning against nothing at all. That looks impossible, because it should fall over. There is also a colourful flower bed at the front left and a grey rock at the bottom centre.
