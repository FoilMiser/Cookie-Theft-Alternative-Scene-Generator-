## Free description

On the left is a red-framed slide with a ladder and a yellow-and-orange chute, with a green rubbish bin behind it, and in the middle is a dark metal swing set with one empty red swing hanging still. A woman with long dark hair, in a yellow dress and red shoes, stands in the centre-left eating a pink ice-cream cone. On the right is a red seesaw: a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers sits on the low end reading an open book, while a small girl in pink sits on the high end, holding the handle, stuck up in the air. Because the man is heavier and busy reading, the seesaw stays tipped down on his side and the girl cannot come back down; she looks toward him with her mouth open, as if surprised or calling out to him. A large tree stands on the right, and there is a grey rock in the bottom left and a bush on the left.
