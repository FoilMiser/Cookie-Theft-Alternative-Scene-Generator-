# Description of test_6.png

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden behind a house, with a wooden fence across the back and a blue sky with a bright sun and two small clouds. On the left is the house, a cream-coloured building with a red roof, a window and a blue back door. A brown dog sits in front of the door holding its own red leash in its mouth, as if asking to be taken for a walk. Next to the house a woman or girl in a yellow dress and red shoes, with short black hair and her back mostly to us, is watering a bed of colourful flowers with a teal watering can. In the middle a man in an orange cap, an orange high-visibility vest and blue trousers is raking a small pile of fallen leaves. Behind him, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is jumping in the air with both arms up under a washing line, where a pair of blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel hang from pegs. On the right there is an empty swing set with one red seat, a birdhouse on a tall pole, and a closed wooden gate in the fence with a paved path leading from it to the bottom right. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the bottom, maybe ready for the raked leaves. Nothing looks clearly impossible; the oddest touch is the dog carrying its own leash, and the child is shown off the ground mid-jump.
