# pic_68

## Free description
A sunny back garden, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right and a wooden fence with a gate on the left. On the paved path at the front left, a young man (or teenage boy) with dark skin, wearing a green T-shirt and beige trousers, is holding an enormous silver dinner fork in both hands like a broom or rake. He is pushing a little heap of dirt/debris with it towards a red dustpan lying on the path, and a small puff of dust rises. The fork is as big as a garden tool, which is strange: it looks like a kitchen fork being used to sweep. Elsewhere in the garden there is a bird box on a tall pole, two potted plants, bushes, a flower bed, a hose reel, a wooden bench and a grey wheelbarrow. No other people or animals are visible.
