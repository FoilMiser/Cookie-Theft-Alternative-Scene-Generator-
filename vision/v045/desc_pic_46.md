# pic_46

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the house on the left, a fence across the back, an empty swing set on the right, and a gate and path at the right. An elderly grey-haired woman with glasses in a teal top sits on a bench at the front left reading a red book. A man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans waters the flower bed with a teal watering can, and the water pours out onto the flowers. At the front right, on the path, a boy in a green T-shirt and beige trousers is sweeping with a giant fork, much bigger than a real one, held like a broom; dust and dirt are being pushed toward a red dustpan on the ground. A grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the front centre. The swing is empty and still.
