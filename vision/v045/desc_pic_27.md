# pic_27

## Free description
A sunny park with a pond on the left, a lamppost in the centre, a winding path and bench on the right, and a flowerbed at the front left. A white duck swims across the pond (wake lines behind it) towards several pale bread crumbs floating on the water, pecking at one. A girl in a pink dress with dark hair stands just to the right of the pond, holding a small paper bag and reaching towards the water, so she appears to be the one feeding the duck. In the front centre a white goat with a red collar is grazing on a tuft of grass, tied by a rope to a stake. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress stands on the path, bending slightly and sprinkling seeds from a paper bag; the seeds fall in a dotted line to the ground. Two small birds, one blue and one red, are on the ground at her feet pecking at the seeds. The bench is empty.
