## Free description
A sunny back garden: a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a long wooden fence with a gate on the right, and a paved path running up to the gate. There are no people. A tan/light-brown dog with a red collar sits by the blue door, holding a yellow leash (with a ring/handle) in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. A garden hose runs across the lawn from the left to a sprinkler in the middle, which is spraying arcs of water to the right - straight onto a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is wet and standing in a puddle. A small blue bird is perched on top of the fence. There is a flower bed in front of the house, two potted plants near the fence, and a tree and bush behind the house. The odd thing is the cardboard box getting soaked by the sprinkler - it seems to be in the wrong place and will be ruined.
