# pic_48

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the house on the left, a fence across the back, and a gate and path on the right. A girl in a pink dress stands on the lawn holding the strings of a red and a blue balloon in one hand, and reaching up with her other hand towards a yellow balloon that has escaped and is floating up into the sky, its string dangling. A washing line between two posts holds blue trousers and a yellow-and-red striped towel. A brown puppy with a red collar lies asleep on the grass at the front right. A hose reel with hose lies at the front left by the flower bed. The odd thing is a cardboard box that is floating in the sky above the washing line, tilted, with nothing holding it up.
