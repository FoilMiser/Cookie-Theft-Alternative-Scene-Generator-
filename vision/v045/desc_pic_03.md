# pic_03

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house (red roof, blue door) on the left, a wooden fence along the back, a gate and paved path on the right. In the centre a woman with dark hair in a yellow dress is hanging washing on a clothesline strung between two wooden posts; she is reaching up with both hands to peg a white T-shirt. A pair of blue jeans already hangs on the line, pegged with clothes pegs. Both the jeans and the T-shirt are dripping water, so they are freshly washed. At her feet is a wicker laundry basket full of more clothes (blue, white, pink). Around the garden are a flowerbed, a hose reel, a wheelbarrow, a watering can and two potted plants.
