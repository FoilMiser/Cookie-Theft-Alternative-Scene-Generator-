# pic_02

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a gate and paved path on the left. In the centre a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a handle; about three boards next to him are already blue, the rest is still natural wood. Behind him on the grass is a paint tin and a paint tray with blue paint. A white dog with brown spots is walking away to the left toward the path, and it has left a trail of blue paw prints leading from the paint tray; its paws are blue. The boy has his back to the dog and does not seem to have noticed. A watering can and a wheelbarrow sit in the foreground and there is a flowerbed near the house.
