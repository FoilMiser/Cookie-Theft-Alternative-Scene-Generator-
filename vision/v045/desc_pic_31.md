# pic_31

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof on the left, a wooden fence along the back, and a gate on the right. An older man in a grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers holds a garden hose up and looks at the nozzle, from which only a small drop of water is coming. The hose runs from a hose reel by the house across the grass, past the flowerbed. A brown dog with a red collar is standing on the hose, and the hose looks thicker or bulged at that spot. So it seems the dog is blocking the water and the man is puzzled why the hose is not working; water might spurt out at him if the dog moves. On the right, a man in a red shirt and blue trousers with a moustache is sweeping a pile of leaves on the path with a yellow broom. There are two potted plants and a grey wheelbarrow on the grass.
