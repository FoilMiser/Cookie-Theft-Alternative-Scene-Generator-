A sunny playground on grass, with a dark metal railing fence and hedges behind. On the left is a red seesaw: a girl in a pink dress is sitting on the high end, up in the air, holding the handle and looking surprised or calling out. On the low end, an older man with grey hair and a moustache, in a cap, brown waistcoat and white shirt, sits reading an open book; his weight is holding his end down so the girl is stuck up high, and he does not seem to notice her. On the right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts slides down a slide with his arms raised. There is a green rubbish bin by the fence, a grey rock at the front right, a tree on the left and a bush on the right.
