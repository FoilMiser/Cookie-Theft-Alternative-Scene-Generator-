## Free description
This is a back garden with a house on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back and a paved path on the right leading to a wooden gate. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands by the fence and uses a long-handled roller to paint it blue. So far only a few fence boards on the right are blue. A paint tin sits on the grass behind him. In the front centre an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, rakes a small pile of brown leaves. On the left there is a round flowerbed with colourful flowers, and in the front right there is a teal watering can. A blue bird flies in the sky near the clouds. Nothing looks clearly wrong or impossible.
