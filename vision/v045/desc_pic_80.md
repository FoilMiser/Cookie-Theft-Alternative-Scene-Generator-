## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house on the left (blue door, one window), a wooden fence across the back, a gate on the right and a paved path running down the right side. In the middle an older man in a grey cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat holds the end of a green garden hose up near his face and looks at the nozzle; only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose runs from a reel near the house, across the lawn, to the man. A small white dog with brown patches is standing right on top of the hose, and there is a dark bulge in the hose just under the dog's feet, so the dog seems to be blocking the water. On the right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping a small pile of brown leaves on the path with a broom. An empty swing set stands behind her, and an empty wheelbarrow sits on the grass. A flower bed with colourful flowers is next to the house. It looks as if the water will spurt out once the dog steps off the hose.
