# pic_04

## Free description
A busy sunny park with a pond, a lamp post, a bench, a path and trees. On the left an old man in a grey cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is watering a flowerbed by pouring water out of a frying pan, which is an odd tool to water flowers with. On the pond a white duck swims toward bits of bread floating on the water, and a girl in a pink dress stands at the edge holding a small bag, apparently feeding it. In the front-centre a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is crying, holding an empty ice-cream cone; the pink ice cream has fallen on the ground and a white dog with brown spots is eating it. Behind, an old woman with glasses in a teal top is sweeping a pile of leaves with a broom, and next to her a white goat on a rope tied to a stake is grazing. On the path a boy in a red cap and blue top holds a kite handle and looks up worriedly: his purple kite is stuck in the tree on the right. At far right a brown dog sits up holding an open newspaper as if reading it, which is impossible/silly. A blue bird flies in the sky.
