## Free description
This is a busy town street with a row of colourful shops and a wide paved pavement on a sunny day. On the left a woman in a yellow dress walks along carrying a blue shopping bag. Next to her a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts points up at a tree, where a blue-and-white beach ball is stuck in the branches. A brown dog with a red collar is sitting up on its back legs holding an open newspaper, as if reading it, which is impossible for a real dog. Behind it, an older grey-haired woman with glasses sweeps a pile of leaves with a broom. In the middle a girl in a purple top sits on a bench reading a red book, with a sandwich on the bench beside her and a blue bird perched on the bench right next to the sandwich, apparently about to take it. On the right an old man in a cap and brown waistcoat walks while reading an open book, heading towards a cardboard box on the ground in front of him so he may trip over it. Behind him a little girl in a pink dress jumps with her arms up near a fruit-and-vegetable stall. In the front centre a red bicycle stands with square wheels, which is impossible. A red bird flies in the top left.
