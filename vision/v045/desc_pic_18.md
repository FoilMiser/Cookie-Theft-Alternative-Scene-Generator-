# pic_18

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate at far left, a stone path down the left side and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the left an old man with a white moustache, grey cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is digging with a spade, with a pile of earth next to the hole. In the middle-right a man in a red T-shirt and blue trousers is watering a round flowerbed with a teal watering can. Oddly, the water does not pour down onto the flowers: it shoots upward out of the spout in an arc, with drops flying up into the air, which looks impossible for a watering can. On the far right a woman in a yellow dress sits on a wooden bench drinking from a red cup through a straw. A washing line between two posts at the back holds a yellow-and-red striped top and a pair of blue jeans.
