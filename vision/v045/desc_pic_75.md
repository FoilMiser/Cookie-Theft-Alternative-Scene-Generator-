## Free description
This is a back garden on a windy, sunny day. The house is on the left, with a blue door, and a wooden fence and gate are at the back. White wind lines and small green leaves blow across the sky from left to right. A man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans, with brown hair and a moustache, stands in the middle reaching up toward a blue cap that is flying away in the wind. It is presumably his, since he is bareheaded. In front, a boy in a red cap and blue top kneels by the flower bed, digging with a small trowel next to a potted pink flower, apparently planting it. Strangely, an orange fish is sitting in the branches of the tree behind the house, which is impossible and looks out of place. The garden also contains a swing set with an empty red swing, a hose reel, a wheelbarrow, a watering can, a birdhouse on a pole and a paved path leading to the gate.
