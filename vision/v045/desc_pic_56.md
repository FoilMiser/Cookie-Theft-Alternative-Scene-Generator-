## Free description
A sunny park with a winding path on the left, a pond on the right, a lamp post in the middle and hills behind. A small girl in a pink dress stands on the grass holding the strings of a yellow and a blue balloon; a purple balloon has escaped and is floating up and away with its string trailing, and she is reaching up toward it - she has just lost it. A white duck swims on the pond. In the front right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie kneels by a flower bed, digging with a small trowel next to a pink flower in a terracotta pot, about to plant it. An empty bench is near the path, a grey rock at the front and a green bin at the bottom right. Nothing looks impossible.
