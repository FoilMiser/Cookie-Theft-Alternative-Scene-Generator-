## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, a gate on the right and a paved path at the right edge. An older man in a grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is watering the flowerbed with a teal watering can, with streams of water falling onto the flowers. A grey cat with a white chest is walking along the top of the fence near the tree on the left. In the sky above the fence, a closed cardboard box is hanging in mid-air, tilted, with nothing holding it. Nobody is throwing it and there is no rope, so it looks impossible, or as if it is falling. On the right is a swing set with one empty red swing seat. Nobody else is in the garden.
