## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house on the right and a wooden fence with a gate on the left. On the path at the far left, an older man with a grey moustache, flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is raking up a few leaves. In the middle back, a little girl in a pink dress holds two balloons (purple and yellow) on strings in one hand and reaches up with the other; a third, blue balloon is floating away up into the sky with its string dangling, so she seems to have just let it go. In front of her, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts has both arms raised, apparently jumping or cheering, possibly reaching for the escaping balloon. On the grass a blue bird is pecking, and a grey cat is crouched low creeping towards it as if about to pounce. An orange cat is curled up asleep near the flower bed. A washing line holds a yellow striped towel and blue jeans; there is also a hose reel and a bench.
