## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house (blue door) on the left, a wooden fence with a gate on the right and a paved path leading to it. A woman in a yellow dress stands in the middle with her arms out in front, ready to catch a red ball flying toward her; a dashed line shows it was thrown by a small girl in a pink dress further right. In the front right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is kicking an orange ball on the path, with motion lines showing the kick. At the back right, a white-and-brown dog plays with a yellow-green tennis ball near the gate. At the front left, a girl with braided hair in a yellow top and orange shorts sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. A hose runs along the front to a sprinkler that is spraying water onto a cardboard box sitting in a puddle. There is also a grey wheelbarrow in the front, with a dark square object at its front end. The soaked cardboard box looks out of place.
