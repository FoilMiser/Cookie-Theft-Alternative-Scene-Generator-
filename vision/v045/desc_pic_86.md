## Free description
A sunny park with a pond, a lamp post, a bench, a winding path, trees and a green bin. On the left a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is watering the flower bed, but she is pouring the water out of a frying pan instead of a watering can. Beside her a man in a red shirt and jeans walks along holding a pink ice-cream cone. In the background on the right a girl in a pink dress holds a purple and a blue balloon and reaches up towards a yellow balloon that is floating away into the sky, its string dangling. The bench is empty. The frying pan used as a watering can is the strange part; the girl has just lost a balloon.
