## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the right, a fence, a gate on the left and a swing set in the middle. On the left a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands on the path flying a yellow kite high in the sky on a long string. In the centre a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie holds the nozzle of a garden hose up near his face. He looks at it as if puzzled that no water is coming out. A brown dog with a red collar is standing right on the hose, which runs back to a hose reel by the flower bed, so the dog is blocking the water. Behind them a man in a green shirt and beige trousers stands with his hands on his hips and a white bucket upside down on his head, which looks odd and silly. The swing set has one red seat and nobody is on it.
