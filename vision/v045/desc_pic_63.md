This is a back garden with a house on the left, a wooden fence and a paved path on the right leading to a wooden gate. In the centre-left a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is hanging washing on a clothes line strung between two wooden posts. A pair of blue jeans and a white T-shirt are already pegged up, and both are dripping water. A laundry basket full of clothes sits on the grass under the line. To the right a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts is swinging happily on a red swing in a metal swing frame. On the far right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers rakes leaves on the paved path. There is also a hose reel, a grey wheelbarrow and a flowerbed in the garden. It looks like an ordinary busy day of family chores and play.
