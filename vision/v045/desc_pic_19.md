A sunny park with green hills, a pond with reeds on the left, a flowerbed, a winding sand path on the right, trees, a black lamp post and a green bin. A man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands by the lamp post with his hands on his hips, wearing an upside-down white bucket on his head like a hat, which looks silly or odd. A white goat with a red collar is eating grass in the middle; its rope runs to a peg in the ground. A woman in a yellow dress walks along the path carrying a blue shopping bag, looking left. On the far right a little girl in a pink dress holds a reel and flies a yellow kite high in the sky.
