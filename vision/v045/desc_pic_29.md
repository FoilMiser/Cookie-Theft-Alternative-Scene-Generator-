# pic_29

## Free description
A sunny park with a pond on the left, a lamppost in the centre, an empty winding path and an empty bench on the right, a flowerbed and green bin at front left, and a grey rock in the front. A woman in a yellow dress with dark hair stands at the near edge of the pond holding a paper bag. She reaches out with her other hand towards the water, where a few pale bread crumbs float. A white duck swims towards her (wake lines behind it) and is pecking at the crumbs. She is the only person in the scene. It is a calm feeding-the-duck moment.
