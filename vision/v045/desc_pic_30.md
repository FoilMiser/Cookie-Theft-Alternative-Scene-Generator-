## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden picket fence, a gate on the far left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. An older woman with white hair and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle. A section of about three boards of the fence is already painted bright blue, and she is rolling right next to it. A small paint pot with blue paint sits on the grass near her feet. At the front left, a younger woman with dark hair in a yellow dress is sweeping a small pile of brown leaves on the paved path with a yellow broom. Also in the garden are a teal watering can, a grey wheelbarrow, a flowerbed by the house and an empty bench. Nothing alarming is happening.
