## Free description
A busy town square on a sunny day, with a row of colourful shops with striped awnings behind. On the left a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue-and-white beach ball that is stuck in the branches of a tree. In the middle a child in a yellow shirt sits on a bench licking a pink ice cream. A small brown dog with a red neckerchief sits up on its hind legs in front of the child, begging. A man in a green shirt and beige trousers walks along pushing a pram with a sleeping baby under a pink blanket. On the right a little girl in a pink dress holds a yellow balloon on a string near a fruit and vegetable stall. A red bird is perched on top of the street lamp. There is also a café table with two chairs, a wooden crate, a bin, a potted plant and a flower box.
