## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with no people or animals in it. A wooden fence with a gate on the left runs across the back, a cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the right, and a paved path runs down from the gate on the left. In the middle-left there is an empty swing set with a single red seat hanging still. On the right, in front of the house, there is a round bed of colourful flowers, and next to it a hose reel with the hose lying on the grass. A teal watering can sits on the grass in front of the swing set. The odd thing is a cardboard box that is hanging in the sky above the fence, tilted, with nothing holding it up. Nobody is there to have thrown it, so it looks like it is floating or falling.
