# pic_45

## Free description
This is a windy, sunny back garden: white wind lines blow across the sky. On the far left by the gate and path, a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers is reaching up towards his orange cap, which the wind has just blown off his head; a few leaves blow with it, and he looks surprised. Behind him there is a birdhouse on a pole. A washing line between two posts holds a striped yellow-and-red towel and a pair of blue trousers flapping in the wind. In the front centre-left a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans drinks through a straw from a red cup, but he is in a sitting pose with nothing under him, as if sitting on an invisible chair. In the centre a woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds a garden hose nozzle up, with a drop of water coming out; the hose runs across the lawn to a reel on the right. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar stands right on top of the hose, which may be why little water is coming out. A flower bed and the house with a blue door are on the right.
