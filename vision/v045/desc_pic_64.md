# pic_64

## Free description
This is a back garden seen the other way round: the house is on the right and the wooden gate and paved path are on the left. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans is painting the fence blue with a roller on a long pole; a few boards in the middle are already blue. On the ground near him are a paint tin and a paint tray with blue paint. A trail of blue paw prints leads from the tray across the grass to a white dog with brown spots on the left, and the dog's paws are blue, so it has walked through the paint. At the back left a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts has just thrown a red ball (a dotted line shows its path) toward a small girl in a pink dress, who holds her hands out to catch it. In the front a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie kicks an orange ball. There is also a watering can, a grey wheelbarrow, a hose reel and a flowerbed. The mishap is the dog spreading blue paint across the lawn.
