# pic_04 description

## Free description

It is a sunny day in a park with a pond, hills, a lamp post, a bench, a path and trees. In front on the left, an old man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat is watering a flowerbed, but he is pouring the water out of a frying pan instead of a watering can. A white duck swims on the pond, heading toward a trail of small pale crumbs or pellets on the water, and a girl in a pink dress at the pond's edge holds a small brown bag as if she has been feeding it. In the centre front, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is rubbing their eyes and crying, holding an empty ice-cream cone. The pink ice cream has fallen on the ground, and a white dog with brown patches is licking it up. At the back, a grey-haired woman in glasses and a green top is sweeping leaves with a broom, and a white goat stands next to her eating grass. Its lead or rope seems to trail to a peg in the ground. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue top is standing on the path holding a kite reel with the string going up, but the purple kite is caught in the tree. Further right, a brown dog sits up and holds an open newspaper as if reading it, which is impossible. A blue bird flies in the sky.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The water from the frying pan: either a deliberate "wrong tool" oddity, or the man is just emptying a pan. It reads as watering the flowers with a frying pan.
- The girl's bag could be bread for the duck or a snack bag. The pale dots on the water could be bread crumbs or pebbles/lily pads; the duck heading to them suggests food.
- The crying child: the dropped ice cream is the obvious cause, but the child is covering the eyes rather than looking at it. It could also be read as rubbing the eyes from the sun.
- The goat in a park is unusual. The line from it could be a lead tied to a stake, or the woman's broom handle area. It seems to belong to a separate stake, not to the woman.
- The kite: it is either stuck in the tree, or the boy is still flying it and it is just behind the tree. The string goes slack into the leaves, so it looks stuck.
- The dog reading a newspaper is impossible, and it looks deliberate. Nothing seems to float unsupported apart from the bird and kite.
- The old man's figure is drawn in front of the pond edge. He appears to stand on the grass, not in the water.
