# pic_36

## Free description
A sunny back garden. In the centre-right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the wooden fence blue with a roller on a long handle; part of the fence is already blue. On the grass behind her are a paint tin and a flat paint tray with blue paint in it. A white dog with brown patches is walking away from the tray towards the left. A trail of blue paw prints runs from the tray to the dog, and its feet look blue, so the dog has stepped in the paint and is spreading it around. The woman has her back to it and does not seem to have noticed. On the left an older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, is sweeping a pile of brown leaves on the paved path with a yellow broom. There is a house with a blue door on the right, plus a flower bed, a bush, a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow.
