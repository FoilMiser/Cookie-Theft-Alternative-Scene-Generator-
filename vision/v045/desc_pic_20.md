# pic_20

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate at far left, a stone path down the left side and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A woman in a yellow dress is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; a section of fence to her right is already blue. Her paint tray and a paint tin sit on the grass behind her. A trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the grass to a small white dog with brown spots on the path, whose feet are blue, so the dog has stepped in the paint. In the front centre a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is digging with a spade next to a pile of earth. On the right a man in a red T-shirt and blue trousers is watering the flowerbed with a teal watering can, but the water arcs upward from the spout into the air instead of pouring down, which looks impossible.
