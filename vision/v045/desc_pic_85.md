# pic_85

## Free description
The back garden on a sunny day, with nobody in it. There is the cream house with the blue door on the left, a flower bed, the wooden fence, the gate and the path on the right. Two potted plants stand on the grass near a bush, and an empty wheelbarrow and a teal watering can are at the front. The odd thing is in the tree behind the house: an orange goldfish is sitting on a branch, as if it were a bird. A fish out of water in a tree is impossible.
