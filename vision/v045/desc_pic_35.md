## Free description
A quiet sunny day in a park with rolling green hills behind. A man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers stands at the edge of a blue pond holding a long fishing rod. The line hangs down into the water and ends at a red-and-white float with a small ripple around it, so he is waiting for a bite. There are bulrushes at both ends of the pond. Behind him are a tall black lamp post and a green bin. To the right are a wooden bench and a winding sandy path, with a big tree beside it. In the front left is a bed of colourful flowers and at the bottom centre a grey rock. Nobody else is around and nothing looks strange.
