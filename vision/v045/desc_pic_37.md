# pic_37

## Free description
A sunny back garden. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, with a wooden fence and gate at the back and a paved path on the right. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands beside a round flower bed holding a small grey watering can. The water from the spout does not fall down onto the flowers. Instead it arcs upward and back into the air above the flowers, with droplets flying up at the top. This looks wrong, as if the water is flowing uphill. Two plant pots with green plants sit on the grass by a bush, and a grey wheelbarrow is at the front. No one else is in the picture.
