## Free description
A sunny but windy back garden: white wind lines streak across the sky and across the gate on the left. On the left an older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, stands on a paved path. She reaches up with her mouth open as her blue hat blows away in the wind, along with a few small leaves or seeds. In the centre a woman in a yellow dress is painting the wooden fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle. One section of the fence is already blue, and a small paint tin sits on the grass by her feet. A tall pole with a birdhouse on top stands left of centre. Strangely, an orange fish is sitting in the branches of the tree on the right, behind the house, which is impossible. On the right there is a cream house with a red roof and a blue door. There is also a flower bed, a bush, a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow on the grass.
