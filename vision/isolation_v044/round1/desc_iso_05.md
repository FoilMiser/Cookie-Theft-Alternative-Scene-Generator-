# iso_05

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden, with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a wooden gate at the right end, and a paved path leading from the front right to that gate. On top of the fence, a grey cat is crouched low, creeping along towards a small blue bird that is perched on the fence a short way to its right. The cat seems about to pounce on the bird. In the middle of the lawn, a boy in a red shirt and blue trousers holds a garden hose up near his face and looks at the nozzle, from which only a single drop of water is coming. The hose runs back along the grass to a hose reel by the house, and a brown and white puppy is standing right on top of it. The hose looks fatter on the reel side of the puppy, as if the puppy's paws are pinching it and blocking the water. So it looks as though the water is stuck, and if the puppy steps off, the boy may get a sudden spray in the face. On the right, a girl or young woman in a yellow dress is digging a hole in the lawn with a spade, with a mound of soil beside her. A colourful flower bed sits in front of the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Why only a drop of water comes out: my main reading is that the puppy is standing on the hose and blocking the flow, because the hose is visibly swollen on the reel side of the puppy and thinner past it. Another reading is that the boy has simply not turned it on fully, or that the bulge is just a drawing quirk. The bulge makes the "blocked hose" reading the stronger one.
- What the boy is aiming at: he seems to be looking at the nozzle. But the nozzle points up and to the left, roughly towards the fence where the cat is, so it could also be read as him about to spray the cat.
- Cat and bird: the cat is clearly crouched in a stalking pose. The bird faces the cat and does not look alarmed, so it may not have noticed. It could also be read as the cat just walking along the fence.
- The digger could be a teenage girl or an adult woman. I could not tell her age.
- The hose runs up beside the boy's legs to his hands, and in one stretch it overlaps his trouser leg. It looks as though it passes in front of his leg rather than through it, but the overlap is slightly unclear.
- The puppy's feet sit on, and partly overlap, the hose. That looks deliberate (standing on it) rather than a clipping error.
- Nothing appears to float unnaturally.
