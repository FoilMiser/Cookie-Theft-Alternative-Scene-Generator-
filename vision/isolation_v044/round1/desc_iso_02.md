## Free description

This is a sunny day in a park, with a big yellow sun at the top right, two white clouds, green hills and trees in the background, a blue pond with reeds on the left, a tall black lamp post in the middle and a sandy path winding from the front right towards the back. In the middle on the right, a girl in a pink dress holds the strings of a yellow and a blue balloon in one hand. She stretches her other arm up towards a purple balloon that is floating higher up. The purple balloon's string ends in a little curl just above her raised hand, so it looks as though it has just slipped away and she is trying to grab it back. It will probably float off if she doesn't catch it. In the front centre, a brown dog sits up on its back legs and holds an open newspaper in its front paws as if it is reading it. That is impossible for a real dog and is the oddest thing in the picture. At the bottom right, a white goat with a red collar is tied by a rope to a small stake in the ground and has its head down, eating a tuft of grass beside the path. An empty wooden bench stands in front of the girl. A flower bed, a green bin and a grey rock are in the foreground.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Purple balloon: read one way, it has just escaped from the girl and she is reaching to catch it. Read another way, she is still holding it and its string is simply drawn short. The string's end hangs a little above her fingers, so "escaping" seems the more likely reading.
- Dog's eye: there is a dark ring around the dog's eye that could be a pair of glasses (which would fit the "reading" joke) or could just be a large dark eye marking. I could not tell which.
- The dog reading a newspaper is plainly impossible. It looks like a deliberate "what's wrong" element, but taken literally it is just a dog holding up a paper.
- The goat's rope runs from its collar down to a small stake near the bottom edge. It is a thin, pale line and easy to miss, and at first glance it could also be read as a dropped lead.
- The lamp post is very tall compared with the people and trees, and it stands alone on the grass near the pond's edge. That is slightly odd but not clearly wrong.
- Nothing seems to float or pass through anything else, apart from the purple balloon, which floats as a balloon should.
