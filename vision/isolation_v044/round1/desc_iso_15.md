# Picture iso_15

## Free description

It is a sunny day in a back garden. A tall wooden fence runs across the back, with a wooden gate on the far left, a paved path running from it to the bottom-left corner, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue front door on the right. On the left, an old man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers is painting the fence blue with a long-handled paint roller. He has painted a section about three planks wide, from top to bottom, and a small paint tin with blue paint stands on the grass by his feet. On top of the fence, a grey cat is creeping along in a low, stalking crouch towards a red bird perched a little further to the right. The bird faces the cat, and the cat looks about to pounce, so the bird may be about to fly off. At the front of the garden, a brown dog with a red neckerchief sits up on its back legs and holds an open newspaper in its front paws as if reading it. This is the odd part, because dogs don't read. A teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow sit on the lawn near the front, and there is a bed of colourful flowers in front of the house.
