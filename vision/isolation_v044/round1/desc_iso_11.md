## Free description

It is a sunny day in a park: a yellow sun is in the top right, there are two white clouds, green hills and trees in the background, a blue pond on the left with bulrushes, and a sandy path that winds from the bottom of the picture up towards the right. In the middle front a girl in a pink dress is holding the strings of a blue and a red balloon in one hand. She is reaching up with her other arm and looking up at a purple balloon that is floating above her and to the left, with its string hanging loose. It looks as if the purple balloon has just got away from her and is drifting off towards the pond, and she is trying to catch it; its string end is only just above her fingers, so she might still grab it or it might float away. Behind a wooden park bench, a white goat with dark horns and a red collar is bending down eating a tuft of grass; it is tied by a rope to a small stake. At the bottom right, on the path, a brown-and-white puppy with a red collar is lying down with its front paws on an open newspaper, as if it is reading it (or tearing it up). There is a flower bed with colourful flowers at the bottom left, a green rubbish bin in the bottom-left corner, and a tall black lamp post in the centre. The odd things are a goat tethered in a park and a dog apparently "reading" a newspaper.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Purple balloon: it could be one the girl has just let go of (and she is reaching to catch it), or one she is simply pointing at. The loose, trailing string and her gaze make "it escaped and she's trying to grab it" the more likely reading. The bottom of its string ends just above her raised hand, so it is unclear whether she can still reach it.
- Dog and newspaper: could be read as the dog "reading" the newspaper (funny or impossible), or as the dog lying on it / chewing or tearing it.
- Goat's tether: the stake it is tied to is drawn right where the top rail of the bench is, so it could be read as tied to the back of the bench or to a stake in the ground behind the bench.
- A goat in an ordinary town park is a bit unusual, but it does not look like a drawing mistake.
- The lamp post is very tall and thin compared with the people and the bench.
- Nothing seems to pass through anything else, and nothing else seems to be floating apart from the balloons.
