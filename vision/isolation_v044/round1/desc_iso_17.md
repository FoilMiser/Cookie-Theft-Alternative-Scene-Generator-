# Picture iso_17

## Free description

It is a sunny day in a back garden. A wooden fence runs across the back, with a wooden gate on the far left, a paved path leading down to the bottom-left corner, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. On the left of the lawn stands a dark blue-grey A-frame swing set with one empty red swing seat hanging still. In the middle-right, an old woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses is watering a bed of colourful flowers with a teal watering can. She wears a teal top, a grey skirt and brown shoes. Strangely, the water from the can does not pour down onto the flowers: it arcs upwards into the air, with droplets at the top of the arc, like a fountain. By the house door on the right, a brown dog with a red collar sits holding a red lead in its mouth, as if asking to be taken for a walk. At the front of the lawn is a grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles. Oddly, its wheel is a square instead of a circle, which would not roll.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The water stream is the main oddity, and it can be read two ways. (a) It shoots upwards out of the watering can and falls as drops from the top, which a watering can could not do. (b) It is meant to fall from above down to the can or the flowers, which also makes little sense. Either way, the water is not falling onto the flowers the way it should.
- The wheelbarrow's wheel is square, a dark square frame with a pattern inside and an axle bar going into its middle. It could be a deliberately impossible square wheel, or a drawing glitch where a wheel was meant to be round. It looks wrong.
- The dog with the lead in its mouth: probably asking for a walk, but it might simply be chewing or carrying the lead. The lead's loop lies on the grass beside the door step.
- The swing hangs perfectly still and nobody is near it. It is just there and not being used.
