# Description: iso_07

## Free description
A sunny back garden with green grass, a tall wooden fence along the back, and bushes and a tree behind the fence. On the right is the side of a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door. In the middle-right, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses is standing by a round flowerbed full of colourful flowers, holding a grey watering can. Strangely, the water from the can does not fall onto the flowers. It arcs upwards into the air, with drops at the top, as if it were flowing uphill or spraying like a fountain. On the far right, a brown dog with a red collar sits by the house door holding a green garden hose (or possibly a lead) in its mouth. On the left there is an empty swing set with one red seat hanging still, a wooden gate in the fence, and a pale paved path running from the gate down to the bottom-left. At the bottom centre lies a grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles, and its single wheel looks square rather than round. Nobody is playing on the swing or using the wheelbarrow.

- The green thing in the dog's mouth could be a garden hose (the dog bringing a hose to help with watering) or a lead (the dog wanting to go for a walk). Its coiled shape and green colour look more like a hose.
- Wheelbarrow wheel: it appears to be a dark rounded square with spokes inside rather than a circle. This looks like a deliberately impossible square wheel, or it could be a drawing glitch.
