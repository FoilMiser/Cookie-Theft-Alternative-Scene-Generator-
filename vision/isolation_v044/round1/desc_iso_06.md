# Description of iso_06

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden: a sun in the top-left corner, two white clouds, a tall wooden picket fence across the back with a hedge and a tree behind it, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue front door on the right. An older man with grey hair, a grey moustache and a grey-blue cap stands on the lawn in front of the fence, left of centre. He wears a white shirt, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers, and he is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller. About three boards' width of fence next to him is already painted bright blue, and an open tin of blue paint stands on the grass by his feet. On top of the fence a grey cat creeps along the pointed tops in a low, stalking crouch towards a red bird perched a little further along. The bird is facing the cat, and the cat looks about to pounce on it. In the front of the lawn, near the middle, a light-brown dog with a red collar lies on its belly with its front paws on an open newspaper and its head down over the pages, as if it is reading it. A red food bowl full of dog food sits on the grass below and to the left of the dog, and the dog is ignoring it. There is a flower bed with many colourful flowers in front of the house, a small round bush by the house wall, and a pale paved path running from a closed wooden gate in the left of the fence down to the bottom of the picture. The odd thing is the dog "reading" the newspaper; the cat balancing on the pointed fence tops and stalking the bird is the thing that seems about to happen.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The dog with the newspaper can be read two ways: it is "reading" the paper like a person (the impossible/funny reading), or it is just lying on the paper and sniffing or about to chew or tear it. Its posture, with head bent over the open pages and paws holding them, looks more like reading.
- The cat's pose could be a slow, creeping stalk or a crouch just before a leap. Either way it is heading for the bird, but I can't tell how soon it will pounce. The bird does not look alarmed.
- The cat walks or crouches on the pointed tops of the pickets, which is a precarious place to stand. It looks deliberate rather than like the cat is floating, but the contact between its paws and the fence tops is small.
- The man's cap is grey-blue; it is unclear whether that is just its colour or blue paint.
- The painted section is the same blue as the house door, which may just be a matching colour choice.
- Nothing else looks like it is floating or passing through anything. The roller head rests on the fence and the tin sits on the grass.
