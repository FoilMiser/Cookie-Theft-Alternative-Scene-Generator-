# iso_14

## Free description

This is a sunny day in a cartoon park. There is a pond on the left, a tall black lamp post in the middle, an empty wooden bench, a winding sandy path on the right, trees and bushes, a flower bed and a green bin. A girl in a pink dress stands near the middle-right holding the strings of a yellow balloon and a blue balloon in one hand. She is reaching up with her other hand towards a purple balloon whose string ends just above her fingers, so it looks like the purple balloon has just slipped away and is floating off. Her mouth is open as if she is surprised. In the front, a brown dog with a red collar or bandana sits up on its back legs and holds an open newspaper with a front paw, looking at it as if it were reading. A dog reading a newspaper is the obviously impossible thing in the picture. On the right, next to the path, a white goat with horns and a red collar has its head down eating a tuft of grass. It is tied with a rope to a small stake in the ground. A grey rock lies in the front.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Purple balloon: I read it as having just escaped from the girl's hand, because its string ends just above her fingers. It could also be read as her reaching up to catch a balloon that is floating past. Either way, it is not being held.
- Dog and newspaper: The dog could be reading the newspaper, which is impossible, or it could just be holding, grabbing or fetching it. Its pose and the way it faces the page make it look like reading. The newspaper also seems to stand upright on its own or float slightly above the grass, held up only by the paw.
- Girl's hands: It is not completely clear which hand holds the two balloon strings. They go to her lower hand, at about waist height.
- Goat's rope: It runs from the collar down past the goat's front leg to the stake. It looks like a tether, but it is drawn thin and the goat stands very close to the stake.
