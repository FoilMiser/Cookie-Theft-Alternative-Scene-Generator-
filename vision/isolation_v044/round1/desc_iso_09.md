A sunny back garden: a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the left, with a tree behind it. A tall wooden fence with a gate on the right runs across the back, and a paved path leads from the front-right up to the gate. Near the fence in the middle, a man with brown hair and a moustache, in a red T-shirt and blue jeans, is digging with a spade. The spade blade has soil on it, and a mound of dug earth is piled next to him. On the right, a grey cat is crouched low and creeping towards a small red bird standing on the grass. The cat looks as if it is about to pounce, and the bird does not seem to have noticed it. In the front-centre, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers holds the nozzle of a green garden hose up near his chest and looks at it, but no water is coming out. The hose runs along the ground from a hose reel by the house, and a white dog with brown patches and a blue collar is standing on it. It looks as if the dog may be squashing the hose and stopping the water. There is a flower bed with colourful flowers on the left, a small bush by the house, and an empty grey wheelbarrow at the front.
