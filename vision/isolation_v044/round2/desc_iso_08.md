It's a sunny day on a paved town street or square lined with four colourful two-storey shops (pink, pale green, peach and cream), each with a striped awning. The sun is in the top left corner and there are two small clouds. A young woman with dark hair in a ponytail, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, is walking along the pavement near the shops, eating a pink ice-cream cone. In the centre front a brown dog with a red collar or bandana sits up on its back legs holding an open newspaper in its front paws, as if it were reading it, which is odd because dogs can't read. On the left is a market stall with crates of green, yellow, orange and red fruit, and nobody is there to sell it. Also on the left are a tall black street lamp, a cardboard box and a potted plant. On the right there is a tree in a round raised planter, a green rubbish bin, and two small round café tables with two chairs each, all empty. A flower box sits in front of the peach shop. Apart from the newspaper-reading dog, nothing looks wrong or about to happen.
