# iso_04

## Free description

It is a sunny day in a back garden. A tall wooden fence runs along the back, with green hedges and a tree behind it, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door stands on the right. In the middle of the garden a man with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans is hanging out washing. He reaches up with both hands to peg a white T-shirt onto a clothesline strung between two wooden posts. A pair of blue jeans already hangs further along the line, and both garments are dripping water, so the washing looks freshly washed. A wooden basket full of more clothes (pink, white and blue) sits on the grass beside him. On the right, a brown dog with a red collar or bandana sits by the blue door holding a yellow lead or cord in its mouth; the cord runs down to a loop lying on the ground, as if the dog wants to go for a walk. On the left there is an empty swing set with a red seat, a closed wooden gate in the fence and a paved path running from the gate to the front of the picture. A teal watering can, a grey wheelbarrow and a teal hose reel with a yellow nozzle lie around the lawn, and a flower bed full of colourful flowers grows near the house. Nothing looks dangerous or about to go wrong; the most unusual thing is the dog holding the cord in its mouth.
