# iso_07

## Free description

It is a sunny day in a back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, hedges and a tree behind it, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left. In the middle of the garden a boy in a red cap, a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes is raking up a small pile of brown dead leaves with a long-handled rake. Above and behind him a clothesline between two wooden posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes; they are not dripping, so they look dry or nearly dry. In the front of the picture a brown dog with a red collar or bandana is sitting up on its back legs, holding open a newspaper with its front paws and seemingly wearing round glasses, as if it is reading the paper. That is the strange, impossible part of the picture, since dogs cannot read. On the right is an empty swing set with a red seat, a closed wooden gate in the fence and a paved path running from the gate to the bottom right corner. A grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can stand on the lawn at the front, and a flower bed full of colourful flowers grows next to the house. Nothing seems about to go wrong; the boy is simply doing his garden job while the dog "reads".

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The dark rings around the dog's eyes could be glasses or just large, dark-ringed eyes; either way the dog is posed like it is reading the newspaper, which is the impossible element.
- The boy's tool reads as a rake (thin grey head at the bottom touching the leaves), but at this size it could also be taken for a broom.
- The brown heap at the boy's feet looks like dead leaves, though it could be twigs or cuttings. Dead leaves alongside a bed of fully blooming flowers is a slightly odd mix of seasons, but not impossible.
- Nothing obviously floats or passes through anything else; the boy stands below the clothesline without touching the washing.
