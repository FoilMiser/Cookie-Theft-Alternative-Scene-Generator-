## Free description

It's a sunny day in a back garden. There are two clouds and a bright sun in the top right corner. On the left is the side of a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door. A brown dog with a red collar sits by the door holding a yellow lead in its mouth, as if it wants to go for a walk. In the middle of the lawn an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses is raking a small pile of fallen leaves. She wears a teal cardigan and a grey skirt. Behind her is a wooden fence and a washing line strung between two posts, with a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes pegged on it. On the right a birdhouse stands on a tall pole, there are two potted plants, and a paved path leads to a wooden gate in the fence. In the foreground are a coiled garden hose, an empty wheelbarrow and a teal watering can, and next to the house there is a round bed of colourful flowers. Nothing looks impossible. The only hint of what might happen next is the dog with its lead, which suggests it is waiting to be taken out.
