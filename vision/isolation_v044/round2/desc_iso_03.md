# desc_iso_03

## 1. Free description

This is a sunny back garden behind a cream-coloured house with a red roof. The sun is in the top right and there are two white clouds. A wooden fence runs across the back, with a wooden gate on the right and a paved path running from the gate down to the bottom-right corner. In the middle of the lawn an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses is raking a small pile of brown leaves. She wears a teal top and a grey skirt. Behind her, a washing line hangs between two wooden posts, with a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes pegged on it. On the left, by the blue back door of the house, a brown dog with a red collar sits holding a yellow cord in its mouth. The cord trails down to the grass and ends in a loop, so the dog may be bringing its lead because it wants a walk. Elsewhere in the garden are a bed of colourful flowers, a hose reel with its hose, an empty wheelbarrow, a watering can, two potted plants and a birdhouse on a tall pole. Nothing looks clearly impossible, and nothing seems about to happen apart from the woman carrying on with her raking.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous

- The yellow cord in the dog's mouth could be a dog lead (it ends in a loop like a handle), suggesting the dog wants a walk. It could also be a thin yellow garden hose that the dog has picked up. It is separate from the teal hose on the reel.
- The dog's pose: it seems to be sitting on its haunches, but it could also be read as half-rising or begging. It is unclear whether it is facing the door (waiting to go in) or just sitting beside it.
- The rake handle passes in front of the woman's body, from her hands down to the leaves. This reads as normal raking, not as an object passing through her.
- Nothing appears to be floating or unintentionally strange. The washing line, birdhouse pole and posts all stand in front of the fence in a believable way.
