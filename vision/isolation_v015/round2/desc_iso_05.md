# iso_05

## What I see
A girl with dark hair pulled up into a bun sits on a wooden park bench on green grass. She wears a pink dress and yellow shoes, and holds an open red book in both hands, looking down at it as if reading. A tan path curves through the grass on the right side. Behind the bench, at the top, there is a green slatted trash can and the base of a dark lamp post (its top is cut off by the edge of the picture). A soft darker-green shadow lies on the grass under the bench.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The red object she holds is most likely an open book, but because it is small and plain it could also be read as a tablet, phone or small box.
- The curved dark shape at the very top-left corner is cut off; it could be part of the lamp head or another object entirely.
- The trash can and lamp post have no visible ground line or shadow, so they look a little pasted onto the flat green background rather than standing on it.
- The girl is small compared with the bench, and her legs are thin and dangle down in front of the seat with her feet off the ground. This reads as a child sitting, but the legs look slightly stiff and stick-like.
- The bench shadow is shifted to the left of the bench rather than directly under it. This is probably meant as lighting, but it looks slightly off.
- Her body sits in front of the backrest rails and the book overlaps the top rail. I did not see anything clearly passing through anything else.
