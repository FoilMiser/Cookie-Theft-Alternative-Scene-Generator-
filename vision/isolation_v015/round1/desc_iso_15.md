## What I see
A sunny backyard. A tall wooden fence with a gate runs across it, a tree stands behind the fence, and a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the right. On a concrete path at the left, a small child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts waves one raised hand; small motion lines next to the hand show the waving. A bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers walks across the grass carrying a cardboard box. In the middle, a person in a red shirt and blue jeans holds a green garden hose nozzle out in front with both hands. One drop of water hangs from the nozzle. The hose runs down to the ground, loops past a white goat and leads back to a hose reel beside a flower bed full of colorful flowers. There is also a grey wheelbarrow in the foreground, a round green bush, and a wooden bench next to the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The goat's hooves sit right on the hose, so it looks like the goat is standing on it. That would kink the hose and explain why only a drop comes out of the nozzle. The other reading is that the hose just runs behind or under the goat and the hooves only seem to touch it.
- Next to the goat's back legs, the hose suddenly swells into a long, thick bulge. It could be water pressure building up behind a kink, or it could just be an odd drawing glitch in the hose's width.
- The person with the hose is looking at the nozzle, and only a single drop is coming out. They could be puzzled that no water is flowing, or they could simply be about to spray. I can't tell which from their face.
- I can't tell which way the man with the box is walking. His face points left toward the child and the gate, but his stride could be read either way. The child could be waving at him or at someone else.
- The wheelbarrow looks small next to the adults. It lies at a low angle with its handles stretched out to the left almost along the ground, so it could be resting normally or tipped and dropped.
- The hose comes straight down from the nozzle and makes a tall loop that stands up off the ground, which looks a little stiff. The hose reel is drawn half overlapping the edge of the flower bed.
