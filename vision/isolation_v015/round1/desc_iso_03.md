- a wooden park bench
- a tree in a round planter
- three planters of flowers along the shop fronts
- a dark grey trash can, and a green one
- two small round cafe tables, each with two chairs for sitting outdoors

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The cat is very small for the scene and drawn as a compact orange blob, so it is hard to read. It could be a cat curled up asleep, or a cat lying on its side or rolling. At a glance it could also be taken for a dropped orange or a small toy. A small white bit on its left side could be a paw or the tip of its tail. I can't tell which way it faces.
- The produce in the stall crates is just clusters of colored circles. They could be fruit (apples, oranges, lemons, limes) or vegetables (tomatoes, peppers), so I can't say what exactly is for sale.
- Only the cat and the cardboard box cast shadows. The bench, stall, trash cans, tables, tree and lamp have none, so the cat's shadow makes it stand out a little as if it were added separately.
- The bench is drawn straight on with thin vertical posts, so it is hard to tell whether the top bars are a backrest or a second seat plank.
- The cafe chairs are thin side-view outlines. Their seats are just short horizontal bars, so they look a bit like stools with tall backs.
- The tree canopy covers part of the cream building's awning and windows. This looks like normal overlap, not an error.
- The lamp head overlaps a window of the green building. That also reads as ordinary overlap.
- I see nothing clearly floating or passing through other objects.
