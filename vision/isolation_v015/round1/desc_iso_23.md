# iso_23

## What I see
A girl with dark hair pulled back in a bun, wearing a pink dress and yellow shoes, kneels inside a wooden-framed sandbox on green grass. With both hands she is building or patting a tan sandcastle that has square towers and a small red flag on top. A grey rock sits on the grass to the left of the sandbox. Along the top edge are cut-off parts of playground equipment: grey slanted poles with a rung (probably a ladder), two red upright posts, and a yellow-and-orange sloped piece at the top right that looks like part of a slide.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The sandcastle is plain tan blocks, close to the sand color. It could also be read as a cardboard box or a stack of wooden toy blocks, though the flag makes "sandcastle" the likeliest reading.
- The red flag's pole touches the top of the left tower only barely, so the flag looks a little like it is floating above the tower rather than stuck into it.
- It is hard to tell how her legs are arranged. One yellow shoe shows just behind the castle and the other sits behind her body, so she could be kneeling or sitting back on her heels. The castle covers her lower body.
- The castle is right at the front of the sandbox, almost on the inner edge of the frame. It overlaps her arms and knees, so it is not clear whether it is in front of her or right against her.
- The equipment at the top is too cut off to identify for certain. The red posts could belong to a swing set or a climbing frame, and the yellow/orange piece could be a slide or a railing.
- Nothing else looks clearly wrong. She has a grey oval shadow beneath her, which fits her sitting on the sand.
