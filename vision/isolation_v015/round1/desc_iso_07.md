## What I see
A sunny backyard scene: a child with dark curly hair, a yellow top and orange shorts stands on a beige paved path near a wooden gate. The child holds up one arm and is flying an orange diamond-shaped kite with a bow-tied tail high in the sky. Behind the child is a tall wooden fence with a hedge and a large tree behind it. A clothesline between two posts holds a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans. Across the lawn are two potted plants by the fence, a teal watering can, a gray wheelbarrow with wooden handles, a bed of colorful flowers, a teal garden hose reel, and on the right a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue front door with a step. There is a sun and two white clouds in the sky.

- The wheelbarrow's wooden handles appear to come out of the top-left rim of the gray tub rather than from underneath it, which looks slightly odd; the wheel is small and partly hidden under the front of the tub.
- The blue jeans on the clothesline extend right up to (and seem to overlap) the right-hand clothesline post.
- The towel and jeans both hang at a slant, which could read as blowing in the wind or just as tilted drawing.
- The child and the wheelbarrow have shadows on the ground, but the watering can, potted plants and hose reel do not, so the watering can in particular could look as if it is hovering slightly above the grass.
- The hose reel sits right against the edge of the flower bed and slightly overlaps the soil.
- The kite string is very thin and hard to follow; it seems to attach near the lower part of the kite.
