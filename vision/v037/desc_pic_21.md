# pic_21

## Free description
This is a busy town square on a sunny day, with a row of colourful shops with striped awnings across the back and a paved pavement in front. On the left, a blue-and-white striped beach ball is stuck in the branches of a tree, and a small girl in a pink dress is pointing up at it; nearby a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping a pile of autumn leaves with a yellow broom. An older man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat walks past carrying a yellow shopping bag. In the centre a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts is jumping in the air with both arms raised, and behind him a woman in a purple top sits on a bench reading a red book, with a sandwich beside her on the bench and a blue bird standing right next to the sandwich, as if about to take it. To the right of her, an older woman with grey hair and glasses is watering a planter of flowers with a teal watering can. On the far right a man in a green shirt walks along reading an open book, heading towards a cardboard box lying on the ground, so he may trip over it. In the front centre, a brown dog with a red neckerchief sits on the ground holding an open newspaper in its front paws as if reading it, which is impossible for a real dog. A red bird flies in the sky at the top left, and there is a fruit and vegetable stall on the right.
