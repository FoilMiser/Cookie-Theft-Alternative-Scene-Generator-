## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a blue sky, two clouds and the sun in the top left. A wooden fence runs across the back with a gate on the left, a stone path leads in from the gate, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the right. In the centre, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt holds the nozzle of a garden hose up close to her face and peers into it. Only a single drop seems to come out. The hose runs from a hose reel by the house, along the ground, and loops round up to her hands. A white goat stands right on the hose between her and the reel, pinching it, so the water is blocked. The hose looks swollen between the goat and the reel. It looks like as soon as the goat steps off, the water will spray straight into the woman's face. There are also two potted plants by the fence and a flower bed near the house.
