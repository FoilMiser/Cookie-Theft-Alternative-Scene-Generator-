# pic_73

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence and gate on the left, a paved path, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. At the front left, a boy with short dark hair, a green shirt and khaki trousers is standing on the path sweeping dust and bits of dirt into a red dustpan, but instead of a broom he is using a giant silver fork, like an oversized dinner fork, which looks wrong. In the back centre, a boy with brown hair in a red shirt and blue trousers is painting the fence with a long-handled paint roller; a section of the fence is already painted bright blue, and a tin of blue paint stands on the grass behind him. On the right, a woman with a dark ponytail in a purple top and teal trousers is watering a flower bed with a garden hose; the hose runs back to a hose reel by the house. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the front centre, and two potted plants are near the fence. Everyone is busy with garden jobs, and the giant fork used as a broom is the strange thing.
