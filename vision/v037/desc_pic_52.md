## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a lawn and a wooden fence across the back. The sun is at top left and there are two clouds. On the left a paved path runs from a gate in the fence to the front. An old man in a grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands on the path sweeping a pile of autumn leaves with a yellow broom. Two potted plants stand near the fence behind him and a bush stands further along. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the front centre. Right of centre a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers holds a garden hose. Water sprays out of it in an arc onto a flower bed. The hose loops around the front of the flower bed back to a teal hose reel near the house. On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door, with a tree behind it and a bush beside it. An empty wooden bench is in the front right. Both people are doing garden jobs, and nothing seems about to go wrong.
