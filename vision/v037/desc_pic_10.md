## Free description
A sunny playground with a railing fence, hedges, a slide, a swing set with an empty seat, a sandbox and a tree. In the front, a child with braided hair in a yellow top and orange shorts holds an empty ice cream cone and puts a hand to their head in dismay, because the pink scoop has fallen on the grass. A brown dog with a red collar is bending down and licking up the dropped ice cream. To the left, a white dog with a brown ear and blue collar is sitting up holding open a newspaper as if reading it, which is impossible. On the right, a grey-haired older woman with glasses sits on a bench drinking from a red cup through a straw. Next to the tree, a small girl in pink is flying a yellow kite high in the sky. A blue bird is flying in the top right corner.
