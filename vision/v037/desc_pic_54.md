## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a lawn and a wooden fence across the back. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door. A woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers stands left of centre with both arms stretched out in front of her. A red-and-white ball is in the air just in front of her hands, and a dashed line shows its path in an arc from a small girl in a pink dress. The girl has thrown the ball and the woman is about to catch it. By the blue door a brown dog with a red collar sits holding a red lead in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. In the front centre a white dog with brown spots crouches playfully over a yellow tennis ball and wags its tail, shown by motion lines. On the right an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses sweeps a pile of autumn leaves on the paved path with a yellow broom. A teal watering can is on the grass beside the path, and a flower bed and a bush are near the house. A wooden gate is in the fence on the right, the sun is at top right and there are two clouds.
