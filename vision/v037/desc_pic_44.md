## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the house on the right and a wooden gate and paved path on the left. On the path at the left, an older man with grey hair and a moustache, a grey flat cap, a brown waistcoat over a white shirt and dark trousers is sweeping a pile of colourful leaves with a broom. In the centre, a man with dark skin, short dark hair, a green T-shirt and khaki trousers holds the nozzle end of a dark teal garden hose up in front of him and peers at it. Only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel beside the flower bed on the right, down and across the bottom of the lawn, then up to his hands. A white goat with small horns is standing with its back hoof right on the hose, where the hose looks squashed or pinched. So the goat seems to be blocking the water, and the man looks puzzled about why nothing is coming out. If the goat steps off, the water will probably spurt out at the man. The goat looks toward the left and does not seem to notice. A clothesline in the middle holds a yellow red-striped top and blue jeans hanging still, two potted plants sit near the fence, and a flower bed and bush are in front of the house.
