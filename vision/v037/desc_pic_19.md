## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a wooden gate on the far left with a paved path running from it, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. Near the gate there is a birdhouse on a tall pole, and in the left-centre a swing frame with one empty red swing. On the right, a woman with a dark ponytail in a purple top and teal trousers stands beside a flower bed, holding a small teal watering can tilted towards the flowers. The odd thing is that the stream of water comes out of the spout and curves upward into the air, with drops at the top, instead of pouring down onto the flowers. Water flowing up out of a watering can like that is impossible. An empty wheelbarrow is on the grass at the front, a wooden bench is in front of the house, and there is a tree behind the house. The sun is in the top left with two clouds.
