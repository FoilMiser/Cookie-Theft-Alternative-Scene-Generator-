# pic_45

## Free description
This is a busy sunny park with a pond on the left, hills behind, and a lot happening at once. At the far left a white dog with a brown ear and a blue scarf or collar sits upright on the grass holding open a newspaper with its front paws, as if reading it, which is impossible for a real dog. Next to it an older man with a grey moustache, grey flat cap and brown waistcoat is watering a flower bed by pouring water out of a black frying pan, which is a very odd tool to water with. On the pond a white duck swims toward a scatter of small beige pieces (bread or food) floating on the water, with motion lines behind it. Near the lamp post a small girl in a pink dress faces the pond holding a small brown item, possibly what the food came from. In the front centre a girl with braided hair, a yellow top and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and has her other hand to her head, looking upset. Her pink ice cream lies splattered on the ground with sprinkles, and a brown dog with a red collar is sniffing or licking it up. On the path an older woman with a grey bun, glasses, teal cardigan and grey skirt sweeps a pile of autumn leaves. Further back a white goat grazes near the top of the path. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie holds a kite string and looks up: his purple kite is stuck in the leaves of the big tree. A blue bird flies across the sky near the lamp post.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The dog reading a newspaper is clearly impossible. It looks like a deliberate absurdity rather than a drawing glitch.
- Watering with a frying pan is odd. It reads as a deliberate mistake or absurdity, though someone might read it as improvising with whatever was to hand.
- The pink-dressed girl's brown item could be a bag of breadcrumbs (she has been feeding the duck), a drink carton, or a small box. She is fairly far from the pond for throwing food.
- The yellow-top girl's hand on her head could mean crying or dismay at the dropped ice cream, or just a "oh no" gesture. She still holds the cone, which looks empty.
- The boy could be tugging the string to free the stuck kite, or simply staring at it.
- The white streaks behind the duck could be motion lines or a wake.
- A goat wandering loose in a public park is unusual, though not impossible.
- The swept leaves are autumn-coloured while all the trees are green.
