# pic_06

## Free description
A sunny playground with a railing fence and hedges behind, a tree on the left, and a slide, bush and litter bin on the right. On the left, a red seesaw is steeply tilted: a small girl in a pink dress is stuck high up on the raised end, holding on with her mouth open as if surprised or worried. On the low end sits an older man with a grey cap, glasses and moustache, calmly reading an open book and not noticing her, his weight keeping her up in the air. In the middle, a child with braided hair in a yellow top and orange shorts stands licking a pink ice cream cone. On the right there is a sandbox with a red bucket and blue spade, a grey rock, and an empty slide. The odd thing is that a grown man is using the seesaw as a seat to read, leaving the girl stranded at the top.
