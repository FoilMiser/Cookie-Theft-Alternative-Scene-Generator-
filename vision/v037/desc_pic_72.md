## Free description
This is a busy sunny park with a winding path, a bench, a tall lamp post and a pond. On the left, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and black trousers stands pointing up at a blue-and-white beach ball that is stuck in the branches of a tree. Behind him, a woman with a dark ponytail in a purple top and teal trousers is digging a hole with a spade, with a pile of soil beside it. Next to the lamp post, an older man with a grey cap, grey moustache and beard, white shirt and brown waistcoat is standing and eating a pink ice cream. On the right, a small girl in a pink dress holds the strings of a purple and a blue balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other towards a red balloon that has escaped and is floating away over the pond, its string dangling. At the far right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a yellow balloon next to a flower bed. At the front, a black-and-white cat is crouched low, creeping towards a red bird on the grass, and looks about to pounce on it.
