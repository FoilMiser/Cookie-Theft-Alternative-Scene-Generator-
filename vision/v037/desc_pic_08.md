# pic_08

## Free description
A sunny but windy back garden, with white wind streaks in the sky and across the fence. A house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, a gate and paved path are on the right. In the middle, a grey-haired older woman with glasses, a teal top and grey skirt has both arms raised high and her mouth open in surprise, because a blue cap has blown off and is flying through the air just above her. A few leaves are blowing along with the cap in the wind lines. Around her are a flower bed, two potted plants by the fence and a teal watering can on the grass. She seems to be trying to grab the cap before it blows away.

## Inventory
- Older woman, centre: grey hair, glasses, teal top, grey skirt, brown shoes; arms raised, surprised expression, reaching for her blown-off cap.
- Blue cap, centre in the air above-left of the woman: tumbling in the wind.
- Leaves: a few small leaves blowing near the cap.
- Wind streaks: white wavy lines in the sky (left and right) and beside the cap.
- Potted plants: two terracotta pots with green plants, centre-right by the fence.
- Watering can: teal, bottom right by the path.
- Flower bed: colourful flowers, left front.
- House with blue door and window, tree behind the house, wooden fence, gate, path, sun and two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- She could be trying to catch the cap that just blew off, or it could be read as her having thrown it up. Her surprised face suggests it blew off.
- The wind lines trail to the right of the cap, which could mean the cap is being blown to the left; direction is not certain.
- The wind is strong enough to take the cap, but the tree, plants and flowers are not bending at all.
