## Free description
It is a sunny but very windy day in a park. White wind streaks cross the sky and leaves are blowing about. A blue pond with reeds is on the left, a black lamppost stands in the middle, and a sandy path winds from the front right to the back. In front of the pond, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans has both arms raised high and a startled face, with wide eyes and an open mouth. His blue cap has just blown off and is tumbling through the air above the pond, out of his reach, with wind lines and a couple of leaves around it. The cap looks like it might land in the pond. Further back on the right, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers walks a white dog with brown spots on a red leash near the path. There is a wooden bench by the path, trees on the left and right, a flower bed and green bin at the front left, and a grey rock at the front.
