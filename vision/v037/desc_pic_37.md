## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden. A wooden fence runs across the back, with a wooden gate on the far left and a paved path leading from it toward the front. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the right, with a flower bed in front of it. A swing set stands left of centre with its single red seat hanging still and empty. On the path at the front left, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers sweeps a pile of colourful autumn leaves with a yellow broom. Strangely, a closed cardboard box is up in the sky above the fence in the middle of the picture, tilted at an angle. Nothing is holding it, nobody is throwing it, and there are no motion lines. It just seems to be floating, or perhaps falling. The man is looking toward the right and does not seem to notice it. A teal watering can sits on the grass at the front.
