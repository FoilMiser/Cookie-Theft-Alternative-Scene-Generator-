# pic_33

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, with a wooden bench in front of it and a flower bed. A wooden fence runs across the back, with a gate and paved path on the right. A clothesline in the middle holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. An orange cat walks along the top of the fence. An old man in a flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands by the flower bed holding a teal watering can. The stream of water from the can strangely goes up into the air above the flowers in a curved arc, with drops at the top, instead of pouring down onto them. On the right, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is jumping in the air with both arms raised and a big happy face. His shadow is on the ground below his feet. A grey wheelbarrow stands on the lawn at the front.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Water direction: the stream seems to go upward from the can and end in a point with drops above the flowers, which is against gravity. Another reading is that the water is spraying up and out like a fountain before falling. Either way, it does not look like normal pouring.
- The boy's jump can be read two ways: jumping for joy or cheering, or jumping up to reach for something such as the cat on the fence. His face points at the viewer, not at the cat, so the joy reading seems more likely.
- The cat on top of the fence looks deliberate (cats do walk on fences). It is not floating.
