## Free description
This is a sunny park with a pond on the left, a tall black lamp post in the middle and a winding path on the right. An old man in a flat cap stands on the grass at the front edge of the pond, fishing. He holds a long rod with both hands, and its line drops to a red-and-white float in the water, which makes a small ripple. A white duck is swimming on the pond to the left of the float. On the right, beside the path, a wooden ladder stands upright on the grass on its own. It is not leaning against anything: not the tree, not a wall, not the lamp post. A straight ladder could not do that, so it looks wrong. There is also a green rubbish bin next to the lamp post and a grey rock at the front centre. Nothing dramatic seems about to happen, though the float might be about to get a bite.
