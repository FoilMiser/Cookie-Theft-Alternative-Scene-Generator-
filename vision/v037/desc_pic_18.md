## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, and a wooden gate on the far right with a paved path leading from it. In the middle, an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and a grey skirt, is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle. A section of about three fence boards is already painted blue. A paint tin and a tray of blue paint sit on the grass at her feet. A trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the grass to the path on the right. There a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is walking away towards the gate, and its paws are blue. The dog has clearly stepped in the paint tray and is spreading blue paw prints, and it seems the woman hasn't noticed yet because she is facing the fence. There is also a flower bed and a bush by the house, a tree behind the house, and an empty wheelbarrow and a teal watering can at the front.
