## Free description
This is a park on a sunny day, with green hills and hedges in the distance, a winding sandy path on the left, and a blue pond on the right. A small girl with dark hair in a pink dress and yellow shoes stands at the left edge of the pond, holding a small paper bag and reaching out over the water. Small pale pieces of bread (or crumbs) are floating on the water in front of her, and a white duck is swimming towards them and pecking at one, with white wake lines behind it showing it is moving. So the girl appears to be feeding the duck. Around the pond are a few bulrushes and a bush; elsewhere there is a tall black street lamp, an empty wooden bench by the path, trees, a grey rock, a bed of colourful flowers and a green litter bin. Nothing looks wrong or impossible; it is a calm duck-feeding scene.
