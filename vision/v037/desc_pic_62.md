# pic_62

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house, red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a gate and stone path on the right. A woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers stands by the fence painting it blue with a roller on a long handle. A section of fence to her right is already blue, and a small paint tin sits at her feet. In the front centre, a garden sprinkler fixed to a hose shoots arcs of water straight onto a cardboard box on the lawn. The box is getting soaked, with water dripping down it and a puddle around its base. On the path at the right, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, is sweeping a pile of dirt towards a red dustpan. Strangely, she is sweeping with a giant silver fork instead of a broom, and a little dust cloud rises as she sweeps. There are also two potted plants and a flower bed. The odd things are the giant fork used as a broom and the box being drenched by the sprinkler.
