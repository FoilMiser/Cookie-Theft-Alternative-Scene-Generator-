# pic_68

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a gate with a paved path on the right. A woman or older girl with a dark ponytail, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, is standing beside a flower bed watering the flowers with a teal watering can; water is pouring out onto the flowers. In the front centre, an orange cat is curled up on the grass with its eyes closed, apparently asleep, and a red food bowl with kibble is on the grass just in front of it. Two potted plants sit near the fence on the right. Something is clearly wrong: an orange fish (like a goldfish) is sitting on a branch of the tree behind the house, as if it were a bird. A fish cannot live in a tree, so this looks impossible.
