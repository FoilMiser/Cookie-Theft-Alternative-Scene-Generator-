On the left, a grey-haired older woman in dark glasses is walking along while reading an open book, and there is a cardboard box on the ground right in front of her feet, so she looks about to trip over it. Behind her is a fruit and vegetable stall with crates of produce and no one serving. In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress is watering a planter box of flowers with a teal watering can. An older man in a cap with a moustache sits on a bench eating a pink ice cream cone, and a small white dog is standing up on its hind legs next to him, begging for the ice cream. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is taking a photo with a camera, pointed toward the man and dog. A red bird is perched on top of a street lamp. A teal bicycle is parked in a bike rack, and there is a tree, a green bin and a small café table with two chairs on the right.
