This is a playground in a park on a sunny day, with a dark metal railing fence and green bushes along the back. On the left is a red slide with a ladder and a yellow-and-orange chute, and in front of it a wooden sandpit where a small girl with dark hair in a pink outfit kneels, building a sandcastle with a little red flag on top. In the middle, a man with dark skin and short dark hair, in a green shirt and khaki trousers, stands on the grass eating a pink ice-cream cone. On the right is a red seesaw on a grey stand; nobody is on it, and it rests tilted with the left end up and the right end down on the ground. A green litter bin, two bushes, a tree on the right and a grey rock near the sandpit complete the scene. Nothing looks wrong or impossible; it is a quiet playground scene.
