## Free description

A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller; a section of fence planks behind the swing set is already blue, and an open paint tin sits on the grass by his feet. Near her are a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can. On the right is a flower bed and a bush. Up in the tree behind the fence, an orange goldfish is sitting on a branch like a bird, which is impossible.
