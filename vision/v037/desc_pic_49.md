## Free description
It is a sunny day in a playground, with a metal railing fence and hedges across the back. In the front left a child in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts holds a red and a yellow balloon. He reaches up with his other hand towards a blue balloon that is floating away near a tree, and he looks worried or upset. A blue bird sits on a branch of the tree. An old man in a grey cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat sits on a bench drinking from a red cup through a straw, with his eyes closed as if enjoying it. In the centre is a swing set with one empty red swing seat, and a green litter bin stands behind it. On the right is a red slide with a ladder. Next to it a boy in a red cap and blue top flies an orange kite high in the sky, holding the string up in one hand. In the front centre a red bird stands on the grass and an orange cat crouches right next to it, stalking it, so the cat looks about to pounce. An empty wooden sandpit and a grey rock are in the front right.
