# Description: test_6.png

## Free description

It is a sunny, windy day in a back garden. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door. A tall wooden fence runs across the back, with green trees or hedges behind it. In the middle of the lawn a man with dark skin and a beard, in a green shirt and khaki trousers, is raking a small pile of green and brown leaves. He has turned his head to the right and seems to be looking at the other man. On the right, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands on a paved path that leads to a wooden gate in the fence. His arms are thrown up and his mouth is open in surprise, because his red cap has just blown off his head. The cap is in the air just above and to the left of his raised hands, with a few leaves, and white wind lines show a gust blowing from right to left. He looks as if he is grabbing for the cap. It may blow away across the garden, perhaps towards the man raking or his leaf pile. On the clothesline behind them, a pair of blue trousers and a yellow-and-red striped towel are blowing sideways. At the front, an orange cat lies curled up asleep on the grass. It does not seem to notice anything.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The red-shirted man's raised arms could mean two things. He might be reaching up to catch his cap after the wind blew it off, which fits the wind lines and his surprised face. Or he might have tossed it up himself, cheering. The wind lines and the leaves flying with the cap make the first reading more likely.
- The centre man's expression is hard to read. He could be calmly watching the other man, or he could be mildly worried that the gust will scatter his leaf pile. His mouth looks small and slightly open.
- The cat is clearly lying with its eyes shut, but it is not certain whether it is curled up asleep or rolling on its side or back with its paws up.
- The gust looks very local. Its wind lines seem to start at the gate and only reach the cap area, yet the centre man's leaf pile is not disturbed at all. The clothes on the line do slant the same way, so the wind is broadly consistent.
- The blue trousers on the clothesline hang at a steep angle and their legs extend to the left past the left post. They look as if they pass in front of the post, which reads as wind-blown but is slightly odd.
- There is a small green oval at the foot of the birdhouse pole. It could be a grass tuft or a shadow.
- Nothing else looks floating, overlapping or impossible.
