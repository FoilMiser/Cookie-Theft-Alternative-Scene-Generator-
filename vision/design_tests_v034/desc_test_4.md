## Free description

This is a cartoon of a back garden on a grey, overcast day, with three large dark storm clouds hanging in the sky. In the centre foreground, a young woman (or older girl) with a dark ponytail, a yellow dress and red shoes is digging a small hole in the lawn with a long-handled spade. There is soil on the spade blade and a pile of dug-up earth beside the hole. Her head is turned to the right rather than looking down at her digging. Behind her, a washing line runs between two wooden T-posts, with a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes pegged out to dry. At the back right, near the wooden fence, a small girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes points up towards the sky and the dark clouds with her mouth open, as if calling out or warning someone. The obvious thing about to happen seems to be rain, which would soak the washing on the line, and the little girl looks like she is pointing this out. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door, with a colourful flower bed in front of it, and a teal watering can stands on the grass at the front right beside a paved path that leads to a closed garden gate. Nothing in the picture looks clearly impossible.
