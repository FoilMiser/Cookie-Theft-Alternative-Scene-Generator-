## Free description
This is a back garden under a grey, overcast sky with three dark storm clouds — it looks like rain is coming. A girl in a purple T-shirt and teal trousers stands in the middle of the lawn pointing up at the dark clouds with a worried face. On the right a woman in a yellow dress is sweeping a small pile of brown leaves on the path with a yellow broom, looking down at her work and not at the sky. The house with a blue door is on the left, with an empty bench in front of it and a flower bed. Two potted plants stand near the fence, and a gate is at the back right. It seems a storm is about to start and the girl is warning about it.
