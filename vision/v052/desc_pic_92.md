# pic_92

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence along the back, a gate and a paved path on the left. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence blue with a roller on a long handle; a few planks are already blue. At her feet are a paint can and a paint tray with blue paint in it. A small white dog with brown spots is trotting away toward the path on the left, and it has blue paint on its paws: a trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the grass to the dog, so it has stepped in the paint. The woman has her back to the dog and doesn't seem to notice. Also in the garden are two potted plants, a teal watering can, an empty grey wheelbarrow and a round flower bed by the house.
