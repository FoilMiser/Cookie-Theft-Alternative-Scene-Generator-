## Free description
A quiet, sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back, and a gate with a paved path on the left. The only person is a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes standing beside a round flower bed near the house, holding a teal watering can. The water coming out of the can's spout curves upward into the air, with drops at the top, rather than pouring down onto the flowers; that looks wrong or impossible. Two potted plants and a bush sit on the grass near the back, and an empty grey wheelbarrow stands at the front. There are no animals and nothing else is happening.
