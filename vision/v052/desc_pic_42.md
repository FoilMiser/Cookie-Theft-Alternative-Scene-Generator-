# pic_42

## Free description
A sunny town square in front of a row of coloured shops with striped awnings, with a fruit stall on the left, a lamppost, flower planters, a bench, a bike rack with a teal bicycle, and a tree on the right. On the left a girl in a purple top sweeps leaves with a yellow broom. Beside her a man in a red T-shirt is using a giant silver fork to push a pile of dust/dirt toward a red dustpan — he is using a fork as if it were a broom, which is very odd. In the centre an older grey-haired woman in a green top and grey skirt walks a white-and-brown spotted dog on a red lead. A small girl in pink sits on the bench drinking from a cup. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue-and-white beach ball that is stuck in the branches of the tree, looking concerned. Potted plants sit in the bottom corners.
