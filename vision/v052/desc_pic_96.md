## Free description
A sunny playground with a grass lawn, a dark metal railing fence and hedges behind it. On the right there is a red seesaw. An elderly man with grey hair, a grey cap, a brown waistcoat over a white shirt and dark trousers sits on the low left end of the seesaw, reading an open newspaper or book and not looking up. On the high right end, a small girl in a pink dress sits holding the handle, raised up in the air with her feet dangling; she has a hand near her mouth and looks worried or uneasy. Because the man stays down on his end, the girl appears stuck at the top. On the left there is an empty red-and-yellow slide, a green rubbish bin by the fence, a bush and a grey rock; a tree stands at the far right.
