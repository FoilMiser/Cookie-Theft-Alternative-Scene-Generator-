## Free description
A busy, sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back and a gate with a path on the right. An older woman with grey hair and glasses is painting the fence blue with a paint roller; part of the fence is already blue, and a paint tin and a tray of blue paint sit on the grass at her feet. A white-and-brown dog has blue paint on its paws and has left a trail of blue paw prints from the paint tray across the grass. A man in a green shirt stands in the middle with his hands raised toward a red-and-white ball in the air; a dotted line shows the ball travelling between him and a small girl in a pink dress, who seems to have thrown it (or is about to catch it). At the front, a boy in a red cap is walking while reading a book and is about to trip over a cardboard box right in front of him. On the bottom left an older man sits on a bench reading a red book, while a blue bird beside him is pecking at (stealing) his sandwich. Odd things: the wheelbarrow in the middle has a square wheel, and a woman by the gate is wearing a metal bucket on her head with her hands on her hips. Also a child in a yellow top is jumping with arms raised near the path, an orange cat sleeps on the path, and a red bird flies in the sky.
