## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, trees, a winding sand-coloured path on the left and a blue pond on the right. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands at the edge of the pond holding a long fishing rod; the line goes into the water, where a red-and-white float sits with a small ripple around it, so she is fishing and waiting for a bite. In the front, a white goat with a red collar is bending down to eat a tuft of grass; a rope runs from its collar to a short wooden stake in the ground, so it is tethered. Near the path on the left a wooden ladder stands upright in the open grass, leaning on nothing at all, which looks impossible — it should fall over. A tall black lamp post stands in the middle of the grass. A green rubbish bin is in the bottom-right corner. Nothing else seems about to happen apart from a possible fish bite.
