## Free description
This is a sunny park with a pond, lamp post, bench, winding path and a flower bed. A girl with a dark ponytail, purple T-shirt and teal trousers is walking across the grass in the front centre while eating a pink ice-cream cone. She is looking down at her ice cream rather than where she is going. A white duck is swimming on the pond behind her. In front of her, slightly to the right, is a grey rock lying on the grass. It looks as if she might trip over the rock because she isn't watching her step. A green bin stands next to the lamp post and an empty bench is on the right. Nothing looks impossible.
