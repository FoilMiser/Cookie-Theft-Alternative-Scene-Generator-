# pic_88

## Free description
A back garden under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds, so it looks like rain is coming. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans is painting the wooden fence blue with a paint roller on a handle; a few planks are already blue and an open paint can stands on the grass by his feet. In front of him, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and black trousers stands looking up and pointing his finger up toward the sky, as if warning about the dark clouds (rain would spoil the fresh paint). An orange cat is walking along the top of the fence on the right. On the left is the cream house with a blue door, a flower bed, and a coiled garden hose on a reel; a teal watering can is on the grass front-right and a paved path leads to a gate on the right. The man seems unaware of the boy or the clouds and keeps painting.
