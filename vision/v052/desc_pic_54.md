## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a wooden gate in the fence at the far left. A man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands near the fence, painting it blue with a paint roller on a long handle; three fence boards are already blue and the rest are still plain wood. A small paint tin with blue paint sits on the grass beside his feet. In the front left, a woman in a yellow dress is sweeping a small pile of brown leaves on the paved path with a yellow broom, looking down at her work. On the right is a round flower bed with colourful flowers and a green hose reel with the hose lying on the grass. A teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow stand on the lawn at the front. Nothing seems about to go wrong; it looks like an ordinary day of garden chores. I did not notice anything impossible.
