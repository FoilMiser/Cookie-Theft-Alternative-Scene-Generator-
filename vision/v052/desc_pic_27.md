## Free description
The same kind of back garden: a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right, a wooden fence, and a gate and path on the left. A woman with dark hair in a yellow dress and red shoes stands just left of the flower bed. She is watering the flowers with a garden hose, and an arc of water comes from the nozzle onto the flowers. The hose runs from her hand down across the lawn, around the front of the flower bed, to a hose reel standing next to the bench at the front right. An empty wheelbarrow is in the front centre and two potted plants stand near the fence. She is the only person there. Nothing looks impossible.
