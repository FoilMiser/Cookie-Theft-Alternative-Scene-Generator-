# pic_66

## Free description
A sunny park with a winding path, a pond, a lamp post, trees and hills. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a purple and a yellow balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other toward a blue balloon that has escaped and is floating away high in the sky, its string dangling. Near the pond a girl in a pink dress holds a piece of bread and is feeding a white duck; bread crumbs float on the water and the duck is pecking at them while swimming. In the front, a white dog with brown spots crouches playfully with its bottom up and tail wagging, next to a yellow-green tennis ball. An empty bench is beside the path, and there is a flower bed and a bin on the right.
