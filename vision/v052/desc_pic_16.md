# pic_16

## Free description
A busy town square on a sunny day, with a row of coloured shop buildings with striped awnings. On the left, by a fruit stall, a girl in a yellow top and orange shorts is rubbing her eye and crying, holding an empty ice-cream cone; her pink ice cream has fallen on the ground and a brown dog is licking it up. In the middle, a man in a green shirt is reading a red book in a sitting position, but there is no chair or bench under him — he is sitting on thin air. Behind him a woman in a purple top sits on a real bench reading a red book, while a blue bird pecks at a sandwich lying on the bench beside her. An older man in a brown waistcoat scatters seeds from a paper bag for a red bird on the ground. A beach ball is stuck in the branches of the tree on the right, and a little girl in a pink dress points/reaches up at it. An older woman with grey hair walks past carrying a yellow shopping bag, and on the far right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps leaves with a broom. In the front left there is a red bicycle whose wheels are square, which would not work.
