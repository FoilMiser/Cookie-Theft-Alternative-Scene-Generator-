## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house (red roof, blue door) on the right, a wooden fence across the back, a gate and paved path at the left, and a washing line with a yellow striped top and blue jeans in the middle. On the left, a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts holds the strings of a red and a purple balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other, looking upset, because a blue balloon has escaped and is floating away into the sky with its string dangling. In the centre-right, an old grey-haired woman with glasses, teal top and grey skirt is watering a flower bed with a watering can. Oddly, the water does not pour down from the spout: it shoots upward in an arc from the can, with drops flying above the flowers, which is not how a watering can works. On the far right, a woman in a yellow dress sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands at the front.
