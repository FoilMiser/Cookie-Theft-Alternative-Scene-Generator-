# pic_03

## Free description
A sunny playground with a metal railing fence at the back, a slide on the left, a sandpit at the front left and a seesaw on the right. An older man in a grey cap and brown vest sits on the low end of the red seesaw, reading a newspaper, not paying attention. A small girl in a pink dress sits on the raised high end, holding the handle with one hand and holding her other hand to her mouth, looking surprised or worried. Because the man is heavy and sits still, she is stuck up in the air. In the middle at the back, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands by the fence eating a pink ice cream. A red bucket and blue spade lie in the empty sandpit, and a grey rock sits nearby. Nobody is using the slide.
