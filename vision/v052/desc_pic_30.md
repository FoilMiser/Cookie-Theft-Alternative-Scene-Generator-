# pic_30

## Free description
A sunny town street with a row of colourful shops behind a wide paved square. On the left, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands with his hands on his hips wearing a metal bucket upside down on his head, in front of a fruit and vegetable stall. Next to him a brown dog in glasses sits up on its haunches reading a newspaper, like a person. In the middle, a grey-haired old woman waters a flower box with a watering can, and the water is landing on the flowers. Behind her, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and rubs his eye as if crying; his pink ice cream lies splattered on the pavement and a white spotted dog is licking it up. On the right, a blue-and-white beach ball is stuck in the branches of a tree, and a little girl in a pink dress points up at it while a woman in a purple top sweeps a pile of leaves with a broom. In front, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie scatters seeds from a paper bag and a blue bird pecks at them; at bottom left a red bird eats seeds while a grey cat creeps towards it, as if about to pounce. Odd things: the man with a bucket on his head and the dog reading a newspaper.
