# pic_40

## Free description
A back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a blue door on the right. In the middle a young man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is digging with a spade, piling brown earth onto a small heap beside him. In front of him, right of centre, an older grey-haired man in a cap and brown waistcoat sits drinking through a straw from a red cup — but he is sitting on nothing; there is no chair or bench under him. On the left there is a swing frame with one empty red swing. In the foreground a lawn sprinkler, attached to a hose that runs off to the right, is spraying arcs of water to the left, and the water is landing on a cardboard box, which is getting wet and sits in a puddle. There is a flower bed with colourful flowers next to the house. The cardboard box getting soaked seems like something going wrong that nobody has noticed.
