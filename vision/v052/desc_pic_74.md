# pic_74

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress is hanging washing on a clothesline between two posts; blue jeans, a striped towel and a white T-shirt are pegged up, and the T-shirt is dripping water. A laundry basket full of clothes sits on the grass below the line. At the front left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds a grey watering can over a flower bed, but the water comes out of the spout and arcs upward into the air instead of pouring down onto the flowers, which looks impossible. On the path at the right, a small girl in a pink dress holds a string reel and is flying an orange kite high in the sky. An empty wheelbarrow stands on the grass at the front and two potted plants sit near the fence.
