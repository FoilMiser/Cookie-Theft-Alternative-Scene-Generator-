## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a house with a blue door on the right and a tree behind the fence. A man in a green shirt and beige trousers stands in the middle holding the end of a green garden hose up near his face, looking at the nozzle as if puzzled — no water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel by the house, across the lawn, and a white dog with brown spots is standing right on the hose, where it looks pinched or kinked under its paws. So the dog seems to be stopping the water, and it may spray out (perhaps into the man's face) when the dog steps off. On the left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping a small pile of dead leaves on the paved path with a broom. A clothesline between two posts holds a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans. A flower bed with colourful flowers sits in front of the house, and an empty grey wheelbarrow stands in the front centre.
