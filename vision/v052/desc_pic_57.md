# pic_57

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house (red roof, blue door) on the left, a wooden fence across the back with a gate at the right, and a paved path at the right. An old grey-haired woman with glasses, a teal top and grey skirt stands in the front-centre watering a flower bed with a hose; a stream of water arcs from the nozzle onto the flowers. The hose runs back to a hose reel on the left. Further back by the fence, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts holds up a kite handle, flying an orange diamond kite with a bow tail high in the sky. Very oddly, an orange fish is sitting on a branch of the tree behind the house, which is impossible. There are two potted plants and a bush near the back right, and an empty grey wheelbarrow on the lawn at the front. Apart from the fish, it is a calm garden scene.
