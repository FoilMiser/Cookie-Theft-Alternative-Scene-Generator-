# pic_24

## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, a pond on the left, a lamp post in the middle and a winding sandy path on the right. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt stands at the edge of the pond. She is holding a paper bag and tossing bread to a white duck. The duck swims towards her and pecks at bread pieces floating on the water. In the background, a woman in a yellow dress runs to the right across the grass, with speed lines behind her. In the front, a white goat with a red collar is tied by a rope to a small wooden stake and is grazing on a tuft of grass. A wooden bench stands empty beside the path. A flower bed and a green bin are in the front left. Nothing looks impossible.
