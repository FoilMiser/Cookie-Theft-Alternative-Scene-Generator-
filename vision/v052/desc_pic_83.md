# pic_83

## Free description
A sunny park with hills, trees, bushes, a tall black lamp post, a winding sandy path on the right, a grey rock and a green rubbish bin in the front. A woman with a dark ponytail, a yellow dress and red shoes stands at the right edge of a blue pond, holding a brown paper bag in one hand and a piece of bread in the other. She leans toward the water and is feeding a white duck. The duck is swimming toward her, with small ripple lines behind it, and is pecking at bread crumbs floating on the water. Several small crumbs are scattered on the pond near the duck. Reeds grow at both ends of the pond. Nothing looks impossible; it is a calm duck-feeding scene.
