## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills, a pond, a winding sandy path, a wooden bench, a tall black lamp post and a flower bed. A man in a green shirt and beige trousers stands just to the left of the lamp post, behind the pond's right edge, eating a pink ice-cream cone. In the front centre a white goat with a red collar is bent down grazing on a small tuft of grass. The goat is tied by a rope to a short wooden stake in the ground. A green bin stands in the bottom-left corner next to a bed of colourful flowers. Nobody else is in the park. Nothing obviously dramatic is happening; the goat is tethered and eating, the man is enjoying his ice cream. A goat in a park is a bit unusual, but it is tied up, so it looks deliberate.
