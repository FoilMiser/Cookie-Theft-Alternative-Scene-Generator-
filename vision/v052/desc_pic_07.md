## Free description
A sunny playground with a railing fence at the back, a swing set in the middle, a slide on the right and a sandpit at the front right. On the left, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers sits on the low end of a red seesaw, reading an open book. A small girl in a pink dress sits on the raised high end, holding the handle with one hand and her other hand at her mouth, looking surprised or worried, stuck up in the air. In the front centre, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands eating a pink ice-cream cone. The swing is empty, nobody is on the slide, and the sandpit has a red bucket and blue spade in it but no children. A grey rock lies on the grass at the front right and a green bin stands by the fence.
