## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence with a gate at the back left, and a paved path on the left. A girl in a pink dress is jumping in the air with both arms raised and a happy face; there is a shadow under her, showing she is off the ground. A man in a green shirt and beige trousers kneels by the flowerbed, digging with a small trowel to plant a pink flower that is still in a pot beside him. By the blue door, a brown dog sits holding a yellow lead in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. A garden hose on a reel lies on the grass in front, and a teal watering can is at the front left. Two potted plants stand near the fence.
