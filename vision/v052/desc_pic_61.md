## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house (red roof, blue door) on the right, a fence along the back, and a gate and paved path at the left. In the centre, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller; three boards are already blue. On the ground behind him is a paint tin and a flat paint tray with blue paint in it. A white dog with brown spots has walked through the paint tray: a trail of blue paw prints runs from the tray across the grass to the dog, whose paws are blue, and it is heading toward the path. The man has his back to it and hasn't noticed. At the far left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping leaves on the path near the gate. Two potted plants, a flower bed, a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow are around the lawn. It looks like the dog is about to track blue paint onto the path (and maybe the woman's swept area).
