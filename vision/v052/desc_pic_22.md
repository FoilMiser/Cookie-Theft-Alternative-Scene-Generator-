## Free description
A sunny playground with a dark railing fence across the back, a red-and-yellow slide on the left, a sandbox in the front left and a big tree on the right. In the middle, a small girl in a pink dress holds the strings of a purple and a red balloon in one hand. She reaches up with her other hand, looking upset, towards a yellow balloon that has floated higher than the others. Its string hangs loose and nobody is holding it, so it seems to have got away and is drifting up. On the right, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is kicking an orange ball, which is just leaving his foot. A small brown dog lies on the grass between the slide and the girl, apparently resting or asleep. A red bucket and a blue spade sit in the sandbox, and nobody is using it. Nothing looks impossible. The main event is the girl losing her yellow balloon.
