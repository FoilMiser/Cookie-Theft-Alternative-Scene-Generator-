# pic_62

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a tree behind the fence. On the far left a man in a green shirt is walking in through an open gate while reading an open book or newspaper; a cardboard box sits on the ground just in front of him, so he looks about to trip over it. In the front left a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red lead along the path. In the middle an old woman with grey hair and glasses holds a garden hose nozzle up and looks at it, but no water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel by the house across the lawn, and a brown dog is standing right on the hose, which seems to be why the water is blocked. At the back, by the fence, a girl in a pink dress stretches her arm up toward a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of the tree, which is far out of her reach. On the right a boy in a red shirt sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. There is a flower bed with colourful flowers in front of the house.
