# pic_48

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a house with a blue door on the right, a gate on the left and a paved path. A man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is painting the fence blue with a long-handled paint roller; a few fence panels are already blue. On the grass by his feet are a paint tin and a paint tray with blue paint. A white dog with brown spots has walked away from the paint tray and left a trail of blue paw prints across the grass — it has stepped in the paint. On the left, a man in a green T-shirt walks along the path carrying a cardboard box. Very strangely, an orange fish is sitting in the tree behind the fence, which is impossible. There is also a wheelbarrow, a flower bed and a hose reel.
