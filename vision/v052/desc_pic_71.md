## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the left, and a paved path running from the gate down the left side. In the middle front, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and black trousers is jumping in the air with both arms raised, smiling; his shadow is on the grass below him, so his feet are off the ground. Behind him a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is watering a flower bed with a garden hose; water arcs from the hose nozzle onto the flowers, and the hose runs back to a hose reel near the house. On the left there is a metal swing set with one empty red swing. Something odd: an orange fish is sitting in the leaves of the tree behind the house, as if it were a bird in the tree. That fish in the tree looks wrong or impossible.
