# pic_49

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a house on the left, a wooden fence, a gate and a paved path on the right. A boy in a red cap and blue top stands near the fence flying a yellow kite high in the sky, holding the string reel up. An older man in a grey cap and brown vest holds a garden hose and looks at the nozzle, puzzled, because no water is coming out. A brown dog is standing on the hose, which seems to be squashing it and stopping the water. On the right an older woman with grey hair and glasses is sweeping dirt on the path towards a red dustpan — but she is using a giant silver fork instead of a broom, which is very odd. There are flowers, two potted plants and a wheelbarrow too.
