# pic_64

## Free description
A sunny park with a winding path on the left, a pond on the right, a black lamp post, trees and hills. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a wooden bench reading a red book, not looking at what is beside her. Next to her on the bench lies a sandwich, and a blue bird has landed on the bench right beside it and is pecking at it, apparently about to steal it. In the middle front, a girl in a pink dress walks along licking a pink ice cream cone. The rest of the park is empty: a flower bed at the right, a grey rock at the front, and a green bin in the bottom right corner.
