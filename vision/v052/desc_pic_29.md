## Free description
A back garden on a sunny but windy day, with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a wooden fence, a gate at the back right and a paved path. White wind lines streak across the sky, and leaves are blowing off the tree near the house. At the back right, an elderly woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt reaches up with an open mouth as her orange hat is blown off her head and away over the fence. On the left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie kneels by the flower bed with a small trowel, digging or planting next to a pot with a pink plant. In the middle, a girl in pink sits on the swing, laughing, swinging with motion lines behind her. A grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can stand in the front. The hat flying away is the surprising event.
