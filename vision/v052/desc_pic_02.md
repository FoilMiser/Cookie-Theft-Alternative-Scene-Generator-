# pic_02

## Free description
A busy sunny park with a pond, a winding path, a lamp post, a bench and hills behind. In the front left, an older man in a cap and brown vest is watering a flowerbed by pouring water out of a frying pan instead of a watering can, which looks wrong. Beside the flowerbed, a brown dog wearing glasses sits up and reads a newspaper, which is impossible. On the pond a white duck swims with little pieces of bread floating near it, and a girl in a pink dress at the pond edge holds a slice of bread, so she seems to be feeding the duck. In the front centre, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and rubs their eyes, crying; the pink ice cream has fallen on the ground and a white spotted dog is licking it up. Further back, a grey-haired woman in a teal cardigan sweeps a pile of leaves with a broom, and a white goat tied to a stake is grazing nearby. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue jacket holds a kite string, but his purple kite is caught in the top of a tree. A blue bird flies across the sky.
