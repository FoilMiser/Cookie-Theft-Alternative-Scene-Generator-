# pic_23

## Free description
A back garden on a sunny day, with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left and a tall wooden fence along the back. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands at the fence painting it blue with a paint roller on a long handle. A section of a few boards to her left is already blue, and the rest of the fence is still natural wood. A paint tin with blue paint stands by her feet. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps a pile of dry leaves on the paved path with a broom. In the middle there is an empty swing set with one red seat hanging still. Around the garden there is a flower bed, a coiled hose on a reel, a wheelbarrow, a watering can, a birdhouse on a tall pole and a wooden gate at the back right. Nothing looks strange; it is a scene of garden chores.
