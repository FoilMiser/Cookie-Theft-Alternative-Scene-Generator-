On the bottom left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sits on a wooden bench, calmly drinking from a red cup through a straw. A blue bird sits on top of the fence in the middle, and a small birdhouse on a tall pole stands near the gate on the right. A hose reel with a hose lies on the grass next to the bench, and there is a flower bed in front of the house. Nothing looks impossible; the wind is the main event.
