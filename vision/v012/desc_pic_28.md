# pic_28

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny town square or pedestrian street in front of a row of colourful shops with striped awnings. On the left, by a fruit and vegetable stall, a gust of wind is blowing an orange cap into the air along with some leaves, and a young man in a blue hoodie throws his arms up with his mouth open in surprise. Next to him, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is walking along reading an open book, and he is about to walk into or trip over a cardboard box on the ground right in front of him. In the middle, a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts holds a yellow and a red balloon while reaching up, looking upset, as a purple balloon floats away above him. On the ground in the middle, a black and white cat is creeping toward a red bird, as if about to pounce on it. On the right, an old woman with grey hair and glasses is sweeping dirt into a red dustpan, but she is using a giant fork instead of a broom, which is impossible or silly. At the far right, an old man in a cap and waistcoat is happily eating a pink ice cream cone. An orange cat is lying on the ground on the left and a white dog with brown spots is lying stretched out at the front, both seemingly asleep. There is also a street lamp, a bench, a flower planter, a bin, a tree in a round planter and two potted plants in the front corners.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The giant fork used as a broom is clearly impossible or silly; it is the size of a broom but shaped like a dinner fork.
- The cap: it seems to belong to the young man in the blue hoodie (his hair is bare and he is reaching up), but it could belong to the reading man.
- The cat and bird: the cat looks like it is stalking the bird, but it could just be walking past.
- The orange cat and the spotted dog lying flat are most likely sleeping, but could also be read as lying collapsed.
- The reading man may or may not actually trip; the box is right in front of his foot.
- Only the left side of the picture has wind lines; nothing else (the balloons, the tree, the awnings) seems affected by the wind, which is a little inconsistent.
