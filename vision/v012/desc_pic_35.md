# Picture 35

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a stone path running from the gate down the right side. In the bottom left, a person with brown hair, a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes is sitting on a wooden bench, drinking from a white cup or carton and looking to the right. In the middle of the lawn on the right, a man with dark skin, a short beard, a green T-shirt and khaki trousers is raking a small pile of green and brown leaves with a long rake. At the bottom centre, an orange cat is lying on the grass with its eyes closed, apparently asleep. There is a colourful flower bed in front of the house, two potted plants near the fence on the right, a teal watering can beside the path, a bush next to the house and a tree behind it. The sky is blue with two white clouds and a sun in the top right. It is a calm scene and nothing seems about to happen, other than the man continuing to tidy up the leaves.
