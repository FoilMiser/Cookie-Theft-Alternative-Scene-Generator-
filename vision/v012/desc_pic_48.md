This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden picket fence across the back, a house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a paved path running from a wooden side gate at the left down to the bottom-left corner. In the centre front, a girl in a yellow dress and red shoes stands with her hands on her hips, wearing an upside-down blue bucket on her head like a hat. At the front left, a girl with a brown ponytail, blue hoodie and dark trousers is on the path, kicking (or just having kicked) an orange ball, with motion lines by her feet. A flower bed with red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers is in front of the house, and a tree and bush are behind the fence; the sun and two clouds are in the sky. The painter has his back to the dog and doesn't seem to have noticed the paint being tracked across the lawn.
