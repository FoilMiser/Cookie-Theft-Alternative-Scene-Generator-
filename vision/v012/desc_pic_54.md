# Picture 54

## Free description
This is a town shopping street on a grey, overcast day, with dark rain clouds above a row of four colourful shops with striped awnings. An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses stands in front of the shops pointing up at the clouds, as if saying it is going to rain. In the middle, an old man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat sits on a bench licking a pink ice-cream cone, while a brown dog with a red collar stretches up towards him, clearly wanting the ice cream. On the left, a bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers walks along carrying a yellow shopping bag. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is pushing a teal pram with a baby inside, next to a fruit stall with crates of red, orange, yellow and green fruit under a striped canopy. A little girl in a pink dress walks in the front holding a yellow balloon. In the front centre there is a red bicycle standing on its own, and its wheels are square, which is impossible; it could never roll. There are also a tree in a round planter, bins, potted plants, a flower box and a lamppost on the pavement.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The bicycle's square wheels are impossible; also it stands upright on its own with no stand visible.
- The elderly woman could be pointing at the clouds or at something in an upstairs window; she is looking up towards the sky, so the clouds seem most likely.
- The dog's pose is very stretched out and long, with front paws up and back legs extended far behind; it could be read as jumping up at the man or as stretching/playing.
- The old man could be licking the ice cream or holding it out of the dog's reach; he does not seem to be looking at the dog.
- The pram's hood is drawn at the back and the baby at the front, so it is a little hard to tell which end the woman is pushing from, but her hands are on the handle and she is walking right.
- The fruit stall appears unattended; nobody is drawn behind it.
