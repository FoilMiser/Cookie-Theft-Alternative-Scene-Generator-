# Picture 10

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence and gate at the back right, and a paved path running from the gate to the bottom right. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top, grey skirt and brown shoes, stands next to the flower bed by the house. She is holding a dark frying pan out over the flowers, and a stream of water is arcing out of the pan onto the flowers, as if she is using it as a watering can. This is odd, because a real teal watering can sits unused on the grass at the front right. There is an empty wheelbarrow at the front centre and two potted plants on the grass near the path. Nobody else is in the picture.
