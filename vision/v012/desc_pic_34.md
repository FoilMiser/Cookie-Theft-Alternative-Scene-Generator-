# Picture 34

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the left, and a stone path down the left side. At the back, between two wooden posts, there is a washing line with a yellow-and-red striped towel or shirt and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. Under the washing line, a woman or girl with a dark bob, a yellow dress and red shoes is digging a hole with a shovel, with a small pile of dirt beside it. In the centre-right, a woman with a long dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is watering the flower bed with a teal watering can, but the water does something impossible: instead of pouring down, it shoots upward in an arc into the air with drops at the top, like a fountain. On the right, a boy with curly dark hair, a yellow top and orange shorts is sitting on a wooden bench happily eating a pink ice cream cone, and a white dog with a brown spot and a blue collar is sitting up on its back legs beside him, begging for the ice cream. On the grass at the bottom there is a red dog bowl full of food that the dog is ignoring. There are also two potted plants near the fence on the left, a tree behind the house, and a blue sky with two clouds and a sun in the top left.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water from the watering can goes upward instead of down, which looks impossible. Another reading is that it is a separate jet or sprinkler coming up out of the flower bed, but it seems to come from the can.
- The person digging could be a woman or an older girl; size and dress do not settle it.
- The striped yellow item on the line could be a towel or a shirt.
- It is unclear why someone is digging a hole under the washing line; it could just be gardening.
- The dog begging for ice cream while its own food bowl sits full nearby may be intended as a small joke, or may just be scenery.
