## Free description
This is a sunny back garden. A wooden fence runs across the back, with a gate on the left, and a house with a red roof and a blue door stands on the right. In the middle at the back, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is reaching up to a washing line, pegging up laundry: a white T-shirt, a yellow towel with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans. At the front right, a boy in a red shirt and blue jeans is watering a round bed of colourful flowers with a teal watering can. The water looks wrong: instead of pouring down onto the flowers, it shoots upward from the spout in an arc, with drops flying up above it, like a fountain. A brown and white dog sits in front of the blue door with a red lead in its mouth, as if it wants to be taken for a walk. On the left there is a swing set with one empty red swing, and a paved path runs from the gate down to the front. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the front, and a wooden bench stands at the right near the house. There is a birdhouse on a pole behind the fence, a big tree behind the house, and a sun and two clouds in the sky.
