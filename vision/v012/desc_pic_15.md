# Picture 15

## Free description
This is a sunny playground in a park, with a bright sun in the top right, two small white clouds, a wooden-railed fence across the middle and a line of green bushes behind it. On the left, a big seesaw is tipped steeply. A small girl in a pink dress sits on the high end, up in the air, holding the handle with her legs dangling. A bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers sits on the low end, which is down on the ground. He is reading an open book and not looking at the girl, so she seems to be stuck up in the air while he reads. In the middle there is a metal swing frame with one empty red swing seat. On the right, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is sliding down a yellow and orange slide with a red frame, a blue platform and a ladder, with one hand up at their head. There is also a green rubbish bin by the fence, a tree on the far left, a bush on the far right edge and a grey rock in the bottom right. Nothing looks impossible. The main odd thing is the grown man sitting on the seesaw reading while the little girl is left up in the air.
