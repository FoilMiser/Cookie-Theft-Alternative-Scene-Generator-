# Picture 08

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden. A wooden fence runs across the back, a cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the right, and a paved path runs from a gate at the back left down to the front left corner. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans walks down the path with a white, spotted dog on a red lead. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands by the gate with her hands on her hips, and she seems to be wearing a blue bucket upside down on her head. In the middle, a girl or woman in a blue top with a ponytail walks along reading an open book. A blue bucket sits on the grass just ahead of her, so she might walk into it. A man in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest stands between the washing-line poles and raises one hand, with motion lines, as if waving. Further back, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts points up at a blue ball caught in the tree behind the fence. A black cat creeps low across the grass toward a red bird that is pecking at the ground; it looks like the cat is about to pounce. An old man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat sits on a bench at the front right reading a red book with his mouth open. The wheelbarrow at the front has a square wheel, which could not roll.
