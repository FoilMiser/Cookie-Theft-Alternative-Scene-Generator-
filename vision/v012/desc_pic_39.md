# Picture 39

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall brown wooden picket fence across the back, a wooden gate at the far left, a paved path at the bottom-left, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a flower bed in front of the house. A woman with short black hair in a yellow dress is painting the fence blue with a long-handled brush; she has finished a section about three boards wide, top to bottom. On the grass behind her is a paint tin and a shallow tray of blue paint. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has evidently stepped in the paint tray: a trail of blue paw prints runs from the tray across the lawn to the dog, and the dog's paws are blue. The dog is walking away to the left toward the path, and the woman, facing the fence, does not seem to have noticed. A red dog bowl full of food sits at the bottom of the picture. Nothing looks impossible; the problem is the dog tracking blue paint across the garden.
