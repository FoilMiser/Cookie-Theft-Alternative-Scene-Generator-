On the right, a boy in a red shirt and blue jeans is standing by a winding path and scattering seeds from a cup or bag in his hand; the seeds fall in a trail onto the path. Two small birds, one red and one blue, are pecking at the seeds on the path. The bench is empty. Nothing looks dangerous; the only slightly odd thing is a goat wandering loose in what looks like an ordinary town park.
