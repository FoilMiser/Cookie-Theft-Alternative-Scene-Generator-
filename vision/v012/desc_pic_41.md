# Picture 41

## Free description
This is a sunny playground in a park, with a dark railing fence and a line of trees across the back, a big tree on the left, a swing set in the middle, and a red-framed slide with a ladder on the right. On a seesaw at the left, a little girl in a pink dress is stuck up high on the raised end, while an older man with a grey beard and cap sits on the low end calmly reading a book, so the seesaw cannot move. Behind them, a boy in a blue hoodie sits on a park bench drinking from a cup, and an older woman with grey hair walks past carrying a cardboard box. On the right, a small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is crying, rubbing his eye and holding an empty ice-cream cone; his pink ice-cream scoop has fallen onto the grass and lies there in a melting puddle. A brown dog with a red collar has its nose right at the fallen ice cream and appears to be about to eat or lick it. A blue bird is flying in the sky. The swing is empty, and there is a green rubbish bin by the fence and a grey rock in the bottom-right corner. Nothing is physically impossible, but the dropped ice cream and the adult using the seesaw as a reading seat while the girl is stuck in the air are the obvious problems.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The older man could be deliberately sitting on the seesaw to read (ignoring the girl), or simply using it as a seat without noticing her; either way the girl is left up in the air.
- The dog could be eating/licking the ice cream or just sniffing it.
- It is not certain the dropped ice cream belongs to the crying boy, but the empty cone in his hand strongly suggests it does.
- The older woman is very close to the bench; she appears to be walking behind or level with it rather than through it.
- The girl looks small and a bit perched on the very end of the seesaw, but she does not seem to be floating.
