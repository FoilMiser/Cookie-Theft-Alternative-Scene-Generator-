# Picture 20

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a sun in the top left and two clouds. A cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the right, and a wooden fence with a gate on the left runs along the back. On the paved path at the left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping dirt into a red dustpan. She is using a giant silver dinner fork instead of a broom, which is very odd. In the middle, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers holds the end of a garden hose up near his face. Only a single drop of water is coming out, because a brown dog in a red collar is standing on the hose further along. If the dog steps off, the man looks likely to get sprayed. In the background by the fence, an older woman with grey hair and glasses throws her arms up in surprise as the wind blows her orange hat off her head, with wind lines and leaves blowing past. There are two potted plants on the lawn, a colourful flower bed with a hose reel beside it on the right, and a red dog-food bowl with kibble at the bottom centre.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The man's nozzle seems to point back up toward his face, as if he is peering into it to see why it isn't working. It could also just be held up in front of him pointing sideways. Either way only one drop comes out.
- The dog standing on the hose may be what stops the water (the usual reading), or the hose may just be lying under the dog by chance.
- The fork is far too big to be a real dinner fork. It looks deliberately silly rather than like a garden fork or rake, but it could be read as an odd-looking rake.
- The wind is strong enough to blow the hat off, but nothing else is affected (trees, flowers and the dust by the sweeper stay still). That may be an unintended inconsistency.
- The older woman's raised arms could mean she is grabbing for the hat or just throwing her hands up in surprise.
