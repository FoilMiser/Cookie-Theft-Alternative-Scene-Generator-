# Picture 33

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a stone path running from the gate down the right side. In the centre, an older man wearing a grey flat cap, a white shirt, a brown vest and dark trousers is digging a hole in the lawn with a shovel; there is a lump of soil on the shovel blade and a pile of dug-up dirt next to the hole. In the bottom left corner, a girl in a pink dress is sitting on a wooden bench eating a pink ice cream cone. A brown dog with a red bandana or collar is next to the bench, stretched up with its head raised toward the girl, as if begging for her ice cream. Something is clearly wrong in the tree behind the house: an orange fish is sitting in the leaves, which is impossible. There is also a colourful flower bed in front of the house, two potted plants near the fence on the right, a teal watering can by the path, and bushes beside the house and peeking up behind the lawn near the fence. The sky is blue with two clouds and a sun in the top right.
