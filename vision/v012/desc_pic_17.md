# Picture 17

## Free description
This is a calm, sunny park with rolling green hills and bushes in the background, a sun in the top right and two clouds. There is a round blue pond on the left-centre with reeds (cattails) at both ends. An older man in a grey cap, white shirt, brown vest and dark trousers stands on the grass at the right edge of the pond, fishing with a rod. His line goes down to a red-and-white float in the water, with ripples around it. In front of the pond, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers sits leaning back on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket with a wicker picnic basket. His eyes are closed as if he is dozing or enjoying the sun. A tall black lamppost stands just behind the fisherman. To the right, a wooden ladder stands upright on the grass at an angle, leaning against nothing, which could not really happen. A sandy path winds from the bottom right back toward the hills, past a large tree on the right. There is also a grey rock at the bottom centre, a green rubbish bin cut off in the bottom left corner, a small tree and bush on the left, and a bush on the right.
