A bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers walks toward the left. He holds a red lead attached to a small white dog with a few dark spots and a blue collar, which trots ahead of him toward the gate and path. The goat appears to be standing on or right next to the hose. In front of the walking man is a grey wheelbarrow, and its wheel is drawn as a square, which is impossible for a working wheel. A red dog bowl with food sits on the grass in the front. A clothesline behind holds a yellow-and-red striped item and a pair of blue jeans. A goat in a back garden is also rather unusual.
