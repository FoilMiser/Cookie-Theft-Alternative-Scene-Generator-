# Picture 52

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park with green hills, trees, bushes, a pond with bulrushes, a tall black lamppost and a winding sandy path. An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, stands at the edge of the pond throwing crumbs or bread to a white duck. The duck is swimming towards the crumbs, which are flying through the air and floating on the water. In the front, a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket next to a picnic basket, leaning back with his eyes closed and a contented smile. To the left of the picnic, a wooden ladder is standing up on the grass all by itself, tilted slightly, with nothing to lean against; that looks impossible. There is also a grey rock in the foreground and a green rubbish bin cut off at the bottom-right corner. Nothing else looks like it is about to happen, except perhaps the ladder toppling over.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The ladder: it stands on its own in the open with nothing behind it to lean on, which is physically impossible. One reading is that it is a deliberate oddity; another is that it is simply drawn badly and meant to be leaning on something not shown. Either way, there is nobody near it and no reason for it to be there.
- The boy's closed eyes could mean he is resting/sunbathing, dozing, or just smiling.
- The thing in the woman's hand could be a paper bag, a cup, or a slice of bread.
- The boy's legs extend off the blanket onto the grass; he sits right at the edge of the blanket.
