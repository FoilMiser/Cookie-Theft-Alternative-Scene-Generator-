# Picture 04

## Free description
This is a sunny park scene with the sun in the top-right, two white clouds, green hills and trees in the background. In the middle, an elderly man with a grey beard and a flat cap, wearing a white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers, is standing at the edge of a pond fishing with a long rod. His line goes down into the water and ends at a red-and-white float that is right next to a white duck swimming in the pond, so it looks as though he might hook the duck or the duck might go for the bait. A blue bird is perched on top of the black lamp post next to him, and there is a green rubbish bin beside the lamp post. On the right, a woman with dark hair in a ponytail, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, is walking along a winding path while reading an open book held out in front of her, not looking where she is going. A blue bucket is standing on the edge of the path just ahead of her, so she looks about to trip over it. There is also an impossible detail: an orange fish is in the leaves of the big tree on the right. Other things are a flower bed, a grey rock, a second rubbish bin cut off at the bottom-left, reeds at the ends of the pond, a small tree on the left and some bushes.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fish in the tree is clearly out of place; it could be read as a real fish (impossible) or as some kind of decoration, but nothing is holding it up.
- The float next to the duck: either the man is about to catch the duck by mistake, or the duck is just swimming near the line; nothing is hooked yet.
- The bucket on the path might belong to the fisherman (a bait/fish bucket) even though it is far from him, or it may just have been left there; either way it is in the reading woman's way.
- Whether the woman will actually trip is a guess; she is heading toward the bucket and is looking at her book.
- The fishing line looks very long and thin and comes down from high up; it is slightly odd but plausible.
- The bird is sitting right on the very top of the lamp, which is normal for a bird.
