# Picture 02

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden, with the sun in the top-left, two white clouds, and a tall wooden fence across the back with a wooden gate in the left corner and green hedges and a tree showing over the top. On the right is a cream-coloured house with a red roof, a window and a blue door with a step. In the middle of the lawn a girl or young woman with brown hair in a ponytail, wearing a blue long-sleeved top, dark trousers and white shoes, is standing holding the end of a garden hose up close to her face with both hands and looking straight into the nozzle. The hose runs from her hands down to the grass, across the lawn, and back to a teal hose reel next to the house. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is standing on the hose, which might be why no water seems to be coming out; it looks as if, when the dog steps off, the water may spray her in the face. Behind her is a clothesline between two wooden posts with a yellow-and-red striped towel or top and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. There are two potted plants on the left of the lawn, a paved path leading from the gate down to the bottom-left, a flower bed with colourful flowers in front of the house, and a red dog bowl with food in it at the bottom of the picture.
