It is a sunny day in a back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, a washing line in the middle, and a gate with a paved path at the right. A smiling boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on a red swing on a metal swing set at the right, with motion lines showing he is swinging. At the back right, by the gate, a girl or woman in a yellow dress and red shoes sweeps the path with a broom. In the middle of the lawn a garden hose runs in from the left edge to a sprinkler, which is spraying three arcs of water to the right. One of the arcs lands on a cardboard box sitting on the grass, and water drops are splashing on top of the box, so the box is getting wet instead of the flowers or lawn. The flower bed by the house is not being watered. A teal watering can and an empty wheelbarrow sit on the grass at the front, and the washing line holds blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped top.
