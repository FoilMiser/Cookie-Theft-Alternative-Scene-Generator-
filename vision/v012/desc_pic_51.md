## Free description
This is a back garden on a bright, sunny day with a few white clouds. A tall wooden fence with a gate on the left runs across the back, with trees behind it, and there is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the right. On the left there is a grey metal swing set, and a little girl in a pink dress is sitting on the red swing seat, holding the ropes and swinging; she has her eyes closed and is smiling happily, and small motion lines show the swing is moving. In the middle there is a washing line between two wooden posts with a yellow-and-red striped shirt and a pair of blue jeans pegged out to dry. In the front there is a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles, both sitting on the grass with nobody using them. A flower bed of colourful flowers is on the lawn near the house, and a paved path runs from the gate down the left side. The girl is the only person in the picture, and nothing looks wrong or about to go wrong.
