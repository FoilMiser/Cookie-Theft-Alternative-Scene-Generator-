# pic_25

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a house with a red roof and blue door on the left and a wooden fence with a gate on the right. A girl in a pink dress stands in the middle front holding a kite string with both hands raised and looking up. Her purple kite with a bow-tied tail is up in the tree behind the house, with bits of leaves drawn over it, so it looks stuck in the branches; the string hangs down in a slack curve to her. To the right, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is reading a red book while sitting in mid-air: he is in a seated position, but there is no chair, bench or anything under him, which looks impossible. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is lying stretched out on the grass at the front left, apparently asleep. There is a round flower bed with colourful flowers by the house, two potted plants near the man, an empty grey wheelbarrow at the front, and a teal watering can on the grass at the front right. A paved path runs from the gate down the right side. The sun is top right, with two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The kite could be stuck in the tree (leaves drawn over it, string sagging) or flying just in front of the tree with leaves overlapping it. I read it as stuck.
- The man is sitting on nothing. He might be meant to be on an invisible or missing chair; to me it looks like he is floating in a sitting position. His shadow is on the grass below him.
- The dog is lying in a sprawled position; most likely sleeping, but it could also be read as lying collapsed.
