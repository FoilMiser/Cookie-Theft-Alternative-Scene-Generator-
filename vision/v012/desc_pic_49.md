# Picture 49

## Free description
This is a back garden on a grey, overcast day, with three dark storm clouds overhead. There is a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate on the left, trees and bushes behind it, and a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the right. In the middle of the lawn a boy or young man in a red shirt and blue jeans is looking up with wide eyes and pointing at the dark clouds, as if he has noticed that rain is coming. Next to him a girl or young woman in a purple top and teal trousers is raking up a small pile of leaves. On the left, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses is on the paved path, sweeping dirt into a red dustpan. The strange part is what she is sweeping with: it is not a broom but a giant silver fork, about as tall as she is, and it is raising a little cloud of dust. Two potted plants stand by the fence, and a bed of colourful flowers sits on the lawn near the house. It looks like a storm may be about to start while they are still doing garden chores.

- Boy / young man (centre): brown hair, red T-shirt, blue jeans, belt; looking up open-mouthed and pointing at the dark clouds.
- Girl / young woman (centre-right): dark hair in a ponytail, purple top, teal trousers; holding a long rake and raking a small pile of leaves at her feet.
- Dark storm clouds (top centre): three heavy grey-blue clouds in a grey sky; suggest rain is coming.
- Giant fork (left, front): silver dinner-fork shape, very oversized, used by the woman like a broom.
- Dirt pile and dust cloud (left, front): small heap of dirt/pebbles with a puff of dust where the fork meets the ground.
- Red dustpan (left, front): on the ground at the end of the path, catching the swept dirt.
- Rake (centre-right): long wooden handle with metal tines, gathering leaves.
- Leaf pile (centre-right, front): small heap of green and brown leaves.
- Two potted plants (left-centre, back): terracotta pots with green leaves by the fence.
- Flower bed (right): round bed of red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers.
- Paved path (left): runs from the gate down to the front of the picture.
- Wooden gate and fence (back): tall plank fence across the yard with a gate at left.
- House (right): cream walls, red roof, blue window, blue front door with step.
- Tree and bush (back right): tree behind the fence, small bush by the house corner.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The woman's tool looks clearly like a giant kitchen/dinner fork; it could possibly be read as a garden fork or pitchfork, but its shape (curved silver handle, four rounded tines) is a table fork. Either way, a fork is the wrong tool for sweeping.
- The boy could be pointing at the clouds, or at something in the sky like a bird or the first raindrop; nothing besides the clouds is drawn there.
- Unclear whether the girl is looking at the boy, the flowers, or just ahead.
- Ages are hard to judge: the red-shirt and purple-top figures could be teenagers or young adults.
