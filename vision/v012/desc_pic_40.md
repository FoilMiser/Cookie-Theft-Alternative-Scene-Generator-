# Picture 40

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden seen the other way round from the usual layout: a cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, a wooden picket fence runs across the back with a wooden gate on the right, and a paved path leads to the gate at the bottom-right. In front of the house, an older woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal top and grey skirt is watering a bed of colourful flowers with a hose that comes from a hose reel by the house. A small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands on the lawn on the right holding the string of an orange kite that is flying high in the sky near a cloud. At the back by the fence, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is standing with her hands on her hips, wearing an upside-down blue bucket on her head like a hat. On the grass at the bottom there is an empty grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can near the path. The odd thing is the bucket being worn as a hat; otherwise the scene is a calm garden afternoon with nothing about to go wrong.
