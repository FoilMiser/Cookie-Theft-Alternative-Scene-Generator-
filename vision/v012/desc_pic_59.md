# pic_59

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence. A cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the left, and a wooden gate with a paved path leading to it is on the right. A brown-and-white dog sits on the doorstep in front of the blue door holding its red lead in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. Its eyes are closed in a happy, squinting expression, and the lead trails onto the grass in a loop. In the centre, a woman with dark hair in a ponytail, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, reaches up with both hands to peg a white T-shirt onto a clothesline. A pair of blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped item already hang there. On the right, a man with brown hair in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is painting the wooden fence blue with a long-handled brush. A section of the fence next to the clothesline is already blue, and a grey paint tin with blue paint sits at his feet. A flower bed of colourful flowers is in front of the house, and a red dog bowl with food is on the grass near the front. The sun is in the top right and there are two white clouds. Nothing looks clearly impossible.
