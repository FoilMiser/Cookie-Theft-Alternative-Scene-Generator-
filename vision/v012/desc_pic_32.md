# Picture 32

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the left, and a stone path down the left side. Near the fence on the left, a small girl in a pink dress is jumping in the air with both arms up and her knees bent, looking happy; her shadow is on the ground below her, showing she is off the ground. In the centre, a person with brown hair, a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes is drinking from a white cup. Oddly, this person is in a sitting position, with knees bent and thighs straight out, but there is no chair, bench or anything underneath them; they appear to be sitting on thin air. At the bottom centre, a white goat with small curved horns has its head down and is eating a tuft of grass in the lawn, which is an unusual animal to find in a back garden. A teal watering can is on the grass on the left, and there is a colourful flower bed in front of the house. There is also a bird house on a pole behind the fence, a tree behind the house, a bush beside it, and a blue sky with two clouds and a sun in the top left.

- Person (centre): brown hair to the shoulders, blue hoodie, dark trousers, white shoes; drinking from a white cup while in a seated pose with nothing to sit on.
- Goat (bottom centre, front): white goat with small curved horns, facing left; head down eating a clump of grass.
- White cup (centre): held to the person's mouth.
- Grass tuft (bottom centre): small clump of taller grass that the goat is eating.
- Watering can (bottom left): teal watering can on the grass.
- Path (left): pale stone path leading from the gate toward the front.
- Gate (far left): closed brown wooden gate in the fence.
- Bird house (upper left, behind the fence): small bird house with a red roof on a pole.
- Flower bed (right, in front of the house): oval soil bed with red, yellow, orange, purple and pink flowers.
- House (right): cream house, red roof, window, blue door with grey step; bush beside it and tree behind it.
- Sky: two white clouds and a yellow sun with orange rays (top left).

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The person in the centre looks like they are sitting on an invisible chair; another reading is that they are squatting or crouching, but the upper body is fully upright and the thighs are level, which looks like sitting with no seat. This looks wrong or impossible.
- The goat in a garden is unusual; it could possibly be read as a white lamb or sheep, but the horns suggest a goat.
- The girl is clearly in the air (shadow separate from her feet); this reads as an ordinary jump, though she could be read as floating.
- The centre person could be a boy, girl or young woman.
- The white object the person holds could be a cup or a small carton.
