# Picture 37

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden picket fence across the back, a wooden gate at the far left, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a paved path at bottom-left, a flower bed and a wooden bench on the right. A small boy by the gate has just thrown a red-and-white ball; a dotted line shows it flying up toward the top of the fence, where it looks like it may go over. An orange cat is walking along the top of the fence close to where the ball is heading. A woman in a yellow dress stands on the lawn with both arms stretched out toward the left, as if trying to catch something. In the middle, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is drinking from a white cup while in a sitting position, but there is no chair, bench or anything at all under her — she is sitting on thin air. Behind her, an older woman with grey hair is walking to the right carrying a cardboard box. On the right, an older man with a grey beard and cap is watering the flower bed with a hose that comes from a hose reel by the house. At the bottom, a lawn sprinkler on a hose is spraying arcs of water straight onto a cardboard box sitting on the grass, and the box is getting wet. The seated-on-nothing woman is impossible, and the box being soaked by the sprinkler and the ball possibly going over the fence look like problems about to happen.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The woman in purple is posed as if sitting on a chair, but there is no chair — either an impossible "sitting on air" mistake or she is crouching very oddly; it reads most like sitting on nothing.
- The woman in yellow's outstretched arms could mean she is trying to catch the ball, or that she is reaching toward the boy (e.g. to stop him or to have him throw to her). She is facing away from where the ball is actually going.
- It is not clear whether the ball will go over the fence, hit the fence top, or hit the cat.
- The box on the ground might be a second box that the older woman has already put down, or an unrelated parcel; either way the sprinkler is wetting it.
- The sprinkler's hose runs across the paved path, which is a possible tripping spot.
