# Picture 21

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a sun in the top right and two clouds. A cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the left, and a tall wooden fence with a gate on the right runs along the back. A paved path runs from the gate to the bottom right corner. In front of the house, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts sits on a wooden bench drinking from a glass. Next to him, a woman with a brown ponytail and a blue hoodie is watering the flower bed by pouring water out of a black frying pan. That is odd, especially since a green watering can is sitting unused on the grass at the bottom right. In the middle, a clothesline holds blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel. On the right, a person in a purple top and teal trousers throws both arms up with a surprised face as the wind blows their orange hat away, with wind lines and leaves flying. There is also an empty wheelbarrow on the lawn at the bottom centre.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The pan is dark grey/black and looks like a frying pan, but it could be read as a saucepan or skillet. Either way it is a cooking pan used to water plants.
- The person with the flying hat: could be a woman or a man. The face and hair don't make it clear.
- The raised arms could mean they are trying to catch the hat or just throwing their hands up in surprise.
- The wind blows the hat away, but the laundry on the line, the trees and the water pouring from the pan don't move with it. That may be an unintended inconsistency.
- The boy's cup is small and white. It could be a glass of water, milk or juice.
