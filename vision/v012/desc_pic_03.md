# Picture 03

## Free description
This is a sunny town street or pedestrian square with a row of four colourful shop buildings (pink, light green, peach and cream) with striped awnings, the sun in the top-left and two clouds. On the left in front of a fruit stall, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, is sweeping the pavement with a broom. In the middle, a little girl in a pink dress is reaching up with one hand toward a blue balloon that is floating away just above her hand, while she still holds a yellow and a purple balloon by their strings in her other hand. In the centre-right, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is standing with his eyes closed, licking a pink ice-cream cone and looking very happy. On the far right, an elderly man with a grey beard and a flat cap, in a white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers, is walking along carrying a yellow shopping bag. There is a strange, impossible detail: an orange fish appears inside the green leaves of the tree on the right, as if it is in the tree. Other things in the scene are an empty bench, a black lamp post, two planters of flowers, a red bicycle locked in a bike rack at the front, a small round café table with two chairs, a cardboard box and a potted plant on the left.
