# Picture 18

## Free description
This is a back garden on a grey, gloomy day. Three dark storm clouds fill the sky, and it looks like rain is about to start. A house with a cream wall, red roof, window and blue door is on the right, and a tall wooden fence with a gate on the left runs along the back. An older man in a cap and brown vest is painting the fence blue with a paintbrush. A few planks on the left are already blue, and a pot of blue paint sits at his feet. In the middle, a clothesline between two wooden posts holds a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress kneels by a colourful flower bed, tending the flowers, with a pot holding a pink flower beside her. At the bottom left, a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts looks up with his mouth open and points at the sky, as if warning the others about the storm. If it rains, the fresh paint and the washing will get wet and the people will be caught outside. There is also an empty wheelbarrow on the lawn, a garden hose reel by the flower bed, a wooden bench at the front right and a paved path on the left. Nothing looks physically impossible.
