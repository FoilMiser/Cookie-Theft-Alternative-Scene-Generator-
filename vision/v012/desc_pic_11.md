# Picture 11

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, a washing line in the middle, and a gate with a paved path at the right. A man with dark hair and a beard, wearing a green shirt and beige trousers, stands by the flower bed next to the house and waters the flowers with a teal watering can; drops are falling onto the soil. On the right, a smiling boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on a red swing on a metal swing set, holding the ropes, with motion lines showing he is swinging. The washing line has a pair of blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped top hanging on it. An empty wheelbarrow stands at the front centre. Oddly, a cardboard box is up in the sky above the gate at the top right, tilted, as if floating or falling. There is also a grey shadow on the path with nothing above it.
