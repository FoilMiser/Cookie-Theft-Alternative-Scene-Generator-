# pic_27

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a house with a red roof and blue door on the left and a wooden fence with a gate on the right. An old man with a grey beard, a grey flat cap, a white shirt, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands by the flower bed watering the flowers, but he is using a frying pan: water is pouring out of the pan in a stream onto the flowers, like it was a watering can. This is odd, because a real teal watering can is standing unused on the grass at the front right. In the middle, by the fence, a girl in a pink dress is jumping in the air with her arms up and a big smile, her shadow on the grass below her. An orange cat is walking along the top of the fence near the tree on the left. There are two potted plants, an empty grey wheelbarrow at the front, and a paved path running from the gate down the right side. The sun is top right, with two clouds, and a tree stands behind the house.
