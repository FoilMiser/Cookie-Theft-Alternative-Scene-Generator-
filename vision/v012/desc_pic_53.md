# Picture 53

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny day in a park with hills, trees, a pond, a lamppost, a bench and a winding path. On the far left, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses is taking a photo with a camera, pointed to the right towards the other people. Next to her, a young man or teenage boy in a blue hoodie is raking a pile of leaves, and right in front of him a black-and-white cat is lying asleep on the grass. On the bench, a man in a green shirt is reading a red book and doesn't notice that a blue bird has landed beside him and is pecking at his big sandwich. Up on the hill behind, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a red and a yellow balloon, but is reaching up as his blue balloon floats away into the sky. A woman in a yellow dress is feeding crumbs to a white duck on the pond. In the front, a garden sprinkler attached to a hose is spraying arcs of water onto a cardboard box, which is getting wet. On the right, a brown dog is sitting up holding an open newspaper in its paws as if it were reading it, which is impossible, and a little girl in a pink dress is walking past a flower bed holding a yellow balloon.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The dog reading a newspaper is impossible; it could alternatively be read as a dog that has just grabbed a newspaper (e.g. fetching it), but it is drawn sitting upright holding it open in front of its face like a reader.
- The woman's camera could be aimed at the boy raking, at the man on the bench, or at the whole scene; I can't tell her exact target.
- The sprinkler spraying the box could be read as an accident (the box will be ruined) or as the sprinkler simply watering the lawn with the box in the way.
- The bird could be eating the sandwich or just standing on/next to it; its beak is right at the sandwich.
- The boy raking has his eyes drawn as closed/downcast lines; he could be looking at the leaves or squinting.
- The cat could be asleep or lying down; it does not look hurt, but its position right by the rake might mean it is about to get raked or disturbed.
- The dog's body pose (sitting with the newspaper) looks a little twisted and flat.
- The man on the bench seems not to notice the bird; the boy on the hill appears to be standing right on the ridge of the hill at the back, which makes him look small and far away.
