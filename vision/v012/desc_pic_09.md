# Picture 09

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a wooden fence along the back, a gate and a paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A girl or young woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands in the middle of the lawn in front of the washing line, raising one hand with motion lines as if she is waving. She is facing toward the right. On the right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. On the left there is a metal swing set with an empty red swing. The washing line holds a yellow-and-red striped top and a pair of blue jeans. Strangely, a cardboard box is up in the sky above the fence, tilted, as if it is flying, floating or falling into the garden. There is also a grey shadow on the grass near the right leg of the swing set with nothing above it to cast it.
