# Description: test_4.png

## 1. Free description

This is a cartoon of a back garden on a gloomy day. The sky is flat grey, and three big dark storm clouds hang over the middle of the picture. A wooden picket fence runs across the back with a hedge behind it, a wooden gate on the far left, and a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue front door on the right. In the garden a girl in a purple top and teal trousers stands on the left-centre, looking up with her mouth open in an "o" and pointing up and to the right with one arm, apparently at the storm clouds. Near the middle a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is jumping in the air with both arms raised. His eyes are squeezed shut in a big grin and his shadow is on the grass below his feet, so he looks thrilled, almost as if he is cheering for the coming rain. On top of the fence a small red bird is perched towards the left, and an orange cat walks along the fence top on the right near a tree. The cat faces left, towards the bird, so it might be stalking it. The garden also has a colourful flower bed, a wooden bench, a teal watering can, a grey wheelbarrow and a paved path running from the gate. It looks as though rain is about to start. The odd thing is that the boy is celebrating while the girl seems surprised or worried.
