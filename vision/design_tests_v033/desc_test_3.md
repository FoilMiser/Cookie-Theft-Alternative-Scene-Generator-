An older man in a grey cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is painting that blue section with a long-handled brush or roller. Next to him a younger bearded man in a green T-shirt is looking up and pointing towards the sky, as if warning that rain is coming, which would spoil the wet paint. An orange cat is walking along the top of the fence on the left. A house with a blue door stands at the left, with a flower bed in front of it, and a wheelbarrow and a watering can sit on the lawn at the front.
