# iso_08

## What I see
A cartoon wheelbarrow stands on green grass, with a soft darker-green shadow under it. It has a gray, open-topped tray (wider at the top than the bottom), two brown wooden handles sticking out to the upper right, and one dark leg under the back of the tray. On the left there is a dark wheel with a small square hub, and a blue-gray bar runs from the hub toward the leg like a frame rail or axle arm. Nobody is using it; it just stands there, as if left out for hauling dirt or garden material. At the bottom-left edge there is part of a gray rock or boulder, and across the top edge there is the edge of a blue area outlined in dark, probably a pond or pool of water.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The wheel is square, not round: a dark square frame with lines running from the center to the corners and edges. It could be read as a spoked wheel drawn the wrong shape, or as a grate, window or square crate rather than a wheel. This looks unintentionally strange.
- The wheel sits at the front-left at about mid-height of the tray, partly above the shadow, so it does not clearly touch the ground the way a working wheel would.
- The blue-gray bar from the wheel hub ends in a rounded tip under the tray and does not visibly join the handles or the leg, so the frame looks disconnected. It could be a frame rail or a loose axle/handle piece.
- The two brown handles appear to come out from behind the tray's right side rather than from the frame, and they overlap so much they could be read as one thick handle.
- The gray shape at bottom left is cut off; it is most likely a rock, but could also be a gray mound or the top of some other object.
- The blue shape at the top is cut off; it is most likely water (pond), but could also be sky or a blue object.
