# iso_01

## What I see
A wheelbarrow-like cart sits on green grass. It has a gray, open-topped trapezoid tub, two long brown wooden handles sticking out to the left, and one dark leg under the tub. A dark blue-gray bar runs from the right side of the tub to a dark square object with a lighter riveted square plate and a small square in the middle. That square is where a wheelbarrow's wheel would be, so it seems to be the wheel. In the top-left corner, cut off by the edge, is a round brown dirt flower bed with purple and yellow flowers on green stems. No person or animal is in the picture.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The square object on the right could be read two ways: (a) the wheelbarrow's wheel, drawn square instead of round, or (b) a separate metal box or plate that the bar is attached to. Its position, joined to the cart by an axle-like bar, points to (a). A square wheel looks unintentionally strange because it could not roll.
- The bar to the square is drawn over the front of the tub instead of coming from under it, so the way it attaches looks odd.
- Only one leg shows. That could just be the side view hiding the second one, but the cart looks like it rests on one leg plus the square.
- The handles pass behind the tub and seem to end there, so it is unclear how they attach to the frame.
- The flower bed is cut off, so I can't tell how big it is or what else is in it.
