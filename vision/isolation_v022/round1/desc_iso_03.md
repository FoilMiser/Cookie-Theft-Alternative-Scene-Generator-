# iso_03

## What I see
A wheelbarrow-like cart sits on green grass with nobody using it. It has a grey trapezoid-shaped tray, a brown wooden handle sticking out to the left, and one dark leg under the tray. On the right, where the wheel would normally be, is a dark square frame with a cross inside it, joined to the tray by a grey-blue rod that meets a small hub in the middle of the square. In the top-left corner there is a brown dirt flower bed with green stems and at least one purple flower with a yellow center. A darker green oval shadow lies under the cart.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The square object on the right can be read two ways: (a) the cart's wheel, drawn as a square with four spokes, or (b) a separate square window frame or grate that the rod happens to overlap. The rod meeting its center hub points toward (a), but a square wheel would not roll, so it looks unintentionally strange.
- Only one handle and one leg are visible. A wheelbarrow usually has two of each, so the other pair may be hidden behind the first, or simply not drawn.
- The handle seems to pass behind the tray instead of attaching clearly to its underside, and the rod comes out of the tray's front corner on the outside. The way the parts connect is hard to make sense of.
- The flower bed is cut off at the top-left edge. There may be a second flower at the top edge (a yellowish shape), but too little of it shows to be sure.
