# iso_05

## What I see
A wheelbarrow stands on green grass, with a darker oval shadow beneath it. It has a grey trapezoid-shaped tray, one long brown wooden handle sticking out to the left, and a single dark leg under the tray. On the right end is the wheel, which is drawn as a dark square frame with green showing through and thin dark spokes meeting at a small square hub. A grey-blue bar runs from the tray to that hub, like an axle or wheel arm. Behind it is a wooden fence: a horizontal row of orange-brown planks along the top edge and two vertical posts. No people or animals are visible.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The wheel is square, not round. It looks unintentionally strange, because a square wheel could not roll. It could also be read as a square frame or grate rather than a wheel. Because of its spokes and its position, I take it to be the wheel.
- The grey-blue axle bar is drawn across the front of the tray and sticks out past it to the wheel hub. It looks pasted on rather than built into the barrow.
- Only one handle and one leg are visible. A wheelbarrow usually has two of each, so the second ones might be hidden behind the first, or they might simply be missing.
- There is a brown rounded shape at the far right edge, cut off by the frame. It could be a mound of dirt, a rock, a log or stump, or part of a pot or sack. I can't tell which.
- The fence posts don't clearly connect to the top rail: they hang down into the grass as separate sticks. So the fence could be read as a fence seen from above, or as a rail with posts floating in front of it.
