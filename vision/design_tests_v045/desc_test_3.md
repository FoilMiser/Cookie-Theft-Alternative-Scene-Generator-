# Description of test_3.png

## 1. Free description

This is a sunny back garden. On the left is a cream house with a red roof and a blue door, a wooden fence runs across the back, and a sun and two clouds are in the sky. In the middle-back, a woman in a yellow dress is pegging a wet white T-shirt onto a washing line strung between two wooden posts. Wet blue jeans are already hanging there, and both items drip water. A wicker basket of folded laundry sits on the grass below the line. To her right, a girl or young woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds a garden hose nozzle up at about chest height. Only a single drop of water is coming out of it. The hose runs from a reel near the bench at the front left, across the lawn past a flower bed, and under a white-and-brown spotted dog with a blue collar, which stands on it. Where the dog stands, the hose has a thick dark bulge just before its feet, so the dog seems to be squashing or kinking the hose and stopping the water. On the right, a man with a moustache in a red shirt and blue trousers carries a cardboard box along a paved path that comes in from a wooden gate in the fence. In the front there is an empty wheelbarrow, and a birdhouse stands on a tall pole by the fence.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous

- Hose and dog: the dog seems to be standing on the hose, which would explain the bulge and why only one drop comes out of the nozzle. But the bulge could also be read as just a drawing glitch or a lump in the hose rather than an intended kink.
- The hose goes from the girl's hands down to her feet before running back across the grass. It makes a loop around her legs, which looks a little odd.
- The nozzle points up and to the left, roughly towards the washing line. It could mean she is about to spray (and soak) the drying clothes or the woman, or she may simply be puzzled that no water is coming out.
- The clothes on the line are already dripping, which fits freshly washed laundry. It could also be read as them having been sprayed by the hose.
- Man's direction: his face and body point right, towards the gate, but his stride and position on the path could mean he is walking down the path towards the front/left. It is unclear whether he is arriving with a delivery or leaving.
- The dog's front paws and head are drawn somewhat stiffly, and the dog sits right on top of the hose line, so the line passes behind/under its feet.
- Nothing appears to be floating or impossible apart from the possible hose-kink effect.
