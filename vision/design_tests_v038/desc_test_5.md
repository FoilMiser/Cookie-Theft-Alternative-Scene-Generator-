# Description of test_5.png

## 1. Free description

This is a cartoon of a back garden on a grey, overcast day, with three dark storm clouds gathering in the sky. A wooden fence runs across the back of the garden. A man in a green shirt and tan trousers stands in front of it, painting the fence blue with a long-handled paint roller. A patch in the middle of the fence is already blue and he is rolling at its right-hand edge, with an open paint tin on the grass beside him. On the left, a girl in a yellow dress and red shoes stands on a paved path, looking up with her mouth open in an "o". She points up toward the dark clouds, as if warning that rain is coming and will spoil the wet paint. The man faces the fence and does not seem to notice her or the clouds. At the bottom of the picture an orange cat is crouched low and creeping toward a small red bird pecking at the grass, so the cat looks about to pounce. On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a blue front door and a window, with a flower bed and a bush in front of it. Nothing looks physically impossible. The main tension is the coming rain against the freshly painted fence, plus the cat stalking the bird.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous

- What the girl is pointing at: most likely the dark clouds, as a warning about rain. Her hand is raised up and to the right, though, so she could be pointing at or calling to the man, or at something on the fence.
- The girl's expression: her round open mouth could mean surprise or alarm ("oh no, rain!"), or she could be calling out or whistling.
- Cat and bird: the cat looks like it is stalking or about to pounce on the bird. Its stretched pose could also read as simply running past. The bird faces the cat but seems unaware and keeps pecking.
- The man's roller is on the boundary between the blue and unpainted planks. That could mean he is still painting rightward, or that he has just finished the blue patch. Only one patch is blue, so either the job is unfinished or he is painting only that part.
- Minor drawing oddities: the man's legs and body are quite thin and elongated. The cat's front legs, with white paws, are drawn bent close together, which makes its pose a little awkward to read.
