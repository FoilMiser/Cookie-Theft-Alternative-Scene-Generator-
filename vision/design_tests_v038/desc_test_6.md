By the house door, a brown dog sits holding a red lead in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. A garden hose on a reel lies on the grass near the flower bed. The things that seem about to happen are: rain falling on the wet paint, the white dog spreading more blue paw prints, and the boy catching the ball.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous

- The man in red might be pointing at the clouds to warn about rain, or he might be pointing at something else in the sky, such as a bird or plane, but I cannot see anything else up there. Clouds seem the most likely reading.
- The white dog's blue paw prints start near the paint tray. I read this as the dog having stepped in the tray. Its paws look blue, but they are small and could just be its shading.
- The girl's pose is a bit unclear. Her arm reaches up to the left as if she has just thrown the ball, but her legs and feet look as if she is striding to the right or away. She could be throwing while walking, or the body and arm do not quite match.
- The brown dog is holding the lead in its mouth, and the lead does not seem to be clipped to its collar. It reads as "wants a walk", but it could also be holding a toy or rope.
- The hose reel stands on its own in the middle of the grass and is not connected to a tap or the house. That is slightly odd but not impossible.
- The boy painter's roller is on a blue section to his left, while he stands in front of an unpainted section. That is fine, but it is not clear whether he is painting new wood or going over paint again.
- The older man's hole sits right beside the shovel blade and is very small and dark. It could be read as a hole he is digging or as just the shadow of the dirt.
- Nothing obviously passes through anyone or floats, apart from the thrown ball, which is clearly meant to be in mid-air.
