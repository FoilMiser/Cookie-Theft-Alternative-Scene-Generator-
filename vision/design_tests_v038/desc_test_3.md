## 1. Free description

This is a sunny day in a back garden. There is a yellow sun in the top-left corner, two small white clouds, a green hedge, and a tall wooden fence across the back with a closed wooden gate at the far left. On the left a girl with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping a small pile of orange, red and brown autumn leaves with a yellow broom. She is standing at the edge of a pale paved path that runs from the front-left corner back to the gate. In the middle a man (or teenage boy) in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is reaching up to peg a white T-shirt onto a washing line strung between two wooden posts. The white T-shirt is dripping water, a pair of blue jeans is already pegged further along the line, and a basket of clothes sits on the grass under the line. On the right an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, kneels beside a round flower bed full of colourful flowers and reaches towards a small pot with a pink flower, as if she is about to plant it. A cream house with a red roof, a four-pane window and a blue door takes up the right edge. A garden hose on a reel lies on the grass in the front right, and an empty grey wheelbarrow stands in the front centre. Nothing looks impossible. The only slightly odd thing is that fallen autumn leaves are being swept while the tree is fully green and the flowers are in full bloom.
