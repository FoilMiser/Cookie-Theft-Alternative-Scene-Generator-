# Picture 1

## Free description

This is a sunny day in a park, with a blue sky, a bright sun at top left, two white clouds, green hills and a line of bushes in the distance. An old man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat walks along a winding sand-coloured path on the left, carrying a yellow shopping bag at his side. Further back, near the top of the path, a woman in a yellow dress stands eating a pink ice cream cone, with her eyes closed or looking down. In the middle, a little girl in a pink dress stands at the edge of a pond holding a small brown paper bag and reaching out with her other hand; small pale pellets (they look like breadcrumbs or seeds) lie on the water in front of her, so she seems to be feeding the white duck. The duck is on the pond facing her, moving toward the food, with white wake lines trailing behind it. In the front centre, a brown dog with a red neckerchief sits upright on the grass like a person, holding open a newspaper in its front paws as if reading it. That is the impossible part of the picture: dogs do not read newspapers. An empty wooden bench, a tall black street lamp, two green bins, a flower bed, a grey rock, trees and bushes fill out the park. Nothing dramatic looks about to happen; the old man is walking toward the viewer and is not obviously heading for anyone.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The woman's eyes: either closed in enjoyment or looking down at the ice cream. Her face also has some reddish and dark marks around the mouth and cheek that I could not make out clearly; they might be a rosy cheek and a smile, or smudges of ice cream.
- The small pale pellets could be breadcrumbs, seeds or small stones just under the water. Because the girl has a bag and is reaching out, I read them as food she has thrown.
- The girl's reaching hand: it could be throwing food, or just held out. No food is visibly in the air.
- The white lines behind the duck could be a wake or could read as sticks or scratches lying on the water. I take them as motion lines.
- The duck's body is tilted a little and its shape is slightly odd, as though it is leaning or partly turned.
- The old man's figure looks a little odd: he is seen from the side, the arm hangs straight down the front of the body and the bag covers his upper legs, so the legs seem to come out from behind it.
- The old man's bag has no clear handle shape at this size; it could be a shopping bag or a yellow box held by a strap.
- The dog is deliberately the strange thing as far as I can see, but its pose is also a bit awkward: its back legs splay forward and its body is thin and bent, so it could read as a stretching or sprawled animal at first glance.
- Size oddity: the woman in the back is drawn nearly as tall as the old man in front, and she seems to float slightly above the hill line because her head reaches up past the bushes on the horizon, which makes depth a little hard to judge.
- The tall lamp seems very tall compared with the people and could read as a post rather than a street lamp.
