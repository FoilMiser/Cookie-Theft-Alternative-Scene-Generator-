# Picture 3

## Free description

This is the back garden of a cream house with a red roof on a sunny day, with a tall wooden fence, a gate at the right and trees behind the fence. A brown and white dog sits on the doorstep in front of the blue door at the left, holding a red lead in its mouth, and the lead trails down to a loop on the ground, so the dog seems to be asking to go for a walk. In the middle, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is kicking an orange ball; his leg is stretched out, the ball is just off his foot and curved lines show the swing. He is kicking to the left, in the direction of the flower bed and the house, though the ball is still some way from them. On the right, an old woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a green top and a grey skirt stands on a paved path, sweeping a pile of red, orange and brown autumn leaves with a yellow-bristled broom. Her eyes seem to be turned away from the boy and the ball. A flower bed with mixed flowers is near the house, and an empty wheelbarrow and a teal watering can sit on the grass in the front. A birdhouse on a tall pole stands against the fence near the boy. Nothing looks impossible, but it seems possible that the ball will fly into the flowers or toward the dog.
