# Picture 4 description

## 1. Free description

It is a sunny day in a playground on a large green lawn, with a dark railing fence and a line of bushes behind it and a lot of empty grass in the middle. On the left, a seesaw with a red plank is tilted steeply: a small girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes sits on the raised left end, high off the ground, holding a handle with her legs dangling and small curved motion lines under her feet. A grey-haired older man in a blue-grey cap, brown waistcoat, white sleeves and dark trousers sits on the lowered right side with his feet on the grass, holding an open book and seemingly paying no attention to the girl. The result is that his weight holds the seesaw down and the girl appears stuck up in the air until he gets up or notices her. At the front right, a child with dark skin and braided hair, wearing a yellow top and orange shorts, is kneeling in a wooden sandpit with a hand on a sandcastle that has three towers and a small red flag. Behind on the right stands a tall slide with a red frame, a yellow and orange chute and a dark ladder, with nobody on it. There is also a tree at the far left, a green bin by the fence, a bush at the far right and a grey rock beside the sandpit. Nothing looks clearly impossible or about to fall, but an adult quietly reading on a children's seesaw while a small child is left up in the air is the odd part of the picture.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous

- Is the man reading, or looking up? He holds a book, but his head seems tipped back with the cap brim pointing up and to the left, and his face is hard to make out. He could be reading and ignoring the girl, or looking over the book in her direction.
- Is the girl stuck or simply playing? Her expression is not clearly upset. She could be stranded in the air by the heavy man, or just happily sitting there waiting for the seesaw to move.
- The motion lines under the girl's feet could mean she is kicking or swinging her legs, or that the seesaw has only just moved up.
- The man's white forearm looks like one long, stiff, thick tube lying across the book, and it could almost be read as a bandaged arm or plaster cast rather than a sleeve.
- The man's position: he is not sitting at the very end of the plank. The plank continues past him to the right and appears to run behind or through his legs, so it is hard to tell exactly how he is sitting on it. No seat or handle is visible at his end.
- The size difference between the man and the girl is large; she looks tiny for someone on the same seesaw. This could just mean she is very young.
- The child in the sandpit: the legs and feet are cramped and partly hidden behind the sandcastle, with a blue shoe showing near the castle's base and another to the right, so I could not tell whether the child is kneeling or squatting.
- The child's hand on the castle could be patting and finishing it, or about to knock it down. Building looks more likely.
- The sandcastle is large compared with the child, reaching about chest height, though this may be because it is closer to the viewer.
- The child's shadow extends over the sandpit's frame and out towards the grass, which looks slightly off.
- The lower end of the slide's chute stops just above the grass with no visible support, so it looks as if it is hovering slightly.
- There is a dark curved shape at the top of the ladder, near the slide's platform, that I could not make out; it may be a handrail.
- The tree trunk ends in a rounded bottom on the grass with no roots, so it looks a bit like a post stuck on the lawn.
- Shadows are inconsistent: the man and the child in the sandpit have shadows on the ground, but the girl, seesaw base, slide, bin, tree and rock do not.
- The humps behind the fence could be bushes or distant hills. I read them as bushes.
