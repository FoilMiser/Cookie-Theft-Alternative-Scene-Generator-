# Picture 5

## Free description

This is a sunny town street or pedestrian square, with a row of four colourful shop buildings (pink, green, tan and cream) along the back and a paved area in front. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands in the middle distance sweeping a pile of autumn-coloured leaves with a long broom. In the front centre a small girl in a pink dress holds an empty ice-cream cone and has her hand up to her face, as if she is crying or upset, because her ice cream has fallen on the ground beside her. A big brown dog with a red collar has its head down right next to the fallen ice cream and looks like it is about to eat or lick it. On the right a woman in a yellow dress is walking to the left with her eyes on an open book she is holding, and a red ball is lying on the ground right in her path, so she seems about to trip over it or kick it without noticing. On the left, something impossible is going on: a white dog with brown patches is sitting upright like a person and holding open a newspaper, as if reading it. Behind that dog is a fruit stall with crates of fruit and a striped awning, but nobody is serving at it. There is also an empty bench, a teal bicycle parked at a rack, a tree in a round planter, a tall street lamp, a rubbish bin, a potted plant and a cardboard box.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The fallen ice cream could be read two ways: as a dropped scoop in a melted puddle, or at first glance as a pink brimmed hat or a doughnut lying on the ground. The empty cone in the girl's hand is what points to ice cream.
- The girl's raised hand could mean crying and rubbing her eye, or she could be eating something or touching her face. I could not clearly see tears. It is also hard to tell which way she is looking, and whether she has noticed the dog.
- The brown dog could be about to eat the ice cream or could merely be sniffing it; I cannot tell if it has already started. It could also have caused the drop, but nothing shows that.
- The red ball could be just lying still or could be rolling; no child or player nearby seems to own it. The woman in yellow has not yet reached it, so the trip is only about to happen.
- The white dog is the clearly impossible thing in the picture. I read it as a dog reading a newspaper; the less likely reading is that it is only holding or has grabbed the paper. Its pose is very human, while the brown dog behaves like a normal dog.
- The leaves being swept look autumnal, but the only tree in the scene is fully green and is on the far side of the picture, so it is not clear where they came from.
- The bicycle's frame and pedals overlap the grey rack, so that area looks a bit jumbled and I cannot tell if the bike is locked or just leaning; there is a small stand-like shape at the bottom.
- Only one of the sweeping woman's shoes is clearly visible; her legs sit close together and the other foot is lost behind the leaves and broom.
- The lamp post passes directly in front of a window and the awning of the green building, which is just overlap but slightly crowded visually.
- The stall is unattended, which could be meant as odd or could simply mean the seller is not drawn.
- The tops of the sun's rays are cut off by the top edge of the picture.
