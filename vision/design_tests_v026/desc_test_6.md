# Picture 6

## Free description

This is a sunny park with green grass, a winding sandy path on the left, rolling hills and a line of bushes in the background, and a blue pond on the right. An older woman with grey hair in a bun, wearing glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt, is walking along the path holding a red lead. On the end of the lead a white dog with brown spots walks ahead of her to the left, on the grass beside the path. In the middle, a younger woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers holds a brown paper bag or box and is sprinkling seed from her other hand; a trail of little dots falls through the air down to a red bird and a blue bird, which are pecking at the seed on the ground. To the right, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands on the grass at the edge of the pond, holding a long fishing rod with the line dropping to a red-and-white float that is making a ripple in the water. Nothing has been caught yet; he seems to be waiting. In the front right there is a round bed of bright flowers, and there is a grey rock near the bottom centre. A tall lamp post stands on the grass in the middle with a green rubbish bin near it, and another green bin is in the bottom right corner. Everything looks calm and ordinary, and I did not see anything clearly impossible happening; nobody seems to be in any danger.
