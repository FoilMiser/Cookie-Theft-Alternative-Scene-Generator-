# Picture 2 description

## 1. Free description

It is a sunny day on a paved town square or pedestrian street, with a row of four colourful two-storey shop buildings along the back (cream, tan, pale green and pink), most of them with striped awnings. Three people are in the square, and none of them is touching or talking to another. At the front, left of centre, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes walks to the right, carrying a cardboard box in front of her. At the front in the centre, a street sweeper in an orange cap, orange high-visibility vest and blue trousers sweeps a pile of red, orange, yellow and brown leaves with a long broom. Further back, right of centre, a man in a red cap, blue jacket and black trousers holds a small camera to his face and points it to the left, apparently photographing the sweeper or the woman behind him. On the right is a fruit stall with a striped canopy and four crates of fruit, but nobody is tending it, and a loose wooden crate sits on the ground next to it. The woman is walking towards the sweeper and his leaf pile, so she might bump into him or walk through the leaves if she carries on, although there is still a gap between them. Around the square are a small table with two chairs, a tree in a round planter, two bins, a lamp post, a flower box and a potted plant. Nothing looks clearly impossible; the one mild oddity is a pile of autumn-coloured leaves when the only tree is fully green and some way from the pile.

- The sweeper's gaze: he could be looking down at his work, or sideways at the photographer as if he has noticed him. His large side-glancing eye could be read either way.
- The woman and the sweeper: she could be about to collide with him or disturb the leaves, or she could just be passing by. Nothing in the picture settles this.
- The woman's face has a dark mark around the mouth that looks a bit like a moustache. I read her as a woman because of the dress, but this looked odd.
- The woman's grip on the box: only one arm is visible, and her hand sits against the side or front of the box rather than clearly underneath it, so the box looks slightly stuck to her hand instead of supported.
- The woman's dress looks like two separate pieces with a very narrow join at the waist, so it could be read as a dress or as a top and skirt. Her neck and torso are also unusually thin.
- The photographer's body: the head is drawn side-on, but the seams on the jacket look more like a view from behind, and his feet point in different directions, so it is hard to tell how his body is turned.
- The sweeper's far foot is hidden by the leaves, and the leaves appear to sit on top of his foot. This could mean he is standing in the pile or be a drawing overlap.

- The wooden crate might belong to the stall, or it might have been left or dropped there. I could not tell whether it has anything to do with the woman's cardboard box.
- The leaves do not obviously come from anywhere, since the tree is fully green and there are no other fallen leaves on the ground.
- Shadows are inconsistent: the people and the crate have soft shadows, but the bins, table, plant pot, planter and lamp post do not, so those objects look slightly pasted on.
- The sun's top rays are cut off by the top edge of the picture, and the woman's shadow is close to being cut off at the bottom edge.
