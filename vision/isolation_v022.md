# The v0.22 isolation bench: the square wheel and the ladder, each read on its own

v0.22 takes the v0.21 flaws that are about how things look (`flaws_v021.md`) to the bench first, as in v0.12 to v0.20: the
template forced alone into a picture, cut out with little else around it, and read blind by fresh readers with the
isolation prompt given in `isolation_v012.md`. One round, eight cut-outs (`isolation_v022/round1`; `map.json` names the
case behind every image), six readers, none of whom saw two drawings of the same wheel. The changes that are about where
things go (no frying pan in the street, a jumper clear of standing structures behind, nobody's feet on another event's
thing, nothing further along a reading walker's way past her obstacle, no flower bed anywhere on a runner's line, a kick's
line clear of people further back, a sprinkler's hose never straight off the picture's bottom) were read in the whole test
scenes that followed (`design_tests_v022.md`).

## The barrow's square wheel, three ways

The v0.12 wheel, an open square with eight spokes set off the diagonals round a round hub, read twice as "a square dark
frame with a spoked wheel inside it rather than a plain round wheel" (v0.20 and v0.21, one half point each). Three
drawings with nothing round in them were each read twice, once in a garden and once in a park.

| Image | Drawing | What the reader said |
|---|---|---|
| `iso_01` (garden), `iso_06` (park) | A: a solid square wheel, a light grey plate inside a thick dark tyre, a square hub and four bolts | "A dark square object with a lighter riveted square plate ... where a wheelbarrow's wheel would be, so it seems to be the wheel", but "(b) a separate metal box or plate that the bar is attached to"; "a square wheel, which would not roll ... or (b) a separate square object such as a metal plate, bracket, or box" |
| `iso_03` (garden), `iso_07` (park) | B: the open square tyre, a cross of two bars to the middles of its sides, a square hub | "A dark square frame with a cross inside it ... (a) the cart's wheel, drawn as a square with four spokes, or (b) a separate square window frame or grate"; "a dark wheel with a hub and four spokes ... or ... a small square window frame or grate leaning against the front of the wheelbarrow" |
| `iso_05` (garden), `iso_08` (park) | C: the open square tyre, eight straight spokes from a square hub to its corners and the middles of its sides | "The wheel ... drawn as a dark square frame with green showing through and thin dark spokes meeting at a small square hub ... Because of its spokes and its position, I take it to be the wheel"; "a dark wheel with a small square hub ... a spoked wheel drawn the wrong shape, or ... a grate, window or square crate" |

No reader saw a round wheel inside a square in any of the three, and all six called the square wheel strange because it
could not roll. The plate (A) was offered as "a metal box or plate" and the cross (B) as "a window frame or grate" in
both of their readings; the eight spokes (C) led both readings as a wheel, the grate or crate only a second reading. C is
the v0.22 wheel. Its square axle plate replaces the round bolt the barrow's fork used to show over the hub.
