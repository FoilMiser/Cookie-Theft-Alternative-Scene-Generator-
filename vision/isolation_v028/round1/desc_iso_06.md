## What I see

A red seesaw on a grey triangular support stands on green grass, with a dark metal railing fence behind it. A large man with dark skin, a short dark beard and a green T-shirt sits on the low end, cross-legged or with one leg over the other, reading an open book and not looking at the other rider. A small girl with dark hair in two buns and a pink dress sits on the raised high end, holding the seesaw's handle, with her mouth in a small "o" and curved motion lines under her feet. The seesaw is tipped steeply down on the man's side, as if his weight is keeping her stuck up in the air.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The girl's expression could be surprise or worry about being stuck high up, or simply calm. Her mouth is small and her eyes look straight out, not at the man.
- The motion lines under her feet could mean her legs are swinging or kicking, or that the seesaw is wobbling.
- The man does not seem to be sitting on the plank properly: his body sits below and beside the board, with the low end of the plank running behind or through his legs, and his bottom seems to hover off the ground. His legs look tangled and it is hard to tell which leg is which; one foot sits under the other at the bottom.
- The book's lower edge appears to touch or rest on the plank, and his hand holding it seems to sit on the plank too, so the book could be read as balanced on the seesaw.
- The girl seems to sit in front of the plank or off its side, rather than on top of it, and the dark handle post rises close to her body. Her feet dangle below the plank on the near side.
- There is a dark curved shape cut off at the top right above the fence, and a dark vertical edge at the right border, and I cannot tell what they are.
