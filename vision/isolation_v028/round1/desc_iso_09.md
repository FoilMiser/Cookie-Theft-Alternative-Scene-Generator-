He holds a long dark fishing rod with both hands, angled up and to the left, and a thin line curves from the rod tip down to a red and white float on the water with a white ripple ring around it. A small grey reel sits near his hands. Tall brown-topped reeds (bulrushes) grow at the right edge of the pond, a bed of pink, red, orange and purple flowers in brown soil is in the bottom left corner, and green bushes and hills with blue sky are in the background.
