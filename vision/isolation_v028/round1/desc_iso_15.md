## What I see

Two children are sitting on a red seesaw on green grass, facing each other. The child on the left wears a pink dress and yellow shoes and is on the raised end, with their feet in the air. The child on the right wears a yellow top and orange shorts, has curly or braided dark hair and is on the lowered end, with their feet near the ground. Both are holding small dark handles in front of them. The seesaw pivots on a grey triangular stand; behind it are a dark railing or fence along the top, part of a brown tree trunk at top left, and the slanted dark legs of what could be a swing frame at top right.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The lower end of the seesaw (right) does not touch the ground, even though it is tilted down: it stays well above the shadow. The tilt could read as a seesaw in mid-motion or one that has stopped short of the ground.
- The right child's feet hang just above or at the small oval shadow patch. It could read as feet resting on the ground or dangling just above it.
- The dark shape at top right could be a swing set frame or some other play frame; only the legs are visible.
- The brown rectangle at top left stops abruptly with a rounded end on the grass and has no roots or base, so it could be a tree trunk or a wooden post. It looks a little as if it is hanging down from the top edge rather than growing from the ground.
- The fence bars at the top also stop in mid-air above the grass: only a top rail and short uprights are visible, with no bottom rail or posts reaching the ground, so it looks hung or floating.
- The left child's arms and the handle overlap so that it is hard to see the hands actually gripping it, and the same is true on the right. The left child's body seems slightly merged with the plank where the skirt meets it.
