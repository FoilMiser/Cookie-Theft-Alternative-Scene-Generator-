## What I see

A child with dark skin and dark curly or braided hair, in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts, stands on a tiled pavement holding an empty ice-cream cone in one hand, with the other hand raised to the side of the head as if upset or dismayed. On the ground in front of them is a splat of melted pink ice cream with coloured sprinkles scattered around it. A tan dog with a red collar or bandana is bending down with its head low over the splat, its tongue showing, apparently about to lick it up. At the top edge there is the bottom of a dark blue-grey rectangular object and the base of a dark post or lamp pole.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The hand at the head could be read as dismay (hand on head after dropping the ice cream) or as scratching the head, or holding something to the ear like a phone.
- The child's face is small and partly covered by the raised hand and arm, so the expression is hard to make out. It could be sad or just looking down.
- The cone looks empty but its opening is hard to see; it seems held point-down and tilted, and the hand gripping it sits oddly at the rim.
- The blue-grey rectangle at the top is cut off. It could be a bench, a sign or a bin. The dark post could be a lamp post or a bollard.
- The dog's neck joins the body abruptly, with the head looking like a round ball set in front of the red collar. Its front and back legs are thin and bend at sharp angles, and the white paws look like little shoes.
- The dog's muzzle is close to the splat but does not quite touch it, so it could be sniffing rather than licking.
