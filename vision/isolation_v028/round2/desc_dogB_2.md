## What I see

On a tiled pavement, a child with dark skin and braided or twisted hair stands in a yellow top and orange shorts, holding an empty ice-cream cone in one hand and raising the other hand to the side of the head. In front of the child, a pink splat with colored sprinkles lies on the ground, which looks like the fallen ice cream, with a few small dots scattered around it. A tan dog with a red collar or bandana and white paws is bent over with its head down at the edge of the splat, apparently licking it up. The child's raised hand and lowered head suggest dismay or disappointment. At the top of the picture are the bottom edge of a dark blue-grey object and the base of a dark post, both cut off.

- The cone is held low at the hip, angled with the point up or sideways, and looks to be pressed against or partly tucked behind the hand. It is hard to tell whether it is truly empty or just seen from the side, and a small pink or dark mark near the hand might be leftover ice cream.
- The small dark mark under the dog's muzzle could be its tongue or part of the splat; it is not clear that the mouth actually touches the ice cream.
- The dog's ear is a large dark oval that almost looks like a separate shape stuck on the side of the head, and the head looks like a round ball joined abruptly to the neck at the collar, so its face is hard to read when seen head-down.
- The dog's front legs look a little odd where they join the chest, and its white paws look like socks or shoes more than paws.
- The dark blue-grey shape at the top could be a bench seat, a sign or a planter base, and the dark post could be a lamp post or a bollard; neither can be identified from what is shown.
