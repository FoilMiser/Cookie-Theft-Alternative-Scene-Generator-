## What I see

A large cartoon tree with a brown trunk and a big rounded dark-green leafy crown stands against a pale blue sky and rolling green hills. A round ball with blue and white wedge-shaped panels and a small yellow circle at its top sits in the upper part of the tree, resting on or just above a short brown branch that angles out from the top of the trunk. Small clumps of lighter green leaves sit on either side of the ball, partly overlapping it, and two more leaf tufts are drawn as decoration on the crown. No person or animal is visible; the ball looks like a beach ball that has got stuck in the tree.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The ball could be read as stuck in the tree, wedged between the leaf clumps, or as simply balanced on top of the branch. The branch runs along its underside and may be supporting it.
- The ball could be a beach ball (most likely, from the panels), but the small yellow circle near its top could also make it read as an odd eye-like or badge-like object at first glance.
- The ball and the branch are drawn fully on top of the dark-green crown, with no leaves in front except the small clumps, so it looks a little pasted on rather than nestled inside the foliage.
- The branch is drawn in front of the crown and meets the trunk at a sharp point at the top; the trunk tip and the branch look like a slightly awkward join, with the trunk ending in a narrow spike inside the leaves.
- The lighter-green leaf clumps next to the ball look as though they are floating, since they are not attached to anything visible other than the crown behind them.
