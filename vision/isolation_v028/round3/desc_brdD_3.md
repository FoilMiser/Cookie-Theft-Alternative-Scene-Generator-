## What I see

A man with dark skin, short dark hair and a small beard walks to the right along a paved pavement, wearing a green short-sleeved shirt, tan trousers, a dark belt and brown shoes. He holds a red lead in his hands, and it runs in a straight line to the blue collar of a white dog with brown spots and brown ears, which is walking ahead of him with its tongue out. Behind them is a row of pastel shopfronts with doors and windows, a dark lamp post or pole, and at the left edge a wooden stall with crates of red and orange fruit. He is taking the dog for a walk past a street market or shops.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The man's hands are bunched together at his waist and the two hands and forearms overlap, so it is hard to tell which hand actually grips the lead; the lower hand could be a second hand or just a lump below the wrist.
- The man's head and face look turned slightly towards the viewer while his body walks in profile, and the neck is short and sits oddly on the shoulders, so the head looks a little set on rather than joined.
- The lead is perfectly straight and taut even though the dog does not look as if it is pulling; it reads as a stiff rod as much as a slack lead. Its end meets the dog at the neck, behind the ear and collar.
- The dog's legs are thin and tube-like and end in flat white oval paws; the near and far legs are close together and hard to separate, and its long tail stands straight up and looks stiff.
- The dark shape at the far right edge, partly cut off at about head height, could be the side of a bench, a bin or a sign; I cannot tell which. A small dark bit also sits above it at the very edge.
- The base of the dark pole sits on the pavement with no visible shadow, while the man and dog do have shadows, so the pole looks slightly less grounded. Its top is cut off by the frame.
- The stall at the left is cut off, showing only one leg and part of a table top, with a green and white awning or sign above it, so its full shape is unclear.
