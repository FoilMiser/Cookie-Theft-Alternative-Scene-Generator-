## What I see
A white goat with dark curved horns and a red collar stands on green grass in front of a wooden plank fence. Its head is lowered toward a small dark-green tuft of grass on the left, so it looks like it is grazing or sniffing the grass. A thin tan line runs from the goat's collar down to the ground and curves over to a short wooden stake in the grass, so the goat looks tethered to a peg. Two tall dark-brown posts stand in front of the fence on either side of the goat. At the top edge, cut off by the frame, hang a yellow item and a pair of blue trousers, which look like laundry on a washing line.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The tan line from the collar could be a rope or tether tied to the stake, or a thin bent stick or cane. It looks fairly rigid near the collar, and only the curve near the ground makes it read as a rope.
- The goat's mouth is near the grass tuft but does not clearly touch it. It could be eating, or just sniffing or looking down.
- The goat's pose looks hunched and awkward. The back is strongly arched, the hind legs bend outward and backward at odd angles, and the front legs are thin and sit close together under the chest. It looks a bit like the goat is crouching or straining rather than standing normally.
- The rope seems to pass very close to, or in front of, the goat's front legs. It is hard to tell whether it goes around a leg or just hangs in front of it.
- In the bottom-right corner a dark-brown rounded shape is cut off by the frame. It could be a tree stump, a mud puddle, a pot or barrel, or part of another animal. I can't tell which.
- The yellow and blue items at the top are mostly out of frame. The blue one looks like trousers, but the yellow one could be a shirt, a towel or a sign.
- The two tall posts could be fence posts or washing-line posts. They stand in front of the fence rather than being part of it.
