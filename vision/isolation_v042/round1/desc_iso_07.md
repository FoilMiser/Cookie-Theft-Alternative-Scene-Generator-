# iso_07

## What I see
A tan-orange cartoon dog sits upright on green grass. It has a floppy brown ear, a white muzzle and a red collar or neckerchief with a white patch. With its front paws it holds an open newspaper (printed columns and dark headline bars) up in front of it, as if reading it. Behind it at the top right is the edge of a blue pond with a dark outline, with two green reed stalks at its top-left edge. A smooth grey rock lies on the grass at the lower left.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The printed side of the newspaper faces the viewer, not the dog. So the dog could be reading it, or it could be holding it up to show it to someone.
- The dog's pose is very human-like: it sits on its haunches with its torso tall, thin and stretched, and its hind legs fold forward along the ground. The body looks oddly long and narrow for a dog, and it is hard to tell which leg is which in the lower body.
- The paws touch only the left edge of the newspaper. The paper looks stiff and stands up on its own, almost floating, rather than being gripped.
- The green stalks at the top are cut off, so they could be reeds or cattails at the pond's edge, or the legs or stems of something else.
