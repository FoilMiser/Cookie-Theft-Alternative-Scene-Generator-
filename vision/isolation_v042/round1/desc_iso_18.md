- The wooden crate at the right stands alone on the paving with no clear link to the stall. It could be spare stock or just a prop.
- The café chairs are drawn side-on and face away from their tables, so the seating looks a little stiff, but nothing passes through anything else.
- The street lamp's top overlaps the window of the green building behind it. I read this as depth (the lamp is in front), not as an error.
