## What I see

A woman with dark hair tied back, a purple sleeveless top and teal trousers stands on the left. She holds a dark teal garden hose with a yellow spray nozzle up at about shoulder height, and her other hand holds the hose lower down. The hose runs down past her legs, along the grass behind and under a brown dog, then to a hose reel on a dark base by the house corner. The dog has a red collar, stands side-on with its tail up, and faces the woman and the nozzle. To the right is a round bed of brown soil with colourful flowers (yellow, orange, purple, pink, red).
