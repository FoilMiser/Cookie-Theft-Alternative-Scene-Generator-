# iso_23

## What I see
A white duck with an orange beak and a black dot eye floats on a blue pond, facing right. It has a drawn wing outline and a small pointed tail, with a soft shadow beneath it on the water. Two cattails (green stems with brown tops) stand at the right end of the pond, close to the duck's beak. A round green bush sits at the right edge of the pond, and a tree with a brown trunk and green crown stands on the grass above it. The rest is flat green grass with a darker green band along the top.

- The bush overlaps the pond's right outline, so it seems to sit partly on the water rather than on the bank.
- The cattails stand just inside the pond edge, very close to the duck's beak. The duck could be pecking at them or the two could simply be near each other.
- The tree trunk ends in a rounded, outlined bottom just above the bush, with no visible ground or roots. It reads as standing on the grass behind, but it could also look slightly cut off or floating.
- The tree crown is cut off by the top of the cut-out.
