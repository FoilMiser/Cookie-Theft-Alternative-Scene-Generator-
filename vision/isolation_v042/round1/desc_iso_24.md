# iso_24

## What I see
A tan/orange cartoon dog with a dark brown floppy ear, white muzzle, black nose and a red collar or bandana is reared up on its hind legs on green grass. It holds an open newspaper out in front with both front paws. The newspaper is folded open into two pages showing dark headline bars, a picture box and lines of text. Its tail curls up at the left, and there is a shadow on the grass under it. Part of a blue pond with a dark outline is at the top right, with two green stalks (reeds, cut off at the top) at its upper-left edge.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- It is unclear whether the dog is reading the newspaper or just holding it up. The printed side faces the viewer rather than the dog, and the dog's head is above the paper looking right, not down at the print. So it could be reading, or it could be presenting or offering a paper it fetched.
- The pose is odd for a dog. It sits upright like a person or kangaroo, the torso and neck are long and nearly vertical, and only one bent hind leg with a large foot shows at the bottom. It could be sitting on its haunches or kneeling or crouching on its hind legs.
- The newspaper is large compared with the dog, about as tall as its whole torso.
- The front legs join the paper at its left edge. The upper paw sits on the top corner and the lower paw sits on the side, so the grip looks more like the paws are stuck to the paper than holding it.
- The green stalks at the top are cut off, so their tops can't be seen. They could be reeds or cattails at the pond edge.
