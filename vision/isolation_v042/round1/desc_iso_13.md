# iso_13

## What I see
A white goat with curved dark grey horns and a short raised tail stands on green grass, bending its head down toward a small tuft of dark grass, as if it is grazing. A small pink thing sits at its mouth, close to the grass tuft. In the bottom-right corner, partly cut off, is a wheelbarrow with a grey metal tray, a dark wheel and orange-brown handles. In the top-right corner is part of a dark green bush or tree canopy. A soft shadow lies under the goat.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The pink thing at the goat's mouth could be its tongue or a small pink flower or bit of food it is eating.
- The head is a flat, round white disc with an oval and a dark dot inside it. It reads more like a face pasted onto the front of the body than a goat's head. There is almost no visible neck, so the head seems to attach straight to the chest.
- A small white flap hangs under the front of the body near the head. It could be a beard, an ear or part of a front leg, and it is hard to tell which.
- The legs are very thin sticks with dark hooves. The front legs look bent or crossed, which makes the stance a little awkward.
- The wheelbarrow is cut off at the frame edge, so only the tray, part of the wheel and the handles show. It seems to have nothing to do with the goat.
