## What I see
A sunny backyard. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door. A wooden fence runs along the back, with a gate on the right and a paved path leading to it. A woman with dark hair, in a yellow dress and red shoes, stands beside a round bed of mixed flowers (red, yellow, orange, pink, purple). She is pouring water onto them from a teal watering can, and a stream of blue drops falls on the flowers. In the middle of the lawn, a man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers holds a long rake angled down to the grass, raking a small pile of brown leaves. To the right is a dark grey A-frame swing set with one red swing seat hanging on two ropes or chains. There is a tree behind the house, a small bush by the door, hedges beyond the fence, two clouds and a sun.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The rake's head is small and grey and sits right in the leaf pile, so it is hard to see clearly. It reads as a rake, but it could also be taken for a broom or a garden fork.
- The man's hands are both near his chest, one high on the handle and one a little lower. The rake handle sticks up past his top hand, so his grip looks slightly awkward, though not broken.
- The man's lower face has a darker shade that could be a beard or stubble, or just shading.
- The woman's water stream looks like separate falling drops more than a pour. It still reads as watering.
- The swing set's top bar is drawn in front of the fence, which is fine. Its legs stand on the grass and do not look like they are floating.
- Nothing looks clearly detached or passing through anything else.
