# iso_46

## What I see
A grey cat crouches low on top of a wooden picket fence, body stretched out and tail held straight behind. It looks like it is stalking or creeping towards something. To the right on the same fence top sits a small blue bird with a white belly and a yellow beak, facing left towards the cat. Behind the fence are a green hedge or bushes, a tree with a brown trunk and a large round green canopy, and a light blue sky.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The cat's limbs are hard to read. The hind legs look folded at several sharp angles and seem tangled together, which makes the body look flattened, almost lizard-like or rat-like. At a glance it could be read as a rat or weasel rather than a cat, though the pointed ear and face suggest a cat.
- There is a white shape just below the cat's head. It could be a raised white paw, a white chest or muzzle patch, or an open mouth showing teeth.
- The cat's feet rest on the pointed tops of the pickets. It reads as balancing on the fence, but its body is drawn so low and long that it almost looks like it is lying along the fence edge or hovering just above it.
- The bird is right next to the tree trunk, so it could be read as perched on the fence in front of the tree or sitting against the trunk. It seems to be on the fence top.
- I cannot tell whether the bird has noticed the cat (it faces the cat) or is simply perched there.
