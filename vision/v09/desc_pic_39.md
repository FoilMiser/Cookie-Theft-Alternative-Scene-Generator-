# Picture 39

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden, with a yellow sun at top left, two clouds, and a tall wooden fence with green bushes and a tree behind it. On the left there is a wooden gate in the fence, and a path with oval stepping stones leads from the gate down to the front of the picture. A man with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands near the gate, painting the fence blue with a brush or roller on a long handle; one section next to the gate is already blue, and an open tin of blue paint is on the grass at his feet. In the middle, between two wooden posts, there is a washing line with a white T-shirt, a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. A second man, with dark skin, a beard, a green shirt and tan trousers, stands under the line reaching up with both hands to peg the washing. On the right there is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue front door, with a flower bed of colourful flowers beside it and an empty wooden bench in the front right corner. In the front there is a teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow on the grass. One odd thing: a tan box at the left corner of the house appears tilted and half hidden, as if it is sticking out of the house wall or leaning behind the house.
