# Picture 37

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park, with a yellow sun at top right, two clouds, green hills and bushes in the background, and a tree on each side. There is a blue pond on the left with reeds (cattails) at each end. An older man in a grey cap, brown vest, white shirt and dark trousers stands on the grass to the right of the pond, fishing with a rod; his line goes down to a red-and-white float in the water, with a little ripple around it. A black lamppost stands just behind him. In the front on the left, a woman with a brown ponytail, blue hoodie and dark trousers is kneeling beside a flower bed full of red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers. She holds a small tool down near a terracotta pot with a pink flower in it, so she seems to be about to plant it. On the right, a winding path runs from the back down to the front, with an empty wooden bench beside it. A woman with short dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes stands on the path holding a small white bag and scattering seeds or crumbs on the grass. A red bird and a blue bird are pecking at the seeds near her feet. Everyone is calm and busy with their own activity; nothing looks impossible.
