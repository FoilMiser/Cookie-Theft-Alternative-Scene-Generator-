# Picture 06

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a gate on the left, a stepping-stone path, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A man in an orange cap, an orange high-visibility vest with reflective stripes, and blue jeans or overalls stands by the fence painting it blue with a long-handled brush or roller. A few boards near the left end are already blue. Behind him on the grass are a paint can and a paint tray, both with blue paint in them. A trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray to a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar, which is trotting away to the right with blue paint on its paws. The dog has clearly stepped in the paint tray and is tracking paint across the lawn, and the man, facing the fence, has not noticed. The dog is heading toward the flower bed and the house, so it may spread more paint. A teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow sit on the grass at the front, and there is a hose reel and an empty bench by the house.
