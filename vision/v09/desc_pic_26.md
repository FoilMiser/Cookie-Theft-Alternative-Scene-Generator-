# Picture 26

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden, with the sun in the top right and two white clouds, but it is windy. White wind lines and a few green leaves blow across from the left. In the middle, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands with both arms raised and his mouth open, looking up. His orange baseball cap has just blown off and is tumbling through the air to his upper right, above his reach. On the left is a cream house with a red roof and a blue door. A brown and white dog sits in front of the door holding its red lead in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. An empty wooden bench is in the front left and a colourful flower bed is beside the house. On the right are two potted plants in terracotta pots and a stepping-stone path leading to a gate in the back fence. A birdhouse on a pole is visible above the fence. In the front right is a grey wheelbarrow whose wheel appears to be a square, which would not roll and looks wrong.
