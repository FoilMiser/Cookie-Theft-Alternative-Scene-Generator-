# Picture 09

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny park with a winding sandy path, a pond, a lamppost and rolling green hills behind. On the left a blue ball is stuck up in a tree, and a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands under it with both arms raised, looking upset, as if he wants his ball back. Nearby a park worker in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest is raking leaves into a small pile on the path. Further back, a white dog with brown spots has its nose down in something pink on the ground that looks like a dropped scoop of ice cream, while a girl in a pink dress stands just behind holding something to her mouth, perhaps an empty cone. An older woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a bench reading a red book; beside her on the bench is a very large sandwich, and a blue bird is leaning in to peck at it, so it looks like the bird is about to steal her sandwich without her noticing. In the front centre a brown dog with a red collar is sitting up holding an open newspaper with its paws as if reading it, which is impossible. A man in a red shirt and blue trousers is digging a hole with a spade next to a mound of dirt by the pond, and a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is watering a bed of colourful flowers with a teal watering can. An orange cat is crouched low in the grass near the lamppost, and a large red bird is flying in the sky in front of a cloud. There is a green litter bin in the bottom right corner.

- The girl's hands are near her mouth holding something small; it could be an empty ice cream cone or something she is eating/drinking.
- The bearded man's face is drawn very dark, so his face and beard almost blend into one shape and it is hard to see his expression.
- The red bird in the sky is quite large compared with the other things in the picture and is drawn overlapping the cloud.
- The blue bird on the bench is also big relative to the sandwich and the woman.
- The man digging holds the spade with one hand while his other hand is near his chest, which is an odd way to dig.
