# Picture 03

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate in the fence on the left, a stepping-stone path leading to the gate, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. On the left stands a metal A-frame swing set with one empty red swing seat. A child in a yellow top and orange shorts is high in the air in front of the fence with both arms raised, jumping for joy, with a shadow on the grass well below. In front, a small girl in a pink dress holds the strings of a blue balloon and a yellow balloon in one hand and reaches up with her other hand toward a purple balloon that is floating away with its string dangling free. It looks like she has just let go of it. In the centre-right a woman in a yellow dress is watering a flower bed with a green watering can, and water pours from the spout onto the flowers. On the far right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. A coiled garden hose on a reel sits next to the flower bed.
