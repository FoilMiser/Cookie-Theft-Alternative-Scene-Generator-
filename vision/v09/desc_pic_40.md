# Picture 40

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden, with a yellow sun at top right, two clouds, a wooden fence with bushes behind it, and a tree behind the house. On the left there is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door, with a flower bed of colourful flowers in front of it. A hose reel sits beside the flower bed, and the hose runs across the grass to a young woman with a brown ponytail, blue hoodie and dark trousers, who is spraying water in an arc onto the flowers. Behind her is a washing line on two wooden posts, with a pair of blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel hanging on the left half. In the front centre, a woman with short dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes is digging with a spade; there is a small hole with a pile of dirt beside it. On the right, a man with brown hair, a red shirt and blue jeans is walking toward the house carrying a cardboard box with a white label. Further right there is a grey swing set with one red swing seat hanging empty, a birdhouse on a pole sticking up behind the fence, and a wooden gate in the fence. A path with oval stepping stones runs from the gate down to the front right, and a teal watering can sits on the grass beside it. Everyone is busy with garden chores and nothing looks impossible.
