# Picture 01

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park, with a bright sun in the top-left corner, two white clouds, and rolling green hills and a line of bushes in the background. In the middle of the picture a woman with a dark ponytail, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, stands beside a tall black lamp post and tosses small brown crumbs (they look like bread) toward a pond; she holds a small white bag or carton in her other hand, and three crumbs are in the air between her hand and the water. A white duck with an orange beak is swimming in the pond toward the crumbs, leaving wake lines behind it. In front, a girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket with her legs stretched out, next to a wicker picnic basket. A winding sandy path runs from the bottom-left corner up into the park, and there is an empty wooden bench beside it. On the right there is a bed of colourful flowers. One thing looks wrong: a wooden ladder stands upright at a slant on the far right with nothing to lean against. It is just balanced on the grass, which would not really be possible.
