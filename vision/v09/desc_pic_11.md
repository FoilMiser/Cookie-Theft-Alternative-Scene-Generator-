# Picture 11

## Free description
This is a sunny town street or pedestrian square in front of a row of colourful two-storey shops (pink, pale green, peach and cream) with striped awnings. On the left there is a fruit and vegetable stall with crates of green, yellow, orange and red produce under a striped canopy, and a woman in a yellow dress stands in front of it holding a small camera up to her face, taking a photo toward the right. In the front left a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. In the back centre, in front of the green shop, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is running or jogging toward the left. In the front centre a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds out an ice cream cone, and right next to him a brown dog with a red collar has its head down eating a pink scoop of ice cream that has fallen on the ground and is melting into a puddle, so it looks like the boy dropped his ice cream and the dog is eating it. There is a black lamppost, a planter box of flowers by the green shop, a teal bicycle parked in a bike rack, a tree in a round planter on the right, and an empty café table with two chairs in the bottom right corner. Nothing seems impossible, but the dog eating the dropped ice cream is the main little incident.
