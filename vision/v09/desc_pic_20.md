# Picture 20

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence and gate on the left, a stepping-stone path down the left side, a tree behind the fence on the right, and a cream house with a red roof, blue door and window on the right. In the middle, a girl with a brown ponytail, blue hoodie and dark trousers is holding the nozzle of a green garden hose up close to her face and peering into it; only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel by the house, along the grass, and a white goat is standing right on top of it, which would squash the hose and stop the water. So it looks like water may suddenly spray her in the face when the goat steps off. On the left, a lawn sprinkler fed by a second hose is spraying arcs of water, but the water is landing on a cardboard box sitting on the grass, with drops on top of it, instead of on any plants. Strangely, there is an orange fish sitting in the leaves of the tree, which is impossible. There is also a teal watering can on the grass at the front left, a colourful flower bed, a green bush, and a wooden bench by the house.
