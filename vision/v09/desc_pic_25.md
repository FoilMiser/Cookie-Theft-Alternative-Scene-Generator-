# Picture 25

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny park with rolling green hills, a pond, a lamppost and a winding path. In the front left, a little girl in a pink dress has just thrown a red ball. A dotted line shows it arcing up over the edge of the pond. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands in front of the pond with both arms held out, as if waiting to catch it. Next to him, a wooden ladder stands upright on its own at the edge of the pond with nothing to lean against, which looks impossible. In the front centre, a bearded man in a green shirt is walking along reading an open book and heading toward a red bucket of water on the grass, so he may be about to trip over it. In the background, a child with a yellow balloon walks past, a woman in a yellow dress walks a spotted dog on a red lead, and an older woman with grey hair and glasses walks along the path eating an ice cream. On the right, a woman in a purple top reads a red book on a bench. Beside her a huge sandwich lies on the bench, and a blue bird is at the end of it, seemingly about to steal it. On the far right, a blue ball is stuck in a tree, and a person in a blue hoodie points up at it. There is also a flower bed, a green bin at the far left edge and a few trees and bushes.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The man by the pond could be getting ready to catch the ball, or simply reaching out with arms extended. The ball's arc seems to be heading up and over the pond's edge rather than straight to his hands.
- The ladder: it stands upright with no support, half over the pond. It could be read as standing in the water or standing on the bank. Either way it looks physically impossible or unintentionally strange.
- The reading man may be about to walk into or trip over the bucket, or may pass beside it.
- The blue thing on the bench is most likely a bird stealing the sandwich, but it is small and could be read as a blue cup or object. There is a small white item under it that I couldn't identify.
- The sandwich is unusually large for one person.
- The older woman's strap could be a handbag strap or a camera strap.
