# Picture 15

## Free description

This is a busy, sunny town square or pedestrian street lined with colourful two-storey shops that have striped awnings, with a bright sun in the top right and two small clouds. On the left, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands near a tree with both arms raised, and a blue ball with a white stripe is stuck up in the tree's leaves. A tan dog with a red collar is lying flat on the pavement at the far left, apparently asleep. In the middle, a grey-haired older woman in glasses and a teal top sits on a wooden bench reading a red book, while behind her a brown-haired person in a blue hoodie jumps in the air with arms up, eyes closed and smiling, as if celebrating. An older man in a cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat stands by a lamp post holding a teal watering can, but the stream of water runs between the can and the shop awning above (it seems to go upward onto the awning, or drip down from it into the can) rather than onto the flower box nearby. At the front, a bearded man in a green shirt is drinking from a white cup in a seated position, but there is no chair or bench under him; he is sitting on thin air. Near him a black-and-white cat is crouched low, creeping toward a red bird that is pecking at the ground, so the cat looks about to pounce. On the right, a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has its head down in a pink ice cream with sprinkles that has fallen on the ground, and a girl in a pink dress just beyond it is holding an ice cream cone up to her mouth. There is also a fruit and vegetable stall with crates of produce, a cardboard box on the ground, a teal bicycle in a bike rack, an empty café table with two chairs, and two potted plants in the front corners.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The bearded man in green is posed as if sitting on a chair, but there is no chair, bench or seat under him. He appears to be sitting on thin air. This looks impossible.
- The watering can's water: a beaded chain of blue drops runs between the can's spout and the awning. It could be read as (a) the man pouring water upward onto the awning, which is physically odd, or (b) water dripping down off the awning into his can. Either way it is not going onto the flowers. The stream also looks more like a string of beads than flowing water, and it seems to cross the lamp post; I can't tell whether it passes in front of or behind the post.
- The boy by the tree has both arms up. He could be reaching or jumping for the stuck ball, stretching, or cheering. His face is turned to the right rather than up at the ball.
- The girl's cone: I can't tell whether it still has ice cream on it or is empty. One reading is that the pink scoop the dog is eating fell off her cone; another is that she is eating her own ice cream and the dropped one belongs to someone else.
- The spotted dog's white head merges with the pink ice cream, so the white blob on top of the scoop could be read as the dog's head or as something white on the ice cream.
- The jumping person could be a woman or a teenager. They might be dancing, cheering, or just jumping for joy; I can't see any reason for it.
- The tan dog on the left might be sleeping or just lying down. Its eyes are hard to make out.
- The cat could be stalking the bird or just walking low. Its pose suggests it is about to pounce.
