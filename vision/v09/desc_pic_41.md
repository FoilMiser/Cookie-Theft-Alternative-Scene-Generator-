# Picture 41

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny park, with a yellow sun at top right, two clouds, a tree on the left, and a grey fence and green bushes across the back. On a bench at the left, a bearded man in a green shirt reads a red book, while a blue bird lands beside him and pecks at a big sandwich left on the bench; he doesn't seem to notice. In the middle, a girl with a brown ponytail and a blue top flies a yellow diamond kite high in the sky. Just behind her, a brown dog with a red collar has its nose in a pink dessert with sprinkles that is lying on the ground, and a small girl in a pink dress stands nearby holding what looks like an ice-cream cone to her mouth. A woman in a yellow dress stands at the back taking a photo with a camera, and on the far right an older man in a cap and brown vest waves one hand high, with little lines by his hand as if he is calling out or waving hard. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a yellow balloon by the slide, and the slide itself is empty, as are the red seesaw in the front left and the sandbox (with its bucket and shovel) in the front right. In the front centre an older woman with grey hair and glasses walks along reading an open book, heading straight toward a cardboard box on the grass, so she looks about to trip over it. Something that could not really happen: in the front, a white dog (or cat) with brown patches sits up holding a newspaper open in its paws, as if reading it. An orange cat lies flat on the grass in the bottom right corner.
