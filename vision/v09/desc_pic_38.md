# Picture 38

## Free description
This is a sunny day at a playground, with a yellow sun at top left, two white clouds, a green hedge and a grey rail fence along the back, and a tree on the right. On the left there is an empty slide with a red frame, a grey ladder and an orange-and-yellow chute, and a green rubbish bin stands by the fence near the bottom of the slide. A grey rock lies on the grass in the front left, and there is a small bush at the far left edge. On the right there is a red seesaw with two people on it. A small girl in a pink dress, with dark hair in a bun and yellow shoes, sits on the left end holding the handle; her end is slightly raised and her feet are off the ground. A young woman with a brown ponytail, blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes sits on the right (lower) end with her hands resting on her knee and her feet flat on the ground. They are facing each other, as if playing on the seesaw together. Apart from them the playground is empty and quiet.
