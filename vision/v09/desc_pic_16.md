# Picture 16

## Free description

This is a quiet, sunny park with rolling green hills and a line of bushes in the background, a bright sun in the top left and two white clouds. A sandy path winds from the front of the picture back toward the hills on the left, past a large tree. In the middle of the grass there is a tall black lamp post with a green rubbish bin beside it. A brown cardboard box is hanging in mid-air, tilted, above the grass to the left of the lamp post, with a grey shadow on the ground below it. Nobody is near it, so it seems to be floating. On the right is an oval pond with reeds/cattails at each end, and a white duck is swimming calmly on it. In the front, an older man in a cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat with braces and dark trousers sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket with a brown picnic basket beside him; his eyes are closed and he is smiling, looking relaxed or dozing in the sun. To his right is a round flower bed full of purple, orange, red, pink and yellow flowers, there is a grey rock on the grass in front, and another green bin is partly visible at the bottom-right edge. Apart from the floating box, the scene is peaceful and not much is happening.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The cardboard box is floating in the air with no one holding or throwing it. Its shadow is on the grass below and slightly to the left, next to the bin. It could be read as the box falling, being blown by wind, or about to land near/in the bin, but nothing in the picture explains why it is up there. This looks impossible or deliberately odd.
- The man's closed eyes and smile could mean he is enjoying the sun, laughing, or asleep.
- The thing beside the man could be a picnic basket or a bucket; it is brown with a handle and looks empty or closed from this angle.
- The man is sitting partly off the blanket, with his legs on the grass next to the basket. That isn't wrong, just a little awkward.
