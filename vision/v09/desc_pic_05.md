# Picture 05

## Free description
This is a sunny town street or pedestrian plaza in front of a row of colourful two-storey shops with striped awnings (red-and-white, green-and-white, orange-and-white), and the sun is in the top-right corner. In the centre a woman in a yellow dress sits on a wooden bench with her eyes closed, happily licking a pink ice-cream cone. A brown dog with a red collar or bandana is stretching up toward her from beside the bench, looking up as if it wants her ice cream. Behind them, near a black lamp post, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping the pavement with a broom. On the right is a fruit stall with a teal-and-white awning and crates of red, orange, yellow and green fruit, with a cardboard box on the ground next to it. In the front, a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is sitting up and holding an open newspaper as if reading it, which is impossible. A small orange cat is lying on the pavement at the bottom right, a red bird is flying over the rooftops at the top left, and two bicycles are parked in bike racks: a teal one at the back and a red one at the front.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The white dog reading a newspaper is impossible; it sits like a person and holds the paper up.
- The brown dog could be begging for or about to grab the ice cream, or it could just be greeting the woman. Its body is stretched out diagonally, and I could not tell whether its front paws are on the ground or raised.
- The orange cat could be lying asleep, or crouching low as if about to pounce. Its pose is small and hard to read.
- The woman eating ice cream has her eyes closed, so she does not seem to notice the dog.
- The bike racks are grey loops that overlap the wheels and frames; they look like they pass through the bikes, which is how bike racks normally look, but the overlap is a little confusing.
- The red bird has an odd shape (one wing raised awkwardly), but it reads as flying.
