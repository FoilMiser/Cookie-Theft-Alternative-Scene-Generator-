# Picture 04

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a gate on the left, a stepping-stone path, a birdhouse on a pole sticking up behind the fence, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A girl with a brown ponytail, in a blue hoodie and dark trousers, stands in the middle of the lawn holding the nozzle of a garden hose up near her face with both hands and looking straight into it, as if wondering why no water is coming out. The hose runs from the nozzle down across the grass to a hose reel next to the flower bed. A white goat with small horns stands right on top of the hose, which would squash it and block the water. It looks like if the goat steps off, the water will suddenly spray the girl in the face. Having a goat in a back garden is itself a bit unusual. There are two potted plants on the grass on the left, a teal watering can near the path at the front, and an empty wooden bench by the house.
