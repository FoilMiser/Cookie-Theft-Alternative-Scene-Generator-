# Picture 13

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a sun in the top right. A small girl in a pink dress near the house is flying an orange kite with a bow tail high in the sky, holding its long string. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks past a clothesline that has blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel pegged on it, and she is holding something white and open, like a book or papers; a blue bucket stands on the grass near her. On the right a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits smiling on a swing, and further right a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers has both arms up reaching for his blue cap, which the wind has blown off into the air along with some leaves (wind lines are drawn). At the bottom left a young man in a blue hoodie sits on a bench happily eating a pink ice cream cone, while a brown dog with a red bandana stands up on its back legs beside him, begging for the ice cream. In the middle an orange cat is crouched and stalking toward a red bird on the ground, so the cat looks about to pounce on the bird. A blue bird sits on top of the fence near the tree, and a white dog with brown spots is in a play-bow on the stepping-stone path at the bottom right. There is a flower bed, a hose reel by the bench, a red dog bowl of food, and a grey wheelbarrow at the front, and the wheelbarrow's wheel looks like a square box, which is impossible for a wheel.

- The woman's white object could be an open book she is reading while walking, or papers/laundry she is about to hang; it looks most like an open book.
- It's windy enough to blow the man's cap off and fly a kite, but the laundry on the line hangs straight down, which is a bit inconsistent.
- The person on the bench could be a teenage boy or a young man.
- The blue bird appears to sit on the top edge of the fence right where it meets the tree; it could also be read as sitting in the tree.
- The kite string runs across in front of the fence and tree up to the kite; it seems normal.
