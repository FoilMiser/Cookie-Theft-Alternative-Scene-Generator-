# Picture 14

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a tall wooden fence across the back with a wooden gate on the left, and a stepping-stone path leading from the gate to the front left. The sun is in the top left with two white clouds. In the middle a woman in a yellow dress is reading a red book in a sitting position with her knees bent, but there is no chair, bench or anything under her, so she is sitting on thin air, which is impossible. In the front centre a little girl in a pink dress is kicking an orange ball with one leg stretched out. To the right an older man with a grey beard, a blue-grey cap, a brown vest and white shirt is kneeling on one knee beside a flower bed full of colourful flowers, reaching in to tend the plants, with a small pot of pink flowers in front of the bed. There are two potted green plants near the fence on the left and a teal watering can on the grass at the front left. A tree stands behind the house and a bush is next to it. The only clearly wrong thing is the woman sitting with nothing beneath her.
