# Picture 27

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house on the left, a fence with a gate at the back right, and a stepping-stone path on the right. A boy or young man in a red shirt and blue jeans sits on a bench in the front left reading a red book. Beside him, a woman with a dark ponytail, in a purple top and teal trousers, is watering the flower bed with a frying pan, which is pouring a stream of water onto the flowers. That is clearly wrong, especially since a real teal watering can sits unused on the grass at the front right. In the centre, a washing line between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel, and a black and white cat is walking along the line like a tightrope. A little girl in a pink dress stands below, holding a blue and a yellow balloon in one hand and reaching up with the other toward a red balloon that has floated away. In the front, an orange cat is creeping toward a blue bird that is pecking at the ground, so the cat seems about to pounce. On the right is a swing set with one empty red swing, and a white dog with brown spots lies flat on the path. The scene is lively, with several small things about to happen.
