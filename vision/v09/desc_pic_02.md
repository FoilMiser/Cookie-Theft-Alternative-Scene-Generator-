In the middle of the grass a tan dog with floppy ears and a red collar is sitting up on its haunches like a person and holding an open newspaper in its front paws as if reading it, which is impossible. On the winding path at the bottom right a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is lying stretched out on its belly. A blue bird is perched in the leaves of the big tree on the right. There is a flower bed at the bottom left and a green trash can partly cut off in the bottom-left corner.
