# Picture 07

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden seen the other way round: the cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, the sun is in the top-right corner, and a wooden gate and stepping-stone path are on the right. A woman with a dark ponytail, in a purple top and teal trousers, stands at a flower bed next to the house pouring water from a teal watering can. Something impossible is happening with the water: instead of falling down onto the flowers, the stream leaves the spout, dips briefly, then curves upward into the air above the flowers, with droplets at the top. Water is flowing upward against gravity. Behind her a clothesline is strung between two wooden posts, with a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel or cloth with red stripes pegged on it. Two potted plants sit on the grass near the gate, and an empty grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles is resting on the lawn at the front.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water going upward from the watering can is impossible. It could be read as water flowing the wrong way, or as a small fountain or spray shooting up, but it clearly comes out of the can's spout.
- The droplets at the top of the stream could be falling back down onto the flowers, or still rising; they are just floating above the bed.
- The yellow striped item on the line could be a towel, a T-shirt or a tea towel.
- Nobody is near the clothesline or the wheelbarrow; they seem to be just background.
