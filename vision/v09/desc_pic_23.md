# Picture 23

## Free description
This is a public playground under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds, so it looks like rain is coming. On the left, a purple kite is caught in the branches of a large tree. A small child with curly hair, in a yellow top and orange shorts, stands below it holding the string up as if trying to pull the kite free. Beside the tree is a red seesaw. A little girl in a pink dress sits on the raised left end, and a bearded man in a green shirt sits on the lowered right end reading a book, seemingly ignoring her. Behind them, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a bench reading a red book. Next to the bench, a person in a blue hoodie stands pointing up at the sky with an open mouth, perhaps noticing the clouds. In the centre front, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans holds a camera to his face and is taking a picture toward the left of the scene. Behind him is a swing set with one empty red swing. On the right are a red and yellow slide, a sandpit with a red bucket and a blue spade in it, and a woman or girl in a yellow dress licking a pink ice-cream cone. Eating ice cream and reading outdoors while a storm gathers is a bit odd, and the kite is stuck.
