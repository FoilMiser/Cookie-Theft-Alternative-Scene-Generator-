# Picture 24

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden, with the sun in the top left and two white clouds. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the right, and a wooden fence with a gate on the left runs across the back. In the front left, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands on the path holding a giant fork. It is shaped like an ordinary dinner fork but is nearly as tall as he is. He is jabbing it into the path near a stepping stone, which kicks up dust and a small pile of rubble with some white bits in it. Behind him is a metal swing set with one empty red swing. In the background, an older man with a grey beard and a flat cap, in a white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers, is painting the fence blue. One section of the fence is already blue, and a paint tin sits on the grass by his feet. A white goat with small horns stands in the middle of the lawn with its head down, eating a tuft of grass. A teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow sit on the grass at the front, and there is a flower bed by the house. The giant table fork used as a digging tool is clearly odd or impossible, and a goat in a back garden is unusual.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The giant fork could be read as a garden fork or pitchfork, but it has the flat, rounded shape of a dinner fork, scaled up hugely.
- The man may be digging up the path, breaking a stepping stone, or trying to dig a hole. The white bits in the rubble could be broken stone, paper or litter.
- The goat could possibly be read as a sheared sheep or lamb, but the horns and shape suggest a goat. Its body bends at a slightly awkward angle.
- The painter is painting the fence blue rather than a wood colour. That may be intended or just a colour choice.
- There is a large dark shadow under the man with the fork, spreading off to the left.
- Nothing looks like it is floating.
