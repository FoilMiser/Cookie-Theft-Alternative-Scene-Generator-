## Unsure or ambiguous

- The kite overlaps the tree's leaves. It could be (a) caught or tangled in the tree, or (b) flying in the sky just in front of the tree. The string sags in a curve, which slightly suggests it is stuck rather than flying high.
- The man's hands could be held out to catch the ball or could be clapping. The ball is above and to the right of his hands, heading up and to the left, so it might go over his head or toward the kite girl's legs instead of into his hands.
- The dotted line makes it look like the boy threw the ball to the man. It could also be read as the ball's path on its way to the boy, but the trail ends at the boy's hand, so "boy threw it" seems more likely.
- The cat could be asleep, resting or rolling on its back next to its food. It isn't eating.
- The kite girl looks like she is standing against or partly overlapping the fence. I read it as her standing on the grass just in front of it.
- The girl on the swing has her hands slightly outside the ropes rather than clearly gripping them. It's a small drawing detail and not obviously wrong.
