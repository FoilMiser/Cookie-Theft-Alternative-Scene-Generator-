# Picture 36

## Free description
This is a sunny day at a playground, with a yellow sun at top left, two white clouds, a long green hedge and a grey rail fence across the back, and a tree on the right. On the left there is a slide with a red frame, a grey ladder and an orange-and-yellow chute; a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits at the bottom of the slide, leaning back with his legs stretched out on the grass. In front on the left there is a wooden sandbox with sand, a red bucket and a blue shovel in it, and a grey rock beside it, but nobody is playing in the sandbox. On the right there is a red seesaw. An older man with grey hair, a grey cap, a brown vest, a white shirt and dark trousers sits on the low end of the seesaw, reading an open book (or a paper) instead of watching what is going on. A small girl in a pink dress with dark hair in a bun and yellow shoes sits on the high end, holding the handle, with her feet dangling in the air. The man's weight is holding his end down, so the girl is stuck up in the air while he reads. Nothing looks clearly impossible, but it is a bit funny to see a grown man riding a seesaw with a child and reading at the same time.
