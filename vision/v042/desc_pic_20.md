## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a blue sky, a sun at top right and two white clouds. A cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, a wooden fence runs across the back, and on the far right there is a gate with a paved path leading down to the bottom-right corner. An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller; the boards beside the house are already blue and an open paint tin stands at her feet. In the centre-right, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers holds both hands out in front of him, ready to catch. A little girl in a pink dress on the path at the right has just thrown a red ball, and a dotted line shows it arcing from her hand up toward the man, who is about to catch it. Strangely, a brown cardboard box is hanging in the sky above the fence in the middle of the picture, tilted, with nothing holding it up and nobody near it. A teal watering can sits on the grass near the path, and there is a bench and a flowerbed on the left.
