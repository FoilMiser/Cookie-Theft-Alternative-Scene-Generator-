# Picture 04

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny playground in a park with a black railing fence across the back. On the far left a man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers runs to the left, with speed lines behind him. Near the slide a tan dog with a red neckerchief sits up on its back legs, holding an open newspaper in its front paws as if reading it, which is impossible. In the centre a teal bicycle stands on its kickstand, but its wheels are square instead of round. In front of the bicycle a white dog with brown spots crouches in a play bow, sniffing at a yellow-green tennis ball. Behind them a child with braided hair, a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts holds a purple and a red balloon in one hand. The child reaches up with the other hand towards a yellow balloon that has floated away above their head, with its string dangling free. Further right an old man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown vest holds a camera up to his eye, pointing it to the left. On a bench a man in a red shirt and jeans reads a red book while a little blue bird beside him on the bench pecks at his sandwich, which he does not notice. In the front right a girl in a pink dress holds a kite string with both hands raised, but the purple kite is stuck in the branches of the tree. A red bird flies across the top right of the sky.
