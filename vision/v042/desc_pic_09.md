## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with no people in it. A tall wooden picket fence runs across the back, with a wooden gate in it on the far left, and a pale paved path leads from that gate down towards the bottom-left corner. An orange cat with a white chest is walking along the very top of the fence, roughly in the middle, heading to the right, with its tail held up. On the right is the side of a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door with a grey step. A tree shows above the fence just left of the house. On the lawn are two potted plants near the fence, a round green bush in the middle, another bush beside the house, and a round bed of colourful flowers in front of the house. At the front are a teal watering can on the grass and an empty grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles. The cat looks steady and nothing else is moving, so nothing in particular seems about to happen.
