## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. A girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes is sitting on the red seat of a swing set on the right of centre. She is swinging towards our left, and short motion lines trail behind her on the right. Her eyes are shut and she is smiling, so she looks happy and is enjoying it. No one is pushing her. Near the gate a birdhouse with a red roof sits on top of a tall pole; it looks empty. In front of the house there is an empty wooden bench and a round flower bed, with a bush beside the house and a tree behind it. At the front of the lawn are an empty grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can. Nothing looks wrong or about to go wrong.
