## Free description
A sunny park with the sun top left, two clouds, green hills, a big tree on the left, a sandy winding path, a tall black street lamp, a blue pond with bulrushes on the right, a bed of colourful flowers at the front right and a green rubbish bin in the bottom right corner. In the middle, a man with short dark hair, in a green t-shirt and khaki trousers, is standing on the grass in front of the pond eating a pink ice cream cone. A white duck is swimming on the pond just behind him, and in the picture its orange beak lines up right next to his ice cream, so it looks as if the duck is reaching over to peck or snatch the ice cream. The man doesn't seem to notice; he is looking ahead and eating. A grey rock sits on the grass at the front centre. Apart from the duck's beak seeming to reach the ice cream, nothing looks wrong.
