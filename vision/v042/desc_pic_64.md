## Free description
This is a sunny back garden: a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, and a wooden fence with a gate at the right. There is a sun at top right and two clouds. Next to the house door, a brown dog with a red collar sits facing the door. A red lead hangs from its collar and lies on the ground in a loop, as if the dog is waiting to be taken for a walk or to be let in. In the centre-left, a man with dark skin, a green shirt and beige trousers stands by the flower bed holding a teal watering can. The water is odd: instead of pouring down onto the flowers, a thin blue stream arcs upward from the can's spout into the air, with drops at the top. That looks wrong. On the right, a man with brown hair and a beard, a red shirt and blue trousers stands on the lawn raking a small pile of brown leaves. A paved path runs from the gate to the front-right corner.
