## Free description
A back garden under a grey, overcast sky with three dark storm clouds, as if rain is coming. A wooden fence runs across the back, with a gate at the far left and a birdhouse on a tall pole beside it. A paved path runs down the left side. Left of centre is a swing frame with one empty red seat. In the middle, a white goat with curved horns is bending down to eat a tuft of grass. It is tied by a rope to a small stake in the ground. Next to the goat, a boy with dark skin and braided hair, in a yellow shirt and orange shorts, points up at the dark clouds. On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door. A brown dog with a red collar sits by the door holding a red lead in its mouth, as if it wants to go for a walk. A colourful flower bed is in front of the house. A teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow are on the grass at the front. It looks as though it is about to rain.
