## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden picket fence across the back, a tree behind the fence, and a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the right. On the left a man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers walks along a paved path in front of a wooden gate. He holds a red lead attached to a white dog with brown spots, which trots ahead of him to the right onto the grass. On the right, near the house, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers kneels beside a round flower bed full of colourful flowers. She reaches towards the flowers as if tending, planting or picking them. A small terracotta pot with a pink flower sits at the front edge of the bed. The odd thing is a closed cardboard box floating in mid-air in the sky above the fence, tilted at an angle, with nothing holding it up. Nobody in the scene is looking at the box. It might be about to fall into the garden, but there are no motion lines to show that it is moving.
