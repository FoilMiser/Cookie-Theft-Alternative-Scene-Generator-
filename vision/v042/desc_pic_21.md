## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a blue sky, a sun at top right and two white clouds. A cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, with a tree behind it and a flowerbed in front. A wooden fence runs across the back, with a gate on the far right and a paved path leading from it down to the bottom-right corner. On that path stands a woman or girl with dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes, bending slightly and looking down. She is holding a huge silver fork, shaped like an ordinary dinner fork but about as long as her legs, and is pushing its prongs along the path through a small pile of dirt or gravel, which is throwing up a little cloud of dust. A red dustpan lies on the path just in front of the pile, so she seems to be sweeping the dirt into it using the fork. Using a giant eating fork as a broom is odd and not a normal garden tool. An empty wheelbarrow sits in the middle of the lawn, and there are two potted plants and bushes by the fence.
