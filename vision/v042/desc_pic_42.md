# pic_42

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny town street with a row of four colourful shops (cream, peach, pale green and pink) and a wide paved pavement in front. A blue-and-white beach ball is stuck in the branches of a tree on the left, and a small boy in a yellow shirt is pointing up at it. Nearby a brown puppy with a red collar sits next to an open newspaper that stands upright on the ground, with one paw on it as if it is reading it or has grabbed it. A girl in a pink dress is jumping in the air with her arms up, looking happy. Further back, a boy in a red cap sweeps leaves into a pile, and a man in a red shirt walks past carrying a yellow shopping bag. In the middle, a woman on a bench is reading a red book while a blue bird on the bench beside her pecks at her sandwich; she hasn't noticed. A worker in an orange cap and hi-vis vest holds a watering can by a flower planter, but the stream of water shoots up into the air instead of pouring down onto the flowers, which looks wrong. On the right, an old man is walking while reading a book and is heading straight for a cardboard box on the ground, so he looks about to trip over it. There is also a fruit stall on the right, another box by it, a street lamp, potted plants in the bottom corners and a red bird flying near the sun.
