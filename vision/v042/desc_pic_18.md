# pic_18

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a blue sky, a bright sun at top left and two white clouds. A wooden fence runs across the back with a gate at the far left and a paved path leading down to the bottom-left corner; a cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the right. On the left of the lawn is a metal swing frame, and a small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is swinging on the red seat, smiling, with motion lines behind him showing he is moving. At the back right, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller; a section of fence boards is already blue and an open paint tin sits by his feet. In the middle front, a girl or young woman with dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers is reading a red book in a sitting position. Oddly, there is no chair, bench or anything under her; she is posed as if seated on an invisible chair. Around the lawn are a teal watering can, an empty grey wheelbarrow, a hose reel with a hose, and a flowerbed.
