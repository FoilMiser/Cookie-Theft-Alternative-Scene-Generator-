## Free description
A sunny back garden: a cream house with a red roof, window and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a wooden gate on the right, and a paved path leading to the gate. A man with short dark hair, green shirt and khaki trousers stands in the middle holding the nozzle of a green garden hose and looking down at it; only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel on the left across the lawn, and a white goat with small horns and a red collar is standing on the hose with its feet. Between the reel and the goat the hose looks swollen/flattened in a bulge, as if the water is blocked there by the goat. So the goat is stopping the water and the man is puzzled why nothing comes out. On the right, on the paved path, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, teal cardigan and grey skirt is sweeping a pile of brown leaves with a broom. There is an empty wheelbarrow on the lawn in front, a flower bed near the house, and two potted plants by the fence. A goat in a back garden is unusual but not impossible.
