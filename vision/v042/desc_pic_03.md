# Picture 03

## Free description
This is the same kind of sunny back garden as in picture 2: a wooden fence at the back, a tree, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. On the left a man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers stands on the paved path, sweeping a small pile of brown dead leaves with a yellow broom. In the centre a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and black trousers holds a green garden hose in both hands. He is looking closely at the nozzle, which points up and to the right. No water is coming out. The hose runs across the grass from a hose reel by the house. A white goat with small horns and a red collar is standing on the hose with its back feet. Just behind the goat, between it and the reel, the hose is swollen into a fat bulge, as if the goat is blocking the water and pressure is building up. It looks like something is about to happen: when the goat steps off, water will probably burst out of the nozzle into the boy's face. There is also a birdhouse on a tall pole, two potted plants by the fence, a flower bed and an empty wooden bench in the front right corner.

## Inventory
- Man, left, front-middle: dark skin, short dark hair, green T-shirt, khaki trousers. He is sweeping leaves on the path with a yellow broom.
- Boy, centre, middle: red baseball cap, blue hoodie, black trousers, white shoes. He is holding the hose nozzle with both hands and peering at it.
- Goat, centre-right, front-middle: white with small grey horns and a red collar. It is standing on the hose and looking to the left.
- Garden hose: centre to right. Green or teal, running from the reel by the house across the grass to the boy's hands. It has a big bulge just behind the goat's feet.
- Hose reel: right, by the house corner. Round and dark teal, with the hose attached.
- Hose nozzle: in the boy's hands, pointed up and right. Nothing is coming out.
- Broom and pile of leaves: left, on the path at the man's feet. Brown leaves being swept.
- Birdhouse on a pole: left-centre, back. A small red-roofed birdhouse on a tall wooden post.
- Two potted plants: left-centre, by the fence. Green leafy plants in terracotta pots.
- Flower bed: right-centre. Colourful flowers in a brown soil oval.
- Bench: front right. Wooden and empty.
- House: right. Cream walls, red roof, window and blue door.
- Gate (far left), fence, tree behind the fence, bush by the house, sun (top-left) and two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- I read the bulge in the hose as water backed up because the goat is standing on it. Another reading is that it is just a thick or kinked section of hose. The bulge is quite large and looks flattened, so it might also look like a puddle or a second hose lying there.
- Near the boy's legs the hose curls down and runs very close to, or slightly over, his right leg. It is hard to tell whether it passes in front of or behind him.
- It is not clear whether the boy knows the goat is on the hose. He seems only to be puzzled by the nozzle.
