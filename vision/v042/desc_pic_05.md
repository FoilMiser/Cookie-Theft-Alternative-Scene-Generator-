# Picture 05

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills and bushes behind, two clouds and a sun in the top right. In the centre-left foreground a man with brown hair and a moustache, in a red T-shirt and blue jeans, stands on the grass at the near edge of a blue pond. He is holding a fishing rod with both hands. The rod stretches out over the water, and the line hangs down to a red-and-white float bobbing on the surface, with a small ripple ring around it. He seems to be fishing calmly and watching the float. The ripple could mean a fish is nibbling, or the float has just landed. Around the park are a tall black lamp post with a green rubbish bin beside it, an empty wooden bench, a winding sandy path on the right, trees on the left and right, a grey rock, and a colourful flower bed in the front left. Nothing looks wrong or impossible.
