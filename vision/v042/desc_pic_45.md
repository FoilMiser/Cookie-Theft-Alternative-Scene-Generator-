## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back and a gate with a paved path on the right. A man with a moustache in a red shirt and blue jeans is watering the flower bed with a hose, which runs from a hose reel near a wooden bench at the bottom left. Further back, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence blue with a long roller. A section of fence beside her is already blue, and a paint tin and paint tray sit on the grass. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has clearly stepped in the paint tray: its paws are blue and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the tray across the lawn to where the dog is now, heading toward the path. In the front centre stands an old woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt, hands on her hips, with what looks like an upside-down white bucket on her head as a hat. That bucket on her head looks odd and silly. Nobody seems to have noticed the dog's paw prints yet.
