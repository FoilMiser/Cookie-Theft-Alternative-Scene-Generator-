## Free description
This is a park on a sunny but windy day: white wind streaks blow across the sky and the scene, and leaves are being blown off a tree on the left. A young woman or girl with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers is walking along a winding sand-coloured path, eating a pink ice-cream cone. On the right is a blue pond with reeds at each end. An old woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt stands near the pond, reaching up with one hand and looking alarmed. An orange cap is flying through the air above the edge of the pond, blown by the wind, and it is about to land in the water. Her hand is well below it, so she won't catch it. In the middle there is an empty wooden bench, a tall black lamp post and a green litter bin, with a grey rock on the grass at the front. Rolling green hills, hedges and a couple of trees make up the background.
