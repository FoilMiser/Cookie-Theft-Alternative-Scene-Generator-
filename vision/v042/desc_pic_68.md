# pic_68

## Free description
A sunny back garden: a cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, a wooden picket fence runs across the back, a gate is on the far right and a paved path runs down the right side. A washing line between two wooden posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel or cloth with red stripes, pinned with pegs. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers, with a dark ponytail, stands beside a round flower bed on the left holding a teal watering can, apparently watering the colourful flowers. The water, however, looks wrong. Instead of pouring down from the spout onto the flowers, a light-blue stream comes out of the spout and curves upwards into the air above the flowers, with drops at the top, as if the water were flying up like a fountain. Two potted plants stand by the fence on the right, and an empty grey wheelbarrow is on the lawn at the front. Nobody else is in the garden.
