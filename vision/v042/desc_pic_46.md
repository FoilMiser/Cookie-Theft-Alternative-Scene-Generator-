## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a gate with a paved path on the right. An old woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, sits on a wooden bench at the bottom left sipping a drink through a straw from a red cup. A woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is watering the flower bed, but she is pouring the water out of a black frying pan instead of a watering can, which is an odd thing to use. In the front centre, a white goat with small horns and a red collar is tied by a rope to a stake in the ground and is eating a tuft of grass. Two potted plants and a round bush stand on the lawn toward the back right. Everything else looks calm and ordinary.
