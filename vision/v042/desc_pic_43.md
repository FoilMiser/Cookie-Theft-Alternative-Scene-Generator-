## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left and a wooden picket fence across the back. A man with brown hair and a moustache, wearing a red shirt and blue jeans, is painting the fence blue with a long-handled paint roller. A few boards to his left are already painted bright blue, and a paint tin stands by his feet. A grey cat is walking along the top of the fence right on the freshly painted blue section, so it may get blue paint on its paws. Strangely, an orange fish is sitting on a branch of the tree behind the house, which doesn't make sense, since fish don't live in trees. The cat is walking away from the fish, toward the man. On the right there is an empty swing set with a red seat, a wooden gate and a paved path. A grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can are on the lawn at the front. A flower bed with colourful flowers and a small bush are in front of the house.
