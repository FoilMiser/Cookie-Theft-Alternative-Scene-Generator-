## Free description
It's a sunny back garden with a cream house on the left (blue door, red roof), a wooden fence along the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path down the right side. In the centre, a boy in a red T-shirt and blue jeans holds the nozzle of a garden hose up near his face. Only a single drop is coming out, and he is peering at it as if puzzled why there's no water. The hose runs back along the grass to a hose reel by the flower bed. Halfway along it, a tan dog with a red collar is standing right on top of the hose, and the hose bulges out just where the dog is standing. That looks like the dog is pinching it and blocking the water. It looks as if the boy may get sprayed in the face when the dog steps off. Further back on the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is digging a hole in the lawn with a shovel, with a mound of dirt beside him. On the path at the right, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses is sweeping dirt toward a red dustpan, but she is using a huge silver dinner fork as tall as she is instead of a broom. That is clearly odd or impossible. Dust puffs rise from the pile.
