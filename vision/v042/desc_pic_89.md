## Free description
This is a busy, sunny park scene with a pond, a winding path, a lamp post, a bench, trees and a flower bed, and lots going on at once. On the far left, a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is sitting up and holding open a newspaper as if reading it. That is impossible and clearly odd. Next to it, an older man with grey hair and moustache, a grey cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is holding out a frying pan and pouring water out of it onto the flower bed, using a frying pan instead of a watering can. That is also odd. On the pond a white duck is swimming (motion lines behind it) towards pieces of bread floating on the water. A girl in a pink dress standing at the edge of the pond is holding a small paper bag and throwing bread to it. In the front centre, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts with braided hair is rubbing their eyes and crying, holding an empty ice-cream cone. Their pink ice cream lies splattered on the grass, where a brown dog with a red collar is licking it up. On the path to the right, an older woman with a grey bun and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, is sweeping a pile of brown leaves with a broom. On the far right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is holding a string and looking up at his purple kite, which is stuck in the branches of a tree. Behind the path, a white goat tied to a stake is eating grass, and a blue bird flies in the sky near the lamp post.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Clearly impossible or odd things: (1) a dog reading a newspaper; (2) a man watering flowers by pouring water from a frying pan.
- The crying child is shown rubbing an eye. I read it as crying over the dropped ice cream, but it could be read as just rubbing a tired or itchy eye.
- The kite is stuck in the tree. Reading 1: the boy is tugging the string to free it. Reading 2: he is just looking up at it, wondering what to do.
- The girl at the pond is standing a little away from the water's edge, but the bread pieces and the open bag make it fairly clear she is feeding the duck.
- Nothing appears to float unintentionally, except the bread on the water, which is intended. The water stream from the pan lands in the flower bed as expected.
