# Picture 01

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park, with green hills and a row of bushes in the background, a blue sky, two white clouds and a bright sun in the top right. In the left-centre foreground an elderly man with grey hair and a moustache stands on the grass. He wears a blue-grey flat cap, a white long-sleeved shirt, a brown vest and dark trousers. He holds an ice cream cone with a pink scoop up to his mouth and seems to be eating or licking it. His other arm hangs at his side. Behind him is an oval blue pond with reeds or cattails at both ends. A tall black lamp post stands in the middle of the park. On the right is an empty wooden bench beside a winding sandy footpath, and a tree with a red bird perched on one of its branches. Nothing seems about to happen, and nothing looks wrong or impossible. It is a calm, ordinary scene.
