## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, and a wooden fence across the back. A girl in a purple top and teal trousers stands right of centre holding a garden hose nozzle up close to her face, peering into it. Only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel on the left, across the lawn, back to her. A small white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is standing right on top of the hose in the front centre. Just behind the dog the hose is swollen into a big fat bulge, so the dog is blocking the water. If the dog steps off, the water will probably burst out and spray the girl in the face. Behind them is a washing line between two posts with a pair of jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel or shirt pegged on it. On the right is a swing set with an empty red seat, a wooden gate in the fence, and a paved path running from the gate to the bottom of the picture. There is also a round flower bed, a bush by the house, and a tree behind the house.
