## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, a winding sandy path on the left, a tall lamp post, trees and a blue pond with cattails. A boy with short dark curly hair, in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts, stands on the grass at the left edge of the pond. He holds a small brown paper bag in one hand and reaches the other hand out over the water, throwing food. Small cream-coloured crumbs, like bits of bread, are scattered on the water near the edge. A white duck swims toward the crumbs with its head down, pecking at them, with little wake lines behind it. The boy is feeding the duck. A grey rock lies at the front, a flowerbed is in the bottom right, and a green rubbish bin stands in the bottom right corner. Nothing looks wrong or impossible.
