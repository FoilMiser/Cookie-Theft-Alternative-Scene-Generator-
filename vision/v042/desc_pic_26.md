# pic_26

## Free description
A sunny playground with a long dark metal railing fence across the back, hedges behind it, a tree on the left, and a sun and two clouds in the sky. On the left is a red seesaw on a grey triangular pivot. A small girl with dark hair in a pink dress sits on the left end, which is high up in the air; her feet dangle and her mouth is open in an "O", so she looks surprised, worried or like she is calling out. On the low right end sits an elderly man with grey hair and moustache, a blue-grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers, reading an open book and paying no attention to the girl; his weight keeps her stuck up in the air. On the right is a slide with a red frame, blue platform, yellow-and-orange chute and a ladder at the back. A child with curly dark hair, yellow top and orange shorts is partway down the slide with arms raised, apparently enjoying the ride. A green rubbish bin stands by the fence in the middle and a grey rock lies in the bottom-right corner. The main odd thing is the mismatched seesaw: a grown man reading on one end while a little girl is left stranded high on the other.
