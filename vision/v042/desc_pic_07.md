# Picture 07

## Free description
This is a quiet, sunny playground with a black railing fence and green bushes along the back, a sun in the top left and two clouds. In the front left a child with braided hair, a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts kneels in a wooden sandbox. The child is building a sandcastle with three towers, the middle one topped by a small red flag, and holds a small blue spade or tool in one hand. On the left behind the sandbox is a slide with a red frame, a ladder and a yellow-and-orange chute. Nobody is on it. On the right is a red seesaw tilted with its left end up in the air and its right end down on the ground. Nobody is on it either. There is a green rubbish bin by the fence, a tree on the right, a few bushes, and a grey rock next to the sandbox. Nothing seems about to happen and nothing looks wrong. The child is simply playing alone.
