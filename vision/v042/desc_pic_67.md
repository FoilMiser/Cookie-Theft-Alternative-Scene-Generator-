## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden picket fence across the back, a wooden gate on the far left and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. In the middle, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence blue with a long-handled paint roller; one section of the fence is already bright blue. Behind her on the grass are a paint tray with blue paint and a paint tin. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is walking away from the paint tray to the left, and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the tray to the dog. The dog has clearly stepped in the paint and is tracking it across the lawn. The woman faces the fence and does not seem to have noticed. In the front-left corner, an old man with grey hair, a moustache and a flat cap, wearing a white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers, sweeps a small pile of leaves on the paved path with a yellow broom. The dog seems about to carry the blue paint further, possibly towards the path or the old man.
