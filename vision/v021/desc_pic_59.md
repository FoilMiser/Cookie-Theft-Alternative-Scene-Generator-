## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park, with green hills and a line of bushes in the background, a bright sun in the top left and two white clouds. A sandy path winds from the back of the park down to the front on the left side, past a large tree. In the front left, a woman with short dark hair in a yellow dress and red flat shoes is running quickly to the left. Speed lines behind her show how fast she is going, and she is crossing the path with one foot on it and one on the grass. In the middle of the picture there is a tall black lamp post, and a wooden ladder is on the grass just in front of it. The ladder slants up toward the base of the lamp post, but its top doesn't rest on the post or on anything else, so it isn't clear whether it is standing up or lying on the ground. On the right there is an oval pond with reeds or cattails at each end, and a white duck is swimming in it. There is a smaller tree and a bush to the right of the pond, and a green trash can in the bottom right corner. Nobody else is around, and nothing in particular seems about to happen apart from the woman running out of the picture to the left.
