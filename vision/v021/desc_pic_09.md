# pic_09

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden beside a cream-coloured house with a red roof and a blue front door on the left, and a tall wooden fence across the back with a wooden gate at the right end. An older man with grey hair and a grey beard, wearing a blue-grey flat cap, a white shirt, a brown waistcoat/vest and dark trousers, stands in the middle of the lawn watering a flower bed. He holds the nozzle of a long dark-teal garden hose, which runs back across the grass to a hose reel on the ground at the corner of the flower bed, and a stream of water arcs from the nozzle down onto the colourful flowers (red, yellow, purple, pink, orange). In the front right, a brown dog with a red collar/bandana is sitting upright on its bottom like a person, legs stretched out, holding an open newspaper in its front paws and apparently reading it. The dog's paws look like white cartoon hands gripping the paper. A red dog bowl with food in it sits on the grass just in front of and to the right of the dog. Near the fence are a round green bush and two terracotta pots with leafy plants, and a paved path runs from the bottom right corner up to the gate. A tree stands behind the house, two white clouds float in a blue sky, and the sun is in the upper right. The obviously impossible thing is the dog reading a newspaper.
