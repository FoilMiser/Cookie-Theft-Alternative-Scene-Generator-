# Picture 61

## Free description
This is a busy back garden on a sunny day, with a wooden fence across the back, a gate in the fence on the left and a cream-coloured house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. On the left a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is raking leaves near the gate, with a small pile of leaves at his feet, and a birdhouse on a tall pole stands next to him. A small girl in pink is swinging happily on a swing set, with her eyes closed or squinting in a smile. A black-and-white cat is creeping along the top of the fence toward a red bird perched further along the fence, so the cat seems to be stalking the bird. In the tree behind the fence, a blue ball is stuck in the branches, and a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands by the fence pointing up at it. In the middle, a garden sprinkler on a hose is spraying arcs of water that land on a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is getting soaked and has a puddle around it. In front there is an empty grey wheelbarrow, and a white goat with small horns is eating grass in the middle of the garden. Next to the house there is a bed of colourful flowers. On a bench on the right, a woman in a yellow dress is reading a red book, while a blue bird beside her pecks at her sandwich without her noticing. Several things look wrong or about to go wrong: the cat may pounce on the bird, the bird is stealing the sandwich, the box is being ruined by the sprinkler, the ball needs rescuing, and there is a goat loose in a back garden.
