# Picture 17

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden behind a house, with a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate in the fence on the right, and a paved path running from the gate down to the bottom right corner. The house is on the left. It has cream walls, a red roof, a window and a blue door, with a flower bed of mixed coloured flowers and a small bush in front of it. In the middle of the garden there is a washing line strung between two wooden posts, with a pair of blue jeans and a white T-shirt pegged on it. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is standing under the line with both arms raised, pegging the white T-shirt onto the line. On the right, on the path, a child with a brown ponytail, a blue hoodie and dark trousers is kicking an orange ball hard. Motion lines show the ball flying to the left, toward the middle of the garden where the woman and the washing are, so it might hit the laundry, the woman or the flower bed. The odd, impossible thing is that an orange goldfish is sitting in the leaves of the tree behind the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- I cannot tell what the ball will hit. Its path points left toward the washing line and the woman, but it might simply roll onto the lawn or toward the flower bed.
- The child is probably a girl because of the ponytail, but it could be a boy with long hair.
- The fish in the tree is clearly impossible. Fish do not sit in trees, and nothing explains why it is there.
- The woman's hands are at the corner of the T-shirt. She is probably pinning it with a peg, but she could also be taking it down.
