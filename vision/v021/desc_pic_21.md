# Picture 21

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills and trees in the background, a round blue pond with reeds or cattails on the left, a flower bed in the front left, a tall black lamp post in the middle, and a winding sandy path on the right. In the front left, a person with a brown ponytail, a blue hoodie and dark trousers is scattering seed from a bag or carton onto the grass. A red bird and a blue bird are pecking at the seed at their feet. In the middle, near the lamp post, a little girl in a pink dress is standing holding the string of a yellow balloon. On the right, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is sitting on a wooden bench, eating or licking a pink ice-cream cone. A brown dog with a red collar is stretched up in front of him with its front paws raised and its head reaching toward the ice cream, as if begging for it. Nothing is going badly wrong, but the dog looks keen to get some of the man's ice cream.
