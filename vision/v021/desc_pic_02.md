# Picture 02

## Free description
This is the same kind of sunny back garden: a wooden picket fence across the back with a gate on the far left, a paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the back centre, a girl or young woman with a brown ponytail, in a blue hoodie and dark trousers, stands with her arms held out and hands together, ready to catch a red ball that is in the air just in front of her hands. A dotted line shows the ball's path arcing from a little girl on the right, who wears a pink dress and yellow shoes and has her arm out as if she has just thrown it. In the front right, a bearded man with dark skin in a green shirt and khaki trousers kneels on one knee by the round flower bed, reaching toward the flowers; a terracotta pot with a pink flower stands at the front edge of the bed, as if he is about to plant it or has just put it there. Something impossible is happening in the sky: a closed cardboard box is floating, tilted, in mid-air above the fence, and nobody is paying attention to it. The sky is otherwise blue with the sun top left and two white clouds, and there is a tree behind the fence on the right.

- The dotted line makes it look like the little girl threw the ball, but it could also be read as a game of catch in progress where the ball is going either way (the line suggests from the little girl to the older girl).
