# Picture 35

## Free description
This is a windy, sunny back garden with a wooden fence along the back, a gate on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands on the paved path with both arms up and her mouth open in surprise, because the wind has just blown her red cap off; the cap is flying away with some leaves along white wind lines. Next to her is a metal swing set, and its red swing seat is hanging at a tilt as if it is moving. Behind, the washing on the clothesline (a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans) is blowing sideways in the wind. In the middle, a young person in a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white trainers is digging a hole in the lawn with a shovel, with a mound of soil beside the hole. On the right, a man in a red shirt and jeans is watering the flower bed with a teal watering can, but the water is shooting upward in an arc out of the spout, with drops flying up, instead of pouring down onto the flowers — that looks wrong or impossible. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands on the lawn at the front.

- Digger, centre, on the lawn: brown hair, blue hoodie, dark trousers, white trainers; bent over, digging a hole with a shovel.
- Man, right of centre, by the flower bed: brown hair, red T-shirt, blue jeans, dark shoes; holding a teal watering can over the flowers.
- Red cap: in the air above the woman, flying away in the wind with leaves.
- Leaves and wind lines: green/brown leaves near the cap; white wind lines on the left and across the sky.
- Swing set: dark blue-grey A-frame, left of centre; red seat hanging on two ropes, tilted to one side as if swinging or blown.
- Clothesline: two wooden posts with a line; yellow towel with red stripes and blue jeans pegged on it, both blowing out sideways.
- Shovel: held by the digger, blade in the hole with a clump of soil.
- Hole and dirt mound: on the lawn in front of the digger; the dirt mound is to the left of the hole.
- Watering can: teal, held by the man in red; the water stream curves upward out of the spout and drops fly up above it.
- Wheelbarrow: grey with wooden handles, front centre, empty.
- Flower bed: right, oval of brown soil with red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers.
- House: right, cream walls, red roof, window, blue door with a step.
- Tree: behind the house.
- Fence and gate: wooden fence across the back; brown gate at far left.
- Sun and clouds: sun upper left, two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The upward water from the watering can is the clearest oddity. One reading is that it is simply impossible (water does not pour upward from a watering can). Another reading is that the strong wind is blowing the water up, but the wind elsewhere blows sideways, not upward, so this does not really explain it.
- The tilted swing seat could mean someone just got off it, or that the wind is pushing it.
- The digger could be a boy or a young man; the face and size are in between.
- It is unclear why the hole is being dug (planting, or something else); nothing is waiting to be planted.
- The wheelbarrow is empty and unattended; it may be meant to carry the dug soil.
