# Picture 05

## Free description
This is a quiet, sunny back garden with a wooden picket fence, a gate on the far left, a paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the middle of the garden is a washing line strung between two wooden posts, with a yellow top or towel with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it, hanging near the right-hand post. The only person is an old woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, standing at the left edge of the round flower bed on the right and watering the flowers with a teal watering can. The water coming out of the can, however, curves upward into the air with drops at the top, instead of pouring down onto the flowers, which is impossible. On the left, two terracotta pots with green leafy plants sit on the grass, and in the front centre an empty wheelbarrow stands on the lawn. The sky has the sun in the top left and two white clouds, and there is a tree behind the fence on the right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water from the watering can goes upward instead of down; it could be read as an impossible stream or as water being flicked/sprayed up, but it does not look like it is reaching the flowers.
- The yellow striped item on the line could be a shirt, a jumper or a towel.
- The old woman's body and the watering can overlap the front of the flower bed, so it looks a little as if she is standing in or right against the flowers.
- The washing line sags a lot in the middle and both pieces of laundry hang close to the right post rather than spread along the line; not impossible, just slightly unusual.
