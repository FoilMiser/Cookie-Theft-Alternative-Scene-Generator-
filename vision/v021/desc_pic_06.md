# Picture 06

## Free description
This is the same town street or square as in a sunny scene, but here the sky is grey with three dark storm clouds and no sun, so it looks like rain might be coming. A blue bird with a yellow beak is perched on top of the street lamp on the left. Near the shops on the right, an old man in a flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers points up toward the sky with his mouth open, as if saying "look" about the clouds (or perhaps the bird). In the front left a woman with dark hair in a yellow dress and red shoes is happily eating a pink ice-cream cone with her eyes closed. In the middle, a little girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes sits on a wooden bench eating a pink ice cream, and a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar stands up on its back legs beside the bench, begging and looking up at her ice cream. On the right a boy with curly hair in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands holding a yellow balloon. In the front centre a teal bicycle is parked, but its wheels are square instead of round, which is impossible. Around the square are a fruit stall and a cardboard box on the left, flower planters in front of the green shop, a potted plant, two rubbish bins, a tree in a planter and a café table with two chairs.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The bicycle's wheels are square, which could never roll; this looks like a deliberate impossibility or a drawing error.
- The old man is pointing up and slightly left: he could be pointing at the dark storm clouds (warning of rain) or at the bird on the lamp post.
- The woman eating ice cream has a dark mark just under her nose/at her mouth; it could be read as her open mouth, a smear of ice cream, or a small moustache.
- The dog is between the woman and the girl; it faces the girl on the bench, but it could be read as begging from either of them.
- Eating ice cream outdoors under storm clouds is a little odd but not impossible. No rain is actually falling yet.
