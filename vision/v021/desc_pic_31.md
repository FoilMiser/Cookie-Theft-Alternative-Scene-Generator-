# Picture 31

## Free description
This is a sunny, breezy back garden with a wooden fence along the back, a gate on the far left, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. On the left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands on the paved path and is pushing a small pile of dirt into a red dustpan, but instead of a broom she is using a gigantic silver dinner fork, nearly as long as she is tall. In the middle of the lawn, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers has both arms flung up in surprise because the wind has just blown his blue cap off his head; the cap is flying away to the upper left along with some leaves, and white wind lines streak across the sky. In the front of the lawn, a garden sprinkler attached to a long hose is spraying arcs of water to the left, and the water is landing right on a cardboard-looking box that is sitting in a puddle and dripping, so the box is getting soaked. On the right there is a round bed of colourful flowers and a bush next to the house, and a tree behind the house is losing leaves in the wind. The strange things are the giant fork being used as a broom, and the box left in the path of the sprinkler.

## Inventory
- Woman, left front, on the paved path: dark hair in a ponytail, purple short-sleeved top, teal trousers, dark shoes; holding a huge silver fork and using it like a broom to push dirt into a red dustpan.
- Man, centre back, standing on the lawn in front of the fence: dark beard, green shirt, khaki trousers, brown shoes; both arms raised high, startled face, reacting to his cap blowing off.
- Giant fork: silver dinner fork, oversized (about the woman's height), held by the woman, used as a sweeping tool.
- Dirt pile and dust: small grey-brown heap with puffs of dust, at the foot of the path, being pushed toward the dustpan.
- Dustpan: red, lying on the ground at the edge of the path, receiving the dirt.
- Blue cap: in the air above and to the left of the man, flying away in the wind.
- Leaves: a few small green/brown leaves blowing near the cap and near the tree at the right.
- Wind lines: white wavy lines in the sky near the cap and near the tree, showing a strong breeze.
- Sprinkler: black sprinkler head on the lawn, centre-right front, attached to a long teal hose that runs off to the right edge; spraying three arcs of water to the left.
- Box: tan/brown box (looks like cardboard, with lighter vertical strips) on the lawn, centre front, sitting in a puddle and being hit by the sprinkler water.
- Water/puddle: blue arcs of spray and droplets, and a puddle under the box.
- Flower bed: right, oval of brown soil with red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers.
- House: right, cream walls, red roof, window with four panes, blue front door with a step.
- Tree: behind the house, green, leaves blowing off.
- Bush: small green bush beside the house.
- Fence and gate: wooden picket fence across the back; brown wooden gate at far left.
- Sun and clouds: yellow sun upper left, two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The box could be a cardboard box (which would be ruined by the water) or a wooden crate with slats; the vertical light strips could be tape or slats.
- The fork is impossibly large for a dinner fork; it could be read as a deliberate joke (wrong tool for sweeping) or as a garden fork drawn to look like cutlery. It looks like a kitchen fork, not a garden fork.
- It is not certain whether the sprinkler was set up badly or the box was left there by mistake; either way the box is in the spray.
- The wind appears to blow from right to left (the cap flies left, wind lines trail to its right), which matches the leaves leaving the tree on the right.
- Nothing appears to float or pass through anything else, apart from the cap and leaves, which are clearly being blown.
