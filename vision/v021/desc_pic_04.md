# Picture 04

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden picket fence, a gate on the far left, a paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the centre front, a girl or young woman with a brown ponytail in a blue hoodie and dark trousers is flying an orange kite, holding the string up in one hand while the kite, with a tail of little bows, flies high in the sky over the left side of the garden. Behind her, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands beside the round flower bed holding a teal watering can, but the water coming out of the spout curves upward into the air (with drops at the top) instead of falling down onto the flowers, which is impossible. On the far right, in front of the house, an old woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. On the left there is an empty swing set with a red seat, and in the front centre an empty wheelbarrow stands on the grass. The sky has the sun in the top left and two white clouds, and there is a tree behind the fence on the right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water from the watering can goes upward in an arc rather than pouring down; it could be read as an impossible stream, or (less likely) as the man flicking/spraying water up into the air. Either way it does not land on the flowers.
- The kite string runs diagonally across the picture, in front of the fence area; it looks fine but is long and thin, so its exact path is a little hard to follow near the swing set.
- The kite flier could be read as a teenage girl or a young woman.
