## Free description
This is a sunny town square in front of a row of colourful shops with striped awnings. In the centre a man in a blue hoodie sits on a wooden bench happily licking a pink ice cream cone with his eyes closed, while a brown dog with a red bandana stretches up toward him from the front, apparently trying to get at his ice cream; the man does not seem to notice. On the left a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is walking toward the right, also holding a pink ice cream cone. On the right, a blue-and-white ball is stuck up in the branches of a tree, and a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands below pointing up at it with a worried look. A blue bird is perched on top of a street lamp, and a red bird is flying in the top right corner. At the far left there is a fruit and vegetable stall with a striped canopy and crates of produce, but no seller; a bicycle is parked in a bike rack in front of the shops. In the front right there are two small round café tables with chairs, all empty. It looks like the dog may be about to snatch the man's ice cream.
