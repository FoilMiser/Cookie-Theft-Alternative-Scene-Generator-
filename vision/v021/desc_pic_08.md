# Picture 08

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden seen the other way round: the cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, and the wooden gate and paved path are on the right. An old woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, stands at the back by the fence painting it blue with a long-handled roller; a section of about three boards to her left is already blue, and a small can of blue paint sits on the grass by her feet. In front of the house is a round bed of colourful flowers with a teal garden hose reel beside it on the ground, and a green bush next to the house. On the right is an empty swing set with a red seat. In the front centre an empty wheelbarrow is parked, and to its right a teal watering can sits on the grass, not being used. The sky is blue with the sun in the top right and two white clouds, and a tree stands behind the house. Nothing looks clearly impossible; the old woman is the only person and she is quietly painting the fence.
