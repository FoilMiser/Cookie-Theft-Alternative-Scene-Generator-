# Picture 64

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills behind, a sun in the top left and two white clouds. A sandy path winds down the left side past a large tree, and a tall black lamp post stands in the middle. Just to the left of the lamp post, a wooden ladder stands upright at a slant, leaning to the left. It isn't touching the lamp post or anything else, so it seems to be standing up all by itself. On the right there is a pond with reeds at each end and a white duck swimming in it, plus a smaller tree and a bush beyond it. In the front, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is running fast to the right, shown by speed lines behind her. Right in front of her is a bed of colourful flowers, and she looks as if she is about to run straight into it and trample the flowers. There is also a grey rock at the bottom centre and a green trash can in the bottom right corner. The two odd things are the ladder standing up with no support and the runner heading directly for the flower bed.

## Inventory
- Woman runner: front right of centre. Dark hair in a ponytail, purple short-sleeved top, teal trousers, dark shoes. Running fast to the right, toward the flower bed (speed lines trail behind her on the left).
- Duck: right, in the pond. White duck with an orange beak, swimming and facing right.
- Ladder: centre left, middle distance, just left of the lamp post. Wooden ladder with orange-brown rails and dark rungs, foot on the grass and top leaning left into the air, about level with the hills. It isn't propped against anything.
- Lamp post: centre. Tall black post with a lantern top.
- Flower bed: front right. Soil mound with orange, red, pink, purple and yellow flowers, directly in the runner's path.
- Pond: right. Blue oval pond with cattails at each end, where the duck swims.
- Path: left. Beige winding path from the back to the bottom edge.
- Rock: bottom centre. Grey rounded stone.
- Trash can: bottom right corner. Green ribbed bin.
- Trees: a large tree on the left beside the path and a smaller tree on the far right.
- Bushes: one on the left under the tree, one on the far right next to the pond.
- Sun and clouds: sun top left, two white clouds top centre and centre right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The ladder has nothing holding it up, and its top rises above the horizon line, so it can't be lying flat on the ground. It looks like it is standing on its own at an angle, which is physically impossible, unless it is about to fall over.
- I can't be sure the runner will actually hit the flower bed. She might swerve, stop or jump over it, but as drawn the flowers are directly ahead of her.
- Her face is turned right in the direction she is running; it's hard to tell whether she has seen the flowers.
