# Picture 27

## Free description
This is a crowded playground on a sunny day, with the sun in the top-right corner, two white clouds, a dark railing fence across the back and hedges behind it. On the left, a girl with a brown ponytail, a blue top and dark trousers is holding a kite string and looking up, but her purple kite is stuck in the tree on the left. An old man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat sits on a bench reading a red book. Next to him on the bench is a long sandwich, and a blue bird is swooping down onto it, apparently stealing it, while he keeps reading. In the middle, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is walking along with his nose in an open book. A red-and-white ball lies right by his feet, and a red bicycle stands just in front of him, so he looks likely to trip. The bicycle is strange: its wheels are square instead of round. In the front centre, a brown dog wearing a red bandana is sitting up like a person and holding a newspaper open with its paws as if reading it, which is impossible. A small girl in a pink dress holding a yellow balloon stands between the swing set (whose single red swing is empty) and the slide on the right. On the far right, a woman with a long dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is carrying a cardboard box and walking off to the right. In the front right there's a sandpit with a red bucket and a blue spade in it, a small white puppy curled up asleep on the grass, and a grey rock. A red bird is flying in the sky on the upper left.
