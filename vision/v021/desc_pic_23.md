# Picture 23

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny park with green hills behind, a round blue pond with reeds on the left, a tall black lamp post, a winding sandy path on the right, and trees and bushes around the edges. At the edge of the pond, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is throwing bits of bread from a bag. A white duck is swimming quickly toward the pieces floating on the water, and one piece is still in the air. In front, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is hurrying to the left holding up an ice-cream cone. He is heading straight into the handles of a grey wheelbarrow, so he looks about to trip over it. On the bench, an old woman with grey hair and glasses is reading a red book while a blue bird pecks at the sandwich beside her. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans is walking down the path reading an open book. A red bucket stands right in front of him, and further along the path an orange cat is curled up asleep, so he may trip over the bucket or step on the cat. At the top of the path, a woman in a yellow dress carrying a yellow bag is walking with her head tipped back, looking up. In the bottom right, a little girl in a pink dress is pointing up at a blue ball stuck in the branches of the big tree. The wheelbarrow's wheel looks oddly square.
