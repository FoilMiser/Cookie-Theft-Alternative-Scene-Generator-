# Picture 30

## Free description
This is a back garden under a grey, overcast sky, with three dark storm clouds overhead and no sun, so it looks like rain is coming. A house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, with a tree behind it, a bush, and a flower bed of colourful flowers in front. A man with brown hair, a red shirt and blue jeans is standing near the house raking a small pile of leaves. In the centre, a washing line between two wooden posts still has a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes hanging on it, which will get wet if it rains. On the right there's a swing set with one empty red swing. Further right, on a pale path that leads in from a wooden gate in the fence, an older woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt is pointing up at the dark clouds with her mouth open, as if warning someone that a storm is coming. In the front centre, a brown dog wearing a red bandana is sitting up and holding a newspaper open with its paws as if reading it, which is impossible. A teal watering can sits on the grass in the front right, which seems a bit pointless with rain on the way.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The woman could be pointing at the storm clouds to warn that rain is coming, or at the washing on the line (to say it should be brought in). Her finger points up and to the left, towards the clouds.
- The man doesn't seem to have noticed the storm, or the woman. He could be looking towards her or towards the washing line.
- The dog reading a newspaper is impossible.
- The watering can being out when rain is coming could be a deliberate joke, or it could just be a garden object.
- The vertical lines of the fence behind the swing set make the swing's ropes a little hard to pick out. They seem to be single ropes, but the fence lines can make them look doubled.
- No rain is falling yet.
