# Picture 36

## Free description
This is a calm, sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the middle-left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands with her hands on her hips wearing an upside-down silver bucket on her head like a hat, which looks odd. In the middle, a man in a red shirt and jeans is reaching up to peg a white T-shirt onto the clothesline; a pair of blue jeans is already hanging next to it. On the right, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on a wooden bench near the house, reading a red book. There is a bed of colourful flowers and a bush by the house, and at the front of the lawn there is a teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow. The only strange thing is the bucket being worn as a hat.
