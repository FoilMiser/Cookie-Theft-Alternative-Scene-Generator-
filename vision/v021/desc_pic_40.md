## Free description
This is a busy, sunny back garden seen the other way round: the cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the right, and a gate in the fence with a paved path is on the left. In the middle, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is hanging laundry on a clothesline between two posts - a white T-shirt she is pegging up and a pair of blue jeans already hanging. A woman in a yellow dress is holding the nozzle of a garden hose and looking at it, but no water is coming out, because a white dog with brown spots is standing on the hose further back towards the hose reel, and the hose has a bulge where the water is trapped. On top of the fence a black-and-white cat is stretched out, creeping towards a small blue bird perched on the fence, as if about to pounce, while a red bird flies in the sky above. A blue ball is stuck up in the branches of the tree beside the house, and a little girl in a pink dress is pointing up at it. By the gate a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is holding a blue and a yellow balloon and reaching up towards a red balloon that seems to be floating away. A white goat is grazing on a tuft of grass in the left foreground, which is unusual for a garden. Oddly, an older man in a cap, brown waistcoat and white shirt is sitting reading a red book on the path, but there is no chair or bench under him - he is sitting on thin air.
