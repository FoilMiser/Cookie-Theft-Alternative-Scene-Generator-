# Picture 47

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence and gate at the back, and a paved path on the right. An older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, stands near the back by the fence with her hands on her hips, and she has an upside-down metal bucket on her head, which is odd. In the middle, a girl in a purple top and teal trousers holds the end of a garden hose up to her face and peers into the nozzle; only a single drop of water is coming out. The reason is that a brown dog with a red collar is standing on the hose further back, and the hose has swollen into a big bulge on the reel side of the dog. It looks as if the water will burst out into the girl's face as soon as the dog steps off. On the right, a boy in a blue hoodie and dark trousers is sweeping the path with a broom. There is also a clothesline with blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel pegged on it, an empty swing set with one red seat, a birdhouse on a pole, a flower bed and a hose reel. The sun is in the top right and there are two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The bucket on the woman's head is strange. It could be a bucket she put on as a joke or as a "hat", or it might be a sun hat drawn like a bucket, but it looks like a metal pail.
- The bulge in the hose reads as water trapped because the dog is standing on the hose. It could also be read as a lump or wide part of the hose itself.
- The dog's back paw seems to rest on the bulge and its front paw on the narrow hose, so it is a little unclear which part it is pressing.
- The girl's face is very close to the nozzle. It looks as if she is checking why no water is coming out, but she might just be adjusting the nozzle.
- Nothing seems to float, and nothing passes through anything else. The bucket is the clearly odd item.
