## Free description
This is a sunny back garden seen the other way round: the cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the right, and a gate in the fence with a paved path is on the left. An older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, is watering the flower bed in front of the house, but she seems to be pouring the water out of a grey frying pan or saucepan rather than a watering can, which is odd. A young man with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans is sitting on a wooden bench on the right, drinking from a red cup through a straw with his eyes closed and pink cheeks, looking content. A black-and-white cat is walking along the top of the fence near the middle. There are two potted plants and a round bush on the grass by the fence on the left, and an empty wheelbarrow in the front centre. A big tree stands behind the house. Apart from the pan being used for watering, nothing looks wrong.
