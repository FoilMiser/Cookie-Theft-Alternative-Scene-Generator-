# Picture 18

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny pedestrian shopping street with a row of two-storey shops in different colours, some with striped awnings, and a grey paved square in front. On the far left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is walking away to the left carrying a yellow shopping bag. Next to her, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is pointing up at a blue ball stuck in the branches of a tree. In the middle, an old man in a cap is walking to the right while reading an open book. A blue bucket stands on the ground in front of him, so he looks about to trip over it. Behind him, an old woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a bench reading a red book. She has not noticed that a blue bird is pecking at the sandwich lying next to her on the bench. A woman in a yellow dress is watering a flower box in front of a shop with a grey watering can, and water is dripping down the side of the box. At the front, a little girl in a pink dress is crying and rubbing her eye while holding an empty ice-cream cone. Her pink scoop has fallen on the ground, and a brown dog with a red collar is sniffing or licking it. On the right, a person in a blue hoodie stands in front of a fruit stall with both arms up, smiling with eyes closed. A bearded man in a green shirt is scattering seed from a bag, and a red bird is pecking at the seed on the ground.
