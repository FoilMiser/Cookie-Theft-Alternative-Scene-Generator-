# Picture 37

## Free description
This is a quiet, sunny back garden with no people in it. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door, with a bed of colourful flowers and a bush in front of it. Behind the house is a leafy tree, and sitting in the middle of the tree's leaves is an orange fish, which is impossible since fish do not live in trees. Along the back fence there is a clothesline with a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes hanging still. On the right stands a metal swing set with a red seat hanging straight down, and a paved path leads up to a wooden gate in the fence. At the front of the lawn there is an empty grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can. Everything is still and orderly, apart from the fish in the tree.
