# Picture 49

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence and gate at the back, and a paved path on the right. A purple kite is caught in the leaves of the tree behind the house, and a girl in a blue top and dark trousers stands by the fence pulling up on its string, trying to get it free. Along the top of the fence, an orange cat is creeping toward a red bird perched further along, so it looks like the cat is about to go after the bird. In front of the flower bed, an older man with a grey beard and a cap kneels on one knee, reaching into the flowers, with a pot of pink flowers next to him; he seems to be planting. In the middle of the lawn, a white goat with small horns has its head down, eating a tuft of grass, which is unusual for a house garden. On the right, a bearded man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. In the front centre is a grey wheelbarrow, and its wheel is drawn as a square, which is impossible for a working wheel. There is also an empty wooden bench at the front left and a birdhouse on a pole; the sun is in the top right with two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The wheelbarrow's wheel is square. That is impossible for a wheel that rolls, and it looks like a deliberate "something wrong" detail.
- The white animal in the middle reads as a goat (small curved horns, head down grazing). It could possibly be read as a young sheep or a white dog, but the horns point to a goat. A goat in a suburban back garden is unusual.
- The kite might be read as flying just in front of the tree, but leaf clumps are drawn over it, so it looks stuck in the branches.
- The older man's hands hold something white near the flowers. It could be a trowel, gloves, or just his shirt sleeves; I can't tell exactly.
- The cat on the fence could be read as just walking along or as stalking the bird; its low pose and direction suggest stalking.
- Apart from the square wheel, nothing looks to float or pass through anything else.
