# Picture 01

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden picket fence running across the back, a wooden gate in the fence on the far left, a paved path leading from the gate toward the front, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands at the back by the fence, painting it blue with a long-handled roller; a section of about three fence boards to his left is already blue, and a small paint can with blue paint sits on the grass by his feet. In the middle of the garden a bearded man with dark skin, in a green shirt and khaki trousers, stands with his hands on his hips wearing an upside-down white/grey bucket on his head like a hat, which is odd. In the front left, a person with a brown ponytail (a girl or young woman) in a blue hoodie and dark trousers is kicking an orange ball; her foot is just touching the ball on the edge of the path and there are motion lines behind her leg, so the ball is about to fly off. On the right in front of the house is a round bed of colourful flowers (yellow, orange, red, pink, purple), with a green bush beside the house and a tree behind the fence. The sky is blue with the sun in the top left and two white clouds. The strangest thing is the man wearing a bucket on his head.
