# Picture 22

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate in the fence on the left, and a paved path running from the gate down to the bottom left. The house is on the right. It has cream walls, a red roof, a window and a blue door, and a tree stands behind it. In the front left, a young man or boy in a red shirt and blue jeans is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. The dog is heading left toward the path and the gate. In the middle right, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is watering a flower bed of mixed coloured flowers with a teal watering can, and water is pouring onto the flowers. By the blue door of the house, a small brown and white dog is sitting holding the end of a red lead in its mouth, looking happy, as if it also wants to be taken for a walk. Nothing seems wrong or impossible. The scene is calm and a little funny because of the waiting dog.
