# Picture 28

## Free description
This is a back garden on a sunny day, with a wooden picket fence across the back, a wooden gate and a pale paved path on the far left, and a house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. On the path, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is sweeping dirt towards a red dustpan, but instead of a broom he's using a giant silver dinner fork, which is absurd. Small puffs of dust are rising from the dirt pile. In the middle, a girl with a brown ponytail, a blue top and dark trousers is holding the nozzle of a dark green garden hose out in front of her, but no water is coming out. The reason is visible: a white goat with small curved horns is standing on the hose in the front centre. Its hind hoof is on the hose, and the hose bulges up on the other side of its foot, towards the hose reel by the house. A goat in a back garden is itself unusual. On the right, by the blue door, a brown-and-white dog is sitting with a red leash in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. A washing line strung between two wooden posts holds a yellow towel with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans. There's also a small birdhouse on a tall pole, two potted green plants on the grass, a flower bed with colourful flowers in front of the house, a bush, and a tree behind the house.

## Inventory
- Bearded man, far left, front (on the path): dark skin, beard, green shirt, khaki trousers, brown shoes. He's sweeping dirt into a dustpan with a giant fork.
- Girl, centre, mid-ground: brown ponytail, blue long-sleeved top, dark trousers, white shoes. She's holding the hose nozzle out to the right with both hands, apparently waiting for water or puzzled that none comes.
- Goat, centre-right, front: white with small grey curved horns, facing left, mouth slightly open. It's standing on the garden hose.
- Dog, right, mid-ground (by the house door): brown and white, floppy ears. It's sitting with a red leash in its mouth, and the leash trails onto the grass.

Objects:

- Dustpan, left, front (on the path): red, with a pile of dirt and puffs of dust beside it.

- Washing line, centre, back: two wooden posts with a line between them, holding a yellow towel with red stripes and blue jeans on pegs.

- Red leash, right: in the dog's mouth.
- Gate and path, far left: brown wooden gate and pale paved path.
- House, right: cream wall, red roof, window, blue door with a yellow knob, grey step.
- Tree behind the house, bush beside the house; sun at top left; two white clouds at the top.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The man's tool is shaped exactly like a dinner fork (four tines, smooth silver handle), just hugely oversized. It could perhaps be read as a garden fork or pitchfork, but the shape is a cutlery fork, which looks like a deliberate absurdity.
- The goat: I read it as standing on the hose and blocking the water, because its hoof is on the hose and the hose swells beside it. It could also be read as the goat just standing near the hose. Its open mouth could be bleating or chewing.
- The girl's expression could be puzzled or just concentrating. She's looking at the nozzle.
- The dog with the leash could be asking for a walk, or it could just be holding the leash.
- Nothing seems to float. The goat in the garden, the giant fork and the blocked hose are the odd parts.
