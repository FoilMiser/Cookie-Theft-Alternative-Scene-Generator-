## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream-coloured house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a gate in the fence on the right with a paved path leading to it. An older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, is painting the fence blue with a brush; a few boards to her left are already painted blue. A paint can and a paint tray full of blue paint sit on the grass by her feet. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has evidently stepped in the paint tray: a trail of blue paw prints leads from the tray across the grass to the dog, whose paws are blue, and it is walking away to the right. A black-and-white cat is walking along the top of the fence near the tree on the left. There is a wooden bench and a flower bed with colourful flowers in front of the house, a small bush beside the house, an empty wheelbarrow in the front centre, and a birdhouse on a tall pole by the fence. Oddly, a cardboard box is floating in the sky near the sun in the top right, with nothing holding it up, which looks impossible. The woman does not seem to have noticed the dog spreading paint around.
