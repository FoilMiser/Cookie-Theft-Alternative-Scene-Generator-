# Picture 33

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny park with a pond on the left, green hills and trees behind, a tall black lamp post in the middle, a wooden bench, and a curving path on the right. At the pond, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is throwing bits of bread onto the water from a white bag or cup, and a white duck is swimming toward the floating crumbs. In front of the pond, an older man with a grey beard and a cap, wearing a brown vest, is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. Behind them, a man in a red shirt and jeans is walking toward the path carrying a cardboard box. In the front centre, a small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is holding an empty ice-cream cone and rubbing his eye as if crying, because his pink ice cream has fallen on the ground, and a brown dog with a red collar is bending down to sniff or eat it. On the right, a little girl in a pink dress is pointing up at a blue ball that is stuck in the branches of a tree. At the far right, an older woman with grey hair and glasses in a teal cardigan is walking along carrying a yellow bag. A green rubbish bin stands in the bottom-left corner.
