# Picture 45

## Free description
This is a sunny day at a playground on a grassy field, with a dark metal railing fence across the back and green hedges or trees behind it. On the right, two children are on a red seesaw: a girl in a pink dress sits on the left end and a child in a yellow top and orange shorts sits on the right end, facing each other and holding the handles. The seesaw is tipped only slightly, with the right end a little lower, so the child on the right has their feet on the grass while the girl's feet hang off the ground. On the left there is a slide with a red frame, a grey ladder and a yellow-and-orange chute; nobody is on it. In the front left is a wooden sandbox with a red bucket and a blue spade in it, and nobody playing there, with a grey rock beside it. A green bin stands by the fence, a small bush is on the far left and a tree is on the far right. The sun is in the top left and there are two white clouds. Nothing looks wrong or impossible; it is a calm, ordinary scene.
