# Picture 25

## Free description
This is a busy back garden on a sunny day, with a wooden picket fence across the back, a house with a red roof and a blue door on the right, and a pale paved path leading in from a wooden gate at the far left. An old man with a white beard, a grey cap, a white shirt, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers is painting the fence blue with a brush. A few planks next to the gate are already blue, and there's a paint tin and a paint tray of blue paint at his feet. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has clearly stepped in the paint tray, because a trail of blue paw prints leads from the tray across the grass to its feet. The old man doesn't seem to have noticed. On top of the fence an orange cat is creeping along towards a small blue bird perched there, as if about to pounce. In the middle, a girl with a brown ponytail, a blue top and dark trousers is holding a kite string and looking up, but her purple kite is stuck in the leaves of the tree behind the fence on the right. In the front right, a man with brown hair, a red shirt and jeans is kneeling by a flower bed full of colourful flowers, reaching in among them. A small pot with a pink flower sits beside the bed. An empty grey wheelbarrow is parked in the front centre, and a red bird is flying in the sky near the sun.
