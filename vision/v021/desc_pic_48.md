# Picture 48

## Free description
This is a sunny park playground on grass, with a dark metal railing fence at the back and green hedges behind it. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes walks to the right pushing a teal pram with a blue hood; a baby is asleep inside under a pink polka-dot blanket. In the front centre, an orange cat crouches low and creeps toward a small red bird that is pecking at the ground just in front of it, and it looks like the cat is about to pounce on the bird. Up in the tree on the left, a blue bird is perched on a branch. The playground has an empty red seesaw tipped down on its left end, an empty swing set with one red seat in the centre, and a slide with a red frame and yellow/orange chute on the right. There is a green bin by the fence and a few round bushes. The sun is in the top right and there are two clouds. Apart from the cat stalking the bird, nothing looks odd.
