# Picture 03

## Free description
This is a sunny town street or small square with a row of colourful shops along the back (pink, pale green, peach and cream buildings with striped awnings), a street lamp, a tree in a round planter, and paved ground. On the left is a fruit and vegetable stall with a striped awning and crates of green, yellow, orange and red produce, with a cardboard box on the ground beside it. An old man in a flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands near the stall with one hand raised high, and little motion marks around his hand show he is waving. In the middle, a man with short brown hair in a red shirt and blue jeans is pushing a teal pram, and a baby lies asleep inside under a pink spotted blanket. In the front centre, a little girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes holds the strings of a purple and a yellow balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other hand toward a red balloon that has got away from her and is floating up with its string dangling. On the front left, a boy with curly hair in a yellow shirt and orange shorts walks along holding a yellow balloon. On the right there are two small round café tables with chairs and a green rubbish bin. The red balloon drifting away looks like it is about to be lost.
