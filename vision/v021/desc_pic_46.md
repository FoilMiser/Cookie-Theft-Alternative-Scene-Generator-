# Picture 46

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden next to a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the left. A tall wooden fence runs across the back, with a wooden gate on the right and a paved path leading to it. An older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, stands by a flower bed and waters the colourful flowers (red, yellow, purple, pink and orange) with a garden hose. The water arcs from the nozzle onto the flowers, and the hose runs back across the grass to a hose reel at the left. In the front centre, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts stands looking up and holds the string of an orange diamond-shaped kite, which flies high in the sky with a little bow-tie tail. A grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles sits empty on the grass in front, and two potted plants and a round bush are by the fence. The sun is in the top right and there are two white clouds. Everything looks normal and peaceful; nothing seems about to go wrong.
