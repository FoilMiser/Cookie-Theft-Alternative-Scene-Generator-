# Picture 36

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a gate on the left with a pale paved path running down to the bottom-left corner, a birdhouse on a pole, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. In the front centre, a small child with dark curly hair, wearing a yellow top and orange shorts, is flying a kite: they hold the string up in one hand, and the string runs up to an orange diamond-shaped kite with a bow-tied tail high in the sky. A blue bird with a white belly and yellow beak is flying in the sky near the sun, not far from the kite. On the right, a woman with a dark ponytail, in a purple top and teal trousers, is standing by the flower bed watering the flowers with a grey round object held by a handle; water arcs out of it onto the flowers. A teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow sit unused on the grass at the bottom, and there are two potted plants and a bush near the fence. Nothing looks clearly impossible, though the woman's watering tool is unusual.
