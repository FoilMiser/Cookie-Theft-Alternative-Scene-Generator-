## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate and paved path on the left, and a house with a red roof and blue door on the right. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, is painting the fence blue with a paintbrush; a section of about three boards is already blue. Behind her on the grass are a paint can and a paint tray with blue paint in it. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has walked through the paint: its paws are blue and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the grass to where the dog is now, on the path heading towards the gate. The woman is facing the fence and does not seem to have noticed the dog or the paw prints. There is also a colourful flower bed by the house, a watering can and a wheelbarrow on the grass at the front, and a tree behind the fence.
