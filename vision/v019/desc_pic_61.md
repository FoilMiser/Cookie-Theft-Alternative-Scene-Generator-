# Picture 61

## Free description
This is a busy back garden on a sunny day: a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, a paved path running down the right side, and a washing line with blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel hanging on it. A blue-and-white ball is stuck in the branches of the tree behind the house, and a young woman in a blue hoodie stands below pointing up at it. In the middle background an older man in a grey cap, white shirt and brown vest is painting the fence blue with a roller on a long handle; one section of the fence is already blue, and a paint bucket stands at his feet. Next to a birdhouse pole there is a grey tray of blue paint, and a trail of blue paw prints leads from it across the grass to a white dog with brown spots on the path, so the dog has clearly walked through the paint. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is jumping with his arms up near a blue bird perched on top of the fence. In the front, a sprinkler on a hose is spraying arcs of water onto a cardboard box, which is sitting in a puddle, and an orange cat is curled up asleep on the grass just below. On the left, a bearded man in a green shirt sits on a bench reading a red book, next to a hose reel and a flower bed. Two things look impossible: a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is sitting in mid-air reading a red book with no chair under him, and a brown dog sitting on the path at the bottom right is holding and reading a newspaper with its paws like a person.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The man in the red shirt is sitting on thin air with no chair, bench or stool under him; this could be a deliberate "impossible" element or an object missing from the drawing.
- The brown dog holding a newspaper is not something a real dog can do; it has white glove-like paws gripping the paper.
- The grey tray with blue liquid could be a paint tray (most likely, given the blue fence paint) or a dog's water bowl filled with blue water.
- The boy with raised arms could be trying to catch or reach the bird, or simply jumping/cheering.
- The bird could be perched on the fence or just landing / about to fly off.
- The sprinkler watering a cardboard box seems like a mistake (the box is getting soaked), rather than anything impossible.
- The red mark on the brown dog's head could be a bow, a collar, or a red ear.
