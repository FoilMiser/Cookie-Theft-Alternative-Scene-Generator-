# Picture 49

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny back garden, drawn with the house on the left this time and the gate and path on the right. On top of the wooden fence, an orange cat is creeping along towards a blue bird. A red bird is also perched on the fence further right, and neither bird seems to notice the cat. A yellow diamond kite with a bow tail flies high in the sky. Its long string comes down to a small girl in a pink dress standing on the path on the right, looking up and holding the string. In the middle, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead, heading to the right. Next to her, a person with a brown ponytail and a blue hoodie walks along reading an open book instead of looking where they're going. A red bucket sits on the grass just in front of them. On the lower left, a sprinkler on the end of a hose is spraying arcs of water right onto a cardboard box. The box is getting soaked and is sitting in a puddle. There are also a flower bed in front of the house, two potted plants by the fence and a tree behind the house.
