# Picture 32

In the middle, a man with brown hair, wearing a red T-shirt and blue jeans, is reaching out with both hands to catch a red ball. A dashed curved line runs from a small child further back by the fence (dark curly hair, yellow top, orange shorts) to the ball, showing that the child has just thrown the ball to the man. By the house door on the left, a tan-and-white dog is sitting holding a red lead in its mouth, as though it wants to be taken for a walk. In the front centre, a white dog with brown spots is crouched down in a playful bow with its tail wagging, a yellow-green tennis ball in front of its paws. On the right, a woman with a dark ponytail, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, is sweeping the path with a broom. There is also a flower bed near the house, a bush, two potted plants by the fence, and a red bowl of dog food on the grass at the bottom. Nothing looks clearly impossible.
