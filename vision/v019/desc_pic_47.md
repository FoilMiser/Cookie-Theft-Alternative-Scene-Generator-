# Picture 47

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden: a wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate on the far left with a paved path leading from it, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the path at the bottom left, a girl or young woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is sweeping. She isn't using a broom, though. She holds an enormous silver dinner fork, as tall as a broom, and pushes a small pile of dirt with it into a red dustpan on the ground. A little cloud of dust rises from the pile. Using a giant fork as a broom is the odd thing here, because the prongs would let the dirt slip through. In the front middle of the lawn is an empty grey wheelbarrow. There are two potted plants by the fence, two round bushes, a flower bed of colourful flowers in front of the house, and a tree behind the fence on the right. Nobody else is in the picture.
