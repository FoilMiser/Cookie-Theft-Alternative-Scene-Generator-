# Picture 52

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden behind a cream-coloured house with a red roof and a blue door on the left. An older man with a grey beard and a grey cap, wearing a white shirt and brown waistcoat, kneels on one knee next to a round bed of colourful flowers and seems to be planting or digging with a small hand tool beside a pot holding a pink flower. Further back on the right, a young woman with a brown ponytail, a blue hoodie and dark trousers is painting the wooden fence blue with a brush; a section of the fence behind her is already blue and an open paint tin sits on the grass by her feet. In the middle of the garden a washing line between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. A wooden bench stands at the front left, an empty wheelbarrow at the front centre, and a green watering can at the front right next to a paved path that runs to a wooden gate in the fence. A birdhouse on a tall pole stands beside the woman. The strange thing is that there is an orange fish sitting in the leaves of the tree behind the house, which is impossible. The sky is blue with two white clouds and a bright sun at top right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fish in the tree is the clearest oddity; fish do not live in trees. It could be read as a deliberate "what's wrong" item or as a stray drawing element.
- The man's tool is small and hard to make out: it could be a trowel or a small fork; he could be planting the pink flower or weeding.
- The man's eyes/face: he may be wearing glasses; hard to tell.
- The woman holds the brush up near the blue section; she could be starting a new board or finishing; also could read as a roller instead of a brush.
- The blue painted section sits partly behind the birdhouse pole, so it is unclear whether she painted around the pole.
