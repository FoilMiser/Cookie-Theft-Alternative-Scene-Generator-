# Picture 10

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills, a winding sandy path, a black lamp post, a pond with a white duck, and a flower bed. On the left, a young man in a blue hoodie and black trousers is pointing up, mouth open, at a blue ball that is stuck in the branches of a tree. Further back on the path, an older man in a cap and brown vest is eating a pink ice-cream cone. In the middle, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a wooden bench with her eyes closed, happily eating a pink ice-cream cone, while a brown dog with a red collar crouches at the end of the bench with its head stretched up toward her ice cream, as if it might try to grab it. Near the lamp post a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds the string of a yellow balloon. At the front right, a girl in a pink dress holds a purple and a yellow balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other toward a red balloon that is floating away above her, its string dangling just out of reach. The duck is swimming on the pond, and the sun and two clouds are in the sky.
