# Picture 33

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills in the background, a big tree on the left, a smaller tree on the right, a black lamp post in the middle, a winding sandy path coming down from the back left, a wooden bench, and a pond with reeds on the right. At the bottom left, a brown dog with a red collar is sniffing or eating a pink ice cream that has fallen on the ground, and a child next to it (dark curly hair, yellow T-shirt, orange shorts) is holding an empty ice-cream cone and covering their eyes as if crying. Further back on the path, a park worker in an orange cap, orange high-visibility vest and blue trousers is sweeping with a broom. A girl in a pink dress sits on the bench with something white held to her mouth, as if drinking or eating. In the middle front, a young woman with a brown ponytail and a blue hoodie is walking while reading an open book, and a red ball lies on the grass right in front of her, so she looks about to trip over it. An orange cat is curled up asleep on the grass. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is holding a watering can over a flower bed, but the stream of water goes up and backward into the air toward the pond instead of falling onto the flowers, which is impossible.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The watering can water is the main impossibility: it arcs upward from the can toward the pond instead of falling onto the flowers. It could also be read as water being drawn up out of the pond into the can, but either way it defies gravity and misses the flower bed.
- What the girl on the bench is holding is unclear: it could be a cup she is drinking from, or something she is eating (a snack or sandwich).
- The child at bottom left has a hand over the eyes, which reads as crying; it could also be rubbing the eyes or shading them. Together with the empty cone and the dog at the spilled ice cream, crying is the more likely reading.
- The reading woman is walking toward the left; the red ball is just ahead of her feet, so it reads as "about to trip", though she could also just step past it.
