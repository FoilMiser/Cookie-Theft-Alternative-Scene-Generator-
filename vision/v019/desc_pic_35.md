# Picture 35

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a gate on the left with a pale paved path running down to the bottom-left corner, a birdhouse on a pole, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. In the middle, a man with a dark beard, wearing a green T-shirt and khaki trousers, is hanging washing on a clothesline strung between two wooden posts; a white T-shirt, a yellow towel with a red stripe and a pair of blue jeans are pegged on the line, and he is reaching up to peg the T-shirt. An orange cat is walking along the top of the fence on the right. On the left there is a grey metal swing set with one empty red swing. In the front right, a young woman with a brown ponytail, in a blue hoodie and dark trousers, is standing by the flower bed holding a grey round pan-like object by its handle, with water pouring out of it; the water falls at the near edge of the bed, around the grass. This looks odd because she is watering with what looks like a frying pan or saucepan, while a proper teal watering can sits unused on the grass at the bottom left. A grey wheelbarrow is also on the grass at the bottom.
