# Picture 12

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the left, a small boy in a yellow t-shirt and orange shorts is sitting on a swing, smiling, with little motion lines showing he is swinging. At the back, a girl or young woman with a brown ponytail, in a blue top and dark trousers, stands with her arms stretched out and hands together, about to catch a red ball. A dotted line shows the ball has been thrown in an arc by a small girl in a pink dress at the back right, who is still in her throwing pose. In the front centre, a bearded man in a green t-shirt and khaki trousers stands with his hands on his hips, and he has a blue bucket upside down on his head like a hat, which looks odd. A teal watering can sits on the grass at the front left, and there is a flower bed and a bush by the house. The sun is in the upper left with two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The blue thing on the man's head is most likely an upside-down bucket, but it could also be read as a tall blue hat.
- It is not clear why he has a bucket on his head or what he is looking at; he seems to be just standing and watching.
- The ponytail person could be a teenage girl or an adult woman.
- The direction of the throw is read from the dotted line: from the girl in pink to the ponytail girl. It could conceivably be read the other way, but the girl in pink's follow-through pose supports the first reading.
