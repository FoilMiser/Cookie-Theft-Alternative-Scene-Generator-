# Picture 27

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny playground with a metal railing fence across the back and trees behind it. On the left, a girl in a pink dress is sliding down a red-and-yellow slide. Near the bottom of the slide, a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar sits up holding a newspaper open with its front paws, as if reading it, which is impossible. In the front centre, a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice cream cone and rubs his eye as if crying. His pink scoop has fallen onto the grass and is melting into a puddle, and a brown dog with a red collar has its nose down right at it, about to eat it. A woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers walks along reading an open book. A cardboard box sits on the ground right in her path, so she looks about to trip over it. Behind her, an older man in a cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat sits on a bench reading a red book. A blue bird beside him on the bench is pecking at or taking his long sandwich without him noticing. On the far right, a person with a brown ponytail and a blue top holds a kite string up toward a purple kite that is stuck in the big tree. A red bird is perched on top of the fence, the swing set in the middle has one empty swing, and an orange cat is curled up asleep in the bottom-left corner beside an empty sandpit.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The kite flyer could be a girl or a young woman. They are either still flying the kite and it has snagged in the tree, or they are tugging the string to free it.
- The blue bird could be eating the sandwich where it lies, or pulling it away. Either way the man hasn't noticed.
- The woman with the book is walking toward the box and seems about to trip over it, but she hasn't reached it yet.
- The boy's gesture looks like crying or rubbing his eye. It could also be shading his eyes, but crying fits the dropped ice cream.
- Unintentionally odd: the brown dog's back foot seems to sit on or overlap the right edge of the sandpit frame. It could be standing on the frame or just in front of it.
- Clearly impossible on purpose: the white dog reading a newspaper.
