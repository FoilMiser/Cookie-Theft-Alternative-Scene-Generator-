# Picture 22

## Free description
This is a windy, sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door, with a tall wooden fence and a gate at the far left. White wind lines are drawn in the sky and across the left of the picture. On the left, a young man in a blue hoodie and dark trousers has both arms thrown up and looks startled, open-mouthed. His red baseball cap has blown off and is flying away to the upper right, along with a few small leaves. In the middle, the towel and jeans on the washing line are blowing sideways in the wind. Below the washing line, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is in a sitting position drinking from a white cup, but there is no chair or seat under him. He is sitting on thin air, which is impossible. At the back right near the fence, a brown dog with a red collar crouches playfully next to a yellow-green tennis ball, with its tail wagging. A teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow are on the grass at the bottom, and a flower bed is in front of the house. The cap looks about to be blown over the fence or out of reach.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The man in red is in a seated pose with no chair under him. He might be meant to be sitting on an invisible chair (impossible), or a chair is simply missing from the drawing. He could also be read as squatting, but his pose looks like sitting.
- The young man's raised arms could be read two ways: trying to catch his flying cap, or simply throwing his hands up in surprise.
- The dog could be playing with the ball, about to pick it up, or asking someone to throw it.
- It is windy, but the tree, bushes and flowers don't move. Only the cap, leaves and washing show the wind.
