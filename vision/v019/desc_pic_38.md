# Picture 38

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence along the back, a gate in the fence on the far left, a paved path running from the gate down to the bottom left, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. A small girl in a pink dress is sitting on a red swing on a grey A-frame swing set at left-centre; she is smiling with her eyes closed, and small motion lines beside her show she is swinging. In the middle, a man with a dark beard, green shirt and khaki trousers is holding a garden hose and spraying a stream of water toward the flower bed on the right. The hose runs from a hose reel next to the house, round the front of the flower bed, and up to his hands. Behind him a clothesline between two wooden posts has a yellow towel with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. In the front there is a teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow lying on the grass. The odd thing is a cardboard box hanging in the sky at the upper left, just below the sun, tilted at an angle as if it were flying or falling, with nothing holding it up and nobody throwing it.
