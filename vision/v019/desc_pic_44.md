## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence along the back, a closed gate on the far left, a paved path running from the gate to the bottom left, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. Behind her a clothesline between two wooden posts has a yellow towel with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. Two potted plants sit on the grass near the fence on the left, and a teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow stand on the lawn at the front. It is a calm, ordinary scene and nothing looks floating or impossible.
