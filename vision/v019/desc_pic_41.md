## Free description

There is a flower bed with colourful flowers in front of the house, a green bush beside the house, two potted plants on the grass by the fence on the left, and a teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow on the lawn in front. Nothing is floating or obviously impossible; the main event is the dog trying to get the girl's ice cream.
