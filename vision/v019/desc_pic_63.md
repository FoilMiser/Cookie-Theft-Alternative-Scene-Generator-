# Picture 63

## Free description
This is a playground on a sunny day, with a sun at the top left, two clouds, and a long dark metal railing fence with green bushes behind it. On the left is a slide with a red frame, a ladder and a yellow-and-orange chute, with a green rubbish bin behind it. On the right, two children are playing on a red seesaw: a girl in a pink dress sits on the slightly higher left end with her feet off the ground, and a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on the lower right end with his feet touching the ground. They are facing each other and holding the handles, so they seem to be seesawing together happily. In the front centre stands a red bicycle with no rider, and its wheels are black squares instead of circles, which is impossible, because a bike with square wheels could not roll. There is a grey rock at the bottom left, bushes on both sides, and a tree on the right edge. Apart from the square-wheeled bicycle, the scene looks normal.
