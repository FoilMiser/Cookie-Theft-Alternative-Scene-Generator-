# Picture 54

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream-coloured house with a red roof and blue door on the right and a wooden fence across the back. A young woman with a brown ponytail, wearing a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white trainers, stands in front of a round bed of colourful flowers and holds a green watering can. The odd thing is that the water from the can does not pour down onto the flowers; instead it shoots upward in an arc into the air, with drops at the top, which water would not do. On the left there is a wooden gate in the fence and a paved path running from it down to the front of the picture. A birdhouse on a tall pole stands near the gate, with two pots of green seedlings and a round bush beside it. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the front. A large tree stands behind the house, and the sky is blue with two clouds and a bright sun at top left.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water: the most literal reading is that it is flowing upward out of the can, which is impossible. A second reading is that it is a spray arcing up and over the flowers, but it does not come down onto them, so it still looks wrong.
- The flowers are not getting wet; the water goes past and above them.
- Nothing else looks out of place; the wheelbarrow and potted plants are just sitting there.
