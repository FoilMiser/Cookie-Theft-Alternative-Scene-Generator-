# Picture 64

## Free description
This is a park on a sunny day, with the sun at the top left, two clouds, rolling green hills, a winding sandy path from the bottom left, an empty wooden bench, a tall black lamp post and a green rubbish bin next to it. On the right is a blue pond with cattails at each end, and a white duck is floating calmly on it. In front of the pond, a person with shoulder-length brown hair, wearing a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes, sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket next to a wicker basket. They are leaning back with their eyes closed and face tilted up, looking relaxed, perhaps enjoying the sun or humming/whistling. The odd thing is a tall wooden ladder at the right, standing on the grass at a slant with nothing to lean against; its top appears to stick up into the pond area, and it could not really stay up like that. There is also a grey rock in the front centre and bushes and trees around the edges. Nobody else is in the park, and apart from the free-standing ladder it is a peaceful scene.
