# Picture 29

## Free description
This is a sunny playground with a dark metal railing fence across the back and trees behind it. On the left there is an empty red-and-yellow slide, and in the front left an empty sandpit with a red bucket and a blue spade left in it. On the right, an older man with a grey beard, a grey-blue cap, a white shirt, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers sits on the low end of a red seesaw. His end is right down on the ground, and he is reading an open book, not paying attention. Because he is so much heavier, a little girl in a pink dress is stuck high up on the other end, holding the handle with her legs dangling in the air. Small motion lines under her feet suggest she is kicking or wiggling her legs. The man doesn't seem to notice she is stuck up there. There is a green rubbish bin by the fence, a bush on the left, a big tree on the right and a grey rock in the bottom-left corner. The sun and two clouds are in the sky.
