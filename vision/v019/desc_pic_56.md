# Picture 56

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills, a round blue pond edged with reeds, a black lamp post, trees, a wooden bench and a winding sandy path on the right. At the front left, an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, kneels beside a round bed of colourful flowers and seems to be planting, with a pot holding a pink flower next to her. In the middle of the grass, a white goat with a dark curved horn has its head down and is eating a tuft of grass. On the right, a young man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands on the path sprinkling seeds or crumbs from a white bag, and a line of seeds falls to the path where a red bird and a blue bird are pecking at them. The pond is empty, with no ducks or fish. The goat is a little unusual for a town park, and it is close enough to the flower bed that it might go for the flowers next. The sky is blue with two white clouds and a sun at top right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The goat appears to have one visible dark horn (the other may be hidden by the angle). A goat in a park could be read as odd/out of place, or simply as a park with animals.
- Whether the goat is eating just grass or is about to eat the woman's flowers is not shown; it is only eating a small tuft of grass for now.
- What the woman holds is hard to make out: possibly a small trowel, a seedling, or she is pressing soil with her hand.
- What the young man holds could be a bag of birdseed, a carton, or a paper bag of breadcrumbs.
- The dotted line of seeds hangs in the air between his hand and the ground; this reads as seeds falling, not as floating.
