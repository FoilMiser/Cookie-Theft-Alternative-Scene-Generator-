# Picture 57

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, with a wooden fence across the back. A man with brown hair in a red shirt and blue jeans kneels by a round bed of colourful flowers and is working in it, with a pot holding a pink flower in front of the bed. Toward the back right, a woman with dark hair in a yellow dress and red shoes is painting the fence blue with a brush; a section of the fence to her left is already painted blue, and a paint tin sits on the grass beside her. At the foot of the birdhouse pole is a tray of blue paint, and a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has walked through it, leaving a trail of blue paw prints across the grass onto the path, where it is now walking off toward the gate with blue feet. In the front middle a boy in a blue hoodie and dark trousers is raking a small pile of leaves. A green watering can stands on the grass next to him. The sky is blue with two clouds and a sun at top right.
