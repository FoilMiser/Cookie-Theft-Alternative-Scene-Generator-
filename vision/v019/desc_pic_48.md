# Picture 48

## Free description
This is a sunny day at a park playground with a dark metal fence and green hedges along the back. In the front left, a small girl in a pink dress kneels in a wooden sandpit, building a sandcastle with a little red flag on top. In the front middle, an orange cat is crouched low and lunging forward, about to pounce on a red bird. The bird has its head down, pecking at the ground, and doesn't seem to notice. Further back, near the fence, a white dog with brown spots is in a play-bow with a yellow-green tennis ball in front of it, as if wanting someone to play. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks towards the left holding up a pink ice-cream cone. She seems not to be watching the cat and bird. Behind them are an empty slide on the left, a green rubbish bin, an empty wooden bench and a big tree on the right. A grey rock and a bush sit at the far left.

- The cat could be caught mid-pounce or just stalking low. Either way it is aimed at the bird, which seems unaware.
- The dog could be inviting play, or it could be about to grab the ball. Nobody is playing with it.
- The woman might be the girl's mother walking over, or just someone passing by. She is facing left, towards the girl and the animals.
