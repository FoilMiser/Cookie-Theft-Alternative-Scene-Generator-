# Picture 28

## Free description
This is a sunny but windy back garden. A cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the right, and a wooden fence with a gate on the left runs across the back. A paved path leads from the gate down to the bottom-left corner, and a birdhouse on a tall pole stands near the gate. On the left, a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts sits smiling on a red swing hanging from a dark grey swing frame. In the middle, a woman with dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers has both arms raised and her mouth open in surprise. She is reaching up toward a blue cap that the wind is blowing away above her head. White wind lines and a couple of leaves blow along with the cap, and more wind lines show in the sky at the upper right. On the right there is a colourful flowerbed, a teal hose reel beside it and an empty wooden bench in front of the house. In the front-left there is a grey wheelbarrow, and its wheel is drawn as a square, which is impossible because it couldn't roll. The sun and two clouds are in the sky.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The cap is most likely the woman's, blown off her head. It could also have blown in from elsewhere and she is trying to catch it.
- The swing: the seat hangs fairly straight down, but there are short motion lines beside it. The boy may be swinging gently or just sitting.
- The square wheel on the wheelbarrow could be a deliberate "wrong" detail or a drawing error. Either way, it is a square, not a circle.
- The wind is strong enough to blow off a cap, yet the tree, flowers and bench look undisturbed. Only the cap and leaves are moving.
