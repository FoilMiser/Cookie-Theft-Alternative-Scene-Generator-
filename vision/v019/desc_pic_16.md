# Picture 16

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence and gate at the back, and a paved path on the right. Several people are doing garden chores. At the back near the house, a man with brown hair in a red t-shirt and blue jeans is walking to the right carrying a cardboard box. In the middle front, a woman with a dark ponytail in a purple top and teal trousers is raking a small pile of leaves. On the path at the right, a child in a yellow outfit with short dark hair is sweeping dirt toward a red dustpan, but instead of a broom the child is using a giant silver fork, almost as tall as they are, which is clearly strange. A small puff of dust rises from the pile of dirt by the dustpan. Around the garden there is an empty grey wheelbarrow, an empty swing set, a wooden bench at the front left and a flower bed by the house. The sun is in the upper right with two clouds.
