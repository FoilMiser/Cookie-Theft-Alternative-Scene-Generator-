# Picture 11

## Free description
This is a back garden on a sunny but very windy day: white wind lines streak across the sky and the garden, and leaves are being blown off the tree. In the middle of the lawn a young man with brown hair, in a blue hoodie and dark trousers, is reaching up with both hands, mouth open in surprise, toward his red cap, which has been blown off his head and is tumbling through the air just beyond his fingertips. A couple of leaves are flying along with the cap. On the right, the swing on the swing set has been blown far over to one side, with its ropes slanting steeply. A birdhouse sits on a tall pole by the gate at the back right, and a paved path runs from the gate to the front right. On the left there is a cream house with a red roof and blue door, a small bush, and a bed of colourful flowers. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the front. The sun is in the upper right with two clouds.

## Inventory
- Young man (centre): brown hair, blue hoodie, dark trousers, white shoes; both arms stretched up, reaching for his blown-away cap, looking surprised.
- Red cap (centre-right, in the air above his hands): a red baseball cap tumbling away in the wind.
- Leaves (upper left near the tree, and around the cap): small green and brown leaves being blown through the air.
- Wind lines (sky upper right, near the tree, and beside the cap): white streaks showing strong wind blowing from left to right.
- Swing set (right): dark grey A-frame; the red swing seat is blown far to the right with its ropes at a steep angle.
- Birdhouse (back right): small wooden birdhouse with a red roof on a tall post near the gate.
- Gate (back right) in the wooden fence; paved path from the gate down to the front right.
- Wheelbarrow (front, right of centre): grey, empty, resting on the grass.
- House (left): cream walls, red roof, blue door with step, one window; small green bush beside it.
- Flower bed (left, in front of house): mixed red, orange, yellow, pink and purple flowers.
- Tree behind the fence on the left; hedges/trees behind the fence.
- Sun (upper right) and two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The swing seat is swung out so far that it overlaps the right-hand leg of the swing frame; it could be read as hanging in front of the leg (perspective) or as passing through it, which looks a bit odd.
- The swing being held out at such a steep angle could be read as being blown by the wind or as having just been let go by someone (no one else is in the picture).
- The cap could be read as just blown off his head or as something he threw; the wind lines and his surprised face suggest it was blown off.
- Despite the strong wind, the flowers, bush and the man's hair do not appear to be blowing, which is a little inconsistent.
