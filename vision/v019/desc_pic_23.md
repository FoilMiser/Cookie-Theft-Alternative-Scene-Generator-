# Picture 23

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny park with rolling green hills, a pond on the left, a tall street lamp in the middle and a sandy path winding down on the right. An elderly woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt stands at the edge of the pond fishing with a rod. Her float is bobbing in the water with ripples around it. Behind her, two women walk on the grass to the right. One is in a yellow dress eating a pink ice cream; the other is in a purple top and teal trousers carrying a yellow shopping bag. An old man in a flat cap and waistcoat sits on a bench reading a red book. Right beside him on the bench, a blue bird is pecking at his long sandwich, and he doesn't seem to notice. On the path, a person in a blue hoodie with a brown ponytail is walking along reading an open book. A blue bucket is on the path right in front of them, so they look about to trip over it. At the far right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts points up, frowning, at a blue ball stuck in a tree. In the front middle, a little girl in a pink dress cries over her dropped pink ice cream, which a brown dog is sniffing or eating. In the bottom left corner, next to a flower bed, a white dog with brown patches sits up holding an open newspaper as if reading it, which is impossible.
