# Picture 14

## Free description
This is a sunny park with a pond, green hills, a black lamp post and a winding path on the right. A man in a red t-shirt and blue jeans stands at the right edge of the pond holding a fishing rod with both hands; the line hangs down into the water, where a red-and-white float bobs with a ripple around it. At the far left edge of the pond, a little girl in a pink dress is holding something white (it looks like bread or a bag) and dropping crumbs; small brown crumbs are floating on the water and scattered on the grass near her. A white duck is swimming across the pond toward the crumbs, with motion lines behind it. On the right, next to the path, a wooden ladder stands upright and leaning, but there is nothing for it to lean against, which looks impossible. There is also a green rubbish bin in the front left, a grey rock in the front centre, trees on both sides and a bush on the right. The sun is in the upper right with two clouds.
