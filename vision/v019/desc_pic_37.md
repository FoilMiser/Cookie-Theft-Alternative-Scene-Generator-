# Picture 37

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with no people in it. There is a wooden fence across the back with a closed gate on the left, a pale paved path running from the gate down to the bottom-left corner, and a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the right, with a large tree behind it. The only animal is a white goat with small dark curved horns in the front centre-right, with its head down eating a small tuft of grass. There is a flower bed of mixed colourful flowers in front of the house, two potted plants and two green bushes near the fence, a teal watering can on the grass at the bottom left, and an empty grey wheelbarrow at the bottom centre. The sun is in the top-left corner and there are two white clouds. Nothing is happening apart from the goat grazing; the odd part is simply a goat loose in an ordinary back garden with the gate shut and no one around.
