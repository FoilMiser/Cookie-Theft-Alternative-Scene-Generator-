# pic_06

## Free description

This is a busy, sunny town street with a row of colourful shops (cream, peach, green and pink) with striped awnings. On the far left, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is holding a paper bag and scattering bird seed, which is falling to the ground where a small blue bird is pecking it up. Next to him, a tree has a blue ball stuck in its branches, and a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts is pointing up at it with a frown, as if the ball got stuck there. In the background, a little girl in a pink dress is holding two balloons (yellow and blue) while a third, purple balloon has slipped free and is floating away above her; she is reaching up after it. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans is watering a box of flowers on the pavement with a watering can. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress is pushing a pram with a sleeping baby under a pink polka-dot blanket, past a fruit and vegetable stall with a box beside it. At the front, a black-and-white cat is crouched low and creeping towards a red bird that is pecking at the ground, so it looks like the cat is about to pounce. There is also a street lamp and an empty bench.
