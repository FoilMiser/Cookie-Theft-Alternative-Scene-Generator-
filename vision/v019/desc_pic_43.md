# Picture 43

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence along the back, a closed gate on the far left, a paved path running from the gate to the bottom left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the left, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is digging a hole in the lawn with a shovel, right beside the path, with a small mound of dirt next to the hole. In the middle, a clothesline between two wooden posts holds a yellow towel with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans. On the right, an older man with a grey beard, a cap, a brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers is painting the fence blue with a paintbrush; three boards are already painted blue and a paint tin sits on the grass by his feet. There is a flower bed in front of the house and an empty wooden bench in the bottom right. The odd thing is a closed cardboard box floating in the sky above the left clothesline post, tilted, with nothing holding it up and nobody throwing it.
