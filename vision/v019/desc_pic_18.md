# Picture 18

## Free description
This is a sunny town street or square with a row of colourful two-storey shops: pink, light green, peach and cream, each with a striped awning. At the far left is a fruit stall under a teal-and-white canopy, but nobody is tending it. In front of it, a woman in a yellow dress carrying a yellow shopping bag walks off to the left. Next to her, a bearded man in a green shirt is scattering seeds or crumbs from his hand to a blue bird and a red bird pecking on the ground. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers is walking to the right while reading an open book. A blue bucket stands on the pavement just ahead of her, so she looks about to trip over it. An old man in a flat cap and waistcoat is watering a box of flowers with a teal watering can. Next to the flower box, a white dog with a brown ear is sitting up holding an open newspaper as if reading it, which is impossible. A child in yellow and orange sits on a bench reading a red book. In the front middle, a small girl in a pink dress is crying and holding an empty cone, while a brown dog sniffs or licks her dropped pink ice cream on the ground. On the right, a young man in a blue hoodie points up at a blue ball stuck in the branches of a tree. Two bicycles are parked in bike racks, a teal one at the back and a red one at the front.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The white animal reading the newspaper is most likely a white dog with a brown ear, but it could also be read as a cat or another white creature. Its face is partly hidden by the paper.
- The green-shirt man's white object could be a cup of seed, a paper bag or a drink. The trail of dots from his hand reads as seed or crumbs for the birds.
- The reading woman will probably hit or trip over the bucket. The lamp post is a bit further back, so she may not hit that.
- Whether the girl dropped the ice cream herself or the dog knocked it is not shown. She is crying and the dog has it now.
- Whose ball is in the tree is not shown; the young man seems to want it down.
- The two birds are quite large compared with the people.
- Unintentionally strange: nothing obvious beyond the newspaper-reading dog, which looks deliberate.
