# Picture 53

## Free description
This is a sunny playground with a metal fence and trees behind it. On the far left a small girl in a pink dress holds the strings of a yellow and a purple balloon, while she reaches up with her other hand toward a red balloon that has got away and is floating off near a tree, its string dangling. Behind her, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans sits on a bench reading a red book, and he hasn't noticed that a blue bird has landed on the bench beside him and is grabbing his sandwich. In the middle, a bearded man in a green shirt and beige trousers walks along pushing a teal pram with a sleeping baby under a pink spotted blanket. Next to him an older grey-haired woman in a teal top and grey skirt walks while reading an open book, and there is a blue bucket on the grass right in front of her feet, so she looks about to trip over it. Behind them is a swing set with one empty red swing. On the right, a child with braided hair in a yellow top and orange shorts is sliding down a slide. An empty sandpit sits at the front right. The sky is blue with two clouds and a sun at top right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The red balloon: most likely it slipped from the girl's hand (she is reaching after it), but it could also be someone else's balloon or tied to the tree; the string does not reach anything.
- The bird is small; it could be pecking at the sandwich or carrying it off. It looks like a blue bird such as a jay, but the shape is not clear.
- The bucket near the woman: it might belong to the sandpit (beach-style bucket) left out of place; the woman may or may not actually trip.
- The child on the slide has one arm raised; could be waving or holding on.
- The swing is empty and still; nothing odd about it, but it might be read as just having been left.
