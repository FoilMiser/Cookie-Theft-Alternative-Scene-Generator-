# Picture 09

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence along the back, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a paved path running from a gate on the left. In the middle of the lawn an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, is holding the nozzle of a garden hose up close to her face with both hands and peering straight into the end of it. Only a single drop of water is coming out of the nozzle. The hose runs from a hose reel by the house corner across the grass to her, and a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is standing on the hose partway along. Just on the reel side of the dog's back paw the hose is drawn swollen and fat, as if water is backed up behind the spot where the dog is standing. It looks like the dog is blocking the water, and when the dog steps off, the water will probably spray straight into the woman's face. Around the garden there is an empty swing set, a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow lying on the grass, and a bed of colourful flowers next to the house. The sun is shining in the upper left and there are two small clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Whether the dog's paw is actually pressing on the hose or just standing beside it is hard to be sure of from the drawing, but the bulge right next to its back paw makes it look like the dog is kinking/blocking the hose.
- The woman's expression is hard to read; she could be puzzled, or simply inspecting the nozzle.
- The drop at the nozzle could be read as a single drip of water or a small highlight on the nozzle.
- Nothing appears physically impossible; the main oddity is the woman looking directly into a hose that is about to spray.
