# Picture 55

## Free description
This is a paved town square in front of a row of coloured shops with striped awnings, under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds and no sun, so it looks like rain is coming. On the left, a young woman in a blue hoodie and dark trousers is in a sitting position drinking from a white cup, but there is no chair or bench under her, so she is sitting on nothing in mid-air. In the back, an older grey-haired woman in a teal top and grey skirt is taking a photo with a camera, and next to her a teal bicycle is parked in a bike rack. An older man in a cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is watering a planter of flowers with a watering can, even though it looks about to rain. A child with braided hair in a yellow top and orange shorts sits on a bench reading a red book. On the right, a bearded man in a green shirt and beige trousers stands by a fruit and vegetable stall and points up at the sky with a surprised look, as if he has felt a raindrop or noticed the clouds. At the front, a little girl in a pink dress holds an empty ice cream cone and covers her face as if crying, because her pink scoop of ice cream has fallen on the ground, and a brown dog with a red collar is sniffing or licking it. There is also an empty café table with two chairs, a lamp post, a bin and two potted plants.
