# Picture 46

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a wooden gate on the far left and a paved path running from the gate to the bottom left. On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a blue door and a window. There is a flower bed of colourful flowers in front of it and a garden hose reel beside it. In the middle, a girl in a yellow dress and red shoes holds the nozzle of a long green hose and peers at it. Only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose runs from the reel across the lawn, and a white dog with brown spots is standing right on it. The section of hose between the dog and the reel is swollen with trapped water. So the dog is blocking the water, and it looks like the hose will spray the girl in the face when the dog steps off. Behind the swing set, a white goat with small horns is eating a tuft of grass, which is an unusual animal to find in a garden. On the right near the house, a person in a blue hoodie, dark trousers and a tall blue hat stands with hands on hips, watching. A red dog bowl with food is on the grass at the bottom.

## Inventory
- Girl, centre front: dark short hair, yellow dress, red shoes. She holds the hose nozzle with both hands and looks at it, puzzled because no water is coming out.
- Person, right, back (by the house): short brown hair, tall blue hat, blue hoodie, dark trousers, white shoes. Stands with hands on hips, watching.
- Dog, front right of centre: white with brown spots and a blue collar. Standing on the hose and looking left, towards the girl.
- Goat, back left of centre (behind/next to the swing set): white with small dark horns and dark hooves. Head down, eating a tuft of grass.
- Garden hose: dark green/teal. It runs from the reel by the house, along the bottom of the lawn and under the dog's paws, then up to the nozzle in the girl's hands. There is a bulge in the hose between the dog and the reel.
- Water drop: a single drop at the nozzle.
- Hose reel, right, beside the flower bed and house.
- Swing set, left of centre: grey metal frame with one red seat. Empty.
- Dog bowl, bottom centre-left: red, filled with dog food.
- Flower bed, right: oval of soil with red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers.
- House, right: cream walls, red roof, blue door, one window.
- Wooden gate, far left in the fence, and a paved path leading from it towards the viewer.
- Tree behind the fence (right), bush beside the house, hedges behind the fence, sun (top left), two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The tall blue hat could be read as a blue bucket worn on the head, or as a fez- or chef-style hat.
- The person by the house could be a man or a woman, and I can't tell if they've noticed the dog on the hose.
- The goat in a family back garden is odd. It could be a pet, or it could have wandered in.
- The bulge in the hose reads as water building up behind the dog's paw. Without the dog, it could look like just a thick section of hose.
- The girl may be about to get sprayed in the face, but that is an inference, not something shown.
