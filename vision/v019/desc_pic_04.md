# pic_04

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden seen the other way round: the house with the red roof and blue door is on the left, the sun is in the top right, and a wooden gate and paved path are on the right. In the middle, a woman (or girl) in a yellow dress and red shoes is standing at a clothesline, reaching up with both hands to peg a white T-shirt onto it; a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes are already hanging there. A black-and-white cat is walking along the top of the wooden fence towards a birdhouse on a tall pole. Strangely, there is an orange fish sitting in the leaves of the tree behind the house, which is impossible. The rest of the garden has a flower bed full of colourful flowers, a hose reel, an empty wooden bench, two potted plants, a wheelbarrow and a watering can on the grass. Nothing seems about to go wrong except that the cat could be heading for the birdhouse.
