# Picture 40

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny park with a winding sand-coloured path, a tall black lamppost in the middle, rolling hills behind, and a pond on the right with a white duck swimming in it. On the far left a little girl in a pink dress is holding a string, but her purple kite is stuck in the branches of a tree. Up the path, an old man with a grey beard, cap, brown waistcoat and white shirt is walking along carrying a yellow bag. On a bench in the middle a boy in a blue hoodie is reading a red book, and he hasn't noticed that a blue bird has landed next to him and is pecking at his sandwich on the bench. Near the pond an old woman with grey hair in a bun is throwing bread crumbs to the duck. On the right a man in a red shirt and jeans is watering a flower bed with a watering can, and a woman in a purple top with a camera is taking a photo, pointing toward the left. In the foreground a small white puppy is lying asleep on the path, and a garden sprinkler at the bottom is spraying water onto a cardboard box sitting in a puddle on the grass. The strangest thing is in the bottom left corner: a brown dog with a red collar is sitting up and holding open a newspaper as if it were reading it, which is impossible.
