## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence along the back, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a paved path running down the left side past a wooden gate. A man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands near the swing set with his hands on his hips, and he is wearing a blue bucket on his head that comes down over the top of his face. Behind him on the right, a man in an orange cap and orange safety vest is painting part of the fence blue with a brush on a long handle; a few boards are already blue and a small paint can sits by his feet. In the middle front, a woman (or older girl) in a purple top and teal trousers is holding the end of a garden hose up close to her face and peering into the nozzle, where only a single drop of water is coming out. The reason no water is coming out seems to be that a white dog with brown spots is standing on the hose, and the hose between the dog and the hose reel looks swollen, as if water is building up. It looks like she is about to get sprayed in the face as soon as the dog steps off. Other things in the garden are an empty swing set with one red seat, a birdhouse on a tall pole by the gate, a flower bed full of colourful flowers, a watering can and a wheelbarrow lying on the grass. The oddest things are the man with a bucket on his head and the woman looking straight into the hose nozzle.
