# Picture 25

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden. A cream-coloured house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the left, and a wooden fence with a gate on the right runs across the back. A paved path leads from the gate down to the bottom-right corner. In the middle, an older man with a grey beard, a grey-blue cap, a white shirt, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers is watering a flowerbed full of colourful flowers. He is pouring the water out of a frying pan instead of a watering can, which is strange. A teal watering can sits unused on the grass at the bottom right. At the bottom left, a girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench eating a pink ice cream (or something pink) held up to her mouth. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is on the grass just in front of the bench, looking up at her with its mouth open. It seems to be begging for the ice cream or barking. A red bird flies across the sky near two white clouds, and the sun is in the top right. A grey wheelbarrow is on the grass at the bottom, and there are two potted plants and a bush near the fence. The odd thing is the man using a frying pan to water the flowers while a proper watering can lies nearby.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The man's tool is a frying pan or shallow saucepan. Either way it is not a watering can, and the water pours out in a long steady stream, more than a pan would hold.
- The pink thing the girl holds is most likely an ice cream cone. It could also be a lolly or a sweet.
- The dog might be begging for the girl's food, or it might be barking.
- The man's face points left, toward the pan and flowers. He seems to be looking at what he is doing.
- Unintentionally odd: nothing floats or passes through anything. The only oddity is watering with a pan while the watering can sits unused.
