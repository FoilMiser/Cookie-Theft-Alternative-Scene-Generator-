# Picture 17

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden behind a cream-coloured house with a red roof, a window and a blue front door. A tall wooden fence runs across the back, with a wooden gate at the far left and a tree and hedges behind it. In the background, near the fence and right of centre, a small girl in a pink dress is flying a yellow kite. The kite is high in the sky and she holds the string up with one hand. In the front middle, a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts is jumping in the air with both arms up, smiling with his eyes shut. His shadow is on the grass below his feet. On the paved path at the bottom left, a woman (or older girl) with short dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes is sweeping a small pile of dirt towards a red dustpan. Instead of a broom she is using a giant silver dinner fork almost as tall as she is, and a little puff of dust is rising. That oversized fork used as a broom is the strange, impossible part of the picture. Left of centre there is an empty swing set with one red seat hanging still. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits at the bottom centre, and a bed of colourful flowers is next to the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The person with the fork could be an adult woman or an older girl; the proportions don't make it clear.
- The fork could be meant as a rake or as a broom. It is being pushed along the ground into the dirt next to a dustpan, which reads most like sweeping.
- I can't tell why the boy is jumping. It could be for joy, cheering about the kite, or reaching for something, but nothing is above him.
- The girl with the kite is very small. She is either far away at the back or a very small child. Her face is turned up and away, so it is hard to see.
- The wheelbarrow looks slightly tipped or flat on the ground with no legs clearly visible, but this may just be the drawing angle.
- Unintentionally strange: nothing else. The giant fork is the only clearly impossible thing.
