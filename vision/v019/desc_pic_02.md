# pic_02

## Free description

This is the same kind of back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a path down the left side and a house with a red roof and blue door on the right, but the sky is grey and covered in three dark storm clouds. On the path at the left, a man in an orange cap, orange safety vest and blue trousers is pointing up at the sky with his mouth open, looking worried, as if he has noticed rain coming. In the middle, a woman (or girl) in a yellow dress and red shoes is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red lead, heading towards the right. Further right, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is walking along carrying a cardboard box. Behind them, a clothesline strung between two wooden posts has a yellow towel with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans pegged to it, so it looks like the washing is about to get rained on. At the front of the picture there is a red dog bowl full of food on the grass, and the dog is walking away from it. On the right there is a colourful flower bed and a wooden bench in front of the house. Nobody except the man in the orange vest seems to be paying attention to the weather.
