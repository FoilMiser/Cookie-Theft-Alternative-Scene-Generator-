This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate in the fence on the left, a pale paved path running from the gate down to the bottom-left corner, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. On the left-centre of the lawn, a girl with a brown ponytail, wearing a blue hoodie and dark trousers, is kicking an orange ball; her kicking leg is stretched out toward the left, the ball is right at her foot, and curved motion lines under her leg show it swinging. On the right, in front of the fence, an older man in a grey flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is digging a hole in the lawn with a long-handled shovel; there is a mound of dirt beside the hole and some dirt on the shovel blade. In front of the house there is a bed of colourful flowers, a teal hose reel, and a wooden bench. A teal watering can sits on the grass near the bottom left, and a birdhouse stands on a tall pole near the left side of the fence. The strange thing is a brown cardboard box hanging in mid-air in the sky above the fence in the middle of the picture, tilted at an angle, with nobody near it who could have thrown it. The sun is shining in the top-left corner, and there are two small white clouds.
