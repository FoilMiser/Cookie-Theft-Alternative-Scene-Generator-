# Picture 26

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, and a wooden fence with a gate on the right runs across the back, with a paved path from the gate to the bottom-right corner. A tan dog with a white chest and a red collar sits by the blue door holding its red lead in its mouth. The lead trails onto the ground in a loop, as if the dog wants to go for a walk. In front of a colourful flowerbed stands a person in an orange cap, an orange high-visibility vest with reflective stripes, a grey shirt and dark blue trousers, holding a teal watering can low down. The water coming out of the can goes up in a curve into the air with drops at the top, like a fountain, instead of pouring down onto the flowers. That looks wrong. Further back, a woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers stands between two wooden posts, reaching up to peg a white T-shirt on a washing line. Blue jeans and a yellow item with a red stripe already hang on the line. A red dog bowl with dry food sits on the grass near the bottom, and two potted plants stand near the fence. The sun and two clouds are in the sky.
