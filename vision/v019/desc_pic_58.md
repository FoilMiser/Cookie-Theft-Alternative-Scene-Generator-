# Picture 58

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills, a round blue pond edged with reeds, trees, bushes, a black lamp post, a wooden bench and a winding sandy path on the right. A white duck is swimming on the pond. In the front middle, a young man with brown hair in a red shirt and blue jeans sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket with a wicker picnic basket beside him; his eyes are closed and he is smiling with pink cheeks, as if relaxing in the sun or laughing. The strange thing is a tall wooden ladder just to the right of the lamp post: it is leaning at an angle, but its top is resting against nothing, just open air, so it should fall over. In the front there is a grey rock on the grass, a bed of colourful flowers on the left and a green rubbish bin in the bottom left corner. The sky is blue with two white clouds and a bright sun at top right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The ladder: literally it is leaning against nothing and should not stand up, which is impossible. Another reading is that it is propped against something hidden behind the lamp post, but nothing is visible there; the lamp post is to its left and does not touch it.
- The man's expression could be read as laughing, sleeping/dozing in the sun, or blushing (the pink cheeks).
- No food is visible outside the basket; it is unclear whether he is about to eat or has finished.
