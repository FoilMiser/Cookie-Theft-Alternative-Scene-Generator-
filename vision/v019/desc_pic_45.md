This is a sunny day at a playground. A tall dark metal fence runs across the back, with green hedges or trees behind it. There is a sun in the top left and two white clouds. On the left is a slide with a red frame, a ladder and a yellow-and-orange chute. In the middle is an empty swing set with one red seat, and there is a green rubbish bin between the slide and the swings. In the front right is a red seesaw. An older man with grey hair and a blue-grey cap sits on the low end, which rests on the ground. He is reading an open book and not looking at the girl. Because the man is reading and keeping his end down, the girl looks stuck at the top, waiting for him to push off. There is a large tree on the right, a bush on the far left and a grey rock in the bottom left.
