# Picture 30

## Free description
This is a back garden under a grey, stormy sky. Three dark storm clouds hang overhead and there is no sun, so it looks like rain is coming. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, and a wooden fence with a gate on the right runs across the back, with a paved path from the gate to the bottom-right corner. A man with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands in the middle watering a colourful flowerbed with a green garden hose. The hose runs back along the grass to a hose reel at the left end of the flowerbed. Watering the flowers just before a storm seems pointless. Behind him, blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes are still hanging on the washing line, about to get rained on. A tan dog with a red collar sits by the blue door holding its red lead in its mouth, as if it wants a walk. On the path at the right, a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts looks up and points at the dark clouds, as if warning that rain is coming. A grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can sit on the grass at the front, and two potted plants stand by the fence.
