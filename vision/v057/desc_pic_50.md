## Free description
A sunny park with hills, a winding sandy path on the left, a pond on the right and a green bin in the bottom right corner. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans is walking across the grass in the front centre while licking a pink ice-cream cone. In the middle of the open grass a tall wooden ladder stands straight up on its own, not leaning against anything, which is impossible. A grey rock sits on the grass in front of and slightly to the left of the man. He is looking at his ice cream, not where he is going.
