## Free description
A sunny park with rolling hills and trees, an oval pond on the left, a lamp post, a green bin, an empty wooden bench and a winding path on the right. A girl or young woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks across the grass in the centre front, holding a pink ice cream cone to her mouth and licking it. A white duck floats on the pond. A flower bed with colourful flowers is at the lower left and a grey rock lies near the centre bottom. The scene is calm; nothing seems about to happen and nothing looks impossible.
