# pic_32

## Free description
A sunny park with a pond on the left, green hills and trees in the background and a sandy path winding off on the right. An older man in a cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers stands at the front edge of the pond holding a paper bag, tossing bread crumbs towards a white duck. The duck is swimming in the pond and pecking at crumbs floating on the water. On the right, a girl in a pink dress is walking along the path eating a pink ice-cream cone. In the middle of the grass stands a tall wooden ladder, completely upright and free-standing with nothing to lean on. That ladder is the impossible part: it should fall over, since it is not leaning against a tree, wall or anything. A green bin sits bottom left and a grey rock front centre.
