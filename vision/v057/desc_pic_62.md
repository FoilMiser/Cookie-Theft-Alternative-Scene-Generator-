## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a house with a blue door on the right, a gate on the left and a paved path running down the left side. On the path a man in a green shirt walks along reading an open book, and a cardboard box sits on the path just ahead of his feet, so it looks like he is about to trip over it. In the middle an older man in a flat cap and brown vest is digging with a shovel, with a mound of earth beside him. To his right a woman in a yellow dress is painting the fence pink with a long-handled roller; a few fence boards are already pink. A paint tray and an open pink paint tin sit on the grass behind her. A white dog with brown spots is walking away from the paint tray, and a line of pink paw prints leads from the tray to the dog, so it has stepped in the paint and is tracking it across the lawn. There is also a birdhouse on a pole, two potted plants, a flower bed and a bench by the house.
