# pic_106

## Free description
A sunny town street with a row of four colourful shops (pink, pale green, peach, cream) with striped awnings, and a paved square in front. On the left a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks along carrying a cardboard box, in front of a fruit and vegetable stall. In the front centre an orange cat is crouched low, stalking forward towards a blue bird. The blue bird is pecking at seeds on the ground and does not seem to notice the cat, so the cat looks about to pounce on it. A red bird sits on a branch of the tree on the right. A teal bicycle is parked in a bike rack in the middle background, and there is an empty wooden bench, a street lamp, a flower box, a green bin and a potted plant. Nothing looks impossible; the main event is the cat about to catch the bird.
