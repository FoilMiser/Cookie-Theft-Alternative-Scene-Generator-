## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house on the left, a tall wooden fence, and a paved path on the right leading to a wooden gate. In the middle a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers is reaching up to hang washing on a clothesline between two posts; a pair of blue jeans and a white T-shirt are already pegged up and are dripping water. A wicker laundry basket full of clothes sits on the grass beside him. On the right a woman in a yellow dress is sweeping a pile of brown leaves with a yellow broom. There is a bed of colourful flowers by the house, two potted plants, a birdhouse on a tall pole, a red wheelbarrow and a teal watering can in the front. Everything looks like ordinary garden chores and nothing seems obviously wrong.
