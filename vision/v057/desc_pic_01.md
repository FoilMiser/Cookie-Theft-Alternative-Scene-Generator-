## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a paved path running from a gate at the back left down to the front. On the left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping a small pile of brown leaves on the path with a broom. In the middle, an older man in a grey cap, brown vest and white shirt is painting the fence pink with a paint roller; about three fence boards are already bright pink. A pink paint tray and an open paint tin sit on the grass near his feet. A small white dog with brown spots is walking away from the paint tray, and it has left a trail of pink paw prints on the grass; its paws look pink, so it has apparently stepped in the paint. A flower bed of colourful flowers is in front of the house, and a teal watering can and a red wheelbarrow sit in the foreground. It looks like the dog is about to track pink paint further across the garden without anyone noticing.
