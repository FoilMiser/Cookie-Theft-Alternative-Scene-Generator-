# pic_25

## Free description
A sunny park with a round pond, a lamp post, a bin, an empty bench, a winding path and trees. On the left front a child with dark curly hair in a yellow top and orange shorts holds a reel and flies a yellow kite high in the sky. Next to the child, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is in a sitting position, drinking from a cup through a straw. But there is no chair, bench or anything under him: he is sitting on thin air, which is impossible. On the right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers takes seeds from a paper bag and sprinkles them for two small birds, one red and one blue, which are pecking on the ground. There is a grey rock in the front centre.
