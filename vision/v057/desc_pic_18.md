A sunny but windy day in a park with hills, trees, a winding path, a tall lamppost and a pond on the right. White wind lines are drawn in the sky. On the left, by the path, an older woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal cardigan and grey skirt is reaching up with one arm as a red cap is blown off and away into the air above her, along with a few small leaves. She looks surprised. In the front centre, a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts is standing calmly and licking a pink ice-cream cone. A green rubbish bin stands at the bottom right. The cap seems about to blow further away unless she catches it.
