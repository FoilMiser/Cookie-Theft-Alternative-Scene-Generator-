## 1. Free description

It's a sunny day in a park: there's a pond on the left, a path curving down the right side, green hills behind, and a big sun in the top right. A boy in a yellow and orange outfit stands beside the pond and throws breadcrumbs to a white duck, which is swimming toward a little trail of crumbs on the water. At the front left a brown dog wearing glasses sits up and reads a newspaper like a person. Next to it is a flower bed, and a white goat is tied to a short post and grazing on a small tuft of grass. In the middle a girl in a pink dress holds two balloons, one yellow and one purple. She is reaching up toward a red balloon that has got away and is floating up past a tall street lamp, where a red bird sits on top. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up into a tree, where a blue-and-white beach ball is stuck in the branches. At the front right a woman in a yellow dress digs a hole with a shovel, with a pile of dirt beside her. A garden hose runs along the bottom to a sprinkler, and the sprinkler's arcs of water are landing on a cardboard box sitting on the path, so the box is getting wet and there is a puddle under it. The odd parts are the dog reading a newspaper while wearing glasses, and the box being soaked by the sprinkler.

- Dog reading a newspaper with glasses: this is impossible in real life. It looks drawn on purpose as the absurd element, but I'm noting it either way.
- Box under the sprinkler: it's unclear whether the box was left there by someone or whether the point is that the water will damage it. Nobody nearby seems to own it.
