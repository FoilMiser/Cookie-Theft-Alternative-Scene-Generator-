The garden has a wooden fence, a gate and path on the left, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a flower bed and two potted plants. An older woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal cardigan and grey skirt stands in the middle of the lawn pointing up at the dark clouds, looking worried. It looks like rain is about to start, and she may be about to go inside or bring things in. A teal watering can sits on the grass in the front left. There is no one else in the scene.
