## Free description
A busy, sunny town square paved in grey tiles, with a row of colourful two-storey shops with striped awnings behind. On the left, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans walks carrying a yellow shopping bag past a fruit-and-vegetable stall. Behind him, a man in a green shirt is scattering seeds or crumbs from a paper bag for a red bird pecking on the ground. In the middle front, a little girl in a pink dress holds an empty ice-cream cone with her hand to her mouth, looking upset, while a white dog with brown spots licks up her dropped pink ice cream from the ground. On a bench in the middle, an older woman in a teal cardigan and glasses reads a red book; next to her on the bench a blue bird is grabbing or pecking at her sandwich, which she has not noticed. In front of the bench, a brown dog wearing glasses sits up holding and reading a newspaper, which is clearly impossible/silly. To the right, a woman in a purple top sweeps leaves with a broom, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in a tree, and an older man in a grey cap pushes a red bicycle whose wheels are square, which is another impossible detail.
