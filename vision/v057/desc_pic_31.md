## Free description
A sunny back garden enclosed by a tall wooden fence with a gate on the left, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a paved path in the front left. Three people are in the garden. In the back centre, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts is flying an orange kite high in the sky, holding the string up with both hands. At the front left, an older man in a cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is digging with a spade, with a pile of soil next to the hole. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is watering a round flowerbed by pouring water out of a black frying pan, while a green watering can sits unused on the grass just behind him. That frying pan is the odd thing: a cooking pan is being used as a watering can, with a stream of water pouring out of it. A wooden bench stands in front of the house.
