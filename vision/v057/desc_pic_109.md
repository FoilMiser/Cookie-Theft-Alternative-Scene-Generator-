# pic_109

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a paved path, a swing set and a house with a blue door on the right. A man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans walks across the garden carrying a cardboard box. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie waters a flower bed with a teal watering can; the stream of water arcs upward and over in a large curve rather than pouring down. In the front a brown dog with a red collar crouches in a playful bow with a yellow-green tennis ball in front of it, wagging its tail, as if wanting someone to throw the ball. A red wheelbarrow stands near the dog. The swing hangs empty.
