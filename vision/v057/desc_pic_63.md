# pic_63

## Free description
A sunny back garden with the house (blue door) on the left, a wooden fence along the back, a gate on the right and a paved path running down the right side. A woman in a yellow dress stands by a flower bed near the house, holding a teal watering can. Strangely, the water does not pour down from the spout: a thick blue stream arcs upward from the spout, like a fountain or a bent hose, and drops spray up above the flowers. Behind her, a washing line between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. Two potted plants stand near the fence on the right, and a red wheelbarrow sits empty on the lawn at the front. No one else is in the picture.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water stream goes upward from the watering can, against gravity; it could be read as water squirting up like a fountain, or as a blue hose attached to the can. Either way it looks wrong.
- The woman is not tilting the can much, which makes the upward stream odder.
