## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path running down the right side. A woman in a yellow dress stands by the fence holding a long-handled paint roller and is painting the fence pink; a section of about three boards is already pink. A paint tray with pink paint and an open pink paint tin sit on the grass by her feet. A white dog with brown spots is walking away from the paint area towards the flower bed, leaving a trail of pink paw prints behind it, so it has clearly stepped in the paint. An old grey-haired woman in a green cardigan sits on a bench at the front left reading a red book, and a blue bird perched on the end of the bench is pecking at a sandwich that lies on the bench beside her, apparently stealing her lunch without her noticing. On the right, a brown dog wearing glasses sits up on its haunches holding and reading a newspaper, which is impossible/silly. A red wheelbarrow stands empty in the front middle, and two potted plants sit near the fence.
