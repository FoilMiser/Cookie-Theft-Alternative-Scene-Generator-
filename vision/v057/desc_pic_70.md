## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a paved path, and a cream house with a blue door on the right. A small girl in a pink dress stands in the middle of the lawn holding the strings of two balloons, a yellow one and a blue one. A third balloon, a red one, is floating high above her near the top of the fence, with its string dangling loose beneath it. The girl is reaching one arm up toward the red balloon, and she looks worried, so it seems she has just let go of it and it is drifting away. A metal swing set with an empty red seat stands to the left. A watering can and a red wheelbarrow are on the grass at the front, and there is a flower bed near the house. Nothing looks impossible. The main event is the escaped red balloon.
