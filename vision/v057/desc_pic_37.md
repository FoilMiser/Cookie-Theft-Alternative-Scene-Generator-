## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with red roof and blue door on the left, a long wooden fence with a gate on the right, and a paved path running down the right side. A man with short dark hair and a beard, in a green shirt and beige trousers, is standing beside a round flowerbed and pouring water onto the flowers from a black frying pan. A teal watering can stands on the grass right next to him, unused. A red wheelbarrow sits empty at the front centre and two potted plants stand near the gate. The odd thing is the frying pan being used as a watering can, with water streaming out of it, while the proper watering can sits right there. Nobody else is in the garden.
