## Free description
A sunny back garden with the cream house on the left, a wooden fence along the back, a gate and path on the right, a flower bed and a wooden bench at front left. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands in the middle holding a kite reel up in the air, but his kite is stuck in the tree behind the house; the string runs from the tree down to the grass in a loose loop and then up to his hand. In the centre-right, an older man with grey hair and a cap, wearing a brown vest over a white shirt, is painting the fence bright pink with a long-handled paint roller; a few fence boards are already pink. A pot of pink paint is on the grass by his feet. At the front centre a small brown puppy is lying curled up asleep on the grass. A teal watering can stands at the front right.
