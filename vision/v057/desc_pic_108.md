## Free description
A sunny park with a winding path on the left, a pond on the right, a street lamp and a bin in the middle. A woman with dark hair in a purple T-shirt and teal trousers stands near the path eating a pink ice-cream cone. In the front centre a white goat with horns and a red collar is grazing on a small tuft of grass. The goat is tied by a rope to a short wooden stake in the ground. Just in front of the stake there is a grey rock. Nothing much is happening; it is a quiet scene of a woman eating ice cream and a tethered goat eating grass.
