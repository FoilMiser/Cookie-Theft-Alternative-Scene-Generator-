## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a swing set in the middle-right, a bird house on a pole and a gate at the back right with a path leading forward. A man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands by the flower bed next to the house, pouring water onto the flowers out of a frying pan instead of a watering can. Oddly, a teal watering can is standing right beside him on the grass, unused. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps leaves on the path with a broom. A red wheelbarrow stands empty in the front. The swing hangs empty. The main odd thing is watering flowers with a frying pan.
