# pic_121

## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, trees, bushes and a winding sandy path on the left. A boy in a red cap, blue hooded top and dark trousers stands on the grass by a round pond, holding a fishing rod out over the water. The fishing line ends in a red-and-white float bobbing in the water with a little ripple around it. A white duck is swimming on the right side of the pond, and there are bulrushes at the pond's edges. In the middle of the field a tall wooden ladder stands upright on its own, leaning against nothing — it should fall over, so it looks impossible. There is a grey rock in the foreground and a green litter bin at the bottom right.
