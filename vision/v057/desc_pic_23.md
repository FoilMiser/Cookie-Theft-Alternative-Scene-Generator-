# pic_23

## Free description
A sunny town square paved with grey tiles, in front of a row of colourful shops with striped awnings. On the left a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie looks up unhappily and points at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of a tree. In the centre a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench drinking from a cup with a straw. In front of her an elderly grey-haired woman with glasses is sweeping a pile of dust towards a red dustpan, but she is using a giant fork instead of a broom, which is odd. On the right a little girl in a pink dress holds a yellow balloon on a string, standing near a fruit stall with crates of red, orange, yellow and green fruit. A red bird sits on top of a street lamp. A bicycle is parked in a bike rack, and there is a café table with two chairs, a bin, a wooden crate and two potted plants.
