## Free description
A sunny town square paved with grey tiles, lined with colourful two-storey shops with striped awnings. In the front centre, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping a small pile of dead leaves with a yellow broom. Behind her, a bearded man in a green shirt and beige trousers sits on a wooden bench drinking from a red cup through a straw. On the right, a little girl in a pink dress points up at a blue-and-white beach ball that is stuck in the branches of a tree. On the left is a fruit and vegetable stall with a striped canopy, a wooden crate and a potted plant. On the right are two small round café tables with black chairs, and a green bin by the tree. The girl seems to want the ball down from the tree.
