On the left, an old man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands on the paving path sweeping a small pile of grey dust into a red dustpan, but he is using a giant dinner fork instead of a broom, which looks wrong and silly. In the middle right, a boy in a red cap and blue top kneels at a flower bed, planting a small seedling with his hand; a tray of little seedlings sits at the edge of the bed. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. A blue bird is perched on the bench right next to her and is pecking at her sandwich, which lies on the bench, while she does not notice. The bird looks about to steal or eat her sandwich.
