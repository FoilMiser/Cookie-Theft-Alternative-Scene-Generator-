## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence at the back, a gate and paved path on the right, and a grey metal swing frame with a single red swing seat in the centre-right. An older man with grey hair, a flat cap, a white shirt and a brown waistcoat sweeps a pile of brown leaves on the path with a yellow broom. A teal watering can sits on the grass near him, and a coiled garden hose on a reel lies on the lawn on the left near the flower bed. A cardboard box hangs in the sky above the fence, tilted, with nothing holding it up and no one nearby to have thrown it. The swing hangs still and empty. Apart from the floating box this is an ordinary chore scene.
