## Free description
A sunny, quiet park with a winding path on the left, a tall black lamp post in the middle, a pond with reeds on the right, trees, bushes and hills. There are no people. A white goat with small horns and a red collar is in the foreground centre, bending its head down to eat a tuft of grass. A rope runs from the goat's collar to a short wooden stake in the ground, so it is tethered. An empty wooden bench is on the left beside the path, and a green bin stands in the bottom right. Nothing seems about to happen and nothing looks impossible.
