A boy in a red T-shirt and jeans is painting the fence bright pink with a paint roller; several fence boards are already pink. Next to his feet are a tin of pink paint and a paint tray, and a white-and-brown dog has walked through the paint and is leaving a trail of pink paw prints across the grass. A cardboard box is floating in the sky at the top left with nothing holding it up, which is impossible. There is also a flower bed by the house, a teal watering can and a red wheelbarrow in the front.
