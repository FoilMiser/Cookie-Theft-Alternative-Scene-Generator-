A woman with dark hair in a yellow dress stands in the middle holding the nozzle of a long garden hose with both hands, pointing it upward, but no water is coming out of it. The hose runs from a hose reel next to the house, across the grass, to her. A white dog with brown spots is standing right on top of the hose, which would pinch it and stop the water. At the hose reel end there is a small puddle and drip of water leaking out. Behind her is a washing line between two posts with blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel hanging on it. A red wheelbarrow sits front centre and two potted plants stand by the fence; a flowerbed sits near the house.
