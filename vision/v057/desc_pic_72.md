# pic_72

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left, a wooden fence and gate at the back, and a paved path on the right. In the centre a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie holds the nozzle of a garden hose up in front of him and peers into it, because no water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel by the house, across the lawn, to his hands. A white dog with brown spots is standing right on the hose, squashing it, which is presumably why the water has stopped. If the dog steps off, the water will probably spray into the boy's face. On the right a woman in a yellow dress sweeps a small pile of brown leaves on the path with a broom. A clothesline in the background has blue jeans and a yellow striped towel pegged on it. A small puddle is under the hose reel.
