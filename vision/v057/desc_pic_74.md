## Free description
A windy, sunny day in a park with hills, a pond, a lamp post, a bench, a flower bed and a winding path. White wind lines streak across the sky. An older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, reaches up as her red cap blows off her head into the air, along with a couple of leaves. On the left a man in a green shirt walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead along the path. On the right, near the pond, an older man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat bends over and sprinkles seed from a paper bag. A blue bird and a red bird peck at the seeds on the ground. The bench is empty. Nothing looks impossible. The cap is flying away on the wind.
