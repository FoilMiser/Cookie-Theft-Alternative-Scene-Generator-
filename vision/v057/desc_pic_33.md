## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with red roof and blue door on the left, a long wooden fence with a gate on the right, and a paved path running down the right side. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands by a round flowerbed near the house, pouring water onto the flowers from a black frying pan. A teal watering can stands on the grass right beside him, unused. On the right, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is sweeping a small pile of dry leaves on the path with a yellow broom. A red wheelbarrow sits empty at the front centre and two potted plants stand by the fence. The odd part is the frying pan being used to water flowers while a real watering can sits next to it.
