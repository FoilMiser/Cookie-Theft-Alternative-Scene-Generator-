## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and path on the left, and a house with a blue door and a bench on the right. On the left a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts kicks an orange ball. In the middle a girl in a pink dress holds a red and a purple balloon and reaches up after a blue balloon that has slipped away and is floating up over the fence. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is watering the flower bed by pouring water out of a frying pan, which is an odd tool for the job. A proper teal watering can stands unused on the grass just behind him. A red wheelbarrow sits empty at the front.
