# pic_85

## Free description
A sunny park with a round pond on the left, rolling hills, trees, a tall lamp post in the centre and a winding sandy path on the right. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands by the pond holding a small paper bag and throwing bread crumbs towards a white duck, which is swimming and pecking at crumbs floating on the water. Further back on the path, a girl in a pink dress runs along holding a yellow balloon on a string. In the front right, a woman in a yellow dress pushes a teal pram with a sleeping baby under a pink spotted blanket. A green rubbish bin is at the front left and a grey rock sits on the grass in the front centre. Everything looks peaceful and normal.
