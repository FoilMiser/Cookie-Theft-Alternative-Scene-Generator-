## Free description
A sunny park with a pond, rolling hills, trees and a winding path. In the centre a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands fishing in the pond with a long rod; his line ends in a red-and-white float in the water with a ripple around it, as if something is biting. On the right, along the path, an older man in a cap and brown waistcoat holds a paper bag and scatters seeds for two birds, a blue one and a red one, which are pecking at the ground. In the front right an older woman with grey hair and glasses walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead along the path. There is an empty bench, a street lamp, a green bin, and a flower bed in the front left. Everything looks calm and ordinary.
