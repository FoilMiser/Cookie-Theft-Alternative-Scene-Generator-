In the centre front, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie waves one arm high (motion lines), smiling, as if waving to someone. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress waters a colourful flower bed with a green watering can, water pouring out. Nothing looks impossible apart from the escaping balloon.
