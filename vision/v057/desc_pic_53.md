# pic_53

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house and blue door on the left, a flower bed, a wooden fence with a gate on the right and a paving path on the right. In the middle a woman in a yellow dress is hanging washing on a clothesline between two wooden posts; she is pegging up a white T-shirt, and a pair of blue jeans already hangs on the line. Both pieces of clothing are dripping water. A basket of more laundry sits on the grass beside her. In the front right an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses and a teal cardigan is sweeping a pile of dry leaves on the path with a broom. There is a birdhouse on a tall pole, two potted plants, a red wheelbarrow and a teal watering can on the lawn.
