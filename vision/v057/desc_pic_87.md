# pic_87

## Free description
A sunny park with a round pond on the left, rolling hills behind and a winding sandy path on the right. In the front centre-left, a girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes walks to the right while eating (licking) a pink ice-cream cone. Just ahead of her, in the centre, a white goat with a red collar has its head down eating a small tuft of grass. The goat is tied by a long rope from its collar to a short wooden post in the ground. A tall black lamp post stands behind the goat with a green rubbish bin beside it. There are cattails at both ends of the pond, trees on the left and right, several bushes, and a grey rock in the front. Nothing looks about to go badly wrong; it is a calm scene, though the girl is walking toward the goat with food in her hand.
