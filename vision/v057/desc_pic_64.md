# pic_64

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and path on the left, and a house with a blue door on the right. On the path a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks while reading an open book, and a cardboard box sits right in front of her on the path, so she looks about to trip over it. Near the fence a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is raking a small pile of leaves. In the middle front a little girl in a pink dress holds a kite string reel up in the air, looking up. Her yellow kite is stuck in the tree at the top right, beside the house; the string sags slack from the tree down to the ground in a loop near her feet and back up to her reel. A washing line holds a striped yellow towel and blue jeans, and there is a birdhouse on a pole and a flower bed by the house.
