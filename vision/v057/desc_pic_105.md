# pic_105

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof on the left, a wooden fence and a gate with a path on the right. A man in a red shirt and blue trousers holds the nozzle of a garden hose up to his face and peers into it, looking puzzled, because only a drip comes out. The hose runs across the lawn from a hose reel by the house, and a brown dog with a red collar is standing right on the hose, squashing it, with its eyes closed looking pleased. That seems to be why the water is not coming through. If the dog steps off, the water may spray the man in the face. A small puddle has formed by the hose reel. In the background, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers rakes a small pile of leaves. On top of the fence, a grey cat creeps along towards a blue bird perched further along the fence, as if about to pounce.
