## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left, a wooden fence along the back with a gate on the right, and a washing line with jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel pegged on it. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands in the middle holding the nozzle of a green hose and looking into the end of it; no water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel by the house across the lawn to him. A white dog with brown spots is standing on the hose, and just behind its paws the hose has a bulge, so the dog seems to be blocking the water. A small drip or puddle is at the bottom of the reel. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress is sweeping a pile of dry leaves on the paved path with a broom. There is a flower bed in front of the house and two potted plants on the lawn.
