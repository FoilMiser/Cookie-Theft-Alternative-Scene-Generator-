## Free description
A back garden on a sunny day, with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate and a paving path on the right. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands in the middle near the fence raking a small pile of leaves. A boy in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands in front on the right holding a green garden hose; he holds the nozzle up close to his face and looks into it, and a small drop of water seems to come out. The hose runs from a hose reel by the house across the lawn and under a brown dog. The dog, with a red collar, stands on the hose, eyes closed and smiling, which may be why the water is not flowing. On top of the fence a grey cat is creeping along toward a small blue bird perched further along the fence; the cat looks like it is about to pounce. There is also a small puddle or drip under the hose reel. There is a flower bed in front of the house and two potted plants by the fence.
