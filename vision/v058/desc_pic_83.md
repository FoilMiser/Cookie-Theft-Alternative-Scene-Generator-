## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the right, a wooden fence and gate at the back and a paved path on the left. A man with a moustache in a red T-shirt and blue trousers sweeps a pile of leaves on the path with a broom. In the middle, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds the end of a green garden hose up to her face and peers into the nozzle, as if wondering why no water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel next to the house, across the lawn, to her. A brown dog with a red collar is standing on the hose, which would squash it and stop the water; the dog has its eyes closed and seems to be sniffing the flowers. The hose reel is leaking a little, with drips and a small puddle beneath it. It looks like the woman is about to get sprayed in the face when the dog steps off the hose.
