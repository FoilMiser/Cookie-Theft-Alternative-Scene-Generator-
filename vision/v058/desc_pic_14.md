## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house (blue door) on the right, a wooden fence along the back with a gate on the left, and a path at the bottom left. On the left, by the path, a small girl in a pink dress holds a blue and a purple balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other toward a red balloon that has floated away above the fence; she looks upset. In the back, a man with a beard in a green shirt and beige trousers is painting the fence pink with a roller on a long pole; a section is already pink and a pink paint tin is at his feet. In the front centre, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie waves one hand in the air (small motion lines show he is waving). On the right, a woman in a yellow dress waters a flower bed beside the house with a teal watering can. The red balloon is drifting away and will probably be lost.
