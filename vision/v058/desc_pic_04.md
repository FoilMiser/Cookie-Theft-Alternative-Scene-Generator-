An older man in a grey flat cap and brown waistcoat stands on the grass holding a long fishing rod, with the line going into the pond and a red-and-white float on the water with ripples around it. However, he is standing well back from the pond, and the rod and line look stretched over a long distance. A woman in a yellow dress sits on a wooden park bench reading a red book. A small boy in a yellow top and orange shorts stands near the path on the right, holding a red balloon on a string. There is a grey rock in the foreground and a green bin in the bottom-left corner. Everything seems calm; nothing seems about to go wrong.
