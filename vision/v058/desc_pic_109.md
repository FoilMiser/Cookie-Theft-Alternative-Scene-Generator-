## Free description
A sunny park with a pond on the right, a winding path on the left, a lamp post, trees and bushes. In the front a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks to the left pushing an empty red wheelbarrow. Just to her right a small girl in a pink dress holds a kite spool up with both hands, flying a yellow diamond-shaped kite high in the sky. The kite string runs from her hands in a long line up to the kite, passing close behind the woman's head. The kite has a short tail with two small bows. There is a green rubbish bin in the bottom right corner. The scene is peaceful.
