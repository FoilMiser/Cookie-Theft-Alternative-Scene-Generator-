# pic_80

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left, a wooden fence across the back and a path on the right. An old woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses sits on a wooden bench at the front left, reading a red book. Beside her on the bench is a sandwich, and a blue bird standing at the end of the bench has its beak at the sandwich, apparently eating or stealing it while she is not looking. In the middle at the back, a woman in a yellow dress is painting the fence bright pink with a long-handled roller; a few boards are already pink. A paint tray and a pink paint tin sit on the grass near her. A white dog with brown spots is walking away from the paint tray towards the left, leaving a trail of pink paw prints, so it has stepped in the paint and is tracking it across the lawn. On the right a brown dog sits on its haunches wearing glasses and holding a newspaper, as if reading it, which is impossible for a real dog. A red wheelbarrow stands at the front centre, and there are two potted plants by the fence.
