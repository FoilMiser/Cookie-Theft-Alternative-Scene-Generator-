## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence along the back, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a paved path running from a gate at the back left down to the front left. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands on the path near the gate, sweeping a small pile of brown leaves with a broom. In the middle, an older man in a grey flat cap and brown waistcoat is painting the fence with a pink paint roller; a few fence boards behind and to the right of him are already bright pink. A paint tray and an open tin of pink paint sit on the grass at his feet. A small white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is walking away from the paint tray toward the left, and it has pink feet and is leaving a trail of pink paw prints across the grass, so it has clearly stepped in the paint. The man seems not to have noticed the dog. A watering can and a red wheelbarrow sit unused in the foreground, and there is a flower bed by the house.
