## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a path, and a cream house with a blue door on the right. On the left, an older man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat is sweeping dirt on the path toward a red dustpan. He is not using a broom: he is pushing the dirt with a huge silver dinner fork, which looks wrong. In the middle right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie kneels at a flower bed and plants a small seedling, with a tray of more seedlings beside him. On the far right, a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench reading a red book. Next to her on the bench is a sandwich, and a blue bird stands right beside it, leaning toward it, so it looks about to steal or peck the sandwich while she isn't looking. A teal watering can, a red wheelbarrow and two potted plants sit on the lawn, unused.
