## Free description
A busy, sunny town street with a row of colourful shops (pink, green, peach, cream) and a wide paved pavement in front. On the left, a man in a red shirt and blue trousers walks along carrying a yellow shopping bag past a fruit-and-vegetable stall; behind the stall, a man in a green shirt is scattering seed from a bag for a red bird pecking on the ground. In the centre, an old woman with glasses sits on a bench reading a red book, while a blue bird on the other end of the bench is grabbing at a sandwich left there, apparently stealing it. Next to her, a woman in a purple top sweeps a pile of leaves with a broom. In front, a small girl in a pink dress holds an empty ice-cream cone up to her face looking upset, while a spotted dog licks the pink ice cream that has fallen in a splat on the ground. A brown dog wearing glasses sits upright in the front centre holding and "reading" a newspaper, which is impossible and comic. On the right, a boy in a blue hoodie and red cap points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in a tree, and further right an older man in a cap and brown waistcoat stands beside a red bicycle.
