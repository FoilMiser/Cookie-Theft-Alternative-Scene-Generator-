# pic_27

## Free description
A sunny park with rolling hills, a winding sand path on the left, trees and a round blue pond on the right. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands on the grass holding a fishing rod out over the pond; his line goes down to a red-and-white float in the water, with a little ripple ring around it, as if something might be biting. A white duck is swimming on the right side of the pond. In the middle of the grass stands a tall wooden ladder, upright and leaning on nothing at all, which is impossible: it should fall over. There is a grey rock at the front and a green rubbish bin in the bottom right corner. Nobody else is in the scene.
