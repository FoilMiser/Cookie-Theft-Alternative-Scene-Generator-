## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a gate on the right reached by a paving path. A woman in a yellow dress stands in the middle of the lawn holding the nozzle of a long blue-green hose up near her face, looking at it, as if checking why no water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel by the house, across the lawn, and a white spotted dog is standing right on the hose (its front paw on it), which seems to be blocking the water. At the reel end there is a small drip/puddle of water. Behind her a washing line holds blue jeans and a yellow striped towel. There is a flower bed with colourful flowers, two potted plants by the fence, and a red wheelbarrow in the front. The likely joke is that she may get sprayed in the face when the dog steps off the hose.
