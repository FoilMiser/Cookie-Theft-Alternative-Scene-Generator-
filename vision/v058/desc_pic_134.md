## Free description
A sunny playground with a fence, a red slide, a swing set, a sandbox, a bin, a rock and a tree. On the right, an older man with grey hair and a cap sits on the lowered end of a red seesaw, and a small girl in pink is on the raised end holding the handle, mouth open as if surprised or calling out. The swing is empty and the slide unused; the sandbox has a red bucket and blue spade. Nothing looks impossible.
