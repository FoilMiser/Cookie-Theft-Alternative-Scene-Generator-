# pic_72

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a gate and paved path on the left. A girl in a pink dress stands in the middle of the lawn holding the strings of two balloons, a yellow one and a blue one, in one hand. A third balloon, a red one, is floating up above her, higher than the fence, with its string hanging loose below it and not attached to anything. The girl reaches her other arm up toward it, so it looks as if she has just let go of the red balloon and it is drifting away. Her face looks worried or upset. An empty swing set stands to the left, and a watering can and a red wheelbarrow are on the grass in the front. There is a round flower bed near the house.
