## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house (red roof, blue door) on the left, a wooden fence with a gate on the right, a paved path down the right side, a birdhouse on a tall pole and an empty swing set. A man with brown hair in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands by the flower bed pouring water onto the flowers from a dark frying pan. A teal watering can stands on the grass right next to him, unused, which makes the frying pan look odd. On the right, on the path, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping a small pile of brown leaves with a broom. The swing hangs still with nobody on it. An empty red wheelbarrow is in the foreground.
