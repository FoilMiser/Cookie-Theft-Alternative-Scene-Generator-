A sunny back garden with a house and blue door on the left, a flower bed, a wooden fence, a gate on the right and a path leading to it. A metal swing set with an empty red swing stands on the right side of the lawn. A woman in a yellow dress stands on the path near the gate, sweeping a small pile of leaves with a yellow broom.
