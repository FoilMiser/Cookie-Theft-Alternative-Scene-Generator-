## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a wooden fence and a gate at the back right, and a paved path running down the right side. A man with a beard, green shirt and beige trousers stands on the path at the right. He is sweeping a little pile of dust or dirt towards a red dustpan, but instead of a broom he is using a huge silver fork, as big as a broom. Dust is puffing up from the pile. On the lawn there is a red wheelbarrow and a teal watering can, nobody using them. A flower bed of colourful flowers is in front of the house, and two potted plants stand near the fence. The odd thing is the giant fork being used as a broom.
