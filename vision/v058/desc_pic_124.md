To his right, a woman in a yellow dress is painting the fence pink with a long-handled roller; a few boards are already pink. A pink paint tray and an open paint can sit on the grass near her. A white dog with brown spots is walking away from the paint tray, leaving a trail of pink paw prints: it has stepped in the paint.
