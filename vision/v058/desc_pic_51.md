# pic_51

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a path, a flower bed and a house with a blue door on the right. On the left front, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks quickly along the path (speed lines behind her) while reading an open book. A cardboard box sits on the path right in front of her, so she looks about to trip over it. Behind her, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans rakes a small pile of leaves on the lawn. A clothesline in the middle holds a yellow striped towel and a pair of jeans. A small girl in a pink dress stands in the middle front holding a kite string reel and pointing up. Her kite string runs up to a yellow kite that is stuck in the branches of the tree near the house, so the kite seems caught. A birdhouse on a tall post stands near the fence.
