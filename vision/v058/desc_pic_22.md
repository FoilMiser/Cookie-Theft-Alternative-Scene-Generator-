# pic_22

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left (blue door, window, red roof), a wooden fence with a gate on the right, a paved path, a flower bed, a swing set, a hose reel and a watering can. On the right, an older man in a flat cap, brown vest and dark trousers is sweeping a small pile of leaves on the path with a yellow broom, looking down at his work. The swing set in the middle has a single empty red seat hanging still. Strangely, a closed cardboard box is up in the sky above the fence, tilted at an angle, with nothing holding it up and nobody nearby who could have thrown it. It may be read as falling, but it simply looks like it is floating. The man does not seem to notice it. The rest of the garden is calm and ordinary.
