## Free description
A sunny back garden with a gate and paving path on the left, a wooden fence at the back, and a cream house with a blue door on the right. A child with curly dark hair, a yellow top and orange shorts stands in the middle of the lawn holding a string reel up high, flying a yellow diamond-shaped kite with a bow-tied tail high in the sky. A lawn sprinkler at the front left, fed by a hose running across the lawn from the right, is spraying arcs of water toward the path, where they land on a cardboard parcel sitting on the path, which is getting wet (a small puddle under it). In the tree by the house there is an orange goldfish sitting in the branches, which is impossible: a fish does not belong in a tree. There is also a flower bed, a couple of bushes, and a wooden bench on the right.
