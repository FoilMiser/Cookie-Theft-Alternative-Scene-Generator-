# pic_99

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a tree behind it, a wooden fence, a gate and a path on the right. A yellow kite is stuck in the branches of the tree. A child in a yellow top and orange shorts stands in the middle of the lawn holding the kite's string handle and pointing up at the kite; the string runs from the child's hand, loops on the grass, and goes up to the kite in the tree. To the right, an older man in a grey cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is painting the fence pink with a paint roller; a few boards are already pink and an open pink paint tin sits on the grass by his feet. A brown dog lies asleep on the grass at the front. A wooden bench, a flower bed and a teal watering can are around the garden, unused.
