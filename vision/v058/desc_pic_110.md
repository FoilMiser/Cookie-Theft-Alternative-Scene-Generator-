# pic_110

## Free description
A sunny park with a pond on the left, a winding path on the right, a lamp post with a rubbish bin beside it, and trees. In the front a young girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes walks or skips to the right, holding a pink ice cream cone near her mouth. In the middle a white goat with a red collar grazes on a small tuft of grass; it is tied by a rope to a short wooden stake in the ground. A grey rock sits in the front centre. The girl is heading in the goat's general direction but is not interacting with it. Nothing looks impossible, though a goat tethered in a park is a little unusual.
