A man with brown hair and a moustache, in a red T-shirt and blue jeans, kneels on the lawn planting flowers into a round flower bed; a tray of small seedlings sits in front of the bed. Strangely, an orange fish is in the tree behind the fence, among the branches, which is impossible. A watering can, a red wheelbarrow and two potted plants are on the lawn.
