## Free description
A sunny but windy back garden: white wind lines streak across the sky and over the fence. A woman in a yellow dress stands on the lawn near the fence reaching up with one hand as her red cap blows off her head into the air, with a few leaves blowing along with it; she looks surprised. Next to her is a washing line between two wooden posts with a red-and-yellow striped towel and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it, flapping in the wind. On the right is a cream house with a red roof and blue door, a flower bed and a wooden bench. On the left a paved path leads to a wooden gate in the fence. In the foreground are a teal watering can and a red wheelbarrow. The hat looks about to fly away beyond her reach.
