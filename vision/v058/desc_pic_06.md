## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house (red roof, blue door) on the left, a wooden fence with a gate on the right, a paved path down the right and a washing line holding blue jeans and a yellow striped garment. A woman with a dark ponytail in a yellow dress stands by the flower bed holding a small teal watering can. Instead of water pouring down out of the spout, a stream of water curves upward from the spout and arcs up over the flowers, with drops flying at the top, like a fountain. That looks physically impossible for a watering can. There are two potted plants by the fence and an empty red wheelbarrow in the foreground. Nobody else is in the garden.
