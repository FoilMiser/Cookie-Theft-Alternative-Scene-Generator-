## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path running down the right side. A man with a beard, in a green T-shirt and beige trousers, stands by a flower bed near the house, bending over it. He is pouring water onto the flowers from a dark frying pan, not from a watering can. A teal watering can is standing on the grass right next to his feet, unused, which makes the frying pan choice look odd or silly. Water streams out of the pan onto the flowers. There are two potted plants and a bush by the fence, and an empty red wheelbarrow in the foreground. Nobody else is in the garden.
