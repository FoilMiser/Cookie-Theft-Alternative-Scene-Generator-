# pic_05

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house (red roof, blue door) on the left, a wooden fence with a gate on the right and a paved path down the right side. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands by the flower bed near the house pouring water onto the flowers from a dark frying pan. A teal watering can is standing on the grass right beside him, unused, which makes using a frying pan look odd. On the right, a woman with a dark ponytail in a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping a small pile of brown leaves at the edge of the path with a broom. There are two potted plants and bushes by the fence and an empty red wheelbarrow in the front.
