# pic_102

## Free description
A busy sunny park with a pond on the left, a lamp post and bin in the middle, a bench and a sandy path on the right. A woman in a yellow dress stands next to the lamp post with her hands on her hips, wearing a grey metal bucket upside-down on her head like a hat — an odd, silly thing to do. To the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is bending slightly and scattering seeds from a paper bag for two small birds, one red and one blue, which are pecking at the seeds on the grass. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie walks in the front left carrying a yellow bag. On the far right, an older man with a flat cap, glasses, and a brown waistcoat strides along eating a pink ice-cream cone. At the bottom, a garden hose leads to a lawn sprinkler that is spraying arcs of water right onto a cardboard box sitting on the edge of the path, so the box is getting wet and there is a puddle under it.
