# pic_108

## Free description
A sunny town street with a row of colourful shops with striped awnings, and a paved square in front. On the far left a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks toward the left edge carrying a cardboard box, passing a fruit stall. In the front centre an orange cat crouches low and creeps toward a small blue bird that is pecking at seeds on the ground; the cat looks about to pounce. A red bird sits on a branch of a tree on the right. A bicycle is parked in a bike rack, and there is an empty bench, a lamp post, a flower planter, a rubbish bin and a potted plant. No one seems to notice the cat.
