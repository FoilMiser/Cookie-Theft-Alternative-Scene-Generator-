## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, trees, a round pond on the left, a tall lamp post in the middle, and a winding path on the right. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands by the pond holding a brown paper bag and throwing small pieces of bread towards the water. A white duck in the pond is eating pieces of bread floating near it. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. A flower bed is at the front left, with a green rubbish bin in the corner and a grey rock at the front centre. Nothing looks clearly wrong; it is a calm, ordinary scene.
