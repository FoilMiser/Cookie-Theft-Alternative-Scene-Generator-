# pic_93

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate at the back left, a tree and a cream house with a blue door on the right. A small girl in a pink dress is on the paved path at the left. Her arms are up and she seems to be jumping for joy: her feet are off the ground and her shadow is below her. In the middle, a man with a beard and a green shirt is drinking from a red cup with a straw. He is in a sitting position, but there is no chair, bench or anything else under him, so he seems to be sitting on thin air. On the right, an older man in a grey cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is watering a round bed of colourful flowers with a teal watering can, and water is pouring onto the flowers. Two potted plants stand on the grass near the fence. The impossible part is the man sitting in mid-air.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The seated man has no seat. Either something is missing, or it is meant to look impossible (sitting on air).
- The girl could be jumping (shadow is under her, feet in the air) or just cheering. I read it as a jump.
