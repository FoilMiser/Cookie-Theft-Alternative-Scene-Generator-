## 1. Free description

It is a sunny day in a back garden. A tall wooden fence runs across the back, with a wooden gate on the far left, and a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue front door stands on the right. A pale paved path runs from the gate down to the bottom-left corner. In the middle of the lawn a dark-skinned man in a green shirt and khaki trousers holds a garden hose nozzle up near his face. Only a single drop of water is coming out of it, and he is looking at it. The hose runs down from his hands, loops across the lawn and goes back to a hose reel beside the flowerbed near the house. A brown-and-white dog with a red collar is standing right on the hose. Just behind the dog, on the reel side, the hose looks swollen, as if water is backed up behind the dog's feet. It looks like the dog is blocking the water. When the dog steps off, the water will probably burst out of the nozzle, maybe into the man's face. In the background an elderly grey-haired woman with glasses is raking a small pile of leaves by the fence. On the left a white goat with a red collar is grazing on a tuft of grass, apparently tied by a thin cord to a small stake in the ground.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous

- The small thing in front of the goat could be a tether stake, with the cord tying the goat in place, or it could be a small sprinkler or garden fitting with a thin hose or wire. Because the line seems to go to the goat's collar, I read it as a tether, but it is small and hard to be sure of.
- Swelling in the hose: I read the swollen section next to the dog as water trapped behind the dog's feet. It could also just be a drawing quirk, or a kink or knot in the hose.
- The nozzle seems to point roughly up and back towards the man's own face rather than away from him. That fits the "water is about to squirt him" reading, but the angle is not completely clear.
- A goat in a small suburban back garden is a little unusual, but not impossible.
- The woman is drawn quite small and right up against the fence. That fits her being far back, but she looks almost flattened against the fence boards.
- Nothing seems to float or pass through anything else. The dog's feet sit on the hose rather than through it, and the hose goes behind the man's legs and back out properly.
