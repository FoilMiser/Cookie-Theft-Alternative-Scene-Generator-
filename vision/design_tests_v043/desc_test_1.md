## Unsure or ambiguous

- The dog may really be "reading" the newspaper, which is impossible and maybe the joke. Or it may just be lying on or pawing at a newspaper someone dropped. Its head angle and paw position make it look like it is reading.
- The old woman's glasses have dark lenses. They could be sunglasses or ordinary glasses drawn dark.
- The woman's head and shoulders overlap the pond in the drawing. Her feet and shadow are on the grass in front of the pond, so I read her as standing in front of it, not in the water. At a glance she could look like she is standing at the pond's edge or partly in it.
- The woman and the box: she seems about to trip over the box because she's reading. Or the box may just be lying nearby and she'll walk past it. It is very close to her leading foot and directly in her direction of travel.
- There is no clear reason for a cardboard box to be sitting in the middle of the grass. It seems out of place.
- The boy's direction: his cap brim and face point right and the ball is to his right, so he is kicking toward the right edge (away from everyone). His upper body and outstretched left arm make his pose look a little twisted, as if his torso faces the viewer while his head faces right.
- The ball's orange-with-white-stripe pattern could be a football or a small play/beach ball.
- The lamp post is very tall and thin compared with the people and trees. That is slightly out of scale, but probably just a style choice.
- Nothing seems to float in the air or pass through anyone. Each figure, the box and the ball has a ground shadow.
