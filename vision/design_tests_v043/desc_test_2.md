# Description of test_2.png

## 1. Free description

This is a sunny day on a shopping street. A row of four colourful two-storey shops (pink, pale green, peach and cream) runs along the back, with striped awnings over the shop windows, a bright sun in the top left and two small white clouds. In the front centre a young woman with dark hair in a ponytail, in a purple top and teal trousers, is walking with her head down, reading an open book she holds out in front of her. She seems to be walking towards the left, and a cardboard box sits on the pavement just ahead of her feet, so it looks like she is about to trip over it because she isn't looking where she's going. Further back, in front of the peach shop, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, is standing and licking a pink ice-cream cone. On the left there is a fruit stall with a teal-and-white striped canopy and four crates of green, yellow, orange and red fruit, with another cardboard box on the ground in front of it. In the bottom right a brown-and-tan dog with a red collar is lying on the pavement with its front paws on an open newspaper, as if it were reading it, which is the oddest thing in the scene. There is also a street lamp, a tree in a round planter, a green bin and two potted plants in the front corners.
