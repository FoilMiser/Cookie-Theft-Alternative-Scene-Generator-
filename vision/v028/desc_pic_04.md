# Picture 4

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with the house on the left, a wooden fence at the back and a gate and path on the right, and there are no people in the picture. A tan-and-white dog with a red collar sits in front of the blue door facing us, holding a red lead in its mouth, with the rest of the lead trailing down and looped on the ground; it looks as if it is asking to be taken for a walk. In the middle of the lawn a small white animal is curled up with its head down, apparently asleep. An orange goldfish appears in the leaves of the tree behind the fence, which is impossible. A teal watering can is on the grass by the path, and two potted plants, two bushes and a flower bed complete the garden. Otherwise the scene is very calm, and nothing seems about to happen.
