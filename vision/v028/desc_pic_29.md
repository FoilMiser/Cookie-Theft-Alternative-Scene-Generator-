# Picture 29

## Free description

This is a paved town square on a sunny day, with a row of colourful shops with striped awnings behind. On the far left, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes walks to the left carrying a yellow shopping bag. In the centre a small girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench, drinking from a red cup through a straw. In front of her, a boy in a red baseball cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is walking to the left while reading an open book, not looking where he is going. A blue bucket stands on the ground just ahead of him, so he looks likely to walk into it or step in it. On the right, an older man in a grey flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat stands in front of a fruit stall holding a camera up to his eye and pointing it to the left, taking a photograph. A teal bicycle is parked in a grey metal rack near the shops, and there is a planter of flowers behind the bench. A closed cardboard box sits on the ground beside the stall, and there is a dark grey bin and a plant pot at the front right. Nothing looks impossible.
