# Picture 20

## Free description

This is the back garden of a cream house with a red roof, but the sky is grey with three big dark storm clouds and no sun, so it looks as if it is about to rain. A woman or teenage girl with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers stands in the middle of the lawn pointing up at the sky, looking surprised or worried, with wide eyes and an open mouth. Behind her a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is kneeling at the flower bed working on the flowers, with a small pot of pink plant at the edge of the bed, and he does not seem to have noticed the clouds. To the right an old woman with grey hair and glasses stands with her hands on her hips wearing a metal bucket upside down on her head, which looks silly and has no obvious reason. She could possibly be wearing it as a rain hat, but nobody reacts to it. A watering can sits on the grass at the lower right and there are two pots of green plants by the fence. No rain is falling yet, but it seems likely that it will soon.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The bucket: Reading 1, it is simply a silly or impossible thing to do. Reading 2, she is wearing it as a makeshift rain hat because of the clouds. The bucket sits low on her head, down to her glasses, and looks too big.
- The woman pointing: she could be pointing at the clouds (the most likely) or at something else up in the sky to the right; nothing else is visible there.
- The man's hands are mostly hidden behind the flowers, so I cannot see if he is picking flowers, weeding, or planting the pot. His face is angled down and is hard to read. He seems unaware of the sky.
- The small pot's position on the rim of the bed could mean it is about to be planted or has just been dug up.
- The house window still shows a bright pale blue even though the sky is grey, which looks slightly inconsistent.
