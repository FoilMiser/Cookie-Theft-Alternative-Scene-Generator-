# Picture 16

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny town street or pedestrian square with shops along the back, a tree in a planter, a tall street lamp, a fruit stall and paving stones in front. At the far left, a street sweeper in an orange cap and hi-vis vest is sweeping a pile of autumn leaves with a broom. A small girl in a pink dress stands by the tree pointing up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck high among the branches. In the centre, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks along reading an open book, not looking where she is going, and a cardboard box sits on the ground near her. A man in a green shirt sits on a bench reading a red book, while a blue bird beside him on the bench is pecking at his sandwich, which he has not noticed. An older man in a cap and waistcoat walks along in the background carrying a yellow shopping bag. To the right, a child in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice cream cone and rubs his eye as if crying, because his pink ice cream is splattered on the ground. A brown dog with a red collar is licking up the spilled ice cream. At the bottom right, a small white dog with a blue neckerchief sits up on its haunches holding and apparently reading an open newspaper, which is impossible and funny. A red bird flies in the sky at top left.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The child could be crying or just rubbing an eye or scratching a head; the empty cone and the splat suggest crying over the dropped ice cream.
- The dog could be seen as licking the ice cream or just sniffing it.
- The white dog could be read as (a) truly reading the paper, which is impossible, or (b) merely sitting beside a newspaper standing on the ground. It looks to me as if its paws are holding it; the paper stands up by itself and is not shown tilting toward its eyes, and the dog is actually looking away.
- The box in front of the walking woman could be an obstacle she is about to trip on, but it sits to the side of her path rather than directly under her feet, so I could read it either way.
- The man on the bench might also be holding the sandwich or have put it down; it appears to be sitting on the bench.
- The sun is partly cut off at the top of the picture, and the street lamp overlaps the green shop's window and awning.
- The older man's bag and legs overlap in a way that makes his lower half a little hard to read; he also seems to be on the raised kerb line near the shop.
