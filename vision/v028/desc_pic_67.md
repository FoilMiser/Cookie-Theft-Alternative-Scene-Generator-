# Picture 67

## Free description

This is a sunny paved town street or square with a row of colourful shops behind, each with a striped awning. In the middle, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is jumping in the air with both arms raised and a big happy expression; his feet are clearly off the ground because his shadow is below and separate from him. To his right, a man with brown hair in a red shirt and blue trousers is running to the right, with short speed lines behind him showing how quickly he is going. A red bird with a large raised wing is flying across the sky at the top right. On the left, a fruit stall with a striped awning has crates of green, yellow, orange and red fruit, but no one is serving at it. There is also a lamp post, a bin, a potted plant at the front left and a tree in a round planter on the right. I cannot see what the boy is excited about or what the man is running to or from, so the two of them may or may not be connected. Nothing is obviously impossible, though the man is running in the direction of the tree's planter.
