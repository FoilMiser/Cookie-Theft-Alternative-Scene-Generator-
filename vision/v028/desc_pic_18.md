# Picture 18

## Free description

This is a sunny park with a blue pond on the left, a lamp post in the middle and a sandy path winding away on the right. A child with dark skin, wearing a yellow shirt and orange shorts, stands at the front edge of the pond flying an orange diamond kite; the long string runs over the pond up to the kite high in the sky. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands at the right-hand end of the pond holding a brown paper bag and throwing bits of bread or crumbs onto the water, and a white duck is swimming towards those crumbs. A small girl in a pink dress stands near the pond eating a pink ice cream. At the front centre a woman or teenage girl with a long dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is reading a red book in a sitting position, but there is nothing under her, so she appears to be sitting on thin air. An empty bench stands not far away to her right, which makes this look more wrong. On the right a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is walking along by the path carrying a yellow shopping bag. Nobody is interacting with anybody else, and nobody seems to notice the woman sitting on nothing.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The woman with the book: she has the pose of someone sitting on a chair or bench, but there is no seat at all. Reading 1: it is a deliberate oddity (sitting on air). Reading 2: the bench or chair has been left out by mistake. Either way it looks strange.
- The boy with the bag: he could be feeding the duck or just about to take something out of the bag; the crumbs in the water make feeding the likelier reading. One crumb lies at or just past the pond's edge, possibly in mid-air on the way to the water.
- The white lines behind the duck are detached from its tail and sit a little way behind it; they read as a wake or ripples but could be read as scratches on the water.
- The walking woman's feet are on the grass right next to the path rather than clearly on it; she is walking away from the path direction. Her body looks rather stiff and the bag hides her front leg.
- The child's lower garment could be long orange shorts or a short skirt; the kite string runs right across the pond, which is fine but long, and the child seems to be standing almost on the water's edge.
