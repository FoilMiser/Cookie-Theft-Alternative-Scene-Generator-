# Picture 1

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right and a tall wooden fence across the back. An older grey-haired woman with glasses is painting the fence blue with a long-handled brush or roller, and she has finished about three boards; a paint tin and tray are on the grass behind her. A white dog with brown spots has walked through the paint: its feet are blue and a trail of blue paw prints runs across the lawn from the paint tray to the dog, who is heading left towards the path. On top of the fence a black-and-white cat is creeping along towards a red bird that is perched just next to the painted section, and it looks like the cat is about to pounce. A small girl in a pink dress holds a red and a purple balloon, but a third, blue balloon has got away from her and is floating up just out of reach while she stretches for it. A man in a green shirt sits on a bench at the bottom right reading a red book. In the middle of the lawn a tan dog is sitting up on its bottom holding open a newspaper as though reading it, which is impossible, and an orange kitten is curled up asleep nearby. An orange goldfish is in the leaves of the tree behind the fence, which is also impossible. A blue bird flies in the sky at the upper left, and a watering can and a wheelbarrow are left on the grass in the foreground.
