# Picture 28

## Free description

This is a busy paved town square on a sunny day, with a row of colourful shops with striped awnings behind. On the left a little girl in a pink dress is pointing up at a tree, where a blue-and-white beach ball is stuck in the branches, so it seems her ball has got caught and she can't reach it. In the centre a woman with long dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a bench eating a pink ice cream cone, while a brown dog with a red neckerchief sits up on its hind legs right next to her, begging for the ice cream. In front of them an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and thick glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, is walking along with her nose in an open book. A red bucket with blue water in it is on the ground just to the left of her, in the direction she seems to be walking, so it looks as though she may walk into it because she isn't looking. At the back right, a woman in a yellow dress stands by the lamp post taking a photo with a camera, pointing towards the left. On the right, a man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers is walking a white spotted dog on a red lead, both heading left, in front of a fruit stall with crates of apples, oranges and lemons. Lots of separate small things are going on at once, and no one seems to be paying attention to anyone else.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- What the photographer is photographing is not clear: it could be the ball in the tree, the people on the bench, or the scene in general.
- It is not certain the elderly woman will reach the bucket; she might be heading left into it, or just pass close by. Her feet are a little hard to read for direction: the rear shoe looks turned up.
- The bucket could be read as a tripping hazard, or just something left lying around for cleaning or for the plants.
- The woman on the bench may or may not have noticed the dog; her eyes look down or closed towards her ice cream. It could be her own dog or a stranger's.
- Who owns the ball is a guess; the girl pointing makes it look like hers.
- The photographer's head overlaps the awning, and she stands very close to the lamp post.
- The dog's front paws are held up in the air, which could look like it is climbing towards the bench, but it reads mostly as begging.
