# Picture 19

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path running down the right side. In the middle one person, a man or older boy with brown hair, red T-shirt and blue jeans, is hanging out the washing on a line strung between two wooden posts. A pair of blue jeans already hangs from the line with pegs, and he has both arms raised, pegging up a white T-shirt. He is looking at the T-shirt and concentrating on the job. Around him are a round bed of colourful flowers, two pots of green plants, an empty wheelbarrow and a teal watering can, all just sitting there. The sky is clear apart from two small white clouds, so it seems a good drying day. Nothing looks wrong or dangerous and nothing seems about to happen besides him finishing the washing.
