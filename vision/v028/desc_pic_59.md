A cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, a wooden fence runs across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path runs down the right side. The only person in the picture is a girl in a pink dress standing on the path at the bottom right. She is looking up with her mouth slightly open and has one arm raised, pointing up and to the left with a finger. It looks as if it is about to rain, and she may be pointing at the dark clouds, although no rain is falling yet. A tall wooden post with a small red-roofed birdhouse on top stands by the fence, in roughly the direction she is pointing, but no bird can be seen. The rest of the garden is still and unattended, with a colourful flower bed, an empty bench, two potted plants, an empty wheelbarrow and a teal watering can left on the grass. Nothing looks wrong or impossible.
