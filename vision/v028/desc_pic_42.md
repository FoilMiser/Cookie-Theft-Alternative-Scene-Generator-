# Picture 42

## Free description
This is a sunny paved town square or street with a row of coloured shops with striped awnings along the back. On the left, an older woman with grey hair in a bun and round glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, has one arm raised high and is waving, with small motion marks around her hand. In the middle front, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands holding the string of a yellow balloon, which floats above him. Behind him, an older man in a blue-grey cap, white shirt, brown vest and dark trousers sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. At the bottom right, an orange cat is low to the ground and moving fast or creeping forward toward a red bird that is standing on the paving a short distance in front of its nose. It looks as if the cat is about to pounce on the bird, and the bird does not seem to have noticed, as it is bent down pecking. On the right at the back there is a market stall with crates of red, orange, yellow and green produce, and nobody is serving at it. Nothing looks impossible; it is an ordinary street scene with the one bit of tension between the cat and the bird.
