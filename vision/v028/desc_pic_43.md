# Picture 43

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, and a paved path running to a gate on the right. In the middle, a woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers stands with both arms stretched forward and up to catch a red ball. A dashed curved line shows the ball's path through the air from a small girl in a pink dress, who is running or stepping away to the right with one arm reaching back toward the woman, as if she has just thrown it. In the front centre, a white dog with brown patches is bowed down with its front low and its tail in the air and wagging, with a yellow-green tennis ball right in front of its nose, as if inviting play. By the blue door, a second dog, brown and white, sits with a red lead in its mouth, the loop trailing on the grass. On the right, on the path, an old woman with a grey bun and glasses, teal top and grey skirt is sweeping a pile of coloured leaves with a broom. A teal watering can stands unused on the grass. Nothing looks clearly impossible, though the ball has not quite reached the woman's hands yet.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl's movement could be read two ways: she has just thrown the ball to the woman and is running on, or she is running away after the throw. The dashed line ends near her hand, so I read her as the thrower.
- The woman's hands are slightly below and to the left of the ball, so she could be about to catch it, or the ball could sail over her hands.
- The white dog could be about to grab the tennis ball, could be nudging it, or could just be bowing to play. It is not clear who the tennis ball belongs to.
- The ball and the dashed line are drawn across the fence, so they are easy to confuse with the fence pattern, but they read clearly once seen.
- The white dog's back legs and body bend in an unusual way, but it still reads as a dog.
