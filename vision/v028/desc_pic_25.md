# Picture 25

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, and a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, stands in the middle of the lawn holding the end of a garden hose up in front of her face with both hands. She appears to be peering at the nozzle, and no water is coming out of it. The hose runs from a reel by the flower bed, along the grass, and up to her hands. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is standing on the hose in the front centre, with its tongue out, looking towards the woman. Just behind the dog's back foot the hose swells up into a big fat bulge, as if water is trapped there because the dog is standing on it. It looks as though if the dog steps off, the water will rush through and spray the woman in the face. Behind them a washing line between two posts holds blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. Nothing else is moving; the rest of the garden is calm.

- Tree and bush: tree behind the fence by the house, small bush beside the house.
- Sun and two clouds: top of picture.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The woman could be (a) puzzled about why no water is coming and looking down the nozzle, or (b) just adjusting or fixing the nozzle. Her face is turned to it, so I lean towards (a).
- The dog might be standing on the hose by accident; it does not look as if it has noticed. Its back foot is the clearest contact with the hose; the front feet are next to or just past it.
- The hose rises quite stiffly from the ground up to her hands, a bit like a rigid pipe rather than something floppy.
- The bulge is very large and looks a little like a separate slug-shaped object lying on the hose until you see it is part of the hose.
- The hose reel slightly overlaps the edge of the flower bed.
