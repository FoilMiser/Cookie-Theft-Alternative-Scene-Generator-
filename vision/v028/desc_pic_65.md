# Picture 65

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate on the left and the side of a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. There are no people or animals anywhere in the picture. The striking thing is a closed cardboard box hanging in the sky in the middle of the picture, tilted at an angle, well above the fence. Nothing is holding it up and no one is there who could have thrown it, so it looks either impossible or as if it is in the middle of falling or being tossed. Everything else is calm and ordinary: a paved path leading to the gate, a bird house on a tall pole, two potted plants, a watering can on the grass, a flower bed and a wooden bench near the house. The box looks as if it is about to drop onto the lawn if it is really falling.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The box could be read two ways: as an object frozen in the middle of a fall or throw (tilted, as if tumbling), or simply as a box floating in the air, which would be impossible. There is no thrower, no wind and no window or ledge it could have come from.
- The picture feels unfinished because no person is in it, and the box is the only thing going on. It could be intended as the oddity or it could be something left over from a scene with a person who has been removed.
- The bird house pole is very tall and very thin, with a small green tuft at its base, and it looks slightly stuck on rather than planted. It passes in front of the fence without touching anything.
