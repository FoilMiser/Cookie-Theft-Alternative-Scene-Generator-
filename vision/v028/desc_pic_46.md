# Picture 46

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence along the back, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a paved path leading to a gate on the left. Three people are all doing garden jobs. On the path at the left, an old woman with a grey bun and glasses, teal top and grey skirt is using a long rake on a small scattering of green and brown leaves at her feet. In the middle back, an old man in a blue-grey cap, white shirt, brown vest and dark trousers is digging with a spade, bending over a hole, with a heap of brown earth next to it and more earth on the spade. Right of centre, a person with short dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes is holding a hose and spraying an arc of water onto a round bed of colourful flowers. The hose runs from their hand down across the grass in a long curve around the front of the flower bed to a teal reel at the right. An empty red swing hangs still in a swing frame on the left and an empty wheelbarrow stands at the front. Everything seems calm and ordinary, with no one looking at anyone else.

## Inventory
- Left, front, on the path: old woman with grey bun, glasses, teal top, grey skirt, brown shoes, raking leaves with a long-handled rake.
- Centre, back: old man with blue-grey cap, white shirt, brown vest, dark trousers, brown shoes, digging with a spade, leaning over.
- Right of centre, middle: person with short dark hair, yellow dress, red shoes, standing, watering flowers with a hose.
- Rake and leaves (bottom left): rake head resting on the small pile of green, cream and brown leaves.
- Spade, hole and heap of earth (centre): spade blade in or at a small dark hole, with earth on it; a brown mound of soil just to the right.
- Hose and reel (right): dark teal hose running from a wheel-shaped reel on a dark stand, along the grass, up to a yellow nozzle in the person's hand.
- Water (right): pale blue arc from the nozzle falling on the flowers, with drops at the end.
- Flower bed (right): oval bed of brown soil with yellow, orange, purple, pink and red flowers.
- Swing frame (left of centre): dark blue-grey A-frame with one empty red seat, hanging still.
- Wheelbarrow (front centre): grey with wooden handles, empty, unused.
- Bush (right): near the house, partly behind the wall.
- House, path and gate: cream house with red roof and blue door at right; pale paved path running to a closed gate on the left.
- Tree behind the fence on the right; sun at top left; two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The person with the hose could be a woman or a girl; the size and short hair make the age hard to judge. The yellow dress is short and looks rather like a sack, and the figure could also be read as an older child.
- The old man's hole is very small and shallow; the dark patch under the spade could be a hole or the spade's shadow. The mound could be read as a molehill-like heap next to it.
- The old woman's tool could be read as a rake or a broom at first glance, but the head looks like the tines of a rake. The rake handle is thin and passes in front of her body and leg.
- The hose rises almost straight up from the grass to the hand, without any slack, which looks a little stiff. The hose also runs along right in front of the flower bed edge, close to the person's feet.
