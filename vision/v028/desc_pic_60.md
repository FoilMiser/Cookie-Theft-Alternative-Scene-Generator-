# Picture 60

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path running down the right side. The only person is an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and round glasses, wearing a teal jumper, grey-blue skirt and brown shoes. She stands at the right-hand edge of a round flower bed, holding a small teal watering can out in front of her at waist height. The odd thing is the water: instead of pouring down onto the flowers, the stream leaves the spout and curves upward and away, rising to about the height of her head, with a few drops at the very top. So the water appears to be flowing upward against gravity, and none of it is landing on the flowers just below the can. She does not seem to react to this and looks calm, facing the flowers. The rest of the garden is quiet, with two potted plants and a bush by the fence and an empty wheelbarrow on the lawn at the front. Nothing else looks wrong.

## Inventory
- Elderly woman, centre-left, mid-ground: grey hair in a bun, round glasses, teal jumper, grey-blue skirt, bare legs, brown shoes; standing by the flower bed, facing left, holding a watering can.
- Watering can: in the woman's hands, small and teal, about the same colour as her jumper; tilted forward with the spout pointing at the flowers.
- Water: pale blue stream running from the spout up and to the left in a curve, ending high above the flowers with a pointed tip and two small separate drops beside it; seems to be travelling upward.
- Flower bed: left of centre, oval of brown soil with red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers; directly below the can but not getting any visible water.
- House: left side, cream walls, red roof, one window, blue door with a grey step.
- Potted plants: right of centre, back, two terracotta pots with green leafy plants.
- Bush: right of centre, back, round green bush near the pots.
- Wheelbarrow: front, right of centre, grey tray, wooden handles, one black wheel; empty and not in use.
- Fence and gate: wooden fence with a brown gate with a round black knob at the far right.
- Path: pale paving slabs from the gate to the bottom right.
- Tree and hedge: large tree behind the fence next to the house, and a line of green hedge or trees behind the fence.
- Sky: blue with a bright yellow sun top right and two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water could be read two ways. Reading 1: it shoots up out of the spout against gravity, which is impossible and is the strange thing in the picture. Reading 2: the stream is coming down from above into the can, as if the can were being filled from nowhere. The pointed drop tip at the top and the drops around it suggest upward travel, and I lean to reading 1.
- The can is small and nearly the same teal as her jumper, so it is partly lost against her and took a moment to recognise. Its shape is less clear than in other gardens.
- Her expression is hard to read at this size because the glasses cover much of the small face; I would call it neutral.
