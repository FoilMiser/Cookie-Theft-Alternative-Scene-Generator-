# Picture 8

## Free description
It is a sunny day at a playground with a dark metal railing fence and hedges at the back. On the right a red seesaw is tilted right over: an older man in a cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers sits at the low end with his feet on the ground, reading an open book and paying no attention, while a small girl in a pink dress is stuck up at the high end. She holds onto the handle in front of her with her feet dangling in the air, small curved lines under them, and her mouth is open as if she is calling out or saying "oh". He is holding her up there just by sitting still, and it looks as if she can't get down until he stops reading and lets the seesaw move. On the left a small boy in a yellow top and orange shorts is sliding down a slide with his arms raised. A sandpit at the lower left holds a red bucket and a blue spade, with no one playing in it. A grey rock, a green bin, a bush and a large tree round out the scene.
