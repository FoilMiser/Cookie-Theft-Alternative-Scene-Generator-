# Picture 68

## Free description

This is a busy sunny park with a pond on the right, a winding sandy path on the left, a tall lamp post in the middle and hills in the distance. On the far left a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands under a tree eating a pink ice cream. Next to him, a person in a yellow dress and red shoes holds a paper bag and scatters seed in a long trail down to a little red bird that is pecking at it. Further back an orange cat is creeping or running along low to the ground toward a blue bird standing on the grass, so it looks as if the cat is about to pounce. A child with braided hair sits on a bench reading a red book, and beside the bench a white goat with dark horns has its head down eating a tuft of grass. The oddest thing is at the front, where a tan dog with floppy brown ears and a red collar is sitting up and holding open a newspaper in its front paws as if reading it, which is impossible. Another strange thing is the wheelbarrow at the front, which has a square black wheel that could not roll. At the pond, a man in a red shirt and blue trousers holds a paper bag and throws bread to a white duck swimming toward him, and some of the bread has landed on the grass. At the far right an older woman with grey hair, glasses and a teal cardigan walks out of the scene carrying a yellow shopping bag, past a flower bed.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The newspaper-reading dog is clearly impossible in real life and looks like a deliberate oddity. It could also be read as a dog that has simply grabbed a newspaper, but its upright sitting pose and the paws holding it open look more like reading.
- The square wheelbarrow wheel could be read as a mistake in the drawing or as a deliberate impossibility. It looks like a square black frame with a criss-cross pattern inside, rather than a round wheel.
- The goat is a slightly unusual animal to find in a public park, and its body is thin with long, stiff legs. I read it as a goat because of the horns, though someone could call it a sheep or kid.
- The orange cat could be running or stalking. The blue bird seems unconcerned, facing toward it.
- A few bread pieces lie on the grass past the water's edge. Either the man missed the pond or they are meant to look like they are floating and the pond outline is slightly misleading.
- The person in yellow could be a woman or an older child. The seed trail is a neat dotted line rather than a natural scatter and it seems to hang in the air close to the hand.
- Nothing is passing through anything else, but the man in red overlaps the edge of the pond in a way that makes the bag look as if it is sitting on the water.
