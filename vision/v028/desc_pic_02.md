# Picture 2

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a house on the right and a wooden fence across the back. On the path at the far left, a boy in a red shirt and blue trousers is sweeping a pile of dirt towards a red dustpan, but he is using a huge silver dinner fork instead of a broom, which is the wrong tool and strangely large; puffs of dust rise from the pile. A woman in a yellow dress walks to the right carrying a cardboard box. A woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is walking to the left with her eyes on an open book, and a blue bucket sits on the grass ahead of her, so she may be about to walk into it. A small boy in a yellow top and orange shorts stands by the fence pointing up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck on a branch high in the tree. On the bench at the right an older man in a cap and waistcoat reads a red book, while a blue bird next to him on the bench goes for the sandwich beside him, and he has not noticed. The wheelbarrow in the foreground has a square wheel, which could not roll. A puppy and an orange kitten are both curled up asleep on the lawn at the bottom, and a red bird sits on the fence.
