## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a gate at the back right. A man in a red shirt and blue trousers stands in the middle of the lawn holding the end of a green-blue garden hose up close to his face and peering into the nozzle. Only a single drop of water is coming out of it. A brown dog with a red collar is standing on the hose a little way along, and the hose is swollen into a fat bulge on the side between the dog's back foot and the hose reel, while it stays thin on the man's side. So the dog seems to be blocking the water, and the man does not seem to have noticed; it looks as if the water could burst out at him if the dog steps off. On the right, on the paved path, a girl in a pink dress is jumping in the air with both arms up and a big smile. There is an empty swing set, a flower bed, and an empty bench. Something is plainly wrong in the tree behind the house: an orange goldfish is sitting in the leaves, which is impossible.
