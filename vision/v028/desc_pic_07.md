# Picture 7

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a park with hills and hedges behind, a winding sandy path on the left and a blue pond on the right. At the lower left an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt is walking to the left, pushing a blue-and-teal pram in which a baby lies asleep under a pink spotted blanket. An older man in a cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands at the near edge of the pond holding a long fishing rod, and his line runs down to a red-and-white float sitting in a small ripple on the water; no fish is visible yet. As in a park with something out of place, a wooden ladder stands on the grass between the path and the lamp post, leaning at a slant with nothing supporting it and no one on it. A tall black lamp post stands in the middle. A grey rock lies at the front and a green bin stands at the bottom right. Everything else looks calm; the man might get a bite, and the woman seems to be just passing through.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The ladder could be read as impossibly free-standing, or as something just abandoned that should fall over. It has a small shadow at its feet, and its top touches nothing.
- The fisherman's small float ring could mean a fish has just nibbled, or could simply be where the float sits. I can't tell.
- There is a small white shape hanging at the woman's chest; it could be a collar, a scarf end or a necklace.
- The woman walks on the grass beside the path rather than on it, with her back foot close to the path edge. That might be intentional or just how she's placed.
- The pram looks quite low and small next to her, and its flat box shape with only two visible wheels looks a little toy-like. The baby's head is small within it, close to the hood.
- The man's body overlaps the edge of the pond; he reads as standing on the grass in front of it, but for a moment he could seem to be standing in the water.
