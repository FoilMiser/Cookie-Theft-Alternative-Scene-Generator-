# Picture 64

## Free description

This is a sunny park with a pond, green hills in the distance, a winding sandy path and a tall lamp post in the middle. In the front, an older man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown vest is walking to the left, pushing a teal pram with a sleeping baby under a pink spotted blanket. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands on the path with one arm raised, holding a string. The string runs up to a purple kite that is caught among the leaves of a big tree, so he seems to be trying to tug it free. Behind him, further back, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is walking to the left, carrying a cardboard box in both arms. A small white dog with brown patches is near the middle of the grass with its head down, and no one appears to be with it. There is also an empty bench, a flower bed at the front left and a grey rock at the front. Nothing looks impossible, though the kite is stuck and the boy is not going to be able to fly it unless it comes loose. The pram is heading in the general direction of the flower bed, but it is not close enough to look like an accident is about to happen.
