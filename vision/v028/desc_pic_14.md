# Picture 14

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back and a gate and paved path at the right. A woman with a long dark ponytail, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, stands facing the fence holding a long-handled paint roller and painting the fence blue. Three fence panels are already blue; the rest are still plain wood. A small paint tin sits on the grass behind her. In the front of the picture a hose runs along the ground from the left to a sprinkler, which is throwing arcs of water to the right. The water is landing on and around a cardboard box on the grass, which is getting wet and sitting in a small puddle, with drips running down its sides. Nobody seems to have noticed. High up in the sky at the right, a second cardboard box is hanging in mid-air, tilted, with nothing holding it up. That box looks impossible, unless it is falling or has been thrown, and there is no one nearby who could have thrown it.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The box in the sky could be read as (a) falling down, about to land, (b) floating impossibly, or (c) a drawing mistake. There are no motion lines to tell which, and nothing around it explains how it got there.
- The wet box on the ground could be a parcel someone left out by accident or just an object in the way of the sprinkler; it does look like it is being ruined.
- The two boxes look similar, so I could not tell whether there is any connection between them, for example one being dropped next to the other.
- The roller's top reaches above the fence line, and its head looks flat against the fence, slightly offset from the blue area.
- The woman's hands and the roller handle overlap around her chest, so the grip is a little hard to make out.
- The paint tin sits on the grass with blue on the rim, and it may be a small bucket rather than a tin.
