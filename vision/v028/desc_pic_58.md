# Picture 58

## Free description
This is a sunny playground or park with a railing fence across the back, a tree on the left, an empty swing set in the middle and a red slide with a ladder on the right. On the left a small girl in a pink dress is stuck high up on the raised end of a red seesaw, with her feet dangling and little motion lines under them and her mouth open as if calling out. At the low end of the seesaw a man in a green shirt and beige trousers sits with his legs crossed reading an open book, apparently not noticing her, and his weight is holding her up in the air. In the centre a delivery man in an orange cap, orange high-visibility vest and blue trousers walks along carrying a cardboard box. To his right a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone in one hand and has the other hand up to the face, looking as though crying or upset. On the ground in front of the child is a pink splat of dropped ice cream with sprinkles, and a tan dog with a red collar has its head down at it and seems to be eating it, so the ice cream appears to have fallen off the cone. At the far right a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and black trousers stands looking up and flying a yellow kite on a long string. A red bird perches on top of the fence near the tree. Nothing looks physically impossible except perhaps a small grey round shape on the delivery man's chest that I could not explain.

- The child's raised hand could mean crying and rubbing the eyes, or just holding the head in dismay. The upset reading seems the more likely.
- The girl's open mouth and the marks under her feet could mean she is calling to the man to get her down, or just that she is bouncing about. Either way she is high up and he is not looking at her.
- The dog's face is a little hard to read because the head is turned down and foreshortened, so "eating the ice cream" is inferred from where the head is.
- The delivery man and the upset child are close together, but I saw nothing showing that he caused the spill; the two could be unrelated.
