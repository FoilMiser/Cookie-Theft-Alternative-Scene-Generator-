## Free description

This is a sunny, somewhat windy back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a wooden gate and paved path on the left. On the path at the front left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers throws both arms up and looks up in surprise, because an orange cap has been blown off and is flying through the air above her along with a few leaves; white wind lines stream across from the left. In the centre foreground a man in a green T-shirt and tan trousers is digging with a spade: he has a heap of soil beside him and a clod of earth on the blade. To the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hooded top stands at a flower bed holding out a frying pan and pouring water from it onto the flowers. Pouring water from a frying pan is the odd thing here; you would expect a watering can. Behind them a washing line between two posts holds a yellow and red striped cloth and a pair of blue jeans, and both are blown out sideways by the wind. A bird box stands on a tall post near the gate. It looks as if the cap will keep sailing away unless the woman can grab it.

- The woman's hands could be reading as trying to catch the cap or as a surprised gesture of throwing her arms up.
