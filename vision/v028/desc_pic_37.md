## Free description

This is a windy, sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a tall wooden fence across the back and a gate and paved path on the left. In the centre back, a woman in a yellow dress with red shoes throws both arms up and looks up in surprise as an orange cap flies off through the air above and left of her, with wind lines and leaves blowing past. On the left, by the path, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps a pile of autumn leaves with a yellow broom. In the front centre, an old man with a blue-grey cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat walks to the right carrying a cardboard box under his arm. On the right, an old woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and blue-grey skirt, stands by the flower bed holding a teal watering can, and the water arcs strangely upward in a blue stream with droplets flying above it, going up instead of falling on the flowers, which looks impossible. At the front left, a small girl in a pink dress with her hair in bunches is waving one hand in the air toward someone, with a little curved mark by her hand to show the waving. An empty bench is at the bottom right, and more leaves are blowing from the tree at the top right. It looks as though the cap will sail away, and the girl seems to be greeting the old man.
