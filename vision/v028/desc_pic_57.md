# Picture 57

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path running down the right side. A man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands by a round flower bed and waters the flowers with a green hose, and the water arcs down onto the blooms with a few drops splashing. The hose runs from a reel on a stand next to the flower bed, across the lawn and up to his hand. In the middle foreground a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is reading a red book. She is posed as if she were sitting on a chair, with her knees bent and her feet on the ground, but there is no chair, stool or anything else under her, so she appears to be sitting on thin air. On the right a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on a swing, smiling, with small motion lines showing the swing is moving. An empty wheelbarrow stands on the lawn just to the right of the woman, and an empty wooden bench stands by the house on the left. Nobody seems to be paying attention to anyone else, and nothing looks about to happen apart from the ordinary watering and swinging. The one thing that looks wrong or impossible is the woman sitting on nothing.
