# Picture 44

## Free description
This is a sunny playground on a large green lawn, with a dark metal railing fence along the back and bushes beyond it. In the front left, two children are sitting on a red seesaw, a small girl in a pink dress on the left end and a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts on the right end. The girl's end is tilted up and the boy's end is tilted down, and each child is holding a small handle and looking toward the other. In the bottom right corner, a man with short dark hair and a beard, wearing a green T-shirt and beige trousers, is walking along holding a pink ice cream in a cone close to his mouth. Behind them there is a swing frame with one empty red seat, not moving, a slide with a ladder on the right, and a green rubbish bin by the fence. A big tree stands on the left. Nothing seems about to go wrong; it looks like a calm scene, with the man strolling and not looking at the children.
