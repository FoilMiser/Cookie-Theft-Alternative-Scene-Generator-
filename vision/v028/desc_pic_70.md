# Picture 70

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate on the left and the side of a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the left, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers stands on a paved path, holding a yellow-bristled broom and sweeping a small pile of red, orange, yellow and brown leaves. Toward the middle, at the back, a man in a red shirt and blue trousers is reaching up to a washing line strung between two wooden posts, pegging up a white T-shirt; a pair of blue jeans is already hanging next to it. Between them stands a blue-grey swing frame with a red seat hanging still, with nobody on it. A teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow are on the grass at the front, both unused, and there is a flower bed by the house. It looks like an ordinary afternoon of chores in the garden, and nothing appears impossible or about to go wrong.
