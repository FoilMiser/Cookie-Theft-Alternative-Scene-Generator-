# Picture 10

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back and a gate at the far right. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and dark skirt, stands by the round flower bed watering the flowers from a teal watering can. At the right, a boy with brown hair in a red T-shirt and blue trousers stands on the paved path sweeping with what is very clearly a giant silver fork, not a broom. He is pushing a small pile of dirt or dust towards a red dustpan lying on the path, and a little cloud of dust is rising. An orange cat is walking along the very top of the fence, in the centre of the picture, with one end of the swing frame close by. In the middle of the lawn stands a dark blue swing set with a single red seat, which is empty and hanging still. A grey wheelbarrow sits empty in the front centre. The odd thing is the boy sweeping with an oversized fork, which is impossible to do properly and looks like it should be a broom. Otherwise the scene is calm.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fork could be read as a real fork that is wrongly huge (a joke or error) or as an odd rake or broom head; it looks far more like a dining fork than a rake.
- The swing's ropes and frame overlap the fence lines behind them, so the upper part of the swing is a little hard to separate visually from the fence.
- The swing's far legs appear to stand on the grass while the near right leg comes close to the path; nothing obviously passes through anything, but the depth is hard to read.
- The dustpan lies half on the grass and half on the path, and I could not tell if the handle is meant to be pointing away from the boy.
- The boy's hands look a little tangled around the fork handle.
- The cat's legs are quite long and thin, so it could look slightly like another animal at first glance.
