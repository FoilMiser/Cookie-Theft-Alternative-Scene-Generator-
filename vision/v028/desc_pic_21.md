# Picture 21

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left and a wooden fence and gate at the back. A small girl with a dark bun and a pink dress is in the middle of the lawn, jumping in the air with both arms raised; her feet are off the ground and her shadow is on the grass underneath her. She looks happy, as if jumping for joy, with her head tipped back. To the right is a swing set with a red seat, hanging still and empty, and nobody else is in the garden. A wooden bench stands empty at the lower left, in front of the house. There is also a round flower bed, an empty wheelbarrow, a teal watering can and a paved path. Nothing looks wrong, and nothing else seems about to happen beyond the girl landing, or perhaps going to the swing.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Why she is jumping is not shown. Reading 1: she is jumping for joy. Reading 2: she is jumping towards or has just jumped off the swing, though she is some way from it and not facing it.
- Her face is tilted up and to the side and is rather squashed and hard to read; I cannot be sure if her eyes are closed or looking up.
- Her arms are very thin and her body is small, so at first glance she could be read as falling rather than jumping; the shadow directly below does indicate she is off the ground on purpose.
- The swing is perfectly still and empty; it is not clear if it is relevant to her.
- Nothing is passing through anything else.
