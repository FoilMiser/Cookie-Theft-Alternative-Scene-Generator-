# Picture 22

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left and a wooden fence and gate at the back. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes is alone in the middle of the lawn, kicking an orange ball with a white stripe. His leg is swung forward with the ball right at his foot, arms out for balance, and curved motion lines show the swing of his leg. The ball is heading left, directly towards the round flower bed, and it looks as if it may be about to land in the flowers and damage them, or carry on to the house. There is also an empty wheelbarrow, a teal watering can and two pots of green plants around the garden. The weather is clear with two small white clouds. Nothing looks impossible; the only tension is where the ball will go.
