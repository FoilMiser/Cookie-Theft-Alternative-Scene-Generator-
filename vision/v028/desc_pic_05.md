# Picture 5

## Free description
It is a sunny day at a small playground or park with a dark metal railing fence across the back and hedges beyond it. A girl in a pink dress is going down a yellow-and-orange slide on the right, seated part way down with both arms raised, looking towards the bottom of the slide and seeming to enjoy it. She is the only person in the picture. At the bottom right there is a sandpit with a wooden frame and a red bucket and blue spade in it, but no one is playing there. On the left there is a big tree, a bush and an empty wooden bench, and a green bin stands by the fence behind the slide. A grey rock lies on the grass beside the sandpit. Nothing looks wrong or dangerous; she will presumably just reach the bottom of the slide.
