# Picture 47

## Free description
This is a back garden on a gloomy day, with a grey sky and three big dark storm clouds overhead, and no sun. A cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, a wooden fence runs across the back, and a paved path leads to a gate on the right. In the centre, a person with short dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller; a patch of several boards is already bright blue and a paint can with blue on it stands on the grass nearby. To the right of them, a boy with dark curly or braided hair, a yellow shirt and orange shorts faces the viewer with one hand raised, with small motion marks around it, looking as if he is waving or calling out. Further right, on the path, a person in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is looking up and pointing at the sky with one arm stretched out. It looks as if rain is about to start: the pointing person seems to be noticing the clouds, the boy seems to be calling for attention, and the painter is carrying on regardless, which would spoil the wet paint. No rain is falling yet. A bench, a hose reel, a wheelbarrow, a watering can and a flower bed are all around, unused.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The person in the red cap could be pointing at the storm clouds to warn the others, or could be pointing at something in the sky I cannot see. There is nothing else visible in the sky.
- The boy's raised hand could be a wave hello, a wave to get the painter's attention, or a warning. His face looks mild, not alarmed, and he is looking at the viewer, not at the painter.
- The boy in the yellow shirt and the painter in the yellow dress wear almost the same yellow, which could suggest they belong together, or could be a coincidence.
- The painter's head faces right toward the fence, but the body is drawn in a way that makes it hard to tell if we see them from the side or from the back.
- The left-hand board of the blue patch is not painted to its pointed tip; its top looks flat where the roller is, unlike the two blue boards beside it. This could mean that board is still being painted.
- The hose reel slightly overlaps the edge of the flower bed, and the reel has only a short stub of hose with no long hose attached.
