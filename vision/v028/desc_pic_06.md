# Picture 6

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a park with hills and hedges in the distance, a winding sandy path on the left and a blue pond on the right. A man in a green shirt and tan trousers is sitting on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket at the front of the picture, leaning back on one arm with his legs stretched out, and a wicker picnic basket is on the blanket next to him. He seems to be relaxing, looking off to the right towards the pond. A white duck with an orange bill is swimming on the pond, with reeds at both ends. The oddest thing is a wooden ladder in the middle of the grass: it stands at a slant, leaning on nothing at all, with no wall or tree to support it and no one on it, and it looks as if it should fall over. A tall black lamp post stands nearby. A grey rock lies at the front and a green bin stands at the bottom right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The ladder could be read as an impossible free-standing ladder, or as something simply left there by a worker that ought to have fallen down. There is a small shadow under its feet so it does seem to be standing on the ground, and nothing supports its top.
- The man's lower arm goes behind the basket, so I can't see his hand or whether he is reaching into the basket or leaning on the blanket.
- His legs extend off the end of the blanket with his shoes on the grass; that looks fine, but his hips sit near the corner of the blanket and could look slightly unsettled.
- He might be looking at the duck, at the pond in general, or at nothing in particular; his head is turned towards the right and slightly towards us.
- The path narrows quickly and disappears behind the big tree; it could be read as ending at the tree rather than carrying on.
