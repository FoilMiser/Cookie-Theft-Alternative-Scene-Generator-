# Picture 3

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden, this time with the house and blue door on the left and the gate and path on the right. A man or older boy in a red shirt and blue trousers stands beside the flower bed tipping a teal watering can towards the flowers. The water does something strange: instead of falling on the flowers, it comes out of the can and curves upward and away in a long arc, ending in drops in the air above the flowers. At the back, an older man in a cap and waistcoat is digging with a spade, with a small hole and a heap of soil beside him. On the path at the lower right a small girl in a pink dress holds a very long string and is flying an orange kite, which is high in the sky with a tail of two small bows. A wheelbarrow stands empty on the lawn at the front, and two potted plants stand near the gate. Nobody seems to be in any danger, and the only thing that looks impossible is the water going up.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water arc could be read as water impossibly flowing upward, or as water being flung out in a strong curving jet. Either way it misses the flowers and the drops are pointing up rather than falling.
- The arc starts close to the flowers rather than clearly from the can's spout, so it could also be read at first glance as a thin pale hoop or a bent pipe rising from the flower bed.
- I cannot tell whether the older man is starting a hole or filling one in; the spade is in the soil at the hole.
- The kite string passes in front of the fence and gate. That looks natural, but it is long enough to draw the eye across most of the right side.
- There is no spool visible in the girl's hand, only a raised hand with the string.
