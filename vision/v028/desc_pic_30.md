# Picture 30

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the middle of the lawn a woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers stands with both arms stretched out, reaching for a red-and-white ball in the air just in front of her hands. A little girl in a pink dress is further right, one arm stretched out towards the woman, as if she has just thrown the ball; a dotted line curves through the air from her hand towards the ball, showing its path. So they are playing catch and the woman is about to catch it. A black-and-white cat is walking along the top of the fence, above them. At the front, a sprinkler on the end of a hose is shooting three arcs of water to the left; the highest arc lands on a closed cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is getting wet, with water running down its sides and a puddle around its base. No one seems to have noticed the box getting soaked. At the front right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sits on a bench, sipping a drink through a straw. There is also a birdhouse on a tall pole by the gate.
