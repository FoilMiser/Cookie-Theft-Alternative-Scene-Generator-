# Picture 31

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with no people in it. There is a wooden fence along the back with a gate on the left, a paved path leading to the gate, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the front centre stands a white goat with black hooves and a small dark curved horn, with its head bent right down to the ground, nibbling at a small tuft of grass. Next to it is a grey metal wheelbarrow, with nothing in it, and further left a teal watering can on the grass. Behind these stands a blue-grey swing frame with one red seat hanging still and empty. On the right there is a flower bed and a coiled hose on a reel. A goat in a domestic garden is somewhat unexpected, but it is not doing any harm so far. Nothing else is happening and nothing looks about to happen, except that the goat may keep eating; the flowers are close by.

- Grass tuft: small darker green clump with a bit of white or cream in it, under the goat's mouth.
- Wheelbarrow: grey with wooden handles and a black wheel, front centre-left, empty, standing still.
- Watering can: teal, front left on the grass, not being used.
- Swing set: blue-grey A-frame, left of centre, with one red seat on two ropes, empty and still.
- Hose reel: teal hose coiled on a reel with a dark stand, right, beside the flower bed.
- Flower bed: right, brown soil with yellow, orange, purple, pink and red flowers.
- Paved path: left, leading to the gate.
- Gate: wooden, far left, closed.
- House: right, cream with red roof, blue door and a window.
- Tree and bush: tree behind the fence by the house, bush beside the house.
- Sun (top left) and two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The animal reads to me as a goat because of the dark ridged horn, but it could be taken for a white dog, lamb or sheep at first glance; its shape is quite lanky and it has only one visible horn.
- Its head is hard to make out: the white face with a dark eye and small dark markings looks slightly skull-like, and the cream-coloured bit under the chin could be a beard, or a flower it is eating, or part of the tuft.
- The way it bends is a little awkward: back very arched, legs thin, head tucked low.
- How the goat got in is not shown; the gate looks closed. It may be a pet, or a stray that has wandered in.
- With no people, I can't tell whether the garden tools are in use or just left out.
