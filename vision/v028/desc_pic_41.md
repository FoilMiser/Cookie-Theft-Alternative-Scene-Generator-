# Picture 41

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence along the back, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a paved path leading to a wooden gate on the left. A small child in a pink outfit sits on a red swing seat in the swing frame on the left, smiling, with little motion lines behind the swing showing it is moving. In the centre-right, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands at a round flower bed holding out what looks like a dark frying pan by its handle. Water is pouring out of the pan in a stream onto the flowers, as if the pan were being used as a watering can. That is the strange part: a frying pan is not what you would water flowers with, and I see no watering can anywhere. By the blue door, a brown and white dog sits upright with a red lead in its mouth, the loop of the lead trailing on the grass, looking as if it wants to go for a walk. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands on the grass at the front. Nobody seems to be paying attention to the dog or the child; the man is looking down at the flowers.
