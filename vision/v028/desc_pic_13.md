# Picture 13

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden, with a house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a tall wooden fence across the back and a gate and paved path on the left. A boy in a blue hoodie, red cap and dark trousers stands by the fence looking up, with both hands raised, holding a string that leads to a purple kite. The kite is stuck or tangled up in the tree on the right, overlapping its leaves. In the front left, a dark-skinned child with curly hair, a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts is kicking an orange ball on the path. To the right, an older man with grey hair, a blue cap, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers walks across the grass while reading an open book, and he is not looking where he is going. A blue bucket stands on the lawn just to the left of him, close to his path, so he might bump into or trip over it, though it isn't directly in front of his feet. On top of the fence, an orange cat is crouching and stretched forward towards a red bird perched on the fence a little to its left; the cat looks like it is about to pounce on the bird. A blue bird is flying in the sky at top left, near the sun.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The kite could be read as (a) stuck in the tree, or (b) simply flying in front of the tree. Because leaf clumps are drawn over it and the string sags, I read it as stuck.
- The boy could be read as flying the kite happily or tugging to free it; his expression is hard to read.
- The bucket could be read as an obstacle the man is about to walk into, but he is moving past it, not squarely into it, so it may or may not be a danger.
- The cat's stance could be read as stalking or pouncing, or as just walking along low. It looks like a stalk.
- The cat stands on the jagged fence tops, which look too narrow to balance on, and its legs meet them awkwardly.
- The child's kicking leg and the ball overlap at the foot, and the ball and foot sit near the edge of the path.
- The light-green clumps of leaves on the tree look like they float on top of the main tree shape.
