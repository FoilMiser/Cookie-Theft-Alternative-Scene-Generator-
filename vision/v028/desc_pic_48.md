# Picture 48

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence along the back, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a paved path leading to a gate on the left. There is one person, a man with short dark hair and brown skin, in a green T-shirt, beige trousers and brown shoes, in the middle of the lawn. He is reading an open red book and is in a sitting position, with his knees bent and his body leaning back, but there is no chair, bench or anything else under him. He appears to be sitting on thin air, which looks impossible. His feet are on the grass, but nothing is holding the rest of him up. An empty wooden bench stands well away to the right, so the seat he could be using is not under him. He seems absorbed in the book and unaware of anything odd. Around the garden there is a birdhouse on a tall pole, two potted plants, a teal watering can, a flower bed, a hose reel and a couple of bushes, none being used.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The man's pose could be read two ways: he really is sitting on nothing, as if on an invisible chair (which is what it looks like), or he has been drawn leaning back or slipping as if about to fall over backwards. His shadow is only a small patch by his feet, which does not settle it.
- He could be read as sitting in a chair that has been left out of the picture; nothing in the scene explains it either way.
- The man is drawn large compared with the bench and the flower bed, though he is also close to the front of the picture.
- The bottom of the birdhouse pole has a small dark and green mark where it meets the grass; it could be a base or a shadow.
