## Free description

This is a busy, sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof on the right, a wooden fence across the back and a gate and paved path on the left. At the back left, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is painting the fence blue with a long brush, with a paint tin at his feet; a section by the gate is already blue. Next to him a small girl in a pink dress is in mid-throw, and a red and white ball flies from her along a dashed arc across to a man in a green T-shirt and tan trousers, who holds his hands out to catch it. In the front left, an old man with a blue-grey cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat stands on the path sweeping with what is clearly a giant fork, far too big, brushing a little pile of dirt and a puff of dust toward a red dustpan on the ground; using a fork as a broom looks silly and wrong. In the front centre, a woman with a long dark ponytail in a purple top and teal trousers walks along reading an open book, not looking where she is going, and just ahead of her in her path is a blue bucket on the ground, so it looks as if she may be about to walk into it or trip over it. On the right, an old woman with grey hair and glasses waters the flower bed with a teal watering can. A red bird perches on top of the fence near the tree, and an empty bench is at the bottom right.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The big fork: it could be read as a garden fork or a huge eating fork. It looks like a dinner fork, shiny and far too large, but its long handle may be meant as a garden tool. Either way it is being used as a broom, which is wrong, and is clearly out of scale.
- The woman and the bucket: she is walking toward it but it could be read as just sitting nearby; it is close but slightly to the side, so she may or may not actually hit it.
- The two paint containers: the grey tin clearly goes with the painter, but the separate blue bucket in the front could belong to him too, or be for something else.
- The ball's flight: I read it as the girl throwing to the man, with the dashed line behind it; it could also be read as the man having thrown it to the girl, but the dashes trail back toward the girl.
- The girl's thin legs and running pose are a bit awkward, and it is unclear whether she is running or standing leaning.
- The ball appears to overlap the top edge of the fence and is at nearly the same height as the catcher's head, and his hands are not yet near it.
- The dust puff around the fork looks like a soft grey cloud; it reads as dust being kicked up.
- The bird just sits there and doesn't seem involved, though the ball is heading in its general direction.
