# Picture 32

## Free description

This is a sunny park with a pond on the left, green hills behind, and a sandy path winding down on the right. A man or teenage boy with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands at the edge of the pond, holding a brown paper bag and reaching out with his other hand, scattering crumbs for a white duck, which is swimming towards him. Crumbs lie on the water and on the grass at the pond's edge. A little girl in a pink dress stands near the lamp post holding the string of a yellow balloon. An older man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is walking at the far end of the path carrying a yellow shopping bag. At the front right is the main small drama: a child with dark braided hair, a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts is holding an empty ice cream cone and rubbing one eye, as if crying, because the pink ice cream has fallen on the ground. The ice cream lies in a pink splat with coloured sprinkles on the path, and a brown dog with a red neckerchief has its nose right down to it, about to lick it up. A red bird is flying across the sky at the top left. An empty wooden bench stands by the path.
