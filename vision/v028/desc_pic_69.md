# Picture 69

## Free description

This is a sunny park with a pond on the right, a winding sandy path on the left, a lamp post in the middle and hills in the distance. By the pond, an older man in a flat cap, white shirt, brown vest and dark trousers is standing and holding a long fishing rod, with the line dropping into the water to a red and white float surrounded by a small ripple ring. To the right of him, a man in a red shirt and blue trousers is holding a camera up to his face with both hands and pointing it to the left, as if taking a picture of the fisherman. Back on the left, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is walking to the left, pushing a teal pram with a sleeping baby under a pink spotted blanket, and he is close to the edge of the path. The bench in front of him is empty. There is also a grey rock at the front, a green bin at the front right, and several trees and bushes. No fish is visible and nothing looks wrong or impossible. It seems to be a calm, ordinary day in the park, and the float's ripple might suggest a fish is nibbling.
