## Free description

This is a sunny playground or park with a dark metal railing fence running across the back and a lot of people doing separate things. At the far left a blue-and-white beach ball is stuck up in a tree, and a small girl in a pink dress stands below it pointing up at it. On the right a spotted dog bows down playfully to a tennis ball, wagging its tail, in front of an empty slide. At the far right a boy in a blue hoodie stands with his hands on his hips wearing a metal bucket upside down on his head, which is silly or odd. In the front centre a red bicycle stands on its own, and it has square wheels, which could not roll. There is also an empty sandpit with a red bucket and blue spade, a red bird on the fence, a bin and a rock.
