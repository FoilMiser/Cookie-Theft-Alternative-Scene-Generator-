# Picture 11

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills in the distance, a pond on the right and a winding sandy path on the left. A man with dark skin and short dark hair, wearing a green T-shirt and tan trousers, stands at the left beside the path, holding a paper bag and scattering seed or crumbs. The seeds fall in a trail down to two small birds, one blue and one red, which are pecking at them on the path. A boy or young man with brown hair, in a red T-shirt and blue trousers, sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket in the front centre-right, leaning back on one arm with a wicker picnic basket next to him. He is looking to the left, towards the man and birds or towards the ladder. A wooden ladder stands upright on the grass in the middle of the park with nothing to lean against, which looks impossible since it would fall over. Beside it is a tall black street lamp. The rest of the scene has a flower bed, a green bin, a grey rock, bushes and trees, and nothing else seems about to happen.
