## Free description

This is a sunny, windy back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back, and a gate, paved path and bird box on a post on the left. There is only one person: a man with brown hair in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands in the middle of the lawn with both arms thrown up, looking up in surprise. Just above his hands an orange cap is flying through the air, tumbling, with white wind lines and a few leaves blowing past it, so it looks as if the wind has just taken his cap off and he is trying to grab it. On the left stands a big grey-blue swing frame with a single swing with a red seat, hanging still and empty. At the bottom there is an unattended teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow on the grass, and on the right there is a flower bed by the house. The swing does not seem to be moving even though it is windy. It looks as if the cap may fly over the fence.
