# Picture 27

## Free description

This is a sunny park with a pond on the left, green hills behind, and a sandy path winding down on the right. A man with short dark hair and a small beard, dark brown skin, a green T-shirt and khaki trousers is walking to the right in the front centre. He holds a red lead in his hands, and the lead goes to a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar, which is walking ahead of him. On a wooden bench to the right of them, an older man with grey hair, a blue flat cap, a white shirt and brown waistcoat is sitting and reading a red book, with one hand near his chin. He seems absorbed in the book and does not look up. The dog and man are heading in the general direction of the bench. Nothing dramatic is happening; it is an ordinary, calm scene of someone walking a dog while someone else reads. Nothing looks impossible or wrong.
