# Picture 62

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back, a gate at the far left, and a paved path running down the left side. A man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands in the middle holding the nozzle of a green hose up close in front of his face and peering down at it, because only a single drop of water is coming out. A white goat with small grey horns is standing on the lawn right on top of the hose, and just past its back foot the hose swells into a fat bulge on the ground, between the goat and the reel by the flower bed. So it looks as if the goat is squashing the hose and stopping the water, the water is backing up, and the man has not noticed and is looking in the wrong place. If the goat moves, or he looks into the nozzle a moment longer, the water might suddenly spray him. To the left a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts swings happily with eyes closed and a big smile, with motion lines by the seat. The strangest thing is a cardboard box hanging tilted in the sky at the upper left, above the fence near the sun, with nothing holding it up and nobody looking at it. It looks as if it is floating or perhaps falling, with no explanation for how it got there.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The floating box could be read as something impossible (a box hanging in the air), as a box caught mid-fall or thrown over the fence, or as a mistake in the drawing. There are no motion lines or thrower, and nothing else in the scene relates to it. It looks out of place.
- The man's head is bent so far forward that his face is partly hidden, and the neck and shoulder area looks slightly awkward. I read him as peering into the nozzle; he could also just be shaking it or trying to clear it.
- The bulge in the hose could be swelling from trapped water, which fits the goat standing on it, or it could be some kind of connector, but it looks like swelling. It sits on the reel side of the goat, which fits water being blocked there.
- The goat's front foot sits just above the hose line and only the back foot clearly presses on it, so it might be standing on the hose with one foot or both.
- The goat may be innocently standing there or may be doing it deliberately; it does not look at the man.
