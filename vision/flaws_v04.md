# v0.4 flaw list (inspection + blind screen) and what v0.5 did about each

Every scene and every gallery tile was inspected at full resolution. Severity: **A** = misreads the
scored event or adds an unintended oddity a participant could report; **B** = weak or ambiguous
reading; **C** = polish.

## Status after v0.5

All A-level items and the B-level items above are fixed in v0.5, with three partial exceptions:

- S10 empty sky: the garden gains a tree line beyond the fence and the park's hills sit higher, but the top third of most pictures is still sky (kept free for weather, kites, balloons and birds).
- S13 songbirds stay larger than life: at true size a bird alone falls under the 3%-of-width rule.
- S15 edge density: simpler facades bring the empty street from 0.041 to 0.037 (garden 0.031, park 0.017, playground 0.022); it is still the busiest setting.
