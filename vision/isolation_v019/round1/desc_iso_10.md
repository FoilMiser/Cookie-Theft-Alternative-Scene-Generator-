## What I see
A lawn scene with a wooden plank fence across the top and a flower bed at the left, with orange, purple and yellow flowers in brown soil. A dark teal garden hose comes up from the bottom edge to a lawn sprinkler on a dark round base at the right. The sprinkler throws three arcs of water to the left. The highest arc lands on a small wooden box or crate, and the two lower arcs land on the grass in front of it, with drops flying off. The crate is wet: there are blue splashes at its top corners and drip streaks down its sides, and it sits in a light-blue puddle.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The water is aimed at the box and the bare grass, not at the flowers. That may be the point of the scene, or it may be a sprinkler pointed the wrong way.
- The fence post just above the crate comes down to almost exactly the crate's top edge. This makes it look as if the post is resting on the crate or going into it, instead of standing behind it.
- The fence posts end in rounded bottoms partway down the grass with no visible base. They look a little like they are hanging or floating, not planted in the ground.
- The flower bed and the leftmost (orange) flower are cut off at the left edge.
