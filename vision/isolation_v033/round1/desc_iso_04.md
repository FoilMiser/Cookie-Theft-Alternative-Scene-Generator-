# iso_04

## What I see
A back garden: a blue door with a cream frame on the left, a wooden plank fence behind, and a green bush. In front of the bush is an oval bed of brown soil with many colourful flowers (red, yellow, purple, pink, orange). On the left, a teal hose reel on a dark stand feeds a long teal garden hose that runs along the grass and up to an older woman on the right. She has grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top, a grey-blue skirt and brown shoes. She holds a yellow nozzle out in front of her with both arms straight. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar stands in the middle, tail up, facing the woman. A grey wheelbarrow is partly visible at the far right edge.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- No stream of water comes out of the nozzle, only one blue drop hanging near it. She could be about to water, the water could be off or dribbling, or the blue drop could be part of the nozzle.
- What she is aiming at is unclear. Her arms point left toward the dog and the flower bed, so she could be about to water the flowers or about to spray the dog.
- Her pose is stiff: both arms stick straight out at shoulder height and seem to cross in front of her chin or neck. The arms look slightly oddly joined to her body.
- Near the dog's back legs the hose looks wider and flattened, like a dark oval. This could be a puddle of water, a shadow, or a bulge in the hose drawing. The dog's paws appear to stand on or overlap the hose and this dark patch.
- A single tall wooden post stands in the middle of the fence and reaches down much lower than the fence boards, into the grass. It reads as a lone post standing in front of the fence (perhaps a clothesline pole), and it is unclear what it is for.
- The hose path is a little confusing. It goes down from the reel, along the bottom, under the dog, then up to the woman's hands, so it looks very long and loops in front of her.
