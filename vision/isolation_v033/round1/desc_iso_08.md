# iso_08

## What I see
A back-garden scene. An older man with grey hair, a grey cap, a white long-sleeved shirt, brown vest and dark trousers stands on the right. He holds a hose nozzle out in front of him with both hands and looks at it. Only a single drop of water is coming out of it. The dark teal hose runs from a hose reel on the left, across the lawn and up to his hands. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar stands on the hose in the middle of the lawn, facing the man. Behind them are a flowerbed of red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers, a green bush, a wooden fence, and a blue door with a grey step at the far left. A grey wheelbarrow is partly visible at the right edge.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The hose right under the dog's feet is drawn flat and wide. It can be read two ways: the dog is standing on the hose and squashing it, which would explain the single drip, or this is just where the hose lies flat on the grass. It is not clear whether the dog's paws are on the hose or just next to it.
- The single drop can be read as the water being blocked, or as the man just starting or stopping the water. He seems to be peering at the nozzle, maybe puzzled, though his expression is hard to read at this size.
- The hose's path is hard to follow. Near the man's feet it seems to loop back on itself, and a second strand runs along the bottom edge. I can't tell which strand leads to the nozzle, or whether one hose crosses over itself.
- The man's arms look stiff and oddly long, like one straight white tube from shoulder to hands. They seem to come out of his chest rather than his shoulders.
- The hose reel stands alone on the lawn, a short way from the house wall. It reads as a reel on a stand, but it could also be a tap or a post.
- Only part of the wheelbarrow shows (a wheel and part of the tray), so its purpose in the scene is unclear.
