## What I see
A back-garden scene: on the left is a blue house door with a cream frame and a grey step. Behind it is a fence with a wooden top rail and post, a round green bush, and an oval bed of brown soil full of red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers. An older man with grey hair, a grey cap, a white shirt, a brown vest and dark trousers stands on the right with both arms held straight out. He holds a hose nozzle with a single light-blue water drop coming from it, so he seems to be watering, or about to water, the flowers. A teal garden hose runs from a hose reel on the left, across the bottom of the lawn and up to his hands. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar stands on the grass between the flower bed and the man, facing him with its tail up.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The man stands well away from the flower bed. Only one drop falls, straight down near his hands, so no water reaches the flowers. He could be watering, or just holding the hose and about to start.
- His arms are very stiff, straight and long. The white sleeves stick straight out from his chest like one board, which looks a bit unnatural.
- The yellow bit at the nozzle could be the nozzle itself or a trigger. I can't tell which.
- The dog's feet sit right on the hose line, so it looks as if the dog is standing on the hose (or the hose runs through its feet).
- Just left of the dog, part of the hose is drawn as a wide, flat, lumpy band. It could be a flattened or squashed piece of hose, or a puddle the same colour as the hose.
- The tan rectangles in the fence look like bricks, so the back boundary could be a brick wall with a wooden top rail, or a wooden plank fence.
- At the far right edge there is a grey object with a wheel, cut off by the crop. It looks like a wheelbarrow, but too little of it shows to be sure.
- The hose reel on the left seems to sit on a dark block or stand. It is not clear whether it is mounted on the wall or sitting on the ground.
