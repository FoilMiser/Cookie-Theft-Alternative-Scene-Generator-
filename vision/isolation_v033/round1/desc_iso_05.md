## What I see

A small girl in a pink dress, with her hair in two buns, sits on the raised left end. She holds a dark T-shaped handle and her yellow shoes dangle in the air, and her mouth is a small open "o", so she looks surprised or worried. A much larger man in a green shirt and tan trousers sits on the lowered right end, with his feet on the ground. He is looking down at an open book with a red cover that rests on his raised knee, so he seems to be reading and not paying attention to the girl. The tilt matches the weights: the heavy man's end is down and the light girl is stuck up in the air.
