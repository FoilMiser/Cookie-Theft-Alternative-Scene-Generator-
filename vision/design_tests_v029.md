## What this says

- **The sleeping dog.** Asleep in all three pictures, in the first breath, and nobody has it sniffing, sitting or collapsed. That was the risk in a body lying stretched out, which v0.13 had turned away from.
- **The duck.** Eating in both. One reader still finds the body "slightly odd", where the bench's first two drawings had it falling over.
- **The trip.** It is in three pictures, and all three readers say it in the free description, unprompted. All three still offer the walker going past as a second reading, and test 5 is a picture where a path makes that easier.
- **The dog at the door.** "As though it is asking to be taken for a walk", and also, under "unsure", "could be simply holding it". One picture.
- **The hose.** No "stiff" or "rigid pipe". The reader follows it as one hose, and says the part by the leg is unclear. The joint at the fist is no longer named.
- **The rules.** The ladder is alone and called odd, and the reader does not tie it to the lamp, which the rule now keeps at least 160 units off. The bucket has no rain to explain it. Each rule removes the case it was written for, so these pictures say only that nothing else took its place.
- **The fish.** It reads as a fish "as if it were a bird" and as impossible, which the limb did not spoil. "It looks a little as if this branch was added on top of the tree".

Nothing here asked for a new rule. Six pictures are six readers, and the screen is the test.
