# iso_02

## What I see
A man in a red short-sleeved shirt, blue jeans with a brown belt, and dark shoes stands on green grass in a backyard in front of a tall wooden plank fence, with green bushes and blue sky behind. He reaches up with both arms to a clothesline strung between two wooden T-shaped posts, and his hands are at the clothes peg on the right shoulder of a white T-shirt, so he seems to be pegging the shirt up (or taking it down). A pair of blue trousers hangs from two pegs further right on the same line. On the grass to his left sits a woven wooden laundry basket heaped with pink, white and blue rounded items, which look like clothes waiting to be hung.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- I can't tell whether he is pinning the T-shirt up or unpinning it. His hands are on the peg and the line, which could also read as just holding the line.
- The items in the basket are smooth rounded mounds. They could be bundled laundry, or they could be read as balls or cushions.
- His proportions look a little odd: the legs are long and thin compared with a narrow torso, and he looks tall next to the fence. Both feet point to the left while his upper body and face turn up toward the line, so the twist at the hips is a bit stiff.
- Both raised arms come from about the same spot near his far shoulder, and from this angle I can't clearly tell the two arms apart.
- Nothing seems to float or pass through anything else. The basket stands clear of the left post, and the clothes hang from visible pegs.
