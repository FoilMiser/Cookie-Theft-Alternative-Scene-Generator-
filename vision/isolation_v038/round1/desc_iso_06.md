## What I see
A young man or teenage boy with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands on green grass in a back garden. A tall wooden plank fence runs behind him, with green bushes and blue sky above it. A clothesline runs between two wooden posts, and he is reaching up with both hands to the line at the shoulder of a white T-shirt. He seems to be pegging it up, or perhaps unpegging it. The white T-shirt is held by clothespins and has water drops falling from it, so it looks freshly washed and wet. A pair of blue trousers hangs from pegs further along the line to the right. On the grass at the lower left is a rectangular tan basket, wooden or wicker, holding rounded bundles of pink, white and blue laundry.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- I cannot tell if he is hanging the white T-shirt up or taking it down. The drips point to hanging up wet washing, but his hand pose alone fits either.
- The water drops hang in the air under the shirt, apart from it. That reads as dripping, but they could also look like small blue objects floating.
- The pink, white and blue mounds in the basket are probably folded or bunched clothes. They could also be read as balls or cushions.
- The basket's horizontal slats make it look like either a wicker laundry basket or a small wooden crate.
- His figure is quite tall and thin, with long, narrow legs and a slim torso. His dark shoes are small, blob-like shapes, and one foot seems to be stepping back.
- The trousers hang straight down, with pegs only at the top corners of the waistband, and their top edge sits slightly below the sagging line. They look a little stiff.
- Nothing seems to pass through anything else, and no body parts look detached. His arms reach up and to the left of his head, toward the peg, and look properly joined.
