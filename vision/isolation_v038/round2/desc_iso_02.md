# iso_02

## What I see
A young man with brown hair, a red short-sleeved shirt, blue trousers and dark shoes stands on green grass in front of a wooden plank fence. Green bushes or treetops and blue sky show above the fence. A clothesline runs between two tall T-topped wooden posts, one at each side of the picture. A pair of blue trousers hangs from the line on the left, held by two clothespins, and a white T-shirt hangs on the right with blue water drops falling from it. The man reaches up with both hands to the white T-shirt's upper left corner, where a clothespin is, so he seems to be pinning wet laundry on the line to dry.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- His hands are at the corner of the white shirt. He could be pinning it up (hanging it out) or unpinning it (taking it down). The dripping water makes hanging it out more likely, but the pose alone could be read either way.
- Only the white shirt drips. The blue trousers show no drops, so they could be already dry or just drawn without drops.
- Both arms come from about the same point on his upper chest and shoulder, and both hands come together at the shirt's corner. The arms look a little thin and stretched, but not clearly detached.
- His body is very slim and his legs are long compared with his large head. His two shoes are small dark blobs, one partly in front of the other, so his footing is hard to read: he could be stepping forward or standing on tiptoe to reach.
- The clothesline sags only slightly and seems to pass in front of the fence. Nothing looks clearly floating, but the white shirt's top edge sits right along the line, so it is hard to tell whether the shirt hangs from the line or just overlaps it apart from the two pins.
