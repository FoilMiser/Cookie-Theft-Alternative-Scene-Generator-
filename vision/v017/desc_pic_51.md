# Picture 51

## Free description
This is a sunny day at a park playground, with a dark metal railing fence and hedges at the back, a sun at top right and two white clouds. In the bottom left, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is flying a yellow diamond-shaped kite. Its long string runs up from his hand to the kite high in the sky. Near the fence, a person in a blue hoodie is jumping in the air with both arms up and a big happy grin. Next to them, a red bicycle stands on the grass, and strangely its wheels are square instead of round. A swing set in the middle has one empty swing, and an empty slide stands on the right. In the front, a girl in a pink dress is crying and rubbing her eye. She is holding an empty ice-cream cone because her pink scoop with sprinkles has fallen and splatted on the grass. A brown dog with a red collar is bending down to sniff or eat the fallen ice cream. A small white puppy is curled up asleep on the grass in the middle-left. On the right is an empty sandbox with a grey rock beside it. The square bicycle wheels are clearly impossible.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The bicycle's wheels are drawn as squares rather than circles. This looks deliberately "wrong", as a bike with square wheels couldn't roll.
- The person in blue is off the ground with a shadow beneath them. Most likely they are jumping for joy, but they could also be read as floating in the air. It's unclear what they are celebrating (perhaps the kite).
- The small white animal is most likely a sleeping puppy. Because it is so small and round, it could also be read as a white cat or a lamb.
- The brown dog could be sniffing the ice cream or already licking it up.
- The girl could be crying because she dropped the ice cream, or because the dog is eating it. Probably both.
- Nothing else looks like it is passing through other objects.
