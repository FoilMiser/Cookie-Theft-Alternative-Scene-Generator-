# Picture 53

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park with green hills and trees in the background. On the left, a young man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is jogging or running beside a winding sandy path, arms bent and one foot kicked up behind him. In the middle, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands on the grass holding a fishing rod; his line reaches far out into a pond on the right, where a red-and-white float is bobbing with ripples around it, so he may have a bite. In the front, a young woman with brown hair, a blue hoodie and dark trousers sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket with a wicker picnic basket; she is smiling with her eyes closed and pink cheeks, looking very happy or laughing. An empty wooden bench stands near the path. There is a tall black lamp post with a green rubbish bin beside it, another bin in the bottom-right corner, a grey rock at the bottom centre, and a round bed of colourful flowers on the right. Bulrushes grow at the edges of the pond. Nothing looks dangerous or impossible; it is a calm, ordinary park scene.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The young man in red could be jogging/running or walking quickly; his pose and kicked-up back foot suggest running. He is partly on the grass beside the path rather than on it.
- The young woman's closed-eye smile could mean she is laughing, enjoying the sun, or that her eyes are shut; she is sitting near the edge of the blanket with her legs and shoes off it.
- The float with ripples could mean a fish is biting, or simply that the float has just landed.
- The fisherman stands quite far back from the pond, so his line is very long, but this does not look impossible.
