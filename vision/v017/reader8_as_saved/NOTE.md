# Three descriptions saved under rotated numbers

Reader 8 was given pic_52 to pic_58 and saved its descriptions of pic_56, pic_57 and pic_58 under the wrong numbers:
the file it named desc_pic_56.md describes pic_57 (a teenager sweeping with a giant fork in a garden), desc_pic_57.md
describes pic_58 (a man fishing, a dropped ice cream, a square-wheeled wheelbarrow in a park) and desc_pic_58.md describes
pic_56 (a grandmother planting flowers, a goat grazing, a man feeding birds in a park). Scorer c found the rotation from
the descriptions alone and scored each key against the description that matches it. After scoring, the three files were
renamed to the pictures they describe (only the "# Picture NN" line changed); the files as the reader saved them are
kept here. The blind images were checked against key_map.json and are the pictures the key names.
