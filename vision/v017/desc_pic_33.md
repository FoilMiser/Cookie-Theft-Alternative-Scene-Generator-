# Picture 33

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills, trees, a winding sandy path on the left, a wooden bench, a tall black lamp post, a green litter bin and a blue pond. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, wearing a teal top and a grey skirt, stands next to the lamp post near the pond. She is throwing crumbs or seeds towards the water. Some crumbs are falling through the air and some are on the ground and in the pond. A white duck swims towards the crumbs. In front of the pond, a man in a flat cap, a brown waistcoat over a white shirt and dark trousers sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket. He leans back with his eyes closed and a big smile, with a picnic basket next to him. He looks relaxed, as if laughing or dozing in the sun. On the right, next to a flower bed, a wooden ladder stands up in the grass at an angle with nothing to lean against. That looks impossible, because it should fall over. A grey rock sits at the front. Some white lines run from behind the duck to just above the man's head.
