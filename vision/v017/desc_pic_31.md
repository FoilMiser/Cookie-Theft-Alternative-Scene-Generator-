# Picture 31

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden, with a wooden fence across the back, a gate in the fence on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. A woman with short dark hair, in a yellow dress and red shoes, stands to the right of centre, facing right towards a round flower bed full of colourful flowers. She is holding a teal watering can and seems to be watering the flowers. But the water does not fall down onto the flowers. It comes out of the spout in an arc that curves upward into the air, with drops at the top, like a fountain. That is impossible for a watering can. Behind her, near the fence, are two potted plants and a round green bush. A paved path runs from the gate down to the bottom left corner. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the front. A tree shows above the fence near the house, and the sky has a sun in the top left and two small clouds. Nobody else is in the garden.
