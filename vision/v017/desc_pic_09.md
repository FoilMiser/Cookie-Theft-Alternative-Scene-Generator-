## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream-coloured house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, and a tall wooden fence across the back. In the middle of the lawn a woman with short dark hair, wearing a yellow dress and red shoes, is reading a red book. She is posed as if sitting on a chair, with her knees bent and her feet on the grass, but there is no chair, bench or stool under her. She is sitting on thin air, which is impossible. Behind her a clothesline strung between two wooden posts has a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes pegged on it. Near the house there is a round flower bed full of red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers, with a coiled garden hose on a reel next to it. On the right are two potted plants, a birdhouse on a tall pole, a teal watering can on the grass, and a paved path leading to a wooden gate in the fence. Nobody else is in the picture. Apart from the missing seat, the scene looks calm and ordinary.
