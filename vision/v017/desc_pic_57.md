# Picture 57

## Free description
It is a sunny, calm day in a back garden. A house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, with a tree behind it and a small bush beside it, and a wooden fence runs across the back with a wooden gate on the right and a paved path leading up to it. A pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes hang still on a washing line between two posts. On the right of the lawn there is a metal A-frame swing set with one red-seated swing, empty. At the bottom right, a young man in a blue hoodie and dark trousers is sweeping dirt on the path into a red dustpan, but instead of a broom he is using a giant silver dinner fork, which is clearly odd. A little cloud of dust rises from the pile of dirt by the dustpan. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the bottom centre, and a round bed of colourful flowers is near the house.

## Inventory
- Young man (right, front, on the path): brown hair, blue hoodie, dark trousers, white shoes; bent slightly, holding a huge silver fork like a broom and sweeping dirt towards a dustpan.
- Giant fork: a silver dinner-fork shape, about as long as a broom, being used to sweep.
- Dustpan and dirt (right, front): red dustpan lying on the path with a small pile of dirt/grit in front of it and a puff of dust in the air.
- Swing set (centre-right): dark grey/blue metal A-frame with a single swing with a red seat; nobody on it, hanging still.
- Washing line (centre, back): rope between two wooden posts with blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel pegged on it, hanging straight down.
- Wheelbarrow (centre, front): grey wheelbarrow with brown handles, empty, on the grass.
- House (left): cream walls, red roof, a window and a blue front door with a step.
- Flower bed (left, front): round bed of soil with red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers.
- Bush (left, next to the house): small round green bush.
- Tree (left, behind the house): large green tree.
- Fence and gate (back): wooden picket fence with a brown gate at the right end.
- Path (right): pale paved path from the bottom of the picture to the gate.
- Sky: sun in the top right, two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The giant fork used as a broom is the obvious odd/impossible element; it could alternatively be read as a garden fork or rake, but it is shaped and coloured like a shiny kitchen fork, not a garden tool.
- The dustpan is lying on the ground by itself rather than being held, so he is sweeping into it from a distance.
- It is unclear whether the grey puff is dust or smoke; dust from sweeping seems most likely.
