# Picture 49

## Free description
This is a busy back garden on a sunny day. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, a tall wooden fence with a gate is at the back right, and a paved path runs down the right side. A blue ball is stuck up in the tree behind the house, resting on a branch. A young person in a blue hoodie standing by the fence is pointing up at it with a surprised look. A young man in a red T-shirt and jeans is watering the flower bed with a hose, which runs from a hose reel at the bottom left. In the middle front, an old man with a grey beard and cap is walking along reading an open book and not looking where he is going. He is heading towards a red bucket on the grass. Right beside him, a bearded man in a green shirt has his hands up to catch a red ball. A boy in a yellow shirt on the right has just thrown the ball, shown by a dotted arc. On top of the fence, a black-and-white cat is creeping along towards a red bird perched near the gate. A woman in a yellow dress on the path by the gate is waving. Several things look about to happen: the cat may pounce on the bird, the old man may trip over the bucket, and the ball may hit or land near the two men.
