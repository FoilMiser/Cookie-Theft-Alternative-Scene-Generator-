# Picture 59

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park, with a bright sun in the top left, two small white clouds, and green rolling hills and a line of trees in the background. An older man in a grey flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat with braces and dark trousers stands on the left edge of a winding sandy path, holding a camera up to his eye and taking a picture towards the right. In the middle of the grass a brown dog with a red collar is crouched in a "play bow", front legs stretched out and rear end up, with its nose right next to a yellow-green tennis ball; little marks by its tail show it is wagging. The dog seems to want someone to throw the ball, but nobody else is in the park. Behind them is an empty wooden bench, a tall black lamp post with a green bin and a small bush beside it, and a blue pond with reeds at each end. On the right there is a round bed of colourful flowers, another green bin in the bottom right corner, and a grey rock in the front middle. Trees and bushes are on both sides. Nothing looks clearly wrong or impossible; it is a calm scene.
