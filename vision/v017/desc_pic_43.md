# Picture 43

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with no people in it: a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence along the back with a gate at the left, and a concrete path in the front left. In the front centre, a red bird is pecking at the ground on the lawn. Just behind it, an orange cat is creeping low toward the bird, as if it is stalking it and about to pounce. The bird does not seem to notice the cat. Near the fence on the left there is a birdhouse on top of a tall pole, which appears empty. There are two potted plants and a bush on the lawn, a flower bed by the house, a hose reel next to the flower bed, and an empty wooden bench by the house. The main thing that seems about to happen is the cat catching or chasing the bird.
