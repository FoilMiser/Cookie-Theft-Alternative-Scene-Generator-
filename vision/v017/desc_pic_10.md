## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills, a pond on the left and a winding sandy path on the right. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and a grey skirt, is standing on the path happily eating a pink ice-cream cone. Behind her, near a tall black street lamp, a younger woman with dark hair in a yellow dress and red shoes is walking to the right carrying something yellow, probably a bag. A wooden ladder stands at the right-hand edge of the pond, tilted at an angle as if leaning against something, but there is nothing for it to lean on. It just stands up by itself, which is impossible. An empty wooden bench is next to the path. At the bottom left there is a flower bed with colourful flowers and a green rubbish bin. There are cattails/reeds at both ends of the pond, trees on the left and right, and bushes. Nobody is using the ladder, and nothing in the scene seems about to happen.
