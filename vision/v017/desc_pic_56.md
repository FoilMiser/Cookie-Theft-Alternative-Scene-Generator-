# Picture 56

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a park with green hills, trees and a pond with bulrushes in the background. At the front left, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, is kneeling on one knee beside a round bed of colourful flowers, reaching down to the soil as if planting or weeding; a pink tulip in a terracotta pot sits next to her, ready to be planted. In the middle front, a white goat with a curved dark horn has its head down and is eating a tuft of grass, which is a little unusual for a park. On the right, a young man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands on the winding sandy path holding a small white bag or carton and scattering seeds or crumbs, which fall in a trail to the ground. Two small birds, one red and one blue, are pecking at the seeds on the path near his feet. An empty wooden bench stands beside the path and a tall black lamp post stands in the middle of the park. Nothing looks dangerous; it is a quiet, pleasant scene.
