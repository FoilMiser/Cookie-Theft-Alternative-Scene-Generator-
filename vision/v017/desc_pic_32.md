# Picture 32

## Free description
This is a busy back garden on a sunny day. The house, with its blue door, is on the left, a wooden fence runs across the back, and there is a gate on the right. In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress stands with her back to us. She is reaching up to a washing line and pegging out clothes: blue jeans, a yellow towel with red stripes and a white T-shirt. To her right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers, with a dark ponytail, holds her hands out in front of her, ready to catch. A red-and-white ball is flying towards her hands. A dotted line shows that it was thrown by a small child with curly dark hair, a yellow shirt and orange shorts, standing on the path by the gate on the right. So she is about to catch it. At the front right, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is sweeping the path with a broom. On the far left, a brown-and-white dog sits by the blue door holding a red lead in its mouth, as if it wants to go for a walk. There is also a flower bed, a bush, an empty wheelbarrow and a teal watering can on the grass. Nothing looks clearly impossible.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The dog with the lead in its mouth probably wants a walk. It could also just be carrying or holding the lead.
- The woman in yellow could be hanging the white T-shirt up or taking it down. She is reaching up to it with both hands.
- The child could be a boy or a girl. The dotted line makes it look as though the child threw the ball, but they could be playing catch and the ball might be in any part of a back-and-forth.
- Nothing looks unintentionally strange. The yellow woman's head overlaps the towel, but she is simply standing in front of the line.
