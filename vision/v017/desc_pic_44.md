# Picture 44

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence along the back with a gate on the left, and a concrete path in the front left. A woman with dark hair in a bun, wearing a yellow dress and red shoes, stands at the fence painting it blue with a brush. So far only a short section of about three fence boards has been painted blue, while the rest of the fence is still brown. A can of blue paint sits on the grass behind her feet. In the front of the garden there is an empty grey wheelbarrow lying on the grass and a teal watering can. Two potted plants stand on the lawn near the fence, and a flower bed with colourful flowers is by the house. Nothing else is happening; it is a quiet garden scene with one person working. The fence being half blue and half brown looks unfinished rather than wrong.
