# Picture 04

## Free description
This is a back garden under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds, so rain looks like it is on the way. The house (cream with a red roof and blue door) is on the right, and the gate and paved path are on the left. An older man in a cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands on the path at front left. He points up at the dark clouds, wide-eyed and with his mouth open, as if warning that it is going to rain. In the middle, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers walks a white dog with brown spots to the right on a red lead. On the right, a man or boy in a red shirt and jeans is watering the flower bed with a teal watering can, even though it is about to rain. Something odd is happening with the water: a blue stream curves upward out of the watering can in an arc, with drops at the top, instead of pouring down. A yellow striped item and a pair of jeans hang on a washing line in the middle, and they may get wet. A red dog bowl of food sits on the grass near the path, and a hose reel stands beside the flower bed.
