# Picture 41

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence and gate on the left, and a concrete path in the front left. An old man with grey hair, a cap, white shirt, brown vest and dark trousers is holding the nozzle of a garden hose and looking at it, but only a single drop of water is coming out. The reason seems to be that a white goat is standing on the hose in the middle of the lawn; the hose is swollen into a big bulge on the side between the goat and the hose reel, as if the water is blocked. Behind the man is a clothesline between two posts with a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. On the left is a metal swing set with an empty red swing, and a teal watering can sits on the grass in front of it. On the right, an old woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, sits on a bench reading a red book, not noticing. There is a flower bed by the house and a hose reel next to it. The odd things are the goat in a back garden and the bulging, blocked hose.

- Fence, tree, bush, hedge (background); sun (top left) and two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The bulge in the hose: it could be water building up because the goat is pinching the hose, or it could be a hose fitting/connector drawn large. It looks like a swelling.
- It is not certain the man has noticed the goat; he looks at the nozzle, not at the goat.
- The striped item on the line could be a towel or a shirt.
- The goat appears to be standing calmly on the hose; whether it is doing it on purpose is unclear.
