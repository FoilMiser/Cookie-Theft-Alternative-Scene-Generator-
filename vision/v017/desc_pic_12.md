## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back, and a paved path to a wooden gate on the left. A man with a short beard, in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers, is digging a hole with a shovel near the fence; there is a mound of soil beside the hole. In front of the house, a girl or young woman with a brown ponytail, wearing a blue hoodie and dark trousers, is kneeling by a colourful flower bed and planting a pink flower in a small pot. At the front, a lawn sprinkler connected to a hose is spraying arcs of water. The water is landing on a cardboard box sitting on the grass instead of on plants or the lawn. The box is getting soaked: water is running down its sides and a puddle has formed underneath, so it looks like it will be ruined. Nobody seems to have noticed. There are also two potted plants near the fence on the left and a teal watering can at the front left.
