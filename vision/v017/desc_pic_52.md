# Picture 52

## Free description
It is a sunny, windy day in a back garden. A house with a red roof and a blue door stands on the left, and a tall wooden fence runs across the back of the garden with a wooden gate at the right end and a paved path leading up to it. A strong wind is blowing, shown by white swoosh lines in the sky and across the middle of the picture. On a washing line strung between two posts, a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes are pegged up and are being blown sideways by the wind. A young man in a blue hoodie and dark trousers stands on the grass at the right with both arms raised and his mouth open in surprise, looking up at his orange cap, which the wind has just blown off and is carrying away along with a few leaves. He seems to be trying to grab it, but it is already above and away from him. A black-and-white cat is walking along the top of the fence. Near the house there is a round bed of colourful flowers and a hose reel on the grass. Something impossible is going on in the tree behind the house: an orange fish is sitting in the leafy part of the tree.

## Inventory
- Young man (right of centre, front): brown hair, blue hoodie, dark trousers, white shoes; arms raised above his head, mouth open, looking up/left at his flying cap, apparently trying to catch it.
- Cat (centre-right, back, on top of fence): black with white chest and paws; walking along the top edge of the fence.
- Fish (left, back, in the tree canopy): orange fish with stripes; sitting/floating among the leaves of the tree.
- Orange cap: in the air just above and to the left of the young man, tumbling away in the wind with a couple of green/brown leaves.
- Washing line (centre, back): rope between two wooden posts, with blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel pegged on it, both blown out sideways to the left by the wind.
- Wind lines: white swooshes in the upper-left sky, above the washing line, and beside the flying cap, showing the wind blowing from right to left.
- House (left): cream walls, red roof, a window and a blue front door with a step.
- Flower bed (left-centre, front): round bed of soil with red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers.
- Hose reel (left, front): teal coiled garden hose on a black stand, sitting on the grass beside the flower bed.
- Bush (left, beside the house): small round green bush.
- Tree (left, behind the house): large green tree with a brown trunk.
- Fence and gate (back, full width): wooden picket fence with a brown wooden gate at the right end.
- Path (right, front): pale paved path running from the bottom of the picture up to the gate.
- Sky: sun in the top right, two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fish in the tree is clearly impossible; it is not clear whether it is meant to be a real fish or something like a toy or kite, but it is drawn like a normal fish with no string or support.
- The young man's raised arms could be read either as reaching to catch the cap or as throwing his hands up in surprise/dismay.
- The hose reel is not connected to a tap or anything else; it just stands on the grass.
- The cap and leaves float in the air, which is intended by the wind, but the leaves are not obviously coming from any tree near the man.
