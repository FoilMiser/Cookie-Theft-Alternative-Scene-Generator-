# Picture 39

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left and a wooden fence with a gate at the back right. A young person with brown hair, in a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes, sits on a wooden bench in the front left, drinking from a small white cup or carton. In the middle of the lawn there is a flower bed with colourful flowers, and two potted plants stand on the grass further back. On the right, a man with a beard, a green T-shirt and khaki trousers is raking leaves on the concrete path, with a small pile of green and brown leaves at his feet. A teal watering can sits on the grass near the path. A small orange cat is curled up asleep on the grass near the front centre. Nothing dramatic seems about to happen; it is a calm garden scene. The cat is drawn very small compared with the people.
