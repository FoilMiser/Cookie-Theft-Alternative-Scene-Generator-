# Picture 27

## Free description
This is a busy town square or high street on a sunny day, with a row of colourful shops (pink, green, peach and cream) behind a wide paved area. On the left, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is running to the left past a fruit stall that has crates of yellow, orange and red fruit. In the middle, a bearded man in a green shirt walks along reading an open book, and he is about to walk right into a red bucket of water on the ground in front of him. Nearby, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts holds a yellow and a blue balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other toward a purple balloon that has floated away. A woman in a yellow dress is sweeping in front of a shop door. On the right, a man in a blue hoodie stands with both arms raised looking up, frustrated, at a blue ball stuck in a tree. At the far right, an older woman with grey hair and glasses scatters seed from a bag for a small blue bird pecking at the ground. In the front, a little girl in a pink dress is crying and holding an empty ice cream cone, while a brown dog eats her pink ice cream off the ground. Something impossible is happening too: a white dog sits upright in front of the bench, holding open a newspaper and reading it like a person. A red bird is flying in the sky near the sun.
