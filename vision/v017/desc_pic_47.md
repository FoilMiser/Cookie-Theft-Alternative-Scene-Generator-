# Picture 47

## Free description
This is the same kind of back garden, with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence and a gate on the right. This time the sky is grey and there are three dark storm clouds overhead, so it looks as though it is about to rain. An old man with a grey beard and a cap is standing by the flower bed watering the flowers. Oddly, he is pouring the water from a frying pan rather than a watering can. Further back, a young woman with a brown ponytail and a blue hoodie is painting the fence blue with a long-handled brush. A section of the fence to her left is already blue. Her paint tin and a paint tray of blue paint sit on the grass at her feet. Next to her, a woman in a yellow dress is pointing up at the dark clouds with her mouth open, looking alarmed. It seems the rain will spoil the fresh paint, and it makes the watering pointless. A white dog with brown spots has walked through the paint tray and is trotting off to the right, leaving a trail of blue paw prints on the grass. An empty wooden bench is in the front left, and a red dog bowl with food is at the bottom.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The man's watering tool looks like a frying pan (round, shallow, with a long straight handle). It could possibly be read as a saucepan or skillet, but it is clearly not a watering can, which is strange.
- The woman in yellow could be pointing up because she has seen the storm coming, or she could be pointing at something else in the sky. The dark clouds are the only thing up there.
- The painter could be holding a long-handled brush or a small paint roller on a pole. The tip is a small blue blob.
- The dog has clearly stepped in the paint, but nobody seems to have noticed yet.
- It looks odd to water flowers and paint a fence just before a storm, but the picture seems to show that on purpose.
- Nothing looks like it is floating or passing through anything else.
