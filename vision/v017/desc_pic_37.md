# Picture 37

## Free description
This is a quiet, sunny back garden. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, a wooden fence runs across the back, and a gate and paved path are on the right. A woman with a dark ponytail, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, stands at the front left of centre beside a flower bed. She is watering the flowers with a teal watering can, and water sprinkles down out of the spout onto the bed. Behind her, a washing line between two wooden posts has blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes pegged on it. On the right there is a tall pole with a small red-roofed birdhouse on top, and two potted plants on the grass in front of the fence. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits at the front. There is a tree behind the house and the sun is in the top right. She is the only person, there are no animals, and nothing looks wrong or impossible.

## Inventory
- Woman (left of centre, front, beside the flower bed): dark hair in a ponytail, purple top, teal trousers, dark shoes. She is watering the flowers with a watering can.
- Watering can (left of centre, in the woman's hand): teal and tipped forward. Water sprinkles out of the spout.
- Water (left of centre): light blue drops falling onto the front edge of the flower bed.
- Flower bed (left, in front of the house): red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers in brown soil.
- Washing line (centre, back): two wooden posts and a line, with blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes hanging up.
- Birdhouse on a pole (right, back): a small wooden birdhouse with a red roof and a round hole, on a tall pole.
- Two potted plants (right, by the fence): green plants in terracotta pots.
- Wheelbarrow (front centre-right): grey and empty, with wooden handles pointing right.
- Gate and path (right): a wooden gate in the fence, with a paved path running from it to the bottom right.
- House (left): cream walls, red roof, window, blue door. A green bush stands beside it.
- Tree (back left, behind the house) and the sun and two clouds (sky).
