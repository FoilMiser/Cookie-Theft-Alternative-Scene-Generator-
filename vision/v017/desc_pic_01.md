# Picture 01

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden. A cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the left, and a wooden fence with a gate runs across the back. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on a red swing on a metal swing set on the right. He is smiling with his eyes shut, and small lines beside the swing suggest it is moving. In the front left, a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is crouched down in a play pose with its nose and paws at a yellow-green tennis ball. Its tail is up, with wag lines. A red dog bowl full of food sits on the grass in front of the swing set. There is a flower bed with mixed colourful flowers near the house, and a paved path runs from the gate down to the front right. The odd thing is a closed cardboard box hanging in mid-air in the sky above the fence. Nobody is holding or throwing it, so it is either flying or falling, or it is floating for no reason.
