# Picture 48

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park, with a yellow sun at top left, two white clouds and a dark metal railing fence with hedges behind it. On the left, a girl in a pink dress is partway down a slide that has a red frame and a yellow-and-orange chute. In the front centre, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is holding a blue balloon and a purple balloon in one hand. He is reaching up with his other hand towards a red balloon that has slipped away and is floating off with its string dangling. He looks upset. On the right, two women are playing catch. The woman in the blue hoodie on the far right has just thrown a red ball, shown by a dotted line through the air. The woman in the yellow dress has her hands up ready to catch it. A small orange cat is curled up asleep on the grass just in front of the two women. A large tree stands on the far right, and a grey rock, a bush and a green rubbish bin are around the edges. Apart from the escaping balloon, nothing looks wrong.
