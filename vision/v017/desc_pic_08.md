# Picture 08

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the house (cream with a red roof and blue door) on the right, and the wooden gate and paved path on the left. In the middle, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt holds a garden hose nozzle up in front of her face and peers into it. Only a single drop is coming out. The reason seems to be the white dog with brown spots standing on the hose behind her, pinning it down with its back paw. The hose is swollen into a bulge on the far side of the dog, between the dog and the hose reel by the house. It looks like the water will burst out into the woman's face as soon as the dog steps off. On the left, a delivery man in an orange cap, an orange hi-vis vest and blue trousers walks across the grass toward the house carrying a cardboard parcel. There is also a bird house on a pole by the fence, a flower bed, an empty bench by the house, a teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow at the front.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The dog could be deliberately standing on the hose or might not know it is there. Either way its paw seems to be blocking the water.
- The bulge in the hose suggests trapped water building up. It could also be read as just a thicker section or a loop of hose.
- The woman could be checking why no water is coming out, or trying to adjust or unblock the nozzle. Her face is close to it, so she may get sprayed.
- The delivery man is walking on the grass rather than the path. He may be heading to the house door, or just passing through.
