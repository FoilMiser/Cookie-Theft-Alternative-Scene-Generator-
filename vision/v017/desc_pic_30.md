# Picture 30

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a closed gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence blue with a paintbrush. A section of the fence on the right is already painted, and a tin of blue paint sits on the grass by her feet. In the middle there is a washing line between two wooden posts, with a yellow striped towel and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. A white goat with small horns is in the front centre, bending down to eat a tuft of grass. On the left there is a swing set with an empty red swing, and a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow are on the grass at the front. There is a flower bed and an empty wooden bench near the house. Something impossible is in the tree behind the fence: an orange fish is sitting among the leaves, as if a fish were living in the tree.

## Inventory
- Woman: right-centre, back (by the fence); long dark hair, purple top, teal trousers, dark shoes; standing and painting the fence blue with a paintbrush.
- Goat: centre, front; white goat with small dark horns and dark hooves; head down, eating a tuft of grass.
- Fish: upper right, in the tree; orange fish; sitting in the tree's leaves, which a fish couldn't do.
- Paint tin: centre-right, by the woman's feet; grey tin with blue paint.
- Paintbrush: in the woman's hand; blue paint on the tip, touching the fence.
- Painted fence section: right, behind the woman; several fence boards painted blue.
- Washing line: centre; rope between two wooden posts; holding a yellow towel with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans, both pegged up to dry.
- Swing set: left; dark grey A-frame with one red swing seat; empty and still.
- Watering can: bottom left, near the path; teal watering can on the grass.
- Wheelbarrow: bottom centre; grey with black wheel; empty, resting on the grass.
- Flower bed: right, middle; round bed of yellow, orange, red, pink and purple flowers.
- Bench: bottom right; empty wooden bench.
- House: right edge; cream walls, red roof, window, blue door with step.
- Gate and path: far left; closed wooden gate and pale paved path.
- Tree: upper right, behind the fence; the fish is in it.
- Sun and clouds: sun top left; two white clouds at the top.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fish in the tree is clearly impossible. It could be read as a real fish stuck in the tree or as a fish-shaped decoration hanging there, but nothing is holding it up.
- The white animal is most likely a goat because of its horns, but it could be read as a lamb or kid. Either way, it is unusual to see it in a back garden.
- The towel's stripes could be red bands on yellow or a striped tea towel. The hanging items are clear enough.
- Nobody is near the swing, watering can or wheelbarrow. They are just lying around the garden.
