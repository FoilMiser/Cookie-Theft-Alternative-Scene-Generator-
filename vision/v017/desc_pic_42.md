# Picture 42

## Free description
This is a sunny park with a winding path on the left, a lamp post in the middle, a pond on the right and green hills behind. A child with dark curly hair, a yellow top and orange shorts stands on tiptoe near the lamp post, reaching up and holding a kite string, but the purple kite is stuck in the branches of the big tree on the left. A brown-and-white puppy lies asleep on the path in the front left. There is an empty park bench beside the path. A man in an orange cap, orange hi-vis vest and blue jeans walks across the grass carrying a cardboard box. On the right, by the pond, an old woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, is digging a hole in the park lawn with a shovel, with a mound of dirt beside the hole. The odd things are the kite stuck in the tree and an elderly woman digging a hole in the middle of a park lawn.
