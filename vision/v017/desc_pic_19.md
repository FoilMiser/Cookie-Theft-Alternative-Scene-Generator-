# Picture 19

## Free description
This is a sunny town square in front of a row of colourful shops (cream, peach, pale green and pink buildings) with striped awnings. In the middle, a little girl in a pink dress is holding the strings of a yellow and a red balloon in one hand while reaching up with her other hand toward a purple balloon that has come loose and is floating away; she looks worried. To the right, an older man in a grey cap and brown vest is bending slightly and sprinkling seeds or crumbs from his hand, and a small red bird on the ground is pecking at them. On the far right, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is waving with one arm raised high, with little motion lines around his hand. A blue bird is flying across the sky at the top left. There is a fruit stall on the right with crates of red, orange, yellow and green fruit, a street lamp, a tree in a planter on the left, a green rubbish bin, an empty café table with two chairs, and a potted plant in the bottom-left corner. Nothing looks impossible; the main thing that is about to happen is the purple balloon floating away.
