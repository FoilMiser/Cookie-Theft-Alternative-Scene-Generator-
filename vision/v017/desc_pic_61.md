# Picture 61

## Free description
This is a busy back garden on a sunny day, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, a clothesline with blue jeans and a yellow striped towel hanging on it, and a gate with a path on the right. A woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench at the front left reading a red book, and does not notice that a blue bird on the bench is pecking at her big sandwich. A girl in a blue hoodie with a ponytail is holding the end of a garden hose and looking straight into the nozzle, but no water comes out because a brown dog is standing on the hose. The hose has a big swollen bulge just behind the dog's feet, so when the dog steps off, the water will probably spray the girl in the face. A grey-haired older woman is painting the fence blue with a long-handled brush; the part next to the house is already blue. In the middle, a person in a green shirt walks along reading a book and is about to step on or trip over a red ball on the grass. On the right, a man in a red shirt is raking leaves near the gate, while a white dog with dark spots crouches beside a tennis ball, wagging its tail and wanting to play. At the front right, an older man in a cap is reading a red book in a sitting position, but there is no chair under him, which is impossible. Beside him a boy in yellow and orange jumps in the air with his arms up, cheering. Strangest of all, there is an orange fish in the leaves of the tree behind the house, and a red dog food bowl lies on its own on the grass at the front.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The orange fish in the tree is impossible; it looks deliberately out of place.
- The older man on the right is sitting in mid-air with no chair or bench under him; this is impossible (or there is a chair that has been left out of the picture).
- The person in the green shirt could be a man or a woman; the hair is tied back in a bun.
- The boy is clearly off the ground (shadow below him); this reads as jumping for joy rather than floating.
- The hose bulge could be read as water built up behind the dog's paws (the usual reading) or just a thick part of the hose; together with the girl peering into the dry nozzle, the first reading seems more likely.
- The dog bowl is on its own in the middle of the grass, away from both dogs; not wrong, just a bit odd.
- The bird could be eating the sandwich or about to fly off with it.
