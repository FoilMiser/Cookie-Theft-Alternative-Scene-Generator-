# Picture 36

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence at the back, a gate and path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A tall pole with a little red-roofed birdhouse on top stands near the gate. On the left, a girl with a brown ponytail, a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white trainers has just kicked an orange ball. Motion lines show the ball moving left towards the path. In the middle at the back, an older man in a flat cap, a white shirt, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers is raking a small pile of leaves by the fence. On the right, a woman with a dark ponytail, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, is watering the flower bed. Oddly, she is not using a watering can. She is pouring water from what looks like a dark frying pan or saucepan held by its handle. That is a strange thing to water flowers with. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits at the front. Everyone seems busy with their own activity.
