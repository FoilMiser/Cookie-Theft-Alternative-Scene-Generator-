The kite is high in the sky to the left, with a tail of little red and blue bows. A woman with a brown ponytail, blue hoodie, black trousers and white trainers is watering a colourful flower bed with a green watering can, and water is pouring onto the flowers. On the far right, in front of a cream house with a red roof and a blue door, an older man in a grey-blue flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat sits on a wooden bench drinking from a white mug. A grey wheelbarrow is parked on the grass at the bottom centre. There are two potted plants and a round bush along the fence on the left, and a tree shows above the fence on the right. Everyone is calmly doing their own thing, and I could not see anything wrong or impossible.
