# Picture 62

## Free description
This is a park on a sunny day, with the sun in the top left, two white clouds, green hills and trees in the background, a winding sandy path on the left, an empty bench, a tall black lamp post and a blue pond on the right. A woman in a yellow dress with dark hair and red shoes stands near the lamp post, throwing small brown crumbs of bread towards the pond from a white bag or cup in her hand. A white duck is rushing across the water towards the crumbs, with speed lines behind it, and some crumbs are already floating on the water. In the front, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is pushing a teal and navy pram with a sleeping baby under a pink spotted blanket, walking to the left while looking towards the pond. Strangely, a brown cardboard box is hanging in the air above the path near the big tree on the left, with a shadow on the ground under it, as if it is floating or falling. There is also a flower bed and a green bin on the right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The cardboard box in the air is the odd part: it could be floating (impossible) or falling/thrown and caught mid-fall. Nobody is near it, and its shadow on the grass suggests it is meant to be above the ground.
- What the woman is holding could be a paper bag of bread or a white cup.
- The man could be looking at the duck/woman as he walks, or just looking ahead in the direction he is walking (left); his head is turned left towards the pond.
- The duck's lines could be speed lines (rushing for food) or ripples/wake in the water.
