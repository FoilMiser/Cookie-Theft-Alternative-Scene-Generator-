# Picture 35

## Free description
This is a back garden on a sunny but very windy day. White wind lines blow across the picture and leaves are being blown off the tree near the house. On the left, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands on the path with both arms up and a surprised, worried face. His orange cap has just blown off his head and is flying away over the gate. Near the middle, a woman in a yellow dress stands with her back to us, painting the wooden fence blue with a paintbrush. One section of the fence is already blue. A paint tin and a paint tray with blue paint sit on the grass beside her. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has stepped in the blue paint. It is walking away across the lawn and leaving a trail of blue paw prints, and the woman does not seem to have noticed. A black-and-white cat walks along the top of the fence. On the washing line, a yellow striped towel and blue jeans are being blown almost sideways by the wind. A red dog bowl with food sits on the grass at the front, and there is a flower bed and a house with a blue door on the right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The man's reaction could be read two ways: he could be reaching up to grab his cap, or throwing his hands up in surprise. His cap is already past him, over the gate.
- The leaves near the man could be blowing from the tree or from somewhere else. They are small green or brown specks.
- The cat on the fence could be about to jump down or just walking along. It doesn't seem to be doing anything else.
- The clothes on the washing line are drawn slanted, almost flat sideways, from the wind. They look a little stiff or unnatural, but this is probably meant to show the wind.
- The woman painting may not have noticed the dog walking through her paint. The dog has blue feet and is leaving prints across the lawn.
