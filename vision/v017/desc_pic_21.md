# Picture 21

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a house (red roof, blue door) on the right, a wooden fence and gate across the back, and a paved path down the left side. In the middle, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts has just thrown a red ball, shown by a dotted line arcing from his hand, toward a woman in a yellow dress who is holding out her hands ready to catch it. On the left, a woman (or older girl) in a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping the path with a broom. A blue bird is perched on top of the fence above the ball game. In the front of the picture, a garden sprinkler attached to a long hose is spraying arcs of water, but the water is landing on a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is getting soaked and has a puddle and drips around it. There is also an empty swing set with a red seat, a teal watering can on the grass, a birdhouse on a pole near the gate, and a flower bed of colourful flowers by the house. The odd thing is the sprinkler wetting a cardboard box instead of the plants.
