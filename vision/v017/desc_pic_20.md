# Picture 20

## Free description
This is a sunny but windy back garden: white wind streaks are drawn in the sky and across the middle of the picture. A house with a red roof and blue door is on the right, and a tall wooden fence with a gate runs across the back. In the centre, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, has both arms raised and her mouth open in surprise as a red cap is blown up and away from her by the wind, along with some small leaves. On the left, on a paved path, a boy in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is sweeping a small pile of dirt into a red dustpan, but he is using what looks like a giant silver dinner fork instead of a broom, which is odd; a little puff of dust rises from the pile. A small white puppy with a brown ear is curled up asleep on the lawn. On the right there is a flower bed full of colourful flowers and a hose reel next to the house. The woman is about to lose her cap unless she can catch it.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The boy's sweeping tool: it looks like an oversized eating fork (silver, flat handle, four prongs), which would be an absurd tool to sweep with. Another reading is that it is a garden fork or rake, but its shape and colour look like cutlery rather than a garden tool.
- The cap is generally read as the woman's, blown off her head; it could also be someone else's cap blowing past that she is trying to catch.
- The puppy could be sleeping or just lying still; its eyes appear closed.
- The cap and leaves are in mid-air, but that is explained by the wind rather than being a drawing error.
