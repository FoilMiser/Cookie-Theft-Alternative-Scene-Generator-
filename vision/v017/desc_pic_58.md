# Picture 58

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a park with green hills and trees behind. A bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands on the grass beside a pond on the left, fishing; his line runs down to a red-and-white float bobbing in the water with ripples around it. A white duck is swimming at the far side of the pond, and bulrushes grow at its edges. A red bird is perched on top of a tall black lamp post in the middle, and there is a green rubbish bin next to the lamp post and another in the bottom-left corner. In the front, a child with dark curly hair, a yellow top and orange shorts is crying and rubbing one eye while holding an empty ice-cream cone; the pink ice cream has fallen on the grass, and a brown dog with a red collar has its nose down at it, sniffing or eating it. A winding sandy path runs off into the distance on the right, past a big tree. In the bottom-right corner there is a wheelbarrow, but its wheel is drawn as a square with spokes, which would not roll — that looks wrong. A round bed of colourful flowers is at the bottom left.
