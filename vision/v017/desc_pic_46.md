# Picture 46

## Free description
This is a back garden on a sunny day, with a bright sun at top right and two white clouds. On the left is the side of a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue back door with a grey doorstep. A tree rises behind the house. A tall wooden picket fence runs across the back of the garden, with a wooden gate at the right end. A paved path leads from the gate down to the bottom right corner. The only living creature is a tan-and-white dog sitting in front of the blue door. It holds a red lead in its mouth, and the lead trails down to the grass, as if the dog is waiting to be taken for a walk. Next to the dog is a teal hose reel, and beside that is a round flower bed full of red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers. Two potted plants stand by the fence on the right. A grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can sit on the lawn in front. There are no people in the picture, and nothing looks wrong or impossible.
