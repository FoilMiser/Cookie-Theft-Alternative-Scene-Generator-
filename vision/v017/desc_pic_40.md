# Picture 40

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny park with a pond on the left, a winding path on the right, a lamp post in the middle and green hills behind. A woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers stands by the pond throwing bread crumbs to a white duck, which is swimming toward the food. In front of her, an old man with a grey beard, cap, brown vest and dark trousers is holding out a frying pan and pouring water from it onto a flower bed, as if the pan were a watering can. Right next to him a white goat with small horns is eating a tuft of grass. A man in a red T-shirt and jeans walks across the grass at the back carrying a yellow bag. On a bench at the right, a bearded man in a green shirt is eating a pink ice-cream cone while a brown-and-white dog with a red bandana jumps up at him, clearly about to try to get the ice cream. On the far right, a girl in a blue top is holding a kite string, but her purple kite is stuck in the branches of a tree. In the front, a lawn sprinkler is spraying water onto a cardboard box that is standing in a puddle, so the box is getting soaked. A small girl in a pink dress stands on the path in the back with one arm raised, and a red bird flies in the sky at top left. Odd things include watering flowers with a frying pan, a goat in a city park, the sprinkler soaking the box, and the kite stuck in the tree.
