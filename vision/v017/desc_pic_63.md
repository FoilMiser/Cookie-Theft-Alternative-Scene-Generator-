# Picture 63

## Free description
This is a busy park on a sunny day, with the sun in the top left, a winding sandy path, a bench, a tall lamp post, a pond, a flower bed, trees, bushes and two green bins. At the far end of the path a park worker in an orange cap, orange high-visibility vest and blue overalls is sweeping with a yellow broom. On the bench a girl in a pink dress is sitting and drinking from a small white cup. At the front left a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is crying and rubbing his eye, holding an empty ice-cream cone, because his pink sprinkled ice cream has fallen on the ground and a brown dog with a red collar is eating it. In the middle a girl in a blue hoodie with a ponytail is walking along reading an open book and is about to trip over a red ball on the grass right in front of her. On the right a woman in a yellow dress is holding a teal watering can over the flower bed, but the water is shooting upwards in an arc into the air instead of pouring down onto the flowers, which is impossible. On the far right a small orange cat seems to be lying asleep on the grass.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water from the watering can goes up into the air instead of down; it could be read as water defying gravity (impossible) or, less likely, as a jet of water coming up from somewhere towards the can. Either way it looks wrong.
- The small orange animal on the right is most likely a sleeping cat, but it is small and could also be a kitten, a fox cub or a toy.
- The girl on the bench could be drinking from a cup, a juice carton or eating something white.
- The boy's hand to his face could be wiping tears (crying) or rubbing his eye; with the dropped ice cream, crying seems most likely.
- It is not certain the boy's ice cream is the one on the ground, but the empty cone and his upset look strongly suggest it.
