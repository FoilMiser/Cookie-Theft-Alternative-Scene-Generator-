# Picture 38

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, and a tall wooden fence with a gate across the back. A brown-and-white dog sits by the blue door holding its red leash in its mouth, with the rest of the leash looped on the ground, as if it wants to go for a walk. In the middle of the lawn a young man in an orange cap, orange hi-vis vest and blue jeans is digging a hole in the grass with a shovel; there is a pile of dirt next to the hole and dirt on the shovel blade. Behind him is a metal swing set with one empty red swing. On the right, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers stands on the concrete path and is sweeping dirt and gravel toward a red dustpan, but instead of a broom she is using what looks like a giant dinner fork. A small cloud of dust rises where she is sweeping. There is also a flower bed with colourful flowers near the house, a tree behind the house, and a bush by the door. The odd thing is the woman sweeping with an oversized fork.

- The person digging could be read as a teenage boy or a young adult worker (the hi-vis vest suggests a worker).
