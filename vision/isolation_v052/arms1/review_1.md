# Review: pair_1.png (upper arms, shoulders, sleeves)

## 1. How the rows differ

Row a draws thinner upper arms and narrower, more sloped shoulders, so the sleeves look like tubes stuck onto the torso. Row b draws thicker sleeves and rounder, broader shoulders, so the arms look like part of the garment and the figures look sturdier. The difference is clearest on the older woman and the older man and smallest on the side-view teenager.

## 2. Per figure, left to right

1. **Teenager waving: b.** In b the raised sleeve rises out of a fuller shoulder and the hanging arm is a proper sleeve width down to the cuff, where in a the hanging arm is thin and the raised arm meets the torso at a pinched point.
2. **Older woman reaching up: b.** In a the raised arm is a narrow, spindly tube that tapers sharply from a slight shoulder, while in b the cardigan sleeve has believable width and the shoulders read as rounded.
3. **Older man sweeping: b.** In a the near shoulder pokes out as a small pointed bump above the vest and the upper arms are narrow, while in b the shirt shoulder is a smooth round curve and the sleeves have a consistent width.
4. **Teenager with arms out, from the side: no difference.** Both rows attach two straight, tube-like arms in front of a large hood lump; b's arms are thicker but its shoulder ball is bulkier, so neither is clearly better.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Teenager waving: the hanging arm is thin for a hoodie sleeve; the raised arm narrows where it meets the shoulder, so the joint looks pinched; a rounded cap edge shows inside the torso at the armpit.
- Older woman: the raised arm is very thin and tapers almost to wrist width at the top, like a stick; the shoulders are narrow and sloped for a cardigan.
- Older man: the near shoulder has a small pointed bump sticking above the vest line; the upper arms are narrow next to the bulky vest.
- Teenager arms out: the arms are thin tubes that start from the front of the chest, not from a shoulder; the far arm and near arm overlap with little sense of which shoulder each comes from.

### Row b
- Teenager waving: the hood or shoulder mass behind the neck is a large lump that crowds the raised arm.
- Older woman: the hanging arm is puffy, close to a balloon shape around the elbow; the raised sleeve is wide enough to look slightly heavy at the shoulder.
- Older man: the sleeves are a little wide for a thin older man; otherwise clean.
- Teenager arms out: the shoulder is a big round ball that bulges out of the side of the torso; the arms are still straight tubes; a stray horizontal line crosses the torso below the arms.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- Teenager arms out: the hood lump behind the neck is very large and reads more like a growth on the back of the neck than a hood.
- Teenager waving: the waving hand is a flat mitten with spread fingers that is large next to the head.
- Older man: the hand gripping the top of the broom handle sits on the handle like a ball, and the handle passes over the hand rather than through the grip.
- At picture size (the small figures) the two rows are nearly indistinguishable; b's arms read slightly more clearly, a's slightly more spindly.

## 4. Choice

**Row b**, fairly sure. Its rounder shoulders and fuller sleeves look friendlier and read more clearly as clothed arms; its faults (some puffiness, a bulky shoulder ball on the side-view teen) are milder than row a's spindly, pinched arms.
