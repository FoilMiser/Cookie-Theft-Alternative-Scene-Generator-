# Review of pair_10 (upper arms, shoulders, sleeves)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the tops look sleeveless. The bare upper arm starts right at the shoulder as a rounded skin-coloured lump laid over the edge of the garment, with only faint seam lines on the cloth. In row b each figure has a short sleeve in the garment colour that covers the shoulder and ends in a visible hem, and the bare arm comes out below that hem. Row b's shoulders are a little wider and squarer.

## 2. Per figure

1. **Woman, arms held out (yellow):** b. In b a yellow cap sleeve with a hem caps the shoulder and the arms come out of it cleanly. In a the bare arm lump sits on the front of the dress with nothing joining it to the torso.
2. **Man sweeping (green):** b, slightly. In b the green sleeve comes further down the near upper arm and also covers the far shoulder near the broom, so the arm clearly belongs to the shirt. In a the brown arm starts high and looks pasted onto the shirt front, but the difference is smaller than in the other figures.
3. **Woman who has just thrown (purple):** b. In b the raised arm comes out of a purple sleeve at the shoulder. In a a large bare skin bulge stands out past the shirt outline and reads as a swollen shoulder.
4. **Girl pushing (pink):** b. In b a pink sleeve with a hem covers the shoulder and both arms come out of it. In a the bare upper arms bulge out over the dress with crease lines and look stuck on.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Woman (yellow): the upper arm starts as a bare skin lump over the dress front, with no sleeve or armhole. The seam lines across the chest lead nowhere.
- Man (green): the near upper arm overlaps the shirt high up with no sleeve edge, so the shoulder joint is unclear. The far arm also shows bare skin right at the shoulder next to the broom.
- Thrower (purple): a big rounded skin bulge at the raised shoulder pushes outside the shirt silhouette, and the shirt reads as sleeveless. The back arm comes out from behind the torso with no visible shoulder.
- Girl (pink): the bare upper arms swell over the dress at the shoulder, and the crease lines under them look like wrinkles in the skin rather than the cloth.

### Row b
- Woman (yellow): the cap sleeve sticks out a little stiffly and boxy from the shoulder. That is minor.
- Man (green): the sleeve hem on the near arm is hard to make out against the arm, so the gain over a is small.
- Thrower (purple): the shoulder is broad and square, so the torso looks a bit wide and blocky. The back arm still has no sleeve showing and comes out from behind the hip area looking thick.
- Girl (pink): the sleeve top rises into a small peak next to the neck, which looks a little puffed or hunched.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- Woman (yellow): the hands are flat paw shapes stacked one over the other, with unclear fingers.
- Thrower (purple): the back hand is a large mitten-like blob. The raised hand tapers to a point with no fingers.
- Girl (pink): the two hands overlap into one lump at the end of the arms.
- Man: the hand at the top of the broom is drawn in front of the shirt and looks very large.
- At the small whole-figure size the difference between the rows is barely visible, especially for the girl.

## 4. Choice

**Row b**, fairly sure. Its sleeves clearly attach the arms to the clothing and avoid row a's bare, swollen shoulder lumps. That reads as friendlier and clearer in a cartoon style, even though the gain is small at picture size.
