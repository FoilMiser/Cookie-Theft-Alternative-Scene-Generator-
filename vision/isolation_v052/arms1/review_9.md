# Review of pair_9: upper arms, shoulders and sleeves

## 1. How the rows differ

Row a draws short, wide, flat-ended sleeves that flare out from square shoulders. Each sleeve ends in a straight outlined hem, and the thinner arm comes out from under it. Row b draws narrower, rounded, puffy cap sleeves on softer shoulders. The arm starts higher and thicker and overlaps the sleeve edge, and in several places there is no outline where the arm meets the sleeve.

## 2. Per figure (left to right)

1. Man, front: **a**. The flared sleeves are wide, but the hems are clean and outlined, while b leaves pale, unoutlined notches where the arms meet the sleeves.
2. Man, turned: **a**. In a both sleeves read as sleeves (the far one is a small tab), while in b the near arm meets its sleeve with a soft, unoutlined seam and the far arm comes straight off the body with no sleeve at all.
3. Man, side: **a**. The rectangular sleeve with a straight hem over the arm reads clearly, while in b the rounded top of the arm sits over the curved sleeve edge, so the arm looks like a cap pasted onto the shirt.
4. Woman, back: **b**. The rounded sleeves and narrower shoulders look more natural than a's wing-like flared sleeves, even with small seam notches.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Man, front: the sleeves flare out like wings, much wider than the arms, so the shoulders look very broad and square. A gap is left between each sleeve opening and the thin arm.
- Man, turned: the near sleeve flares well past the arm. The far sleeve is only a small, stiff tab on the right edge of the body.
- Man, side: the sleeve is a flat rectangle with no shoulder roundness, and the arm starts abruptly under the hem.
- Woman, back: same wing-like flared sleeves and boxy shoulders as the front man, which look stiff and oversized.

### Row b
- Man, front: there is no outline where the arm meets the sleeve. Pale, skin-coloured notches show at the seam on both sides, and the upper arms flare wide at the top as if stuck on.
- Man, turned: the near sleeve's seam with the arm is soft and unoutlined. The far arm has no sleeve: it starts straight from the side of the shirt.
- Man, side: the outlined top of the arm curves over the sleeve's hem, so the arm seems to lie in front of the sleeve rather than coming out of it.
- Woman, back: small unoutlined notches where the arms meet the sleeves. Otherwise this is the cleanest figure in row b.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- The turned and side men have a large, stiff mitten hand with a separate thumb loop that looks swollen at picture size.
- The woman's hair from behind is one dark block that covers the neck and upper back.

## 4. Choice

**Row a**, fairly sure. Its sleeves are exaggerated, but the outlines are closed and consistent, so it reads as a clean, friendly cartoon. Row b's softer shoulders are nicer in shape, but its missing seam outlines, missing far sleeve and pasted-on look are flaws a viewer can see.
