# Review of pair_6 (upper arms, shoulders, sleeves)

## 1. How the rows differ

Row a draws short, tight, puffed sleeves. They are rounded caps that hug the top of the arm, and the arm seems to come out from under a bump. Row b draws ordinary T-shirt sleeves. They flare a little away from the arm and end in a clear straight hem, and the arm comes out of an open sleeve. Row b's shoulders are also rounder and slope into the sleeve. Row a's shoulders are flatter and boxier: the child has a flat top line, and the man has an odd break at the near shoulder.

## 2. Per figure

1. **Child, front: b.** In b the shoulders slope into clear sleeves with a hem, and the arms come out of them. In a the shoulder line is flat and square, and the puffed caps sit on the arm like small pads.
2. **Woman in dress, turned: b.** In b the near sleeve is a clear short sleeve with a hem line. In a the near sleeve is a small puff with a crease line, and the arm looks attached at a seam rather than coming out of a sleeve.
3. **Girl, side: b.** In b the sleeve has a straight hem across the upper arm and reads as a sleeve. In a it is a rounded pink bump with the arm pushed up into it, so the join looks like a lump.
4. **Bearded man, turned: b.** In a the near arm's brown skin rises above the sleeve and is cut off by a straight diagonal edge against the green shirt, so that arm seems to have no sleeve and to sit on top of the shirt. In b both sleeves are drawn whole, with hems.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Child: the top of the shirt is a flat, squared line from neck to shoulder, which looks boxy. The puffed sleeve caps look like shoulder pads stuck onto the arms.
- Woman: the near sleeve is a small puff with a crease, and the arm meets it at a visible seam. The far shoulder and sleeve hardly show, so the far arm seems to come straight out of the bodice.
- Girl: the sleeve is a round bump with no hem, and the arm looks jammed up into it.
- Man: on the near arm, the skin runs up over where the sleeve should be and ends in a hard straight diagonal against the shirt, so the sleeve is missing and the arm looks pasted on. The far shoulder outline is lumpy.
- At picture size the puffed caps are hard to read as sleeves. The arms look as if they hang from small knobs.

### Row b
- Child: the sleeves stand a little wide of the arms, so the T-shirt looks slightly stiff and boxy at the sides.
- Woman: the far sleeve is only a small sliver at the back edge and is easy to miss.
- Man: the sleeves are broad and stiff, and the near shoulder makes the torso look very wide and heavy.
- Girl: no clear fault. The hem line sits a little high, but it reads well.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- Woman: the hand on the right, by her far side, is a cupped shape with a loop-like thumb. It looks unfinished.
- Child: the shorts have a small notch at the crotch that looks like a cut-out.
- Girl: the head is very large next to the narrow body, even for a cartoon. The hand at the bottom is a small, unclear lump.
- Man: the near hand is a plain mitten with a curl for a thumb, much like the woman's.

## 4. Choice

**Row b.** Its sleeves read at once as sleeves, the shoulders flow into the arms, and it has no missing-sleeve error like the man's in row a. That suits a friendly, clear cartoon style. **Sure.**
