# Review: pair_5 (upper arms, shoulders, sleeves)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the sleeves are slight. The raised arms come out of thin tube sleeves or almost bare shoulders, and the hanging arms have no sleeve edge, or only a small rounded cap. In row b every arm comes out of a short T-shirt sleeve with a clear hem line, so the shoulder reads as shirt and the upper arm reads as skin. The sleeves on the raised arms are also wider and flare a little at the cuff.

## 2. Per figure

1. **Man waving: b.** In b both arms have a visible red sleeve with a hem, while in a the raised arm comes almost straight out of the body like a tank top and the hanging arm has no sleeve at all.
2. **Woman reaching up: b.** In b the hanging arm has a clear square-hemmed short sleeve and the raised arm has a sleeve with a cuff, while in a the hanging-arm sleeve is a vague bump with a crease and the raised sleeve is a thin purple tube.
3. **Child pointing up, from the side: b.** In b the near arm hangs from a clear rectangular short sleeve, while in a the arm starts at a small rounded yellow cap that looks like a shoulder pad rather than a sleeve.
4. **Man with both arms raised: no difference.** b has a clearer cuff on the right sleeve, but its shoulders are boxier and the left shoulder has small notches, and a has a slimmer, odder tube sleeve, so neither is clearly better.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Man waving: the raised arm has only a tiny angled sleeve stub, so the shoulder looks bare. The hanging arm has no sleeve, and the shirt side runs straight into the skin.
- Woman reaching up: the raised-arm sleeve is a narrow purple tube that tapers up the arm, and the shoulder on that side is very narrow. The hanging-arm sleeve is an unclear bump with a stray crease line.
- Child pointing up: the near arm's sleeve is a small rounded cap that does not read as a T-shirt sleeve, and the arm seems pasted on top of the torso.
- Man with both arms raised: the right sleeve is a thin red tube. The left shoulder outline has an inward kink, with a crease line inside the shirt where the arm should start.

### Row b
- Man waving: there is a small ragged notch on the outline of the raised-arm shoulder.
- Woman reaching up: the raised-arm sleeve is bulky and boxy, close to a puffed sleeve, and a little large next to her slim arm.
- Child pointing up: the sleeve is clear but a bit stiff and boxy, which is acceptable.
- Man with both arms raised: the shoulders are very square and broad, the right sleeve flares out like a bell, and there are small notch or crease marks on the left shoulder and side of the shirt.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- Man with both arms raised: the near forearm and hand lie across his face and hide one eye and the top of the head. The pose reads poorly, especially at small size.
- Child pointing up: the raised arm comes out from behind the head, with only a yellow sliver at the neck, so it is unclear which shoulder it belongs to.
- Woman reaching up: the hanging hand is a blunt stump at the bottom of the large drawing.

## 4. Choice

**Row b, fairly sure.** Its short sleeves with hems make each shoulder and upper arm easy to read, which suits a friendly, clear cartoon style. Its faults are small: boxy shoulders and a few outline notches. Row a's bare or tube-like shoulders look unfinished.
