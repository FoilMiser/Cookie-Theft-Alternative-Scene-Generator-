# Review of pair_8 (arms1)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a each figure wears a short sleeve: a clothing-coloured shape with an outline and a hem edge, and the bare arm comes out of the bottom of it. In row b the sleeves are gone from the near or raised arm. That upper arm is drawn in skin colour all the way up, with a rounded, ball-like cap that sits on top of the shirt or dress, so the arm looks laid over the clothing instead of coming out of it.

## 2. Per figure (left to right)

1. **Woman, both arms out (yellow dress): a.** In a, the near arm comes out of a clear yellow cap sleeve. In b, a thick bare upper arm is pasted over the dress front, with no sleeve and a bulging round shoulder.
2. **Man sweeping (green shirt): a.** In a, the near arm comes out of a green short sleeve with a visible hem. In b, the brown upper arm starts high on the chest as a rounded blob over the shirt, and the shirt has no sleeve.
3. **Woman who has just thrown (purple shirt): a.** In a, the raised arm comes out of a purple sleeve, so it reads as a T-shirt. In b, the raised arm is bare and swells into a big rounded shoulder cap that overlaps the shirt's top edge.
4. **Girl pushing (pink dress): a.** In a, the puffed pink sleeve makes the shoulder and the start of the arm read clearly. In b, the bare arms run up into a rounded skin-coloured shoulder over the pink, with no sleeve.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Woman (yellow): the cap sleeve is a stiff, angular box shape. The far arm comes out of the side of the torso below the sleeve, with no shoulder or sleeve of its own.
- Man (green): the sleeve hem is a bit wide and flat. The sleeve and fold lines on the shirt are slightly busy.
- Thrower (purple): the sleeve on the raised arm is a jagged, angular shape where it meets the arm. The far arm comes out from behind the hip, far too low, with no visible shoulder.
- Girl (pink): the puffed sleeve stands up as a sharp-cornered block above the shoulder line.

### Row b
- Woman (yellow): the sleeve is missing. The upper arm is a thick skin-coloured shape with a round cap pasted over the dress, and leftover seam lines remain on the dress where the sleeve was.
- Man (green): the sleeve is missing. The upper arm starts as a round lump on the chest and looks stuck onto the shirt rather than attached at the shoulder.
- Thrower (purple): the sleeve is missing. The shoulder is a big round bulge overlapping the shirt neckline, and leftover crease lines remain on the shirt.
- Girl (pink): the sleeve is missing. The rounded shoulder cap swells over the dress, and the two arms merge into one heavy shape at the shoulder.
- At picture size, every figure in b looks like it wears a sleeveless top that the clothing outline does not support.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- Woman (yellow): the hands are small and claw-like, with knobbly fingers.
- Thrower: the far arm sits low near the hip, so the far shoulder is missing.
- Man: the broom-holding hand at the top is a dark lump that is hard to read.

## 4. Choice

**Row a**, fairly sure. Its sleeves give a clear, friendly cartoon read of where each arm joins the body. Row b's bare, ball-shaped shoulders look pasted on, though row a's sleeves are a little stiff and angular.
