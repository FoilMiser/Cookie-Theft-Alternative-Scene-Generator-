# Review of pair_4 (upper arms, shoulders, sleeves)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a, a raised arm usually comes out of a sleeve that is a narrow tube. On the woman and on the right arm of the man with both arms raised, that tube runs a long way up the arm, so the sleeve looks long next to the short sleeve on the other arm. In row b the shoulders are rounder and broader, and the sleeves are short caps at the shoulder. The exception is the waving man in row b, who has no visible sleeve on either arm.

## 2. Figure by figure (left to right)

1. **Man waving: a.** Row a shows a short red sleeve around the root of the raised arm and a small sleeve on the lowered arm. In row b both arms come straight out of the shirt with no sleeve, so the T-shirt looks sleeveless.
2. **Woman reaching up: b.** In row b the raised arm has a short purple cap sleeve that matches the sleeve on her lowered arm. In row a the purple sleeve on the raised arm is a long tube that reaches nearly to the elbow, so it does not match the short sleeve on the other side.
3. **Child pointing up, from the side: b.** In row b the near sleeve is a small rounded cap that follows the shoulder. In row a it is a boxy rectangle that sticks out from the body. The difference is small.
4. **Man with both arms raised: b.** In row b the short red sleeve on the right arm and the rounded shoulders read as one T-shirt. In row a the right sleeve is a long red tube up the arm, and the left shoulder has a busy step and seam lines.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Man waving: the sleeve on the raised arm is a thin band, and the shirt outline has a small step where the arm joins the shoulder. The shoulder looks narrow.
- Woman reaching up: the sleeve on the raised arm is a long purple tube up the upper arm. It looks like a long sleeve, while the lowered arm has a short sleeve.
- Child pointing up: the near sleeve is a stiff box that stands out from the torso and does not follow the shoulder.
- Man with both arms raised: the right sleeve is a long tube up the arm, like the woman's. The left shoulder has a stepped outline with extra seam lines.

### Row b
- Man waving: neither arm has a sleeve. The raised arm comes out of the top corner of the shirt, and the lowered arm overlaps the shirt edge with no cuff, so the shirt looks sleeveless and does not match the other figures.
- Man with both arms raised: the left (near) arm has no cuff where it leaves the shirt, only fold lines. The two sides do not match.
- Child pointing up: a sliver of yellow shirt shows between the chin and the raised arm. Row a has the same sliver.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- Man with both arms raised: the near arm crosses over the face and covers one eye and part of the nose. The pose reads as hiding the face rather than raising both arms.
- Child pointing up: the raised arm seems to grow out of the cheek and jaw, with no shoulder visible behind the head. The pointing hand is a plain tube with a ring at the tip.
- Man waving: the hand on the lowered arm is a featureless mitten with an open loop drawn on it.

## 4. Choice

**Row b, fairly sure.** Its rounded shoulders and short cap sleeves are friendlier and clearer on three of the four figures. Before using it, the waving man's missing sleeves need fixing.
