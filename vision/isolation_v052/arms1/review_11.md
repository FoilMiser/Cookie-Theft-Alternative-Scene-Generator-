# Review of pair_11

## 1. How the rows differ

Row b draws the upper arms a little thicker, and the sleeve leaves the body higher, closer to the top of the shoulder. Row a's sleeves are thinner and join the torso lower, so the shoulder reads more like a separate lump. In b the hood behind the side-view teenager's neck is also larger. Most of the other changes are small.

## 2. Figure by figure

1. **Teenager waving: b.** In b the raised blue sleeve rises from the top of the shoulder in one continuous line. In a it starts from a rounded bump down at chest level, under the hood strings, and the arm looks bolted on.
2. **Older woman reaching up: b (slight).** In b the raised teal sleeve is fuller and ends in a clear cuff at the wrist. In a it is a thin tube that pinches where it meets the cardigan's top corner.
3. **Older man sweeping: no difference.** Both rows draw the white sleeves over the brown vest in much the same way. Row b's near sleeve is a little thicker and reaches the hand better, but row a reads just as clearly.
4. **Teenager with arms out, side view: a (slight).** Both rows start the arms from a rounded stub on the chest. Row b adds a large hood lump behind the neck that reads like a hunched back or a raised shoulder, and a does not have it.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Teenager waving: the raised arm starts from a round cap low on the chest, below where the shoulder should be, so there is no clear shoulder line.
- Older woman: the raised sleeve is thin and pinches at the shoulder corner, and it has no cuff at the wrist.
- Older man: the near sleeve narrows sharply at the elbow, and the forearm looks thin next to the big hand.
- Teenager side view: the arms come out of the middle of the chest as rounded stubs, with no shoulder above them, and the upper arm runs straight from the torso.

### Row b
- Teenager waving: there is still a rounded bump on the chest below the hood strings, though less of one than in a.
- Older woman: the hanging arm's shoulder comes to a sharp, boxy point at the top.
- Older man: the vest still shows a brown cap above the near white sleeve, so the join looks layered rather than worn.
- Teenager side view: the hood lump behind the neck is large and looks like a hump. The arms still start as stubs on the chest, as in a.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- On both teenagers a horizontal line crosses the hoodie at mid-chest, which looks like a seam in the body.
- The waving teenager's lowered hand hangs below the hoodie cuff as a large mitten, and it looks detached.

## 4. Choice

**Row b, toss-up.** The waving teenager is clearly better in b and the older woman is slightly better. The side-view teenager is slightly better in a because of b's hood hump. The older man is about the same in both rows.
