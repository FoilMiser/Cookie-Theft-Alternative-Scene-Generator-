# Review of pair_3: upper arms, shoulders and sleeves

## 1. How the rows differ

Row b draws every upper arm and sleeve wider and puffier than row a, with bigger, rounder shoulder caps that sit higher and push out further from the body. Row a keeps the arms slimmer, and the sleeves read more as cloth tubes hanging from the shoulder. On the worker the rows also differ in how the T-shirt sleeve meets the bare arm: in row a it is a short sleeve that the arm comes out of, and in row b it is a grey ball sitting on top of the shoulder.

## 2. Per figure (left to right)

- **Teenager, hooded top, front: a.** Row b's shoulder caps are large balloons that bulge over the hood and make the arms look inflated, while row a's slimmer sleeves hang more naturally from the shoulders.
- **Older woman, cardigan, turned: no difference.** Row b's fuller sleeves suit a soft knit cardigan but look a little heavy, and row a's are neater but thin, so neither is clearly better.
- **Older man, waistcoat, side: a.** In row b the white sleeve is a wide pillow-like shape whose top bulges across the waistcoat's armhole, too bulky for a thin older man, while row a's narrower sleeve sits inside the armhole more believably.
- **Worker, safety vest, turned: a.** In row b the grey sleeve is a round cap stuck on top of the shoulder and the bare arm seems to start below it, out from under the vest, so the arm looks detached; row a shows a proper short sleeve with the arm coming out of it, and on the far side as well.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Teenager: a small bump or step where the shoulder cap meets the sleeve, so the shoulder looks like a separate piece sitting on the arm.
- Older woman: the near (right-hand) shoulder has a small peaked bump like a shoulder pad, and the back sleeve is a plain narrow tube with little shape.
- Older man: the top of the sleeve still pokes up as a rounded blob above the waistcoat armhole, so the shoulder floats on top of the waistcoat instead of sitting inside it.
- Worker: the near short sleeve is a flat grey patch with a hard lower edge, and the far shoulder barely shows a sleeve at the vest edge.

### Row b
- Teenager: oversized balloon shoulder caps that rise to hood level and overlap it; the arms look inflated and heavy for a teenager.
- Older woman: puffy sleeves that widen the silhouette a lot; the near shoulder is a big round lump.
- Older man: wide pillow-shaped sleeve whose top swells across the waistcoat edge; the arm is out of proportion with the thin body, and the hand at its end looks large and blunt.
- Worker: the grey sleeve is a detached ball on top of the shoulder, the skin upper arm starts lower and seems to come out from under the vest, and the far arm shows no sleeve at all. The fault shows clearly even at picture size.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- The teenager's hands are curled claw-like blobs at the cuffs.
- The older man's trouser waistband and belt area below the waistcoat is a lumpy, unclear shape.
- On the turned figures (woman, worker) the far hand is a flat blob pressed against the side, with little sense of the arm behind the body.

## 4. Choice

**Row a**, fairly sure. Its slimmer arms and sleeves read more cleanly and look friendlier at picture size. Row b's balloon shoulders and the worker's detached sleeve are the clearest faults on the sheet.
