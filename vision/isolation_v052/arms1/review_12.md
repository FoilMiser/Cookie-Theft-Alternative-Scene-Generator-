# Review of pair_12 (upper arms, shoulders, sleeves)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a each sleeve is a separate shape with an outlined hem, and the arm comes out from under that hem, so the sleeve reads as cloth over the arm. In row b the sleeves are rounder, puffier caps with no hem line, and the top of each upper arm is cut flat or overlaps the sleeve, so the arm looks pasted onto the side of the body rather than coming out of the sleeve. Row b's upper arms also look thicker and start higher, up at the shoulder.

## 2. Figure by figure

- **Child from the front: a.** The outlined sleeve hems show clearly where the shirt ends and the arm starts, while in b the arms sit beside puffed caps and look attached from the outside.
- **Woman in a dress, turned: a.** In a the near arm comes out from under a hemmed sleeve, while in b the top of the near arm is cut off flat into the sleeve with no hem, and the shoulder shows a stray crease.
- **Girl from the side: a.** In a the pink sleeve has a hem and the arm hangs out from under it; in b the skin meets the pink in a flat, unoutlined cut, so the arm looks stuck on.
- **Bearded man, turned: a.** In b the near upper arm is a very wide brown block laid over the green sleeve, covering the shirt, while in a the sleeve covers the shoulder and the arm comes out below it.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Child: the sleeves are boxy and stick straight out sideways, so the shoulders look very wide and square.
- Woman: the far arm is a thin strip behind the body, and its sleeve hardly shows.
- Man: the sleeves are long and stiff, close to tube shapes, and the shoulder line is a flat ledge.
- All figures: the upper arms are long compared with the sleeves, so the arms look a little stretched.

### Row b
- Child: puffed cap sleeves with no hem; the arms are joined at the side below the cap and look glued to the torso.
- Woman: the near arm's top edge is cut straight across into the sleeve; there is a loose crease line on the sleeve and no hem.
- Girl: flat, unoutlined join where the arm meets the sleeve, so the arm looks pasted on.
- Man: the near upper arm is far too thick and overlaps the shirt, so it reads as a slab stuck onto the chest; the far sleeve is a rounded lump with no hem.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- The hands are mitten blobs with a single curl line; the woman's and the man's far hands look swollen.
- The child's shorts have a small odd notch at the crotch.
- The girl's head is very large for her body, and her ear sits far back on the head.

## 4. Choice

Row a, fairly sure. Its hemmed sleeves with the arms coming out from under them read as clean, friendly cartoon clothing. Row b's pasted-on arms, worst on the man, look like a building fault.
