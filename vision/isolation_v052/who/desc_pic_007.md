# pic_007

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a gate at the far left and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands in the middle-back using a long-handled paint roller to paint the fence blue; a few boards beside him are already blue. On the grass at his feet are a paint tin and a paint tray with blue paint. A white dog with brown spots walks away to the left, and its paws are blue; a trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray to the dog, so the dog has stepped in the paint and is tracking it across the lawn. On the paved path at the left, a man in a green shirt carries a cardboard box. Oddly, an orange fish is sitting in the tree at the top right, on a branch, which is impossible. There is also a flower bed, a hose reel, a wheelbarrow and some bushes.
