# pic_033

## Free description
A sunny town street with a row of colourful shops. On the left a woman in a purple top sweeps dead leaves with a yellow broom. Next to her a man in a red shirt is sweeping dust and dirt towards a red dustpan, but he is using a giant silver fork instead of a broom, which is absurd and impossible to sweep with properly. In the centre front an older woman with grey hair and glasses walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. A girl in a pink dress sits on a bench drinking from a cup. On the right a blue-and-white beach ball is stuck in the branches of a tree, and a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at it with a worried face, as if he wants it back. A teal bicycle is parked in a bike rack. There is also a fruit stall, a lamp post, flower planters and potted plants.
