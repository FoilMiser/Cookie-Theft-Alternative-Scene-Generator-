The sky is grey and overcast, with three dark storm clouds overhead, so it looks like rain is coming. There is a flower bed with colourful flowers on the left, a wooden bench in the front left corner, and two potted plants on the grass near the fence. It seems the girl is warning about (or noticing) the coming rain while the woman carries on with her chores. Nothing looks impossible.
