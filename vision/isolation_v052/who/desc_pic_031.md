# pic_031

## Free description
A sunny park with a blue pond, rolling green hills, trees, a lamp post, a bench and a winding sandy path. At the front left a woman in a yellow dress is watering a flower bed, but she is pouring the water out of a frying pan instead of a watering can, which is odd. Next to her a man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers bends over and scatters seeds from a paper bag for a red bird on the ground. A small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts runs to the right while licking a pink ice-cream cone, and he is running right past (almost over) the red bird. In the middle distance a girl in a pink dress holds a red and a purple balloon and reaches up toward a yellow balloon that has floated away into the sky. On the right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks along the path carrying a yellow shopping bag. A green rubbish bin stands in the bottom-left corner.
