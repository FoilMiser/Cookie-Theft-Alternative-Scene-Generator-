# pic_012

## Free description
A sunny day in a park with a pond, a lamp post, a bench, trees and a winding path. On the left a woman in a yellow dress is watering a flower bed, but she is pouring the water out of a frying pan instead of a watering can. Next to her, a man in a green shirt is shaking seeds out of a paper bag, and a red bird on the ground is pecking at them. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is running across the grass holding a pink ice-cream cone. Further back, a girl in a pink dress holds a red and a purple balloon and reaches up with a worried face toward a yellow balloon that has escaped and is floating away into the sky. On the right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks along the path carrying a yellow shopping bag. The bench is empty. The odd thing is the frying pan being used as a watering can.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The frying pan used for watering looks deliberately wrong; it could also be read as a saucepan, but it has a long handle like a frying pan.
- The yellow balloon: the girl may have just let it go, or it may belong to someone else; her raised hand and face suggest she lost it.
- The running boy with an ice cream might be about to trip or drop it, but nothing clearly shows that.
