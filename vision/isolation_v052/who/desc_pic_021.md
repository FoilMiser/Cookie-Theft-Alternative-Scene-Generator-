# pic_021

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a tall wooden fence, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. At the far left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands on the paved path near the wooden gate. She is sweeping a small pile of dead leaves with a yellow broom. Just behind her is a birdhouse on top of a tall wooden pole. In the middle stands a dark grey swing set with one empty red swing hanging still. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress kneels on the grass at the edge of a round flower bed. She uses a small trowel to plant a pink flower that is still in an orange pot. A wooden bench stands empty at the front right. A teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow sit on the grass at the front. Nothing looks clearly wrong.
