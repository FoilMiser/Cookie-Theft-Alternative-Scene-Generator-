## Free description
A sunny town square paved with grey tiles, in front of a row of colourful shops with striped awnings. On the left a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands with his hands on his hips in front of a fruit stall, and he is wearing a metal bucket upside down on his head like a hat, which is odd. Next to him a brown dog wearing glasses sits up and reads a newspaper, which is impossible. In the middle an old woman with grey hair waters a box of flowers with a watering can. Behind the bench, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is crying with his hand over his eyes, holding an empty ice-cream cone; his pink ice cream lies on the ground and a white dog with brown spots is licking it up. On the right a blue-and-white ball is stuck in the tree, and a small girl in a pink dress points up at it. A woman in a purple top sweeps leaves with a broom at the far right. In the front a boy in a red cap scatters seeds from a paper bag for a blue bird, while in the centre-front a grey cat creeps towards a red bird that is pecking at seeds, as if about to pounce.
