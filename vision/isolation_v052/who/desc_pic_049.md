## Free description
A sunny town square paved with grey tiles, in front of a row of colourful shops with striped awnings, with a market stall of fruit and vegetables on the left. A man in a red shirt and jeans stands with hands on hips wearing an upside-down metal bucket on his head. Beside him a brown dog wearing glasses sits upright reading a newspaper, which is impossible. An elderly woman with glasses is watering a planter of flowers with a watering can. Behind the bench, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is crying with a hand over the eyes, holding an empty ice-cream cone, while a white-and-brown dog licks the pink ice cream that has fallen on the ground. On the right, a blue-and-white beach ball is stuck in the branches of a tree, and a little girl in a pink dress points up at it; a woman in a purple top sweeps leaves nearby. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie scatters seeds from a paper bag for a blue bird pecking at his feet, and a red bird pecks at seeds in the front left-centre. A grey cat creeps low across the front, apparently stalking the birds.
