# pic_024

## Free description
It is a sunny day on a paved shopping street with four coloured shop buildings, striped awnings, a lamp post and a fruit stall at the left. At the front left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sweeps dead leaves with a yellow broom. Next to her, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is sweeping a pile of dust towards a red dustpan. He is using a giant silver fork instead of a broom, which is strange. In the middle, an older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. A little girl in a pink outfit sits on a wooden bench drinking through a straw from a cup. A teal bicycle is parked in a bike rack behind her. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of a tree. The ball seems to be caught there, and he looks puzzled or upset about it. Potted plants stand at the front corners and flower planters sit by the shops.
