## Free description
A busy, sunny back garden next to a cream house with a red roof and a blue door. On the bench at the bottom left an old man in a flat cap sits reading a red book, while a small blue bird pecks at the sandwich lying next to him on the bench, unnoticed. An old woman with grey hair is painting the fence blue with a roller; part of the fence is already blue and a paint tin and tray are on the grass below her. A trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray to a white dog with brown spots, whose paws are blue, so the dog has walked through the paint. A man in a green T-shirt has his arms out towards a red ball in the air, and a small girl in a pink dress reaches up, so they are playing catch. A woman in a purple top stands near the gate with her hands on her hips, wearing an upside-down metal bucket on her head. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts jumps with his arms raised, cheering. In the front a boy in a red cap walks fast while reading a book and is about to walk into a cardboard box on the grass. An orange cat sleeps on the path, and a red bird flies in the sky. The wheelbarrow in the middle has a square wheel, which is impossible.
