# pic_051

## Free description
A sunny park with a pond, hills, trees, a winding path, a street lamp and a bench. In the front left a woman in a yellow dress is watering a flower bed, but she is pouring the water from a frying pan instead of a watering can, which is odd. Next to her a man in a green shirt scatters seeds from a paper bag, and a red bird on the ground pecks at them. A child in a yellow top and orange shorts runs past with a pink ice-cream cone, and looks close to running into or stepping on the bird. Further back a little girl in a pink dress holds red and purple balloons, and a yellow balloon has escaped and is floating away into the sky; she reaches up towards it looking upset. On the right a woman in a purple top walks along the path carrying a yellow shopping bag. A green bin stands in the bottom left corner.
