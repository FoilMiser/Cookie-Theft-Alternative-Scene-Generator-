## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left, a wooden fence, a gate on the right and a paved path. At front left a young girl in a pink dress sits on a bench eating a pink ice cream, and a white dog with brown spots sits up on its hind legs beside her, begging. In the centre front a brown dog sits holding a newspaper open in its paws as if reading it, which is impossible for a real dog. Behind it a man in a green shirt walks across the lawn carrying a cardboard box. In the centre a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie waves one hand high, with little motion marks around it, as if calling to someone. On the right a woman in a yellow dress sweeps dead leaves on the path with a yellow broom. There is a flower bed, two potted plants and a teal watering can.
