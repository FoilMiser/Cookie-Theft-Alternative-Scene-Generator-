# pic_026

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a gate at the far left and a cream house with red roof and blue door on the right. A man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands in the middle-back painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller; part of the fence next to him is already blue. A paint tin and a paint tray of blue paint sit on the grass near his feet. A white dog with brown spots walks to the left with blue paws, leaving a trail of blue paw prints from the paint tray, so it has stepped in the paint. At the top of the path on the left, by the gate, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple T-shirt and teal trousers sweeps a pile of leaves with a yellow broom. The dog is heading towards the path and the woman. There are two potted plants, a flower bed, a watering can and a wheelbarrow on the lawn.
