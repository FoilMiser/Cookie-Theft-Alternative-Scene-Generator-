# pic_036

## Free description
A busy, sunny back garden with a fence across the back, a gate on the left and a cream house with a blue door on the right. A man with dark skin in a green shirt is walking in through the gate while reading an open book, and a cardboard box sits on the ground just in front of him, so he looks about to trip over it. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie walks a white spotted dog on a red lead along the path at the front left. An older woman with grey hair and glasses in a teal top holds the nozzle of a garden hose in the middle of the lawn, but no water is coming out. The hose runs back across the grass to a hose reel by the house, and a brown dog is standing right on the hose, which would block the water. Near the house a girl in a pink dress stretches her arm up toward a blue-and-white beach ball that is stuck in the branches of a tree. On the right a boy in a red shirt sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. There is also a flower bed and a bush by the house.
