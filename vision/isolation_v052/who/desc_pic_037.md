# pic_037

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a fence across the back, a gate on the left and a cream house with a blue door on the right. A man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands in the middle painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a stick; a section of the fence is already painted blue. On the ground near him are a paint tin and a paint tray with blue paint. A white dog with brown spots has blue paint on its paws and is walking away toward the left, leaving a trail of blue pawprints on the grass that leads back to the paint tray, so it has evidently stepped in the paint. On the left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sweeps a pile of leaves on the path by the gate with a broom. The dog is heading toward the path where she is. There are also two potted plants, a watering can and an empty wheelbarrow on the lawn, and a flower bed by the house.
