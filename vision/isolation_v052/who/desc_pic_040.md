# pic_040

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a fence across the back, a gate on the left and a cream house with a blue door on the right. A man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is painting the fence blue with a roller near the right; a section is already blue, and a paint tin and paint tray sit on the grass behind him. A white dog with brown spots has blue paws and is walking left across the lawn, leaving a trail of blue pawprints that leads back to the paint tray. On the path at the left, a man with dark skin and a beard in a green T-shirt carries a cardboard box, walking forward from the gate. Very oddly, an orange fish is sitting in the branches of the tree behind the fence, which is impossible. There is also an empty wheelbarrow, a hose reel with its hose and nozzle lying on the grass, and a flower bed by the house.
