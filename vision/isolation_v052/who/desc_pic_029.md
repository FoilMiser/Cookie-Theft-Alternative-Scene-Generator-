## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a wooden gate on the left. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; a few planks are already blue. At his feet are a small paint tin and a paint tray with blue paint. A white dog with brown spots is walking away from the paint tray towards the left, leaving a trail of blue paw prints on the grass, and its paws are blue - it has stepped in the paint. On the left, a man in a green shirt is walking along the paved path carrying a cardboard box. Odd thing: an orange fish is sitting in the branches of the tree behind the fence, which is impossible. There is also a wheelbarrow, a flower bed and a hose reel on the lawn.
