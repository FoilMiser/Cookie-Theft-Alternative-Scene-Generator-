# pic_048

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, and a cream house with a blue door on the right. In the background a man in a green shirt is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle. Three fence panels are already blue and the rest are still plain wood. A tin of blue paint sits on the grass near his feet. On the left a woman in a yellow dress sweeps a pile of dead leaves on the paved path with a yellow broom. In front there is a teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow. On the right a flower bed with colourful flowers sits near the house, and a coiled garden hose on a reel lies beside it with the nozzle on the grass. Nothing looks clearly wrong. It is an ordinary garden-chores scene.
