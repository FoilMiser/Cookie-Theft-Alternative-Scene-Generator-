# pic_045

## Free description
A busy, sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and blue door. On a bench at the front left, an old man in a cap and brown waistcoat reads a red book and does not notice that a blue bird is pecking at the sandwich lying next to him. An old woman with grey hair is painting the fence blue with a roller; a paint tin and tray are on the grass, and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray to a white spotted dog, whose paw is blue, so the dog has stepped in the paint. A man in a green shirt stands with his hands raised as if to catch, and a red-and-white ball is flying through the air on a dotted path from a small girl in a pink dress, who seems to have thrown it. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts jumps with his arms up, cheering. A woman in a purple top stands by the gate with a metal bucket upside down on her head and hands on her hips. In the front, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is walking while reading a book and is about to trip over a cardboard box in his way. An orange cat sleeps on the path, and a red bird flies in the sky.
