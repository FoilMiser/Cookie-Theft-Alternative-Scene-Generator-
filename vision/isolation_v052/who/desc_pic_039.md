## Free description
A busy, sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof, with lots of people and animals and several odd things going on. An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses is painting the wooden fence blue with a paint roller; part of the fence is already blue, and a paint can and tray sit at her feet. Blue paw prints lead across the grass from the paint tray towards a white dog with brown spots, which has blue paint on its feet — the dog has clearly stepped in the paint. A man in a green shirt stands in the middle reaching up toward the fence, and a red-and-white ball is flying through the air (with a dotted line) from or towards a little girl in a pink dress, so they seem to be playing catch. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is walking while reading a book and is about to trip over a cardboard box on the ground in front of him. On the bench at the left, an older man in a cap is reading a red book while a blue bird pecks at the sandwich lying next to him on the bench, and he hasn't noticed. On the right, a woman in a purple top is wearing an upside-down metal bucket on her head as if it were a hat, and a child in a yellow top and orange shorts jumps with arms raised. An orange cat sleeps curled up on the path at the bottom right, and a red bird flies in the sky. A wheelbarrow stands on the grass with what looks like a square wheel, which could not roll.
