# Review of pair_3.png (upper arms, shoulders, sleeves)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the sleeves are drawn as puffed, bulging caps with crease lines inside them, and the bare arm comes out from under the sleeve with a step or notch, so the arm looks attached under the sleeve rather than coming out of it. In row b the shoulders are rounded and the sleeves are plain short sleeves with one seam line and a clean hem, and the arm comes out of the hem in one smooth line. Row b's shoulders also slope down more gently into the arm.

## 2. Per figure

1. Child from the front: **b**. In a the shoulders are flat and square with puffed caps that the arms hang under like a doll's, while in b the shoulders round off and the sleeves end cleanly over the arms.
2. Woman in a dress, turned: **b**. In a the near sleeve bulges and has a fold line, with a notch where the arm comes out, while in b the sleeve is a smooth short sleeve and the arm continues from it without a step.
3. Girl from the side: **b**. In a the sleeve ends in a hard horizontal cuff with the arm set back under it, while in b the sleeve hem is angled and the arm flows out of it, though the difference is smaller than for the others.
4. Bearded man, turned: **b**. In a the near shoulder has a notch where the brown arm meets the green sleeve and the sleeve has a stray wrinkle line, so the arm looks pasted on, while in b the shoulder is one smooth curve with a seam line.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Child: shoulders are flat and boxy and wider than the chest. Puffed sleeve caps with crease lines look like a costume, and each arm starts under the cap with a visible step.
- Woman: the near sleeve bulges like a puff sleeve and has an inner fold line. There is a notch where the upper arm comes out, and the upper arm looks narrower than the sleeve opening.
- Girl: the sleeve ends in a hard straight cuff with the arm tucked under it, which leaves a step in the outline at the back of the arm.
- Man: there is a notch or step at the near shoulder where the arm meets the sleeve, and the sleeve has a stray wrinkle line. The far shoulder has a small bump in its outline.

### Row b
- Child: the sleeves are fairly wide and stiff, standing slightly out from the arms. Otherwise clean.
- Woman: the near upper arm is fairly thick compared with the forearm, so the arm looks like a tube. This is minor.
- Girl: there is a small notch at the bottom of the sleeve hem where the arm comes out. This is minor.
- Man: the near upper arm is very broad and the arm looks heavy, almost as wide as the torso side. The seam line sits high and is a little hard to read.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- Child: the orange shorts have a small white notch at the crotch that reads as a cut-out gap.
- Girl: the head is very large for the body even for a cartoon, and the nose is a small notch cut into the face outline rather than a shape.
- Woman: the neck meets the dress collar in a straight, flat line that looks stiff.
- Man: the beard sits as a separate dark band along the jaw and looks stuck on.

## 4. Choice

Row **b**. Its plain sleeves and rounded shoulders read more clearly and look friendlier, while row a's puffed caps and notches make the arms look attached. **Fairly sure.** At the small picture size the difference is slight, but b still looks cleaner.
