# Review of pair_11 (upper arms, shoulders, sleeves)

## 1. How the rows differ

Row b gives every figure larger, rounder shoulder caps that sit higher and stick out further. The tops of the sleeves swell out like balloons and then narrow down the arm. Row a keeps the shoulders lower and more sloped, with slimmer upper arms that hang more straight, so the outlines look calmer and more natural.

## 2. Per figure (left to right)

- **Teenager, hooded top, front:** a. In row a the sleeves hang as even tubes from a modest shoulder bump. In row b the shoulder caps balloon out wide and rise nearly to the hood, which makes the teen look padded.
- **Older woman, cardigan, turned:** a. Row a has soft, sloped shoulders that suit an older woman. In row b the shoulders are square and the near sleeve puffs out at the top, which makes her look broad and bulky.
- **Older man, waistcoat, side:** a, but only by a small margin. Both rows draw the shirt sleeve as a round cap over the waistcoat. Row b's cap is a bigger, rounder blob, and in the small figure it reads as a lump on the shoulder.
- **Worker, safety vest, turned:** a. In row a the grey short sleeve runs smoothly into the tan arm. In row b the sleeve looks like a separate pad sitting on top of the shoulder, and the bare upper arm below it is wider than the sleeve opening, so it looks swollen.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Teenager: there is a small notch or gap at each armpit, so the arms look slightly stuck on beside the torso. The sleeves are the same width from shoulder to cuff, with no taper.
- Older woman: the far arm is very thin and partly hidden behind the body, so it barely reads as an arm.
- Older man: the round sleeve cap still sits on top of the waistcoat edge like a separate piece, not under the armhole.
- Worker: the shoulder line is quite low and sloped, so he looks a little narrow-shouldered for a worker.

### Row b
- Teenager: the shoulder caps are oversized and puffy and rise almost to the hood. The shoulders look far too wide and the arms look inflated.
- Older woman: the shoulders are square and high, and the near sleeve has a puffed top. She looks broad and stiff.
- Older man: the shoulder cap is a large round blob over the waistcoat, and the upper arm is wide at the top and tapers sharply. At picture size it reads as a lump.
- Worker: the sleeve looks like a pad stuck on the shoulder. The bare upper arm bulges wider than the sleeve, giving a step in the outline.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- The hands on all figures are mitten-like blobs with a single C-shaped mark. The worker's and woman's near hands are large next to their arms.
- Older man: there is a bunched lump at the back of the trouser waist under the waistcoat.
- Teenager: the hood is drawn as a flat collar ring around the neck, with little sense of depth.

## 4. Choice

Row **a**, fairly sure. Its sloped shoulders and even sleeves give a friendlier, clearer cartoon look. Row b's puffed shoulder caps make every figure look padded and are the main thing you notice at picture size.
