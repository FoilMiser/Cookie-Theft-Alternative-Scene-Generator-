# Review of pair_12 (upper arms, shoulders, sleeves)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the upper arms come out of the garment's sleeve opening or shoulder edge. The cloth stays on top at the shoulder, and the arms are fairly slim. In row b the upper arms are thicker skin-coloured tubes with rounded ends. Those rounded ends are drawn on top of the clothing, so a round skin shoulder cap sits over the dress or shirt and hides the sleeve hem.

## 2. Figure by figure (left to right)

- **Woman, both arms out (side):** a. In a the arms leave the yellow sleeve cleanly; in b a round skin knob sits on top of the dress where the sleeve should be, so she reads as sleeveless with a bulging shoulder.
- **Man sweeping:** a. In a the near arm hangs out of a visible green short sleeve with a hem; in b the brown upper arm starts inside the chest area and covers the sleeve, so the arm seems to grow out of his shirt front.
- **Woman who has just thrown (side):** a. In a the purple top flows smoothly into the raised arm at the shoulder; in b a skin cap is stuck over the top's edge next to the neck, and both arms look heavier and pasted on.
- **Girl pushing (side):** a. In a the arms come out from under a pink short sleeve; in b the near upper arm is laid over the dress from the shoulder down, hiding the sleeve and giving her an oversized shoulder.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Woman with arms out: the two arms start from almost the same spot and run nearly parallel, so the far shoulder is hard to read. The sleeve is only a few faint lines.
- Man sweeping: the shoulder line on the near side is plain and there is little sign of a deltoid. That is acceptable, but a little flat.
- Thrower: the top looks sleeveless and the raised arm meets it at a sharp corner. The back arm comes out from behind the torso low down, near the waist, which looks a little odd.
- Girl: the arms are thin for her big head, and the sleeve edge is only a faint line.

### Row b
- Woman with arms out: a round skin knob sits on top of the yellow dress, and the sleeve hem is covered, so the shoulder looks swollen.
- Man sweeping: the near upper arm is drawn over the green shirt, starting inside the chest. The sleeve disappears, and the arm reads as attached to his front, not his shoulder.
- Thrower: a skin shoulder cap overlaps the purple top right beside the neck, so the joint looks pasted on. Both arms look thicker than in a.
- Girl: the near upper arm is laid over the pink dress from shoulder to elbow. The sleeve is hidden, and the shoulder looks broad and bulky for a child.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- Woman with arms out: the hands are curled, claw-like shapes with no clear palm direction.
- Thrower: the forward hand is a flat pointed paddle with no fingers, and the back hand is a mitten-like blob.
- Man sweeping: the top hand gripping the broom is a bulky lump, and the stick passes awkwardly in front of the lower hand.
- Girl: the hands overlap into one merged blob at the end of the arms.
- At picture size the differences are small. Row b's shoulders read only slightly bulkier, mostly on the first woman and the thrower.

## 4. Choice

Row a. Its sleeves and shoulders read as clothing over a body, which is clearer and friendlier for a cartoon. Row b's skin caps over the clothes look like parts stuck on top. Confidence: fairly sure, because at picture size the difference is small.
