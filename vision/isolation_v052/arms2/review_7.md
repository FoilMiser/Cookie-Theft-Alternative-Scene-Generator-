# Review of pair_7 (upper arms, shoulders, sleeves)

## 1. How the rows differ

The differences are small. Row b draws the shoulders a little broader and squarer and joins the upper arms to the body more smoothly. It also gives the older woman's raised sleeve a flared cuff and makes the older man's crossing sleeve reach all the way to his lower hand. Row a has narrower, rounder shoulders, and in a few places the arm narrows where it meets the body. On the two teenagers the rows look almost the same.

## 2. Figure by figure

1. **Teenager waving: no difference.** Both rows show the same thick raised sleeve with a rounded lump where it meets the chest, and the same hanging arm with a cuff. Row b's raised arm is only slightly wider at the top.
2. **Older woman reaching up: b.** In row b the raised sleeve rises from a broad shoulder in one smooth line and ends in a visible cuff at the wrist. In row a the arm narrows at the shoulder, so it looks thin and stuck on.
3. **Older man sweeping: b.** In row b the white sleeve crossing his body runs all the way to the lower hand on the broom, and a cuff shows at the upper hand. In row a that sleeve ends short and rounded, and the lower hand looks barely joined to it.
4. **Teenager with arms out, side view: no difference.** Both rows show the same two parallel tube arms coming out of a hood bump. Row b's hood bump is a little larger, but the arms and shoulder join look alike.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Older woman: the raised arm narrows where it meets the shoulder, so it looks attached rather than grown from the cardigan. The sleeve has no cuff at the wrist.
- Older man: the crossing sleeve ends in a rounded stump just short of the lower hand, so the hand looks loose. The arm also looks a bit short for where the hand is.
- Teenager waving: the raised sleeve ends in a rounded lump on the chest that reads like a separate pad, not a shoulder.
- Teenager side view: the near arm starts with a rounded end placed on top of the torso, like a tube stuck on. There is no shoulder shape under the hood bump.

### Row b
- Teenager waving: the same lump on the chest where the raised arm ends. The wider top of the arm partly merges with the hood edge behind the head.
- Teenager side view: the hood bump behind the neck is large and reads like a swelling on the shoulder. The near arm still starts as a rounded tube end on the torso.
- Older woman: the hanging arm's shoulder is broad and square, which makes the cardigan look a bit boxy. The flared cuff on the raised arm is slightly heavy for the thin wrist.
- Older man: the crossing arm is long and straight, with little sign of an elbow.

### Other things that look wrong
- The older man's lower hand on the broom is large and mitten-like in both rows.
- On the side-view teenager, the hands are drawn as cupped, curled shapes that are hard to read at picture size.
- On the waving teenager, the pocket on the hoodie is a floating oval that looks like a patch.

## 4. Choice

**Row b.** Its shoulders join more smoothly, the woman's sleeve reads clearly as a sleeve, and the man's arm reaches his hand. At picture size the gain is small, and both teenagers look the same in either row.

How sure: **fairly sure.**
