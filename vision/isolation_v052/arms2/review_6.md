# Review of pair_6: upper arms, shoulders and sleeves

## 1. How the rows differ

Row b draws thicker, fuller sleeves and rounder, wider shoulders than row a, so the arms read as soft tubes of cloth rather than thin limbs. Row a's sleeves are narrower and its shoulders sit tighter to the body, with harder corners where the sleeve meets the torso. At the small size the difference is slight. Row b looks a little chunkier, and row a looks a little more spindly.

## 2. Figure by figure

1. **Teenager waving: b.** The raised blue sleeve in b is wider at the upper arm and joins the shoulder in one smooth curve. In a it is a thin tube with a small knob where it meets the hoodie.
2. **Older woman reaching up: b.** In b the cardigan sleeves have body and the near shoulder has a soft rounded top. In a the raised sleeve is thin and the hanging arm looks narrow and stiff against the torso.
3. **Older man sweeping: b.** In b the near shoulder of the cream sleeve is a full, smooth round that sits naturally under the vest. In a the top of that shoulder has a small notch or step where the sleeve meets the vest edge, and the upper arm looks pinched.
4. **Teenager with arms held out, side view: a.** In b the shoulder at the back of the figure swells into a large round ball behind the neck that reads as a lump or a separate object. Row a's version of that bump is smaller and reads more plausibly as a hood or shoulder.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Teenager waving: the raised sleeve is thin, and a small separate-looking knob sits where it joins the hoodie. A hard horizontal line across the chest under the arm makes the shoulder look bolted on.
- Older woman: both sleeves are narrow. The raised arm looks like a stick, and the hanging arm is a straight, stiff band along the side.
- Older man: the near shoulder has a notch or step at its top edge, so the sleeve outline does not run cleanly into the vest. The upper arm narrows oddly below it.
- Teenager arms out: the arms are thin, and the two forearms run almost parallel in a slightly wooden way. The rounded bump behind the neck is still visible and a little ambiguous.

### Row b
- Teenager waving: the hanging arm ends in a rounded, slightly bulbous cuff above the hand, which looks puffy. The shoulder knob under the raised arm is still there, only larger.
- Older woman: the near shoulder is broad and puffy, close to a padded or puff-sleeve look, which makes her torso read wider.
- Older man: no clear fault in the shoulder or sleeve. The sleeve is heavy but reads fine.
- Teenager arms out: a large round ball swells at the back of the shoulder and neck. It is the most noticeable fault in either row and reads as a lump rather than a hood or shoulder.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- Teenager waving: the hanging hand at the side is a large mitten shape that looks oversized.
- Older man: the hand on the lower broom shaft is a big mitten, and the upper hand partly covers the broom shaft in a confusing way.
- Teenager arms out: the hands are cupped upward like claws, and the pose reads more as "sleepwalking" than "holding out".

## 4. Choice

**Row b, fairly sure.** Its fuller sleeves and rounded shoulders suit a friendly cartoon style better and read more clearly at picture size. Its one serious fault is the oversized shoulder ball on the side-view teenager, and that should be reduced.
