# Review of pair_1.png (upper arms, shoulders, sleeves)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the sleeve is part of the shirt or dress outline. It ends in a hem, and the upper arm comes out from under it, so the arm reads as attached at the shoulder. In row b the sleeves are rounder and puffier, and the top of the upper arm is a flat-cut skin shape laid over the sleeve or shirt. On the three turned figures it looks pasted on, with a hard straight edge where skin meets cloth. On the front-facing child the sleeves stick out as puffs and the arms hang outside them.

## 2. Per figure

| Figure | Better row | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Child, front | a | In a the arms come cleanly out from under neat short sleeves; in b the puffed sleeves bulge sideways and the arms hang off their outer edge, which makes the shoulders look wide and the arms look stuck on. |
| Woman in dress, turned | a | In a the near arm continues out of a sleeve with a hem; in b the near upper arm starts with a straight-cut top laid over the yellow sleeve, so it looks like a separate piece placed in front of the dress. |
| Girl, side | a | In a the arm comes out below a short sleeve hem; in b the arm's flat top sits on top of a puffed sleeve, and a corner of the sleeve pokes out behind it. |
| Bearded man, turned | a | In a the near arm hangs from the T-shirt sleeve with a visible hem; in b the near upper arm is a thick slab with a flat top that covers the sleeve and shoulder, so there is no clear sleeve and the shoulder looks boxy. |

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Child: the sleeves are boxy, and their straight inner seam lines make the shoulders look square and stiff. The upper arms are a little thin next to the wide sleeves.
- Woman: the near sleeve is a flat panel with faint hem lines, so it is hard to tell where the sleeve ends. No sleeve shows on the far shoulder, and the far arm just appears from behind the dress.
- Girl: there is a small notch or kink in the outline at the back of the sleeve hem.
- Man: the sleeve hem lines are faint. The far-side sleeve barely reads, so the far arm seems to start from the shirt's side.

### Row b
- Child: the sleeves are puffed balloons that stick out past the arms. The arms hang outside the sleeve edge with a visible step, and the shoulders look too wide for a small child.
- Woman: the near upper arm has a hard straight top edge across the sleeve, so it looks pasted on rather than coming out of the sleeve. The sleeve fold line sits oddly above it.
- Girl: the arm's flat top is laid over the sleeve, and a lump of sleeve shows behind the arm.
- Man: the near upper arm is a thick rectangular slab whose top covers the sleeve. The sleeve hem disappears and the shoulder corner looks square and heavy. At thumbnail size the arm reads as a block hanging off the shirt.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- Child: the shorts have an odd round notch at the crotch.
- Woman: the dress has sharp pinch dents at the waist on both sides. The hands are mitten blobs, and the far hand has a loose curl for a thumb.
- Man: the far hand's thumb is a loose curl that is hard to read. The mouth is a small dark shape sitting on the beard.
- All figures: the hands are rounded mittens that do not clearly show which way they face.

## 4. Choice

Row a. It reads as cleaner and friendlier because the arms grow out of the sleeves instead of being stuck on top of them. How sure: sure.
