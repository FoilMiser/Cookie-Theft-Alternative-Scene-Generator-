# Review of pair_5 (upper arms, shoulders, sleeves)

## 1. How the rows differ

Row a gives the long sleeves a rounded, puffed cap that sits on or above the shoulder line. The teenager's hoodie and the woman's cardigan look like inflated balloon sleeves, and the woman's are gathered at the wrist. Row b lowers and slopes the shoulders so each sleeve runs on from the body as one smoother, straighter tube. The worker's short sleeve in row b is larger and covers the whole shoulder under the vest, where row a shows a small sleeve on a narrow, rounded shoulder.

## 2. Per figure

1. **Teenager, hooded top, front: b.** In row a the shoulders are two round puffs that rise above the hood and make the body look padded, while row b's shoulders slope down from the hood into plain sleeves.
2. **Older woman, cardigan, turned: b.** Row a's sleeves balloon out and pinch in at the cuffs like a costume sleeve, while row b's sleeves are straight and sit naturally on a sloped shoulder.
3. **Older man, waistcoat, side: no difference.** Both rows draw the same pale sleeve with a round cap coming out of the waistcoat armhole, and I can see only a very slight change in the size of the cap.
4. **Worker, safety vest, turned: b.** In row b the short dark sleeve covers a full shoulder and leads into the arm, while row a's sleeve is a small cap on a narrow, knobby shoulder that sits awkwardly under the high, square vest.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Teenager: the shoulder caps are separate round bulges above the shoulder line, so the hoodie looks stuffed. The shoulders are very square and wide for a teenager.
- Teenager: there is a visible crease or step where each puffed cap meets the sleeve. The sleeves are thick, even tubes ending in narrow cuff bands, which gives an inflated, toy-like look.
- Woman: the sleeves are balloon-shaped, widest at mid-arm and pinched at the wrist. The near sleeve bulges well past the body outline, and the far shoulder has a bump on top.
- Woman: at full-figure size the puffed sleeves still read as bulky, as if the arms were padded.
- Man: the sleeve cap is a round knob wider than the arm below it, so the shoulder joint looks like a ball.
- Worker: the shoulder is narrow and rounded with a small sleeve, and the vest sits higher and wider than the shoulder. Arm and torso look like separate parts.
- Worker: at the top-left of the vest, where it meets the sleeve, the outline is untidy, with dark edges overlapping.

### Row b
- Teenager: the sleeves widen a little toward the wrist and splay slightly outward, and the cuffs are less clearly marked than in row a.
- Woman: the near sleeve is a plain straight cylinder with little shape at the elbow, so it is slightly stiff.
- Man: the same round knob-like sleeve cap as in row a. The cap looks a little broader here, and the waistcoat armhole outline around it is more prominent.
- Worker: the upper arm is very thick and the shoulders very broad, so the figure looks heavy. The large sleeve slightly crowds the vest's side edge.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- Man: the belt or waistband sticks out behind the trousers at the back, past the body outline.
- All figures: the hands are simple curled mitten shapes, and the man's hand sits against the trouser outline so the two are hard to separate.
- Teenager: in the large drawing the hands are cut off at the bottom of the frame.

## 4. Choice

**Row b, fairly sure.** Its sloped shoulders and smoother sleeves read as friendlier and clearer, most of all on the teenager, the woman and the worker. Row a's puffed caps and balloon sleeves look padded and fussy.
