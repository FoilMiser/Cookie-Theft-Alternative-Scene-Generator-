# Review of pair_9 (upper arms, shoulders, sleeves)

## 1. How the rows differ

Row a draws short, tight sleeve caps, and the upper arms come out high on the shoulder. On the man and the throwing woman the arm looks attached near the neck or laid over the shirt. Row b draws longer sleeves that cover more of the upper arm and puts the arm lower on a broader, rounder shoulder. On the girl, though, b's sleeve runs into a long, straight arm line that starts at the neck. In the small whole figures the difference is slight, and it shows mostly on the man and the throwing woman.

## 2. Per figure, left to right

1. **Woman, both arms out (side): b.** The sleeve in b is a clean tube that flows out of the dress outline. In a the sleeve top ends in a corner, and the round end of the upper arm shows as a bump over the dress edge.
2. **Man sweeping: b.** In b the green sleeve covers the top of the near arm and ends in a clear hem. In a the near arm looks pasted onto the front of the shirt, with almost no sleeve and a ragged edge at the shoulder.
3. **Woman who has just thrown (side): b.** In b the raised arm comes out of a sleeve cap on a real shoulder. In a it sprouts from the top of the torso right beside the neck, so the shoulder looks missing.
4. **Girl pushing (side): a.** In a the puffed sleeve reads as a rounded shoulder that the arms hang from. In b the top arm line runs straight from the neck to the hands, so the arm looks too long and the shoulder flat.

## 3. Faults

**Row a**
- Woman, arms out: the sleeve top ends in a hard corner, and the round end of the upper arm shows over the dress edge, as if the arm were stuck on.
- Man sweeping: the near arm sits on top of the shirt front with almost no sleeve. The sleeve edge at the shoulder is jagged and does not read as a hem.
- Woman who has just thrown: the raised arm joins the body right at the neck, high above where a shoulder should be. The purple top looks sleeveless, and the joint is a round blob.
- Girl pushing: the puff sleeve is small, and the far arm's join behind it is a little unclear (minor).

**Row b**
- Woman, arms out: the line where the far arm meets the dress, lower down, is a little hard to read (minor).
- Man sweeping: the near sleeve is long and reaches nearly to the elbow, so the upper arm looks short (minor).
- Woman who has just thrown: a long sleeve or hem line cuts across the chest well into the torso and looks like a stray stroke. The shoulder is broad and makes the upper body look bulky.
- Girl pushing: the arm starts at the neck with no shoulder bump. The sleeve hem line runs across the body, and the arms look stretched.

**Other things that look wrong (both rows)**
- Girl: in the large view the head is very large and the neck very thin, and the head seems to float over the body.
- Woman who has just thrown: the back hand is a lumpy mitten shape, and the forward hand tapers to a point with no fingers.
- Woman, arms out: the hands are bunched and claw-like, and the waist of the dress has a notch on the left side.
- Man sweeping: the broom handle passes in front of the near hand, which makes the grip hard to read.

## 4. Choice

**Row b**, fairly sure. It gives the man and the throwing woman believable shoulders and sleeves and slightly cleans up the first woman. Its main weakness is the girl, where row a is better, and the stray chest line on the throwing woman.
