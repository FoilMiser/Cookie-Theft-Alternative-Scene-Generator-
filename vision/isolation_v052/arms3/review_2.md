# Review of pair_2.png (arms3)

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the short sleeves are small caps or narrow strips that sit on top of the shoulder. The raised upper arms come out of them at a kink, and some arms look as if they start above the shoulder line. In row b the sleeves are larger. They wrap the root of each arm and follow the direction of the upper arm, with a visible hem, so the arm seems to grow out of the shoulder. The shoulders in row b also read a little broader and rounder.

## 2. Per figure, left to right

1. **Man waving: b.** In a, the raised arm leaves a squared red bump at a sharp angle. In b, the red sleeve runs up along the arm and ends in a clean hem.
2. **Woman reaching up: b.** In a, the purple sleeve on the raised arm is a thin strip beside the neck, so the arm seems to start behind the head. In b, the sleeve is a full cuff around the arm root and the lowered arm also has a clear sleeve hem.
3. **Child pointing up, from the side: b.** In a, the near arm is bare from the top of the shoulder and looks pasted over the shirt. In b, a yellow sleeve with a hem covers the upper arm and the arm comes out below it.
4. **Man with both arms raised: b.** In b, the red sleeve on the right raised arm is wider and wraps the arm, and the shoulders are broader. In a, that sleeve is a narrow tube and the left arm comes out of a crease in the shirt.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Man waving: the raised arm's sleeve is a boxy cap and the arm bends out of it at a kink. The shoulder reads as square and stiff.
- Woman reaching up: the raised sleeve is only a sliver, and the arm seems to start at the neck or behind the head instead of at the shoulder. The raised arm also looks thin where it meets the body.
- Child pointing up: the near upper arm has no sleeve over it. It starts at the very top of the shoulder and is laid over the shirt like a separate piece.
- Man with both arms raised: the right sleeve is a narrow tube. The left arm starts from a crease in the front of the shirt rather than from a shoulder, and the shoulders look narrow.

### Row b
- Man waving: the raised sleeve is long, which makes the shirt look slightly stretched up that arm, but it is acceptable.
- Woman reaching up: the raised sleeve is a bit bulky and stands out from the shoulder like a separate cuff.
- Child pointing up: the hem line on the sleeve is faint, and the sleeve edge on the back is a little ragged.
- Man with both arms raised: the left arm still starts from a crease on the chest, so its shoulder is unclear.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- Man waving: the hanging arm on the right has no sleeve and comes out from under the shirt edge, so that side looks sleeveless. The hand at its end is a loose loop with no clear fingers.
- Man with both arms raised: the near forearm and hand cover most of his face, including one eye. In the small whole figure this reads as a man hiding his face, not raising both arms.
- Child pointing up: the pointing finger is drawn as a long tube with a rounded tip, more like a stick than a finger. The head is very large with almost no neck, and the far raised arm comes from behind the head.
- Woman reaching up: the lowered hand is a stubby mitten shape.

## 4. Choice

**Row b**, fairly sure. Its sleeves and shoulders read as cloth wrapped around a real shoulder, which is clearer and friendlier. The gain is small at the size the figures have in a picture, and some faults are the same in both rows (the face covered by an arm, the sleeveless hanging arm).
