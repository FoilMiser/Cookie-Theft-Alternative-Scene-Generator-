# Review of pair_6 (arms, shoulders, sleeves)

## 1. How the rows differ

Row a gives every figure short, rounded "puff" sleeves. Each sleeve bulges out and then pinches in at a gathered cuff, and the arm comes out of it narrower than the sleeve. Shoulders are rounded and slope down. Row b gives straight T-shirt sleeves with a flat hem, broader and squarer shoulders, and upper arms that are wide where they leave the sleeve and taper to the wrist.

## 2. Per figure

- **Man, front: b.** The straight hem and the arm that fills the sleeve read as an ordinary T-shirt; in a the pinched cuffs make the arms look as if they hang from narrow stalks.
- **Man, turned: b.** The near sleeve in b is a clean shape with a clear hem; in a it is a lumpy puff with a crease, and the arm sits pinched under it.
- **Man, side: b.** In b the sleeve is a clear rectangle on the side of the torso with a hem line. In a the sleeve hardly separates from the torso and shows only as a small bulge at the cuff.
- **Woman, back: a.** In b her shoulders become very wide and square and her upper arms thick, so she looks bulky. The rounder, narrower shoulders in a suit her better, even with the puff-sleeve pinch.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Man, front: the sleeves balloon out and pinch at the cuff. The arm is thinner than the sleeve opening, so it looks loosely stuck on, and the shoulders droop.
- Man, turned: the near sleeve is a lumpy puff with an odd crease line. The arm starts low and looks pinched where it joins.
- Man, side: the sleeve is barely drawn; only a cuff bump marks it, so the shirt reads as sleeveless with a bracelet.
- Woman, back: the sleeves pinch at the cuff (less badly), and the arms look thin under them.
- All figures: the gathered cuffs read as puff or padded sleeves, not a plain T-shirt.

### Row b
- Man, front: the shoulders are very wide and the upper arms thick at the top, which makes him slightly heavy and wing-like.
- Man, turned: the near shoulder is broad and boxy; otherwise clean.
- Man, side: no real fault; the sleeve is a little stiff and rectangular.
- Woman, back: the shoulders are very broad and square, the sleeves flare outward, and the upper arms are thick, so the figure looks bulky and masculine. This shows at small size too.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- Man, front: a skin-coloured wedge shows between the sleeve and the torso at the armpits.
- Man, turned: the far arm has no sleeve at all and comes straight out of the torso edge. The far hand is an open loop shape that reads oddly.
- Man, side: the hand overlaps the trouser leg, and the line of the leg runs through the fist.
- Man, front: the hands curl inward with small hook lines, which look a little claw-like.
- Woman, back: the hair is a solid helmet-and-block shape that covers the neck entirely.

## 4. Choice

**Row b**, fairly sure. Its sleeves and shoulders read clearly as a plain T-shirt on all three views of the man, which suits a friendly, clear cartoon. Its main cost is the over-broad shoulders, which are worst on the woman.
