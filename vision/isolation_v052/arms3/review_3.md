# Review of pair_3.png (upper arms, shoulders, sleeves)

## 1. How the rows differ

Row a gives the shirts square, boxy shoulders and T-shirt sleeves with a straight, flat hem, and the arm comes straight down out of the sleeve. Row b rounds the shoulders off and makes the sleeves shorter and puffier, with a bulging, uneven hem. In b the top of the arm shows as a rounded lump where it meets the sleeve. On the side view (figure 3), b drops the separate sleeve shape almost entirely, so the arm looks stuck onto the side of the shirt.

## 2. Figure by figure

1. Man, front: **a**. The flat hems and square shoulders read straight away as a plain T-shirt, while b's puffed, lumpy sleeve ends look like cap sleeves with a bump where the arm comes out.
2. Man, turned: **a**. The near sleeve in a is a clear, even T-shirt sleeve, while in b it is short and bulging, with a hem that tilts and a notch where the arm joins.
3. Man, side: **a**. In a the sleeve is a clear shape with a hem across the arm; in b there is only a faint curved line on the shirt, and the rounded top of the arm sits in the middle of the torso like a separate tube.
4. Woman, back: **no difference**. Both read as a short-sleeved top. b's shoulders are softer, and its sleeve hem bulges a little on the outer side, but at picture size the two look the same.

Count: a = 3, b = 0, no difference = 1.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Figure 1: the shoulders are very square and wide, almost like shoulder pads. Where the sleeve hem meets the torso, the arm makes a small pinch into the red shirt.
- Figure 2: the near sleeve is wide and hangs a little off the shoulder. The far shoulder has no sleeve at all, and the far arm comes straight out of the side of the torso.
- Figure 3: the sleeve is a stiff rectangle with a flat hem, and the arm hangs far forward over the front of the torso.
- Figure 4: the shoulders are square and broad for the woman, and the sleeve hems are straight slants that look a little stiff.

### Row b
- Figure 1: the sleeves are puffed, with lumpy hems, and the top of the arm bulges out where it leaves the sleeve, so the join looks swollen.
- Figure 2: the near sleeve is short and puffy, with a tilted, notched hem. The far arm again has no sleeve and comes out of the torso side.
- Figure 3: there is no clear sleeve, only a faint curved line on the shirt. The arm starts as a rounded stub halfway down the torso and looks pasted on.
- Figure 4: the sleeve hem bulges outward on the outer side, and the top of the arm shows a small bump below it.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- Figure 2: the far (right) hand in the large drawing is an open outlined loop rather than a filled hand, and the far arm has no shoulder or sleeve.
- Figure 3: the near hand overlaps the pants, with the pant-leg line running past it, and the arm hangs well in front of the body. In the small figure, the two legs nearly merge into one.
- Figure 2: the lower lip sits low under the mustache, almost on the chin.
- Figure 4: the hair is a dark helmet with a lower block and a hard horizontal seam across it, so it reads more like a hood than hair.

## 4. Choice

**Row a.** It gives a cleaner, more readable T-shirt with a clear sleeve on every view, and it does much better on the side view. I am **fairly sure**.
