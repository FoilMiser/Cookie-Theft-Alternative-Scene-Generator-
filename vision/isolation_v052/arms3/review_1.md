# Review of arms3/pair_1.png

## 1. How the rows differ

In row a the shoulders are narrow and slope down, and on raised arms the sleeve is drawn as a thin tube that runs up the upper arm, so it looks stuck onto the corner of the shirt. In row b the shoulders are broader and rounder, the sleeves are short, puffed caps that stay at the shoulder, and the arm comes out from under the cap. Row b's lowered arms are also thicker at the top and start lower, under a rounded shoulder.

## 2. Per figure

1. Man waving: **no difference**. Both rows have a short sleeve on the raised arm and a bare lowered arm. Row b's shirt top is a little broader, but at picture size the two look almost the same.
2. Woman reaching up: **b**. In row a the purple sleeve on the raised arm is a long narrow tube with a notch at the armpit and looks grafted on. In row b it is a short cap and the arm comes out of it naturally.
3. Child pointing up, side view: **b**. In row b the near arm hangs from a rounded shoulder bulge. In row a it looks like a flat stick pasted to the side of the shirt under a straight hem line.
4. Man with both arms raised: **b**. Row b has rounder, broader shoulders and a short red cap on the right arm. Row a's sleeve is a thin tube along the upper arm on a narrow, sloping torso top.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Woman: the sleeve on the raised arm is a long thin tube stretched along the upper arm. It meets the torso at a pinched point with a notch, so it reads as a separate piece.
- Man with both arms raised: the same tube sleeve on his right (viewer's right) arm. The torso narrows toward the neck, so the shoulders look sloped and weak.
- Child: the near arm is narrow and joins under a flat horizontal sleeve hem with no shoulder shape, so it looks glued on.
- Man waving: there is little shoulder mass on the raised side, and the sleeve is a small wedge.

### Row b
- Woman: the lowered arm (viewer's left) is thick and begins with a flat, square cut at the sleeve hem, which looks a little stumpy.
- Child: the near arm is quite wide for a child and starts low under the shoulder bulge, which makes it look heavy.
- Man with both arms raised: the fold lines where the near arm leaves the shirt are a bit busy.
- Man waving: same as row a. The shirt has a sleeve on the raised arm and none on the lowered arm.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- Man waving: the shirt has a sleeve on the raised side but none on the lowered side, so it looks like a tank top on one side only.
- Man with both arms raised: the near arm crosses right over his face and hides one eye and most of the face. The hand ends on top of his head.
- Child: the pointing arm comes out from behind the head with no visible shoulder or sleeve, so it seems to grow from the head or neck. A small yellow sliver also shows behind the neck.

## 4. Choice

**Row b**, fairly sure. Its rounder shoulders and short cap sleeves read as friendlier and clearer, especially on the woman and on the man with both arms raised. Row b's lowered arms are a bit heavy, and the pose problems above are the same in both rows.
