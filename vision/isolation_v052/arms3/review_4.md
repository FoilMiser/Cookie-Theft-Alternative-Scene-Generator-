# Review of pair_4.png (upper arms, shoulders, sleeves)

## 1. How the rows differ

Row b draws the upper arms and sleeves thicker and the shoulders broader and rounder. In row a the arms are thin and tube-like and come out of narrow shoulders. In row b there is more cloth at the shoulder cap and the arms look more solid. Row b also makes the shoulder or hood bump behind the neck bigger on both teenagers.

## 2. Figure by figure

1. **Teenager waving: b.** In b the raised arm and the hanging arm are thicker, and a shoulder rises behind the head. In a both arms look thin, like sticks, next to the bulky hoodie.
2. **Older woman reaching up: b.** In b the cardigan has a rounded shoulder on the lowered side, and the raised sleeve is thick enough to match the body. In a the lowered arm is narrow and the raised arm comes out of a slim, sloped shoulder that looks weak.
3. **Older man sweeping: b, by a small margin.** In b the cream sleeves are fuller and the near shoulder cap is rounded above the vest. In a the near upper arm is thinner and has a small notch where it meets the vest. The far sleeve is much the same in both rows.
4. **Teenager with arms held out, side view: no difference.** In both rows the arms start from a rounded end in the middle of the chest, with no shoulder above them. Row b's arms are a little thicker, but its bump behind the neck is bigger and reads less clearly, so neither row is clearly better.

## 3. Faults

### Row a
- Teenager waving: both arms are thin for the hoodie. The raised arm meets the chest at a round lump below the drawstrings, which looks like a separate ball, not a shoulder.
- Teenager waving: the hanging arm is a narrow tube that tapers toward the wrist and sits apart from the body.
- Older woman: the lowered arm is thin and stick-like. The raised arm joins at a narrow point on a sloped shoulder, so the shoulder line is almost missing.
- Older man: the near upper arm is thin, and there is a notch or step at the top where the sleeve meets the vest. The far sleeve sticks out past the vest as a pointed puff at the elbow.
- Teenager side view: there is no shoulder. Both arms start from a rounded end on the chest and look stuck on, and the bump behind the neck is a lump with no clear shape.

### Row b
- Teenager waving: there is still a round lump where the raised arm meets the chest, below the drawstrings. The bump behind the head is large and slightly shapeless.
- Older woman: the base of the raised sleeve is very wide and runs far down the side of the body, so it looks a little like a wing or cape. The lowered shoulder is somewhat boxy or padded.
- Older man: the far sleeve still sticks out past the vest as a pointed puff, as in a.
- Teenager side view: the hood or shoulder bump behind the neck is a big balloon, like a neck pillow. The arms still start from a rounded cap in the middle of the chest with no shoulder joint, so they still look stuck on.

### Other things that look wrong (both rows)
- Teenager waving: the horizontal line across the hoodie under the drawstrings looks like a stray seam, and the big pocket shape is placed high and to one side.
- Teenager side view: there is a horizontal line across the torso, and the small light tabs at the chest (drawstrings) poke out oddly near where the arms start.
- Older man: the hand on the broom gripping lower down looks like a large flat mitten.

## 4. Choice

**Row b.** Its fuller arms and rounder shoulders suit a friendly, clear cartoon style better, and in the small whole figures the arms read more easily. The side-view teenager is equally weak in both rows. **Fairly sure.**
