# Description of test_3

## Free description

It is a sunny day in a back garden with a wooden fence, a tree, and a house with a red roof on the right. Three people are doing jobs in the garden. In the front centre, a girl in a yellow dress is raking a small pile of brown leaves. In the middle back, an older woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt is using a paint roller on a long handle to paint the wooden fence blue. A paint tin stands on the grass by her feet. Part of the fence behind and to the left of her is already blue. On the right, an older man in a cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is watering a round flower bed of colourful flowers with a teal watering can. There is an empty metal swing set with a red seat on the left, a paved path running down the left side, and a gate in the fence at the far left. Nothing looks dangerous or about to go wrong. The odd thing is that the woman is holding her roller up in the air to her left, well away from the blue part of the fence, and the blue section stands apart from where she is working.
