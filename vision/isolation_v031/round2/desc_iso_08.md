## What I see

Two cartoon children are on a red seesaw in a grassy playground, with a dark railing fence running along the back. On the left, the lower end, a dark-skinned child with braided or coiled hair in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on the plank holding on with both hands. On the right, the raised end, a smaller, lighter-brown child with short dark hair in a pink dress or top sits holding on the same way, and they face each other. The seesaw is a red plank on a grey triangular support with a round pivot, and a soft shadow lies on the grass beneath it. A dark grey pole at the upper left and a brown post or block at the upper right are cut off by the edge of the picture.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The dark pole at upper left and the brown shape at upper right are only partly shown. The pole could be a swing-set leg or a lamp post; the brown shape could be a tree trunk or a post.
- The left child's end of the plank is low but does not touch the grass, and the shadow is one flat ellipse, so the seesaw reads as slightly hovering above the ground at that end. The support does stand on the ground.
- The left child's foot (grey shoe) hangs below the plank and does not reach the ground, even though that is the down end. The foot and shoe look a little awkwardly shaped, as if flattened or doubled.
- There are small dark shapes at each child's hands and in front of their bodies. These could be seesaw handles, but they are small and hard to make out, and could also be read as dark marks passing through the torso or thigh.
- The right child's lower half is hard to read: the pink garment could be a dress or a top with shorts. Only one leg and a yellow shoe are clearly visible, and the leg seems to emerge from behind the plank, so the sitting position looks a bit odd.
- The two children differ in size, so they could be different ages, or the difference could be just the way they are drawn.
