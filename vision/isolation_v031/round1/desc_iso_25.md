## What I see

An older man with grey hair, a blue-grey flat cap, a brown vest over a white long-sleeved shirt, dark trousers and brown shoes sits at the low end of a red seesaw, reading an open book he holds in front of him. A young girl with dark hair in two buns, wearing a pink dress and yellow shoes, sits at the raised end of the same seesaw, holding a dark handle with her hands near her chest and her mouth in a small round "o". The seesaw pivots on a grey-blue triangular stand on green grass, with a dark soft shadow under it and a dark metal railing fence across the top of the picture. Short curved lines under the girl's feet look like motion marks, as though her end has just moved or her legs are swinging.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The girl's round mouth could be read as surprise or alarm at being lifted up, or as her just talking, whistling or going "ooh" for fun.
- The curved lines under her feet could be movement lines (seesaw or feet moving) or could be read as thin bits of something on the ground or in the air, such as wire or grass; they float in the air beneath her.
- The man looks absorbed in the book and does not look at the girl, so it could be read as him ignoring her or simply not noticing she is stuck up in the air. It is also unclear whether he is deliberately weighing his end down.
- The man's seating looks a little odd: his legs drop down past the plank with one foot resting forward and the other just behind, and his lower body seems to overlap the board, so it is hard to tell whether he sits on top of the plank or whether his leg passes in front of or through it.
- The man's arm with the book looks long and rather tube-like, and the hand at the end of it seems to rest on the plank rather than clearly gripping the book.
- The girl's lower legs bend back sharply and her feet hang well below the plank. She looks small and slightly balanced on the board rather than firmly seated on it.
- There is a dark curved shape cut off at the very top right edge of the picture and a dark vertical edge at the right border; I cannot tell what either one is.
