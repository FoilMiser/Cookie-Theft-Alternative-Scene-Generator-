## What I see
There are no people or animals in this one. On the green lawn in the centre is a round dark teal coil on a dark stand, which looks like a garden hose reel, with a length of teal hose curving away to the lower left and ending in a yellow pointed nozzle lying on the grass. To the left is a brown soil flower bed with tall green-stemmed flowers in red, orange, pink, purple and yellow. At the top right is the front of a cream house with a blue door, a small round yellow door knob and a grey doorstep, and a dark green bush shows at the top behind the flowers. The hose seems to be for watering the flowers.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The coil could be read as a hose reel on a stand or, less likely, as a wheel or a round fan or speaker sitting on a small box, though the hose coming out of it makes the reel reading the more likely one.
- The hose leaves the reel at its lower left rim and appears simply to start there, without a visible connection point. It also passes in front of the dark stand, so its join with the reel is a little unclear.
- The yellow nozzle is a plain pointed wedge, which could be read as a spray nozzle or as a pencil tip or a funnel. No water is coming out of it.
- The round yellow thing on the door could be a door knob or a peephole; it sits high and central on the door, which is an unusual place for a knob.
- The doorstep's bottom edge lines up with the top of the reel, and the reel overlaps the step slightly, so it is hard to tell whether the reel is standing in front of the door or touching the step.
- The flowers look large compared with the door, but that may be down to the cartoon style or the framing.
