# iso_10

## What I see
An older man with grey hair and a grey-blue flat cap stands on green grass in front of a wooden plank fence. He wears a brown waistcoat over a white long-sleeved shirt, dark grey trousers and brown shoes. He holds a teal watering can out in front of him, and a stream of blue water drops pours from its spout onto a round bed of brown soil. The bed holds a row of colourful flowers (yellow, orange, red, pink, purple) on green stems. A dark green bush sits at the right edge, partly cut off.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The watering can is small and looks tucked close against his hand and waist. I can see one hand on it, but cannot clearly see how the handle is gripped or where the other arm is; only one arm is visible.
- The water stream starts as a band of thin blue lines and breaks into drops. It passes in front of the flowers and seems to end at about stem height, in mid-air among the flowers, rather than clearly landing on the soil. It could read as watering the flowers or as water falling in front of them.
- His feet overlap, so one shoe sits nearly on top of the other, which makes the stance look a little narrow and pinched.
- His neck and head sit slightly forward of the torso, and the torso looks narrow from the side. This is mildly stiff but not obviously broken.
- The flowers are tall relative to him, reaching about his thigh. This may just be the cartoon style.
