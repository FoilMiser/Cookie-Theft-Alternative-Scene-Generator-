## What I see

An older man with grey hair and a moustache or dark mark by his mouth, wearing a blue-grey flat cap, white shirt with rolled sleeves, brown waistcoat, dark trousers and brown shoes, stands on green grass in front of a wooden plank fence. He holds a teal watering can in front of him and is watering a round bed of brown soil. Blue streams of water arc from the can down onto the flowers, which are bright red, orange, yellow, pink and purple on tall green stems. A dark green bush sits behind the flower bed at the right.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The watering can is small and mostly hidden by his hands and the first flowers; the spout is hard to pick out, so the water seems to start from a short point just past the can. The can could also read as a teal scoop or funnel at first glance.
- The water is drawn as several dotted or dashed blue lines that overlap into a lattice, so it could read as water drops or as a net or string hanging over the flowers. It runs in front of the flowers rather than clearly landing on them.
- His far arm is not clearly visible; the hands holding the can merge together, and it is hard to say which hand is doing what.
- The man's torso looks narrow and his legs are long and thin, and his feet overlap at the bottom, so he looks slightly stiff and tall for his width. His trousers bend oddly near the hip, as if he is leaning backwards while the arm reaches forward.
- The shape on his face near the mouth could be a moustache or a smile line; it is not clear which.
- The flowers are very tall and thin for a garden bed, with stems standing straight up like sticks, which looks a little stiff but not broken.
