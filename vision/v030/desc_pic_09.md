# Picture 9

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house on the left, a wooden fence and gate behind, and a paved path on the right. On the left a boy in a blue hoodie stands with his hands on his hips and a metal bucket upside down on his head, as if it were a hat, which is the odd thing here. Next to him, a small girl in a yellow top and orange shorts is flying a yellow kite; the string runs from her raised hand up across the sky to the kite above the clouds on the right. On the right a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is painting the wooden fence with a roller, and a section of the fence is already bright blue. On the grass behind him are a paint tin and a paint tray. A white dog with brown spots is walking to the left away from the paint tray, leaving a line of blue paw prints on the grass behind it, so it has clearly stepped in the paint. Its paws are blue, and the man does not seem to have noticed. A hose reel, a wheelbarrow and a watering can lie around the garden, and nobody is using them.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The bucket could be read as a silly hat that the boy has chosen to wear, or as a bucket stuck on his head; he looks calm with hands on hips, so it seems deliberate.
- The roller looks a little like a brush or mallet on a stick; the blue block at the top is roughly roller-shaped and rests on the top of the fence.
- The blue area ends in a neat rectangle and runs into the fence's lower rail; the top roller end sticks up slightly above the fence, so it is partly against the sky.
- The hose reel sits very close to the flower bed and overlaps its edge.
- The kite string passes in front of the fence, trees and a cloud-free part of the sky without any problem, but it seems unusually long and thin for the small girl.
