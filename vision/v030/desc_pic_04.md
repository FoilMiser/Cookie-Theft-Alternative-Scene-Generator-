# Picture 4

## Free description
This is a windy, sunny day in a back garden with a house on the right, a wooden fence and a gate at the left end of the fence, and a paved path down the left. In the back middle, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is standing on the lawn with both arms raised and his mouth open, reaching up in surprise for his orange cap, which has been blown off and is flying just above and to the left of his hands. White wind lines sweep across the sky, and small leaves are being blown off the tree on the right and around the man's head. In front of him, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is kicking an orange ball with his leg stretched out to the left, with motion lines under his foot. On the right an older woman with grey hair and glasses is holding a teal watering can over the flower bed, but the wind is bending the stream of water, so that it arcs up and away toward the house rather than falling on the flowers, with drops flying off the top. The bent water stream looks odd, but it fits the wind. No animals are visible.

## Inventory
- Man in red shirt and blue jeans (back, centre): brown hair; both arms stretched up, mouth open, surprised, trying to catch his flying cap.
- Boy in red cap and blue hoodie (front, centre): dark trousers, white shoes; one leg swung forward, kicking the orange ball toward the left, arms out for balance.
- Older woman (right of centre, by flower bed): grey bun, glasses, teal top, grey skirt; holding a teal watering can, watering the flowers but the water is being blown.
- Orange cap (centre, in the air above the man): tumbling in the air, blown off the man's head.
- Orange ball with a white stripe (front, centre-left): right at the boy's foot, low off the ground, being kicked; a small shadow under it.
- Stream of water (right of centre): curves up from the watering can and bends over to the right, with two or three drops at the top, not reaching the flowers in the normal way.
- Teal watering can: held by the older woman, partly hidden among the flowers.
- Flying leaves: a few by the tree on the right and a few by the man's head, carried by the wind.
- White wind lines: in the sky at left, near the man's hat, near the tree, and at the upper right.
- Flower bed (right, by the house): red, yellow, orange, purple and pink flowers on a mound of soil.
- House (right), blue door, window, red roof; wooden fence and brown gate (back left); paved path (front left); tree (back right); two clouds and sun (upper left).

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The man could be trying to catch his hat as it blows away, or he could be throwing his arms up in alarm; both fit. He could also be cheering, but the open mouth and the hat suggest surprise.
- The cap could be read as a strange orange bird or leaf at a glance because of how it is tilted; the button on top makes it read as a cap.
- The water stream shoots upward against gravity, which looks strange until you notice the wind. It could also be read as a hose-like arc, as the stream starts below the flowers' height and rises.
- The woman's left side overlaps the flowers, and the can is hard to see among them; she looks close enough to be standing against or in the flower bed.
- The boy is wearing his own red cap and the man's cap is orange, so the flying hat belongs to the man rather than to the boy, but this is inferred.
