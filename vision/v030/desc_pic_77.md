# Picture 77

## Free description

A sunny park with a pond on the left, a winding path on the right, hills behind, a tall black lamp post, and a large tree on the right. A man with short dark hair and a beard, in a green shirt and beige trousers, stands by the pond, leaning his head down and sprinkling seeds or crumbs from a paper bag; a trail of small dots falls from his hand to the ground, where a small red bird is pecking at a scattering of them. In the front centre, a girl with dark braided hair, a yellow top and orange shorts holds an empty ice cream cone in one hand and puts her other hand up to her head, looking upset or dismayed. Her ice cream has clearly dropped: a pink splat with sprinkles lies on the grass by her feet, and a brown dog with a red collar or bandana is bending down and licking it. A smaller girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes stands further back near the lamp post, holding a yellow balloon on a string. The story seems to be that the girl has dropped her ice cream and the dog is eating it, while the others carry on with what they are doing. Nothing looks impossible.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The braided girl's hand on her head could mean she is upset, shocked or worried, or she could just be scratching her head. Her expression is hard to make out at this size. The fallen ice cream makes upset the most likely reading.
- The dots near the ice cream splat: there are a few small dots near the girl's feet and the dog. They could be spilled sprinkles, or they could be some of the man's seeds. It is not clear.
- The man's seeds fall quite far and land near the bird, but the falling line looks a little stiff and evenly spaced, like a dotted line.
- The dog's head: it is pointed straight down and seen from above, with a rounded, ear-coloured shape, so it takes a moment to read it as a head. It also looks a little odd or doll-like.
- The man overlaps the pond, so he seems to be standing at the edge or in front of it. His feet are on grass, so I read him as in front of the pond and not in it.
- The man's bag seems to pass in front of his hip and overlap the pond edge; it is clearly held but the holding arm is partly hidden.
