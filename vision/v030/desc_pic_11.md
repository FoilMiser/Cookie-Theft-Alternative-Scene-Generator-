# Picture 11

## Free description
This is a calm, sunny park with green hills and trees in the background, a blue pond on the left, a winding path on the right, a tall black lamp post in the middle and a green rubbish bin beside it. In the front on the left, a woman or older girl in a yellow dress and red shoes is walking to the right while reading an open book held out in front of her, with her eyes on the book. Directly ahead of her, right in her path, is a closed cardboard box on the ground; she is not looking at it and seems about to trip over it. In the middle distance on the right, a small girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes sits on a wooden park bench reading a red book. A round flower bed sits at the front left just behind the walking woman. Nothing looks impossible, but the walking reader looks about to bump into or fall over the box.
