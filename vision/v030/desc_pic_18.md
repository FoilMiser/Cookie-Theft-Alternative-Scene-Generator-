# Picture 18

## Free description
This is a very busy sunny back garden with a cream house and red roof on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a stone path on the right. A boy in a red cap and blue top stands near the fence holding a string that leads up to a purple kite stuck in the tree; he seems to be tugging at it, trying to free it. A red bird is perched on the fence just above him, and an orange cat is creeping along the top of the fence toward the bird, looking as though it is about to pounce. On the right by the fence, an older man in a flat grey cap and brown waistcoat is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller, and a paint tin stands near him beside a shallow paint tray. A white dog with brown spots is on the path at the far right, and a trail of blue pawprints leads from the paint tray across the grass to the dog, whose paws are blue, showing it has stepped in the paint and walked it across the garden. In the centre, an older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan, is raking a small pile of leaves. Next to her, a white goat with a horn is eating a tuft of grass at the side of the raked pile. At the front right, a brown dog with a red collar is crouched with its rear up and tail in the air, playfully reaching for a yellow-green tennis ball on the edge of the path. On the left, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans kneels by the flower bed, reaching to plant or adjust a terracotta pot with pink flowers. Nearest the house, a woman with a dark ponytail, in a purple top and teal trousers, sits on a bench reading a red book, and does not notice that a blue bird is standing beside her on the bench, pecking at her sandwich. A green hose on a reel lies on the grass at the front, with its nozzle lying loose and not spraying, and at the front is a grey wheelbarrow with something odd about its wheel.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The wheelbarrow's wheel is drawn as a dark square with spokes, not a circle. This looks impossible, as a square wheel would not roll; it might be a deliberate oddity. The wheelbarrow also faces left with its handles to the right.
- The cat is on the fence and the red bird is a little way to its left; the cat appears to be heading the other way (head to the left, tail to the right), toward the bird. I read this as about to pounce, but it could be just walking.
- The red bird looks right, away from the cat, and seems unaware of it. It sits just above the boy and could be read as watching the boy.
- The kite's string runs from the boy's hand up to the kite in the tree. It could be read as him having just got it stuck, or trying to pull it free. There are a couple of light green leaf clusters on top of the kite, which look as if the tree's foliage is tangled over it.
- The pawprint trail runs between the tray and the white dog on the path. The dog could be read as having walked through the tray and off along the path. The trail does not quite reach the tray, as it starts just to the right of it, and it goes across the grass and on to the path.
- The kneeling man's reach: his hands are hidden behind the flowers or the pot, so he might be tending the flower bed rather than the pot. The pot appears in front of the flower bed, with the man's arm behind.
- The woman's bench partly overlaps the hose reel area and the bird's tail; the hose reel sits just to the right of the bench.
- The brown dog's front paw is close to the ball; I can't tell if it is touching. The two curved lines by its tail suggest a wagging tail. It seems playful.
- The goat's horn is single and dark, curved at the back of its head; the white body is hard to read and the back legs look stiff. It could be read as a goat or, less likely, a lamb or dog; the horn suggests goat.
