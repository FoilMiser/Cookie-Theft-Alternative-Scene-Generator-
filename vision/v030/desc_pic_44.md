# Picture 44

## Free description
This is a sunny playground on green grass, with a dark metal railing fence and green hills behind it. At front left, a small child with dark skin and braided hair, wearing a yellow shirt and orange shorts, is kneeling at a wooden sandbox building a sandcastle with three towers and a small red flag. On the right is a red seesaw on a grey triangular support. A little girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes is sitting on the high right end, looking startled with her mouth open and her feet dangling in the air with small motion lines beneath them. A large man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is sitting on the low left end, weighing it down, and he is calmly reading a book without looking at her. Since he is heavy and she is light, she is stranded up in the air and cannot get down, and he does not seem to have noticed. In the middle, a swing set with one red seat hangs empty, and a slide with a red frame stands empty at the back left. There is also a green bin, a grey rock, a bush and a big tree on the right.
