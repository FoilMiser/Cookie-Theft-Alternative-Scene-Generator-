A wooden fence runs across the back, with a wooden gate at the right end and a paved path leading from the gate towards the front of the picture. The only animal is a small orange cat curled up on the grass near the front, eyes closed, apparently asleep. Nothing much seems to be happening or about to happen, and nothing looks impossible.
