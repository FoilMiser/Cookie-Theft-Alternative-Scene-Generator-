# Picture 29

## Free description
A sunny park or playground with green grass, a dark railing fence along the back with bushes behind it, a tree on the left and a slide on the right. Two children are sitting on a red seesaw in the front left, facing each other. A smaller child with dark hair in a bun and a pink dress sits on the left end, which is tilted slightly higher, and a larger child with curly dark hair, a yellow top and orange shorts sits on the right end, which is tilted lower. Both are holding the handles in front of them. Their feet are off the ground or just near it. The playground otherwise looks empty, with a tall slide with a red frame and a ladder, a green rubbish bin by the fence, a bush and a grey rock. Nothing looks about to go wrong; it is a calm scene of two children playing, with the heavier-looking child down and the lighter one up, as you would expect.
