# Picture 1

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park with a pond, a winding path, a bench, a tall lamp post and green hills behind. In the front left an older man in a cap and brown waistcoat is holding out a frying pan and pouring water from it onto a flower bed, as though it were a watering can. To the far left a white spotted dog sits upright on the grass holding an open newspaper and appears to be reading it. In the middle a girl in a yellow top and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and has a hand up to her head, looking upset, while a pink splat of ice cream with sprinkles lies on the ground in front of her. A brown dog with a red collar is bending down to lick up the spilled ice cream. Further back, a small girl in a pink dress stands at the edge of the pond throwing crumbs from a paper bag to a white duck, which is swimming quickly toward the food with its beak down. On the right an older woman with grey hair and glasses sweeps a pile of autumn leaves with a broom on the path, and behind her a white goat grazes on a tuft of grass. At the far right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is holding a string that leads up to a purple kite stuck in a tree. A blue bird flies across the sky near the lamp post. The dog reading a newspaper and the man watering with a frying pan are the things that look wrong or impossible.

- White dog with brown patches and blue neckerchief (far left, middle distance): sitting up on its haunches holding an open newspaper, as if reading.
- Girl (front, centre): dark skin, braided hair, yellow top, orange shorts; holding an empty ice-cream cone in one hand, the other hand to her head, looking down at or away from the dropped ice cream, seems upset.
- Brown dog with red collar (front, centre-right): head down, licking or sniffing the pink ice-cream splat on the ground.
- Small girl in pink dress (middle, by the pond's right edge): holding a brown paper bag and reaching out toward the water, feeding the duck.
- White duck (on the pond, right side): swimming toward the crumbs, beak dipped, with white speed lines behind it.

- Tall black lamp post (centre) and green dustbin beside it.
- Winding sand-coloured path (right), two trees, a bush, hills, two clouds and a sun.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl's raised hand: could be crying and rubbing her eye, or holding her head in dismay.
- Whether the small girl is throwing crumbs at this moment or has just thrown them; the crumbs lie partly on the water and partly at the edge.
- The white lines behind the duck could mean it is rushing, or could just be ripples.
- The kite in the tree: the boy could be trying to pull it free, or could still think he is flying it; it reads as stuck.
- The broom and the older woman's arm overlap oddly with her skirt, but it still reads as sweeping.
- The goat has a dark curved horn or ear at the back of its head that is slightly hard to make out, and looks a bit like a dog-shaped animal at a glance.
- The newspaper is nearly as big as the dog and looks to be standing on its own in front of it rather than clearly held.
