# Picture 68

## Free description
A sunny playground with a slide, swing set, sandpit, bench and a dark railing fence at the back. In the front centre, a child with curly dark hair, a yellow top and orange shorts stands with a hand raised to their head, looking upset or dismayed, holding an empty ice cream cone in the other hand. On the ground in front of the child is a splatter of pink ice cream with sprinkles, and a tan dog with a red collar is bending down with its head to the ground, right next to the splatter, sniffing or licking it. Behind and to the left, a small white dog with a brown ear and a blue collar sits upright holding an open newspaper with its front paws, as if reading it, which is impossible for a real dog. On the right, a little girl in a pink dress stands near a tree, flying a yellow kite high in the sky on a long string; the kite has a tail with small bows. An older person with grey hair and glasses, wearing a teal jacket and shorts, sits on a bench drinking through a straw from a red cup. A blue bird flies in the sky at the upper right. The swing is empty and still, and a sandpit in the front left has a red bucket and a blue spade in it.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The child's hand to the head could be read as scratching their head, being upset, or crying. The expression is small and hard to read.
- The tan dog's head is partly turned so that the face is hard to see; it could be licking the ice cream, sniffing the ground or just standing next to it. The dog's head looks a little awkward, almost as if it were bent back on itself.
- The small white dog reading a newspaper is plainly impossible; it could be meant as an absurd detail or could be simply a dog sitting behind a paper left by someone.
- The kite string passes in front of the tree and very close to the older person's bench; it runs behind or just over the bench but does not seem to touch them.
- The older person could be a man or a woman; the figure wears shorts with bare legs.
