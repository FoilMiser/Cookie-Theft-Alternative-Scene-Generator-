# Picture 45

## Free description
This is a sunny town square paved with light grey slabs, with a row of colourful two-storey buildings with striped awnings behind it. On the left, an elderly man in a blue-grey cap, brown vest and dark trousers is walking along reading an open book, and a cardboard box is on the ground right in front of him, so he seems about to trip over it. Behind him is a fruit stall with a striped canopy, and a woman in a yellow dress is sweeping a pile of autumn leaves with a broom in front of a shop. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands further back, waving with one raised hand, with small lines by her hand. A red bird perches on top of a tall black street lamp. On a bench in the middle, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is sitting and reading a red book, while a blue bird on the bench beside him pecks at a sandwich he has not noticed. A red bicycle with square black wheels stands in front of him, which looks impossible. On the right, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans walks along eating a pink ice cream. At front right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is pointing up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of a tree; his mouth looks open as though he is calling for help or noticing it. At far right a brown dog with a red neckerchief is sitting upright and reading a newspaper, which a real dog could not do. There are also potted plants at both front corners and a green bin on the right.
