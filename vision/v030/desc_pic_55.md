# Picture 55

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence along the back, a gate in the fence at the far left with a paved path leading from it, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the back of the lawn, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is digging with a spade; there is a hole in front of him and a small heap of soil beside it. In the middle foreground an older man with a grey cap, grey hair, white sleeves and a brown vest stands holding a garden hose up in front of him with both arms. He seems to be looking straight into the nozzle end of the hose, and a single water drop is coming out of it near his face, so it looks as if he is checking why it isn't running properly. The hose runs from a reel next to the flower bed on the right, across the lawn and up to his hands. A white goat with small curved horns is standing right on the hose, near the point where the hose runs along the ground, and it seems that the goat standing on the hose is why the water has stopped. An orange fish is sitting in the tree behind the fence at the upper right, right where the trunk meets the leaves, which is impossible and does not belong there. If the man's water suddenly comes back on, or the goat steps off, he is about to get sprayed in the face.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The goat and the hose: the goat's feet appear to be on the hose where it is slightly flattened on the ground, which reads as blocking the water, but the feet could also just be standing beside or just behind it.
- The man's action could be read two ways: he is looking into the nozzle to see why it has stopped, or he is simply about to drink or adjust the nozzle. Looking into it seems more likely.
- The yellow bit at the end of the hose near his hands could be the nozzle tip; the man's hands and the nozzle are a bit bunched together and hard to separate.
- The fish in the tree looks deliberately out of place, but it is also oddly joined to the top of the trunk; the trunk seems to bend into it, so it could almost be read as the end of a branch shaped like a fish. Either way, it is a fish where there should not be one.
- The man's arm and vest area look slightly odd: the white sleeve wraps over the vest in a jagged shape.
- Goat's body looks a bit stiff and flat, with very straight legs, and its tail end has a squared-off shape.
