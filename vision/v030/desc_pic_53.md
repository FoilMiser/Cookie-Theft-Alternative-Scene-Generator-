# Picture 53

## 1. Free description
This is a sunny playground on a large lawn with a dark metal railing running across the back and bushy trees beyond it. On the right, two children are on a red seesaw: a small girl with a bun, a pink dress and yellow shoes sits on the left end, and an older-looking child with braided hair, a yellow top and orange shorts sits on the right end. They face each other and hold black handles, and the seesaw is tilted slightly down on the right-hand side, the side of the bigger child. In the front centre, an older woman with a grey bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey-blue skirt, is walking to the left and eating a pink ice cream. On the left there is a tall slide with a red frame, a ladder and an orange and yellow chute, with nobody on it. A green bin stands by the railing behind the slide, and a grey rock lies on the grass at the front left. Nothing seems about to go wrong, and nothing looks impossible. It reads as a calm, everyday scene with much of the top half just open sky.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous
- The seesaw's low end does not reach the ground, so it could be (a) in mid-motion, or (b) resting at a slight tilt because the right-hand child is heavier.
- The right-hand child's feet hang just off the ground rather than pushing on it, so it is hard to say whether the seesaw is moving.
- The woman has a white strip down the front of her cardigan, which could be a blouse showing or something she is carrying, such as paper. A blouse seems more likely.
- The woman might be connected to the children or just passing by. She is not looking at them.
- The seesaw plank seems to pass in front of the children's legs in a slightly odd way, but they read as sitting on it.
- Nothing is floating or passing through anything else in an obvious way.
