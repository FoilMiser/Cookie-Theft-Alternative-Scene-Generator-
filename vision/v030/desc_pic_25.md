# Picture 25

## Free description

This is a back garden under a grey sky, with three heavy dark clouds overhead and no sun. A man with dark skin, a beard or goatee, a green T-shirt, khaki trousers and brown shoes stands on the grass in the middle of the picture. He has one arm stretched up, pointing at the sky, and is looking up with wide eyes, as if he has noticed the storm clouds. It looks as though it is about to rain, though no rain is falling yet. Behind him to the left is a swing frame with an empty red seat hanging still, and further left a small red-roofed birdhouse on a tall post by a closed gate. A cream house with a blue door stands on the right, with a colourful flower bed and a bush in front of it. A teal watering can sits on the grass near the path. Nobody else is there, and nothing looks impossible.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- His expression could be read as worry, surprise or simple interest; the wide eyes favour surprise or alarm.
- Pointing could be aimed at the clouds, which I think is the intended reading of what he sees, or at something else in the sky that is not drawn.
- The dark patch on his lower face could be a beard or a shadowed open mouth.
- His raised arm seems to join the sleeve at a narrow, slightly pinched point at the shoulder, so it looks a little disconnected.
- The windows still look bright blue, as they would under a clear sky, which does not quite match the grey weather.
- Nothing is floating or passing through anything else.
