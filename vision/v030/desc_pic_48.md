# Picture 48

## 1. Free description
This is a sunny park with a pond on the right, a winding sand-coloured path on the left, a tall lamp post in the middle, and green hills behind. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes stands on the grass in front of the pond, fishing with a long rod. His line runs down to a red float sitting in a small ripple ring on the water. In the foreground, left of him, a small girl with dark hair in a bun, wearing a pink dress and yellow shoes, is striding or running to the right. She holds a pink ice cream up near her mouth and seems to be heading in the general direction of the boy or the pond. The wooden bench by the path is empty, and a green bin stands beside the lamp post. It is a calm scene and nothing obviously wrong or impossible is happening. The only thing that might be about to happen is the girl reaching the boy.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl could be (a) running up to the boy, or (b) just walking past in the same direction. They do not look at each other.
- Her stride could be read as a walk or a run; the legs are wide apart, so running or skipping seems likely.
- The ice cream is small and it is hard to see the cone; it could at first glance be a flower or lollipop, but ice cream seems the best reading.
- The ripple ring could be a fish nibbling or just the float resting on the water.
- Nothing looks floating or passing through anything. No one seems to be in any trouble.
