# Picture 2

## Free description
This is a sunny town square with a row of coloured shops behind a paved area. On the left an older woman with grey hair and glasses walks along reading an open book, and she is heading toward a cardboard box sitting on the ground right in front of her, so she looks about to trip over it. Behind her is a fruit stall with crates of green, yellow, orange and red fruit and another box beside it. In the centre back, a woman in a yellow dress is using a teal watering can to water a planter of flowers on the pavement. An older man in a cap and brown waistcoat sits on a bench eating a pink ice cream. A small white dog with brown spots and a blue neckerchief sits up on its hind legs beside him, begging and looking up at the ice cream. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is taking a photograph with a camera held up to his face. A red bird perches on top of a street lamp, a bicycle is parked in a rack, and a small cafe table with two chairs stands empty at the lower right. Nothing looks plainly impossible; the main thing about to happen is the woman walking into the box.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The bird's position: at first glance it could be sitting on the lamp or on the window edge behind; it reads as perched on the lamp.
- What the boy is photographing is not clear; he could be photographing the woman with the book, the dog, or the buildings.
- The dog could be begging for the ice cream, or just sitting up beside the bench.
- The man's face is a bit hard to read because the ice cream is close to his mouth; he has some dark marks on his cheek that could be stubble or age marks.
- The woman in yellow's watering can and the water stream overlap the flowers in a slightly tangled way, but it reads as watering.
- The older woman's book overlaps her arm and seems to float a little ahead of her hands.
