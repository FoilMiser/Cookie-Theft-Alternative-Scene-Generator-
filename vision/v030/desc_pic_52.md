# Picture 52

## 1. Free description
This is a sunny park with a pond on the left, a winding sand-coloured path on the right, a tall lamp post near the middle, and green hills behind. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes stands at the right-hand edge of the pond, holding a tan paper bag against his body and stretching his other hand out over the water. A line of small pale crumbs floats on the water from near him toward a white duck, which is swimming with two white wake lines behind it and has its orange beak down at the crumbs. So the boy is feeding the duck, and the duck is eating. In the foreground, a child with tightly braided hair, wearing a yellow top and orange shorts, is walking or running to the left while holding a pink ice cream up near the mouth. A grey rock sits on the grass just behind and to the right of the child. The wooden bench by the path is empty. There is a flower bed and a green bin at the bottom left, and nothing looks impossible or wrong.

## 2. Inventory

- Boy, centre-right, middle distance, at the pond edge: light skin, brown hair, red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers, white shoes. He is standing, holding a paper bag, and reaching out one hand toward the water to feed the duck.
- Duck, centre, on the pond: white with an orange beak. It is swimming to the right, with wake lines behind it, and pecking at crumbs on the water.
- Child, centre, front: dark skin, tightly braided hair, yellow top, orange shorts, grey-blue shoes. The child is walking or running left and holding a pink ice cream up near the mouth.

- Pale crumbs: a loose trail of small oval pieces floating on the water between the boy and the duck.
- Pond: large blue oval on the left, with brown-topped reeds (cattails) at both ends.
- Pink ice cream: small cone in the child's hand.
- Grey rock: front, right of the child, on the grass.
- Wooden bench: right, beside the path, empty.
- Lamp post: tall, black, right of centre, just behind the boy.
- Flower bed: front left, a brown soil oval with red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers.
- Green rubbish bin: bottom left corner.
- Path: pale sand colour, winding from the back to the bottom edge on the right.
- Trees and bushes: a large tree far right, a smaller tree far left, a few round bushes, and hills in the background.
- Sun and two white clouds: sun top right, sky clear and blue.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous
- The pale ovals on the water could be (a) crumbs or bread, which seems likely, or (b) small pebbles or stepping stones. The bag and the duck's beak favour crumbs.
- The child's stride could be a walk or a run. The rock sits behind the child, so it does not look like the child is about to trip on it, but the child might have just passed it.
- The child could be a girl or a boy; the hair and clothes do not settle this.
- The boy's bag is drawn partly behind his arm and looks a bit detached, as though it is resting against his front rather than clearly gripped.
- The crumbs overlap the reeds near the pond's right edge slightly.
- Nothing is clearly floating in the air or passing through anything else.
