# Picture 50

## 1. Free description
This is a sunny paved town square in front of a row of four colourful shops with striped awnings and flower planters. In the bottom right, an orange cat with white paws is crouched low with its tail stretched out behind, creeping toward a small red bird standing on the paving just in front of it. The cat looks as if it is about to pounce, and the bird does not seem to be flying away yet. In the front left of centre, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie walks along carrying a yellow shopping bag in one hand, with his head turned to the left. In the middle, a girl with braided hair, a yellow top and orange shorts sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. Further back on the right, a worker in an orange cap, orange high-visibility vest and navy trousers stands near the lamp post and waves one raised arm, with small curved motion marks beside his hand. Next to him is a fruit stall with crates of red, orange, yellow and green fruit under a teal and white awning, with nobody serving at it. Nobody seems to have noticed the cat and bird, and it is not clear who the worker is waving at. Nothing looks physically impossible.
