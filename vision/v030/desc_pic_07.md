# Picture 7

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house on the left, a tall wooden fence and gate at the back and a paved path on the right. In the centre-left a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is holding out a black frying pan and pouring water from it onto a flower bed, using it as if it were a watering can, which is the odd thing here. To the far left in front of the house a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sits on a bench reading a red book, not paying attention. An orange cat walks along the very top of the wooden fence. On the right of the fence there is a tall wooden post with a little birdhouse with a red roof on top; it is empty. A grey wheelbarrow sits on the lawn and two potted plants and a bush are near the fence. Nothing seems about to go wrong; the frying pan is the main strange thing.

- The cat's feet are drawn right on the fence tips, so it looks balanced in an unlikely way, but a cat walking along a fence is plausible.
- The post with the birdhouse stands directly in front of the fence; it could be read as passing through the fence rails, but it is more likely just in front of them.
