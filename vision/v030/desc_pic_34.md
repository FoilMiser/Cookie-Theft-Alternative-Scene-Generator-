# Picture 34

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence and gate at the back, a paved path on the left, and a cream house with a blue door and red roof on the right. On the left, a man with short dark hair, a green top and beige trousers stands sweeping a pile of autumn leaves with a yellow broom. In the centre, an older man with grey hair and a grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers walks to the left reading an open book that he holds out in front of him. Right in front of him on the grass is a cardboard box, so he is about to walk into or trip over it because he is looking at the book and not where he is going. At the front right near the flower bed an orange cat lies curled on the grass, its eyes closed, asleep. Two potted plants and a bush stand by the fence. Nothing looks impossible; the only thing about to happen is the older man hitting the box.
