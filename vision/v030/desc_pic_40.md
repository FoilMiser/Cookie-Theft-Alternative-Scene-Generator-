# Picture 40

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green rolling hills behind, a winding sandy path on the left, a blue pond on the right and a tall black lamp post in the middle. At the front, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, round glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt is walking while pushing a teal pram with a dark blue hood. In the pram a baby lies asleep with eyes closed, covered by a pink blanket with small white dots. A white duck is floating on the pond, with bulrushes (cattails) at both ends of the water. On the left, a wooden ladder is standing upright on the grass beside the path, and it is not leaning against anything: there is no wall or tree behind it holding it up, which looks impossible. A green bin is at the bottom right corner, and there are trees and bushes around the park. Nobody is near the ladder and no one is using it.
