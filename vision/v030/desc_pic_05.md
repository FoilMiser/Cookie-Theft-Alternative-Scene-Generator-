# Picture 5

## Free description
This is a calm, sunny day in a park with a pond on the right, a winding path on the left, a tall lamp post in the middle and hills in the distance. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans is walking toward the left, holding a red lead attached to a white dog with brown spots, which walks ahead of him. The man is smiling or looking ahead and he is passing in front of the pond. A grey rock sits on the grass in the foreground, a little way ahead of the dog. There is a flower bed at the lower right and a green dustbin in the bottom right corner. Nothing looks wrong or impossible, and it seems an ordinary peaceful scene.
