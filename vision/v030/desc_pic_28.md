# Picture 28

## Free description
A sunny shopping street or town square with a row of four coloured buildings with striped shop awnings, and a paved plaza in front. In the front left, a person in a yellow outfit with red shoes walks to the right carrying a yellow shopping bag. In the middle distance, a small girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench drinking from a cup with a straw. In the front centre, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie walks to the left while reading an open book held out in front of him; a cardboard box sits on the ground directly in his path, just in front of him and to the left, so he looks about to walk into or trip over it because he is looking at the book. On the right, an older man in a grey flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands with a camera raised to his eye, facing left. A teal bicycle is parked in a metal rack by the buildings, and a fruit or vegetable stall with a striped canopy stands on the right at the back. Nothing looks impossible; the main thing about to happen is the boy colliding with the box.
