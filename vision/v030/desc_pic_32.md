# Picture 32

## Free description
A sunny back garden with no people in it. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door; a wooden fence runs across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path leads from the gate to the front right. A small white puppy with brown patches lies curled on the grass in the centre, with its eyes closed, apparently asleep. In the front are a grey wheelbarrow, a teal watering can and a coiled teal garden hose on a reel with a yellow nozzle lying on the grass, not being used. A flower bed with colourful flowers sits near the house, and two potted plants stand on the grass near the fence. The scene is quiet; nothing seems about to happen, and nothing looks impossible.

- Watering can: teal, front right on the grass, near the path, not in use.
- Flower bed: multi-coloured flowers in round brown soil, left, by the house and the hose reel.
- Two potted plants: terracotta pots with green leaves, right of centre, near the fence.
- House: cream, red roof, window, blue door with yellow knob and step, left.
- Gate: wooden, in the fence on the right, at the top of the path.
- Path: pale paving stones running from the gate to the front right.
- Bushes: green, one by the house and one by the fence in the centre.
- Tree behind the fence by the house; hedges behind the fence.
- Sun and two clouds.
