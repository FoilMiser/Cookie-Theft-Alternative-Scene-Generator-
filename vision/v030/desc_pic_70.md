# Picture 70

## Free description
A back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and blue door, wooden fence and gate, on a sunny day. In the middle-back, a man or older boy with brown hair, a red shirt and blue trousers stands reaching up with both hands to a washing line strung between two wooden posts; he is pegging up a white T-shirt, and a pair of blue jeans already hangs on the line. On the left, next to the door, a tan dog with a red collar sits up holding a red lead in its mouth, the rest of the lead trailing down to a loop on the ground, as though it wants to be taken for a walk. On the far right, a boy in a blue hoodie and dark trousers stands on the paved path with hands on his hips, and he is wearing a metal bucket upside down on his head like a hat, which looks silly and odd. In the middle-right there is a grey metal swing frame with a red seat, empty. A wheelbarrow and a teal watering can sit in the front on the grass; a birdhouse on a tall pole stands near the gate; a flower bed is by the house. Nothing is moving dramatically, but the bucket on the head is the strange part.
