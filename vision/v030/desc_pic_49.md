# Picture 49

## 1. Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, and a wooden fence and gate across the back. A young man or older boy in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is walking to the left across the lawn, looking down at an open book he is holding in both hands. A closed cardboard box sits on the grass directly in his path, right beside his front foot, and because his eyes are on the book he does not seem to have seen it. He looks about to trip over the box. Behind him, near the fence, an older woman with a grey bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey-blue skirt, is raking a small pile of green and brown leaves on the grass. She does not appear to be watching him. An empty wheelbarrow, a teal watering can and two potted plants stand around the lawn, and there is a flower bed near the house. Nothing looks impossible; the risk of the reader walking into the box is the main thing going on.

## 2. Inventory
People and animals:
- Young man or boy, centre, middle/front: light skin, brown hair, red T-shirt, blue jeans, dark shoes. He is walking left while reading an open book held in front of him, with his head tilted down.
- Older woman, centre-left, back, near the fence: grey bun, glasses, teal cardigan, grey-blue skirt, brown shoes. She is standing and raking leaves with a long-handled rake.

Objects:
- Open book: white pages with a red cover, in the reader's hands.
- Cardboard box: centre-left, front, closed, on the grass immediately in front of the reader, apparently a trip hazard.
- Rake and leaves: wooden-handled rake in the woman's hands, with a small pile of green and brown leaves by its head.
- Wheelbarrow: front, right of centre, grey with a black wheel and wooden handles, empty and standing still.
- Watering can: front right, teal, standing on the grass by the path.
- Two potted plants: right of centre, back, in terracotta pots with green leaves.
- Flower bed: left, a brown soil oval with red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers.
- House: left, with a blue door, step, window and red roof.
- Wooden fence with a brown gate: across the back; the gate is at the right, closed.
- Paved path: right side, leading back to the gate.
- Tree and bushes: a tree behind the house corner, a bush by the house, and a bush near the pots.
- Sun and two white clouds: sun top right, sky clear and blue.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous
- The reader could be an older boy or a young man; his build and face could be either.
- He might (a) trip over the box in the next step, or (b) notice it and step round it. The first seems more likely because he is looking at the book.
- The woman could be gathering leaves as ordinary garden work, or could be about to look up; nothing shows she has noticed the reader.
- The rake head and the leaves overlap at the ground, so it is hard to see exactly where the tines end, but it reads as raking.
- Nothing is floating or passing through anything else. The rake handle passes in front of her body and her hands hold it normally.
