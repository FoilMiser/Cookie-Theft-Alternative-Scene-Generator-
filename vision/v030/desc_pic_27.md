# Picture 27

## Free description

This is a sunny park with green hills, a winding sandy path, a blue pond and a colourful flower bed. On the left, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes stands by a big tree and points up at a blue and white beach ball stuck in its branches. In the back, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is digging a hole with a spade, a mound of soil beside her. Next to the lamp post an older man in a flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands eating a pink ice cream. On the right of centre, a girl in a pink dress holds the strings of a purple and a blue balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other towards a red balloon that has got away and is floating above the pond with its string trailing free. At the far right a child in a yellow top and orange shorts stands beside the flower bed holding a yellow balloon, looking towards the girl. At the front, a black and white cat crouches low, facing a red bird on the grass only a short distance away, and looks about to pounce. The bird does not seem to be fleeing. Nothing looks impossible, and the bench is empty.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The red balloon could have just slipped from the girl's hand and she is trying to catch it, or she may have let it go on purpose and is watching it or waving. The end of its string is close to her raised hand but not in it.
- The cat could be stalking the bird and about to pounce, or just creeping along. The bird faces the cat rather than away from it and does not seem alarmed, which is a little odd.
- The pink thing at the older man's mouth reads as an ice cream, but it is small, and the cone is hard to see; it could be something else, such as a flower.
- The boy's finger points up and slightly to the left of the ball, into the tree, rather than exactly at it, though the ball is the obvious thing he would be pointing to.
- The woman may be digging for planting or some other job; nothing shows what the hole is for.
- The people do not seem to be related to one another; each is doing a separate thing.
