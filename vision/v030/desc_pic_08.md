# Picture 8

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house on the left, a wooden fence and gate behind, and a paved path on the right. At the back a woman in a yellow dress and a small girl in a pink dress are playing catch: the girl has just thrown a red ball, shown by a dashed curved line through the air, and the woman has her hands out in front of her, about to catch it, with her mouth open. In the middle foreground an older man in a cap and a brown waistcoat walks along carrying a cardboard box under his arm. On the right a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is on the path and is holding a giant fork, using it like a broom to sweep a pile of dirt toward a red dustpan, and a puff of dust is rising. The giant fork is the wrong and impossible-looking thing here. A teal watering can sits on the grass, unused, and there is a flower bed and two potted plants. The ball is about to reach the woman.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The woman could be read as just having thrown the ball rather than about to catch it, but the dashed line starts from the girl's hand, which suggests the girl threw it.
- The dashed line rises above the fence top behind the woman, and the ball is drawn in front of the fence, so its height compared to the fence is a bit unclear.
- The man's fork and arms overlap in a slightly confusing way near his chest; a white shape at his hand looks like part of the handle.
- The older man's face and the box both face right, and he could be delivering the box or just walking; there is no clear destination.
- The man on the path overlaps the gate behind him, and the gate seems to pass behind his head.
