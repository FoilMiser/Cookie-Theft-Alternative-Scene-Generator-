# Picture 38

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence with a gate across the back, and a stone path running along the right. In the middle of the lawn, a boy or young man with brown hair, a red shirt and blue jeans is in a seated pose reading a red book, but there is no chair, bench or anything else under him, so he seems to be sitting on thin air. To the right, a small boy with dark skin and curly dark hair, in a yellow shirt and orange shorts, sits smiling on a red swing that hangs from a grey metal swing frame; small motion lines by the swing suggest it is moving. At front left, a small tan-and-white puppy is curled up asleep on the grass near a flower bed. A washing line between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel or shirt with red stripes. A grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can stand on the grass at the front, and a birdhouse on a tall pole stands near the gate. The strange thing is the reader sitting with nothing to sit on.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The seated reader has no visible seat: this could be deliberate (he is sitting on nothing, which is impossible) or a chair that has gone missing from the drawing. His shadow is only a small patch by his feet.
- His lower body looks somewhat stiff and his legs go down at an angle, so it could also be read as him leaning or sliding, though a sitting pose is the clearest reading.
- The garment on the line could be a towel, a striped shirt or a blanket; it has no sleeves that I can see.
- The swing boy's eyes look closed with a smile, which could be enjoyment or could just be a drawing style. There are thin motion lines to the right of the swing that could also be read as grass marks.
- The back swing ropes pass in front of the fence and look thin; nothing looks wrong, but the boy's legs hang rather stiffly.
- The wheelbarrow and watering can are not being used by anyone.
