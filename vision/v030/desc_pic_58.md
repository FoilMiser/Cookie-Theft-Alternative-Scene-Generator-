# Picture 58

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence at the back, a gate at the far left, a paved path leading from it, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the front left a man or boy in a red T-shirt and blue trousers is standing on the path holding a very large silver fork as if it were a broom or rake, using it to push dust and bits of dirt into a red dustpan on the ground. A little cloud of dust is rising where the fork touches the ground. This is odd: you would expect a broom, not a giant dinner fork. A black cat with white paws and chest is walking along the top of the fence towards a birdhouse on a tall pole. In the middle of the garden a sprinkler at the end of a hose is sending arcs of water through the air, and the water is falling right on a cardboard box that is sitting on the grass in a small puddle, so the box is getting wet. A clothesline between two posts has a yellow towel with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans hanging from it. Two potted plants stand by the fence near the birdhouse pole. A flower bed sits in front of the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fork could be read as a deliberately silly object used instead of a broom, or as a strange rake; it clearly has the shape of a dinner fork.
- The person's grip on the fork looks odd, as the top of the fork's handle crosses in front of his chest and one hand wraps around it awkwardly near his body.
- The arcs of water: one arc reaches the box, while two shorter arcs land on the grass in front of the box. They could also be read as water jumping back up from the grass.
- It is unclear if the sprinkler watering the box is an accident or a joke; the box just appears to have been left in the wrong place.
- The cat is on top of the fence pickets and appears to be standing on the points, which seems slightly awkward. It might be stalking the birdhouse, or simply walking along.
- The birdhouse pole bottom has a small green tuft at the base and stands close to the fence; it overlaps the fence and rails.
