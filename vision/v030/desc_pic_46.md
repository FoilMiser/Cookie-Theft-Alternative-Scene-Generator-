# Picture 46

## 1. Free description
This is a sunny park with a pond on the right, a winding sand-coloured path on the left, a tall lamp post in the middle, and green hills behind. An older woman with grey hair in a bun and round glasses stands on the grass at the edge of the pond, fishing with a long rod; her line runs down to a red float sitting in a small ripple ring on the water. On the left, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands at the back on the grass, licking a pink ice cream cone with his eyes apparently shut. In front of him a girl with braided hair sits on a wooden bench, also eating a pink ice cream, and she looks as if she is smiling or laughing with her eyes closed. A white dog with brown patches and a blue neckerchief is sitting up on its hind legs right next to the bench, front paws raised, with its head tipped up toward the girl. It looks as though the dog is begging for her ice cream, and she has not noticed or is not paying attention. The girl might lose the ice cream, or the dog might get a lick of it. Nothing looks impossible; the dog's upright pose is the most unusual thing in the picture.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous
- The dog could be (a) begging for the girl's ice cream, or (b) simply sitting up to greet or play with her. The begging reading seems most natural.
- The girl's closed eyes could mean she is enjoying the ice cream or that she is laughing at the dog.
- The ripple ring round the float could be (a) a fish nibbling, or (b) just the float sitting on the water.
- The man and the girl may or may not be together; they do not look at each other.
- The dog's body looks slightly contorted, with its front legs reaching out to the left at an awkward angle, and it is hard to tell how many front legs are showing.
- Nothing is clearly floating or passing through anything else.
