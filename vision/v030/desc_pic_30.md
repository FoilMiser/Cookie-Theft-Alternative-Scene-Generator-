# Picture 30

## Free description
A sunny park or playground with green grass and a dark railing fence along the back. In the front left, a child with curly dark hair, a yellow top and orange shorts holds the strings of two balloons (red and yellow) in one hand, while a third, blue balloon has floated up and away to the left; the child reaches up with the other hand and looks up at it unhappily, so it seems the blue balloon has just got away. The blue balloon's string hangs loose and is not attached to the child's hand. On the bench behind, an older man in a grey cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat sits drinking from a red cup with a straw, with his eyes apparently closed or looking down. On the far right a boy in a red cap and blue top stands by the slide flying an orange kite high in the sky, holding the string up in one hand. At the front centre a red bird stands on the grass and an orange cat is crouching or running right behind it, as if about to catch or pounce on the bird; the bird is pecking at the ground and does not seem to notice. A blue bird sits on a branch in the tree on the left. There is also an empty swing set, a green rubbish bin, a sandpit and a grey rock.
