# Picture 17

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house and red roof on the left, a tall wooden fence with a gate on the right, and a stone path at the right. A dark-skinned man with a short beard, wearing a green T-shirt and beige trousers, stands to the left of centre watering a round flower bed with a teal watering can; the water sprays down onto the flowers in many thin streams. At the top of the tree behind the fence, there is something plainly impossible: a large orange fish is perched on a bare branch among the leaves, as if it were a bird. On the grass at the front right, a small orange cat is lying on its side, curled up and apparently asleep or resting, with its eyes closed. A dark swing set stands on the right with its red seat hanging still and empty. An empty bench sits at the front left. Nobody seems to notice the fish in the tree.

## Inventory
- Left-centre: man with dark skin, short dark hair, short beard, green T-shirt, beige trousers, brown shoes; standing at the flower bed, pouring water from a watering can onto the flowers.
- Front right, on the grass: small orange cat with a white paw or chest, lying on its side with eyes shut; sleeping or resting.
- Orange fish: on a curved brown branch in the tree, upper left, in the air with a fin and tail, looking to the left; impossible and out of place.
- Watering can: teal, in the man's hands, with water spraying down into the flowers.
- Flower bed: round, left of centre, red, yellow, orange, purple and pink flowers.
- Swing set: dark blue-grey A-frame, right; red seat hanging straight, empty and still.
- Bench: wooden, empty, front left.
- Bush: small, by the house, behind the flower bed.
- Path: pale stone, right, leading to the gate.
- Gate: brown wooden, far right back.
- House: cream, red roof, blue door, window, left.
- Tree: behind the fence near the house, with the fish on a branch.
- Sun: top right, with rays. Two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fish in the tree could be a real live fish where a bird should be, or some kind of ornament or toy fish on a branch. It looks like a real fish drawn the way a bird would normally be.
- The fish's branch is the only visible branch on the tree; it curves out from the trunk specially to hold the fish, and part of the fish and branch stand out against the green foliage.
- The cat could be asleep, stretched out playfully, or could conceivably be hurt, but its peaceful face suggests sleeping. Its body is curled in a slightly hard-to-read pose, with its head toward us and its back end behind and above it.
- The water's dashed thin lines read as spray; they land on the flowers at the front of the bed rather than falling in a clean arc.
- The man's upper body seems narrow, and his arm holding the can looks slightly short.
