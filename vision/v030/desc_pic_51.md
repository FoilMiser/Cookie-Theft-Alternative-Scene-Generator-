# Picture 51

## 1. Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, and a wooden fence and gate across the back. A small girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes has just thrown a red ball, and a dashed line shows its curved path through the air toward a woman in a purple top and teal trousers. The woman has both arms stretched out in front of her, ready to catch the ball, which is right near her hands. In the front middle of the lawn, a white dog with brown spots is crouched in a play bow with its tail up and wagging, its nose close to a yellow-green tennis ball on the grass. By the blue door on the left, a brown dog with a red collar sits with a red lead in its mouth, the other end lying in a loop on the ground, as if it is asking to go for a walk. On the path at the far right, an older woman with a grey bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey-blue skirt, is sweeping a pile of red and orange leaves with a straw broom. A teal watering can stands on the grass beside her. Nobody seems to be paying attention to the brown dog. Nothing looks impossible; the picture shows several ordinary activities happening at once.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous
- The dashed line could show (a) the girl throwing the ball to the woman, which seems right, or (b) the ball travelling the other way. The woman's open hands and the ball close to them suggest she is about to catch it.
- The white dog could be (a) playing with the tennis ball, or (b) about to pick it up. It is a separate game from the red ball.
- The brown dog holding its own lead could mean it wants a walk; it could also just be a funny pose.
- The older woman's broom head runs almost off the bottom edge of the picture, and the leaf pile sits against her foot.
- The white dog's bowed front legs look rather flat and splayed.
- Nothing is floating except the thrown ball, which has the dashed line to explain it.
