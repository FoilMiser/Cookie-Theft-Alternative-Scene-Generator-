# Picture 36

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence and gate, a paved path on the left, and a cream house with a blue door and red roof on the right. In the centre right, a person with short dark hair in a yellow outfit and red shoes stands holding a garden hose and spraying water in an arc onto the flower bed, with drops landing on the flowers. The hose runs along the ground from a reel on the right, around the front of the flower bed. Behind them, near the fence, a man with short dark hair, a green top and beige trousers stands painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller; the last few fence boards on the right have already been painted blue, and the rest are still brown. The odd thing is a cardboard box hanging in mid-air in the sky above the fence on the left, tilted at an angle, with nothing holding it up. A tall post with a little birdhouse stands by the fence on the left, close to the floating box. Nobody seems to notice the box; it may be about to fall, and it looks impossible or out of place.
