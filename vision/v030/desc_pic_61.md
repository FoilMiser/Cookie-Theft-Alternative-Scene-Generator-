# Picture 61

## Free description
A sunny playground with a dark metal fence along the back, a large tree on the left, a swing set in the middle, a tall red slide at the right, and a sandpit in the front right. A small girl in a pink dress with yellow shoes is on the raised left end of a red seesaw, up in the air, with her mouth open as if startled or calling out. Little motion lines near her feet suggest she is bouncing or being jolted. On the low right end of the seesaw a large man in a green shirt and beige trousers is sitting with his legs crossed, reading an open book. He seems not to notice the girl and his weight is pinning his end to the ground, so she is stuck up high. Further back on the slide a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is sliding down, with both arms raised in the air, looking happy. The swing set is empty and hangs still, and a green rubbish bin stands behind the slide. The sandpit has a red bucket and blue spade in it but nobody is playing with them. A grey rock lies by the sandpit.
