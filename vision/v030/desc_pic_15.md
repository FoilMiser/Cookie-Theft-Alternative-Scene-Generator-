# Picture 15

## Free description
This is the same kind of back garden, but with a grey overcast sky and three dark storm clouds, and no sun. It seems as if rain is about to start, although none is falling yet. At the front in the middle, a dark-skinned man with a dark beard, wearing a green shirt and beige trousers, is pointing up to the sky with one arm and looking up at the clouds with wide eyes, as though warning the others or noticing the weather. In the middle left, an older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, is holding a black frying pan out over a flower bed and pouring water from it onto the flowers, which is an odd thing to use instead of a watering can. At the back right, an older man in a flat grey cap and brown waistcoat is painting the wooden fence blue with a long-handled roller, with a paint tin on the grass near his feet; part of the fence is already blue. It looks odd that they are watering flowers and painting a fence outdoors just as it is about to rain. Nobody seems to be paying attention to the man who is pointing.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- A frying pan pouring water is plainly the wrong tool for watering flowers; this could be deliberate silliness, or she might be emptying the pan. I read it as watering with a pan.
- The man pointing may be warning about rain, or showing somebody something up in the sky; there is nothing in the sky except the clouds.
- The sky is grey but the sunlit-looking green of the grass and bright colours don't change, so the scene doesn't look fully stormy. No rain is falling.
- The pointing man's lower arm hangs in a slightly bent, stiff way and his trousers bulge at the hips, which looks a bit odd.
- The window of the house still shows bright blue sky-coloured panes even though the sky is grey, so it looks slightly mismatched.
