# Picture 12

## Free description
This is a sunny playground with a dark metal railing fence and trees behind it, with a slide on the left, a sandpit at the front left, a wooden bench on the right and a big tree on the far right. A girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes is going down the slide with her arms up in the air, looking cheerful. In the middle, a workman in an orange hard cap or cap, orange high-visibility vest and blue trousers is walking to the right carrying a cardboard box. On the right, a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts holds a red balloon and a yellow balloon by their strings in one hand, while his other arm reaches up and he looks up at a purple balloon that has floated away above him. The purple balloon's string hangs loose, out of reach, so it seems he has just let it go and it is escaping. A red bucket and a blue spade sit in the sandpit, and a grey rock lies beside it. No one seems to be in danger, though the workman is walking near the boy and the sandpit.
