# Picture 54

## 1. Free description
This is a back garden under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds overhead and no sun, so it looks as if rain is coming. On the left is a cream house with a red roof and blue door, with a wooden fence and gate across the back. A young man or boy in a red shirt and blue jeans is reaching up with both hands at a washing line, where a pair of blue jeans and a white T-shirt are pegged out. An older man in a grey cap, white sleeves and a brown waistcoat holds a garden hose nozzle up in front of him and looks at it, with just one drop of water at the end. The hose runs back along the grass to a reel on the left, and a brown dog with a red collar is standing on it; the hose is swollen on the reel side of the dog's paws and thin after them. So the dog appears to be blocking the water, and the man looks puzzled about why nothing is coming out. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers looks worried and points up at the dark clouds, and a man in a green shirt walks a white spotted dog on a red lead along the path, heading right. In the front, a child in a yellow T-shirt and yellow shorts with red shoes reads a red book in a sitting pose, but there is no chair or bench under the child, which looks impossible since the only bench is empty over on the left.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous
- The child's pose is the strangest thing: it looks like (a) sitting on an invisible chair, with the hips well above the ground and shins vertical, or (b) possibly meant to be sitting on the grass with knees up, though the height of the hips does not match that. There is no seat drawn.
- The young man could be (a) hanging the washing out, or (b) taking it down because of the coming storm. The clouds make the second reading attractive, but the pose fits both.
- The woman could be pointing at (a) the dark clouds, which her upward-left finger suggests, or (b) the birdhouse behind her, which seems less likely because it is on the other side of her hand.
- The older man's arms and torso are drawn oddly: the white sleeves look like a flat flap across his chest, and it is hard to tell if he is seen from the front or the back.
- The hose comes up from the ground very close to the older man's legs and rises steeply to the nozzle, which looks slightly stiff.
- The bulge in the hose could be (a) water backing up behind the dog's paws, or (b) a drawing quirk; the single drop at the nozzle supports the first reading.
- It looks strange that someone is watering the garden when rain looks imminent, though that could just be how the scene is.
- No rain is actually falling yet.
