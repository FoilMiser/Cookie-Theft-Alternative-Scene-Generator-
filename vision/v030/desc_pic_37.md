# Picture 37

## Free description
This is a sunny playground or park on green grass, with a dark metal railing fence and low green hills behind it. At front left, a young girl with dark hair in a pink dress is kneeling at the edge of a wooden sandbox, building a sandcastle with three towers and a small red flag. In the middle, an elderly man with grey hair, a blue-grey flat cap, a brown vest over a white shirt and dark trousers is walking along while reading an open book held out in front of him. He is not looking where he is going, and a cardboard box sits on the grass just ahead of him at about shin height, so it looks like he is about to trip over it. Further back, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands by the fence licking a pink ice cream cone, with his eyes closed. On the left is a playground slide with a red frame, a dark ladder and an orange-and-yellow chute, with nobody on it. A green bin stands near the fence, a tree is on the right, and there are a couple of bushes, a grey rock, a sun and two clouds. Nothing looks impossible; the main thing that seems about to happen is the reading man walking into the box.
