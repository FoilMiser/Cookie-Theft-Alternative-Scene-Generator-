# Picture 72

## Free description

A sunny park with a pond on the left, a winding path on the right, green hills behind, a tall black lamp post in the middle, and a large tree on the right. In the front centre, an older man with grey hair, a blue-grey flat cap, a white shirt with a brown waistcoat and dark trousers is walking along pushing a teal pram; a baby with closed eyes lies inside under a pink blanket, apparently asleep. Behind him in the distance on the right, a woman with short dark hair, in a yellow dress and red shoes, is walking towards the right carrying a cardboard box in front of her. On the right, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands on the path with one arm raised, holding a string and looking up. The string runs to a purple kite that is caught in the leaves of the large tree, so the kite seems stuck and he may be trying to tug it free. A small white-and-brown puppy is lying curled on the grass in the middle, seemingly asleep, between the man and an empty bench. Nothing looks about to go badly wrong, but the kite is clearly stuck rather than flying.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The kite could be stuck in the tree, or it could be flying just in front of the tree. I read it as stuck, because leafy clumps overlap it and the string hangs slack in a curve.
- The boy's action could be either trying to pull the kite free, or simply flying it and holding on. His pose alone does not settle this.
- The puppy could be asleep or could be hurt or lying down for some other reason; the closed eyes and curled shape suggest sleep.
- The man's direction of gaze: he looks forward over the pram, not down at the baby, and he does not appear to notice the others.
- The boy's feet are on the path but he stands quite close to the bench; his body slightly overlaps the path edge, which looks fine and is not a clear collision.
- The leafy clumps on top of the kite look like separate pieces pasted on the tree, which is a little unusual against the flat tree crown.
