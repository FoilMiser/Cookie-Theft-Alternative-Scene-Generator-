# Picture 59

## Free description
A sunny back garden, seen with the cream house and red roof on the left, a wooden fence at the back, and a gate at the back right with a paved path running from it. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie on the path at the right has just thrown a red ball, shown by a dashed curved line, with his arm still extended. A man or older boy in a red T-shirt and blue trousers stands in the middle right with both hands held out in front of him and his mouth open, looking toward the ball and about to catch it. At the back left next to the house, a woman with dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers is digging with a spade, next to a heap of soil and a hole. A sprinkler in the middle of the lawn on a hose is spraying arcs of water to the right, and the water lands on a cardboard box sitting on the grass in a small puddle, right next to the man. A clothesline with blue jeans and a yellow striped towel hangs behind the woman and the sprinkler. A grey wheelbarrow stands empty in the foreground.
