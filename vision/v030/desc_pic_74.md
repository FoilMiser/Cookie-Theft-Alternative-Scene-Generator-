# Picture 74

## Free description

A sunny park with a pond on the right, a winding path on the left, green hills behind, a tall black lamp post near the middle, and a large tree on the left. In the middle-front, a young man or older boy with brown hair, in a red top and blue trousers, stands eating or licking a pink ice cream cone, with his eyes squeezed shut and a pleased expression. To the right, in front of the pond, a man with short dark hair and a beard, in a green shirt and beige trousers, is walking towards the right pushing a teal pram; a baby with closed eyes lies in it under a pink blanket, apparently asleep. On the left, a tall wooden ladder is standing by itself on the grass, leaning at an angle against nothing. It has a shadow beneath it and no wall or tree anywhere near its top. That is the odd thing here: a ladder cannot lean on thin air, so it looks impossible or as if it is about to fall over. Neither person is paying attention to it. There is a green bin at the bottom right.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The ladder: it could be read as impossibly balancing, or as just about to topple. Either way, it is not leaning on anything. It also overlaps the edge of the path at its middle, passing in front of the path curve without touching the ground there.
- The man in red: he could be a teenager or a grown man; his body is adult-sized, but his face and proportions look youthful. His eyes could be closed in enjoyment or squinting.
- The pram-pushing man's relationship to the baby is not shown; he may be the father.
- The man in red's left arm hangs down in an odd position with the hand curled at the hip, which looks slightly stiff but is not wrong.
- The lamp post and bush seem to be far behind the man in red, and his head is close to the lamp post's base. This looks like a depth overlap rather than a real collision.
