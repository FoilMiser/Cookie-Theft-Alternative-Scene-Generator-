# Picture 10

## Free description
This is a sunny, breezy back garden with a cream house and red roof on the right, a tall wooden picket fence across the back, and a stone path on the left leading to a wooden gate. White wind lines streak across the fence and up near the tree, and a few leaves are blowing off the tree. An older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, stands on the left with both arms thrown up and her mouth open, looking toward a red cap that is flying through the air in front of the fence; it looks as if the wind has just blown her hat away, or she is trying to catch it. In the middle, a girl or woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is standing with her back partly to us, painting the fence with a long-handled blue roller; a section of the fence is already blue and a paint tin stands by her feet. On the right, an older man in a flat grey cap, brown waistcoat and white shirt is watering a round flower bed with a teal watering can. The water from his can arcs strangely upward in a high curve, with drops at the top, rather than falling straight down onto the flowers, as if the wind is catching it or it is flowing the wrong way. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands on the grass at the front. The hat may be about to land or to be chased, and nothing else seems urgent.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water from the watering can goes up and curves over in a tall arc, with the drops at the top, rather than down onto the flowers. This could be the wind blowing it, or a mistake or an impossible thing: water flowing upward.
- The woman's raised arms could mean she is reaching to catch the hat, or simply surprised and alarmed. She is some way below and to the right of the hat, so she is not actually close to catching it.
- The cap's owner is not certain. Nobody in the picture is hatless in an obvious way except the woman and the painter, and the cap looks like a boy's baseball cap rather than something either of them would wear, so it may or may not be the woman's.
- The painter's age is unclear (could be a girl or a slim young woman). Her dress could also be a tunic with a belt.
- The object near the start of the wind lines looks like a small brown leaf or seed; I can't be sure which.
