# Picture 3

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house on the left, a tall wooden fence with a gate behind and a paved path running along the right. There is only one person in the picture: an older woman with grey hair and glasses, standing on the path at the right. She is holding a giant dinner fork by its handle and using it like a broom to sweep a small pile of dirt along the path. A little cloud of dust is rising from the pile, and a red dustpan lies on the ground just to the left of it, ready to catch the dirt. The fork is far too big and is the wrong tool, and that is the odd thing in the picture. The rest of the garden is quiet: a flower bed near the house, two pots of green plants by the fence, a grey wheelbarrow on the lawn and some bushes. Nothing else is moving, and no animals are visible.

- The woman's hands and the fork's handle overlap with her top in a slightly confusing white shape near her chest.
