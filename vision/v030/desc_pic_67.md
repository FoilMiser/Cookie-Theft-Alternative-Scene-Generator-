# Picture 67

## Free description
A busy paved town square or shopping street on a sunny day, with a row of colourful shops with striped awnings behind. On the left, a street sweeper in an orange cap and hi-vis orange vest brooms a pile of red, orange and brown leaves. In the middle, a little girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench eating an ice cream or holding something pink to her mouth, and a small white dog with brown ears and a blue collar sits up on its hind legs in front of her, begging for food. Behind them a child in a yellow top and orange shorts stands with one arm reaching up; three balloons (purple, blue, red) are around him, with the purple one floating away high up on a loose string while he seems to hold the others. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie walks along reading an open book, not looking where he is going, and a cardboard box lies on the ground right in his path, so he seems about to trip over it. A market stall with fruit and vegetables stands behind him. A red bird perches on top of a street lamp, and a blue bird flies in the sky at the upper left.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The balloons: one reading is that the purple balloon has slipped away and the child is reaching up to catch it while holding the other two; another reading is that the child is holding all three on separate strings. The strings look like they could come together near the child's hand, but the purple string curves off and does not clearly connect.
- The object at the girl's mouth is hard to make out: it looks like an ice cream or a pink lolly, or she could just be holding her hand to her mouth.
- The dog is small and at the front of the bench; it could be begging for the girl's food or just sitting up for a trick. Its pose is a bit odd, with its body very upright and its front paw held out.
- The boy's walk looks like he might bump into the box next, but he might also pass it, as he is close but slightly to the side; the box is a little in front and left of him.
- The tree is drawn quite large and overlaps the buildings; the leaves being swept do not match the green tree, as they are autumn-coloured.
