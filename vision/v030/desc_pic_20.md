# Picture 20

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house on the right, a wooden fence along the back and a closed wooden gate at the far left. One person is there: a man or older boy with brown hair, a red top and blue jeans, standing at a washing line in the middle of the picture. He has both arms raised and is pegging a white T-shirt onto the line, looking up at his hands. A pair of blue jeans already hangs on the line just to his right. Around him the garden holds two potted plants, a paved path, a teal watering can, a grey wheelbarrow, a colourful flower bed and a bush by the house. Nobody else is present and nothing looks about to go wrong. Nothing looks impossible; it reads as an ordinary chore on a fine day.
