# Picture 71

## Free description

A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a wooden gate at the far right where a paved path begins. A woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers stands near the middle-left, holding a hose and spraying water in an arc onto a bed of colourful flowers beside the house. The hose runs back along the ground to a hose reel near the house. On the right, on the path, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is in the air with both arms raised; his feet are clearly above his shadow, so he seems to be jumping, and his face looks happy or excited. High in the sky, above the fence and roughly in the middle-right, a cardboard box is hanging tilted in mid-air with nothing holding it up. That is the odd thing in the picture: a box has no reason to be in the sky, and it has no visible thrower, string or support. It may be falling, or it may have been tossed up, and the boy could be jumping to catch it, although he is not directly beneath it. The woman shows no sign of noticing the box or the boy.
