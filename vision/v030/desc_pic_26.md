# Picture 26

## Free description

This is a busy paved town square on a sunny day, in front of a row of colourful shops with striped awnings. On the left, a blue and white beach ball is stuck in a tree, while a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts jumps with arms up, and a small girl in a pink dress points up at it. In the front, something impossible is happening: a brown dog with a red neckerchief is sitting up like a person and holding open a newspaper. In the centre, a man in a green T-shirt sits on a bench reading a red book, while a blue bird beside him is right next to his sandwich on the bench and looks about to take it. To his right an older woman with grey hair and glasses is holding a watering can by a box of flowers, but the stream of water arcs upward and away from the flowers, ending in drops in the air, which looks wrong. Further back, a worker in an orange cap and orange safety vest is sweeping a pile of autumn leaves with a broom. On the right, a man in a red top and blue jeans is walking along reading an open book, heading straight for a cardboard box on the ground, so he looks about to trip over it. Behind him a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is walking with a yellow shopping bag past an unattended fruit stall. A large red bird is flying in the sky at the top left.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The jumping boy could be reaching for the ball, or simply jumping for joy, since his eyes are closed and he is smiling and is not directly under the ball.
- The dog is doing something a dog cannot do. It could be read as genuinely reading, or as just holding or playing with the newspaper. The printed side faces the viewer, so it is unclear whether the dog could read it from where it sits.
- The water could be read as flowing upward, which is impossible, or as sprayed in a strange high arc that misses the flowers. Either way it does not go onto the flowers.
- The blue bird could be about to steal the sandwich, or could be merely perched next to it; the man does not seem to have noticed.
- The woman's hands and the can run together, so the exact grip is hard to make out, and her position relative to the flower box is unclear: she could be standing behind it or to one side of it.
- The red bird looks large relative to the buildings and people.
- The fruit stall has no seller, and I can't tell whether the boy with the bag has just bought something there. He partly hides the crates at the right end of the stall.
- The sun's upper rays are cut off by the top edge of the picture.
