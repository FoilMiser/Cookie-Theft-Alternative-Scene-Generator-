# Picture 43

## Free description

In the front left, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and round glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, stands at the edge of the path holding a brown paper bag and scattering seed or crumbs with her hand. A line of small dots falls from her hand to the ground, where two small birds, one red and one blue, are pecking at the food on the path. A white duck floats on the pond behind him, with bulrushes at the ends of the water. There is a flower bed with colourful flowers at the right, a large grey rock at the front, a green bin in the bottom right corner, and trees and bushes around the park. Everything looks calm and nothing appears to be going wrong; it is an ordinary afternoon in the park.
