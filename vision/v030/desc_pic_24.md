# Picture 24

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house on the right, a wooden fence along the back and a closed gate on the far left. On the left, a man with dark skin in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers stands on the paved path, raking a small pile of leaves and looking down at them. Next to him a child with curly dark hair, a yellow top and orange shorts sits on a red swing, smiling and holding the ropes, with small motion lines showing the swing moving. On the right of centre a small girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes stands on the grass holding a string in her raised hand and looking up. The string curves up past the washing line to a purple diamond-shaped kite with a bowed tail, which is caught in the leaves of the big tree beside the house. So her kite appears to be stuck in the tree, and she may be trying to tug it free. A yellow and red striped item and blue jeans hang on the washing line, and a watering can, wheelbarrow and flower bed are also on show. Nobody seems to have noticed the girl's problem. Nothing looks impossible.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The kite could be read two ways: stuck in the tree, which is what the green leaf clumps drawn over it suggest, or flying in front of the tree. I lean to stuck.
- The girl could be tugging at the string to free the kite, or she could simply be holding it and looking at it.
- The swing has motion lines on its left side, yet the seat hangs nearly straight below the bar, so it does not look strongly swung to one side.
- The child's relationship to the man is not shown; they could be parent and child but nothing in the picture says so.
- The string runs across the fence and past the washing line post in a big loop, which looks slightly strange for a kite line under tension, though it does not visibly pass through anything solid.
