# Picture 69

## Free description
A calm park on a sunny day with green hills in the background. A woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers stands on the grass at the edge of a blue pond on the right side of the picture. She holds a brown paper bag at her waist and reaches out with the other hand, scattering small round pieces of bread or food onto the water. A white duck in the pond swims toward her and has its head down at the floating pieces, eating them; little white lines behind it show its movement through the water. There is a tall black street lamp in the middle of the scene, a winding sandy path on the left, trees and bushes at both sides, a grey rock in the bottom centre and a green bin in the lower right corner. Nothing looks wrong or impossible; it is a pleasant, ordinary moment of feeding a duck.
