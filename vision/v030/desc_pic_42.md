# Picture 42

## Free description
This is a busy sunny playground with a dark railing fence, green hills, a slide and a bin at the back, and a big tree on the right. At front left, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is running to the right with her arms pumping and motion lines beside her. A small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands in the middle holding two balloons (red and yellow) on strings in one hand and reaching up with the other towards a purple balloon that has floated away above him, out of his reach. Behind him a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is standing and taking a photo with a camera held to his face. To the right, a boy in a red shirt and blue jeans sits on a bench reading a red book, not noticing that a blue bird on the bench beside him is pecking at a sandwich next to him. A red bird flies high in the sky. At front right, a little girl in a pink dress is holding a line that goes up to a purple kite, which is stuck in the big tree. Near her, on the far right, a brown dog with a red neckerchief sits upright on its haunches holding up and reading a newspaper with its front paws, which is impossible for a real dog. In the middle front, a red bicycle has square wheels instead of round ones, which also looks impossible; beside it a white dog with brown spots is crouched down playing with a green tennis ball. A red bucket and a blue spade have been left in an otherwise empty sandbox at front left.
