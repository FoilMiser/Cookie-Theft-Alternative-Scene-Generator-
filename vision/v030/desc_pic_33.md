# Picture 33

## Free description
A sunny park or countryside scene with no people or animals visible. A large blue pond with bulrushes at both ends lies on the left, with green hills and rows of trees and bushes in the background. A pale winding path runs from the front right up into the distance. A tall black street lamp and a green rubbish bin stand in the middle, near the far side of the pond. An odd thing is a wooden ladder standing upright on the grass by the path, on the right, leaning against nothing at all; its top rises into the air against the hills and there is no wall or tree holding it up. There is also a flower bed with colourful flowers at the front left, a grey rock at the front centre, and two trees on the left and right. Nothing seems to be happening, but the unsupported ladder looks wrong or impossible, as it should fall over.
