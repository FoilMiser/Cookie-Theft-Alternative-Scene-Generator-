# Picture 73

## Free description

A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right, a wooden fence along the back, and a wooden gate at the far left at the top of a paved path. A man with short dark hair and a beard or moustache, in a green shirt and beige trousers, stands right of centre, holding a hose and spraying water in an arc onto a colourful flower bed by the house. The hose runs round the bed and back to a hose reel near the house. In the middle-left there is a metal swing set with one red seat, and it is empty and hanging still. In front of it, a grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass with nothing visibly in it. A wooden post with a small birdhouse on top stands beside the gate. There is an empty wooden bench at the far right, in front of the house. The man is the only person, and it is a calm, ordinary scene with nothing obviously about to happen. Nothing appears impossible.
