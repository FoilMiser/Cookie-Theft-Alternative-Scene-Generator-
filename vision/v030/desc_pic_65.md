# Picture 65

## Free description
A windy day in a back garden with a cream house, red roof, blue door, wooden fence and gate. White wind lines cross the sky and the middle of the picture. A woman or girl in a yellow dress and red shoes, in the middle-right, reaches up with both arms, mouth open, trying to catch her orange hat, which has blown off and is tumbling in the air along with a few leaves. A man in a green shirt and tan trousers stands in front of her with his back to the viewer, holding a garden hose nozzle up in both hands and looking at it; only a single water drop comes out of the nozzle. The reason is visible: a white goat with small dark horns is standing on the hose, right where it runs along the grass, squashing it and blocking the water. The hose bulges on the reel side of the goat, between the reel and the goat. Oddly, an orange fish is stuck at the top of a bent branch or trunk of the tree on the left, as if the tree is growing a fish, which is impossible. Leaves are also blowing off the tree in the wind.
