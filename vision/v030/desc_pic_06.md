# Picture 6

## Free description
This is a sunny park with a large pond on the left, a winding path on the right, a tall lamp post, an empty bench and hills behind. On the left an older woman with grey hair and glasses is kneeling on one knee next to a flower bed, holding a small trowel or tool and planting a pink flower that is still in a terracotta pot. In the middle a white goat has its head down and is eating a small tuft of grass. On the right a man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands by the path holding a brown paper bag and scattering seeds; the seeds are falling in a line down onto the path. Two small birds, one red and one blue, are on the path pecking at the seeds. Nothing looks impossible or strange here; everyone is doing an ordinary outdoor thing.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The tool in the woman's hand is thin and I could not quite make out whether it is a trowel, a dibber or just a stick.
- The white animal is most likely a goat because of its curved horn and lean shape, but its body is quite plain, and at a glance it could be read as a white dog or a lamb.
- The man's hand holding the seeds is at the bag's side, so he could be pouring out from the bag or throwing by hand; both are close readings.
- The pot of pink flowers sits at the edge of the flower bed and slightly overlaps the soil mound, so it is a little unclear whether it is in the bed or next to it.
