# Picture 41

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence and gate at the back, and a stone path along the right. On the right at the back, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, is painting the fence blue with a roller on a long handle; she has already done a section of it. On the ground near her are a grey paint tin and a paint tray holding blue paint. A white dog with brown spots is walking away from the tray to the left, and it has clearly stepped in the paint: its paws are blue and it has left a trail of blue paw prints across the grass. On the left, a woman in a yellow dress stands with her hands on her hips wearing a silver bucket upside down on her head, which is a very odd thing to do. At the front right, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is running and kicking an orange ball, and he appears to be about to send it across the stone path. A grey wheelbarrow stands empty on the grass, and there is a flower bed next to the house. Nobody seems to have noticed the dog or the paw prints yet.
