## What I see

A large cartoon tree with a brown trunk and a dark green, cloud-shaped canopy stands on green grass under a pale blue sky. An orange fish with a black eye, a curved line for a gill and a triangular tail sits inside the canopy, at the end of a brown branch that curves out to the left from the trunk. A second brown branch goes up and to the right, ending at a small lighter green clump of leaves. Around the tree are rolling green hills and bushes behind, a pale cream winding path on the left, and a dark green bush in the lower right. No person or animal other than the fish appears.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The fish in the tree is the strange part. A fish does not belong in a tree, so it could be a deliberately odd object placed there, or the fish could be something else misdrawn, such as a large orange fruit or leaf on the branch end. Its eye, gill line and tail make it read most strongly as a fish.
- The fish seems to rest on or be attached to the very tip of the branch, with the branch running into its tail end. It could be sitting on the branch or could be growing from it like fruit.
- The branch and fish are drawn on top of the canopy rather than among the leaves, so they look pasted onto the green, and the branches appear to pass in front of the foliage rather than through it.
- The small lighter green clump on the right looks like a separate leafy tuft overlaid on the canopy. It could be a bunch of leaves on the branch end or a small bush that looks out of place, and it does not blend with the canopy.
- The canopy outline has small notches on its left and right sides where the rounded lobes meet, which look slightly unintentional.
- The cream path stops at the bottom edge and its upper end comes to a point at the left of the trunk, so it is not clear where it leads; it seems to end abruptly on the grass.
