## What I see

A brown-haired man or boy in a red T-shirt, blue trousers and dark shoes stands on the grass in a back garden, holding a yellow hose nozzle in his hand and spraying a pale blue arc of water onto a bed of flowers. The flower bed is an oval of brown soil with red, yellow, pink, purple and orange blooms on green stems, and water droplets fall near the base of the arc. A dark teal hose runs from the nozzle down past his side, along the grass and around the bed to a round hose reel on a dark stand at the lower left. Behind him is a wooden plank fence and a green bush, and at the left is part of a cream house wall with a blue door and a grey step.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The hose rises as a stiff, nearly straight segment right next to the man's hip and hand, then curves away. It is not clear whether it properly connects to the nozzle at his hand, as the top of the hose appears to end beside the nozzle just below his hand rather than feeding smoothly into it.
- The water arc lands in the middle of the flowers and appears to pass in front of some blooms and stems; the end of it looks like a solid tapered band rather than spray, so it could read as a ribbon or tube instead of water.
- The man's face is turned to the side but shows features in a slightly odd way: the eye and small nose or mouth marks are bunched toward the front edge of the head, and there is a small pinkish mark near the mouth that I could not identify. It could be a nose, a mouth, or a whistling expression.
- His legs look very long and thin with the feet close together and overlapping, so the stance looks slightly stiff. His feet could read as standing still or stepping.
- The reel's hose end loops down from the reel and doubles back at its lower right before running along the grass, which looks like a tight kink at the join.
