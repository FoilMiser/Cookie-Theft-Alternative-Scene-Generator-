## What I see

A backyard with green grass, a wooden fence, and part of a cream house with a blue door on the right. A boy in a red cap, blue hooded top, dark trousers and white shoes stands at the left, holding the nozzle end of a dark teal garden hose up in front of him with both arms stretched out. The hose runs down from his hands, along the grass and across to a hose reel on a stand at the lower right, beside a round dirt flower bed full of yellow, orange, red, pink and purple flowers. A white goat with small dark curved horns stands in the middle of the lawn, facing left toward the boy, with its rear hooves on or right next to the hose. The corner of a grey object, possibly a wheelbarrow, shows at the bottom left.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The hose gets noticeably fatter and flatter along the stretch just to the right of the goat's back hooves, from about the goat's hind foot to partway toward the reel. This could be a bulge of water backed up because the goat is standing on the hose and pinching it, or it could just be an odd drawing artefact. On the left side of the goat the hose is thinner, which fits the pinched reading.
- The boy could be aiming the hose and spraying, or could be looking at the nozzle because nothing is coming out. No water is visible at the nozzle.
- The boy's head is turned so that his face is partly hidden, and he seems to look down at or past his arm; the face is hard to read, and the head seems to sit low against the shoulder.
- The boy's two arms are drawn very close together and straight out, and the hands around the nozzle are small and fiddly, so it is hard to tell which fingers belong to which hand. A small blue-grey teardrop-shaped thing hangs near his forearm or wrist, and it could be a drip of water or part of his sleeve.
- The goat's body is quite blocky and its legs are thin and straight, with only two legs clearly visible from this side, so it looks slightly stiff. There is a pale shape under its chest or at its near shoulder that could be a front leg in the background or a fold of its body.
- The grey object at the bottom left is cut off, so I cannot be sure it is a wheelbarrow. Dark curved shapes under it could be a wheel or a coiled object.
- Two small blue shapes at the very top edge, above the fence, are cut off and I cannot tell what they are.
