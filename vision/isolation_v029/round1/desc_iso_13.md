## What I see

A woman with dark hair tied back, wearing a purple top, teal trousers and dark shoes, stands on green grass at the edge of a blue pond. She holds a brown paper bag or box against her front with one hand and reaches her other arm forward. A white duck with an orange beak is at the water's edge, its head down toward a scattering of small pale oval pieces, which look like bread or seed crumbs. The crumbs lie on the water near the duck and on the grass, so she appears to be feeding the duck. Two brown cattail reeds stand at the left end of the pond.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The duck's pose is odd: its body is tilted and its head is stretched low and forward, so it could be pecking at the food or could look like it is lying on its side or tipped over. The wing and tail shapes on top read somewhat oddly, and the body looks twisted.
- The white lines to the right of the duck could be ripples or wake in the water, or motion lines, or could be read as stray sticks; they look like detached strokes.
- The brown rectangular object could be a paper bag or a cardboard box. It seems to sit behind or against her body with no clear hand gripping it, so it could look like it is floating at her waist.
- Her outstretched hand has no visible falling crumbs, so it is not certain whether she is tossing food or just holding her hand out. The crumbs nearest the duck sit on the water line and the others sit on the grass, which could be read as either thrown food or objects simply lying there, and some look like small stones or pebbles.
- Her legs are drawn close together and very long, with the feet crossed or overlapping at the bottom so that the shoes look merged; the proportions are a bit stretched.
