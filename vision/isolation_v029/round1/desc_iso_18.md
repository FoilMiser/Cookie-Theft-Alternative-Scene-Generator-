## What I see

A man with short brown hair, a red T-shirt, a brown belt and blue trousers stands on a lawn and waters a flower bed with a garden hose. He holds a yellow nozzle in his hand, and a blue-white arc of water falls onto the flowers, with drops at the end. The flowers are yellow, orange, purple, pink and red in a brown oval bed. A dark teal hose runs from his hand, down and along the ground around the front of the bed, to a round hose reel on a dark stand at the right. Behind are a wooden fence, a green bush and part of a cream house with a blue door and doorstep.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The hose rises from the ground to his hand as a stiff, near-vertical section, and it appears to stand up on its own near his hand, so it is a little unclear how it joins the nozzle.
- The hose passes in front of the lower edge of the flower bed, and where it starts near his feet it could be read as touching or lying across the edge of the bed; it does not look like it passes through anything, but the overlap is close.
- His face has some small dark marks around the mouth and cheek that are hard to read; it could be a mouth with stubble or just a smudge-like detail.
- His feet are dark, flat shapes that overlap each other, so his stance is hard to read, and his legs look thin and rather stiff compared with his upper body.
- The reel looks rather large for the hose, and the way the hose connects to it, curling round at the base, could be read as either plugged into the reel's centre or simply tucked behind it.
