# iso_08

## What I see
A man with dark brown skin and short dark hair, in a green short-sleeved shirt, beige trousers and brown shoes, stands on grass at the edge of a blue pond, leaning slightly and reaching one arm out over the water. A white duck is on the pond with its orange beak near several small pale round pieces, some on the water and some on the grass, so the man appears to be feeding the duck. A tan box or bag stands at the man's waist, apparently what the food comes from. Bulrushes stand at the left side of the pond, and a purple flower in a brown pot or mound is cut off at the bottom right corner.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The tan rectangle could be a paper bag or a cardboard box. It sits behind the man at waist height with no clear hand holding it, so it seems to float or be stuck to his body rather than being carried. It could also be read as a post or sign on the far bank.
- The small pale ovals could be bread or seed thrown as feed, or they could be pebbles or stepping stones. Some lie on the grass outside the pond edge, which supports either reading.
- The duck's body looks oddly shaped: it is tilted, with the head low and the wing shown as a separate oval, so it could be read as swimming with its head down to eat or as lying on its side.
- The white lines behind the duck could be wake lines on the water or tail feathers, and they extend past the pond's edge onto the grass.
- The man's near hand is a dark, stubby shape that is hard to read, and it is not clear whether it is open, dropping food or holding something.
- His legs are very long and tube-like, and they look bent backward slightly; the feet overlap so they read as a single lump.
- The dark marking around his mouth and chin could be a beard or just the mouth drawing.
- The dark band at his waist could be a belt, but it extends slightly beyond his body on the left.
