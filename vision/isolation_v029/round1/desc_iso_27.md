## What I see

A brown-haired man in a red short-sleeved shirt, blue trousers with a belt and dark shoes stands on the grass at the edge of a blue pond, leaning forward with one arm stretched out. He holds a brown paper bag against his body with the other arm, and his open hand reaches toward the water, so he seems to be scattering or tossing food. A white duck with an orange bill is in the water with its head down, pecking at a line of small pale oval pieces that are scattered on the water and on the grass. White lines behind the duck look like motion or splash marks. Bulrushes stand at the right edge of the pond, and part of a potted purple flower shows in the bottom left corner.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The pale ovals could be bread pieces, seeds or grain, or possibly pebbles; the context suggests food, but their shape alone does not settle it.
- The pieces on the grass and the pieces on the water look the same, and they appear to sit on top of the water surface rather than floating in it. The ones on the grass are outside the pond, which could be spilled food or just a scattered trail.
- The duck's pose is odd: its head is close to its body and its neck hardly shows, so the head looks like a separate ball stuck on the front. The body seems to tilt with the tail end raised, and it could be read as dipping or feeding, or as lying on its side or collapsed.
- The small dark or orange mark under the duck's body might be a foot, but it is too small to be sure.
- The white lines behind the duck could be a splash, a wake or marks of quick movement.
- The bag seems to float in front of the man's torso, with only a slight sense of the arm holding it, so it is not fully clear how he is gripping it. It also looks more like a box or block than a soft bag.
- The man's legs are long and straight relative to his upper body, and the shoes overlap in a dark clump, so the feet are hard to separate.
