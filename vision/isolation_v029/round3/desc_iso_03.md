## What I see
A boy or young man with short brown hair, a red short-sleeved shirt, blue trousers with a brown belt and dark shoes stands on green grass in a back garden, watering a flower bed. He holds a yellow hose nozzle in both hands and a pale blue arc of water runs from it down onto a round bed of brown soil, with drops splashing at the end of the arc. The bed holds tall flowers in red, yellow, purple, pink and orange on green stems. A dark teal hose runs from the nozzle down past his legs, around the front of the flower bed, and across the grass to a round hose reel on a dark stand at the lower left. Behind the scene are a wooden plank fence, a rounded green bush, and on the left the corner of a cream building with a blue door that has a round yellow knob and a grey step.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The hose does not clearly connect to the nozzle or his hands. A short upright piece of hose rises from the loop near his hip to just under his hands, and I cannot tell whether it joins the yellow nozzle or just ends behind his forearm, so it looks slightly cut off.
- The hose's route is a bit odd: it rises from the grass and loops up beside the flower bed to his waist height with no visible support, and some of it passes in front of the bed's edge. It could be read as lying on the ground or as hanging in the air.
- The water arc is drawn as a thick solid band that looks like a pale blue tube as much as a stream of water. It could be read as water or as a second, lighter hose. It also lands in among the flowers and seems to overlap the stems instead of going behind or in front of them clearly.
- The hose reel could be read as a hose reel on a stand or as a wheel or large spool; the hose end at its base curls in a small hook, so the connection there is not fully clear.
- There are small marks around his mouth and cheek (a dark spot and a smudge) that I cannot make out; they could be an open mouth, freckles or a stray mark. His eye and profile look a little unusual, with the face turned sideways while the body is more three-quarter.
- His legs are very long and thin compared with his upper body, and his feet are bunched together and overlapping, so it is hard to tell which shoe belongs to which leg.
- The blue door and building are cut off at the left edge, so I can't tell if it is a house, a shed or something else. The grey step sticks out from the wall and seems a little detached from the door.
