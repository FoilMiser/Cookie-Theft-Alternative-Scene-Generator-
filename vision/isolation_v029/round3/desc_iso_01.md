## What I see

A brown dog with white paws, a white chest patch and a red collar sits on green grass next to a blue door in a cream house wall. The dog is holding the end of a red leash in its mouth; the leash hangs down in front of it, across the grey doorstep, and ends in a loop lying on the grass. The dog seems to be waiting at the door, as if asking to go for a walk. At the left edge there is part of a green bush and a brown curved shape that may be a flower bed or soil.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The red leash could be read two ways: the dog carrying its own lead in its mouth, or the lead simply attached to its muzzle or collar and hanging down. It seems to go into the mouth rather than to the collar, but the end is hard to separate from the muzzle.
- The red loop on the grass could be the leash handle or a separate ring or hoop; it looks connected to the leash.
- The tail and hind leg area is a little confusing: the tail is drawn in several folded or overlapping lobes, and the hind leg shapes are not clearly separated from it.
- The white patch on the chest is a rather sharply defined oval that could read as a marking or as a separate stuck-on shape.
- The brown curved shape at the far left is cut off, so I cannot tell what it is. There is also a small dark mark on the left edge above it that I cannot identify. A tan box-like shape at the top left, behind the bush, is also too cropped to identify.
- The body is quite tall and upright for a dog, with long straight front legs, so the proportions look slightly stiff.
