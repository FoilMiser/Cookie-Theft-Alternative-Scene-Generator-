## What I see

A boy or young man with brown hair, a red T-shirt, blue trousers and dark shoes stands on green grass in a garden, watering a bed of flowers with a hose. He holds a yellow nozzle in one hand and a stream of water arcs from it down onto the flowers, with droplets falling near the soil. The flower bed holds yellow, orange, purple, pink and red flowers in brown soil. A dark teal hose runs from him around the front of the bed to a hose reel on a dark stand on the right. Behind are a wooden fence, a green bush and part of a cream house with a blue door and a grey doorstep.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The hose near the person is unclear: a short thick teal section stands upright just below his hand, near his waist, and appears to stop or have a gap before the longer hose continues from below it. It could be read as the hose rising to the nozzle, or as a separate handle or grip. The link between the nozzle and the hose is not clear, and it looks as if the hose is partly broken or hidden there.
- The hand holding the nozzle sits right at the front, and only one arm is clearly visible; the other arm is not obvious, so it may be hidden or may be missing.
- The face has small dark marks around the mouth and cheek that could be a mouth, freckles or a stubble-like smudge; they look slightly messy and the expression is hard to read.
- The water stream is drawn as a wide solid blue-and-white band rather than loose water, so it could also be read as a solid curved tube or ribbon, though the drops at the end suggest water.
- The legs look long and thin and close together, and the shoes appear to overlap, so the footing is a little hard to separate.
- The flower stems overlap heavily and the water ends among them, so it is hard to see exactly where the water lands.
