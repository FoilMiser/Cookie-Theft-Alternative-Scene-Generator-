## What I see
A tan and brown dog with a red collar sits on green grass beside a blue front door with a yellow round knob, set in a cream-coloured house wall with a grey step or doorsill. The dog holds the end of a long red leash in its mouth, and the leash hangs down from its mouth to the ground, where the rest lies in a small loop on the grass in front of the door. The dog is facing toward the door, and it seems to be waiting to go for a walk, or asking to. Part of a green bush and a brown round shape (possibly a pot or a tree trunk) are cut off at the left edge.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The dog's body is oddly proportioned: its torso is very upright and long, almost like a standing person, with thin straight front legs. The back part of the body slopes down to the right and the hind leg and tail area are a jumble of narrow shapes, so it is hard to tell which is the tail and which is the hind leg.
- White patch on the chest looks like a separate oval stuck on, and the white paws look like separate rounded pieces that are not clearly joined to the legs. One white paw sits away from the others on the right.
- The dog's muzzle sits right against the edge of the door, so it could read as the dog touching or sniffing the door, or just as being in front of it.
- The dog's scale relative to the door looks fairly large, reaching more than halfway up the door, but plausible for a big dog.
- The items at the left edge are cut off and I can't identify them for sure.
