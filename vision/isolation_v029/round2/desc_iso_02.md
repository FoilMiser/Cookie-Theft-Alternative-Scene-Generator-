## What I see

A woman with dark hair tied back, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, stands on grass at the edge of a blue pond with reeds or bulrushes at its left end. She is looking toward the water and holding a brown paper bag or box against her body with one hand while the other arm reaches out, as if she has just scattered food. Small pale oval pieces, which look like bread or seed, lie on the water and on the grass in front of her. A white duck with an orange beak has its head bent down to the pieces at the water's edge, pecking at them. White lines beside the duck's tail suggest movement or splashing.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The pale ovals could be bits of bread or food, or could be read as small pebbles. Some are on the water and some are on the grass, and those on the water do not look as though they are floating in it; they sit on top like flat discs.
- The duck's pose is awkward. The head and neck bend sharply down and forward, the beak points down and back in an odd direction, and the head looks almost joined to the body as a separate ball. It could be feeding or could look as if it is tipping over.
- The duck's position is ambiguous. It sits close to the pond's lower edge, with a dark shadow-like patch beneath it on the water, so it could be swimming or standing at the edge.
- The white lines at the duck's rear could be motion or splash marks, or could be read as stray sticks lying across the pond's edge.
- The brown bag or box sits behind and partly against her waist. It is not clear whether she is really gripping it, as it seems to rest against her body and its top corner looks folded or stepped. It could be a paper bag or a cardboard box.
- Her reaching hand is a small, stubby shape, and no food is visibly leaving it, so she could be throwing food or simply holding her hand out.
- Her feet are dark and overlap into one lumpy shape, and they point sideways, which looks a little odd against the way her body faces.
- There are small dark marks around her mouth and on her cheek that I cannot read clearly. They could be a mouth or a slightly smudged expression.
- Her lower body seems narrow and long, and her belt or waistband looks like a dark curved band sitting apart from the top and trousers.
- The dark green band across the top of the picture could be distant hedge, a far bank or just a background strip.
