## What I see

A woman with dark tied-back hair, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, stands on grass holding a hose with a yellow nozzle in her outstretched hands. A pale blue arc of water sprays from the nozzle and falls with droplets onto a round brown flower bed filled with red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers. She is watering the flowers. The dark teal hose runs from her hands down past her feet and across the grass in a long loop back to a round hose reel on a small box at the lower left. Behind her is a wooden plank fence, a rounded green bush, and at the left a cream house wall with a blue door that has a yellow round knob and a grey step.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The hose rises vertically from the ground up to the nozzle in front of her body and seems to stand stiffly in the air between her waist and the ground before looping away. It could be read as her holding it up or as a rigid pipe; the upper stretch close to her body looks odd.
- Only one hand is clearly visible on the nozzle; the second arm seems hidden or merged with her body, so I can't tell whether she uses one hand or two.
- The hose passes behind or very close to her feet at the right, and I can't tell whether it goes behind her legs or touches them.
- The water arc is drawn as a thick, solid band that looks more like a curved glass tube or ribbon than loose spray, and it thins to the nozzle. Its lower end lands in the flowers with a few separate droplets.
- The hose reel could be read as a tyre or wheel on a box, or a coiled hose reel; the hose attaches at its lower side with a sharp small loop.
- Her face is in profile with a dark mark near the mouth that is hard to read at this size, and it looks slightly smudged.
- Her dark feet overlap and point in an odd direction, and the legs are long and slim compared with the upper body.
- The door and step at the left are cut off by the edge, and the step appears to stick out past the wall line, so it could be a doorstep or a separate slab.
