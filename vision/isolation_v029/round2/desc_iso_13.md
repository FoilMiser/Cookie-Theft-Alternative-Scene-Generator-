## What I see

A man with short brown hair, wearing a red T-shirt, blue trousers with a brown belt, and dark shoes, stands on a green lawn beside a round flower bed. He holds a dark teal garden hose with a yellow nozzle in front of him, and a pale blue arc of water runs from the nozzle down onto the flowers, with droplets splashing at the bottom, so he is watering them. The bed has brown soil and many colourful flowers (red, yellow, purple, pink, orange). The hose runs along the grass, around the bed, to a round hose reel on a dark base at the lower left. Behind him is a wooden plank fence and a green bush, and at the left edge is part of a cream wall with a blue door that has a yellow round knob.
