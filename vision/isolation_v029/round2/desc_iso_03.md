## What I see

A brown-haired man in a red T-shirt and blue trousers stands on a green lawn holding a garden hose with both hands in front of him. A stream of water arcs from the yellow nozzle down onto a bed of brown soil full of colourful flowers (yellow, orange, purple, pink and red), and drops of water fall among the stems. He is watering the flowers. The dark teal hose runs from his hands down to the ground, loops across the grass around the flower bed, and connects to a round hose reel with a dark base on the right. Behind him is a wooden fence, and on the right there is a green bush and the corner of a cream house with a blue door and a grey doorstep.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The hose near the man looks broken up. A short piece rises from his hands and stops at about hip height, and then the longer hose starts again just below it with a slight gap or offset, so the two sections do not join cleanly. It could be one hose passing behind his hand or leg, or two separate pieces.
- Only one hand is clear on the nozzle. The other arm's position is hard to separate from the body, so it is not obvious whether he holds the hose with one hand or two.
- The hose reel connection at the bottom right bends sharply into the reel's base, and it is unclear whether the hose feeds into the reel, wraps around it or simply ends beside it. The reel could also be read as a wheel or a tap fitting. It stands on the grass with no visible link to a tap or the house.
- The water stream looks thick and solid, like a pale blue band with a white core rather than loose spray, and it ends among the flowers with separate drops. It could be read as a curved solid object at first glance.
- His feet are dark and overlapping, forming one lumpy shape that points sideways, and it is hard to tell one foot from the other.
- There are small dark marks around his mouth and cheek that look slightly smudged, so his expression is hard to read. His eye is drawn large and looks sideways toward the flowers.
- A few of the flowers overlap one another, and some of the stems are hidden by the water, so the exact number of plants and how they sit in the soil is hard to tell.
- The dark line around his waist could be a belt, but it sits slightly apart from both the shirt and trousers and looks a bit like a separate band.
