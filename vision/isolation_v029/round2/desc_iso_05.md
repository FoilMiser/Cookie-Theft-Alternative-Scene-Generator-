## What I see

A woman with dark hair tied back, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, stands on grass at the edge of a blue pond. She holds out one hand toward a white duck with an orange beak, which is on the water with its head bent down toward a line of small pale oval pieces. The pale ovals are scattered on the water and on the grass near her feet, and look like bread or seed being thrown or dropped for the duck, so she appears to be feeding it. A brown paper bag or cardboard box is at her waist, seemingly held in her other arm. Two cattail reeds stand at the left end of the pond.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The brown object at her middle could be a paper bag of food she is holding or a small box; it also overlaps her body in a way that makes it hard to tell whether she holds it or it is floating behind her at hip height. Its back edge seems to sit behind her torso, with no clear hand or arm supporting it.
- The pale ovals could be bread, seeds, crumbs or pebbles. Several lie on the grass and others sit on the water, with the same look in both places, and they do not visibly sink or sit on anything. They could also be read as stepping stones or as things dropping out of her hand.
- The duck's beak looks orange with a darker line and points down into the water or onto one of the ovals, so it could be eating or dipping its beak. The duck's body also looks tilted and a pale oval wing marking sits on its side, which is somewhat odd.
- The white lines to the lower right of the duck could be ripples, a wake or motion marks; they stand out as bright straight strokes and seem slightly disconnected from the duck.
- Her body proportions look unusual: her legs are long and tube-like and bend at the bottom into shoes that overlap each other, and her waist and belt look thin. Her forward arm appears bent, with the hand ending in a small fist-like shape rather than clearly open.
- Her head is turned so that the face looks partly in profile with a dark marking near the mouth, which could be a mouth or a smudge.
