# Picture 19

## Free description
This is a sunny playground with a dark metal fence and trees behind it. On the left, a girl in a pink dress and an old man are on a red seesaw. The man's end is down on the ground and the girl's end is up in the air. The old man wears a grey cap, a brown vest and a white shirt and has grey hair and a moustache. He is sitting on his end of the seesaw reading an open book, not playing. Because he is heavier and sits still, the girl looks stuck up high. She is holding on to the handle and her mouth is open, as if she is calling out or surprised. In the middle there is a swing frame with one red swing seat, and nobody is on it. On the right, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts is sliding down a red-and-yellow slide with both arms up, as if shouting "wheee". There is also a green rubbish bin by the fence, a tree on the far left, a bush on the right and a grey rock in the bottom right corner.
