# pic_12

## Free description
This is the same kind of sunny back garden: a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence with a gate on the right, and a paved path leading to the gate. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and round glasses, wearing a teal top, a grey skirt and brown shoes, stands beside a round flower bed full of colourful flowers. She is holding a teal watering can low down in front of her, with its spout pointing toward the flower bed. A curved blue stream of water comes out near the spout and arcs up over the flowers, with a few water drops at the top of the arc, so she is watering the flowers. Behind her a washing line holds blue jeans and a yellow red-striped towel. Two potted green plants stand on the grass near the fence on the right, a grey wheelbarrow sits in the front, and a wooden bench is at the bottom left. The odd thing is the direction of the water: it seems to rise upward out of a low-held watering can rather than pouring down.
