## Free description
This is a sunny playground surrounded by a dark metal railing fence with hedges behind it. They look upset, as if they just lost it. An old man with grey hair, a white shirt and a brown waistcoat sits on a bench drinking from a red-and-white cup through a straw. In the middle is a swing set with one red swing hanging empty. On the right is a red-framed slide with a yellow chute and a ladder, and behind it a boy in a red cap and blue top is flying an orange diamond-shaped kite high in the sky near the sun. In the front centre, an orange cat is crouched low, creeping towards a red bird on the grass, and looks about to pounce on it. A blue bird sits on a branch of the tree on the left. There is also an empty sandpit, a grey rock and a green bin.
