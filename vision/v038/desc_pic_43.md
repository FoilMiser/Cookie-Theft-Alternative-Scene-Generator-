# pic_43

## Free description
A sunny back garden seen the other way round from a typical layout. A cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the left, and a wooden gate in the tall fence is on the right, with a paved path leading from it toward the front right. A green garden hose runs along the ground from the left to a small sprinkler in the centre. The sprinkler is spraying arcs of water to the right, and the water lands on a cardboard box sitting in a puddle, so the box is getting soaked. To the right of the box, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes is in a sitting position reading a red book, but there is no chair, bench or anything under him. He is sitting on thin air. On the right, a dark-skinned man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers walks along the path holding a red lead attached to a white dog with brown spots, which walks in front of him toward the right. A flower bed of colourful flowers and a bush sit by the house. The impossible thing is the boy sitting on nothing. The soaked box is an odd detail too.
