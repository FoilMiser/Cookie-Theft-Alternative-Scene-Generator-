# Picture 27

## Free description
This is a sunny town street with a row of colourful shops and a paved square. On the left, a woman with long dark hair and a yellow dress walks along carrying a yellow shopping bag. A teal bicycle is parked in a bike rack behind her near the tree. In the middle, a girl in a pink dress sits on a bench drinking from a cup with a straw, with a flower planter behind her. In front, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie walks while reading an open book. He is heading toward a cardboard box on the ground just in front of him, so he might trip over it. On the right, an old man in a grey cap, brown vest and white shirt holds a camera up to his eye and takes a photo toward the left. There is a fruit and vegetable market stall on the right with a small wooden crate beside it, a lamp post in the centre and a potted plant in the bottom right corner.
