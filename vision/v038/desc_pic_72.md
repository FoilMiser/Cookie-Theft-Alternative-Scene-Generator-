This is a sunny playground with a slide, a swing set, a sandpit, a bench and a big tree, all inside a dark railing fence. The sun is in the top left and a blue bird flies in the top right. In the front centre, a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and puts his other hand to his head in dismay. His pink ice cream has fallen on the ground in a splat with sprinkles, and a brown dog with a red collar is licking it up. To the left, beside the sandpit, a white dog with a brown ear sits upright like a person, holding an open newspaper in its front paws as if reading it. That is clearly impossible. On the bench at the right, an old man with white hair and glasses, in a teal jacket and shorts, sips a red drink through a straw. At the far right by the tree, a small girl in a pink dress holds the string of a yellow kite flying high in the sky. The swing and the slide are empty.
