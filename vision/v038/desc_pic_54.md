# pic_54

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house, a red roof and a blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back and a wooden gate at the right end of the fence. A woman (or teenage girl) with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers stands by the fence, painting it blue with a roller on a long handle. A few planks to her right are already blue, and a small paint tin sits on the grass by her feet. In the middle of the lawn, a garden hose runs from the left to a sprinkler. The sprinkler shoots arcs of water to the right, and the longest arc lands on a cardboard box sitting on the grass. The box is dripping and stands in a puddle, so it is getting soaked. On the paved path at the bottom right, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt is sweeping a pile of dirt towards a red dustpan. Instead of a broom she is using a giant silver dinner fork, which is clearly the wrong tool and looks silly. A little cloud of dust rises from the pile. Two potted plants stand by the fence and a flower bed sits near the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The old woman's tool is clearly an oversized dinner fork, not a rake or broom. A fork would not sweep dirt properly. It could be meant as a joke or as the "wrong" detail.
- The cardboard box in the path of the sprinkler is odd. Either someone left it there by mistake and it is being ruined, or the sprinkler is simply aimed badly.
- The painter's roller head sits right at the edge of the blue section, so she seems to be moving left to right along the fence. I could not tell whether she has noticed the soaked box.
- The painter is quite small compared with the fence, which comes up to about her shoulders. That looks plausible for a tall garden fence.
