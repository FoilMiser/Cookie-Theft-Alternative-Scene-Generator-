# pic_60

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house, a red roof and a blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back with a gate at the right, and a paved path down the right side. A boy in a red cap, a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes stands by the flower bed, watering the flowers with a teal watering can. A woman (or teenage girl) with a dark ponytail, a yellow dress and red shoes stands at the fence near the gate, painting it blue with a long-handled roller. A couple of planks to her right are already blue, and a paint tin sits on the grass by her feet. In the front centre, a boy with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans is reading a red book. He is in a sitting position, knees bent as if on a chair, but there is no chair, bench or anything else under him. He is sitting on thin air, which is impossible. The other two carry on with their jobs and do not seem to notice.

## Unsure or ambiguous

It could be read as (a) him impossibly sitting in mid-air or on an invisible seat, or (b) him doing a "wall sit" or squat. There is no wall behind him, so (a) looks more likely. His feet do touch the ground.
- The woman painter is small and far back by the gate, so details of her face and the roller are small but readable.
- Apart from the seated boy, nothing looks floating or impossible.
