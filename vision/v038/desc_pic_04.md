## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house on the right (red roof, window, blue door) and a tall wooden fence along the back, with a gate and paved path on the left. High in the sky an orange diamond-shaped kite with a tail of little bows is flying. Its string runs down to a boy with dark curly hair, a yellow top and orange shorts, who stands by the back fence holding it up with one raised arm. In the middle a man in a red T-shirt and blue trousers stands with his hands on his hips, wearing an upside-down grey metal bucket on his head, which looks silly and odd. In the front, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and black trousers is kicking an orange-and-white ball toward the right, with motion lines showing the kick. The ball seems headed toward the hose reel and flower bed near the house. Around the garden are two potted plants by the fence, a teal watering can at the front left, an empty wheelbarrow at the front centre, and a green hose on a reel lying on the grass at the right. Apart from the bucket worn as a hat, nothing looks impossible.
