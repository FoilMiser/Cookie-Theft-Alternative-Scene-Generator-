# Picture 07

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny park with green hills behind, a blue pond on the left, a lamp post in the middle and a winding path on the right. At the front left an old man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat is watering the flower bed by pouring water out of a black frying pan, which is a very odd thing to water flowers with. Next to him a white dog with brown ears sits holding an open newspaper as if reading it, which is impossible. On the pond a white duck swims toward a trail of floating crumbs that a girl in a pink dress, standing on the bank, seems to be throwing from a small bag. In the front centre a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice cream cone and clutches their head in dismay. The pink ice cream has fallen on the ground, and a brown dog with a red collar is licking it up. On the path an old woman with grey hair and glasses is sweeping a pile of autumn leaves with a broom, even though all the trees are green. Behind her a white goat is eating grass at the edge of the path. On the far right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie holds a kite string, but his purple kite is stuck in the tree. A blue bird flies across the sky, and the bench is empty.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Watering flowers with a frying pan is odd and probably deliberate. The water stream from a pan looks impossible-ish, since a pan wouldn't hold that much.
- Dog reading a newspaper: impossible. Probably a deliberate oddity.
- Autumn leaves being swept while every tree is fully green: a possible mismatch, or just ordinary leaves.
- The floating crumbs could be bread crumbs or small stones/pebbles. Given the duck and the girl's bag, I read them as bread.
- The girl's brown item could be a paper bag or a piece of bread.
- I can't tell how the ice cream fell. Most likely the child dropped it, though the dog could have knocked it.
- A goat loose in a public park is unusual but not impossible. It could also be read as a sheep or a white dog, but the horns suggest a goat.
