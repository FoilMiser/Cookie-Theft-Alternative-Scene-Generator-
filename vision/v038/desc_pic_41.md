# pic_41

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a wooden gate at the far left, a paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the right. In the centre-left is a dark grey metal swing frame. A small dark-skinned child with curly hair, a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on the red swing seat, smiling broadly, and there are motion lines behind the swing showing it is moving. On the right, near the house, a woman with dark hair in a yellow dress is digging with a shovel. There is a shovelful of soil on the blade and a heap of dug soil on the grass beside her, just behind a bed of colourful flowers. A teal watering can sits on the grass in the front, near the swing. The strange thing is a cardboard box floating in mid-air in the sky above the fence, in the centre of the picture. Nobody is throwing it, and nothing is holding it up. Apart from the floating box, the scene looks like an ordinary afternoon in the garden.
