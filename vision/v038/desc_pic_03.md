# Picture 03

## Free description
This is a sunny park with rolling green hills in the background. On the left is a blue pond with bulrushes at both ends. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and black trousers stands on the grass at the right edge of the pond, holding a fishing rod with both hands. The line runs from the rod tip down to a red-and-white float in the water, with a small ripple ring around it, so he seems to be waiting for a bite, or maybe something has just nibbled. A tall black lamp post stands in the middle, and a wooden bench sits empty to the right beside a winding sandy path. There are trees on both sides, a few bushes, a flower bed of colourful flowers at the front left, a green bin in the bottom left corner and a grey rock at the front centre. He is the only person, and there are no animals or fish in sight. It is a quiet, calm scene, and nothing looks wrong or impossible.
