# pic_47

## Free description
This is a sunny but very windy back garden, with a house on the right, a wooden fence across the back, a gate at the far left and a paved path down the left side. A woman with long dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes stands on the grass near the path with both arms thrown up and a startled look on her face. Just above her and slightly to the right, an orange cap is flying through the air, with white wind lines and a few green and brown leaves blowing along with it. It looks as if the wind has just blown the cap off her head and she is reaching up to grab it. Behind her, a washing line hangs between two wooden posts, with a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue trousers pegged on it. Both are blowing sideways in the wind, and there are more wind lines in the sky. A colourful flower bed, a bush, a tree and an empty wooden bench by the house complete the scene. The cap looks about to be carried further away.
