## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house, a red roof and a blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back with a gate at the right, and a paved path down the right side. A woman (or teenage girl) with a dark ponytail, a yellow dress and red shoes stands at the fence, painting it blue with a long-handled roller. A section of about three planks to her left is already blue. Behind her on the grass are a paint tin and a flat paint tray full of blue paint. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is walking away from the tray to the right. It has blue paws and has left a trail of blue paw prints across the grass from the tray, so it has obviously stepped in the paint. The woman has her back to the dog and does not seem to have noticed. It looks as if the dog is about to spread blue paint further around the garden. A grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can stand on the lawn at the front, and there is a flower bed near the house.
