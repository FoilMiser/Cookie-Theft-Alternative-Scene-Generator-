## Free description
This is a windy, sunny back garden, with white wind lines in the sky and near the gate, and leaves blowing off the tree by the house. The laundry on the clothesline in the middle (a yellow-and-red striped shirt or towel and blue jeans) is blown out sideways to the left. On the path at the left, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, has both arms thrown up and her mouth open, because a red cap is flying away from her toward the gate on the left. An orange cat is walking along the top of the fence near the middle-left. In the middle, an elderly man with a grey cap, white shirt, brown vest and dark trousers holds a garden hose nozzle out in front of him and looks at it, but only a single drop is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel at the right, across the lawn and up to his hands. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is standing right on the hose in the front centre. Just behind the dog's foot the hose is swollen into a bulge, so the dog seems to be blocking the water. A flower bed sits in front of the house, which has a red roof and blue door on the right.
