## Free description
This is a sunny back garden seen the other way round from the earlier ones: the cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the right, the wooden gate is at the back left, and a paved path runs from the gate down the left side. On the left, by the gate, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands with his hands on his hips and a white bucket upside down on his head like a hat. That looks silly and out of place. In the middle, a young man in a red shirt and blue jeans is hanging washing on a clothesline between two wooden posts. He is pegging up a white T-shirt, and a pair of blue jeans already hangs there, both dripping water. Below the line on the grass is a wooden laundry basket full of pink, white and blue clothes. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers kneels by the flower bed, tending the flowers next to a small pot with a pink flower. There is also a swing set with an empty red swing, a teal watering can, a grey wheelbarrow and a wooden bench at the front.
