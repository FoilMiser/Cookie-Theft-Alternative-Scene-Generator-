# Picture 05

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny playground with a railing fence, a red slide on the left and a big tree on the right. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is holding a red and a yellow balloon in one hand and reaching up with the other toward a purple balloon that has just floated out of reach. A man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is taking a photo with a camera, pointed toward the left. A man in a red shirt sits on a bench reading a red book, with a sandwich on the bench beside him, and a blue bird is standing at the sandwich as if about to steal it while he isn't looking. A girl in a pink dress at the front right holds a kite string, but her purple kite is stuck in the leaves of the tree. A white dog with brown spots is crouched playfully over a tennis ball with its tail wagging, and a brown dog at the front right sits holding an open newspaper as if reading it, which is impossible. In the front centre a red bicycle stands on square wheels, which is also impossible, since it couldn't roll. On the far left a girl in a yellow dress is running toward the left edge, with speed lines behind her. A red bird flies across the sky, and the sandpit with a bucket and spade is empty.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Square bicycle wheels: impossible, and clearly strange. It looks like a deliberate oddity rather than a drawing error, but it could be read either way.
- Dog reading a newspaper: impossible for a real dog. Either a joke or a deliberate oddity.
- The running girl has no obvious reason to run. She could be running away from something, or toward something off the left edge.
- I can't tell what the man in green is photographing. His camera points left, toward the boy with the balloons, the slide, or the running girl.
- The blue bird could just be sitting next to the sandwich, or actively stealing it. It looks like it is pecking at it.
- The purple balloon could be one the boy just let go of, or one that drifted in. Its string ends just above his hand, so it looks like he just lost it.
