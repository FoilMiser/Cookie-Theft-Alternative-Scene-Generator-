# Picture 06

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills behind, a blue pond with bulrushes on the left, and a winding sandy path on the right. In the front centre a girl or young woman in a yellow dress with long dark hair sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket with her legs stretched out, smiling and looking to the right. A wicker picnic basket sits on the grass beside her, and her hand rests near it. Further back, on the path, a girl in a pink dress stands eating a pink ice cream cone, holding it up to her mouth with her eyes squeezed shut. A tall black lamp post stands in the middle, and a wooden bench sits empty beside the path. Around the park are trees on both sides, a few bushes, a flower bed at the front left, a green bin in the bottom left corner and a grey rock at the front centre. There are no animals. It is a calm, ordinary scene, and nothing looks wrong or impossible.
