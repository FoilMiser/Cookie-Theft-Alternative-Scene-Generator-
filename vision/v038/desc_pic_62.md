# pic_62

## Free description
This is a sunny town street with a wide paved pedestrian area in front of a row of four coloured shop buildings (pink, grey-green, peach and cream), each with a striped awning. On the left is a fruit and vegetable stall with a teal-and-white striped canopy and crates of yellow, orange and red produce, but nobody is serving at it. In front of the stall, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers walks to the right. He has a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. In the middle, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a wooden bench eating a pink ice-cream cone. In front of her, a brown dog with a red neckerchief sits up on its hind legs with its front paws raised, looking up as if begging for her ice cream. She seems to be busy with her ice cream and not looking at the dog. A teal bicycle is parked in a bike rack behind the bench, and there is a tall black street lamp, a tree in a round planter, a green bin and a potted plant. The brown dog may be about to get, or keep begging for, some of the ice cream.
