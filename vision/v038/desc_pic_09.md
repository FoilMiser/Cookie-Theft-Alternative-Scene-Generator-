# Picture 09

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house on the right (red roof, window, blue door), a tall wooden fence across the back, and a gate and paved path on the left. In the middle a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and black trousers holds a garden hose nozzle up close and looks into it, but only a single drop of water is coming out. A white goat is standing on the hose, and the hose is swollen between the goat and the reel by the house. It looks like the water is blocked and will spray the boy in the face when the goat moves. On the left, on the path, a boy with dark curly hair, a yellow shirt and orange shorts is jumping in the air with both arms up and a big happy face, with his shadow on the ground below him. There is an empty swing on a swing set, a bush and a flower bed near the house. Something impossible is in the tree at the back right: an orange goldfish sits up among the branches, at the end of a curved branch where the trunk bends.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- A fish in a tree is impossible. The tree trunk also bends sideways at an odd angle to end at the fish, which makes the tree shape itself look strange.
- The swollen part of the hose could be meant to show water trapped behind the goat's hoof, or it could just be the hose drawn thicker.
- The jumping boy could be cheering or excited about something (he faces the middle of the garden), or just jumping for fun. I can't see a clear reason.
- A goat in a back garden is unusual but not impossible.
- The hose boy peering straight into the nozzle looks like a set-up for getting sprayed in the face.
