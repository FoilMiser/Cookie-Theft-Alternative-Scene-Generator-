# Picture 08

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny back garden with a cream house on the right, a tall wooden fence along the back, and a gate and paved path on the left. A girl or young woman in a yellow dress is holding a garden hose nozzle up and peering at it, but only a single drop is coming out. That is because a white dog with brown spots is standing on the hose, and the hose bulges between the dog and the reel, so it looks like the water will burst out (probably in her face) when the dog steps off. Behind her an old woman with grey hair and glasses stands with her hands on her hips, wearing an upside-down metal bucket on her head, which looks silly. An orange cat is walking along the top of the fence. A girl in a pink dress at the back holds a kite string, but her purple kite is stuck in the tree. On the right a man in a red shirt sits on a bench reading a red book, and a blue bird beside him is at his sandwich, as if stealing it. On the left a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie rakes leaves on the path, and a brown-and-white puppy lies asleep on the path in front of him. At the front an empty wheelbarrow has a square wheel, which is impossible. There is also an empty swing and a birdhouse on a pole by the gate.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The swollen part of the hose could be meant to show water trapped behind the dog's paw (my reading), or it could just be the hose drawn thicker there.
- The square wheelbarrow wheel is impossible. It could be a drawing glitch or a deliberate oddity.
- The bucket on the old woman's head: odd and probably deliberate.
- The cat on top of the fence is plausible, not wrong.
- The blue bird could be just sitting beside the sandwich, or stealing it. It looks like it is pecking it.
- The boy raking leaves on a summery green day, when no trees are dropping leaves, is a mild mismatch.
