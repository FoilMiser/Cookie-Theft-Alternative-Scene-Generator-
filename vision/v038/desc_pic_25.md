# Picture 25

## Free description
This is a sunny playground with a dark metal fence and bushy trees behind it. In the centre front, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is walking along eating a pink ice cream cone. On the right, two children are on a red seesaw. A girl in a pink dress is on the left end, which is slightly higher, and a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts is on the right end, which is slightly lower. Both are holding the handles and look calm. The slide on the left, the green rubbish bin by the fence and the grass are otherwise empty. There is a big tree on the far right, a bush on the far left and a grey rock in the bottom left. Nothing looks dangerous or wrong.
