## Free description
This is a sunny back garden, with the house on the left (blue door, window, red roof), a wooden fence across the back, a gate at the far right and a paved path down the right side. In the middle, a woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers holds both hands out in front of her, and a red-and-white ball is in the air just in front of her hands. A dashed curved line runs from the ball down to a small girl in a pink dress, who is stepping forward with one arm out, so the two of them are playing catch. A brown dog with a red collar sits by the blue door holding a red lead in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. At the front, a white dog with brown spots is in a playful bow with its front end down and its tail up and wagging, and a yellow tennis ball sits in front of its nose. On the path at the right, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt is sweeping a pile of autumn leaves with a broom. A teal watering can sits on the grass near her, and a colourful flower bed and a bush are near the house.
