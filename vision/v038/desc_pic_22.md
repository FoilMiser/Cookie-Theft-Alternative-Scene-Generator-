# Picture 22

## Free description
This is a quiet, sunny park with rolling green hills, trees, a winding sandy path, a wooden bench and a tall black lamp post. On the right there is a blue pond with reeds (bulrushes) at both ends. An old woman with grey hair in a bun, round glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt stands at the front edge of the pond. She is holding a brown paper bag and tossing small crumbs or bits of bread to a white duck. A few crumbs are in the air between her hand and the duck, and one is on the water. The duck is swimming toward her and pecking at the food, with little ripple lines behind it. The bench is empty, and there is a green rubbish bin by the lamp post. There is a colourful flower bed in the bottom right and a grey rock near the bottom centre. Nothing looks dangerous or out of place.

- The small pale pieces could be bread crumbs, seeds or pellets.
- The crumb on the water near the bottom edge of the pond could also be read as lying on the bank.
