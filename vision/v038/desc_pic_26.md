# Picture 26

## Free description
This is a back garden with a wooden fence, a wooden gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a blue door and red roof on the right. In the middle, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is hanging washing on a line strung between two wooden posts. He is reaching up to peg a white T-shirt, and a pair of blue jeans already hangs on the line. Both are dripping water. At his feet is a wicker laundry basket full of clothes (pink, white and blue), with something blue hanging over the edge and dripping. He is the only person in the picture. Around the garden are two potted plants, a teal watering can and an empty wheelbarrow at the front, a hose reel with its hose and yellow nozzle lying on the grass by the house, a colourful flower bed and a bush. Nothing looks dangerous or wrong.
