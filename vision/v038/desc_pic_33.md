# pic_33

## Free description
This is a calm, sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate at the far left, a paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the path at the left, a girl or young woman with a dark ponytail, in a yellow dress and red shoes, is sweeping a pile of red, orange, yellow and brown autumn leaves with a yellow broom. In the middle, a clothesline between two wooden posts holds a yellow-and-red striped shirt or towel and a pair of blue jeans, hanging straight down. On the right, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, stands at the flower bed watering the flowers with a teal watering can. Oddly, the stream of water from the can rises upward in an arc toward the top of the flowers, with drops at the top, instead of pouring down. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands in the front centre, two potted plants sit by the fence, and a wooden bench is at the front right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water from the watering can travels upward from the spout in a curved jet, like a hose or fountain, and drips at the top. A watering can would pour downward, so this looks impossible. Another reading is that the water is falling into the can from above, but there is no source up there.
- The can seems to be held low and partly in front of the flower bed edge, so the woman and can overlap the flowers. She may be standing in or right at the edge of the bed.
- Autumn leaves are being swept, but no nearby tree is shedding and the flowers are in full bloom. This is a small seasonal mismatch that may not matter.
