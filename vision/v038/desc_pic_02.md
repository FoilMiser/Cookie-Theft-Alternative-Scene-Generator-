# Picture 02

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house on the left (red roof, window, blue door), a tall wooden fence across the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. In the middle a man in a red T-shirt and blue trousers is reaching up to peg a white T-shirt onto a washing line strung between two wooden posts. A pair of blue jeans already hangs on the line, and both garments are dripping water. A laundry basket full of clothes sits on the grass by his feet. Behind him a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers holds his arms out toward a red ball flying through the air. A dashed curved line traces the ball's path back to a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie near the gate, who has his arm stretched out as if he has just thrown it, so the man looks about to catch it. At the front right a girl in a yellow dress stands with her hands on her hips, wearing an upside-down grey metal bucket on her head like a hat, which looks silly and out of place. An empty wheelbarrow sits in the front centre, and a wooden bench and a flower bed of colourful flowers are at the front left.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Ball direction: the dashed trail runs from the boy's hand to the ball, which reads as the boy having thrown it to the man in green. It could also be read as the man having thrown it and the boy about to catch it. I lean toward the first reading.
- The man in green has his hands closed like fists rather than open, so he may not be catching, just gesturing or bracing.
- The bucket on the girl's head is the strangest thing in the picture. It could be a child playing dress-up or a deliberate "something wrong" detail.
- The clothes dripping on the line make sense, since they are freshly washed. Nothing else floats or overlaps strangely.
