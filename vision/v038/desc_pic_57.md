# pic_57

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house, a red roof and a blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back with a gate at the right, and a paved path down the right side. A woman (or teenage girl) with a dark ponytail, a yellow dress and red shoes stands beside the flower bed, watering the flowers with a teal watering can. A stream of water pours onto the flowers. By the blue door at the far left, a brown-and-white dog with a red collar sits holding its red lead in its mouth. The lead trails to a loop on the ground, as if the dog wants to go for a walk. In the tree behind the house, an orange goldfish sits on the end of a branch. That is impossible, since fish live in water, not trees. A grey wheelbarrow stands on the lawn at the front, and there are two potted plants and a bush by the fence. Nobody seems to have noticed the fish.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The dog holding its own lead probably means "I want a walk". It could also just be carrying it around.
- The woman is watering flowers on a bright sunny day, which is ordinary and looks fine.
