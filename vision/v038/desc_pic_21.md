# Picture 21

## Free description
This is a back garden with a tall wooden fence, a wooden gate on the left and a cream house with a blue door and red roof on the right. Near the gate, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller. A few boards next to the gate are already blue. A paint tin and a tray of blue paint are on the grass by his feet. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is walking away from the paint tray, and a trail of blue paw prints runs from the tray to the dog. The dog has clearly stepped in the paint and has blue feet, and the man has not noticed. In the middle-right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is watering a flower bed with a garden hose, and the water arcs onto the flowers. The hose runs back to a hose reel by the house. In the bottom left, a boy in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands on the paved path. He is sweeping dirt toward a red dustpan with what looks like a giant silver dinner fork instead of a broom, and a little dust cloud rises. There is also an empty wheelbarrow at the bottom centre and a wooden bench by the house.
