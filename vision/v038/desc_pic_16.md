## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the cream house and blue door on the left, a grey swing set on the right, and a wooden gate with a paved path at the far right. A woman with long dark hair in a ponytail, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, stands beside a round flower bed. She holds the nozzle of a garden hose, and an arc of blue water sprays from it down onto the colourful flowers, with drops landing among them. The hose runs from a hose reel on the grass at the left, across the lawn, and up into her hand. The swing set has one empty red swing hanging still, and a grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the front. Everything looks normal; she is simply watering the flowers.
