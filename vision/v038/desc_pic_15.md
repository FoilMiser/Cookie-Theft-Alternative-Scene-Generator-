On the right, a man with brown hair in a red shirt and blue trousers stands on the edge of the path holding a paper bag, and a trail of small dots falls from his other hand down to the path. Two small birds, one red and one blue, are on the path pecking at the dots, so he is feeding the birds with seeds or crumbs. The slightly unusual thing is a goat grazing loose in a public park.
