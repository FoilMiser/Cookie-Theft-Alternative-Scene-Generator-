# pic_69

## Free description
This is a back garden on a gloomy day. The sky is grey and three dark storm clouds hang overhead, so it looks like it is about to rain. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door stands on the left, with a flowerbed in front of it, and a wooden fence with a gate runs across the back. In the middle of the lawn a small girl in a pink dress has just thrown a red-and-white ball. A dotted line shows it arcing through the air towards a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie, who holds his hands out to catch it. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers points up at the dark clouds with her mouth open, as if warning everyone about the coming rain. At the far right, an old man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat stands on the paved path. He is sweeping a little pile of dirt towards a red dustpan, but instead of a broom he is using a giant silver fork shaped like a dinner fork. A puff of dust rises where the fork meets the ground. That fork is the strangest thing in the picture, because you cannot sweep with a fork and it is far bigger than any real cutlery.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The old man's tool could be read as an oversized garden fork, but it looks like a shiny table fork, not a garden tool with a wooden handle. Either way it is odd to sweep with it. Another reading is that he is digging or scraping the path with it, but the dustpan, the pile of dirt and the dust cloud suggest sweeping.
- The woman could be pointing at the clouds to warn of rain, or pointing at something else in the sky that is not drawn. The clouds are the only thing up there.
- The girl is quite a bit smaller than the boy, so she is either a younger child or simply standing further forward. The size difference is noticeable.
- The weather is stormy, yet everyone carries on playing and working outdoors with no umbrellas or coats.
- The red dustpan is lying on its side at the edge of the path, partly on the grass. It is not clear whether the old man is holding it or has put it down.
