## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house, a red roof and a blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back with a gate at the right, and a paved path running down the right side. A woman with dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes sits on a wooden bench at the bottom left, reading a red book. In the middle of the garden, a washing line hangs between two wooden posts. A pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes are pegged on it. Above the washing line, a closed cardboard box hangs tilted in mid-air in the sky, with nothing holding it up. It is not clear whether it is falling, flying or just floating, and that looks impossible. On the grass at the right, a small white puppy with a brown patch is curled up asleep next to a red food bowl with kibble in it. There is a flower bed in the centre-left and two potted plants by the fence. Nobody seems to have noticed the box.
