# Picture 23

## Free description
This is a back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and path on the left, and a cream house with a blue door and red roof on the right. On the left, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts is swinging on a swing, with motion lines showing he is moving. In the middle, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is raking a small pile of leaves under a washing line. A yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans hang on the line. At the bottom centre, a girl in a pink dress holds a kite string and looks up. The string goes up to a purple diamond kite that is stuck in the leafy tree at the top right, with leaves drawn over part of it. Its tail with small bows hangs down. So the girl's kite seems to be caught in the tree. There is also a colourful flower bed in front of the house.
