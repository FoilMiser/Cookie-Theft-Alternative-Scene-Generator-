# Picture 24

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny town street with colourful shops behind a paved square. A red bird flies in the top left. On the left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping a pile of autumn leaves with a broom. Next to a tree, a girl in a pink dress points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches. An old man in a grey cap and brown vest walks past in front, carrying a yellow shopping bag. In the centre, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts jumps in the air with both arms up, with his shadow on the ground below him. On a bench, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers reads a red book. A sandwich lies next to her, and a blue bird is standing right beside it, looking like it is about to take it. To the right, an old woman with glasses and grey hair is watering a planter of flowers with a teal watering can. Oddly, the water arcs upward and away from the flowers instead of pouring down onto them. In front, a brown dog with a red bandana sits on the ground holding an open newspaper as if reading it, which is impossible. On the right, a man in a green shirt walks while reading an open book, heading toward a cardboard box on the ground, so he might trip over it. There is also a market stall of fruit and vegetables on the right, a lamp post, and potted plants in the front corners.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water from the old woman's watering can goes up and over to the left in an arc, with droplets at the top, rather than down onto the flowers. It looks physically wrong. Another reading is a spray or fountain going over the planter; either way it does not land on the flowers.
- The dog "reading" a newspaper is impossible in real life. It could also be read as the dog just holding or playing with a newspaper.
- The man reading the book seems to be walking left toward the cardboard box and may trip over it. He could also be walking past it.
- The jumping boy could be jumping for joy, or reaching for something, but nothing is above him. The shadow under him shows he is in the air.
- The blue bird could be stealing the sandwich, or just sitting next to it; the woman is not looking.
- The girl pointing at the ball may want help getting it down; the boy sweeping nearby does not seem to notice.
- The beach ball sits on a branch in the tree. That is plausible if it was thrown up there, but it is unusual.
