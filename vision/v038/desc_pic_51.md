## Free description
This is a back garden under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds and no sun, so it looks like rain is coming. At the front, an older woman with grey hair and glasses drinks from a red cup with a straw while sitting with her knees bent, but there is no chair or seat under her at all, so she is sitting on thin air. A child in a yellow top and orange shorts reads a red book on a bench by the house, an orange cat is curled up asleep at the front left by the path, and a red pet food bowl full of food sits on the grass nearby.

- The woman's pointing could be at the storm clouds (warning that rain is coming, perhaps onto the wet paint) or, less likely, at the ball up to her right. Her finger points upwards towards the clouds.
