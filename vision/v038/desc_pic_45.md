## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with no people or animals in it at all. A cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue front door fills the right side, and a tall wooden fence runs across the back, with a wooden gate at the far left and a paved path leading from the gate down to the front left corner. The strangest thing is a closed cardboard box hanging in the middle of the sky, tilted at an angle, with nothing holding it up and nobody nearby who could have thrown it. It looks as if it is falling or flying through the air, but there are no motion lines to say which way. The rest of the garden is ordinary: a birdhouse on a tall pole, two potted plants by the fence, a watering can lying on the grass, a round flower bed full of colourful flowers, a garden hose on a reel with its yellow nozzle on the grass, and a wooden bench by the house. The sun shines at top left and two white clouds float in a blue sky. Everything is still and empty except for the floating box.
