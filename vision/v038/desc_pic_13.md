# pic_13

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden seen the other way round: the cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the right, and the wooden gate and paved path are on the left. There are no people or ordinary animals in the scene. A grey swing set with one empty red swing stands on the left side of the lawn, and a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow sit on the grass at the front. On the right, near the house, there is a round flower bed with colourful flowers and a green hose reel with its hose and yellow nozzle lying on the grass. The strange thing is in the tree behind the fence on the right: an orange fish, like a goldfish, is sitting at the end of a branch in the tree, as if it were a bird perched there. A fish in a tree is impossible, so that is clearly the odd thing in this picture.
