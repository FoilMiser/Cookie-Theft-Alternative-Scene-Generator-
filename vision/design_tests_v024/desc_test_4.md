# Picture 4

## Free description

This is a paved town square or shopping street on a sunny but windy day, with a row of colourful shops behind, white wind lines in the sky and across the scene, and green leaves blowing out of a tree on the left. In the middle background, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is throwing both arms up with a startled look, as an orange cap tumbles through the air just above and to the right of her, apparently blown off by the wind. At right of centre, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands with his hands on his hips, wearing what looks like a silver-white bucket upside down on his head, which is the oddest thing in the picture. In the front centre, a person in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is holding a camera up to their eye, pointing it to the right, possibly at the man with the bucket. In the front right, an older man in a blue-grey cap and brown waistcoat is eating a pink ice cream with a pleased expression, and behind him a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks to the right carrying a cardboard box past a fruit stall. A teal bicycle is parked in a rack near the woman in yellow, and an empty bench, a small cafe table with two chairs, a planted tree, a bin and a potted plant are spread around the square. Nobody seems to be reacting to the man's bucket hat or helping with the flying cap, and it looks like the cap may be about to land somewhere beyond the woman unless she catches it.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The item on the bearded man's head could be an upside-down metal bucket or could be some kind of large white hat or chef's hat. Its slanting top and rim read mostly as a bucket, which would be silly or wrong for a hat.
- The orange cap could belong to the woman in yellow (she is not wearing anything on her head and is reaching up) or it could have come from somebody else; nothing shows where it came from. Her raised arms could mean she is trying to catch it or just that she is surprised.
- The person with the camera could be photographing the bearded man, or the older man, or the fruit stall; the camera just points right. They could be a boy or an adult, as they are about as tall as the older man.
- The older man's face is hard to read: the features sit oddly on a large round head, and the pink near his mouth could be his lips, tongue or the ice cream. His eyes could be closed in enjoyment or squinting. The hand holding the cone also sits close to his face in a slightly cramped way.
- The older man overlaps the fruit stall and the woman with the box overlaps the stall's corner, so the far right is crowded and it is difficult to see what is behind what. The box looks almost attached to the stall, so it could be read as her taking a box from it or simply walking past.
- The wind affects only the cap, leaves and lines: the woman's dress and hair, the shop awnings and the other people's clothes show no movement, and nobody else's cap is blowing off, which looks a bit inconsistent.
- The leaves are drawn out in the open away from the tree, with one near the teal door and a few near the cap; they look a little like they are floating, but with the wind lines this seems intended.
- The woman in yellow stands right against the shop front and looks large compared with the door behind her, so the size and distance are a little off.
- The lamp post stands directly in front of the green building's window and awning, and the bicycle wheels look small and thick and the frame cramped inside the rack, so it is a bit hard to see how the bike is locked or fixed to the rack.
- The awnings on the shops seem to run along at one height while the far-left awning is partly hidden by the tree; I cannot see all of the left shop's front.
