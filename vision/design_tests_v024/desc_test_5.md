# Picture 5

## Free description

This is a sunny day in a back garden, with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and green hedge and a tree showing behind it. In the middle front, a curly-haired child in a yellow top and orange shorts has just thrown a red ball, shown by a dashed curved line rising from the child's hand to the ball in mid-air. A boy in a backwards red cap and blue hoodie stands facing the child with both hands held together in front of him, apparently ready to catch it, and the ball looks about to reach him. At the back left, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is walking left towards the house carrying a cardboard box. A clothes line strung between two wooden posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. On the right, an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, is walking to the right with a white, brown-spotted dog on a red lead, near a concrete path that leads up to a wooden gate. An orange cat is curled up on the path in the front right with its eyes closed, apparently asleep, and the dog does not seem to have noticed it. A round flower bed full of colourful flowers sits near the house. Nothing looks impossible to me; it reads as an ordinary garden scene.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The box blends in with the fence behind it because both are a similar brown, so at first glance it is easy to miss or to read as part of the fence. It also looks small and held mostly by one visible arm.
- It isn't clear where the woman in yellow is going: she could be carrying the box to the blue door, or just walking across the garden. She is further back than the door, so she isn't heading straight at it.
- The boy's hands together could mean he is getting ready to catch, or could be read as clapping or pressing his hands together as if praying. The incoming ball makes catching the likeliest reading.
- The ball sits just above and to the left of the boy's hands, with the dashed line ending just short of it. The dashed line is a drawn device rather than a real object, so taken literally it could be read as a string attached to the ball.
- The white band on the ball curves a little like a smile.
- The child's raised hand looks pointed rather than an open hand, so it is a little unclear whether it has just let go of the ball.
- The child could be a boy or a girl; I can't tell.
- The cat's shape is jumbled: the head is clear, but the body, legs and tail are hard to separate, and at a glance it could be read as an orange heap. Lying in the middle of a path with its eyes shut, it reads as asleep, though it could also be read as simply lying down or hurt.
- It could be read that the dog is about to disturb the cat, as they are both on the path, but the dog faces away from it and is walking the other way.
- The older woman and the dog are walking across the path rather than along it, and the dog is heading off the right edge of the picture. Her hand visually overlaps the bottom corner of the gate, though she appears to be in front of it rather than touching it.
- The boy's feet point towards the viewer while his upper body faces left, which looks a little twisted.
- Nothing looks like it is floating except the ball, which is meant to be in the air; it has no shadow on the grass below it.
