# Picture 1

## Free description

This is a sunny day in a park, with green grass, a winding sandy path, a round blue pond, trees and rolling hills behind. In the front left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is walking along the edge of the path with her eyes on an open book she holds out in front of her. A red bucket sits on the path just ahead of and to the right of her, so she looks as if she might walk into it or trip over it because she is not looking where she is going. Further back on the path, an older man with grey hair, a blue-grey cap and a brown waistcoat is walking away from the bucket area, carrying a long yellow object that hangs from one hand. In the middle, a small child in a yellow top and orange shorts stands near a tall lamp post, holding a pink ice cream up near their mouth. To the right of centre, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands at the edge of the pond, holding a small white container and reaching out with his other hand toward a white duck, with small orange-brown bits of food scattered on the water in front of it. An orange cat is lying on the grass between the bench and the man, apparently asleep with its eyes closed. A wooden bench in the middle is empty. Nothing looks clearly impossible, and apart from the bucket in the reading woman's way there is no obvious accident happening yet.
