# Picture 2

## Free description

This is a sunny day in a grassy park or playground, with a dark railing fence and a line of green bushes running across the back. On the left, two children are on a red seesaw: a girl in a pink dress is up on the high end with her feet off the ground, and a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is on the low end with a foot on the grass. They face each other. Behind them a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is walking to the left with a white, brown-spotted dog on a red lead. To the right of her a man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers has one arm raised above his head and is waving; small curved marks either side of his hand show it moving. In the front, a boy in a red cap worn backwards and a blue hoodie is balancing on one leg and kicking an orange ball, which is just leaving his foot, with swoosh lines under his leg. The ball looks about to fly off to the right, where nobody is standing. On the right is a red-framed slide with a ladder and an orange and yellow chute, and nobody is using it. Nothing in the picture looks impossible to me; it reads as an ordinary park scene.
