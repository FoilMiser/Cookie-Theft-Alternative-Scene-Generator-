## What this says

- **The trip outside the street.** The question v0.29's screen left open. It is in a garden, a park and a playground here, and all three readers say it in the free description. All three still give a second reading below, as they did in the street.
- **The hose.** No sleeve, no gap, and nobody has it stop at the waist. "Stiff upright curve" stays, as the bench said it would. A hose held at the waist has to rise, and readers have named every way of rising since v0.25.
- **The crumbs.** Nobody remarks on crumbs off the water, in either of the two pictures with the duck.
- **The reel.** "A yellow nozzle lying on the grass", with no stub. Once, in test 2.
- **The fish.** Impossible, or odd, in all three. The tuft has gone and with it what readers said of it, and "like a hook or a pipe" is what it cost. One bench reader said the same of the limb run past the fish, "one bent pipe", so that is twice in eight readings of a limb with no other arm. On the v0.31 list.

Nothing here asked for a new rule. Six pictures are six readers, and the screen is the test.
