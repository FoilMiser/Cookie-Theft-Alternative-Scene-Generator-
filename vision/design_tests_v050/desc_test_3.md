## Free description

It's a sunny day in a back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and trees and bushes behind it. A young boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands in the middle of the lawn holding the strings of a blue and a purple balloon. He reaches up with his other hand toward a red balloon floating higher up. The red balloon looks like it has escaped, and its string is trailing down out of his reach. Behind him and to the right, a grey-haired older woman in a teal top and grey skirt is painting the fence with a paint roller. One section of the fence, to her left, is already blue, and a small paint tin sits on the grass by her feet. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress sweeps a small pile of dead leaves on a paved path with a broom. A swing set with a single red swing stands empty in the middle-right. In the front there is a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can, and a flower bed full of colourful flowers sits near the house. One odd thing: the woman is holding the roller over plain, unpainted wood, although the blue section is to her left, a little away from her.
