## Free description
A sunny but windy back garden: white wind lines are drawn in the sky and across the middle of the picture. In the centre a man with a short beard in a green shirt and beige trousers is reaching up with one arm, looking surprised, because his red cap has been blown off his head by the wind and is flying away to the right just above his hand, with a few leaves blowing alongside it. He looks like he is trying to grab it. On the right, next to the blue door of the house, a brown dog with a red collar is sitting holding a yellow lead in its mouth, as if waiting to go for a walk. At the front, a small white puppy with brown spots is curled up asleep on the grass. The rest of the garden has a fence, a gate and path on the left, two potted plants, a bush, a flower bed, a green watering can and a grey wheelbarrow.
