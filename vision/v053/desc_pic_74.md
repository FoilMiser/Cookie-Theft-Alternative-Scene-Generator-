# pic_74

## Free description
A sunny but windy back garden: a cream house with red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, gate and paved path on the right. White wind lines are drawn in the sky and across the middle of the picture. A man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers with a beard stands in the centre reaching up with one hand toward a red cap that the wind has blown off and is carrying away, with a few leaves flying near it. He looks surprised. Nobody else is in the scene. Two potted plants stand on the grass on the right, a flower bed is in front of the house and a teal watering can sits at the front right.
