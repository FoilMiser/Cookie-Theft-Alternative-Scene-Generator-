# pic_03

## Free description
A back garden on a sunny day, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left and a tall wooden fence across the back. An old woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, stands by a round flower bed full of colourful flowers, holding a grey watering can. A thin blue stream of water comes out of the can's spout, but instead of falling down it curves upward in an arc above the flowers, with water droplets at the very top, which looks odd. On a washing line between two posts hang a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. On the right there is a birdhouse on a tall pole, two potted plants, a wooden gate in the fence, and a paved path leading to it. A wooden bench sits in the front left by the house, and an empty grey wheelbarrow stands on the grass in front. A tree is visible behind the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The water line could also be read as a bendy blue garden hose rather than water.
- The droplets at the top look like they are floating in the air above the flowers.
