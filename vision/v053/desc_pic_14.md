# pic_14

## Free description
A back garden under a grey overcast sky with three dark storm clouds and no sun. There is a wooden fence with a gate on the left, a paved path, and a cream house with a blue door and red roof on the right. On the left an older grey-haired woman with glasses, a teal cardigan and grey skirt is sweeping a small pile of brown leaves near the path with a broom. In the centre a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at the dark clouds with a worried look, as if warning that it is about to rain. An empty wheelbarrow sits in the foreground, a hose with a yellow nozzle lies by a hose reel near the house, and there is a flower bed and two potted plants. It looks like rain is about to start and the woman has not looked up yet.
