## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden picket fence, a wooden gate at the far left, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue front door at the right. In the middle, a woman with dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers is standing at the fence holding a long-handled paint roller, painting the fence boards blue; a short section of the fence (about two or three boards) is already blue and the rest is still plain wood. On the grass near her feet are a paint tin and a flat paint tray with blue paint in it. A small white dog with brown patches and a blue collar is walking away from the paint tray towards the paved path on the left, and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the tray to the dog, so the dog has clearly stepped in the paint and its feet are blue. The woman is facing the fence and does not seem to have noticed the dog. It looks like the dog is about to walk blue paw prints onto the path. Other things in the garden are two potted plants, a green watering can and a grey wheelbarrow at the front, and a round flower bed with colourful flowers next to the house.
