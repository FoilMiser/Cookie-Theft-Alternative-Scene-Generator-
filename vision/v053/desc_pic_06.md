## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, trees, a winding sandy path on the left and a blue pond on the right. A little girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench beside the path, eating a pink ice-cream cone. A light-brown dog with a red collar sits up on its hind legs right next to the bench, looking up at the girl as if begging for some ice cream. On the right, in front of the pond, an old man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat stands eating his own pink ice-cream cone, next to a round bed of colourful flowers. There is a tall street lamp in the middle, a green bin in the bottom right corner, and bushes and reeds around the pond. Nothing looks impossible; the dog may be about to get a lick of ice cream or be refused.
