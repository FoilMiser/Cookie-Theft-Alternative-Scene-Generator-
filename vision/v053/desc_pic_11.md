# pic_11

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence and gate on the left, a paved path, and a cream house with a blue door and red roof on the right. In the centre a man in a green shirt and beige trousers holds the nozzle of a garden hose and looks down into it with a puzzled frown, as if no water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel by the house across the lawn, and a brown dog with a red collar is standing right on the hose, which would block the water. Behind them, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks a white spotted dog on a red leash along the fence. On the left a woman in a yellow dress is in a sitting position drinking a red drink through a straw, but there is no chair, bench or anything under her, so she seems to be sitting on thin air. There is a flower bed in front of the house and a bush near the fence.
