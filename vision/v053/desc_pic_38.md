A sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left, a fence, bird-box pole, gate and stone path on the right. A woman in a yellow dress is pegging wet washing onto a line between two posts: blue jeans and a white T-shirt, both dripping water, with a basket of more laundry on the grass below. An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a bench at the front left reading a red book; next to her on the bench is a sandwich, and a small blue bird is pecking at it without her noticing. On the path at the right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a red and a blue balloon, while a yellow balloon floats away high above him; he looks up at it unhappily, as if he just let it go. A coiled garden hose on a reel, a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can are on the lawn, and a flower bed sits near the house. The bird is about to eat the sandwich, and the yellow balloon is about to be lost.
