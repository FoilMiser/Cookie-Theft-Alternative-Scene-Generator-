## Free description
A sunny playground surrounded by a dark metal railing fence, with bushes and a big tree on the right. On the left a child in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts is sliding down a red-and-yellow slide with one arm raised. Behind, an older woman with grey hair and glasses in a teal top and grey skirt walks across carrying a cardboard box. In the middle-right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie holds a kite reel up; its string goes up to a purple kite that is stuck in the branches of the tree. At the front, a girl in a pink dress is crying and holding an empty ice-cream cone, while a brown dog eats the pink ice cream that has fallen on the ground in front of her. At the front left is a sandpit with a red bucket and blue spade, and a grey rock. On the right a red bicycle stands on the grass, but its wheels are drawn as squares, which is impossible. There is also a green bin and a wooden bench by the fence.
