# pic_18

## Free description
A sunny park with rolling hills, a pond on the left, a lamp post in the middle and a winding path on the right. A woman with dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers stands at the front edge of the pond feeding a white duck: she holds a paper bag in one hand and a piece of bread in the other, and small crumbs are floating on the water. The duck is swimming towards the crumbs (there are motion lines behind it) and is pecking at them. Behind, to the right of the lamp post, a white goat with a red collar is grazing on a tuft of grass; it is tied by a rope to a short wooden stake. On the right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands on the path holding a red balloon on a string. A wooden bench sits beside the path, with a large tree to its right. A green bin is in the bottom-left corner and a grey rock in the bottom centre. Nothing looks impossible.
