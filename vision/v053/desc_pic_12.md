## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence and gate on the left, a paved path, and a cream house with blue door and red roof on the right. An older grey-haired man in a cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is painting the fence blue with a paint roller; a few fence boards near the house are already blue. Next to his feet are a paint tray with blue paint and a paint can. A white dog with brown spots is walking away from the paint tray towards the middle of the lawn, and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the tray to the dog, so it has stepped in the paint and is tracking it across the grass. The man is facing the fence and does not seem to have noticed. On the left a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping a small pile of brown leaves with a broom near the path. A teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow sit in the foreground, and there are two potted plants and a flower bed.
