## Free description
A busy sunny park with a pond, hills, a lamp post, a bench and a winding path. On the left, a man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat is watering a flower bed by pouring water out of a frying pan instead of a watering can, which is odd. Beside the flower bed, a brown dog wearing glasses sits up and reads a newspaper, which is impossible. In the pond a white duck swims towards some small bits of bread floating on the water, while a girl in a pink dress at the pond's edge holds a slice of bread, apparently feeding it. In the front centre, a girl in a yellow top and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and covers her eyes, crying, because her ice cream has fallen on the ground, where a white spotted dog is licking it up. Further back, a white goat on a rope tied to a stake grazes on grass, and an elderly woman in a teal cardigan sweeps a pile of leaves near the path. On the right, a boy in a red cap holds a kite string, but his purple kite is stuck in the branches of a tree. A blue bird flies across the sky between the clouds.
