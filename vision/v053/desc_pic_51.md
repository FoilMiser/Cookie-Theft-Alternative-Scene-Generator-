## Free description
A busy park playground on a sunny day, with a metal railing fence across the back. In the middle a small girl in a pink dress holds two balloons (red and purple) and reaches up with her other hand toward a yellow balloon that has escaped and is floating away high in the sky with its string dangling. In the front-centre a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is crying, rubbing an eye, holding an empty ice-cream cone; right next to them a brown dog is licking the pink ice cream that has fallen on the ground. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of a tree. Behind him an old man in a cap and brown waistcoat sits on a bench reading a red book, while a blue bird on the bench is pecking at his sandwich without him noticing. An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses walks past carrying a cardboard box. On the left there is a red slide with a yellow chute, a sandpit with a red bucket and blue spade, a grey rock, and a white dog with brown spots crouched in a play-bow in front of a tennis ball. A red bird sits on the fence at far left, and a red bicycle stands on the right in the foreground.
