# pic_69

## Free description
A sunny back garden seen the other way round: the cream house with a blue door is on the right, the gate and paved path are on the left, and a wooden fence runs across the back. A young girl in a pink dress in the centre-right is flying a yellow diamond-shaped kite high in the sky; its string runs from her raised hands up to the kite. An older woman with grey hair, glasses and a teal cardigan stands at the back painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller, with a paint tin at her feet; several boards behind her are already blue. In the front left, a woman in a yellow dress is bent over on the path sweeping dust into a red dustpan, but she is using a huge silver dinner fork instead of a broom, which is very odd. An empty wheelbarrow stands on the grass in the front centre. A birdhouse on a tall pole, two potted plants and a flowerbed by the house complete the scene.
