# pic_21

## Free description
A busy, sunny park with a pond, lamp post, bench and winding path. In the front left a woman in a yellow dress is watering a flower bed, but she is pouring the water out of a frying pan (or saucepan) rather than a watering can, which looks odd. Next to her a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is scattering seeds from a paper bag; the seeds fall to the ground where a red bird is pecking at them. Just to the right a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is running towards the left while eating a pink ice-cream cone; he is heading towards the bird and the falling seeds and may run into the bird or the seeds. In the background, near the lamp post, a girl in a pink dress holds a red and a purple balloon and reaches up towards a yellow balloon that is floating away out of reach. On the right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks along the path carrying a yellow shopping bag. A green bin is in the bottom-left corner.
