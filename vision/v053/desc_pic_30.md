## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. At the fence on the right, an older grey-haired man in a cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is painting the fence boards blue with a long-handled roller; a few boards are already blue, and a paint tin sits on the grass behind him. In the middle of the lawn a brown dog with a red collar is sitting upright on its haunches wearing glasses and holding open a newspaper with its paws, as if reading it, which is impossible for a real dog. In the front left a girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench eating a pink ice cream cone, and a white dog with brown spots is standing up on its hind legs beside her, looking up at her ice cream as if begging for it. There is also a flower bed, a grey wheelbarrow and a green watering can on the lawn.
