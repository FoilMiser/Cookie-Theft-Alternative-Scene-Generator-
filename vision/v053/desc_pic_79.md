# Picture 79

## Free description
A sunny day in a park with green hills, trees, a winding sand-coloured path, a tall black lamp post and a blue pond with reeds. On the left of centre, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sits on a wooden bench and eats a pink ice-cream cone, smiling. In front of the bench, a small white dog with brown patches sits up on its hind legs, looking up at the boy as if begging for the ice cream. On the right, a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers walks along in front of the pond, also holding a pink ice-cream cone up to his mouth. Behind him is a round flower bed with colourful flowers, and a green rubbish bin stands in the bottom-right corner. Nothing looks clearly dangerous; the most likely next event is that the dog gets (or keeps begging for) some ice cream.
