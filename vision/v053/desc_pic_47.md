# pic_47

## Free description
A back garden on a gloomy day: the sky is grey and three dark storm clouds hang overhead, suggesting rain is coming. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the wooden fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; a few boards are already blue, and an open paint tin sits on the grass by her feet. To the right, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, points up at the sky, apparently warning that it is about to rain on the fresh paint. A small white puppy with a brown patch lies asleep on the lawn in the foreground. Around them are a cream house with a red roof and blue door, a flower bed, a birdhouse on a pole, a gate with a paved path, an empty grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can.
