## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a long wooden fence across the back, and a wooden gate and paved path on the right. In the front left corner a woman with dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a wooden bench reading a red book, with her eyes on the page. Next to her on the bench is a sandwich, and a small blue bird is standing right beside it pecking at it, with a few crumbs around, so the bird looks like it is eating or stealing her sandwich. The woman is absorbed in her book and does not seem to notice. In the middle of the garden a washing line between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes, pegged on. There is also a green hose reel with a hose lying on the grass, a flower bed, two potted plants by the fence, a grey wheelbarrow and a green watering can in the front.
