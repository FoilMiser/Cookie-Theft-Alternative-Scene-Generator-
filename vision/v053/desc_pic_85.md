# Picture 85

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a wooden gate and paved path on the right. A woman in a yellow dress stands beside a flower bed holding a teal watering can, but the water does not pour down: a blue stream curves upward out of the spout and rises high above the flowers, with droplets at the top, which looks impossible. In the centre, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans pegs washing on a line strung between two wooden posts; there are blue jeans, a yellow-and-red striped towel/top and a white T-shirt that is dripping water. A laundry basket full of clothes sits on the grass beside him. On the right, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts stands on the path flying a yellow kite high in the sky. There are also two potted plants, an empty grey wheelbarrow at the front and a wooden bench at bottom left.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water going upward from the watering can is the strangest thing: it could be read as water defying gravity, or as a garden hose (a blue tube) poking up out of the flowers and spraying; it looks like a stream from the can, rising, not falling.
- The dripping from the white T-shirt is normal for wet washing, not odd.
- The kite string passes close to the washing area but seems not to touch anything.
