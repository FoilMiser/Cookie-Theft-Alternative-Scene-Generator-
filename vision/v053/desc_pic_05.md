## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, trees, a winding sandy path on the left and a blue pond on the right. An old man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat stands at the edge of the pond holding a paper bag and tossing bread to a white duck; small pieces of bread float on the water and the duck is swimming toward them with its beak near one. In the front left, a white goat with a red collar is tied with a rope to a short wooden stake beside the path and is eating a tuft of grass. In the front right, a little girl in a pink dress walks or skips to the left while eating a pink ice-cream cone. There is a tall street lamp in the middle, a grey rock in the front, a green bin in the bottom right corner, and bushes and reeds around the pond. Nothing looks impossible, though a goat tied up in a park is a little unusual.
