## Free description
A sunny, windy back garden seen with the house on the right (cream walls, red roof, blue door) and a gate and paved path on the left. Wind lines cross the sky and leaves blow off the tree. At the left, an older woman with grey hair and glasses, teal cardigan and grey skirt, stands by the gate reaching up as her blue hat is blown away by the wind. In the middle a smiling boy in a yellow T-shirt is swinging on a swing set, with motion lines showing movement. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is painting the fence blue with a roller; part of the fence by the house is already blue. A paint tin and paint tray sit on the grass near him, and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the tray to a white dog with brown spots and blue paws in the centre, which is walking away from it. There is a flower bed by the house, and a teal watering can and grey wheelbarrow at the front left.
