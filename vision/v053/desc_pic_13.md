# pic_13

## Free description
A sunny playground on a grassy field with a dark metal railing fence along the back and a tree on the right. On the left, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is sliding down a red-framed yellow slide with one arm raised, looking happy. On the right is a red seesaw: an older man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat sits on the low end reading an open newspaper, with his feet on the ground. On the high end, up in the air, a small girl in a pink dress clutches the handle and holds a hand to her mouth, looking worried or scared. The man is heavy and absorbed in his paper, so the girl is stuck up high and he does not seem to notice. A green rubbish bin stands by the fence and a grey rock lies in the front left.
