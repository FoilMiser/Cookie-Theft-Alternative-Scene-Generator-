## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left, a wooden fence and gate at the back, and a paved path on the right. A woman with dark hair in a yellow dress is standing between the two clothesline posts, reaching up to peg a white T-shirt onto the line. Blue jeans already hang on the line. Both the jeans and the T-shirt are dripping water, so the washing is wet. A laundry basket full of more clothes (blue, white, pink) sits on the grass beside her. Two potted plants stand near a tall post with a red-roofed birdhouse on top. In the foreground there is a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can, and a flower bed is near the house. Nobody else is present and nothing looks impossible.
