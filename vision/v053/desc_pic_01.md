A small white dog with brown spots and a blue collar sits up on its hind legs beside the bench, looking up at her as if begging for some of the ice cream. On the right, an old man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat sweeps a small pile of dry leaves with a broom, next to a fruit and vegetable stall with crates of red, orange, yellow and green produce. A green bicycle is locked in a bike rack in front of the shops, and there is a street lamp, a tree in a round planter, a green bin, a small café table with two chairs, flower boxes, a wooden crate and a potted plant. Nothing looks clearly impossible; the main thing that seems about to happen is the dog getting (or not getting) some ice cream.
