# pic_04

## Free description
An empty back garden on a sunny day, with no people or ordinary animals. A cream house with a red roof and blue door stands on the right, and a wooden fence runs across the back with a gate on the left and a paved path leading from it. A washing line between two posts holds a yellow towel with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans. There is a round flower bed of colourful flowers near the house, an empty wooden bench in the front right, two potted plants near the path, a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow lying on the grass in front. The strange thing is that an orange goldfish is sitting up in the branches of the tree behind the house, out of water, which is impossible. Otherwise the garden is calm and nothing is happening.
