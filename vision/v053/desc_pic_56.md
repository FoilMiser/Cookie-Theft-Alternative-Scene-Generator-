## Free description
A park on a sunny day with rolling green hills in the background, a pond on the left and a winding sandy path on the right. A woman with dark hair in a yellow dress and red shoes stands at the edge of the pond holding a long fishing rod; the line drops into the water where a red-and-white float bobs with a ripple around it. A white duck is swimming on the pond a little to the left of the float, near it. In the middle there is a tall black lamp post with a green rubbish bin beside it. On the right, a wooden ladder stands upright on the grass beside the path, leaning against nothing - it seems to be standing up all by itself, which looks impossible. There is also a flower bed in the front-left, a grey rock in the front-centre, several trees and bushes, and reeds at the edges of the pond.
