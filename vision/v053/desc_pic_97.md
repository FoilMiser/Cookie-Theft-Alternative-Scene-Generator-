# pic_97

## Free description
A busy back garden on a sunny day, with a wooden fence at the back, a gate on the left, a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right, a tree, a bluebird flying and two clouds. On the left a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping a pile of brown leaves on the path with a broom. Behind him a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is painting the fence blue with a paint roller; a section of fence is already blue and a paint tin sits at his feet. In the middle an older woman with glasses and grey hair, in a teal top and grey skirt, holds a green garden hose and looks at the nozzle. The hose runs across the lawn to a hose reel near the bench, and a brown dog with a red collar is standing on the hose. That would explain why no water is coming out. A man in a green shirt and beige trousers stands with his hands on his hips and a white bucket upside down on his head, which is odd. A small girl in pink is pointing up at a blue and white beach ball stuck in the tree. A grey cat is walking along the top of the fence. On the right a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench drinking from a red cup with a straw. There is a grey wheelbarrow with a square wheel instead of a round one, which looks impossible, and a bed of colourful flowers.
