## Free description
A sunny back garden with a gate and path on the left, a long wooden fence, and a cream house with a blue door on the right. A man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is coming in through the gate reading an open book (or paper) as he walks, and he is about to trip over a cardboard box sitting on the path right in front of his feet. In the centre an older woman with grey hair and glasses holds up a garden hose; the hose runs across the lawn to a reel by the house, and a brown dog with a red collar is standing on the hose, which would block the water flow. Behind them by the fence a little girl in a pink dress is reaching up toward a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of a tree, out of her reach. In the front left a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie walks a white spotted dog on a red lead. On the right a boy in a red shirt sits on a bench reading a red book.
