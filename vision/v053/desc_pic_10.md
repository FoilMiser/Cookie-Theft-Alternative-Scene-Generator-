## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house (blue door, red roof) on the left, a long wooden fence with a gate on the right, and a paved path leading to the gate. There are no people or animals in the picture. A dark green garden hose runs along the bottom from the left to a small lawn sprinkler in the front centre. The sprinkler is spraying several arcs of water to the right, and the farthest arc lands right on a closed cardboard box sitting on the lawn. The box has water dripping down it and a puddle around its base, so it is getting soaked. There is a round flower bed with colourful flowers near the house, two potted plants by the fence, and bushes. The odd thing is that a cardboard box has been left out where the sprinkler is wetting it.
