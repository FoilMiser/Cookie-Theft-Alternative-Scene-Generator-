## Free description
A sunny back garden with a gate and paved path on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a cream house with a blue door on the right. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence blue with a roller on a pole; a section of fence behind her is already blue. On the ground near her is a paint tray with blue paint and an open paint tin. A white dog with brown spots is walking away from the paint tray toward the left, leaving a trail of blue paw prints across the grass, so it has clearly stepped in the paint. On the left an older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, is sweeping a pile of dry leaves on the path with a yellow broom. A teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow sit on the lawn in front, and a flower bed is by the house.
