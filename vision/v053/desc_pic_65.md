## Free description
A sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left. A tall wooden fence runs across the back of the garden, with a wooden gate at the right end and a paved path coming down from the gate to the front right. A man in a red shirt and blue trousers stands by the fence on the right, painting the fence boards blue with a paint roller on a long handle. A few boards beside him are already blue, and a small paint tin stands on the grass at his feet. In the centre front, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt is raking a small pile of dead leaves. A round flowerbed full of colourful flowers sits in front of the house, and a teal watering can stands on the grass near the path. A blue bird flies across the sky, with two clouds and a bright sun at the top right. Nothing looks clearly wrong; it is an ordinary day of garden chores.
