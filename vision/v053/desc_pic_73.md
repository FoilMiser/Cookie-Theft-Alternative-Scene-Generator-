# pic_73

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, a gate and paved path on the right. In the middle a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts holds the strings of a yellow and a blue balloon in one hand while reaching up with the other toward a red balloon that has escaped and is floating up above the fence, its string trailing. He looks up at it with a worried face. To the right a girl in a pink dress is jumping in the air with both arms raised, happy; her shadow is on the grass below her. Strangely, an orange fish is sitting on a branch of the tree behind the house, which is impossible. There is a wooden bench at the front left, a flower bed in front of the house, a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can at the front.
