## Free description
A sunny playground with a dark metal railing fence across the back, a tree on the left, a sun at top right and two clouds. On the left a red seesaw is tipped steeply. A small girl in a pink dress sits at the raised end, high in the air, with her feet dangling. She seems to be calling out or saying something, with one hand raised near her mouth. An older man with grey hair, a blue cap, a white shirt and a brown waistcoat sits on the low end, which rests on the ground. He has his feet planted and is reading an open book or newspaper, apparently ignoring her. So the girl seems stuck up in the air because he is holding the low end down. In the middle a swing frame has one empty red swing seat. On the right a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is sliding down a yellow and orange slide with a red ladder, one arm stretched up. At the bottom right there is a sandpit with a red bucket and a blue spade, and a grey rock. A green bin stands by the fence.
