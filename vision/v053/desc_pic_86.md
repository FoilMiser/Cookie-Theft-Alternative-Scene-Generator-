A sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and blue door, with a tall wooden fence, a tree, a swing set, a birdhouse on a pole, a wooden gate and a paved path on the right. A brown dog sits by the blue door holding a yellow leash in its mouth, as if it wants to go for a walk. The water from the can does not fall down onto the flowers: it comes out of the spout and arcs upward and back to the left, with drops at the top, which looks impossible. In front, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses and a green cardigan is digging with a shovel, lifting soil next to a small pile of dirt. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands to her right. The swing is empty and still.
