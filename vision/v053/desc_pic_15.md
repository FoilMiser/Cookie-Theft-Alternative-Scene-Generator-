## Free description
A busy, sunny paved town square in front of a row of colourful shops with striped awnings, with a fruit stall, a street lamp, a bench and a tree. On the left a man in a red shirt and blue trousers stands with his hands on his hips wearing a metal bucket upside down on his head like a hat. Next to him a brown dog sits upright wearing glasses and reading a newspaper, which is impossible for a real dog. In the middle an older woman with grey hair waters a flower planter with a watering can. Behind the bench a boy in a yellow shirt holds an empty ice cream cone and rubs his eye, crying, because his pink ice cream has fallen on the ground, where a white spotted dog is licking it up. On the right a blue-and-white beach ball is stuck in the branches of the tree; a little girl in pink points up at it, next to a woman in purple sweeping leaves. In the front right a boy in a red cap scatters seeds from a paper bag to a blue bird, while at the bottom centre a grey cat is creeping towards a red bird that is pecking at seeds, as if about to pounce.
