# Picture 80

## Free description
A sunny back garden enclosed by a tall wooden fence, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right and a wooden gate and paving-stone path on the left. In the centre a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers jumps in the air with both arms raised, smiling; his shadow is on the grass below him, so his feet are off the ground. Behind him is a washing line between two wooden posts with a yellow-and-red striped top and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. A grey cat walks along the top of the fence on the left. Strangely, an orange fish sits in the branches of the tree behind the fence on the right, which is impossible in real life. There are two potted plants, a teal watering can on the grass, and a flower bed near the house.
