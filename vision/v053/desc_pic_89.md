## Free description
A sunny town street with four coloured shop buildings with striped awnings, a fruit stall, a lamp post, flower planters, a bench, a bike rack and a tree on the paved square. On the left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping a small pile of leaves with a broom. Next to her, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is pushing dust towards a red dustpan, but he is using a huge silver fork instead of a broom, which looks wrong. In the centre-front, an older woman with grey hair and glasses in a green top and grey skirt is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red leash. A young girl in a pink dress sits on the bench drinking from a cup. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is pointing up at a blue-and-white beach ball that is stuck in the branches of the tree. A green bicycle is parked in the bike rack.
