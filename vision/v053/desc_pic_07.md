## Free description

On the left, a red seesaw is tilted: a little girl in a pink dress sits on the raised left end holding the handle, high in the air, while an old man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat sits on the lower right end with his feet on the ground, reading an open book or newspaper. The girl has her hand near her open mouth and looks toward the man, as if calling to him or complaining that she is stuck up in the air, while he seems not to notice. In the middle is a swing set with one empty red swing, and a green bin stands by the fence. On the right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts kneels in a sandbox building a sandcastle with a little red flag, using a red spade, next to a red-framed yellow slide. There is a tree on the far left, a bush on the right and a grey rock in the bottom right.
