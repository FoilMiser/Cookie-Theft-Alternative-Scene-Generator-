# pic_08

## Free description
A busy, sunny playground enclosed by a dark railing with hedges behind. In the middle, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers has just thrown a red ball (a dotted line shows its path) toward a woman in a yellow dress, who stands facing him with her hands raised, ready to catch it. In the front left, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a kite-string reel up high, but the string leads to a purple kite that is caught in the branches of the tree on the left, so the kite is stuck. In the middle front, a white dog with brown spots crouches in a playful bow with a yellow-green tennis ball between its front paws, wagging its tail. On the right, a little girl in a pink outfit kneels in a sandbox building a sandcastle with a red flag, next to a red-framed yellow slide. There is an empty bench by the fence, a grey rock and a bush on the right.
