# Description: test_1.png

## 1. Free description

This is a sunny day in a park. There is a pond on the left, green hills and a line of trees behind, a tall black lamp post in the middle, and a sandy path winding from the bottom of the picture to the back on the right. In the middle foreground an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, reaches up with her right arm. Her mouth is open in surprise as an orange baseball cap flies away above her, over the right edge of the pond. White wind streaks and a few small green and brown leaves blow along with the cap, and more wind streaks show in the sky, so it looks like a gust has just blown her cap off. The cap looks like it might come down in the pond. On the right, a woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers stands on the path next to an empty wooden bench and eats a pink ice-cream cone with her eyes closed. She seems happy and takes no notice of the cap. In the bottom right corner a white goat with curved dark horns has its head down and is eating a small tuft of grass. A goat wandering loose in a tidy park is a bit unexpected. There is also a colourful flower bed at the bottom left.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous

- Which way the cap is going: the wind streaks run from over the pond to the cap. So the cap could be blowing to the right, away from the pond, or the gust could be carrying it back left over the water. Either way, it is not clear whether it will land in the pond.
- Whose cap it is: it seems to belong to the elderly woman, since she is reaching for it and has nothing on her head. But no one is shown actually wearing it, so it could also be a stray cap she is trying to catch.
- The wind seems very local. It blows the cap and leaves, but the ice-cream woman's long hair hangs straight, and the trees, flowers and cattails do not bend at all.
- The ice-cream woman's eyes look closed or squinted. This could mean she is enjoying the taste, or that she is squinting into the sun or wind.
- The elderly woman's open mouth could mean surprise, dismay, or that she is calling out for help.
- The goat's place in the park is unclear: there is no fence, lead or owner, so it could be a loose or escaped goat. The thin sprig near its mouth (green with a little pink) could be grass or a small flower it is chewing.
- Nothing else looks physically wrong. The people stand on the ground with shadows, and nothing passes through anything else. The only thing in the air is the cap, with the leaves, which makes sense with the wind.
