# Description: test_2.png

## 1. Free description

It is a bright sunny day in a back garden. A wooden fence runs across the back with green bushes and a tree behind it, and a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the right. On the left a man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands on a pale paved path in front of a gate in the fence, raking a small pile of brown leaves. In the middle a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is reaching up to peg washing onto a clothesline between two wooden posts. A white T-shirt and a pair of blue jeans already hang there and are dripping water, and a basket of more laundry sits on the grass below. In front of him a girl in a yellow dress holds the nozzle of a long green garden hose up near her face and looks into it, but only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose runs across the lawn to a hose reel by the house wall, and a white dog with brown spots is standing on it with its back paws. Just past the dog's paws, on the reel side, the hose bulges up fat, as if the water is blocked there and building up. It looks like the water will spray the girl in the face when the dog steps off the hose. A colourful flower bed sits in front of the house, and an empty grey wheelbarrow stands at the front of the lawn.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous

- The hose bulge: I read it as the dog standing on the hose and pinching it, so the water backs up and only a drip reaches the girl. It could also be just a thicker hose joint or a drawing quirk, but its position right at the dog's paws makes the "blocked hose" reading strong.
- The girl looking into the nozzle: she might be peering in to see why no water comes out, or she might just be holding it up near her face. Either way it suggests she is about to get splashed.
- The raking man's face has a brown mark above his mouth. It could be a moustache or a smudge or dirt.
- The gate behind the raking man: it is a brown panel with a black knob. A diagonal line next to it makes it look either slightly open or like a closed gate drawn at an angle. I can't tell which.
- The man at the clothesline stands right at the line, with his body overlapping where the line passes. It reads as him standing just in front of the line, not as the line going through him.
- The dripping washing is normal for wet laundry, but drops falling from clothes on a sunny day next to the dripping nozzle could be meant to draw the eye. I don't think it is an error.
- The wheelbarrow is empty and nobody is using it. It seems to be just set dressing.
- Nothing obviously floats or passes through anything, apart from the points above.
