## Free description
A sunny park with a pond, a lamppost, a winding path on the right and trees. At the front left a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is flying a yellow diamond kite, holding the string spool up in his hand and looking up at it; the kite flies high with a bow-tied tail. A garden hose runs along the bottom of the picture from the left, past a flowerbed, to a sprinkler in the centre-front. The sprinkler is spraying arcs of water right onto a cardboard box, which is getting wet and sitting in a small puddle. At the bottom right there is a wheelbarrow, but its wheel looks square rather than round. A green bin stands by the lamppost. Nobody seems to be noticing that the box is being soaked.
