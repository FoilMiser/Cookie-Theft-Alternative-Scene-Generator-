# pic_002

## Free description
A sunny day in a park with green hills, trees, a pond with reeds, and a winding sandy path on the left. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits at the left end of a wooden bench beside the path, reading a red book. A sandwich has been left on the bench next to her, and a blue bird is standing on the bench pecking at it; she is looking at her book and does not seem to notice. In the centre-front a young girl in a pink dress walks or skips across the grass while eating a pink ice-cream cone. A tall black lamppost stands in the middle, a flowerbed of colourful flowers is at the front right, a green bin at the bottom right, and a grey rock at the bottom centre. It looks like the bird is about to eat (or steal) the woman's sandwich.
