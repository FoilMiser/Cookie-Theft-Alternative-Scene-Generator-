# pic_062

## Free description
A sunny town square with grey tiles and a row of colourful shops behind (cream, peach, green, pink) with striped awnings. In the centre-left a woman in a purple top and teal trousers bends slightly and scatters seeds from a paper bag onto the ground; seeds fall in a trail. Two small birds, one blue and one red, peck at the seeds at her feet. Behind her an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan, sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. A red bicycle stands in a bike rack at the front centre-right. There is a tree in a planter and a green bin on the left, a café table with two chairs at the front left, a lamp post, a fruit stall with crates of fruit on the right, a wooden crate and a potted plant at the far right. Nothing looks dangerous or wrong; it is a peaceful scene.
