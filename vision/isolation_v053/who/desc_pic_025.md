## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a house with a blue door on the right, and a path running down the left side from a wooden gate. A man with brown hair and a moustache, in a red T-shirt and blue jeans, stands in the centre painting the fence blue with a long-handled paint roller; one section of the fence is already blue. A paint tin and a roller tray of blue paint sit on the grass near his feet. On the left, by the gate, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is sweeping a pile of dry leaves with a broom. A white dog with brown spots is walking along the path near her, and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray to the dog - it has evidently stepped in the paint and is tracking it across the lawn. Neither person seems to have noticed the dog yet. There are two potted plants near the fence, a flower bed by the house, a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow at the front.
