## Free description
A sunny park with a pond in the middle-left, rolling hills, trees, a tall black lamp post and a winding sandy path on the right. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands in front of the pond, holding up a spool and flying a yellow diamond kite high in the sky; the string runs from his hand to the kite. A flower bed is at the front left, and a grey wheelbarrow stands at the front right by the path. A green bin is beside the lamp post. The odd or problem element is the box being soaked by the sprinkler; nobody seems to have noticed.
