# pic_010

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back and a wooden gate with a paved path on the right. In the front left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands by a round flower bed holding a grey watering can. The water from the can does not pour down: it shoots out as a long blue stream that curves up and back over the flowers, with drops flying above it, which looks wrong for a watering can. In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress is reaching up to peg a white T-shirt onto a washing line that already holds blue jeans and a yellow striped towel; the T-shirt is dripping water. A wicker laundry basket full of clothes sits on the grass below her. On the right, a small girl in a pink dress stands on the path holding a string and flying an orange kite high in the sky. A grey wheelbarrow stands empty on the lawn at the front, and two potted plants sit near the fence. Nothing seems about to go badly wrong apart from the odd watering.
