## Free description
A sunny town square with colourful shops and striped awnings. At front left-centre a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal skirt is bending slightly and sprinkling seeds from a paper bag onto the ground. Two small birds, one blue and one red, are on the ground in front of her pecking at the seeds as they fall. Behind her, in the centre, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses sits on a wooden bench reading a red book; she wears a teal cardigan and grey skirt. A red bicycle is parked in a bike rack at the front centre-right. On the right there is a fruit and vegetable stall, a wooden crate and a potted plant; on the left a tree in a planter, a green bin and a small café table with two chairs. A tall black lamp post stands in the middle right. It is a calm, everyday scene with nothing clearly wrong.
