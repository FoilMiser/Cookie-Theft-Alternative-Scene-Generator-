## Free description
A sunny playground on a grassy area enclosed by a dark metal railing fence, with a tree on the left and a slide on the right. Two children are on a red seesaw at the front left. The child on the left, in a pink outfit, is on the raised end, and the child on the right, with curly hair and a yellow top with orange shorts, is on the lower end with one foot on the ground. Both are holding the handles and seem to be enjoying the seesaw. In the middle of the picture, near the fence, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers stands eating a pink ice-cream cone. Behind her is a green bin. On the right is a red-framed slide with an orange and yellow chute and a ladder, which nobody is using. There is a bush at the right and a grey rock on the grass at the front right.
