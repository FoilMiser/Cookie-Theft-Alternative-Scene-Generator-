## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the centre front a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is jumping in the air with both arms raised, smiling; his shadow is on the grass below him. Next to him a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is watering a flower bed with a hose; the hose runs from a hose reel on the ground near the flower bed. A metal swing set with an empty red swing stands on the left. Oddly, an orange fish is sitting in the branches of the tree behind the house, which is impossible. Otherwise it is a cheerful garden scene.
