## Free description
A sunny park with a pond, rolling green hills, trees, a lamp post and a winding sandy path on the right. A child with dark curly hair, a yellow top and orange shorts stands near the pond flying a yellow diamond kite, holding the string reel up high. In front of the child is a flower bed full of colourful flowers. A dark hose runs along the ground to a sprinkler in the front centre, which is spraying arcs of water to the right straight onto a cardboard box, which looks wet and has a puddle under it. On the far right a grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass. Nobody seems to have noticed the box getting soaked.
