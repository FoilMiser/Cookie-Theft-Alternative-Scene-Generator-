# pic_047

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a gate on the far left, a paved path running down from it, and a cream house with a blue door on the right. A man in a red shirt and blue trousers stands in the centre painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; a section of the fence is already blue. A paint can and a paint tray with blue paint sit on the grass by his feet. A white dog with brown spots has walked through the paint: a trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the grass to the dog, which now has blue paws and is on the path. At the far left a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sweeps a pile of leaves on the path with a yellow broom. Nobody seems to have noticed the dog's paw prints yet. There are also two potted plants, a watering can, a wheelbarrow and a flower bed by the house.
