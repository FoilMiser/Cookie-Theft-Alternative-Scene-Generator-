## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A woman in a yellow dress walks across the lawn in the front centre carrying a cardboard box in both arms, heading towards the left. On the right a bearded man in a green shirt and beige trousers is watering a flower bed with a teal watering can, and water streams over the flowers. A grey wheelbarrow stands on the grass on the left, empty. An empty wooden bench stands by the house in the bottom right. Nothing seems about to go wrong between the people; they are each doing their own task. The wheelbarrow's wheel looks odd.
