## Free description
This is a sunny park with a pond, a lamp post, a bench, trees and a winding path, and five people. In the front left a woman in a yellow dress is watering a flower bed, but she is pouring the water out of a frying pan instead of a watering can. Next to her a man in a green shirt and beige trousers holds a paper bag and is sprinkling seeds onto the grass, where a red bird is pecking at them. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is running towards the bird while eating a pink ice cream cone, so he may be about to scare it off or run into it. Further back, a girl in a pink dress holds a red and a purple balloon and reaches up towards a yellow balloon that is floating away above her, so she seems to have just lost it. On the right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks along the path carrying a yellow shopping bag. The bench near the path is empty.
