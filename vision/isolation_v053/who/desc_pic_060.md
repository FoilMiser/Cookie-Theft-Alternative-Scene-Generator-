In the centre a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is jumping in the air with both arms raised, looking happy and excited (his shadow is on the ground below him). The swing set on the left has a red seat hanging still. Something odd: an orange fish is sitting in the tree behind the fence, on a branch, which is impossible/out of place. Otherwise it is a cheerful garden scene.
