# pic_038

## Free description
A sunny town street with a row of coloured shop buildings with striped awnings, a lamp post, flower planters and a paved pedestrian area. On the left, beside a fruit and vegetable stall, a woman in a purple cardigan and teal trousers sweeps a pile of leaves with a yellow broom. Next to her a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is using a giant silver fork to push dust toward a red dustpan, which is strange because a fork is not a sweeping tool and it is far too big. In the centre an older woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal top and grey skirt walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. Behind her a girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench drinking from a cup. On the right, a blue-and-white ball is stuck in the branches of a street tree, and a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands below pointing up at it, looking worried. A green bicycle is parked in a bike rack in front of the shops, and there are potted plants at the bottom corners.
