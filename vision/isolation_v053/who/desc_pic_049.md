# pic_049

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a cream house with a blue door on the left, and a paved path and gate on the right. In the centre a woman in a yellow dress is pegging laundry on a washing line between two posts: blue jeans, a red-and-yellow striped towel and a white T-shirt, which is dripping water. A laundry basket full of clothes sits on the grass below the line. At front left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands by a flower bed holding a grey watering can; a long blue curved stream of water rises from the can up and back over the flowers, ending in drops in the air. On the right, a small girl in a pink dress stands on the path flying an orange kite high in the sky. A grey wheelbarrow stands on the grass at front centre and there are two potted plants by the fence.
