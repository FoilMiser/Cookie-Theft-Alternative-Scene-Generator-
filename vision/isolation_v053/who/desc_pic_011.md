## Free description
A sunny day in a park with a metal railing fence across the back, a tree on the left and a red slide on the right. A woman in a purple top and teal skirt sits on a bench eating a pink ice cream cone, and a brown dog with a red collar sits up on its hind legs beside her, apparently begging for the ice cream. In the centre a woman in a yellow dress walks along reading an open book; she is not looking where she is going, and a cardboard box sits on the grass right in front of her, so she looks about to trip over it. Behind her, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands with his hands on his hips wearing a grey bucket upside-down on his head, which is odd. On the right an older man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat pushes a teal pram with a baby in it; a grey rock lies on the ground ahead of the pram, so the pram may bump into it. An orange cat sleeps curled up on the grass at front left, and a blue bird sits on the fence rail.
