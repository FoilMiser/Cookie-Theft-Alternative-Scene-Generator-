# pic_027

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. In front, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal skirt holds a grey watering can over the flower bed, but the water comes out as a blue stream that curves upward into the air above the flowers with drops at the top, as if water were flowing up instead of down, which looks impossible. Behind her, a woman in a yellow dress stands on tiptoe pegging a white T-shirt onto a clothesline; the T-shirt is dripping water. Already on the line are blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes, and a wicker laundry basket full of clothes sits on the grass below. On the right, a small girl in a pink dress stands on the path flying an orange kite with bow tails high in the sky, holding the string spool. There are two potted plants by the fence and a grey wheelbarrow with a round wheel in the foreground.
