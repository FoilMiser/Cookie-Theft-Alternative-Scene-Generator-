In the middle, a man in a red shirt and blue trousers is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; a section of fence planks next to him is already blue. At the far left, a woman in a purple top and teal skirt is sweeping a small pile of brown leaves on the path with a broom, next to a wooden garden gate. A white dog with brown spots is walking along the grass towards the path, and a trail of blue paw prints leads from a paint tray on the ground back to the dog, so the dog has stepped in the paint and is tracking it across the garden. The dog seems about to walk onto the path the woman is sweeping, and neither adult seems to have noticed. There is also a paint can, two potted plants, a watering can, a wheelbarrow, and a flower bed near the house.
