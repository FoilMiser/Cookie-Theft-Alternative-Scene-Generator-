A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the left, a woman in a purple cardigan, white top and teal trousers sweeps a pile of brown leaves on the paved path with a yellow broom. Next to her a tall pole holds a red-roofed birdhouse. In the middle stands a grey swing frame with one empty red swing seat. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress kneels on the grass by a flower bed, using a small trowel to dig, with a pot holding a pink plant next to her, so she seems to be planting it. In the foreground there is a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow with a round black wheel, and a wooden bench sits in front of the house. Nothing looks clearly wrong or impossible.
