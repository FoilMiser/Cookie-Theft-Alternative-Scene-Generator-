# pic_050

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a gate with a paved path on the right. In the middle, a woman with dark hair, a purple top and a teal skirt is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; a section of the fence to her left is already blue, and a small paint tin stands by her feet. On the right, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers sweeps a pile of brown leaves on the path with a yellow broom. A grey swing frame with an empty red swing stands on the lawn, and a birdhouse sits on a tall pole by the gate. In the foreground there is a hose reel with a hose ending in a yellow nozzle, a grey wheelbarrow with a round wheel and a teal watering can. A colourful flower bed sits next to the house. Nothing looks clearly wrong.
