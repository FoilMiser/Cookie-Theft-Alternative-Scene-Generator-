## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back and a wooden gate and paved path on the right. An older man with grey hair, a grey flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands by the fence raking a small pile of brown leaves. A clothesline between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. In the middle of the lawn stands a grey wheelbarrow, and its wheel is drawn as a square rather than a circle, which looks wrong. In the front, a garden hose runs to a sprinkler that is spraying arcs of water onto a cardboard box sitting on the grass; the box is standing in a puddle with drips on it, so it is getting soaked. There is a colourful flower bed beside the house and a bush by the house wall. Nobody seems to have noticed the box getting wet.
