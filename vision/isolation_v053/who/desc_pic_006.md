# pic_006

## Free description
A sunny park with a pond, a lamp post, a bench, a winding path, trees and a flower bed. In the front left a woman in a yellow dress is watering the flower bed, but she is pouring the water out of a frying pan instead of a watering can, which looks wrong. Behind her, by the pond, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers holds a paper bag and scatters seed or crumbs onto the grass. A red bird is pecking at the crumbs on the ground just in front of a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts, who is walking/running while eating a pink ice-cream cone; he is heading toward the bird and might step on it or startle it. In the middle distance a girl in a pink dress holds a red and a purple balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other toward a yellow balloon that has floated away above her — she seems to have just lost it. On the right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks along the path carrying a yellow shopping bag. A green bin stands in the bottom-left corner.
