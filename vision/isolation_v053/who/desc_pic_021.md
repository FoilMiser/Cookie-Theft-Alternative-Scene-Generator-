## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back and a wooden gate and paved path on the right. In the middle, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; a section of the fence is already blue and a paint tin stands on the grass by her feet. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping a small pile of brown leaves on the path with a yellow broom. A metal swing set with one empty red swing stands in the centre-right of the lawn. A birdhouse on a tall pole stands near the gate. In the front there is a hose reel with a hose lying on the grass, an empty grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can. A round bed of colourful flowers sits near the house. Everything looks normal and calm.
