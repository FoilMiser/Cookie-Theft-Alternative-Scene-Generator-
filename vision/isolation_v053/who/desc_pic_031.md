A sunny park with a pond, lamp post, bin, winding path, trees and a flower bed. The water lands on a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is getting wet and has a puddle around it. On the far right an empty wheelbarrow sits on the grass, but its wheel is a square instead of a round one, which looks wrong. No one else is in the scene.
