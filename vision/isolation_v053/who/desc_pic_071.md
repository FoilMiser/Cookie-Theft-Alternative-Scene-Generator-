# pic_071

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left (blue door, window), a wooden fence with a gate on the right and a paving-stone path leading to the gate. In the middle background an elderly man with grey hair, a flat cap, a white shirt and brown waistcoat is raking a small pile of dead leaves near the fence. A washing line between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel. Under the washing line stands an empty grey wheelbarrow, but its wheel is square instead of round, which is wrong. In the foreground a sprinkler on a hose is spraying arcs of water to the right onto a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is getting soaked and has a puddle around it. There is a flower bed in front of the house.
