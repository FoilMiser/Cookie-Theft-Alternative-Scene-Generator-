# pic_055

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a cream house with a blue door on the left, and a paved path and gate on the right. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence blue with a paint roller; a section of several planks is already blue and a paint tin stands at her feet. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps brown leaves on the path with a yellow broom. In the middle there is a swing frame with one empty red swing. A coiled garden hose lies at front left, a grey wheelbarrow at front centre and a teal watering can on the grass nearby. A birdhouse on a tall pole stands near the gate, and a flower bed is in front of the house.
