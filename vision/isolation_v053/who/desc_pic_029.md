# pic_029

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back and a gate with a paved path on the right. In the middle, an elderly man with grey hair, a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is raking dry leaves on the lawn, standing between the posts of a washing line. The washing line holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow striped towel. At the front, a green hose leads to a lawn sprinkler that is spraying arcs of water — and the water lands right on a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is now standing in a puddle and getting soaked. Next to it is a grey wheelbarrow whose wheel is drawn as a square, which would not roll. Two potted plants stand near the fence and a round flower bed is on the left. The box looks like it will be ruined if nobody moves it.
