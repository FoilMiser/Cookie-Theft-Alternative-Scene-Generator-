# pic_072

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence with a gate and paved path on the right, and a flowerbed in front of the house. In the middle an older man with grey hair, a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is raking a small pile of leaves; he stands between the two posts of a washing line, on which hang a pair of blue jeans and a yellow striped towel. At the front, a garden hose runs from the left to a sprinkler that is spraying arcs of water straight onto a cardboard box, which sits in a small puddle getting soaked. To the right of the box is a wheelbarrow whose wheel looks square instead of round. Two potted plants stand near the fence. The man does not seem to notice the box getting wet.
