# pic_051

## Free description
A sunny town street with a row of colourful shops with striped awnings, a lamp post, flower boxes, a bike rack and a tree on a paved square. At the front left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sweeps a pile of brown leaves with a yellow broom. Next to her, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is "sweeping" dust towards a red dustpan using what looks like a giant silver fork instead of a broom, which is strange. In the centre an older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. Behind them, a young girl in pink sits on a bench drinking from a cup. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of the tree.
