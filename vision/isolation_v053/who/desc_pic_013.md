## Free description
A sunny playground with a dark metal railing fence across the back and a tree on the left. Two children sit on a red seesaw in the front left: a girl in pink on the left end, which is up, and a boy in yellow on the right end, which is down. In the centre-back a woman in a purple top and teal skirt stands near the fence eating a pink ice-cream cone. To the right is a red-framed slide with an orange/yellow chute and a ladder; nobody is on it. A green bin stands by the fence, a bush is at the far right and a grey rock lies in the front right. Nothing seems about to go wrong; the scene is calm. The woman may be watching over the children.
