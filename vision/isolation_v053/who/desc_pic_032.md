# pic_032

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house with a blue door on the left, a wooden fence and a gate on the right, and a paved path running down the right side. In the centre an elderly man with grey hair, a flat cap, a white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is raking a small pile of leaves. He stands under a washing line strung between two wooden posts, on which hang a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. At the front, a garden hose runs across the lawn to a sprinkler that is spraying arcs of water onto a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is standing in a puddle and getting soaked. Nobody seems to have noticed the box getting wet. To the right of the box is a grey wheelbarrow, and there are two potted plants near the fence and a flower bed in front of the house.
