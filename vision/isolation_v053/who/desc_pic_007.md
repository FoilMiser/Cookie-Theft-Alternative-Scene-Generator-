# pic_007

## Free description
A busy sunny park with a pond, a lamppost, a path on the right and trees. At the front left a woman in a yellow dress is watering a flowerbed, but she is pouring the water out of a frying pan instead of a watering can. Behind her, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers holds a paper bag and scatters seeds or crumbs on the ground, and a red bird is pecking at them near his feet. Just right of the bird a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts walks along eating a pink ice-cream cone. Further back, a girl in a pink dress holds a red and a purple balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other towards a yellow balloon that is floating away into the sky; she looks upset. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal skirt walks down the path carrying a yellow shopping bag. An empty bench stands in the middle-right and a green bin is in the bottom left corner. The odd thing is the frying pan being used to water the flowers.
