# pic_070

## Free description
A sunny town square paved with grey tiles, in front of a row of colourful shops with striped awnings. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks on the left carrying a cardboard box. At the front centre, an orange cat is crouched low, creeping towards a blue bird that is pecking at seeds on the ground just in front of it — the cat looks like it's about to pounce on the bird. A red bird is perched on a branch in a tree on the right. A green-and-teal bicycle is parked in a bike rack in the middle background, and there is an empty bench in the centre. On the left there is a fruit stall with crates of green, yellow, orange and red fruit, a wooden box and a potted plant; on the right a bin and a café table with two chairs. The blue bird doesn't seem to have noticed the cat.
