## 1. Free description

In the middle-left there is an oval pond with cattails at each end, and a white duck is swimming across it to the right, toward bits of food in the water. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands at the right edge of the pond next to a tall black lamppost, tossing small brown crumbs (probably bread) from a small bag or cup to the duck; one crumb is still in the air. In the front left, a woman with a brown ponytail and a blue hoodie is walking while reading an open book, and a wooden box sits on the grass just ahead of her, so she looks about to trip over it because she is not watching where she is going. Nearby on the left, an orange cat is curled up asleep on the grass, and a green trash can stands in the bottom left corner. On the right, a person in a yellow dress sits on a wooden park bench eating a pink ice cream cone, while a brown dog with a red collar or bandana sits up on its hind legs beside the bench, begging for the ice cream. A winding sandy path runs from the bottom right up into the background, and further back an older man with a cap, vest and white shirt walks along carrying a yellow shopping bag. In the bottom right corner, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands licking his own pink ice cream cone. Nothing looks clearly impossible; the main thing that seems about to happen is the reading woman bumping into the box, and possibly the dog getting the ice cream.
