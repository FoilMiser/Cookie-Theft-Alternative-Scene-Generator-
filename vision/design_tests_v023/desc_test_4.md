## Free description

This is a sunny day in a park playground. There is a big yellow sun in the top left, two white clouds, a dark metal railing fence across the back with green bushes behind it, and a leafy tree on the right. On the left is a red-framed slide with a ladder and a yellow-orange chute. Under the end of the slide, an orange cat is crouched low and creeping toward a small blue bird that is pecking at the ground with its head down. The bird does not seem to notice, so the cat looks like it is about to pounce. In the front centre, a dark-haired woman (or older girl) in a yellow dress and red shoes is running to the left, with one arm reaching forward and speed lines behind her. She seems to be running toward the cat and bird, perhaps to stop the cat. In front of the slide there is an empty sandbox with a wooden frame, and a grey rock sits at the far left edge. On the right, two children are on a red seesaw. The child on the left in a yellow top is on the lower end, and the girl on the right in a pink dress is on the higher end. Behind them, along the fence, an older man in a grey cap, white shirt and brown vest is pushing a teal pram toward the left. A baby is asleep in the pram under a pink spotted blanket. Nothing looks clearly impossible. The main tension is the cat stalking the unaware bird.

- Why she is running is not clear. Reading 1: she is rushing to stop the cat from catching the bird, since she is heading in their direction with her arm out. Reading 2: she is simply jogging or playing and the cat and bird have nothing to do with her.
- The cat's intent is open to two readings. It could be stalking and about to pounce on the bird, or just crouching and stretching. Its low crouch facing the bird suggests stalking.
