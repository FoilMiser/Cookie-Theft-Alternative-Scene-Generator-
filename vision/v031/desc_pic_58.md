# pic_58

## Free description
This is a back garden on a sunny but windy day: the sun is in the top-right corner and there are white wavy wind lines drawn in the sky. A cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, with a bed of colourful flowers in front of it, and a wooden fence runs across the back with a gate on the right and a paved path leading down to the bottom-right corner. In the middle of the lawn a man with dark skin, a green T-shirt, khaki trousers and brown shoes stands with both arms stretched straight up. Just above his head, slightly to the left, a red cap is flying through the air, with white wind streaks and a couple of small leaves blowing beside it. His mouth is open in an "O" and he looks surprised; it looks as though the wind has just blown his cap off and he is trying to catch it. The cap is just above his hands and he has not caught it yet. Two potted plants stand near the fence on the right, and a teal watering can stands on the grass at the bottom-right next to the path.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Direction of the cap: the wind streaks trail to the right of the cap, so it could be read either as the cap being blown away to the left, or as wind coming from the left and pushing the cap to the right. Either way it is being blown off/away.
- The man's pose could be read as trying to catch the cap or as throwing his arms up in surprise (or even having just tossed it up himself); the surprised face and wind lines make "blown off by the wind" the stronger reading.
- Nothing else looks strange; the cap floating is explained by the wind.
