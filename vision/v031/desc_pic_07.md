# Picture 07

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills behind, a winding sandy path on the left, a pond with bulrushes on the right and a tall black lamp post in the middle. A blue-and-white beach ball is stuck high in the branches of the big tree on the left, and a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and black trousers stands below pointing up at it. Behind him a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is digging a hole with a shovel, with a pile of dirt beside her. Near the lamp post an older man with grey hair, a grey flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and black trousers is standing eating a pink ice cream. On the right a girl in a pink dress holds a purple and a blue balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other after a red balloon that has got away and is floating over the pond. At the front right a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a yellow balloon beside a colourful flower bed. At the bottom centre a black-and-white cat is crouched low and creeping towards a red bird on the ground, as if about to pounce on it. An empty wooden bench stands beside the path.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The cat could be stalking the bird to pounce, or simply walking past low to the ground; its crouch suggests stalking.
- The beach ball looks wedged in the tree; it could also be read as just landing there.
- The red balloon's string end is just above the girl's raised hand, so it may have just slipped away or she may be about to catch it.
