## Free description
This is a sunny park with hills and hedges behind, the sun in the top-right corner and two white clouds. On the left is a blue pond with cattails at each end and a white duck swimming on it. Near a tall black lamp post in the middle, an older man with grey hair, a grey flat cap, a white shirt and a brown waistcoat holds a brown paper bag and is scattering seeds or crumbs onto the ground. A small blue bird and a small red bird are on the ground at his feet, pecking at the seeds. On the right there is an empty wooden bench, a sandy path winding from the back to the bottom, and a big tree. At the front left there is a bed of colourful flowers and a green rubbish bin, and a grey rock lies at the bottom centre. The scene looks peaceful and ordinary; nothing seems about to go wrong.
