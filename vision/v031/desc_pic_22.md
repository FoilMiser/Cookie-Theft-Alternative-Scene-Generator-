## Free description
This is a sunny day in a back garden. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, and a tall wooden fence runs across the back with a wooden gate on the right. A paved path leads from the gate to the front right. Near the house, a woman in a yellow dress is digging with a long-handled spade, and a mound of soil beside her shows where she has been digging. In the middle, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers is reaching up to peg a white T-shirt onto a washing line. A pair of blue jeans is already hanging there. There is a flower bed full of colourful flowers in front of the house, a swing set with an empty red seat on the right, a tall pole with a birdhouse on top, and a teal watering can on the grass. In the front centre, a boy in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is drinking from a red cup with a straw. He is posed as if sitting on a chair, with his knees bent and thighs level, but there is no chair or anything else under him. He is sitting on thin air, which looks impossible.
