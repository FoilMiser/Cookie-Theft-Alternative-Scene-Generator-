# pic_49

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the middle, a woman with dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers stands at the fence holding a paint roller on a long handle, painting the fence boards blue; a strip of about three boards to her right is already blue. At her feet are a paint tin and a paint tray full of blue paint. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is walking away to the left, and it is leaving a trail of blue paw prints behind it that start at the paint tray, so it has walked through the paint and its feet are blue. The woman is facing the fence and does not seem to have noticed the dog. On the right, a brown dog with a red neckerchief sits by the house door holding a red lead in its mouth, as if it wants to go for a walk. Odd thing: a brown cardboard box is up in the sky on the left, below the sun, tilted, with nothing holding it, as if it is floating or falling.
