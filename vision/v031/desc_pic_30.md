# Picture 30

## Free description
This is a busy sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and birdhouse on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the left path a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie rakes leaves, and a brown-and-white puppy lies curled up asleep on the path in front of him. An orange cat walks along the top of the fence. Near the empty swing, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses stands with her hands on her hips, wearing a metal bucket upside down on her head like a hat. At the back, a small girl in a pink dress pulls on a kite string, but the purple kite is caught in the tree behind the fence on the right. In the middle front, a person in a yellow dress holds a garden hose pointed forward, but only a single drop of water comes out. A white dog with brown spots is standing on the hose, which would explain the blocked water. On the right, a man in a red shirt sits on a bench reading a red book. A sandwich sits beside him on the bench, and a blue bird is right next to it, about to peck at or steal it. At the front left, the wheelbarrow has a square wheel instead of a round one, which wouldn't roll.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Dog and hose: the hose passes right under the dog's feet. It most likely shows the dog standing on the hose and blocking the water. Alternatively the hose just runs behind the dog and the trickle is because the tap is off.
- The square wheel on the wheelbarrow is drawn as a dark square frame. It could be read as an impossible square wheel, or possibly as a box or crate tucked under the front of the barrow. The first reading seems more likely.
- The bucket on the woman's head reads as a silly or odd hat. It could also be a pale chef-style hat, but it looks like a metal pail.
- The kite looks tangled in the tree, partly hidden by leaves. It could also be read as flying just in front of the tree.
- The blue bird by the sandwich could be eating it, about to steal it, or just sitting next to it.
- The cat on the fence top is plausible, not impossible.
