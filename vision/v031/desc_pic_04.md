# Picture 04

## Free description
This is a sunny paved town square or shopping street in front of a row of colourful two-storey shops with striped awnings. On the left a street worker in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest with blue trousers is sweeping a pile of autumn leaves with a broom, next to a tree growing in a round planter. In the middle a girl in a pink outfit sits on a wooden bench eating a pink ice cream, while a white dog with a brown ear patch and blue collar sits up on its hind legs in front of her, begging for the ice cream. Behind them a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a blue and a red balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other after a purple balloon that has slipped away and is floating upward. On the right is a fruit and vegetable stall with crates of red, orange, yellow and green produce. In front of it a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is walking along while reading an open book, and there is a cardboard box on the ground right in his path — he looks about to trip over it. A blue bird flies at the top left, and a red bird is perched on top of a black street lamp. There is another box beside the stall, a flower box with colourful flowers in front of the shops, and a potted plant in the bottom right corner.
