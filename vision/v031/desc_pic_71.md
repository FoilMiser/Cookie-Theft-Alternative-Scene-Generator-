## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the left, a paved path running from the gate to the front left, a tree behind the house and a small bush. In the front left, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is kicking an orange ball, with motion lines under his kicking leg, sending it toward the left. In the centre, a child with braided hair in a yellow top and orange shorts holds a long string and looks up at a yellow kite flying high in the sky with a bow tail. On the right, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and grey skirt is holding a teal watering can over the flower bed. Strangely, the water from the can's spout shoots upward in an arc, with drops at the top, instead of pouring down onto the flowers, which is impossible for a watering can. Nothing else looks wrong.
