## Free description
A busy sunny park with a pond on the left, a lamp post and green bin in the middle, a bench, a winding sandy path on the right and a large tree on the far right, with a blue bird flying in the sky. Several odd things are going on. An older man in a cap and brown waistcoat is watering the flower bed by pouring water out of a frying pan instead of a watering can. On the far left, a white dog with brown ears and a blue neckerchief is sitting up and holding open a newspaper as if reading it. In the front centre, a child with braided hair in a yellow top and orange shorts holds an empty ice cream cone and puts a hand to their head, looking upset. Their pink ice cream lies splattered on the ground, and a tan dog with a red collar is bending down to lick it up. A small girl in a pink dress stands at the edge of the pond holding a little paper bag and throwing crumbs to a white duck, which is swimming quickly (speed lines) toward the crumbs floating on the water. On the path, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and grey skirt is sweeping a pile of orange and brown autumn leaves with a broom. Behind her, a white goat with small horns is grazing on a tuft of grass, which is unusual for a park. On the far right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie holds a kite string. His purple kite is caught up in the leaves of the big tree.
