## Free description
This is a back garden under a grey, overcast sky with three big dark storm clouds overhead, so it looks as if it is about to rain. On the left is a cream house with a red roof and blue door, with a flower bed in front of it. A woman with a long dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers stands next to the flower bed watering the flowers with a garden hose; the hose runs back along the grass to a hose reel at the bottom-left. In the front centre, a boy in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is in a sitting position with his knees bent, drinking from a red cup through a yellow straw, but there is no chair, bench or anything under him: he appears to be sitting on thin air. Behind him, a small child in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts stands pointing up at the dark clouds and looking up at them. On the right is a metal swing set with one empty red swing, a birdhouse on a tall pole, a wooden gate in the back fence, and a paved path to the bottom-right. The odd things are the boy sitting on nothing and the woman watering flowers just as rain seems about to come.
