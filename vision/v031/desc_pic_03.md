# Picture 03

## Free description
This is a sunny park with rolling green hills in the background, a winding sandy path leading from the front-left into the distance, and a pond on the right with bulrushes at its ends. A man in a green shirt and beige trousers stands at the left edge of the pond holding a brown paper bag and tossing bread crumbs onto the water. A white duck in the pond is swimming up to the crumbs and pecking at one, leaving a little wake behind it. On the right a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is walking to the right pushing a teal pram with a navy hood; a baby with closed eyes, under a pink spotted blanket, is asleep inside. In the middle of the picture there is a tall black lamp post and a green rubbish bin. On the left, next to the path and the big tree, a wooden ladder stands leaning at an angle but there is nothing for it to lean against — it seems to be propped up on thin air, which looks wrong. There is also a grey rock at the front, bushes, and a smaller tree on the far right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The ladder leans against nothing — it should fall over. It could be read as leaning against an invisible wall/tree, or as something left there for no reason; either way it looks unsupported and strange.
- The man's lower body overlaps the edge of the pond from where I look; he seems to be standing on the grass in front of it, but could be read as standing right at the water's edge.
- The duck could be swimming towards the man or just eating crumbs that are already on the water.
