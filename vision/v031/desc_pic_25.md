# Picture 25

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate at the far left, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. On the left, a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers stands on a paved path, raking a small pile of leaves. In the middle of the garden a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on a swing hanging from a metal A-frame. Short lines behind him suggest he is swinging. Behind him a washing line between two wooden posts holds a yellow-and-red striped top and a pair of blue jeans. A small girl in a pink dress stands in the middle of the lawn, looking up and holding a kite string. The string runs up to a purple kite that is caught in the leaves of the tree behind the fence on the right. The kite looks stuck, so she probably can't fly it until someone gets it down. A watering can and an empty wheelbarrow sit on the grass at the front, and there is a flower bed in front of the house.
