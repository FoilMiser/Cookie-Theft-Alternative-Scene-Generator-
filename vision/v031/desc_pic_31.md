# Picture 31

## Free description
This is a busy sunny town street with a row of four coloured shop buildings with striped awnings, and a red bird flying in the sky at top left. On the left, a blue-and-white beach ball is stuck in the branches of a tree in a planter. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts jumps with his arms up, and a girl in a pink dress points up at the ball, so they seem to be trying to get it back. In the middle, a man in a green shirt sits on a bench reading a red book. A sandwich sits beside him and a blue bird is right next to it, about to take it. In front of him, a brown dog sits on the pavement holding open a newspaper as if reading it, which is impossible for a dog. Further right, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses holds a watering can by a flower planter, but the stream of water curves upward into the air instead of falling onto the flowers. A street sweeper in an orange cap and hi-vis vest sweeps a pile of leaves near the lamp post. At the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie walks past the market stall carrying a yellow shopping bag. In the front right, a man in a red shirt walks along reading an open book, heading towards a cardboard box on the ground that he will probably trip over.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Water direction: the stream from the watering can rises in an arc with droplets above it, which is impossible. It could also be read as water spraying up from the planter like a fountain. Either way it isn't falling on the flowers.
- The dog with the newspaper: it is held open in front of the dog as if being read. The dog's head is turned up and to the side, so it may be holding the paper rather than reading it. Either way it is odd.
- The jumping boy could be trying to reach the ball or just cheering. His eyes look closed and he is smiling.
- The blue bird could be eating the sandwich, about to steal it, or just perched beside it.
- The man in red seems to be walking left towards the box. If he keeps walking while reading, he may trip, though he might pass beside it.
