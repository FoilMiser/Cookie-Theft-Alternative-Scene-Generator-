## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a wooden gate on the right, and a paved path running from the gate down to the bottom-right corner. A woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers stands in the middle-left with both arms stretched out in front of her towards the right. A red ball is in the air just in front of her hands, and a dashed curved line runs from the ball back to a small girl in a pink dress further right, who is running with one arm stretched out towards the woman — so it looks like they are playing catch and the girl has just thrown the ball to the woman, who is about to catch it. At the front, a white dog with brown spots is crouched down in a playful bow with its tail wagging, a yellow-green tennis ball right in front of its nose. Beside the house door, a brown dog sits holding a red lead in its mouth, as if it wants to go for a walk. On the path at the right, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt is sweeping a pile of orange and red autumn leaves with a broom. There is also a colourful flower bed in front of the house and a teal watering can on the grass near the path.
