# Picture 08

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence and a gate at the back-left, a paved path running down the left side, and a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the right. In the centre-front a woman (or older girl) with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers is drinking from a red cup through a straw while sitting in a seated position — but there is no chair, bench or anything under her, so she appears to be sitting on thin air, which looks impossible. To the left is a metal swing set with one red swing seat hanging empty. On the grass in front are a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow, and on the right a hose reel with a green hose ending in a yellow nozzle lies near a round flower bed full of colourful flowers. A tree and bushes show behind the fence and beside the house. No one else is in the garden and nothing else seems about to happen.
