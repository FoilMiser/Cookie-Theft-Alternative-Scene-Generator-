## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a long wooden fence across the back with a wooden gate on the right, and a paved path running from the gate to the bottom-right corner. An older woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; a section of fence to her left is already blue. On the grass by her feet are a tin of blue paint and a paint tray with blue paint. A trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray to a white dog with brown spots, whose paws are blue — the dog has stepped in the paint and is walking off, and the woman has not noticed. At the front left, a garden sprinkler attached to a hose is spraying water in arcs onto a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is getting soaked and has a puddle around it. On the path at the right, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers is sweeping a small pile of dirt or gravel towards a red dustpan using a giant silver dinner fork, about as tall as a garden tool, which raises a little dust cloud. There is also a colourful flower bed, two potted plants near the fence, and an empty grey wheelbarrow at the front.
