# Picture 32

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a wooden gate with a paved path on the right. An orange cat walks along the top of the fence. In front of the house, a person in a yellow dress is watering the flower bed by pouring water out of a frying pan instead of a watering can or hose, which is odd. In the middle of the lawn, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses digs a hole with a shovel, with a mound of soil piled up beside the hole. An empty wheelbarrow stands at the front, and two potted plants sit on the grass near the fence. Apart from the frying pan being the wrong tool for watering, everything looks normal.
