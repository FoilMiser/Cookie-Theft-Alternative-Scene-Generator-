## Free description
It is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the left, and a house with a blue door on the right. At the back in the middle, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long pole; a few boards to his left are already blue and a tin of blue paint is by his feet. Two potted plants sit on the grass to the left of him. At the front, a lawn sprinkler fed by a long hose from the right is spraying arcs of water to the left, and the water lands on a cardboard box sitting on the grass. The box is dripping wet with a puddle under it, so it looks like it is being ruined. On the right there is a round flowerbed with colourful flowers near the house. Very oddly, an orange goldfish is sitting in the tree behind the house, on a branch among the leaves, which is impossible.
