## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a house on the right and a wooden gate and paved path on the left. On the left a small child with dark skin and curly dark hair, in a yellow shirt and orange shorts, sits on the red seat of a grey A-frame swing set. The child is smiling with eyes closed, and there are motion lines beside the seat, so the child seems to be swinging. In the middle at the back, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long pole. A few fence boards to his left are already blue, and a small tin of blue paint sits on the grass by his feet. On the right, a person with brown hair in a red shirt and blue jeans stands by a round flowerbed full of colourful flowers, holding a green watering can. The odd part is the water: instead of pouring down onto the flowers, a long blue stream arcs upward from near the can, higher than his head, with drops flying off the top. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands on the lawn at the front.
