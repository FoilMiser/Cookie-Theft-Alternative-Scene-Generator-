# Picture 02

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny park playground with a metal railing fence and hedges at the back. On the far left front a woman in a yellow dress is running fast towards the left edge (speed lines trail behind her), arms stretched out ahead, past an empty sandbox that has a red bucket and blue spade in it. In the middle-back a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a red and a yellow balloon in one hand while reaching up with the other towards a purple balloon that is floating away above him with its string dangling. Next to him a man in a green shirt and beige trousers is holding a camera to his eye and taking a photo, pointed towards the left. On a bench to the right a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is reading a red book; a sandwich lies on the bench beside him and a small blue bird is perched right next to it, apparently about to take it while he isn't looking. On the right, a purple kite with a bow-tied tail is stuck in the branches of a tree, and a girl in a pink dress at the front-right is holding its string and tugging, trying to get it down. In the front-centre a red bicycle stands upright but its wheels are square, which is impossible. Next to it a white dog with brown patches is in a playful bow with a yellow tennis ball by its nose, tail wagging. At the bottom right a brown dog with a red collar sits holding an open newspaper with its paws as if reading it, which is also impossible/silly. A red bird flies in the sky near the sun, and the slide on the left is empty.
