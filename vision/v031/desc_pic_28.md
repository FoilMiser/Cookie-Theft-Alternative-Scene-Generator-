# Picture 28

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a wooden gate with a paved path on the right. In the middle of the lawn, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is jumping in the air with both arms raised, looking happy or cheering. His shadow is on the grass below him. In front of him, an orange cat lies on the grass with its eyes closed, apparently asleep or lolling on its side. On the path at the right, a woman with dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers holds an enormous silver dinner fork, like a giant table fork. She is using it like a broom to push a pile of dirt and pebbles into a red dustpan, and a puff of dust rises from the pile. Using a giant eating fork to sweep is the strange part. There are also two potted plants by the fence, two bushes and a flower bed in front of the house.
