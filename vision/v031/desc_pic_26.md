# Picture 26

## Free description
This is a sunny, empty back garden with no people or animals in it. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door. A tree stands behind the house and a small bush grows beside it. A wooden fence runs across the back, with a wooden gate at the far right, and a paved path leads from the gate towards the front right corner. In the garden there is a flower bed of colourful flowers, a wooden bench at the front left, two potted plants near the fence, a birdhouse on a tall pole, and a teal watering can on the grass near the path. The odd thing is a closed cardboard box hanging in mid-air in the sky above the fence, tilted at an angle. Nobody is nearby who could have thrown it, and it isn't attached to anything. It is either floating or in the middle of falling.
