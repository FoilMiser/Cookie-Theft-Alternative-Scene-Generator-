# pic_38

## Free description
This is a sunny playground in a park, fenced off with dark railings and with trees behind. On the left a small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds the strings of a red and a yellow balloon in one hand. With his other arm he reaches up, frowning, towards a blue balloon that is floating away beside the tree, so it seems he has just let it go. A blue bird sits on a branch of that tree. An old man in a cap and brown waistcoat sits on a bench drinking from a cup through a straw, looking content with his eyes closed. In the middle stands an empty swing set, and on the right a slide with a ladder. At the far right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is flying an orange kite high in the sky, near the sun. In the foreground an orange cat is crouched low, creeping up on a red bird pecking on the grass; the cat looks about to pounce. There is also an empty sandpit, a grey rock and a green bin.
