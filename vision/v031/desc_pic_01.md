# Picture 01

## Free description
This is a sunny playground in a park, with a dark metal railing fence across the back and green hedges and treetops behind it. A man in a green shirt, beige trousers and brown shoes is walking across the grass in the front-centre, holding a pink ice-cream cone up near his face as if eating it. Behind him in the middle of the picture is a metal A-frame swing set with a single red swing seat hanging empty and still. On the right a girl in a pink dress is sitting on a red-framed slide with a yellow and orange chute, sliding down towards the left with both arms raised in front of her as if enjoying the ride. A green rubbish bin stands by the fence between the swings and the slide. A large tree stands on the left, with bushes on both sides and a grey rock in the lower right. Nothing looks about to go wrong, and I could not see anything impossible; it is a calm everyday scene.
