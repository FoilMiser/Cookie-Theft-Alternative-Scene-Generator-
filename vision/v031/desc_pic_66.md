# pic_66

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate on the right and a paved path leading from the gate to the front right. In the middle, a young man (or teenage boy) in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is reaching up to peg a white T-shirt onto a washing line between two wooden posts. A pair of blue jeans is already hanging on the line. On the right, an older man in a cap, brown waistcoat and white shirt holds the nozzle of a garden hose out in front of him and looks at it, but only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose runs from a reel next to the house, across the lawn to him. A tan dog with a red collar and its tongue out is standing right on the hose, and the hose bulges just behind the dog's back paw. So the dog seems to be blocking the water, and when it steps off, the water will probably spray the man who is peering at the nozzle. Behind him, by the fence, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands with her hands on her hips, wearing an upside-down metal bucket on her head, which is strange.
