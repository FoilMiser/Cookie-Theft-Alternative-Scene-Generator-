## Free description
This is a quiet, sunny park playground with a dark metal railing fence and hedges at the back. In the front-left a small girl in a pink dress is kneeling in a wooden sandbox, building a sandcastle with three towers and a little red flag on top, holding a small tool in her hand. In the middle-right a man in a green shirt and beige trousers is standing still and eating a pink ice-cream cone, his eyes looking closed or squinting as if enjoying it. Behind them on the left is an empty slide with a red frame and yellow/orange chute, and a green rubbish bin stands by the fence. A large tree stands on the right with bushes on either side, and there is a grey rock by the sandbox. Nothing seems about to happen and nothing looks wrong or impossible; it is a calm scene.
