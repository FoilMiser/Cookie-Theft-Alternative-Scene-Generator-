# Picture 28

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof on the left, a wooden fence along the back, a gate in the fence on the right, and a paved path leading down from that gate. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands by a round flower bed in front of the house, watering the flowers with a teal watering can, and water is pouring onto the plants. Further back, in front of the fence, a girl or young woman with a dark ponytail, in a purple top and teal trousers, is raking fallen leaves into a small pile with a long rake. At the front of the picture, a white dog with brown spots is crouching with its front legs stretched out and its back end up, its tail wagging, and a yellow-green tennis ball is right at its nose. It looks as though the dog wants to play. Two terracotta pots of green plants stand on the grass near the path. Everyone looks busy and calm, and nothing looks impossible or wrong.
