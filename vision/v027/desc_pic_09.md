# Picture 09

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, a garden gate at the far right and a paved path running down the right side. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands by a round flower bed and tips a green watering can, but the water does not fall onto the flowers: it comes out in a long arc that rises back up and to the left, away from the bed, ending in a few drops in the air. So the water seems to be flowing the wrong way, upward out of the can. Further back on the right, a boy in a red shirt and blue trousers holds a long-handled roller or wide brush up against the fence and is painting it blue; a section of the fence is already blue, and a paint tin stands by his feet. On the left, in front of the blue house door, a brown and white dog sits holding a red lead in its own mouth, with the lead trailing to a loop on the ground, as though it wants to go for a walk. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands on the grass at the front. The woman looks down at the can and nobody else is reacting to anything.

## Inventory

- Woman, centre-left, front-middle: dark hair, yellow dress, red shoes; standing beside the flower bed, tipping a watering can.
- Boy or young man, right of centre, at the back: brown hair, red shirt, blue trousers; standing at the fence, painting it blue with a long-handled roller.
- Dog, far left, in front of the house door: brown with white chest and paws, red collar; sitting upright, holding its own red lead in its mouth.
- Watering can: teal/green, in the woman's hand; being tipped.
- Water arc: pale blue stream with drops at the top, going from the can spout up and to the left over the edge of the flower bed; seems to run upward rather than down.
- Flower bed: round brown soil patch with red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers; left of the woman.
- Blue painted part of fence: a block of several planks in the middle right, already painted.
- Long-handled roller: blue head, wooden handle; held by the boy against the fence.
- Paint tin: grey with blue paint on it and a handle; on the grass by the boy's feet, right of him.
- Red lead: from the dog's mouth down to a loop on the grass.
- Wheelbarrow: grey with a wooden handle, front centre-right on the grass; empty and unattended.
- Garden gate: brown wooden door in the fence, far right, at the top of the path.
- House: cream with red roof, blue door and a window, far left.
- Tree: large green tree behind the fence next to the house.
- Paved path: beige slabs, right side, leading to the gate.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The water arc could be read two ways: as water impossibly flowing upward and backward from the can, or as a poorly aimed pour that sprays upward. Either way it does not land on the flowers from above in a natural way; the end near the can touches the spout and the flower heads, and the drops hang at the high end.
- The boy's tool could be a paint roller or a wide flat brush on a long pole. The head looks like a tall, narrow blue rectangle.
- The roller head seems to stand slightly clear of the fence's blue section, at the top of an unpainted plank, so it is not fully clear if it is touching.
- The dog holding its own lead could be read as a dog asking for a walk, or simply as an odd pose. The lead passes down the front of its body.
- The wheelbarrow's handles point right and it has only a short leg visible; it looks a little small but otherwise normal.
- The woman's watering can is small and looks like it merges with her hand.
