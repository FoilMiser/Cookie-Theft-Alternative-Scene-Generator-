# Picture 32

## Free description

This is a busy paved town square on a sunny day, with a row of coloured shops behind it, and a lot of small stories going on at once. On the left, a woman in a yellow dress with a yellow shopping bag walks along, and beside her a small girl in a pink dress points up at a blue ball that is stuck in the branches of a tree. In front of them, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is holding a huge fork, as tall as he is, and using it like a broom or shovel, trying to push a heap of dirt towards a red dustpan on the ground. A brown dog with a red scarf sits upright in the middle of the square, reading a newspaper like a person. On the bench behind it, a woman with a ponytail, in a purple top and teal trousers, is reading a red book and does not notice that a blue bird has landed on the bench and is pecking at her sandwich. On the right, a man in a red shirt and blue trousers is walking along reading an open book, while an old woman with grey hair and glasses behind him sweeps fallen leaves with a broom. The man looks as if he may step into the blue bucket or the pile of leaves, because he is not looking where he is going. A red bird flies overhead. The huge fork and the reading dog are both plainly impossible or silly.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The fork: I am not sure what the boy is doing with it. It could be a giant fork used as a dustpan brush or broom, or he may simply be holding an oversized object. The tips are on the ground next to the dirt, and the dust puff suggests he has just scraped it.
- The shape near the top of the fork is rounded and pale where he grips it, and it may be the handle or a napkin wrapped around it. It looks odd where it meets his chest.
- The ball could be stuck in the tree or lodged on the visible branch. I read it as stuck, and the branch looks as though it was drawn only to hold it up.
- The bird could be eating the sandwich or just standing next to it.
- The red bird in the sky is flying towards the tree but does not seem connected to anything else.
- The old woman stands very close behind the man, and his book and arm overlap the stall; she and the stall's post overlap too, which makes the group on the right a bit crowded.
- The man could step into the leaves or bucket, but the bucket is actually a little to one side of him. He is walking to the left, so the bucket is ahead of him.
- The dog's newspaper looks too large and the pose is human; it seems deliberately silly, and nobody reacts to it.
- The brown dog's rear leg and tail are thin and spread on the ground, and the pose could also be read as the dog slumped rather than sitting.
