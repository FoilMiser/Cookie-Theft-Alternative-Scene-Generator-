# Picture 22

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a cream house with a red roof and blue door at right, and a gate and concrete path at left. An older man with grey hair, a blue-grey flat cap, a white shirt with a brown vest, dark trousers and brown shoes stands on the path at far left, using what looks like a giant silver fork as if it were a broom or rake. He is pushing it along the path towards a small pile of dirt and a red dustpan, with little puffs of dust rising, so he is sweeping up with a fork, which is the wrong tool and looks silly. On the right, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes is kneeling on one knee at the edge of a flower bed, planting or setting down a small potted plant with pink flowers. A blue bird perches on top of the fence at centre. A metal wheelbarrow stands empty in the front of the picture, and two potted green plants sit on the grass near the gate. Nothing seems about to happen, though the fork is odd.
