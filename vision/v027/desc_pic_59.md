# Picture 59

## Free description

This is a busy sunny park with a pond, hills in the distance, a tall lamp post in the middle and a winding path on the right. Several odd things are going on. At the left, a brown dog with a red neckerchief sits upright on the grass like a person, holding an open newspaper with its paws and seeming to read it. Near the centre, a boy with dark skin in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds the strings of two balloons, yellow and red, while a third, blue balloon has got away and is drifting up beyond his reach; he stretches his other arm up towards it and looks upset or worried. On a bench on the right of centre, a grey-haired older woman in glasses reads a red book, and a blue bird stands on the bench beside her, pecking at or about to take her sandwich. Farther back on the path, a spotted white dog is bending forward with its tail up, about to pick up or play with a yellow tennis ball. A girl in a pink dress stands on the path holding a string that goes up to a purple kite stuck in the big tree. At the front right, a person in a yellow dress has a metal bucket upside down on their head like a hat, and stands with hands on hips. A white duck swims on the pond and a red bird flies in the sky.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The figure at the far right is hard to place: it could be a woman or girl in a yellow dress. The bucket on the head could be read as a silly hat worn on purpose, or as something that has fallen and landed there; the upright pose with hands on hips suggests it is on purpose, or that the person is annoyed about it.
- The girl's kite could be read as stuck in the tree (the kite sits among the leaves and the line is slack) or as simply flying in front of the tree. Her raised hand could mean she is trying to tug it free. Stuck seems more likely.
- The blue balloon's string ends in mid-air with nobody holding it, so I read it as having escaped. It could alternatively be read as still attached to the boy's raised hand, but the string does not reach him.
- The blue bird could be eating the sandwich or just standing next to it. The woman appears not to have noticed either way, as she keeps reading.
- The spotted dog's pose could be read as a play bow next to the ball, or as nosing or fetching the ball; there are little motion marks by its tail, suggesting wagging.
- The dog reading a newspaper is clearly impossible in real life, and I take it as a deliberate joke or absurdity, not a drawing error. The same goes for the bucket hat.
- The kite string runs across the tree trunk and loops low before reaching the girl, which looks a bit unusual for a line under tension, so it suggests a slack line.
