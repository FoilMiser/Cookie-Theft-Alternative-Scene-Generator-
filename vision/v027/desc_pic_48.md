# Picture 48

## Free description
This is a playground on a sunny day, with a dark metal railing fence across the back, hedges behind it, a tree on the right, and a red-and-yellow slide on the left. In the right-centre foreground there is a red seesaw on a grey triangular support. An older man with grey hair, a grey-blue flat cap, a brown waistcoat and white shirt sits on the low end of the seesaw, reading an open book and paying no attention to anything else. A small girl in a pink dress sits on the raised high end of the seesaw, up in the air, with her feet dangling and her mouth in a small "o", looking worried or surprised. The seesaw is tipped steeply because the man is heavier, so the girl is stuck up high, and he does not seem to notice. The slide on the left is empty, and there is a green rubbish bin by the fence, a grey rock at the front left, and a bush on the far left. Little curved lines under the girl's feet suggest she is kicking or wobbling. The odd thing about the picture is that a grown man is sitting on a child's seesaw reading a book, leaving the girl stranded in the air.
