This is a sunny playground or park with a long dark metal railing fence across the back and a line of bushes behind it. On the right is a red-framed slide with a dark ladder at the back and an orange and yellow chute. A small girl with dark hair in a pink dress and yellow shoes is sliding down it, sitting about halfway down with both arms raised in the air, looking happy. To the left there is a large tree, a small bush, an empty wooden bench and, nearer the slide, a green bin. A grey rock lies on the grass at the front right. A large part of the grass in the front is empty. The girl is the only person or animal in the picture, and nothing seems about to happen beyond her reaching the bottom of the slide.
