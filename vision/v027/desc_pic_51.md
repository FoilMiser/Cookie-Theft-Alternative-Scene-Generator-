# Picture 51

## Free description
This is a windy, sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the left, and a bird box on a tall pole. On the far left, a woman or teenager with dark hair, in a purple top and teal trousers, stands on a paved path with both arms up and a surprised face, reaching for a blue cap that is blown up into the air above her, along with a few leaves, while white wind lines streak across from the left. In the middle back, an elderly woman with a grey bun and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, is painting the fence blue with a roller on a long handle; a section of fence is already blue, and there is a paint tin and a tray of blue paint on the grass by her feet. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has walked through the paint tray and is leaving a trail of blue paw prints across the grass, and its feet are blue too. It is heading away from the tray to the left, and the woman painting has not noticed. At the front, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is kicking an orange ball on the lawn, with motion lines near his foot. There is also a flower bed near the house and an empty bench at the front right.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The white wind lines at the far left cross over the top of the gate and past the cap, and they look a bit like ribbons or streamers rather than wind.
- The paint roller handle looks short and thin, and the elderly woman reaches out at an odd stiff angle. The roller head could also be read as a paintbrush.
- The boy's ball seems to be at his foot right now, so it could be just about to be kicked or just kicked. It is heading towards the right and the flower bed.
