# Picture 64

## Free description

This is a sunny playground on grass with a dark metal railing fence along the back. In the front left there is a red seesaw on a dark grey triangular support. A small girl in a pink dress sits on the raised end at the left, and an elderly man sits on the low end, so the seesaw is tilted down on his side. Because he is reading a book and not paying attention, the man does not look as if he is playing, and the girl is stuck up in the air with her feet dangling, with little curved lines by her feet suggesting she is kicking or swinging them. She is looking towards the viewer or at the man and looks a little surprised or fed up, as if she is waiting for him to notice her. An elderly woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt stands further back near the fence, eating a pink ice cream. On the right there is a tall red and orange slide, and in the front right there is a wooden sandpit with a red bucket and a blue spade in it, with nobody playing there. A green rubbish bin, a tree, a bush and a grey rock complete the scene.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The girl's expression and small motion lines can be read two ways: she may be happily swinging her feet, or she may be annoyed and stranded in the air because the man is not playing. I lean towards the second, but it is not certain.
- It is not clear whether the man is deliberately sitting on the seesaw to read, as if it were a bench, or is playing with the girl and has got distracted.
- The man's feet reach the ground, and his leg overlaps the plank, so his position on the seesaw looks slightly awkward. The low end of the plank passes behind and beside his lower leg rather than clearly under his bottom.
- The girl appears to sit very close to the end of the plank, with a small handle in front of her, and her body seems thin and slightly stiff against it.
- The woman's ice cream is held right against her mouth and is small, so it is partly hidden and could be mistaken for a nose or something else at first glance.
- Nothing appears to be floating or passing through anyone, apart from the slight overlap of the man's leg and the plank.
