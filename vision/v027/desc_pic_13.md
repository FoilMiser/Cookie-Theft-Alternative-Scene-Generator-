On the left is a red-framed slide with an orange and yellow chute coming down to the right and a ladder at the left; there is also a green bin, a small bush and a grey rock. At the front left is a square wooden sandpit, and a small girl with dark hair in a pink dress crouches at the back left corner of it, building a sandcastle with three towers and a tiny red flag on top. The boy is facing to the right, so the ball seems to be going right, away from the girl and her castle. On the right there is an empty wooden bench and a large tree. Neither child is looking at the other.
