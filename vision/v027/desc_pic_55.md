# Picture 55

## Free description

This is the back garden of a cream house with a red roof, but the weather is gloomy: the sky is grey and three dark storm clouds hang overhead, and there is no sun. Three people are in the garden. In the middle-right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is watering a flower bed with a hose, and a stream of water arcs from the nozzle onto the flowers. On the far right, an older woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a wooden bench drinking from a red cup through a straw. At the front left, a boy with dark skin in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands at the edge of the path, pointing up and to the right with one arm raised, looking upward. He may be pointing at the dark clouds, or at the tall birdhouse on a pole nearby. It looks as though it may be about to rain, which would make watering the flowers rather pointless. There is also a wheelbarrow, two potted plants, a fence, a gate and a hedge in the background.
