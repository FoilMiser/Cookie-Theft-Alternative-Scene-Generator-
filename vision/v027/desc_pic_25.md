# Picture 25

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a cream house with a red roof on the right, and a paved path on the left leading to a wooden gate. An old woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller or brush; one section of the fence is already blue, and a paint can and paint tray sit on the grass behind her. A white dog with brown spots is walking towards the path, and it has left a trail of blue paw prints leading away from the paint tray, so it has evidently stepped in the paint. A small girl in a pink dress is reaching up with one arm: she holds red and purple balloons while a blue balloon has got away and is rising beside the fence, just out of her reach. A man in a green shirt sits on a bench at the bottom right reading a red book, and he does not seem to notice anything. Several things are impossible or silly: a brown dog with a red collar or scarf is sitting up on the grass reading a newspaper like a person, and a big orange goldfish is sitting in the tree above the fence. A black cat is creeping along the top of the fence towards a red bird perched on the fence, so the cat looks about to pounce. A blue bird flies across the sky, an orange cat is asleep on the lawn, and a watering can and a wheelbarrow have been left out on the grass.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The blue balloon: it could have just slipped from the girl's hand, or she could still be attached to it. Its string curls down near her raised hand but does not visibly reach it, so I read it as escaping.
- The woman's tool could be a paint roller or a wide brush on a pole; the head is a blue rectangle.
- The cloth at the brown dog's neck could be a collar or a small scarf.
- The fish looks as if it is painted on or stuck to the tree rather than perched. It has no branch under it, and I cannot tell whether it is meant to be in the tree or floating in front of it.
- The fish in the tree and the newspaper-reading dog look deliberately absurd. Nothing else in the picture reacts to them.
- The paint can has a blue patch on it but it is unclear whether it is spilled or just its contents; the tray overlaps the can slightly.
- The man's head appears to overlap the house wall and the bench is partly cut against the lower edge of the door step, but it reads as depth rather than an error.
- The girl's arm holding the balloon strings looks thin and the strings gather loosely near the hand, so it is hard to see the actual grip.
