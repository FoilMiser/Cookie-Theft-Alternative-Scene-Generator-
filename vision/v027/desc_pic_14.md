# Picture 14

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate at the far left, a paved path on the left and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A cardboard box is hanging high up in the empty sky, above the fence and to the left of the tree, with nothing holding it up, and it is tilted as though it is falling or flying. On the grass in the middle there is another cardboard box, standing in a small puddle with blue water drips on its sides. A garden sprinkler at the end of a long dark hose throws three arcs of water to the left, and the highest arc lands on this box, so the box is getting wet; the two lower arcs land on the grass just in front of it. On the left, a boy in a red shirt and blue trousers stands at the edge of the path with a broom, sweeping up a pile of colourful autumn leaves, looking down at his work. He does not seem to notice the wet box or the box in the sky. Two potted plants stand at the back by the fence, and a flower bed sits in front of the house. The box in the sky looks impossible, unless it is in the middle of being thrown or dropped; nobody is there who could have thrown it.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The box in the sky could be read as falling, as thrown, as flying, or as simply floating. There is no motion line and no person or vehicle near it, so I cannot tell which. It overlaps nothing but sky and looks clearly separate from everything else.
- The two boxes look like the same kind of box, so one could be read as having fallen from the sky, or the sky box as about to land on the other one, but it is not directly above it.
- The wet box could be a parcel left out by mistake; the drips and puddle suggest it has been getting wet for a while.
- The broom handle passes in front of the boy's body and his hands seem to wrap around it plausibly, but the bristle head sits over the leaf pile and partly over his foot, so the broom and foot overlap.
- The boy's lower legs and foot look a little merged with the broom head and the shadow.
- The hose's end disappears at the right edge of the picture, so I cannot see where it is connected.
