# Picture 38

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden beside a cream house with a red roof and a blue door, with a tall wooden fence along the back and a gate on the right. At the back, a bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers is hanging laundry on a washing line strung between two posts; a pair of blue jeans is already pegged up and he is reaching up to peg a white T-shirt. In the front middle, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is digging with a spade, with a pile of dug-up brown earth next to the hole he is making. At the bottom right, a small girl in a pink dress stands on or beside a paved path and waves one arm high, with little motion lines beside her hand, as if saying hello to someone or trying to get attention. Nobody seems to be looking back at her: the boy is looking down at his digging and the man is looking up at the washing. There is also a flower bed in front of the house, an empty bench at bottom left, two potted plants and a tall birdhouse on a pole. Nothing in the picture looks impossible, and it seems to be an ordinary busy day in the garden.
