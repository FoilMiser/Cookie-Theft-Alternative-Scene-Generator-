# Picture 40

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path in the bottom right corner. A woman with long dark hair, in a purple top and teal trousers, stands with both arms stretched out to the right, about to catch a red ball that is in the air right by her hands. A dashed curved line runs from the ball back to a little girl in a pink dress, so it looks as if the girl has just thrown it to her. In front of the blue door, a tan dog with a white chest sits holding its own red lead in its mouth, as if it is asking to go for a walk. In the front of the lawn, a white dog with brown patches crouches with its front end down and its tail up and wagging, its nose right by a yellow-green tennis ball, ready to play. On the right, an old woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, is sweeping a pile of autumn-coloured leaves with a broom at the edge of the path. A teal watering can sits on the grass with nobody using it, and there is a flower bed full of colourful flowers near the house. Nothing looks clearly wrong or impossible; it seems like an ordinary busy garden scene.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Direction of the ball: the most likely reading is that the girl threw it and the woman is about to catch it. The other reading is that the woman has just thrown it and the dashed line shows where it will fall, down towards the girl.
- The girl's pose could be running towards the woman or just finishing a throw.
- The tan dog's lead seems to be both attached at the collar and held in its mouth; I read this as the dog wanting a walk, but it could simply be chewing it.
- Autumn leaves are being swept while the tree is fully green and the flowers are in bloom, which is slightly odd.
- The old woman has a thin white strip down the front of her top; it could be a scarf or part of her clothing.
- Nothing is floating or passing through anything, apart from the ball, which is explained by the dashed line.
