# Picture 66

## Free description

This is a sunny park with a pond at the right, a winding path at the left, and hills and hedges in the distance. A man in a red T-shirt and blue trousers stands at the front right, bending forward with a paper bag under one arm and sprinkling seeds or crumbs from his hand. The seeds fall in a line down to the grass, where a red bird and a blue bird are pecking at a small scatter of seed. Beside him a small girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes stands holding a yellow balloon by its string, and she appears to be looking towards the man or the birds. There is a wooden ladder standing or leaning upright on the grass near the centre-left, with nothing obvious to lean on, which looks odd. A tall lamp post stands just to its right but does not seem to touch it. Nobody is using the ladder. A green rubbish bin stands at the bottom right, and trees and bushes surround the scene.
