# Picture 11

## Free description

This is a sunny park with hills and bushes behind, a large oval pond in the middle-left with bulrushes at each end, a tall black street lamp, a wooden bench, a winding path on the right, trees, bushes, two green bins and a grey rock. An old man with grey hair, a blue-grey flat cap, a brown waistcoat over a white shirt and dark trousers stands at the front edge of the pond. He holds a paper bag and reaches out with his other hand, scattering pieces of bread or food. There is a single white duck on the pond right in front of him, swimming towards the food; two small white lines behind it suggest a wake. Pale pieces of food lie on the water near the duck, and some more lie on the grass below the pond edge. The man is the only person there, and the rest of the park is empty. Nothing seems about to go wrong; it is a quiet scene of a man feeding a duck.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The man's legs and feet are on grass but his upper body overlaps the pond, which is just how he stands in front of it; he could be read as standing at the bank, though he looks close to standing in the water as his body covers part of the pond.
- Some food pieces lie on grass rather than water; this could be food that missed the pond or a small stray scatter. The lower pieces are clearly below the pond line.
- The bag overlaps his body and the arm seems to come from the shoulder a bit stiffly; the bag could be read as hanging from his arm or as held in front of him.
- The duck's two white lines behind it could be a wake or could be read as small feet or tail marks; they fall on the edge of the pond and slightly out onto the bank.
- The duck's body has a pale outlined wing that looks like an oval shape; it reads as a wing but could look like a plate on its back.
