# Picture 37

## Free description

This is a sunny park with a blue pond, trees, hills, a winding path and a tall lamp post. A bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers stands in the middle at the edge of the pond and is fishing with a long rod. He holds the rod in both hands and points it out over the water to the left, and the line drops from the rod's tip to a red-and-white float sitting on the water, with a little ripple ring around it. The man looks towards the float and appears to be waiting calmly for a bite. He is the only person in the picture, and there are no animals. A bench, two green litter bins and a rock are nearby, all unused. Nothing odd or impossible stands out, though the man seems to be standing very close to, or almost in, the edge of the pond.
