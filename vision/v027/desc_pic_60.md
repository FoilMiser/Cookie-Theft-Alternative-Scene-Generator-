# Picture 60

## Free description

This is a sunny park with green hills in the background, a pond on the right, a winding path on the left, a tall black lamp post in the centre, and trees at both sides. There are two people in the picture. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands in front of the pond, holding a long fishing rod out over the water, with the line going down to a red-and-white float sitting in a ripple. He seems to be waiting for a bite. On the left, a woman with long dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers walks along the path to the left side of the picture, holding a pink ice cream cone up near her face with her eyes closed, enjoying it. They are not paying attention to each other. There are also green bins, some bushes, a flower bed at the lower right and a grey rock on the grass in front. Nothing strange or dramatic seems to be happening or about to happen.
