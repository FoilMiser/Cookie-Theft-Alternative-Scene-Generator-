# Picture 46

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence with a gate at the back left, and a paved path in the bottom left corner. A woman with long dark hair, in a purple top and teal trousers, stands on the lawn holding the end of a garden hose up in front of her with both hands, peering at the nozzle, with only one drop of water coming out. The reason seems to be that a white goat is standing on the hose further along, and just behind the goat the hose bulges fat, as if water is building up behind the blockage. It looks as if, once the goat steps off, the water will burst out and spray the woman in the face. A black and white cat is walking along the top of the fence near the tree. A washing line behind her has a yellow and red striped garment and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. Up in the sky on the left, a cardboard box hangs tilted in mid-air with nothing holding it up, which looks odd or impossible. A goat in a garden is also a little unexpected.
