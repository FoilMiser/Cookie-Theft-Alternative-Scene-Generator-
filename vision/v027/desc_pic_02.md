# Picture 02

## Free description
This is a back garden on a grey day, with three big dark storm clouds in the sky, so it looks as if it is about to rain. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, and a tall wooden fence with a gate runs across the back. In the middle-left a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is standing beside a round flower bed and pouring water onto the flowers from what looks like a frying pan or saucepan, not a watering can. That is the strangest thing in the picture: she is holding the pan out by its long handle and a stream of water is pouring out of it. On the right, a man or boy in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is standing on a paved path, looking up with a surprised face and pointing up at the sky, apparently at the dark clouds. A black and white cat is walking along the very top of the fence on the left, in front of a tree. It also seems a bit silly to be watering the flowers when it is about to rain. A wheelbarrow is standing empty on the grass near the front, and two potted plants stand by the fence.
