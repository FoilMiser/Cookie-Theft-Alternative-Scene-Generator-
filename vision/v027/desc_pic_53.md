# Picture 53

## Free description
This is a sunny paved town square or street, with a row of colourful two-storey buildings (pink, pale green, peach and cream) and shop awnings behind, a tall black lamp post, a fruit stall and a tree in a round planter. On the left, a man with dark skin and a beard, in a green T-shirt and tan trousers, walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. In the centre front, a small girl in a pink dress holds an empty ice-cream cone and rubs her eye, looking as if she is crying, because her pink ice cream with sprinkles has fallen on the ground and made a pink splat. A brown dog with a red collar is bending down right beside the splat, sniffing or about to eat the fallen ice cream. On the right, an elderly woman with a grey bun, in a teal top and grey skirt, is holding up a camera to her eye and taking a photo pointed towards the left side of the picture. Behind her at the far right, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts points up with an angry or upset face at a blue ball that is stuck in the branches of the tree. A red bicycle stands in the middle back, oddly with square wheels, and there is an empty bench next to it. There is also a flower box, a rubbish bin and a potted plant at the bottom right.
