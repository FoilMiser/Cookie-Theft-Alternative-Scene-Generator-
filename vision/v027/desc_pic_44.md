# Picture 44

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence with a gate at the back left, and a paved path in the bottom left corner. At the back, an old man in a blue-grey cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller, and he has already finished a section of a few boards. A tin of blue paint sits on the grass by his feet. In the middle front, a boy or young man in a red shirt and blue jeans is reading a red book while sitting in a chair-like pose, with his knees bent, but there is nothing under him, so he seems to be sitting on thin air. A wooden bench stands empty over on the far right. On the right, a person with short dark hair in a yellow dress and red shoes is watering the flower bed with a garden hose, and water arcs from the nozzle onto the flowers. The hose runs across the lawn from a reel at the edge of the flower bed. The person sitting on nothing is the one thing that looks impossible; everything else looks like an ordinary afternoon in the garden.

- Person in yellow, right of centre, middle distance: short dark hair, yellow belted dress, red shoes; holding a hose nozzle and spraying the flowers.

- Blue-painted section of fence: left of centre, about three boards wide, solid blue.
- Tin of blue paint with a handle: on the grass by the old man's feet.
- Red book: in the hands of the person in the seated pose.
- Garden hose and reel: dark teal hose on a reel at the right edge of the flower bed; the hose runs along the grass in a long loop and up to the nozzle.
- Arc of water with drops: from the nozzle down onto the flowers.
- Flower bed with red, orange, yellow, pink and purple flowers: right, by the house.
- Wooden bench: far right, front; empty.
- Bird house with a red roof on a tall pole: left, standing in front of the painted part of the fence; no bird visible.
- House with red roof, blue door and window: right. Tree and bush beside it. Wooden fence and gate: back left. Paved path: bottom left. Sun and two white clouds: in the sky.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The person reading: they could be sitting on an invisible seat, which looks impossible, or doing some kind of squat or leaning back. A person could not really hold this pose while reading comfortably. Their feet do seem to touch the ground, so they are not floating, and the shadow lies slightly to the right of their feet.
- The empty bench on the far right looks as though it might be where they ought to be sitting, but it is quite a long way from them.
- The hose goes up stiffly from the ground to the hand, almost like a pipe, and there is a small visible joint or gap near the top before the nozzle. It may just be a drawing detail.
- The bird house pole is directly in front of the left edge of the blue section. It looks as if the old man may have painted behind it, or the pole may just happen to be there.
- The paint tin looks very small for the amount of painting, though that does not really look wrong.
