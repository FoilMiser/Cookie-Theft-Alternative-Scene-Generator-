# Picture 18

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a cream house with a red roof and blue door at right, and a gate with a concrete path at left. A woman or girl with short dark hair, in a yellow dress and red shoes, stands in the middle right of the garden holding a hose, and water arcs from the nozzle down onto a round flower bed of colourful flowers. The hose runs along the grass from a teal hose reel next to the flower bed. In the front of the picture a small white puppy with brown markings is curled up or lying on the grass with its eyes closed, apparently asleep, next to a red bowl of dog food. An empty swing set stands at left of centre, and the swing is not moving. The odd thing is an orange goldfish in the tree at top right: it sits in the middle of the green leaves, well above the ground, where a fish could not be. Nobody reacts to the fish; the person just keeps watering.

## Inventory
People and animals:
- Woman or girl, centre right: short dark hair, yellow dress, red shoes; standing and watering the flowers with a hose.
- Puppy, centre foreground: small, white with brown patches, lying curled on the grass, eyes closed, seems asleep, in a dark oval of shadow.
- Orange fish, upper right: goldfish-like, in the tree's leaves, not moving, looks out of place.

Objects:
- Hose and reel, right: teal hose running from a reel on a dark base round to the person's hand; the yellow nozzle sprays water.
- Water, centre right: arc of blue water with droplets falling on the flowers.
- Flower bed, right: round soil bed with red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers.
- Dog bowl, centre foreground: red bowl filled with brown kibble, on the grass close to the puppy.
- Swing set, left of centre: dark blue-grey A-frame with one red seat, empty and hanging still.
- House, right: cream walls, red roof, blue door with a step, one window.
- Fence and gate, back and far left: wooden picket fence, with a brown gate at the left that has a round knob and a diagonal brace.
- Path, left foreground: pale concrete path leading from the gate to the front of the picture.
- Tree and bushes: tree behind the fence at right, green hedge line behind the fence, bush by the house.
- Sun and clouds: yellow sun with orange rays at top left, two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fish in the tree is clearly strange. It could be read as a fish impossibly stuck in the tree, or as a flat decoration or balloon hanging from the tree. It does not look attached to anything.
- The puppy's shadow is a very wide oval, much wider than the puppy, and looks a little like a patch or hole on the grass.
- The puppy's pose is compact and hard to read; it could be sleeping, or sniffing the ground with its head down.
- The person could be read as a woman or a girl; the yellow garment looks like a dress with a belt, but the skirt part is narrow and lumpy.
- The hose seems to rise from the ground up to the hand at a sharp angle near the person's body, so it looks a bit stiff where it meets the hand.
