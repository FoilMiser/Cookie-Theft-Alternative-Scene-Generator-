# Picture 49

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills and hedges in the background, a pond on the right with a white duck on it, a winding sandy path on the left, trees, a tall black lamp post and a green rubbish bin. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and round glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, walks along near the path at the front left carrying a yellow shopping bag. A small girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench in the middle, reading a red book. A woman or older girl in a yellow outfit and red shoes is on the right in front of the pond, reading a red book too, but she looks as though she is sitting on nothing: there is no chair or bench under her, and her body is in a seated pose in mid-air. At the bottom centre, a garden sprinkler at the end of a long blue-green hose is sending arcs of water to the left, and the water is falling directly onto a cardboard box on the grass, which has a puddle around it. So the box looks like it is getting soaked, and nobody has noticed. There is a round flower bed at the front right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The person in yellow is the strangest thing: she seems to be sitting in mid-air with no chair, bench or rock under her. It could be read as an impossible floating sitter, or as a mistake where a seat is missing. Her shadow on the grass is below her feet.
- The same person could be an adult woman or a teenage boy or girl; the short hair and yellow outfit make it hard to say. The yellow outfit could be a dress or a top with shorts.
- The cardboard box getting wet could be the point of the picture (a parcel being ruined) or just a coincidence of where the sprinkler landed. Water also seems to run down the sides of the box.
- The sprinkler arcs could be read as spraying onto the box deliberately, or simply reaching that far.
- The elderly woman's feet are placed a bit oddly: she is half on the path and half on the grass, and her walking pose looks stiff, with the bag hanging in front of her legs.
- Both the girl and the person in yellow have nearly the same red book, which could be a coincidence or could mean something.
