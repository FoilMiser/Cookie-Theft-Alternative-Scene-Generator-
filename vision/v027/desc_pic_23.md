# Picture 23

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a house and red roof at left, a wooden fence along the back, a gate and concrete path at right, and a flower bed by the house. A bearded man with dark skin, in a green shirt and tan trousers, stands by the flower bed holding a teal watering can. The water is odd: instead of falling on the flowers, it arcs upward and backwards from the can to above the bed, with droplets at the top, so it seems to be going up in the air, which looks impossible or at least wrong. A child with dark skin and braided hair, in a yellow shirt and orange shorts, stands near the fence with one arm up, holding a long string that runs up and across to a purple kite that has got tangled in the leaves of the tree at upper left. The kite is stuck, so the child seems to be trying to free it or pull it down. In the foreground, a woman or girl with short dark hair, in a yellow dress and red shoes, is digging with a shovel, pushing dirt into or out of a hole next to a mound of soil. Two potted green plants and a bush sit on the grass at right. Everyone is busy with their own task and nobody is looking at anyone else.
