# Picture 39

## Free description

This is a sunny town square paved with stone slabs, in front of a row of colourful shops with striped awnings. In the middle, a small girl in a pink dress is holding the strings of a purple balloon and a yellow balloon in one hand and reaching up with her other hand towards a red balloon that has got away and is floating out of her reach, with its string dangling. To the right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a small yellow balloon on a string and looks to the left, towards the girl. On the far right, an older man in a flat cap, white shirt and dark trousers stands and waves one raised arm, with little motion marks around his hand. On the left, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes walks along and eats a pink ice cream, in front of a market stall with crates of fruit and vegetables. The red balloon seems about to float away for good. Nothing in the picture looks impossible.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The man's wave: he could be waving to the boy or girl, to someone off the picture, or to the viewer. His gaze goes to the left, so he may be greeting the children. Another reading is that he is trying to catch the balloon or warn about it, but his hand is nowhere near it.
- The red balloon: it most likely slipped from the girl's hand, as her empty hand is raised towards it and its string dangles free, but it could also be that she is just pointing at it or that it is someone else's balloon.
- The boy could be watching the girl, or could be looking at the red balloon. He does not seem to be reacting strongly.
- The boy's yellow balloon string appears to run down past his face and to his hand, and it overlaps his head slightly.
- The balloon strings held by the girl spread outward and meet at her hand; the strings look stiff, but they read clearly.
- The woman with her eyes closed while walking could be enjoying her ice cream or simply not looking where she is going.
