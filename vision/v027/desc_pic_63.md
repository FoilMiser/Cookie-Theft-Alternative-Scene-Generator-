# Picture 63

## Free description

This is a sunny park with a pond, a winding path, hills and hedges in the distance. A brown dog with a red scarf or collar sits on the grass at the front of the picture holding an open newspaper with its front paws, as if reading it like a person, which is the impossible or silly thing in the scene. On the left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie waters a bed of bright flowers with a teal watering can. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt stands beside the pond holding a paper bag and throwing crumbs towards a white duck, which is swimming over towards the food. Some of the crumbs are in the water and some have landed on the grass. A man in a red T-shirt and blue trousers walks along at the back right carrying a cardboard box in both arms. In the middle right, a white goat with dark horns has its head down eating a tuft of grass beside the path. On the right, a man in an orange cap and an orange high-visibility vest digs with a spade, and a pile of dirt is heaped beside the hole. A lamp post and a green bin stand in the centre behind everyone. No one is paying attention to anyone else.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The dog could be read as a pet just holding up or playing with a newspaper, or as truly reading it like a human. It is also possible the newspaper is the only odd item and it has been brought to the dog by someone. Its pose looks intended as person-like.
- The crumbs on the grass could be food that fell short of the pond, or they could be small pebbles or some other thing. They are the same colour and shape as those in the water.
- The woman's bag hides part of her hand, so it could be read as her holding the bag at her waist with one hand and throwing with the other. Her left hand extending out looks like the throwing hand.
- The goat's head is close to its front legs and its neck looks oddly short and bent. There is a small dark marking on the head that could be a horn or an ear, so the head is a bit hard to read.
- The digging man's spade blade sits partly in the hole and partly in a mound of dirt, with a blue-grey blade visible. It is not fully clear whether he is pushing the spade in or lifting soil out.
- The flower bed and water drops overlap a bit near the can, so the stream looks as if it is falling on the flower stems rather than from above them.
- Nothing appears to be floating in a clearly unintended way, and no one is passing through an object.
