# Picture 41

## Free description

This is a sunny playground or park, with a grassy lawn, a dark metal railing fence across the back, a slide on the left and a large tree on the right. An old man in a blue-grey cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers is sitting on the low end of a red seesaw, reading an open book. At the other, high end of the seesaw sits a little girl in a pink dress, stuck up in the air, with her legs dangling and small curved lines by her feet as if she is kicking them. She has her mouth open and looks surprised or upset, while the man is absorbed in his book and doesn't seem to notice her. Because he is so much heavier and is not getting up, it looks as if she can't get down. An old woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, walks across the middle of the lawn to the right, holding a pink ice cream up to her mouth. Nobody is using the slide, and nothing else is going on. Apart from the girl being stranded, nothing looks impossible.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The girl could be upset and stuck because the man is too heavy, or she could just be playing and enjoying being up high; her open mouth reads more like surprise or worry.
- The curved lines under her feet could mean kicking or wriggling, or could show that the seat is bobbing up and down.
- The old woman's hand hides the lower part of what she holds, so it could be an ice cream cone or a pink lollipop.
- She has a thin white strip down the front of her top; it could be a scarf or part of her clothes, or something tucked under her arm.
- The old man's lower hand sits very near the seesaw beam, so he may be holding the book with one hand and the seesaw with the other. His legs and the beam overlap closely, which is a little hard to read.
- It looks a bit odd for an adult to be sitting on a seesaw reading, but it does not look like a drawing mistake. Nothing is floating or passing through anything.
