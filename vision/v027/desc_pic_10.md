# Picture 10

## Free description

This is a sunny park with green hills and a line of bushes behind, a large oval pond in the middle-left with bulrushes at each end, a tall black street lamp in the centre, a wooden bench, and a winding path running down the right side past a large tree. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, round dark glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt kneels on one knee at the front left, beside a flower bed and a pot with a pink plant; she seems to be planting or working in the soil with a small hand tool. In the front middle, a white goat with a dark horn has its head right down, eating a small tuft of grass. On the right, a boy or young man in a red shirt and blue trousers stands on the edge of the path holding a brown paper bag and scattering small seeds from his hand. The seeds fall in a line down to the path, where a red bird and a blue bird are pecking at them. Nobody seems to notice the goat, and the goat is not close to the flowers. Nothing looks about to go badly wrong; it is a calm scene.

- The woman's tool is very thin and hard to make out. It could be a small trowel, a dibber, or a stick; she could be planting the pink plant from the pot or digging a hole next to it.
- The pink plant in the pot could be a flower or a clump of pink leaves; it looks a bit like a tulip head or a hand.
- The goat's head is turned so that it is hard to read: there is a dark curved horn pointing up on the right and the face is pressed down; it could be read as a sheep or a kid at first glance. Its legs look stiff and its body is angled sharply downward.
- The man's hand with the seeds and the falling seeds make a neat line; it could be read as pouring or as the seeds dropping from a hole in the bag, but the hand position suggests sprinkling on purpose.
- The bag looks like it overlaps his body and hip, a little flat against him, as though not quite held.
- The man's feet are on the grass but the seeds and birds are on the path; the man stands right at the path's edge so this reads as normal.
