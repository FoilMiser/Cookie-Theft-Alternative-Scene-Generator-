His shadow is below him on the pavement, so he is jumping, and his closed eyes and big smile show that he is delighted or cheering. To his right a man in a red T-shirt and blue trousers is running to the right, with speed lines behind him showing that he is moving fast. He seems to be running out of the picture away from the boy, or perhaps past him. High up in the sky a red bird flies near a cloud. A fruit and vegetable stall with a striped awning stands at the back left, in front of the shops, with four crates of fruit on it and no one tending it. A lamp post, a rubbish bin, a potted plant and a tree in a round planter complete the scene. Nothing appears to have gone wrong, and the two people are not clearly connected, though the boy could be cheering the runner on.
