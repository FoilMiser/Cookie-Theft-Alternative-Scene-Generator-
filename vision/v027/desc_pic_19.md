# Picture 19

## Free description
This is a sunny but windy back garden with a house and red roof at left, a wooden fence along the back, a swing set and a gate at right. A man or older boy with short brown hair, in a red shirt, blue trousers and dark shoes, stands in the middle with both arms raised high, looking up in surprise with his eyes wide. His red baseball cap is blowing away in the air just above and to the right of him, along with a few leaves, and white wind lines sweep across from the left; he seems to be trying to catch it. The wind is also visible higher up, where leaves are coming off the tree and there are more white wind lines in the sky. The swing seat at right is tilted, swinging slightly in the wind. At left, a man with dark skin and a beard, in a green shirt and tan trousers, sits on a wooden bench reading a red book and does not seem to notice anything. In the front, a small tan and white dog lies curled on the grass with its eyes closed, apparently asleep. A hose reel and a flower bed sit by the house.
