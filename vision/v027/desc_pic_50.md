# Picture 50

## Free description
This is a sunny park with hills and hedges behind, a pond on the left with a white duck, a winding sandy path on the right, trees, a tall lamp post and a green rubbish bin. An older man in a grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands in the middle by the pond, holding a long fishing rod out over the water, with the line running down to a red-and-white float in a ring of ripples. A man with dark skin and a short beard, in a green T-shirt and tan trousers, sits on a wooden bench eating a pink ice-cream cone. A brown dog with a red neckerchief sits up on its hind legs right beside him, begging with its front paws raised and looking up at the ice cream, so it seems the dog wants a lick or may be about to grab it. On the far right, a delivery person in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest with blue trousers strides along carrying a cardboard parcel, heading towards the left. There is a round flower bed at the front left, another rubbish bin in the bottom left corner, and a grey rock at the front centre. Nothing looks clearly impossible, though the duck is near the fishing float and the rod reaches a long way across the pond.
