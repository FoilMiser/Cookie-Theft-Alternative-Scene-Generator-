# Picture 05

## Free description
This is a busy back garden on a sunny day, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right and a tall wooden fence with a gate along the back. Five adults and a child are all doing different things. In the front left an older man in a cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is on the path sweeping a pile of dirt towards a red dustpan, but he is using a huge silver fork as if it were a broom, which is plainly wrong and funny. Behind him, a man in a red T-shirt and jeans is painting the fence blue with a brush, and he has already painted part of it; there is a paint pot by his feet. In the centre back, a little girl in a pink dress has just thrown a red and white ball, whose path is shown by a dotted line going up, and a bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers has his hands up ready to catch it. On the right an older woman with grey hair and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, is watering the round flower bed with a green watering can. In the front centre a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is walking to the right while reading an open book. She is not looking where she is going, and a blue bucket is just ahead of her on the grass, so she looks as if she might walk into it or trip over it. A red bird stands on top of the fence above the catching man.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The silver thing the older man holds is shaped like a fork; it could be meant as a garden fork or a giant table fork. It looks much more like a table fork, and it is far too big and is being used to sweep. It could also be read as a strangely drawn broom, but I think it is a fork.
- The ball's path could be read two ways: the dotted line goes from the girl's hand up to the ball, so she threw it towards the bearded man, but the ball looks too high and could also be heading over the fence and past him.
- The man painting the fence has the brush handle in his hand, but the blue head at the top looks more like a big blue block or roller, and it seems to stick up above the fence, so I can't tell exactly what tool it is.
- The little girl seems to be drawn very small compared with the adults, and her arm is out towards the ball, so she might be throwing it or reaching after it.
- The reading woman could be about to walk into the bucket, or she could be passing beside it.
- The man painting seems to be standing a little away from the fence for the length of his arm, and the blue paint panel looks like a flat block rather than brush strokes.
- The dust cloud looks soft and pale compared with everything else, and sits partly over the path and grass, but it reads as dust.
