# Picture 33

## Free description

This is a sunny playground with a railing fence along the back, a tree on the left, a swing set in the middle and a red-framed slide on the right. On the left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue ball that is stuck on a branch in the tree. An older man in a flat cap sits on a bench reading a red book, and a small blue bird on the bench beside him is going for a sandwich that lies next to him; he does not seem to notice. A woman in a yellow dress stands behind the swings taking a photo with a camera, aimed up and to the left. In the front middle, a small girl in a pink dress is rubbing her eye as if crying and holds an empty ice-cream cone in her other hand; the scoop has fallen on the ground in a pink splat, and a brown dog with a red collar is sniffing or licking it. To the right, an orange cat lies curled up asleep on the grass. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts kneels in a sandbox building a sandcastle with a little flag on top. On the far right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands with her hands on her hips and a white bucket upside down on her head, which is the odd thing in the picture. A red bird flies at top left, and a red bicycle with black, square wheels lies at the front left, which is also clearly wrong since square wheels could not roll.
