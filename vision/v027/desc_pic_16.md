# Picture 16

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate at the far right and a paved path on the right. In the middle front, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes holds up the nozzle of a dark teal hose with both hands, looking down into the end of it, and a single drop of water is at the tip; nothing is coming out. A white goat with a dark horn is standing with its feet on the hose behind her, and there is a big bulge in the hose on the near side of the goat, between the goat and the hose reel, showing that the water is blocked. So the hose has been stopped by the goat standing on it, and the woman is peering at the nozzle to find out why; if the goat moves, the water could squirt straight into her face. Behind them, a man with dark skin, a beard, a green shirt and tan trousers has his hands out, and a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers has just thrown a red ball to him; a dashed line shows the ball's path through the air and the ball is close to the man's hands. On the right, a small girl in a pink dress sits on a red swing on a dark metal frame, smiling. A flower bed, a grey wheelbarrow and the hose reel are also there.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The woman's pose could be read as looking into the nozzle or as simply holding it out in front of her face; the nozzle is close to her eyes and her head tilts down at it, so peering seems the better reading. The front of her face is hidden by her own arm and hair, which makes her look a little odd, as though the head is on backwards or turned away.
- The drop near her hands could be water dripping from the nozzle or could be taken for something she is holding.
- The goat could be read as deliberately standing on the hose or just happening to stand there. The hose passes under its feet, but looks close to passing through or behind the back foot.
- The bulge could be read as a blockage, as a lump in the hose, or as an extra object lying on it, like a dark blue fish or bag. It is on the reel side of the goat, which fits water being stopped there.
- The ball is at the man's hands but has not clearly reached them; the ball is slightly above and to the right, so it is about to be caught. The man's clasped hands and the ball are close enough that it could be read as just caught or just missing him.
- The man's eyes look wide and light, which could be glasses; hard to tell.
- The girl's swing ropes are thin and pass close to the swing frame, and the girl's hands seem to hold the ropes, though the hands and ropes run together. The two lines at the right of the swing suggest it is moving, though she sits straight.
