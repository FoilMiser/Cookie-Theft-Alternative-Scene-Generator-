# Picture 57

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and blue door. On the right, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is standing beside a flower bed, holding a dark round object out in one hand by a short handle, and water is pouring out of it onto the flowers. The object looks like a frying pan or saucepan rather than a watering can, which is the odd thing about the scene: he is watering the flowers with a pan. On the left, a small child in pink with dark hair sits on the red seat of a blue-grey swing set, smiling with eyes closed, and small motion lines beside the swing suggest that it is swinging. At the back, an orange cat is walking along the top of the wooden fence, heading left. A grey wheelbarrow stands in the foreground, empty. The sky is blue with the sun at top left and two small white clouds. Nothing seems about to go badly wrong, though the cat is high up on the fence.
