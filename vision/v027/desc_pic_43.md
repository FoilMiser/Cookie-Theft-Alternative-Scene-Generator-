# Picture 43

## Free description

This is a back garden under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds and no sun, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence and gate at the back, and a paved path on the right. It looks as though it is about to rain, although no rain is falling yet. In the middle, a person with brown hair in a red shirt and blue trousers is reaching up to peg a white T-shirt on a washing line, next to a pair of blue jeans that is already hanging there. On the right, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands on the path with his arm raised and his finger pointing up, apparently at the storm clouds, and may be warning the others. At the front left, a person with short dark hair in a yellow dress and red shoes is holding a teal watering can by the flower bed. The odd thing is that the water leaves the spout low down and curves upwards in an arc, with drops at the top, so it is going up instead of pouring down. The washing is likely to get soaked if it rains, and watering the flowers just before a storm seems pointless. An empty wheelbarrow, two potted plants and a bird house on a tall pole complete the scene.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The water going up: it could be deliberately impossible, with water defying gravity, or it could be a drawing mistake meant to be water pouring onto the flowers. The drops above the top of the arc seem to go up too.
- The bearded man could be pointing at the storm clouds, at the bird house, or at something in the distance. His raised finger points up and to the left, so the clouds seem most likely, but the bird house is also in that direction.
- The person hanging the washing could be a man or an older boy, and the person in yellow could be a woman or an older girl; the drawing does not make this clear.
- The person in yellow is drawn with the head tilted down and somewhat awkwardly placed on the neck, and the watering can is small and partly tucked against the dress, so it is not obvious at first what they are holding.
- It is not clear whether the people have noticed the clouds, apart from the man pointing.
