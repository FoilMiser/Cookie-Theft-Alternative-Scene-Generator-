# Picture 61

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, and a tall wooden fence with a gate runs across the back. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie kneels on one knee beside a flower bed, and he seems to be putting a potted pink plant into or next to the bed, or tending to it. A man in a red T-shirt and blue trousers stands near the fence holding a long rake, raking a small pile of leaves on the grass. An empty swing set with a red seat stands right of centre. In the front of the picture a garden hose runs along the ground from the left to a sprinkler, whose water arcs go to the right. The arcs of water land on or near a cardboard box sitting on the lawn, and the box looks wet, with drips running down it and a puddle under it. So the odd thing is that a cardboard box has been left out where the sprinkler is soaking it, and nobody seems to have noticed. A birdhouse on a tall pole stands near the gate, and a paved path leads from the gate towards the front right corner.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The boy's action could be read two ways: he may be planting or placing the pink plant from the pot into the bed, or just tending the flowers while the pot happens to stand there. His hand is close to the pot and the flower stems.
- The box could be read as a deliberate part of the story (a parcel left out and being ruined by the sprinkler) or simply as something the water happens to reach. Why it is on the lawn is not clear.
- The sprinkler has three main arcs. Two land on the grass short of the box and only the longest appears to reach it, so it is the drips and puddle on the box that make it look wet, more than the arc itself.
- The swing set's rear legs and hanging ropes overlap the fence lines, which makes the area behind it slightly busy to read but not wrong.
- The rake's lower end sits in the leaves right at the man's feet, and the handle crosses in front of his body. This looks normal but is tight.
- Nothing appears to be floating or passing through anyone.
