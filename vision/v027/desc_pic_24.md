# Picture 24

## Free description
This is a sunny park with a winding path at left, a tall lamppost near the middle and a blue pond at right. A child with dark skin and braided hair, in a yellow shirt and orange shorts, stands at the edge of the pond holding a small brown paper bag and reaching out with one hand, tossing small pale crumbs into the water near the bank. A white duck with an orange bill is swimming towards the crumbs, with white wake lines trailing behind it, so it is about to eat them. Further left, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes walks towards the viewer on the grass beside the path, eating a pink ice cream cone with his eyes closed. A green trash bin stands right beside the lamppost, and a second green bin sits at the far right foreground. A grey rock is in the foreground, and there are trees and bushes around. It is a calm picture and nothing looks impossible.
