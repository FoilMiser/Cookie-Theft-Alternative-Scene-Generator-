# Picture 20

## Free description
This is a sunny park with a sun at top left, a tall lamppost in the middle, a winding path at left and a blue pond at right. A woman with short dark hair, in a yellow dress and red shoes, is running across the foreground at far left, moving away towards the left, with short speed lines behind her. Just behind her, a man with dark skin and a beard, in a green shirt and tan trousers, is walking and pushing a teal and blue baby stroller. A baby with its eyes closed lies in the stroller, tucked under a pink blanket with white dots. To the right, a man or older boy with short brown hair, in a red shirt and blue trousers, sits on a red and white checked picnic blanket with a picnic basket in front of him, leaning back and looking towards the left and smiling. The runner seems to be going fast and has got ahead of the man with the stroller, so she may be out jogging; she does not seem to be with anyone. There is a flower bed, a green bin and a grey rock around the scene, and the pond is empty. Nothing seems impossible.
