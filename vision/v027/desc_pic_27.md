This is a paved town square on a sunny day, with a row of four coloured shop buildings behind it, each with a striped awning. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is walking on the left, carrying a yellow shopping bag, and she is near a tree in a raised planter. In the middle, a small girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench drinking from a cup. In front of her, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is walking along while reading an open book held out in front of him, not looking where he is going. On the right, an older man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat stands near a fruit stall, lifting a camera to his face and taking a photograph, aiming towards the left of the picture. A blue bucket and a dark grey litter bin stand on the paving near the boy, and the bucket is rather in his path, so he might bump into or trip over something. A green bicycle is parked in a stand behind the bench. Nothing looks impossible; it reads as an ordinary street scene.
