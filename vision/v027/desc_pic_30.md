# Picture 30

## Free description

This is a sunny park with green hills in the distance, a pond on the left, a large tree on the right, a tall black lamp post and a winding sandy path. At the front, an older man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is walking while pushing a teal pram with a sleeping baby tucked under a pink spotted blanket. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands on the path looking up with one arm raised, holding the string of a purple kite, but the kite is stuck in the big tree, tangled in the leaves. In the distance, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is walking while carrying a cardboard box in her arms. A small white dog with brown patches is curled up and asleep on the grass in front of the bench. A round flower bed is at the bottom left, and a grey rock lies on the grass near the man. The empty bench stands beside the path. The man looks as though he might keep walking past the rock, and the boy is unlikely to get his kite back without help.

- Boy, right, on the path: red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers, white shoes; standing with his head tilted back and one arm raised, holding a kite string.
- Woman, back right of centre: short dark hair, yellow dress, red shoes; walking to the right while carrying a cardboard box in front of her.
- Small dog, centre right, on the grass: white with brown patches; curled up asleep with its eyes closed.
- Pram, front centre: teal body, dark blue hood, black wheels and handle, with the baby inside.
- Pink blanket, front centre, in the pram: pink with white dots, covering the baby.

- Bench, right of centre: wooden and empty.
- Lamp post, centre: tall and black with a lantern on top.
- Flower bed, front left: round bed of mixed coloured flowers.
- Grey rock, front centre: smooth rounded stone on the grass, close to the man's feet.
- Trees and bushes: a large tree on the right, a smaller one on the left, and a bush behind the pond near the lamp post.
- Sun and clouds, top: bright sun upper right and two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The kite could be stuck in the tree or just flying behind it. I read it as stuck, because clumps of leaves sit on top of it.
- The rock could be something the man is about to trip over or it could be just part of the scenery; it is near but slightly to the side of his path.
- The woman is on the grass rather than on the path, and she is drawn rather large for how far back she seems to be. She also looks a little as if she is walking up the hill.
- The boy stands on the path, but one of his legs and the path edge are close together; his raised arm crosses the path curve, and it reads as normal overlap.
- The string makes a large loose loop down near the boy's hand before going up to the kite, so it looks slack rather than tight.
- The dog's head and body form a small round shape; it could be read as lying down or as a curled-up puppy.
