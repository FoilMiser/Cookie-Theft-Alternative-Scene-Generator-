# Picture 29

## Free description

This is a sunny but windy back garden, with white wind lines drawn across the sky, a cream house with a red roof on the right, a wooden fence behind, and a paved path on the left leading to a gate. A man or boy in a red shirt and blue trousers stands on the path with both arms up and his mouth open, looking surprised, while his red cap blows off his head and sails up and away, along with a few leaves. In the middle, a girl with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers holds the end of a green garden hose in both hands and looks at the nozzle, with a single drop of water at its tip. Nothing is coming out of the hose because the white spotted dog is standing on it, and the hose has a big bulge between the dog and the hose reel, so water seems to be trapped behind the dog's feet. If the dog moves, the water will probably burst out and could spray the girl in the face. Beside her, a boy in a yellow shirt and shorts sits reading a red book, but there is no chair under him; he looks as though he is sitting on thin air. A washing line between two posts holds a yellow striped towel and a pair of blue trousers, both blowing in the wind.

## Inventory

- Man or boy, front left, on the path: brown hair, red shirt, blue trousers, dark shoes; standing with both arms raised and mouth open, reaching up after a cap blown away.
- Boy, front centre: short dark hair, yellow shirt and shorts, red shoes; in a sitting position with knees bent, reading a red book, with nothing visible under him.
- Girl, centre: dark ponytail, purple top, teal trousers, dark shoes; standing, holding up the hose nozzle in both hands and peering into it.
- Spotted dog, front right of centre: white with brown spots, blue collar, tongue out; standing with its feet on the hose.
- Red cap, far left, in the air above the man: blown off his head by the wind.
- Leaves, far left, in the air: a few small leaves blowing past the cap.
- Wind lines, across the sky and at the far left: white wavy lines showing a strong breeze.
- Garden hose, front centre to right: dark teal, running from a reel by the flower bed, under the dog and up to the girl's hands, with a large swollen bulge just past the dog's feet.
- Water drop, centre: a single drop at the nozzle in the girl's hands.
- Hose reel, right: round and dark teal, near the flower bed.
- Washing line, centre back: rope between two wooden posts, with a yellow and red striped towel and blue trousers pegged up and blowing sideways.
- Red book, front centre: open in the seated boy's hands.
- Bird house on a pole, left of centre: red roof, round hole, standing in front of the fence.
- Flower bed, right: round bed of mixed coloured flowers in front of the house.
- House, right: cream walls, red roof, blue door, one window.
- Wooden gate, back left: at the top of the path, directly behind the man.
- Sun and clouds, top: bright sun upper left and two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The seated boy: either he is sitting on an invisible seat, which looks impossible, or a chair or stool has been left out of the drawing by mistake. He casts a shadow on the grass under him as if he were floating just above it.
- The hose bulge sits on the reel side of the dog, near its feet. It could be water building up behind a blockage or simply a swollen section of hose; the dog does not seem to notice.
- The figure on the path could be a tall boy or a young man.
- The cap may belong to the figure on the path, which is what I assume, but it is not on his head and he has no other hat. It could also just be passing by on the wind.
- The man's head and arms overlap the wooden gate and its dark round knob, which sits right beside his head and looks a bit like part of him.
- The hose passes close by the girl's legs without touching them; she appears to stand beside it.
- It is odd that the washing and cap are blowing hard, yet the tree, the bushes and the flowers look perfectly still.
