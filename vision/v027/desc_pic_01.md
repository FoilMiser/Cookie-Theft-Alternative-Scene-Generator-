# Picture 01

## Free description
This is a sunny day at a playground on green grass, with a dark metal railing fence and a line of bushes behind it. In the front centre a woman or older girl with a long dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is walking to the left while holding up a pink ice-cream cone close to her mouth, as if about to lick it. To the right of her, two small children are sitting on a red seesaw: a little girl in a pink dress on the left end and a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts on the right end. The seesaw is tilted only very slightly, with the girl's end a little higher or roughly level, even though the two children look about the same size. Each child seems to be holding a black handle in front of them. On the left there is a tall slide with a red frame, a grey ladder and a yellow and orange chute; nobody is on it. Nothing dramatic seems about to happen; it looks like an ordinary, calm moment. The oddest thing is that the boy's feet reach down and appear to touch or nearly touch the ground while the girl's feet dangle above the grass.
