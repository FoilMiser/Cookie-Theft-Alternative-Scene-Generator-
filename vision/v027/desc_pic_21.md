# Picture 21

## Free description
This is a busy sunny park with a pond at right, a winding path at left and a tall lamppost in the middle. A small girl in a pink dress at far left stands pointing up at a big blue and white ball that is stuck on a branch among the leaves of the large tree; it looks as if it got caught up there. An older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, walks along the path carrying a yellow shopping bag, while a white dog with brown ears and a blue bandana sits up beside her on the path, begging on its hind legs. A man or boy in a red shirt and blue trousers sits on a bench eating a pink ice cream cone with his eyes closed, and a woman in a yellow dress stands by the lamppost, also eating a pink ice cream. A white goat with a dark horn bends down in the middle eating a tuft of grass. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers walks at centre right with his head down, reading an open book, and there is a blue bucket right ahead of him, so he may be about to walk into or trip over it. At far right, a brown dog with a red bandana sits upright like a person reading an open newspaper held in its paws, which is impossible and funny. Behind them, a white duck floats on the pond, and on the far path a red bird stands on the ground with an orange cat behind it, crouched and creeping or stalking towards the bird, so the cat may be about to pounce.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The blue bucket could be an ordinary object left on the grass, or a trip hazard the reading boy is about to hit. He is headed roughly past it, slightly to its right, so it may not be in his way at all.
- The cat and bird: the cat may be stalking the bird, or just running along the grass behind it in a different direction. Its body stretches long and thin with a long tail.
- The bird stands right at the top of the path, close to the tree, and could be read as sitting on the path or on the grass edge.
- The white dog next to the older woman could be hers, begging from her bag, or could be doing its own trick. There is no lead. Its pose is thin and upright and it looks rather like a tall stretched shape.
- The goat's head looks twisted or low with a single dark curved horn, so it could be read as a sheep or a dog at first glance.
- The brown dog's arms hold the newspaper in a human way; I read this as a deliberately impossible thing, but the dog's body and legs look a bit bent and awkward.
- The ball in the tree could be seen as a blue planet-like sphere because of its white stripe, and there is a cluster of small leaves around it like a nest. Lighter leaf clumps on the tree look like small bushes stuck in the foliage.
- The woman by the lamppost stands close to the pole and looks a bit tall and narrow.
