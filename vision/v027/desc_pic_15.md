# Picture 15

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate at the far right and a paved path on the right. An orange fish is in the tree beside the house, sitting among the green leaves, which is impossible; it looks like a goldfish drawn flat on the foliage. In the centre, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands at the fence painting it with a long-handled roller, with a section already painted blue. By his feet is a paint tin and a shallow paint tray on the grass with blue paint in it. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is walking away from the paint tray towards the left, and it has left a trail of blue paw prints on the grass, with its paws also blue. A second dog, brown and white, sits at the far left in front of the house door holding a red lead in its mouth, with the lead trailing to a loop on the grass. The painting boy has his back to the spotted dog and does not seem to have noticed the paw prints. There is also a flower bed, a green hose reel, two potted plants, a wheelbarrow and a green watering can, none of them being used.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The fish in the tree could be read as an impossible picture element, as a toy or decoration hung in the tree, or as a stray piece of the drawing. It has no strings or support and is flat, so it seems to be simply placed in the tree.
- The spotted dog could be read as having stepped in the paint tray and walked away from it, which the prints and blue paws suggest. The prints start at the tray and end at the dog, so it is walking from right to left; the last print seems to sit just behind or under its back foot.
- The paint tray seems small for the roller, and the tin stands partly behind it, so the two objects overlap.
- The roller head and the blue section touch; the head overlaps the top of the blue part, and it looks like he is painting towards the right from the left edge of the blue area, though the unpainted part is on his left, so it is not clear why he is on that side.
- The brown dog holding its lead looks like it is asking for a walk, or could just be an odd pose.
- The lead's loop sits next to the hose reel and might be read as attached to it, but it seems separate.
