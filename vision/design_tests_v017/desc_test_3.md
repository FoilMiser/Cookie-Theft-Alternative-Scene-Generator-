In the middle of the lawn, a sprinkler on a tall pole is spraying water in arcs. The water is landing on a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is sitting in a puddle and looks soaked and stained. That seems to be because a white-and-brown spotted dog is standing on the hose further along. The hose between the dog and the hose reel by the flower bed looks swollen, as if the water is backed up. If the dog steps off, the water might suddenly spray out, possibly at the woman. Behind her, a towel and a pair of jeans hang on a clothesline. At the front, there is an empty wheelbarrow whose wheel looks square rather than round. Next to it, a white goat with horns has its head down, nibbling at a tuft of grass. A goat in an ordinary suburban garden is unusual.
