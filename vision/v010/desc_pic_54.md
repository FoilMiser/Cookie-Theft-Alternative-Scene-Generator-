## Free description
This is a sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right, enclosed by a tall wooden fence with a gate on the left. In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is reaching up to a washing line between two wooden posts, pegging out laundry: a white T-shirt, a yellow towel with red stripes, and a pair of blue jeans. On top of the fence on the left, a red bird is perched and an orange cat is creeping along toward it, as if about to pounce. At the front, a boy in a blue hoodie is reading a red book in a sitting position, but there is no chair, stool or anything under him; he is sitting on thin air, which is impossible. Around the garden there are two potted plants, a teal watering can, an empty wheelbarrow, a flower bed full of colourful flowers near the house, and an empty wooden bench. A paved path runs from the gate down to the front left corner. Apart from the boy sitting on nothing and the cat stalking the bird, it is a calm, ordinary laundry-day scene.
