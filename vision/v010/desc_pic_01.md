# Picture 01

## Free description
This is a sunny town street with a row of four colourful shop buildings (cream, peach, pale green and pink) with striped awnings, a bright sun in the top right, two small clouds and a red bird flying in the top left. In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress sits on a wooden bench with her eyes closed, eating a pink ice cream cone. A brown dog with a red collar is stretched out right next to her legs, with its head raised up toward her ice cream, as if it is about to grab it while she isn't looking. In the front centre, a white dog with a blue neckerchief is sitting up like a person and holding open a newspaper in its paws as if reading it, which is impossible for a real dog. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping the pavement with a broom, in front of a fruit and vegetable stall that has crates of red, orange, yellow and green produce. An orange cat is lying flat on the ground at the front left, apparently asleep. There is also a tree in a round planter, a bicycle parked in a bike rack, a street lamp, two rubbish bins, a cardboard box beside the stall, and potted plants in both front corners.
