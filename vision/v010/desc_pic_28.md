# Picture 28

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park, with a bright sun in the top-left corner, two white clouds, green rolling hills and a line of trees in the background. On the left a man with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans is walking to the left and pushing a teal pram with a baby lying in it under a dark blue hood. The pram is partly on a winding sand-coloured path that runs from the back of the park down to the front. In the middle background a woman with short black hair, a yellow dress and red shoes stands near a tall black lamp post and fishes with a rod. Her line runs out to a small oval pond on the right, where a red-and-white float bobs with a ripple around it, and there are cattails at both ends of the pond. In front of the pond a bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket, leaning back on one hand, with a woven picnic basket beside his legs. On the right there is a round flower bed full of colourful flowers, and a green rubbish bin is partly cut off in the bottom-right corner. A grey rock sits on the grass at the front centre. Nothing looks clearly impossible, but the picnic basket is drawn over the seated man's legs.
