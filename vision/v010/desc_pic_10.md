## Free description
This is a sunny back garden seen the other way round: the cream house with a red roof and blue back door is on the right, and the wooden gate in the back fence is on the left, with a paved path running from the gate down the left side. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands on the path near the gate with her hands on her hips and a blue bucket upside down on her head like a hat. Near her is an empty swing set with one red seat. A garden hose runs from the left edge across the path, under the swing set, to a sprinkler in the middle of the lawn, and the sprinkler is spraying arcs of water onto a closed cardboard box sitting on the grass, so the box is getting wet. On the right, a man in an orange cap and an orange high-visibility safety vest over a grey shirt, with blue trousers, is watering the flower bed by the house with a teal watering can. A grey wheelbarrow stands at the front centre-left, and a clothesline in the middle holds a yellow striped towel and blue jeans. Behind the fence at left there is a birdhouse on a pole. The odd things are the bucket on the woman's head and the sprinkler watering a cardboard box instead of plants; the man watering by hand while a sprinkler is on nearby is also a bit odd.
