# Picture 40

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden: a bright sun is in the top left, there are two white clouds, and a wooden fence runs across the back with green hedges and a tree behind it. A cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the right. A woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers stands in the middle, watering a bed of colourful flowers with a hose. The water arcs from the nozzle onto the flowers, and the hose runs back across the grass to a hose reel beside the flower bed. Further back on the right, near the house, a small girl in a pink dress holds up the string of a yellow kite with a bow-tied tail flying high in the sky. A washing line between two posts holds a yellow towel with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans. On the left, a cardboard box is tilted in mid-air in front of the fence with its shadow on the grass well below it. Nobody is near it, so it seems to be floating or falling for no reason. Also in the garden are a wooden gate at the left end of the fence with a pale stone path leading from it, a birdhouse on a pole behind the fence, a teal watering can, and an empty grey wheelbarrow in the foreground.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The cardboard box hangs in mid-air with its shadow on the ground below and nothing holding it up. It could be falling or blown by the wind, or it simply floats impossibly.
- The girl is drawn much smaller than the woman. She could be a toddler, or she just looks small because she is further back.
- The light-blue water stream is drawn as segmented blobs, so at a glance it could also read as a second, lighter piece of hose.
- The hose seems to start from the woman's hip/waist rather than clearly from the nozzle in her hand. There is a slight disconnect.
- The kite string runs close to the tree and the right washing-line post but does not seem to touch them.
- The watering can and wheelbarrow have no shadows, but the people and the box do.
