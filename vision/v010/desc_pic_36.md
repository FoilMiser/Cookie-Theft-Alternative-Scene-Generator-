# Picture 36

## Free description

This is a busy, sunny day in a park with a pond, hills and trees. In the front centre, a small child with curly dark hair, a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone. The child's other hand is rubbing an eye as if crying, because the pink scoop of ice cream, with sprinkles, has fallen and lies splattered on the grass. A tan dog with a red collar has its nose right at the dropped ice cream and is sniffing or licking it. Behind them, a young person with a brown ponytail, a blue top and dark trousers stands near a black lamp post and flies an orange diamond-shaped kite high in the sky. The kite's string runs from their raised hand up to the kite. On the left, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers sweeps the curving path with a broom. Next to her is an empty wooden bench. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress with short dark hair and red shoes walks past the pond carrying a cardboard box. There are green rubbish bins by the lamp post and in the bottom-right corner.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The kite's top point touches the bottom of a cloud. It could be read as the kite flying in front of the cloud or as tucked just under or into it. Kites flying that close to a cloud is a bit unrealistic.
- The child's hand at the face could mean crying or wiping tears. It could also just be rubbing an eye or shading the face. Together with the dropped ice cream, crying seems most likely.
- The dog's head is drawn a bit oddly: rounded, with a dark ear patch, and pointed straight down. It is clearly at the ice cream, but I can't tell whether it is licking or only sniffing.
- From her face and the way she holds the box, the woman in yellow seems to face left. Her legs are mid-stride, so she reads as walking left, but her direction is not completely clear.
- I can't tell whether the kite flyer is a girl, a boy or a woman. The ponytail suggests a girl or young woman.
- I can't tell what is in the cardboard box. It could be a parcel or a delivery.
- The sweeping woman is sweeping a path that already looks clean. That is just an observation, not something wrong.
