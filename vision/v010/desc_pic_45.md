# Picture 45

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny park with a sun in the top left, two clouds, a dark railing fence with hedges behind, a tree on the right, a bush on the left and a red-and-yellow slide on the left. In the middle-back, a girl/young woman with a brown ponytail and a blue hoodie has thrown a red ball along a dotted arc. A bald, bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers holds up his hands to catch it. In the front left, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans drinks from a white cup in a sitting position beside the sandpit, but there is nothing under him. He appears to be sitting on thin air. A woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers walks a white spotted dog on a red lead. Behind them, a tan dog with a red collar lies asleep on the grass. On the right, an elderly man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat sits on the low end of a red seesaw reading a book. A small girl in a pink dress sits on the high end, up in the air and holding on. A boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts sits on a park bench drinking from a cup. There is also a green litter bin by the fence, an empty sandpit and a grey rock.
