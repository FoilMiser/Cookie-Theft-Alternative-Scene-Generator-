# Picture 44

## Free description
This is a sunny town square or high street with a paved pedestrian area in front of a row of four colourful two-storey shops, a sun in the top right and two clouds. Three of the shops have striped awnings (red-and-white, green-and-white and orange-and-white), and there is a flower planter on the pavement under one shop window. On the left, a man with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans walks along with his eyes closed, happily licking a pink ice-cream cone. In the middle-right, a woman with a black bob, a yellow dress and red shoes holds a camera up to her face and takes a photo pointing left, roughly toward the man. Between them is a wooden bench, and behind them a black street lamp stands on the pavement. On the right, a market stall with a teal-and-white striped canopy displays crates of fruit (red, orange, yellow and green), and a cardboard box sits on the ground beside it. Around the square there is a tree in a round planter, a green litter bin, a small round café table with two black chairs in the bottom left, and a dark grey bin in the bottom right. Nothing looks dangerous or obviously impossible. It reads as a calm, everyday scene.
