# Picture 41

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the house on the left this time: cream walls, a red roof, one window and a blue door, with a tree just behind it. The sun is in the top right and there are two white clouds. A wooden fence runs across the back, and part of it near the middle-right has been painted bright blue. A man in an orange cap, an orange high-visibility vest with reflective stripes and blue trousers stands in front of the fence. He reaches up with a long-handled brush or roller with blue paint on it, painting the fence blue. A paint tin with blue paint on it sits on the grass by his feet. A black cat with white paws walks along the top of the fence to the right of him. In the left foreground, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers holds a teal watering can over a bed of colourful flowers. Oddly, the stream of water curves up into the air away from the spout instead of pouring down onto the flowers, with drops at the top of the arc. On the right is a wooden gate at the end of the fence with a pale path leading down from it, plus two potted plants and a grey wheelbarrow on the grass.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water from the watering can goes up and away from the flowers, rising in an arc with drops at its top, instead of falling down. That is physically impossible for a watering can. Another reading is that she is splashing water up into the air rather than watering. Either way, the water is not reaching the flowers.
- The small green bush below the blue fence section looks cut off along its top edge right at the bottom of the fence. It looks like only the lower half of a bush, or a bush that is behind the fence yet showing below it. That is an odd overlap, since the fence reaches the ground.
- The man stands in front of the unpainted part, reaching to the edge of the blue part. It is unclear if he is starting a new board or touching up the edge.
- The man's tool could be a long-handled paintbrush or a small roller.
- The cat could be walking away from the painter, or just strolling along the fence.
