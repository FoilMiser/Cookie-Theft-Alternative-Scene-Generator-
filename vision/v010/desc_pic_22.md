## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream-coloured house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a tree behind the house, a tall wooden fence across the back and a wooden gate at the right end of the fence with a paved path leading up to it. A strong gust of wind is blowing: white wavy wind lines run from near the man at the fence towards the right, and a few green leaves are flying through the air with them. An orange baseball cap is tumbling through the air to the right of centre, in front of the fence. A man in a blue top and dark trousers stands at the back by the fence with both arms thrown up over his head and a surprised or alarmed face. A woman in a yellow dress in the middle foreground also has both arms raised high and is looking up and to the right, as if reaching for or reacting to the flying cap. Neither of them is wearing a cap, so it may have blown off one of them. On the left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is watering a round flower bed with a teal watering can, but the stream of water curves upward out of the spout into the air, with drops above it, instead of falling onto the flowers. A blue ball is sitting up in the leaves of the tree behind the house, apparently stuck there. It looks like the cap is about to be blown further away, perhaps over the fence or towards the gate.
