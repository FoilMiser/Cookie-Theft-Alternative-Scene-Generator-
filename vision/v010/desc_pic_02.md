# Picture 02

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green rolling hills and a line of trees in the background, a sun in the top left and two clouds. A sandy path winds from the back down to the front left, past an empty wooden bench. In the middle, a young girl in a pink dress stands at the edge of a pond holding a small white object and reaching her hand out toward the water, and there are little brown pieces (like bread or food) floating in the water near her. A white duck is swimming on the pond toward that food, with wake lines behind it. In the front, a woman in a yellow top sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket, smiling with her eyes closed, next to a brown picnic basket. On the right, a wooden ladder stands upright with its top end at the edge of the pond, leaning against nothing, which looks impossible. There is also a bed of colourful flowers in the front right, a grey rock in the front centre, a street lamp, a green rubbish bin by the lamp and another green bin cut off in the bottom-right corner, and trees and a bush on both sides.

- White duck (centre-right, on the pond): swimming, with lines behind it showing movement toward the floating food on the left.
- Pond (centre-right): blue oval pond with reeds/cattails at the edges.
- Floating food: small brown pieces scattered on the water near the girl, like bread crumbs or duck food.
- Picnic blanket: red-and-white checked, on the grass in the front.

- Ladder (right): wooden ladder standing at an angle with its top at the pond edge and its bottom on the grass, not leaning on anything.

- Path (left): winding sandy path from the back to the front.

- Rubbish bins: green bin next to the lamp; green bin partly visible at the bottom-right edge.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The ladder standing free with nothing to lean against is the main thing that looks wrong/impossible; it could also be read as leaning into the pond.
- The white object in the girl's hand is too small to identify for sure: it could be a bag of bread, a piece of bread or a small cup.
- The picnic basket overlaps the woman's legs: her red shoes stick out on the far side of the basket, so it could be (a) sitting in her lap/over her legs or (b) just placed in front of her. She also sits at the right edge of the blanket with her legs partly off it.
- The woman's closed eyes could mean she is laughing/content or just relaxing in the sun.
