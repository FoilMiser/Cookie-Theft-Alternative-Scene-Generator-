# Picture 39

## Free description

This is a sunny playground on a lawn, with a low metal railing fence across the back and a line of trees behind it. On the left is a red-framed slide with a ladder. A child with curly dark hair, a yellow top and orange shorts is sliding down it with both arms raised, as if cheering. In the middle stands a metal swing set with one red swing seat hanging empty and still. On the right, front, is a red seesaw on a grey triangular stand. A bearded man in a green T-shirt and tan trousers sits on the low end, which is down on the ground, and reads an open book instead of looking at his partner. A little girl in a pink dress with dark hair sits on the high end, up in the air, holding the handle and looking toward the man. Because the man is heavier and isn't pushing off, she seems stuck up in the air. An empty wooden bench and a large tree are on the right, a green rubbish bin is behind the slide, a grey rock is at the bottom left, and a bush is at the left edge.
