# Picture 30

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a sun in the top-left, two white clouds, a wooden fence across the back and trees behind it. A cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the right, and a wooden gate on the left opens onto a pale paved path running down the left side. On the left a small child in pink sits on a red swing hanging from a grey metal A-frame swing set, smiling with eyes closed. In the middle a bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers holds both hands out in front of him, ready to catch. A red-and-white ball is in the air, and a dashed line shows that it was thrown up from the hand of a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts in the front-centre. At the back, an older girl with a brown ponytail, a blue top and dark trousers holds a string up toward a purple diamond kite with a bow tail. The kite is drawn in front of the leafy tree near the house, as if flying there or caught in the branches. A black-and-white cat lies on the grass at the front, right beside the boy's feet, so the boy might step on or trip over it. There is also a teal watering can near the swing and a flower bed with colourful flowers next to the house.
