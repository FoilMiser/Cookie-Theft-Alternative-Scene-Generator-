# pic_09

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue back door on the left, a wooden fence along the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path running from the gate to the front right. A woman with a short black bob and a yellow dress sits on a bench at the front left reading a red book. A sandwich is sitting on her lap or the bench beside her, and a blue bird has landed right next to it and is pecking at/eating the sandwich while she keeps reading and does not notice. A blue ball with a white stripe is stuck up in the leaves of the tree behind the house. A little girl in a pink dress stands near the back fence pointing up at the ball in the tree. In the middle of the lawn, a white goat with a small horn has its head down and is eating a tuft of grass, which is unusual for a back garden. There is a flower bed of colourful flowers, a clothesline with blue jeans and a yellow striped towel, two potted plants near the path, a grey wheelbarrow at the front and a teal watering can at the front right. The odd things are the goat in the garden, the ball stuck in the tree, and the bird stealing the sandwich.
