# Picture 32

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a sun in the top-left, two white clouds, a wooden fence across the back, trees beyond it, and a red-roofed birdhouse on a post behind the fence. A cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the right, and a wooden gate on the left opens onto a pale paved path running down the left side. A young man with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands at the edge of the path, waving one raised hand (with motion lines) and with his mouth open as if calling out to someone. On the right an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt kneels on one knee at a flower bed and holds a small terracotta pot with a pink flower, as though about to plant it. She faces the flowers with her back toward the young man and does not seem to notice him. A garden hose runs across the front of the path to a lawn sprinkler in the middle of the lawn. The sprinkler is spraying water in arcs (drawn as dashed blue lines) onto a closed cardboard parcel sitting on the grass, with drops landing on top of the box. The parcel looks like it is about to get soaked, which may be why the young man is calling out.
