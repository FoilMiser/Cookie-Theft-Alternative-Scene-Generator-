# Picture 31

## Free description
This is a sunny shopping street paved with grey slabs, with a sun in the top-left, two white clouds and a row of colourful two-storey shops (pink, pale green, peach and cream) with striped awnings. On the left, a bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers walks to the right pushing a teal pram with a baby under a dark blue hood. Behind him is a market stall with a teal-and-white striped canopy and crates of green, yellow, orange and red fruit or vegetables, but no stall keeper, and a cardboard box sits on the ground beside it. In the centre a little girl in a pink dress holds a red balloon and a purple balloon in one hand. She is reaching up with her other hand, looking worried, toward a blue balloon that is floating away above her with its string dangling out of reach. To the right a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a yellow balloon and looks toward the girl. In the background, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers walks to the left carrying a yellow shopping bag. There is also a black street lamp, a planter of flowers, a tree in a round planter, a green rubbish bin and two potted plants in the front corners. The main event seems to be the girl losing her blue balloon.
