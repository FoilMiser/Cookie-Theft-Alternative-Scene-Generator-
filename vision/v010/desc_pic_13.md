## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the cream house with a red roof and blue back door on the right, a wooden fence along the back with a gate on the left, and a paved path running down the left side. At the front left, a boy in a red shirt and blue trousers is sweeping a pile of dirt on the path using a giant silver fork, as big as a rake, and a cloud of dust is rising; a red dustpan lies on the ground nearby. In the middle, a woman with dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers is holding up the end of a green garden hose and looking at it, but only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose runs along the ground to a hose reel by the flower bed, and a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is standing on the hose, which looks like it is blocking the water - she may get sprayed in the face when the dog steps off. On the right, an old man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown vest sits on a bench happily eating a pink ice cream cone, while a brown dog with a red bandana jumps up on his legs, trying to get at the ice cream. A small red bowl of dog food sits on the grass at the front. There are two potted plants by the fence, a clothesline with a yellow striped towel and blue jeans, and a birdhouse on a pole behind the fence. The odd things are the giant fork used as a rake, and the dog standing on the hose so the water stops.
