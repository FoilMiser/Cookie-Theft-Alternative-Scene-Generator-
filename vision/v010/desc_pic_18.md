# Picture 18

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a wooden gate on the far left, a paved path running from the gate down the left side, and a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the right. On the left a man with brown hair in a red shirt and blue jeans is painting the fence blue with a long-handled brush or roller; a few fence boards are already blue. At his feet are a paint tin and a paint tray full of blue paint, and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the tray across the grass to a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar, whose paws are blue, so the dog has clearly stepped in the paint. In the middle an elderly grey-haired woman in a teal top and grey skirt is hanging washing on a clothesline between two posts: a white T-shirt, a yellow towel with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans. The dog is walking toward her, and it looks as if it may track paint further or onto her or the washing. On the right a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands at the corner of the house, partly hidden behind it, looking toward the others. In front of the house is a round flower bed with colourful flowers and a teal garden hose reel beside it, and an empty grey wheelbarrow sits at the bottom of the lawn. Nothing looks physically impossible; the mischief is the dog spreading blue paint, which the painter has not noticed.
