# pic_56

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden next to a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, enclosed by a wooden fence with a gate on the right and a paved path leading from the gate down to the front right. At the back near the fence, a bearded man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers is raking a small pile of leaves. In front of him, a little girl in a pink dress is leaping into the air with both arms up, looking happy and excited; her shadow is on the grass below her. At the front, an old man in a grey flat cap, brown waistcoat and white shirt is reading a red book in a sitting position, but there is no chair or bench under him; he is sitting on thin air, which is impossible. On the right there is a grey swing set with a single red swing seat hanging empty. Other things in the garden are a flower bed full of colourful flowers by the house, a hose reel at the front left, a teal watering can at the front right, and a birdhouse on a pole sticking up behind the fence.
