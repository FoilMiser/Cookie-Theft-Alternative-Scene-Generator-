# Picture 33

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a sun in the top-left, two white clouds, a wooden fence with a gate on the left, a red-roofed birdhouse on a post, and a cream house with a blue door on the right. In the front-left, a man in an orange cap, an orange high-visibility vest and navy overalls stands on the paved path sweeping a small pile of dirt, which raises a puff of dust. Strangely, he is sweeping with a giant silver dinner fork as tall as his legs instead of a broom or rake. In the middle a girl with a brown ponytail, a blue top and dark trousers holds the end of a garden hose up in front of her face and peers into the nozzle, which has only a single drop of water at its tip. The hose runs from a reel by a bench on the right, across the lawn, to the girl. A brown-and-white dog with a red collar is standing right on top of the hose, which would explain why no water comes out. Behind them a washing line between two posts holds a yellow towel with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans. A black-and-white cat is walking along the top of the fence near the tree on the right. A teal watering can stands on or next to a red dustpan at the front, a grey wheelbarrow is on the lawn, and there is a flower bed by the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- There is a white crumpled shape at the man's chest near his hands. It could be a cloth or glove, or the rounded end of the fork handle.
- The girl looking into the nozzle is probably checking why no water is coming out, and the dog standing on the hose looks like the cause. The dog could also just be standing next to the hose rather than on it, but its paws are drawn on the hose line.
- The watering can and red dustpan overlap. The can could be standing on the dustpan, or the two could be side by side.
- It is unclear whether the cat on the fence is walking toward the house or toward the tree. Its head points right, toward the house.
