# Picture 38

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden behind a house. The house is on the right. It has a cream wall, a red roof, a window and a blue door, and a tree stands behind it. A tall wooden fence runs across the back, with a gate at the far left and a pale paved path leading from the gate down to the bottom-left corner. On the left is a metal swing set. A small girl in a pink dress sits on its red swing seat, holding the ropes and smiling with her eyes closed. In the middle, a person with brown hair, a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes is jumping in the air with both arms up, laughing. Their shadow is on the grass below, so they are clearly off the ground. On the right, a bearded man in a green T-shirt and tan trousers holds a dark round spray nozzle or sprinkler head with both hands. A stream of water arcs out of it and falls in drops toward the left end of a flower bed full of colourful flowers. A teal watering can sits unused on the grass near the swing set. The mood is cheerful and playful. The jumping person may be about to be splashed or may be dancing in the water.
