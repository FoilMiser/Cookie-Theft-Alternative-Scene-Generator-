# Picture 05

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a sun in the top left and two clouds. A red bird is flying in the sky and a blue bird is perched on top of the fence. In the centre, a woman in a yellow dress is walking along while reading an open book, and she is heading straight toward a cardboard box on the ground, so she looks about to trip over it. An older woman with grey hair in a teal top and grey skirt is holding a garden hose nozzle up close to her face and looking at it; the hose runs along the ground right under the feet of a white spotted dog, which is standing on it, so the water may be blocked. At the back, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is flying a purple kite whose string goes up to the right, and the kite is right over the tree near the house, so it may be stuck in the tree. On the left, an older man with a cap, beard and brown waistcoat is sweeping with a broom by an open gate, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is walking a brown dog on a red lead, and a girl with a ponytail in a blue top is kicking an orange ball. There is also a grey wheelbarrow on the path, whose front wheel looks square, plus a flower bed, a hose reel by the house and a wooden bench on the right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The wheelbarrow's front wheel looks square (a square with a cross in it) rather than round, which would be impossible for rolling; it could alternatively be read as a box or crate at the front of the wheelbarrow.
- The kite overlaps the tree: it could be (a) caught/stuck in the tree branches or (b) flying just in front of the tree.
- The hose: the dog is standing on it and the woman is looking at the nozzle near her face. One reading is that the dog has blocked the water and she is checking why nothing comes out (and may get sprayed in the face); another is simply that she is about to water something.
- The woman reading the book could just step around the box, but as drawn she is walking straight into it.
- The girl's kick sends the ball toward the left, toward the path and wheelbarrow; it is unclear if anything is meant to get hit.
- The object in the woman's hands could be a book, a map or papers.
