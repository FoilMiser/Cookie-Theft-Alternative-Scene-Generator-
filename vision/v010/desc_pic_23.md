## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right, a tree behind the house, a wooden fence across the back with a wooden gate at the far left, and a paved path running from the gate down the left side. On the left there is a dark grey A-frame swing set with one red swing seat hanging empty and still. In the middle near the fence, a child with curly hair in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is high up in the air with arms raised and knees bent, jumping happily; their shadow is on the grass well below them. In the foreground, a little girl in a pink dress is holding the strings of a blue balloon and a yellow balloon in one hand and reaching up with her other hand towards a purple balloon that has got away and is floating off with its string dangling. To the right, a woman in a yellow dress is watering a round flower bed with a teal watering can, and the water pours down onto the flowers. At the front right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. A hose reel stands next to the bench. It looks like the purple balloon is about to float away out of the girl's reach.
