# Picture 43

## Free description
This is a back garden under a grey, stormy sky with three dark clouds and no sun. A cream house with a red roof, window and blue door is on the right, and a wooden fence with a gate on the left runs across the back. There is something impossible in the tree behind the fence on the right: an orange fish sits among the leaves. On the left, a small girl in a pink dress has thrown a red ball. A dotted line shows it flying up over the fence top toward a bald, bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers, who holds his hands up in front of him to catch it. In the middle, an elderly man in a flat cap, a white shirt, a brown waistcoat and black trousers looks up and points or reaches up with one arm. He is near the washing line, which holds a striped yellow towel and blue jeans. Beside him, an elderly woman with a grey bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey-blue skirt waters the flower bed with a hose that runs to a hose reel. In the foreground, a sprinkler at the end of a long hose sprays arcs of water onto a closed cardboard box that has drops on top, so the box is getting wet. A white goat with horns has its head down eating a tuft of grass at the bottom centre. A wooden bench stands on the grass in front of the house.

- Man, centre-left, back: dark skin, bald head, beard, green short-sleeved shirt, khaki trousers, brown shoes. He faces left toward the girl with both hands raised together in front of his face, ready to catch.
- Elderly man, centre, back (in front of the left washing-line post): grey hair, blue-grey flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat, black trousers. He looks up and points/reaches upward with his raised arm.
- Elderly woman, centre-right: grey hair in a bun, glasses, teal top, grey-blue skirt, brown shoes. She faces right and waters the flower bed with a hose.
- Goat, bottom centre-right: white, small curved horns. Its head is down, eating a tuft of grass, with its back end raised.
- Fish, upper right, in the tree: orange fish with a dark eye and stripe, sitting in the tree's foliage. It does not belong there.
- Red ball, upper left: red-and-white ball above the fence top at the end of a dotted line from the girl's hand. It is flying toward the man.
- Sprinkler, bottom left-centre: small sprinkler head on a long dark hose that comes in from the left edge across the path. It sprays dashed blue arcs of water to the right.
- Cardboard box, bottom centre: closed, taped brown box on the grass with water drops landing on top. The sprinkler is soaking it.
- Garden hose and reel, centre-right: hose from the woman's hands looping across the grass to a teal reel. A light-blue stream falls onto the flowers.
- Flower bed, centre-right: oval of brown soil with red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers.
- Washing line, centre-back: two wooden posts with a yellow red-striped towel and blue jeans pegged on it.
- Bench, right foreground in front of the house: wooden slatted bench.
- House, right: cream walls, red roof, window, blue door with step.
- Gate (left end of fence) and pale path running from it to the bottom left.
- Sky: grey with three dark storm clouds, no sun.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fish in the tree is clearly out of place and impossible as a real situation.
- The elderly man's raised arm could be pointing at the dark clouds (a storm coming), reaching to take the washing in, or pointing at something else such as the fish. He is looking upward.
- It is odd, though possible, that people are watering flowers and running a sprinkler under a stormy sky.
- The sprinkler spraying the cardboard box could be an accident (nobody has noticed the box getting wet).
- A goat grazing in a family back garden is unusual. Its body pose (back end high, legs bent sharply) looks a bit contorted.
- The ball is in front of the fence top between the girl and the man. It may be heading slightly behind or past the man rather than straight to his hands.
- The sprinkler hose crosses over the paved path from the left edge.
