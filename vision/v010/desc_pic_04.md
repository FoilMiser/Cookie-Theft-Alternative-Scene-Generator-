# Picture 04

In the centre, a young man or teenage boy with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans is standing and tipping a small white bag or carton so that seeds or crumbs fall in a line down to the ground. There is an empty wooden bench to the right of centre, a street lamp with a green rubbish bin beside it, a bed of colourful flowers in the front left, a grey rock in the front centre, another green bin in the bottom-left corner, and trees and bushes on both sides.
