On the left there is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door, with a tree and a bush beside it, and a wooden bench and a hose reel in the front-left corner. A woman with black hair, a yellow dress and red shoes stands with her back to us in the middle of the lawn, spraying water from a garden hose in an arc onto a flower bed by the house. On the right, a brown cardboard or wooden box is hanging in mid-air in front of the fence, tilted, with its shadow on the grass well below it, as though it is flying or falling. A wooden gate in the fence on the right leads to a paved path that runs to the front-right corner. A grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can are on the grass at the front, and a red-roofed birdhouse on a post stands behind the fence. It is odd that the woman is watering the flowers when rain looks about to start, and the floating box looks impossible unless it is being blown by wind.
