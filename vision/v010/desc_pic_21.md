# Picture 21

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof, window and blue door on the left, a tall tree behind it, a wooden fence across the back with a wooden gate on the right, and a paved path down the right side. On the left, in front of the house, a woman with a dark ponytail in a purple top and teal trousers is watering a round bed of colourful flowers with a teal watering can, and water is pouring out of the spout. In the middle a young man or boy in an orange cap, an orange high-visibility vest with reflective stripes and navy trousers is raking a small pile of leaves. Behind him, a clothesline strung between two wooden posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. On the right a woman or older girl with a short black bob, a yellow dress and red shoes is digging a hole in the lawn with a spade, with a mound of earth piled beside it. Everyone is busy with their own garden job and nobody is talking to anyone else. Nothing looks wrong, odd or impossible.
