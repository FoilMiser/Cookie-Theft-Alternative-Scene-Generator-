## Free description
This is a busy paved town square or shopping street on a sunny day, in front of a row of colourful two-storey shops with striped awnings. A woman in a yellow dress is walking along at the front left with her nose in an open book, and a red ball is lying on the ground right in front of her feet, so she looks about to trip over it. In the middle, an old man in a flat cap and waistcoat sits on a bench eating a pink ice-cream cone, while a brown dog with a red collar stretches up toward the ice cream as if it is about to snatch it. Behind them a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is running to the left past a street lamp. At the front right, a boy in a blue hoodie is sweeping a pile of dirt, but he is doing it with a giant silver fork instead of a broom, which is impossible or at least very odd; a red dustpan lies on the ground beside the pile. On the far right a small child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands by a fruit stall with one arm raised, as if waving or calling out. A red bicycle is parked at a grey bike stand at the front centre, and a blue bird is flying in the sky near the sun.
