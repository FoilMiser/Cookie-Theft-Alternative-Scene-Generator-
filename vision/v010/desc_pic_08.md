# pic_08

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue back door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path leading from the gate toward the front right. A young man in a red shirt and blue jeans sits on a wooden bench at the front left, reading a red book. Next to him, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is standing at a flower bed and watering the flowers, but she is pouring the water out of what looks like a dark grey frying pan rather than a watering can. In the middle, a little girl in a pink dress holds a yellow and a red balloon by their strings in one hand while reaching up with her other hand toward a blue balloon that is floating up and away from her; its string is just out of her reach. Behind the flower bed, a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is lying stretched out flat on the grass with its head down. A black-and-white cat is walking along the top of the back fence in front of the tree. At the bottom centre, an orange cat is crouched low and creeping toward a small blue bird that is pecking at the ground, so the cat looks like it is about to pounce on the bird. A clothesline in the back holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes, and an empty swing set with one red seat stands on the right. The odd things are the woman watering the flowers with a frying pan and the cat on the fence.
