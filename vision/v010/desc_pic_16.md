# Picture 16

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny park playground with a sun in the top left, two clouds, a railing fence and green bushes behind, a slide on the left, a swing set in the middle, a seesaw on the right and a tree on the far right. On the far left a woman with a brown ponytail and a blue hoodie walks to the left carrying a cardboard box. In front of the slide a small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is crying with his hand over his eyes, holding an empty ice-cream cone; his pink scoop with sprinkles has fallen onto the grass, and a brown dog with a red collar is bending down right next to it, apparently about to eat it. Behind them, by the fence, an elderly grey-haired woman in a teal top and grey skirt is taking a photo with a camera. In the centre front a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is walking while reading an open book, and there is a cardboard box on the ground right in front of his feet, so he looks about to trip over it. Near the fence on the right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is licking a pink ice-cream cone, and on a bench a bearded man in a green shirt reads a red book while a blue bird pecks at the sandwich lying beside him. On the far right a little girl in a pink dress is kicking an orange ball. Two things look impossible: a teal bicycle in the foreground has square wheels, and in the bottom right corner a white spotted animal with a blue collar is sitting on the grass holding up and reading a newspaper like a person.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The white animal reading the newspaper could be a dog (it has brown spots and a blue collar like a dog) or could be read as a goat or cat because of its pointed, horn-like ears. Either way, an animal reading a newspaper is impossible.
- The bicycle's square wheels are impossible for a working bike; it also stands upright on its own with no rider or stand.
- The elderly woman's camera points to the left; it is not clear what she is photographing (perhaps the woman with the box or the slide).
- It is not certain the brown dog is eating the ice cream; its nose is just next to it, so it may only be sniffing.
- The dropped pink scoop looks very large compared to the small cone.
- The man in red might be stepping over the box rather than about to trip; most likely reading is that he hasn't seen it.
- The blue bird could be a pigeon or a jay-like bird; it is drawn bright blue.
