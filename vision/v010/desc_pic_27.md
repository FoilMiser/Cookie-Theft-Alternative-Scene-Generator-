# pic_27

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a tree behind the house, a wooden fence across the back with a wooden gate at the right end, and a paved path leading up to the gate on the right. In front of the blue door, a brown-and-white dog sits holding a red lead in its mouth, with the rest of the lead trailing on the ground; it looks like it wants to be taken for a walk. Right in front of the dog, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is watering a round flower bed with a teal watering can, but the stream of water curves upward from the spout into the air with drops above it, instead of falling on the flowers. She is looking at the flowers and not at the dog. At the back, a woman in a yellow dress stands facing the fence, painting it blue with a long-handled brush; a section of about three boards to her left is already blue, and a paint tin sits on the grass by her feet. An empty grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles is parked at the front, right of centre. Two potted plants stand near the path on the right.
