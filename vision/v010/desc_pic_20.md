# Picture 20

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof, window and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a wooden gate on the right, and a paved path running down the right side. A little girl in a pink dress stands in the front centre, looking up and holding the string of an orange diamond-shaped kite that is flying high in the sky with a tail of red and blue bows. In the middle of the lawn a white goat with small horns is bending down and eating a tuft of grass. On the right a small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is sitting on a swing hanging from a dark grey swing set, smiling with his eyes closed as he swings. Behind the fence on the right a small red-roofed birdhouse stands on a post, and a large tree rises behind the house. In front of the house is a round flower bed full of colourful flowers, with a teal garden hose reel beside it and a bush at the corner of the house. Near the bottom are an empty grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can. The scene looks peaceful; the only unusual thing is a goat grazing in an ordinary back garden.

- Goat (centre, middle): white goat with small horns and a short tail; head down eating a tuft of grass.
- Boy (right, middle): small boy with curly dark hair, yellow shirt, orange shorts; sitting on the swing, smiling with eyes closed, arms out holding the swing.
- Kite (centre-top, sky): orange diamond kite with a tail of red and blue bows; flying high on the girl's string.
- Kite string (centre): thin line from the girl's hands up to the kite.

- Flower bed (left, front): round bed of red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers.
- Hose reel (left, front): teal hose on a reel.

- Watering can (right, front): teal watering can on the grass.
- Birdhouse (right, back): small red-roofed birdhouse on a post behind the fence.
- Gate (far right, back): wooden gate in the fence. Path (far right): paved path.
- Tree (left, back): large tree behind the house. Sky: sun (top right), two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The white animal is most likely a goat (small horns, grazing), but it could be read as a white dog or a lamb.
- The kite string passes very close to the goat's back end; it could be read as passing just beside it or just behind it, but it does not seem to go through it.
- The boy's expression reads as happy (eyes closed, smiling); it could also be read as squinting.
- Nothing floats or passes through anything otherwise; the goat in a back garden is the only odd element.
