## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue back door on the left, a wooden fence along the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path from the gate to the front right. A brown-and-white dog sits right in front of the blue back door holding a red leash in its mouth, with the loop of the leash lying on the ground, as if it wants to be taken for a walk. In the middle, a woman with dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers is reaching up to peg a white T-shirt onto the clothesline, which already has blue jeans and a yellow towel on it. On the right, a man in a red shirt and blue trousers stands with his hands on his hips and a blue bucket upside down on his head, looking toward the left. There is a flower bed of colourful flowers near the house, a grey wheelbarrow at the front, and a teal watering can at the front right. The odd thing is the bucket on the man's head; nobody seems to be paying attention to the dog with its leash.
