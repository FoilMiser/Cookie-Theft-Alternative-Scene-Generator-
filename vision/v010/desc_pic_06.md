# Picture 06

## Free description
This is a town street with four colourful shop buildings under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds and no sun, and it is windy: white wind lines and green leaves are blowing past the street lamp. On the right, a man in a blue hoodie and black trousers has both arms up and a shocked face because his orange hat has blown off and is flying through the air above him. On the left, there is an orange fish sitting in the leaves of a tree, which is impossible, and a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is pointing up at it. In front, a little girl in a pink dress is crying with her hand to her eye, holding an empty ice cream cone, because her pink ice cream has fallen and splattered on the pavement, and a brown dog with a red collar is sniffing or eating it. In the centre, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans sits calmly on a bench reading a red book. In the front centre-right there is a bicycle with square wheels, which could not roll, while another normal bicycle is parked in the rack at the back. Also shown: a black-and-white cat lying asleep at the front left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers carrying a cardboard box on the right, a blue bird perched on top of the lamp post, a fruit and vegetable stall, a flower planter, bins, a spare cardboard box and potted plants.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fish in the tree is clearly impossible; it is drawn sitting in the leaves like a fruit or bird.
- The bicycle with square wheels is clearly impossible to ride; the other bicycle has normal round wheels.
- The orange thing in the air could be a cap or a sun hat; it is most likely the hoodie man's hat. His raised arms could be read as (a) trying to grab it or (b) just surprise/shock.
- The brown dog could be (a) eating the dropped ice cream or (b) just sniffing it.
- The man on the bench reading calmly while a storm blows around him is a little odd but not impossible.
- The cat sleeping on the pavement in a storm is slightly odd but not impossible.
