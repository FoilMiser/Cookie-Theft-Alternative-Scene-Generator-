# Picture 19

## Free description
This is a sunny park with rolling green hills, a sun in the top right, two clouds, a pond on the left, a winding path on the right, a lamp post in the middle and trees on both sides. A white duck is swimming across the pond toward some small brown crumbs floating on the water, and a little boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands at the pond's edge holding a small white piece of something, apparently feeding the duck. Behind the lamp post a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is digging a hole with a shovel, with a pile of earth beside him. Further up the path a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is walking while reading an open book, heading toward a wooden box that sits on the grass in his way, so he may trip over it. On a bench in the middle an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses reads a red book while a blue bird pecks at the sandwich lying next to her. In the bottom centre a brown dog with a red collar is crouching and sniffing at a grey wheelbarrow, whose wheel is drawn as a square with an X in it, which is impossible. On the right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks a white, brown-spotted dog on a red lead, and a small girl in a pink dress is pointing up at a blue-and-white ball stuck in the big tree on the right. A flower bed with colourful flowers sits in the bottom left.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The wheelbarrow's wheel is square, which is impossible for a working wheel.
- The boy's white object could be a piece of bread (most likely, given the crumbs on the water) or could be read as a cup or phone.
- The reading man's direction is a little ambiguous; he faces left toward the box, so he seems to be walking toward it, but he could also be read as walking right along the path.
- The lamp post stands in front of the digging man and overlaps part of his body; it looks like the post is just nearer the viewer, but it could at first glance look as if it passes through him.
- The ball could be stuck in the tree or simply drawn on top of the leaves; the girl pointing at it suggests it is stuck.
- The brown dog might be playing with, sniffing, or biting the wheelbarrow wheel.
