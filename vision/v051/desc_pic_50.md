A girl in a pink dress sits on the left end, which is up in the air, and a child with curly dark hair in a yellow top and orange shorts sits on the right end, which is down near the ground. Both are holding the handles and look content. On the right there is a red-and-yellow slide with nobody on it, and a sandbox in the front right with a red bucket and blue spade. A grey rock is beside the sandbox, a green bin stands by the fence, and a tree is on the left. Nothing looks wrong or about to go wrong; it is a calm, ordinary scene.
