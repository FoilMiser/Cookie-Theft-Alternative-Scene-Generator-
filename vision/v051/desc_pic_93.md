## Free description
A sunny park with a round blue pond on the left, green hills and trees behind, and a sandy path winding off to the right. An older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal sweater and a grey skirt, stands on the grass at the front edge of the pond holding a long fishing rod out over the water. Her line drops to a red-and-white float sitting in the pond. A white duck swims on the pond a little to the left of the float. On the right, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is running across the grass beside the path; speed lines behind him show he is moving fast, heading to the left, toward the pond and the woman. A wooden bench sits next to the path, a tall black lamp post stands behind the pond, and there is a flower bed, a green bin and a grey rock in the foreground. Nothing looks clearly wrong; the scene is calm apart from the running boy.
