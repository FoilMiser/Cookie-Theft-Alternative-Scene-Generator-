# pic_83

## Free description
A sunny park with a pond on the left, a tall street lamp in the middle, a winding path and big tree on the right, and bushes around. A woman with dark hair in a yellow dress and red shoes stands at the edge of the pond holding a brown paper bag and a piece of bread. She is feeding a white duck that is swimming toward her (small ripple lines behind it); the duck is pecking at bread pieces floating on the water. Several bread crumbs float near the duck. A green litter bin is in the bottom left corner and a grey rock lies on the grass at the bottom centre. The scene is calm.
