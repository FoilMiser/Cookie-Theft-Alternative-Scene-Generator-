## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house (blue door) on the left, a wooden fence along the back, a gate and paved path on the right. An old man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat stands by the fence raking up a small pile of dead leaves. A clothesline between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow striped towel. Under the clothesline sits a grey wheelbarrow whose wheel is square instead of round, which is impossible. In the front, a garden hose leads to a sprinkler that is spraying arcs of water — not onto the flower bed but onto a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is getting soaked and is standing in a puddle. A flower bed with colourful flowers is near the house.
