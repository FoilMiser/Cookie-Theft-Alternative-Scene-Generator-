In the front right a little girl in a pink dress holds an empty ice-cream cone and covers her eyes, crying, while a brown dog in front of her licks up the pink ice cream that has fallen on the ground. A red bicycle lies on its side on the ground near a tree, as if it has fallen over. It looks like it is about to rain.
