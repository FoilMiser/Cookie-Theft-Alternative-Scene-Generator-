## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a blue door on the right, a wooden fence along the back and a gate and paved path on the left. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands at the edge of a flower bed near the house, tipping a teal watering can so that water sprinkles down onto the colourful flowers. She is the only person in the picture and there are no animals. Two potted plants sit near the fence on the left, a grey wheelbarrow is parked at front centre, and an empty wooden bench stands at front right. Everything looks ordinary and calm.
