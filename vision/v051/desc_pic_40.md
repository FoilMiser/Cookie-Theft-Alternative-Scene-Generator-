## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the left and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the middle distance a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is digging with a spade; there is a pile of dug earth next to him. In front of him, right of centre, an older man with grey hair and a flat cap, wearing a brown waistcoat over a white shirt, sits drinking from a red cup with a straw — but there is no chair or bench under him, he is sitting in mid-air. On the left stands a swing frame with one empty red swing seat. In the foreground a garden sprinkler attached to a green hose is spraying arcs of water to the left, and the water is landing on a cardboard box, which is getting wet and sitting in a small puddle. A flower bed is in front of the house.
