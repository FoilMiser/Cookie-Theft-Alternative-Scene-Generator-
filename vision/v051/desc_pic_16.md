# pic_16

## Free description
A busy, sunny town square with a row of colourful shops behind and a paved area in front. On the left a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands by a fruit stall, covering their eyes and crying while holding an empty ice-cream cone; the ice cream has fallen on the ground as a pink splat and a brown dog is licking it up. An old woman with a grey bun walks past in the middle carrying a yellow shopping bag. A man in a green shirt sits reading a red book in the centre front, but there is no chair or bench under him — he is sitting on thin air. Behind him, a woman in a purple top sits on a bench reading a red book while a blue bird pecks at her sandwich lying on the bench beside her. An old man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat scatters seeds for a red bird on the ground. A blue-and-white beach ball is stuck in the branches of a tree, and a little girl in a pink dress points up at it. At the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps leaves with a broom. At the front left there is a red bicycle with square wheels, which is impossible, plus a rubbish bin and, at the right, a café table with two chairs.
