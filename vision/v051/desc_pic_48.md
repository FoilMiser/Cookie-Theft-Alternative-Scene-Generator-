## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a house with a blue door on the right, and a tree behind the fence. A man in a red shirt and blue trousers is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller; a few boards to his right are already blue. On the ground behind him are a paint tray with blue paint and a paint can. A white dog with brown spots has walked through the paint tray and is leaving a trail of blue paw prints across the grass, and its paws are blue. The painter is facing the fence and does not seem to notice. On the left, a man in a green shirt walks along the paved path carrying a cardboard box. There is something impossible: an orange fish is sitting in the tree's branches above the fence. A wheelbarrow, a hose reel and a flower bed are also in the garden.
