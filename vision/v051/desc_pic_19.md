# pic_19

## Free description
A sunny park with a pond, a lamp post, trees, an empty bench and a winding path on the right. At the front left a woman in a yellow dress is watering a flower bed by pouring water out of a frying pan, which is an odd tool to use. Next to her a man in a green shirt scatters seeds from a paper bag, and a red bird is eating the seeds on the ground. A child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is running toward the bird while holding an ice-cream cone, and looks like they might trip over or step on the bird. In the middle distance a girl in a pink dress holds a red and a purple balloon and reaches up after a yellow balloon that has escaped and is floating away into the sky. On the right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks along the path carrying a yellow shopping bag. A green bin stands in the bottom left corner.
