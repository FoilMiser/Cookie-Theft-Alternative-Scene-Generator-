# Picture 2

## Free description
A busy park on a sunny day with a pond, a winding path, a lamp post, a bench and trees. On the left an old man in a cap and brown waistcoat is pouring water from a frying pan onto a flower bed, which is odd since he is using a pan instead of a watering can. Beside the flowers, a brown dog wearing glasses sits up holding and reading a newspaper, which is impossible. In the pond a white duck swims, leaving a wake, toward a line of bread crumbs on the water; a girl in a pink dress at the pond's edge holds a slice of bread, so she seems to be feeding the duck. In the front centre a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty-looking ice-cream cone and covers his face with his hand as if crying, while a white spotted dog licks up the pink ice cream that has fallen on the ground. In the background a white goat on a tether is grazing, and an old woman in a teal cardigan sweeps a pile of leaves with a broom near the path. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is flying a purple kite, but the kite is caught in the tree. A bluebird flies across the sky.
