# pic_91

## Free description
A sunny town square paved with grey tiles, in front of a row of pastel shops with striped awnings. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands holding a paper bag and scattering seeds or crumbs onto the ground, where a blue bird and a red bird are pecking them up. Behind her, an old woman with grey hair and glasses, in a green cardigan, sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. On the right there is a market stall with crates of red, orange, yellow and green fruit, but no one is serving at it. A red bicycle is parked in a bike rack at the front. There is also a small café table with two chairs at bottom left, a tree in a planter, a green bin, a tall street lamp, a cardboard box on the ground at right, and a potted plant in the bottom-right corner. It is a calm, peaceful scene.
