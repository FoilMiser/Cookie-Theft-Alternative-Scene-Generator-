# Picture 6

## Free description
A sunny back garden seen from the other side: the cream house with red roof and blue door is on the right, a wooden fence with a gate is at the back left, and a paved path runs along the left. In the centre-left a girl in a pink dress is jumping in the air with both arms raised, looking happy and excited (her shadow is on the ground below her). In the centre-right a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers kneels by a flower bed, digging with a small trowel next to a pot with a pink flower, so he seems to be planting it. On the far right a brown dog with a red collar sits by the door holding a yellow lead in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. A teal watering can, two potted plants and a coiled hose on a reel are on the lawn. Nothing looks impossible.
