# Picture 3

## Free description
A playground behind a dark metal railing fence on a sunny day, with a red-and-yellow slide on the left, a sandpit with a bucket and spade at the front left, a green bin, and a tree on the right. In the centre-back a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands licking a pink ice-cream cone. On the right is a red seesaw: an older man in a cap and brown waistcoat sits on the low end reading a book or newspaper, and a small girl in a pink dress sits on the raised end holding the handle. The girl has her hand near her mouth and looks surprised or worried, perhaps because she is stuck up high while the man ignores her. Nothing else is happening; the slide and sandpit are unused.
