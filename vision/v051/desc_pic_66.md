A sunny park with a winding path, a pond, a lamp post, an empty bench, trees, a flower bed and a bin. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a purple and a yellow balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other towards a blue balloon that has escaped and is floating away into the sky, its string dangling. He cannot reach it; it is already well above him. Next to the pond a girl in a pink dress holds a piece of bread and has thrown crumbs into the water, and a white duck is swimming over and eating them. At the front a white dog with brown spots crouches in a playful bow with a tennis ball in front of it, tail wagging, as if wanting someone to play.
