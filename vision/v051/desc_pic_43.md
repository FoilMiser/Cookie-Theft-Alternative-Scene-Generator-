A man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers stands at the edge of the pond holding a paper bag and tossing bits of bread to a white duck, which is pecking at the crumbs on the water. A little girl in a pink dress holding a yellow balloon on a string is running along near the path toward the front. In the middle of the grass there is a wooden ladder leaning against nothing — it stands upright on its own with no wall or tree to support it. A tall street lamp stands in the centre, a grey rock lies on the grass at the front, and a green bin is in the bottom right corner.
