# pic_42

## Free description
A sunny town street/plaza with a row of colourful shops with striped awnings behind a paved square. On the left, by a fruit and vegetable stall, a girl in a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping leaves with a broom, and next to her a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is "sweeping" dust toward a dustpan using a giant fork instead of a broom — the fork is huge, nearly as long as his body. In the middle an older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. A girl in a pink dress sits on a bench drinking from a cup. A green bicycle is parked in a bike rack. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue-and-white beach ball that is stuck in the branches of a tree; he looks worried. There is a street lamp, two flower boxes and potted plants in the corners.
