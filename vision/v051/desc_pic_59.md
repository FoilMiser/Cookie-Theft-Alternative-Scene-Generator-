# pic_59

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a fence and gate and a path on the left, a washing line in the middle and a house with a blue door on the right. On the left, a child with dark curly hair in a yellow top and orange shorts holds the strings of a red and a purple balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other hand towards a blue balloon that is floating away over the fence, its string trailing - the child seems to have just let it go and looks upset. In the centre-right, an older grey-haired woman with glasses, a teal top and grey skirt is watering a flower bed with a grey watering can. Strangely, the water from the can shoots upward in an arc instead of pouring down, with drops flying up above the flowers. On the far right, a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench reading a red book. A yellow striped top and blue jeans hang on the washing line, and an empty wheelbarrow stands at the front.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water stream: it starts at the can's spout low down and rises to drops at the top, so it looks like water flying upward (impossible for a watering can). Another reading is that the stream is simply drawn from top to bottom, falling onto the flowers, but the can is below the arc so upward is more likely.
- The watering can is grey, not the usual teal one; it might be read as a jug.
- The blue balloon could have been let go deliberately or by accident; the child's reaching pose suggests accident.
