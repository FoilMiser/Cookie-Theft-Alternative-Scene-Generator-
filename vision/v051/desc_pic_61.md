# pic_61

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a fence, a gate and path on the left and a house with a blue door on the right. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands at the fence painting it blue with a long-handled roller; a few planks are already blue. On the grass behind him are a paint can and a paint tray with blue paint. A white dog with brown spots is walking away to the left with blue paws, leaving a trail of blue paw prints from the paint tray across the grass - it has clearly stepped in the paint and is tracking it around, and the man has not noticed. On the far left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sweeps brown leaves near the gate with a yellow broom; the dog is heading towards her and the path. A teal watering can and an empty wheelbarrow are at the front, two potted plants are by the fence, and a flower bed is by the house.
