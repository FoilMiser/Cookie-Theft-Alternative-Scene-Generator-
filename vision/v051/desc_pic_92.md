## Free description
A sunny back garden with a gate and paved path at the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door at the right. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence blue with a roller on a handle; a section of about three planks is already blue. At her feet are a tin of blue paint and a paint tray of blue paint. A small white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has evidently stepped in the paint tray: a trail of blue paw prints runs across the grass from the tray to the dog, and its paws look blue. The dog is walking towards the path on the left, looking pleased, and the woman has her back to it and does not seem to have noticed. Two potted plants, a teal watering can, a wheelbarrow and a flower bed by the house complete the scene. It looks like the dog is about to track blue paint onto the path.
