# pic_102

## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, trees, a pond with bulrushes, a lamp post, a bin and a winding sandy path. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands near the pond flying a yellow diamond kite with bows on its tail; he holds the string spool up in one hand and the string runs up to the kite high in the sky. In the front centre a garden sprinkler, fed by a dark blue hose running along the ground, sprays arcs of water to the right. The water lands right on a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is getting soaked and has a little puddle under it. A grey wheelbarrow stands on the right next to the path, and a flower bed is in the front left. No one seems to have noticed the box getting wet.
