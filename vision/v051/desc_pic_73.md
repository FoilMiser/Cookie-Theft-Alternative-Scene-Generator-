# pic_73

## Free description
A busy, sunny back garden with a cream house on the left, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a path on the right. An old man in a cap and brown waistcoat sits on a bench at the front left reading a red book, while a blue bird beside him on the bench is pecking at his sandwich without him noticing. An older woman with grey hair and glasses is painting the fence blue with a paint roller; a section of the fence is already blue, and a paint tin and tray sit at her feet. Blue paw prints lead from the paint tray across the grass to a white-and-brown spotted dog whose paws are blue, so the dog has stepped in the paint. A man in a green shirt has his arms up as if to catch a red-and-white ball that a small girl in a pink dress has just thrown (a dotted line shows the ball's path from her). A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is walking while reading a book and is about to trip over a cardboard box on the grass in front of him. A child in a yellow top and orange shorts jumps with arms raised, a woman in a purple top stands by the gate with a metal bucket upside down on her head, and an orange cat is asleep on the path. A red bird flies in the sky. The wheelbarrow in the middle has a square wheel, which is impossible.
