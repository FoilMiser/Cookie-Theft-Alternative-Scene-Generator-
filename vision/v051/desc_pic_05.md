# Picture 5

## Free description
A quiet park on a sunny day with a pond with reeds on the left, a winding sandy path on the right, trees and bushes, a tall black lamp post and a green bin. A red bird is perched on top of the lamp post. In the front centre a girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes stands holding a pink ice-cream cone up to her mouth, eating it. She is the only person present. Nothing else is happening and nothing looks wrong.
