## Free description
A sunny park with a winding path, a pond, a lamp post, trees, a flower bed, a rock and a bin. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a bench reading a red book. On the bench next to her is a sandwich, and a blue bird is right beside it, pecking at it. The woman is absorbed in her book and does not notice the bird, which seems to be stealing her lunch. In the middle, a small girl in a pink dress walks across the grass eating a pink ice-cream cone. Nobody else is around.
