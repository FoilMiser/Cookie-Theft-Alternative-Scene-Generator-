## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house on the left (red roof, blue door), a wooden fence across the back and trees beyond. An older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt stands by the fence painting it blue with a paint roller on a pole. A few fence planks to her right are already painted blue, while the rest are still brown, so she is partway through the job. A paint tin with blue paint sits on the grass at her feet. In the garden there is a flower bed with colourful flowers, a metal swing set with one empty red swing, a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can in the front, and a paved path leading to a wooden gate on the right. No one else is present.
