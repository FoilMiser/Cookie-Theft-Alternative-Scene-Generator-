## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left, a wooden fence and gate at the back, and a paved path on the right. A boy in a red cap and blue top is flying a yellow kite high in the sky, holding the string reel up. An older man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat is holding the nozzle of a long garden hose and looking at it, puzzled; no water is coming out. The hose runs from a reel by the house across the lawn, and a brown dog is standing right on the hose, which would block the water. On the path at the right an older woman with grey hair and glasses is sweeping dirt into a red dustpan, but she is using a giant fork instead of a broom, which is odd. There is also a wheelbarrow, two potted plants and a flower bed.
