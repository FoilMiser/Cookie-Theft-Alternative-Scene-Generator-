## Free description
A sunny park with a pond, a lamp post, a bench, a flower bed and a winding path. A girl with dark hair in a ponytail, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, is walking across the grass in the front centre. She is holding a pink ice-cream cone up and looking down at it rather than where she is going. A grey rock sits on the grass a little ahead and to her right. A white duck is swimming on the pond. A green rubbish bin stands next to the lamp post. If she keeps walking without looking, she might trip over the rock.
