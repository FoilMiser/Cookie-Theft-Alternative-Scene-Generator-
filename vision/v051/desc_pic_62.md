# pic_62

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a house with a blue door on the right, and a tree behind the fence. On the far left a man in a green shirt is walking in through the garden gate while reading an open book or paper; a cardboard box sits on the grass just ahead of him. In the front left a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. In the middle an older woman with grey hair and glasses holds up a garden hose with its nozzle pointing upwards; the hose runs across the lawn to a hose reel near the house, and no water is coming out. A brown dog stands right on, or just next to, the hose and looks pleased with itself, which may be why no water is flowing. At the back by the fence a girl in a pink dress reaches up towards a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the tree branches above the fence. On the right a boy in a red shirt sits on a bench reading a red book. The man reading as he walks looks like he could trip over the box.
