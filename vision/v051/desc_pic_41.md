## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, gate and paved path on the left and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the centre a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is jumping in the air with both arms up, looking excited — his shadow is on the grass below him. On the path at the left, an older man with grey hair, a flat cap and a brown waistcoat is sweeping a small pile of leaves with a broom. At the bottom centre-right a grey cat is crouched low, creeping toward a red bird that is pecking at seeds on the grass at the bottom right; the cat looks like it is about to pounce. Two potted plants stand on the lawn behind the boy, a teal watering can is in the front left, and there is a flower bed in front of the house.
