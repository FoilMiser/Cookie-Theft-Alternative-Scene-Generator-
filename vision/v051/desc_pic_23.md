## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back and a gate on the right. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands in front of the fence using a long-handled paint roller to paint the fence blue; a section of the fence to her left is already painted blue, and a small paint tin sits by her feet. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands on a paved path sweeping a small pile of brown leaves with a broom. In the middle stands an empty swing set with a red seat. In front of the house there is a bed of colourful flowers, a coiled garden hose on a reel, a grey wheelbarrow and a green watering can lying on the lawn. A birdhouse on a tall pole stands by the gate. Nothing looks impossible.
