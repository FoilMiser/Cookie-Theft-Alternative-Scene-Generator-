## Free description
A sunny park with a blue pond on the left, a flower bed, a bench, a winding sandy path on the right and a tall black lamp post in the middle. A man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands just beside the lamp post, at the far right edge of the pond, eating a pink ice-cream cone. In the front centre a white goat with small dark horns and a red collar is tied by a rope to a short wooden stake in the ground. The goat has its head down and is eating a small tuft of grass. A green rubbish bin stands in the bottom-left corner. Nothing dramatic seems about to happen; it is a calm scene. The man is standing very close to the pond's edge and the lamp post, almost touching both.
