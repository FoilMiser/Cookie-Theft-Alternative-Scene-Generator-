# pic_27

## Free description
A sunny back garden almost identical in layout to a typical backyard: cream house with a blue door on the right, wooden fence at the back, gate and paved path on the left. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands beside the flower bed spraying water onto the flowers with a garden hose; an arc of water comes from the nozzle in her hand. The hose runs along the ground from a hose reel at front right, near the bench, curving round the flower bed to her. She is the only person and there are no animals. Two potted plants sit by the fence, a grey wheelbarrow is parked at the front, and an empty wooden bench is at front right. Nothing looks impossible.
