## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house (red roof, blue door) on the left, a wooden fence with a gate on the right, and a paved path leading to that gate. In the front left, an older man in a grey cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. On the bench beside him lies a sandwich, and a small blue bird is perched next to it, pecking at the sandwich while the man is not looking - it seems the bird is about to steal his lunch. On the right, an older grey-haired woman with glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt is sweeping brown leaves on the path with a yellow broom. In the middle there is a washing line with blue jeans and a yellow striped top hanging on it, and an empty swing set with a red seat. A hose reel, an empty wheelbarrow and a teal watering can lie around the lawn. A flower bed sits near the house.
