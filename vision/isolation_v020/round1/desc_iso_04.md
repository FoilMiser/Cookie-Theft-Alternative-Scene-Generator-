# iso_04

## What I see
An empty backyard swing set stands on green grass in front of a wooden picket fence with pointed tops. Dark hedges or trees show above the fence. The swing set has a dark blue-gray metal frame: a horizontal top bar held up by an A-shaped pair of legs at each end. One red, flat swing seat hangs from two thin ropes or chains. No one is on it. At the bottom center there is a teal watering can, cut off by the edge, with its spout pointing up and to the left. At the bottom left there is a gray, tray-like object with a brown wooden plank or handle under it, and on the right side a beige paved path or patio cuts diagonally across the grass.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The swing seat is tilted, not level, and the two ropes are not parallel. They hang from points close together on the top bar and spread toward the seat. The right end of the seat overlaps the front leg of the right-hand A-frame, and the right rope seems to disappear behind that leg. So the seat looks as if it passes through the frame leg. It could be meant as the swing caught mid-swing, but it reads as a clipping/overlap error.
- The gray-and-brown object at the bottom left is too cut off to identify. It could be a wheelbarrow (gray tub with wooden handles), a sandbox lid, a bench, or a board leaning on something.
- The watering can is cut off by the bottom edge, so only its top half, handle and spout are visible.
- At the very top right edge there is a tiny dark mark. It may be part of something cut off by the frame, or a stray line.
- The swing-set legs end in rounded tips on the grass with no feet or anchors. That is a simple drawing style but reads fine.
