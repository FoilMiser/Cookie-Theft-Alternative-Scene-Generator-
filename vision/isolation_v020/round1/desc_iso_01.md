# iso_01

## What I see
A woman with dark hair tied back in a ponytail, wearing a purple short-sleeved top, teal trousers and dark shoes, stands on green grass in front of a horizontal-plank wooden fence. She holds out one arm straight in front of her, gripping a dark handle attached to a round gray object, and a blue arc of water pours from the edge of that object down onto a flower bed. The flower bed is an oval patch of brown soil with red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers on green stems, with a few water droplets falling near the soil. She appears to be watering the flowers.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The watering tool does not look like a normal watering can: it is just a flat gray disc on a straight stick handle, with no body, tank or spout. It reads at least as easily as a frying pan or saucepan seen from above or the side (or a ladle) as it does a watering can or a sprinkler rose seen end-on. Water coming out of a pan-shaped object looks odd.
- The water comes out in a single smooth stream from the disc's rim, not as a spray, which adds to the "pouring from a pan" reading.
- The water stream is drawn on top of the flowers and seems to end in the middle of the bed, so it partly overlaps or passes in front of the pink and orange flowers rather than landing clearly on them.
- Her proportions are a bit stretched: the legs are very long and thin compared with a short torso, and the shoes look small and close together, one partly overlapping the other.
- The outstretched arm is very straight and stiff, and the handle seems to stick slightly past her hand on the far side.
- Her face is small, in near-profile and turned toward the flowers, with little detail, so her expression can't be read.
