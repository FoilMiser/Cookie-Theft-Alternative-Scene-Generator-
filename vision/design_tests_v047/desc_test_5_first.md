This is a sunny day on a paved town square in front of a row of four colourful two-storey shops with striped awnings (red/white, green/white and orange/white), with a bright sun in the top-right corner and two small clouds. On the left, a man with dark skin, a beard, green T-shirt and beige trousers is walking along while reading an open book held in front of him. He is not looking where he is going, and a cardboard box sits on the ground just ahead of and below him, so he seems about to trip over it (the tree in a round planter is also right beside him). In the centre, a man with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands holding a pink ice-cream cone up to his mouth, eyes closed as if enjoying it; there are brown marks around his mouth that could be a moustache or ice cream/chocolate smeared on his face. Behind him is an empty wooden bench and a planter box of flowers. On the right, a woman with dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers walks while holding a red leash attached to a white dog with brown spots in front of her. Behind her is a market stall with a teal-and-white striped canopy and crates of red, orange, yellow and green fruit, which nobody seems to be tending. A tall black street lamp stands in the middle background, and in the foreground there is a grey rubbish bin and a small potted plant in the bottom-right corner. Nothing looks clearly impossible; the main "about to happen" moment is the reading man walking toward the box.
