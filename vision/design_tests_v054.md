# v0.54 design tests (whole scenes)

v0.54 changes two figures of the cast in their reading poses and no placement rule (revision 49 stands), so its whole-scene test is the bench of `isolation_v054.md`: six pictures of the man in the red shirt reading, three of them v0.53's screened pictures built again from their own records and three built for the bench, each with the same templates and the same people in both arms, read with the screen's own prompt by twelve fresh readers (`isolation_v054/bench`; the pictures are `blind/pic_NNN.png`, the readings `desc_pic_NNN.md`, and which picture is which is in `isolation_v054/bench_map.json`).

What the twelve readings of the new pose were read for:

- **Who he is.** By the first inventory line that names his red top, a man in 11 readings of 12 with his head up, for 4 of 12 in v0.53's pose read by the six other readers.
- **That he reads.** All twelve descriptions of the new pose have him reading a book, on a bench or as he walks, as all twelve of v0.53's pose do.
- **That the walker does not see the box.** Three of the six pictures hold a walker with an open book and a box in the way: the man in the red shirt in two and the man in green, whose head is up as well, in one. All six readings of the new pose have the walker about to trip, as all six of v0.53's do: "In the centre, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans walks fast (there are motion lines behind him) while reading an open book held in front of his face."
- **Anything new and odd about the head.** No unsure section of the twelve has a line on his head, his neck or where he looks.

The same bench read two drawings of the scene that were not taken (the bicycle's larger square wheels and tread on the barrow's tyre), so no picture of the release holds them and neither has a design test.

No rule was added after these readings, and no picture was built again for them.
