# Picture 56

## Free description
This is a sunny, quiet park with green hills behind, a pond on the left, a lamppost in the middle, a wooden bench and a winding sandy path on the right. In the front left an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt is kneeling on one knee by a colourful flower bed, reaching down to the soil next to a terracotta pot holding a pink flower, as if she is about to plant it. In the middle front there is a white goat with a curved dark horn, bending its head down to eat a tuft of grass on the lawn, which is an unexpected animal to see loose in a park. On the right a young man with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands at the edge of the path holding a small white bag and scattering seeds or crumbs, which fall in a dotted line down to the path where a red bird and a blue bird are pecking at them. The bench is empty and nobody is by the pond. Apart from the goat being there, nothing looks clearly wrong.
