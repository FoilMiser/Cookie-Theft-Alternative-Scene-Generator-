# Picture 57

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and a blue door, with a wooden fence along the back, a closed gate and a stone path on the right. Near the house, a woman (or girl) with a brown ponytail, a blue long-sleeved top, black trousers and white trainers is watering a colourful flower bed with a teal watering can, and the water is sprinkling down onto the flowers. In the middle of the lawn a woman (or teenage girl) with dark hair, a purple T-shirt and teal trousers is digging a hole with a shovel, with a lump of earth on the blade and a pile of dirt next to the hole; she is looking to the right. On the path at the bottom right, a white dog with brown patches and a blue collar is crouched in a play bow with its tail wagging, and a yellow-green tennis ball lies in front of its nose, as if it wants someone to throw the ball. Two potted plants stand on the grass near the fence. Nothing looks wrong or impossible.
