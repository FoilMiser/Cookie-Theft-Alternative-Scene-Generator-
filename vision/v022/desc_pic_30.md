# pic_30

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back and a gate with a paved path on the right. In the bottom left, a man with a beard in a green shirt and khaki trousers sits on a bench, eyes closed, sipping a drink from a red cup through a straw. He looks relaxed. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands by the flower bed watering the flowers with a teal watering can. The water does not pour down onto the flowers, though. It shoots up in a curve into the air above the bed, with drops at the top, which is impossible. Near the back fence, a person with a brown ponytail in a blue hoodie is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller or brush. A section of the fence is already blue, and a tin of blue paint sits on the ground beside them. To the right stands a tall wooden pole with a birdhouse on top (no bird visible), two potted plants, and an empty grey wheelbarrow in the bottom middle.

- The man's closed eyes could mean he is enjoying his drink or that he is dozing.
- The painter's tool could be a paint roller or a brush on a pole; the head is small and hard to make out.
- The blue fence section matches the colour of the house door and the paint tin, which is consistent with the painting.
