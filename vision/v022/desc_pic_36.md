## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the sun in the top left corner and two white clouds. A cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the right, with a tree behind it and a bush beside it; a wooden fence runs across the back with a gate on the far left, and a paved path runs from the gate down to the bottom left. Near the gate there is a birdhouse on a tall wooden pole. On the left side of the lawn is a grey swing set, and a small boy with curly dark hair, a yellow shirt and orange shorts is sitting on the red swing seat, smiling with his eyes closed, with motion lines showing he is swinging. Behind the swing set, a woman with a brown ponytail, blue top and dark trousers is standing at the fence painting it blue with a long brush; a few fence boards are already blue, and a paint tin with blue paint sits on the grass by her feet. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress with short dark hair is kneeling by the flower bed in front of the house, reaching for a small pot holding a pink flower, as if about to plant it. In the foreground are a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow, and a wooden bench stands on the lawn at the bottom right.
