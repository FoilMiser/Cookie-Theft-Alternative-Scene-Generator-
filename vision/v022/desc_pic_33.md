# Picture 33

## Free description
This is a park on a gloomy day: the sky is flat grey and there are three dark grey storm clouds, with no sun. There are rolling green hills and hedges in the background, a small tree on the left, a large tree on the right, a blue pond with bulrushes at each end on the left, and a winding sandy path on the right. In the left foreground an elderly man in a flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is pointing straight up at the sky with a surprised look, wide eyes and an open "o" mouth. Next to him a small girl in a pink dress, with dark hair in a bun, is jumping in the air with both arms raised and a big happy smile; her shadow is on the ground below her. A tall black lamp post stands in the middle of the park. To the right of the lamp post a wooden ladder stands upright on the grass by itself, not leaning against anything, which looks impossible. There is also a green rubbish bin in the bottom left corner and a grey rock in the front centre. It looks as though the dark clouds may be about to bring rain, and the man may be noticing this, though nothing is shown falling from the sky.

- Girl, centre-left foreground: dark hair in a bun, pink dress, yellow shoes; jumping in the air with both arms up, eyes closed and smiling; shadow on the grass beneath her.
- Storm clouds, top: three dark grey clouds in a grey sky.
- Pond, left: blue water with bulrushes/cattails at the left and right ends.
- Lamp post, centre: tall black post with a lantern top; standing on the grass behind the people.
- Ladder, centre-right: wooden straight ladder with rungs, standing upright on the grass on its own, slightly tilted, not touching anything.
- Path, right: sandy winding path running from the bottom of the picture back toward the hills.
- Trees: small tree on the left, large tree on the right; round green bushes at far left and far right.
- Rubbish bin, bottom left corner: green, ribbed.
- Rock, front centre: grey rounded rock on the grass.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- What the man is pointing at: there is nothing in the sky but the dark clouds. Readings: he is pointing at the clouds / a coming storm or a first raindrop; or he is pointing at something that is not drawn.
- The girl's jump could be excitement/joy, trying to reach or catch something above her, or reacting to whatever the man is pointing at. Her happy face contrasts with his surprised/worried face.
- The free-standing ladder looks unintentionally (or deliberately) impossible: a straight ladder cannot stand upright without leaning on something, and there is nothing at its top. It is also odd simply to have a ladder in the middle of a park.
- Whether the lamp is lit is unclear; its glass is white, which could mean it is on or just pale.
- The man's upper body is drawn over the pond, but his feet are well in front of it on the grass, so he is standing in front of the pond, not in it.
