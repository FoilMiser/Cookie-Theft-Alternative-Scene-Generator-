# Picture 55

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny town street with a row of coloured shop buildings (pink, green, peach and cream) with striped awnings, and a wide paved pavement in front. On the left a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands at a fruit stall and is sprinkling seeds or crumbs from her hand onto the ground, where a small blue bird is pecking at them. In the front, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is pushing a teal pram with a sleeping baby under a pink spotted blanket. Just behind him, an older man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown vest is walking while reading an open book, not looking where he is going, and a red ball is lying on the pavement right in his path, so he looks about to trip over it. Further back, a child with curly hair in a yellow top and orange shorts is holding an empty ice-cream cone and has a hand up to their face as if crying, because the scoop of pink ice cream has fallen on the ground; a brown dog with a red collar is sniffing or licking the fallen ice cream. On the right, a little girl in a pink dress walks along holding a yellow balloon on a string. There is also a black lamppost, a tree in a round planter, a cardboard box by the stall and potted plants in the bottom corners.
