# Picture 40

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny park with a pond, a winding path, trees, rolling green hills and a tall black lamp post. On the far left a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is jumping in the air with both arms raised, looking happy. Next to a flower bed, a woman in a yellow dress is holding a green watering can, but the water coming out of it goes upward in a curve toward the pond instead of falling down onto the flowers, which is impossible. An old man with grey hair, a blue cap and a brown vest is walking along while reading an open book, and a red bucket is right in front of his feet, so he looks about to trip over it. Beside him a brown dog is sitting up holding an open newspaper in its front paws as if reading it, which is also impossible. In the background a small girl in a pink dress holds a yellow balloon, and a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. On the right, a woman in a purple top sits on a bench reading a red book while a blue bird has landed on the bench beside her next to her sandwich, and looks about to take it. On the far right, a person in a blue hoodie is pointing up in surprise at a blue-and-white ball stuck on a branch in a tree. A red bird is perched on the top of the lamp post, and there are cattails at the edges of the pond.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water from the watering can: it could be read as water spraying up and backward out of the can (impossible), or as water being sucked up out of the pond into the can. Either way it does not land on the flowers.
- The red item on the newspaper dog's head could be a small red hat, a bow, or a red ear.
- The old man might be walking toward the bucket and about to trip, or he might be standing still; his legs look mid-stride.
- The blue bird could be about to steal the sandwich, or just perching next to it.
- The person pointing at the ball could be a boy or a girl; it is probably their ball.
- The jumping child might be cheering, playing, or celebrating; their shadow is drawn well below their feet, which shows they are in the air.
- The dog reading the newspaper and the upward-flowing water both look deliberately impossible rather than drawing errors.
