# Picture 10

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills, trees, a winding sandy path on the left, a black lamp post in the middle and a blue pond on the right. A man in a red T-shirt, blue jeans and a brown belt stands on the right in front of the pond, holding a camera up to his eye and pointing it to the left. A young girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket in the lower middle, beside a wicker picnic basket, and she is turned toward the man, smiling, as if posing for his photo. The strangest thing in the picture is a tall wooden ladder standing in the grass to the left of the lamp post: it is tilted at an angle but is not leaning against anything, so it should fall over. There is a grey rock in the foreground in front of the blanket and a green rubbish bin in the bottom-right corner. Cattails/reeds grow at both ends of the pond, and there are bushes on the left and right edges. Apart from the ladder, it looks like a pleasant picnic with someone taking a photograph.
