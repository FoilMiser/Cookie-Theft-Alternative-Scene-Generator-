# Picture 44

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream-coloured house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a tall wooden fence along the back with a gate on the left, and a paved path down the left side. In the centre, a woman with dark hair in a yellow dress is holding the end of a garden hose up and looking at it, but only a single drop of water is coming out. The reason is that a white goat is standing right on the hose, squashing it, and the hose is swollen into a big bulge on the other side of the goat, between it and the hose reel. If the goat steps off, the water could suddenly spray out, possibly into the woman's face. By the blue door, a brown-and-white dog is sitting holding its red lead in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. Strangely, an orange goldfish is in the tree behind the house, which is impossible. There is also an empty swing set with a red seat, a grey wheelbarrow at the front, a clothesline with a yellow striped towel and blue jeans, a birdhouse on a pole, a flower bed, and a sun with two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fish in the tree could be read as a real goldfish stuck in the tree (impossible), or as a fish-shaped decoration or toy hung in the tree; it looks like a real fish.
- The bulge in the hose could be read as water backing up because the goat is pinching it, or as a fat section of hose; the drawing suggests trapped water.
- The woman may be about to get sprayed in the face if the goat moves, since she is peering at the nozzle.
- The dog may want a walk, or may simply be holding its lead.
- The wheelbarrow's leg/stand near the handles looks a little like a small dark boot or foot.
- The goat in a domestic back garden is unusual but possible.
