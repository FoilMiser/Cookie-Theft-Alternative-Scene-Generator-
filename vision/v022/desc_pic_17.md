# Picture 17

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. An older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses is painting the fence blue with a paintbrush; one section of the fence behind her is already blue, and a paint can and paint tray sit on the grass at her feet. A white dog with brown spots has stepped in the paint tray: its paws are blue and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the tray across the lawn to where the dog is now walking, towards the path and gate on the right. In front of the house, a girl or young woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is watering a flower bed, but she is pouring the water out of a frying pan instead of a watering can. In the front centre a white goat with dark curved horns has its head down and is eating a tuft of grass. There is a birdhouse on a tall pole near the right, a tree behind the house, two clouds and a bright sun. The odd things are the frying pan being used to water flowers and a goat grazing in an ordinary back garden; the dog looks about to spread blue paint further, maybe onto the path.
