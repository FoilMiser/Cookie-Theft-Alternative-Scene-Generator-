# Picture 41

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream-coloured house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a tall wooden fence along the back with a gate on the left, and a paved path running down the left side. In the middle near the fence, a girl with a brown ponytail in a blue hoodie and dark trousers is reaching up and holding a kite string, but her purple kite is stuck in the leaves of the tree beside the house. In the front centre, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is kicking an orange ball, with motion lines showing the kick. On the path at the front left, a woman with dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers is using a giant silver fork, like an oversized dinner fork, to push a pile of dirt toward a red dustpan, with a little cloud of dust rising. Using a huge eating fork as a broom is strange and impossible. There are two potted plants and a round bush near the fence, a flower bed with colourful flowers in front of the house, and a bright sun and two clouds in the sky.
