# Picture 50

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a small town square or pedestrian shopping street paved with grey tiles. Behind it is a row of two-storey shop buildings in cream, peach, sage green and pink, with striped awnings in red and white, green and white, and orange and white. On the left a tree grows out of a round planter, and next to it a teal bicycle is parked in a grey bike rack. In the middle an older man in a grey cap, white shirt, brown vest and dark trousers sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. Just behind him a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is watering flowers in a grey planter box with a teal watering can, and you can see the water pouring out onto the flowers. To the right there is a tall black street lamp. On the far right is a market stall with a teal and white striped canopy and four crates of fruit: red, orange, yellow and green, perhaps apples, oranges, lemons and limes. There is no stallholder and there are no shoppers. It is a calm, ordinary scene, and nothing seems about to go wrong.
