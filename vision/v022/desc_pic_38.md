This is a sunny back garden behind a house, with a cream-coloured house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a wooden gate in the fence on the right. A boy with brown hair in a blue hoodie and dark trousers stands on the paved path on the right, sweeping the path with a broom that has yellow bristles. There is also a flower bed with colourful flowers by the house, two potted plants near the fence, a birdhouse on a tall pole, a tree behind the house, and a bright sun and two clouds in the sky. The odd things are that there is a goat in an ordinary back garden, and that the sprinkler is soaking a cardboard box that someone has left on the grass.
