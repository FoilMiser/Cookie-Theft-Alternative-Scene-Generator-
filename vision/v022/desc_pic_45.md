# Picture 45

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a fenced playground: there is a sun in the top right, two white clouds, a dark metal railing fence across the back with green bushes behind it, and a tree on the far left. On the left there is a red seesaw, and it is tipped steeply. A small girl in a pink dress is sitting on the high, left end, up in the air, holding the handle. Her feet do not reach the ground and there are little motion lines at her feet, as if she is kicking or wriggling. On the low, right end sits an older man in a grey-blue cap, a white long-sleeved shirt, a brown vest and dark trousers. He is reading an open book or newspaper and does not seem to notice the girl. Because he is so heavy, he keeps his end down and the girl is stuck up high, so she can't seesaw. In the background on the right there is a slide with a red frame, an orange and yellow chute and a ladder, and a green rubbish bin stands by the fence in the middle. In the bottom right there is a sandbox with a red bucket and a blue spade in it, but no children, and a grey rock sits next to it. Nobody else is in the playground.
