# Picture 51

## Free description
This is a playground on a gloomy day. The sky is grey, there is no sun, and three dark storm clouds hang overhead, so it looks like it is about to rain. Behind the bench on the left, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands pointing up at the dark clouds with his mouth open, as if warning that rain is coming. In front of him, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes sits on a bench reading a red book. Next to her on the bench is a big sandwich, and a blue bird has landed on the end of the bench and is pecking at it. She hasn't noticed that her lunch is being stolen. In the bottom left, a delivery worker in an orange cap, an orange high-visibility vest and blue trousers is walking along carrying a cardboard box. In the middle of the grass, a black-and-white cat is creeping up on a red bird that is pecking the ground, and nearby an orange cat is curled up asleep. In the sandbox on the right, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is kneeling and building a sandcastle with a little red flag on top. There is also an empty swing set in the centre, an empty slide on the right, a tree, a bush and a grey rock.
