# Picture 34

## Free description
This is a park on a sunny day, with a bright sun in the top right corner and two white clouds in a blue sky. There are rolling green hills and hedges in the background, a small tree on the left, a large tree on the right, and a winding sandy path running from the bottom right back toward the hills. On the left is a blue pond with bulrushes at each end. An elderly man in a grey-blue flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands just in front of the right-hand side of the pond, holding a long fishing rod out over the water. His fishing line drops down to a red-and-white float bobbing in the pond, with a little ripple ring around it. A tall black lamp post stands in the middle of the park with a green rubbish bin next to it and a round bush behind it. There is another green rubbish bin in the bottom left corner and a grey rock in the front centre. It is a quiet, ordinary scene; nothing looks wrong or impossible, and nothing seems to have been caught yet.
