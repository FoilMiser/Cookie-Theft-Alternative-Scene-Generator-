# Picture 46

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden: there is a sun in the top right, two clouds, a cream house with a red roof and a blue front door on the left, and a tree behind the house. A tall wooden fence runs across the back of the garden, with a brown wooden gate in it on the right. A woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers stands in the middle of the lawn. She holds a paint roller on a long handle and is painting the fence blue; only a small section of a few planks in the middle is blue so far. Behind her on the grass are a grey paint can and a paint tray full of blue paint. A trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the grass to the right. At the end of the trail, on the paving path by the gate, stands a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar, and its paws are blue. The dog has clearly stepped in the paint tray and is tracking blue paint away, and it looks like it will keep spreading paw prints along the path. The woman has her back to the dog and doesn't seem to have noticed. In the front of the garden there is a flower bed of colourful flowers by the house, an empty grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Whether the dog is heading out through the gate, walking up the path toward the house, or just wandering: it faces right, near the gate, so it might be about to leave.
- The woman's direction: she is shown in profile facing the painted section, so she is turned away from the dog. She may be stepping sideways along the fence as she paints.
- The painted section includes the pointed tops of the planks, so it looks like a neat blue rectangle in the middle of the fence rather than a fence being painted from one end. That is a little odd but not impossible.
- The paw prints are drawn as small clusters of dots and pads. They are clearly paw prints, but they come in pairs that don't quite form a natural four-legged gait.
- Nothing is floating or overlapping in an impossible way.
