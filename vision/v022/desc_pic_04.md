# Picture 04

## Free description
This back garden is under a grey, overcast sky with three big dark storm clouds and no sun, so it looks like it is about to rain. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, and there is a wooden fence across the back. In the front left, a woman with dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers is sitting on a wooden bench reading a red book, paying no attention to anything else. In the centre-left, a girl or young woman in a yellow dress is watering the flower bed with a garden hose, and a stream of water is arcing onto the flowers. The hose runs from a reel next to the bench. To the right, a person in a blue hoodie and dark trousers is looking up at the sky with a surprised, open mouth and pointing up at the dark clouds, as if warning that rain is coming. The ironic part is that someone is watering the flowers just before a storm that will water them anyway. On the right there is an empty swing set with a red seat, a birdhouse on a pole, and a closed wooden gate with a paved path leading toward the front.
