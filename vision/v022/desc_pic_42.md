## Free description

On the far left a small girl in a pink dress is standing under a tree and pointing up at a blue-and-white ball that is stuck on a branch in the tree. In the centre at the front, a small brown-and-white puppy is curled up asleep on the grass. On the right near the pond, a woman in a yellow dress is sitting on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket with her legs stretched out and her eyes closed, smiling, with a wicker picnic basket beside her; she looks like she is relaxing in the sun. There are two green rubbish bins, one beside the lamp post and one in the bottom right corner, and a grey rock at the front. The sun is in the top left with two clouds. Nothing looks impossible; the main problem in the scene is the ball stuck in the tree.
