# Picture 22

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a gate with a paved path on the right. In the middle, a man with brown hair, a red T-shirt and jeans is hanging washing on a clothesline between two wooden posts; a pair of blue jeans is already pegged up and he is reaching up to peg a white T-shirt. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress with short dark hair is raking a small pile of leaves on the path by the gate. There is an empty swing set with a red swing to the right of centre. In front of the house there is a wooden bench, a hose reel with a teal hose, and a flower bed. At the front centre there is a grey wheelbarrow, but its wheel is square, which wouldn't roll. A teal watering can sits on the grass at the front right.
