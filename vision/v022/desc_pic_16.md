# Picture 16

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and a blue door, with a tall wooden fence, a closed gate in the top-left corner and a paved path running down the left side. On the path at the far left, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is sweeping a pile of dirt and pebbles toward a red dustpan, but instead of a broom or rake he is using a huge silver dinner fork, which is clearly the wrong (and impossibly large) tool; a little puff of dust rises from the pile. An orange cat is walking along the very top of the fence. In the middle of the garden there is a clothesline strung between two wooden posts, with a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. On the right, a girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes is flying a yellow kite with a bow-tied tail high in the sky on the left, and the kite string runs diagonally across the clothesline, so it might snag on it. Two potted plants stand near the fence, a grey wheelbarrow sits empty in the foreground, and a flower bed with a hose reel is in front of the house. Overall it is a normal garden scene except for the giant fork being used for sweeping.
