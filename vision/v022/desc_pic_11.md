# Picture 11

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and a blue door, enclosed by a tall wooden fence with a closed gate in the top-left corner. There are no people or animals anywhere in the picture. On the left half of the lawn stands a grey metal A-frame swing set with a single red swing seat hanging still and empty. The odd thing is a closed cardboard box up in the sky above the fence, tilted at an angle, as if it is floating, flying or falling, with nobody nearby who could have thrown it. On the right there is a round flower bed full of colourful flowers and a teal garden hose wound on a reel next to it. A teal watering can stands on the grass at the bottom, near a paved path that runs from the gate down to the bottom-left corner. A tree shows above the fence by the house and a bush grows against the house wall. Otherwise the garden is quiet and empty.
