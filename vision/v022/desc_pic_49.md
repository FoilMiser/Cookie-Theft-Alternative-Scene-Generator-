# Picture 49

## Free description
This is a sunny but very windy back garden. White wind streaks run across the sky, and green leaves are blowing off the tree behind the house on the left. A washing line hangs between two wooden posts in the middle, and a pair of blue trousers and a red-and-yellow striped towel or garment pegged to it are being blown sideways by the wind. On the right, near the wooden gate, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans has his arms flung up and his mouth open, because his red cap has just been blown off his head and is flying away above him. In the middle of the lawn a child in a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes stands with hands on hips, wearing an upside-down metal bucket on their head. On a swing set on the right, a smiling child in a yellow top and orange shorts is swinging on a red swing, with motion lines showing the movement. The cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, with a flower bed in front of it, an empty wooden bench in the bottom left, a teal watering can near the path, and a paving path running down from the gate on the right.
