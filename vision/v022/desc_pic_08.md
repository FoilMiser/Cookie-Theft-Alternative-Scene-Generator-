# Picture 08

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left and a wooden fence across the back. In the centre, a man in a red top and blue jeans is holding the end of a garden hose up close to his face with both hands, peering into the nozzle because only a drip is coming out. Behind him, the hose runs back across the lawn, and a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is standing right on it. On the reel side of the dog, the hose is swollen into a big bulge of trapped water, so when the dog steps off, the man is likely to get sprayed in the face. At the back, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, a teal top and a grey skirt is painting the fence blue with a brush, with a paint can at her feet; one section of the fence is already blue. A small blue bird is perched on the top of the fence to her left. On the right, a person in a green shirt and khaki trousers is walking along with their nose in an open book, about to step onto a red-and-white ball lying on the grass right in front of their feet, so they look about to trip. A small tan-and-white puppy is curled up asleep on the grass nearby. In the front right, a boy in a blue hoodie is reading a red book in a sitting position on the paved path, but there is no bench, chair or anything under him, so he is sitting on thin air, which looks impossible.
