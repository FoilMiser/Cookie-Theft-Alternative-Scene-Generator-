# Picture 48

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a fenced playground: there is a sun in the top right, two white clouds, a dark railing fence with bushes behind it, and a tree on the far left. On the left two children are on a red seesaw that is almost level. A girl in a pink dress sits on the left end, and a child with curly dark hair in a yellow top and orange shorts sits on the right end. Behind them, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is walking to the right, pushing a teal pram with a sleeping baby under a pink polka-dot blanket. Coming the other way from the right is a woman in a purple top and teal trousers who is running, with speed lines behind her. She is heading left toward the woman with the pram, and it looks as if they might bump into each other if neither moves. In the front of the picture an orange cat is crouched low, stalking a small blue bird that is pecking at the ground. The cat looks about to pounce on the bird, and the bird doesn't seem to have noticed. On the right there is an empty slide with a red frame and an orange and yellow chute, a bush, and a grey rock in the bottom right.
