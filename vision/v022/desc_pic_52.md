# Picture 52

## Free description
This is a sunny but windy back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and a blue back door, with a tall wooden fence and a closed wooden gate across the back and right. In the middle of the lawn an older man in a grey-blue flat cap, white shirt, brown vest and dark trousers is digging a hole with a shovel; there is a scoop of earth on the shovel and a mound of dirt beside the hole. On the right, standing on a paved path near the gate, a woman (or teenage girl) in a purple top and teal trousers has both arms thrown up and her mouth open in surprise, because an orange cap is being blown up into the air just above and to the left of her, along with a few leaves. White wind lines streak across the sky and past the cap, and the washing on the clothesline (blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes) is swinging sideways in the wind. It looks like she is about to lose the cap over the fence or is trying to grab it. On the left there is a bed of colourful flowers with a coiled garden hose on a reel beside it, and a bush next to the house. Something impossible is in the tree behind the house: an orange fish is sitting in the green leaves of the tree, as if it were a bird.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fish in the tree is clearly drawn as a fish (tail, fins, stripes) sitting among the leaves; it could be read as something deliberately impossible, or just as an orange shape/bird drawn oddly, but it looks like a fish.
- Whose cap it is: the man still has his own cap on, and the woman has nothing on her head, so it seems to be her cap blowing off; she might also be trying to catch it.
- The person on the right could be an adult woman or a teenage girl.
- It is a little odd that it is very windy (washing swinging, cap flying) while the sky is clear and sunny, but that is possible.
- The man seems to be looking to the right toward the woman rather than down at his hole, though he is still digging.
