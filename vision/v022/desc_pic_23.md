# Picture 23

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny park with a pond, green hills, trees, a winding path, a lamp post and a bench. By the pond, a woman in a yellow dress is throwing bread to a white duck that is swimming towards the crumbs floating on the water. In the front left, a little girl in a pink dress is holding a blue and a red balloon, while her yellow balloon has escaped and is floating away over the pond; she is reaching up towards it and looks upset. In the middle front, a brown dog with floppy ears is sitting up like a person and reading an open newspaper held in its paws, which is impossible. On the bench, a woman in a purple top is reading a red book and hasn't noticed that a blue bird is pecking at the big sandwich lying next to her. On the path, an older man in a cap and brown waistcoat, carrying a yellow bag, is walking towards an orange cat that is curled up asleep on the path, so he might trip over it or step on it. On the far right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is pointing up at a blue ball stuck in a tree, and in the background a woman in a blue hoodie is walking along eating an ice-cream cone.
