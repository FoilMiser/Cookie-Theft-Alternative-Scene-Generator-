## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills behind it, a sun in the top-right corner and two white clouds. On the left there is a blue pond with cattails, a tree and a bush, and in front of it a flower bed full of colourful flowers. A blue garden hose runs along the bottom-left of the picture to a black sprinkler. The sprinkler is spraying arcs of water to the right, straight onto a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is getting soaked: water is dripping down its sides and there is a puddle under it. Behind, near a tall black lamp post, an elderly man with grey hair, a blue cap, a brown vest, a white shirt and dark trousers is walking to the right, carrying another cardboard box in both hands. On the right, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, round glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt sits on a wooden bench happily eating a pink ice-cream cone. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is on the path next to the bench, up on its hind legs and looking up at her, apparently begging for the ice cream. A winding sandy path runs from the background down the right side past a big tree and a bush. The odd thing is that the box on the grass is being sprayed by the sprinkler and nobody seems to notice.
