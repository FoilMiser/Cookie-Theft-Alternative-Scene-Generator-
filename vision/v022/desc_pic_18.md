# Picture 18

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny town square or pedestrian street lined with colourful shops with striped awnings, a lamp post, a tree, and a fruit stall on the right. On the far left a woman in a yellow dress is reading a red book in a sitting position, but there is no bench or chair under her - she is sitting on thin air. Next to the tree, a little girl in a pink dress is pointing up at a blue ball stuck in the branches. An older woman with grey hair and glasses walks past carrying a yellow shopping bag, and behind her a man in a red T-shirt and jeans is eating a pink ice-cream cone. In the middle an older man in a cap and brown waistcoat sits on a bench eating a pink ice cream, and a white spotted dog in front of him looks up at him, perhaps hoping for some. Behind them a woman in a blue hoodie is watering a flower box with a watering can, and to the right a man in a green T-shirt is scattering seeds or crumbs for a blue bird and a red bird pecking on the ground. In the front right, a woman in a purple top walks along with her nose in an open book, and there is a cardboard box on the ground ahead of her, so she might trip over it. In the front centre there is a red bicycle with square wheels, which is impossible to ride.

- The dog could be begging for the older man's ice cream (paw raised, looking up) or just walking by.
