# Picture 12

## Free description
This is a busy sunny park with a pond on the left, a lamp post in the middle, a winding path on the right and trees and hills in the background. A young woman with a brown ponytail, a blue hoodie and dark trousers stands in front of the pond flying an orange kite with a bow-tied tail high in the sky; a blue bird flies nearby in the upper left. Further back, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands holding a yellow balloon on a string. In the bottom right, a little girl in a pink dress holds an empty ice-cream cone and has her other hand up to her eye, as if she is crying, because her pink scoop of ice cream has fallen on the path and is melting into a puddle. A brown dog with a red collar or bandana has its head down and is sniffing or licking up the dropped ice cream. At the bottom centre a garden sprinkler, fed by a hose running along the bottom-left edge, is spraying arcs of water onto a cardboard box, which is dripping and standing in a puddle, so it is getting soaked. A flower bed sits in the bottom-left corner. The scene has several small mishaps going on at once: the lost ice cream, the dog eating it and the wet box.
