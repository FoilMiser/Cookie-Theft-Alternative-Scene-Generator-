# Picture 58

## Free description
This is a sunny but windy park with green hills behind, a pond on the left, a lamppost in the middle, a wooden bench and a winding sandy path on the right. In the middle-right, an older woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt has both arms flung up and her mouth open in surprise, because her blue cap has just been blown off and is flying away above her to the right, along with a few leaves. White wind lines stream across the sky and past her. In the front, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is pushing a teal pram to the right; a baby is sleeping inside under a pink spotted blanket. A white duck is swimming on the pond behind him. There are two green rubbish bins (one by the lamppost and one in the bottom-left corner), an empty bench, a flower bed and some bushes and trees. Nothing looks impossible; the main event is the hat blowing away.
