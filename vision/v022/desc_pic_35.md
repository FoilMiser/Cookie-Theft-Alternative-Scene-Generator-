# Picture 35

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden, with the sun in the top right corner and two white clouds. A cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the left, with a tree behind it; a wooden fence with a gate on the far right runs across the back, and a paved path runs from the gate down to the bottom right. A woman in a yellow dress with short dark hair and red shoes is standing on the lawn holding a hose nozzle and spraying an arc of water onto the flower bed in front of the house; the hose runs along the ground from a teal hose reel at the left. In the front centre, a girl or young woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is digging a hole in the lawn with a spade, lifting a spadeful of soil next to a mound of dirt. On the right, an elderly man in a flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is standing on the path using a giant silver fork, shaped like a dinner (table) fork, to sweep a small pile of dirt toward a red dustpan, with little puffs of dust rising. Using an enormous eating fork as a broom is clearly odd and silly. There are also two potted plants and a round bush near the fence.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The man's tool: it looks like an oversized silver dinner fork (curved neck, flat tines, rounded handle). It could alternatively be read as a garden fork drawn in silver, but either way a fork is the wrong tool for sweeping dust into a dustpan, where you would expect a broom or brush.
- The grey puffs near the dustpan could be dust being stirred up, or small stones/gravel.
- Why the girl is digging a hole in the middle of the lawn is not shown (could be for planting something).
- The man's head: cap brim points left and there is a grey patch at the right side of his head; I read it as grey hair at the back of his head (he is looking down-left at the dirt), though it could be read as a beard.
- Apart from the fork, nothing looks unintentionally strange; the hose route from reel to nozzle is continuous.
