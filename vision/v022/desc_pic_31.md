# Picture 31

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a bright sun in the top left corner and two small white clouds. A tall wooden fence runs across the back, with a wooden gate in the fence on the far left and a pale paved path leading from the gate down to the bottom left corner. On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue front door, with a tree and a round bush behind and beside it. In the middle of the lawn there is a clothesline strung between two wooden posts. An elderly man in a grey-blue flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is standing between the posts reaching up with both arms, pegging a white T-shirt onto the line. A pair of blue jeans is already pegged on the line to his right. In front of the house there is a round flower bed full of colourful flowers, with a teal hose reel sitting at its front edge. Two potted plants stand on the lawn to the left of the clothesline, and a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow sit in the foreground. Nothing in the scene looks clearly wrong or impossible; it is a calm laundry-day scene.
