# Picture 54

## Free description
This is a calm, sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and a blue door, with a wooden fence and a closed gate at the back and a stone path on the right. An older woman with grey hair in a bun, a teal top and a grey skirt is standing by a colourful flower bed holding a teal watering can, watering the flowers. The water, however, is drawn as a thick blue stream that curves upward out of the can and ends in a few drops in the air above the flowers, so it looks as if the water is shooting up rather than pouring down. Behind her a clothesline holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes, hanging still. On the right there is a metal swing set with an empty red swing, and a birdhouse on a tall pole by the gate. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands on the lawn in front. No one else is in the picture.
