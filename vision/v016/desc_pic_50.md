# Picture 50

## Free description
This is a sunny park with a dark metal railing across the back, a tree on the left, a bench, a swing set, a green litter bin and a slide. On a red seesaw at the left, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is sitting on the low end reading a book, while a little girl in a pink dress is stuck up in the air on the high end, kicking her legs; the man doesn't seem to notice she can't get down. On the right, a girl in a blue top and black trousers with a brown ponytail is flying a yellow kite high in the sky near the sun. A blue bird is perched on top of the slide. In the middle, a brown dog with a red collar sits on the grass holding an open newspaper as if reading it, which is impossible. At the front right, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts is eating a pink ice-cream cone. An orange cat is curled up asleep on the grass at the bottom centre. The swing, bench and slide are empty.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl on the seesaw has motion lines under her feet: this could mean she is kicking her legs because she is stuck up high, or simply swinging her legs happily.
- Whether the man is ignoring the girl or the seesaw is just at rest is open to interpretation; his attention is on his book.
- The dog reading a newspaper is impossible in real life (dogs can't read).
- The kite is drawn very close to the sun; it is just high in the sky, not touching the sun.
- The cat at the bottom is small; I read it as asleep, curled up, but it could be lying down resting.
