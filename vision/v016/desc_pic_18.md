# Picture 18

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a gate in the far-left corner and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. Three people are in the garden. At the front left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping the paved path with a yellow-headed broom. In the middle, a child in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts has just thrown a red ball, shown by a dotted line curving from the child's hand up to the ball near the top of the fence. To the right of the child, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes holds both arms out in front of her, ready to catch the ball. A blue bird is perched on top of the fence near a tree, and a small birdhouse on a tall pole stands near the gate. There is an empty swing set with one red seat, a teal watering can on the lawn and a bed of colourful flowers next to the house. Something odd is happening at the front: a garden sprinkler, connected to a long hose, is spraying water onto a cardboard box sitting on the lawn, so the box is getting soaked.
