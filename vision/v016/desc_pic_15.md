## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right, a wooden fence with a gate on the far left, and a tree behind the house. On the paving path at the front left, a boy in a red shirt and blue jeans is sweeping a small pile of dirt toward a red dustpan. Oddly, he is using a giant silver dinner fork instead of a broom, and little puffs of dust rise around it. In the middle of the lawn is a grey A-frame swing set with one empty red swing. At the clothesline, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers, with her dark hair in a ponytail, reaches up to peg a white T-shirt onto the line. A yellow towel with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans are already hanging there. To the right, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes paints the fence blue with a long-handled roller. Part of the fence is already blue, and a paint bucket sits at her feet. Near the house there is a flower bed with a hose reel beside it, and a wooden bench sits at the front right. An empty grey wheelbarrow is on the lawn at the front centre. The sun and two clouds are in the sky.
