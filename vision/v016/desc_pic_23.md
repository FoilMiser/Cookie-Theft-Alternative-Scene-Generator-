# Picture 23

## Free description

This is a sunny day in a park, with a bright sun in the top right, two white clouds, green rolling hills, and trees in the background. On the left there is an oval pond with cattails growing at both ends. A man with brown hair, a red shirt and blue jeans stands on the grass just to the right of the pond, holding a fishing rod out over the water. His line drops down to a red-and-white float (bobber) with little ripple rings around it. A white duck is swimming in the pond right next to the float, facing it with its beak almost touching it, so it looks like the duck is about to grab the bait or get caught on the line instead of a fish. Behind the man is a tall black old-fashioned lamp post. In the front right a young woman with a brown ponytail, a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes is running or jogging toward the left, on the grass beside a winding sandy footpath rather than on it. A green rubbish bin is at the very bottom left corner, partly cut off by the edge of the picture.
