## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence, and a path up the right side to a gate. A woman with long dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers is digging a hole in the lawn with a spade, with a mound of soil beside it. To her right, a girl with a brown ponytail and blue hoodie is painting the fence blue with a paintbrush; a few fence boards are already blue, and a paint tin and a paint tray of blue paint sit on the grass. A white dog with brown spots has stepped in the paint tray and is walking away, leaving a trail of blue paw prints across the lawn. In the front right, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses is reading a red book while sitting in a seated position with nothing under her: no chair or bench, just air. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits at the front. The dog's paint trail is a mess about to spread, and the woman sitting on nothing looks impossible.
