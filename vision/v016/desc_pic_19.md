## Free description
This is a sunny playground with a dark metal railing fence and green hedges behind it, under a blue sky with a sun in the top right and two clouds. On the left, a red seesaw is tipped steeply. A little girl in a pink dress is stuck high in the air on the raised left end, with small motion lines under her dangling feet as if she is kicking her legs. On the low right end sits an older man with a grey cap, grey beard, white shirt and brown vest, calmly reading an open book or newspaper with his feet on the ground. He doesn't seem to notice that the girl can't get down. On the right, a child with dark curly hair, a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts is sliding down a tall red slide, with their arms raised up near their head. In the middle there is an empty swing set with one red seat. Around the playground are a wooden bench, a green bin, a large tree on the left and a bush on the right. At the front right is a sandpit with a red bucket and a blue spade, and a grey rock beside it. The odd part is that a grown man is sitting on a seesaw reading, and he is keeping the small girl stuck up in the air.
