# pic_10

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny back garden. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, and a wooden fence with a gate runs across the back. On the left, a bearded man in a green shirt and beige trousers is watering a flower bed by pouring water out of a dark grey frying pan instead of a watering can. In the middle of the lawn, a sprinkler on a teal hose is spraying arcs of water onto a closed cardboard box, so the box is the thing getting watered. Water drops are bouncing off its top. At the back, a man in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest is painting the wooden fence blue with a long-handled roller, and a paint bucket stands at his feet. Part of the fence is already blue. On the right, a boy in a red shirt and blue jeans walks along the paving path with his nose in an open book. A red ball lies on the ground just ahead of him, so he looks about to trip over it. A small white puppy with brown ears is curled up asleep on the grass at the bottom centre. A blue bird sits on the fence by the tree, and a red-roofed birdhouse stands on a tall pole near the fence. The jeans and a red-striped yellow towel hang on a clothesline.
