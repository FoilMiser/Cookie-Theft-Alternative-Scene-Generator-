# Picture 33

## Free description

This is a sunny park with green hills, trees, a pond, a winding sandy path, a lamp post, a bench and a flower bed. In the middle, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt stands at the edge of the pond. She is tossing bits of bread from a bag towards a white duck swimming in the water, and pieces of bread are flying through the air and floating near the duck. On the right, an older man in a cap, a white shirt and a brown waistcoat stands on the path, pouring seeds from a cup or bag. The seeds fall in a dotted line down to two small birds, one red and one blue, pecking on the grass beside the path. In the front, a girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes walks or skips to the right holding a yellow balloon on a string. There is a flower bed with colourful flowers at the bottom left, a grey rock at the bottom centre, and green rubbish bins near the lamp post and in the bottom left corner. Nothing looks strange or impossible; it is a calm, ordinary park scene.
