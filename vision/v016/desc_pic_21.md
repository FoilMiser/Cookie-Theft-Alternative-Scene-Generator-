# Picture 21

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a gate in the far-left corner, a paved path, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. In the middle of the lawn, a young man in a blue hoodie and black trousers has both arms raised high above his head. His eyes are closed, his cheeks are pink and he is smiling, so he looks very happy, as if cheering or jumping for joy. His shadow is drawn a little below his feet, so he may be in mid-jump. Just behind him, a white goat with small horns has its head down and is eating a tuft of grass in the garden. In the front middle there is a grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles, and its wheel looks square rather than round, which would not work. There are two potted plants near the fence, a teal watering can on the lawn, a bed of colourful flowers and a bush by the house, and a tree behind the fence. A goat in an ordinary back garden is unusual, and the square wheel looks impossible.
