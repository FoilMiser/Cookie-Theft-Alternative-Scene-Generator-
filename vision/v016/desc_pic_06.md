# Picture 06

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence, an empty swing set on the right, and a path leading up to a gate. An older man in a cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat sits on a bench by the house reading a red book. Next to him is a big sandwich, and a blue bird is pecking at it; he hasn't noticed and looks about to lose his lunch. On the right, a boy in a red T-shirt and jeans is sweeping a pile of dirt or crumbs on the path into a red dustpan, but he is using a giant fork as if it were a broom, which is odd. A small red pet bowl with food in it sits on the grass near the dustpan. A black cat is walking along the top of the fence. The swing set in the middle is empty, with its red seat hanging still.
