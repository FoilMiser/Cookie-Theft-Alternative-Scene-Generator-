# Picture 32

## Free description

This is a sunny, windy shopping street with a row of colourful shops (cream, peach, green and pink), striped awnings, a street lamp, a bench and a fruit stall. In the centre, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans has his arms flung up and looks surprised, because his red cap has blown off and is flying away with some leaves on white wind lines. On the left, an older man with grey hair, a cap and a brown waistcoat is walking along reading an open book. A red ball lies on the ground right in his path, so he looks about to trip over it. At the bottom centre, a small boy with curly dark hair, a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts is crying with his hand to his eye. He holds an empty ice-cream cone, and his pink ice cream has fallen on the ground. A brown dog with a red collar has its nose right at the spilled ice cream, sniffing or eating it. On the far left, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is running to the left. On the right, near the fruit and vegetable stall, a person in a blue hoodie and dark trousers is jumping with both arms in the air, smiling and looking happy or cheering.
