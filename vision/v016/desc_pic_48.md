# Picture 48

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden gate and paved path on the left and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the front, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is holding the nozzle of a garden hose up near his face and peering at it, because only a single drop of water is coming out. The reason is that a white goat is standing on the hose further along, and the hose is bulging with trapped water just behind its hoof, between the goat and the hose reel. If the goat steps off, the water will probably burst out and spray the man in the face. At the back by the fence, a grey-haired older woman with glasses and a teal cardigan is digging a hole with a spade, with a mound of soil piled next to it. Something odd: a brown cardboard box is hanging in the sky above the fence, tilted, with nobody there to have thrown it. There is a flower bed near the house and a hose reel beside it.

## Inventory
- Man (centre-left, front): beard, green shirt, khaki trousers, brown shoes; holding a hose nozzle up and looking into it; one water drop dripping out.
- White goat (centre-right, front): white with small horns; standing on the hose with a back hoof, pinching it.
- Older woman (centre-right, back by the fence): grey hair, glasses, teal cardigan, grey skirt; digging with a spade.
- Garden hose: dark teal hose running from the reel by the flower bed, down across the front of the picture and up to the man's hands; it has a swollen bulge next to the goat's foot.
- Hose reel: on the ground beside the flower bed, right.
- Spade and hole: spade in the ground with a lump of soil, next to a dark mound of dug soil at the back.
- Cardboard box: brown box floating in the sky above the fence, left of centre, tilted at an angle.
- Flower bed: round bed of mixed yellow, orange, red, pink and purple flowers, right.
- House: cream walls, red roof, window, blue door with step, on the right.
- Tree and bush: tree behind the house corner, small bush by the house.
- Gate and path: wooden gate at far left, paved path running down the left side.
- Sun (top left) and two clouds in a blue sky.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The cardboard box in the sky looks impossible (floating). It could also be read as being thrown over the fence from the neighbour's side, but no thrower is visible.
- The white animal has small horns and looks like a goat, but it could be read as a lamb or even a white dog because of the simple drawing.
- The bulge in the hose is next to the goat's foot; I read it as water trapped because the goat is standing on the hose, but it could just be a lump in the hose.
- The woman could be digging a hole to plant something, or digging something up; there is nothing next to her to plant.
