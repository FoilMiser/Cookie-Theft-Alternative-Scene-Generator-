# Picture 35

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence with a gate at the back right, a paved path on the right, an empty swing set and a birdhouse on a tall pole. A woman with dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes is watering the flower bed with a teal watering can, and a spray of water is falling onto the flowers. In the middle front, a man in an orange cap, an orange high-visibility vest with reflective stripes and blue trousers is waving with his raised hand. Little lines around the hand show the waving, and he seems to be greeting someone off to the left or the viewer. The swing on the right hangs empty and still. Something is clearly wrong: an orange fish is sitting in the leaves of the tree behind the house. A hose reel stands on the grass next to the flower bed.

- Sun (top right) and two white clouds (top centre).
