# Picture 28

## Free description

This is a busy back garden on a sunny day, with the sun in the top right and two white clouds. At the left, a brown-and-white dog sits in front of the blue back door of a cream house, holding a red lead (leash) in its mouth as if asking to go for a walk. In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes with dark hair reaches up with both hands to peg a white T-shirt onto the washing line, which already has blue jeans and a yellow striped item on it. To the right of her, a woman with long dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers holds her arms out in front of her, ready to catch a red ball that is flying through the air. The ball has been thrown by a boy with dark skin and curly hair, in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts, standing on the paved path at the far right by the gate; a dashed line shows the ball's path from his hand. In the front right a man with brown hair, a red shirt and blue jeans is sweeping the path with a broom. A flower bed and a bush are in front of the house, and a teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow stand on the grass at the front.
