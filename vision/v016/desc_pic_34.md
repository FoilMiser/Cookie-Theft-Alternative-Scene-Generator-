# Picture 34

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence with a gate at the back, a paved path on the right and a swing set. On the left, a woman with a dark bob, a yellow dress and red shoes sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. Next to her on the bench is a big sandwich, and a blue bird is pecking at it or stealing it. She doesn't seem to notice because she is busy reading. In the middle, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans holds a teal watering can over the flower bed. The water does not fall onto the flowers: it curves upward into the air in an arc, with drops at the top, which is impossible. On the right, a small child in pink sits on the swing, smiling with eyes closed, with motion lines showing the swing is moving. No one is pushing the child. A grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the bottom centre.

## Inventory

- Man with watering can (centre left, middle ground): brown hair, red T-shirt, blue jeans, dark shoes. He tips a teal watering can towards the flower bed.
- Small child on the swing (right, middle ground): dark hair, pink outfit, smiling with eyes closed. Swinging on the red swing seat.
- Blue bird (front left, on the bench next to the woman): pecking at or grabbing the sandwich.
- Sandwich (front left, on the bench beside the woman): a large filled sandwich, probably the woman's lunch, being taken or eaten by the bird.
- Red book (held by the woman): being read.
- Teal watering can (held by the man): its stream of water arcs up and away instead of falling down.
- Water stream and drops (above the flower bed): curving upward, with drops at the top of the arc.
- Flower bed (left of centre): round, with red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers.
- Swing set (right): dark blue-grey A-frame with one red seat in use.
- Grey wheelbarrow (bottom centre): standing empty on the grass.
- House (far left): cream walls, red roof, a window and a blue door with a grey step.
- Tree (behind the house).
- Wooden fence and gate (across the back; the gate is on the right). A paved path leads to the gate (right).
- Sun (top right) and two white clouds (top centre).

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The water from the watering can flows upward and backward instead of falling onto the flowers. This looks impossible or wrong. It could also be read as the water squirting out in a high arc, but it rises above the spout and ends in drops at the top.
- Sandwich and bird: the bird could be stealing the sandwich, pecking at it, or just standing next to it. Its beak is right at the sandwich. The sandwich may be sitting on a plate or napkin on the bench; it's hard to tell.
- The small child on the swing could be a girl or a toddler. The outfit is pink and the hair is dark and short.
- The wheelbarrow's handles rest on the ground, so it might be tipped slightly. Most likely it is just parked.
