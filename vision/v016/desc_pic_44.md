# Picture 44

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a paved path running down the right side to a wooden gate. On a bench at the front left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is eating a pink ice-cream cone with her eyes closed, and a brown dog with a red neckerchief is stretched toward her, as if hoping for some. Near the flower bed, a grey-haired older woman with glasses and a teal cardigan is raking a small pile of leaves. Behind her, a girl in a blue top and black trousers is flying a purple kite, but the kite is up in the leafy tree beside the house and looks stuck there. In the middle, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is walking along reading an open book, and he is heading straight for a blue bucket on the grass, so he looks about to trip over it. On the right, a man in a red shirt holds his hands out to catch a red ball that a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts has thrown from the path (a dashed line shows the ball's flight). Along the top of the fence, a black-and-white cat is creeping toward a red bird and a blue bird perched on the fence. Something impossible: a white dog sits on the grass holding an open newspaper as if it were reading it. An orange cat is curled up asleep on the path at the bottom right.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The ball: the dashed trail runs from the boy to the ball, so the boy seems to have thrown it to the man in red; it could also be read as the man having thrown it and the boy about to catch it, though the trail suggests the first.
- The brown dog by the bench is in a stretched pose: it could be begging for the ice cream, or just stretching/playing.
- The white dog reading a newspaper is impossible in real life (a dog can't read); it's not clear if this is meant as a joke.
- The older woman's glasses and face are drawn small; I could not make out her expression clearly.
- The blue mark on the white dog's head/neck could be a collar or a blue ear.
