# Picture 46

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, a gate and paved path on the right, and a metal swing set on the right. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is kneeling by the flower bed, reaching into the soil as if planting, with a pink flower in a terracotta pot beside her. At the back, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers with a dark ponytail is painting the fence blue with a long-handled brush; a section beside her is already blue, and a paint tin sits at her feet. In the centre front, a grey-haired older woman with glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt stands with her hands on her hips, and she is wearing a blue bucket upside down on her head like a hat, which looks silly or odd. The swing set has a single red swing hanging empty. On the grass near the front are an empty grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles and a teal watering can near the path. An empty wooden bench and a hose reel are at the front left by the flower bed. Nobody is using the bench, swing, wheelbarrow or watering can.
