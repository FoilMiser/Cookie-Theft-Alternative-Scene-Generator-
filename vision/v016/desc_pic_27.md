# Picture 27

## Free description

This is a back garden on a sunny day, with the sun in the top right and two white clouds. At the bottom left a woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers sits on a wooden bench in front of the house, eyes closed and cheeks rosy, sipping from a white mug and looking relaxed. In the centre a girl with a brown ponytail, a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes stands holding a garden hose, spraying water in an arc toward the flower bed on the left. The hose runs along the grass from a hose reel next to the flower bed up to her hands. Oddly, a closed cardboard box is hanging in mid-air in the sky above the fence, tilted, with nothing holding it up. A washing line in the middle holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow striped item. On the right is a swing set with an empty red swing seat, a small wooden birdhouse with a red roof on top of a tall pole, a wooden gate in the fence, and a paved path leading from the gate. The house on the left is cream with a red roof, a window and a blue door, and a tree stands behind it.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The floating cardboard box is the clearly impossible or odd element: it hangs in the sky with nothing under it and nobody near it. It could be read as falling, as being blown by the wind, or simply as an impossible floating object; nothing else in the picture (e.g. the washing hangs straight) suggests strong wind.
- The water from the hose seems to land at the very edge of the flower bed / near the soil rather than on the flowers themselves - she may be slightly missing, or just watering the bed's edge.
- The woman's closed eyes could mean she is enjoying her drink or that she is dozing; she is clearly holding the mug to her mouth.
- The striped yellow item on the line could be a towel or a striped shirt.
