# Picture 36

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence with a gate at the back right, a paved path on the right and a swing set. A woman with a brown ponytail, a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes is walking to the left towards the house carrying a cardboard box in front of her. On the right, a boy with curly dark hair, a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts sits on the swing, smiling with eyes closed, with motion lines showing he is swinging. No one is pushing him. A grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the bottom centre, and a teal watering can stands on the grass near the path at the bottom right. There is a flower bed with colourful flowers and a small bush by the house. Something is clearly wrong: an orange fish is sitting in the leaves of the tree behind the house.
