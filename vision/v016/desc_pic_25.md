# Picture 25

## Free description

This is a back garden on a sunny, slightly breezy day, with a bright sun in the top right and two white clouds. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door, and in front of it is a bed of colourful flowers. A man with dark skin, a beard and short dark hair, wearing a green shirt and beige trousers, stands beside the flower bed holding a teal watering can. Strangely, the water coming out of the watering can arcs upward into the air like a fountain, with drops at the top, instead of pouring down onto the flowers. In the middle of the garden a washing line holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow striped item, both hanging at a slant as if blown by the wind. On the right a swing set has a small smiling child in a pink dress on the swing, with motion lines showing she is swinging. At the far right, standing on a paved path in front of a wooden gate, a girl with a brown ponytail, a blue hoodie and dark trousers is flying a yellow diamond-shaped kite, which is high in the sky with a tail of little bows. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the bottom centre, and a tree stands behind the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The water from the watering can: it appears to shoot upward out of the spout in a curve (going against gravity) and ends in drops high above the flowers. Reading 1: the water is impossibly flowing up out of the can. Reading 2: it is meant as a spray arc that will rain down onto the flowers. As drawn, the stream rises from the spout rather than falling.
- The slanting jeans and striped item on the line suggest wind, which fits with the kite flying; that seems consistent, not an error.
- The striped yellow item could be a towel or a striped shirt.
- The swing's ropes hang straight down even though motion lines show swinging; minor and probably just stylistic.
- The child on the swing appears to have eyes closed and a big smile, read as happy/enjoying it.
