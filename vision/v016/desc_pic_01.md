# Picture 01

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a paved path running up the right side to a wooden gate. A woman with a short black bob, a yellow dress and red shoes sits on a wooden bench in front of the house reading a red book. Next to her is a round bed of colourful flowers. In the middle of the lawn a child in a blue hoodie and dark trousers is drinking from a white cup, sitting with knees bent as if on a chair, but there is no chair or seat under them at all; they are sitting on thin air. On the right a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers walks along the path holding a red lead attached to a white dog with brown spots. A pair of jeans and a yellow striped towel hang on a washing line between two posts at the back. A birdhouse on a tall pole and two potted plants stand near the fence. The child sitting on nothing is the one thing that looks impossible; everything else looks like an ordinary calm afternoon.
