# pic_62

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a gate in the top left, and a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the right. A birdhouse on a tall pole stands near the gate, and a tree shows above the fence on the right. On the left, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is raking a small pile of leaves on a paved path. In the middle there is a dark grey swing set with an empty red seat, and behind it a brown dog with a red collar crouches in a playful bow with a yellow tennis ball by its front paws, wagging its tail. In the centre, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers holds the end of a garden hose up in front of him and looks at it, but only a single drop of water comes out. The hose runs along the ground to a hose reel by the house, and a white goat is standing on it. Between the goat and the reel the hose is swollen into a big bulge, so the goat's foot is blocking the water. If the goat steps off, the water will probably burst out and spray the man. There is a flower bed with colourful flowers on the right and an empty grey wheelbarrow on the grass at the front.
