## Free description
This is a sunny playground with a metal fence and hedges across the back, a sun in the top left and two clouds. On the left, a child with braided dark hair in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is sliding down a red-and-yellow slide with one arm raised. In the middle there is a dark grey swing set with a single red swing seat hanging empty, and a green rubbish bin stands by the fence. On the right there is an empty wooden bench and a tree. In the front right, an elderly man with white hair and a grey-blue flat cap, wearing a white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers, sits on the low end of a red seesaw with his feet on the ground, reading an open book or newspaper. Because he is heavy and sitting still, the other end is stuck up in the air. A little girl in a pink dress sits at that high end, holding the handle with her legs dangling and small motion lines under her feet, as if she is kicking. The old man is not looking at her and seems unaware that she is stuck up high. In the front left is a sandbox with a red bucket and blue spade, and a grey rock.
