# Picture 39

## Free description
This is a sunny park with rolling green hills and trees in the background. On the left there is an oval pond with cattails (bulrushes) at each end, and a white duck swimming on it. A man with a short beard, green T-shirt and khaki trousers stands on the grass to the right of the pond, holding a fishing rod out over the water; the line hangs down to a red-and-white float bobbing in the pond with a small ripple around it. Behind him is a tall black lamp post. To the right, a sandy footpath winds from the background down to the bottom of the picture, and a wooden ladder stands upright on the grass beside the path, leaning at an angle against nothing at all - there is no wall, tree or building for it to rest on, so it looks impossible. There is a large tree on the right, a smaller tree on the left, several bushes, a grey rock at the front centre and a green rubbish bin at the bottom left edge. Nothing seems about to happen except that the man might get a bite on his line; the main thing that looks wrong is the free-standing, leaning ladder.
