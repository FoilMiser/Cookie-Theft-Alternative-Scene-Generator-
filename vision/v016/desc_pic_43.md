# Picture 43

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back with a gate at the right end, and a paved path on the right. A boy with brown hair in a blue hoodie and dark trousers sits on a wooden bench at the front left reading a red book, and right next to him on the bench is a big sandwich on a plate, where a small blue bird has landed and is pecking at or stealing the sandwich while he isn't looking. On the right, a woman with a black bob, yellow dress and red shoes is sweeping dirt on the path into a red dustpan, but instead of a broom she is using an enormous silver dinner fork, which looks absurd. A little puff of dust is rising from the pile of dirt. An orange cat is walking along the top of the fence in the background. There is also a colourful flower bed, two potted plants, two bushes and an empty grey wheelbarrow at the front centre. It looks like the bird is about to eat the boy's sandwich, and the cat on the fence might be eyeing the bird.
