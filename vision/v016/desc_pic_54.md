# pic_54

## Free description
This is a sunny town square or high street with a row of colourful two-storey shops. The shops have striped awnings in red, green and orange. In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench happily eating a pink ice-cream cone. A small white dog with brown spots jumps up at her, as if begging for the ice cream. In the front left, a small child in a yellow top and orange shorts is crying and holding an empty cone. Their pink ice cream lies splattered on the pavement, and a brown dog with a red collar is sniffing or eating it. A woman with a ponytail, in a blue hoodie, walks along reading an open book and is not looking where she is going. A cardboard box sits on the ground right in front of her, so she is about to trip over it. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sweeps with a broom in front of a fruit and vegetable stall. A red bird flies in the sky, and a blue bird perches on top of the street lamp.
