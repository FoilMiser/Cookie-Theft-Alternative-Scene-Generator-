# Picture 05

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence, and a path up the right side to a gate. A woman with a black bob in a yellow dress is watering the flower bed by pouring water out of a frying pan, which is strange, especially since a hose reel sits right beside the flower bed and a green watering can is lying unused on the lawn. In the middle, an older man with a grey beard, a cap, a brown waistcoat and a white shirt is pegging a white T-shirt onto the washing line next to a pair of jeans and a striped towel. On the right, a child with brown hair tied back, in a blue top and dark trousers, stands on the path flying a yellow kite with a bow-tied tail high in the sky. A grey wheelbarrow sits empty at the front, and two potted plants stand by the fence. Apart from the frying pan being used as a watering can, everything looks normal.

## Inventory
- Woman, front left-centre: black bob, yellow dress, red shoes; facing the flowers, pouring water from a frying pan onto them.
- Older man, centre back: grey beard, grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat, dark trousers; hanging a white T-shirt on the washing line with pegs.
- Child, right (on the path): brown hair tied back, blue long-sleeved top, dark trousers, white shoes; looking up and holding a kite string.
- Frying pan: in the woman's hand, dark grey, with water pouring out onto the flower bed.
- Flower bed: left of centre, colourful flowers, getting water.
- Hose reel: left, next to the flower bed, unused.
- Watering can: front right, green/teal, sitting on the grass unused.
- Washing line: centre back, two posts; blue jeans, a yellow-and-red striped towel, and a white T-shirt being pegged up.
- Kite: upper right in the sky, yellow diamond, tail with little bows; being flown on a long string from the child's hands.
- Wheelbarrow: front centre, grey tub, black wheel, wooden handles; empty, parked.
- Two potted plants: right of centre, in terracotta pots by the fence.
- House: left, cream walls, red roof, window, blue door.
- Tree: behind the house.
- Gate and path: right side.
- Sun and two clouds: in the sky.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Using a frying pan to water flowers looks deliberately odd. It could also be read as the woman emptying dirty water out of the pan onto the flower bed rather than watering on purpose.
- The child flying the kite could be a girl or a boy; the hair seems tied back in a short ponytail.
- The jeans on the washing line are drawn at a slant, as if blowing in the wind or hanging crooked.
- The striped towel is partly hidden behind the man's head and arm.
