# Picture 45

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden seen from the other side: a wooden gate and paved path are on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the right. An older man in a grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is painting the wooden fence blue with a long-handled brush; one section of the fence behind him is already blue. On the grass near his feet are a paint tin and a flat paint tray full of blue paint. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has walked through the paint tray: a trail of blue paw prints leads from the tray across the grass to the dog, and its feet are blue. The man is facing the fence and does not seem to have noticed. Across the middle of the garden there is a washing line between two wooden posts with a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans hanging on it. A flower bed with mixed flowers sits near the house, and a red dog bowl with food is on the grass at the front. The dog may be about to spread more blue paw prints around the garden.
