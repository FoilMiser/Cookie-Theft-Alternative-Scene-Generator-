# Picture 47

## Free description
This is a sunny park enclosed by a dark metal railing, with a tree on the left, a bench, a green litter bin, a slide on the right and a sandpit in the front right corner. A girl in a blue top and black trousers with a brown ponytail has just thrown a red ball, and a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers has his hands up ready to catch it; a dashed line shows the ball's path. At the front left, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts is holding a kite string and looking up, but his purple kite is caught in the leaves of the tree. In the middle, a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is crouched down playfully with its tail wagging, next to a yellow-green tennis ball on the grass. In the sandpit at the front right, a little girl in a pink dress is kneeling and building a sandcastle. A grey rock sits on the grass beside the sandpit. The bench and slide are empty.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The ball: the dashed trail starts at the girl, so she threw it to the man; the opposite reading (man threw, girl catching) seems less likely.
- The dog could be playing with the tennis ball, or just bowing/stretching beside it.
- The rock is a plain grey shape; it could also be read as a small boulder or a stepping stone.
