# Picture 37

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left; a tall wooden fence runs across the back with a wooden gate at the right end, and a paved path leads from the gate down to the bottom right corner. An old man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat sits on a wooden bench at the front left, eyes closed, sipping from a white cup or mug. In the middle of the lawn a woman with a black bob, yellow dress and red shoes is reading a red book while sitting in a seated position, but there is no chair, stool or bench under her - she is sitting on thin air, which looks impossible. Behind her is a washing line strung between two wooden posts with a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes pegged on it. On the right a girl with a brown ponytail, blue hoodie and dark trousers is walking toward the path carrying a cardboard box in both hands. There is a colourful flower bed beside the house, a teal hose reel next to it, an empty grey wheelbarrow at the front centre, two potted plants near the fence, and a small birdhouse on a tall pole. Nothing in particular seems about to happen apart from the girl carrying her box along; the main thing that looks wrong is the woman sitting with nothing under her.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The woman in the yellow dress is in a seated pose with nothing under her. It could be read as an impossible "sitting on air" error, or (less likely) as a missing chair that was meant to be drawn; either way no seat is visible.
- The girl's direction: she is facing right and one foot is on the path, so she could be heading toward the gate/out of the garden or simply crossing to the path; it is not clear where she is taking the box.
- The old man's cup is small and white; it could be a mug of tea/coffee or a paper cup.
- The wheelbarrow's handles are raised and it sits slightly tilted; it looks parked rather than in use.
