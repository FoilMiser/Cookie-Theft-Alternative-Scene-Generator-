# Picture 02

## Free description
This is a busy sunny park with a pond, a winding path, trees, a lamp post and green hills behind. A bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands at the edge of the pond fishing; his line runs down to a red-and-white float bobbing in the water. In front, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket next to a picnic basket, smiling with eyes closed. A garden sprinkler at the bottom is spraying arcs of water toward the blanket and onto a cardboard box sitting next to it, so the child and the picnic look about to get wet. On a bench to the right, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses is eating a pink ice-cream cone while a brown dog with a red collar jumps up at her, clearly after the ice cream. Up on the path a black-and-white cat is leaping toward a blue bird on the ground, as if about to pounce. On the far right a girl in a pink dress points up at a blue ball stuck on a branch in a tree. A red bird flies across the sky at the top left. Off to the far left a ladder stands at an angle with nothing to lean against, which looks odd.

- The water drops above the cardboard box could be sprinkler water landing on it, or water coming out of the box.
- The child on the blanket's eyes are closed. They could be sunbathing/relaxing, or just smiling with eyes squeezed shut.
- The elderly woman's expression is hard to read: she could be enjoying the ice cream with eyes closed, or squinting.
