# Picture 20

## Free description
This is a sunny, windy day in a park with rolling green hills, a pond and a winding path. White wind lines are drawn across the sky. In the back left, a young man in a blue hoodie and black trousers has his arms thrown up and his mouth open in surprise, because the wind has just blown his orange cap off his head and up into the air, along with a few leaves. At the front, a child in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts is holding an empty ice-cream cone and rubbing their eye as if crying. Their pink scoop of ice cream, with sprinkles, has fallen onto the ground in a pink puddle. A brown dog with a red collar is bending down to sniff or lick up the fallen ice cream. On the right, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is walking along pushing a teal pram with a sleeping baby under a pink spotty blanket. In the middle of the grass, a small white puppy with brown ears is curled up, apparently asleep. On the left, a garden sprinkler attached to a hose is spraying water onto a cardboard box sitting on the path. That is an odd thing to find in a park. There is also a street lamp, a green bin, trees, bushes and cattails at the edges of the pond.
