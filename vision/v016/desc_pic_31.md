# Picture 31

## Free description

This is a busy, sunny shopping street with a row of colourful shops (pink, green, peach and cream), striped awnings and a paved pavement. It is windy: white wind lines blow across the sky and the middle of the picture, and leaves are flying about. In the centre, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes has both arms raised and her mouth open in surprise, because a blue cap is blowing away above her head. At the bottom centre, a small girl in a pink dress is crying with her hand to her eye. She holds an empty ice-cream cone, and her pink ice cream with sprinkles has fallen and splatted on the ground. A brown dog with a red collar is sniffing or about to lick it up. On the left, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers walks past a fruit and vegetable stall carrying a yellow bag. A red bicycle stands beside the stall, and its wheels are square, which is impossible. In the middle, an orange cat is creeping or running towards a red bird pecking at the ground, as if about to pounce. On the right, a woman with a brown ponytail and a blue hoodie is running to the right. A tree on the right has an orange fish in its leaves, which is also impossible. A blue bird flies in the sky at the top right.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Several things look impossible or wrong: the bicycle has square wheels, and an orange fish sits in the tree.
- The blue cap may belong to the woman in yellow (it has just blown off her head and she is reaching for it), or it may just be blowing past and startling her.
- The man's yellow object is probably a yellow shopping bag, but it could be a long yellow label or tag.
- The ponytail woman on the right may be jogging, or running after something. Nothing she could be chasing is visible.
- The cat could be stalking the red bird to pounce, or just running past it.
- The dog is either sniffing the dropped ice cream or about to eat it.
