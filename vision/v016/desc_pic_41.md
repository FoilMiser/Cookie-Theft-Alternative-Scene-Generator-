# Picture 41

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills and trees in the background, a sandy path winding down from the left, a tall black lamp post, and an oval pond on the right with a white duck swimming and cattails at both ends. A little girl with dark hair in a bun and a pink dress stands at the front centre-left holding a yellow and a purple balloon by their strings in one hand while stretching her other arm up toward a blue balloon that has escaped and is floating away with its string dangling. In front of the pond, a man with brown hair in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is licking or eating a pink ice-cream cone. At the front right, a boy with curly dark hair in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts is running while holding a yellow balloon on a string. A green rubbish bin is at the bottom right corner, and there are trees on both the left and right sides and several bushes. It looks like the blue balloon is about to float away for good unless someone catches it. Nothing looks impossible to me.
