## Free description
This is a sunny back garden. A cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue front door is on the left, and a wooden fence with a gate runs across the back. A man in a green shirt and beige trousers, with a beard and dark hair, stands next to a round flower bed on the left. He is watering the flowers by tipping a dark grey frying pan, and water pours out of the pan onto the soil. This is the odd part: a teal watering can is sitting unused on the grass at the bottom right, beside the paving-stone path. A clothesline between two wooden posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. Two potted plants stand near the fence on the right. A grey wheelbarrow with a black wheel sits empty on the lawn at the bottom centre. The sun is shining in the top right, and there are two small clouds.
