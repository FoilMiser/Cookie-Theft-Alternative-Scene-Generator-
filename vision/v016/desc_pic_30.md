# Picture 30

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, and a wooden fence with a gate runs across the back. A paved path leads to the gate on the right. Right of centre, a girl with a brown ponytail, a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes is kicking an orange ball. Curved motion lines under her kicking leg show the swing. The ball is just in front of her foot and looks like it is about to fly off to the left. On the left, a brown-and-white dog sits by the blue door with its own red leash in its mouth. Its eyes are closed and it looks happy, as if it is asking to go for a walk. A small red bowl of dog food sits on the grass near the girl's feet. The strange thing in the picture is a cardboard box hanging in the sky above the fence near the tree, with nothing holding it up. There is also a wooden bench, a hose reel, a flower bed full of colourful flowers and a small bush near the house.

- Dog (left, front, by the blue door): brown with a white chest and a red collar or bandana. It sits upright with the red leash in its mouth, eyes closed and looking content. It sits behind the bench, or possibly on it (see below).
- Orange ball (centre-right, on the grass in front of the girl's kicking foot): being kicked.
- Red dog bowl with brown kibble (lower centre-right, on the grass near the girl's feet): the dog's food, sitting on the lawn.
- Red leash (left): runs from the dog's mouth down to the ground and loops on the grass near the hose reel. Nobody is holding the other end.
- Cardboard box (upper centre, in the sky above the fence, near the tree): a closed brown box floating or flying in mid-air, slightly tilted, with nothing holding it.
- Wooden bench (front left): empty except for the dog, which may be perched on it.
- Hose reel (left, by the flower bed): a teal or dark green coiled hose on a stand.
- Flower bed (left of centre): a round bed of soil with red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers.
- House (far left): cream walls, red roof, a window and a blue door with a grey step.
- Tree and small bush (behind and beside the house).
- Wooden fence and gate (across the back; the gate is on the right). A paved path leads up to the gate (right).
- Sun (top right) and two white clouds (top centre).

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The floating cardboard box in the sky looks impossible or wrong. Nothing explains why it is up there. It could be read as thrown or blowing in the air, but no one is near it and there are no motion lines.
- The dog's position is unclear. It could be sitting on the doorstep behind the bench, or perched on top of the bench. Its feet and shadow line up with the top edge of the bench.
- Kicking: the girl may be about to kick the ball, or may have just kicked it. Her foot is very close to the ball. The motion lines suggest the leg has just swung through.
- The dog bowl is close to the girl's planted foot. She might be about to kick or step on it, but it is probably just lying on the lawn.
- The dog holding its own leash probably means it wants a walk. The loose end lying on the ground is a little odd.
