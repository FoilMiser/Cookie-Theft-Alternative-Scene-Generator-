# Picture 42

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back with a gate at the right end, a paved path at the right, and a metal A-frame swing set with a red swing seat near the right. An old man in a flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is standing in the middle holding the end of a garden hose up near his face and peering into the nozzle, where only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose runs from a teal hose reel next to the flower bed, along the grass, and a tan dog with a red collar is standing with a back paw on the hose; just before the dog's foot the hose is swollen into a big bulge, as if the water is blocked there. It looks like the dog is accidentally pinching off the water, and as soon as the dog steps off the hose the water will probably burst out and spray the old man in the face. There is also a colourful flower bed, a bush at the corner of the house and a teal watering can on the grass near the path.

- Dog - front, left of centre: tan/brown dog with a white chest and paws and a red collar; standing on the hose with a back paw, looking to the right.
- House - left: cream walls, red roof, four-pane window, blue door with step.
- Garden hose - from the reel along the front of the picture to the old man's hands: teal hose with a large bulge just behind where the dog is standing.
- Hose nozzle - in the old man's hands: pointed toward him, with a single water drop coming out.
- Hose reel - left: teal reel next to the flower bed, where the hose starts.
- Swing set - right of centre: dark grey A-frame with two hanging chains/ropes and a red seat; one swing.
- Watering can - lower right on the grass near the path: teal, not in use.
- Flower bed - left centre: oval soil bed with red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers.
- Bush - left, at the corner of the house.
- Garden gate and paved path - right.
- Tree - behind the house.
- Sky - two white clouds, yellow sun top right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The bulge in the hose is just to the left of the dog's back paw; I read this as the dog standing on the hose and blocking the water, but it could also be read as a knot or kink in the hose that the dog is merely next to.
- The old man is pointing the nozzle back toward himself and looking into it; this looks like he is checking why no water is coming out, which suggests he may get sprayed, but that is an inference.
- There seems to be only one swing seat on the frame, with the left side empty.
