# Picture 26

## Free description

This is a back garden on a sunny day, with the sun in the top left and two white clouds. A woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers is painting the wooden fence blue with a long-handled brush; a section of the fence boards behind the washing line post is already painted blue. On the grass near her feet are a paint tin and a paint tray with blue paint in it. A trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the grass to a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar, which is walking away to the left onto the paved path with blue paint on its paws - the dog has clearly stepped in the paint. In the front centre a man with dark skin and a beard, wearing a green shirt and beige trousers, stands with his hands on his hips, and he has a blue bucket (it looks like an upside-down paint bucket) on his head, covering it down to his eyes. A black-and-white cat is walking along the top of the fence at the upper left. A red dog bowl with food sits on the grass at the bottom centre. On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door, a flower bed, a bush, and a wooden bench, and a washing line in the middle holds a yellow striped item and blue jeans. A wooden gate is in the fence at the far left.
