# Picture 27

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence along the back with a gate on the left, and a paved path running down the left side. A black-and-white cat is walking along the top of the fence. In the front centre, a woman or older girl with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers is digging a hole in the lawn with a shovel; there is a pile of dirt beside the hole and a clump of soil on the shovel blade. On the right, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt is watering the flower bed in front of the house, but she is using a frying pan: water pours out of the pan in an arc onto the flowers. This is the odd thing in the picture, especially because a proper teal watering can is sitting unused on the grass at the front left by the path. Two potted plants stand on the grass in the left-centre, with a bush near the fence behind them. There is a tree behind the house, hedges behind the fence, the sun in the top left and two clouds.
