# Picture 09

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with no people or animals in it. A tall wooden fence runs across the back, with a wooden gate at the left and a hedge and a tree behind it. On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue front door, with a small bush beside it. A paved path runs from the gate down to the bottom left corner. Two small potted plants in terracotta pots sit on the lawn left of centre, and a teal watering can stands on the grass near the bottom of the path. A round flower bed full of red, yellow, orange, purple and pink flowers is in front of the house. The strange part is a closed cardboard box hanging in the middle of the sky above the fence, tilted at an angle as if it were flying or falling. No one is there to have thrown it, and it isn't attached to anything. Apart from that, the scene is quiet and empty.
