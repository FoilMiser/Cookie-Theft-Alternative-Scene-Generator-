# pic_33

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden seen the other way round: the cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, the sun is in the top right, and the wooden gate and paved path are on the right. A woman with a dark ponytail, a purple T-shirt and teal trousers stands near the house, holding a teal watering can over a bed of colourful flowers. Something impossible is happening: instead of pouring down onto the flowers, the water shoots upward out of the spout in a curved arc, with droplets flying off the top. So the flowers aren't getting any water. Two potted plants stand on the lawn in the middle right, and an empty grey wheelbarrow sits at the bottom. A large tree grows behind the house, and green hedges show above the fence. Apart from the water going the wrong way, it's a quiet, ordinary scene with one person in it.
