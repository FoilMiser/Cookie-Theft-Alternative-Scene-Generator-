# Picture 18

## Free description

This is a windy, sunny day in a back garden with a wooden fence, a house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a gate in the fence on the left. White wind lines are drawn in the sky and across the garden. In the middle, an old woman with grey hair and glasses has her arms thrown up and her mouth open in surprise. A red cap is blowing away above her, along with some leaves, and she seems to be trying to catch it. On the clothesline behind her, a yellow towel with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans are blowing out sideways in the wind. On the left, standing on the paved path by the gate, a woman in a purple T-shirt and teal trousers has her hands on her hips. Oddly, she is wearing an upside-down blue bucket on her head. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress kneels by a colourful flower bed, tending the flowers. Next to her is a pink flower in a clay pot that she may be about to plant. A teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow sit on the grass at the front.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The bucket on the woman's head could be read two ways. It may be deliberate, like a joke or a hat, or it may have been blown or dropped onto her head. Her face is partly under the bucket rim, so her expression is hard to read. With her hands on her hips, she could be annoyed, or just standing calmly.
- The cap has probably just blown off the old woman's head and she is trying to grab it. It could also belong to someone else.
- The wind is strong enough to blow the cap and the laundry, but it is shown only in some places. The kneeling woman's hair, the bucket-woman's hair and ponytail, the flowers and the tree are not moving. This may be a drawing simplification rather than an intended oddity.
- I can't tell if the kneeling woman is planting the potted flower or picking or weeding the bed.
