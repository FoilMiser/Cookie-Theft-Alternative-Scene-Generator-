# Picture 01

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park, with a bright sun in the top-left corner, two white clouds, green rolling hills and trees in the background. A sandy path winds from the bottom of the picture back toward the hills, with a big tree to its left and a wooden park bench to its right. Just behind the bench, a tall wooden ladder stands upright in the grass at a slant, leaning against nothing at all, which looks impossible. In the middle, a woman with a brown ponytail, blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes is fishing; her rod reaches out over a blue pond and the line drops down to a red-and-white float in the water. In front of the pond, a child with dark curly hair, a yellow top and orange shorts holds a small white cup and stretches an arm toward the pond, apparently throwing food; little brown crumbs are scattered on the grass in front of the child and a few more float on the water. A white duck is swimming across the pond toward the crumbs on the water, with motion lines behind it showing it is moving. There are reeds or cattails at both ends of the pond, a black lamppost in the centre, a smaller tree and bushes on the right, a grey rock at the bottom and a green rubbish bin cut off at the bottom-right corner. The free-standing ladder is the one thing that looks wrong.
