# Picture 02

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden, with a yellow sun at top-left, two clouds, a tall wooden fence across the back and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the left, a woman with short dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes is walking along a concrete path, holding a red lead attached to a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar, which is trotting ahead of her toward the right. In the middle of the garden is a washing line strung between two wooden posts, with a yellow-and-red striped shirt or towel and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. High above the washing line, a cardboard box is hanging in the empty sky, tilted at an angle, with nothing holding it up — this looks impossible. Near the house is a round bed of colourful flowers (yellow, orange, red, pink and purple). At the bottom right, a small brown-and-white puppy is curled up asleep on the grass, and near the bottom centre there is a red bowl with dog food in it. Behind the fence you can see a birdhouse on a pole on the left and a leafy tree on the right, and there is a closed wooden gate in the fence at far left. Apart from the floating box, everything looks calm and ordinary.
