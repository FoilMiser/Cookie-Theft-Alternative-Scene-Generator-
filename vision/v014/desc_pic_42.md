## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a gate in the fence at the far left, a paved path running from the gate down the left side, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A boy with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands on the left of the lawn holding the nozzle of a garden hose up to his face and peering straight into it; a single drop of water is coming out of the nozzle. The hose runs from his hands down across the lawn to a hose reel by the flower bed on the right. A brown-and-white dog with a red collar is standing in the middle of the lawn with its back feet on the hose. On the reel side of the dog the hose is swollen into a big bulge, as if water has built up behind the spot where the dog is pressing it flat. It looks like as soon as the dog steps off, the trapped water will shoot out of the nozzle into the boy's face. A small red dog bowl with food sits on the grass at the front left. Behind them a washing line between two posts holds a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans. There is a birdhouse on a pole behind the fence on the left, a tree behind the fence on the right, a bed of colourful flowers in front of the house, a bright sun at top left and two white clouds.
