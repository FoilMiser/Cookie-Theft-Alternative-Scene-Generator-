# Picture 20

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back and a gate in the fence on the right. In the middle, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is hanging out washing. He reaches up with both hands to peg a white T-shirt on the clothesline, where a pair of blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel already hang. On the right, a little girl in a pink dress sits on a swing, smiling with her eyes closed. Small motion lines beside the seat suggest she is swinging. Her arms are stretched out to the sides, so she may not be holding on. At the far right, on the paved path by the gate, a small child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds the string of an orange kite flying high in the sky. There is a colourful flower bed in front of the house, and a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can lie on the grass at the front. The scene is mostly calm and ordinary.

- I can't tell for sure whether the girl on the swing is holding on. Her arms reach out sideways roughly to where the swing ropes are. One reading is that she is holding the ropes. The other is that her hands are out past the ropes and she is letting go while swinging, which would be risky.
- I can't tell which way the kite child is facing. Their head seems turned to the right, toward the gate, rather than up toward the kite, which is to the upper left. They might be looking away from the kite, or they might be facing it and I am misreading the small face.
- The kite is flying high, which needs wind, but the washing on the line hangs straight down with no wind. This may be an inconsistency or just a simplification.
- The yellow towel is behind the man's head, so part of it is hidden.
