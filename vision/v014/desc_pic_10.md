# Picture 10

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left. There is a wooden fence with a gate at the back right and a paved path at the front right. A blue ball with a white stripe is stuck high in the tree behind the house. A person with brown hair in a blue hoodie stands by the fence pointing up at it. In the middle, a young man in an orange cap and an orange high-visibility vest is spraying water from a garden hose. The hose comes from a hose reel by the house, and the water arcs over onto the flower bed. A washing line behind him has blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel pegged on it. At the front left, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a wooden bench licking a pink ice-cream cone. A white dog with brown spots stretches up at her feet, looking up as if begging for the ice cream. On the right, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is walking while reading an open book or paper. She is heading towards a blue bucket on the grass that she might trip over. A man in a red shirt and jeans stands near the path waving one raised hand, with motion lines, as if calling to someone. At the front centre, a brown dog with a red collar crouches with its bottom in the air and its tail wagging, next to a yellow-green tennis ball. It looks like it wants to play.
