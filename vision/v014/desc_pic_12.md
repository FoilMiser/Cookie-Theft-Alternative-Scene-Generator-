# Picture 12

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park with rolling green hills, trees, bushes, a lamp post and a winding sandy path on the right. On the left is a small oval pond with reeds or cattails at each end. A white duck is swimming across the pond, leaving a wake behind it, towards some small brown pieces of food floating on the water. A young woman with a brown ponytail in a blue hoodie and black trousers stands at the edge of the pond scattering food from a small bag or cup. Some pieces have landed in the water and some on the grass. Just to the right, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is fishing in the same small pond with a long rod. Her red-and-white float is bobbing in the water close to where the food is being thrown and the duck is heading. At the front, a bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket next to a wicker picnic basket. His eyes are closed and he is smiling broadly or laughing. There is a grey rock in front of the blanket, and green rubbish bins stand by the lamp post and at the bottom left corner.
