# Picture 19

## Free description

This is a park on a grey, gloomy day. There is no sun, and three dark storm clouds hang overhead as if rain is coming. In the centre front, a woman in a purple shirt and teal trousers looks up with her mouth open and points at the sky. A red bird sits on top of a tall black lamp post, and behind the post an old woman with grey hair and glasses waves her hand. On the path at the back right, a man in a red shirt and jeans is walking along with his nose in an open book. There is a red bucket on the grass right in front of him, so he looks about to trip over it. On a bench on the right, an older man in a flat cap and waistcoat reads a red book while a blue bird pecks at the sandwich beside him. At the bottom right, a little girl in a pink dress is crying and holding an empty cone. Her pink ice cream is on the ground, and a tan dog is sniffing or licking it up. A white spotted dog is playing with a tennis ball on the path, and a child in yellow sits on a red-and-white picnic blanket with a basket. On the far left, a wooden ladder stands upright and tilted on the grass without leaning against anything, which is impossible. There is also a pond with reeds, a colourful flower bed and some trees.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The woman in purple could be pointing at the dark storm clouds (as if noticing rain coming) or at something up near the pond's reeds. Her finger points up and to the left. The red bird on the lamp post is up and to the right of her, so she is probably not pointing at the bird, though she could be.
- I can't tell who the old woman is waving at. It could be the man on the path, the woman pointing, or someone outside the picture.
- The weather looks stormy, but nobody has an umbrella and people are picnicking and sitting out. The sky could be read either as rain about to start or just as a dull day.
- The man reading while walking seems about to trip over or kick the red bucket. Nothing has happened yet.
- I can't tell for certain whether the blue bird is eating the sandwich or just standing by it. Its head is down at the sandwich.
