## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path in the bottom right corner. Near the house, a small child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on a wooden bench drinking from a cup. In the middle, a bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers is holding what looks like a dark frying pan out over the flower bed, and a stream of water is pouring out of it onto the flowers, as if he is watering them with a pan. That is the odd thing: a coiled garden hose on a reel sits right beside the flower bed, and a watering can lies on the grass nearby, yet he is using a pan. Behind him, a washing line between two wooden posts has a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes pegged on it. To the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers, with a dark ponytail, is painting the fence blue with a long-handled brush; part of the fence is already blue and a paint tin stands at her feet. A birdhouse on a post sticks up behind the fence. A grey wheelbarrow sits at the front of the lawn.
