# Picture 17

## Free description

This is a sunny park with a small blue pond on the right and a winding sandy footpath on the left. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands on the grass beside the pond, fishing with a long rod. Her line goes down into the water, where a red-and-white float bobs with a ripple around it. A white duck is swimming in the pond a little to the right of the float. On the left, beside the path, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers holds a camera up to his face and is taking a picture toward the right, in the direction of the woman and the pond. A tall black lamp post stands between them, with a green rubbish bin next to it. There are trees on the left and right, bushes, rolling green hills in the background, a grey rock in the front and another green bin at the bottom-right corner. Nothing looks impossible. It is a quiet, ordinary scene.
