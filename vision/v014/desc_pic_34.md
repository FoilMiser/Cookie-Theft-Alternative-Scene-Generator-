## Free description
This is a sunny but windy back garden. White wind streaks cross the sky, and leaves are blowing near the tree and near the gate. On the left a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands on the path by the gate. His arms are raised and his mouth is open in surprise as his red cap is blown off his head and flies away up and to the left. Next to him, a man in an orange cap, an orange hi-vis vest and blue jeans is painting the fence with a long-handled brush. He has turned a section of the brown fence bright blue, and a pot of blue paint stands on the grass by his feet. On the right an elderly woman with a grey bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt holds a teal watering can over the flower bed. Strangely, the water comes out of the spout and curves upward into the air, with droplets at the top, instead of falling onto the flowers. A hose reel sits unused beside the flower bed, and an empty wheelbarrow is at the front. Something about to happen: the red cap is about to blow away over the fence or gate. Something odd: the painter's cap stays put in the same wind.
