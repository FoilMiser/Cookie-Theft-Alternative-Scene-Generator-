# Picture 13

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left. There is a wooden fence with a gate at the back right and a paved path at the front right. A small girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes stands in the middle of the lawn near the fence, holding a string and flying an orange diamond-shaped kite. The kite is high in the sky near the sun, with a tail of small bows. In the tree behind the house there is an orange fish sitting among the leaves, which is impossible or at least very strange. At the front, a white goat with small horns has its head down and is eating a tuft of grass. A grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles is parked at the front centre, and a teal watering can stands near the path. Two potted plants sit on the lawn by the fence, and a flower bed full of colourful flowers is in front of the house. Nobody else is around.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fish in the tree is clearly drawn as a fish. A real fish in a tree is impossible. It could be read as a mistake in the picture, or possibly as a toy or decoration, but nothing shows that.
- A goat grazing in a home back garden is unusual but possible.
- The girl looks quite small for the size of the fence and kite. She could be a young child, or she could just be drawn small because she is further back.
- The kite string is a very long, thin line, so it is easy to miss that it connects the girl to the kite.
