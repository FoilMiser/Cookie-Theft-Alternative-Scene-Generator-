# Picture 22

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence along the back, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a paved path on the left leading from a wooden gate in the fence. Three people are doing garden and household jobs. On the left, a boy in a blue hoodie and dark trousers stands next to a metal swing set and rakes a small pile of leaves on the grass. In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes reaches up to a clothesline to peg up washing: a white T-shirt, a yellow towel with red stripes, and a pair of blue jeans. On the right, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers holds a teal watering can over a bed of colourful flowers in front of the house. The odd thing is the water: instead of pouring down onto the flowers, it shoots upward out of the spout in a curved stream, with droplets flying up into the air above the flowers. An empty wheelbarrow stands on the grass at the front, and a wooden bench stands at the far right in front of the house. Behind the fence there is a birdhouse on a pole, a big tree behind the house, and hedges. The sky is blue with a sun in the top left and two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water from the watering can goes up, not down. It could be read as (a) water impossibly flowing upward out of the can, or (b) a fountain-like spray going up and about to fall back down onto the flowers. Either way it is not how a watering can normally works.
- The boy's eyes look like closed lines. He could be squinting in the sun, concentrating, or pulling a face; I can't tell which.
- The woman's face is hidden by her raised arms and hair, so I can't see her expression or tell which way her face is turned.
- The birdhouse pole disappears behind the fence, so it is unclear whether it stands in this garden or in the next one.
- The tree is drawn behind both the house and the fence, so it may be in a neighbouring garden.
