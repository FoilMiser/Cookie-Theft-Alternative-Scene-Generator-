# Picture 21

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back and a gate in the fence on the left. At the far left, by the gate, a small child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts looks upset. The child holds a yellow and a purple balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other toward a red balloon that has floated away, its string dangling out of reach. In the middle of the garden, a woman in a purple shirt and teal trousers is watering a colourful flower bed with a garden hose. The water arcs onto the flowers, and the hose runs back along the grass to a hose reel by the house. In the front centre, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands with his hands on his hips, wearing an upside-down blue bucket on his head. There is an empty swing set on the left, a clothesline with a striped towel and blue jeans hanging still, and a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow on the grass at the front. Apart from the bucket on the man's head, nothing looks impossible.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The red balloon could be read two ways. It may have just slipped out of the child's hand and floated away, or the child may be reaching up to grab it before it goes. Either way the child isn't holding it.
- The bucket on the man's head could be worn on purpose as a joke or a hat, or it could be stuck. His expression is hard to read under the bucket rim. It might be a cheeky grin or a neutral look.
- The swing set's legs stand on the edge of the paved path rather than on the grass. This is slightly odd but not impossible.
- The woman is using a hose while a watering can sits unused nearby. This is not strange, just an observation.
