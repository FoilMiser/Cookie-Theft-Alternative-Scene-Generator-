# Picture 11

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left. There is a wooden fence with a gate at the back right, a paved path at the front right, and a flower bed and hose reel near the house. At the back, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red lead, both heading to the right. At the front right, an older man in a cap, white shirt, brown vest and dark trousers is holding the nozzle of a garden hose up to his face and peering into it. A drop is showing at the tip. The hose runs from the reel by the house across the lawn to him. A brown dog with a red collar is standing on the hose in the middle of the lawn. The hose just behind the dog's paws is swollen into a big bulge, as if the water is blocked there. It looks like when the dog steps off, the water will shoot out into the man's face. Separately, a cardboard box is floating in the sky near the sun, which doesn't make sense.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The brown dog's back paw seems to be on the hose. It could also be standing just beside it. The bulge is just behind its feet, which suggests it is pinching the hose.
- The man could be peering into the nozzle to see why no water is coming out. He could also be about to drink from it.
- The cardboard box in the sky could be falling or thrown, or floating impossibly. Nothing explains it.
- The bulge in the hose is a cartoon exaggeration. A real hose would not swell that much.
- It is unclear whether the woman is coming into the garden or heading out towards the gate.
