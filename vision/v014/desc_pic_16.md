# Picture 16

## Free description

This is a busy, sunny playground with lots going on at once. In the front middle, a small child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is crying with a hand over the eyes and holds an empty ice-cream cone. The pink scoop has fallen on the ground beside them in a pink puddle, and a tan dog with a red collar is sniffing or licking it up. Behind them, on a bench on the right, an old woman with grey hair and glasses is reading a red book. A blue bird on the bench next to her is pecking at her sandwich, and she doesn't seem to notice. On the far right, a person in a blue hoodie points up at a blue ball stuck in the branches of a tree. In the middle at the back, a woman in a yellow dress carries a cardboard box. On the far left, someone in a red shirt and jeans is eating a pink ice-cream cone beside the slide. A red bird is flying in the sky. Some things are clearly impossible. At the front left, a white dog is sitting up reading a newspaper like a person. In the middle, there is a red bicycle with square wheels. An orange cat is curled up asleep on the grass at the front, and there is an empty sandbox with a bucket and spade.
