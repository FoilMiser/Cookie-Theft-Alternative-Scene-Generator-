# Picture 23

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof on the left, a wooden fence along the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. A brown-and-white dog sits at the blue back door of the house holding a red lead in its mouth, as if it wants to go for a walk; the lead trails down to the grass in a loop. In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes reaches up to a clothesline to peg up washing: blue jeans, a yellow striped towel and a white T-shirt. To the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds her hands out in front of her, ready to catch a red ball that is up in the air. A dotted line shows that the ball was thrown in an arc by a small child in a yellow top and orange shorts who is standing on the path near the gate. At the front right, a boy in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is sweeping the path with a broom. A red dog bowl full of food sits on the grass at the bottom centre, away from the dog. There is a flower bed in front of the house, a tree behind the house, hedges behind the fence, and the sun in the top right with two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Direction of the ball: the dotted line connects the child's hand to the ball, so the most likely reading is that the child threw it to Woman 2. A second reading is that Woman 2 threw it and the dotted line shows where it will land near the child, but her hands are open in a catching pose, which fits the first reading better.
- There is a small yellow circle just behind the dog's ear. It could be a tag on its collar, but it sits where the door knob would be, so it is probably the door knob showing behind the dog.
- The dog's breed is unclear (it could be a boxer or beagle type).
- The food bowl is far from the dog, out in the middle of the lawn; it is not clear whether this is meant to mean anything.
- The boy's closed-looking eyes could mean squinting, concentrating, or just the drawing style.
