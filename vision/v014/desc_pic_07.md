## Free description
This is a sunny town street paved with grey slabs, in front of a row of four shopfronts (cream, peach, pale green and pink) with striped awnings, and a yellow sun in the top-right corner. In the front centre, a small girl in a pink dress is crying, rubbing her eye with one hand and holding an empty ice-cream cone in the other; her pink scoop of ice cream with sprinkles has fallen and splattered on the pavement. A brown dog with a red collar or bandana, which has no lead, is bending down right next to the fallen ice cream with its tongue out, licking or about to eat it. Behind them, a woman with short dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes is walking to the right carrying a yellow shopping bag. A man with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands in front of the green shop holding a camera up to his face, taking a photo toward the left. On the right, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is walking a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar on a red lead, and behind them is a fruit stall with crates of red, orange, yellow and green fruit. On the left side there is a leafy tree in a round planter, a green rubbish bin, and a potted plant in the bottom-left corner, and there is a black lamppost near the centre-right.
