# Picture 14

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. There is a wooden fence with a gate at the back left and a paved path at the front left. On the left is a grey metal swing set with one red seat, hanging empty and still. At the back, a young woman with a brown ponytail in a blue hoodie is painting the fence blue with a long-handled brush or roller. Several boards are already blue. Her paint tin and paint tray are on the grass behind her. A white dog with brown spots has walked through the paint and is leaving a trail of blue paw prints as it walks away towards the swings. At the front, a boy in a red shirt and blue jeans is drinking from a white cup. He is in a sitting position with his knees bent, but there is no chair or bench under him, so he is sitting on thin air. An empty wooden bench is nearby at the front right. A brown dog is sitting on the doorstep of the house holding a red lead in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. A grey wheelbarrow, a teal watering can and a flower bed of colourful flowers complete the scene.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The boy sitting on nothing is the most striking oddity. He could be meant to be sitting on an invisible chair, or a chair may be missing from the drawing. The empty bench is to his right, not under him.
- The boy's cup could be a mug, a cup, or a small carton.
- The painter could be a teenage girl or a young woman. Her tool could be a brush on a long stick or a roller.
- The brown dog's eyes look closed or squinted. It could be happy or eager, or it could be dozing. The lead in its mouth suggests it wants a walk.
- The swing hangs perfectly still and empty. It is unclear whether anything is meant to be happening with it.
