# Picture 28

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny town square or high street with a row of colourful shops (cream, peach, green and pink buildings with striped awnings) and a tiled pavement in front. It is windy: white wind lines blow across the sky, leaves are flying off a tree, and a man in a green shirt has just had his orange cap blown off his head; he has both arms up in the air and looks surprised as the cap flies above him. In the tree on the left there is an orange fish sitting among the leaves, which is impossible. At the front left, a small child in a yellow top and orange shorts is crying with a hand to one eye and an empty ice-cream cone in the other hand, because the pink ice cream has fallen on the ground; a brown dog with a red collar is licking up the dropped ice cream. In the middle, an old man in a cap sits on a bench happily eating a pink ice cream while a white dog with brown spots stretches up toward him, seemingly after his ice cream. A boy in a red T-shirt waters a flower box with a teal watering can, and a teal bicycle is parked at a bike rack. On the right, an elderly woman with glasses is walking along reading an open book and is about to walk into a blue bucket on the ground in front of her. Further right there is a fruit and vegetable stall with crates of produce, and a girl with a ponytail in a blue hoodie runs off to the right. An orange cat sleeps curled up on the pavement at the bottom, and there is a grey litter bin and a black street lamp.
