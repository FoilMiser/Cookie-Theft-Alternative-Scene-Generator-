# Picture 25

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with no people or animals in it except one odd thing. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door with a grey step, and behind it a big green tree. Sitting in the leaves of that tree is an orange goldfish, which is impossible since fish live in water, not trees. In the middle of the garden is a clothesline between two wooden posts with a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes pegged on it; the right half of the line is empty. In front of the house there is a round flower bed with red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers. On the right, two potted plants stand on the grass, a paved path runs down from a wooden gate in the fence, and a teal watering can sits on the grass near the path. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands at the front centre. A birdhouse on a pole sticks up behind the fence on the right, and there are hedges along the back, the sun in the top right and two clouds.
