# Picture 08

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence. There is a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right, and a wooden gate and a paved path on the left. Many people and animals are busy at once. At the front left a boy in a blue hoodie is sweeping dirt into a red dustpan with a huge silver dinner fork as tall as he is, and a small cloud of dust is rising. Next to him an older man in a cap and brown vest walks along reading an open book, heading towards a red bucket on the grass that he may bump into. At the back a woman in a yellow dress is painting part of the fence blue with a long-handled brush. Her paint tin and paint tray are on the ground, and a white dog with brown spots has stepped in the paint and is leaving a trail of blue paw prints across the lawn. An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses carries a cardboard box under a washing line, where a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans are hanging. A small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts points up at a blue ball stuck in the branches of the tree by the house. In the middle of the lawn a small brown-and-white puppy is curled up asleep, and there is a red bowl of dog food at the front. A white goat with horns is eating a tuft of grass next to a grey wheelbarrow whose wheel looks square. At the front right a bearded man in a green shirt sits on a wooden bench reading a red book beside a bed of colourful flowers.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fork is shaped exactly like a giant table fork. Someone could call it a garden fork or pitchfork. Either way it is the wrong tool for sweeping.
- The wheelbarrow wheel looks like a dark square frame with spokes, not a circle. This is either an impossible square wheel or a drawing quirk.
- The elderly woman's face and box point left, but her stride is unclear, so she could be walking left or right.
- The red bucket is ahead of the reading man and slightly to one side. He may walk into it, or he may just pass it.
- The painter's tool could be a brush on a long stick or a paint roller.
- The reading man could be holding a book or a booklet or newspaper.
- The small boy is most likely pointing at the ball, but he could be pointing generally at the tree.
- A goat in a home back garden is unusual but not impossible.
- The puppy could be asleep or just lying down with its eyes closed.
