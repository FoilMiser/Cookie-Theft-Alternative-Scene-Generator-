# iso_01

## What I see
A cartoon wheelbarrow sits on a plain green background with a soft darker-green oval shadow under it. It has a gray, open-topped tray shaped like a trapezoid, two brown wooden handles sticking out to the upper left, and two short dark legs under the left part of the tray. On the right end, a gray-blue axle bar runs from under the tray to the hub of the wheel. There are no people or animals; the object is for carrying loads such as soil or garden material.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The wheel is drawn as a **square** (a dark square frame with spokes radiating from a central hub) rather than a circle. It could be read as a badly drawn wheel or as a square frame or grate. Either way, a square wheel looks wrong for a wheelbarrow.
- The wheel sits beside the right end of the tray instead of under its front. It is roughly as tall as the tray, so it looks attached to the side rather than carrying the load.
- The handles meet the tray at its upper-left rim, as if they pass over or into the tray's top edge, rather than running underneath the tray as they would on a real wheelbarrow.
- The two legs are dark and close together. They could be read as one leg, or two legs drawn overlapping in perspective.
