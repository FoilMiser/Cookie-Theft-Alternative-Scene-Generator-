# iso_02

## What I see
A tan/orange-brown dog with a darker brown floppy ear, a white muzzle, white paws and a red collar is standing on plain green grass. It is in a "play bow" or stretching pose: its rear end is up on straight hind legs, and its front legs reach far forward and down. Its tail is raised, with motion lines beside it to show wagging. Its tongue sticks out and its head faces right. A yellow-green tennis ball sits on the ground at its front paws, and one paw touches the ball, as if the dog is pawing at it or about to play with it.

- The front legs look unusually long, thin and straight. They stretch out at a shallow diagonal like sticks, so the proportions look a little odd for a dog, and the head sits high above them.
- The front paws press right up against the ball's thick outline, so it is unclear whether a paw is on top of or touching the ball, or slightly overlapping it.
