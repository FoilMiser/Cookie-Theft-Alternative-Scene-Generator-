# desc_iso_03

## What I see
A gray wheelbarrow sits on green grass with a soft darker-green shadow under it. It has a gray trapezoid tray, two brown wooden handles that stick out to the upper left, a dark leg or stand under the tray, and a wheel at the right end attached by a blue-gray axle bar with a round hub. No one is using it; it is just parked. Behind it, at the top, is a wooden park bench (brown seat slat on dark legs, with the top cut off by the frame). A beige curved area along the left edge looks like a path or sand patch.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The wheel looks square: a dark square frame with spokes coming out of the round hub, instead of a round tire. It could be meant as a round spoked wheel, but as drawn it reads as a square. This looks unintentionally strange.
- The wheel is off to the right side of the tray, partly behind it, rather than under the front of the tray, so the wheelbarrow's proportions look odd.
- The dark part under the tray could be one thick leg or two legs overlapping. Either way, the support looks short and stubby.
- The two handles are stacked one above the other instead of running side by side. They could be read as both handles seen from the side, or as one handle plus a crossbar.
- The beige shape on the left could be a path, a sandbox or a patch of dirt. The handles stick out over its edge.
- The bench is cut off at the top, so I can't tell if it has a backrest.
