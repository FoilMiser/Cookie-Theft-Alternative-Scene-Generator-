# iso_01: description

## What I see
A cartoon wheelbarrow stands on a plain green background, with a darker green oval shadow underneath it. It has a grey tray that is wider at the top than at the bottom, two parallel brown wooden handles sticking out to the upper left, and a dark leg (possibly two legs overlapping) under the back of the tray. At the front (right) there is a dark wheel with spokes and a small round hub, joined to the tray by a short grey-blue bar or axle arm. No person or animal is in the picture. The object is a garden or construction wheelbarrow for carrying loads.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The wheel is the main oddity. Its outer rim is drawn as a square or rounded box, not a circle, and the spokes radiate from the hub inside that square. It reads either as a square wheel (which looks wrong) or as a wheel sitting inside a square frame or bracket. A real wheelbarrow wheel would be round.
- The wheel overlaps the lower right corner of the tray. It is unclear whether the wheel is meant to be in front of the tray or under it; the tray's edge and the wheel box seem to cross each other.
- The handles join the tray at its upper-left rim rather than running underneath it as frame rails. This makes them look stuck onto the side of the tray, and they angle upward a lot, as if the barrow is tipped or the handles are raised.
- The dark shape under the back of the tray could be a single thick leg or two legs drawn on top of each other.
- The bottom of the leg and the bottom of the wheel box sit at about the same height on the shadow, so the barrow seems to stand level. The handles then look oddly steep for a barrow at rest.
