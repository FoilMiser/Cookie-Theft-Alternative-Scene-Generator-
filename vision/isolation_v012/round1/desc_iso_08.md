# iso_08

## What I see
A tan/orange-brown dog with a darker brown floppy ear, white paws and a red collar is on plain green grass. It is in a "play bow": its front legs stretch forward low to the ground, its rear end is up, and its tail is raised with motion lines beside it, which shows wagging. Its front paws are right next to a yellow-green tennis ball, and its tongue is sticking out, so it looks like it is playing with the ball or inviting play. A soft shadow is under it, and nothing else is in the cut-out.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The red thing at the neck is most likely a collar, but it could also be a small bandana or scarf.
- The round object reads as a tennis ball (it has a curved seam line), but it could also be a green ball of another kind or a lime.
- Only one hind leg is visible, and it is long, thin and very straight, almost stick-like. The tail is also very long, thin and straight up. These give the back half of the dog an odd, stiff look.
- The body is shaped like a long wedge sloping from the raised hips down to the chest. The front legs are long and thin and reach out diagonally to the ball, which makes the proportions look a little stretched.
- The small dark shape under the nose reads as a tongue sticking out, but it could also be read as an open mouth.
