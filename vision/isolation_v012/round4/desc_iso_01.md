## What I see
A wheelbarrow sits on a plain green background, with no people or animals. It has a grey, open, trapezoid-shaped tub that looks empty. Two brown wooden handles stick out to the left and slope slightly down toward their ends. Two short dark legs hold up the back of the tub. On the right, a grey-blue arm runs from under the tub to the hub of a single wheel. It is an object for carrying loads, and nobody is using it here.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The wheel looks like a square with rounded corners, not a circle. Its grey face has spokes or an X shape inside. It might be meant as a round tyre drawn in a blocky style, but literally it reads as a square wheel, which looks wrong for a wheelbarrow.
- The wheel sits mostly to the right of the tub, and the tub's lower-right corner overlaps the wheel's upper-left corner. The wheel could be read as being in front of the tub or behind it.
- The handles join the tub at its top-left rim, not running underneath it as a real wheelbarrow frame would. They seem to start at the corner of the tub, so it is unclear how they attach.
- The two legs are slightly different lengths and angles. One appears to stand in front of the other. The bottoms of the legs and the wheel are at roughly the same level.
- There is no ground line or shadow, so the wheelbarrow could look like it is floating against the green, which might be grass or just a flat background.
