A man with dark skin and short hair, in a green shirt, beige trousers and brown shoes, sits at the lower left end of the seesaw with his feet on the ground. - The motion lines under the girl's feet could mean she is bouncing or moving up and down, or swinging or kicking her legs. They could also be meant as a shadow or a "dangling" mark, since the seesaw itself looks still.
- Her mouth is a small open "o". She could be calling out to the man, protesting, or just surprised.
- Where the man sits, the red beam crosses between his knees and his feet, at shin level. It looks as if the beam passes in front of or through his lower legs instead of under his seat, so it is unclear how he is actually sitting on it, whether astride, sideways or on the very end.
- Something dark on the man's lower face could be a short beard or stubble, or just shading.
- The dark vertical post at the far right edge is cut off, and I can't tell what it is: a lamp post, a pole or a sign.
