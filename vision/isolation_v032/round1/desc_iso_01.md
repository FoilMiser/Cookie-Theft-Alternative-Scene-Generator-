A man in a green shirt and tan trousers sits on the low left end, reading an open book with lines of text that he holds in his hands. His eyes are on the book, not on the other rider. A girl in a pink dress, with her dark hair in a bun, sits on the raised right end. Her hands are together in front of her chest, her mouth is slightly open, and she holds onto a dark handle behind her.
