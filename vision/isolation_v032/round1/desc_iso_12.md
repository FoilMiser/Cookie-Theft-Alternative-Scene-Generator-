# iso_12

## What I see
A red seesaw on a grey triangular stand sits on green grass in front of a dark metal railing fence. A bearded man in a green shirt, khaki trousers and brown shoes sits on the low left end. He is reading an open book that rests on his knee and has his feet near the ground. A small girl in a pink dress with her hair in two buns sits on the raised right end behind a black T-shaped handle. Her mouth is open in an "o", and motion lines under her yellow shoes suggest she is swinging or kicking her legs. The seesaw is tipped down on the man's side, which fits with him being the heavier rider.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The man's legs are hard to read. They look crossed with one knee raised under the book, but the trousers and shoes sit partly in front of and partly below the beam. It is not clear whether he is sitting on top of the beam or in front of it, and his lower legs look slightly tangled with it.
- The man's hand and the corner of the book rest on or touch the beam. He could be steadying himself on the seesaw, or it could just be where the book's edge meets the beam. There is no handle on his end.
- At the top-right corner, a large dark circular outline is cut off by the frame. It could be a tree canopy, a wheel, a ball, or part of some other play equipment. A dark vertical bar runs along the right edge at the top, perhaps a post or pole. Neither can be identified.
- The fence bars stop a little above the grass with rounded ends. They may be standing far back in the grass, or they may look slightly as if they float.
