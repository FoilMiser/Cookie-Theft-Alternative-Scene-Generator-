## What I see
A playground on green grass, with a dark grey metal railing fence across the back. A red seesaw sits on a grey triangular support. The left end is up in the air and the right end is down near the ground. A small girl in a pink dress, with dark hair in two buns and yellow shoes, sits on the high left end. Her mouth is a round open "o", and curved motion lines under her feet suggest her legs are swinging or kicking. A grown man in a green shirt, khaki trousers and brown shoes sits on the low right end with his legs crossed. He is reading an open book with a red cover that rests on his raised knee, and he is not looking at the girl.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl's expression can be read two ways: surprised or alarmed at being stuck up high, or calling out (perhaps to the man).
- The girl's hands are clasped up near her chin. The dark seesaw handle is a little lower, in front of her. She could be holding the handle, or her hands could be held together near her face without touching it.
- The curved lines under the girl's feet could be motion lines for swinging legs, or marks on the ground or a shadow.
- The man's lower body overlaps the red beam. It is not completely clear whether he is sitting on top of the beam or whether the beam passes behind or through his thighs. His feet are flat on the grass beside the low end.
- A dark shape near the man's back or hip may be the seesaw's second handle behind him. It is hard to make out.
- In the top-left corner there is a partly cut-off green circle with a dark outline, probably a treetop or bush. No trunk is visible, so it looks a little like it is floating above the fence.
- Dark vertical poles are cut off at the left and right edges, and the right one leans slightly. They could be part of a swing frame or a post, but there is not enough of them to tell.
- The fence's vertical bars stop in the grass with no bottom rail. This reads fine as bars set into the ground, but it is slightly unfinished-looking.
