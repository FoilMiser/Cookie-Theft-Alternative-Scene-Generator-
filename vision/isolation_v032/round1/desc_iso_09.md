A man in a green shirt, tan trousers and brown shoes sits on the lower left end with his feet on the ground. He is reading an open book held in his lap and looks down at it, not at the other rider. A girl in a pink dress sits on the raised right end, holding a small black handle, hands near her chest. Small motion lines under her dangling feet suggest she is swinging her legs or that the seesaw has just moved. At the top right, the edge of a dark green round shape (probably a tree crown) and a dark vertical line (perhaps a trunk or post) are cut off by the frame.

- The man's body and the board don't line up clearly. The board crosses at about his lower-thigh or shin height, so it looks like it passes in front of or through his legs, not under his seat. His seat looks like it hangs in the air just behind and above the board.
- The man is facing the girl but his attention is on the book. Her face is turned to the viewer, so they don't seem to interact.
- The motion lines under the girl's feet could mean kicking or swinging legs, or bouncing as her end goes up or down.
- The cut-off dark shapes in the top-right corner are hard to identify (tree, lamp post, or something else).
