# Picture 68

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence and gate at the back, a cream house with a red roof and blue door at the right, and a paved path down the left side. An old woman with white hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt stands on the path at the far left, sweeping dust and dirt towards a red dustpan. What she is holding looks wrong: instead of a broom, it is a giant silver fork, and she is pushing the pile of dirt with its prongs. A small cloud of dust rises from the pile. She is the only person in the picture. The rest of the garden is quiet: an empty swing set stands in the middle, a wheelbarrow is on the grass, and a flower bed sits in front of the house. A bird house on a tall post stands by the gate. The giant fork used as a broom is the odd or impossible thing here.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The tool could be read as a giant table fork used as a broom (the most literal reading) or as a garden fork or rake; its shape looks exactly like a dinner fork, in silver, with four prongs, and is far too large to be one, so the table-fork reading is stronger.
- The woman's left edge and shadow run right up to the edge of the picture and her body is partly over the side of the path; her shadow is unusually large and goes off the frame.
- She looks like she might be looking down at the pile, or off to the right; her glasses make it hard to tell.
- The post of the bird house has a small green tuft at its bottom and appears to stand in the grass at the very edge of the path.
- The dustpan seems to sit partly over the path edge, with its handle sticking up to the right at a slight angle over the grass.
