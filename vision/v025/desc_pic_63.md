# Picture 63

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate at the far left, a cream house with a red roof and blue door at the right, and a paved path running down the left side. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence blue with a roller on a long stick; part of the fence behind her is already blue. A paint tin and a paint tray sit on the grass by her feet. A trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the lawn to a white spotted dog, whose feet are blue, so it has clearly walked through the paint. A small girl in a pink dress and a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts are throwing a red-and-white ball between them; a dotted arc shows its path, and the ball is in the air close to the boy's outstretched hands. A bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers waters a flower bed with a teal watering can, and water streams down over the flowers. A man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans appears to be sitting in mid-air at the edge of the path, sipping a drink through a straw from a red cup, with no chair under him. A brown puppy lies asleep on the grass at the bottom centre, with a red bowl of dog food nearby. A red bird perches on top of the fence. The paint trail seems like a mess the woman has not noticed yet, because she has her back to it.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The man in the red T-shirt has no visible seat. He could be sitting on the raised edge of the path, on an invisible chair, or simply floating; it looks strange because the path is flat and his thighs are horizontal in mid-air. His shadow is on the grass under him, which suggests he is meant to be sitting on something that is not drawn. His bottom also overlaps the edge of the path.
- The ball: it could be read as thrown by the girl to the boy (the dotted line starts at her hand) or the other way around, but the dotted line stops at the ball near the boy, so girl-to-boy seems more likely.
- The first paw print appears a little way from the tray rather than right against it, but the link to the tray still reads clearly.
- The water from the can looks a bit like a beaded bundle or strings rather than loose drops, and it falls over the flowers in a clump.
- The woman's roller extends above the top of the fence by a little; the blue section of the fence also has a slightly different shape at the top of one plank.
- The puppy has no one paying it attention. It could be asleep or just lying with its eyes closed.
