# Picture 30

## Free description
This is a sunny park with a blue pond on the left, hills and trees in the background, a tall black lamp post in the centre, and a sandy path winding away on the right. In the front centre, a boy with brown hair and a red cap worn backwards, wearing a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes, stands holding a pale bag and sprinkling seeds from his fingers, and they fall in a dotted line to a red bird and a blue bird pecking on the grass at his feet. To the right, a bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers sits on a wooden bench holding up a pink ice cream cone and looking at it. A tan dog with a red neckerchief sits up on its back legs in front of the bench with its nose raised and front paws lifted, apparently begging for the ice cream. Behind them, near the lamp post and the edge of the pond, a small girl in a pink dress stands holding the string of a single yellow balloon. A flowerbed of mixed flowers lies at the front left, and there is a green rubbish bin in the bottom-left corner and another one beside the lamp post. Everyone seems calm and nothing looks impossible, though the dog may be about to get or snatch some ice cream.

## Inventory
- Boy, front centre: brown hair, backwards red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers, white shoes; standing and scattering seed for the birds.
- Red bird, front centre, on the grass: pecking at seeds at the boy's feet.
- Blue bird, front centre, on the grass, right of the red bird: pecking at seeds.
- Bearded man, centre-right, on the bench: dark hair, beard, green shirt, tan trousers, brown shoes; sitting and holding up a pink ice cream.
- Tan dog, centre-right, in front of the bench: tan with long brown ears and a red neckerchief; sitting up on hind legs with paws raised towards the man.
- Small girl, centre, back, near the lamp post: dark hair, pink dress, yellow shoes; standing holding a yellow balloon's string in both hands.
- Pale bag, in the boy's hand: holds the seed.
- Seeds, front centre: a dotted trail falling from the boy's fingers, with more around the birds.
- Pink ice cream cone, in the man's hand: what the dog seems to want.
- Yellow balloon, centre, back: held on a string by the girl; floating above her head.
- Wooden bench, centre-right: the man sits on it.
- Lamp post, centre, tall and black: unlit.
- Pond, left and centre: blue with pale highlights and bulrushes at both ends.
- Flowerbed, front left: mixed red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers in a round patch of soil.
- Green rubbish bins, one in the bottom-left corner and one by the lamp post: park bins.
- Winding path, right: sandy-coloured, runs from the bottom edge into the distance.
- Trees and bushes: a big tree on the right, a smaller tree on the left and two bushes.
- Sun (top right) and two white clouds (top centre): a fine day.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The dog: it could be begging for the man's ice cream, or just sitting up to be friendly. Its nose points up at the man and its paw is lifted, so begging seems more likely. The man does not seem to be looking at the dog.
- The boy's cap brim points backward and to the left while his face looks down to the right. I read this as a backwards cap, but at first glance the head and cap look a bit twisted.
- The pale object at the boy's hip could be a paper bag or a carton; it is partly hidden by his arm.
- The boy stands very straight with his feet together while bent at the neck, so his pose is a bit stiff.
- The girl seems to be doing nothing in particular, and I could not tell whether she is watching the others or just standing with her balloon.
- Two bins so close together in one park seems unusual, though not impossible.
