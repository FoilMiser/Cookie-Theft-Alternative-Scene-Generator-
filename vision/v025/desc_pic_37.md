# Picture 37

## Free description
This is a sunny, seemingly breezy back garden with three people and a dog. In the middle, a boy or young man in a red cap, blue hoodie and black trousers is raking a small pile of leaves on the lawn. To his right, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is walking to the left with a white, brown-spotted dog on a red lead. The dog is walking towards the pile of leaves and the person raking, so it might be about to run through the pile. On the right, a child with curly dark hair, a yellow top and orange shorts stands on the paved path looking up and holding a string to a yellow kite flying high in the sky. A washing line between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow and red striped towel, both blowing at an angle. A red bowl of dog food sits on the grass at the bottom right, and nobody is near it. A house with a blue door and a flower bed are on the left. Everything looks ordinary and busy, and no one seems to be interacting with anyone else.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The person with the rake could be a teenage boy or a young man; the size and face don't settle it.
- The red cap's peak points the opposite way to his face, which I read as a cap worn backwards. It could also look like the head was drawn the wrong way round.
- The rake's tines sit right beside and slightly over his shoe, so it looks as if he might rake his own foot. The rake head is small and could at a glance be read as a broom or fork.
- The dog could be heading for the leaf pile, or it could just be walking along. Its head is turned so that it could be looking at the leaves or at nothing in particular.
- The washing leans noticeably sideways. With the kite tail also trailing to the left this reads as wind, but the jeans look stiff, which is slightly odd.
- The bowl looks like dog food, placed away from the dog, and it isn't clear whether that matters.
- Nothing seems to pass through anything else, though the kite string crosses the top of the fence and gate area as a very thin line.
