# Picture 22

## Free description

It is a sunny day in a back garden, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right and a tall wooden fence with a gate at the far left. There are no people or animals anywhere in the picture. The one strange thing is a closed cardboard box high in the air above the fence, near the centre of the picture, tilted at an angle against the sky. Nothing is holding it up and nobody is nearby, so it looks as if it is either floating or in the middle of falling or being thrown. Everything else is still and ordinary: two potted plants by the fence, a teal watering can on the grass, a flower bed in front of the house and an empty bench. It looks as if the box might be about to come down in the garden. The box being in mid-air with no explanation is what looks wrong or impossible.
