# Picture 54

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a long wooden fence behind, and a paved path and gate on the left. A small girl with dark hair, a pink dress and yellow shoes stands on the path at the left with her arm up, flying a yellow kite. The kite is very high in the sky, up between the sun and the clouds, on a long diagonal string, with a tail of small bows. In the middle of the garden, a person with dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes reaches up to hang washing on a clothes line between two wooden posts. A white T-shirt, a yellow and red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans are already pegged on the line. An orange cat walks along the very top of the fence on the right, in front of the tree, which is a bit daring but possible. A teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow sit unused on the grass at the front, and a bed of colourful flowers grows beside the house. The two people do not seem to be interacting with each other, and nothing looks impossible.

- The washing hangs straight down even though a kite is flying well, so there seems to be enough wind for one but not the other. This is a small mismatch rather than anything really wrong.
- The cat stands on the pointed tips of the fence, which looks slightly precarious, and could be read as walking along the fence or as about to jump down. It has a tree trunk directly behind it, so it blends in with that a little.
