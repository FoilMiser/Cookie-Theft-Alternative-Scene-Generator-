# Picture 27

## Free description
This is a sunny park with no people or animals in it at all. On the left is a blue oval pond with bulrushes at each end, with a small tree and a bush beside it, and a green rubbish bin in the front-left corner. In the centre stands a tall black lamp post, with a sandy path winding away on the right past a big tree. A grey rock sits in the front centre. The strange thing is a wooden ladder standing up on the grass just left of the path, leaning at a slight angle with nothing behind it to lean on. Its top ends in empty air, against the distant trees, so it looks as though it should fall over but is standing anyway. There is nobody on it or near it, and nothing else is happening.

- Sun (top right) and two white clouds (top centre): a fine day.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The ladder is the only thing that looks wrong. It could be read as impossibly balancing with no support, or as having been left there by someone who has gone away. It is not leaning against the lamp post or the tree, since both are clearly some way off.
- I looked for a person or animal, in case one was hidden, and found none. The picture could be a plain background with one odd object added.
- The dark oval at the foot of the ladder looks like its shadow.
