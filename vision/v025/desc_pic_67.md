# Picture 67

## Free description

This is a sunny park with rolling green hills and a line of hedges in the distance, a blue pond on the left, and a winding sandy path on the right. An old man in a flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands at the edge of the pond and holds a fishing rod out over the water. The line drops to a red-and-white float in the pond, with a small ripple ring around it. He is fishing and seems calm and patient. The rest of the park is empty of people: a wooden bench beside the path is unoccupied, and there are a street lamp, two green rubbish bins, a flower bed, a grey rock, bushes and trees around. Bulrushes grow at both ends of the pond. Nothing looks clearly impossible. No fish is visible, and it does not look as if he has caught anything yet.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The man's feet are on the grass below the pond, but his body overlaps the pond behind him. He could be standing on the near bank (most likely), though it can look a bit as if the pond is cut off by him and his upper body is in front of the water.
- The rod and line form a sharp angle at the tip, with the line curving from the rod tip down to the float; the line looks slightly stiff but reads as a fishing line.
- His face is drawn from the side with an odd grey patch at the side of his head, which could be sideburns, a beard or an ear; it is not clear which.
- Whether he has had a bite is not clear. The ring round the float could mean a fish is nibbling, or just show that the float is resting on the water.
- The street lamp stands very close to the bin and looks unusually tall compared to the rest of the scene.
