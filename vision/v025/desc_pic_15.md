# Picture 15

## Free description

This is a sunny playground with a grey metal railing fence along the back, a tall slide on the left, and a large tree and a wooden bench on the right. A boy in a red cap worn backwards, a blue hoodie and dark trousers is kicking an orange ball to the right, with one leg swung out and curved motion lines by his other foot. His toe is just touching the ball, which is off the ground, so the ball is about to fly away to the right. At the front left, a girl in a pink dress kneels at a wooden sandpit, building a sandcastle with a little red flag on top. The two children are doing separate things and neither looks at the other. The slide, bench and bin have nobody near them. A grey rock lies on the grass near the sandpit. Nothing looks wrong or impossible, and the ball is heading away from the sandcastle, so it does not look as if the castle is in danger.
