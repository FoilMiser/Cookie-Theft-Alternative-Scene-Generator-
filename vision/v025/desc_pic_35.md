# Picture 35

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with three people, each doing something different. On the left, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is holding out a dark frying pan by its handle and pouring water from it onto a flower bed, which is an odd thing to water flowers with. In the middle, a small girl in a pink dress stands with one arm raised, holding a string. The string sags in a long curve up to a purple kite that is caught in the leaves of the tree behind the house, so the kite seems stuck and the girl seems to be trying to pull it free. On the right, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, is painting the wooden fence blue with a roller on a stick. About three boards of the fence are already blue and a paint tin stands near her feet. An empty wheelbarrow stands on the lawn at the front, and an empty bench is at the bottom left. Nobody is paying attention to anyone else.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The frying pan: it looks like a frying pan being used as a watering can, which seems wrong on purpose or absurd. It could also be read as a shallow saucepan, or as her tipping out dirty water.
- The kite: I read it as stuck in the tree, because leaf clumps overlap it and the string is slack. It could also be read as a kite flying just in front of the tree, though the sag in the string argues against that.
- The lighter leaf clumps are drawn only around the kite, so the tree looks patchy there.
- The girl could be tugging to free the kite or simply holding on; I can't see any pulling movement.
- The roller's position: the roller head sits at the top of the boards and sticks up slightly past the painted part, close to the fence top.
- Figure proportions: both adult women have very long thin legs and the dress and skirt look like separate balloon-shaped pieces joined at a narrow waist, which looks slightly odd.
- Nothing is passing through anything else apart from the kite and leaves overlap, and the water stream going in among the flowers.
