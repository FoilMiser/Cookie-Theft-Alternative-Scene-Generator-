# Picture 44

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back with a gate at the left end, and a paved path at the front left. In the middle, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is holding up the nozzle of a garden hose and peering into it with only a single drop of water coming out. The reason is visible: a white goat with small horns is standing on the hose, and the hose is bulging fat on the reel side of the goat and thin on the man's side, so the water is blocked and might burst out in his face if the goat moves. At the front left, on the path, an old man in a cap, brown waistcoat and white shirt is using a giant silver dinner fork as if it were a broom, pushing a pile of dirt towards a red dustpan with a little cloud of dust rising, which is an impossible or silly tool for the job. At the back, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is painting the fence blue with a roller on a stick, with a paint tin on the grass beside him; part of the fence has already been painted. A birdhouse stands on a tall pole in front of the painted section. A grey wheelbarrow sits empty on the grass at the front, there is a hose reel by a round flower bed, and an empty wooden bench is at the front right.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The goat may be there by accident or may have wandered in; nothing shows where it came from, and it is not on a rope.
- The man might be looking into the nozzle because it stopped working, or he might just be checking it. The single drop and the bulge in the hose both suggest it is blocked.
- The fat bulge in the hose could read as water backed up behind the goat's foot, or simply as a thick section of hose.
- The fork looks like an oversized dinner fork, which could be meant as a joke tool, or could be read at a glance as a garden fork or a rake.
- The dust cloud could come from the sweeping, or could be smoke, though it looks like dust.
- The old man's shadow runs off the left edge of the picture, and he is very close to that edge.
- The boy's painting seems to stop at an uneven edge, and the top of the painted section looks slightly different from the unpainted boards, so the boards there look a little odd.
- The birdhouse pole overlaps the painted area and looks like it is standing in front of the fence, not attached to it.
