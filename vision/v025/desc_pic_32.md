# Picture 32

## Free description
This is a sunny town street with a row of colourful shops with striped awnings at the back and a paved area in front. A blue-and-white ball is stuck up in the leafy tree on the right, resting on a branch, and a small girl in a pink dress stands below the tree on the right pointing up at it. In the centre back, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers stands with one arm raised high, her hand waving, with little curved marks around it to show the movement, and she is looking to the left. In front of her and to the left, a child with dark curly hair, in a yellow top and orange shorts, holds a yellow balloon on a string. In the front centre, a tan dog with a red neckerchief sits on the ground holding open a newspaper as if reading it, which no real dog would do. A red bicycle is parked in a metal rack next to the dog, and a small white café table with two black chairs stands empty on the right. A blue bird is perched on top of the tall black lamp post, and a market stall with crates of fruit stands on the left with a cardboard box on the ground beside it. Nobody seems to be trying to get the ball down yet, though the girl seems to want it back.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The woman's wave could be read two ways: she might be waving hello to someone (perhaps the child, or someone out of view), or she might be trying to get help or attention. She is looking left, away from the tree, so I do not think she has noticed the ball.
- The ball in the tree could belong to the girl, and pointing could mean "my ball is stuck" or simply "look at that". There is nothing to show who kicked or threw it.
- The dog's face is angled down and partly hidden. I read it as reading the newspaper, but a dog doing this looks like a cartoon oddity. There is a dark square on the newspaper by its paw, which could be a photo or something it is holding.
- The child with the balloon looks towards the woman or the viewer, and I could not tell whether the two are connected.
- The bicycle has a small thing hanging below the pedals, which looks like a kickstand or a lock, and the wheels look a bit heavy for the frame. I could not tell exactly what it is.
- The bird sits right in front of a window of the green building, so it is partly confusing against the frame until you see it is standing on the lamp.
- The market stall's poles seem to run down behind or through the row of crates.
