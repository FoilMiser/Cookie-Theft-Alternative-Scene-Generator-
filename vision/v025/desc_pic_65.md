# Picture 65

## Free description

This is a sunny playground in a park, seen with the sun at the upper right and a dark railing fence with hedges along the back. A single boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is sliding down a slide on the right, with both arms raised in the air, as if enjoying the ride. He is sitting on the orange-and-yellow chute about halfway down, with his feet pointing down the slope. The rest of the playground is empty: the swing set in the middle has an unused red seat, the wooden bench on the left is empty, and a green rubbish bin stands near the fence. A big tree stands at the far left, with a bush beside it and another bush at the far right. A grey rock lies on the grass at the lower right. The foreground is a large area of open grass with nothing on it. Nothing looks wrong or impossible; it is a simple picture of one child on a slide.

- His raised arms could be read as cheering in fun or as losing balance; his face looks calm, so fun seems more likely.
- The slide's platform and ladder sit behind him on the right, and the top of the chute meets the frame cleanly, but the ladder overlaps the frame a little, which looks a bit tangled.
