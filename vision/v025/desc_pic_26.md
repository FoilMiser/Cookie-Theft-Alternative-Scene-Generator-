# Picture 26

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills and a line of trees in the distance, a blue pond on the right with bulrushes at each end, and a winding sandy path on the left. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt and carrying a yellow shopping bag, is walking leftwards across the grass, right next to a tall black lamp post. In the front right, an older man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket, leaning back with his knees up and his eyes closed, looking content or drowsy. A wicker picnic basket sits on the blanket in front of him. Between the path and the blanket stands a wheelbarrow with a grey tray and wooden handles, and nobody is using it. The wrong thing is the wheelbarrow's wheel, which is a square instead of a circle, so it could not roll. The man and the woman are not paying any attention to each other, and nothing seems about to happen.
