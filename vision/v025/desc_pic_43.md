# Picture 43

## Free description

This is a sunny playground or park with a dark metal railing across the back, a red-framed slide with a yellow and orange chute on the left, a green bin in the middle, and a bench and a tree on the right. In the foreground, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is walking to the right with his eyes on an open book he is holding, and a red-and-white ball lies on the grass right in front of his feet, so he looks about to trip over it. To the left of him, a little girl in a pink dress holds an empty ice cream cone in one hand and rubs her eye with the other as if she is crying, because her ice cream has fallen on the ground and made a pink melted puddle. A brown dog with a red collar is bending right down over that puddle, about to lick the ice cream up. At the far left, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts holds strings for a purple balloon and a red balloon and reaches up with his other arm towards a blue balloon that has got loose and is floating away. On the bench on the right, a man with a beard, a green shirt and tan trousers sits drinking from a red cup through a straw with his eyes shut. A red bird perches on top of the railing, and a blue bird flies through the sky on the right. A grey rock sits on the grass at the front left. Nothing looks impossible, but several small mishaps are happening at once.
