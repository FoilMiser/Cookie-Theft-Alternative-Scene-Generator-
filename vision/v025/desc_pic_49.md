# Picture 49

## Free description

This is a back garden on a sunny day, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, and a long wooden fence with a gate across the back. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers sits on a wooden bench at the front left, drinking from a red cup through a yellow straw. On the far right, a man with a beard, a green shirt and khaki trousers stands on a paved path, raking a small pile of leaves with a long rake. An orange cat lies on the grass at the front centre with its eyes closed, apparently asleep. A teal watering can stands on the grass near the man with nobody using it. A bed of colourful flowers grows left of centre, and two potted plants stand near the fence. The boy and the man are each busy with their own thing and do not seem to be interacting. Nothing looks wrong or impossible; it seems a quiet, ordinary moment.
