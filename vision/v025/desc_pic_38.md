# Picture 38

## Free description
This is a sunny, seemingly breezy back garden with three people, with the house on the right this time. On the far left, a small girl in a pink dress stands near the gate at the top of a paved path, holding up a string to an orange kite flying high in the sky. Next to her is an empty swing set with a red seat, hanging still. In the middle, a boy or young man in a red cap, blue hoodie and black trousers is bending over and digging with a spade; there is a dark hole in the lawn and a mound of soil beside it. A washing line behind him holds a yellow and red striped towel and blue jeans, both blowing to the right. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is holding a teal watering can near a flower bed. The water from the can looks wrong: it curves upward from the spout, rising in a thick arc with drops at the very top above the flowers, rather than falling on them. An empty wheelbarrow stands on the lawn at the front. Nobody is interacting with anyone else.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water going upward is the strangest thing. Two readings: water impossibly flowing up out of the can, against gravity; or a jet being squirted up in the air so it rains down on the flowers. The thick pale-edged arc also looks more like a solid hoop or band than water at first glance.
- The arc starts down at the can's spout and sweeps up over the flowers, so it is not clear whether any water is reaching them.
- The can's position: it looks low and close to her body and not obviously tipped, which doesn't match the amount of water coming out.
- The person digging could be a teenage boy or a young man.
- The red cap's peak points the opposite way to his face, which I read as worn backwards, though it could look like a drawing error.
- Soil on the spade and the dark shape at the hole overlap, so the spade head is hard to separate from the soil. It could be read as a brown lump or animal at first glance.
- The washing leans sideways as if in wind while the swing seat hangs perfectly still, which is slightly inconsistent, though the kite tail agrees with the washing.
