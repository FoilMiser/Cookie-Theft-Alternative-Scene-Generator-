# Picture 20

## Free description

It is a sunny day in the back garden of a cream house with a red roof and a blue door, with a tall wooden fence and a gate at the right. An older woman with a grey bun and glasses, in a teal top and blue-grey skirt, stands next to a flower bed and is watering the flowers. The odd thing is that she is pouring the water from a dark frying pan held out by its handle rather than from a watering can, which looks wrong. On the right, a smiling child in a yellow top and orange shorts sits on a red swing seat on a tall dark metal swing frame, with small motion lines at the left showing the swing is moving. A black cat with white paws is walking along the very top of the fence, facing right. A grey wheelbarrow with a wooden handle stands empty on the grass at the bottom. Nobody is interacting with anyone else; each is busy with their own thing. Apart from the frying pan, everything looks ordinary.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The frying pan could be read two ways: a deliberately silly choice of tool, or a large dark watering scoop or ladle. It looks most like a frying pan.
- The water seems to come from the rim of the pan and passes in front of some flowers. It looks plausible, though the pan seems small for that much water.
- The swing's motion lines are small and faint, and the child hangs almost straight down between the frame legs. The child could be swinging gently or just sitting, but the lines suggest movement.
- The ropes run behind the child's head and come out lower down at the seat, which looks slightly unusual but not wrong.
- The cat is long-legged and dark, so it could briefly be read as a small dog, but the pointed ears and its position on the fence make it read as a cat.
- Nothing is floating.
