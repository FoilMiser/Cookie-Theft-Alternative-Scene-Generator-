# Picture 47

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path at the front right. Near the middle, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands with his back partly to us, reaching up with both hands to peg a white T-shirt onto a washing line, where a pair of blue jeans is already hanging. A garden sprinkler at the front is fed by a green hose coming in from the left edge, and it is spraying three arcs of water across the grass. The longest arc lands on a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is getting soaked, with water running down its sides and a puddle around its base. Nobody seems to have noticed that the box is getting wet. Strangest of all, a second cardboard box is hanging in mid-air in the sky on the right, tilted, with nothing holding it up, above an empty swing set. It could be falling, but there is no sign of who or what put it there, and it looks impossible. There is an empty swing with a red seat in a grey metal frame on the right, a birdhouse on a tall pole near the gate, and a round flower bed near the house. There are no animals and only this one person in the picture.
