# Picture 17

## Free description

It is a sunny day in a green park, with a round blue pond on the right and low hills and treetops in the distance. An older man in a blue-grey cap, white shirt, brown vest and dark trousers stands at the near edge of the pond, fishing with a long rod. His line drops down to a red-and-white float on the water, with a small ripple ring around it. A white duck sits on the water a little to the right of the float, facing right, away from it. On the left, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands on a sandy winding path, holding a camera up to her eye. The camera points right, towards the fisherman and the pond, so she seems to be about to take a picture. Between them are an empty wooden bench, a tall black lamp post and a green rubbish bin. Nothing has been caught yet, and the two people are not interacting except that she may be photographing him. Nothing in the picture looks impossible.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The camera's target could be read two ways: she may be photographing the fisherman, or the duck and the pond beyond him. Her line of aim goes roughly through both.
- The float is fairly near the duck. It could just be where the line landed, or it could suggest the duck might get caught, but nothing clearly shows that.
- The man's head is a little hard to read: grey hair at the back, and a small round mark in the middle of the side of his face that I take to be an ear.
- The man's legs look quite stiff and his feet are close together. It looks slightly awkward, but he reads as standing.
- Nothing is floating or passing through anything. The man overlaps the pond's edge only because he stands in front of it.
