# Picture 50

## Free description

This is a playground on a sunny day, with a dark metal railing and hedge behind, a tree on the left and a red slide on the right. A small girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes sits on the raised left end of a red seesaw, with her feet dangling and small curved marks under them. A bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers sits on the lowered right end of the seesaw, reading an open book and not looking at the girl. Because he is so much heavier, the seesaw is tipped down on his side and the girl is left up in the air. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers runs across the middle of the picture towards the left, with speed lines behind him. At the front right, a child with curly dark hair, a yellow top and orange shorts kneels in a wooden sandpit, working on a sandcastle. The slide is empty. It looks a little odd for a grown man to sit reading on a child's seesaw while the girl is stuck up high, and she seems unable to come down unless he moves.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The curved marks under the girl's feet could mean her legs are swinging, or that the seesaw has just moved or bumped. Either way she could be read as stuck in the air or as in the middle of a seesaw ride.
- The man could be deliberately playing on the seesaw, or he could have just sat down to read and not realised he is holding the girl up. He does not look at her, so I lean towards the second reading.
- It is unclear where the running boy is heading. He could be running from the slide, running towards the seesaw, or just running for fun. Nobody seems to be chasing him.
- The red bit behind the child in the sandpit is too small to identify. It could be a spade or bucket, or part of something else.
- The sandcastle looks very square, more like cardboard boxes or wooden blocks than sand.
- The man's seat on the seesaw is slightly hard to make out, because his bent legs cover the plank and he looks a little as if he is perched in front of it rather than on it.
- The boy's cap peak points the opposite way from his running direction. This could be a backwards cap, or a slightly confusing drawing.
