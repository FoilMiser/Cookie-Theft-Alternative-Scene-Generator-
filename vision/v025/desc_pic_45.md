# Picture 45

## Free description

This is a sunny but very windy back garden, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path at the front right. On the right of centre, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands with both arms thrown up and his mouth open, looking surprised, as an orange cap flies off through the air above his hands along with a few leaves. White wavy wind lines stream across the fence next to the cap, showing a strong wind blowing from right to left. In the middle, a pair of blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel on a washing line are blown out sideways to the left. More wind lines swirl above the clothes line and in the sky above the house. A black and white cat walks calmly along the top of the fence on the right with its tail up. By the blue door, a brown dog with a red collar sits holding a red lead in its mouth with its eyes shut. A dark teal hose reel sits next to a round flower bed. Nothing looks impossible; the man seems to have lost his cap to the wind and is trying to grab it.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The orange cap may be the man's own, blown off his head, or may just be a cap flying past; he has no hat on and is reaching for it, so it is probably his.
- The man's raised arms could mean trying to catch the cap, or just surprise; his hands are a little below it and not touching.
- The striped item could be a towel, a blanket or a shirt.
- The cat is unbothered by the wind even though it is on top of the fence, which looks slightly odd but is not impossible.
- The dog is also calm in the wind, and its shut eyes could mean happy or asleep.
- The hose reel slightly overlaps the flower bed and is not connected to anything visible, so it seems to just be parked there.
- The man's head looks slightly large for his body and his face is turned in a way that is a bit hard to read, though his expression clearly looks startled.
