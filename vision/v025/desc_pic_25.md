# Picture 25

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate in the fence on the right, and a paved path leading to that gate. At the bottom left, a child with dark curly or braided hair, wearing a yellow top and orange shorts, sits on a wooden bench eating a pink ice cream cone, apparently with eyes closed in enjoyment. Right beside the bench, a white dog with brown patches and a blue neckerchief is sitting up on its back legs with its head raised and a front paw stretched toward the child, as if begging for the ice cream. On the right of the lawn, a small orange-and-white puppy lies curled up asleep on the grass near the path. A round flowerbed full of red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers sits near the house. The odd thing is a closed cardboard box up in the sky above the fence, tilted at an angle, with nothing holding it up and nobody near it. It looks either as if it is floating in the air or as if it is falling and has been caught mid-drop; nobody in the picture is looking at it.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The box could be read two ways: floating impossibly in the air, or falling from above. There is no motion line, no person throwing it and no plane or balcony it could have come from, so I cannot tell which is meant. It looks impossible either way.
- The dog could be begging for the ice cream, or just sitting up and looking up at the child. Its head points up and its paw reaches toward the child, so I lean to begging.
- The child's eyes look closed or nearly closed, so the child could be savouring the ice cream and not noticing the dog, or could be looking down at the cone.
- The puppy is small and tucked up, so it was not obvious at first glance that it is a puppy rather than a cat; the floppy ears and muzzle suggest a puppy. The dark shadow patch under it is much wider than the animal.
- The white dog's pose is slightly awkward, with the body narrow and upright and the legs at odd angles, and it took a moment to read.
