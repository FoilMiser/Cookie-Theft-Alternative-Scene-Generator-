# Picture 41

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate at the right end, and a paved path running down the right side. In the middle, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers with a long dark ponytail stands with both arms stretched out in front of her, towards a red-and-white ball that is in the air just above her hands. A dashed curved line runs from the outstretched hand of a small boy in a yellow top and orange shorts up to the ball, so it looks like he has just thrown it and she is about to catch it. In the front centre, a white dog with brown spots crouches with its chest low and its tail up and wagging, right next to a yellow-green tennis ball, as if it wants to play. By the blue door, a brown dog with a red collar sits holding a red lead in its mouth with its eyes shut, which looks like it is asking to go for a walk. On the right, an old woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt is sweeping the path with a broom. A teal watering can sits on the grass near her, two potted plants stand near the fence, and there is a round bed of colourful flowers near the house. Nothing looks impossible; it reads as an ordinary, busy garden scene.
