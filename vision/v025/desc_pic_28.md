# Picture 28

## Free description
This is a busy sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof on the right, a wooden fence across the back and a paved path leading to a gate on the left. An elderly woman with a grey bun, in a teal top and grey skirt, is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller, and a few boards are already done; a paint can and a tray of blue paint sit on the grass behind her. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is walking away from the paint tray towards the path, and its feet are blue, leaving a trail of blue paw prints across the lawn. A small girl in a pink dress holds a yellow and a purple balloon in one hand, while a third, red balloon has got away with its string dangling, and she reaches up after it. A black-and-white cat is creeping along the top of the fence towards a red bird perched on the fence just ahead of it, so it looks as if the cat might pounce. A bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers sits on a bench at the bottom right reading a red book, not noticing any of this. In the front centre, a tan dog with a red neckerchief sits up on the grass holding open a newspaper as if reading it, which no real dog would do, and a red bowl of dog food sits untouched nearby. An orange cat is curled up asleep at the edge of the path. A blue bird flies overhead, and strangest of all, an orange goldfish is sitting in the leaves of the tree, where a fish could not be.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The cat and the red bird: the cat could be stalking the bird, with a pounce about to happen, or simply walking along the fence. The low crouching body suggests stalking.
- The red balloon: its string ends in mid-air and does not reach the girl's hand, so I read it as having escaped. The girl's upward reach could mean she is trying to grab it, or just pointing at it.
- The tan dog's face is angled away and down, so its features are partly hidden; I read it as looking at the newspaper. There is a dark square on the newspaper by its paw, which could be a photo on the page or a hand-sized clip.
- The goldfish in the tree could be a real fish in an impossible place, or a toy or ornament hung in the tree. It looks like a plain live-looking fish, so I read it as impossible.
- The tan dog holding a paper is plainly human-like behaviour. It could be a joke or the "wrong" thing in the picture, or just cartoon whimsy.
- The man's body and legs look slightly long and stiff on the bench, with his feet well below the seat, so he seems a bit oversized for it.
- The orange cat lies right across the line between path and grass, so it is hard to say which one it is on.
