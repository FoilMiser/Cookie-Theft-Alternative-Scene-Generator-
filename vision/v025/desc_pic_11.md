# Picture 11

## Free description

This is a sunny park with green hills, a winding sandy path on the left and a pond on the right. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands at the front right holding the strings of a blue and a purple balloon in one hand, while a yellow balloon floats just out of reach above his other raised hand. He is looking up at it and frowning, so it looks as if the yellow balloon has just slipped away. Further back, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers carrying a yellow shopping bag is walking to the right, looking straight ahead, and a tall black lamp post is directly in front of her, so she looks about to walk into it. A girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. At the front left, a tan dog is sitting upright like a person beside the path, holding up an open newspaper in its white paws as if reading it, which is impossible and funny. A white duck swims on the pond with bulrushes at its edges. There is also a flower bed and a green litter bin at the bottom right.

- The woman and the lamp post: she could be about to bump into it, or she could be walking past just in front of it. Her front foot is almost at the post's base and her head is level with it, and she is not looking at it, so bumping looks likely. It is not certain.
- The dog is hard to read. Its head is a rounded brown shape with a dark ear and its face is turned away, so I cannot see a clear muzzle or eyes. At a glance the figure could be taken for a monkey, an otter, or a person in a costume. The newspaper is the clearest part.
- The red thing at the dog's neck could be a collar or a bandana. It looks a bit like a separate red object.
- The boy's raised hand could be read as trying to catch the balloon or as waving or pointing at it. It has a small gap from the string end, so it has already got away.
- The boy's expression could be upset or just concentrating.
- The balloons overlap the pond in the picture, but they are in front of it rather than in it.
