# Picture 39

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with three people, with the house on the left. At the front left, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands by a flower bed holding a teal watering can. The water from the can looks wrong: it curves upward from the spout in a thick arc, with drops at the very top above the flowers, instead of falling down onto them. At the front centre, a child with curly dark hair, a yellow top and orange shorts is kicking an orange and white ball with motion lines around the kicking leg, and the ball is heading to the right, towards an empty swing set. Further back, a boy or young man in a red cap, blue hoodie and black trousers is walking to the left carrying a cardboard box with a white label. A washing line behind them holds blue jeans and a yellow and red striped towel, hanging straight down. At the far right are a bird box on a tall pole, a gate and a paved path. Nobody seems to be interacting with anyone else.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water going upward is the strangest thing. Two readings: water impossibly flowing up out of the can, against gravity; or a jet squirted up to rain down on the flowers. The thick pale-edged arc also looks a bit like a solid curved band or hose rather than water.
- She is looking left rather than at the water, and the can doesn't look tipped, so it isn't clear she is deliberately pouring.
- The ball might go on towards the swing set, or it may simply be at the moment of the kick. It looks slightly off the ground, which seems normal for a kick.
- The person carrying the box could be a teenage boy or a young man.
- His red cap's peak points right while he walks and faces left. I read it as worn backwards, though it could look like the head is on the wrong way round.
- It isn't clear where he is taking the box. He could be heading for the house or just crossing the garden.
- Nothing seems to pass through anything else, though he is drawn in front of the fence close to the right washing post.
