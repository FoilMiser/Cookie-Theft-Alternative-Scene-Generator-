# Picture 12

## Free description

This is a sunny park with a pond on the left, a winding sandy path on the right, a lamp post in the middle and an empty wooden bench. At the front left, an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, kneels at a round flower bed, reaching down with something thin and green in her hand, with a pot of pink flowers next to her. It looks as if she is planting or tending the flowers. At the right, a young person in a red shirt and blue jeans stands by the path holding a white packet or container and scattering seed or crumbs, which fall in a trail of little dots to the ground. A red bird and a blue bird are pecking at the seeds on the path near his feet. In the middle at the front, a white goat with a small dark horn has its head down, eating a clump of grass. It is a calm scene and nothing looks wrong or about to go wrong. The pond is empty, with bulrushes at each end, and there is nobody on the bench.
