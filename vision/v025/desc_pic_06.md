# Picture 06

## Free description
This is a sunny park with rolling green hills and a line of bushes in the background, a pond on the left and a winding pale path on the right. An older man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat, white sleeves, dark trousers and brown shoes stands in the middle, right beside the pond, eating a pink ice cream with his eyes shut and a happy look on his face. He is alone. A white duck is swimming on the pond to his left, and reeds stick up at each end of the water. A tall black street lamp rises right behind him. To the right of the lamp a wooden ladder stands on the grass tilted at an angle, leaning on nothing at all. That looks impossible, since it should fall over. Nothing else is going on. There is a flowerbed and a green bin at the bottom left, a grey rock at the bottom centre, and trees and bushes on both sides.

## Inventory
- Older man, centre, middle distance, beside the pond: flat grey-blue cap, brown waistcoat, white sleeves, dark trousers, brown shoes; standing still, holding a pink ice cream cone up to his mouth, eyes shut and smiling.
- Duck, left of centre, on the pond: white with an orange beak; swimming, facing right.
- Ladder, centre-right, back: wooden ladder standing on the grass at a slant with nothing supporting it; nobody is on it.
- Street lamp, centre, directly behind the man: tall black post with a white lamp at the top.
- Pond, left: large oval of blue water with reeds at both ends.
- Path, right: pale winding path going from the bottom edge back towards the hills.
- Flowerbed, bottom left: round bed of dark soil with red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers.
- Green bin, bottom left corner: rubbish bin; not in use.
- Grey rock, bottom centre: sitting on the grass.
- Trees, far left and right: one small, one large, round and green; scenery.
- Bushes, left beside the pond and far right: green scenery.
- Sun (top right) and two white clouds (top centre): sky decoration.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The ladder is the odd thing: either it is standing up by magic, leaning on nothing (impossible), or it is supposed to be propped against something that is not shown. It is close to the lamp post but clearly not touching it, and it leans the other way from the post.
- The ladder could be read as a mistake in the drawing, or as a deliberate silly thing to spot. I cannot tell which.
- The man stands so close to the lamp post that the post looks almost as if it grows out of his shoulder or runs behind his back.
- His pose overlaps the right end of the pond, but he seems to be standing on the grass, not in the water.
- The duck does not seem to be connected to the man in any way; he could be watching it, but his eyes are shut.
