# Picture 57

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back, a gate at the far left and a paved path running from the gate to the bottom left. There is only one person in the picture: a man with short dark hair and a beard, wearing a green T-shirt, tan trousers and brown shoes, in the middle of the lawn. He is reading an open red book held in front of him, and his body is in a sitting position, with his knees bent and his thighs out in front. But there is nothing under him: no chair, stool or bench, so he appears to be sitting on thin air. This is the thing that looks impossible. A real wooden bench does stand empty on the right, in front of the house, some distance away from him. He does not seem to notice anything odd and looks calmly absorbed in the book. Around him are a flower bed, a hose reel, a watering can on the grass, two potted plants, some bushes and a tall pole with a small birdhouse on top. Nothing else is happening.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The man's pose can be read two ways: he is sitting on an invisible or missing chair, or he is floating or levitating in a sitting position. It could also be meant as him about to sit or fall backwards, but he looks too relaxed for that.
- This could be an intended absurdity or an unintended error where a chair has been left out. Either way, it looks strange.
- His shadow falls on the grass slightly to the right of him rather than directly beneath, which adds to the impression that he is not supported by anything.
- His feet look almost flat on the grass but I cannot tell if they are carrying any of his weight.
