# Picture 24

## Free description

It is a dull day in a back garden, with a grey sky, three dark storm clouds and no sun. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the right, and a tall wooden fence with a gate is at the left. On the path at the far left, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers looks up with her mouth open and points at the dark clouds, as if warning that rain is coming. In the middle, a person with brown hair, a red shirt and blue jeans stands by the flower bed and waters the flowers with a hose, seemingly ignoring the sky, which is a bit pointless if it is about to rain. The hose runs along the ground to a teal hose reel next to the bed. An empty swing with a red seat hangs still in a dark metal frame on the left. There is also a grey wheelbarrow at the centre, and its wheel is a square rather than a circle, which looks wrong and could not roll. It feels as if a storm is about to start.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The wheelbarrow's square wheel looks impossible. It could be a deliberate oddity or a drawing error. At first glance it looks like a small crate or box stuck to the front of the barrow.
- Her pointing could be read two ways: she is warning the person with the hose about the storm, or she is simply noticing the clouds herself. The person with the hose is not looking at her. She could also be pointing at something else in the sky, but only the clouds are visible.
- The hose person could be a man or an older boy. I cannot tell for sure.
- The hose is hard to follow near the person's hand. It reaches up towards his waist and then seems to go behind him, and I cannot see it join the nozzle clearly.
- The hose lies across the front of the flower bed, and the water passes in front of some flowers, so the bed looks a little tangled.
- Watering flowers just before a storm could be read as pointless or funny, or just as someone who has not noticed the weather.
- It is not raining yet, so I read the clouds as something about to happen. No drops fall from them.
- Nothing is floating.
