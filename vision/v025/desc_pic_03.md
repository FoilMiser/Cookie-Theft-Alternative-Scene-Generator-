# Picture 03

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a wooden gate on the left, a path running down from the gate, and the corner of a cream house with a blue door on the right. In the centre back a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands at a washing line with her arms up, pegging a white T-shirt onto it. A pair of blue jeans is already hanging on the line beside her. At the front right a man with a beard, in a green shirt and tan trousers, kneels on one knee by a round flowerbed full of colourful flowers, and he is reaching down to a small pot with a pink flower in it, as though planting or moving it. At the bottom centre a sprinkler on a dark hose is spraying three arcs of water to the left. Two of the arcs land on the grass, but the longest one comes down right on top of a cardboard box sitting on the lawn. The box already has water running down its sides and a puddle around its base, so it and whatever is inside are getting soaked, and neither person has noticed. There is also a birdhouse on a tall pole and two potted plants near the gate.
