# Picture 58

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, a gate on the right, a paved path, a washing line and an empty swing set. In the centre-right, an older man in a grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers holds a hose nozzle up close to his face and peers into it, as if wondering why no water is coming out; just one drop hangs at the tip. The reason seems to be at the bottom of the picture: a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is standing on the hose, and the hose just behind the dog is swollen into a big bulge, as if the water is blocked and building up. It looks as if the man is about to be squirted in the face if the dog moves. At the front left, a woman with short dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes sits on a wooden bench drinking from a red cup with a yellow straw. On the far right, a bearded man in a green T-shirt and tan trousers is also drinking from a red cup with a straw, but he is in a sitting position over the path with nothing under him, so he appears to be sitting on thin air. That looks impossible. Blue jeans and a yellow striped garment hang from the washing line at the back, and the red-seated swing hangs still with nobody on it.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The bearded man's pose can be read as sitting on an invisible or missing chair or as floating. Nothing in the picture supports him. His legs and feet also stick out past the edge of the path onto the grass.
- The hose bulge could be read as water trapped behind the dog's foot, or just as a fat section or a separate object lying on the hose, such as a nozzle or a large slug-like shape. I favour the trapped water reading.
- The dog may or may not know it is standing on the hose; it looks unaware. Its back foot is the one clearly on the hose.
- The older man's outstretched arm looks oddly flat and ribbon-like, so it is a little hard to see how his arm joins his shoulder and hand.
- The grey patch at the side of the man's head could be grey hair or a sideburn, or something covering his ear.
- The yellow garment on the line could be a shirt or a towel; it is just a rectangle with red stripes.
- The two people drinking the same drink could be together or could just be a coincidence; they are at opposite sides of the picture and are not looking at each other.
