# Picture 40

## Free description
This is a sunny town square or shopping street with a row of colourful shops at the back and paving stones in front. At the front, a small girl in a pink dress is holding an empty ice cream cone in one hand and has her other hand up to her eye, as if she is crying. On the ground in front of her is a pink splat with a scoop on it, and a tan dog with a red bandana has its head down right at it, tongue out, about to eat it or already licking it. So it looks like she has dropped her ice cream and the dog is getting it. In the middle, a delivery person in an orange cap, orange hi-vis vest and blue trousers is walking to the left carrying a cardboard box with a white label. On the left, a man in a red t-shirt and blue jeans stands near a fruit stall with one hand raised high, waving, with little marks either side of the hand to show movement. The fruit stall has crates of green, yellow, orange and red fruit, but nobody is behind it. There is also an empty bench, a teal bicycle parked at a metal rack, a tree in a round planter and an empty café table with two chairs.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The pink object on the ground: I read it as a dropped, melting ice cream because of the empty cone. But the flat rim with a rounded dome on top could also be read as a pink sun hat lying on the ground.
- The dog could be eating or licking the ice cream, or just sniffing it; the tongue is small.
- The girl's hand at her face could be crying or wiping her eye, or she could be eating or touching her mouth. Her face is small and hard to read.
- The waving man: he could be waving at the delivery person, hailing someone out of the picture, or trying to get attention at the stall. He isn't clearly looking at anyone in particular.
- He could also be the stallholder standing beside his stall, or just a passer-by.
- The delivery person's vest: the silver reflective bands stick out jagged past the sides of the body, which looks a little odd.
- His direction: he could be heading for the stall, the waving man, or a shop.
- The bicycle and rack overlap closely, with a stand or lock near the wheels, so it isn't clear if the bike is locked or just leaning there.
- The tree in front of the cream shop hides most of its shopfront. It is drawn much bigger than the bench and cuts across the awning, which is normal overlap and not a mistake.
