## Free description
This is a sunny park with one person in it. A woman with long dark hair, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, is sitting on the ground at a red and white checked picnic blanket, leaning back on one arm with her knees bent. She seems to have her eyes closed or squinting in a smile, and she looks relaxed and happy. A closed brown picnic basket with a handle sits on the blanket in front of her. Behind her is a blue pond with reeds at both ends, and beyond that are rolling green hills and a line of trees. To the left are a winding sand-coloured path, an empty wooden bench, a tall black lamp post and a green litter bin. In front of her to the right is a round bed of colourful flowers, and there is a grey rock on the grass and a second bin in the bottom right corner. Nothing seems about to happen; it is a quiet picnic with no food out yet and nobody else around.
