# Picture 10

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left, a wooden fence and gate at the back, a swing set on the right and a paved path at far right. A boy in a red shirt and blue jeans stands in the middle holding the end of a garden hose up close to his face and peering into the nozzle, with just a drop of water coming out. The hose runs from a reel on the left across the lawn to him, and a brown dog with a red collar is standing on it. Just before the dog's paw the hose swells into a big fat bulge, as if the water is being held back, while the part after the dog is thin. It looks as though the dog has blocked the water, the boy is looking to see why nothing is coming out, and when the dog moves he will get sprayed in the face. At the back, an old man in a brown waistcoat stands with his hands on his hips and a white or metal bucket upside down on his head, which is strange. Near him a white goat with its head down is eating a tuft of grass. The swing and the bench are empty, and the flower bed is untouched.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The animal at the back reads to me as a goat, but its head is small and white and the horn is tiny, so it could also be read as a white lamb or a dog.
- The bucket on the old man's head could be a deliberate silly hat or a mistake he is unaware of. He does not look bothered.
- It is not fully clear whether the old man is watching the goat or just standing there. He faces its direction.
- The boy's face is close behind his hands, so it could be read as him drinking from the hose rather than looking into it. The very thin flow and the bulge make "looking to see why it stopped" the stronger reading.
- The bulge looks quite extreme and dark, so someone might read it as an object lying on the hose, such as a slug or a flat stone, rather than swollen hose.
- The dog's paw and the hose overlap at the ground, and it is a little hard to tell which paw is actually pressing on it.
