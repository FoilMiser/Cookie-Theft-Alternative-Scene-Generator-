# Picture 02

## Free description
This is a sunny town square paved in grey slabs, with a row of colourful shops behind it. On the left a blue ball with a white stripe is stuck on a branch high up in a tree. A small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands near the trunk, frowning and pointing up at the ball. Next to him a man in a blue hooded top, dark trousers and a red cap has both arms up in the air and is off the ground in a jump, with his shadow on the paving below him and a big smile on his face. In the middle a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans sits on a wooden bench eating a pink ice cream, and a white dog with brown patches and a blue neckerchief sits up on its back legs in front of him, looking up as if begging for it. At the front right an older man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat walks to the left while reading an open book, and a cardboard box sits on the ground ahead of him, so he looks likely to walk into or trip over it. Behind him a bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers walks to the right holding a pink ice cream, passing a market stall piled with fruit and vegetables. There is also an empty cafe table with two chairs at the front left, a green bin, a flower planter, a tall street lamp and a potted plant.

- The ball is very large compared with the branch it sits on, and it looks balanced there rather than wedged in.
- The older man's face is hard to read. His head shows the cap peak on the left and grey hair on the right with no clear eye, so I cannot tell if he is actually looking at the book.
- The boy could be upset about the ball or could just be showing someone where it is.
- The dog could be begging for the ice cream or just looking at the man.
- The bearded man overlaps the stall, so it is unclear if he has just bought something from it or is just passing by.
- Two different men have the same pink ice cream, but there is no ice cream seller to be seen.
