# Picture 07

## Free description
This is a sunny park with rolling green hills and bushes in the background, a pond on the right and a winding pale path on the left. An older man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat, white sleeves and dark trousers walks to the left at the front, pushing a teal pram with a dark blue hood. A baby is asleep inside it with its eyes closed, under a pink blanket with white dots. Behind him on the right, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands in front of the pond holding a camera up to his eye, taking a photo towards the left, which seems to be aimed at the old man and the pram. To the left of the centre a tall black street lamp stands on the grass, and next to it a wooden ladder stands on the grass tilted at an angle, leaning on nothing at all, which looks impossible. Nobody is paying any attention to the ladder. The old man does not seem to notice the photographer. The rest is scenery: trees, bushes, reeds at the ends of the pond and a green bin in the bottom right corner.
