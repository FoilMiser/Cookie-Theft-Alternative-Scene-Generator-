# Picture 60

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back, a gate at the far left, a paved path on the left and an empty swing set. Several small things are going on at once. At the back, a bearded man in a green T-shirt and tan trousers is walking to the right with his eyes on an open book, and a red bucket sits on the grass directly in his way, so he looks about to walk into it or trip over it. On the right, a small child with curly dark hair, a yellow top and orange shorts stands pointing up at a blue ball that is stuck on a branch in the tree above, out of reach. At the front left, a man with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans is walking along the path carrying a cardboard box, heading left. At the front centre, a black cat with white paws is crouched low and creeping up behind a small red bird that has its head down at the ground, so the cat looks about to pounce. High in the sky at the top left, a blue bird is flying with its wings raised. Nothing looks physically impossible, but there are three small accidents waiting to happen or problems: the bucket, the stuck ball and the bird.
