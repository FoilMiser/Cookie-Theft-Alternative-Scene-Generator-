# Picture 13

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the right, and a wooden fence with a gate at the back left. At the back left, an older woman with grey hair in a teal cardigan and grey skirt is painting the fence bright blue with a roller on a long handle, and has finished only a small section of it. Her paint tin and a tray of blue paint are on the grass behind her. A trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the lawn to a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar, whose paws are blue, so it has clearly walked through the paint without the woman noticing. On the right, a woman with a dark ponytail in a purple top and teal trousers is pouring water onto a flower bed from a frying pan or saucepan, which is an odd thing to water with. At the front left on the path, a child with short dark hair in a yellow dress is raking a small pile of leaves. An empty wheelbarrow stands on the grass at the front. It looks like the dog may carry on spreading blue prints, perhaps towards the flower bed or the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The utensil could be a frying pan or a saucepan. It is dark with a grey inside and a long handle. Either way it is not a watering can, and the picture does not say why she is using it.
- The child in yellow could be a girl, given the dress, or a boy, given the short haircut. There is also a small dark mark by the mouth that could be a smile line or look like a little moustache.
- The child could also be a small adult. The proportions do not settle it.
- The rake head is small and partly overlaps the child's red shoes, so it is hard to see clearly. At first glance the long handle could be taken for a broom.
- The blue section of fence is painted in a very straight block, with the rails and boards all painted evenly from top to bottom, which looks neater than a small roller would manage.
- The roller is held quite high, and the woman's arm and the handle are hard to separate where they cross.
- The empty wheelbarrow could be meant for the leaves or may just be there.
