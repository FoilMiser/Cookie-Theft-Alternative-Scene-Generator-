# Picture 14

## Free description

This is a back garden under a grey sky with three heavy dark clouds, so it looks as if a storm or rain is coming, although no rain is falling yet. A cream house with a blue door is on the right, and a wooden fence and gate are at the back. On the path at the left, an old man in a flat blue cap and brown waistcoat is using a gigantic silver fork, like a huge dinner fork on a long handle, to push a small pile of dirt towards a red dustpan, and a puff of dust is rising. That oversized fork is the strange thing in the picture, since he should presumably have a broom. In the centre, a bearded man in a green T-shirt and tan trousers looks up with wide eyes, pointing at the sky as if warning about the clouds. Next to him an older woman with grey hair, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, is watering a flower bed with a hose, even though it looks about to rain, and she takes no notice of him. An empty swing set stands on the left and an empty wheelbarrow stands at the front. It seems likely that rain will come down soon and all of them will get wet.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The fork could be read as a giant table fork used by mistake as a broom, or as an odd garden fork. Its shape is plainly like a dinner fork, with a smooth silver head, which is why I read it as the wrong tool. It seems out of scale either way.
- The man's pointing could be at the clouds in general or at something up to the left that I cannot see. His finger points up and left of the clouds, and his eyes look up. His face could be read as scared or just surprised.
- The woman watering may be an intended joke (watering just before rain), or just a person gardening. She does not react to the sky.
- The hose comes up to the woman from behind her arm and hip, and the join to the nozzle is a little hard to see.
- The hose crosses in front of the flower bed, and the water stream passes in front of some flowers, so depth is a bit hard to judge there.
- The swing and the wheelbarrow are not doing anything and could just be background.
- There is no rain or lightning, so "storm" is my inference from the dark clouds and grey sky.
