# Picture 33

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with nobody in it; no people or animals are visible anywhere. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door stands on the left, a tall wooden fence with a gate runs across the back, and a paved path leads from the gate to the bottom right corner. In the middle of the lawn a sprinkler sits on the grass, fed by a dark teal hose that comes in from the left edge and runs just below a wooden bench. The sprinkler is throwing three thin arcs of water to the right. Two of the arcs land on the grass with small splashes. The third and longest arc comes down on a closed cardboard box that has been left on the lawn. The box is wet, with blue streaks running down its sides and a puddle around its base, so it looks as if it is getting soaked and whatever is inside could be spoiled. Nobody is there to move it or turn off the water. The rest of the garden is calm, with a flower bed, bushes, two potted plants and a bird box on a tall pole.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- I can't tell what is in the box or whether it was left there on purpose. It could be a delivery someone left outside, or just something forgotten.
- The small blue curved marks at the box's top corners could be splashes hitting the box, or they could look like little handles.
- The water arcs are very thin lines. They read as water because of the droplets and splashes, but on their own they could be mistaken for wires or hoops.
- The droplets are small dots hanging in the air beside the arcs; this seems normal for spray, though they look a little detached.
- The bird box pole stands in front of the fence with a small green mark at its base. It is very tall and thin for its position.
- Nothing looks clearly impossible; the only thing that looks wrong is a box being rained on by a sprinkler.
