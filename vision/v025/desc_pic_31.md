# Picture 31

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, a gate on the right, and a paved path leading to it. In the front right, a boy with a backwards red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes has just thrown a red ball with a white stripe; a dashed curved line shows its path through the air from his outstretched hand. To the left of him, a child with dark curly hair, wearing a yellow top and orange shorts, stands with both arms stretched out, ready to catch the ball. Near the gate, a woman with a long dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is walking to the right, holding a red lead attached to a white dog with brown spots, who is walking ahead of her on the path. Over on the left, a tan dog sits on the doorstep in front of the blue door holding its own red lead in its mouth, with the loop of the lead lying on the ground, as if it wants to go for a walk too and has been left behind. A washing line between two wooden posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes, and a flowerbed sits beside the house. Nothing looks impossible, and the ball looks as if it is about to reach the child's hands.

- Woman, back right, near the gate: long dark ponytail, purple top, teal trousers, dark shoes; walking to the right and holding a dog lead.
- White spotted dog, back far right, on the path in front of the gate: white with brown spots, blue collar; walking on the red lead.
- Tan dog, left, on the doorstep in front of the blue door: tan with white chest and paws, brown ears, red collar; sitting facing the viewer with a red lead in its mouth.
- Red ball with white stripe, in mid-air at centre-right: thrown by the boy, heading for the child.

- Tree and low bush, behind/next to the house; a line of treetops beyond the fence.
- Sun (top right) and two white clouds (top centre): a fine day.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The tan dog: it could be asking to go for a walk because it is holding its own lead, or it could simply be carrying or chewing the lead. It could also be read as being left behind while the other dog goes out.
- The ball could be read as thrown by the boy to the child, which the arc and outstretched hands suggest, or as heading a little too high to be caught, because it is still above the height of the child's hands.
- The dashed arc is a drawing convention rather than a real object, and it stops at the ball.
- The woman and the white dog overlap the gate area, so it is not clear whether they are about to go through the gate or just walking along the back.
- The dog's body and the gate are close in colour and overlap, so the dog is a bit lost against the gate.
- The boy's and child's positions are slightly awkward: the boy stands half on the grass and half on the path, and the child's arms look stiff.
