# Picture 18

## Free description

It is a sunny day in a green park, with a blue pond on the left and a sandy path winding up the right side. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands at the right edge of the pond, reaching one hand out over the water and holding a small white bag or cup in the other. Small brown pieces of food are scattered on the water and in the air between her and the duck, so she seems to be throwing food to it. A white duck is swimming towards the food and towards her, with white wake lines behind it showing it is moving. In the foreground a child in a yellow top and orange shorts sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket next to a wicker basket, leaning back on one arm and smiling or laughing. At the upper right, an older man in a cap, white shirt, brown vest and dark trousers is walking and pushing a teal and blue pram. A baby lies in the pram with eyes closed under a pink spotted blanket, apparently asleep. The scene is calm and everyday, and I see nothing impossible in it.
