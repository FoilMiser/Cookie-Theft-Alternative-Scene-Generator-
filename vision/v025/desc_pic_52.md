## Free description

This is a sunny park with a large tree and a winding sandy path on the left, a blue pond on the right, and green hills behind. A child with curly dark hair, a yellow top and orange shorts stands at the edge of the path with one arm raised, holding a string and looking upwards. The string runs in a drooping curve across the path to a purple diamond-shaped kite, which is caught in the leaves of the big tree. The kite seems stuck, so the child may be about to tug on the string or be unable to get it down. In the middle, a woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers holds a camera up to her eye, pointing it to the left, towards the child or the kite. To the right, an older man with a blue-grey cap, grey beard, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers walks to the right with a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. The man and his dog are in front of the pond and do not seem to notice the child's problem. Nothing looks impossible; the stuck kite is the main thing that has gone wrong.
