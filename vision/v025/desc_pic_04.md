# Picture 04

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, the corner of a cream house with a blue door on the left, and a gate and pale path on the right. In the centre a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is digging with a long-handled spade. He has a lump of soil on the blade, next to a small hole and a low mound of dirt on the grass, and he looks down at his work. To the right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on a red swing hanging from a dark metal frame, smiling with his eyes closed, and small lines beside the seat suggest he is swinging. At the bottom left a small white dog with brown patches lies curled on the grass with its eyes shut, apparently asleep. There is a round bed of bright flowers beside the house, and a teal watering can stands on the grass near the path. On the washing line at the back hang a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. The mood is peaceful and nothing looks wrong or about to go badly.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The man could be digging a hole to plant something, or filling a hole back in; the soil on the spade could be going either way. There is nothing near him that he would obviously be planting.
- The soil on the spade looks like a smooth round brown blob, so it could be misread as a stone or some other object he has dug up.
- The dog could be asleep, or it could be lying down with its head low. There is no sign of it having anything to do with the digging.
- The lines beside the swing seat are few and faint. They could mean the swing is moving, but the seat hangs almost straight below the frame, so it could be read as nearly still.
- The swing ropes narrow towards the boy's head and then spread again down to the seat, which looks slightly odd, and it is hard to tell if they pass in front of or behind him.
- Nothing looks like it is floating or passing through anything else.
