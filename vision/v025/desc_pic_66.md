This is a sunny paved town street or plaza with a row of four colourful two-storey shop buildings along the back, each with a striped awning. A single boy with dark curly hair, wearing a yellow shirt and orange shorts, walks across the front of the scene holding a yellow balloon on a string. He is walking to the right and seems to be looking to the left or back over his shoulder; his head and body are not quite pointing the same way. Around him the street is empty of other people: a fruit stall with a teal-striped canopy stands at the left, with boxes of fruit on it, and a cardboard box on the ground beside it. Two small round tables with chairs on each side stand empty at the lower right. A street lamp, a tree in a round planter, two rubbish bins, a planter of flowers and a potted plant are spread around. A red bird flies in the sky at the upper right. Nothing looks clearly impossible; it is just a boy out walking with a balloon. The stall has nobody tending it.
