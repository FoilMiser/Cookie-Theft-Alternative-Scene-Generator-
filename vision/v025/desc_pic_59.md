# Picture 59

## Free description

This is a sunny park or playground with a dark metal railing fence across the back, a large tree on the left and a tall slide on the right. In the centre, a woman with a long dark ponytail or braid, a purple top and teal trousers is walking to the left, pushing a teal and blue pram. A baby is asleep inside it with eyes closed, under a pink spotted blanket. On the right, a girl with dark hair in a bun and a pink dress is coming down the slide with both arms raised, looking as if she is enjoying herself. At the front centre, a black cat with white paws is crouched low with its body stretched out, creeping up on a small red bird right in front of its nose. The bird has its head down at the ground, seemingly pecking, and does not seem to notice; it looks as though the cat is about to pounce. At the left, a red seesaw stands empty, tilted with its right end high in the air and its left end down. At the front right is a wooden sandpit with a red bucket and a blue spade in it, and nobody is playing there. Nothing looks physically impossible; the only tension is between the cat and the bird.
