# Picture 48

## Free description

This is a sunny park with a large blue pond on the right, a winding sandy path on the left, and green hills and a line of trees in the distance. A girl with dark hair, a pink dress and yellow shoes stands alone on the grass at the left edge of the pond. She leans slightly towards the water with one arm stretched out over it, and her other hand holds something small and white. A white duck with an orange beak is on the pond close to her, facing her, with white lines trailing behind it as if it is swimming her way. Small orange-brown bits are scattered on the water between the girl and the duck, so she seems to be feeding it. The duck looks about to reach the food and eat it. No one else is in the picture, and the wooden bench by the path is empty. Nothing looks impossible; it reads as a calm everyday moment.
