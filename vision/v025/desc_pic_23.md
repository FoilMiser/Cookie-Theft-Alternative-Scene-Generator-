# Picture 23

## Free description

It is a busy, sunny town street with a row of four colourful shops with striped awnings, a paved square in front, a tree and a lamp post. On the left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue ball stuck on a branch in the tree. Behind him an older woman with a grey bun and glasses walks to the left carrying a cardboard box. In the middle, a man in a red shirt and jeans sits on a bench reading a red book, not noticing a blue bird beside him on the bench that is going for his sandwich. Behind him an older man in a cap and brown vest waters a box of flowers with a teal watering can. At the bottom left of centre, a tan dog with a red collar sits with its paws on an open newspaper, as if reading it, which is odd. Beside it is a red bicycle whose wheels are square, which is impossible to ride. At the right, a girl in a pink dress holds purple and red balloons on strings while a yellow balloon has got loose and floats away, and she reaches up after it. Further right, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts walks past a fruit stall holding a pink ice cream, and a red bird flies across the sky at the top right.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The bicycle has square wheels, which looks impossible. It could be a deliberate joke or a drawing error.
- The dog's head is hard to read. I see a round tan head with a dark ear and no clear face, as if it is turned away. It could be reading the newspaper like a person, or it could be chewing or playing with it. The newspaper seems to stand up on its own.
- The dog's body looks oddly shaped, stretched out to the right along the ground, so at first it was not obvious that it was a dog.
- The yellow balloon's string ends a little way from the girl's raised hand. She could have just let go, or she could be trying to grab a balloon that belongs to someone else.
- The balloon strings of the purple and red balloons are curved and loose, and they look slightly odd but acceptable.
- The blue bird could be stealing the sandwich or just sitting next to it. The small pale item next to the sandwich could be a cup, a fruit or another bit of food.
- The ball could be stuck on the branch, or it could seem to float in front of the leaves. A short brown branch runs under it, so stuck seems right.
- The lamp post crosses the window of the green shop, and the walking child overlaps the fruit crates. These are just overlaps, not errors.
