# Picture 51

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a long wooden fence with a gate on the left across the back. The strangest thing is an orange goldfish in the middle of the green leaves of the tree behind the fence, which looks impossible because a fish should not be up in a tree. A bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands in the centre-right, pouring water from a teal watering can onto a bed of colourful flowers. At the front right, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. She wears a teal top and a grey skirt. Neither person is looking at the fish or seems to notice it. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands on the grass at the front, and two potted plants and a bush stand near the fence. A paved path runs from the gate down the left side, with nobody on it.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The fish could be read as a real fish impossibly in a tree, or as a toy, ornament or fish-shaped kite stuck in the branches. There is no string or branch holding it, and it looks like a flat picture of a goldfish laid over the leaves, so I read it as a plain "wrong thing in the wrong place".
- The water is drawn as broken, lumpy blue and white streaks, so it is a little messy, but it clearly falls onto the flowers. Some of it overlaps the flowers.
- The woman's bench overlaps the doorstep area. She sits in front of the house, partly in front of the door step, and this is just overlap rather than anything wrong.
- The wheelbarrow may belong to the man's gardening, but it is empty and not near him.
- Apart from the fish, nothing is floating or passing through anything else.
