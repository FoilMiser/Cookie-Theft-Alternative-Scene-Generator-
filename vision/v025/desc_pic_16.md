# Picture 16

## Free description

This is a busy paved town square on a sunny, windy day, in front of a row of four coloured shop buildings with striped awnings. Near the left-centre, a woman with short dark hair in a yellow dress stretches both arms up towards a blue cap that the wind has blown into the air, along with some leaves, and white wind lines blow across the scene. At the far left, a little girl in a pink dress jumps with her arms up, laughing. On a bench in the middle, an older woman with grey hair and glasses reads a red book, and she has not noticed a blue bird on the bench beside her pecking at her sandwich. Behind her, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers waters a flower box with a teal watering can, and to the right a street sweeper in an orange cap and orange safety vest sweeps with a yellow broom. At the bottom right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice cream cone and clutches his head, because his pink ice cream has fallen on the ground and a brown dog with a red collar is sniffing or eating it. At the far right, a bearded man in a green T-shirt and tan trousers stands with hands on his hips and a white or metal bucket upside down on his head, which is odd, in front of a fruit stall. At the bottom centre, a white dog with a brown ear sits upright and holds an open newspaper as if reading it, which is impossible, and a red bird flies across the sky at the top.

- The woman in yellow could be trying to grab the cap, or she could just be throwing her arms up in surprise. She is not wearing a hat, so the cap seems to be hers, but I cannot be sure.
- The woman in yellow has quite short hair, so she could read as a girl or a boy in a dress. She also could be an older child rather than an adult.
- There is a small white object beside the blue bird and the sandwich that could be a paper cup or a sandwich wrapper. I could not make it out.

- The white dog's face is turned away towards the paper and its head is a smooth white ball with a brown ear, so it is hard to read. At a glance it could look like a person in a spacesuit or a small robot. The blue patch at its neck could be a collar or a bandana.
- The bearded man's bucket could be a deliberate silly hat or a mistake. The bucket overlaps the fruit stall behind him, so at first it could be mistaken for part of the stall's display.
- The boy's hand at his head could be read as being upset or as scratching his head.
- It is not clear if the tan dog has already eaten any of the ice cream or is only sniffing it.
- There is no visible rubbish for the sweeper to sweep.
- Some leaves appear to disappear behind the tree, and one wind line passes near the tree's top. That looks fine, but there are a lot of separate things going on, so it is easy to miss items.
