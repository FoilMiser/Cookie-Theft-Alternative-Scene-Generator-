# Picture 42

## Free description

This is a sunny park with a pond on the left, green hills behind, a tall black lamp post in the middle and a winding sand-coloured path on the right. A woman in a yellow dress stands at the edge of the pond holding a small white container and throwing brown crumbs towards a white duck swimming on the water; several crumbs are in the air and floating around the duck. On the far left, a little girl in a pink dress holds strings for a yellow balloon and a red balloon while reaching up with her other arm and looking worried, because a purple balloon has got away and is floating above the pond. An old man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat and white shirt walks across the front of the pond carrying a yellow bag. On a bench on the right, a woman in a purple top reads a red book, while right next to her a blue bird is on the bench at her sandwich, and she does not seem to notice, so the sandwich is about to be stolen. Behind her, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie walks along holding a pink ice cream. On the right, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts points up at a big tree, where a blue-and-white ball is stuck in the branches. In the front centre, a brown dog with a red collar is sitting on the grass holding up an open newspaper with its paws and looking at it as if reading it, which is impossible. An orange cat lies curled up asleep on the grass at the front, right by the path.
