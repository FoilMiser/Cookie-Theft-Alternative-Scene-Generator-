# Picture 05

## Free description
This is a sunny playground on a green lawn with a dark railing fence and bushes behind it. At the front left a red seesaw is tipped steeply. An older man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers sits on the low right end, reading an open book and paying no attention to anything else. A small girl in a pink dress is on the high left end, stuck up in the air with her feet dangling and small curved lines under her feet that suggest she is kicking or that her feet are moving. She looks across at the man and seems unable to come down while he stays on it, and he does not seem to have noticed her. At the front right a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts kneels in a wooden sandbox beside a sandcastle with a little red flag on top, working on it. In the back there is an empty swing set with a red seat hanging still, and on the right an empty red slide with an orange and yellow chute. A tree, a green bin, a bush and a grey rock complete the scene. It looks odd that a grown man is sitting on a child's seesaw reading a book.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl could be trapped up there and unhappy, or she could just be enjoying it; her face is small and looks fairly neutral.
- The curved lines under the girl's feet could mean she is kicking her legs to try to get down, or that she has just bounced up, or that the seesaw has only just tipped.
- The older man's face is hard to read. I can see the cap peak on the left and grey hair on the right and something like an ear in the middle, but no clear eyes, so he could be looking at the book or his head could be turned away from us.
- The man could have sat down without realising a child was on the other end, or could be doing it on purpose.
- The sandcastle overlaps the boy's lower legs, so it is hard to tell whether it is in front of him or whether he is kneeling partly into it.
- The sandcastle has very straight, boxy edges and could at first be taken for a cardboard or wooden model rather than sand.
- Nothing appears to float, apart from the girl's seesaw end being high up, which is normal for a seesaw.
