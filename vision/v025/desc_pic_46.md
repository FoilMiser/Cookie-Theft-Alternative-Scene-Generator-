# Picture 46

## Free description

This is a sunny, windy back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path at the front right. At the far right, near the gate, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands with both arms up and a surprised look, as a blue cap flies off into the air above him with a few leaves, next to white wavy wind lines. The wind is blowing from left to right. In the middle, blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel on a washing line are blown out sideways to the right, and leaves are flying off the big tree behind the house. A boy in a yellow top and orange shorts sits on a red swing in a grey metal frame, facing us and smiling, with small motion lines beside him showing he is swinging. In the front centre, a brown dog with a red collar crouches with its chest low and its tail up and wagging, next to a yellow-green tennis ball. A teal watering can sits on the grass near the path, and a round bed of colourful flowers is near the house. Nothing looks impossible; the man has lost his cap to the wind while the boy and the dog carry on playing.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The blue cap is probably the man's own, blown off his head, or could just be flying past him; he has no hat on and is reaching towards it.
- His raised arms could mean trying to catch the cap or just surprise; his hands are below the cap and not touching it.
- The man's face is looking up and slightly sideways, and it is a little hard to tell whether he is looking at the cap directly.
- The man stands behind the swing frame and near the edge of the path, so he might look at first as if he is in or touching the frame, but he seems to be behind it.
- The swing barely tilts, and the seat hangs almost straight down, so the small motion lines are the main sign that it is moving; it could be read as still or slowly moving.
- The striped item could be a towel, a blanket or a shirt.
- The dog could be inviting play with the ball, sniffing it, or having just dropped it. It takes no notice of the wind.
