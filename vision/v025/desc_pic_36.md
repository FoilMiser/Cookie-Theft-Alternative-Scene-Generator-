# Picture 36

## Free description
This is a sunny park with one person in it. A man with short dark hair and a beard, wearing a green t-shirt, khaki trousers and brown shoes, is standing on the grass facing right and holding a long fishing rod in both hands. The rod slants up and out over a blue pond, and a thin line drops from its tip to a small red and white float sitting on the water with a ripple ring around it. No fish is visible and he has not caught anything yet, so he seems to be waiting for a bite. The float's ripple could mean something has just moved it, or simply that it landed there. Around him the park is empty: a winding sandy path on the left, a big tree, a tall black lamp post, a green bin, bushes and a grey rock. Another green bin is in the bottom right corner, and rolling hills and trees fill the background. Nothing looks wrong or dangerous.
