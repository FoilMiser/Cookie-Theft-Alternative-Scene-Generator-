# Picture 55

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, a tall wooden fence runs across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path runs from the gate down to the bottom right corner. Three people are on the lawn, each busy with their own activity and not paying attention to one another. In the middle, an older man in a grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers holds the nozzle of a long teal hose and sprays an arc of water down onto a flower bed to his left. At the back, in front of the fence, a small child with curly dark hair, a yellow top and orange shorts stands with one arm raised, holding a string. The string sags across the fence and goes up to a purple kite, but the kite is caught among the leaves of the tree behind the house, so it looks stuck rather than flying. In the front, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers swings one leg out to the right and is kicking an orange ball, with curved motion lines showing the swing of his leg. The ball is right at the end of his shoe and looks about to fly off to the right, towards the path. The stuck kite is the main thing that looks wrong; nothing looks physically impossible.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The kite could be read two ways: it has become stuck in the tree and the child is trying to pull it free, or it is simply flying low and passing in front of the tree. The leaf clumps drawn over it make me favour stuck.
- The ball could be just about to be struck or could have just left the foot, since it is almost touching the shoe.
- The red cap's peak seems to point to the left while the boy faces right, so the cap may be worn backwards, or the shape may just be hard to read.
- The man has a grey patch at the side of his head, which I take as grey hair or a sideburn, though it could be something covering his ear.
- The upper end of the hose seems to arrive near the man's waist and the nozzle is out at his hand; the connection between them is hidden or not clear, so the hose and nozzle look slightly disconnected.
- The child at the back is drawn quite small, which I read as distance rather than as a much younger child, but it could be either.
