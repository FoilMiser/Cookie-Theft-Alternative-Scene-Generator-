# Picture 09

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, a long wooden fence runs across the back with a gate at the right, and a paved path leads from the gate down to the bottom right corner. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is kneeling on the grass beside a round flower bed, working at the plants, and a small pot of pink flowers stands at the edge of the bed. In the middle of the lawn a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands with her hands on her hips, and she has a white or metal bucket upside down on her head, like a hat. On the path at the right, an old man in a flat blue cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is sweeping with a broom that has a yellow head. The bucket on the woman's head is the odd thing in the picture; she looks calm about it and nobody is reacting to it. A teal watering can and a hose reel sit unused on the grass, and two potted green plants stand near the fence. Nothing seems about to happen; each person is getting on with their own activity.
