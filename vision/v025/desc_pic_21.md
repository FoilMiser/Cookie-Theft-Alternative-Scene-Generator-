# Picture 21

## Free description

It is a sunny day in a back garden, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a tall wooden fence with a gate at the far left. A girl in a pink dress is in the middle of the lawn, jumping with both arms raised and a big smile; her feet are off the ground and her shadow is on the grass below her. Behind her, near the fence, a white goat with small dark horns has its head down and is nibbling a small tuft of grass. The strange thing is an orange goldfish in the big tree beside the house, sitting among the leaves far from any water, which is impossible. There is a birdhouse on a tall pole at the left, two potted plants, a watering can, an empty wheelbarrow and a flower bed by the door. No other people are present. Other than the fish, and perhaps a goat in a small garden, it is an ordinary, cheerful scene.

## Inventory

People and animals:
- Centre, foreground: girl with dark hair tied back, pink dress, yellow shoes; jumping in the air with both arms up, smiling.
- Centre-right, back, near the fence: white goat with small dark horns; standing with its head down, eating a small tuft of grass.
- Upper right, in the tree: orange goldfish with a forked tail, one eye showing; appears among the leaves, not moving, facing right.

Objects:
- Birdhouse: small box with a red roof and a round hole, on a tall wooden pole, left of centre.
- Potted plants: two terracotta pots with green leaves, left of centre near the fence.
- Watering can: teal can on the grass, bottom left, next to the path; not in use.
- Wheelbarrow: grey tub, black wheel, wooden handles, bottom centre; empty and not in use.
- Flower bed: brown soil with yellow, orange, purple, pink and red flowers, right of centre.
- Grass tuft: small darker tuft with something white in it, where the goat is eating.
- House: cream wall, red roof, window and blue door, on the right.
- Fence and gate: tall brown wooden fence along the back, with a closed gate at the far left.
- Path: pale paved path running from the gate to the bottom left.
- Tree and bushes: large tree behind and beside the house, two round bushes.
- Sky: yellow sun at the top left and two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The fish could be read two ways: a real fish impossibly in a tree, or a decoration or toy hung in the tree. It looks like a plain fish with nothing holding it, and it could also look as if it is pasted on top of the leaves.
- The girl could be jumping for joy, or jumping up to reach something. There is nothing above her to reach, so joy seems more likely. It is not clear what she is happy about, or whether she has noticed the goat or the fish.
- The gap between her feet and her shadow is clear, so she reads as airborne, not floating, but a still picture could suggest floating.
- The goat's rear legs look merged and I can count fewer than four distinct legs. It could also be read as a white sheep or a dog at first glance, but the horns point to a goat.
- There is a white shape in the tuft of grass by the goat's mouth, which could be a small flower or part of the goat's beard.
