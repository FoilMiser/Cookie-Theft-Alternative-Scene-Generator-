# Picture 08

## Free description
This is a sunny playground on a green lawn with a dark railing fence and bushes behind it. On the left, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is sliding down a red-framed slide with an orange and yellow chute, with both arms stretched forward and up, seemingly enjoying the ride. On the right a red seesaw is tipped steeply. An older man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers sits on the low left end, reading an open book and ignoring everything around him. A small girl in a pink dress sits on the high right end, up in the air, with her feet dangling and small curved lines under them suggesting they are moving. She looks over at the man and seems stuck up there while he stays down. At the bottom left a wooden sandbox has nobody in it, just a red bucket and a blue spade left at one side. It looks odd that a grown man is on a child's seesaw reading a book. A tree on the right, a green bin by the fence, a bush behind the slide and a grey rock at the bottom left finish the scene.
