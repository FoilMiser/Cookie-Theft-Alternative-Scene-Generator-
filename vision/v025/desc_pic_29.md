# Picture 29

## Free description
This is a sunny town street or square with a row of colourful shops with striped awnings across the back and a paved area in front. At the far left, a man with brown hair, wearing a red shirt and blue jeans, is running leftwards, with speed lines behind him, as if hurrying to get somewhere, and he is about to run out of the picture. In the middle, a small girl in a pink dress stands holding the strings of a yellow and a blue balloon in one hand, while a purple balloon has got loose with its string dangling just out of her reach; she stretches her other hand up towards it and looks unhappy. To her right, an older man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers stands holding a white bag and sprinkling seeds from his other hand, and the seeds fall in a dotted line to a blue bird pecking on the ground at his feet. On the right there is a market stall with a striped canopy and crates of red, orange, yellow and green fruit, though nobody is tending it. A tree in a round planter stands on the left, and a tall black lamp post stands on the right. Nobody seems to notice the girl's balloon.

- Sun (top right, slightly cut off at the top edge) and two white clouds (top centre): a fine day.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The purple balloon: the string stops in mid-air and does not join the girl's hand, so I read it as having escaped. It could also be read as her still trying to grasp it just after letting go.
- The running man: I cannot tell why he is running. He could be late for something, chasing something out of the picture or fleeing. He could be running away from the girl's area, but nothing links him to her. He seems unconnected with everyone else.
- The girl is looking upwards and frowning, which I took as being upset about the balloon, but it might be plain concentration.
- The older man's bag is pale and thin, so it could be a paper bag, a carton or even a newspaper. The seed trail is what tells me it is seed.
- The market stall's front poles appear to run down behind or through the row of crates, and only two table legs are visible. This looks a little odd but not impossible.
- The blue bird is small and low, and it is partly hidden by seeds. It took a moment to see it as a bird with its head down.
