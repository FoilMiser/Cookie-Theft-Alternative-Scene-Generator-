# Picture 62

## Free description

This is a sunny playground in a park, with a slide on the left, a swing set in the middle, a tree on the right and a dark railing fence with hedges behind. A boy in a red cap and blue top runs across the left foreground, with speed lines behind him. A brown dog wearing a red collar or scarf sits up on the grass like a person, holding an open newspaper and appearing to read it. A bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers walks through the middle of the scene with his eyes on an open book, and he seems not to be looking where he is going; a blue bucket sits on the grass just ahead of him, so he may be about to trip over it. An old man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat sits on a bench reading a red book, while a blue bird on the bench next to him pecks at a sandwich he has left there, apparently without him noticing. On the right a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands under the tree, pointing up at a blue ball stuck on a branch. Nearby a small girl in a pink dress holds a yellow balloon on a string while a white spotted dog in a play bow, tail wagging, faces a yellow-green tennis ball on the ground right beside her. A bicycle in the middle of the grass looks wrong: its wheels are square rather than round. A red bird flies across the sky at upper right. The dog reading a newspaper and the square-wheeled bike are the clearly impossible things here.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The bicycle's wheels are square. It could be read as a deliberate impossibility or a drawing glitch; it looks deliberate because both wheels are drawn the same way. The bike is also standing up with nobody holding it, with the pedal crank sticking down low.
- The red item at the brown dog's neck could be a collar, a bandana or a bow.
- The running boy: his head and the speed lines suggest motion, but it could be read as either him running to the left (face and leading arm point left) or something being odd about the direction, because the speed lines are on the right, which fits running left.
- The spotted dog could be crouching to play with the ball, or sniffing or about to grab it. The ball also could be read as belonging to the girl, as it sits almost touching her leg.
- Whether the walking man will actually hit the bucket is unclear: it is a little to his right and in front of him.
- The old man's face has an odd look at this size, with his hand raised near his chin; he may be stroking his chin or holding the book up close.
- The dog's newspaper seems to be standing up on the ground in front of it, with the paws on it, rather than clearly held in the air.
