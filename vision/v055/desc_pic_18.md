A sunny park with rolling green hills, a pond on the left, a tall black lamp post in the middle, and a winding sand path on the right. A woman with dark hair tied back, in a purple top and teal trousers, stands at the edge of the pond holding a brown paper bag and a piece of bread. She is throwing bread to a white duck; several small bread pieces are floating on the water, and the duck is swimming toward them (little wake lines behind it) with its beak down near the crumbs. Behind and to the right, a white goat with a red collar is grazing on a tuft of grass; it is tied with a rope to a short wooden post. On the path at the right, a small boy with dark skin, in a yellow shirt and orange shorts, is walking while holding the string of a red balloon. An empty wooden bench sits beside the path. There is a green bin at bottom left, a grey rock at the bottom centre, trees and bushes around. Nothing looks clearly wrong.
