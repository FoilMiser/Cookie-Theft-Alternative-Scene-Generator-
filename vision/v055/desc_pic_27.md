## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house (red roof, blue door) on the left, a wooden fence with a gate at the back right and a paved path running down the right side. A clothesline between two posts in the middle holds blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel. In the front left a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. Beside her on the bench is a sandwich, and a small blue bird is perched next to it, pecking at it — crumbs are falling — while she reads and does not notice. Next to the bench is a hose reel with a hose lying on the grass. There is a flower bed, two potted plants, a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can in the garden.
