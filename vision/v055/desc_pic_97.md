## Free description
A busy back garden on a sunny day, with a wooden fence, a gate on the left and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps a small pile of brown leaves on the path with a broom. Behind him a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans paints the fence blue with a paint roller; a few planks are already blue and a paint tin sits at his feet. In the centre an elderly woman with glasses and a grey bun, in a teal top and grey skirt, holds a garden hose and its nozzle, but the hose looks like it isn't spraying — a brown dog with a red collar is standing right on the hose, between her and the hose reel. A man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands with his hands on his hips and, strangely, has a white bucket upside down on his head. A little girl in a pink dress points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of the tree. A grey cat walks along the top of the fence, a blue bird flies in the sky, and a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench at the right sipping a red drink through a straw. An empty wheelbarrow stands near the centre, and its wheel looks oddly square rather than round.
