## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house (red roof, blue door) on the left, a wooden fence with a gate on the right and a path down the right side. At the back right an older grey-haired man in a cap and brown waistcoat is painting the fence blue with a paint roller; a few boards are already blue and a paint tin is on the grass behind him. In the middle of the lawn a brown dog with a red collar is sitting up on its haunches wearing glasses and holding a newspaper, as if reading it — which is impossible. In front left a girl in a pink dress sits on a bench eating a pink ice cream, and a small white spotted dog sits up begging beside her, looking up at the ice cream. There is a flower bed, a wheelbarrow and a watering can.
