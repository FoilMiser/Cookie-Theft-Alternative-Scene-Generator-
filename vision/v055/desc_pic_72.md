It is a sunny day in a back garden. There is a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, and a tall wooden fence across the back. A brown dog sits by the blue door holding a lead in its mouth, as if it wants to go for a walk. In the middle, a girl in a pink dress waves, and a man in a green T-shirt walks towards the front carrying a cardboard box. Further back, a woman in a purple top is using a long-handled roller to paint the fence blue; one section of the fence is already blue. Next to her on the grass are a paint tin and a paint tray. A trail of blue paw prints runs from the paint tray across the grass towards the path, where a small white dog with brown spots stands with blue-stained paws. The dog has clearly stepped in the paint and is tracking it around. On the right, by the gate, a man in a red shirt sweeps a pile of leaves with a broom. A green watering can stands on the grass at the front.
