# pic_46

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a gate at back right, a paved path on the right, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left. On a wooden bench at the front left, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is sitting reading a red book. Beside him on the bench is a sandwich, and a small blue bird is standing on the bench pecking at the sandwich, with crumbs scattered — the boy does not seem to notice. In the centre-left a woman with dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers is spraying a flower bed with a garden hose that runs from a hose reel next to the bench. On the right, a girl in a pink dress is jumping with both arms raised, off the ground (her shadow is below her), looking happy. Behind her stands a metal swing set with one red swing seat, and further right a birdhouse on a tall pole.
