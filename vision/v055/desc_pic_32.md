# pic_32

## Free description
A windy, sunny back garden: white wind lines are drawn in the sky and over the fence. A man in a green shirt and beige trousers stands in the middle reaching up with one arm, looking surprised, as a red cap blows away from him in the wind together with a few leaves — his hat has just been blown off. On the right, sitting by the blue door of the house, a brown dog holds a yellow lead in its mouth, as if waiting to go for a walk. In the front, a small white spotted dog is lying curled up asleep on the grass. There is a path and gate on the left, two potted plants, a bush, a flower bed, a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The man's raised arm could be read as reaching to catch the cap or as having thrown it; the wind lines and surprised face suggest it blew off.
- The sleeping dog could also be read as lying down/hiding from the wind.
- The cap floats in front of the fence; that is intended as flying, not odd.
