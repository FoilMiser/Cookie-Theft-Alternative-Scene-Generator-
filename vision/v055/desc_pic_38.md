# pic_38

## Free description
A sunny back garden with the house on the left. At the bottom left, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a bench reading a red book. Beside her on the bench is a sandwich, and a small blue bird is pecking at it. She has not noticed, as she is absorbed in her book. In the centre, a woman in a yellow dress is pegging wet laundry onto a washing line: blue jeans and a white T-shirt, both dripping. A basket of more clothes sits on the grass below. On the right, on the path, a small boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts holds the strings of a red and a blue balloon. A yellow balloon with a trailing string is floating away up into the sky, and the boy reaches up after it, looking upset. A hose reel, a wheelbarrow and a watering can are on the lawn.
