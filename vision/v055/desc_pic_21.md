## Free description
A busy sunny park with a pond on the left, a tall lamp post in the centre, a bench and a winding sand path on the right. At the front left, a woman in a yellow dress is watering a bed of colourful flowers, but she is pouring the water out of a frying pan rather than a watering can, which looks odd. Next to her, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers holds a paper bag and is scattering seeds or crumbs onto the ground, where a red bird is pecking at them. A small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts, holding a pink ice-cream cone, is running toward the bird from the right and seems about to run into it or scare it away. In the back right, a girl in a pink dress holds red and purple balloons and reaches up toward a yellow balloon that is floating away out of reach. On the path at the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks along carrying a yellow shopping bag. The bench is empty, and there is a green bin at bottom left.
