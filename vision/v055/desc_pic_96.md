# pic_96

## Free description
A back garden on a sunny day, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence with a gate at the back right, and a paved path running to the gate. An elderly man with grey hair and a blue cap, wearing a white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers, is watering a round bed of colourful flowers. Oddly, he is not using a watering can: he is pouring the water out of what looks like a grey frying pan or saucepan held by its long handle. Water streams from the pan onto the flowers. Nearby are two potted plants and a bush, and an empty grey wheelbarrow stands in the front. Apart from the strange choice of a pan for watering, the scene is ordinary and quiet.
