## Free description
A busy, sunny park with a pond, a lamp post, a bench, a winding path and trees. Several odd or impossible things are going on. At the far left, a brown dog is sitting up wearing glasses and reading a newspaper like a person. Next to it, an old man in a cap and brown waistcoat is watering the flower bed by pouring water out of a frying pan instead of a watering can. On the pond, a white duck is swimming and eating pieces of bread floating on the water, and a little girl in a pink dress at the pond's edge holds a slice of bread, seemingly feeding it. In the front centre, a girl in a yellow top and orange shorts is crying, holding an empty ice-cream cone. Her pink ice cream has fallen on the ground and a white spotted puppy is licking it up. Further back, a white goat on a rope tied to a stake is grazing on a patch of grass. An elderly woman with glasses is sweeping a pile of leaves on the grass near the path. On the right, a boy in a red cap holds a kite string, but his purple kite is caught in the tree. A blue bird flies across the sky.

- The girl in pink is fairly far from the duck. She might be feeding it, or just holding bread while watching. The bread already on the water suggests someone threw it.
