# pic_04

## Free description
A sunny back garden with no people or ordinary animals in it. A cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the right, a tall wooden fence runs across the back with a gate on the left, and a paved path leads from the front-left to the gate. A washing line between two posts holds a yellow red-striped towel and a pair of blue jeans. There is a flower bed of colourful flowers near the house, a wooden bench, two potted plants, a teal watering can and an empty wheelbarrow on the lawn. The strange thing is an orange goldfish sitting up in the leaves of the tree behind the house, as if it were a bird in the tree. A fish in a tree is impossible.
