## Free description
A sunny back garden with a gate and path on the left and the house on the right. In the middle, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is jumping in the air with both arms raised, smiling and happy. His shadow is on the grass below him, so he is off the ground. On the path at the left, an old man in a cap and brown waistcoat is sweeping a pile of leaves with a yellow broom. At the bottom, a grey cat is crouched low, creeping towards a red bird that is pecking at seeds on the grass. The cat looks as if it is about to pounce on the bird. A watering can and two potted plants are on the lawn, and a flower bed sits by the house.
