# pic_03

## Free description
A sunny back garden of a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, enclosed by a tall wooden fence with a gate on the right and hedges and a tree behind. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and grey skirt stands by a flower bed full of colourful flowers. She is holding a grey watering can, and a thin blue stream of water rises in an arc from the can's spout up over the flowers, with a few drops at the top. A washing line between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes, pegged up to dry. Elsewhere there is a wheelbarrow on the grass, two potted plants, a birdhouse on a tall pole, a wooden bench by the house, and a paved path leading to the gate. The odd thing is the water: it shoots upward out of the watering can like a fountain instead of pouring down onto the flowers.
