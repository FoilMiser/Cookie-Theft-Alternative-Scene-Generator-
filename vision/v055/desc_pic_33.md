# pic_33

## Free description
A back garden on a grey, overcast day: three dark storm clouds hang in the middle of the sky and there is no sun. An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, stands on the lawn in the front centre-right. She points one finger up at the dark clouds and looks up at them, a little worried. It looks as if it is about to rain. A teal watering can stands on the grass beside her. Watering seems pointless if rain is coming, and that may be what she is thinking. The rest of the garden is calm: a house with a blue door on the left, a flower bed, two potted plants, a bird box on a pole, a wooden fence with a gate on the right, and a paved path running along the right side. Nothing looks impossible.
