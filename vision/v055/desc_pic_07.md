# pic_07

## Free description
A sunny playground with a railing fence and hedges behind, a tree on the left, a swing set with one empty red swing in the middle, a red slide with a yellow chute on the right, and a sandpit at the front right. On the left a seesaw is tilted steeply: a small girl in pink sits on the raised end, high in the air, holding the handle, with a hand near her mouth and a worried or upset look. On the low end, which is down on the ground, an elderly man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat sits reading an open newspaper, apparently not paying attention to the girl. Because he is so much heavier and is just sitting there, the girl is stuck up in the air and the seesaw cannot go up and down. In the sandpit a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts kneels building a sandcastle with a red flag, holding a red spade. There is a green bin by the fence and a grey rock at the front right. The odd part is the grown man using the seesaw as a seat to read the paper while the child is stranded at the top.
