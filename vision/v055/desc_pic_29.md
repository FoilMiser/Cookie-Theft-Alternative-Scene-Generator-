## Free description
A sunny back garden with a fence and gate at the back left, a paved path on the left, a swing set, a clothesline with a striped towel and jeans, and a house with a blue door on the right. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands in the middle holding up the end of a garden hose and looking at the nozzle, apparently puzzled because no water is coming out. The hose runs along the grass from a hose reel near the house, and a brown dog is standing on the hose, which would block the water flow. On the right a man in a red shirt and blue trousers sits on a bench reading a red book. In the front a small white-and-brown spotted dog crouches in a play bow next to a yellow tennis ball, wagging its tail. There is a flower bed near the house.
