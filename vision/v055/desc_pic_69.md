## Free description
A sunny back garden seen the other way round: the cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the right, and the gate and paved path are on the left. In the front left, a woman in a yellow dress is sweeping dust and grit on the path towards a red dustpan. Instead of a broom she is using a giant dinner fork, which is the wrong tool and oddly oversized. In the middle back, an elderly woman in a teal cardigan is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller. Several planks to her left are already blue, and a paint tin stands by her feet. On the right, a small girl in a pink dress is flying a yellow kite with a long string and bow-tie tail high in the sky. There is also a birdhouse on a tall pole by the fence, two potted plants, an empty wheelbarrow at the front, and a flower bed by the house.
