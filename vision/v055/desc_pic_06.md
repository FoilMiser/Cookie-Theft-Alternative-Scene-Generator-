## Free description
The same kind of sunny park: hills, trees, bushes, a winding sandy path on the left, a tall street lamp and a blue pond with bulrushes on the right. A small girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench beside the path eating a pink ice-cream cone. A tan/brown dog with a red collar sits up on its hind legs right next to the bench, facing her and looking up, as if begging for some of her ice cream. On the right an elderly man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat stands near the pond, also eating a pink ice-cream cone. Next to him is a bed of colourful flowers, and there is a green litter bin in the bottom-right corner. The pond is empty of animals. Nothing looks impossible; it seems the dog is hoping the girl will share or drop her ice cream.
