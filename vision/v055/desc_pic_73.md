# pic_73

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left and a wooden fence at the back. A boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts holds a yellow and a blue balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other hand towards a red balloon that is floating away above him; its string hangs loose in the air. He looks worried that he has lost it. To the right, a girl in a pink dress is jumping with both arms raised; her shadow is on the grass below her, so she is in mid-air. Oddly, an orange fish is sitting in the branches of the tree beside the house, which is impossible. There is a wooden bench at the front left, a grey wheelbarrow and a green watering can at the front, a flower bed, and a path leading to a gate on the right.
