## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a wooden gate on the right, and a paved path leading to that gate. There are no people or animals in the picture. A dark green garden hose runs along the front from the left to a small sprinkler head in the grass. The sprinkler is spraying three arcs of water to the right, and the water is landing on a cardboard box that sits on the lawn. The box has drips running down it and is sitting in a small puddle, so it is getting soaked. A round bed of colourful flowers is near the house, and two potted plants stand by the fence. The odd thing is that a cardboard box has been left out where the sprinkler is wetting it; whatever is inside may be getting ruined, while the flowers are not being watered.
