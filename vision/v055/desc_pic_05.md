# pic_05

## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, trees, bushes, a winding sandy footpath on the left, a tall street lamp and a blue pond on the right with bulrushes at its edges. An elderly man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat stands at the edge of the pond holding a paper bag and tossing bits of bread to a white duck. The duck is swimming towards the crumbs and pecking at one of them, with ripple lines behind it. In the foreground a white goat with a red collar is tied by a rope to a small wooden stake and is bending down eating a tuft of grass next to the path. A girl in a pink dress walks across the grass at the front right, eating a pink ice-cream cone. There is a grey rock on the grass and a green litter bin in the bottom-right corner. A goat tethered in a town park is a little unusual, but nothing looks impossible.
