# pic_94

## Free description
A playground behind a dark railing fence, on a sunny day. On the left a seesaw is tipped steeply: a small girl in a pink outfit sits at the high end, stuck up in the air, holding the handle with her legs dangling. At the low end sits an elderly man with grey hair and a cap, wearing a white shirt and brown waistcoat, reading an open newspaper and not looking at her. Because he is so much heavier and is just sitting there, the girl cannot come down; she has one hand near her mouth and looks toward him as if calling out or feeling stuck. In the middle is a swing frame with a single empty swing. On the right a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is sliding down a red-and-yellow slide with one arm raised, apparently enjoying it. In the front right there is a sandpit with a red bucket and a blue spade but nobody playing in it. A green bin, a grey rock, a tree and a bush complete the scene.
