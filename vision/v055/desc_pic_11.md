# pic_11

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a paved path on the left and a cream house with a blue door on the right. In the centre a man in a green shirt and beige trousers holds the nozzle of a garden hose up near his face and is peering at it, because no water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel by the house, and a brown dog with a red collar is standing right on the hose, squashing it, which is why the water is blocked. It looks like when the dog steps off, the water will burst out into the man's face. On the left, a girl with dark hair in a yellow dress is in a sitting position drinking through a straw from a red cup, but there is no chair or seat under her, so she appears to sit on thin air. At the back, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. A flower bed sits in front of the house.
