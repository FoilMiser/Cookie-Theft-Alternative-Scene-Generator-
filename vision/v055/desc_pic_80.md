## Free description
A back garden on a sunny day, with a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate on the left, a paved path, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the centre, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is jumping in the air with both arms raised, looking delighted; his shadow is on the grass below him. Behind him is a washing line between two posts with a yellow-and-red striped shirt and blue jeans pegged on it. A grey cat walks along the top of the fence on the left. Odd: an orange fish appears in the tree behind the fence on the right, as if sitting in the branches, which is impossible/out of place. There are two potted plants, a teal watering can on the grass and a flower bed near the house.
