# Picture 92

## Free description
A sunny town square with colourful shops, a fruit stall on the left, a street lamp, a tree in a round planter and a green bin. In the front left a woman in a yellow dress holds a camera up to her eye, taking a photo towards the right where the children are. In the middle a child with dark curly hair, yellow top and orange shorts has just thrown a red ball (a dotted line shows its path upward). On the right a small girl in a pink dress holds her hands out, ready to catch it. The children seem to be playing catch while the woman photographs them. Nothing looks impossible.
