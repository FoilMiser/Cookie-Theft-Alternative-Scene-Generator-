## Free description
A sunny day in a park with green hills, trees, a pond with bulrushes, a lamp post, a green litter bin and a winding sandy path on the right. In the front centre-left a child with short dark curly hair, a yellow top and orange shorts is flying a yellow diamond-shaped kite. The child holds a small spool up in one hand, and the kite string runs from it to the kite high in the sky. The kite has a tail with two small bows. Next to the child is a round bed of colourful flowers. A dark blue garden hose runs along the ground across the front of the picture to a small sprinkler. The sprinkler sprays arcs of water to the right, and they land on a cardboard box sitting on the grass. The box is standing in a small puddle and water is dripping off it, so it is getting soaked. On the far right, at the edge of the path, is a grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles.
