# pic_63

## Free description
A sunny town street with colourful shops along the back, a fruit stall, a lamp post and a tree. On the left a small girl in a yellow top is crying and holding an empty cone, while a brown dog licks up her pink ice cream that has fallen on the ground. In the front middle a man in a green shirt is sitting and reading a red book, but there is no chair or bench under him; he is sitting on nothing. Behind him a woman in a purple top sits on a bench reading a red book, and a blue bird is pecking at a sandwich lying on the bench beside her. An older man in a cap and waistcoat is scattering seed from a bag for a red bird on the ground. An older woman with a yellow shopping bag is walking along the back. A blue-and-white beach ball is stuck in the tree and a girl in a pink dress is pointing up at it. On the right a boy in a red cap is sweeping leaves, and at the bottom left a red bicycle has square wheels, which is impossible. There is also a café table with two chairs and a grey bin.
