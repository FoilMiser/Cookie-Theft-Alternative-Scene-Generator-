# pic_68

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house on the left, a wooden fence, and a gate and path on the right. A clothes line runs between two wooden posts in the middle, with a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes pegged on it. Under the line, an elderly man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is raking leaves into a small pile. At the front, a garden hose runs in from the left to a sprinkler that sprays arcs of water. The water lands right on a cardboard box sitting in a puddle, so the box is getting soaked. Beside the box is a grey wheelbarrow, and its wheel is a black square with spokes instead of a round wheel, which is impossible. Two potted green plants stand on the grass at the right, and there is a flower bed at the front left.
