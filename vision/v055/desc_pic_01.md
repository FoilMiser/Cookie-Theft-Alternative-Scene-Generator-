## Free description
A sunny shopping street with a row of four colourful two-storey shops with striped awnings behind a wide paved square. In the middle a small girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench eating a pink ice-cream cone. A white puppy with brown spots and a blue collar sits up on its hind legs in front of the bench, looking up at her as if begging for the ice cream. In the foreground an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses and a teal top walks to the right carrying a yellow shopping bag. On the right an elderly man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat sweeps a pile of brown leaves with a broom. Behind him is a market stall with crates of red, orange, yellow and green fruit under a striped canopy. A green bicycle is parked in a bike rack, and there is a tree, a street lamp, flower boxes, a bin, a café table with two chairs, a wooden crate and a potted plant. Nothing looks clearly impossible; the main "story" seems to be the dog hoping to get some of the girl's ice cream.
