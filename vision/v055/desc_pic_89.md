# Picture 89

## Free description
A sunny town street with colourful shops, a fruit stall, a street lamp, flower boxes, a bench, a bike rack and a tree. On the left a woman in a purple top sweeps a pile of leaves with a yellow broom. Next to her a man in a red T-shirt is pushing dust or dirt towards a red dustpan, but he is using a huge silver fork, which is strange — it looks like an oversized dinner fork rather than a broom or garden tool. In the middle an older woman with grey hair and glasses walks a white dog with brown spots on a red leash. A small girl in pink sits on the bench drinking from a cup. On the right a beach ball is stuck in the branches of the tree, and a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at it, looking worried.
