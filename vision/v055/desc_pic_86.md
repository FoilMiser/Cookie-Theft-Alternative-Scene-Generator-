# Picture 86

## Free description
A sunny back garden next to a cream house with a red roof and blue door, closed in by a wooden fence with a gate on the right and a paved path leading to it. Near the house a brown dog sits by the door holding a yellow leash in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. A man in a red shirt and blue trousers stands by a round flower bed, tipping a green watering can over the flowers. Oddly, a long thin blue stream or hose rises from the can's spout in a high arc back up over the flower bed, with drops at its top, instead of pouring down onto the flowers. In the front, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses and a green cardigan is digging with a spade next to a pile of soil. To the right there is an empty grey wheelbarrow, a swing set with a red seat, and a birdhouse on a tall pole by the gate.
