## Free description
A back garden on a sunny day with a cream house (red roof, blue door) on the left, a wooden fence and gate across the back, and a paved path on the right. In the centre-back, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is pegging washing on a line: jeans, a yellow-and-red striped shirt and a white T-shirt that is dripping water; a laundry basket of clothes sits at his feet. On the left, a woman in a yellow dress is watering the flower bed with a teal watering can. Odd: the water coming out of the can arcs upward away from the flowers, rising into the air with drops at the top, instead of pouring down onto the plants. On the right, a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts stands on the path flying a yellow diamond-shaped kite high in the sky. There is also a wheelbarrow, two potted plants and a bench.
