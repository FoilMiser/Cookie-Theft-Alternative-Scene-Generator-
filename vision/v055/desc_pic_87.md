# Picture 87

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a gate with a paved path on the right. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and black trousers is watering a round bed of colourful flowers with a green watering can; the water is pouring onto the flowers. On the right, near the gate, a girl or young woman in a purple top and teal trousers is raking a small pile of dead leaves. Strangely, a closed cardboard box is up in the sky above the fence, tilted, with nothing holding it up. Nobody seems to be looking at the box. Otherwise it is a calm garden-chores scene.

- Right, near the gate: girl/woman with dark hair, purple shirt, teal trousers, raking leaves.
- Watering can (green): pouring water onto the flower bed.
- Flower bed with multicoloured flowers: being watered.
- Rake and small pile of brown leaves: being raked together.
- Cardboard box: in mid-air in the sky above the fence, tilted, apparently floating or falling.
- House with blue door, tree and bush on the left; gate and path on the right.
