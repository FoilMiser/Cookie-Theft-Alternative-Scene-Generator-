## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a blue door on the right. At the back right, an older man with grey hair, a grey cap, a white shirt and brown waistcoat is painting the fence blue with a paint roller; a few panels near the house are already blue. On the ground near his feet are a paint tray with blue paint and a paint tin. A white dog with brown spots is walking away to the left in the middle of the lawn, and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray to the dog, so it has stepped in the paint and is tracking it across the grass. The painter has not noticed. On the left, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is sweeping a pile of brown leaves with a broom next to the path. In the front are a teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow; two potted plants stand near the fence and a flower bed is by the house.
