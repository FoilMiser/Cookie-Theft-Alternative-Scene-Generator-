# pic_40

## Free description
A sunny, peaceful park with green hills, trees, a winding sandy path, a lamp post and a pond. On the left, a woman with dark hair in a yellow dress sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. On the right, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands at the edge of the pond holding a brown paper bag. He is holding out a piece of bread towards a white duck. The duck is swimming close to him and eating pieces of bread floating on the water. A grey rock sits at the bottom centre, a flower bed at the bottom right and a green rubbish bin in the far bottom-right corner. Nothing seems wrong. It is a calm scene of reading and feeding the duck.
