# pic_25

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence along the back, a house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a paved path running from a wooden gate at the back left down to the front left corner. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands near the middle of the fence, painting it blue with a long-handled paint roller; a section of about three fence boards behind her is already blue. On the grass by her feet are a paint tin and a paint tray with blue paint in it. A small white dog with brown spots is walking away to the left towards the path, and it has left a trail of blue paw prints leading back from the paint tray, so it seems to have stepped in the paint. The dog's paws also look blue. The woman does not seem to have noticed. Around the garden there are two potted plants, a round flower bed with colourful flowers near the house, a green watering can and a grey wheelbarrow in the foreground.
