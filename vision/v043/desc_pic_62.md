# pic_62

## Free description
A sunny park with a winding path on the left, a tall lamp post, a pond with reeds on the right, trees, bushes and hills behind. An old woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, holds a paper bag and scatters seeds or crumbs onto the grass for two small birds, one blue and one red, which are pecking at them. Behind her, in front of the pond, a man in a red T-shirt and blue trousers holds a camera to his eye and is taking a photo, pointing toward the left (towards the old woman / the path). Next to the path a wooden ladder stands upright on the grass, leaning slightly but not resting against anything - nothing is holding it up, which looks impossible. A green rubbish bin stands in the bottom right corner.
