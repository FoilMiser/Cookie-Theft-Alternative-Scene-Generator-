This is a back garden under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds overhead, so it looks like rain is coming. A house with a blue door is on the left, a wooden fence runs along the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path runs down the right side. An old woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, kneels by the flower bed next to the house, gardening; there is a small pot with a pink flower at the edge of the bed. In the middle front, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is walking while reading an open book, looking down at the book instead of where he is going. A cardboard box sits on the grass right in front of him, so it looks like he is about to trip over it. On the path to the right, a smaller boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is pointing up at the sky, apparently at the dark clouds, and looks surprised. Nobody else seems to have noticed the weather. A teal watering can is on the grass near the path, and two potted plants stand by the fence.
