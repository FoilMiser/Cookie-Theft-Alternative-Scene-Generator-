# Picture 91

## Free description
A busy, sunny town square in front of a row of colourful shops with striped awnings. On the front left a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers walks to the right holding a red lead, attached to a white dog with brown spots that trots ahead of him. In the background a street sweeper in an orange cap and orange high-vis vest sweeps a pile of brown leaves with a yellow broom by the lamp post. In the centre, a woman in a yellow dress is doing something odd: she is "sweeping" with an enormous silver fork. She holds it in both hands with the prongs on the ground and pushes a little pile of dirt toward a red dustpan, raising a small dust cloud. Behind her a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a bench eating a pink ice cream. A small tan dog with a red collar sits up on its hind legs in front of her, begging for the ice cream. On the right an older woman with grey hair in a bun, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, holds a camera to her eye and is taking a photo toward the left of the scene. On the far right a small girl in a pink dress points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of a tree. The giant fork being used as a broom is clearly wrong or impossible, and the ball looks stuck in the tree and may need getting down.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The giant fork: either it is meant as a joke or an "impossible" object, or it is meant as a broom or rake and is drawn wrongly. Either way it is not a normal sweeping tool, and it is many times the size of a real fork.
- It is not clear what the older woman is photographing. She faces left, toward the bench, the begging dog and the fork woman, and away from the girl and the ball in the tree.
- The small tan dog could belong to the woman on the bench or be a stray; it has no lead.
- The ball could have been thrown into the tree by the girl, or just be stuck there; she seems to want it back.
- The sweeper and the fork woman are both sweeping at once; it is unclear whether they are connected.
