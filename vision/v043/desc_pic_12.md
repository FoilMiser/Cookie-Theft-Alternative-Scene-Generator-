## Free description
A sunny playground with a metal railing fence at the back, a tree on the left and a slide with a ladder on the right. On the left is a red seesaw on a grey triangular stand. A small girl in a pink dress sits on the high end, up in the air, holding the handle, with her feet dangling and her mouth open as if surprised or worried. An old man with grey hair, a beard, a flat cap, a white shirt and a brown waistcoat sits on the low end of the seesaw, which is down on the ground, and he is reading an open book rather than paying attention to the girl. Because he is heavier and not pushing off, the girl seems stuck up in the air. In the middle-right a man with dark skin in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers stands eating a pink ice-cream cone, facing toward the seesaw. The slide on the right is empty. There is a grey rock at the bottom right and a bush near the slide.
