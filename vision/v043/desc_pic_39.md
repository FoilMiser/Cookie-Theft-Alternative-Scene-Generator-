# Picture 39

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house, red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a gate with a paved path on the right. There are no people or animals anywhere in the picture. The striking thing is a closed cardboard box hanging in the sky above the fence, just right of the tree, tilted at an angle as if tumbling. Nobody is around who could have thrown it, and there are no wind lines or other signs of what is holding it up or moving it. It looks as if it is floating or falling out of the sky for no reason. Otherwise the garden is calm: a flowerbed, two potted plants by the fence, a couple of bushes, and a teal watering can on the grass near the path.
