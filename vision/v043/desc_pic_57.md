# pic_57

## Free description
A sunny back garden behind a wooden fence, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right and a paved path running down the left side. Several people are busy with garden jobs. On the left a man with a moustache walks down the path carrying a cardboard box, and a woman in a yellow dress digs with a spade next to a pile of soil. In the middle a woman stands with her hands on her hips with a white bucket upside down on her head, which looks silly. A man in a green shirt is holding a garden hose and looking at the nozzle, but only a single drop is coming out, because a small white-and-brown dog is standing on the hose further along and the hose behind the dog looks swollen. Near the fence on the right a boy in a red cap points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of a tree. In the front right an old man on a bench reads a red book while a blue bird on the bench next to him is right at his sandwich, about to steal it. A wheelbarrow in the front has a square, grid-like thing where its round wheel should be, which looks wrong. Washing (a striped top and blue jeans) hangs on a line, a birdhouse sits on a pole, and a red bird flies near the roof.
