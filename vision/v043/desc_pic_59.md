# pic_59

## Free description
A sunny town square with a row of coloured shops with striped awnings, a lamp post, a tree in a round planter, and a paved grey pavement. On the left a beach ball is stuck in the tree, and a boy in a yellow T-shirt points up at it while a small girl in a pink dress beside the tree throws both arms in the air, looking happy. A man in a red T-shirt walks past carrying a yellow shopping bag, and behind him a boy in a red cap sweeps a pile of leaves with a broom. In the middle a woman sits on a bench reading a red book, and a blue bird is at the sandwich lying on the bench next to her, about to take it. A worker in an orange cap and orange safety vest waters a window box of flowers with a watering can, but the water shoots up and away in an arc above the box instead of falling onto the flowers, which looks impossible. On the right an old man with a cap walks while reading an open book, heading straight towards a cardboard box on the ground that he is about to trip over. At the bottom a brown dog lies on the ground with its paws on an open newspaper as if reading it. There is also a fruit stall, a café table with two chairs, a potted plant, and a red bird flying in the sky.
