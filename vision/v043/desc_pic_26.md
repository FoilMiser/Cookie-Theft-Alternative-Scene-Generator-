A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the middle of the garden a woman in a yellow dress stands between two wooden posts, reaching up to peg washing onto a clothesline. A white T-shirt and a pair of blue jeans already hang on the line, both dripping water. Below her on the grass is a wooden laundry basket full of pink, white and blue clothes. In the front left, an old man in a cap and brown vest sweeps a small pile of dry leaves on the paved path with a broom. A teal watering can sits on the grass near him, and two potted plants and a birdhouse on a tall pole stand near the fence. On the right there is a flower bed, a coiled garden hose on a reel with its nozzle lying on the grass, and a wooden bench next to the house. Nothing looks wrong or impossible; it is an ordinary chores scene.
