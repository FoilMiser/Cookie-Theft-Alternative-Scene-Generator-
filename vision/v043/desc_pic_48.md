# pic_48

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house on the left, a wooden fence across the back, a gate and a paved path on the right, and a bird house on a tall pole. A man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is painting the fence blue with a roller on a long handle. A section of fence to his right is already blue, and a paint tin and a paint tray of blue paint are on the grass at his feet. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has blue paws. A trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the lawn to the dog, so it has walked through the paint and is now heading left toward the flower bed. At the bottom left, a girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes sits on a wooden bench reading a red book, not noticing anything. Something impossible: a closed cardboard box is floating in the sky near the sun at the top right, tilted, with nothing holding it up. A grey wheelbarrow stands empty at the front.
