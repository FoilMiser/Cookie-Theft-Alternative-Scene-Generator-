# pic_42

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back and a paved path on the right leading to a wooden gate. A small tan dog with a red collar sits by the blue back door. A red lead is clipped to its collar and lies on the ground, as if the dog is waiting to go for a walk. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands at the flower bed holding a teal watering can. Oddly, the water does not fall onto the flowers: it arcs upward from the spout into the air, and drops fly off the top. In the front centre, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers walks to the left while reading an open book. He is not looking where he is going, and a cardboard box sits on the grass right in front of his feet, so he looks about to trip over it. Behind him are a grey wheelbarrow and a swing set with an empty red seat.
