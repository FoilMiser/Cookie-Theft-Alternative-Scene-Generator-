## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house (red roof, blue door) on the left and a tall wooden picket fence running across the back, with a wooden gate on the right at the end of a paved path. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands at the fence with a long-handled paint roller and is painting the fence blue; one section of the fence is already blue, and a paint tin with blue paint sits by his feet. A grey cat is crouched on top of the freshly painted blue section, head lowered, creeping toward a red bird perched on the fence to its left; a blue bird sits further along the fence on the right. In the front, a bearded man in a red shirt and blue jeans is walking while reading an open book, and a closed cardboard box is on the grass right in front of him, so he looks about to trip over it. A small white-and-brown puppy is curled up asleep on the grass at the lower left. On the path at the right, a brown dog with a red collar is lying on an open newspaper with its front paws on it. There is a flower bed of colourful flowers beside the house, a bush, two potted plants, a tree behind the house, the sun and two clouds.
