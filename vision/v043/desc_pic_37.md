# Picture 37

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a park with green hills, trees, a pond on the left and a winding sandy path on the right. A man in a green shirt and beige trousers stands near a black lamp post at the right edge of the pond. His eyes are closed and he is eating a pink ice-cream cone, looking like he is enjoying it. Below him, a child with braided hair, a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on a wooden bench, also eating a pink ice-cream cone. A small white dog with brown patches and a blue collar sits up on its back legs next to the bench, front paws raised. It is looking up towards the child, as if begging for some of the ice cream. A flowerbed and a green rubbish bin are at the front left. Nothing seems to be going wrong yet, but the dog may be hoping to get some ice cream.
