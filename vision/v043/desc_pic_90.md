# Picture 90

## Free description
A sunny playground surrounded by a dark metal railing fence with hedges behind it. On the left two children sit at opposite ends of a red seesaw on a grey triangular stand. A boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts sits on the left end; his feet are on the ground, and that end is slightly lower. A girl in a pink dress sits on the right end, slightly higher, with her feet dangling. Each holds a handle, and they face each other. The seesaw is almost level, as if mid-ride or balanced. In the centre an old man with grey hair and a moustache stands licking a pink ice-cream cone with his eyes closed, looking contented. He wears a grey flat cap, a white shirt, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers. On the right are an empty red-and-yellow slide with a grey ladder, and a sandpit at the front with a red bucket and blue spade but nobody playing in it. There are also a green litter bin, a tree on the left, a bush, a grey rock, the sun at top right and two clouds. Nothing looks impossible.
