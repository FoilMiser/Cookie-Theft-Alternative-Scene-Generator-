# Picture 24

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the right, and a gate and paved path are on the left, with a wooden fence along the back. An old woman with grey hair and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan, a grey skirt and brown shoes, stands in the middle of the lawn with her hands on her hips. She is wearing a metal bucket upside down on her head like a hat, which is silly and out of place. She looks calm or slightly pleased, as if nothing is unusual. To her left is an empty swing set with one red seat, and behind it a birdhouse stands on a tall pole. A teal watering can and an empty wheelbarrow are at the front, and a flower bed with colourful flowers and a bush are next to the house. Nobody else is in the picture.
