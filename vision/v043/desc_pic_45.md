# pic_45

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house on the right, a wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate at the far left, a bird house on a tall pole and a paved path at the front-left. At the front left, a boy in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands on the path holding a giant silver fork, like a dinner fork but taller than half his body. He is using it like a broom to sweep a little pile of dirt toward a red dustpan, and a grey dust cloud puffs up. Using an enormous fork as a broom is clearly odd. In the middle, an older woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. At the front, a lawn sprinkler connected to a long hose is spraying arcs of water to the left. The water lands on a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is getting soaked: drips run down it and a puddle is forming underneath. A flower bed, two potted plants and some bushes complete the garden.
