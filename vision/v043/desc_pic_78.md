## Free description
It is a calm, sunny day in a back garden. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door. A tree stands behind the house, a wooden fence runs along the back with a wooden gate on the right, and a paved path leads from the front right back to the gate. A washing line between two posts holds a pair of blue trousers and a yellow-and-red striped towel hanging straight down, so there is no wind. In the centre, a white goat with small curved horns and a red collar stands with its head down eating a tuft of grass. A thin rope runs from its collar to a small wooden stake in the ground, so it is tied up. On the right, at the edge of the path, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and a grey skirt, is sweeping a pile of dry brown leaves with a yellow broom. Two potted green plants stand on the grass near the fence. A garden hose on a reel with a yellow nozzle lies at the front left, near a flower bed by the house. A grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can sit in the front. A goat in a back garden is a little unusual, but it is tethered and calmly grazing, and nothing looks impossible.
