## Free description
This is a busy, sunny back garden with a house on the right, a wooden fence along the back with a gate on the left, a path down the left side, and an empty swing set. A red bird flies in the sky at the top left, and an orange cat walks along the top of the fence. A blue-and-white beach ball is stuck up in the branches of the tree behind the house. In the middle, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a purple and a yellow balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other toward a red balloon that has floated away above him; he looks upset. To the right, a girl in a pink dress points up toward the tree, the ball or the cat. Next to her a white goat with a red collar is eating grass, with a rope trailing from its collar to a small peg in the ground. At the front, a spotted white dog is crouched on the path with a tennis ball, tail wagging as if it wants to play. A brown dog with a red collar is lying on an open newspaper and pawing at it, apparently tearing it up. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sits on the bench reading a red book while a small blue bird pecks at the sandwich beside him, and he hasn't noticed. Oddly, the wheelbarrow at the front has a square wheel.
