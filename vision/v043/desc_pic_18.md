# Picture 18

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden. A cream house with a red roof, a blue door and a window is on the left, and a wooden fence with a gate runs along the back. A boy with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands in the middle holding a green garden hose. He holds the hose nozzle up near his face and looks into it, and a single drop of water is coming out. The hose runs back across the grass to a hose reel near the bench. A white dog with brown patches and a blue collar is standing on the hose between the reel and the boy, apparently squashing it so the water is blocked. It looks as if the boy is about to get sprayed in the face when the dog steps off. On the right, an old woman with grey hair and glasses is sweeping a pile of brown leaves on the paved path with a broom. There is an empty swing set with one red seat, a birdhouse on a tall pole, a wheelbarrow, a flower bed full of colourful flowers and a wooden bench.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The dark teal patch under the dog's feet could be a flattened or kinked part of the hose, or a puddle of water. Either way, the dog is standing on the hose.
- The boy could be looking into the nozzle because no water is coming out, or he could be about to drink from it. It reads most strongly as "checking a blocked hose".
- The water drop is very small and easy to miss.
- The boy's face is close to the nozzle and partly hidden, so his expression is hard to read.
