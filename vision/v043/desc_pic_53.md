## Free description
A sunny back garden. The house (red roof, blue door) is on the left, a gate and paved path are on the right, a clothesline stands in the middle, and a wooden bench sits at the front left. An elderly man with grey hair, in a blue-grey flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers, stands at the front centre-right holding a garden hose with a yellow nozzle pointed forward. Only a single drop of water is coming out of it. The hose runs from a hose reel next to the flower bed, across the grass and right under a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar. The dog is standing on the hose, eyes closed and tail up, looking pleased with itself. Its weight on the hose seems to be blocking the water, which would explain why the man's hose is only dribbling. At the back right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is raking a small pile of leaves near the fence. A blue bird is perched on top of the fence. The clothesline holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes.
