# pic_46

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house on the right, a wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate at the far left, a bird house on a pole and a paved path at the front-left. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is painting the fence blue with a roller on a long handle. A section of fence to her right is already blue, and a paint tin and a paint tray of blue paint sit on the grass at her feet. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has clearly stepped in the paint tray. Its paws are blue, and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the tray across the lawn to where the dog is now trotting, toward the path, looking pleased with itself. On the right, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is watering the flower bed with a hose, and water sprays onto the flowers. Something impossible: an orange goldfish is sitting up in the tree behind the house, on a branch, as if it were a bird. A grey wheelbarrow stands empty at the front.
