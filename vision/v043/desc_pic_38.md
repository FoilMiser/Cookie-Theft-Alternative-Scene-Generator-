# Picture 38

## Free description
This park scene is under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds, so it looks as if it might rain soon. In the front centre, a child with braided hair, a yellow shirt and orange shorts is crying with a hand over their eyes. They are holding an empty ice-cream cone. Their pink ice cream, with coloured sprinkles, has fallen and splatted on the grass, and a brown dog with a red collar is licking it up. On the left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is scattering seeds from a paper bag onto the path, where a blue bird and a red bird are pecking at them. Further back on the grass, a girl in a pink dress is pointing up at the sky, perhaps at the dark clouds. Near the lamp post, a white dog with brown patches crouches in a playful pose beside a yellow tennis ball and wags its tail. On the right, an older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, stands at the edge of the pond throwing bits of bread from a paper bag. A white duck is swimming over to eat them.
