# pic_81

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a wooden fence along the back, a house with a blue door on the right, and a paved path running down the left side from a wooden gate in the fence. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands in the middle of the garden painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle. She has already painted one section of the fence blue, just to her right. A paint can and a paint tray with blue paint are on the grass behind her. A small white dog with brown spots has walked through the paint: its feet are blue and a trail of blue paw prints runs across the grass from the paint tray to where the dog is now, walking away to the left. The woman is looking at the fence and does not seem to have noticed the dog. In the front left, an old man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat is sweeping a small pile of leaves on the path with a yellow broom. A watering can and an empty wheelbarrow are on the grass at the front, and there is a flower bed and a wooden bench on the right near the house.
