# Picture 89

## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, trees and a curving sandy path that runs from the back down to the front left. In the centre a white goat with dark curved horns and a red collar has its head down and is nibbling a small tuft of grass. A tan rope runs from the goat's collar to a small wooden stake in the ground, so it is tied up. Near the front right a girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes, with dark hair in a bun, is walking while eating a pink ice-cream cone held up to her mouth. Her face is turned to the left, toward the goat, so she may be walking toward it. Behind them are an empty wooden bench beside the path, a green litter bin, a tall black lamp post and a blue pond with reeds at both ends. On the right front is a round bed of colourful flowers, and in front of the goat is a grey rock. No adult or owner is visible with either the girl or the goat. Nothing looks impossible; it is a calm scene.
