# pic_69

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and path on the left and a cream house with a blue door on the right. In the middle a man with dark skin, in a green shirt and khaki trousers, holds a garden hose and looks closely into its nozzle, which is only dripping a single drop of water. The hose runs across the lawn to a hose reel by the flower bed. A brown dog with a red collar is standing with its back paws on the hose, and on the reel side of the dog the hose is swollen, as if the water is blocked there. It looks as though the water will burst out into the man's face as soon as the dog moves. Behind him a washing line between two posts holds a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans. On the left there is a birdhouse on a tall pole and two potted plants; a grey wheelbarrow is at the front and an empty wooden bench is at the front right. There are flowers by the house, a tree, bushes, the sun and two clouds.
