# Picture 94

## Free description
A sunny playground with a dark metal railing fence and hedges at the back. On the left is a red seesaw on a grey triangular stand, steeply tilted. A large man in a green shirt and khaki trousers sits on the low right end, which is down on the ground. His knees are up and he is reading an open book held in front of him, looking down at it. At the high left end, up in the air, sits a small girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes, holding the handle with her feet dangling. Her mouth is open in an "o", as if surprised, worried or calling out. Because the man is so much heavier, she looks stuck up in the air while he ignores her and reads. In the background a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts stands near the fence licking a pink ice-cream cone with his eyes closed, not paying attention. On the right is an empty red-and-yellow slide with a grey ladder, plus a green litter bin, a bush, a grey rock, a tree on the left, the sun at top right and two clouds. The odd thing is a grown man sitting on the seesaw reading, leaving the child stranded at the top.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl's open mouth could be read as fear or distress at being stuck in the air, or as surprise or excitement. Either way she looks unable to get down while the man stays seated.
- The man could be her father sitting there unaware or not bothering, or a stranger who has sat on the seesaw to read. There is no sign he notices her.
- The man sits sideways near the end of the plank with his shins in front of it and his feet on the ground. It looks a little as if the plank passes behind or through his legs, though it reads as him perched on its edge.
- The boy with the ice cream has his eyes closed and does not seem to notice the seesaw.
