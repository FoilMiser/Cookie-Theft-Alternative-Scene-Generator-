# pic_44

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house on the left, a wooden fence across the back, a gate and a paved path on the right, and a washing line strung between two posts in the middle. An older man with grey hair, a blue-grey cap, a white shirt, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers holds a garden hose. He has raised the nozzle to his face and is peering into it, and only a single drop is coming out. The hose runs back across the lawn to a reel near the house. A brown dog with a red collar is standing right on the hose, and the hose bulges fat just behind the dog's feet. So the dog is blocking the water, and the man is looking into the nozzle to see why. It looks like he is about to get sprayed in the face when the dog steps off. On the right, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt is sweeping a pile of brown leaves on the path with a yellow broom. Blue jeans and a yellow striped towel hang on the washing line, and two potted plants stand by the fence.
