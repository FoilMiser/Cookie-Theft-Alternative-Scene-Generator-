# Picture 34

## Free description
This is the same kind of back garden: a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a wooden fence with a gate on the right, and a paved path at the front right. The day is sunny, but white wavy lines in the sky and in front of the fence show that it is windy. An older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt stands in the middle of the lawn. She is reaching up with one arm and has her mouth open, looking surprised. Just above and to the left of her hand, a blue hat is being blown through the air, along with a couple of small leaves. The wind lines next to the hat suggest the wind has just blown it off her head and it is getting away from her. She seems to be trying to grab it but it is just out of reach. There is nobody else in the garden. An empty wheelbarrow and a teal watering can are on the grass at the front, and two potted plants stand near the fence.
