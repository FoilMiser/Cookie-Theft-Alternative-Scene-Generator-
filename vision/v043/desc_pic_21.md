# Picture 21

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the right, and a gate and paved path are on the left, with a wooden fence along the back. On the swing set at the left, a child with curly dark hair, a yellow top and orange shorts is swinging and laughing with eyes closed. Motion lines show the swing moving. In the middle, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie has just thrown a red ball. A dotted line shows it arcing towards a man with a moustache, a red shirt and blue jeans, who has his hands out ready to catch it. In the front, a girl in a pink dress holds a red and a purple balloon by their strings in one hand. With her other hand she reaches up towards a blue balloon that has floated free, because its string is no longer in her hand. It looks as if the blue balloon is getting away. At the front right an orange cat is curled up asleep near the flower bed, and a teal watering can stands on the grass at the front left.
