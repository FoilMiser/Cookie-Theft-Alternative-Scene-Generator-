# Picture 19

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, and a wooden fence with a gate at the back right. A washing line strung between two wooden posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel or top, pegged up to dry. On the paved path at the right, a woman or girl with dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes is sweeping. She is using a giant silver fork as if it were a broom, which is strange. She is pushing a small pile of dirt, with a little cloud of dust, towards a red dustpan lying on the path. Two potted plants stand by the fence, there is an empty wheelbarrow in the front middle, and a flower bed full of colourful flowers sits in front of the house. Nobody else is in the picture.
