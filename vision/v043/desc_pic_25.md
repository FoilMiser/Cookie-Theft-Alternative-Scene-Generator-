# pic_25

## Free description
A sunny day in a park with a pond, a lamp post, a bench and a winding path. In the front left, an old man in a cap and a brown vest is watering a flower bed, but he is pouring the water out of a frying pan instead of a watering can. On the pond a white duck swims with motion lines behind it toward pieces of bread floating on the water. A girl in a pink dress stands at the pond's edge holding a paper bag and seems to have thrown the bread. A white goat on a rope tied to a stake is eating a tuft of grass near the pond. In the centre front, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is crying and holds an empty ice-cream cone; the ice cream has fallen on the ground and a brown dog with a red collar is licking it up. Behind the bench, a white puppy with brown spots lies on an open newspaper. On the path, an elderly woman with glasses and a grey bun sweeps a small pile of leaves with a broom. On the far right, a boy in a red cap and blue top holds a kite string, and his purple kite is caught in the tree; he looks up at it with a startled face. A blue bird flies across the sky.
