# Picture 92

## Free description
A sunny back garden. A cream house with a red roof, a blue door and one window is on the left, a tall wooden fence with a gate is at the back right, and a paved path leads up to the gate. In the middle of the garden a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is hanging washing on a line strung between two wooden posts. He reaches up with both hands to peg a white T-shirt onto the line. A pair of blue jeans already hangs from the line on the left, and both the jeans and the T-shirt are dripping water. A wicker laundry basket on the grass beside him holds more clothes (blue, white and pink). On the right stands an empty grey A-frame swing set with a red seat. A flower bed of colourful flowers sits next to the house, and at the front of the garden are an empty grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can. Nothing looks impossible; it is an ordinary washing day.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The man is drawn quite small and stands close to the fence, between the posts, so at first glance he could be read as being behind the line rather than in front of it. He is clearly pegging the T-shirt.
- The drips could be read as water dripping from freshly washed clothes, or as small decorative marks. They look like water drops.
- Nobody is using the swing, wheelbarrow or watering can; they seem to be just garden things.
- Nothing looks unintentionally strange.
