## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. Just right of centre a small girl with dark hair, in a pink dress and yellow shoes, stands on the lawn looking up and holding a string with both hands raised. The string runs up across the sky to a yellow diamond-shaped kite flying high on the upper left, with a curly tail with two little bows. So she is flying a kite. Around the garden there are two potted plants, a couple of bushes, a flower bed by the house, a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow at the front. A tree stands behind the house and the sun and two clouds are in the sky. Nothing looks wrong or about to happen; it is a calm scene.
