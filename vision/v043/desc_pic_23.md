# Picture 23

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back and a gate and paved path on the right. An old man with grey hair, a moustache, a flat cap, a brown waistcoat over a white shirt and dark trousers is kneeling at the flower bed, tending or planting flowers. A small pot of pink flowers sits at the edge of the bed in front of him. In front of him, a man with brown hair and a moustache, in a red shirt and blue jeans, is digging a hole in the lawn with a spade. He is lifting a clump of soil, and a pile of dug earth lies beside him. He looks like he is straining or frowning with effort. Something impossible is happening in the tree behind the house: an orange fish is sitting on a branch, as if it were a bird. There is also an empty wheelbarrow, a teal watering can, two potted plants and a bush by the fence.
