A purple diamond-shaped kite with bow tails is caught in the leaves of the tree on the left. A woman in a yellow dress sits on a wooden bench near the tree, drinking from a red cup with a straw. In the middle, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie has just thrown a red ball. A dotted line shows its arc rising through the air towards a man with a moustache, in a red shirt and blue jeans, who holds his hands out in front of him ready to catch it. In the bottom right, a small girl in a pink dress crouches in a sandpit beside a sandcastle with three towers and a little red flag, holding a small tool. A grey rock sits at bottom right and a bush is near the slide. The sun is at top right with two clouds.
