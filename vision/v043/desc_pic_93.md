# Picture 93

## Free description
A sunny back garden. A cream house with a red roof, a blue door and one window is on the left, a tall wooden fence runs along the back with a wooden gate at the right, and a paved path leads up to the gate. In the middle of the lawn a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes, with a long dark ponytail, walks to the right carrying a closed cardboard box in both arms. The box has a strip of white tape or a label on top. She seems to be heading across the garden toward the right, perhaps toward the path and gate, or toward two potted plants. Those two terracotta pots of young green plants stand on the grass near the fence to the right of her. A teal watering can sits on the grass at the front right, and a round bed of colourful flowers is next to the house. No one else is in the garden. Nothing looks impossible; it is a quiet scene.
