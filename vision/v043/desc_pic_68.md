## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and path on the left, and a cream house with a blue door on the right. At the back left an older man with grey hair, a flat cap and a moustache, wearing a white shirt and brown waistcoat, is sweeping a pile of dead leaves with a yellow broom. In the middle a woman with a dark ponytail, in a purple top and teal trousers, is holding a garden hose and looking closely into the nozzle, which is only letting out a single drop of water. The hose runs across the lawn to a hose reel by the house. A brown dog with a red collar is standing with its back paws right on the hose; on the reel side of the dog the hose looks swollen, as if water is blocked there. It looks like that when the dog steps off, the water will suddenly spray into the woman's face. Behind them is a washing line between two posts with a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans hanging on it. There is a flower bed by the house and a tree and bushes in the background.
