# Picture 04

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden picket fence, a wooden gate at the far left, a paved path in the lower left and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. An orange cat is walking along the very top of the fence, with its tail up. In the foreground, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is kicking an orange ball towards the left. Little motion lines under his foot show the kick, and he is looking down at the ball. On the right, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is watering a flower bed. She is not using a watering can: she is pouring water from what looks like a frying pan or saucepan, and a stream of water with drops falls onto the flowers. That is an odd thing to water plants with. There is a bush in the middle, a tree behind the fence on the right, and the sun at top left with two clouds.
