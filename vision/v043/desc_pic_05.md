# Picture 05

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with no people in it. There is a wooden picket fence at the back, a wooden gate at the far left, a paved path in the lower left, and a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the right. Near the left, a small red-roofed birdhouse sits on top of a tall wooden pole, with two potted green plants in terracotta pots beside its base. In the tree behind the fence on the right, an orange goldfish is sitting on a branch among the leaves. That is impossible, since fish live in water, not in trees. In the front there is a teal watering can by the path and a grey wheelbarrow resting on the grass. On the right there is a round flower bed with colourful flowers and a wooden bench in front of the house. Two bushes and the sun at top left with two clouds complete the scene.
