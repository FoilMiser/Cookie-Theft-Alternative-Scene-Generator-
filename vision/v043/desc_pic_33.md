# Picture 33

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, and a tall wooden fence with a gate at the right runs across the back. An older man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat and white shirt stands by the fence, painting it blue with a roller on a long handle. A section of the fence boards is already blue. A paint bucket and a paint tray full of blue paint sit on the grass at his feet. A white dog with brown patches is walking away to the right along the paved path. A trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray to the dog, and the dog's paws are blue, so it looks like it stepped in the paint tray and is tracking paint across the garden. The man is facing the fence and does not seem to have noticed. In the middle, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is watering a bed of flowers with a hose that runs from a hose reel beside the house. At the front left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. An empty wheelbarrow stands on the grass at the front.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Whether the man has noticed the dog is unclear; he is looking at the fence, not at the dog.
- The hose rises from the ground up to the woman's hands in a fairly tight curve; it looks a little stiff but not impossible.
