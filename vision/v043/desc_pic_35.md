# Picture 35

## Free description
A sunny back garden, this time with the house on the right and a gate and paved path on the left. At the back by the fence, a small girl in a pink dress is flying a purple kite, but the kite is caught in the leafy tree beside the house. Its string sags in a long curve from her raised hand to the tree. In the middle, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers has both arms up in the air and a big open-mouthed grin. His shadow is a little below his feet, so he seems to be jumping, as if cheering. Beside him is a metal swing set with one red seat hanging empty and still. To the right, an older man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat and white shirt is watering a flowerbed with a teal watering can. At the front right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a bench reading a red book. An empty wheelbarrow lies on the grass at the front.

## Inventory
- Girl, centre-back by the fence: dark hair, pink dress, yellow shoes; holding the kite string up with a raised arm, looking up.
- Boy, centre: red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers, white shoes; both arms raised, mouth open in a grin, apparently jumping (his shadow sits below his feet).
- Older man, centre-right: grey hair, moustache, flat cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt, dark trousers; pouring water from a teal watering can onto the flowers.
- Woman, front right: dark hair, purple top, teal trousers; sitting on a bench reading a red book.
- Kite: purple diamond kite with small bows on its tail, caught in the tree at upper right; leaf clumps are drawn over it.
- Kite string: dark line running in a sagging curve from the girl's hand to the kite in the tree.
- Swing set: dark grey A-frame with one red seat hanging still, nobody on it, left-centre.
- Watering can: teal, held by the man, with water streaming onto the flowers.
- Flowerbed: oval bed of mixed coloured flowers in front of the house, right.
- Bench: wooden, front right, with the woman on it.
- Wheelbarrow: grey, empty, front left-centre, handles pointing left.
- House: on the right, blue door, red roof, one window; small bush beside it.
- Gate and path: wooden gate in the fence at the far left, paved path running to the front left.
- Background: tree behind the house, hedge behind the fence, two clouds, sun at top left.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The kite is clearly in the tree, but the girl does not look upset, so she may not have noticed yet, or she may be tugging to free it.
- The boy could be jumping for joy, cheering, or reacting to something (perhaps the kite); his face reads as happy rather than worried. Because of the gap between him and his shadow he could also look as if he is floating, though jumping is the obvious reading.
- Nothing else looks unintentionally strange.
