He holds a pink ice cream cone up to his mouth with his eyes closed, as if he is enjoying it. On the grass to the left of the bench, a small white dog with brown patches and a blue collar sits up on its hind legs with its front paws held out toward the bench, as if begging for the ice cream. - His closed eyes could mean contented eating, smiling, or squinting.
- The lamp post is cut off by the top edge, so only its pole and base show. It stands on the grass behind the bin and does not look like it is floating.
- His long lower legs come down in front of the bench's front edge with his shoes near the right front leg. This looks plausible, but his legs seem a bit long for his body.
