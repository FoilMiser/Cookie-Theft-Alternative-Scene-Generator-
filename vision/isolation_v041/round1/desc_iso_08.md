# iso_08

## What I see
A sunny park scene with a woman in the foreground raking leaves. She has dark hair in a ponytail and wears a purple top, teal trousers and dark shoes. She holds a long wooden rake with both hands and looks down at a small pile of brown leaves at the rake's head. Behind her is a blue pond with cattails/reeds at both ends. Around the park are a flower bed of red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers in brown soil, a green rubbish bin (bottom left), a grey rock, a wooden park bench, a winding sandy footpath, a tall black lamp post, round green trees and bushes, rolling green hills, two white clouds and a yellow sun.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- This cut-out is almost a whole scene, not a small crop, so there is no single focus apart from the woman raking.
- The woman's upper body overlaps the lower edge of the pond. Her feet are on the grass below the pond, so she reads as standing in front of it. At a glance, though, she could look as if she is standing at or in the water's edge.
- Her lower hand sits at hip level near the rake handle. It looks like it grips the handle, but it is close enough to her hip that it could also read as a hand resting on her waist.
- The rake head is small and grey and partly hidden by the leaves, so it is hard to see its tines clearly. It could be read as a rake or as a broom or leaf scoop.
- There is a small dark mark by her mouth. It could be a mouth, a shadow, or something she is holding.
- The lamp post is very tall and stands alone on the grass behind the pond, not beside the path. This isn't impossible, but it is slightly odd placement.
- Nothing appears to float or pass through anything else. The bench, bin, rock and flower bed all sit on the grass.
