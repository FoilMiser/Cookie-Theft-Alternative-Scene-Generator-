## What I see
A sunny autumn park: blue sky with two clouds and a sun, green hills, two trees with orange, yellow and green leaves, a round blue pond with cattails at each end, a tall black lamp post, a wooden bench, a winding sandy path, a grey rock, a few green bushes, a bed of colourful flowers in brown soil and a green trash bin in the bottom-left corner. In front of the pond a woman with dark hair in a ponytail, a purple T-shirt and teal trousers holds a long-handled rake with both hands. She is looking down and raking a small pile of brown leaves at her feet.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The tool's head is small and grey at the bottom of the handle. It reads most as a rake, but at this size it could also be taken for a broom or hoe.
- The brown bits at her feet look like fallen leaves, but they could also be read as twigs or general debris.
- The lamp post is very tall and thin, taller than the trees, and stands on its own behind and to the right of the pond. Its height looks out of scale with the rest of the scene.
- The woman stands just in front of the pond's near edge, so her upper body is drawn over the water. Her feet are clearly on the grass, so I read her as in front of the pond, not in it, but at a glance the overlap is noticeable.
- Her legs look long compared with her short torso, and the trousers are quite wide and straight. Nothing looks detached.
- The trees are autumn-coloured, but the grass, bushes and hills are fully summer-green. That is mildly inconsistent but not clearly an error.
- Nothing appears to float or pass through other objects.
