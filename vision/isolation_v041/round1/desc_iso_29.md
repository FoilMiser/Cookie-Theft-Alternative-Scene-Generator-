# iso_29

## What I see
A girl or young woman with dark hair tied back, wearing a yellow dress and red flat shoes, stands on green grass in front of a wooden plank fence. She holds a teal watering can in one hand and tips it toward a round bed of brown soil. A stream of blue water drops from the spout onto the flowers. The bed holds about ten flowers on green stems, coloured yellow, orange, purple, pink and red. She is watering the flowers.

- Her red shoes are simple rounded blobs and look a little like slippers, or like they sit on top of her feet rather than around them.
- There is a small dark mark at her mouth and nose area. It could be her nose and mouth, or something she is holding near her face. Most likely it is just the facial features.
