# iso_35

## What I see
A sunny town street with four two-storey shops in a row. Each shop has a striped awning (orange/white, green/white, plain peach, red/white) and shop windows and doors. Nobody is in the picture. In front of the shops is a wide paved square. On the left is a market stall with a teal-and-white awning and four crates of fruit (green, yellow, orange, red) on a wooden table. There is also a tall black street lamp, a leafy tree in a round planter, a small green bin with a lid, two potted plants in the front corners, a cardboard box on the ground at the lower left, and a second cardboard box in the middle of the scene.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The middle cardboard box looks like it is floating. Its bottom edge lines up with the kerb in front of the peach and green shops, so one reading is that it sits on the far pavement against the shopfront. But a grey oval shadow sits on the paving well below it, around the middle of the square, so the other reading is that the box is in mid-air (falling or being thrown) above that shadow. Either way, the box and its shadow do not match, which looks unintended.
- The box in the lower left has only a very faint shadow. It does seem to be resting on the ground.
- The tree's leaves cover part of the cream shop's red-and-white awning and windows. This reads as the tree standing in front, so it looks fine.
- The street lamp is drawn over the green-and-white awning and the upper window. It reads as standing in front of the building, but it is close enough that it could also look attached to the building.
- The stall's awning posts end at the table top, and the table has its own legs. This reads as one stall, though the awning frame and table look like separate pieces joined together.
