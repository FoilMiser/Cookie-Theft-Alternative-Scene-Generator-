# iso_03

## What I see
A woman (or older girl) with dark hair tied in a bun and ponytail stands on a green lawn in front of a wooden plank fence. She wears a yellow dress and red flat shoes. She holds a teal watering can by its top handle and tips it forward, so water comes out of the long spout in several blue dashed streams. The water falls onto a round bed of brown soil full of flowers in yellow, orange, purple, pink and red. She is watering the flowers.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Only one of her arms is visible, the one gripping the can's handle. The other arm cannot be seen and is presumably hidden behind her body.
- The water streams are drawn over the flower heads and stems. Most of the water seems to land on the red and pink flowers in the middle, not on the soil. This reads as water in front of or falling onto the flowers, but a few streams look as if they pass through the flowers.
- Her dress is quite boxy and bulges out at the back, so her body looks a little stiff and leans backward.
- Her age is unclear: she could be a young woman or a teenage girl.
- The flower bed runs off the right edge of the cut-out, so its full size can't be seen.
