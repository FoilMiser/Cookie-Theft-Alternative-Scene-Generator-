# iso_06

## What I see
A sunny back garden, with a sun, two clouds and green hedges behind a tall wooden picket fence. A cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door with a step is on the left, with a tree and a bush beside it. A wooden bench and a round flower bed of colourful flowers are on the lawn. A clothesline between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes, fixed with pegs. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress with dark hair stands at the edge of a paved path that leads to a wooden gate in the fence. She holds a broom with both hands and sweeps a small pile of dark brown leaves. Two terracotta pots with green plants, a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can sit on the grass.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The leaf pile sits just off the path, on the grass, while the woman stands on the path's edge. She could be read two ways: sweeping leaves off the path onto the grass, or sweeping leaves on the grass.
- The leaf pile is small and dark, so it could also be read as dirt or twigs rather than leaves.
- Only one of her red shoes is clearly visible. The other foot is hidden behind the broom bristles.
- The tree's trunk ends at the top of the fence, so the tree looks as if it stands behind the fence, not in this garden. That is plausible, but its canopy also overlaps the corner of the house roof.
- The wheelbarrow is fairly small next to the woman, and its handles point away from the wheel, toward the watering can. It reads as a wheelbarrow, but the way the grey tray sits on the frame and legs is a little simplified.
- The two plant pots look slightly large next to the woman, though they are farther back, so this may just be perspective.
- The gate is plain and flat-topped, unlike the pointed fence boards. It reads as a gate with a diagonal brace and a round knob.
- Nothing appears to be floating or to pass through anything else.
