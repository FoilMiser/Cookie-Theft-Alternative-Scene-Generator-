## What I see
A sunny back garden. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a blue door and a window. There is a tall wooden fence across the back with a wooden gate on the right. A woman or girl with dark hair in a ponytail and a yellow dress stands on a paved path near the gate. She holds a long-handled broom with yellow bristles and is sweeping a small pile of brown leaves. Around the garden there is a clothesline on two posts with blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes pegged on it, a flower bed with colourful flowers, a wooden bench, two potted plants, a grey wheelbarrow, a teal watering can, a round green bush by the house, and an autumn-coloured tree behind the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The person could be an adult woman or an older girl. The figure is small and simply drawn, so her age is unclear.
- The sweeping tool reads mainly as a broom (straw-coloured bristles). Because there is a pile of leaves, it could also be read as a rake.
- I can clearly see only one leg and one red shoe. The broom head and the leaf pile are where the other foot would be, so her stance looks slightly odd, as if she has one leg or the second foot is hidden behind the bristles.
- The leaf pile sits right where the path meets the grass, partly on each. That is not wrong, but it is hard to tell whether she is sweeping the path or the lawn.
- The tree behind the house has orange, yellow and green blobs inside the canopy. These read as autumn leaf clusters, but they could also be read as fruit or as separate small bushes inside the tree.
- The bench is seen straight from the front, so its backrest and seat slats flatten together. It reads as a bench but could look like a low rack or fence section.
- The two potted plants stand on the lawn in front of the fence. The left pot is slightly lower than the right one, which reads as being nearer. Nothing looks like it is floating.
