# iso_07

## What I see
A black-and-white (tuxedo) cat on green grass, crouched low with its body stretched out and its tail held straight back, facing right toward a red bird. It looks like it is stalking or creeping up on the bird. The red bird (cardinal-like, red body with a darker red wing and orange legs) faces away from the cat to the right, with its head tipped down toward the ground as if pecking or feeding, and its tail pointing up and back. At the far right edge is a curved beige/sand-colored area with a dark outline, which looks like the edge of a path or patch of dirt. Both animals have soft shadows under them.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The bird's pose: it reads as bending forward to peck at the ground, but because the body is tilted steeply nose-down with the pointed tail sticking straight up, it could also read as the bird tipping or falling over.
- The bird's pointed red shape at upper left: I read it as a tail, but on its own it looks like a cone or horn shape; someone could mistake it for a crest, which would flip which way the bird seems to face.
- The bird's beak is very small (a tiny yellow/orange bit under the head) and hard to make out; the white patch on the head could be an eye or a cheek marking.
- The cat's legs are hard to read: the front paws seem folded under the chest and one hind paw sits mid-body, so the limbs look a bit bunched or tangled. It could be read as a stalking crouch, as crawling, or as the cat lying/sliding on its belly.
- Proportions: the bird is large relative to the cat, roughly the size of the cat's head and chest, bigger than a real songbird next to a cat would be.
- The beige area at right is cut off by the frame; it could be a path, sand, or a dirt clearing.
