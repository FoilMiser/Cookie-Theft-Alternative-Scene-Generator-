# iso_01

## What I see
An older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top, grey skirt and brown shoes, stands on a lawn on the left holding a garden-hose nozzle up in front of her face with both hands; only a single blue drop of water shows at the nozzle, with no spray. The hose runs down from her hands, loops along the grass and back to a dark teal hose reel at the lower right. A brown-and-white dog with a red collar stands in the middle on the grass, facing the woman, with its tail up and its hind feet on or right next to the hose. Behind the dog is an oval bed of brown soil full of yellow, orange, red, pink and purple flowers, which are not being watered. The background is a wooden plank fence, a small green bush at the far left edge, and the cream wall of a house with a blue door and a grey step on the right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Which way the nozzle points: the drop sits on the side toward the woman, so it could be read as her looking into the nozzle (puzzled that nothing comes out) or simply aiming it forward toward the dog and flowers.
- Whether the dog is standing on the hose: its back paw sits right on the hose line. One reading is that the dog is pinching the hose and stopping the water; the other is that it is just standing next to the hose.
- Just to the right of the dog's back feet, a section of hose is much fatter than the rest (a swollen dark-teal blob). It could be deliberate (water backing up behind a blockage) or a drawing glitch or an overlapping shape. Either way it looks odd.
- The small red shape at the dog's mouth could be a tongue sticking out or part of the collar or mouth line. Its expression is hard to read.
- The woman's face is very small, so her expression (confused, focused, neutral) cannot be made out.
- The woman's skirt is a flat grey block that looks slightly separate from her body, and her pose is stiff, but this is probably just the drawing style.
- The green bush at the left edge is cut off, so it is unclear whether it is a bush or part of a tree.
