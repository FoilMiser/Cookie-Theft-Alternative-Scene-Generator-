A man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands at the right end of the pond and throws bread crumbs to a white duck. The duck is swimming toward the crumbs, and some crumbs are still in the air. A woman in a yellow dress is running fast to the left in front of the pond, with motion lines behind her. In the front, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses reads a red book. She is in a sitting position but there is no bench or chair under her, which looks impossible. On the right, an older man in a cap and vest walks to the left while reading an open newspaper. Behind him, a woman in a purple top rakes leaves into a pile on a winding path, and she seems to be looking toward him. A red bird sits on a branch in the big tree on the right. There is also a tall black lamp post, two green trash cans and a grey rock on the grass.
