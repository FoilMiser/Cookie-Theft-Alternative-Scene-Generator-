## Free description

This is a cartoon of a sunny park playground, with a yellow sun at the top right, two white clouds, a dark metal railing fence across the middle and green hedges behind it. On the far left a little girl in a pink dress stands by a tree and points up at a blue ball that is stuck on a branch in the tree. Next to her is a red seesaw. A boy in a yellow shirt sits on the raised left end, high in the air, with motion lines under his dangling feet. An old man in a grey cap sits on the low right end with his feet on the ground and reads a book, apparently not noticing that the boy is stuck up there. In the middle background a woman in a purple top walks to the right with a pram holding a sleeping baby under a pink spotted blanket. The pram is behind her, so she seems to be pulling it rather than pushing it. In the centre a brown dog sits up and holds an open newspaper in its front paws as if reading it, which is impossible. To the right of the dog an older grey-haired woman in a teal top and grey skirt holds a camera to her eye and points it left, apparently photographing the dog with its newspaper. Further right there is an empty slide with a red frame, a blue ladder and an orange-and-yellow chute. At the far right a man in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest walks along carrying a cardboard box like a delivery worker, and a grey rock sits on the grass in the bottom-right corner.
