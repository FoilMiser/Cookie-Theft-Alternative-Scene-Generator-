## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left, a wooden fence, a gate and a bird box on a pole on the right. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans is watering a flowerbed, but he is pouring the water out of a grey frying pan (or saucepan) instead of a watering can. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping a pile of dead leaves along the paved path with a broom. A metal swing set with one empty swing stands in the middle, and a red wheelbarrow sits empty on the lawn at the front. The odd thing is the man using a pan to water the flowers.
