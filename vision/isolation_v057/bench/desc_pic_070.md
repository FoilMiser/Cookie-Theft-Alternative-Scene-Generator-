# pic_070

## Free description
A windy, sunny day in a park with a pond, a lamppost, a winding path, trees and a flowerbed. White wind lines streak across the sky and over the pond, and leaves are blowing off the tree on the left. An elderly woman with glasses and a teal cardigan stands by the pond reaching up with one hand as an orange cap blows away above the water; it looks like it is about to land in the pond. Behind, an elderly man in a cap and brown waistcoat is scattering seed from a paper bag for a blue bird and a red bird pecking on the ground. In front left, a man in a green shirt walks along the path with a white spotted dog on a red lead. An empty bench sits in the middle and a grey rock is at the front.
