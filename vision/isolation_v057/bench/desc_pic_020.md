## Free description
A sunny playground in a park with a metal railing fence across the back. On the left, a small girl in a pink outfit sits on the raised end of a red seesaw, holding the handle, while an old man with grey hair, a cap, a brown waistcoat and white sleeves sits on the low end on the right with his feet on the ground. Because he is heavier, the girl is stuck up in the air; she looks a little surprised or worried, and he looks across at her. In the middle background, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers is walking along eating a pink ice cream cone. On the right there is an empty red-and-yellow slide, a green bin by the fence, a bush and a grey rock. A tree stands on the far left.
