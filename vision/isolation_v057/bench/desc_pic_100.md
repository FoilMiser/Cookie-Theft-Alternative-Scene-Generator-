## Free description
It is a sunny day in a park with green hills, trees, a pond with reeds, a lamp post and a winding sandy path. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands on the grass at the edge of the pond, holding a fishing rod with both hands. The fishing line hangs from the rod tip into the pond, where a red-and-white float sits with ripples around it, so he is fishing and waiting for a bite. No one else is in the park. There is an empty wooden bench beside the path, a green bin by the lamp post, a flower bed at the front left and a grey rock at the front. Nothing looks strange.
