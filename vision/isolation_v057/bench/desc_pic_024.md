# pic_024

## Free description
A sunny playground in a park, fenced at the back with a dark metal railing and bushes beyond. On the left a seesaw is tipped down on the right: a small girl in a pink outfit sits up on the high left end holding the handle and pointing or reaching toward the man. On the low right end a man in a green shirt and beige trousers sits reading an open book, with his feet on the ground, apparently not paying attention to the girl. The girl looks like she is stuck up in the air and wants him to play, while he keeps the seesaw down by sitting and reading. To the right there is a red and yellow slide with a ladder, and in the front right a sandbox with a red bucket and blue spade in it, next to a grey rock. A green bin stands by the fence and a tree stands on the far left. No one is using the slide or the sandbox.
