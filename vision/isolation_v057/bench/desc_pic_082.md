## Free description
A sunny park with rolling hills, a round pond on the left, a winding sandy path on the right and trees on both sides. An older man with grey hair, a cap and a brown waistcoat stands by the pond holding a paper bag and throwing bread to a white duck, which is swimming towards the crumbs and eating. A small girl in a pink dress is walking along the path on the right eating a pink ice cream cone. In the middle of the grass, a tall wooden ladder stands upright on its own, leaning against nothing, which looks impossible. A grey rock is at the front and a green bin is in the bottom left corner.
