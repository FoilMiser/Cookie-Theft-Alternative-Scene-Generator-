## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, a gate and paved path on the right, two bushes and two potted plants by the fence. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is pouring a strong stream of water onto a flowerbed next to the house, using a frying pan instead of a watering can. On the right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping a pile of brown leaves with a yellow broom at the edge of the path. A red wheelbarrow sits in the front centre. It's an ordinary gardening scene; the frying pan for watering is the odd part.
