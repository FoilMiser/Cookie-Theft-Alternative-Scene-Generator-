## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, a winding sandy path on the left, a tall street lamp in the middle, a pond on the right and a green bin in the bottom right corner. White wind lines in the sky and over the pond show it is a windy day. An older grey-haired woman with glasses in a teal cardigan and grey skirt stands in the front right with one arm stretched up and her mouth open in surprise: her orange cap has just been blown off and is flying away over the pond, along with a couple of leaves. The cap looks like it is about to land in the water. On the left, near the path, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands eating a pink ice-cream cone. Nothing else is happening.
