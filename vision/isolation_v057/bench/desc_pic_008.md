## Free description
It is a sunny day in a park. There are rolling green hills and trees in the background and two white clouds in the sky. On the left is a small round pond with reeds at its edges. An older man in a grey cap, brown vest and dark trousers stands at the front edge of the pond. He holds a paper bag and is tossing bread crumbs toward a white duck swimming in the pond, which is pecking at the crumbs on the water. On the right, a girl in a pink dress walks or stands on a winding sand-coloured path, eating a pink ice-cream cone. In the middle of the grass a tall wooden ladder stands upright on its own, leaning against nothing, which looks impossible. There is a green rubbish bin in the bottom-left corner and a grey rock at the front.
