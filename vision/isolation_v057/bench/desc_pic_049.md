## Free description

On the right, an elderly grey-haired man in a cap, brown waistcoat and white shirt sits on the low end of a red seesaw, reading a newspaper. In the centre, a brown dog with a red collar crouches in a play bow with a yellow-green tennis ball by its front paws, tail wagging, as if wanting someone to play. In the front left there is a sandpit with a red bucket and blue spade in it, and a grey rock beside it. Nobody is using the slide, swing or sandpit.
