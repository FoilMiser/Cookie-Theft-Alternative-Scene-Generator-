# pic_071

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a playground surrounded by a dark metal railing, with a tree on the left, a slide on the right and a sandpit at the front right. A small girl in pink sits on the raised end of a red seesaw, holding its handle, high up in the air. A man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers sits on the low end of the seesaw, which is down on the ground, and he is reading an open newspaper or book instead of playing. Because he is not pushing up, the girl is stuck up in the air; her face looks a bit unhappy or surprised. Nobody is using the slide or the sandpit. A red bucket and a blue spade are in the sandpit, and there is a grey rock nearby and a green bin by the railing.
