# pic_016

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house on the left, a wooden fence across the back and a gate and paved path on the right. A yellow kite is caught in the branches of the tree behind the house; its long string runs down to the ground, loops in a tangle on the grass, and goes up to a child in a yellow top and orange shorts who is holding the string spool up and looking up at the stuck kite. In the middle back, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is painting the fence pink with a paint roller; a section of fence planks is already bright pink. On the grass near him are a pink paint tin and a paint tray. A trail of pink paw prints leads from the paint tray to a white-and-brown dog on the right, whose paws are pink — the dog has evidently walked through the paint. A girl in a pink dress in the front right has both arms up and seems to be laughing or cheering (possibly jumping, as her shadow is a little below her). A red wheelbarrow stands in the foreground and a birdhouse sits on a tall pole.
