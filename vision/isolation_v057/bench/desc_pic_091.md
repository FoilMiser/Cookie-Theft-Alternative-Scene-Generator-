# pic_091

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, a gate on the right and a paved path down the right side. Next to the house a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is watering the flower bed, but instead of a watering can he is pouring water out of a dark frying pan held by its handle. On the right, on the path, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping a small pile of dry leaves with a broom. In the middle stands an empty swing set, and an empty red wheelbarrow sits in the foreground. A birdhouse on a tall pole stands by the gate, and a tree grows behind the house. The odd thing in the scene is the frying pan being used as a watering can.
