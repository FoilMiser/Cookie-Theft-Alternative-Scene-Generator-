## Free description
A sunny, quiet back garden with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, a gate on the right and a paved path down the right side. The only person is a man in a green shirt and beige trousers standing by the flower bed next to the house. He is watering the flowers, but he is pouring the water from a grey cooking pan held by its long handle rather than from a watering can or hose. Further back are a bush and two terracotta pots with green plants, and an empty red wheelbarrow sits in the foreground. A tree grows behind the house. The strange thing in the picture is the pan being used to water the flowers.
