# pic_050

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path down the right side. An older grey-haired woman with glasses sits on a wooden bench at the front left reading a red book; beside her on the bench is a sandwich, and a blue bird is perched at the end of the bench pecking at / taking the sandwich without her noticing. In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress is painting part of the fence pink with a paint roller on a long pole; a pink paint tray and a paint tin sit on the grass behind her. A white dog with brown spots is walking away from the paint tray toward the flower bed, leaving a trail of pink paw prints, so it has clearly stepped in the paint. On the right, a brown dog is sitting up on its haunches wearing glasses and a red collar, holding and reading a newspaper, which is impossible for a real dog. There is also an empty red wheelbarrow at the front and two potted plants by the fence.
