## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence and gate on the left, a paved path down the left side, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the middle, a little girl in a pink dress holds two balloons, yellow and purple, on strings in one hand, while a third, blue balloon is floating away up into the sky with its string trailing; she reaches up with her other arm and looks upset, as if she has just let it go. On the left, on the path, a child with dark skin and curly hair in a yellow top and orange shorts is kicking an orange ball, with motion lines under the kicking foot. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie pours water onto a round flower bed from what looks like a grey pan on a long handle rather than a watering can. An empty red wheelbarrow sits in the front centre and a wooden bench stands on the right by the house. The blue balloon seems about to drift away over the fence.
