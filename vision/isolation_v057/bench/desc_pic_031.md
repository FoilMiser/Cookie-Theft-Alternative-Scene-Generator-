# pic_031

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and path on the left and a cream house with a blue door on the right. On the left a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is kicking an orange ball on the path, with motion lines under it. In the centre-left a girl in a pink dress holds two balloons (yellow and purple) on strings; a third, blue balloon has escaped and is floating up above the fence with its string dangling free. She looks upset and reaches up after it. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is pouring water over a flowerbed, but he is pouring it from a frying pan rather than a watering can, which is odd. There is a red wheelbarrow in the front and a wooden bench by the house.
