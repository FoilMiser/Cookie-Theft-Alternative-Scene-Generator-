## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house on the left (blue door, window), a long wooden fence across the back, a gate on the right and a paved path running down the right side. A man in a red cap and blue hoodie stands at the fence holding a long-handled paint roller and painting it pink; a few fence planks are already pink, and a pink paint tin and a paint tray sit on the grass behind him. A trail of pink paw prints leads from the paint tray across the grass to a small white dog with brown spots near the path, whose paws are pink, so the dog has evidently stepped in the paint tray. In the middle front, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a kite spool up in the air; the string runs up to a yellow kite that is caught in the tree beside the house. On the right front, a girl in a pink dress has both arms raised and looks like she is jumping or cheering. An empty red wheelbarrow sits in the foreground, a flower bed is by the house, and a birdhouse on a tall pole stands near the fence. The man seems not to have noticed the dog yet; the dog's footprints are the main mischief in the scene.
