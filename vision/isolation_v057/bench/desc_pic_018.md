## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue front door. A tall wooden fence runs across the back with a wooden gate on the right, and a paved path leads from the front right up to the gate. A man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers stands next to a round flower bed full of colourful flowers and is watering them. However, the water pours out of what looks like a grey frying pan or saucepan rather than a watering can, which is odd. There are two potted plants and a bush near the fence, and an empty red wheelbarrow sits on the lawn at the front. No one else is in the garden.
