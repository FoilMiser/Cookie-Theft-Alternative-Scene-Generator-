## Free description
A sunny park with a pond on the left, trees, hills and a winding path on the right. An old man with grey hair, in a flat cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers, stands at the pond's edge holding a paper bag and throwing bread crumbs to a white duck swimming in the pond; crumbs float near the duck. On the right a girl in a pink dress walks along the path eating a pink ice-cream cone. In the middle of the open grass a tall wooden ladder stands upright on its own, leaning against nothing - it seems to be balanced in mid-air, which is impossible. There is a grey rock in the foreground and a green bin at bottom left.
