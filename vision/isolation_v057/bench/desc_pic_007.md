## Free description
A sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and a blue door, enclosed by a tall wooden fence with a gate on the right. In the middle, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers is hanging washing on a clothesline strung between two wooden posts; a pair of blue jeans and a white T-shirt are already pegged up and both are dripping water. A wicker/wooden laundry basket full of clothes sits on the grass at his feet. In the front, a girl in a yellow top and orange shorts is throwing a red ball (a dotted line shows its path) toward a smaller girl in a pink dress, who stands with her arms out ready to catch it. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is raking a small pile of leaves next to a paved path that leads to the gate. A flower bed with colourful flowers lies in front of the house. Nothing looks clearly wrong; the ball seems about to be caught by the little girl.
