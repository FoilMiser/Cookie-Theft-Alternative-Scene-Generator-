## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence at the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path. A woman in a yellow dress is painting the fence pink with a long-handled paint roller; a few boards are already pink, and a paint tray and open paint can sit on the grass behind her. A white dog with brown spots walks away from the paint area toward the flower bed, leaving a trail of pink paw prints, so it seems to have stepped in the paint. On the left an older woman with grey hair, glasses and a teal cardigan sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. Next to her on the bench, a blue bird is biting a big sandwich, apparently stealing it while she isn't looking. On the right, a brown dog wearing glasses sits up and reads a newspaper, which is impossible. A red wheelbarrow stands at the front and two potted plants sit by the fence.
