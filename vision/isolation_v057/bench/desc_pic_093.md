An empty red wheelbarrow stands in the front, and a wooden bench sits by the house on the right. The kite string runs diagonally across the picture and passes very close to, or across, the digging man's arm/shoulder area. Everyone is busy with their own task and nothing seems about to go wrong, except the kite string could tangle with the man.
