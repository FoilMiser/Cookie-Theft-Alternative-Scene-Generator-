## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a house with a blue door on the right, a paved path down the left side, and a bench by the house. On the left, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is kicking an orange ball, which is right at his foot on the path. In the middle, a girl in a pink dress is holding the strings of a yellow and a purple balloon; a third, blue balloon has escaped and is floating up into the sky, with its string trailing. She is reaching up towards it with a worried face, as if she has just let go of it. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is watering the flower bed with a grey watering can, and water is pouring onto the flowers. A red wheelbarrow stands in the foreground.
