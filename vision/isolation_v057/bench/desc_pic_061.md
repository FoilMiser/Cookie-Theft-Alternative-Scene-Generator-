## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, a winding sandy path, a round blue pond with reeds on the right, a tall black lamppost in the middle, trees, bushes, a flower bed and a green bin. On the left, a woman with dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers walks along beside the path, leading a brown dog on a red lead. In the middle, a small child with dark skin and curly hair, in a yellow top and orange shorts, holds up a kite reel; the kite string runs slack, loops on the grass, and goes up to a yellow kite that is caught in the branches of the tree on the left. So the kite is stuck in the tree rather than flying. In the front centre-right, a bearded man in a green shirt and beige trousers sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket next to a picnic basket holding a bottle and a loaf of bread (baguette). The scene is calm; the stuck kite is the one problem.
