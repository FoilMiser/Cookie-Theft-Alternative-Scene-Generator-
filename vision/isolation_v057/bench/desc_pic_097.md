## Free description
A sunny park with a winding path, lamp post, pond, trees and a flower bed. A young boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands in the middle holding a kite reel and looking up; his kite string runs back across the path to a large tree on the left, where the yellow kite is stuck in the branches. At bottom left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks a brown dog on a red lead along the path. In front right, a bearded man in a green shirt and beige trousers sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket next to a picnic basket holding a bottle and a baguette. A green bin stands at bottom right.
