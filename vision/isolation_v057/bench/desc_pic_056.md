# pic_056

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left, a wooden fence across the back, a gate on the right and a paved path in the bottom-right corner. A woman in a yellow dress is painting part of the fence bright pink with a long-handled roller; a pink paint tray and paint tin are on the grass behind her. A white dog with brown spots has walked through the pink paint and is leaving a trail of pink paw prints across the grass towards the flower bed; its feet are pink. On the left, a grey-haired older woman in glasses and a teal cardigan sits on a bench reading a red book, and right beside her a blue bird is stealing (pecking at) a sandwich on the bench. On the right, a brown dog is sitting up wearing glasses and holding a newspaper as if reading it, which is impossible/funny. There are two potted plants near the fence, a flower bed, and a red wheelbarrow at the front.
