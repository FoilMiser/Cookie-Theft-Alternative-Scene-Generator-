# pic_042

## Free description
A sunny fenced playground with a slide, a swing set, a sandpit and a seesaw. On the seesaw, an elderly man in a cap and brown waistcoat sits on the low end with his feet on the ground, reading a newspaper. At the other end a small girl in pink is stuck high up in the air, holding the handle and looking down at him with a worried or puzzled face. Because he is much heavier and not paying attention, she cannot come down. The swing hangs empty, the slide is empty, and a red bucket and blue spade lie in the sandpit. A blue bird perches on the fence at the far left.
