## Free description
A sunny playground with a railing fence, a tree on the left and a slide on the right. On the seesaw at the front left, a small girl in pink sits up on the high end while an old man with grey hair and a cap, in a brown waistcoat, sits on the low end reading a newspaper, so the seesaw is stuck with the girl in the air. The girl looks towards him with an open mouth as if calling him. In the middle background a man in a green shirt and beige trousers walks along eating a pink ice-cream cone. A green bin stands by the fence and a grey rock lies at the front right. The slide is empty.
