A sunny but windy back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the left, and a house with a blue door on the right. White wind lines blow across the sky. A woman in a yellow dress stands on the left reaching up as her red hat is blown off her head by the wind, with a few leaves flying with it. In the middle, a washing line strung between two posts holds a yellow striped towel and a pair of blue jeans pegged up and flapping in the wind. A flower bed is by the house, a bench sits front right, and a teal watering can and a red wheelbarrow are in the foreground. The scene suggests she is about to lose her hat over the fence.
