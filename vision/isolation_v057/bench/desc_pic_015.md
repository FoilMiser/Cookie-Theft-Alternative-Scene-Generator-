## Free description
A sunny back garden next to a cream house with a red roof and a blue door, enclosed by a wooden fence with a gate on the right. A man with dark skin and a short beard, in a green shirt and beige trousers, stands by a round flower bed full of colourful flowers. He is "watering" the flowers, but he is pouring the water from a dark frying pan (a pan with a long handle) rather than a watering can. Water streams out of the pan onto the flowers. A red wheelbarrow sits empty on the lawn at the front, and two potted plants and a bush stand near the fence. A paved path runs down the right side to the gate. Nobody else is in the garden. The odd thing is using a frying pan as a watering can.
