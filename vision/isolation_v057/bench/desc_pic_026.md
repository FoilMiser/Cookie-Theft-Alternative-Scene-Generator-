# pic_026

## Free description
A sunny day in a fenced playground with a tree, a bench, a slide and a sandpit. A yellow kite is caught in the top of the tree on the left; its string runs down to the grass and over to a child in a yellow top and orange shorts, who is holding the spool up high and looking up at it. A man in a red shirt and blue trousers sits on a bench reading a red book and is paying no attention to anything else. In the middle a brown dog with a red collar is crouched in a playful bow next to a yellow-green tennis ball, tail wagging, as if inviting someone to throw it. On the right a small girl in pink is kneeling in the sandpit building a sandcastle with a little flag on top, holding a red spade. Behind her stands a red and yellow slide; there is a green bin by the fence and a grey rock beside the sandpit. The kite looks stuck and the child seems to be trying to free it or has not noticed it is snagged.
