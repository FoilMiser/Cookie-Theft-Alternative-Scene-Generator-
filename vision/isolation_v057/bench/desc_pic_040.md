## Free description
A sunny playground with a metal railing fence, a tree on the left, a slide on the right and a sandpit at the front. On the left a man in a green shirt and beige trousers sits on the low end of a red seesaw, and a small girl in a pink outfit sits on the high end, holding its handle with her feet dangling in the air. Because the man is much heavier, his end is down on the ground and the girl is lifted up; they look at each other. The slide on the right is empty. In the front right a sandpit holds a red bucket and a blue spade, with nobody playing in it, and a grey rock lies next to it. A green rubbish bin stands by the fence in the middle. Nothing looks clearly wrong.
