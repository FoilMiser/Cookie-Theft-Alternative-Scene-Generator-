# pic_053

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a gate on the right where a paved path comes in. A child in a yellow top and orange shorts stands in the middle of the lawn holding a kite reel up, but the yellow kite is caught in the branches of the tree behind the house. The kite string runs from the tree down to the grass, lies slack in a loop on the ground, and then goes up to the child's hand, so the kite is stuck and not flying. To the right an old man in a cap and brown waistcoat is painting the fence bright pink with a paint roller; a section of the fence is already pink, and a pot of pink paint stands on the grass beside him. In the front a brown dog is lying on the grass asleep. There is also a bench, a flower bed and a teal watering can lying around.
