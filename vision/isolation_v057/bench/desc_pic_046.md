# pic_046

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, a gate on the right, and a paved path in the bottom-right corner. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands in the middle holding a kite reel up; its string goes up to a yellow kite that is caught in the branches of the tree behind the house. The string has a loose loop lying on the grass near his feet. To the right, an older man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat is painting the fence pink with a paint roller on a long handle; a few planks are already pink, and a tin of pink paint sits on the grass by his feet. A small brown dog is lying asleep on the grass at the front. There is a flower bed and a wooden bench on the left and a teal watering can on the right near the path.
