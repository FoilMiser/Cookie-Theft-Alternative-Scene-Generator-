# pic_087

## Free description
A sunny but windy back garden with a gate and paved path on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a cream house with a blue door on the right. White wind lines show a breeze blowing across the garden. A woman with dark hair in a yellow dress stands in the left-centre, reaching up with one hand as her red cap is blown off her head along with a few leaves; she looks surprised. Next to her a washing line strung between two wooden posts holds a red-and-yellow striped towel and a pair of blue jeans pegged up and blowing. In the front of the garden are a teal watering can and an empty red wheelbarrow, and on the right are a flower bed and an empty wooden bench by the house. No one else is present.
