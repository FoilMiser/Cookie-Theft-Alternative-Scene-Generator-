## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate at the far right, and a paved path running down the right side. A man with a short dark beard, in a green shirt and beige trousers, stands near the centre-left bending slightly over a round flower bed. He is pouring water from a grey watering can onto the colourful flowers, and a stream of water with droplets falls onto the bed. Nobody else is in the garden. An empty red wheelbarrow sits in the front centre of the lawn, and two potted plants and a small bush stand by the fence. Nothing seems about to happen beyond the watering; the scene is calm. The watering can is a little odd-looking: it has no spout, so the water pours straight out of the rim of a round pan-like bowl.
