## Free description
A sunny playground with a slide on the left, a swing set in the middle, a tree on the right and a sandbox at the front left. On the seesaw at the right, an old man with grey hair, a cap and a brown waistcoat sits on the low end reading a newspaper, while a small girl in pink sits on the raised end up in the air, looking towards him with an open mouth as if calling him. Because he is sitting and reading, the seesaw is stuck and she cannot come down. A small blue bird perches on top of the fence at the far left. The swing hangs empty, the slide is unused, and a red bucket and blue spade sit in the sandbox with a grey rock beside it.
