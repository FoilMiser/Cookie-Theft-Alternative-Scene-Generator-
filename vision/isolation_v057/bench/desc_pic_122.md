## Free description
A sunny back garden next to a cream house with a red roof and blue door, with a wooden fence and gate across the back. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands by a round flower bed pouring water onto the flowers from a dark frying pan instead of a watering can - water streams out of the pan. On the right, a woman with dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers sweeps a small pile of brown leaves on the paved path with a yellow broom. An empty red wheelbarrow stands on the lawn at the front, and there are two potted plants and bushes by the fence. The odd thing is watering flowers with a frying pan.
