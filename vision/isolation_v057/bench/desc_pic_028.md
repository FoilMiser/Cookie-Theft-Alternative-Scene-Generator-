# pic_028

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back with a gate at the right, and a paved path down the right side. A bearded man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers stands beside a round flower bed and is pouring water onto the flowers. Oddly, he is not using a watering can: he is holding a black frying pan by its handle and water is pouring out of the pan onto the flowers. That is the strange thing in this picture, since a frying pan is not meant for watering. Elsewhere there are two potted plants near the fence, a bush, and an empty red wheelbarrow on the grass at the front. No one else is present.
