# pic_025

## Free description
It is a sunny day at a playground surrounded by a dark metal railing fence, with a tree on the left and a red climbing slide on the right. On the left a seesaw is tilted: a small girl in a pink outfit sits on the raised end holding the handle. An older man in a grey cap, brown vest and dark trousers sits on the low end of the seesaw, but he is reading a newspaper and not paying attention to the girl, so she is stuck up in the air. In the middle at the back, a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers walks along while eating a pink ice-cream cone. A green rubbish bin stands near the fence and a grey rock lies at the front right. Nobody is on the slide.
