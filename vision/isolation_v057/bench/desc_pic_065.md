# pic_065

## Free description
A sunny, windy park with hills, a winding sandy path on the left, a tall street lamp, a pond on the right, a flower bed and a green bin at the bottom right. White wind lines in the sky show a strong breeze. An older grey-haired woman with glasses in a teal cardigan stands near the path at the back, reaching up as her red cap is blown off her head along with some leaves. At the front left, a bearded man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers walks along holding a red lead attached to a white dog with brown spots, which walks on the path beside him. On the right, by the pond, an older man in a grey cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat holds a paper bag and scatters seeds on the ground for two birds, one blue and one red, which are pecking at the seeds by his feet. An empty wooden bench stands in the middle.
