## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, trees and a winding dirt path on the left. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands on the grass to the right of centre, holding a fishing rod out over a round pond. His line ends in a red-and-white float bobbing in the water, with a ripple ring around it. A white duck swims at the right end of the pond, near some reeds. In the middle of the field a tall wooden ladder stands straight up on its own, leaning against nothing, which looks impossible. A grey rock sits in the front, and a green rubbish bin is in the bottom-right corner. No one else is around; the boy seems to be waiting for a bite.
