# pic_006

## Free description
A sunny day in a park with green hills, a path, a lamp post and a pond. On the left an older man in a grey cap and brown vest is bent over, scattering seeds or crumbs from a paper bag, and a blue bird is pecking at them on the ground by his feet. Next to him a small child in a yellow top and orange shorts has just thrown a red-and-white ball; a dotted line shows its path upward. A man in a red shirt and blue trousers, standing in the middle of the grass, holds both arms out in front of him, ready to catch the ball. On the right an older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. Behind her is a round pond with reeds, and to her right is a bed of colourful flowers and a green rubbish bin. A grey rock lies in the grass in the front. Nothing looks clearly wrong; the ball is in mid-air between the child and the man, about to be caught.
