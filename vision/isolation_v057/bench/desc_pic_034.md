# pic_034

## Free description
A sunny back garden seen the other way round: the house with a blue door is on the left, the gate and paved path are on the right, and a wooden fence runs across the back. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands in the middle flying a yellow kite, but the kite is stuck in the branches of the tree beside the house, with its string running from his hand up into the tree. An older man with grey hair, a cap and a brown waistcoat is painting the fence pink with a roller; a few boards to his right are already pink, and a pink paint tin sits on the grass by him. A brown puppy is lying asleep on the grass at the front. A bench and a flower bed are by the house, and a teal watering can is near the path.
