## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path running down the right side. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands by a round flower bed and waters the flowers with a green garden hose; the hose runs back to a hose reel on the ground at the front left. Behind him, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is walking briskly to the right with her eyes on an open book she is reading. Directly in her path, just in front of her feet, is a closed cardboard box on the grass, so she looks about to trip over it because she is not looking where she is going. On the right, a small girl in a pink dress is jumping in the air with both arms raised, looking happy. Nothing looks physically impossible; the main point of tension is the woman about to walk into the box.
