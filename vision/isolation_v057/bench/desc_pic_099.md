In the middle a man in a green shirt and beige trousers is hanging wet washing on a clothesline between two posts; blue jeans and a white T-shirt are pegged up and dripping water, and a basket of more laundry sits on the grass below. A little girl in a pink dress stands to the left of him with her arms out, apparently ready to catch it, though the ball is going higher and back toward the clothesline and the wet washing. On the far right by the gate a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is raking a small pile of leaves. There is a flower bed by the house and a tree behind it. The thing that seems about to happen is the ball either reaching the girl or flying on into the clean laundry.
