A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and path on the left and a cream house with a blue door on the right. An older man with grey hair, a flat cap and a brown waistcoat is digging a hole in the lawn with a shovel, with a pile of earth beside him. In the centre-front a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is flying an orange diamond kite, holding a spool with the string stretching high into the sky. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is pouring water onto a flowerbed from a frying pan, which is an odd tool for watering. There is a red wheelbarrow at the front and a wooden bench by the house. The kite string runs diagonally across the scene, passing close behind the digging man.
