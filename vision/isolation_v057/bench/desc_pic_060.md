## Free description
A windy, sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and path on the left, and a cream house with a blue door on the right. White wind lines blow across the sky. A woman in a yellow dress stands left of centre reaching up with one arm towards a red cap that is being blown away by the wind above her head, along with a few small leaves. Her face looks surprised. A washing line strung between two posts holds a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans, which are blowing sideways. In front are a teal watering can and a red wheelbarrow; on the right are a flower bed and a wooden bench. The scene shows her hat being blown off by a gust.
