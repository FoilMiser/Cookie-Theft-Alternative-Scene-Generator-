# pic_072

## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, trees, a winding sandy path on the right and an oval pond on the left. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands at the edge of the pond holding a brown paper bag and tossing bread crumbs onto the water. A white duck is swimming toward the crumbs and pecking at them. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. A tall black lamp post stands in the middle. In the foreground there is a flower bed, a green bin in the bottom left corner and a grey rock. Everything looks calm and ordinary.
