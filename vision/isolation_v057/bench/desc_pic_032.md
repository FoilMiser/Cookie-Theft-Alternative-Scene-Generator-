## Free description
A sunny park with a pond, a lamppost, a winding sandy path, trees, a flowerbed and a bench. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands at the edge of the pond holding a brown paper bag and tossing bits of bread towards a white duck. The duck is swimming in the pond and pecking at pieces of bread floating near it. A woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench beside the path, reading a red book. In the front left there is a colourful flowerbed and a green bin, and a grey rock sits in the front centre. Everything looks calm; nothing seems about to go wrong.
