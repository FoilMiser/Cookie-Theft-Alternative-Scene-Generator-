## Free description
A sunny playground with a metal railing fence, a slide on the left, a swing set in the middle and a tree on the right. On the right an older man in a grey cap and brown vest sits on the low end of a red seesaw with his hands on his knees, and a small girl in a pink outfit sits on the high end holding the handle, feet dangling in the air. The man's end is down on the ground so the girl is held up; they are looking toward each other, and both have their mouths open as if talking. A small blue bird is perched on top of the fence at the far left. The slide and the single swing are empty. At the front left a sandpit holds a red bucket and a blue spade, with a grey rock beside it, and a green rubbish bin stands by the fence. Nothing looks clearly wrong.
