# pic_067

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back and a gate on the left with a paved path. An old man in a cap and brown waistcoat is digging a hole in the lawn with a shovel, with a pile of earth beside him. In the middle front a small child in a yellow top and orange shorts holds a reel and flies an orange kite high in the sky; the string runs straight up from the child's hand to the kite. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is watering a flower bed next to the house, but he is pouring the water from what looks like a grey frying pan or saucepan rather than a watering can. An empty red wheelbarrow stands at the front and a wooden bench is at the bottom right.
