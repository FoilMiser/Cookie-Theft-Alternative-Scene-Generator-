## Free description
A playground on a sunny day with a metal railing fence at the back. On the left is a red and yellow slide and a bush, in the middle a swing frame with one empty red seat, and a green bin by the fence. In the centre front, a brown dog with a red collar crouches in a playful "bow", tail wagging, with a yellow-green tennis ball right at its nose, as if wanting someone to throw it. On the right, an old man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat and white shirt sits on the low end of a red seesaw with his hands on his knees. A girl in pink sits on the high end, up in the air, holding the handle and looking surprised or worried. A sandpit with a red bucket and blue spade is at the front left with a grey rock, and a tree stands on the right.
