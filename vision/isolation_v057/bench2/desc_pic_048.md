## Free description
A sunny playground surrounded by a dark metal railing fence with bushes behind it. On the left is a red climbing frame with a ladder and a yellow-and-orange slide, nobody on it. In the middle is a swing set with one red swing seat hanging empty, and a green rubbish bin stands near the fence. On the right an older man with grey hair, a flat cap, a brown waistcoat and white shirt sits on the low end of a red seesaw, while a girl in a pink outfit sits on the raised high end holding the handle; she looks surprised or worried. A small blue bird is perched on the fence at the far left. In the front left is a sandpit with a red bucket and blue spade in it, and a grey rock lies on the grass beside it. A large tree stands on the right. The seesaw is tipped down on the man's side, as you would expect with a heavier adult.
