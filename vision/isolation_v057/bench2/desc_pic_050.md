## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, a tree behind the house, and a wooden gate at the back right leading onto a paved path. Near the house, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is watering a round flower bed by pouring water out of a black frying pan, while a green watering can sits on the grass just behind him unused. On the right, a girl in a purple top and teal trousers stands on the path sweeping a small pile of dry leaves with a broom. Two potted plants stand on the grass near the fence, and a couple of bushes grow along it. A red wheelbarrow stands empty in the front centre. Two clouds and the sun are in the sky. It is a calm chores scene; the odd thing is watering flowers with a frying pan when a watering can is right beside him.
