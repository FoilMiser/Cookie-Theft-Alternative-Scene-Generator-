## Free description
A sunny back garden, with a tall wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate on the left, a paved path running down the front left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the middle of the garden, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts stands with their back to us, holding the string of an orange kite flying high in the sky on the left. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is watering a round flower bed full of colourful flowers, but he is pouring the water out of a black frying pan, not a watering can. A green watering can stands on the grass right next to him, unused. In the front, an older man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is digging a hole in the lawn with a spade, with a pile of soil beside him. A wooden bench stands by the house in the front right. The odd thing is the boy using a frying pan to water the flowers when a watering can is right there.
