## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence at the back, a wooden gate at the back left and a paved path running from the gate to the front left. On the right, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is watering a flower bed by pouring water out of a black frying pan, while a green watering can sits on the grass just behind him unused. In the middle, a girl in a pink dress holds the strings of a red and a purple balloon in one hand and reaches up with her other hand toward a blue balloon that is floating away above the fence; she looks upset, so it seems she has just let go of it. On the left, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is kicking an orange ball beside the path, with motion lines under his foot. A red wheelbarrow stands empty in the front centre and a wooden bench sits at the front right by the house. Two clouds and the sun are in the sky. About to happen: the blue balloon will keep drifting away and the ball will roll off from the kick. Odd: watering flowers with a frying pan while a watering can is right there.
