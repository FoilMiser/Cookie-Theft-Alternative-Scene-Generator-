## Free description
A sunny playground surrounded by a dark metal railing fence, with trees and hedges behind it. On the right, an old man with grey hair and a grey cap sits on the low left end of a red seesaw, his feet planted on the ground. A young girl in a pink outfit sits up on the high right end, holding the handle, her legs dangling in the air. Her mouth is open and she looks surprised or worried, as if she is stuck up high because the heavier man is keeping his end down. In the middle, a brown dog with a red collar crouches in a play bow, wagging its tail, with a yellow tennis ball right in front of its nose, as if inviting someone to play. On the left there is a red ladder slide with a yellow chute, and in the centre an empty swing set with one red seat. In the front left there is a sandbox with a red bucket and blue spade in it, and a grey rock beside it. Nobody is using the slide, swing or sandbox.
