## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the middle back, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands by the fence flying an orange kite, holding the string up with both hands; the kite is high in the sky at the top left with a little bow tail. In the front left, an older man with grey hair, a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is digging a hole in the lawn with a spade, with a mound of dug-up earth beside him. On the right, a younger man in a red cap and blue top is watering a flower bed by pouring water out of a frying pan, while a green watering can stands unused on the grass beside him. A wooden bench stands in front of the house. The odd part is the frying pan being used for watering when there is a watering can right there.
