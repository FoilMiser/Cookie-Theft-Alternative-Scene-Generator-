A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a paved path running down the left side, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. Three people are in the garden. In the middle at the back, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is flying an orange kite high in the sky, holding the string up with both hands. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is watering a round flower bed, but he is pouring the water out of a black frying pan instead of a watering can. A green watering can sits on the grass just beside him, unused. This is the odd thing in the picture: using a frying pan to water flowers when a watering can is right there. A wooden bench stands in the front right by the house.
