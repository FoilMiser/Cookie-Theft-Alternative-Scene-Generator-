## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a gate on the left, a paved path running down the left side and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the middle, a small boy in a yellow top and orange shorts is flying an orange kite: he holds a spool up in one hand, and a long string runs up to the kite high in the sky, which has a tail with two little bows. Behind him on the left, an older man with grey hair, a flat cap and a brown waistcoat is digging with a shovel, lifting soil next to a small pile of dirt. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is pouring water onto a round flower bed of colourful flowers, but he is pouring it from what looks like a frying pan or saucepan rather than a watering can. An empty red wheelbarrow sits at the front, and a wooden bench stands at the front right beside the house. Nothing seems about to go wrong; the main odd thing is the pan being used to water the flowers.
