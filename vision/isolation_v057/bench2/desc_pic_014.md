## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path running down the right side. Two people are in the garden. On the left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is watering a round flower bed by pouring water out of a black frying pan. A green watering can stands on the grass right next to him, unused, which makes the frying pan look odd and out of place. On the right, a woman in a purple top and green trousers is sweeping a small pile of dry leaves on the path with a yellow broom. A red wheelbarrow stands empty in the front middle, and two potted plants sit on the grass by the fence. Apart from the frying pan, everything looks like normal gardening.
