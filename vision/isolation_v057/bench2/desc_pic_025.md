# pic_025

## Free description
A sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left. A wooden fence runs across the back with a brown gate on the right, and a light stone path leads from the gate to the bottom right. A man with a short beard, in a green T-shirt and beige trousers, stands by a round flower bed near the house and waters the flowers by pouring water from a black frying pan. A green watering can stands on the grass right next to him, unused. A red wheelbarrow stands empty on the lawn at the front, and two potted plants sit near the fence. There is no one else in the garden. The strange part is that he waters with a frying pan when a watering can is right beside him.
