# pic_018

## Free description
A sunny back garden seen from the other side: a cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, a wooden fence runs across the back, and a gate and paved path are on the right. A single man with dark skin, a short beard, a green shirt and beige trousers stands by a round flower bed near the house. He is watering the flowers by pouring water out of a frying pan. A green watering can stands on the grass just behind him, not being used. Further back by the fence there are a bush and two terracotta pots with green plants. An empty red wheelbarrow sits on the grass in the front. There is nobody else in the garden. The strange thing is that he waters the flowers with a frying pan while the watering can is right beside him.
