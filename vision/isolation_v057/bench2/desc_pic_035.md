# pic_035

## Free description
A sunny back garden seen the other way round from usual: the cream house with red roof and blue door is on the left, a wooden fence runs across the back, and a gate and paved path are on the right. Near the house a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is watering a round flower bed, but he is pouring the water out of a frying pan rather than a watering can. A teal watering can is standing on the grass right beside him, unused, which makes the frying pan look even stranger. In the middle of the lawn there is a grey metal swing set with a single red seat; nobody is on it. On the right, on the path, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping a small pile of dead leaves with a yellow broom. A birdhouse on a tall pole stands by the fence near the gate, and a red wheelbarrow sits empty at the front. The odd thing is the frying pan being used to water flowers.
