## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden picket fence, a gate at the far left, a paved path down the left side, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. An older man with grey hair, a grey flat cap, a brown waistcoat and white shirt is digging in the lawn with a spade, with a small heap of dug soil beside him. In the front centre a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is flying an orange kite, holding a reel/spool up with the string running high into the sky to the kite near the sun. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is pouring water onto the flowerbed from what looks like a frying pan or saucepan rather than a watering can. A red wheelbarrow sits empty on the grass at the front, and a wooden bench stands at the front right by the house. The odd thing is the boy watering flowers with a pan.
