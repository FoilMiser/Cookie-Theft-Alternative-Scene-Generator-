# pic_021

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence with a gate at the back right, and a paved path down the right side. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands beside a round flower bed on the left. He is tipping a black frying pan so that water pours out of it onto the flowers, as if using it as a watering can, which looks odd. On the right, a girl with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers stands at the edge of the path sweeping a small pile of brown leaves with a yellow broom. An empty red wheelbarrow stands on the lawn at the front centre. Two potted plants and two small bushes are near the fence. The two children are working separately and not looking at each other.
