# pic_020

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back and a gate at the far right with a paved path leading to it. Centre-left, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is watering a round flower bed by pouring water out of a black frying pan. A green watering can stands on the grass right next to him, not being used, which looks odd. On the right, a girl or young woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is sweeping a pile of dead leaves on the path with a yellow broom. Two potted plants stand on the grass in the middle-right, and there are a couple of round green bushes by the fence. An empty red wheelbarrow stands at the front. The odd thing is watering flowers with a frying pan when a watering can is right there.
