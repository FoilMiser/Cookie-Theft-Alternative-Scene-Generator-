## Free description
A sunny playground on grass with a dark metal railing fence and bushes behind it. On the left, a small girl and a man are on a red seesaw. The girl in a pink outfit sits on the raised left end, holding the handle, while the man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers sits on the lowered right end with his feet on the ground. The seesaw tilts down on his side, as you would expect since he is much heavier, so the girl is up in the air. The girl looks toward the man and appears to be pointing or reaching toward him, perhaps asking him to push up. On the right is a red-framed slide with an orange/yellow chute and a ladder, nobody on it. In front right is a sandbox with a red bucket and a blue spade in it, and a grey rock beside it. A green bin stands by the fence in the middle, and a tree stands at the far left. Two clouds and the sun are in the sky. Nothing looks impossible.
