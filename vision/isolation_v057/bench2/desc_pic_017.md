## Free description

In the middle-left, a boy or young man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is watering a round flower bed, but he is pouring the water out of a black frying pan rather than a watering can. On the right, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is sweeping a small pile of dead leaves on a paved path with a yellow broom. An empty red wheelbarrow stands on the grass at the front, and a birdhouse on a tall pole stands by the fence near the gate. Nothing else seems about to happen; the main oddity is watering flowers with a frying pan.
