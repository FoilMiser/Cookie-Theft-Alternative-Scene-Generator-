A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a gate in the fence on the right. A man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands by a round flower bed full of colourful flowers and is pouring water onto them — but he is pouring it from a dark frying pan, not a watering can. On the right, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands on a paved path, sweeping a small pile of dead leaves with a broom. In the middle of the garden there is an empty swing set with a red seat, and in the front a red wheelbarrow sits on the grass. A birdhouse on a tall post stands by the fence. Nobody is on the swing; the two people are each busy with their own chore.
