# pic_022

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a paved path running down the left side, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the left, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts kicks an orange ball along the path, with motion lines showing the kick. In the middle, a girl in a pink dress holds the strings of two balloons, yellow and purple, while a third, blue balloon has got away and is floating up above the fence with its string trailing; she reaches up toward it with a worried face. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie waters a flower bed — but he is pouring the water from a frying pan, not a watering can. A red wheelbarrow sits in the front of the lawn and a wooden bench stands by the house. The blue balloon looks about to be lost for good.
