A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path running down the right side. Only one person is in the picture: a man with short dark hair and a beard, wearing a green T-shirt and beige trousers, standing beside a round flower bed. He is watering the flowers by pouring water from a black frying pan. A green watering can stands on the grass right next to his feet, unused, which makes the frying pan look odd. A red wheelbarrow stands empty in the front middle, and two potted plants sit on the grass near the fence. Otherwise the garden is quiet and tidy.
