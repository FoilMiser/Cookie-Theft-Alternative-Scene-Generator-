On the path at the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping a small pile of brown leaves with a broom. A birdhouse on a tall pole stands near the gate, and an empty red wheelbarrow sits at the front. Nobody is on the swing. The main odd thing is the pan being used for watering.
