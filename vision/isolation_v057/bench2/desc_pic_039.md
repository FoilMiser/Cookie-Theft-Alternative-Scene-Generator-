## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path running down the front right. There is only one person: a man with a beard and dark skin, in a green T-shirt and beige trousers, standing beside a round flower bed near the house. He is watering the flowers by pouring water out of a black frying pan. A green watering can stands on the grass just behind him, unused. Further back there are two potted plants and a green bush near the fence, and in the front there is an empty red wheelbarrow. The odd thing is that he is using a frying pan to water the flowers even though a watering can is right next to him.
