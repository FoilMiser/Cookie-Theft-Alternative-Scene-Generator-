## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, a round pond edged with reeds, a winding sandy path on the right, trees, a bench and a tall lamppost. In the front left, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts has just thrown a red ball (a dotted line shows its path), and a girl in a pink dress opposite him holds out her hands to catch it; a flower bed is between them. In the centre-back, an old woman with grey hair in a bun, a teal top and grey skirt, holds a paper bag and scatters seeds, and a red bird and a blue bird peck at the seeds on the ground by the lamppost. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress walks a brown dog on a red lead along the path; the dog walks ahead, pulling slightly. The bench is empty and a grey rock lies on the grass in front. Everything looks calm; the girl seems about to catch the ball.
