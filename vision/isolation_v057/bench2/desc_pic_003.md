# pic_003

## Free description
A sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left. A wooden fence runs across the back with a brown gate on the right, and a light stone path leads from the gate down to the bottom right. Near the house, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is watering a round bed of colourful flowers, but he is pouring the water out of a black frying pan, not a watering can. A green watering can stands on the grass right beside him, unused. On the right, a girl in a purple top and green trousers is sweeping a small pile of brown leaves on the path with a yellow broom. A red wheelbarrow stands empty on the lawn in the front centre, and two potted plants sit near the fence. The odd thing is the frying pan being used to water the flowers while a proper watering can is right there.
