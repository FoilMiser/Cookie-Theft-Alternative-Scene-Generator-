## What differs between the arms

No reading in any arm meets (a), because every reading that names a pan also names another vessel somewhere (most often saucepan) or names the pan only in U. Readings that call the thing a watering can first or only are 1 of 10 in C, against 1 of 5 in O, 2 of 5 in S, 4 of 9 in P and 4 of 10 in B. Saying that a pan is a wrong or odd thing to water with is 7 of 10 in C, 3 of 5 in S and in O, 5 of 9 in P, and 3 of 10 in B. In B, two of the six (b) readings give the pan only in U, behind a generic "container", and one C reading does the same. Ladle or scoop as the other name appears in S (1 of 5), B (1 of 10) and C (3 of 10), and not in O or P.
