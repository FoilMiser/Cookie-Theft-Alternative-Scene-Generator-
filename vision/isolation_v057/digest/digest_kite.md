## What differs between the arms

In S and O every reading (4 of 4 each) says the kite is stuck but also offers flying just in front of or low near the tree, so none falls under (a). In B, 6 of 8 readings say stuck with no flying reading anywhere, and 2 of 8 (both T2_park_s1022) still offer flying in front of the tree. No reading in any arm reads the kite as flying first or only. On the string, 1 of 4 S readings and 1 of 4 O readings call it drooping or slack, while all 8 B readings say it reaches or loops on the ground; 4 B readings (pic_061, pic_066, pic_053, pic_016) tie the slack string to the kite being stuck, and 3 (pic_066, pic_026, pic_016) call the string odd or oddly drawn, with one more (pic_046) unsure whether the string meets the child's hand.
