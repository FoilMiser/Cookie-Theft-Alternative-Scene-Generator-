## What differs between the arms

In S, 3 of 4 readings remark that the figure overlaps the pond's rim or edge, two of them raising standing in or partly in the water; in O all 4 make such a remark, two raising the water. In B, 2 of 8 readings say nothing about where the figure stands, 4 of 8 remark that it stands back from the edge with the rod or throw reaching the water, 1 says it is on the grass and not in the water, and 1 (pic_032) still finds the body overlapping the front rim. The action is read in all 16 readings; a doubt sits beside it in 1 of 4 S readings (hooking the duck), 0 of 4 O readings and 2 of 8 B readings (both T3_park_s23758, a bread piece over the grass short of the pond).
