A tall wooden fence runs across the back of the lawn, with a wooden gate at its left end, and a house with a red roof and a blue front door stands on the right. In the middle at the back, a boy in a red shirt and blue jeans stands by the fence. He holds a paint roller on a long handle and is painting the fence blue. The hose runs from her hands around the flower bed to a hose reel on the ground. At the front left, a boy in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands on a paved path and sweeps dirt toward a red dustpan. The odd thing is what he sweeps with: a huge silver dinner fork held like a broom, not a real broom. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands on the grass at the front centre.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The sweeping tool: it looks like an oversized kitchen or dinner fork (silver, smooth, curved tines), not a broom. Reading 1: it is deliberately the wrong tool, a giant table fork used as a broom, and that is the "odd" thing in the picture. Reading 2: it is meant to be a garden fork or rake and is just drawn in a cutlery style. Either way, a fork could not really sweep dirt into a dustpan, because the dirt would go through the gaps between the tines.
- The painter: he stands in front of the unpainted part of the fence, and the roller touches the left edge of the blue section. This could mean he is extending the blue to the left, or he is touching up the edge of the part already done. Either way he is painting it blue.
- The dustpan is on the ground at the edge of the path with nobody holding it. That is plausible, but it means the dirt is being pushed into a pan that no one is steadying.
- The wheelbarrow is empty and unattended. Its handles rest low near the ground, so it looks slightly tipped or parked. It does not seem to be part of any action.
- The blue of the fence paint matches the blue of the house door. That may just be a shared colour, or it may hint that the same paint was used.
- Nothing else looks like it is floating or passing through anything. The hose runs around the flower bed plausibly, and the bush beside the house is partly hidden behind the house wall in a normal way.
