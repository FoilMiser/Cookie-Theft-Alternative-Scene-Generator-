# Description of test_2.png

## Free description

This is a cartoon of a back garden with a lawn, enclosed by a tall wooden plank fence. There is a wooden gate in the fence at the far left and a cream-coloured house with a red roof, a blue door and a pale-blue window on the right. The sky is grey and three dark storm clouds hang overhead, so it looks as though rain is coming. On the left, a girl in a yellow dress stands on a pale paving path and sweeps a small pile of dirt toward a red dustpan. She is not using a broom. She is using a giant silver dinner fork, which is the obvious wrong or impossible thing in the picture. In the middle of the lawn, a child in a purple top and teal trousers looks up with an open, surprised mouth and points up and to the right, apparently at the dark clouds. On the right, a brown-and-white dog sits beside the blue door holding a red lead in its mouth, as if it wants to go for a walk. Nearby are a round bed of colourful flowers and a garden-hose reel with its hose and yellow nozzle lying on the grass. Taken together, it looks as if a storm is about to start: the walk, the gardening and the sweeping might all be interrupted.
