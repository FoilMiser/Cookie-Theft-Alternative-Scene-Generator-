## 1. Free description

The picture shows a back garden on a grey, overcast day. A tall wooden fence with a gate on the left runs along the back, with a green hedge behind it. A cream house with a red roof, a blue window and a blue door stands on the right. Three dark grey storm clouds hang in the sky, so it looks like rain is about to start. On the left, a girl in a yellow dress stands at the edge of a paved path and sweeps a small pile of orange, red and yellow autumn leaves with a broom, looking off to the left. Next to her, a girl in a purple top and teal trousers looks up with her mouth open in surprise. She points up and to the right toward the dark clouds, as if warning the others that rain is coming. In the centre, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, digs a hole in the lawn with a shovel. A mound of dug-up soil sits beside the hole. In front of the house there is a bed of colourful flowers. Nothing looks clearly impossible. One small oddity is that there is a pile of autumn leaves while the tree and hedge are fully green and the flowers are in bloom, and there is no tree near the path for the leaves to have fallen from.

- The figure in purple could be read as a girl or as a young woman. She is about the same height as the girl in yellow.
- Why the elderly woman is digging is unclear. There is no plant, bulb or sapling nearby to put in the hole, so she could be planting something or just digging.
- The girl in yellow looks to the left, away from the others and the clouds. She could be focused on her sweeping or looking toward the gate. She does not seem to have noticed the clouds.
- Seasonal mismatch: the autumn-coloured fallen leaves don't match the fully green tree, green hedge and blooming flowers, and no tree stands near the path.
- The figures cast soft shadows on the grass even though the sky is overcast. This is minor and could just be the drawing style.
