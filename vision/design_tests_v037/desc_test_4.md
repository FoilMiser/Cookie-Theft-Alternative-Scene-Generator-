# Description of test_4.png

## 1. Free description

This is a back garden on a grey, overcast day, with three big dark storm clouds hanging in the sky, so it looks as if it is about to rain. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door. Next to it is a round flower bed full of red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers. A tall wooden fence runs across the back, with hedges behind it and a closed wooden gate on the right. In the middle of the lawn, an elderly grey-haired woman with glasses is raking a small pile of leaves. To her right, a girl in a yellow dress is looking up and pointing at the dark clouds with her mouth open, as if warning the others that rain is coming. On the right, a boy in a green shirt is standing on the paved path that leads up to the gate. He is using a giant silver fork, shaped like a dinner fork, to sweep dirt into a red dustpan on the path, and a little puff of dust is rising where the tines touch the ground. The odd thing is the fork: it is a huge kitchen-style fork being used as a broom, which is not the right tool and is far too big to be a real fork.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous

- The fork: it looks like an oversized silver kitchen or dinner fork, not a garden tool. One reading is that this is meant to be wrong or silly, since he should be using a broom. The other is that it is meant to be a garden fork drawn oddly, though the silver colour and the shape of the handle and tines look like cutlery rather than a garden fork. Either way it is the strangest thing in the picture.
- The red object on the path is most likely a dustpan with a long handle. It could also be read as a small red shovel or scoop.
- The grey heap at the dustpan could be dirt, dust or small stones.
- It is not clear whether the girl is warning the others about the coming rain or just pointing out the clouds in surprise. She is not clearly looking at or talking to either of the others.
- The woman seems to glance towards the girl, but it is hard to tell whether she is reacting to her or just raking.
- Nothing seems to float or pass through anything else. The figures stand on the ground and have shadows.
