# v0.39 isolation bench

Each visual was forced into a picture with little else and cut out, then read blind by fresh readers (the method of `isolation_v023.md`), two cut-outs to a reader and never two takes of one thing. Three rounds: round 1, twenty-two readers and 44 cut-outs (`isolation_v039/round1`); round 2, six readers and 11 cut-outs (`isolation_v039/round2`); round 3, six readers of one goat each (`isolation_v039/round3`). `map.json` in each says which cut-out is which case.

What was drawn is Sam's `figure-fixes-v038.md`, done as one cast round (builder 0.39.0), with the two scene changes of revision 37 beside it:

- the cast, behind switches read when the cast is built (`castlib`): `TPDB_ISECT39` (no intersection lines where overlap parts meet: shoes, belt, buckle, hands, paws, the dog's chest), `TPDB_SHOE39` (one shoe, flat and toe forward, an ankle and a calf on bare legs, the trouser cuff over the shoe, planted feet level), `TPDB_MID39` (a mid-section: the tee tucked into the trousers, a hoodie or cardigan hemmed over the waistband, the dress and the skirt in one piece with a hem edge, a seat, the hip filled where a thigh turns forward), `TPDB_BELT39` (a flat band that bends with the trousers), `TPDB_BEN39` (the man in red an adult: a little taller, broader, a smaller head, sideburns, "C" a moustache too), `TPDB_QUAD39` (three-bone hind legs, a pastern on the front, paws the leg's end, the far legs a shade darker) and `TPDB_ART39` (the animals redrawn one by one: the dogs' deep chest and tuck-up and a muzzle grown from the head, the beagle shorter in the leg and longer in the ear, the black cat a dark grey with a whisker pad, whiskers, wedge ears and a slimmer body, the goat boxy and brown with a white face and legs, a long wedge head, split hooves, and only a goat's poses);
- the scene, as module flags: `templates.YAW39` (revision 37's animal angles, `ANIMAL_YAWS`: 45, and 135 where no direction is needed, beside 90) and `draw.HEAP39` (the broom's heap).

Each thing was read twice: **O**, v0.38's cast (`BENCH_CAST=assets/cast/out038`) with every animal at 90, and **N**, the v0.39 cast with all its switches on and the animals at revision 37's angles. The first three letters of a case name the thing.

And the goat's coat: brown on white legs read as a blanket on all three.

**Chosen: D**, `TPDB_GOAT39` **D**. Of the four coats it is the only one with "a goat" from every reader and nothing taken for a cover or a separate head; brown, all over or in part, pulled the name toward the antelope and the deer, and the edge between brown and white was read as a line between two things wherever it was put. What all four share is the graze: the rump high, the back arched, the neck hidden behind the lowered head at 45 and 135. That is the pose, not the coat, and it goes on the list for v0.40.
