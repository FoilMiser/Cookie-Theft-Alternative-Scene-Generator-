# Picture 07

## Free description
A sunny playground with a dark metal railing fence at the back. On the left, a red seesaw is tilted steeply: a small girl in a pink dress is stuck up high on the left end with her legs dangling, holding her hand to her mouth and looking worried or surprised. On the low right end sits an old man with grey hair, a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat, calmly reading a book or newspaper and not paying attention to her. Because he is so much heavier and is just sitting there, the girl cannot come down. On the right front, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and black trousers is standing and eating a pink ice cream cone. In the background there is an empty swing, a green litter bin, a red ladder with a yellow/orange slide, a bush and a tree, and a grey rock in the front right.

- Girl, left, dark hair in a bun, pink dress, yellow shoes: stuck at the raised end of the seesaw, hand to mouth, looking unhappy/surprised.
- Old man, centre-left, grey hair, grey flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat, dark trousers: sitting on the low end of the seesaw reading a book/newspaper.
- Boy, right front, red cap, blue hoodie, black trousers, white shoes: standing, eating a pink ice cream cone, looking happy.
- Red seesaw on grey stand: left, steeply tilted down to the right.
- Open book or newspaper: held by the old man.
- Pink ice cream cone: held by the boy.
- Swing frame with one empty red seat: centre back.
- Green litter bin: centre-right back. Slide with red ladder: right back.
- Grey rock: front right. Tree: far left. Bush: far right. Sun top right, two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The man may have sat on the seesaw without noticing the girl, or may be her grandfather playing a joke; he seems oblivious.
- The girl's gesture could be worry, surprise, or calling out; hard to say which.
- The girl appears to be sitting slightly above/at the very tip of the plank; it reads as sitting but she looks a little detached from the seat.
