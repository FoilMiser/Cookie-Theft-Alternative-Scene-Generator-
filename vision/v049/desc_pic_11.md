# pic_11

## Free description
A busy sunny park with a pond, a path, a bench, a lamp post and several people and animals. On the left, an old man in a cap and brown waistcoat pours water onto a flower bed — but from a frying pan rather than a watering can. Next to him, a brown dog wearing glasses sits up reading a newspaper, which is impossible. In the middle, a white duck swims in the pond with bread pieces floating around it, and a small girl in a pink dress at the pond's edge seems to have thrown the bread. In the front, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts covers his face with his hand, looking upset, holding an empty cone; his pink ice cream has fallen on the ground and a white spotted puppy is licking it up. Behind, a white goat grazes on the grass, apparently tied to a stake, and an old woman with glasses sweeps leaves into a pile with a broom. On the right, a boy in a red cap holds a kite reel; his purple kite is stuck in the tree. A blue bird flies across the sky.
