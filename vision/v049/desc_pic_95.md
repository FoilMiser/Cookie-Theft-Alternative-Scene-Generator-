# pic_95

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a gate on the left, a paved path running down from the gate, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A girl in a yellow dress stands in the middle-right, holding a teal watering can over a round bed of colourful flowers. Strangely, the water from the can's spout does not pour down onto the flowers; it arcs upward into the air, with droplets flying up above her head. There are two potted plants and a bush by the fence, and a tree behind the fence. A grey wheelbarrow sits empty on the lawn at the front. Nobody else is in the picture. The water going up instead of down looks impossible.
