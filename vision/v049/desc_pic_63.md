A sunny park with green hills, trees, bushes, a winding sandy path on the right, an empty wooden bench, a tall black street lamp, a grey rock, a green bin and a flower bed. In the middle an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, stands at the edge of a blue pond holding a fishing rod with both hands. The line runs from the rod tip down into the pond, where a red-and-white float sits on the water with a small ripple around it. She is fishing and waiting for a bite. There are reeds at both ends of the pond. Nothing looks wrong or impossible.
