## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a paved path, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. On the left a small child in a yellow top and orange shorts holds a blue and a red balloon; a purple balloon has floated away high into the sky above them, and the child is reaching up toward it, looking upset. In the centre a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie has just thrown a small red ball (a dotted arc shows its path) toward a woman in a purple top and teal trousers on the right, who has her hands up ready to catch it. In front of the boy a brown dog sits up on its back legs holding an open newspaper with its front paws as if reading it, which is impossible for a real dog. A bed of flowers is by the house, and a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow are on the grass at the front. The ball looks about to be caught by the woman.
