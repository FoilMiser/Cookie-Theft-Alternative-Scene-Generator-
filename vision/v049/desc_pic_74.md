# pic_74

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a house on the right, a fence and gate at the back left, and a path at the front left. A small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands near the fence holding a purple and a yellow balloon. He is reaching up toward a blue balloon that has floated away above the fence, so he seems to have just let it go. In the middle, a grey-haired grandmother with glasses and a teal cardigan is digging with a spade, next to a pile of earth. A washing line behind her holds a yellow striped top and a pair of blue jeans. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie holds a green watering can by the flower bed. The water comes out of the can in a tall arc that goes upward first, with drops at the top, and then down onto the flowers. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits in the front centre.
