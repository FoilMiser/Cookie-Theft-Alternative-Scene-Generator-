A man in a green shirt and beige trousers is painting the fence blue with a roller on a long handle; a few planks are already blue and a paint tin stands at his feet. In the front, a sprinkler on a hose sprays water onto a cardboard box, which is wet and in a puddle, instead of onto the flower bed. An empty wheelbarrow and two potted plants are also on the lawn. The bird on wet paint and the sprinkler soaking the box look like problems.
