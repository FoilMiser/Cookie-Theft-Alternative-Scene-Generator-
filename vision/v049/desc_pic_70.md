## Free description
This is a back garden on a sunny day, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door at left, a long wooden fence, a gate at right and a paved path at bottom right. A man with brown hair and a moustache, in a red T-shirt and blue trousers, stands by the fence painting it blue with a paint roller on a handle. A few fence boards to his right are already painted blue, and the rest is still plain wood. An open tin of blue paint sits on the grass behind him. In the foreground an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt rakes a small pile of brown leaves. A blue bird flies across the sky. There is a flower bed with mixed flowers by the house and a teal watering can on the grass at bottom right. Nothing looks impossible.
