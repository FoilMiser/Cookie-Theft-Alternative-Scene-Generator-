# pic_22

## Free description
A busy sunny town square with a row of coloured shops, a fruit stall, a lamp post, planters, a bench and a tree. On the left an elderly woman with glasses and a yellow shopping bag walks past the fruit stall. In the front centre-left a child in a yellow top and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and covers their face, crying, because the pink ice cream has fallen on the ground; a brown dog is licking up the spilled ice cream. On the bench in the centre a woman in a purple top reads a red book, while a blue bird beside her is pecking at her sandwich on the bench, unnoticed. Behind, a boy in a red cap sweeps a pile of leaves with a broom. A small girl in a pink dress points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the tree branches. On the far right an older man in a flat cap scatters seed from a paper bag for a red bird pecking on the ground, and in the front right a man in a green shirt sits reading a red book. At the front centre a teal bicycle is drawn with square wheels, which is impossible.
