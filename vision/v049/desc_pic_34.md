## Free description
A sunny but very windy day in a park with a pond, a winding path, a lamp post and rolling green hills behind. White wind lines streak across the sky and over the pond, and a few leaves are blowing off the big tree on the left. A woman in a yellow dress stands in front of the pond, reaching up with one arm and looking worried: a blue cap is flying away from her through the air, carried by the wind toward the pond. It looks like the hat is about to land in the water. On the left, a man in a red shirt and blue trousers walks a brown dog on a red lead along the path. In the middle, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, holds a paper bag and scatters seeds onto the path. A blue bird and a red bird are pecking at the seeds by her feet. There is a flower bed with colourful flowers at the front right.
