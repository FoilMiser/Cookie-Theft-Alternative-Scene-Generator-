## Free description
A busy back garden on a sunny day. On the left a man with brown hair and moustache, red shirt and blue jeans, sits on a bench reading a red book. A girl with dark curly hair, yellow top and orange shorts holds a kite string, but her purple kite is stuck in the top of the tree behind the fence. An older man with grey hair and a cap, brown waistcoat, walks across the lawn carrying a cardboard box. In front, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers holds the nozzle of a garden hose, but a brown dog is standing right on the hose, which would stop the water. On the right, a small girl in a pink dress holds a purple and a blue balloon, and reaches up toward a red balloon that has escaped and is floating away into the sky.
