# Picture 05

## Free description
A busy, sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence with a gate at the back right, and a paved path on the right. Near the fence on the left, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts has just thrown a red-and-white ball; a dotted line shows its path through the air towards a boy in a red cap, blue top and black trousers, who has his arms out ready to catch it. In the middle front, a small girl in a pink dress is holding a string and flying a yellow kite that is high in the sky at top right, with a bow-tied tail. On the right, on the path, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers is holding a huge silver fork, like a giant dinner fork, and using it to sweep or scrape a pile of dirt/gravel on the path, with dust puffing up; a red dustpan lies next to the pile. Using a giant fork as a broom is the odd thing in the picture. There is also a birdhouse on a tall pole, a bench at bottom left, a flower bed, two potted plants, a bush, a wheelbarrow and a watering can.
