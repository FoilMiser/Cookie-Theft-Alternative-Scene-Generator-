## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left, a wooden fence, a gate and a path on the right. An elderly man with grey hair, a blue flat cap, a white shirt, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands next to the flower bed. He is watering the flowers, but instead of a watering can he is pouring water from what looks like a frying pan or saucepan held out by its handle. A stream of water arcs out of the pan onto the flowers. This is an odd choice of tool for watering. An empty wheelbarrow stands in the front centre, and there are two potted plants and a bush by the fence. No one else is in the picture.
