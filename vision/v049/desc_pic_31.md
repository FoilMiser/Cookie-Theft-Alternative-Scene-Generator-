## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence along the back, a gate and paved path on the left and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. An old woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal sweater and a grey skirt is painting the fence blue with a paint roller. A section of it is already blue, and a small paint tin sits on the grass beside her. To the right, an old man in a blue cap, white shirt and brown vest walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. An orange cat walks along the top of the fence above them. The dog might notice the cat. At the front, a lawn sprinkler on the end of a long hose is spraying arcs of water. The water lands on a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is getting soaked, with a puddle and drips around it. That looks like something going wrong. There is also a flower bed next to the house.
