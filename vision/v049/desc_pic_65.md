## Free description
It is a sunny day in a park with rolling green hills, trees, a pond, a winding sand path, a lamp post and an empty wooden bench. A young girl in a pink dress stands at the right-hand edge of the pond and holds a small bag or cup, as if she has just thrown food into the water. A white duck on the pond has its head dipped toward a scattering of small pale pellets (bread or seed) floating on the water, and white lines behind it show it is swimming forward. In the foreground a white goat with a red collar grazes on a tuft of grass. A long rope runs from its collar to a short stake in the ground, so it is tied up. On the right, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt walks along pushing a teal pram. A sleeping baby lies in the pram under a pink blanket. There is a flower bed with mixed coloured flowers at bottom left and a green litter bin in the bottom-left corner. Nothing looks dangerous. The only slightly unusual thing is a goat tied up in a park.
