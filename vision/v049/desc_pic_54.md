# pic_54

## Free description
A back garden with a cream house on the left, a wooden fence, a gate and a path on the right. A man (or teenage boy) with short dark hair, green shirt and beige trousers sits on a wooden bench at the front left, reading a red book. Right next to him on the bench is a sandwich, and a blue bird is standing on the bench pecking at it — he seems not to notice, so the bird is stealing his lunch. On the right path, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, sweeps a pile of brown leaves with a broom. In the middle is a clothesline between two posts with blue jeans and a yellow striped towel hanging on it. A metal swing set with an empty red swing stands on the right, and there is a wheelbarrow and watering can on the lawn.
