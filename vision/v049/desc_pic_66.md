This is a back garden on a sunny day, with a tall wooden fence, a gate on the left, a paved path, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. An elderly man in a grey cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers stands in the middle and waters a flower bed with a green garden hose. The water arcs from the nozzle onto the flowers. The hose runs from a hose reel on the right, loops around the front of the flower bed and comes up to his hands. On the left of the middle ground, an orange cat crouches low and creeps toward a red bird that is pecking at a small pile of seeds on the lawn. The cat looks like it is about to pounce on the bird. A birdhouse stands on a tall pole near the left. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands at the bottom of the picture. Oddly, a cardboard box hangs in the sky at upper left, tilted, with nothing holding it up, as if it were floating or falling.
