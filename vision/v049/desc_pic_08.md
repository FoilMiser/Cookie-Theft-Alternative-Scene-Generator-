# Picture 08

A sunny back garden with no people. A cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, a wooden fence with a gate runs across the back, and a paved path runs down the right side. In the middle of the lawn, a small white puppy with brown patches and a blue collar is lying curled up on the grass with its eyes closed, apparently asleep. Behind it, a washing line between two wooden posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow top with red stripes. There is a colourful round flower bed by the house, a bush, two potted plants by the fence on the right, and a teal watering can on the grass in the front right. It is a calm, quiet scene and nothing looks wrong.
