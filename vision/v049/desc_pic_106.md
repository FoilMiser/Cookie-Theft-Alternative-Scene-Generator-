## Free description
A sunny, windy day in a park with a pond, rolling hills and a winding path. White wind lines streak across the sky and over the pond, and a few leaves are being blown through the air. A man with a moustache, in a red shirt and blue trousers, stands near the pond reaching up with one arm as his red cap is blown off his head and away to the upper right. He looks surprised. Further left, a girl in a purple top and teal trousers walks along the path eating a pink ice-cream cone, apparently not noticing. There is an empty bench, a green bin and a tall lamp post in the middle. It looks like the cap might end up in the pond.
