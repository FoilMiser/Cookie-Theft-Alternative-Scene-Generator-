## Free description
A windy day in a back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left and a wooden fence across the back; white wind lines blow across the sky. On the right, a man with a moustache in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is standing on the path reaching up with one arm, looking surprised, as his orange cap is blown off his head and flies away, with a few green leaves blowing with it. In the middle of the lawn an older man with grey hair and a moustache, wearing a brown waistcoat over a white shirt and dark trousers, stands with his hands on his hips — and he has an upside-down grey metal bucket on his head like a hat. An orange cat is walking along the top of the fence near the tree on the left. A washing line between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes, which are blowing sideways in the wind. The swing seat on the right is tilted, as if pushed by the wind. The strange thing is the man wearing a bucket on his head.
