# pic_100

## Free description
A busy sunny playground with a railing fence, a slide on the left, a sandpit at the front and a tree on the right. In the centre-front a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and has a hand over the eyes, crying, because the pink ice cream has fallen on the grass; a brown dog is licking up the dropped ice cream. Behind them a grey cat is creeping low toward a red bird that is pecking at seeds on the grass, as if about to pounce. On the right an old man in a cap sits on a bench reading a red book, and next to him on the bench is a sandwich that a small blue bird is pecking at without him noticing. In front of him a boy in a red cap and blue top holds up a kite reel; the string runs up to a purple kite that is stuck in the tree. A girl in a pink dress on the far left holds a yellow balloon. An orange cat is curled up asleep near the sandpit, and a white dog with brown patches is in a play bow by a tennis ball at the bottom right. A red bicycle stands on the right, and its wheels are drawn as squares, which is impossible for a working bike.
