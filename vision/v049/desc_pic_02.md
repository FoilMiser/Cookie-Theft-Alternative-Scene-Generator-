# Picture 02

## Free description
A sunny back garden with no people in it. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the right, a wooden fence with a gate runs across the back, and a paved path comes in from the gate down the left side. In the middle of the lawn there is a metal swing frame with a single red swing seat hanging still. A brown dog with a red collar is sitting next to the blue door of the house holding a yellow lead (leash) in its mouth, as if it wants to be taken for a walk. Near the house there is a round flower bed of colourful flowers. In the front of the picture, a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow sit on the grass. Nothing looks impossible; the scene is calm and waiting.
