## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the left and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. An old man in a blue cap, white shirt and brown vest stands at the right end of the fence, painting it blue with a roller. A few planks are already blue. At his feet are a paint tin and a paint tray with blue paint in it. A white dog with brown spots is walking away from the paint tray towards the left, leaving a trail of blue paw prints on the grass. It has obviously stepped in the paint, and the man has not noticed. On the left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps a small pile of leaves with a broom near the path. A watering can and an empty wheelbarrow are on the grass at the front, and a flower bed sits by the house.
