## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a house on the left, a wooden fence across the back and a gate and paved path on the right. A woman in a yellow dress holds a teal watering can over a flower bed. Instead of pouring down, the water shoots upward from the spout in a long arc, with drops flying off the top, which looks wrong or impossible. Behind her, a clothesline between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel. In the middle there is an empty metal swing set with a red seat. On the right, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers sweeps a pile of brown leaves on the path with a yellow broom. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands in the front centre. No one seems to be paying attention to anyone else.
