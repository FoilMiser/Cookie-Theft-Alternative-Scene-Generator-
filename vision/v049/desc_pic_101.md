# pic_101

## Free description
A sunny playground with a railing fence, a slide on the left, an empty swing set in the middle, a litter bin, a tree on the right and a sandpit at the front left. On the right a red seesaw is in use: an old man with grey hair and a blue cap, wearing a brown waistcoat, sits on the low left end with his feet on the ground. Oddly, he is reading an open book or newspaper instead of paying attention. A small girl in a pink dress with yellow shoes sits on the raised right end, high up, holding the handle with one hand and the other hand near her mouth, looking a bit worried or surprised. Because the man is heavier and not pushing off, she seems stuck up in the air. The swings are empty and nobody else is around.
