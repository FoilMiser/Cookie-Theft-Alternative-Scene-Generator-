# Picture 01

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path running down the right side. Near the house, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is bending over a round flower bed and watering the flowers, but he is pouring the water out of what looks like a grey frying pan or saucepan rather than a watering can. A stream of water curves from the pan onto the flowers. Behind him near the fence, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers is walking towards the right carrying a cardboard box in both hands. In the middle-right foreground, a small child in a yellow top and orange shorts is jumping in the air with both arms raised and a happy face; the shadow on the grass is below them, so they are off the ground. Two potted plants stand on the grass near the fence. The main odd thing is that the man is watering the flowers with a pan.
