## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, trees, a lamp post, a winding sandy path and a pond on the right. On the left, a garden sprinkler on a hose is spraying arcs of water to the right, and the water is landing on a cardboard box sitting on the grass beside the path. The box has a small puddle around it and looks wet. In the centre front, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is digging a hole with a shovel, with a pile of dirt next to it. Behind him, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands at the edge of the pond, fishing with a long rod; his line and red-and-white float are in the water, with ripples around the float. On the right, an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses is walking along, licking a pink ice-cream cone. Nobody seems to have noticed the box getting soaked. The box being sprayed by the sprinkler is the oddest thing in the picture.
