# pic_38

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left, a wooden fence, a gate and a path on the right. A woman with dark hair in a ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers stands by the flower bed holding a grey watering can. Strangely, the water from the can's spout goes up into the air in a tall arc away from the flowers, with drops at the top, instead of pouring down onto the flowers. That looks impossible for a watering can. On the right near the path, an elderly man with grey hair, a blue cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is sweeping a pile of leaves with a yellow broom. An empty wheelbarrow stands in the front centre, and there are two potted plants and a bush by the fence.
