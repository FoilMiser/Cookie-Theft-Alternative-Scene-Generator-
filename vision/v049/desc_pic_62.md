# pic_62

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a gate and path on the left and a cream house with a blue door on the right. In the middle a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is hanging wet washing on a line between two posts: a white T-shirt and a pair of blue jeans are pegged up and dripping water. A wooden laundry basket full of more clothes is on the grass under the line. On the left a small boy with dark skin in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts holds the strings of a red and a blue balloon, and is reaching up with his other arm toward a purple balloon that has floated away high into the sky with its string trailing. He looks upset, as if he has just let go of it. At the front a brown dog is lying down asleep on the grass. There is also a hose reel, an empty bench, a flower bed and a teal watering can.
