## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a house on the left, a wooden fence across the back and a gate and path on the right. An older man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat is painting the fence blue with a paint roller. A section of the fence behind him is already blue. A paint tray of blue paint sits on the grass near him, and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the tray to a small white dog with brown spots. The dog's paws are blue, so it has stepped in the paint and is walking it across the lawn. In the middle, a girl in a purple top and teal trousers holds the nozzle of a green garden hose, but no water is coming out. A brown dog in the front centre is standing on the hose, which would block the water. The hose runs from a reel at the front left, under the dog, to the girl. A cardboard box is hanging in the sky near the sun at the top right with nothing holding it, which looks impossible. An empty wheelbarrow stands in the front, and there is a flower bed by the house.
