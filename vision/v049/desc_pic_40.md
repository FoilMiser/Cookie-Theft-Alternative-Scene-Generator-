## Free description
A sunny park with a pond, a winding path, a lamp post, trees and hills. On the left path, a boy with dark skin and curly hair in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is crying, rubbing his eye, and holding an empty ice-cream cone; his pink ice cream has fallen onto the path. A brown dog is licking up the dropped pink ice cream. Behind him, an elderly man in a blue cap and brown waistcoat is raking leaves beside the path. In the front centre, a lawn sprinkler attached to a hose is spraying arcs of water, and the water is landing on a cardboard box, which is soaked and sitting in a puddle. On the right, a girl in a purple top and teal trousers sits drinking from a red cup through a straw, but there is nothing under her to sit on. Beside her, a man in a green shirt walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. A green rubbish bin stands near the lamp post.
