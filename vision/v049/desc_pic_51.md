## Free description
A back garden on a sunny day, with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back and a gate on the right. A woman with dark hair in a ponytail, purple top and teal trousers, stands on the stone path on the right, sweeping a small pile of dust or dirt toward a red dustpan. Oddly, she is sweeping with a giant silver fork instead of a broom — the fork is about as big as she is. A grey cat walks along the top of the fence. An orange cat lies curled up asleep on the grass in the middle. A flower bed full of colourful flowers is in front of the house, and there are two potted plants, a wheelbarrow and a teal watering can on the lawn.
