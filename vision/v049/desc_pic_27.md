This is a sunny town street with a row of coloured shops behind a paved square. On the left, a man in a red shirt and blue trousers stands with his hands on his hips, wearing an upside-down metal bucket on his head. That is clearly silly. In the middle, an old woman is watering a box of flowers with a watering can. Further right, a child in a yellow shirt is rubbing an eye as if crying and holding an empty ice-cream cone. A small girl in a pink dress points up at it, while a woman in a purple top sweeps leaves with a broom. In front, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie scatters seeds from a bag for a blue bird. A grey cat is creeping towards a red bird that is pecking at seeds at the bottom, as if about to pounce.
