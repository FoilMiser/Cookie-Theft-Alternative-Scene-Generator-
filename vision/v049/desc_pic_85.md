On the left, a girl in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence blue with a paint roller; three fence boards are already blue. A paint tin and a paint tray with blue paint are on the grass next to her. A white dog with brown spots is walking away along the path, leaving a trail of blue paw prints from the paint tray, so it has stepped in the paint. In the middle, a boy in a green T-shirt is walking to the right while reading an open book, and a cardboard box sits on the grass right in front of him, so he looks about to trip over it. On the right, a man in a red T-shirt and jeans is watering the flower bed with a teal watering can, but the stream of water arcs upward out of the spout into the air with drops at the top, as if water were flowing up rather than down onto the flowers.
