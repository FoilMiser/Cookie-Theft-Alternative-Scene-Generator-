# Picture 78

## Free description
A sunny park with rolling hills, a winding sandy path on the left, a tall black lamp post with a green bin beside it in the middle, and a pond with bulrushes on the right. A wooden ladder stands upright on the grass beside the path, leaning against nothing at all, which looks impossible because it would fall over. In front of the pond, a man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket, smiling with his eyes closed, one hand resting by a wicker picnic basket. To his right, a woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers walks to the right pushing a teal pram with a sleeping baby under a pink polka-dot blanket. There is a grey rock at the front, a large tree and a bush on the far left, and a smaller tree and bushes on the far right. Everyone looks calm and relaxed. The free-standing ladder is the odd thing.
