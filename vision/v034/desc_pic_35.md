## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, a sandy path winding from the bottom toward the back, a large tree on the left and a smaller tree on the right. There are no people in the picture. A wooden ladder stands upright on the grass just right of the path, leaning slightly, but it is not leaning against anything - its top ends in mid-air - so it looks as if it should fall over. A tall black lamp post stands in the centre with a green rubbish bin beside it. On the right is an oval pond with reeds/bulrushes at both ends and a white duck with an orange beak swimming on it. In the foreground a garden sprinkler, connected to a dark hose that runs off the right edge, is spraying arcs of water to the left, and the water is landing on a cardboard box that sits on the grass; the box is dripping and sitting in a small puddle. A grey rock lies in the front centre. The odd things are the free-standing ladder and the box being soaked by the sprinkler.
