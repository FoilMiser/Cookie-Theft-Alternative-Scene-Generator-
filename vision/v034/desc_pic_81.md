# Picture 81

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate at the far left with a paved path down the left side, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the path at the left, an older man with grey hair, a flat cap, a white shirt and brown waistcoat is sweeping a pile of autumn leaves with a yellow broom. In the middle, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers holds the nozzle of a long garden hose out in front of him with both hands, but only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel by the house, along the grass at the front, and then up to his hands. A white goat with small dark horns stands on the grass between him and the flower bed, and its hooves seem to be standing on the hose, which would explain why the water has stopped. A clothesline behind holds a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans. Two potted plants stand by the fence and a colourful flower bed is in front of the house. A goat in a back garden is unusual.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Is the goat on the hose? Its back hoof sits right on the hose and the hose bulges there, so one reading is that the goat is blocking the water. The other is that the hose just runs past its feet and the water is off for another reason.
- The man's face is mostly turned away. He may be puzzled at the nozzle, or about to spray the goat or the flowers.
- The goat is close to the flower bed but facing away from it, so it is not clearly about to eat the flowers.
