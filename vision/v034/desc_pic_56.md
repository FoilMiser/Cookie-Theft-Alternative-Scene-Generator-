## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills, trees and bushes, a blue pond on the left and a winding sand-coloured path on the right. A white duck swims on the pond. At the front centre a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie holds a brown paper bag and scatters seeds or crumbs from his other hand; the crumbs fall in a dotted trail to the ground, where a blue bird and a red bird are pecking them up at his feet. On the right an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt walks along by the path pushing a teal pram, in which a baby sleeps under a pink spotted blanket. An empty wooden bench stands in the middle-right, and a tall street lamp and a green rubbish bin stand behind it. A round bed of colourful flowers is at the front left. It is a calm scene; nothing looks like it is about to go wrong.
