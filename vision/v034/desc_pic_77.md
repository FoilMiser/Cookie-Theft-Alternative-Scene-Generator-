# Picture 77

## Free description
A sunny, empty back garden with no people in it. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door, with a wooden bench in front of it and a round flower bed of bright flowers nearby. A wooden fence runs across the back, with a gate at the far right and a paved path leading from the gate down the right side. Behind the house is a large tree, and sitting on the end of one of its branches is an orange goldfish, which is impossible since fish live in water. A birdhouse on a tall pole stands near the middle-right, with two potted plants at its foot. At the front there is a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can on the grass near the path. Apart from the fish in the tree, the scene looks like an ordinary quiet garden.
