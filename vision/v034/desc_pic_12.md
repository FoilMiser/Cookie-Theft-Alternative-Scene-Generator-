## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence and trees behind it, a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right, and a wooden gate on the left. A paved path runs from the gate down to the bottom left. An older man stands in the middle-right of the lawn and waters a bed of colourful flowers with a garden hose. He wears a grey-blue flat cap, a white shirt, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers. The hose runs from a reel on the ground near a wooden bench by the house, across the grass, to the nozzle in his hands. A stream of water arcs from the nozzle onto the flowers. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands in the front centre. Two potted plants and a birdhouse on a tall pole are on the left side of the lawn. Everything looks ordinary, and nothing seems wrong.
