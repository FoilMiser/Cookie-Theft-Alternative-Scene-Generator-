## Free description
This is a sunny town street with a row of colourful shop buildings (pink, pale green, peach and cream) with striped awnings, a paved pavement, a sun at top left and two clouds. On the left is a fruit and vegetable stall with crates of green, yellow, orange and red produce, and a red bird is perched on top of a black lamp post. In the front left an old woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt is walking while reading an open book, and a cardboard box is sitting on the pavement right in front of her, so she looks about to trip over it. In the middle-back a woman in a yellow dress is watering a planter box of flowers outside a shop with a teal watering can. In the centre an old man in a grey cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat sits on a bench eating a pink ice-cream cone, and a small white dog with a blue collar sits up on its hind legs next to the bench, looking at him as if begging for some. Behind him a teal bicycle is parked in a bike rack. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is holding a camera up to his face and taking a photo. There is also a tree, a green rubbish bin, a small cafe table with two chairs at the bottom right, a second cardboard box by the stall and a potted plant at the bottom left. Nothing looks impossible; the main "about to happen" moment is the old woman walking into the box.
