# Picture 04

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a house with a red roof and a blue door on the right, a sun at top left and two clouds. An orange cat is walking along the top of the fence, and a red-roofed birdhouse stands on a pole at the left. A purple kite is caught in the tree at top right, and a small girl in a pink dress standing by the fence is holding its string and pulling, apparently trying to free it. On the left by an open gate, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is raking a few leaves on the path, and a brown puppy lies asleep on the path below him. In the middle, an old person with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey shorts, stands with hands on hips wearing a metal bucket upside down on their head, which looks silly. Next to them is a swing set with one empty red swing. In the front centre, a woman in a yellow dress holds the end of a green garden hose and peers into the nozzle, where only a drop of water is coming out; the hose runs across the lawn and a white spotted dog with a blue collar is standing on it, so it looks like the dog is blocking the water and she might get sprayed when the dog steps off. On the right, a man in a red shirt and jeans sits on a bench reading a red book while a blue bird pecks at the sandwich lying beside him. At the front left there is a grey wheelbarrow whose wheel is square, which would not roll. There is also a round bed of colourful flowers and a hose reel next to the bench.

- The old person's lower garment could be grey shorts or a short skirt.
- The dog on the hose: it reads as the dog standing on the hose and stopping the water (hence the single drop), but it could also be read as the dog just standing next to it.
