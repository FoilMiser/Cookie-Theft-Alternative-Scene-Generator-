# Picture 08

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a house with a red roof and a blue door on the right, a sun at top left and two clouds. In the middle-back, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and black trousers is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; a section of the fence is already blue, and a small paint tin sits on the grass beside him. In the front, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is digging a hole in the lawn with a spade, with a pile of brown soil next to the hole. Up in the tree at top right, an orange fish is sitting on a branch, which is impossible because fish live in water. There is also a swing set with one empty red swing on the left, a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow on the grass at the front, a round bed of colourful flowers by the house, a paved path on the left and a wooden gate in the fence.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fish in the tree is clearly impossible/strange; it could be read as a deliberate "what's wrong" item or as a misplaced object.
- The fish could also be read as an orange leaf, fruit or bird at a glance, but the fins, tail and eye make it look like a fish.
- The painted section of fence is partly to the left of where the boy is standing, behind the swing frame, so it reads as the part he has already done.
- The woman's hole: she may be digging to plant something, but nothing to plant is shown nearby.
