# pic_72

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills, a pond on the left and a winding path on the right. An elderly man stands on the bank at the near side of the pond, fishing. He has grey hair, a grey flat cap, a brown waistcoat over a white shirt, and dark trousers. His fishing rod bends out over the water, and the line drops to a red-and-white float sitting in a small ripple. A white duck swims on the pond to the left of the float. In the middle there is a tall black lamppost with a green rubbish bin beside it. On the right, next to the path, a wooden ladder stands upright on its own on the grass, leaning slightly but not resting against anything. That looks impossible, because nothing is holding it up. There is a grey rock at the front, and trees and bushes around the edges.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The free-standing ladder is the obvious oddity. A ladder that tall cannot stand up by itself, and it is not touching the nearby tree. It could be read as about to fall over.
- The man stands very close to the water, with his legs drawn over the bottom edge of the pond outline. He reads as standing on the bank in front of the pond, but at first glance he could look as if he is at or in the water's edge.
- The fishing line is very thin and faint, so the float could at first be mistaken for something on its own in the water.
