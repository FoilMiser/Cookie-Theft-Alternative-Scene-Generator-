# Picture 07

## Free description
This is a sunny, mostly empty playground with a dark railing fence and bushes along the back, a sun at top left and two clouds. On the left there is a red climbing frame with a ladder and a yellow slide, and in the middle a swing set with one empty red swing; no one is using them. On the right there is a red seesaw. An old man with grey hair, a grey cap, a white shirt and a brown waistcoat sits on the low end of the seesaw, which is resting on the ground, and he is reading an open book. On the high end, up in the air, sits a small girl in a pink dress holding the handle, with her mouth open as if surprised, worried or calling out. Because the man is heavier and absorbed in his book, the girl seems to be stuck up in the air and he is not paying attention to her. There is also a green rubbish bin by the fence, a bush on the left, a tree on the right and a grey rock at the bottom left. Nothing looks physically impossible.
