# pic_16

## Free description
This is a back garden under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds, and there is no sun, so it looks like rain is coming. On the paved path at the front left, a woman or older girl in a yellow dress with dark hair sweeps dirt into a red dustpan. Oddly, she is not using a broom. She is using a huge silver fork, shaped like a giant dinner fork, with little dust puffs rising. In the centre a woman in a purple T-shirt and teal trousers looks up with her mouth open and points at the sky, probably at the dark clouds. Washing still hangs on the clothesline behind her: a yellow striped towel and a pair of blue jeans. On the right, by the blue door of the house, a brown-and-white dog sits holding a red lead in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. An empty wheelbarrow stands in the front centre, and a hose reel with the hose and its yellow nozzle lies on the grass at the front right. Two potted plants and a bed of colourful flowers complete the garden.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fork is the oddest thing. It looks like an oversized table fork (silver, thin rounded handle, four fine tines) used as a broom, which is not a real sweeping tool. Another reading is that it is a garden fork, but its shape and colour are those of cutlery.
- The woman in purple could be pointing at the storm clouds (warning of rain) or at the washing on the line (to bring it in). Her gaze is upward at the sky, so the clouds seem more likely.
- The person in yellow is about the same height as the woman in purple, so she could be an adult woman or a teenage girl.
- The dog holding its lead suggests it wants a walk, but nobody is paying attention to it.
