## Free description
A sunny playground with a dark metal railing fence and hedges behind; a large tree is on the left and a red-framed slide with a yellow-and-orange chute and a ladder is on the right. On the left a red seesaw is steeply tilted: a small girl in a pink dress sits at the high end, up in the air, holding the handle with her mouth open as if calling out or surprised. At the low end, near the ground, sits a grown man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers, calmly reading an open book on his lap and not looking at her. His weight keeps her stuck up in the air. In the centre a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands eating a pink ice-cream cone with his eyes closed. A green rubbish bin stands by the fence, a bush at the far right, and a grey rock at the bottom right. Nobody is on the slide.
