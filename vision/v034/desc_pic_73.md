## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. In front of the house, a woman with dark hair in a ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is watering the flower bed. Oddly, she is pouring the water out of a frying pan instead of a watering can. At the back, standing right in front of the fence, a small girl in a pink dress is flying a yellow kite. The kite is high in the sky on a long string, with a little bow tail. In the middle, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands on the grass at the front, and two potted plants sit by the fence. Apart from the frying pan, everything looks normal.
