# pic_21

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny shopping street. Behind a paved square stand four colourful shop buildings with striped awnings, a black street lamp and a fruit-and-vegetable market stall. On the left a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping a pile of autumn leaves with a broom. Next to him is a tree with a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in its branches, and a small girl in a pink dress is pointing up at it. In front of her an old man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat walks along carrying a yellow shopping bag. In the middle a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is jumping in the air with both arms up. A woman in a purple top sits on a bench reading a red book. Her sandwich is on the bench beside her, and a small blue bird standing right next to it looks about to peck at or steal it. Below the bench, a brown dog sits holding open a newspaper with its front paws as if reading it, which is impossible. Further right an older grey-haired woman with glasses is holding a watering can by a flower box, but the water arcs upward into the air instead of falling onto the flowers. On the right a man in a green shirt walks to the left reading an open book, and a cardboard box sits on the ground right in his path, so he looks about to trip over it.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Watering woman: the water stream curves up and away from the flowers, with drops at the top, instead of pouring down onto them. This could be an intended "wrong thing" (water flowing upward) or just a badly drawn arc. Either way it does not seem to reach the flowers.
- Dog with newspaper: this is clearly impossible, but the dog's head faces up and to the side rather than at the paper.
- Jumping boy: he could be jumping for joy, or trying to reach something. He is not near the tree with the ball, so he probably is not reaching for it.
- Blue bird: it could be about to steal the sandwich, or just standing beside it. Its beak is very close to the sandwich.
- Man with the book: he walks left with his eyes on the book, and the box is directly ahead of his feet. I read this as "about to trip", but he could also be walking past it.
- The girl may be pointing at the ball in the tree or at the red bird in the sky. Her arm points up and right, toward the tree.
