# Picture 01

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park, with a bright sun at top left, two white clouds, rolling green hills and a line of bushes in the background. A sandy path winds from the back of the park down to the bottom of the picture, with a wooden bench beside it and a tall black lamp post in the middle. On the far left a small girl in a yellow top and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and has her other hand up at her face, as if she is crying or upset. Just in front of her, a brown dog with a red collar or bandana is licking a pink puddle of melted ice cream with sprinkles on the ground, so it looks like she dropped her ice cream and the dog is eating it. In the middle-right, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers stands at the edge of a pond holding a brown paper bag and tossing crumbs into the water. A white duck on the pond swims toward the crumbs floating near it. In front on the right, a woman in a yellow dress with long black hair and red shoes is watering a round bed of colourful flowers with a teal watering can, and water is pouring out onto the flowers. There is a grey rock at the bottom centre and a green rubbish bin at the bottom right. Nothing looks impossible; the scene reads as an ordinary park moment, with the dropped ice cream as the small mishap.
