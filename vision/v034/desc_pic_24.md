This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and paved path at the far left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the left of the lawn stands a dark-grey A-frame swing set with one empty red swing. Next to the gate is a tall pole with a small red-roofed birdhouse on top. It shoots up out of the spout in an arc, with drops flying above it, which looks wrong or impossible. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands in front, and an empty wooden bench is on the right in front of the house. No one else is there and nothing else seems about to happen.
