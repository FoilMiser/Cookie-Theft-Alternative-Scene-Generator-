# pic_63

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a flower bed, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, two potted plants and a paved path on the right. A woman with long dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence blue with a roller on a long pole; one section of the fence is already blue and a paint tin stands on the grass by her feet. In the middle front, a lawn sprinkler attached to a hose is spraying arcs of water straight onto a cardboard box sitting on the grass; the box is dripping wet and standing in a puddle, so it is getting soaked. On the path at the right, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, is sweeping a small pile of dirt into a red dustpan. Oddly, she is not using a broom: she is sweeping with a giant silver dinner fork, as big as a broom. A little puff of dust rises from the pile she is sweeping.
