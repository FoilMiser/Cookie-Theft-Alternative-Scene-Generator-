# Picture 80

## Free description
A sunny playground with a dark metal railing fence across the back and bushes behind it. On the left is a red seesaw on a grey triangular stand. A small girl in a pink dress sits on the raised end, high in the air, holding the handle with her mouth open in an "o". On the lower end sits an older man with grey hair, a grey cap, a white shirt and brown waistcoat, calmly reading an open book with his feet on the ground, so the girl is stuck up in the air. In the front middle, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts with braided hair stands eating a pink ice cream cone with eyes closed, enjoying it. On the right is a slide with a red frame, a yellow-and-orange chute and a ladder, and in front of it a wooden sandpit holding a red bucket and a blue spade. A green bin stands by the fence, there is a grey rock at front right, a tree on the far left and a bush on the far right.

## Inventory
- Girl, left on the seesaw's high end: small girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes, holding the seesaw handle, mouth open as if surprised or calling out.
- Older man, centre-left on the seesaw's low end: grey hair, grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat, dark trousers, sitting and reading a book, not looking at the girl.
- Child, centre-front: dark-skinned child with braided hair, yellow top, orange shorts, standing and eating a pink ice cream cone, eyes closed.
- Seesaw: left, red plank on a grey triangular pivot, tipped down on the man's side.
- Open book: held by the older man.
- Ice cream cone: held by the child in front, pink scoop.
- Slide: right, red frame, orange and yellow chute, ladder at the back, nobody on it.
- Sandpit: front-right, wooden frame of sand with a red bucket and a blue spade in it.
- Green bin: centre-right, by the fence.
- Grey rock: front-right.
- Tree: far left. Bush: far right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl's open mouth could mean she is calling to the man to let her down, or simply enjoying being up high.
- The man may be keeping the girl up in the air without noticing because he is reading, or he may be holding the seesaw still on purpose.
- The child's closed eyes could mean enjoying the ice cream or squinting.
- Nothing looks physically impossible: the heavier man is on the low end.
