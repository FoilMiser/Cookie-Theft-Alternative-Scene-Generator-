## Free description
This is a sunny back garden seen from the other side: a cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, and a wooden fence runs across the back with a gate and path on the right. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle. A section of planks to his left is already blue. Behind him on the grass are a paint tin and a paint tray with blue paint. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has blue feet, and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the lawn to it. So the dog has stepped in the paint and is walking off, spreading it. On the path on the right a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is sweeping a pile of autumn leaves with a broom. There is also a flower bed by the house, a birdhouse on a tall pole, two potted plants, a wheelbarrow and a teal watering can.
