# Picture 76

## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, a pond with bulrushes at the left, a tall black lamp post in the middle and a sandy path curving from the back to the front right. On the right, a purple kite is stuck in the leaves of a big tree; its string runs down to a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers who stands by the path, looking up and holding the string with his arm raised, apparently trying to free it. In the foreground, an older man with grey hair, a flat cap, a white shirt and brown waistcoat walks to the left pushing a teal pram with a sleeping baby tucked under a pink polka-dot blanket. Further back on the right, a woman with long dark hair in a yellow dress and red shoes walks to the right carrying a cardboard box. A small white puppy with brown patches lies on the grass in the middle, apparently asleep. There is a wooden bench beside the path, a grey rock at the front, and a flower bed at the front left. Nothing looks impossible, but the kite is clearly caught in the tree.
