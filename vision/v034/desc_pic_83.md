# Picture 83

## Free description
A busy, sunny park with hills, a pond on the left, a tall lamp post with a green bin in the middle and a sandy path curving down the right. At the front left, an older man with grey hair, a flat cap, a white shirt and brown waistcoat is watering a flower bed by pouring water out of a black frying pan, which is a strange thing to water with. At the far left, a white dog with brown patches sits upright reading an open newspaper, which is impossible. On the pond, a white duck swims toward small floating crumbs, and a small girl in a pink dress stands on the grass nearby holding something out, apparently feeding it. In the front middle, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts stands with a hand on their head looking upset, holding an empty ice cream cone. The pink ice cream has fallen on the ground, and a brown dog with a red collar is sniffing or licking it. Behind them, a white goat with dark horns eats a tuft of grass. An older woman with grey hair, glasses and a teal cardigan sweeps a pile of leaves on the path. On the far right, a purple kite is stuck in a big tree, and a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie holds its string and looks up at it. A blue bird flies in the sky near the lamp post, and there is an empty bench beside the path.
