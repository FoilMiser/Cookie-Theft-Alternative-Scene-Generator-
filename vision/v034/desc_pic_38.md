## Free description
A sunny but very windy back garden, with white wind lines drawn in the sky and across the fence. The wooden gate and paving path are on the left and the cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the right. A man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers stands in the middle-left of the lawn with both arms thrown up and a startled face, because his blue cap has been blown off his head and is flying away to the right, along with a couple of leaves. On the path at the left a woman (or older girl) with long dark hair in a ponytail, wearing a yellow dress and red shoes, is sweeping a small pile of autumn leaves with a broom and looking toward the man. The washing on the line - a yellow red-striped towel and blue jeans - is being blown sideways by the wind. A round flower bed with colourful flowers is by the house, and a teal hose reel with a hose and yellow nozzle lies on the grass at the right. The wind is the main event.
