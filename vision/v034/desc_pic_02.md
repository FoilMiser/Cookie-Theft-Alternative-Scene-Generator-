# Picture 02

## Free description
This is a sunny town street with a row of colourful two-storey shop buildings (pink, pale green, peach and cream) with striped awnings, a paved pavement in front, a sun at top left and two clouds. On the left is a fruit and vegetable stall with a striped awning and crates of green, yellow and red produce; an old man in a grey cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is walking forward while reading an open book, and a cardboard box sits on the ground right in front of him, so he looks about to trip over it. Behind him a woman in a yellow dress is sweeping a pile of autumn leaves with a broom near a black lamp post, and a red bird is perched on top of the lamp. In the middle-back a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is waving one hand up in the air (motion lines show she is waving), perhaps at someone in a window. In the centre a man in a green shirt sits on a bench reading a red book, while a blue bird on the bench beside him pecks at his sandwich, apparently stealing it without him noticing. On the right a man in a red shirt and blue jeans walks along eating a pink ice-cream cone. A beach ball (blue and white) is stuck in the branches of a tree on the right, and a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie in front is pointing up at it. Two things look impossible: a red bicycle at the front centre has square wheels, and a brown dog at the bottom right is sitting up and reading a newspaper like a person.
