## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the house (cream walls, red roof, blue door) on the left and a wooden gate with a paved path on the right. An older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses stands at the back fence, painting it blue with a paint roller on a long handle. A section of about three fence boards is already blue. A paint can with blue paint and a paint tray sit on the grass at her feet. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has evidently stepped in the paint tray. A trail of blue paw prints leads from the tray across the grass to the dog, which is now walking onto the path near the gate with blue paint on its paws. The woman is facing the fence and does not seem to have noticed. An empty wheelbarrow and a teal watering can are on the grass at the front, and a bed of colourful flowers grows in front of the house.
