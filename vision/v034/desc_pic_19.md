# pic_19

## Free description
This is a sunny but windy day in a back garden. White wind streaks are drawn in the sky and across the middle of the picture. The house (cream walls, red roof, blue door) is on the left, and a wooden gate with a paved path is on the right. In the centre an older woman with grey hair and round glasses stands with both arms thrown up high and her mouth open in surprise. Just above her and to the left, a blue hat is tumbling through the air, blown off by the wind, along with a few small brown and green leaves. She seems to be trying to grab it or reacting to it flying away. She wears a teal top, a grey skirt and brown shoes. A bed of colourful flowers grows in front of the house, two potted plants stand on the right of the lawn, and a teal watering can sits at the front right by the path.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The woman's raised arms could mean she is trying to catch the hat, or that she is throwing her hands up in dismay.
- The hat is drawn against the fence, high above her head. That seems intended to show it flying away and does not look like a mistake.
