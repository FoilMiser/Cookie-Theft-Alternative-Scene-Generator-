# pic_74

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a gate and paved path on the left and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. Three people are doing garden jobs. On the far left, a person in a green top and beige trousers stands on the path, sweeping a small pile of dirt and pebbles towards a red dustpan. Strangely, they are using a giant silver dinner fork instead of a broom, and a little puff of dust rises. In the middle at the back, a young man or boy in a red shirt and blue trousers is painting the wooden fence blue with a roller on a long handle. He has already painted one section, and a tin of blue paint stands on the grass near him. On the right, a woman with dark hair in a ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is watering the flower bed in front of the house with a garden hose. The hose runs across the grass from a hose reel. An empty grey wheelbarrow is at the front, and two potted plants sit by the fence.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The giant fork is the main oddity: it is a kitchen-style fork the size of a broom, used for sweeping. It could also be read as a rake drawn in a fork shape, but it looks like a dinner fork.
- There is a dark round dot just above and behind the sweeping person's head. It could be the knob of the gate behind them, or it could be read as a hair bun. So the person could be read as a man with short hair or as a woman with her hair in a bun.
- The sweeper's top has a frilly or uneven neckline, which adds to the uncertainty about whether this is a man or a woman.
- The painter could be read as a teenage boy or a young man.
