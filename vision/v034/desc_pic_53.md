## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a wooden gate on the left and the side of a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the right. On the paved path at the left, an elderly man in a grey flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers sweeps a small pile of autumn leaves with a yellow broom. In the middle-right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands holding a garden hose and sprays a stream of water onto a round bed of colourful flowers. The hose runs from his hand around the flower bed to a hose reel beside it. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands on the grass at the front centre, and an empty wooden bench is at the front right beside the house. Two potted plants and some bushes sit near the fence. Everyone is busy with garden chores; nothing looks like it is about to go wrong.
