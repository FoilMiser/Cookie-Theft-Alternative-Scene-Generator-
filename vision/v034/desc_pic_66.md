## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with no people or animals in it at all. A tall wooden fence runs across the back, with a darker wooden gate in it on the far left. A pale paved path leads from that gate down and out of the bottom-left of the picture. On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue front door with a grey step. In front of the house there is a round flower bed full of colourful flowers, and in the bottom-right corner there is an empty wooden bench. Two potted plants stand on the grass near the fence, and a teal watering can stands on the grass near the path. The strange thing is a closed brown cardboard box hanging tilted in mid-air in the sky, above the fence near the middle. Nobody is there to have thrown it, and nothing is holding it up. It looks as if it is falling or flying through the air.
