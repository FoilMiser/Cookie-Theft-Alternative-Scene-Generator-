## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and path on the left, two potted plants, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, with a flower bed in front of it. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, stands in the middle holding the end of a garden hose up close to her face, peering into the nozzle as if wondering why no water is coming out. The hose runs from her hands down to the ground, along the lawn, and back to a hose reel next to the house. A white goat with small horns is standing on the hose, with its back hooves right on top of it, so it seems to be squashing the hose and blocking the water. The hose looks fatter on the reel side of the goat than on the woman's side. It looks like the moment the goat steps off, the water will squirt straight into the woman's face.
