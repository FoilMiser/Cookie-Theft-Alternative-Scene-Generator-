# Picture 82

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, a gate at the far right and a paved path running down the right side. In the middle, a woman with long dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes is drinking from a red cup through a straw. She is in a sitting position with her knees bent, but there is no chair, bench or anything under her, so she is sitting on thin air. On the path at the right, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt is sweeping a pile of autumn leaves with a yellow broom. At the front there is a grey wheelbarrow, and on the left a hose reel with its hose and yellow nozzle lying on the grass. A round flower bed with colourful flowers is next to the house, and two potted plants and some bushes are near the fence. The woman sitting on nothing is the impossible part.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The woman in yellow is clearly drawn sitting but has no chair. Either the chair is missing (impossible) or she is squatting, though her pose looks like sitting on a chair.
- Her feet touch the ground, so she is not floating overall. Only her seat is unsupported.
