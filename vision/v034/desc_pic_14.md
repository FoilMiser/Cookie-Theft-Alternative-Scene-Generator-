A child in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts sits on a wooden bench at the front left and reads a red book. A girl in pink sits on a swing on the right. Small motion lines beside the swing suggest she is swinging. A clothesline between two posts in the centre holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow striped towel or shirt. A teal watering can stands at the front right near the path.
