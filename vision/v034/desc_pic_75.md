# Picture 75

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate at the far left, a paved path running from the gate down the left side, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the left, a small child in a yellow top and orange shorts stands on the path with one arm stretched out, having just thrown a red ball; a dotted line traces the ball's arc up through the air. A woman with long dark hair in a yellow dress and red shoes stands on the grass facing the child, both arms held out in front of her with hands cupped, ready to catch the ball, which is in the air just in front of her. In the middle of the garden, a man in a white shirt, brown waistcoat with braces and dark trousers stands with his hands on his hips, wearing an upside-down metal bucket on his head like a hat. Behind him, between two clothesline posts, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers reaches up with both hands to the line, pegging up (or taking down) a white T-shirt; a pair of blue jeans already hangs on the line. A teal watering can sits on the grass near the path, and a round flower bed full of colourful flowers is in front of the house. The odd thing is the man wearing a bucket on his head.
