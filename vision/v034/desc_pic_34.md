# pic_34

## Free description
A sunny back garden with the cream house (red roof, blue door) on the left and the wooden gate and paving path on the right. An elderly man with grey hair and a short beard, wearing a grey flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers, stands at the flower bed by the house holding a teal watering can. Strangely, the water does not pour down from the spout: it shoots upward in a curved stream from the can, with drops spraying out at the top, like a fountain. In the middle-right a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is jumping in the air with both arms stretched up, eyes closed and smiling; his shadow is on the grass beneath him. An orange cat is walking along the top of the fence above and to the right of the boy. A washing line with blue jeans and a yellow red-striped towel stands in the middle. An empty wooden bench is at the bottom left and an empty grey wheelbarrow is at the front.
