# pic_55

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a wooden gate on the right and the side of a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the left. A brown dog with a red collar sits by the blue door holding a red lead in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. In the middle a woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers stands with her arms stretched out, and a red-and-white ball is in the air right by her hands. A dashed line curves from the ball down to a small girl in a pink dress, who has her arm stretched out, so the two are playing catch. At the front centre a white dog with brown spots crouches in a play bow with its nose at a yellow tennis ball, its tail wagging. On the paved path at the right an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt sweeps a pile of autumn leaves with a yellow broom. A teal watering can stands on the grass near the path, and a flower bed and a bush are in front of the house.
