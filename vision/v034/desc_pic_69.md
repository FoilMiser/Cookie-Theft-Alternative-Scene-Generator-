## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left. A tall wooden fence runs across the back, with a gate on the far right and a paved path leading from it to the bottom right. A woman with dark hair in a ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers stands by the flower bed in front of the house. She is watering the flowers with a teal watering can, and you can see the water pouring onto them. At the front centre, an orange cat lies on the grass with its eyes closed, curled up or rolled over, apparently asleep or lazing. A little to its right is a red pet bowl full of food. Two potted plants stand by the fence. The very odd thing is an orange fish sitting up in the branches of the tree behind the house, where a fish could not be.
