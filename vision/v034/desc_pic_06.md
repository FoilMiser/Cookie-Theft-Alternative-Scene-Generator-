This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a house with a red roof and a blue door on the right, a tree and bushes, a sun at top left and two clouds. On the left, near a wooden gate at the end of a paved path, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers stands with his hands on his hips wearing a metal bucket upside down on his head, which looks silly. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers kneels on the grass beside a round flower bed, holding or placing a pot with a pink flower in it, as if she is about to plant it. In the front there is a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow lying on the grass, and an empty wooden bench sits by the house at the bottom right. The scene is a calm gardening/chores scene; the only odd thing is the bucket worn as a hat.
