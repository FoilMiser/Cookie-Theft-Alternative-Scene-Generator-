This is a sunny town street or pedestrian square in front of a row of four coloured shop buildings with striped awnings. In the middle a girl in a pink dress sits on a bench eating something pink (looks like an ice cream), and a white dog with brown ears and a blue collar sits up on its back legs in front of her, begging and looking up at her. Further right there is a market stall with crates of red, orange, yellow and green fruit or vegetables, with another cardboard box beside it. A potted plant sits at the bottom right corner and a flower box is near the shops.
