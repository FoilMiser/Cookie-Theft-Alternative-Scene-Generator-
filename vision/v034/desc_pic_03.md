# Picture 03

## Free description
This is a sunny playground in a park, fenced off at the back by a dark railing with bushes behind it, with a sun at top left, two clouds and a blue bird flying at top right. On the left there is a red climbing frame with a ladder and a yellow slide, and in the middle a swing set with one empty red swing; nobody is using either. On the right, a small girl in a pink dress stands near a tree flying a yellow kite with a bow tail, and the long kite string runs from her hands up into the sky. Next to her an old man with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey shorts, sits on a bench drinking from a cup through a straw. In the front, a girl in a yellow top and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and has a hand up to her face as if crying, while a brown dog with a red collar licks a pink puddle of melted ice cream with sprinkles at her feet, so it looks like she dropped her ice cream and the dog is eating it. On the left near the slide, a white dog with a blue collar is sitting up and reading an open newspaper, which is impossible for a real dog. At the front left there is a sandpit with a red bucket and a blue spade in it, a grey rock beside it, and a green rubbish bin by the fence.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- White dog reading a newspaper: impossible behaviour; clearly strange.
- The girl's raised hand: could be crying/wiping her eyes or just holding her head in dismay at the dropped ice cream.
- It is implied, not shown, that the ice cream fell from her cone; the empty cone plus the puddle beside her make this the natural reading.
- The blue bird is quite close to the kite; it could be read as just flying by or as heading toward the kite/string.
- The kite string is a single thin line from the kite to the girl; it is continuous and looks correct.
