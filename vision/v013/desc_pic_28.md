# Picture 28

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the sun in the top left and two white clouds. On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door; a tall wooden fence runs across the back with a gate on the left, a paved path leads from the gate down the left side, and a small birdhouse on a pole stands behind the fence on the left. A woman in a yellow dress with dark hair and red shoes is standing at the fence, painting it blue with a paintbrush; a section of about three boards is already blue, and a grey paint can with blue paint is on the grass beside her. Strangely, a cardboard box is hanging in the middle of the sky above the fence, tilted, with nothing holding it up - it is either floating or falling, which looks impossible. A teal watering can sits on the grass in the front left, and a round flower bed is in front of the house. In the bottom right a small white puppy with a brown ear is lying on the grass, apparently asleep. The woman is the only person in the picture.
