# pic_09

## Free description
A busy, sunny shopping street with four colourful shops (cream, peach, green, pink) and a lot going on at once. On the left a teenager in a blue hoodie points up at a blue-and-white ball stuck in the branches of a tree. In the middle a little girl in a pink dress holds a blue and a yellow balloon and reaches up toward a red balloon floating loose near a shop door, so she seems to have lost it. An older man with a white beard, cap and brown waistcoat is watering a flower box with a teal watering can. A red bird sits on top of the street lamp, and a woman in a yellow dress on the right is waving at someone (motion lines by her hand). In the front a boy in a yellow shirt is rubbing his eye and crying, holding an empty ice-cream cone. His pink scoop has fallen on the ground and a brown dog with a red collar is eating it. Several things are clearly wrong or impossible. A red bicycle in the bottom left has square wheels. The man in the red shirt at the fruit stall is sweeping a pile of dirt into a red dustpan with a giant fork instead of a broom. An orange cat lies curled up asleep in the middle of the pavement.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The bicycle's wheels are square, which is impossible for a working bike. The bike also stands upright on its own with no kickstand visible.
- The giant fork is far too big and is the wrong tool for sweeping. It could also be read as raking or digging the dirt rather than sweeping it.
- The red balloon floats a long way from the girl, next to the shop door. It could be her lost balloon, or a balloon tied to or decorating the shop door. Its string ends in mid-air, so I read it as loose.
- The blue object in the tree is probably a ball, but it could be read as a balloon or a round ornament.
- The cat could be sleeping, or lying hurt in the middle of the walkway. It looks relaxed, so asleep is more likely.
- The boy might be crying or just rubbing his eye. The empty cone and the dropped scoop suggest crying over the lost ice cream.
- The woman in yellow is waving, but it isn't clear at whom. It could be the man at the stall or the viewer.
- It isn't clear where the pile of dirt on the pavement came from.
