## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tree behind the house, a wooden fence and gate across the back, and a paved path down the right side. An orange fish is sitting in the leaves of the tree behind the house, which is impossible. At the front left, an older man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat and white shirt sits on a bench eating a pink ice cream cone, and a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar stretches up toward him, probably wanting the ice cream. A green garden hose runs from a hose reel next to the flower bed across the lawn to a man in an orange cap, orange high-visibility vest and blue trousers, who is holding the nozzle and peering at it because only a drip is coming out. A white goat with small horns is standing on the hose, and the hose bulges just behind the goat, so the goat seems to be blocking the water. It looks like when the goat steps off, the water may suddenly spray the man in the face. There is also a grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles at the bottom, a teal watering can by the path, and two potted plants near the fence.
