# Picture 15

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park, with green hills and a line of bushes in the background, a bright sun in the top right and two white clouds. On the left there is a round blue pond with a few bulrushes (cattails) at its edges. A woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers stands on the grass just in front of the pond, holding a pink ice-cream cone up to her mouth. Her eyes are closed and she looks like she is enjoying it. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is pushing a teal-and-navy pram or stroller with a baby inside. She is walking to the left, at the edge of a winding sandy footpath, and the pram is on the grass. In the middle of the picture a tall black lamp post stands on the grass, and next to it a wooden ladder stands tilted upright in the open grass with nothing to lean against, which looks impossible. There is an empty wooden bench beside the path, a bed of colourful flowers in the bottom left, a green bin at the bottom-left edge, and large trees on the left and right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The ladder is standing up on its own at a slant in open grass, not touching the lamp post, a tree or any wall. It looks impossible, or like it is about to fall over. I can't tell why it is there (maybe left behind by a worker).
- The ice-cream woman's upper body overlaps the pond in the picture, but her feet and shadow are on the grass in front of it, so I read her as standing in front of the pond rather than in it.
- The woman with the pram is drawn mostly from behind/side, so her face is hard to see. She seems to be walking slightly off the path, with the pram on the grass.
- The baby is very small and is just a round face under the hood. It could also be read as a doll or a bundle.
