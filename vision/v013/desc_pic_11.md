# pic_11

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back and a gate in the far right corner reached by a paved path. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, is watering a round flower bed of colourful flowers. The odd thing is that she is pouring the water out of a black frying pan, held out by its handle, with a stream of water arcing from the pan onto the flowers. Meanwhile a real teal watering can sits unused on the grass in the bottom right. In the front a white goat with small horns has its head down, eating a tuft of grass. Next to the goat is an empty grey wheelbarrow. A blue bird flies across the sky near the top right. Two potted plants stand on the lawn near the fence. The frying pan used as a watering can is the clearly wrong or impossible part, and a goat loose in a back garden is unusual too.
