# Picture 24

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a sun in the top right and two white clouds. On the left is a cream house with a red roof and a blue door; a brown-and-white dog sits on the doorstep holding its red leash in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. There is a round flower bed next to the house, and across the middle of the garden is a washing line between two wooden posts with a pair of blue jeans, a yellow towel with red stripes and a white T-shirt pegged on it. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands under the line reaching up with both hands to peg up the white T-shirt. To the right, a woman with long dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers has her hands out ready to catch a red-and-white ball that is flying through the air; a dotted line shows it was thrown by a small child in a yellow top and orange shorts who is on the path by the garden gate at the right. In the front right a boy in a red shirt and blue jeans is sweeping the path with a yellow broom. On the grass at the bottom centre there is a red bowl of pet food. Nothing looks impossible.
