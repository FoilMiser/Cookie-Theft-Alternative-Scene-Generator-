# Picture 07

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and blue door, enclosed by a wooden fence with a gate on the right and a birdhouse on a pole behind it. An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, is painting the fence dark blue with a paint roller on a long handle; one section of the fence is already blue, and a paint can and paint tray sit on the grass at her feet. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has evidently stepped in the paint tray, because a trail of blue paw prints leads from the tray to the dog and its paws are blue. Next to it, a white goat with its head down is eating a tuft of grass. A little girl in a pink dress is jumping in the air with her arms up near the house, and on the right by the gate a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a red and a purple balloon while reaching up for a blue balloon that is floating away. At the bottom left a young man in a blue hoodie sits on a bench reading a red book, beside a garden hose reel and a flower bed. In the front, a black cat is creeping toward a blue bird pecking on the grass, and a red bird flies in the sky above the house. Two things are impossible: a brown dog with a red collar is sitting on the grass reading a newspaper, and the grey wheelbarrow in the front centre has a square wheel.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The animal eating grass in the centre: it looks like a white goat (small horns, no spots), but could be read as a white dog or a lamb. A goat in a back garden is unusual.
- The wheelbarrow's wheel is a square, which is impossible for a wheel to roll.
- The brown dog sitting and reading a newspaper is impossible.
- The blue paw prints: the spotted dog seems to have stepped in the paint tray and walked off; the woman painting does not appear to have noticed.
- The girl in pink may be jumping for joy or jumping to reach something; she is shown in mid-air with her shadow on the ground below.
- The black cat seems to be stalking the blue bird, but it could just be walking past it.
- The woman's tool could be a paint roller on a pole or a long-handled paint brush.
