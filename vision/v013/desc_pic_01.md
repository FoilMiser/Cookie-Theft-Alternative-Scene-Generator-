# Picture 01

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden behind a house. A cream-coloured house with a red roof, a blue door and one window is on the left, and a tall wooden fence with a wooden gate runs across the back. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and a grey skirt, stands in the middle-left watering a round bed of colourful flowers (red, yellow, orange, purple, pink). She holds a teal watering can, and a light-blue arc of water (or possibly a hose) curves up from near her hands over the flowers, with drops falling above the flowers. Behind her, a clothesline between two wooden posts has a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes pegged on it. On the right there is a dark A-frame swing set with one empty red swing seat, and a paved path runs from the gate down to the bottom right corner. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the bottom centre. Nobody else is in the garden and there are no animals. The odd part is the water: it seems to rise upward out of the watering can in an arc instead of pouring down, or, if it is a hose, the hose end hangs in mid-air and isn't connected to anything.
