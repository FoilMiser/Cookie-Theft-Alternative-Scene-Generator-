# Picture 04

## Free description
This is a sunny shopping street with four colourful shops with striped awnings, a fruit stall on the left, a street lamp, a tree in a planter and a bike rack. In the middle a worker wearing an orange cap and an orange high-visibility vest is watering a box of flowers outside a shop with a teal watering can. A woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench eating a pink ice-cream cone with her eyes closed, and a brown dog with a red collar stretches up toward her, looking at the ice cream as if begging. On the left a man in a red shirt and blue jeans walks along reading an open book or map, not watching where he is going, and a red bucket stands on the pavement right in front of him, so he looks about to trip over it. At the far left a small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts raises one hand by his head with little curved lines next to it, either waving or listening to something. On the right a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is running or jogging to the right. In the front centre, something impossible: a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is sitting up on its haunches holding open a newspaper and reading it like a person. A teal bicycle is parked in the bike rack.
