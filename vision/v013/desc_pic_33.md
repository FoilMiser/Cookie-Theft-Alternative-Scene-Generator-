On the right, a small child in pink is sitting on a swing on the swing set, smiling with eyes closed, with little motion lines suggesting the swing is moving, but nobody is pushing or standing near the child. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits on the lawn at the front. There is also a hose reel by the flower bed, a tree behind the house, and a sun and two clouds in the sky.
