## Free description
This is a sunny back garden, laid out the other way round from a typical scene: the cream house with the red roof and blue door is on the right, and the wooden gate and paved path are on the left, with the sun in the top left. An older man in a grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands in the middle watering a colourful flower bed with a garden hose; the hose runs from a reel next to the flower bed, loops along the ground to his hands, and water sprays in an arc onto the flowers. On the right, a young boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on a wooden bench in front of the house, drinking from a white cup. On the left-centre, a bearded man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers is drinking from a white mug in a seated position, but there is nothing under him: no chair or bench, so he appears to be sitting on thin air. There is a teal watering can on the lawn near the path, two potted plants by the fence, a tree behind the house, bushes along the fence, and two clouds in the sky.
