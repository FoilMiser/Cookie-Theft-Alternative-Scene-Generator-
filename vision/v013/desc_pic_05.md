# Picture 05

## Free description
This is a calm, sunny park with green hills, a blue pond on the left, bushes, two trees, a tall street lamp in the centre, an empty wooden bench and a winding sandy path on the right. A white duck with an orange beak is swimming on the pond among cattails. Next to the lamp post, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands holding a small white bag or carton and scattering seeds or crumbs, which fall in a dotted line to the ground. A red bird and a blue bird are pecking up the seeds at his feet. In the front, a small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket with his legs stretched out, smiling with his eyes closed, with a wicker picnic basket beside him. There is a flower bed with colourful flowers at the bottom left, a green bin at the far left edge, and a grey rock on the grass at the bottom centre. Nothing much seems to be about to happen, and nothing looks wrong or impossible; it is a peaceful scene.
