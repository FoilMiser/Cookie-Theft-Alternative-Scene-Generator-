# Picture 26

## Free description
This is a quiet, sunny back garden with the sun in the top left, two white clouds, and a red bird flying near the sun. On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door; at the back is a tall wooden fence with a wooden gate on the left, and a paved path runs from that gate down the left side to the front. Behind the fence on the left there is a little birdhouse with a red roof on a pole. A tree stands behind the fence near the house, and an orange fish is sitting in its leaves - which is impossible. In the left-centre a little girl in a pink dress is sitting on a red swing seat on a dark metal swing frame, smiling, with motion lines showing she is swinging. On the grass in the front there is a teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles. To the right there is a round flower bed, a teal hose reel next to it, and a wooden bench near the house. The girl is the only person in the picture.
