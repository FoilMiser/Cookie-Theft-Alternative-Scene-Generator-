## Free description
This is a sunny, windy back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a closed gate at the left, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. At the back left, a man in an orange cap, orange high-visibility vest and blue trousers is painting the fence blue with a long-handled brush. A paint can and a tray of blue paint sit on the grass at his feet. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has evidently stepped in the paint tray. It has left a trail of blue paw prints across the grass to the paved path on the left, where it is walking, and its paws are blue. In the middle, a gust of wind (drawn as white lines) is blowing a blue hat into the air, along with a few green and brown leaves. An older woman with grey hair and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, has her arms thrown up and looks surprised. She seems to be trying to grab the hat. At the front left, a dark-skinned child with curly hair, a yellow top and orange shorts is jumping in the air with arms up and a big smile. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits at the bottom centre, and a colourful flower bed is in front of the house. The man doesn't seem to have noticed the dog tracking paint around.
