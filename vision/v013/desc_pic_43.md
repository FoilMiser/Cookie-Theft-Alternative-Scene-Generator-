# Picture 43

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden, with a house on the left (red roof, cream walls, a window and a blue door), a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, a stone path leading to the gate, and a sun and two clouds in the sky. In the middle, a girl with a brown ponytail, a blue top and dark trousers is holding the end of a green garden hose with both hands, close to her face, and seems to be looking straight into the nozzle. No water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel in the bottom-left corner along the ground, and a small white goat is standing on it at the front. Just behind the goat's back hoof the hose has a big swollen bulge, as if water is trapped because the goat is pinching the hose. It looks like the girl is about to get sprayed in the face as soon as the goat steps off. There is a flower bed with colourful flowers in front of the house, an empty wheelbarrow and a watering can on the grass, and two potted plants by the fence. The goat looks out of place in an ordinary garden.
