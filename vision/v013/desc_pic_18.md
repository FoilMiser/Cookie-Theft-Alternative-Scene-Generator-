## Free description
This is a busy, sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a sun in the top left, two clouds, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the far left a small child with curly dark hair, a yellow top and orange shorts has just thrown a red ball. A dotted line shows it flying high in the air towards a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes, who has her hands up ready to catch it. In the middle, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, is walking to the right carrying a cardboard box. An orange cat is walking along the top of the fence. On the right, an elderly man with a grey beard, a flat cap, a brown waistcoat and a white shirt is watering a flower bed with a hose that comes from a hose reel near the house. In the front left, a woman with a long dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is drinking from a white cup while in a sitting position, but there is no chair or anything under her, which looks impossible. In the front centre, a lawn sprinkler fed by a hose across the path is spraying water onto a cardboard box sitting on the grass, so the box is getting wet. An empty wooden bench stands in the bottom right.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The sprinkler is pointing at and soaking a cardboard box on the lawn. This could be an accident (someone left a parcel in the wrong place) or the box could be something meant to be watered. It looks like a mistake that is about to ruin the box.
- The woman in yellow could be about to catch the ball, or her hands together in front of her could be read as clapping. Her head is drawn mostly from behind, so I can't see her expression.
- It's unclear whether the box the elderly woman carries is related to the box being sprayed. She might be bringing more boxes, or it might be unrelated.
- The child's hand and the start of the dotted line are a little apart, but the dotted line clearly suggests the child threw the ball.
- The cat is drawn on the top edge of the fence against the bushes. It looks like it is walking along the fence, not floating.
