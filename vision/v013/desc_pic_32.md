# Picture 32

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence along the back, and a wooden gate and paved path on the right. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, stands in the middle of the lawn holding the nozzle of a garden hose up close to her face and peering into it, because no water is coming out (just a single drop). The reason is that a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is standing right on the hose, and the hose is visibly swollen on the reel side of the dog, so the water is blocked. As soon as the dog steps off, the water will probably spray straight into the woman's face. On top of the fence near the tree on the left, an orange cat is walking along. On the right, a young man in an orange cap, orange T-shirt and blue overalls is on the path, using a huge silver table fork as if it were a broom or rake to push a pile of dirt and pebbles toward a red dustpan, with little puffs of dust rising. There is also a red dog bowl full of food on the lawn, two potted plants near the fence, a flower bed and a hose reel by the house, and a sun and two clouds in the sky.

- The fork: it is drawn exactly like a giant table/dinner fork, which is odd (wrong tool, wrong size). It could alternatively be meant as a garden fork or pitchfork, but its shape is a dinner fork.
- The young man may be sweeping dirt into the dustpan (toward the left) or may have just knocked over a pile; the dust puffs suggest movement.
- The cat walking on top of the fence is plausible for a cat but notable.
