# Picture 22

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny day in a park with a pond, rolling green hills, a sun in the top left, two white clouds and a blue bird flying in the top right. On the left a tall tree has an orange fish sitting in its leaves, which is impossible, and under it on the grass a small white puppy is lying down asleep. A winding sandy path runs from the front up into the park; next to the top of it a boy in a blue hoodie is jumping in the air with both arms raised and a happy face (his shadow is on the grass below him). In the front left a woman in a yellow dress sits on a wooden bench licking a pink ice-cream cone, and a brown dog with a red collar is on the path in front of her, rearing up and looking at her as if begging for the ice cream. In the middle an older man with grey hair, a cap, white shirt and brown vest stands on the grass by a lamp post and a green bin, fishing with a long rod; his line goes all the way into the pond, and the float lands right beside another man's legs. That man, in a red shirt and blue jeans, stands at the near edge of the pond holding a white bag of bread and throwing crumbs to a white duck, which is swimming quickly toward him (it has speed/wake lines behind it); some crumbs are on the water and a few have landed on the grass at the pond edge. On the right, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is drinking from a cup in a sitting position, but there is no chair or bench under him - he is sitting on thin air. In the front, a lawn sprinkler attached to a garden hose is spraying water to the left, and the spray is landing on a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is getting wet. A bed of colourful flowers is in the front right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fish in the tree is clearly impossible.
- The bearded man on the right is posed as if seated but there is nothing under him - sitting on air. Possibly a chair was meant to be there.
- The fishing line is extremely long and its float lands right next to the red-shirt man; it could be read as about to snag him / tangle with the duck feeding, or just as the angler fishing in the same pond.
- The man in red's white item could be a bag of bread or a carton/bottle; the brown dots look like crumbs.
- A few crumbs lie outside the pond on the grass - could be crumbs he dropped, or just drawn slightly off the water.
- The white lines behind the duck could be speed lines (rushing to the bread) or ripples in the water.
- The dog could be begging for the ice cream or jumping up at the woman.
- The sprinkler watering a cardboard box looks deliberate-but-silly; the box getting soaked could be the "something wrong" here, or the box is just left out on the lawn.
- The jumping boy's shadow is on the grass rather than on the path; he seems to be off to the side of the path, in mid-air.
