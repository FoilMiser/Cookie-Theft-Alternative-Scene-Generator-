# Picture 30

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream-coloured house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a wooden gate and a paved path on the right. An older man in a grey flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers sits on a wooden bench in the bottom left, reading a red book. Beside him there is a long sandwich on a plate, and a small blue bird is right next to it, apparently pecking at or about to steal the sandwich while he isn't looking. In the middle of the lawn, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers is also reading a red book, but he is in a sitting position with nothing under him: there is no chair, bench or swing, so he seems to be sitting on thin air. On the right there is a metal swing set with one empty red swing. At the front, a lawn sprinkler attached to a long hose is spraying arcs of water to the left, straight onto a closed cardboard box sitting on the grass, which has drops of water on top and is getting soaked. There is also a flower bed with brightly coloured flowers next to the house, a hose reel near it, a tree and a bush behind the fence line, and a sun and two clouds in the sky.
