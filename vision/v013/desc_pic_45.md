# Picture 45

## Free description
This is a sunny playground in a park, with a railing fence across the back, trees and hedges behind it, two clouds and a sun in the sky. On the left, a girl with a brown ponytail, a blue top and dark trousers is holding up a kite string, but her purple kite is caught in the leaves of a big tree. She is looking up at it, apparently stuck. On the far right, a small girl in a pink dress stands by the fence flying an orange kite, which is high in the sky near the sun on a long string. In the front centre, a girl with dark hair in a ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is running to the right with one arm reaching forward. In the middle is a swing set with one empty red swing. There is a red slide with a ladder on the right, a red seesaw tilted with no one on it at the front left, a green bin by the fence, a bush, and a grey rock in the bottom-right corner. The main problem in the scene is the kite stuck in the tree.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl in blue stands just behind the raised end of the seesaw, so at a glance she could be read as standing on or next to the seesaw. From where her feet are, she is most likely behind it on the grass.
- The running girl's goal is unclear. She could be (a) running to help with the stuck kite (though she is heading right, away from it), (b) running towards the slide or the small girl, or (c) just running and playing.
- The small girl in pink is much smaller than the others. She could be a younger child or just further away.
- The purple kite is partly hidden behind leaf clusters drawn on top of it. That's consistent with it being tangled in the tree.
- The orange kite's string passes close to the top of the slide but doesn't seem to touch it.
