On the left a young man in a blue hoodie and black trousers stands eating a pink ice-cream cone with his eyes closed, looking like he is enjoying it. She is reaching up with her other hand toward a blue balloon that is floating away above her with its string dangling, so it looks like she has just let it go. She looks worried or upset. Behind her and to the right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts walks or skips along holding his own yellow balloon. On the right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is running toward the left, in the same direction as the boy and the loose balloon. There is a fruit stall with crates of fruit on the left, a street lamp, a flower box, a tree in a round planter, a bin and potted plants around the square. Nothing looks impossible. The main event is the blue balloon about to drift out of reach.
