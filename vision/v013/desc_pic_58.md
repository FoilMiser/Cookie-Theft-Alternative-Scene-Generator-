## Free description
This is a sunny back garden, with a wooden fence across the back and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. At the bottom left, a boy with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue trousers stands on the paved path. He is sweeping dirt into a red dustpan, but he is using a giant silver dinner fork instead of a broom. A small cloud of dust rises where the fork's prongs scrape the pile. At the back left, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is painting the fence blue with a long-handled brush. A section of boards behind her is already blue, and a small paint can sits on the grass near her. In the middle, a clothesline between two wooden posts holds a yellow towel or shirt with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans. At the bottom centre, an empty grey wheelbarrow rests on the grass. Next to it, a white goat with small horns has its head down and is eating a tuft of grass. A flower bed of yellow, orange, purple, red and pink flowers is on the right in front of the house, and there is a garden gate at the far left of the fence. The obviously wrong thing is the boy sweeping with a huge fork. A goat in a family's back garden is also a little unexpected.
