# Picture 21

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a sun in the top left, two clouds, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the left, on a paved path near a wooden gate, a woman or girl with a short dark bob and a yellow dress is sweeping a little pile of dirt towards a red dustpan. Instead of a broom, she is using what looks like a giant silver dinner fork, which is odd. A small cloud of dust is rising from the dirt. In the middle, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is digging a hole in the lawn with a spade, with a lump of soil on the blade and a mound of earth beside the hole. Behind her a washing line between two posts holds a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans. On the right, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top, sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. A blue bird has landed on the bench beside her and is pecking at her sandwich, and she hasn't noticed. There is also a colourful flower bed in front of the house, two potted plants on the grass by the fence, an empty wheelbarrow at the front, and a birdhouse on a pole behind the fence on the left.
