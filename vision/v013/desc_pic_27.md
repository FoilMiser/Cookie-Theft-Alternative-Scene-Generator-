# Picture 27

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the sun in the top left and two white clouds. On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door, and a tree behind the fence beside it; a tall wooden fence runs across the back with a gate on the left, and a paved path leads from the gate down the left side. On the left a boy with curly dark hair, a yellow shirt and orange shorts is on a swing, smiling with his eyes closed, with motion lines showing he is swinging. In the centre front a girl in a pink dress is holding the strings of a purple balloon and a blue balloon in one hand, while her other arm reaches up toward a yellow balloon that has escaped and is floating away above her with its string dangling; she is looking up at it with her mouth open. In the bottom right, on the grass below the flower bed, a small orange cat is curled up asleep. A red bowl of pet food sits on the grass at the bottom near the path. Nothing looks impossible; the main event is the lost balloon.
