# Picture 03

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills, a blue pond with cattails on the left, a tall street lamp in the middle, a wooden bench, and a winding sandy path on the right. An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, is kneeling on the grass at the bottom left beside a flower bed, reaching to a terracotta pot holding a pink flower, as if she is planting it. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is crouched in a playful bow next to a yellow tennis ball, wagging its tail. Below it an orange cat is crouched low, creeping toward a red bird that is pecking on the grass. In the background a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a blue and a purple balloon by their strings, while a third, yellow balloon has escaped and floats away above him; he is reaching up toward it. On the right a woman with a ponytail in a blue top and dark trousers stands on the path holding up a kite string, but her purple kite is stuck in the branches of the big tree. At the bottom right a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is raking a small pile of leaves. Two small mishaps are shown: the balloon getting away and the kite caught in the tree.
