# Picture 02

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny shopping street with four colourful two-storey shops (pink, pale green, peach, cream) with striped awnings, and a paved pavement in front. It is windy on the left: a woman in a yellow dress is standing with both arms up and her mouth open in surprise because her red cap is blowing off her head, with wind lines and leaves flying around it. Behind her is a fruit stall with crates of green, yellow, orange and red fruit, and a cardboard box on the ground beside it. In the centre a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks to the left carrying a yellow shopping bag, and next to her a woman with a ponytail in a blue top throws a red-and-white ball, which arcs (dotted line) toward a little girl in a pink dress on the right who holds her arms out to catch it. At the girl's feet an orange cat runs toward a red bird pecking on the ground, and a blue bird sits on top of the street lamp. In the front, a small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and rubs his eye as if crying, because his pink scoop of ice cream has fallen on the ground; a brown dog with a red collar is sniffing or licking the dropped ice cream. A small black cat lies on the pavement in the bottom right corner. The obviously wrong thing is the teal bicycle at the bottom right: its wheels are square instead of round.
