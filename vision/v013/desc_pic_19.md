# Picture 19

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a sun in the top right, two clouds, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left. Next to the house, a man with dark skin and a beard, in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers, is kneeling by a colourful flower bed. He is reaching into the soil with a small tool, and a pot with a pink flower sits beside him, so he seems to be planting it. Behind him there is a washing line between two wooden posts with a pair of blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel pegged on it. On the right, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, has both arms raised and her mouth open, looking surprised. Above her, a blue cap is flying through the air with wind lines and a couple of green leaves, as if a gust of wind has just blown it away and she is reaching for it. On the far right, a boy with brown hair in a blue hoodie and dark trousers is sweeping the paved path with a broom. A grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can stand on the lawn at the front, and a wooden gate is in the fence on the right. One odd thing is that the wind is strong enough to blow the cap away, but the clothes on the washing line hang perfectly still.
