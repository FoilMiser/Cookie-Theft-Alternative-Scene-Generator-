## Free description
This is a sunny shopping street with four colourful shops with striped awnings, a fruit stall on the left, a street lamp, a planter box of flowers, a bicycle in a bike rack and a tree in a planter on the right. On the far left a woman in a yellow dress holds a camera up to her face and is taking a photo toward the right. Next to her an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, is walking a brown dog with a red collar on a red leash; the dog walks ahead of her with its tongue out. On the right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is running toward the left, with one arm stretched out in front of her, perhaps hurrying to help catch the balloon. It looks as if the yellow balloon is about to float out of reach. Nothing looks impossible here.
