# pic_101

## Free description
A sunny fenced park with a tree on the left, a bench, and a slide on the right. A boy in a yellow shirt stands at the front left holding a kite-string handle in one hand and pointing up with the other. His string runs up to a yellow kite that is caught in the branches of the tree, so the kite seems stuck. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans sits on a bench reading a red book, not paying attention. In the middle a brown dog with a red collar crouches in a play bow beside a tennis ball, tail wagging. At the front right a girl in a pink outfit kneels in a sandbox building a sandcastle with a small flag on top, holding a red spade. The slide is empty.

## Inventory
- Front left: boy in yellow shirt and orange shorts, holding a kite string reel, pointing up at the kite.
- Left-centre back: man with brown hair in red shirt and blue jeans, sitting on a wooden bench, reading a red book.
- Centre: brown dog with red collar in a play bow, tail wagging, near a tennis ball.
- Front right: girl in pink outfit kneeling in the sandbox, building a sandcastle with a red spade.
- Yellow kite with bow tail, tangled in the tree branches at the left; its string runs down to the boy (a slack loop lies on the grass).
- Tennis ball on the grass by the dog.
- Sandcastle with a little red flag in the sandbox.
- Bench, tree, slide with red ladder (right, empty), green bin, bush, grey rock, sun and two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The boy's pointing could be read as asking for help to get the kite down, or as still flying it; the kite clearly sits in the tree, so stuck is the stronger reading.
- Part of the kite string loops on the ground near the boy's feet while also running up to the tree, which is plausible slack.
- The girl's spade/bucket is small and red; hard to tell if it is a spade or a bucket.
