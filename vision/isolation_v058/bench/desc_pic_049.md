## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills and a line of bushes in the background. In the front right, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts stands looking up, holding a kite reel high in one raised hand. A long string runs from the reel up across the sky to an orange diamond-shaped kite flying high in the middle of the picture, with a tail of small bows trailing beneath it. The string passes over a round blue pond with reeds at both ends. Behind, in the centre, there is a tall black lamp post and a green bin. On the left a sandy path winds from the back to the front past a large tree and a bush. In the front centre an empty red wheelbarrow sits on the grass next to the path. The boy is alone; he seems to be enjoying flying his kite.
