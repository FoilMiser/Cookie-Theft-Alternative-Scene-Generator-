# pic_116

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. A boy in a yellow shirt stands in the middle holding a kite-string handle and pointing up; his string runs to a yellow kite that is caught in the tree behind the house. An older man with grey hair, a flat cap, brown vest and white shirt is painting the fence bright pink with a long-handled roller, with an open pink paint tin at his feet. A small section of fence is already pink. A brown dog lies curled up asleep on the grass at the front with its eyes closed. A flower bed and bench are by the house, and a teal watering can sits at the front right.
