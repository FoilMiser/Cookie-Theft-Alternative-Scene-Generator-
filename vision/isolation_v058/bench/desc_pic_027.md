# pic_027

## Free description
A sunny fenced playground with a tree on the left, a slide on the right, a sandbox at the front right and a green bin by the fence. On the front left a red seesaw is in use: a small girl in pink is on the raised left end and a man in a green shirt and beige trousers sits on the low right end with his feet on the ground. Because the man is much heavier, the girl's end is stuck up in the air. The girl holds the handle and her mouth is open, as if she is calling out or worried, and her legs hang down below the bar. The man sits calmly with his hands on his knees, looking toward the girl. The sandbox has a red bucket and blue spade but no one is in it, and the slide is empty. There are no other people.
