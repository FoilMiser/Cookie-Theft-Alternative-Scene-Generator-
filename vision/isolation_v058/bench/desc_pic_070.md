## Free description
A back garden on a sunny day, with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a flower bed in front of it, a wooden fence along the back, a gate at the right and a paved path down the right side. In the front, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers has just thrown a red ball with his right arm stretched out; a dotted line shows the ball flying from his hand toward a small girl in a pink dress. The girl stands with her arms held out in front of her, ready to catch it. Behind them, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is painting the fence pink with a long-handled roller; three boards are already pink and an open tin of pink paint is at his feet. An orange cat is walking along the top of the fence. The ball looks about to reach the girl.
