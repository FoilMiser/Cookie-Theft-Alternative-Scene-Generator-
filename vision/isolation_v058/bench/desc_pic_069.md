# pic_069

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a house with a blue door on the right, a tree and a clothesline. In the front left a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is walking down the paved path while reading an open book. A cardboard box sits on the path right in front of her, so she looks about to trip over it. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans is raking a small pile of leaves in the middle back. In the centre front a small girl in a pink dress points up toward a yellow kite that is stuck in the tree on the right. The kite's string runs down from the tree to a spool lying on the grass by the girl's feet. A yellow striped towel and a pair of blue jeans hang on the clothesline. There is also a birdhouse on a pole and a flowerbed by the house.
