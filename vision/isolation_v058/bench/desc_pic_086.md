# pic_086

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. A yellow kite is caught in the tree by the house; its long string hangs down to a spool lying on the grass. A boy in a yellow shirt stands near the spool, pointing up at the stuck kite. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is painting fence boards pink with a roller; next to him are a paint tin and a pink paint tray. A white dog with brown spots has walked through the paint tray: its paws are pink and it has left a trail of pink paw prints across the grass toward the path, where it stands looking pleased. A girl in a pink dress in front jumps with both arms up, looking excited. A birdhouse on a tall pole stands near the fence, and a red wheelbarrow sits on the grass in front. The painter has not noticed the dog's paw prints.
