## Free description
A busy back garden on a sunny day, with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a house with a blue door on the right, a tree and a flowerbed. On the path at the left, a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts holds a purple and a blue balloon but is reaching up with a frown, because a red balloon has escaped and is floating away above the fence. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is painting the fence pink with a paint roller; a pink paint tin stands at his feet and some fence boards are already pink. On top of the fence a blue bird sits while a grey cat creeps along toward it, as if about to pounce. In the middle, a white dog with brown spots crouches playfully over a yellow tennis ball. A girl in pink holds a kite string and points up: her orange kite is caught in the tree near the house. A garden sprinkler is spraying arcs of water onto a cardboard box sitting on the path, which is getting wet with a puddle under it. At the bottom, an orange cat is asleep on the grass, and a brown dog wearing glasses sits upright reading a newspaper, which is impossible for a real dog. A long hose runs from the sprinkler off to the right.
