# pic_112

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a gate and paved path on the left, a wooden fence along the back, and a cream house with a blue door on the right. An older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, is painting the fence bright pink with a paint roller on a long handle. A section of three boards is already pink, and an open pink paint tin sits on the grass at her feet. On the left, a young girl in a pink outfit is swinging on a swing (motion lines) and smiling. A red wheelbarrow is in the middle, a teal watering can at the front left, a hose reel at the front right, plus a flower bed and a wooden bench by the house.
