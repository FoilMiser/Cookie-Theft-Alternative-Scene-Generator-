A sunny park with green hills, a winding sandy path, a lamppost, a bin, a pond with reeds, trees and bushes. At the front right a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is flying an orange kite. He holds the spool up with both hands. The kite flies high in the sky in the centre, and its long string runs diagonally down across the pond to his hands. A red wheelbarrow with wooden handles stands on the grass beside the path at the front. Nobody else is in the picture. Everything looks calm and normal, apart from the wheelbarrow's wheel, which looks odd.
