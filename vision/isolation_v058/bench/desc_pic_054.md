## Free description
A sunny playground surrounded by a dark metal railing fence, with bushes beyond. On the right, an old man with grey hair, a cap, a brown waistcoat and white shirt sits on the low end of a red seesaw, with his feet on the ground and hands on his knees. A small girl in a pink outfit sits on the high end of the seesaw, holding its handle, her legs dangling in the air. In the middle, a brown dog with a red collar crouches in a play bow with its tail wagging, a yellow-green tennis ball right in front of its nose. On the left is a red slide with a yellow chute, and a sandpit in the front with a red bucket and a blue spade in it. In the middle back stands a swing frame with one empty swing, and a green bin. A grey rock lies in the bottom-left corner and a tree stands at the right. Nothing looks wrong; the heavier man keeps the girl up in the air.
