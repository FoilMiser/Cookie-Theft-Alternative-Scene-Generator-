## Free description
A sunny playground on a grassy field, with a dark metal railing fence and bushy hedges behind it. In the front right, an old man with grey hair and a flat cap, wearing a brown waistcoat over a white shirt, sits on the low end of a red seesaw. A small girl in pink sits on the raised other end, holding the handle and looking at him with a worried or surprised face, her feet dangling in the air. The old man also looks a bit surprised. The seesaw is tipped steeply toward the man because he is heavier, so the girl is stuck up high. On the left is a red ladder slide with a yellow chute, and in the middle is a swing frame with one empty red swing seat. In the front left is a sandpit with a red bucket and a blue spade in it, and a grey rock beside it. A blue bird perches on the fence at the far left. No one is using the slide, swing or sandpit.
