## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a house on the right and a path running down the left. A lot of people are busy with jobs. On the left, a boy in a red cap sweeps leaves on the path, and a man in a red shirt paints the fence pink with a roller. In the middle, an older woman with grey hair and glasses holds a hose nozzle up and looks at it, but no water comes out. A brown dog is standing on the hose, which would explain why it is blocked, and a little water is dripping out at the hose reel. Behind her, a man in a green shirt stands with his hands on his hips and a metal bucket upside down on his head, which looks silly. A small girl in a pink dress points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the tree. A grey cat walks along the top of the fence and a blue bird flies in the sky. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench drinking through a straw.
