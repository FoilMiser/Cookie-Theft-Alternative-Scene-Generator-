# pic_080

## Free description
A busy back garden on a sunny day with a wooden fence, a gate and path on the left, and a house with a blue door on the right. A man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans paints the fence pink with a roller, a pink paint tin beside him. On the path at the front left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps a pile of brown leaves with a broom. An old woman with grey hair and glasses holds a garden hose nozzle up and peers at it, puzzled, because no water is coming out — a brown dog is standing on the hose further along, which seems to be blocking it, and water is dripping from the hose reel by the bench. A man in a green T-shirt stands in the middle with his hands on his hips and a metal bucket upside down on his head, which is odd. A small girl in pink near the house points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the tree. A grey cat walks along the top of the fence, a blue bird flies in the sky, and a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench at the right drinking through a straw. A red wheelbarrow stands empty on the grass.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The bucket on the man's head is strange; he might be wearing it as a joke, or it might be meant as something impossible.
- The dog standing on the hose is my reading of why the nozzle is dry; the dog's foot is right where the hose joins, so it could also just be standing beside it.
- The wheelbarrow's wheel looks square rather than round, which looks unintentionally odd.
- The drip at the hose reel could be a leak or just spilled water.
