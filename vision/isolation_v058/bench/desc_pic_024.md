To the left there is a metal swing set with a single empty red swing seat, and a paved path runs from the gate down to the bottom left. On the right a round flower bed full of colourful flowers sits near the house, and a coiled hose on a reel lies on the grass with its yellow nozzle pointing left. Nobody else is in the garden. The wheelbarrow's wheel looks odd: it is drawn as a square box shape rather than a round wheel.
