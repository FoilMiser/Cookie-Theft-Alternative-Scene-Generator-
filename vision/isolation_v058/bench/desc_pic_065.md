# pic_065

## Free description
A sunny fenced park with grass, a tree on the left and a slide on the right. In the front left a boy with dark skin and curly hair, in a yellow top and orange shorts, holds up a kite spool. The string sags down to the grass in a loop and then goes up to a yellow kite caught in the tree at the far left, so the kite seems stuck. Behind him a man with brown hair, in a red T-shirt and blue jeans, sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. In the centre a brown dog with a red collar crouches in a playful bow with its tail wagging, next to a yellow-green tennis ball. In a sandpit at the front right a small girl in pink is building a sandcastle with a little red flag, holding a red spade. The slide on the right is empty. There is a green bin, a bush and a grey rock.
