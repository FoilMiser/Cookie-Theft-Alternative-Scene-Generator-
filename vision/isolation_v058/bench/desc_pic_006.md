# pic_006

## Free description
A sunny day in a park with green hills, a few clouds, a pond and a winding sandy path. On the left a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks a brown dog on a red lead along the grass beside the path. In the middle a small boy in a yellow top and orange shorts stands holding up a kite reel; its string runs down to the ground in a loose loop and then up to a yellow kite that is tangled in the branches of the big tree on the left. So the kite seems to be stuck in the tree rather than flying. On the right-centre a man in a green shirt sits leaning back on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket next to a picnic basket holding a bottle and a baguette. Behind him is a round blue pond with reeds, and further right there is a flower bed with colourful flowers and a green bin. A tall black lamp post stands in the middle of the park.
