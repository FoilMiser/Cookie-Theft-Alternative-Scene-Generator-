## Free description
A busy back garden on a sunny day, with a cream house with a blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back with a gate at the left, and a paved path down the left side. At the front left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping a pile of brown leaves on the path with a broom. Behind him a man in a red shirt and jeans is painting the fence pink with a roller, with a pink paint tin at his feet, and an empty red wheelbarrow stands on the lawn nearby. In the centre, a grandmother with grey hair and glasses holds a hose nozzle up close to her face and looks into it; the hose runs across the lawn, and a brown dog with a red collar is standing with a paw on the hose, which seems to be blocking the water — a small puddle/drip is leaking at the hose reel. The grandmother looks about to get sprayed in the face if the dog steps off. Behind her, a man in a green shirt stands with his hands on his hips and has a metal bucket upside down on his head, which is odd. A small girl in pink near the fence on the right points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the tree. A grey cat walks along the top of the fence, a blue bird flies in the sky at top left, and a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench at the front right drinking from a cup through a straw.
