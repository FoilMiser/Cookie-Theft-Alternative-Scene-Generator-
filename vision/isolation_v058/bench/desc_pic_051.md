## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a blue door on the right, a wooden fence along the back, a gate on the far left and a paved path leading to it. In the centre a woman in a yellow dress is raking a small pile of brown leaves on the grass. On the left stands a metal swing frame with a single red swing seat hanging still and empty. In the front there is an empty red wheelbarrow, a teal watering can on the grass, and on the right a hose reel with a hose ending in a nozzle. A flower bed full of colourful flowers sits in front of the house, and a tree rises behind the house. It is a quiet scene with only one person.
