# pic_102

## Free description
A sunny park playground behind a metal fence, with a tree on the left and a slide on the right. On the left an old man with grey hair, a grey cap, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers sits on the low end of a red seesaw with his feet on the ground. A small girl in pink sits on the raised end, holding the handle, her feet off the ground. The man looks over at the girl; the seesaw is tilted down heavily on his side, so she is stuck up in the air. In the middle at the back a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers walks along while eating a pink ice-cream cone. Nobody is on the slide. A bin stands by the fence and a grey rock lies on the grass at the bottom right.
