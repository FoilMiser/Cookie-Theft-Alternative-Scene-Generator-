# pic_066

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left (blue door), a wooden fence across the back and a tree behind the fence on the left. In the centre a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a kite string and points up; the yellow kite is caught in the tree, and the string sags in a long loop down to the ground and back up to his hand. On the right an old man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat paints the fence pink with a roller; several boards are already pink and a pink paint tin stands near him. A brown puppy lies asleep on the lawn in the front. There is a bench and a flower bed by the house, a teal watering can in the front right, and a paved path leading to a gate on the right.
