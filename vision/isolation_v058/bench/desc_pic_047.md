## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, a gate at the right and a paved path running to the gate. A yellow kite is caught in the branches of the tree behind the house; its string hangs all the way down to a spool lying on the grass. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands next to the spool, pointing up at the stuck kite. At the back, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is painting the fence pink with a roller; a few fence planks are already pink, and a paint tin and a paint tray sit on the grass behind him. A trail of pink paw prints leads from the paint tray across the grass to a white dog with brown spots on the right, whose paws are pink, so the dog has stepped in the paint tray. A girl in a pink dress in the front right has both arms raised, jumping or cheering. An empty red wheelbarrow sits in the front centre, a flower bed is on the left, and a birdhouse on a tall pole stands in the middle-back.
