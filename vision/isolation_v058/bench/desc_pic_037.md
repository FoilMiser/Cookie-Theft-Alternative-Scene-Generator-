## Free description
A busy back garden on a sunny day, with a wooden fence, a house with a blue door on the right and a tree behind the fence. On the left a man in a red shirt paints the fence pink with a roller; a few boards are already pink and a pink paint tin sits at his feet. In the front left a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps a pile of brown leaves on the path with a broom. In the middle an old woman with grey hair and glasses holds a garden hose and looks at its nozzle, which is not spraying; the hose runs across the lawn to a hose reel on the right, and the dog in the middle is standing on the hose, which would explain the blocked water. A little water drips out at the reel. Behind her, a man in a green shirt stands with his hands on his hips wearing a metal bucket upside down on his head, which is silly. A small girl in pink at the back points up at a striped beach ball stuck in the tree. A grey cat walks along the top of the fence, a blue bird flies in the sky, and a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench on the right drinking through a straw. There is also a red wheelbarrow in the middle and a flower bed near the house.
