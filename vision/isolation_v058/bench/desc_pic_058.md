# pic_058

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, and a path leading to a gate on the right. A boy with dark skin and curly hair, in a yellow top and orange shorts, stands in the middle holding a kite spool up in the air. The kite string sags down to the grass, makes a loop, and rises up to a yellow kite caught in the branches of the tree behind the house. The kite seems to be stuck in the tree. To the right an elderly man in a grey cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is painting the fence pink with a roller on a pole. A section of boards is already pink, and a pink paint pot stands near his feet. In the front a brown dog with floppy ears lies on the grass with its eyes closed, apparently asleep. There is also a flower bed, a wooden bench and a teal watering can.
