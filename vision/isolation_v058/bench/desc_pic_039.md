## Free description
It is a sunny day in a fenced playground with grass, a slide, a swing set, a sandbox, a bin and a tree. On the right, an old man with grey hair, a grey cap, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers sits on the low end of a red seesaw, with his feet on the ground. A small girl in pink sits on the high end, holding the handle, her legs dangling in the air. The seesaw is tipped steeply down on the man's side, so he is much heavier and the girl is stuck up high. The man looks across at the girl. A blue bird sits on top of the fence at the far left. The swing set has one empty swing, and nobody is on the slide or in the sandbox, where a red bucket and a blue spade lie. Nothing looks clearly wrong.
