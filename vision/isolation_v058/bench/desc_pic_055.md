# pic_055

## Free description
A sunny fenced playground with a tree on the left, a slide on the right and a green bin by the fence. On the front left, a small girl in pink is on the raised left end of a red seesaw while an older man with grey hair, a cap, a white shirt and a brown vest sits on the low right end with his feet on the ground. The girl holds the handle with her mouth open and legs dangling; the man sits calmly with his hands on his knees, looking at her. In the middle background, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers walks to the left near the fence, eating a pink ice-cream cone. The slide is empty. A grey rock sits on the grass at the front right with nothing next to it.
