## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the right (blue door), a wooden fence across the back, a side gate and paved path on the left, and a tree behind the fence. On the left a girl in a pink outfit swings happily on a swing; motion lines show she is moving. In the middle an old woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, paints the fence pink with a long roller; several boards are already pink and a pink paint tin sits at her feet. In front there is a red wheelbarrow, a teal watering can, a flower bed by the house, a bench, and a coiled hose on a reel with its yellow nozzle lying on the grass. Nobody else is around, and nothing seems about to go wrong.
