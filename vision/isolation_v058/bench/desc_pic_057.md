## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and path on the left and a cream house with a blue door on the right. On the left a boy in a yellow shirt holds the strings of a purple and a blue balloon, while a red balloon floats high away from him with its string trailing; he reaches up toward it, looking upset, so he seems to have lost it. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is painting fence boards pink with a roller, a paint tin at his feet. In the front, a lawn sprinkler sprays arcs of water right onto a cardboard box sitting on the path, making it wet, with a puddle under it. In the middle a white-and-brown spotted dog crouches playfully in front of a tennis ball. A girl in pink points up at an orange kite caught in the tree; its string runs down to a spool lying at her feet. On top of the fence, a grey cat creeps toward a blue bird. In the front, a brown dog wearing glasses sits up holding and reading a newspaper, which is impossible, and an orange cat sleeps curled up on the grass.
