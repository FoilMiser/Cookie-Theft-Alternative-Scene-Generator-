# pic_034

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence at the back, and a gate and paved path on the left. In the front left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is walking quickly along the path while reading an open book, and a cardboard box sits right in front of her feet, so she looks about to trip over it. In the middle, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is raking a small pile of leaves. Towards the front centre, a small girl in a pink dress holds a kite spool up in the air; her kite string runs down to the grass, loops on the ground, and then goes up to a yellow kite that is caught in the branches of the tree by the house. So the kite is stuck in the tree. Behind them is a washing line between two posts with a yellow striped towel and a pair of blue jeans hanging on it, and a birdhouse on a pole on the left. A flower bed sits in front of the house.
