A yellow diamond kite is stuck in the branches of the big tree on the left; its string hangs down, crosses the path and ends at a kite reel lying on the grass near a young boy. In the front centre a bearded man in a green shirt and beige trousers sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket next to a wicker basket holding a bottle and a baguette. On the right are a round flower bed with bright flowers and a green bin. Nobody seems to be helping the boy yet.
