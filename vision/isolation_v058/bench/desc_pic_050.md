## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the middle a woman with dark hair tied back, wearing a yellow dress, is digging in the lawn with a spade. She has a spadeful of brown earth lifted from a small hole, and there is a pile of dug soil next to it. To her left an empty red wheelbarrow is parked on the grass, and a teal watering can sits near the path. On the right there is a flower bed full of colourful flowers and a wooden bench in front of the house. She is alone; nothing looks odd.
