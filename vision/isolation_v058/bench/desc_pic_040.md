# pic_040

## Free description
A busy, sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and path on the left and a cream house with a blue door on the right. A man in a red shirt is painting fence boards bright pink with a paint roller, a pink paint tin at his feet. In the front left a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps a pile of brown leaves on the path with a broom. In the centre an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses holds a green garden hose and looks at the nozzle, which seems to have no water coming out. The reason looks to be a brown dog standing on the hose with its back paw, and the hose is leaking a little water at the hose reel by the house. Behind her a man in a green shirt stands with his hands on his hips and frowns, and he is wearing an upturned white bucket on his head, which is odd. A small girl in pink near the house points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of the tree. A woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench on the right drinking through a straw from a cup. A grey cat walks along the top of the fence and a blue bird flies in the sky.
