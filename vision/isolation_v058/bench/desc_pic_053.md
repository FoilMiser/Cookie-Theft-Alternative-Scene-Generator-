# pic_053

## Free description
A back garden on a sunny day, with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, a gate at the right and a paved path running down the right side. A yellow kite is caught in the branches of the tree behind the fence at the top left. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands in the middle of the lawn holding a reel up with both hands; the kite string runs from him up to the stuck kite and lies slack in a loop on the grass. An older man in a cap, white shirt and brown vest is painting the fence pink with a paint roller on a long handle; three fence boards are already pink, and an open tin of pink paint is on the grass beside him. A brown puppy lies asleep on the grass at the front centre. There is a flower bed and a wooden bench near the house, and a teal watering can on the lawn at the front right. The kite looks stuck and the boy seems to be trying to pull it free.
