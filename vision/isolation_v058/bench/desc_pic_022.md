## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a paved path on the left leading to a wooden gate. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt stands by the fence. She holds a long-handled paint roller up against it. A section of about four fence boards to her right has already been painted bright pink. A pot of pink paint stands on the grass beside her. On the left a small girl in pink is swinging on a swing set, and motion lines show she is moving. In the front there is an empty red wheelbarrow, a teal watering can, a hose on a reel, a flower bed full of colourful flowers and a wooden bench. Nothing looks like it is about to go wrong. The woman is in the middle of painting the fence pink.
