## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left (blue door), a wooden fence along the back and a tree behind the fence. In the middle a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie has just thrown a red ball toward the left; a dotted line shows its path, and a small girl in pink on the left holds her arms out ready to catch it. On the right, a man in a red shirt paints the fence pink with a long roller, with a pink paint tin at his feet; some boards are already pink. An orange cat walks along the top of the fence just above the pink boards. In the front there is a red wheelbarrow and a teal watering can, a flower bed near the house, a bush in the middle and a paved path to a gate on the right.
