# pic_079

## Free description
A sunny park with hills, a winding sandy path on the left, a round blue pond on the right, and a tall black lamp post in the middle. In the front left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks a light brown dog on a red lead; the dog walks ahead to the left. In the middle, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts holds a kite spool up in the air, but his kite string droops down to the grass, loops on the ground, crosses the path, and goes up to a yellow kite that is stuck in the big tree on the left. In the front right, a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket next to a basket holding a bottle and a loaf of bread. A flower bed with colourful flowers is at the bottom right, and a green bin is in the bottom-right corner. The main oddity is the kite caught in the tree while the boy still holds his spool up.
