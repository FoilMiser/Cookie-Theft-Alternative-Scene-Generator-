A sunny back garden with a house on the left, a wooden fence across the back, a gate and a paved path on the right. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie has just thrown a red ball to the left, and a dotted line shows its path. A small girl in a pink dress stands on the left with her arms out, ready to catch it. On the right a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is painting the fence pink with a roller on a pole. A few boards behind him are already pink, and an open tin of pink paint sits at his feet. An orange cat walks along the top of the fence, right above the freshly painted section. At the front are a flowerbed, a red wheelbarrow and a teal watering can.
