## Free description
A sunny playground with a dark metal railing fence across the back and trees behind it. On the right, an old man in a grey flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat sits on the low end of a red seesaw, with his feet on the ground. A small girl in a pink outfit sits on the high end, up in the air, holding the handle with her legs dangling. Because the man is down and she is up, he seems to be keeping her raised, or they are about to bounce. In the middle foreground a brown dog with a red collar is crouched in a "play bow" with its front legs down and tail up and wagging, nosing a yellow-green tennis ball, as if it wants someone to throw it. On the left there is a slide with a red ladder, an empty swing set in the middle, a green bin by the fence, and at the front a sandpit with a red bucket and blue spade in it, plus a grey rock beside it. A large tree stands on the far right and a bush on the far left. No one is using the slide, swing or sandpit.
