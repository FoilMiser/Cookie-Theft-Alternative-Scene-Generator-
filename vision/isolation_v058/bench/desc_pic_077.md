## Free description
A sunny park with a railing fence along the back, bushes behind it, two clouds and a sun at the top right. On the left, a small girl in pink and an elderly man with grey hair and a flat cap are on a red seesaw. The old man sits on the right end, which rests on the ground, and the girl sits on the raised left end holding the handle, her feet in the air. The old man is much bigger, so his end being down makes sense. The girl looks slightly tense, and the old man looks over at her. In the background in the middle, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers walks along eating a pink ice cream cone. On the right there is an empty red slide, a green bin by the fence and a bush; a grey rock lies in the front right. Nothing looks impossible.
