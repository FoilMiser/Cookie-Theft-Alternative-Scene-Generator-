On the left, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a purple and a blue balloon, while a red balloon has escaped and floats up into the sky with its string trailing; the boy reaches up and looks upset. A lawn sprinkler sprays arcs of water onto a cardboard box sitting on the path, leaving a puddle, so the box is getting soaked. In the middle a white dog with brown spots plays with a tennis ball in a bowing pose. In the front, an orange cat sleeps curled on the grass, and a brown dog wearing glasses sits up holding and reading a newspaper, which is impossible for a real dog.
