# pic_085

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the front left a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is walking briskly along the path (speed lines behind her) while reading an open book, not looking where she is going. Right in front of her on the path is a closed cardboard box, so she looks about to trip over it. In the middle a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is raking a small pile of leaves on the lawn. Behind him a washing line between two posts holds a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans pegged up. A yellow kite is caught in the tree near the house on the right; its string hangs down and ends at a kite reel lying on the grass beside a small girl in a pink dress, who is pointing up at the kite. There is a birdhouse on a tall pole on the left and a round flower bed in front of the house.
