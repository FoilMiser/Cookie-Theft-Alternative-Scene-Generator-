# pic_061

## Free description
A busy, sunny back garden with a wooden fence and a cream house with a red roof on the right. On the left path, a child in a yellow shirt holds a purple and a blue balloon and reaches up, looking upset. A red balloon has escaped and is floating away above the fence, trailing its string. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is painting fence boards bright pink with a roller. A pink paint can stands at his feet, and several boards are already pink. A blue bird sits on top of the fence while a grey cat creeps along the fence toward it, as if about to pounce. On the grass, a white dog with brown spots crouches playfully in front of a yellow tennis ball and wags its tail. A girl in a pink dress holds a kite spool and points up. Her string runs to an orange kite that is stuck in the tree by the house, and loose string lies looped on the grass. A sprinkler on a hose is spraying arcs of water onto a cardboard box on the path, which is getting wet and has a puddle under it. An orange cat sleeps curled up at the front. The oddest thing is a brown dog sitting up like a person, wearing glasses and reading a newspaper.
