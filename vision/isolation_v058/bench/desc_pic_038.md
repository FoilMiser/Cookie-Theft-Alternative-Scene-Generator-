## Free description
A sunny fenced playground with a tree on the left, a swing set in the middle back, a slide on the right, a bin, a bush, and a sandpit at the front right. On the left two children are on a red seesaw. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on the low left end, and a girl in a pink dress sits on the raised right end. In the centre front an older man with grey hair and a flat cap is pushing a teal pram to the left. A sleeping baby under a pink spotted blanket lies in the pram. The sandpit has a red bucket and a blue spade in it, and a grey rock sits beside it. The single swing hangs empty, and nobody is on the slide. Nothing looks wrong or about to go wrong.
