# pic_088

## Free description
A sunny back garden: a cream house with red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, a gate and paved path on the right. A boy with curly dark hair, a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands in the middle of the lawn looking up and pointing at a yellow kite that is stuck in the tree behind the fence on the left. The kite string runs down from the tree to a spool lying on the grass at his feet, so it seems he has let go of it. To the right an old man with grey hair and a grey cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is painting the fence pink with a roller; three boards are already pink and a pink paint pot is on the grass beside him. A brown dog is lying asleep on the grass at the front. A flower bed and a bench are by the house, and a teal watering can sits beside the path. The kite looks stuck and the boy may need help getting it down.
