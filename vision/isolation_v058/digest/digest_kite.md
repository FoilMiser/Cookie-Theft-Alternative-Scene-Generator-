## Closing paragraph

Every one of the 36 readings puts the kite in the tree and calls it stuck, caught or tangled; no reading takes another reading first and none misses the kite, in any arm. Stuck with nothing beside it (class 1) is 7 of 16 old readings (S 3 of 6; O 4 of 10, 2 of 5 readers), 7 of 10 in B (4 of 5 readers) and 9 of 10 in C (5 of 5 readers), while the other reading kept to the unsure section (class 3) is 8 of 16 old, 3 of 10 in B and 1 of 10 in C, and the single class 2 reading is in S. The other reading is worded differently by arm: in old it is the kite flying "just in front of" or "near" the tree (8 of 8), in B it is the child "still flying it" (3 of 3, from two readers), in C once the kite "still flying in front of the tree". A remark that the raised reel does not fit a stuck kite ("as if flying", "may not have noticed") stands in 7 of 16 old readings, 1 of 10 in B and none in C, and if such remarks are counted against plain, plain is 3 of 16 old, 6 of 10 B and 9 of 10 C. What the child holds is said in 16 of 16 old readings and 10 of 10 in B (reel, spool or handle in 9, the string alone in 1) but in none of C's 10, where the reel is named in all 10 as lying on the grass, the child is only said to point, one reader doubts in both readings that the kite is the child's, and 3 readings from 2 readers say the reel was dropped or let go. In B no reader says the reel is lowered or at the side (it is simply held), the raised arm is "pointing" in 9 of 10 and "reaching" in 3 of 10, and "looking up", said in 13 of 16 old readings, is said in 0 of 10 in B and 2 of 10 in C. The slack string is read as a sign of stuck in 12 of 16 old readings, 3 of 10 in B and none in C (where a loop on the ground is mentioned in only 3 of 10), and none of the six readings of T4_garden_s9111_iso mixes the two children or the balloon and the kite. The numbers cannot carry much: 13 of the 15 numbered readers gave both their pictures the same class, so each arm rests on five readers (three who hedge in O, two in B, one in C), S's readers are not numbered in the tally, and each of O, B and C read a different two of the six pictures only once.
