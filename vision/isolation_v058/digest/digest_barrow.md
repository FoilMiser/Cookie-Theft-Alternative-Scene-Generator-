## Closing paragraph

Across the three fresh arms the wheel's squareness, when it is written down at all, is written almost only in the unsure section and without a second reading: class 4 holds 6 of 10 readings in O (3 of 5 readers), 4 of 10 in B (2 of 5) and 7 of 10 in C (4 of 5). No fresh reading in any arm is PLAIN (class 1) and none in any arm is hedged in the description (class 2); the only two PLAIN readings are in S, the v0.57 screen's own reader, so the largest difference in the row lies between S and the fresh arms, which in O read the same drawing, and not between the drawings. A second reading set beside the square wheel (class 3) comes from one reader in each fresh arm, 2 readings in O (reader 4), 2 in B (reader 2) and 1 in C (reader 15), beside 2 of 5 in S; six of the seven hedges are a wheel seen from an angle or in odd perspective and the seventh, in C, is "a wheel guard". The wheel is not seen in 2 readings in O (1 reader), 4 in B (2 readers), 2 in C (1 reader) and none in S; B's excess is one reader, and all four readers who missed it had T4_garden_s552986 as one of their two pictures and missed it on both. What does differ with the new drawings is the wording: "spokes" is written in no old reading (a "cross" in 4 of 15), in 5 of the 6 B readings that mention the wheel and in 6 of the 8 C readings, and the wheel is called a box or a frame in 4 of 15 old readings, 6 of 10 in B (box 4, frame 2) and 6 of 10 in C (frame 6, one of them also "a wheel guard"). No reading in any arm remarks on the wheel's size, C's larger wheel included, and the hub is named once, in S. The hedges sit on T3_garden_s56701 (3 of its 7 readings) and T3_park_s57702 (3 of 7), with 1 on T3_garden_s23030 and none on T3_garden_s56703 or T4_garden_s552986, but O reader 4 and B reader 2 each read exactly those two pictures, so picture and reader cannot be told apart. With five readers an arm, each repeating itself on both pictures (every reader in O and B gave the same class twice, four of five in C), no class count differs between O, B and C by more than one or two readers, and three class-4 readings stand on the edge of class 3 (O reader 10 "as if the wheel were a box or seen through", C reader 3 "a slightly odd drawing", C reader 6 "unintentionally odd").
