With one picture a reader in this row, counts by reading and by reader are the same numbers (S's two readings carry no reader number), so no reader's habit shows twice. What the numbers cannot show: each picture has 4 or 5 old and 4 B readings, 3 of 9 against 0 of 8 is too few to rule out chance, only one of the three fence remarks says "against", and the who-threw, string and throw-distance counts are one reader each.
