In the front right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands holding a kite spool up high. His orange diamond kite is flying well up in the sky, with a bow tail. The kite string runs in a long line from his spool up over the pond to the kite. An empty red wheelbarrow stands on the grass in the front centre, next to the path. There are trees on the left and right and some bushes. This is the only person; nothing seems to be going wrong.
