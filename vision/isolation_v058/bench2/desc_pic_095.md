# pic_095

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence at the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path at the right. A yellow kite is stuck in the tree behind the house, its string trailing down to a spool on the grass. A boy in a yellow top and orange shorts stands in the middle, pointing up at the stuck kite. Further right, an elderly man in a grey cap, brown waistcoat and white shirt is painting part of the fence pink with a roller on a pole, with a paint tin beside him. A brown puppy lies asleep on the grass in the front. A teal watering can stands near the path, a wooden bench is at the front left and a flower bed sits by the house. Nothing looks impossible.
