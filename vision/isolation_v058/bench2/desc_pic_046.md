# pic_046

## Free description
A sunny back garden, with a cream house with a blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. A yellow kite is caught in the branches of the tree beside the house; its string hangs down and runs across the grass to a string spool lying on the ground. Next to the spool a child in a yellow top and orange shorts stands pointing up at the stuck kite. To the right, an old man in a cap and brown vest is painting the fence pink with a roller on a pole; a few planks are already pink and a pink paint tin sits on the grass beside him. In front of the house there is a flower bed, a bush and an empty wooden bench. A brown dog lies asleep on the grass at the front, and a teal watering can stands near the path. Nothing looks impossible; the main problem is the kite stuck in the tree.
