# pic_005

## Free description
A sunny back garden with the house on the left, a wooden fence along the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands in the middle looking and reaching up; he holds a kite string, but the yellow kite is caught in the branches of the tree behind the house, and the slack string loops on the grass near his feet. To the right, an older man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat is painting the fence pink with a long-handled roller; a few boards are already pink and an open pink paint can stands on the grass beside him. In the front centre a brown puppy is lying down asleep on the grass. There is a flower bed and an empty wooden bench by the house, and a green watering can near the path. The main "problem" is the kite stuck in the tree.
