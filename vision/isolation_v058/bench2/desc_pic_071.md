## Free description
A sunny back garden with the house on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands in the middle holding a kite reel and reaching up; his yellow kite is caught in the tree beside the house and the slack string loops on the grass. Near the fence a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is painting the fence pink with a roller; an open pink paint can and a pink paint tray sit on the grass by his feet. A trail of pink paw prints runs from the paint tray across the grass to a white spotted dog near the path, whose paws are pink — the dog has stepped in the paint. In front, a girl in a pink dress jumps with both arms up, looking happy or laughing. An empty red wheelbarrow stands in the front centre, and there is a birdhouse on a pole, a flower bed and the house's blue door.
