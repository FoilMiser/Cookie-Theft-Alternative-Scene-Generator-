## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills and bushes in the background, a winding sandy path on the left, a tall black lamp post in the middle and a round pond with reeds on the right. In the front right, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts with curly dark hair is flying an orange diamond-shaped kite. He holds the reel up with one arm and looks up at the kite, which is high in the sky with a bow-tied tail. The kite string runs in a long diagonal line from his hand, across over the pond, up to the kite. In the front centre there is an empty red wheelbarrow on the grass, and a green litter bin stands near the lamp post. There are trees on the far left and far right, and bushes around. The boy is the only person.
