In the front left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is walking down the paved path while reading an open book. Motion lines behind her show she is moving, and a cardboard box sits on the path just ahead of her feet. She is not looking at it, so she seems about to trip over it. A yellow kite is caught in the branches of the tree behind the house corner. The kite's string runs down from the tree to a spool lying on the grass beside the girl's feet, so the kite looks stuck and the girl seems to be pointing at it. There is also a clothesline with a yellow striped towel and blue jeans, a birdhouse on a pole, and a flowerbed.
