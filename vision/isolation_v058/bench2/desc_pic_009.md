# pic_009

## Free description
A busy back garden on a sunny day, with a wooden fence, a gate at left, a paved path, a cream house with a blue door on the right, a tree and a flowerbed. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping a pile of brown leaves on the path with a broom. Behind him a man in a red T-shirt is painting the fence pink with a paint roller; part of the fence is already pink and a can of pink paint sits by his feet. An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses holds a garden hose nozzle up and looks at it, apparently puzzled because no water is coming out. A brown dog is standing on the hose, which seems to be why the water is blocked, and water is dripping out at the hose reel near the house instead. A man in a green T-shirt stands with his hands on his hips with a metal bucket upside down on his head, which is odd and funny. A small girl in a pink dress points up at a blue and white beach ball stuck in the tree. A grey cat walks along the top of the fence, a blue bird flies in the sky, and a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench drinking through a straw from a cup. A red wheelbarrow stands empty in the middle of the lawn.
