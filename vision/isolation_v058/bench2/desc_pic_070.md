# pic_070

## Free description
A busy, sunny back garden with a gate and path on the left, a wooden fence across the back and a house on the right. On the path a boy in a yellow shirt holds two balloons (purple and blue) and reaches up unhappily toward a red balloon that has escaped and is floating away into the sky. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is painting the fence pink with a roller next to an open paint can. A white spotted dog play-bows next to a tennis ball, and a girl in a pink dress holds a kite string reel and reaches up — her orange kite is caught in the tree by the house, with slack string looping on the grass. On top of the fence a grey cat is creeping toward a blue bird perched there. A garden sprinkler is spraying arcs of water onto a cardboard box sitting on the path, which is getting wet with a puddle forming. An orange cat sleeps on the grass in front, and, oddly, a brown dog wearing glasses sits upright reading a newspaper like a person.
