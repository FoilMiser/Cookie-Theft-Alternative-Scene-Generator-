# pic_008

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence along the back, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a paved path running down the left side from a wooden gate in the fence. Near the fence in the middle, a woman or girl in a yellow dress is raking a small pile of dead leaves on the grass. In the front, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is pushing a red wheelbarrow towards the left. The wheelbarrow is empty. Its wheel is a square, not a round wheel, so it could not roll properly. There is an empty swing set on the left, a teal watering can on the grass near the path, a bed of colourful flowers by the house, and a hose reel with a hose lying on the lawn on the right. Nothing seems about to happen. The square wheel is the only thing that looks wrong.
