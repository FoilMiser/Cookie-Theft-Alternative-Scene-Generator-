## Free description
A sunny playground with a dark metal railing fence and bushes behind it. On the left, a yellow kite is stuck in a tree; its string runs down to the grass, loops, and goes up to a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts who holds the reel and points up at the kite. Behind him, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans sits on a wooden bench reading a red book, not looking around. In the middle, a brown dog with a red collar crouches in a play bow with a tennis ball in front of it, tail wagging. On the right, a small girl in pink kneels in a sandbox building a sandcastle with a little red flag on top, holding a red spade. There is also a slide with a ladder (empty), a green bin, a bush and a grey rock.
