## Free description
A busy back garden on a sunny day, with a house on the right, a wooden fence along the back with a gate on the left, and a path down the left side. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is painting fence boards bright pink with a paint roller; a pink paint tin sits at his feet. On the left, a boy in a yellow shirt holds two balloons (purple and blue) and reaches up towards a red balloon that has floated away above the fence. In the middle, a girl in pink holds a kite string and looks up; the orange kite is caught in the tree near the house. A white spotted dog plays bow-down with a tennis ball, a grey cat creeps along the top of the fence towards a blue bird, and an orange cat sleeps on the grass at the front. A lawn sprinkler fed by a hose is spraying water onto a cardboard box on the path, which is getting wet and has a puddle under it. Oddest of all, a brown dog sits up at the front wearing glasses and reading a newspaper, which is impossible in real life.
