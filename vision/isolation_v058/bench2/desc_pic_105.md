# pic_105

## Free description
A sunny playground with a dark metal railing fence and bushes behind it. On the right, an older grey-haired man in a cap and brown vest sits on the low end of a red seesaw, while a small girl in pink sits high in the air on the other end, gripping the handle and looking surprised or worried; she seems stuck up because he is heavier. A small blue bird is perched on the fence at the far left. In the middle is a swing set with one empty red swing, and on the left a red-and-yellow slide with a ladder. At the front left is a sandbox with a red bucket and blue spade, and a grey rock next to it. A green bin stands by the fence and a large tree is at the far right. No one else is in the playground.
