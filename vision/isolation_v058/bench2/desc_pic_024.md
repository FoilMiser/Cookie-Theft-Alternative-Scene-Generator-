## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, a winding sandy path, a round pond with reeds, a tall lamp post and a flower bed. On the left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks a brown dog on a red leash, heading left along the path. Behind her, a yellow kite is stuck in a tree on the left; its string runs down over the path and across the grass to a small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts who holds the reel and points up at the kite. In front on the right, a bearded man in a green shirt reclines on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket next to a wicker basket holding a bottle and a baguette. A green bin stands at the bottom right and a smaller tree and bushes are near the pond. Nobody is near the pond.
