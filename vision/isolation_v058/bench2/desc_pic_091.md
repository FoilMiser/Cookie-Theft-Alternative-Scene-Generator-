# pic_091

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate at the far right and a paved path down the right side. A yellow diamond kite is caught in the branches of a tree beside the house. A boy in a yellow top and orange shorts stands on the lawn holding the kite spool low in one hand and reaching up/pointing towards the stuck kite with the other; the kite string runs from the tree down, sags loosely and loops on the grass before reaching his hand. To the right, an old man with grey hair, a cap, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers is painting the fence bright pink with a paint roller on a pole; a few planks are already pink and an open pink paint tin sits on the grass behind him. A small brown dog is curled up asleep on the lawn at the front. There is a colourful flower bed and a wooden bench by the house, and a teal watering can near the path.
