# pic_075

## Free description
A busy, sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A man in a red shirt is painting the fence pink with a roller near the gate, with a pink paint pot at his feet, and a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping a pile of leaves on the paved path with a broom. In the middle, a grey-haired older woman holds a garden hose nozzle and looks at it, but no water comes out — a brown dog is standing on the hose, which is pinched under its paw, and a little water drips at the hose reel end. Behind her, a man in a green shirt stands with hands on hips with an upside-down bucket on his head, which is odd. On the right, a little girl in pink points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the tree, and a grey cat walks along the top of the fence. A blue bird flies in the sky. On the far right, a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench drinking from a cup with a straw. There is also a red wheelbarrow and a flower bed.
