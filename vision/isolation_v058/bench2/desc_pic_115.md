## Free description
A lively sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a blue door on the right. On the path, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts holds a purple and a blue balloon while reaching up after a red balloon that has escaped and is floating away above the fence, its string dangling. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie paints the fence pink with a roller, a paint tin at his feet. On top of the fence a grey cat creeps toward a blue bird that is perched there, as if about to pounce. A white spotted dog crouches in a playful bow beside a green tennis ball. A girl in a pink dress points up at an orange kite stuck in the tree by the house; its string runs down to a spool by her feet. In the front, a garden sprinkler attached to a long hose sprays arcs of water that land on a cardboard box sitting on the path, soaking it and making a puddle. A brown dog wearing glasses sits upright reading a newspaper, which is impossible/silly, and an orange cat sleeps curled on the grass nearby. A flower bed and a bush sit by the house.
