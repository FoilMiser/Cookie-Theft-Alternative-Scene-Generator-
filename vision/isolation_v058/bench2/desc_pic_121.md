## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills and hedges in the background, a winding sandy path on the left, a pond with bulrushes on the right, a tall black lamppost and a green rubbish bin in the middle, and trees and bushes on both sides. On the right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is flying an orange kite high in the sky. He holds the reel up in his hand and the string runs taut up to the kite. On the left, an older man with grey hair, a cap, a white shirt and a brown waistcoat is pushing an empty red wheelbarrow off the path onto the grass, heading right. The wheelbarrow's wheel is square, not round, which is impossible for rolling. Otherwise the scene is calm and normal.
