## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, a winding sandy path, a lamp post, and a round pond with reeds on the right. At the front left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks a brown dog on a red lead. His yellow kite is stuck in the branches of the tree on the left; the string runs from the kite down across the path to his hand, with a slack loop lying on the grass. At the front centre-right, a bearded man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket next to a picnic basket holding a bottle and a baguette. There is a flower bed, a green bin at the front right, and trees and bushes around.
