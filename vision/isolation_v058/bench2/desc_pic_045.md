A yellow kite is stuck in the tree behind the house, and its long string runs down to a spool lying on the grass. A white dog with brown spots near the path has pink paws and has left a trail of pink paw prints leading away from the paint tray, so it has evidently walked through the paint. A girl in a pink dress at front right has both arms up, looking excited or cheering. A red wheelbarrow stands empty in the front, and there is a flower bed by the house and a birdhouse on a tall pole.
