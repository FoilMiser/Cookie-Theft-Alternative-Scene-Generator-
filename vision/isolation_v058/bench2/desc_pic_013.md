## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a wooden gate on the left, a paved path, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the left a small girl in a pink outfit is swinging on a swing set; little motion lines behind her show she is moving. In the middle-back an elderly grey-haired woman with glasses, a teal top and grey skirt is painting the fence pink with a paint roller on a long handle. About three or four fence boards to her right are already pink, while the rest of the fence is still plain brown. A small white tin of pink paint sits on the grass by her feet. In the front there is a red wheelbarrow, a teal watering can, a flowerbed full of colourful flowers, a garden hose on a reel, and a wooden bench by the house. Nothing seems about to go wrong; it looks like a calm day of garden jobs.
