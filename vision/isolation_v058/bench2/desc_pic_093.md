# pic_093

## Free description
A sunny park with a dark metal fence and hedge across the back. At the front left, a red seesaw has an old man with grey hair and a grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers sitting on its low right end, and a small girl in pink sitting on the raised left end, up in the air, holding the handle. The man's weight keeps his end down on the ground. In the middle background, a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers is walking along while eating a pink ice-cream cone. On the right stands a red climbing frame with a ladder and a yellow slide, nobody on it. There is a green bin by the fence, a tree on the left, a bush on the right and a grey rock on the grass at the front right.
