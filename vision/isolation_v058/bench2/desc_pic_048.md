# pic_048

## Free description
A busy, sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a paved path on the left leading to a gate. On the path, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is holding a purple and a blue balloon. He is reaching up after a red balloon that has escaped and is floating away over the fence. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is painting part of the fence pink with a roller, with a paint tin at his feet. On top of the fence, a grey cat is creeping towards a blue bird, as if about to pounce. On the lawn, a white spotted dog crouches playfully next to a tennis ball, and a girl in a pink dress points up at an orange kite caught in the tree by the house. The kite string runs down to a reel on the ground at her feet. A garden hose runs across the front to a sprinkler, which is spraying water in arcs straight onto a cardboard box on the path, so the box is getting wet and there is a puddle. An orange cat is asleep on the grass in front. Strangest of all, a brown dog wearing glasses and a red collar is sitting up and reading a newspaper, which is impossible.
