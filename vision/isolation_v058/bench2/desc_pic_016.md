## Free description
A sunny back garden: a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back with a gate at the right, and a stone path running to the gate. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands in the middle of the lawn, reaching one arm up toward a tree. A yellow kite is caught in the tree's branches at upper left; its string runs down to a spool lying on the grass near the boy's feet, so the kite looks stuck. To the right, an older man with grey hair, a cap, a brown vest and dark trousers is painting the fence bright pink with a roller on a long handle; three boards are already pink. An open can of pink paint sits on the grass by his feet. A small brown puppy is curled up asleep in the front centre of the lawn. There is also a flower bed, a wooden bench in the front left and a teal watering can in the front right.
