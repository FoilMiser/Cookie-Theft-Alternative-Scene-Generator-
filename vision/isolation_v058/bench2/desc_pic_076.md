# pic_076

## Free description
A sunny playground with a metal railing fence, a tree on the left and a slide on the right. A yellow kite is caught in the tree's branches; its string runs all the way down to a spool lying on the grass at the feet of a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts, who is pointing up toward the stuck kite. A man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans sits on a bench reading a red book, not paying attention. In the middle a brown dog with a red collar crouches in a playful bow next to a yellow-green tennis ball, tail wagging (there are little motion marks by its tail), as if wanting someone to throw it. On the right a small girl in pink kneels in a sandbox building a sandcastle with a little red flag on top, holding a red spade. A green bin, an empty slide and a grey rock complete the scene.
