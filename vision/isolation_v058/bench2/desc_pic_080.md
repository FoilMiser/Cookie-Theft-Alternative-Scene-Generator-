# pic_080

## Free description
A busy, sunny back garden with a fence, a gate and path on the left, and a cream house with a blue door on the right. A woman in a yellow dress sweeps leaves on the path with a broom, and an elderly man in a cap and brown waistcoat pushes a red wheelbarrow across the lawn. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans paints the fence pink with a roller, with a paint tin at his feet. A man in a green shirt and tan trousers stands with hands on hips wearing an upturned metal bucket on his head, which is odd. Behind him a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the tree, while a grey cat walks along the top of the fence. A woman in purple holds a garden hose with a yellow nozzle; the hose runs along the ground from a reel by the house, and a spotted dog is standing right on the hose. A girl in pink sits on a bench at the far right drinking through a straw, and a red bird flies in the sky. Water seems to be leaking from the hose reel (a small puddle under it), perhaps because the dog is stepping on the hose and blocking the flow.
