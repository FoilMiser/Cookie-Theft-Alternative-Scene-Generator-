# pic_032

## Free description
A busy back garden on a sunny day, with a wooden fence, a house on the right and a paved path on the left. A man in a red shirt is painting the fence pink with a paint roller on a pole. Two boards are already pink, and a pink paint tin stands by his feet. A boy in a red cap is sweeping a pile of brown leaves on the path with a broom. In the middle, an elderly woman with glasses holds a garden hose and looks closely at its nozzle, puzzled. No water is coming out. A brown dog is standing on the hose where it joins near the reel, and water is dripping out at the reel. That explains why the hose is not spraying. Behind her, a man in a green shirt has a metal bucket upside down on his head and his hands on his hips, which is odd. A grey cat walks along the top of the fence. A blue-and-white beach ball is stuck in the tree branches. A little girl in pink points up at it. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench drinking through a straw. A bluebird flies overhead, and an empty red wheelbarrow stands on the lawn.
