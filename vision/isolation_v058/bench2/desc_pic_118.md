# pic_118

## Free description
A sunny playground with a railing fence, a tree on the left and a red slide on the right. A yellow kite is caught in the upper left of the tree. Its long string curves down across the grass to a spool lying on the ground beside a boy. The boy, in a yellow top and orange shorts, stands in the front left, pointing up at the stuck kite. Behind him, a man in a red shirt and jeans sits on a bench reading a red book and is not paying attention. In the centre, a brown dog crouches in a playful bow next to a tennis ball, wagging its tail. In the sandpit at the front right, a small girl in pink kneels building a sandcastle with a little red flag on top, holding a red spade. There is a grey rock beside the sandpit, and a green bin and a bush near the fence. Nobody is on the slide.
