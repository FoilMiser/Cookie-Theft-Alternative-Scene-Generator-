# pic_031

## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, a winding sandy path, a pond with reeds, a tall lamp post, trees and a flowerbed. At the front left a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is walking a brown dog on a red lead along the path. A yellow kite is caught in the branches of the tree on the left; its string runs all the way down and across the grass to a string spool lying on the ground. A small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands next to the spool, reaching his arm up and looking toward the kite, as if he has lost it to the tree. On the right a bearded man in a green shirt sits relaxing on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket, with a wicker basket holding a bottle and a baguette. A green rubbish bin stands at the bottom right. The main "problem" is the kite stuck in the tree.
