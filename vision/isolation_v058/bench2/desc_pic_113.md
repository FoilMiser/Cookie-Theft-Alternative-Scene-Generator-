## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, trees, a winding sandy path on the left and a pond with reeds on the right. An older man with grey hair, a grey cap and a brown waistcoat walks on the path pushing a red wheelbarrow to the right. The wheelbarrow's wheel is drawn as a square, which would not roll in real life. On the right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands near the pond flying an orange kite high in the sky, holding the string spool up. A tall black lamppost and a green bin stand in the middle background. Apart from the square wheel, the scene is ordinary and calm.
