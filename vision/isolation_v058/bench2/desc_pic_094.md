# pic_094

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. At the front left a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is walking quickly down the paved path, with speed lines behind her. She is reading an open book and not looking where she is going. A cardboard box sits on the path right in front of her, so she looks about to trip over it. In the middle a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans rakes a pile of dry leaves. A washing line between two posts holds a yellow striped towel and a pair of jeans. A yellow kite is stuck in the tree beside the house. A small girl in pink stands in the middle front holding the spool and pointing up at it, with the slack string looping on the grass. There is also a birdhouse on a pole and a flower bed by the house.
