## Free description
A sunny park playground with a dark metal railing fence, a slide on the left, a swing set in the middle, a tree on the right and a sandpit in the front left. On the right, an old man and a little girl are on a red seesaw. The old man sits on the low left end with his feet on the ground; the girl in pink sits on the raised right end holding the handle, her feet dangling in the air. He is heavier, so the girl is stuck up high, and they look at each other. The swing set has a single empty swing, the slide is empty, and the sandpit holds a red bucket and blue spade with no one playing. A blue bird perches on the top of the fence at the far left. There is also a green rubbish bin, a bush and a grey rock. Nothing looks impossible.
