# pic_116

## Free description
A sunny park with rolling hills, a winding sandy path on the left, a tall lamp post in the middle and a round pond with reeds on the right. A yellow kite is stuck in the tree on the left; its string runs down from the tree, across the path and along the grass in a loop to a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts in the centre, who holds the reel and points up at the kite. In the front left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks a brown dog on a red lead; the dog walks ahead of her to the left. In the front centre-right, a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers sits leaning back on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket next to a wicker basket holding a bottle and a baguette. To the right is a colourful flower bed and a green litter bin in the corner. Nobody seems to be helping the boy.
