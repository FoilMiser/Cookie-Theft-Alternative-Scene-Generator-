# pic_101

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path down the right side. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is painting fence boards bright pink with a roller; beside his feet are a pink paint tin and a paint tray full of pink paint. A trail of pink paw prints leads from the paint tray across the grass to a small white dog with brown spots, whose paws are pink — the dog has clearly walked through the paint. In the front, a boy in a yellow shirt holds a kite string and points up; the yellow kite is stuck in the tree beside the house. A girl in a pink dress jumps with both arms up, looking happy or cheering. A red wheelbarrow stands empty at the front, and a birdhouse on a tall pole stands by the fence. A flower bed sits in front of the house.

- Boy with kite string (centre-front): dark curly hair, yellow shirt, orange shorts; holding the kite string spool and pointing up at the kite in the tree.
- Girl (centre-right, front): dark hair, pink dress; jumping or bouncing with both arms raised, smiling.
- White spotted dog (right, by the path): pink-stained paws, walking/standing near the path, smiling.
- Yellow kite (top left): caught in the tree branches, string running down to the boy.
- Pink paint roller, paint tin, and paint tray (centre-right): painting equipment; part of the fence is already pink.
- Pink paw prints (centre-right): a line of prints from the tray to the dog.
- Red wheelbarrow (front centre): empty, standing still.
- Birdhouse on a pole (right, back).
- Flower bed (left), bush, tree, house with blue door (left), gate (right), path (right).

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl's pose could be read as jumping for joy, cheering, or reaching for something; she has a shadow a little beneath her, so she may be in mid-air.
- The kite could be read as stuck in the tree or just flying in front of it; it overlaps the branches so it looks stuck.
- The paw prints start beside the tray rather than inside it, but the meaning (dog stepped in paint) is clear.
