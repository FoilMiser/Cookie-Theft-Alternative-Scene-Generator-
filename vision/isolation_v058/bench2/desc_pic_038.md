# pic_038

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, and a paved path down the right side to a gate. A yellow kite is caught in the branches of the tree behind the house. Its string runs all the way down to a reel lying on the grass at the feet of a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts, who is pointing up at it. By the fence, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is painting fence boards bright pink with a roller. An open pink paint tin and a paint tray stand behind him. A white dog with brown spots has pink paint on its paws and has left a trail of pink pawprints from the paint tray across the grass towards the path, so it has evidently stepped in the tray. A girl in a pink dress stands in the front right with both arms raised, cheering or jumping. An empty red wheelbarrow stands on the lawn in front. There is a birdhouse on a tall pole and a flower bed by the house.
