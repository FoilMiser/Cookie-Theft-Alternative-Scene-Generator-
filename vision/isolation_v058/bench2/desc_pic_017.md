A yellow kite is caught in the tree at upper left, with its string running down to a spool on the grass by the feet of a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts, who is reaching up toward it. Next to him on the grass are an open pink paint can and a paint tray with pink paint. A girl in a pink dress stands to the right with both arms raised and a big smile, as if cheering or laughing. A red wheelbarrow stands empty in the front centre. A birdhouse on a tall pole stands in front of the fence, and there is a flower bed by the house.
