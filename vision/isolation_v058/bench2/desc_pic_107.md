## Free description
A sunny park with a dark metal fence and hedge across the back. On the left, a yellow kite is stuck in the branches of a tree, and its string runs all the way down to a small boy in a yellow top and orange shorts at the front left-centre, who holds the string and reaches up toward the kite; there is a slack loop of string on the grass. Behind him, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans sits on a wooden park bench reading a red book. In the middle, a brown dog with a red collar crouches playfully with its tail wagging beside a tennis ball. At the front right, a small girl in pink kneels in a sandpit building a sandcastle with a little flag, holding a red spade. On the right there is a red climbing frame with a yellow slide, a green bin by the fence, a bush and a grey rock.
