## Free description
A sunny playground with a dark metal railing fence across the back, a tree on the left and a red slide with a ladder on the right. In the front left, a small girl in pink and an older grey-haired man in a cap and brown waistcoat are on a red seesaw. The girl's end is up in the air and the old man's end is down on the ground, so his weight is holding her up. The girl is holding the seesaw's handle with one arm stretched forward; the old man sits with his hands on his knees and looks towards her, seemingly a little concerned. In the middle background, a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers walks along in front of the fence, eating a pink ice cream cone. Nobody is using the slide. There is a green litter bin by the fence, a bush at the far right and a grey rock in the front right corner. Nothing looks clearly impossible.
