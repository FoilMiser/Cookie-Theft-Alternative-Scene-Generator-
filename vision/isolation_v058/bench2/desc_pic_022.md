# pic_022

## Free description
A sunny playground with a dark metal railing fence and green bushes behind it. On the right, an older grey-haired man in a cap and brown vest sits on the low end of a red seesaw, while a small girl in pink is up high on the other end, holding the handle and looking surprised or worried. Her feet dangle well off the ground, so she seems to be stuck up in the air because he is much heavier. In the middle, a brown dog with a red collar crouches in a play bow with a yellow-green tennis ball in front of it, tail wagging (motion marks by its tail), as if inviting someone to play. Behind the dog is a swing set with a single empty red swing, and on the left a red-and-yellow slide with a ladder. In front on the left is a sandbox with a red bucket and blue spade in it, and a grey rock beside it. A green bin stands near the fence and a large tree is at the far right. Nobody is using the slide, swing or sandbox.
