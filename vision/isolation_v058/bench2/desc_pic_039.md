On the left there is a red ladder slide, and in the centre a swing frame with a single empty swing seat. In the front there is a sandpit with a red bucket and a blue spade, a grey rock beside it, a green bin by the fence and a bush on the left. It looks as if the girl is stuck up high waiting for the man to let her down.
