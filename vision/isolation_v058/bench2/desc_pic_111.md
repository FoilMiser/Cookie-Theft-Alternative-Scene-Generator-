## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left, a wooden fence along the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path down the right side. An older man with grey hair, a grey cap and a brown waistcoat is painting fence boards bright pink with a long-handled roller, with a pink paint tin at his feet. In the middle, a boy in a yellow shirt holds a kite string and points up; the yellow kite is stuck in the tree next to the house. A brown dog lies asleep on the grass at the front. There is a wooden bench in the front left, a flower bed in front of the house, and a teal watering can on the grass near the path. Nothing looks impossible; the main problem in the scene is the kite caught in the tree.
