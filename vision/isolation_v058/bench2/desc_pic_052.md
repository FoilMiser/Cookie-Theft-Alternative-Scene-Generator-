# pic_052

## Free description
A sunny playground with a dark metal railing fence across the back, a tree on the left and a slide on the right. In the front left a little girl in pink and an older man are on a red seesaw. The girl sits on the raised left end holding the handle, with her feet dangling. The grey-haired man in a cap sits on the lowered right end, and his feet are on the ground. He looks towards the girl. Further back in the middle, a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers walks to the right while licking a pink ice cream cone. The slide on the right is empty. A green bin stands near the fence and a grey rock lies at the front right. The seesaw is tipped because the heavier man is down and the light girl is up.
