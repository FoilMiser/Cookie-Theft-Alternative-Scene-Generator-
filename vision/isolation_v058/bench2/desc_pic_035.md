# pic_035

## Free description
A busy sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and path on the left, and a cream house with a blue door on the right. A woman in a yellow dress sweeps a pile of leaves near the gate, while an old man pushes an empty red wheelbarrow in front of her. A man in a red T-shirt is painting part of the fence pink with a roller, a paint tin beside him. In the middle, a woman in a purple top holds a garden hose and peers into its yellow nozzle at her face, while a white spotted dog stands right on top of the hose behind her, apparently squashing it; water is leaking out at the hose reel. It looks like the water is blocked by the dog and may spray into her face when the dog steps off. Next to her a man in a green shirt stands with hands on hips wearing a metal bucket upside down on his head. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the tree, a grey cat walks along the top of the fence, a red bird flies in the sky, and a girl in pink sits on the bench by the house drinking through a straw. A birdhouse on a pole stands on the left.
