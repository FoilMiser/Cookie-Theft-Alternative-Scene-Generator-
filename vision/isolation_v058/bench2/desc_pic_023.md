# pic_023

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is walking briskly along the paved path while reading an open book, not looking where she is going; a cardboard box sits on the path just ahead of her feet, so she seems about to trip over it. In the middle, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is raking a pile of dry leaves. In front of the clothesline, a small girl in a pink dress holds a kite reel and points up: her yellow kite is caught in the tree at the top right, with its string running from the tree down to the ground and back to her. A clothesline between two posts holds a yellow striped towel and a pair of blue jeans. There is a birdhouse on a tall pole, a flower bed with colourful flowers, and a bush beside the house.
