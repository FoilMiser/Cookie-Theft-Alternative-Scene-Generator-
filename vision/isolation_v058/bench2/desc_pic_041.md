# pic_041

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence at the back, a gate on the right and a paved path leading to it. At the back, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is painting the fence bright pink with a paint roller on a pole; about four planks are already pink. At his feet are a paint can and a paint tray with pink paint. A trail of pink paw prints leads from the paint tray to a small white dog with brown spots near the path, whose paws are pink: the dog has walked through the paint. In the front middle, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts holds a kite string and looks up; the yellow kite is stuck in the tree behind the house, with the string looping slack on the grass. To the right, a girl in a pink dress has both arms raised and looks happy, maybe jumping or cheering (her shadow is a little below her). A red wheelbarrow stands empty in the front, and there is a flower bed of colourful flowers by the house and a birdhouse on a tall pole.
