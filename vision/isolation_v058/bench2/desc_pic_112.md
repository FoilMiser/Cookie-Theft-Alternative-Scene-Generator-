# pic_112

## Free description
A sunny playground with a dark metal railing fence and bushes behind it. On the left, a small girl in pink sits high up on one end of a red seesaw, holding the handle and looking surprised or worried, while an older grey-haired man in a cap and brown vest sits on the low end; she seems stuck in the air because he is heavier. In the middle-back, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers walks along near the fence licking a pink ice-cream cone. On the right there is a red-and-yellow slide with a ladder, a green bin, a bush, and a grey rock at the front. A tree stands at the far left. The slide is empty.
