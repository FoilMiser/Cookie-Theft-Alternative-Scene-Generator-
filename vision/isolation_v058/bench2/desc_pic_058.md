The kite is caught in the branches of a tree at the right, behind the fence, and its string runs from the tree all the way down to the girl's hand, with a loop of slack on the grass. A washing line between two posts holds a striped yellow towel and a pair of blue jeans. There is a birdhouse on a tall pole, and a flower bed with colourful flowers near the house.
