In the middle a brown dog with a red collar crouches in a play bow with its nose at a yellow-green tennis ball, tail wagging. The sandpit holds a red bucket and a blue spade but nobody is in it. The swing hangs still and the slide is empty. It is a calm scene with no obvious problem.
