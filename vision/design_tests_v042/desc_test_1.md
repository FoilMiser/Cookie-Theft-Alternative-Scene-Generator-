# desc_test_1

## Free description

It is a sunny day in a back garden with a wooden fence. A cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, and a paved path runs from a closed wooden gate on the right down to the bottom edge. In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands between two clothesline posts. She is reaching up to peg a white T-shirt onto the line. Blue jeans already hang beside it, and both are dripping water. A wicker basket of more laundry sits on the grass next to her. On the right, a girl in a purple top and teal trousers holds a garden hose with a yellow nozzle. She peers at the nozzle, but only a single drop is coming out. The hose runs from a reel by the house across the lawn, and a white dog with brown spots is standing right on it, halfway along. The hose looks swollen between the reel and the dog and thin after it, so the dog seems to be blocking the water. That suggests that when the dog steps off, water will suddenly burst out of the nozzle, maybe into the girl's face or towards the woman and the laundry. Further right, a white goat with horns and a red collar is tied by a rope to a small stake near the gate and is eating a tuft of grass.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The wide, flat teal shape under the dog's feet could be read two ways. It could be the hose squashed and bulging where the dog stands on it, or it could be a small puddle under the dog. The thicker hose on the reel side makes "pinched hose" the more likely reading.
- Where the water would go when it starts again is unclear. The nozzle points roughly left and slightly up. That is towards the woman, the laundry and the basket, not straight at the girl's face, but she is leaning in close to look at it.
- The dog is drawn mostly from behind with its head turned, so it is hard to tell whether it is looking at the girl, at the goat, or just standing there.
- The clothes are dripping heavily on a sunny day. That is plausible for freshly washed laundry, but it could also be read as the clothes having just been sprayed.
- The goat's rope makes a loose loop on the ground before reaching the stake. It looks fine, but the stake is small and easy to miss.
- Nothing is clearly floating or passing through anyone. The hose runs up alongside the girl's leg to her hands, and the woman's hands overlap the peg and the T-shirt shoulder, both as expected.
