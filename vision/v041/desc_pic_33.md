## Free description
A sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, enclosed by a tall wooden fence with a gate on the right and trees behind it. In the middle of the lawn a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands with both arms stretched out in front of her, hands together, as if ready to catch something. A small red ball with a white stripe is in the air just in front of her hands, and a dashed line curves from it back down to a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts, so the boy seems to have just thrown the ball to her and she is about to catch it. In front, a white dog with brown spots is in a play-bow with its tail wagging (motion lines), nose to a yellow-green tennis ball, as if wanting to play. On the far left, a brown dog sits next to the blue door holding a red lead in its mouth, the rest of the lead trailing on the ground, as though asking to go for a walk. On the right, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses sweeps a small pile of dead leaves on the paved path with a yellow broom. A teal watering can stands on the grass near her. Nothing looks clearly impossible.
