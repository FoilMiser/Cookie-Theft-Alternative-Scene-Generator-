## Free description
It is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. On the left a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sits on a wooden bench, drinking from a red cup through a straw. In the centre a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is reaching up to peg a white T-shirt onto a washing line. The blue jeans and the T-shirt on the line are both dripping water, and a basket of more laundry sits at his feet. On the right, on the path, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is holding a huge silver fork that looks like a giant dinner fork, about as tall as his legs. He is using it to push or scrape dirt along the path towards a red dustpan, and a small cloud of dust is rising. Using a giant eating fork to sweep is strange, because dirt would slip through the prongs. There is also a flowerbed with colourful flowers, two potted plants and an empty wheelbarrow at the front.
