## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, a blue pond with bulrushes at its edges on the left, a tall black lamp post, a wooden bench, a winding sandy path on the right, and trees on both sides. In the front left a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans, with brown hair and a moustache, is kneeling on one knee beside a flower bed and reaching towards a small terracotta pot holding a pink flower, as if about to plant it. In the centre a white goat with small dark horns is bending its head down to eat a little tuft of grass that has a tiny red flower in it. On the right an older man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat stands at the edge of the path holding a paper bag and scattering seeds onto the ground, where a small blue bird is pecking at them. Having a goat loose in a park is a bit unusual, and it is close to the flower bed the man is working on, so it might go for his flowers next. Nobody is on the bench.
