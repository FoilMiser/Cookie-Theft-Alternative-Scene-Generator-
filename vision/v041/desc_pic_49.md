## Free description

This is a sunny day in a park, with green hills, a pond with bulrushes on the left, a tall black lamp post in the middle and a winding sandy path on the right. In the front centre a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and black trousers has just thrown a red ball. A dashed line traces the ball's arc from his hand toward a child standing on the path on the right. That child, in a yellow shirt and orange shorts, holds both arms out toward the ball, ready to catch it. Just behind the boy, a girl in a pink dress walks along holding a yellow balloon on a string. There is a flower bed with mixed flowers at the bottom left and a green bin in the bottom-left corner. The odd thing is that an orange goldfish is sitting on a curled branch of the big tree on the right. A fish out of water up in a tree is impossible. The ball looks about to reach the catcher.
