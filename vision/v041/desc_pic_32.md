# pic_32

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house (blue door, red roof) on the left, a wooden picket fence across the back with a gate at right, and a paved path at right. Beside the flower bed on the left, an elderly man in a grey cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers waters the flowers with a teal watering can. The water stream oddly arcs upwards from the spout into the air, ending in a few drops, instead of falling onto the flowers. In the front centre a little girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes holds up a kite string with one arm and looks up. The string runs high into the sky to a yellow diamond kite with a tail of small bows at top right. To the right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts smiles and waves one raised hand (small motion marks beside it). An empty grey wheelbarrow sits at front right of centre, and two potted plants stand near the gate. Everyone looks happy, and nothing seems about to go wrong.
