## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden, with a wooden fence across the back, a gate on the left, a paved path leading to it, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the middle of the lawn a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is holding a garden hose with both hands, with the nozzle raised in front of his face. He seems to be looking straight at the nozzle, and only a single small drop of water is coming out of it. The hose runs from a hose reel near the bench on the right, across the lawn and up to his hands. A white goat with small horns is standing in the middle of the garden facing the man, and its feet seem to be standing right on the hose. So it looks as if the goat is blocking the water, and if it steps off, the water might spray straight into the man's face. A washing line between two posts behind him has a yellow-and-red striped top and a pair of blue jeans hanging on it. There is also a flowerbed full of colourful flowers, two potted plants, a birdhouse on a pole, a wooden bench and an empty wheelbarrow. Having a goat loose in a small home garden is unusual.
