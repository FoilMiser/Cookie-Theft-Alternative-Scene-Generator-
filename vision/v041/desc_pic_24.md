## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a house on the left, a long wooden fence across the back and a gate and paved path on the right. A woman (or older girl) with dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes is standing by the fence painting it blue with a paint roller on a stick; a section of about three fence boards is already blue and the rest is still natural wood. On the grass behind her is a tin of blue paint and a paint tray with blue paint in it. A line of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the grass to a white dog with brown spots, whose paws are blue: the dog has clearly stepped in the paint tray and is trotting away to the right, looking pleased with itself. The woman has her back to the dog and does not seem to have noticed. In the foreground there is an empty wheelbarrow and a teal watering can, and there is a flower bed and a bush in front of the house. The sun is in the top right with two clouds. The dog is likely to spread blue paint further around the garden.
