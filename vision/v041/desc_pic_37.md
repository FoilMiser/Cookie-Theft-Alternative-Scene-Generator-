# pic_37

## Free description
A sunny back garden seen the other way round: the cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the right, the sun is top left, and a brown gate with a paved path is on the left. On the path at the front left, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, is sweeping a little pile of dirt into a red dustpan, raising a small puff of dust. Strangely, instead of a broom she is holding a giant silver table fork, bigger than half her height, and sweeping with its prongs. In the middle of the lawn stands a dark A-frame swing set with a single empty red swing seat hanging still. A grey wheelbarrow sits empty on the grass at the front centre, a birdhouse on a tall pole stands by the gate, and a flower bed is next to the house. No one else is present.
