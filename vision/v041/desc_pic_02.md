# Picture 02

## Free description

A back garden on a sunny day, with a tall wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate at the far left and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. A man with dark skin, short dark hair and a beard, in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers, stands on a paved path at the front left, sweeping a small pile of dry brown leaves with a yellow broom. In the middle of the lawn is a metal A-frame swing set with one red seat hanging empty and still. A teal watering can sits on the grass in front of the swing. There is a flower bed with bright flowers in front of the house, a bush beside the house and a tree behind it. The strange thing is a closed cardboard box hanging in the sky above the fence in the middle of the picture, tilted at an angle. Nobody is near it and there is nothing for it to hang from, so it is either floating impossibly or falling from nowhere. If it is falling, it would land on the lawn near the swing. The man doesn't seem to have noticed it and keeps sweeping.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The box in the sky is the main oddity. It could be read as floating, or as falling (possibly thrown over the fence from next door), but nobody is shown throwing it and there are no motion lines.
- The man's gaze is to the right, not down at his sweeping. He might be looking at something, but not clearly at the box, which is above and to the right of him.
- The swing seat is empty and hangs straight, so nothing suggests anyone has just used it.
- Nothing else seems to overlap wrongly. The swing frame stands in front of the fence as expected.
