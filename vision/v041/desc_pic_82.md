## Free description

A man in a red shirt and blue jeans, with a moustache, is hanging washing on a clothes line strung between two wooden posts; he is reaching up and pegging a white T-shirt, and a pair of blue jeans already hangs further along. Both the T-shirt and the jeans are dripping water. On the grass below the line is a laundry basket full of clothes (pink, white, blue), with a blue item hanging over the side and also dripping. In the front centre, a woman in a yellow dress is sitting reading a red book. An orange cat is curled up asleep on the lawn at the front right. A teal watering can stands on the grass near the path, two potted plants sit by the fence, and there is a flower bed in front of the house. The woman appears to be sitting on nothing — her pose is like sitting on a chair, but there is no chair or bench under her.
