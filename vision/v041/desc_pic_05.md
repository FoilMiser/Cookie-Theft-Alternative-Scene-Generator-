# Picture 05

## Free description

A quiet park on a sunny day with a pond on the left, a tall lamp post in the centre, a winding sandy path and a big tree on the right, and green hills in the background. A man with dark skin and a beard, in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers, stands on the grass at the right-hand edge of the pond, fishing. He holds a long fishing rod out over the water. The line hangs down to a red-and-white float bobbing in the middle of the pond, with a small ripple ring around it. Nothing is on the line yet, and he is waiting for a bite. There is an empty wooden bench beside the path on the right, a flower bed and a green litter bin at the front left and a grey rock on the grass at the front centre. He is the only person, and there are no animals. Nothing looks wrong or impossible; it is a calm scene.
