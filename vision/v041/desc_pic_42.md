## Free description
It is a busy, sunny town square paved with grey tiles, in front of a row of colourful shops with striped awnings. On the left a man carrying a yellow shopping bag walks past a fruit stall. In front of him a woman in a yellow dress walks to the right while reading an open book, and a cardboard box is lying on the ground just ahead of her, so she looks likely to trip over it. A small girl in a pink dress is jumping with her arms up and laughing behind her, and her shadow is below her feet, so she seems to be in mid-air. In the centre a woman in a purple top is watering a flower planter with a watering can, but the stream of water curves upwards away from the flowers instead of pouring down onto them. An old woman with glasses sits on a bench reading a red book. A sandwich lies on the bench beside her, and a blue bird perched on the end of the bench is reaching for it, as if about to steal it. Next to her a street cleaner in an orange cap and safety vest is sweeping a pile of leaves with a broom. Beside him, oddly, a brown dog is sitting up and holding an open newspaper as if reading it. On the right a boy in a red cap points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of a tree. A red bird flies in the sky at the top right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The watering-can water goes upwards from the can into the air, with drops at the top, instead of falling onto the flowers. Either it is meant to show a splash or arc and is drawn wrong, or it is an impossible detail.
- A dog holding and "reading" a newspaper is impossible. It could also be read as the dog just having picked up a newspaper in its mouth and paws.
- The blue bird could be reaching to steal the sandwich, or just sitting on the bench next to it.
- The cardboard box could be read as a tripping hazard right in the reading woman's path. Its position also makes it look as if she might just walk past it.
- The little girl seems to float slightly above her shadow. This most likely means she is jumping, but it could look like floating.
- It is not clear whether the girl belongs with the reading woman (mother and child?) or is on her own.
