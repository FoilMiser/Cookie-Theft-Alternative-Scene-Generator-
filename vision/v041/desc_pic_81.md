## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, and a path on the right leading to a wooden gate. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans, with a moustache, stands in the middle with his hands on his hips and a white/silver bucket turned upside down on his head like a hat, which looks silly. To his right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence blue with a long-handled paint roller; a section of about three fence boards is already blue. A paint tin and a paint tray full of blue paint sit on the grass beside her. A trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the lawn to a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar, whose feet are blue — the dog has evidently stepped in the paint and is walking off along the path toward the gate. At the front, a garden hose runs in from the left to a sprinkler that is spraying arcs of water to the right, and the water is landing on a cardboard box sitting on the lawn, which is getting soaked and has a puddle around it. The flower bed by the house is not being watered at all by the sprinkler.
