# Picture 08

## Free description

A park on a sunny day, with a pond on the left, a tall lamp post in the centre, a winding sandy path and a big tree on the right, and hills in the background. In the front centre, an old man in a grey flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is sitting on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket. He is leaning back on one arm with his legs stretched out onto the grass. A wicker picnic basket sits on the blanket beside him. His eyes are squeezed shut and he looks content, as though he's relaxing in the sun, laughing or dozing. A white duck is swimming on the pond behind him. The odd thing is a wooden ladder standing upright on the grass next to the path, at the back right. It isn't leaning against a tree, wall or anything else, so it couldn't really stand up on its own. There is also a flower bed and a green litter bin at the front left, and a grey rock at the front centre. Nobody else is around.

## Inventory

- Old man, front centre, on the picnic blanket: grey flat cap, grey hair, moustache, white shirt, brown waistcoat, dark trousers, brown shoes; sitting and leaning back on one arm with his eyes closed, relaxing.
- Duck, centre left, on the pond: white duck with orange beak; swimming calmly.
- Picnic blanket, front centre: red-and-white checked; the man sits on it.
- Picnic basket, front centre, on the blanket: brown wicker basket with a handle.
- Wooden ladder, back right, on the grass beside the path: standing upright and slightly tilted, not leaning on anything, with no one on it; it seems to stand by itself.
- Pond, left: blue water with bulrushes at both ends.
- Lamp post, centre: tall black street lamp.
- Grey rock, front centre.
- Flower bed, front left: red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers.
- Litter bin, bottom left corner: green.
- Sandy path, right: winds from the back to the front.
- Trees: small tree at back left, large tree at right. Bushes, hills, sun (top right) and two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The free-standing ladder is the clear oddity. It could be read as balancing impossibly on its own, or as having been left there by a workman with nothing to lean on. Either way, it should fall over.
- There is a small dark mark near the top rungs of the ladder that I could not make out. It might be a small object or bird, or just a drawing mark. A curved line also shows through the lower part of the ladder; this seems to be the edge of the path behind it.
- The man's closed eyes could mean he is laughing, sleeping, or just squinting in the sun.
- The man's legs stick out past the edge of the blanket onto the grass; this looks natural, not wrong.
