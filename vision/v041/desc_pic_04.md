# Picture 04

## Free description

A busy park on a sunny day, with a pond on the left, a lamp post in the middle, a winding path on the right and many people and animals. At the front left, an old man in a grey flat cap and a brown waistcoat is watering a flower bed, but he is pouring the water out of a black frying pan instead of a watering can, which is odd. At the far left, a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar sits up on its back legs holding an open newspaper as if reading it, which is impossible. On the pond a white duck, with speed lines behind it, is pecking at little pale crumbs on the water. A girl in a pink dress stands on the grass near the pond, holding a small paper bag and stretching her arm towards the water, so she seems to be feeding the duck. In the front centre a child in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts is crying, rubbing one eye and holding an empty cone, because the pink ice cream has fallen on the ground. A brown dog with a red collar is licking it up. Behind them is an empty bench, and further back a white goat is grazing on a tuft of grass next to the path. An old woman with glasses and grey hair is sweeping leaves on the path. On the far right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie holds the string of a purple kite that is stuck in the branches of a tree, and he looks up at it in dismay. A blue bird is flying in the sky near the top of the lamp post.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Watering the flowers with a frying pan looks deliberately wrong. The thing he holds could possibly be a saucepan rather than a frying pan, but either way it is not a watering can.
- A dog reading a newspaper is impossible. Another reading is that the newspaper is just propped up next to the dog and it is sitting behind it.
- The goat in a public park is unusual but not impossible (it might have wandered in).
- The duck's white lines could read as speed lines (rushing to the food) or as a wake/ripples in the water.
- The crumbs: most are on the water, but one or two near the right edge of the pond could be on the bank.
- The big tree on the right has several small, lighter-green bush-shaped blobs drawn on top of its leaves. They may be meant as leaf clumps, but they look a little like separate bushes pasted onto the tree.
- The boy's expression could be worry or shouting. It is also unclear whether he is tugging the string to free the kite or just holding it.
- The bird is flying near the lamp post with nothing obviously happening to it.
