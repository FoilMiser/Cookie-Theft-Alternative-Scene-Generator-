## Free description

On the right, a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers is walking toward the left (toward the bench) in front of the pond, also holding a pink ice-cream cone up near his mouth. A flower bed sits in the bottom right with a green rubbish bin in the corner, and there are bushes around the edges. The sun is in the top left and there are two white clouds. It looks like the dog might get some ice cream, or might try to grab it, and the boy has not noticed it.
