## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and a bird house on a pole on the left, a swing set, a washing line, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the left path a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and black trousers is sweeping a small pile of leaves with a broom. In the centre a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is holding the nozzle of a garden hose up close to his face and peering into it, while only a single drop of water comes out. The hose runs down from his hand, around along the grass, and over to a hose reel by the house, and a white dog with brown spots is standing right on top of the hose. Just past the dog the hose looks swollen, as if water is building up behind where the dog is pressing on it. It looks like the dog is blocking the water, the man is puzzled about why nothing is coming out, and he is about to get sprayed in the face when the dog steps off. A yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans hang on the washing line, and the swing is empty.
