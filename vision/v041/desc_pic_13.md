## Free description
This is a sunny back garden seen the other way round from a usual layout. The cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the right, with a tree behind it. A tall wooden fence runs across the back with a wooden gate on the far left, and a paved path runs from the gate down to the bottom left corner. A grey swing frame with an empty red seat stands on the left. In the front centre a girl or young woman in a purple T-shirt and teal trousers, with a long dark ponytail, is digging in the lawn with a long-handled shovel. The shovel blade is in a small dark hole and holds a clump of soil, and a small mound of dug-up earth with a few pebbles sits next to it. While digging she is looking to the right, toward the house, rather than at the hole. A colourful flower bed is in front of the house, and a hose reel with a yellow-nozzled hose lies on the grass on the right. A teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow sit at the front left. Nothing looks impossible. She is simply digging a hole in the middle of the lawn.
