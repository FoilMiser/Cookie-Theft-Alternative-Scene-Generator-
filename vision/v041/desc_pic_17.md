# pic_17

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with no people or ordinary animals in it. On the left there is a cream house with a red roof, a four-pane window and a blue front door. Behind the house a tree stands up, and on one of its branches sits a bright orange fish, perched where you would expect a bird. A tall wooden fence runs across the back of the garden, with a wooden gate in it on the right; a paved path leads from that gate down towards the bottom right corner. In front of the house is a round bed of colourful flowers (red, yellow, purple, pink, orange). Two plants in clay pots stand on the grass near the fence, and there are two green bushes. In the foreground there is an empty grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can, just sitting on the lawn. The sun shines in the top right and there are two small white clouds. The fish in the tree is the one thing that is clearly wrong: a fish cannot live or sit in a tree.
