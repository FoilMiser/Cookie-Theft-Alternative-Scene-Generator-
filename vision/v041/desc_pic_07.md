# Picture 07

## Free description

A playground on a sunny day, enclosed by a dark metal railing fence with hedges behind, a tree on the left and a bush on the right. On the left is a red seesaw. A small girl in a pink dress sits on the high end, stuck up in the air with her feet dangling and holding onto the handle. A grown man with dark skin, in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers, sits on the low end with his legs crossed, reading an open book. Because he is much heavier, the seesaw is tipped all the way down on his side, and he seems to be ignoring the girl, who has her mouth open as if calling out. In the middle, a young woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers walks to the right while licking a pink ice-cream cone. On the right are a red-framed slide with a yellow-orange chute and a ladder, both empty. At the front right is a sandpit with a red bucket and a blue spade in it, and there is a grey rock beside it. Nothing is physically impossible, but an adult reading on a seesaw and leaving a child stranded at the top is odd and a bit unfair.

- Girl, left, on the raised end of the seesaw: small, dark hair in a bun, pink dress, yellow shoes; sitting high in the air, holding the handle, mouth open.
- Man, centre left, on the low end of the seesaw: dark skin, short dark hair, beard, green T-shirt, khaki trousers, brown shoes; sitting cross-legged, reading an open book, not looking at the girl.
- Woman/teenager, centre: long dark hair, purple top, teal trousers, dark shoes; walking to the right, licking a pink ice-cream cone.
- Seesaw, left: red board on a grey triangular stand, tipped down on the man's side and up on the girl's side.
- Book, in the man's hands: open.
- Ice-cream cone, in the woman's hand: pink scoop.
- Slide, right: red frame, yellow and orange chute sloping down to the left, grey ladder at the back; empty.
- Sandpit, front right: wooden frame with sand; contains a red bucket and a blue spade; nobody playing in it.
- Grey rock, front right, beside the sandpit.
- Metal railing fence: across the back of the playground, with hedges behind.
- Tree, left; bush, right.
- Sun (top right) and two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The girl's expression (open mouth, round eyes) could read as calling to the man, being scared at the height, or just surprised.
- The man may not have noticed the girl at all, or he may be deliberately keeping her up there. He looks absorbed in his book.
- Where the man sits, his crossed legs overlap the seesaw board, so it is slightly hard to see exactly where he is sitting. He seems to sit just above the board, near (but not quite at) its lower end.
- It is not clear whether the woman with the ice cream has anything to do with the seesaw pair.
- Nothing seems to be floating or passing through anything else.
