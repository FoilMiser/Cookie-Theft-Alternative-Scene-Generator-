# Picture 06

## Free description

A paved town square or high street on a sunny day, in front of a row of colourful two-storey shops with striped awnings. A blue bird is flying in the sky at the top right. At the front centre-left, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is walking to the right while reading an open book. A cardboard box sits on the pavement directly in his path, so he is about to trip over it or walk into it. Behind him, by the shops, a child in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts is crying, rubbing one eye and holding an empty cone, because the pink ice cream has fallen on the ground. A brown dog with a red collar is licking the dropped ice cream up. On the right, an old woman with grey hair and glasses is sweeping a pile of leaves with a broom next to a tree in a round planter. On the left is a market stall with a striped canopy and crates of fruit, and another cardboard box sits on the ground nearby. There is also an empty bench in the middle, a lamp post, a bin, two flower planters by the shop windows and a potted plant in each bottom corner. Nothing looks impossible; the boy heading for the box is the main thing about to happen.
