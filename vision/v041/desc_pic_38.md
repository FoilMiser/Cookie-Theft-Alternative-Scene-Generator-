## Free description
A paved town square in front of a row of shops (pink, pale green, peach and cream buildings with striped awnings) under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds. In the middle, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers walks along reading an open book, and a cardboard box sits on the ground right in his path, so he looks about to trip over it. Behind him a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers sits on a wooden bench reading a red book; beside him on the bench lies a sandwich, and a blue bird is perched right next to it, apparently about to peck at or steal it while he is not looking. An elderly woman with glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, stands in front of the green building pointing up into the air. A red bird is perched on top of a black street lamp on the left. An older man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat and white shirt stands near a fruit-and-vegetable stall licking a pink ice cream cone, despite the gloomy weather. On the right a girl in a pink dress jumps in the air with both arms raised, as if cheering or excited. There is also a teal bicycle in a bike rack, a tree in a round planter, a green bin, a planter of flowers, potted plants and a second cardboard box at the front left.
