This is a sunny back garden. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the right, and a wooden fence with a gate on the left runs across the back. On the paved path at the left, a girl in a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping a small pile of dead leaves with a broom. Behind her stands a metal A-frame swing set with an empty red seat. Along the back there is a washing line between two posts, with a yellow-and-red striped top and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. In the centre, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie holds the nozzle of a garden hose up close to his face and peers into it. Only a small drop of water is coming out. The hose runs back across the lawn to a hose reel by the house, and a white dog with brown spots is standing right on top of it. That looks like why no water is coming out: if the dog steps off, the water will probably spray the boy in the face. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the front, and there is a flower bed by the house.
