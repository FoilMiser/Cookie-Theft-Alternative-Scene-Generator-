On the left a girl in a pink dress holds the strings of a blue and a purple balloon in one hand while reaching up with the other towards a yellow balloon that has escaped and is floating away, its string hanging loose. On top of the fence a grey cat creeps to the right towards a small blue bird perched further along the fence. A red bird flies in the sky near the sun at the upper left.
