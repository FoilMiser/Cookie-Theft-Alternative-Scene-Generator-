# pic_31

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house (blue door, red roof) on the left, a wooden picket fence across the back with a gate at right, and a paved path at right. At left a woman or girl in a yellow dress and red shoes, with dark hair in a ponytail, holds a teal watering can over the flower bed. Oddly, the stream of water curves upwards out of the spout into the air, ending in a few drops above the flowers, instead of pouring down onto them. In the middle a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is in mid-stride with his arm stretched out. He has just thrown a red ball (a dotted line shows its path), and it is flying towards a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers, whose hands are raised to catch it. On the right a small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on a red swing on a metal swing frame, laughing with his eyes shut, swinging (motion lines beside him). An empty grey wheelbarrow sits in the front centre. The ball looks about to be caught.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water stream is the strangest thing. It starts at the spout and arcs upwards and back over the flowers, with the drops at the top, so the water seems to flow uphill. Another reading is that it is a stream falling from nowhere down into the can. Either way, the water does not land on the flowers.
- The woman seems to be looking left past the flowers rather than at what she is watering.
- The ball's dotted line starts at the boy's hand, so the obvious reading is that he threw it to the man. It could also be read as the man having thrown it and the boy having just released or missed it, but the man's open hands suggest he is about to catch it.
- On the swing, the ropes cross in front of the child's body at an angle. This looks slightly awkward but readable as swinging.
