# Picture 03

## Free description

A back garden on a windy, sunny day. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, a wooden fence runs across the back and a wooden gate is on the right, with a paved path leading from the gate to the front right. A man with dark skin and a beard, in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers, stands in the middle of the lawn. He is reaching one arm high into the air towards a red baseball cap that is flying just above his hand. White wind streaks blow across the picture next to the cap, and a couple of small leaves are tumbling through the air with it, so the wind has just blown his cap off his head. His eyes are wide and his mouth is open, so he looks surprised as he tries to grab it. There are more white wind lines in the sky at the top left and top right. A flower bed is in front of the house, two potted plants sit on the grass to the right of the man, and a teal watering can stands near the path at the bottom right. The scene makes sense; the only unusual thing is the cap in mid-air, and the wind explains it.

## Inventory

- Man, centre: dark skin, short dark hair, beard, green T-shirt, khaki trousers, brown shoes; reaching up with one arm to catch his cap, looking surprised, not wearing a hat.
- Red cap, centre, in the air just above the man's raised hand: baseball cap being blown away by the wind.
- Small leaves (one brown, one or two green), centre, in the air beside the cap: blown by the wind.
- Wind lines: white wavy streaks beside the cap across the fence, plus more in the sky at the top left and top right; show a strong breeze.
- House, left: cream walls, red roof, blue door with a step, one window.
- Flower bed, front left, in front of the house: soil with red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers.
- Two potted plants, centre right, on the grass: green leafy plants in terracotta pots.
- Watering can, front right, by the path: teal.
- Garden gate, back right: wooden gate in the fence, closed.
- Paved path, right: runs from the gate down to the bottom right corner.
- Tree, behind the house on the left; wooden fence across the back; hedge/trees beyond.
- Sun (top right) and two clouds (top centre).

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Which way the wind is blowing is unclear. The streaks sit to the right of the cap, so they could be a motion trail (the cap moving left) or wind coming from the left and pushing it right.
- He could be about to catch the cap, since it is very close to his hand, or it could be about to blow out of reach.
- Nothing looks unintentionally strange. The cap and leaves float, but the wind lines explain it.
