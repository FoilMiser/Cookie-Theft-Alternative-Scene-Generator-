The man and woman are walking towards each other from opposite sides, although they are still far apart. A fruit stall with crates of fruit is on the left, and a single wooden crate sits on the ground in front of it. An empty bench, a teal bicycle in a bike rack, a lamp post, a tree in a planter, a green bin and potted plants complete the scene. The main event is the yellow balloon escaping, and nobody else seems to notice it.
