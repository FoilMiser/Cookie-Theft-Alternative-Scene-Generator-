# pic_52

## Free description

This is a back garden on a sunny but very windy day. White wind lines streak across the sky, and the clothes on the washing line are blowing out sideways. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the right, and a wooden fence with a gate on the left runs across the back. On the paved path at the left, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans with a moustache reaches up with one hand. An orange cap is being blown away above his head, along with a few leaves; it is probably his, since he is bareheaded. In the middle of the lawn, an old man in a grey flat cap and brown waistcoat is reading a red book in a sitting position. But there is no chair, bench or anything else under him, so he is sitting on thin air. That looks impossible. Next to him, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is digging a hole with a spade, with a mound of soil beside her. A teal watering can sits on the grass at the bottom left, a birdhouse stands on a post, and there is a flower bed by the house.
