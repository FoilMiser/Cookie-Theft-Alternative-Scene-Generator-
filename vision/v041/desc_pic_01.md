# Picture 01

## Free description

A sunny day in a park, with green hills and a line of trees in the background, a sun in the top-left corner and two small clouds. A young woman with long dark hair, in a yellow dress and red shoes, walks along a winding sandy path at the front left, licking a pink ice-cream cone. To her right is an empty wooden park bench. In the middle-front a tan-and-white dog with a red collar is crouched in a "play bow", with its front end down, its rear up and its tail wagging (there are little wag lines by the tail). A yellow-green tennis ball sits right in front of its nose, so it looks ready to pounce on the ball or wants someone to throw it. The dog's eyes are squeezed shut and its tongue is out, so it looks happy and excited. On the right is a pond with bulrushes at each end, and a bed of colourful flowers sits in the front right corner. A tall black lamp post stands in the centre with a green litter bin beside it, and there is a big tree on the left and a smaller tree on the right. Nothing looks dangerous or impossible. The woman is not looking at the dog, and nobody seems to be playing with it.
