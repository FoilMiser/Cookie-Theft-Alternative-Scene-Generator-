## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house (blue door, red roof) on the left, a tall wooden fence along the back with a gate at right, and a paved path leading off at right. In the centre an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt holds a garden hose. She has the yellow nozzle raised up close to her face and is peering into it, as if puzzled that no water is coming out. The hose runs along the ground from a hose reel at front left, and a white goat is standing right on top of it. The goat seems to be squashing the hose and blocking the water. It looks like the woman is about to get sprayed in the face when the goat steps off. On the right a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers sweeps or rakes a pile of brown leaves with a yellow broom or fan rake. Behind them, blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel hang on a clothesline, and a birdhouse sits on a tall pole.
