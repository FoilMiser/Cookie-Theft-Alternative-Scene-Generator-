# pic_67

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a gate on the far left, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a flower bed in front of the house. Near the left a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is sweeping a small pile of dead leaves with a yellow broom. Next to him an older man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; about three fence boards are already blue. On the ground between them are a tray of blue paint and a paint tin. A white dog with brown spots is standing on the path at the far left by the gate, looking happy, and its paws are blue: a trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the grass to the dog, so the dog has clearly walked through the paint and is tracking it around. Neither man seems to have noticed the dog yet. A teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow sit on the lawn in the foreground.
