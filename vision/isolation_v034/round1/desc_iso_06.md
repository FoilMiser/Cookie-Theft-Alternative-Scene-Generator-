They wear a yellow short-sleeved outfit with a belt at the waist, and red shoes. Their feet rest on the grass in front of the bench, with a small shadow under them. Behind the bench is a cream-coloured house wall with a blue door, a yellow doorknob and a grey doorstep. The ground is green grass. ## Unsure or ambiguous
- The yellow outfit could be a dress or skirt, or a shirt with shorts or a romper. Because of that and the short hair, the person could be read as either a boy or a girl.
- The red object is most likely an open book, but it could also be a red folder or notebook.
- The brown rounded shape on the right is cut off at the edge. It could be a soil flower bed, a tree trunk or stump, or a small round table.
- The red round things at the far right edge could be flowers, berries or apples, possibly on the dark green bush.
- The lower legs look quite long and thin compared with the body, and the thighs are hidden under the yellow garment. This makes the sitting pose look a little stiff, with the shins hanging nearly straight down.
- The bench sits right in front of the door, with the grey doorstep showing just behind the person's shoulders. That reads as normal depth, but it is a tight overlap.
- Nothing looks like it is floating or passing through anything else.
