# pic_037

## Free description
A sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, enclosed by a tall wooden fence with a gate on the right. An older man in a flat grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands near the middle of the lawn. He is raking a small pile of dead leaves. He is standing between the two posts of a washing line, where a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes are pegged up. In front of him, a sprinkler on a hose is spraying water in arcs right onto a cardboard box. The box is getting wet and is sitting in a little puddle. Beside the box is a grey wheelbarrow whose wheel is square, which is impossible for a wheel that rolls. There is also a round flower bed full of colourful flowers, two potted plants, and a paved path leading to the gate.
