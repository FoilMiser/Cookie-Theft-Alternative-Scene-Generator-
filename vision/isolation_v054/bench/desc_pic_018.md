## Free description
A sunny park playground with a metal fence and hedges at the back. On the left is a red-and-yellow slide, a sandpit with a red bucket and blue spade, and a grey rock; a small red bird sits on the fence. A white spotted dog crouches playfully with a tennis ball. A girl in a pink dress holds a red and a purple balloon and reaches up as a yellow balloon floats away into the sky, its string far out of reach. An old woman in a teal cardigan walks along carrying a cardboard box. An old man sits on a bench reading a red book while a blue bird on the bench pecks at his sandwich. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a beach ball stuck in a tree. In the front, a child in a yellow top holding an empty cone rubs their eye, crying, while a brown dog licks the dropped pink ice cream. Near the front right is a red bicycle with square wheels, which could not roll.
