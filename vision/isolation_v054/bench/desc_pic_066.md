On the left, a man in a red shirt and blue trousers pushes a teal pram with a sleeping baby under a pink spotted blanket, walking past a fruit and vegetable stall. Behind her, in front of a shop, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers points up at the sky, as if she has felt a raindrop or is pointing at the clouds. In the front right, a little girl in a pink dress holds an empty ice-cream cone and covers her face, crying, because her ice cream has fallen on the ground. A brown dog is licking up the dropped pink ice cream at her feet. A red bicycle stands on its kickstand near a tree on the right, and an empty bench is in the middle of the street.
