## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, a gate on the right, and a paved path going off to the front right. The only person is an older man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat, standing between the two posts of a washing line and raking a small pile of leaves. A pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes hang on the washing line above him. In the front, a garden hose leads to a sprinkler that sprays arcs of water, and the water lands on a cardboard box, which is getting wet and standing in a puddle instead of watering the flowers. A grey wheelbarrow with a wooden handle stands front right, and its wheel looks square. A round flower bed of mixed flowers is in front of the house, and two potted plants stand near the fence.
