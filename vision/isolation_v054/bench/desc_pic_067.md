# pic_067

## Free description

A sunny playground in a park, fenced at the back, with a red slide on the left and a sandpit in front. A little girl in a pink dress holds a red and a purple balloon and reaches up towards a yellow balloon that has escaped and is floating away into the sky. In the front centre, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and rubs his eyes, crying, while a brown dog licks up his dropped pink ice cream from the grass. A white dog with brown spots crouches in a playful bow beside a tennis ball near the slide. An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses in a teal cardigan walks left carrying a cardboard box. An old man in a flat cap sits on a bench reading a red book, while a blue bird pecks at the sandwich he has left on the bench beside him. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of a tree. A red bicycle stands on its kickstand on the grass, and a red bird sits on the fence at the far left.
