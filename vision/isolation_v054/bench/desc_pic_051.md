# pic_051

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left (red roof, blue door), a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate at the right, and a paved path down the right side. An old man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is raking a small pile of leaves on the lawn, standing between the two posts of a washing line. On the line hang a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. In the front, a garden hose runs from the left to a sprinkler that is spraying arcs of water to the right, and the water is landing right on a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is now in a puddle and getting soaked. Next to the box is a grey wheelbarrow. There is a flower bed with colourful flowers near the house, two potted plants near the fence, a bush and a tree. Nobody seems to have noticed the box getting wet.
