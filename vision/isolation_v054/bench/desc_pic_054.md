## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, gate and path on the left and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. There is a swing set with a single empty red swing, and a washing line between two posts with a yellow striped towel and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. A girl in a purple top and teal trousers holds the end of a garden hose and looks closely at the nozzle, which points up toward her. A brown dog stands right on top of the hose further along, which would block the water, so if the dog steps off the girl might get sprayed. In the front, a small white dog with brown spots crouches in a play bow with its tail wagging, next to a tennis ball. On the right a man in a red shirt sits on a bench reading a red book.
