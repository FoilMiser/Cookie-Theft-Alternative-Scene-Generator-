# pic_060

## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, trees, bushes, a pond with reeds and a winding sandy path on the right. A child with dark curly hair, a yellow top and orange shorts stands near the pond, holding a spool and flying a yellow diamond kite high in the sky. A tall lamp post and a green bin stand behind the pond. In the foreground, a hose runs along the grass to a sprinkler that is spraying arcs of water straight onto a cardboard box, which is sitting in a puddle and getting soaked. On the bottom right is a grey wheelbarrow, partly cut off by the edge of the picture, with a square wheel that could not roll. A flower bed with colourful flowers is in the bottom left. The child is the only person in the picture.
