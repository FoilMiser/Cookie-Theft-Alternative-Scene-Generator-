## Free description
A sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof, enclosed by a wooden fence with a gate on the left. On the left a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps a small pile of dead leaves on the paved path with a broom. Next to him a man in a red shirt is painting the fence blue with a paint roller; two boards are already blue and a paint tin stands at his feet. An older woman with grey hair and glasses holds the nozzle of a long green garden hose, but no water is coming out. A brown dog is standing right on the hose, which runs back to a hose reel near the house, so the dog may be blocking the water. Behind her a man in a green shirt stands with his hands on his hips and a metal bucket upside down on his head. A small girl in a pink dress points up at a blue-and-white beach ball caught in the branches of a tree, and a grey cat walks along the top of the fence. On the right a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench drinking through a straw. A blue bird flies in the sky. The wheelbarrow in the middle has a square wheel, which could not roll.
