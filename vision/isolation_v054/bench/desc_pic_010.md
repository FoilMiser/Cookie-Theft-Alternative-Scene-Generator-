# pic_010

## Free description
A sunny day in a park with a lawn, a dark metal railing fence and hedges behind it. In the middle, a little girl in a pink dress holds a red and a purple balloon on strings and reaches her other arm up, while a yellow balloon floats away high in the sky with its string trailing loose, so she seems to have just let it go. At the front, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and rubs his eye as if crying; his pink ice cream has fallen on the grass and a brown dog is licking it up. On the left, a white dog with brown spots crouches playfully over a tennis ball near a red-and-yellow slide, and a sandpit with a red bucket and blue spade sits at the front left. An elderly woman with glasses walks along carrying a cardboard box. An older man in a cap sits on a bench reading a red book, and next to him on the bench a blue bird is pecking at his sandwich without him noticing. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of a tree. A teal bicycle stands on its kickstand at the front right, but its wheels are drawn as square frames, which looks impossible.
