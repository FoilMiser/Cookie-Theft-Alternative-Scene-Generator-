## Free description
A sunny town square in front of a row of colourful shops, with lots of people doing different things. On the left, by a fruit stall, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and rubs one eye as if crying, because the ice cream has fallen on the ground; a brown dog is licking up the pink splat. In the centre, a man in a green shirt reads a red book in a sitting position, but there is no chair or bench under him; he is sitting on thin air. Behind him a woman in a purple top sits on a real bench reading a red book, and she does not notice that a blue bird is pecking at the sandwich lying next to her. An old woman with a grey bun walks past carrying a yellow shopping bag. An old man in a brown waistcoat scatters seeds for a red bird on the ground. A blue-and-white beach ball is stuck in the branches of a tree, and a girl in a pink dress points up at it. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps up a pile of leaves with a broom. In the front left there is a red bicycle whose wheels are square, which could not roll.
