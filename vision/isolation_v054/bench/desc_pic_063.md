## Free description
A calm, sunny park with a pond, rolling hills, trees, a lamp post and a sandy path on the right. An older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, stands at the edge of the pond holding a paper bag and tossing pieces of bread to a white duck swimming in the water. Some bread pieces float on the water and the duck is reaching for one. On the right, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. There is a grey rock on the grass in the foreground and a green rubbish bin in the bottom left corner. Nothing seems about to go wrong, and nothing looks impossible.
