# pic_020

## Free description
A sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, with a wooden fence, a gate on the right and a paved path. In the middle, an old man in a cap and brown waistcoat rakes a small pile of leaves under a washing line. The washing line is strung between two posts and holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. In the foreground, a garden hose runs to a sprinkler that is spraying arcs of water directly onto a cardboard box, which is sitting in a puddle and getting soaked. Next to the box is a grey wheelbarrow whose wheel is square, which would not roll. Two potted green plants stand on the grass to the right, and a flower bed with colourful flowers is near the house. Only one person is in the picture.
