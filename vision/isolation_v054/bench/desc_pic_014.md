## Free description
A town street/pedestrian square in front of a row of colourful shops, under three dark grey storm clouds, so it looks like rain is coming. On the left, a woman in a purple top stands at a fruit stall and reaches up toward the stall's striped awning (or the sky). A man in a red shirt pushes a pram with a baby wrapped in a pink spotted blanket, but he is looking backward/sideways with his mouth open rather than where he is going. In the middle an old woman with grey hair and glasses waves her hand high in the air (there are motion lines around her hand). A little girl in a pink dress holds an empty ice-cream cone and rubs her eye as if crying, because her pink ice cream has fallen on the ground, where a brown dog is now licking it up. On the right a teal bicycle stands in a black bike rack with its kickstand down. There is also a street lamp, an empty bench, a tree, flower boxes, a green bin and a potted plant.
