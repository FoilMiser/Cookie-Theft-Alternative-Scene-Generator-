# pic_046

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a paved path, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A man in a green shirt is walking in through the gate with his eyes on an open book or paper, and a cardboard box sits on the ground just ahead of him, so he looks about to trip over it. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead along the path at the front. In the middle, an older woman with glasses holds a garden hose and looks closely into its nozzle, but no water is coming out. A brown dog is standing on the hose further along, which seems to be blocking the water, so the woman may get sprayed if the dog steps off. At the back, a little girl in a pink dress points up at a striped beach ball stuck in the tree. On the right, a boy in a red shirt sits on a wooden bench reading a red book, next to a flower bed and the hose reel.
