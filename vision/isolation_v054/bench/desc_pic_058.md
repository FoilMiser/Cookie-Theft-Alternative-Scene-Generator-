A sunny back garden enclosed by a tall wooden fence. There is a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right, and a gate with a paved path on the left. In the middle a girl with dark hair in a ponytail, a purple top and green trousers holds a garden hose with both hands and peers closely at the nozzle. It seems no water is coming out. The hose runs along the grass to a hose reel by the house. A brown dog with a red collar is standing with a back paw on the hose, which would stop the water. If the dog steps off, the water may suddenly spray the girl in the face. At the front a white dog with brown spots crouches in a play bow by a tennis ball, tail wagging. On the right a boy in a red shirt and blue trousers sits on a bench reading a red book and is not paying attention. There is also an empty swing on a swing frame, a washing line with a yellow striped towel and blue jeans, and a round bed of colourful flowers.
