# pic_055

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a paved path and a cream house with a red roof on the right. On the path, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps a small pile of leaves with a broom. A man in a red shirt is painting the fence blue with a paint roller; part of the fence is already blue and a tin of blue paint sits by his feet. Next to him is a grey wheelbarrow with a square wheel, which could not roll. In the middle, a man in a green shirt stands with his hands on his hips and a metal bucket upside down on his head, which is odd. An older woman with glasses holds a hose and looks into the nozzle while a brown dog stands on the hose, apparently blocking the water. A grey cat walks along the top of the fence, a blue bird flies in the sky, and a little girl in pink points up at a striped beach ball stuck in the tree. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench drinking from a cup with a straw.
