# pic_012

## Free description
A sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left. A tall wooden fence runs across the back, with a wooden gate on the right and a paved path leading down to the bottom right corner. In the middle, an older man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is raking a small pile of dry leaves on the lawn. Behind him is a washing line between two posts, with blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes pegged on it. In the front, a garden hose runs from the left to a sprinkler that sprays arcs of water straight onto a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which has a puddle around it, so the box is getting soaked. To the right of the box is a grey wheelbarrow whose wheel is a square, which could not roll. There is a round flower bed with colourful flowers in front of the house, two potted plants near the fence, a tree and a bush behind the house, two clouds and a bright sun.
