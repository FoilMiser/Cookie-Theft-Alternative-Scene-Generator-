A sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left. A tall wooden fence runs across the back, with a wooden gate on the right and a paved path leading to it. An old man in a cap and brown waistcoat is kneeling by a flower bed, digging with a small trowel next to a pot of pink flowers. In the middle, a boy in a red shirt and blue trousers is walking quickly to the left (motion lines behind him) while reading an open book held in front of him. A closed cardboard box sits on the grass just ahead of his feet, so he looks about to trip over it because he is not watching where he is going. On the right, an old woman with grey hair, glasses and a teal cardigan is digging with a shovel, with a pile of soil beside her. A teal watering can stands on the grass near the path.
