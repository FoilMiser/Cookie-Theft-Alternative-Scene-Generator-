# pic_034

## Free description
A sunny town square in front of a row of colourful shops with striped awnings. On the left, a girl in a yellow top and orange shorts is crying and rubbing her eye while holding an empty ice-cream cone; her ice cream has fallen on the ground and a brown dog is licking it up. Behind them is a fruit stall. An older woman with grey hair walks past carrying a yellow shopping bag. In the middle, a man in a green shirt is reading a red book in a sitting position, but there is no chair or bench under him, so he seems to be sitting on thin air. A woman in a purple top sits on a bench reading a red book, while a blue bird pecks at a sandwich lying on the bench beside her. An older man in a brown waistcoat scatters seeds for a red bird on the ground. A blue-and-white ball is stuck in a tree, and a small girl in a pink dress points up at it. On the right a boy in a red cap sweeps leaves with a broom. In the front left there is a red bicycle with square wheels.
