## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a gate on the far left. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping a pile of dry leaves on the paved path at the front left. A man in a red shirt is painting the fence blue with a paint roller; a few fence boards are already blue and a paint tin sits at his feet. An older woman with grey hair and glasses is holding a green garden hose and pointing the nozzle, but no water is coming out. The hose runs back across the lawn to a hose reel by the bench, and a brown dog is standing right on the hose, which may be why no water comes out. In the middle, a man in a green shirt stands with his hands on his hips, wearing an upside-down white bucket on his head. A little girl in a pink dress near the fence is pointing up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the tree. A grey cat is walking along the top of the fence, and a blue bird is flying in the sky. A woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench at the front right, drinking through a straw from a red cup. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands on the lawn, and its wheel is square instead of round.
