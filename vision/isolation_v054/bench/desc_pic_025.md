## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, a blue pond edged with reeds, trees, and a sandy path winding off to the right. A boy with dark curly hair, a yellow top and orange shorts stands by the pond holding a string spool up high. He is flying a yellow diamond kite with a bow tail high in the sky, and the string runs out over the pond. Near the front a garden sprinkler on a dark hose is spraying arcs of water straight onto a cardboard box. The box is getting soaked and is sitting in a small puddle. On the left is a round bed of colourful flowers, and the hose seems to run behind or through it. On the right a grey wheelbarrow rests on the grass, and its single wheel is square, which would not roll. A black lamp post and a green bin stand by the pond.
