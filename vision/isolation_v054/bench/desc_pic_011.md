# pic_011

## Free description

A sunny day in a park with green hills, trees, a pond, a lamp post and a winding sandy path on the right. In the front-centre-left a child with dark curly hair, a yellow top and orange shorts stands on the grass beside the pond. The child is flying a yellow diamond kite, holding a red-and-white string reel up in one hand. The kite is high in the sky, with a short tail of two small bows. In the front centre a garden sprinkler, fed by a long blue-green hose that runs along the ground from the left behind the flower bed, sprays arcs of water to the right. The water lands on a cardboard box sitting on the grass, so the box is getting soaked and a puddle is forming under it. A colourful flower bed is in the front left, and an empty grey wheelbarrow stands in the front right corner. A green rubbish bin stands beside the lamp post near the back of the pond.
