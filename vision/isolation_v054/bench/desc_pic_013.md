# pic_013

## Free description
A busy, sunny town street with a row of colourful shops with striped awnings, a lamp post and a paved square. On the left, by a fruit stall, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and is rubbing their eye, crying, while a brown dog licks the pink ice cream that has fallen on the ground. An old woman with a yellow shopping bag walks past the shops. A man in a green shirt is reading a red book in a sitting position, but there is no chair or bench under him: he is sitting on thin air. A woman in a purple top sits on a bench reading a red book, while a blue bird on the bench next to her pecks at her sandwich. An old man in a brown waistcoat scatters seeds or crumbs for a red bird on the ground. A beach ball is stuck in the branches of a tree, and a small girl in a pink dress points up at it. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps leaves with a broom. In the bottom left corner is a red bicycle with square wheels, which could not roll.
