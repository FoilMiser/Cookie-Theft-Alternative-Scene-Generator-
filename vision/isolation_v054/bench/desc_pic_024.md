## Free description

A sunny day in a park with green hills, trees, a pond on the left, a tall lamp post and a sandy path winding off on the right. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, stands at the right edge of the pond holding a brown paper bag. She is tossing pieces of bread into the water. A white duck is swimming towards her, already pecking at the bread, with a few more bread pieces floating near it. On the right, beside the path, a boy with brown hair, a red shirt and blue trousers sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. A grey rock lies on the grass at the front centre, and a green rubbish bin stands in the front left corner. Nothing seems wrong; it is a calm scene.
