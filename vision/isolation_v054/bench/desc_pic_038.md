## Free description
A sunny playground in a park, surrounded by a dark metal railing fence, with a red bird sitting on the fence at the far left. There is a red-and-yellow slide on the left and a sandbox in front with a red bucket and blue spade in it. A small girl in a pink dress holds a red and a purple balloon and reaches up with her other hand toward a yellow balloon that is floating away high in the sky - she seems to have just let it go. A white dog with brown spots is crouched in a play bow next to a tennis ball, tail wagging. At the front, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and rubs his eye as if crying; a brown dog is licking up the pink ice cream that has fallen on the ground. An elderly woman with grey hair walks across carrying a cardboard box. An old man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat sits on a bench reading a red book, while a blue bird is pecking at the sandwich lying on the bench beside him, apparently unnoticed. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in a tree. A teal bicycle stands in a bike rack at bottom right.
