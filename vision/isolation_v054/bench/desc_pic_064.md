# pic_064

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, a gate at the right of the fence and a paved path down the right side. A washing line is strung between two wooden posts in the middle, with a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes pegged on it. An older man in a blue-grey cap, white shirt, brown vest and dark trousers stands between the posts raking a small pile of leaves. In the front, a green hose runs from the left to a lawn sprinkler that is spraying arcs of water. The water is landing on a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which is standing in a puddle and getting soaked. To the right of the box is a grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles; its wheel is square rather than round. There is a flower bed by the house and two potted green plants near the fence.
