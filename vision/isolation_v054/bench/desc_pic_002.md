# pic_002

## Free description
A busy back garden on a sunny day, with a tall wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate on the left, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a paved path down the left side. A man in a red shirt is painting the fence blue with a paint roller; a few planks near the gate are already blue and a paint pot sits at his feet. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping a pile of brown leaves on the path with a broom. In the middle, an old woman with grey hair and glasses holds the end of a green garden hose up near her face and looks at the nozzle, while a brown dog stands right on the hose further along, which would stop the water. Behind her, a man in a green shirt stands with his hands on his hips wearing an upside-down metal bucket on his head. A small girl in a pink dress points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of a tree. A grey cat walks along the top of the fence and a blue bird flies in the sky. On the right a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench drinking through a straw from a red cup. A grey wheelbarrow stands in the middle, and its wheel is a square, not a circle.
