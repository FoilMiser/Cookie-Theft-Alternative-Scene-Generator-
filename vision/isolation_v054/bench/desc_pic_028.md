# pic_028

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left and a white house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the left, a man in a green shirt walks along the paved path reading an open book, not looking where he is going, and there is a cardboard box on the path right in front of his feet; he looks about to trip over it. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead along the fence. In the centre an old woman with grey hair and glasses holds the nozzle of a garden hose and looks at it, but a brown dog is standing on the hose, which seems to be squashing it so no water comes out. A girl in a pink dress by the fence points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in a tree. There is a flower bed in front of the house. On the right a man in a red shirt sits on a bench reading a red book.
