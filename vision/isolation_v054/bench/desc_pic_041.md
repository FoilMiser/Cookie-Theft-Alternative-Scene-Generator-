## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, a pond with reeds, trees, a lamppost, a bin and a winding sandy path. A child in a yellow top and orange shorts stands near the pond holding a string reel high and flying a yellow diamond kite with a bow tail. To the child's left is a bed of colourful flowers. A garden hose runs along the grass to a sprinkler, which is spraying arcs of water straight onto a cardboard box sitting in a puddle, so the box is getting soaked. On the far right stands a grey wheelbarrow whose wheel is square, which could not roll. Nobody else is in the picture.
