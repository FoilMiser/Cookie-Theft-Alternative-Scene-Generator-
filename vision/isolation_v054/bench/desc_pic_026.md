# pic_026

## Free description

A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a gate on the left, a paved path, a swing set, a washing line and a house with a blue door on the right. In the middle a girl with dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers holds the end of a garden hose and looks at the nozzle, as though wondering why no water is coming out. The hose runs from her hands down across the lawn to a hose reel by the house. A brown dog with a red collar is standing with a paw on the hose, which would block the water. In the front a white dog with brown spots is in a playful bow next to a yellow tennis ball, wagging its tail. On the right, a boy in a red shirt and blue trousers sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. Laundry hangs on the line: a yellow striped towel or shirt and a pair of blue jeans. It looks like the dog is about to step off the hose and spray the girl, or is the reason the hose isn't working.
