## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a gate on the left. A blue bird flies across the sky and a grey cat walks along the top of the fence. A man in a red shirt and jeans is painting the fence blue with a paint roller; a few planks near the gate are already blue and a paint tin stands by his feet. In the front left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps a pile of leaves on the path with a broom. An elderly woman in a teal top holds a garden hose nozzle up, but no water is coming out - because the brown dog in front of the flower bed is standing on the hose, which runs from a hose reel by the house. In the middle, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands with hands on hips, oddly wearing a metal bucket upside down on his head. A small girl in a pink dress points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the tree. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench drinking from a cup with a straw. A grey wheelbarrow stands in the middle of the lawn.
