# Picture 32

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden, with a house with a blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back and a closed gate and path on the left. A young woman with a brown ponytail and a blue hoodie is painting part of the fence blue, with a paint tin and a paint tray at her feet. A white dog with brown spots has stepped in the paint tray: its paws are blue, and a trail of blue paw prints runs across the grass to where it now stands on the path. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers is standing near the gate with her hands on her hips and a blue bucket on her head. In the middle, an older man with a grey beard and cap is holding the end of a garden hose up close to his face and peering into it, because only a single drop is coming out. The reason is that a brown dog with a red collar is standing on the hose, and the hose is bulging just behind the dog's foot. When the dog steps off, the water will probably spray straight into the man's face. A washing line between two posts has a striped towel and a pair of jeans hanging on it, there's a birdhouse on a post, and colourful flowers grow in front of the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The woman with her hands on her hips could be cross with the painter, the man or the dog. She is facing right, away from the spotted dog that is making the paw prints.
- The man may be looking into the nozzle to see why no water comes out, or he may simply be holding it close. He is clearly pointing it toward himself.
- It isn't certain which of the brown dog's feet is on the hose, but the hose passes under its back feet and bulges just past them.
- Whether the painter is a teenage girl or a young woman is unclear.
