# Picture 15

## Free description

This is a sunny day in a park. There is a pond on the left with some bulrushes, rolling green hills and bushes in the background, a tall black lamp post in the middle, and a sandy path winding off on the right. An old woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses walks across the grass in front of the pond. She wears a teal top and grey skirt and carries a yellow bag. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes pushes a teal pram with a baby in it, heading left. In the middle stands a wooden ladder, tilted at an angle, with nothing to lean against. Its top is in mid-air in front of the pond, so it looks like it should fall over. The pram is pushed right up against the bottom of the ladder, so it looks as if it is about to bump into it and knock it over. The two women are walking toward each other with the ladder between them.

## Inventory

- Old woman, left-centre, front (in front of the pond): grey hair in a bun, round glasses, teal long-sleeved top, grey skirt, bare legs, brown shoes. She is walking, facing right, and holding a yellow bag that hangs from one hand.
- Woman, right, front (at the edge of the path): short dark hair, yellow short-sleeved dress, red shoes. She is facing left, pushing a pram with both hands on its handle.
- Baby, centre-right, front: only the head shows, a round face peeking out under the pram's dark-blue hood. Lying or sitting in the pram.
- Wooden ladder, centre, front: a tall wooden ladder tilted with its top toward the upper left (ending in mid-air in front of the right edge of the pond) and its feet on the grass. It is not leaning on anything.
- Pram, centre-right, front: teal body, dark-blue hood, a beige oval on top (blanket or cover), and two black wheels. Its front is touching or overlapping the foot of the ladder.
- Yellow bag: carried by the old woman. It is a tall, narrow yellow rectangle, maybe a shopping bag or paper bag.
- Pond, left, middle distance: a blue oval of water with bulrushes at both ends. It is empty: no ducks or fish.
- Lamp post, centre, back: a tall black street lamp standing on the grass.
- Green rubbish bin, bottom-left corner, partly cut off by the edge of the picture.
- Path, right: a sandy path winding from the bottom of the picture toward the hills.
- Trees: one small tree on the left and one large tree on the right. Bushes on the left, centre-back and right.
- Sky: a yellow sun at top right and two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The ladder is standing on its own at a slant with nothing to lean on. This is impossible; it would fall. It could be meant as leaning on something, but there is nothing at its top: the lamp post is well to the right of it.
- The pram's front edge overlaps the foot of the ladder. It could be touching it (about to knock it over), or this could just be a drawing overlap with the pram actually in front of the ladder.
- The beige oval on top of the pram could be a blanket, a cover or a pillow.
- The yellow thing the old woman carries could be a bag, an envelope or a folder. It hangs from a small handle.
- The old woman's face points right, but her feet and stride are drawn a bit stiffly, so it is hard to be sure she is walking the same way she is facing.
