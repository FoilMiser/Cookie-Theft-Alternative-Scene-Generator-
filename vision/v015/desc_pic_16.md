# Picture 16

## Free description

This is a sunny park scene with a pond on the left, a tall lamp post in the middle, a winding path on the right, and green hills behind. A young woman with a brown ponytail and a blue hoodie stands at the right edge of the pond, fishing. Her line goes down to a red-and-white float bobbing in the water, and a white duck is swimming in the pond nearby. To the right, beside the path, an old woman with grey hair and glasses is reading a red book in a sitting position, but there is no bench or chair under her. She is sitting on thin air. On the path at the far right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is kicking an orange ball, which is flying off to the right. In the front of the picture, a red bird is pecking at the ground while a black-and-white cat crouches low behind it, creeping toward it. The cat looks about to pounce on the bird. There is a colourful flower bed at the bottom left and green rubbish bins by the lamp post and in the bottom-left corner.
