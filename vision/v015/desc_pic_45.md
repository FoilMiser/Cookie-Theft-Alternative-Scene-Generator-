# Picture 45

## Free description
This is a park on a sunny day, with the sun in the top left, two white clouds, and a red bird flying in the top right. A low metal railing fence runs across the back with green hedges behind it, and there is a tree on the right. On the left is a playground slide with a red frame and a yellow-and-orange chute; a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is sliding down it with their arms up. In the centre, a girl with a brown ponytail, blue top and black trousers has just thrown a red ball; a dotted line shows it arcing up through the air towards a bearded man in a green shirt and beige trousers on the right, who holds his hands up ready to catch. In the front, a small girl in a pink dress is holding an empty ice-cream cone and rubbing her eye as if crying, because her pink scoop of ice cream has fallen on the ground in front of her. A brown dog with a red collar has its nose down at the dropped ice cream, sniffing or eating it. A grey rock sits on the grass at the bottom left.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The small girl's gesture could be crying/wiping tears or just rubbing her eye; the fallen ice cream suggests crying.
- The dog could be sniffing the ice cream or eating it.
- The child on the slide could be a boy or a girl.
- The dog's head is drawn turned and tilted down so its face is a little hard to read.
