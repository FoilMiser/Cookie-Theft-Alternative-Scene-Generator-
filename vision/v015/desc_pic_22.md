# Picture 22

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate on the right and a paved path leading to it. A lot is going on at once. Up on the fence, a black cat is walking along the top boards, an orange cat sits on the fence where the tree is, and a red bird is perched on the fence a little further along. A blue ball is stuck up in the branches of the tree behind the house. A boy in a blue hoodie stands by the fence pointing up at the black cat (or possibly the ball), with his mouth open as if calling out. In the middle, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks along reading an open book, and there is a red bucket on the grass just ahead of her, so she might bump into it. At the front of the flowerbed, a person in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest kneels and reaches into the flowers, with a potted pink tulip beside them, as if gardening or planting. On the right, a man in a red shirt carries a cardboard box towards the gate, past an empty swing set. In the foreground a white goat with small horns is grazing on a tuft of grass next to a red pet-food bowl, and a white dog with brown spots crouches playfully on the path in front of a tennis ball, wagging its tail. Odd things: the wheelbarrow in the front has a square wheel, which could not roll, and there is a goat in an ordinary back garden.
