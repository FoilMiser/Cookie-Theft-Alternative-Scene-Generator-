# Picture 05

## Free description

This is a back garden on a sunny day, with the sun at the top right, two clouds, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, and a tall wooden fence with a gate on the right. In the middle there is a washing line between two posts with a pair of blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel pegged on it. A small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a red and a purple balloon in one hand and reaches up with his other hand towards a yellow balloon that has got loose and is floating up near the washing line. At the bottom left, a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench reading a red book, and a blue bird has landed beside her and is pecking at her sandwich without her noticing. In front of the fence, a young person in a blue hoodie is jumping high in the air with both arms up and a big smile, their shadow on the grass below. On the right, an older man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat is sweeping the grass with a yellow broom. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers is walking to the left while reading a book, and there is a blue bucket on the grass right in front of her, so she may trip over it. On the path at the far right, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is walking a white spotted dog on a red lead. There is also a colourful flower bed, a bush and a tree beside the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The jumping person is quite high off the ground, level with the fence. Read either as a big joyful jump (the separate shadow on the grass supports this) or, oddly, as floating in the air.
- The yellow balloon: its string hangs free and is not in the boy's hand, so it seems to have escaped; it is possible he only just let go and might still catch it.
- The older man is sweeping the lawn rather than the path, which is a slightly odd thing to sweep.
- The dog on the lead faces left towards the walking woman while the man walks down the path, so the dog may be pulling in a different direction from him.
- The walking woman may or may not trip over the bucket; it is right in front of her and she is looking at her book.
- No objects appear to pass through people.
