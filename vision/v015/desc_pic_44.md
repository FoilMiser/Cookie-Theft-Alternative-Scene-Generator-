# Picture 44

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a bright sun in the top right corner and two small white clouds. On the left is a cream house with a red roof and a blue door, with a wooden bench in front of it and a bed of colourful flowers beside it. In the middle, a girl with a brown ponytail, a blue long-sleeved top and black trousers is holding up a kite string and looking up; the string leads to a purple kite that is up among the leaves of the tree behind the house, so the kite looks stuck in the tree. Behind her, a woman in a yellow dress stands with her hands on her hips and has a blue bucket upside down on her head like a hat, which looks silly or wrong. In the front right, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt is digging a hole in the lawn with a spade, with a mound of soil beside her. A tall pole with a small birdhouse on top stands in the middle right, and a wooden gate in the fence opens onto a paved path running down the right side. Nobody seems to be helping the girl get her kite down.
