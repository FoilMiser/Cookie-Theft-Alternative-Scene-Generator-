# Picture 01

## Free description

This is a back garden on a sunny day, with a bright sun in the top left, two white clouds, and green treetops showing over a tall wooden fence that runs across the picture. There is a wooden gate in the fence at the far left, and on the right is the side of a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door with a grey step. Only one person is in the picture: a girl or young woman in a yellow dress and red shoes, standing on a pale paving-stone path at the bottom left. She is sweeping a small pile of dirt and pebbles into a red dustpan, and a little puff of dust is rising from it. The odd thing is what she is sweeping with: it looks like a giant silver dinner fork, not a broom. In the rest of the garden there are two potted plants near the fence, two round bushes, a flower bed full of brightly coloured flowers in front of the house, and an empty grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles on the lawn at the bottom centre. Nothing else seems to be happening; the garden is calm and tidy apart from her small pile of dirt.
