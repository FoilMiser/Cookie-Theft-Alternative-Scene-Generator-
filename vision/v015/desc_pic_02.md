# Picture 02

## Free description

This is a busy park playground on a sunny day, with a sun at the top left, two clouds, and a blue bird flying across the sky. There is a red-framed slide with a ladder on the left, a sandpit with a red bucket and blue spade at the front left, a metal fence across the back, and a big tree on the right with a blue ball stuck up in its branches. A boy in a blue hoodie on the right is standing with both arms up, looking towards the ball in the tree, as if he wants to get it down. In the front centre, a small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is rubbing his eye as if crying, holding an empty ice-cream cone; his pink scoop of ice cream has fallen on the grass at his feet, and a brown dog with a red collar is sniffing at it, about to eat it. A little girl in a pink dress holds a yellow and a purple balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other towards a red balloon that seems to be drifting away. In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress is walking while reading a book, and there is a blue bucket on the grass ahead of her, so she might trip over it. A bearded man in a green shirt stands nearby holding a pink ice-cream cone, and a woman in a purple top sits on a bench reading a red book. A small orange cat is lying asleep on the grass. The strangest thing is at the front left: a white dog is sitting up next to the sandpit holding an open newspaper in its paws as if it is reading it, which is impossible.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The red balloon: its string curves down to just about the girl's raised hand. It can be read two ways: (a) it has escaped and she is reaching for it as it floats off, or (b) she is still holding it in her raised hand. Her worried face suggests (a).
- The boy with raised arms could be reaching/asking for the ball stuck in the tree, or could simply be cheering or calling out. He is looking up-left, roughly towards the ball.
- The white animal reading the newspaper looks like a dog (white with brown ear and blue collar), though its face is small and partly hidden by the paper; it could possibly be read as another white animal. An animal reading a newspaper is impossible/odd.
- The blue bucket near the walking woman: it is on the grass ahead of her and she is looking at her book, so she may trip over it, but it is not certain she is heading straight at it.
- The orange cat lying on the grass is assumed to be asleep (eyes look closed); it could also be lying still for some other reason.
- The bearded man is holding an ice cream; it is unclear whether he has anything to do with the crying boy (e.g. his father).
