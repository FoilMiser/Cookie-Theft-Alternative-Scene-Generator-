# Picture 21

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a long wooden fence across the back, and a gate at the far right with a paved path running from it to the front. A young woman with a brown ponytail and a blue hoodie is painting the fence blue with a brush on a stick. A few boards beside her are already blue, and a paint tin and a paint tray with blue paint sit on the grass near her feet. A white dog with brown spots has stepped in the paint tray and is walking away toward the house, leaving a trail of blue paw prints across the lawn; the painter hasn't noticed. On the path at the right, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping dirt into a red dustpan, but she is using a giant silver fork instead of a broom. A red bird sits on top of the fence by the tree, a birdhouse on a tall pole stands in front of the fence, and a red dog bowl full of food sits on the grass near the front. There is also a flower bed in front of the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The woman on the path is sweeping with a giant eating fork instead of a broom. This is clearly wrong, or a silly substitute.
- The dog trail: the dog seems to have walked through the blue paint tray and is now leaving blue footprints. This is mischief rather than something impossible. The dog could be heading toward the flower bed or the house.
- The painting tool could be a brush or a small roller on a stick; the tip is just a blue blob.
- The grey pile by the dustpan could be dirt, gravel or dust.
- The birdhouse pole stands in front of the blue-painted section, so it is hard to tell whether the boards behind the pole are fully painted.
- The dog bowl is out on the lawn with no one near it. It is probably the spotted dog's bowl, but nothing shows that for certain.
