## Free description
This is a park on a sunny day, with green hills, trees, bushes, a winding sandy path on the left, a tall black lamp post in the middle, and a blue pond with reeds on the right. Near the back left, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, is sprinkling seeds from a cup or bag, and two birds (one red, one blue) are pecking at the seeds on the ground by her feet. In front of the pond, a dark-skinned man with a beard in a green shirt and khaki trousers holds his hands out to catch a red ball, which a woman with a brown ponytail in a blue top and dark trousers on the right has just thrown; the ball is in mid-air above the pond with a dotted line showing its path. In the front centre, a small dark-skinned child with braided hair in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is holding an empty ice-cream cone and rubbing their eye, looking like they are crying, because the pink sprinkled scoop has fallen onto the ground and melted. A brown dog with a red collar is bending down to sniff or eat the dropped ice cream. At the bottom right, a white goat with small horns is grazing on a tuft of grass right in front of the woman's feet, which is unusual to see in a town park. The ball looks like it is about to reach the man, and the dog looks about to eat the ice cream.
