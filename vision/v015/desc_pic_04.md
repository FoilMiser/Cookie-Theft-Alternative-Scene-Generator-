# Picture 04

## Free description

This is a paved town square or high street in front of a row of colourful shops, under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds overhead, as if it is about to rain. At a fruit and vegetable stall on the left, a bearded man in a green shirt is pointing up at the sky with a surprised face, as though he has just felt a raindrop or noticed the clouds. In front of him an older man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat is pushing a teal pram with a baby lying in it. A small boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a yellow balloon on a string and is looking towards the bench. On the bench a woman in a yellow dress is reading a red book, and right beside her a blue bird has landed on the bench and is pecking at her large sandwich, which she has not noticed. In the middle, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is walking along reading an open book, and a red ball is lying on the pavement just in front of him, so he looks about to trip over it. On the right, a person in a blue hoodie is holding a camera up to their face and taking a photo towards the middle of the scene. There is also a street lamp, a planter of flowers, a tree in a round planter, a cardboard box by the stall, and potted plants in the bottom corners.
