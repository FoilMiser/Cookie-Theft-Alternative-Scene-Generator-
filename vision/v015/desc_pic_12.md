# pic_12

## Free description
This is the same sunny back garden with the cream house and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence and gate at the back, and a path on the right. In the front left, a woman with a dark bob in a yellow dress sits on a wooden bench eating a pink ice cream cone with her eyes closed and pink cheeks, looking happy. A white dog with brown patches and a blue collar is stretched out right beside her legs, reaching its head up towards her, as if begging for the ice cream or wanting attention. In the middle an old man with a grey beard, grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is watering the flower bed with a teal watering can. Oddly, the water does not pour down onto the flowers: it comes out of the spout and arcs upwards into the air, with drops at the top, which water could not do. A small brown-and-white puppy is curled up asleep on the grass in the middle, and a red dog bowl with food in it sits on the grass to the right. There are also two potted plants and a round bush near the fence.
