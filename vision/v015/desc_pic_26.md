# Picture 26

## Free description
A sunny back garden, this time with the cream house (red roof, blue door) on the right, a wooden fence along the back, a gate and a paved path on the left, and the sun in the top left. In the middle, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers with a dark ponytail reaches up to a washing line, pegging out a white T-shirt next to a yellow and red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans. In the front right, a girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. Near the fence on the left there are two potted plants in terracotta pots, and in the foreground there is a teal watering can and an empty wheelbarrow. There is a flowerbed and a bush by the house. Something impossible is happening: an orange fish is sitting in the leaves of the tree behind the house, as if it were swimming in the tree.

- Orange fish — up in the tree behind the house (top right) — sitting among the leaves; fish do not belong in trees.
- Washing line between two wooden posts — centre — holding a white T-shirt, a yellow and red striped towel and blue jeans, with pegs.
- Two terracotta pots with green leafy plants — back left, near the fence — standing on the grass, perhaps waiting to be planted.
- Teal watering can — front left — on the grass, not being used.
- Wheelbarrow (grey tray, wooden handles) — front centre — empty, standing still.
- Flowerbed with mixed colourful flowers — right, in front of the house.
- Bush beside the house; tree behind the house.
- Wooden gate — back left — closed; paved path leads to it.
- House with blue door and window — right.
- Sun (top left) and two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fish in the tree: could be read as a real fish impossibly in a tree, or (less likely) as a fish-shaped decoration/toy hung in the tree. It looks like a deliberate oddity but has no explanation in the scene.
- The striped towel is partly hidden behind the woman's head, so it is hard to see fully.
- The girl's eyes look closed or downcast — reading, or possibly dozing.
