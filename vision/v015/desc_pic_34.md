# Picture 34

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden, with the sun in the top right, a house with a blue door on the left and a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans is painting a section of the fence blue with a long-handled brush, with a paint tin and a paint tray on the grass by his feet. A white dog with brown spots has walked through the paint: its paws are blue, and a line of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the grass to the path, where the dog is now walking toward the gate. In the front, a boy with dark curly hair, a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts is kicking an orange ball, with little motion lines showing it has just been kicked. Strangely, a cardboard box is hanging in the sky above the fence, tilted, with nothing holding it up. A bed of colourful flowers and a bush are in front of the house, and a big tree is behind it. The painter doesn't seem to have noticed the dog's paw prints.
