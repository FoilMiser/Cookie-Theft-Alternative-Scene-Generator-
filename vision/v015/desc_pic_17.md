# Picture 17

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden. A cream-coloured house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the right. A tall wooden fence runs across the back, with a wooden gate at the far left, and a paved path runs from the gate down to the bottom-left corner. An old man with grey hair, a cap, a brown waistcoat and a white shirt stands next to a flower bed, holding a green watering can tipped toward the flowers. The water does not fall onto the flowers. It shoots upward out of the spout in a curved stream, rising into the air, with drops flying up from the top. Water going upward like this is impossible, and the flowers below are not getting watered. There are two potted plants and a bush near the fence, a tree peeking over the fence, and an empty grey wheelbarrow on the lawn in the front.

## Inventory

- Old man, centre-right, middle: grey hair, dark grey-blue cap with a peak, brown waistcoat over a white long-sleeved shirt, dark trousers, brown shoes. He is standing, facing right, holding a green or teal watering can over a flower bed.
- Watering can: teal or green, held tilted in the man's hands, spout pointing toward the flowers.
- Water stream: a light-blue stream that curves up and away from the spout into the air, ending above the flowers with three drops above it. It goes upward instead of down.
- Flower bed, right-centre, middle (in front of the house): brown soil with yellow, orange, red, pink and purple flowers.
- Wheelbarrow, bottom centre, front: a grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles and a black wheel, standing empty on the grass.
- Potted plants, left-centre, back: two terracotta pots with green leafy plants, by the fence.
- Bush, centre, back: a round green bush in front of the fence.
- House, right: cream walls, red roof, a four-pane window and a blue door with a yellow knob, plus a grey doorstep.
- Fence and gate, back: a long wooden fence across the whole garden, with a darker wooden gate (with a round knob) at the far left.
- Path, bottom left: pale paving slabs leading from the gate toward the viewer.
- Tree, right-centre, back: a tree behind the fence, beside the house.
- Sky: a yellow sun at top left and two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The water stream: it could be read as water shooting up out of the can (impossible) or, less likely, as a jet of water coming up from below into the can. Either way, it defies gravity, and the flowers are not getting wet.
- The man's cap could be a flat cap or a baseball-style cap.
- The wheelbarrow handles point to the upper left and its wheel is on the right. It looks like an ordinary empty wheelbarrow, nothing odd.
