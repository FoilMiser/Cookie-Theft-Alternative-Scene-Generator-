# Picture 28

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, a gate and a paved path on the right, a washing line in the middle and a swing set on the right. A young woman (or girl) with a brown ponytail, in a blue hoodie and dark trousers, is watering the flowerbed with a garden hose, the water arcing from the nozzle onto the flowers; the hose runs back to a reel by the house. On the washing line hang blue jeans and a yellow and red striped towel. On the right, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is kicking an orange ball along the path. In the front centre is something impossible: a brown dog with a red collar is sitting up like a person, holding an open newspaper in its front paws and looking at it as if reading. The swing on the swing set is empty.
