## Free description

In the middle front, a white goat with small horns has its head down and is eating a tuft of grass. On the right, a man with brown hair, a red shirt and blue jeans stands on the path holding a small box or bag and scattering seeds or crumbs. A trail of seeds is falling from his hand onto the path, where a red bird and a blue bird are pecking at them. Behind the man there is an empty wooden bench and a large tree. It is a little unusual to see a goat wandering loose in a park, but otherwise nothing looks wrong.
