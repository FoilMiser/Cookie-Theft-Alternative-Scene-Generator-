# Picture 54

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills behind, a pond on the right, a tall black lamppost in the middle and a sandy path winding up from the bottom left. An older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt stands near the edge of the pond holding a small bag and tossing bread or crumbs toward the water. The crumbs are flying in an arc and landing on the water, where a white duck is swimming toward them, leaving a little wake behind it. On the left, a man with brown hair, a red shirt and blue jeans stands on the path holding a camera up to his eye and taking a photo, pointing toward the woman and the pond. In the front, an older man with a blue cap, a brown vest, a white shirt and dark trousers is sitting on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket, leaning back on one hand, with a wicker picnic basket beside him. There is an empty bench by the path, rubbish bins (one by the lamppost and one cut off in the bottom right corner), a flower bed and bushes on the right, and a grey rock in the front. Nothing looks particularly wrong; it is a peaceful park scene.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The two white lines between the duck and the seated man's head are most likely the duck's wake on the water, but because they point down toward his head they could also be read as motion lines aimed at him.
- The seated man's cap overlaps the bottom edge of the pond, so at a glance his head looks like it is touching the water's edge; it is probably just him sitting in front of the pond.
- It is not certain what the photographer is photographing: the woman feeding the duck, the duck itself, or the view in general.
