# Picture 50

## Free description
This is a sunny playground with a railing fence and a line of bushes behind it, a big yellow sun in the top left and two small clouds. On the left there is a tall red slide with a ladder, and in the middle there is a swing set with a single empty red swing. In the front left, a child with short dark curly hair, a yellow shirt and orange shorts is kneeling in a sandbox, building a sand castle that has a little red flag on top. On the right, an old man with grey hair, a blue cap, a brown vest and dark trousers is sitting on the low end of a red seesaw, reading an open book. A small girl in a pink dress is on the other end of the seesaw, stuck high up in the air because his end is down on the ground. Her feet have little movement lines under them, as if she is kicking or swinging her legs. The man is not looking at her at all; he is busy with his book, so she seems to be stuck up there. It looks odd for a grown man to be sitting on a children's seesaw reading. Behind them there is an empty wooden bench, a green rubbish bin by the fence, and a tree on the far right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The child in the sandbox could be a boy or a girl; I read it as a boy but it is not certain.
- The motion lines under the girl's feet could mean she is kicking her legs impatiently, or that the seesaw has just moved and she is bouncing.
- It is unclear whether the man is ignoring the girl on purpose or simply hasn't noticed she is stuck up there.
- The swing set has only one swing on a frame wide enough for more; this may just be how it is drawn.
- The man's cap brim points toward the girl, but his eyes seem to be on the book.
