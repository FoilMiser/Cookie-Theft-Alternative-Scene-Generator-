## Free description

A girl or young woman with a brown ponytail and a blue hoodie is pointing up at the sky with a worried, wide-eyed face. Next to her, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is calmly watering the flower bed with a watering can, even though rain seems to be on its way. Washing (a striped towel and a pair of jeans) is still hanging on the line between two posts and will probably get wet. On the right, a man in a red shirt and jeans sits on a bench in front of the house drinking from a mug, looking relaxed and unbothered. An empty wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the bottom. The scene is about people not reacting to the coming storm, with only the pointing girl seeming to notice it.
