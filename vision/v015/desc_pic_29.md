# Picture 29

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park, with the sun in the top left, two white clouds, and green rolling hills and trees in the background. A sandy path winds from the back of the park down to the bottom left corner, and a wooden park bench stands to the right of the path. A very tall black lamp post stands in the middle of the grass behind the bench. On the right is a pond with reeds or cattails at both ends, and a white duck is swimming on it. In the front, a child with dark curly hair, a yellow shirt and orange shorts is walking to the left, holding up a pink ice cream cone and looking toward the left. A tall wooden ladder is standing just to the left of the child, with its top end near the edge of the pond. It isn't leaning against anything, and nobody is using it, so it looks oddly out of place in a park. The child is walking toward the ladder and might bump into it. A green rubbish bin is partly visible in the bottom right corner.
