# Picture 61

## Free description
This is a sunny but very windy back garden: white wind lines streak across the sky and past the gate on the left. The garden has a tall wooden fence with a gate, a hedge and a tree behind it, and a cream house with a red roof, window and blue door on the right. On the left, a girl with short dark hair in a yellow dress and red shoes stands on the paved path with both arms up and her mouth open in surprise, because her red hat has just been blown off her head and is flying away together with a few leaves. The empty swing on the swing set is being blown sideways, with its chains and red seat swinging out to the right. On the washing line, a striped towel and a pair of blue jeans are flapping sideways in the wind. On the right, a woman with a dark ponytail in a purple top and teal trousers is digging a hole in the lawn with a spade, with a mound of dirt beside it. In the front centre, a brown dog with a red collar or bandana is sitting up on its back legs like a person, holding open a newspaper in its white front paws and apparently reading it. A red dog bowl full of food sits on the grass nearby, and a bed of colourful flowers is in front of the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The dog sitting up and reading a newspaper with hand-like white paws is impossible in real life; it may be an intended oddity.
- The red item on the dog could be a collar, a bandana, or a bow; it is partly behind its head.
- The leaves near the hat could be read as falling from the hat or just blowing past in the wind.
- The wind blows the hat, swing, and washing strongly to the right, but the woman's hair, the tree and the flowers do not seem to move — a small inconsistency that could be unintentional.
- The woman could be digging a hole to plant something, or just digging; there is no plant visible next to her.
- The swing seat hangs at an angle in mid-air — this reads as being blown by the wind rather than as a drawing error.
