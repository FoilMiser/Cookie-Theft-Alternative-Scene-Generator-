# pic_09

## Free description
This is the same sunny town street with a row of colourful shops, but now it is windy: there are white wind lines in the sky, across the street and over the tree, and leaves are blowing about. A woman in a yellow dress stands in the middle with both arms thrown up and her mouth open in surprise, because an orange hat is being blown away up into the air near an upstairs window of the peach building. On the left, a little girl in a pink dress holds an empty cone and rubs her eye as if crying, while a brown dog sniffs or licks her pink ice cream that has fallen on the pavement. A bearded man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers walks past carrying a yellow shopping bag, and a woman with a ponytail in a blue top and black trousers is running or hurrying towards the left. In the front, an orange cat is crouched low and creeping towards a red bird that is pecking at the ground, so it looks like the cat is about to pounce. A blue bird flies in the sky at the top right. Two things are impossible: there is an orange fish sitting in the leaves of the tree, and there is a teal bicycle in the front right whose wheels are square instead of round. Another normal bicycle is parked in a bike rack, and the bench, rubbish bins, potted plant and one café table with chairs are all empty.
