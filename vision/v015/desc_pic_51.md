# Picture 51

## Free description
This is a back garden on a sunny but windy day, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back and a wooden gate on the right. White wind lines blow across the sky and across the right side of the picture. On a clothesline in the middle, a pair of blue jeans and a yellow striped towel are blowing sideways in the wind. On the right, a young person in a blue hoodie is standing on the paved path with both arms up and mouth open in surprise, because the wind has just blown their red cap off their head; the cap is flying in the air with a couple of leaves. In the middle, a woman with a long dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is watering a flower bed, but she is pouring the water out of a black frying pan instead of a watering can, which is strange. On the left, an old man with a blue cap, brown vest and dark trousers sits on a wooden bench with his eyes closed, sipping from a mug. A birdhouse on a tall pole stands near the right side of the garden, and a tree pokes up behind the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The young person on the right could be a boy or a young man; it is not clear how old they are.
- The thing the woman holds is clearly a frying pan, but it could be read either as a joke/mistake (watering with a pan) or as her using whatever was to hand.
- The old man's eyes are closed and his cheeks are pink; he could be enjoying his drink, dozing, or just blinking.
- It is unclear whether the cap is about to be caught or will blow over the fence.
- The wind lines on the right pass across the fence and gate, so it is a bit hard to tell where the wind is coming from, though it seems to blow from right to left.
