## Free description
This is the sunny park playground with a grey railing fence and hedges behind, a slide on the left, a swing frame in the middle, a bench, and a big tree on the right. On the far left a man in an orange cap, orange high-visibility vest and blue trousers walks along carrying a cardboard box, like a delivery person or worker. In the middle back, a boy with brown hair in a blue hoodie and dark trousers is leaping very high into the air with both arms up and a big happy face, with his shadow on the grass well below him. On the see-saw at the right, a man with a beard in a green shirt and khaki trousers sits on the low end reading a book, while a little girl in a pink dress sits stuck up on the high end, apparently waiting for him to push up so she can come down. In the front, a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar crouches in a play bow with its tail wagging, next to a yellow-green tennis ball, and a small orange kitten is curled up asleep on the grass nearby. The impossible thing is a red bicycle in the front centre whose wheels are square instead of round. The slide and the single swing are empty, and there is a grey rock and a green rubbish bin as well.
