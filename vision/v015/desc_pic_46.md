# Picture 46

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the sun in the top left and two white clouds. A yellow diamond kite flies high in the sky with a bow tail, and its long string runs all the way down to a small girl in a pink dress in the front centre, who holds the string up and looks up at it. On the left, a smiling boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is on a swing on a grey A-frame swing set, with motion lines showing he is swinging. In the middle, laundry (a yellow-and-red striped towel or top and a pair of blue jeans) hangs pegged on a clothesline between two wooden posts. On the right, a grey-haired older woman in a teal cardigan and grey skirt is watering a bed of colourful flowers with a green watering can. Oddly, the water does not pour down from the can: a curved blue stream rises upward from near the spout, with drops at the top, as if it is flowing up into the air. A grey wheelbarrow sits empty on the lawn in the front, a green hose reel stands by the cream house with the blue door on the right, and a birdhouse on a pole stands near the wooden gate and path on the left.
