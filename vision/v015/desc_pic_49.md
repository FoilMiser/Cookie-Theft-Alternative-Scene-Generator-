# Picture 49

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the sun in the top right and two white clouds. On the left is a cream house with a red roof and a blue door, with a tree behind it, a bush, a round bed of colourful flowers and a green hose reel. In the middle, a young man or teenage boy with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans is hanging washing on a clothesline between two wooden posts; he is reaching up to peg a white T-shirt, next to a pair of blue jeans and a yellow-and-red item already on the line. In front of him, a grey-haired older woman with glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt is raking a small pile of green and brown leaves on the lawn. On the top of the fence on the right, a black cat is walking along. Two potted plants stand on the grass near the fence, a wooden gate opens onto a paved path running down the right side, and a red bowl of pet food sits on the grass at the front. It is a calm scene of garden chores; the cat on the fence might be heading for the food bowl.
