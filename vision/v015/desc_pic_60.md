## Free description
This is a sunny playground with a dark railing fence and hedge across the back, a slide on the left, a seesaw on the right, a wooden bench, a green bin, a big tree and a bush; the sun is in the top left with two clouds. In the bottom left, a small child with dark curly hair in a yellow top and orange shorts is kneeling in a sandpit, building a sandcastle with a little red flag on top. In the middle, a boy with brown hair in a blue hoodie, black trousers and white trainers is reading a red book in a sitting position — but there is no bench, chair or anything under him, so he looks like he is sitting on thin air. On the right, a bearded man in a green shirt sits on the low end of the seesaw reading a book, while a little girl in a pink dress sits up on the high end holding the handle with her legs dangling. In the background, an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, stands right at the fence eating a pink ice-cream cone; she looks very large compared to the fence, and her head and shoulders rise high above the hedge into the sky. The slide is empty, and the bench is empty.
