# Picture 53

## Free description
This is a calm, sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate on the right and a paved path leading to it. Blue jeans and a yellow striped towel hang still on a clothesline between two posts. An older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt is standing in the middle right, holding the nozzle of a garden hose up close to her face and peering into it, because no water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel next to the house, across the grass, and a brown-and-white dog with a red collar is standing right on top of it. Just behind the dog's paws, the hose has a big swollen bulge where the water is trapped. It looks like as soon as the dog steps off, the water will shoot out and spray the woman in the face. A teal watering can sits on the grass near the path, and there is a flower bed and a small bush next to the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The bulge in the hose is just to the left of the dog's back paw rather than directly under it; I read it as water pressure building up behind the spot the dog is blocking.
- It is not clear whether the dog knows it is standing on the hose or is just standing there by chance.
- The woman could be checking why the hose isn't working, or could be about to drink from it; the first seems more likely.
- The watering can is close by; it could be read as an alternative she could use, or just garden equipment left on the grass.
