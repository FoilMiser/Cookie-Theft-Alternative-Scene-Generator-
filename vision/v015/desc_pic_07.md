# Picture 07

## Free description

This is a simple, quiet playground on a sunny day, with the sun in the top right, two white clouds, a metal fence across the back and green trees behind it. Two children are playing on a red seesaw in the centre-left. A little girl in a pink dress sits on the left end, which is slightly raised so her feet dangle in the air. A boy with curly dark hair in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on the right end, which is lower, with his feet touching the ground. They are facing each other and holding the handles. On the right there is a red-framed slide with a ladder, but nobody is on it, and at the front right there is a sandpit with a red bucket and a blue spade in it, plus a grey rock beside it. There is a tree on the left, a bush on the right, and a green litter bin by the fence. Nothing looks wrong or impossible; it is a calm, ordinary scene.
