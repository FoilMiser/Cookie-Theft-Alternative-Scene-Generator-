# Picture 31

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny back garden, with a house on the left, a wooden fence across the back, a gate on the right and a path leading from the gate down to the bottom right. An older woman with grey hair is carrying a cardboard box to the right. Next to her, an older bearded man in a cap is painting a section of the fence blue with a long brush, with a paint tin at his feet. A man in a green shirt is walking to the left while reading a large open book or paper, and he is about to walk into a wooden crate on the grass in front of him. At the far right, a worker in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest is sweeping the path with a broom near the gate. In the front left, a boy in a red shirt is sitting on a bench licking a pink ice cream with his eyes closed, while a brown dog in a red bandana stretches up toward his ice cream as if to grab it. In the bottom right, a boy in a blue hoodie is reading a red book in a sitting position, but there is nothing under him, so he is sitting on thin air. A sprinkler at the bottom is spraying water onto a cardboard box instead of the flowers. The wheelbarrow at the bottom right seems to have a square wheel. A small orange cat is curled up asleep on the grass in the middle. An empty swing set stands on the right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Boy reading in the bottom right: he could be sitting on an invisible seat, or doing a crouch/"wall sit" against nothing. Either way, nothing is holding him up, which is impossible.
- Wheelbarrow wheel: it looks like a square frame with spokes rather than a round wheel. It could be a badly drawn round wheel, but it reads as square.
- The sprinkler could be watering the lawn and just happen to hit the box, or the box could be getting soaked by mistake. The water drops on the box suggest it is getting wet.
- What the reading man is holding could be a book, a newspaper or a map.
- It isn't clear whether the dog is just begging or is about to snatch the ice cream. It is stretched right up toward it.
- The hose from the reel near the bench and the hose feeding the sprinkler may be the same hose running out of the picture, or two separate hoses.
- The older woman is walking toward the painter and his paint tin, but it isn't clear whether she is bringing him the box or just passing by.
