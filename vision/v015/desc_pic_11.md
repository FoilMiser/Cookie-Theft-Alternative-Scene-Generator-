## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, hedges and a tree behind the fence, and a paved path running along the right side. In the middle a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands on the lawn with her hands on her hips, and oddly she is wearing an upside-down blue bucket on her head like a hat. Behind her a washing line between two posts has a pair of blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel pegged on it. On the right, a smiling boy with curly dark hair, a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts sits on a swing in a grey swing frame, with motion lines showing he is swinging. Further back on the right, next to the gate, a small girl in a pink dress stands on the path flying an orange kite, and the kite string runs from her hand high up into the sky to the kite, which has a little bow tail. In the front there is a grey wheelbarrow, a teal watering can and a teal hose reel, and there is a bed of colourful flowers beside the house. The strangest thing is the bucket being worn as a hat.
