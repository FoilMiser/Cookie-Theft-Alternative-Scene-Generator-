## Free description

In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes reaches up to a washing line, pegging out clothes — blue jeans, a yellow and red striped towel and a white T-shirt. Further right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds her hands out ready to catch a red ball that is flying through the air; a dotted line shows the ball was thrown by a small child in a yellow top and orange shorts running on the path near the gate. In the front right, a boy in a red shirt and blue jeans sweeps the path with a broom, looking off to the side. There is a flowerbed near the house and a red bowl of pet food on the grass at the bottom. Nothing looks clearly impossible.
