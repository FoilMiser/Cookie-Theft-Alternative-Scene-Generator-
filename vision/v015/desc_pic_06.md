# Picture 06

## Free description

This is a paved town square in front of a row of colourful shops on a bright sunny day, with the sun in the top right, two small clouds, and a blue bird flying in the top left. On the left there is a tree in a round planter and two empty café tables with black chairs. In the middle, a little girl in a pink dress holds a red and a yellow balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other towards a purple balloon that has slipped away and is floating off, its string dangling. Next to her, an older man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat holds a white paper bag and is scattering seeds or crumbs onto the ground, and a small red bird is on the pavement at his feet eating them. A tall black street lamp stands behind him. On the right, at a fruit and vegetable stall, a bearded man in a green shirt is waving with one hand raised, as if saying hello to someone. There is a cardboard box by the stall and a potted plant in the bottom right corner. Nothing looks impossible; the main small mishap is the girl's lost balloon.
