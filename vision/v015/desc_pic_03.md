# Picture 03

## Free description

This is a quiet park on a sunny day, with the sun at the top left, two clouds, rolling green hills and trees in the distance. A sandy path winds from the bottom of the picture back towards the hills on the left, past a big tree and a bush. In the middle stands a tall black street lamp with a green litter bin beside it. A bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands on the grass near the lamp holding a long fishing rod; his line goes out into a blue pond on the right, where a red-and-white float is bobbing with ripples around it. On the pond a white duck is swimming towards the right, towards some crumbs floating on the water. At the right-hand edge of the pond, a little girl in a pink dress is feeding the duck, throwing crumbs from her hand, and some crumbs are also scattered on the grass near her. In the front centre, an older man in a grey cap, brown waistcoat and white shirt sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket with a wicker picnic basket beside him. There is a grey rock in the front and another green litter bin in the bottom right corner. Nothing obviously strange or impossible is happening; it looks like a calm day in the park.
