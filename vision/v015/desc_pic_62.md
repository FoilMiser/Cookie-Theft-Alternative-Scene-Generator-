# Picture 62

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a park with rolling green hills, trees, bushes and two white clouds; the sun is in the top left. A winding sand-coloured path runs from the bottom left into the park, with an empty wooden bench beside it. Near the top of the path, a woman with a dark ponytail in a purple top and teal trousers is pushing a teal pram with a blue hood, with a baby inside; she is heading right, straight toward a tall black lamp post that is just in front of the pram. Below her, a little girl in a pink dress with her hair in a bun stands holding a yellow balloon on a string. In the front right, a man with brown hair in a red T-shirt and blue jeans sits on a red-and-white striped picnic blanket next to a brown picnic basket, leaning back with his legs stretched out, smiling. Behind him is a blue pond with cattails at both ends but no ducks. A grey rock lies on the grass at the bottom, and a green bin is partly cut off in the bottom right corner. The scene looks calm and ordinary; the only thing that might be about to happen is the pram bumping into the lamp post.
