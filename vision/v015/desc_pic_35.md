# Picture 35

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden, with the sun in the top left, two white clouds, a wooden fence across the back, a closed gate and a path on the left, and a house with a blue door on the right. A man in a red shirt and blue jeans is standing in the middle of the lawn, watering a bed of colourful flowers with a garden hose. The hose runs from his hand across the grass to a hose reel beside the flower bed. Behind him, a washing line between two posts has a striped towel and a pair of jeans pegged on it. On the right, a boy with dark curly hair, a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts is sitting on a wooden bench in front of the house, reading a red book. In the upper left, a cardboard box is hanging in the sky, tilted, with nothing holding it up, which is impossible. Apart from the floating box, it is a calm, ordinary garden scene.
