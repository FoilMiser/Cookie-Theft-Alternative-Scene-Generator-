# Picture 19

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a long wooden fence across the back, and a gate at the far right with a paved path running down from it. In the middle-right is a grey metal swing set, and a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts sits on the red swing seat, smiling with his eyes shut, swinging (there are little motion lines beside him). On the path at the right, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is sweeping dirt into a red dustpan, but instead of a broom he is using a giant silver fork. A small cloud of dust is rising from the dirt pile. A blue bird sits on top of the fence by the tree. There is an empty wooden bench at the bottom left, a flower bed in front of the house, and an empty grey wheelbarrow on the lawn in front.

- Man, far right, front (on the path): short brown hair, red T-shirt, blue jeans with a brown belt, dark shoes. He is bent slightly forward, looking down, and sweeping with a giant fork held in both hands like a broom.
- Blue bird, left-centre, back: a small blue bird with a white belly and yellow beak, perched on top of the fence beside the tree trunk, facing right.
- Giant fork: a huge silver dinner fork, as long as a broom, held by the man with the tines on the path, pushing dirt.
- Red dustpan, right-front, on the path: red with a long handle, lying on the ground with a small grey pile of dirt or gravel at its edge and a puff of dust above.
- Swing set, right-centre: a grey A-frame metal swing set with one red swing seat on two ropes.
- Bench, bottom left, front: a wooden garden bench, empty.
- Flower bed, left-centre: brown soil with red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers.
- Wheelbarrow, bottom centre, front: grey with wooden handles, empty.
- Bush, left, back: a green bush by the house.
- House, left: cream walls, red roof, a window and a blue door with a yellow knob.
- Fence and gate: a wooden fence across the back with a darker gate at the far right.
- Path, right: pale paving slabs leading from the gate to the front.
- Tree, left-back: behind the fence, next to the house.
- Sky: a yellow sun at top right and two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The man is sweeping with an enormous fork instead of a broom. This is clearly wrong, or a silly substitute. It could possibly be a garden fork or rake, but it is shaped and coloured exactly like a silver eating fork.
- The grey pile by the dustpan could be dirt, gravel or dust.
- The boy's eyes are drawn as closed curves. He is either enjoying himself with eyes shut or squinting while smiling.
- The ropes of the swing are thin and partly hard to follow. They seem to connect the top bar to the seat normally.
