# Picture 55

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny park with hills behind, a pond on the right, a lamppost in the middle and a sandy path winding up from the bottom left. A blue ball is stuck up in the branches of the big tree on the left, and a small girl in a pink dress stands under it with both arms raised toward it, while a brown-and-white puppy lies asleep in the bottom left corner. An older woman with grey hair, glasses and a teal top walks along beside the path carrying a yellow shopping bag, and on the path behind her a young person in a blue hoodie is jumping in the air with both arms up, looking happy. Further back, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is running. In the middle, an older man in a blue cap and brown vest sits on a bench happily eating a pink ice-cream cone, while a white dog with a brown patch and a blue scarf sits up on its hind legs at his feet, begging for the ice cream. In the front, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands on a red-and-white picnic blanket holding the strings of a blue and a yellow balloon, frowning and reaching toward a red balloon that has got away and is floating off. On the right, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is watering a flower bed with a teal watering can. Next to the flower bed, a wooden ladder is standing up at an angle leaning against nothing at all, which is impossible; it should fall over.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The ladder is the clearest oddity: it stands at an angle with nothing to lean on. Its top reaches up near the edge of the pond, so it might be read as "leaning on the pond", but that isn't possible.
- The white animal by the bench has fairly pointed ears and could be read as a cat or a dog; I read it as a dog because of the scarf and the begging pose.
- The young person in the hoodie could be jumping for joy, dancing, or reacting to something (e.g. the ball in the tree); they could be a boy or a young man.
- The girl under the tree could be reaching for the ball, pointing at it, or simply cheering; she seems to want the ball down.
- The red balloon's string end is near the boy's raised hand, so it could be read as either just escaping or just about to be caught.
- The older man's eyes are closed while he eats, so he may not have noticed the begging dog.
- The woman in yellow is drawn slightly above her shadow, which fits running (mid-stride) rather than floating.
