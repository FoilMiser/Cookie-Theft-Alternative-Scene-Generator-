# Picture 59

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a gate on the left, a hedge and tree behind the fence, and a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the right. On the left, a girl with a brown ponytail in a blue top, black trousers and white shoes stands on a paved path flying a yellow kite with a little bow tail high in the sky. In the middle there is a dark grey swing set with one empty red swing seat. Behind it, a washing line between two wooden posts holds a red-and-yellow striped towel and a pair of blue jeans pegged up to dry. Under the washing line, an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, is raking a small pile of leaves. On the right, a man in an orange cap and an orange high-visibility vest with blue jeans is watering a flower bed — but he is pouring the water out of what looks like a dark frying pan. Strangely, a teal watering can sits unused on the grass at the bottom, and a coiled garden hose on a reel sits right next to the flower bed. A grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles is at the bottom centre.
