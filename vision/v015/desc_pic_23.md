# Picture 23

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, a gate on the right with a paved path, and a tree behind the house. On the left, a boy in a blue hoodie sits on a wooden bench reading a red book, with a big sandwich beside him (or on his lap), and a blue bird is right next to the sandwich pecking at it — the boy hasn't noticed the bird stealing his lunch. A green garden hose runs from a reel by the house across the lawn to a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes, who holds the nozzle up close to her face and peers into it, with just one drop coming out. A brown dog with a red collar is standing on the hose, and the hose is swollen into a big bulge just behind where the dog is standing — the water is blocked, so when the dog steps off, the woman is likely to get sprayed in the face. There is an empty wheelbarrow at the front, a birdhouse on a tall pole on the right, a flowerbed and a bush. Something looks impossible: a cardboard box is floating in the sky at the top right, tilted, with nothing holding it up.

- The sandwich could be resting on the boy's lap or on the bench beside him — it overlaps his leg area. There is a small yellow shape under the bird that could be the bird's feet or part of the sandwich/cheese.
- The boy's eyes look closed or downcast and his mouth is a small "o" — he could be reading intently, reading aloud, or yawning/dozing.
- The woman is seen partly from behind; she could be checking why no water comes out or about to drink from it — reading as "checking the blocked hose" is most natural.
- The bulge in the hose is on the reel side of the dog's paw, which fits "water building up behind a blockage".
