# Picture 43

## Free description
This is a back garden on a grey, overcast day: the sky is pale grey and three dark storm clouds hang across the top of the picture, so it looks like rain is coming, although no rain is falling yet. On the left, a small girl in a pink dress with her dark hair in a bun stands on a paved path that runs from a wooden gate in the fence down to the front of the picture. She is looking up and pointing at the dark clouds with one hand, with her mouth open as if she is saying something like "look". On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue front door. A brown-and-white dog sits on the doorstep in front of the blue door holding a red lead in its mouth, with the rest of the lead lying in a loop on the grass, as if it wants to go for a walk. A round bed of brightly coloured flowers is next to the house, and a red bowl of pet food sits on the grass near the girl. The overall story seems to be that the dog wants a walk but a storm is about to arrive. A small yellow circle appears at the dog's ear, which looks like the door's knob drawn on top of the dog's head.
