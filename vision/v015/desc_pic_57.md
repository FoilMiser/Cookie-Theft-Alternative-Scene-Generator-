# Picture 57

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a park with green hills, trees and a light sky with two white clouds; the sun is in the top left corner. A winding sand-coloured path runs from the bottom left up into the park, and an empty wooden bench stands beside it. In the middle, a man with brown hair in a red shirt and blue jeans stands at the edge of a pond, holding a small white bag in one hand and tossing crumbs toward the water with the other; the crumbs are flying through the air in an arc and some are already floating on the water. A white duck with an orange beak is swimming toward the crumbs, leaving wake lines behind it. In the front, a little girl in a pink dress with her dark hair in a bun is walking or skipping along, holding the string of a yellow balloon. To the right, a woman with a dark ponytail in a purple top and teal trousers is walking and eating (or about to lick) a pink ice-cream cone. Behind her, in the lower right, is a round bed of brightly coloured flowers. There is a black lamp post with a green bin next to it in the middle, and another green bin partly cut off at the bottom right corner. Nothing looks clearly wrong; it reads as an ordinary calm day at the park.
