# Picture 18

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof on the left and a long wooden fence across the back, with a gate at the far right. A pale paved path leads from the gate down to the bottom-right corner. A woman with short dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes is watering a flower bed with a green watering can, and the water pours down onto the flowers. In front of the blue back door, a brown-and-white dog sits holding a red lead in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk; the lead trails in a loop on the ground. In the tree behind the house, among the leaves, there is an orange fish, which is impossible since fish don't live in trees. There are two potted plants and a bush near the fence on the right, and an empty grey wheelbarrow on the lawn at the front.

## Inventory

- Woman, left-centre, middle: short dark hair, yellow short-sleeved dress, red shoes. She is standing, facing left, watering the flower bed with a watering can.
- Dog, left, middle (on the doorstep): brown with a white chest, muzzle and paws, floppy ears, eyes closed or squinting happily. It is sitting in front of the blue door holding a red lead in its mouth.
- Fish, left, high up in the tree: an orange fish with a darker stripe and an eye, facing right, sitting in the green leaves of the tree.
- Watering can: teal or green, held by the woman, pouring.
- Water: a light-blue stream (drawn in chunky segments) falling from the spout down onto the flowers.
- Red lead: in the dog's mouth, trailing down to the ground in a loop by its feet.
- Flower bed, left-centre: brown soil with red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers.
- House, left: cream walls, red roof, a four-pane window, a blue door with a yellow knob, a grey step.
- Tree, left-back: a green tree behind the house and fence. The fish is in it.
- Fence and gate: a wooden fence across the back, with a darker wooden gate at the far right.
- Path, right: pale paving slabs from the gate to the front.
- Potted plants, right, back: two terracotta pots with green plants, by the fence.
- Bush, centre-right, back: a round green bush.
- Wheelbarrow, bottom centre-right, front: grey with wooden handles, empty, standing on the grass.
- Sky: a yellow sun at top right and two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The fish in the tree is clearly out of place and impossible. It could be meant as a fish-shaped decoration, but it is drawn as an ordinary orange fish.
- The water stream is drawn as linked blobs rather than a smooth stream, so it looks a bit like a chain or bubbles. It does seem to fall down onto the flowers normally.
- The yellow dot next to the dog's head is the door knob, but because it is right beside the dog it could be mistaken for a tag or a ball.
- The dog could be a beagle or a boxer type. It isn't clear whether it is wearing a collar or whether the red near its neck is just the lead.
