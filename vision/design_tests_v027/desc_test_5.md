# Picture 5

## Free description

This is a cartoon of a sunny back garden, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, and a tall wooden fence with a gate running across the back. On top of the fence, an orange cat is creeping along towards a red bird that is perched a short distance ahead of it, and the bird is facing the cat, so the cat looks as if it may be about to pounce or the bird may be about to fly off. In the middle of the lawn, a sprinkler connected to a long hose is shooting several arcs of water to the right, and the longest arc is landing on a cardboard box that sits on the grass. The box is already wet, with water running down its sides and a puddle around its base, so whatever is inside looks likely to get ruined. On the right, an older person with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey-blue shorts, is sipping a drink through a straw from a red cup, facing away from the sprinkler and box. This person is in a sitting position with knees bent, but there is no chair or bench under them, which looks impossible, as if they are sitting on thin air. At the back right, near the gate, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is raking a small pile of leaves at the edge of the path. No one appears to have noticed the box getting soaked or the cat stalking the bird.
