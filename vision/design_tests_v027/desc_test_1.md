## Free description

This is a sunny day on a paved town street or pedestrian square, with a row of four colourful two-storey shops behind (pink, pale green, peach and cream, with striped awnings). In the left foreground a man in a red shirt and blue trousers walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. Behind him at the left a street sweeper in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest sweeps a small pile of autumn leaves with a yellow broom, next to a market stall of green, orange and red fruit. In the middle an old man with grey hair, a grey flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat stands licking a pink ice cream, and behind him a person in a yellow dress and red shoes walks to the right while reading an open book, not looking where they are going. In the front centre a small girl in a pink dress stands crying with one hand up at her eyes, holding an empty ice cream cone in the other hand. Her pink ice cream has fallen on the ground and made a pink splat beside her, and a brown dog with a red collar is bending its head down right next to it, apparently about to eat it. A red ball sits or hangs in the middle of the pavement a little above the girl's head with nobody near it, which could mean it is bouncing or rolling past. On the right there is a large tree in a round planter and a green litter bin, with nobody around them, and potted plants stand in both bottom corners. The story seems to be that the little girl has just dropped her ice cream and the dog is going to get it.
