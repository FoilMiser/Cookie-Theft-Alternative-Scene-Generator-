# Picture 4

## Free description

This is a sunny day in a grassy playground or park, with an iron railing fence and bushes along the back, a tree at the left and a slide at the right. On the left there is a red seesaw. A little girl in a pink dress sits on the high end, up in the air, and she looks surprised or worried, with little motion lines under her feet. An old man in a grey cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat sits on the low end, which rests on the ground, and he is reading an open book and does not seem to notice her. So the heavy old man is holding the little girl stuck up in the air, and she may be scared or unable to get down. At the back, in front of the fence, a person in a red cap, blue jacket and black trousers stands holding a camera up to their face and taking a picture towards the left, seemingly at the seesaw. In the front right an old woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, walks along licking a pink ice cream. At the right there is a sandbox with a red bucket and a blue spade in it but no child playing there, and the tall slide behind it is also empty. A green litter bin stands in the grass, and a grey rock lies near the sandbox. Nothing looks physically impossible, but the seesaw being used by an adult who is reading instead of playing is an odd sight.
