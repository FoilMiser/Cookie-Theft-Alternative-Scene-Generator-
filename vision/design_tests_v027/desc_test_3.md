# Picture 3

## Free description

This is a sunny day in a park, with a pond on the left, a winding sandy footpath on the right, and green hills and treetops in the distance. An old man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat stands at the near edge of the pond, holding a long fishing rod that reaches out over the water; the line drops to a red-and-white float sitting in a small ripple. Further back, near the top of the path, a young man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is bent over digging with a long-handled spade, with a scoop of soil on the blade, a hole at his feet and a heap of dirt beside it. In the front on the right, an old woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt holds a brown bag or box against her body and scatters seed from her other hand. A line of small seeds runs down from her hand to the path, where a little blue bird is standing among more seeds, pecking at them. At the far right, a young woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is walking to the right, carrying a cardboard box in front of her. An empty wooden bench stands beside the path, and there is a flower bed with bright mixed flowers and a green litter bin in the bottom left corner. Nobody is interacting with anyone else; each person is busy with their own task. Nothing looks clearly impossible, and the only thing about to happen is the bird continuing to eat and the fisherman possibly getting a bite.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The line of dots from the old woman's hand could be seed falling through the air in a curve, or a dotted trail lying on the path. It reads to me as seed falling and leading to the bird.
- The brown item the old woman holds is partly hidden behind her body. It could be a paper bag or a small cardboard box.
- The young digger could be a teenage boy or a grown young man; the figure's age is hard to judge. Likewise it is unclear what he is digging for, as there is no plant or tree beside the hole.
- The old woman's skirt has a lumpy, rounded shape that looks a bit like a sack, and her legs look thin and straight below it. Her head and glasses are drawn at a slightly awkward angle.
- The old man has several small dots on his face, which could be wrinkles, freckles or age spots.
- The grey shadow under the digger spills sideways across the edge of the path, and looks large compared with the other figures' shadows. The lamp post, bench and bin have no ground shadow, while the people do.
- The top of the path ends quite abruptly at the band of green at the horizon, with a flat cut-off end.
- I am not sure whether the old man is standing on the bank or very slightly in front of the water; his body overlaps the pond's outline but his feet are on the grass, so I read him as on the bank.
