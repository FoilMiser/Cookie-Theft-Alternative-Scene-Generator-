# Picture 6

## Free description

This is a sunny town street or paved square in front of a row of four colourful shops with striped awnings. At the far left, in the front, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is running to the right, with short speed lines behind him. Just to his right is a tree growing out of a round planter, and an orange fish is sitting in the middle of its green leaves, which looks impossible because fish do not belong in trees. In the centre, a boy or young man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is sitting on a wooden bench reading an open red book. Behind him and to the right, an old man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is walking to the left past a lamp post, carrying a yellow shopping bag. On the right there is a market stall with a teal-and-white striped top and crates of red, orange, yellow and green fruit or vegetables, but nobody is tending it, and a cardboard box sits on the ground beside it. At the front, a red bicycle is parked in a metal rack, with a dark bin to its right and a potted plant in the bottom right corner. The people are not interacting with each other. The running boy looks as if he is hurrying somewhere, and nothing else seems about to happen.
