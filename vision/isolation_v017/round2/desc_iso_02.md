# iso_02

## What I see
A gray wheelbarrow stands on green grass with a darker green oval shadow under it. It has a brown wooden handle (or two handles overlapping) pointing to the left, a dark leg under the tray, and a blue-gray bar running forward to a hub at the front right. At the top of the picture is the edge of a blue pond or pool with a lighter blue shine on it, and two green upright stalks rise from the water's edge at the top right. In the lower right corner is a smooth gray rock or stone with a white highlight and a small crack line.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The wheelbarrow's wheel looks strange. Instead of a round wheel, a dark square frame surrounds the hub, with spokes running from the hub to the square's corners and edges. One reading is a square wheel, which would look wrong for a wheelbarrow. The other is a round wheel drawn inside or behind a square frame or crate. Either way, the outer rim reads as a square, not a circle.
- The two green stalks at the top right are cut off by the frame. They could be reeds or plant stems growing at the pond edge. They could also be the green legs of a person or animal standing in or beside the water.
- The handle could be one handle or two handles lying exactly on top of each other. The second brown line is only faintly offset.
- The leg is a dark rounded stub that looks a bit detached from the tray, as if it hangs from the bottom edge instead of being joined to a frame.
- The rock is plain. It could also be read as a gray dome or helmet-like object, but a stone seems most likely.
