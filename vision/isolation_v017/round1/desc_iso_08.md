# iso_08

## What I see
A white goat-like animal with a dark curved horn and an upturned tail stands on green grass with its head lowered to a small tuft of darker grass, as if grazing or sniffing at something on the ground. It casts a dark oval shadow beneath it. Part of a wooden park bench is visible at the upper left, a strip of sandy path at the lower left corner, and the edge of a blue pond at the upper right corner.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- There is a small pale, cone-shaped object right at the animal's mouth, pointing down into the grass tuft. It could be the goat's muzzle/snout, or it could be read as a separate object (it looks a bit like an ice cream cone) lying in the grass that the goat is nibbling.
- The head looks odd: it is a very round white shape with a dark eye area, almost like a helmet or skull mask, and it seems only loosely joined to the neck.
- The body pose is strange: the body is a thin, arched, inverted-U shape and only about three legs are clearly visible. One front leg comes down from near the neck/chest and bends at an unusual angle, and the rear leg drops from under the rump, so the legs look stick-like and oddly attached.
- The cream-coloured shape at the top of the neck could be an ear or a second (lighter) horn.
- Because of the single horn and white coat it reads as a goat, but it could also be taken for a sheep, a deer/antelope, or even a white dog.
- The bench is cropped at the left edge and shown from the side; only one leg is visible, but it does not look like it is floating.
