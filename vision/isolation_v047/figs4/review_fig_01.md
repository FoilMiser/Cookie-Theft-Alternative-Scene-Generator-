# Review fig_01

## 1. Who and what
A brown dog with a darker brown ear and large round glasses, sitting up on its haunches and holding an open newspaper in its front paws, reading it. Both versions read the same way; b also wears a red collar.

## 2. Faults in a
- Neck: the body rises in a long, narrow column behind the head, so the neck looks stretched and the head seems perched on a post.
- Near front leg (arm): very long and rubbery. It runs from the shoulder down past the belly before bending up to the paw at the bottom corner of the paper.
- Far front paw: shows only as a small brown blob at the chest beside the paper, with no arm attached, so it looks stuck on.
- Shoulder: a short curved stroke on the upper back reads as a stray crease, not a shoulder joint.
- Hind leg and feet: the haunch is one large rounded mass. The two feet are small brown lumps under it and do not clearly join it as legs.
- Body: the whole figure leans back with no visible seat. The haunch and tail look like they are floating rather than resting on the ground (the seat may be missing on purpose in this isolated view).
- Tail: long, thin and stiff, with a uniform rope-like width.

## 3. Faults in b
- Neck: the same long, narrow neck column as in a.
- Collar: the red collar shows only on the back and side of the neck. It stops at the far paw and does not visibly go round the front, so it looks like a strap, not a ring.
- Near front leg (arm): the same overlong, rubbery arm reaching down to the bottom corner of the paper.
- Far front paw: the same detached brown blob at the chest.
- Shoulder: a wavy line on the upper chest and shoulder looks like an unfinished seam.
- Hind leg and feet: the same blobby haunch, with small feet that do not clearly join it.
- Tail: the same long, thin, stiff tail.

## 4. Differences
- b has a red collar round the neck; a has none.
- b has a wavy line on the front of the shoulder and chest where a has a plain contour and a short stroke on the back.
- The canvases differ slightly in size and framing (b is cropped a little tighter). The figure itself looks the same in scale and pose.
- Otherwise none seen: the head, glasses, ear, newspaper, arms, legs and tail match.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: The head is fine and the glasses sit well. The neck is too long and thin. The near arm is too long, and the far paw floats with no arm behind it. The hips are one big rounded haunch, and the feet are small and poorly joined. The tail is long and thin.
- b: Same as a: too long a neck, an overlong near arm, a detached far paw, a blobby haunch with small, loosely joined feet, and a thin tail. The collar does not fully wrap the neck.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reading as a seated dog with the paper held up works overall. But the body leans back stiffly like a plank, the long arm hangs down to reach the paper's lower corner instead of holding it up in front, and the figure seems to float rather than sit.
- b: The same pose with the same stiffness: a backward-leaning trunk, a low, overlong holding arm, and no clear sitting contact.

## 7. Better drawn
No difference. The two share the same body, pose and faults. The collar in b adds a detail but is drawn incompletely, and the wavy shoulder line looks unfinished, so neither version is clearly better drawn.
