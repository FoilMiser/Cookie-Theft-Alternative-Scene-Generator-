# Review fig 01

## 1. Who and what
A brown dog with a darker brown ear and round black glasses, standing upright like a person and holding an open newspaper in front of it with both front paws. Both versions read the same; b also wears a red collar.

## 2. Faults in a
- Lower body: it ends in a rounded lump with two small crease marks. No hind legs or feet can be seen, so it looks unfinished.
- Tail: long and thin, sticking far out to the left from the lower back. It looks like a separate tube attached to the side.
- Far front paw: only a small brown nub at the top left corner of the paper. There is no arm leading to it.
- Neck and torso: very long and narrow, like a tall tube under the head, and thinner than the lower body.
- Glasses: the ear covers the back of the frame, so it is hard to tell how the glasses sit on the head.

## 3. Faults in b
- Red collar: drawn as a flat square flap sticking out behind the neck. It reads more like a cape corner or a label than a collar.
- Lower body: a shapeless lump that bulges to the left. There are no clear hind legs or feet.
- Bottom left: a small white and dark triangular patch that reads as a stray mark. It could be a tail tip or a paw, but you can't tell which.
- Tail: none visible, apart from that patch.
- Far front paw: as in a, only a small nub on the paper, with no arm.
- Glasses: as in a, partly covered by the ear.

## 4. Differences
- b has a red collar or flap at the back of the neck. a has none.
- a has a long tail curving out to the left. In b there is no clear tail, only the small triangular patch at the bottom left.
- b's lower body bulges further back (to the left), almost like a sitting or crouching shape. a's lower body is narrower and hangs more straight down.
- a's back curves forward a little more. b's figure stands more upright.
- b's figure is slightly smaller in the frame and sits more to the left.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head and muzzle are fine, and the ear and glasses read clearly. The neck and torso are long and tube-like. The near arm is long but joins the shoulder plausibly. The far arm is missing, with only a paw nub. The hips and hind legs are not shaped: there is no thigh, knee or foot. The tail is long and thin and set low on the side.
- b: Same head and arms as a, with the same missing far arm. The collar flap sticks out away from the neck instead of wrapping around it. The lower body is a large lump with no legs or feet. No tail can be made out.

## 6. Pose
- a: Holding the paper up in front of the face reads clearly as reading. The body below the waist has no legs, though, so the figure seems to float or stand on a stump, and the long side tail makes it look stretched.
- b: The reading action is just as clear. The heavy, legless lower body and the stray patch make the bottom half look clumsy and unfinished.

## 7. Better drawn
a, but only slightly. Both have the same unfinished, legless lower body. b adds a collar flap that sticks out oddly and a stray patch where the tail should be, while a at least has a clear tail.
