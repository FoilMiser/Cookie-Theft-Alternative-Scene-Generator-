# Review fig 02

## 1. Who and what
A white dog with brown spots, one large brown ear and a blue collar, seen from the side facing right. In a it is walking (legs in stride). In b it is standing still with its legs nearly straight.

## 2. Faults in a
- Mouth: none drawn. The muzzle is a plain rounded shape with only a nose.
- Ear: very large, and it hangs down over the collar so that it covers much of it.
- Near front leg: long, straight and stiff, angled forward from the chest like a pole. There is no visible elbow or wrist.
- Hind feet: rounded bulb shapes, a little blob-like.
- Belly line: there is a small notch or kink where the near hind leg meets the belly.
- Far legs: shaded grey with almost no outline, so they look faint and flat.

## 3. Faults in b
- Mouth: none drawn, as in a.
- Ear: very large and covers the collar, as in a.
- Chest: the front of the chest runs straight down into the near front leg, with no chest bulge, so the leg looks stuck onto the front edge of the body.
- Front legs: long and thin, close together and almost vertical, a little stilt-like.
- Far legs: only thin grey slivers, barely visible.
- Hind feet: rounded bulbs, as in a.

## 4. Differences
- Legs: in a they are in a walking stride, with the front leg reaching forward and the far legs placed apart. In b they stand straight under the body.
- In a the far front leg can be seen clearly as a grey leg behind the near one. In b it is only a sliver.
- In b the body sits slightly higher and the back slopes down toward the tail a little more. In a the back is flatter.
- In b the neck is a little more upright and the head sits slightly higher.
- In a the near front leg starts further forward under the collar. In b it comes straight down from the front of the chest.
- In b the near hind leg bends in a gentler S-curve.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: The head, collar and upright tail join well. The body is a long oval. The near front leg is too straight and pole-like, and the joint at the shoulder is unclear. The hind legs have a bend at the hock that is reasonable. The feet are rounded blobs. The far legs are faint grey.
- b: The head and tail are as in a. The front legs are long and thin, and the near one joins the body flush with the chest line without any shoulder. The near hind leg has a natural dog-like bend. The feet are rounded blobs. The far legs are almost hidden.

## 6. Pose
- a: It reads as walking because the legs are spread in a stride. The straight, forward-reaching front leg is a little stiff, but the overall pose is natural.
- b: It reads as a dog standing still. The front legs are rather stiff and post-like, but the stance is believable.

## 7. Better drawn
No difference. Both are clean and readable with the same head, ear, spots and feet. a has a stiff front leg and b has stilt-like front legs joined flush to the chest, so their small faults balance out.
