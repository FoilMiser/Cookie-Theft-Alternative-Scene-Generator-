## What I see
An orange cat on green grass, crouched low and creeping forward toward the right with its head stretched out, the way a cat stalks prey. In front of it, a small red bird with its head down is pecking at a few small seeds or crumbs on the ground. The cat looks like it is sneaking up on the bird, and the bird does not seem to notice.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The bird's shape is odd. The body is rounded, but a long flat red piece sticks out to the right that could be a tail, and it is about as big as the body. The head seems to be at the lower left. You could also read the whole thing as a red object such as a whistle or a watering can rather than a bird.
- The bird seems to have only one short leg, or a stub, under it.
- The cat's legs are thin and angular, and the back legs bend a little stiffly. The face is small compared with the body, with a white muzzle patch showing at the front.
- The scattered seeds look like a few tiny grey circles. You can tell they are food mostly from their position.
