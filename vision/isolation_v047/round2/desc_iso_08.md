# iso_08

## What I see
An older grey-haired woman with glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, stands on a paved garden path next to a wooden fence. She holds a very large long-handled fork-shaped tool and is using it to push or sweep a small pile of grey dirt or dust on the path, with a little dust cloud rising. A red dustpan lies next to the pile. A potted plant is at top right and a teal watering can at bottom right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The tool looks exactly like a giant dinner fork (smooth silver handle, four tines). It could be read as a garden fork / rake being used to sweep, or as an oversized kitchen fork, which looks odd for sweeping dirt. A broom would be the expected tool with a dustpan.
- Her hands on the handle are hard to make out; the upper hand seems to sit against her chest and the handle passes in front of her body, so the grip looks awkward.
- The fork's tines are touching the edge of the dirt pile; it's unclear whether she is sweeping the dirt toward the dustpan or digging into it.
- The grey puffs above the pile read as dust, but could also be read as smoke.
