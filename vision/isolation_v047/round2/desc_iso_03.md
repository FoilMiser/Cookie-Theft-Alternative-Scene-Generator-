# iso_03

## What I see
An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and round glasses, wearing a teal cardigan, white top, grey skirt and brown shoes, stands on grass beside a paved path. She is holding a very large silver fork by its long handle and using it like a broom to sweep a small pile of grey dirt/dust (with a dust cloud) toward a red dustpan on the path. Nearby are a teal watering can on the grass, potted plants on the left, and a wooden fence at the top.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The sweeping tool is clearly a giant table fork rather than a broom or rake; this could be a deliberate absurdity, or it could be meant as a garden fork/rake, but its shape is that of an eating fork.
- Her hands on the handle are hard to read: the handle passes across her body and the grip looks loose; the upper hand appears near her chest and the handle seems to pass in front of/through her arm.
- Her feet stand on the grass while the fork reaches onto the path; fine.
- The watering can is cut off on the left and looks large relative to the woman.
