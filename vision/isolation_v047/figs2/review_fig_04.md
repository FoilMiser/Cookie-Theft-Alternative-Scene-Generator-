# Review: fig 04

## Who and what
A grey-blue cat in side view, moving to the right (walking or trotting), holding nothing. In a it reads as a cat striding forward with head up; in b the head is pushed down into the chest and the front leg folds back under the body, so it reads more as a cat crouching, sliding or about to pounce than as walking.

## Faults in a
- Front shoulder/elbow: a small round knob sticks out where the front leg meets the chest, like a separate ball joint.
- Front legs: both front legs are long and tube-like, bent like human arms with a forearm, and end in boot-shaped paws.
- Far front leg: its upper part is hidden and it seems to come out from under the chest/belly rather than from the shoulder.
- Hind paws: rounded, lumpy blobs with an extra overlapping shape, like boots rather than cat feet.
- Hind thigh: a separate bulge on the inside of the hind leg where the far hind leg joins, reads as a lump.
- Neck: the round head is attached straight onto the front of the body with almost no neck.
- Muzzle: very small white blob at the tip of the face with tiny whiskers; nose and mouth hardly read.
- Body: very long, even, sausage-like torso.
- Tail: thin uniform tube with no taper.

## Faults in b
- Face: squashed; the eye is not visible and the muzzle is a black-and-white smear; the face does not read clearly as a cat's face.
- Head and neck: the head is pressed down into the chest with no neck, overlapping the shoulder.
- Front leg: folded back under the chest with the elbow pointing forward and the paw tucked beneath the body; looks broken or unfinished.
- Chest: a white patch on the chest/shoulder looks like a hole or a gap in the drawing rather than markings.
- Second front leg: shows only as a rounded lump under the chest, not a recognisable leg.
- Hind paws and hind thigh: same lumpy boot-like paws and inner thigh bulge as in a.
- Body: same long sausage-like torso; thin untapered tail.

## Differences
- Head position: a holds the head up and level in front of the body; b has it lower and pushed into the chest.
- Eye: a shows a large dark eye; b shows no clear eye.
- Muzzle: a has a small white muzzle with whiskers; b has a larger black-and-white smudged muzzle.
- Front legs: a has both front legs reaching forward, one paw out in front; b has the front leg folded back under the chest.
- Chest: b has a white patch on the chest that a does not.
- Shoulder: a has a small round knob at the front shoulder/elbow; b has the bent front leg in that place.
- Overall silhouette: a is longer at the front (paws reach further right); b is more compact at the front.
- Hind legs, body and tail look the same in both.

## Anatomy
- a: Head round and fair in size but joined with almost no neck. Body long and tube-like. Front legs too long and human-arm-like, with a detached-looking round knob at the shoulder; far front leg seems to start under the belly. Hind legs reasonable in length but with a lumpy inner thigh and blobby boot-shaped paws. Tail thin, no taper.
- b: Head jammed into the chest, no neck, face squashed. Front leg folded backward and oddly joined under the chest; other front leg reduced to a lump; white patch looks like a gap. Hind legs, paws, body and tail as in a (lumpy paws, long torso, untapered tail).

## Pose
- a: Reads as a cat walking or trotting forward, head up; acceptable, though the front legs are stiff and arm-like.
- b: Does not read as a natural walk; with the head tucked and the front leg folded under, it looks like the cat is collapsing, sliding or crouching, and is awkward.

## Better drawn
a. The head, eye and face read clearly and the pose looks like a cat moving forward; b's squashed face, folded front leg and white gap make the figure hard to read.
