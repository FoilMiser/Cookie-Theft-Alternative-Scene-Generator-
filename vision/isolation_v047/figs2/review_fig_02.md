# Review: fig 02

## Who and what
An orange cat in side view, moving to the right, holding nothing. In a the head is pushed down into the chest and the front leg folds back under the body, so it reads as crouching, sliding or about to pounce; in b the head is up and both front legs reach forward, so it reads as a cat walking or trotting.

## Faults in a
- Face: squashed; no eye is visible and the muzzle is a black-and-white smear; the face does not read clearly as a cat's face.
- Head and neck: the head is pressed down into the chest with no neck, overlapping the shoulder.
- Front leg: folded back under the chest with the elbow pointing forward and the paw tucked beneath the body; looks broken or unfinished.
- Chest: a cream patch on the chest/shoulder looks like a hole or a gap in the drawing rather than markings.
- Second front leg: shows only as a darker rounded lump under the chest, not a recognisable leg.
- Hind paws: rounded, lumpy boot-like blobs with an extra overlapping shape.
- Hind thigh: separate bulge on the inside of the hind leg.
- Body: very long, even, sausage-like torso; tail a thin tube with no taper.

## Faults in b
- Front shoulder/elbow: a small round knob sticks out where the front leg meets the chest, like a separate ball joint.
- Front legs: long tube-like legs bent like human arms, ending in boot-shaped paws.
- Far front leg: drawn in a darker shade and seems to come out from under the chest/belly rather than from the shoulder.
- Hind paws: lumpy boot-like blobs, as in a.
- Hind thigh: same inner bulge as in a.
- Neck: the round head sits straight onto the front of the body with almost no neck.
- Muzzle: very small white blob at the tip of the face with tiny whiskers; nose and mouth hardly read.
- Body and tail: same long sausage torso and untapered tail.

## Differences
- Head position: a has the head lowered and pushed into the chest; b holds it up and level.
- Eye: b shows a large dark eye; a shows no clear eye.
- Muzzle: a has a larger black-and-white smudged muzzle; b has a small white muzzle with whiskers.
- Front legs: a has the front leg folded back under the chest; b has both front legs reaching forward, one paw out in front.
- Chest: a has a cream patch on the chest that b does not.
- Shoulder: b has a small round knob at the front shoulder/elbow; a has the bent front leg there.
- Overall silhouette: b is longer at the front; a is more compact at the front.
- Hind legs, body and tail look the same in both.

## Anatomy
- a: Head jammed into the chest, no neck, face squashed. Front leg folded backward and oddly joined under the chest; other front leg reduced to a lump; cream patch looks like a gap. Hind legs reasonable in length but with lumpy inner thigh and blobby paws. Long tube body; thin untapered tail.
- b: Head round and fair in size, but almost no neck. Body long and tube-like. Front legs too long and human-arm-like, with a detached-looking knob at the shoulder; far front leg seems to start under the belly. Hind legs, paws, body and tail as in a.

## Pose
- a: Does not read as a natural walk; with the head tucked and the front leg folded under, it looks like the cat is collapsing, sliding or crouching, and is awkward.
- b: Reads as a cat walking or trotting forward with head up; acceptable, though the front legs are stiff and arm-like.

## Better drawn
b. Its head, eye and face read clearly and the pose looks like a cat moving forward; a's squashed face, folded front leg and cream gap make the figure hard to read.
