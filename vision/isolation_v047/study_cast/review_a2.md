# Cast review a2 (v0.47): eight animal figures

## fig_01

**First read:** a tan dog with a red collar, seen from the side facing right, standing still.

- **Head:** a plain ball sitting on the collar, with no neck or skull shape. It should be a wedge-shaped skull with a stop, flowing into a visible neck.
- **Muzzle:** a small white bump at the front of the ball, with no mouth line and no jaw. It should be a clear box-shaped muzzle about a third of the head's length, with a mouth line and the nose at the tip.
- **Eye:** a dark smudge cut off at the front edge of the head, so it reads as half off the face. Put a full eye inside the head, above and behind the muzzle.
- **Ear:** a large dark oval laid over the middle of the head, like a sticker. Hang the ear from the top back of the skull so it overlaps the head edge and falls down beside the cheek.
- **Collar:** a flat red band at an odd angle that does not wrap around the neck. Draw it as a curved band that follows the neck's round shape.
- **White bib:** a cream sack hangs under the collar and pokes outside the chest outline, so it reads as a tag or a bag. Keep the white chest marking inside the chest outline.
- **Tail:** a very long, thick, straight post standing up, about as wide as a leg at the base. Make it shorter and tapering, with a slight curve.
- **Body:** a straight-sided box with a flat belly and no chest depth. Give it a deep chest behind the front legs and a belly line that tucks up toward the hind legs.
- **Front legs:** straight stilts with no elbow, wrist or pastern. Add an elbow at the chest and a slight bend at the wrist above the paw.
- **Hind leg:** the knee sits at the hip line and the lower leg slants back and then forward again, so the leg looks broken. Draw a thigh, a stifle bending forward, then a hock bending back, with the lower leg near vertical.
- **Stray lines:** a loose crease line at the hip and a notch where the hind leg meets the belly. Remove them, or replace them with one clean thigh contour.
- **Far legs:** shown only as darker paw blobs behind the near feet, with no legs above them. Either draw the far legs in full (darker and slightly offset) or hide them completely.
- **Feet:** flat bun shapes with no toes. Draw rounded paws with two or three toe notches.

## fig_02

**First read:** a tan dog with a red collar, seen from the front, standing and facing the viewer. It reads more like a teddy bear than a dog.

- **Legs:** only two, very long stilts, each about as long as the head and body together, with huge flared thighs. Draw four short legs (front legs in front, hind legs just visible behind and beside them), about the same length as the chest is deep.
- **Ankle knobs:** small knobs stick out at the sides of each ankle, likely the far paws showing through. Remove them, or draw the hind paws as proper feet behind the front ones.
- **Tail:** sticks straight up from behind the top of the head like an antenna. In a front view, either hide the tail or show only its tip curling to one side behind the body.
- **Ears:** flat dark strips pressed against the sides of the head. Let the ears hang down beside the cheeks, with some overlap onto the face.
- **Muzzle:** a flat white disc with a nose, with no mouth and no forward projection. Give it a mouth line, and show the muzzle coming toward the viewer (it should be narrower than the head, with some shading under it).
- **Head:** a circle with no brow or cheeks. Shape a brow above the eyes and widen the cheeks slightly.
- **Body:** built from stacked lobes (shoulder bulges and side bulges, each with its own outline), with inner lines that look like seams. Draw one chest outline, with the shoulders blending into the front legs.
- **Shading:** the right leg has a darker flat half-tone that the left leg does not have, so it looks like a different part. Shade both legs the same way, or not at all.
- **Collar:** a wide red patch that reads as a bib. Make it a narrow band around the neck under the chin.
- **White chest marking:** a hard oval pasted on. Soften it into a chest blaze that tapers down between the legs.

## fig_03

**First read:** a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar, seen in three-quarter view facing right, standing.

- **Brown patches:** each patch is a separately outlined blob that bulges beyond the body outline (on the back, the rump and the tail base), so they read as lumps or growths. Draw the patches as flat colour areas inside the body outline, with no outline of their own where they meet the white.
- **Blob under the belly:** a brown blob with white fang-like marks hangs between the legs and looks like a mouth with teeth, a tick or a wound. Remove it.
- **Ear:** a long brown oval stuck on outside the head outline, covering the collar. Attach it at the top back of the skull, overlapping the head, and let it fall to the cheek.
- **Head outline:** a stray partial line at the brow and front of the face, and a small brown tick under the chin. Close the head outline cleanly and remove the stray marks.
- **Muzzle:** almost absent; there is a nose on a rounded face with no snout. Add a short muzzle projecting forward, with a mouth line.
- **Far legs:** flat grey with no shading form, so they look like grey sticks or socks rather than legs. Use the body colour one step darker for the far legs.
- **Legs:** tubes with no elbow, wrist, stifle or hock, and the near hind leg bows outward at the back. Add the joints, as in fig_01.
- **Stray line:** a short vertical stroke at the hip. Remove it, or make it a proper thigh contour.
- **Tail:** a long straight post. Make it shorter and tapering, with a gentle curve.
- **Feet:** plain ovals. Add toe notches.

## fig_04

**First read:** a grey cat with a white chest, seen from the side facing right, walking.

- **Head:** a perfect circle pushed straight into the front of the body, with no neck. Use a slightly flattened, wider-than-tall cat head joined to the body by a short neck.
- **Ear:** only one ear, a narrow sliver at the top back of the head, too small for a cat. Draw two broad triangular ears (the far one partly hidden) set on top of the head.
- **Face:** the eye is a black smudge clipped by the head edge, and the muzzle is a jagged black-and-white block sticking out, which reads like a mask or a beak. Put a full almond eye inside the head, and give it a small rounded muzzle with a pink nose, a mouth line and whiskers.
- **Whiskers:** none, though fig_05 has them. Add whiskers so the cat reads as a cat.
- **White chest:** a lens shape bulging out of the front of the body below the head. Keep it inside the chest outline, as a bib running from the chin to the chest.
- **Body:** a long, straight-topped box. Give it a slight arch over the loin, a deeper chest and a tucked belly.
- **Front leg joint:** a knotted crease where the near front leg meets the body. Join the shoulder smoothly into the leg.
- **Hind legs:** the near hind leg bends forward like a knee. Bend the hind legs back at the hock, with the heel visibly raised.
- **Leg length:** the legs are long and even, like a dog's. Make them shorter and finer, with small rounded paws.
- **Tail:** a straight vertical stick. Raise it with a curve or a hook at the tip.
- **Walk:** the stride reads, but the legs cross awkwardly at the front. Separate the near and far front legs clearly in tone and position.

## fig_05

**First read:** an orange cat with a cream belly, seen from the front, standing and facing the viewer.

- **Tail:** sticks straight up from behind the top of the head like a pole. Hide it, or show it curling out to one side behind the body.
- **Legs:** only two, very long stilts with large flared thighs, so the cat looks as if it is on stilts. Draw four short legs, with the front paws together under the chest.
- **Ankle knobs:** knobs at the sides of each ankle, the same fault as fig_02. Remove them.
- **Muzzle:** the white two-lobed muzzle with a dividing line reads as buck teeth. Make the muzzle smaller, with a pink nose and a Y-shaped mouth line under it, and no tooth-like gap.
- **Ears:** small and set far apart. Draw them larger and broader at the base, on top of the head.
- **Head and body:** the head merges into the body with no neck or chin line, and the body has doubled side outlines. Add a chin line, and use one body outline.
- **Belly patch:** a large pasted-on circle. Make it a softer chest and belly blaze that narrows toward the legs.

## fig_06

**First read:** a white goat with a red collar and its head down, side view facing right, perhaps grazing or about to butt. The head is hard to read.

- **Head:** the face is hard to read as a goat. The horn rises from the back of the skull, the closed eye sits in the middle, and the nose is a blob at the bottom, so it looks like a dog or a sheep looking down. Draw a long, narrow goat face pointing down and forward, with the horns curving back from the top of the skull, the eye high on the side of the head and the nose at the lowest point.
- **Horn:** only one, flat grey-blue, and it looks bolted on. Draw two horns (the far one partly hidden) that curve backward from the brow.
- **Beard:** a grey triangle sticks out sideways from the back of the jaw like a blade or a tooth. Hang the beard straight down from the chin.
- **Ear:** an outlined shape pasted over the top of the head. Set the ear at the side of the head, behind the eye, pointing sideways.
- **Eye:** closed lines that read as a crease. Use an open eye, or make the closed eye clear.
- **Nose:** a black blob that pokes below the outline. Put the nostril on the muzzle tip, inside the outline.
- **Stray lines:** a long loose stroke on the shoulder and a broken short stroke beside the front leg. Remove them.
- **Neck:** as thick as the chest. Make it narrower than the chest, meeting the head at an angle.
- **Back:** rises to a high hump at the rump, so the goat looks as if it is pitching forward. Keep the back fairly level, with the shoulders slightly higher or level with the hips.
- **Hind leg:** sickle-shaped (bending forward, then back), with a double outline along its back edge. Draw a clear thigh, a hock bending back and a straight cannon bone.
- **Legs:** only two are shown. Add the far legs, in a darker tone.
- **Hooves:** round dark buns, like paws. Draw small split (cloven) hooves.
- **Tail:** a lumpy stub with a notch where it meets the back. Draw a short, upright, tapered tail.

## fig_07

**First read:** a red bird, seen from the side facing right, flying or flapping.

- **Wing:** two large overlapping ovals stand upright over the back and cover half the body, so they read as a shield or a leaf. Draw a wing with a rounded front edge and a tapering, feathered back edge, with notches for the flight feathers, attached at the shoulder.
- **Tail:** a straight rectangle with square corners, sticking out sideways. Draw a tapering, fanned tail with a notched or rounded tip.
- **Leg:** a single dark stub hangs under the belly, with a stray orange mark beside it. In flight, tuck the legs up or show two small legs with toes. Remove the orange mark.
- **Head:** if this is a cardinal, it has no crest. Add a crest if it is meant to be one, or keep it plain and make it a different species.
- **Mask:** a black patch at the beak base reads as a smudge. If it is meant as a cardinal's mask, make it a clear shape around the beak and eye.
- **Beak:** a flat, long open beak. A seed-eater needs a short, thick, cone-shaped beak.
- **Body:** the bird is a ball and the flight pose is unclear. Stretch the body forward a little so it reads as flight.
- **White belly:** a cream patch pasted on at the lower front. Blend it, or drop it.

## fig_08

**First read:** a white duck or goose, seen from the side facing right, sitting or floating. The pose is unclear.

- **Eye:** missing. Add an eye on the head, above and behind the beak.
- **Neck:** a separate cylinder with outlines across both ends, so the neck looks segmented, like a pipe joint. Run one outline from the head down into the body.
- **Head:** a plain circle. Shape a forehead that slopes into the beak, and a slightly longer head.
- **Beak:** a flat orange plate with a thick black lining. Draw a spatulate beak with a curved top edge and a thinner outline.
- **Wing:** a beige oval floating in the middle of the body. Draw a folded wing lying along the side, pointing back toward the tail.
- **Back shape:** an extra beige patch on the back that has no clear meaning. Remove it, or merge it into the wing.
- **Tail:** a narrow rectangle sticking up and back, like a handle. Draw a short, upturned, pointed tail.
- **Body:** a tilted blob, and there are no legs and no water line, so the viewer cannot tell whether the bird is swimming or sitting. Add a water line and ripples for floating, or feet and the ground for standing.
- **Size and species:** it reads as a goose or duck but could be either. Add duck cues (a shorter neck and a curled tail feather) or goose cues (a longer neck and a knob at the base of the beak).

## Top faults

1. **The heads are geometric balls with no muzzle, and the face features are clipped at the edge of the circle** (shared: figs 01, 03, 04, 06, 08, and partly 02 and 05). Give each animal its species head: a wedge skull and box muzzle for the dog, a broad short face for the cat, a long narrow face for the goat, a sloping forehead into the beak for the duck. Place the eyes fully inside the head.
2. **The legs are stilts with no joints** (shared: figs 01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 06). Draw an elbow and wrist on the front legs, and a thigh, a stifle bending forward and a hock bending back on the hind legs. Shorten the legs to match the species.
3. **The hind legs bend the wrong way or kink like a broken leg** (shared: figs 01, 04, 06, and the near hind leg in 03). The hock should bend backward, with a near-vertical cannon bone or lower leg.
4. **The front views show two giant stilt legs with ankle knobs, and the tail stands like an antenna behind the head** (shared: figs 02, 05). Show four short legs, remove the knobs, and hide the tail or curl it out to one side.
5. **Markings and parts look pasted on and stick out of the outline** (shared: figs 01 bib, 02 chest oval, 03 patches and ear, 04 chest lens, 05 belly circle, 06 ear, 08 wing). Keep markings inside the body outline as flat colour areas, and attach ears and wings at real joints.
6. **Stray marks and loose lines** (shared: figs 01 hip crease, 03 fanged blob under the belly and brow line, 04 shoulder knot, 06 shoulder strokes and double hind-leg line, 07 orange mark). Remove all of them. The blob in fig_03 reads as a mouth with teeth and must go.
7. **The goat's head cannot be read**: the horn comes from the back of the skull, the beard juts sideways like a blade, and the nose is a blob (fig 06). Redraw it as a long face pointing down, with two horns curving back from the brow and the beard hanging from the chin.
8. **The bird wings and tails are ovals and rectangles** (shared: figs 07, 08). Draw feathered wings with notched trailing edges and tapering, fanned tails.
9. **Faces are missing parts needed to name the species** (shared: fig 08 has no eye, fig 04 has no whiskers and only one sliver ear, fig 07 has no crest and the wrong beak, figs 01, 02 and 03 have no mouth line). Add the eye, whiskers, two ears, a mouth line and the right beak shape.
10. **Far legs are missing, or shown only as paws or flat grey sticks, and no context is given for the pose** (shared: figs 01, 03, 06, 08). Draw the far legs in a darker body tone. Give fig 08 a water line, or feet and the ground, so swimming or sitting reads.
