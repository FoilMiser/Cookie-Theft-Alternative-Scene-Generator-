# Cast review, sheet p2 (v0.47), figures 01 to 09

## fig_01
**First read:** young adult woman, long straight dark hair, purple short-sleeved T-shirt, teal trousers, dark flat shoes, walking to the right in side view.

- Head too large for an adult (about 1/4.5 of body height). Make it about 1/6 to 1/7.
- Face is in profile but the eye is a front-view round dot. In profile draw the eye as a side-view shape (a small almond or a dot set back from the face edge, with the lid line).
- Nose is a round blob stuck onto the face edge, and the mouth is a tiny red dab on the outline. Draw a small nose bump that is part of the profile line, with lips just below it on the same line.
- No chin or jaw in the profile; the face curves straight down into the neck. Give the profile a small chin and a jaw line that goes back to under the ear.
- Ear sits far back, almost in the hair, and is very small. Put it level with the eye to nose, just behind the middle of the head.
- Hair is a flat dark slab hanging behind the head with a squared-off fringe block. Show it falling from the crown over the back of the head and onto the shoulders, with a parting or a few flow lines.
- Neck is a thick tube with a rounded bottom, sitting on top of the shirt. Slimmer neck that goes into the collar, angled slightly forward in a walk.
- Near shoulder is a hump; the near arm is a separate sausage laid over the sleeve, and the sleeve hem line cuts across it. Draw the arm coming out of the sleeve opening, with the sleeve wrapping round the top of the arm.
- Arms have no elbow; they are straight even-width tubes. Narrow the arm at the elbow and wrist and bend the swinging arm slightly at the elbow.
- Hands are mitten blobs with no thumb or fingers. Draw at least a thumb and a finger block, relaxed and slightly curled.
- Torso is short and flat; no chest or back curve, no waist. Give a small chest curve at the front, a back curve, and a waist above the hips.
- No waistband, and the shirt hem is a dead straight line. Add a waistband line or let the hem lie loosely over the hip.
- Hips and crotch are not defined; the two thighs merge into one shape at the top. Show where the legs split under the hip, with the front thigh overlapping the back one.
- No knees: the legs are bent tubes. Show the knee on the front leg and on the bent back leg.
- Shoes are flat dark ovals with no heel, toe or sole, and both are the same shape. Draw a shoe with a heel, a sole line, and a toe; the back foot should be pushing off on the toe, heel lifted.

## Top faults

1. **Mitten hands with no fingers or thumb.** All figures (01 to 09). Draw a thumb and a finger block, relaxed and slightly curled; for hands resting on something (07) show the fingers over the thigh.
2. **No elbows or wrists; arms are even-width sausages, and in front views they hang detached from the body with a gap.** Elbows missing in all figures; the detached gap in 02, 03, 09 (front views). Narrow the arm at the elbow and wrist, bend swinging or resting arms a little, and let hanging arms touch the sides.
3. **Arms look pasted on at the shoulder: the arm is laid over the sleeve, or comes out of the wrong place, or the outline breaks.** Most figures: 01, 04, 05, 06, 08 clearly; 02, 03, 09 by the squared shoulder-pad sleeves. Draw the arm coming out of the sleeve opening at the shoulder, with the sleeve wrapping round it; attach the far arm at the shoulder behind the body (05 has it at the waist).
4. **No knees and no ankles; legs are straight posts or bent tubes, and the legs merge or pinch at the crotch.** All figures. Show the knee on each leg (fold or shape), narrow at the ankle; split the legs clearly under the hip, without the narrow high slit seen in 02, 03, 09.
5. **Shoes are plain ovals or domes with no heel, sole or toe; walking figures do not push off on the back toe.** All figures. Draw a shoe shape with heel, sole line and toe that matches the view; in walks (01, 05, 08) lift the back heel.
6. **Head too large for adults, with a thick tube neck and squared or humped shoulders.** Shared by most: large head 01, 02, 06, 07, 08 (08 worst), 09; tube neck all; squared shoulders 02, 03, 04, 06, 09. Head about 1/6 to 1/7 of adult height, neck about half the head width, round sloping shoulders.
7. **Side-view faces drawn with front-view parts: round dot eyes, a blob nose stuck on the edge, a dab mouth, no chin; ears missing or misplaced.** Profile faults in 01, 05, 07, 08; ears missing in 02, 03, 09, misplaced or tiny in 01, 04, 05, 06, 08. Build nose, lips and chin into the profile line, use a side-view eye, and put the ear mid-head level with the eye to nose.
8. **Hats, glasses and facial hair look stuck on.** Caps sit on top like lids with floating brims (05, 07, 09); far lens of the glasses floats off the face (04 only); moustache is a pasted bar or block (02, 05); beard is a shapeless chin smudge (06 only). Pull caps down onto the head with the brim attached at the forehead, keep the far lens on the face, shape moustaches to the lip and run the beard along the jaw from the sideburns.
9. **Clothes are flat blocks with no body shape: boxy shirts, no waistband or belt, rectangle skirt and tube dress, pasted rectangle vest.** Most figures; worst in 09 (vest), 08 (dress), 04 (skirt and off-centre placket with buttons outside it, one figure only). Add waistbands, taper to the waist, shape vests with straps and armholes, centre plackets with buttons on them, give dresses a waist and hip.
10. **Stray marks and outline breaks.** One-off faults: crotch smudge (06), dot in the hair (08), notch in the dress hem and broken leg join (08), unfinished arm line (04), unclear grey blob at the waistband (05), dashed outline on the vest stripes (09), floating hand with nothing under it (07). Remove the stray marks, close every outline, and rest the far hand on the knee in 07.
