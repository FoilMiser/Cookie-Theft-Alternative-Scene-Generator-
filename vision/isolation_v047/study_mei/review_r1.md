# Review r1: woman in yellow dress, eight figures

## fig_01
Adult woman, about 25 to 40, standing straight on to the viewer with her arms at her sides.

1. **Shoulders and torso are one rectangular slab.** The top of the dress is a flat horizontal shelf right under the chin, with square corners at both ends. There is no slope from neck to shoulder, no bust and almost no waist. The upper body reads as a cardboard box.
2. **Arms are straight tubes.** Each arm keeps the same width from shoulder to wrist. There is no elbow, no forearm taper and no wrist. They hang stiffly away from the body with a wide gap at the armpits, like a mannequin's arms.
3. **Hands are mitten blobs.** Each hand is a rounded lump with one small curl line. There is no thumb, no finger division, and no sign of which way the palm faces.
4. **Legs are straight tubes with no knees or ankles.** They are the same width from hem to shoe and slightly bowed outward at the calf. Nothing shows a knee, a calf curve or a narrowing at the ankle.
5. **Feet are red ovals with no direction.** The shoes are flat lozenges, and the legs plug into their tops dead centre. There is no toe, no heel and no front-to-back length. The figure looks like it stands on two buttons.
6. **The neck is a pasted-on cylinder.** It is a short tan plug that tucks under the chin and stops at the shelf of the dress. It has no taper into the shoulders and no collarbone or neckline shape.
7. **The face is crude.** The nose is an outlined "O", like a ring stuck on the face. The mouth is a small red rectangle with a dark outline, which reads as a startled open mouth. The eyes are dots set high, close under the brows. There are no ears.
8. **The hair is a helmet with flaps.** It is one dark cap with vertical side panels that drop straight to the shoulders and end bluntly. Behind the neck it fills a dark box shape, with no strands or ends.
9. **The sleeves are puffed blocks.** Each sleeve is a rectangle joined to the torso, and the seam line between sleeve and body only partly closes.
10. **The skirt is a plain rectangle.** It has a ruler-straight hem and a waist seam drawn as a faint line. It has no flare and nothing to show the hips under it.

## fig_02
The same woman in three-quarter view, standing, body and head turned to the viewer's right.

1. **The face is crammed against the edge of the head.** The far eye is cut in half by the head outline, and the "O" nose sticks out past the cheek line. The mouth sits on the very edge. The features look slid off a front-view face rather than drawn turned.
2. **The ear is misplaced.** It is a small tan tab buried in the hair, far back and too high. It does not line up with the eyes or nose.
3. **There is a see-through gap at the near armpit.** Background shows between the near arm and the torso just above the waist, so the arm looks detached from the body.
4. **The shoulder block is still square.** The far shoulder ends in a sharp right-angle corner. The near sleeve is a puff that juts out sideways.
5. **Arms and hands are the same tubes and mittens as fig_01.** The far arm is partly hidden but has the same blob fist. Neither arm bends at the elbow.
6. **The neck is a cylinder with a shading stripe.** It sits off-centre on the shoulder shelf, like a peg in a hole.
7. **Legs and feet are the same as fig_01.** They are tubes with no knees, and the two shoe ovals do not turn with the body. The feet do not agree with the three-quarter turn.
8. **The skirt is still a front-view rectangle.** It does not turn with the hips.

## fig_03
The same woman in full profile facing right, standing.

1. **The profile face has no profile.** The head is a round ball with no brow, nose bridge, lips or chin silhouette. The "O" nose is a circle stuck on the front edge. The eye sits on the very edge of the outline, and the mouth is a red smear at the rim.
2. **The legs cross and taper to points.** The back leg narrows to almost nothing at the ankle and disappears behind the front leg. The front leg bows forward at the knee. Together they look broken or melted.
3. **The feet are stacked shoe ovals.** Two red lozenges overlap, both pointing right, the back one partly under the front one. They do not match where the legs come down, and there are no heels.
4. **The arm is a tube hung on the front of the body.** It starts from a rounded sleeve cap and has no shoulder joint under it. It drops straight to a blob hand on the front of the skirt. Its line runs over the chest so the body looks flat.
5. **The neck is too thick and set too far back.** The wide cylinder joins the back of the skull rather than under it.
6. **The hair is a slab.** The back of the head is a big flat dark mass hanging down to the shoulder, with a hard vertical cut.
7. **The torso has no profile.** The chest is flat, and there is a bump at the upper back where the hair meets the dress. The skirt kicks backward in a lump.

## fig_04
The same woman walking toward the viewer's right, three-quarter view.

1. **The skirt outline is broken at the hem.** Where the front leg leaves the skirt, the hem line stops and the tan leg pokes through a notch. It looks torn or cut.
2. **The legs scissor.** The back leg crosses behind the front leg. Its foot ends to the left of the front foot, so the stride reads as tripping. The back leg is thin at the top and fat at the calf.
3. **The swinging arm has a kinked tube shape.** The back arm (viewer's left) bends in a soft hook with no clear elbow. The outline at the bottom of the sleeve, where the arm joins, has a gap, so the arm looks pasted under the sleeve.
4. **The shoes are flat and disconnected.** The back shoe is a tilted oval that floats. The front shoe has the leg stuck on top with no ankle.
5. **The forward hand is a mitten with a hook mark.** The curl line reads as a scratch.
6. **The shoulder block does not move.** There is no counter-swing in the shoulders, so the walk comes only from the legs.
7. **The face is crowded to one side.** The "O" nose stands proud of the cheek, as in fig_02.

## fig_05
The same woman sitting, three-quarter view, with no seat drawn.

1. **She floats.** The pose is seated, but nothing is under her, and the bottom of the skirt is a rounded sack hanging in the air.
2. **The thighs have vanished.** The knees are round tan caps sitting on top of the skirt, like ends of tubes pushed through cloth. The lower legs are very long, straight and vertical. The legs look like stilts stuck into a bag.
3. **The far arm floats.** The far forearm reaches forward and ends in a blob hand hanging in mid-air. It is not resting on the lap or a knee, and it looks too long.
4. **The near hand is a blob in the lap.** It does not rest on anything specific, and its outline merges with the skirt fold.
5. **The sleeves and shoulders are oversized.** The near sleeve is a big rectangle, and the upper arm comes out of it with a hard seam. The shoulder shelf is very wide on the left.
6. **The torso does not bend.** The upper body is upright, but the hips do not fold, so the dress does not crease at the lap.
7. **The feet are ovals with no ankles.** The legs plug into their tops.

## fig_06
The same woman raising her right arm high, reaching or waving, three-quarter view.

1. **The raised arm grows out of the top of the shoulder.** It comes from a small sleeve flap at neck level. There is no armpit, no lifted shoulder and no stretch in the torso, so the arm looks like a stick pushed into the dress.
2. **The raised hand is a tiny clump.** It is much smaller than the lowered fist and has four bumps on a stub. It reads as a claw.
3. **The raised arm is a long, thin straight tube.** It is thinner than the lowered arm and has no elbow or wrist.
4. **The lowered arm is detached.** There is a gap between the arm and the torso at the armpit, and the blob fist sits on the skirt edge.
5. **The face and neck have the same faults as fig_02.** The nose sticks out past the cheek, and the neck is a pasted cylinder with a shadow stripe.
6. **The legs and feet are the same tubes and ovals as before.**

## fig_07
The same woman sweeping with a broom, looking down.

1. **The grips do not hold the broom.** The upper hand is a blob at shoulder height, with the stick passing through it ambiguously. The lower hand sits beside the stick rather than around it. Neither hand shows fingers closing on the handle.
2. **The upper hand has no arm.** It sits against the shoulder with no visible upper arm or forearm, so it looks like a hand stuck on the sleeve.
3. **The near arm is a long tube across the body.** It has a soft curve where the elbow should be.
4. **The legs and feet are hidden or stunted.** The broom head covers the far foot. The near leg ends abruptly at a shoe that is half cropped, and the shoes peek out on both sides of the broom like ears.
5. **The head does not tilt.** The eyes look down, but the head and neck stay upright, so the downward look is unconvincing.
6. **The proportions shift.** The dress and body look shorter here than in the other figures.

## fig_08
The same woman holding a pink ice cream cone near her mouth.

1. **The hand does not hold the cone.** The cone floats in front of a blob hand, with its tip poking out below the fist. No fingers wrap it.
2. **The holding arm is a bent tube with a sharp corner.** It has no elbow shape. The forearm runs up diagonally from a sleeve that is still a puffed block.
3. **The mouth does not respond.** The cone is at the mouth, but the mouth is the same small red mark, closed and off to one side.
4. **The far arm hangs behind the skirt.** It hangs separately, with a sliver of gap, and a blob fist pokes out at the skirt edge.
5. **The shoulder block is square.** The shoulder pad corner sticks out at the left.
6. **The legs and feet are the same tubes and ovals.** The shoes have no direction.

---

## Top faults

1. **Tube limbs with no joints** (all eight). Arms and legs are straight tubes of one width, with no elbows, knees, wrists or ankles. Fix: taper each limb. An upper arm narrows slightly to the elbow and the forearm narrows to a slim wrist. A thigh narrows to the knee, the calf swells gently and pinches in at the ankle. Bent limbs need a visible elbow or knee corner, drawn as a soft angle, not a curve.
2. **Mitten hands that do not grip** (all, worst in 07, 08 and 06). Hands are blobs with one curl line, the raised hand is a tiny claw, and the broom and cone are not held. Fix: draw a simple hand of palm plus thumb, with three or four finger divisions as short lines. Size it to roughly the face from chin to brow. When holding something, wrap the fingers in front of the object and put the thumb on the other side so the handle visibly passes through the fist.
3. **Box shoulders and torso** (all). A flat horizontal shelf sits under the chin with square corners, and there is no bust or waist. Fix: slope the shoulders down from the base of the neck to a rounded shoulder point. Narrow the dress at the waist and give a slight curve at the bust and hips. Make the sleeves rounded caps that follow the arm, not rectangles.
4. **Arms detached from the body** (02, 04, 06, 08, 01). There are see-through gaps at the armpit, broken outlines where arm meets sleeve, and the raised arm grows from the top of the shoulder (06). Fix: the arm should come out of the sleeve opening with the sleeve edge drawn over it in one continuous line. The underarm should meet the side of the torso with no background showing. A raised arm should lift the shoulder and pull the sleeve and side of the dress up with it.
5. **Faces in turned views slid to the edge** (02, 03, 04, 05, 06, 07, 08). The far eye is cut by the outline, the "O" nose sticks out past the cheek, and the profile has no nose or chin silhouette. Fix: in three-quarter view, move the features inward with a centre line about a third of the way across and the far eye narrower but whole. Make the nose a small bump on the cheek line. In profile, cut a nose point and a chin into the head silhouette, and put the eye a little inside the edge.
6. **Button feet** (all, worst in 03 and 04). The shoes are flat red ovals with the leg plugged into the top centre, and they do not turn with the body. Fix: draw each shoe as a short wedge with a rounded toe and a heel. Join the leg at the back third through an ankle. Point the toes in the direction the body faces or steps.
7. **Seated pose that floats with stilt legs** (05). There is no seat, the skirt hangs as a sack, the knees are caps on the skirt and the thighs are missing. Fix: draw a seat or chair edge, or at least a ground shadow. Show the thighs running forward from the hips, foreshortened, with the skirt draped over them to the knees. Bend the shins down from a clear knee. Rest both hands on the thighs.
8. **Leg crossing and broken hem in walk and profile** (04, 03). The legs scissor, the back leg tapers to a point, and the skirt outline is torn where a leg exits. Fix: in a walk, the front leg steps forward and the back leg trails behind with its heel lifted, and the feet do not cross the centre line. Keep both legs the same width. Close the hem as one unbroken line with the legs coming out under it.
9. **Pasted-on neck** (all). The neck is a fat cylinder plug with a shadow stripe, stuck under the chin and into the shoulder shelf, and set too far back in profile. Fix: make it a slightly narrower column that flares into the slope of the shoulders. Draw a neckline (round or V) on the dress around its base. In profile, place it under the back half of the skull, angled slightly forward.
10. **Helmet hair and crude mouth and nose** (all). The hair is one dark cap with flat side flaps and blunt ends, the nose is an outlined ring, and the mouth is a red box that reads as startled. Fix: give the hair a soft outer silhouette with a parting or fringe edge and tapered ends with two or three strand lines. Draw the nose as a small open curve or a dot without a full ring. Draw the mouth as a short curved line, or a small closed shape when smiling or eating, matched to the action (for example, open toward the cone in 08).
