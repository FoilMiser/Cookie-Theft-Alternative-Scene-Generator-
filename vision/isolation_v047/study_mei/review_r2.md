# Figure review, round 2 (Mei, 8 pictures)

## fig_01
Adult woman, about 30, standing facing front with arms at her sides.

- **Shoulders and torso are a box.** The top of the dress is a flat line straight across, with sharp square corners at each shoulder, like a coat hanger or a cardboard cut-out. There is no slope from the neck down to the shoulder and no chest or waist shape. The bodice is a rectangle that sits on a second rectangle, the skirt.
- **Arms hang like tubes pasted under the sleeves.** Each arm comes out from under a puffed sleeve cap, but the sleeve does not wrap round the arm. It reads as a sleeve sitting on top of a separate stick. Each arm is the same width from shoulder to wrist, with no elbow, no forearm taper and no wrist. The arms stand away from the body, with a gap of background between arm and skirt down the whole length.
- **Hands are stumps.** Each hand is a rounded blob with one small curl line in it. There is no thumb and no fingers. They look like bandaged stumps or boxing mitts.
- **Legs are straight posts.** From the hem to the shoe each leg is nearly the same width. There is no knee, no calf curve and no ankle narrowing. The legs come straight out of the hem with no sign of hips under the skirt.
- **Feet are red domes.** Each shoe is a dark-red half-dome sitting on a black dash. There is no toe, no heel and no sole shape, and from the front they look like two tomatoes.
- **Neck is a plug.** The neck is a short skin cylinder with a shaded band at the top, so the chin looks like a double chin or a collar. It is as wide as the jaw. The head looks stuck onto it rather than growing from it.
- **Mouth looks hurt.** The mouth is a small open red slot with a dark top edge. It reads as a sore mouth or someone about to cry, not a neutral expression.
- **Nose is a drawn ring.** It is an outlined circle in the middle of the face, like a button.
- **Hair is a helmet.** It is one dark slab: a straight fringe across the forehead, then hanging straight down behind the neck and ending in a block at the shoulders. There is no parting and no flow. No ears are shown.
- **Head is too big for the body.** The head plus hair is roughly a fifth of the height, which pushes the adult towards a child or doll look.
- **The waist seam is only a faint shade line**, so the dress has no clear waist.

## fig_02
The same woman, standing with her back mostly to us, head turned to her left (our right).

- **The head reads as a hair ball with a hole in it.** It is almost all dark hair. A small skin crescent at the right edge, with a pink oval inside it for the ear, looks like a gap or wound in the hair rather than the side of a face.
- **There is a skin lump under the head at the right.** The cheek or chin pokes out below the hair as a separate round bump against the shoulder, like a growth on the neck.
- **The right arm (our right) is pasted on.** The top of the arm is a rounded end laid over the sleeve. The sleeve's edge line runs across inside the arm, so the arm sits in front of the dress like a sticker, not inside the sleeve. The arm is a uniform tube with no elbow, and the hand is a flat blob turned outwards.
- **The left arm (our left) is half missing.** It comes out of the side of the dress below the shoulder with its top hidden, and it ends in a stump at the hip. A small black notch in the outline at the waist on that side looks like a tear in the dress.
- **The shoulders are square-cornered**, especially the right one, which ends in a hard right-angle corner.
- **Legs are tubes with no knees.** The far leg tucks behind the near leg. A dark wedge between them at the hem looks like a hole.
- **The feet are two overlapping red blobs** with no shoe shape and no clear direction.
- **The hair hangs as one solid slab** to the shoulder blades, with no strands or edge variation.

## fig_03

- **The near arm is a big pasted-on tube.** It starts as a flat-topped rounded end laid over the front of the sleeve, not inside it. It is far wider than the far arm and swings back with no elbow bend. The hand is a blob with a scribble and no thumb.
- **The far arm comes out of the chest.** It starts from the front of the bodice, below the bust, rather than from the shoulder. It is a thin stick ending in a mitten.
- **The head is in profile but the body is not.** The face points fully right while the shoulders and bodice are drawn close to front-on. Together they read as a twisted neck.
- **The profile face is built wrong.** The front of the face is a flat vertical edge with no brow, nose bridge or chin. The nose is a small ring stuck on the outline. The mouth is a red smear on the edge. The jaw meets the neck in a flat horizontal line.
- **The legs do not bend in the stride.** Both legs are tubes coming out of the hem with no knee. The back leg bends in a soft curve like rubber.
- **The hem is torn where the front leg leaves it.** At the lower right corner of the skirt there is a ragged nick in the outline.
- **Feet are flat red pancakes.** They are long thin ovals with no heel or instep. The leg ends on top of each oval, with no ankle joint. The back foot tilts toe-down like a dropped slipper.
- **The neck is a plug pushed forward of the body's centre**, with the same shaded double-chin band.
- **The bodice back has an odd bump** behind the near arm, and the arm's top end makes the shoulder look dislocated.

## fig_04

- **The back arm is a pasted-on tube.** The upper arm is a thick rounded slab laid over the shoulder and sleeve with a flat top. The elbow is a blunt corner. The forearm hangs straight down along the dress, with the hand blob at the hip. A runner's arm bends at about a right angle and swings back.
- **The front arm is a straight stick from the chest.** It starts from the front of the bodice below the shoulder, with no sleeve and no elbow, and it ends in a flat paddle hand pointing forward. It looks like a pointing gesture, not a run.
- **The kicked-up back leg is broken.** The thigh is hidden in the skirt and the shin sticks out horizontally to the left. The foot is a red leaf shape turned on end at the tip, attached like a separate piece. There is no ankle and no heel.
- **The front leg is a long straight post** from the hem to the foot. It has no knee and looks too long.
- **The hem is notched** at the lower right, where the front leg comes out.
- **The face has the same profile faults as fig_03**: ring nose, red smear mouth and a flat face edge. The head is in profile on a three-quarter body.
- **The hands are mittens** with no fingers.

## Top faults

1. **Arms are pasted on instead of growing from the shoulder (02, 03, 04, 06, 07, 08; milder in 01 and 05).** Arm tops are rounded ends laid over the sleeve, or start from the middle of the chest. Fix: each arm starts inside the sleeve at the shoulder point. The sleeve opening is a closed edge that wraps round the arm. The outline runs continuously from the neck, over the shoulder and down the outside of the arm, with no arm-end shape showing over the dress.
2. **Hands are fingerless blobs that never grip (all eight; worst in 06, 07, 08).** Fix: draw a simple mitten with a separate thumb, plus 2 or 3 finger divisions where the hand closes. A gripping hand curls round the handle or under the box, so the object passes behind the fingers and in front of the palm. The pointing hand is a closed fist with the thumb tucked in and a straight index finger the same width as the other fingers.
3. **Limbs are uniform tubes with no joints (all; worst in 04, 05, 06, 07).** Fix: taper each arm from shoulder to wrist, with a slight bulge at the elbow and a narrow wrist. Taper each leg from thigh to knee to a narrow ankle. When a limb bends, bend it at the joint with a clear angle, not as a rubber curve. Keep the pointing arm to normal length, so the fingertip reaches about mid-thigh when the arm hangs.
4. **Shoulders and bodice are a box with square corners (01, 02, 05, 06, 07).** Fix: slope the shoulders down from the neck in a gentle curve to a rounded shoulder point. Make the shoulders about two head-widths across, not more. Give the bodice a slight chest curve narrowing to a clear waist seam, so the dress is shaped, not two stacked rectangles.
5. **Feet are red domes and pancakes with no ankle (all; worst in 03, 04).** Fix: draw a flat shoe shape with a rounded toe, a heel and a thin sole line. Seen from the front it is a short wedge; seen from the side it is a long shoe whose heel sits under the ankle. The leg should narrow into the top of the shoe at the ankle, not end on top of an oval. The kicked-up foot in 04 should hang from the ankle, pointing back and down.
6. **The profile face is wrong, and the head turns on a front-facing body (03, 04, 06, 07, 08).** Fix: give the profile a brow bump, a small nose that projects from the face line (drawn as part of the outline, not a ring), lips set back, and a chin that projects and then curves under into the jaw. Turn the shoulders and chest to match the head, or turn the head to three-quarter so the neck isn't twisted. Front views (01, 05): make the mouth a closed short curve, not an open red slot. Make the nose two small marks or a short curve, not an outlined circle.
7. **Hem and outline breaks where legs and arms come out (02, 03, 04, 07, 08).** Fix: the skirt hem is one unbroken line. Legs start behind it and the hem overlaps them. Remove the nicks at the hem corners, the skin patch painted over the hem in 08, and the black notch at the waist in 02.
8. **Seated pose has no thighs, no seat and standing-length shins (05).** Fix: show the thighs foreshortened, as short wide shapes coming towards us, with the skirt draped over them and falling between the knees. Set the knees at hip height or a little lower, and shorten the shins to normal length down to the floor. Add a seat or chair edge so she is not floating. Make the skirt follow the lap, with no angular side points.
9. **Props pass through the body or the hands (07, 08).** Fix: the box in 08 sits in front of the body, held at the bottom by both hands with the forearms visible running to it. Its back edge stops in front of the chest outline, and the far arm is drawn, or at least the far forearm, so the far hand is not floating. The handle in 07 passes behind the curled fingers of both hands. Draw the load clearly as either soil (a mound with a few crumbs) or dust (small grey lumps).
10. **Neck plug, hair helmet and oversized head (all; back-view head in 02).** Fix: make the neck narrower than the jaw, joining under the jaw with a curved line, and drop the shaded band that reads as a double chin. Give the hair a parting, a softer fringe, and an end that tapers or flips at the shoulders. Shrink the head slightly so the adult is about 6 to 6.5 heads tall. From behind (02), show the back of the head as hair with a clean edge, a small piece of cheek and jaw on the turned side drawn as part of the head outline, and an ear set in skin, not a hole in the hair. Remove the skin bump below the head.

Also needed: the running pose (04) needs a forward lean and a right-angle arm swing, and the working pose (07) needs a bent back and the head looking down at the tool.
