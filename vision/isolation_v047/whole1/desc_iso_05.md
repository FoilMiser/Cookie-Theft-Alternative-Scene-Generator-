# iso_05

## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, trees, a curving sandy path and a blue pond with bulrushes. On the front left a small girl in a pink dress stands holding a kite reel up, flying a yellow diamond kite with a bow tail high in the sky. In the middle a brown-and-white dog sits upright on its haunches holding an open newspaper in its front paws as if reading it, wearing a red collar or scarf — this is impossible/silly, since dogs don't read newspapers. To the right, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey shorts, sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket with her legs stretched out; on the blanket is a plate with a slice of watermelon, and next to her is a picnic basket with a baguette and a green bottle. An empty wooden bench stands by the path, a tall black street lamp and a green rubbish bin are in the middle background, a grey rock lies in the foreground, and a flower bed with colourful flowers is on the front right. Nothing seems about to happen except perhaps the dog being near the picnic food.
