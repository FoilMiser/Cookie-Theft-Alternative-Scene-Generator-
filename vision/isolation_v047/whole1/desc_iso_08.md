## Free description
This is a sunny back garden beside a cream house with a red roof, a blue door and one window on the left. A tall wooden fence with a wooden gate on the right runs across the back, with a tree and bushes behind it. In the middle-left a girl or young woman in a purple top and teal trousers is watering a round bed of colourful flowers with a grey watering can; blue streams of water are falling onto the flowers. Further back near the fence, an older man with grey hair, a flat cap, a white shirt and a brown waistcoat is raking a small pile of brown leaves. In the bottom right foreground an orange cat is crouched low and creeping forward towards a blue bird that is pecking at seeds on the grass beside a paved path. It looks as if the cat is about to pounce on the bird, which does not seem to have noticed. Two potted plants and a couple of bushes stand on the lawn. Nothing looks clearly impossible.
