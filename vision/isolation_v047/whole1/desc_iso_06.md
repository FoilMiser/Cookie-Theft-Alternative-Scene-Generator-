# iso_06

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills, trees, a pond with reeds, a tall black lamp post and a winding sand-coloured path. In the centre-left a man with dark skin, a short beard, a green T-shirt and beige trousers is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red lead; both are walking to the right. In the middle right a woman with dark hair in a yellow dress sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. On the far right, under a big tree, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt holds a paper bag and is scattering seeds or crumbs, which fall in a line of small dots down to the ground. A small blue bird on the ground at the bottom right is pecking at the scattered seeds. A bed of colourful flowers sits in the bottom left, with a green rubbish bin in the corner. Nothing looks wrong or impossible; it is a calm everyday scene.
