# iso_46

## What I see
An older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal jumper, grey skirt and brown shoes, stands on a lawn holding a grey watering can. A long light blue curved line comes out of the can's spout, arcs up and to the left over a bed of colourful flowers (red, yellow, orange, purple, pink) and ends in a point, with a few water drops above it. She seems to be watering the flowers. There is a wooden fence behind her and a cream wall edge on the left.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The blue curve goes up from the low spout, high into the air, and then stops in a point. Water from a watering can should pour down, not shoot upward, so it looks like a fountain or a jet. The curve could also be read as a stiff blue hose or wand attached to the can rather than as water.
- The drops above the tip seem to float in the air instead of falling onto the flowers.
- The stream crosses in front of the flowers and does not clearly land on them.
- Her expression (small open mouth, glasses) is hard to read and could be surprise or talking.
