# iso_40

## What I see
A child with dark skin and short dark curly hair, in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts, stands on grass in front of a blue pond, with green hills and trees behind. One hand is raised high toward a yellow balloon that has floated up into the sky, its string trailing down and loose. The other arm reaches out to the left and holds the strings of two balloons, one purple and one blue. A small patch of soil with purple, orange and red flowers is in the bottom-right corner. It reads as a child who has let go of (or lost) one balloon and is reaching after it.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The yellow balloon's string ends in mid-air over the pond, well above and to the right of the child's raised hand. So it isn't clear whether the child is still holding it or has already lost it. Reading 1: it escaped and the child is reaching for it. Reading 2: the child is meant to be holding it and the string just doesn't reach the hand.
- The child's face is small. The expression could be surprise or worry (open mouth), but it's hard to make out.
- The arm holding the two balloons sticks out stiffly and horizontally, and the strings meet at a point near the hand rather than clearly inside a closed fist.
- The flower bed is cut off at the corner. The flowers look a bit large compared with the child.
