# iso_38

## What I see
A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers sits on a wooden bench reading a red book, in front of a building with a blue door. Beside him on the bench lies a large sandwich (bread with lettuce and tomato). A small blue bird is standing on the bench next to the sandwich, pecking at it, apparently stealing food while the boy is absorbed in his book. Red flowers in a dirt bed are at the right edge.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The bird's beak is at the end of the sandwich; it could be eating from it or just standing next to it.
- The sandwich is very large relative to the boy, and it sits partly overlapping the bench backrest/seat line, so it is slightly unclear whether it rests on the seat or floats in front of the backrest.
- The boy's eyes point at the book, so he does not seem to notice the bird.
