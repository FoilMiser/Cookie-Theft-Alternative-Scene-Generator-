# iso_12

## What I see
A child with dark curly hair, a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts stands on a paved path in front of a wooden garden gate and fence on a sunny day. In one lowered hand the child holds the strings of a blue and a yellow balloon; the other arm is raised toward the sky, where a red balloon is floating away high above the hedge. The child's face looks upset or frowning, as if they have just let go of or lost the red balloon. A bit of a house roof shows at the right edge.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The raised arm could be reading as reaching after the escaped red balloon, or as waving.
- The red balloon's string trails down and ends in the hedge, so it could also be read as caught/tied in the bushes rather than drifting freely.
- The blue balloon's string seems to run down past the child's hand toward the fence rather than clearly ending in the hand, so it could be read as tied to the gate/fence instead of held.
- The child's expression is small and hard to read; could be sad or just squinting at the sun.
