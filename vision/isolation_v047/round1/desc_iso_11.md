# iso_11

## What I see
A brown and white cartoon dog is sitting up on its hind legs on grass, holding an open newspaper (printed columns and a picture block) in its front paws as if reading it. It has a red collar or scarf at its neck and dark rings around its eyes that look like glasses. Its face is turned toward the paper.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The dark shapes around the eyes could be glasses or just dark eye markings/patches.
- The red item at the neck could be a collar, a bandana/scarf, or a cape; it sticks out behind the neck like a flap.
- The shadow sits mostly to the right of and slightly below the dog, so the dog can look a little detached or as if hovering over the shadow rather than sitting on it.
- The back legs/haunch are compact and the pose is hard to read: the lower body could be read as sitting or as crouching.
