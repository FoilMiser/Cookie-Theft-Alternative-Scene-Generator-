## What I see
A dark-skinned child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands on grass in front of a blue pond, with hills and trees behind. In one hand the child holds the strings of two balloons, one purple and one blue. A third, yellow balloon is high in the sky, drifting away, and the child is reaching up toward it with the other arm, apparently having just let it go or lost it. Flowers grow in a dirt bed in the bottom-right corner.

## Unsure or ambiguous

The child's face (puckered mouth) is hard to read: could be dismay or whistling/blowing.
- The child's raised hand is open but does not touch the string, which supports "lost it".
- The two held strings meet at the lower hand, which looks fine. Nothing obviously floating or intersecting otherwise.
