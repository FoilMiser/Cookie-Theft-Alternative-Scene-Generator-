# iso_55

## What I see
An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt stands on a pale paved path beside a wooden fence and gate. She holds a long pale-grey handle that ends in a huge fork-like head with four tines, which she drags along the ground. Near the tines are a small pile of grey dirt or debris with small orange bits, a puff of dust, and a red dustpan. A potted plant is in the top right and a teal watering can is in the bottom right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The tool she holds looks exactly like a giant dinner fork, not a broom or a garden rake. Read one way it is a rake used to sweep debris into the dustpan; read the other way it is an oversized table fork, which looks strange.
- The handle runs from her hand across her body and seems to pass in front of or through her torso and arm; it is not clearly gripped by both hands.
- The orange bits in the debris pile could be leaves or crumbs.
- The grey blob by the dustpan could also be read as a small animal, such as a mouse or a bird, rather than dirt.
