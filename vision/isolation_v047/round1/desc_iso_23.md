# iso_23

## What I see
A man with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue trousers sits on a wooden park bench reading a red book. Beside him on the bench lies a sandwich (bread with green lettuce and red filling), and a blue bird stands next to it with its head down at the sandwich, as if pecking at or stealing it. The man is looking at his book, not at the bird. Behind are a green rubbish bin, the bottom of a dark lamp post and a beige footpath curving across the grass.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The bird could be a pigeon or a jay, and it may be eating the sandwich or just standing next to it. Its beak seems to touch the bread.
- A few small white marks by the bird's feet could be crumbs or could be its toes/claws. Hard to tell.
- The bench's right side is hidden behind the man's legs, so you can only see one bench leg clearly. The bench looks a bit short for him.
- The bin and lamp post sit high on the grass with no ground shadow, so they look slightly flat or floating, though you can still read them as background.
