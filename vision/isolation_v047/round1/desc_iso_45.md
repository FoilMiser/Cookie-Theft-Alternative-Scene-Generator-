A small drop of water shows at the nozzle. A dark blue hose runs from the nozzle down to the ground, loops across the bottom of the picture and goes to a round hose reel by the house wall on the right, next to a blue door. A brown and white dog with a red collar stands on the lawn in the middle, facing the man, and the hose runs under and behind its feet. Behind them is a round bed of soil with colourful flowers (yellow, orange, red, pink, purple), a wooden fence and a green bush.

- The dog could be standing on the hose and blocking the water, which would explain why only a drop comes out, or it could just be standing near the hose. The hose and the dog's back feet overlap, so this is not clear.
- The dog's back leg looks oddly bent, with a curled, rubbery shape at the hock.
- Near the bottom middle, the hose looks doubled or thickened where two lengths meet or overlap, which looks a little odd.
- The hose reel sits low in front of the door frame and is attached only loosely to the wall. It looks a little as if it is floating.
