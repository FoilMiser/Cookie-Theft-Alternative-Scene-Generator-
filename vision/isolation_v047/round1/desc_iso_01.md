# iso_01

## What I see
A man in an orange cap, orange high-visibility vest with reflective stripes and blue trousers stands on grass, holding a teal watering can. He is watering a raised bed of colourful flowers (purple, orange, red, pink, yellow) in brown soil. A blue pond or lake is behind him. A stream of water comes from the can's spout, along with water droplets.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water stream does not fall onto the flowers. It leaves the spout and arcs upward, ending in drops floating in the air above the flowers. It looks like the water flows up, defying gravity, or like a hose spraying upward rather than a can pouring.
- The can is tilted only a little, so the upward jet looks even more odd.
- The hi-vis vest and cap make him look like a road or park worker rather than a gardener. Either reading works.
