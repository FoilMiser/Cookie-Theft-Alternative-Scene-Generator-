# iso_02

## What I see
An older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top, grey skirt and brown shoes, stands on grass beside a round bed of brown soil full of colourful flowers (red, yellow, purple, orange, pink). She holds a grey watering can in both hands with its spout pointing toward the flower bed, so she is watering the flowers. A long blue stream of water runs from the spout to the flowers, with a few water drops above it. Behind her is a brown wooden fence or wall with a vertical post, and there is a cream-coloured edge (a wall or door frame) at the far left.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water stream looks unintentionally strange: it starts at the spout, which is low near her waist, and curves *upward* and to the left to above the flowers, rather than pouring down from the spout. It looks more like a hose or a stick than falling water; the drops sit above the top of the arc.
- The stream passes in front of the flowers and ends up high among the flower heads rather than landing on the soil.
- The background could be read as a brick wall or as a wooden fence with planks; the tall brown post next to her head is not clearly attached to anything.
- The cream strip on the far left is unexplained (wall, door frame or edge of a building).
