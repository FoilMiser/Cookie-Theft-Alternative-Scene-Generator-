# Review: fig_03

## 1. Who and what
A brown dog with a darker brown ear and black glasses, sitting upright like a person, reading an open newspaper or leaflet held in front of it with its front paws. Both versions read the same, except that in b the lower body has no visible hind legs.

## 2. Faults in a
- Neck and body: tall and narrow, with a long neck rising to a large head, so it looks somewhat like a meerkat or weasel rather than a dog.
- Far front paw: a small stub sticks out at the top left edge of the paper, with no visible arm leading to it.
- Near arm: the long upper arm runs down the side of the body and bends sharply into a long forearm reaching the lower corner of the paper. The arm is long and thin.
- Back: a small stray curved line on the back near the shoulder.
- Hind legs: folded under the body. The near foot is readable, but the far hind leg is only a darker patch behind it.
- Paper: it is held only at one bottom corner and one top edge, so it looks as if it floats.

## 3. Faults in b
- Hind legs and feet: missing. The lower body ends in a rounded sack- or bottom-like shape with two lumps and crease lines, and no feet. It looks unfinished or deformed.
- Lower body: the bulge and creases at the bottom read oddly, almost like buttocks seen from behind.
- Neck and body: the same tall, narrow body with a long neck.
- Far front paw: the same small stub at the top of the paper, with no visible arm.
- Near arm: the same long, thin arm with a sharp elbow.
- Back: the same small stray line near the shoulder.

## 4. Differences
- Lower body: a has folded hind legs with a visible foot resting on the ground; b has no hind legs or feet and a rounded, creased lower body instead.
- In b the bottom of the body is narrower and comes to a rounded end; in a it spreads out wider at the base.
- The head, glasses, ear, arms, paper and tail look the same in both.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: The head is large and fine, with the glasses sitting well. The neck is long and the body tall and thin. The near arm is long and thin; the far arm is barely shown, just a paw stub. The hind legs are folded with a readable foot, and the far leg is unclear. The tail joins naturally.
- b: The head, neck, body, arms and tail are the same as in a. The hind legs and feet are missing, and the lower body is a creased, lumpy sack that looks wrongly formed.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads naturally as a dog sitting up and reading a paper; the folded hind legs support a seated pose. The arm is a bit long and stiff.
- b: The upper half reads the same, but without legs it is unclear whether the dog is sitting or standing. The figure looks as if it floats or is cut off, which is awkward.

## 7. Better drawn
a. Its folded hind legs and foot make a believable seated pose, while b has lost the hind legs and has a strange creased lower body.
