# Review: fig_01

## Who and what
A small round blue bird (cream belly, yellow beak) tipped forward with its head and beak down, pecking at the ground. Both versions read the same; b stands taller on visible legs, a is crouched low with almost no legs.

## Faults in a
- Legs: almost no legs; the orange feet sit directly under the belly as short stubs, so the bird looks like it is sitting on its feet.
- Feet: two small orange marks that are hard to read as feet or toes.
- Head: very large and round, nearly as big as the body; the head circle overlaps the body and wing with a full outline, so it looks stuck on rather than joined.
- Wing: big dark oval laid over the side of the body, pasted-on look, with no clear shoulder join.
- Far wing: the second wing showing above the back reads like a hump or a separate lump.
- Tail: a stiff flat wedge pointing straight up-left.
- Beak: small, sits at the very bottom of the head and points straight down, a little detached from the face.

## Faults in b
- Legs: very unequal; the rear leg is a long thin straight vertical stick, the front leg a short stub under the belly.
- Rear leg: attaches at the back edge of the belly and looks like a prop holding up the rear of the body.
- Feet: small flat orange stubs, hard to read as toes.
- Head: same very large round head with a full outline overlapping the body, stuck-on look.
- Wing: same large pasted-on dark oval, here tilted more upright.
- Far wing: same hump-like second wing above the back.
- Tail: stiff flat wedge pointing up-left.

## Differences
- Legs: a has only stub feet directly under the belly; b has a long visible rear leg and a short front leg.
- Body angle: b is tipped more steeply forward (head lower relative to tail) and stands taller overall; a is lower and wider.
- Wing: in b the wing oval is more upright and narrower; in a it lies more along the body.
- Head position: in b the head is lower and further under, with the beak pointing more steeply down.
- Overall shape: a is wider than tall; b is closer to square.

## Anatomy
- a: Head too big for the body and outlined as a separate circle; wing oversized; legs too short, effectively missing; feet joined straight to the belly. Tail fine in length but stiff.
- b: Head too big and outlined as a separate circle; wing oversized; rear leg too long and thin, front leg too short, so the legs do not match; feet too small. Tail stiff.

## Pose
- a: Reads as a bird pecking, and the low crouch is plausible for a small bird, but with no legs it looks like it is lying on the ground rather than standing.
- b: Reads clearly as a bird standing and bending down to peck; more natural overall, though the one long straight rear leg makes it look stiff and propped up.

## Better drawn
b. It shows the bird standing on legs while pecking, which reads more clearly, whereas a seems to have no legs at all; the mismatched leg lengths in b are a smaller fault.
