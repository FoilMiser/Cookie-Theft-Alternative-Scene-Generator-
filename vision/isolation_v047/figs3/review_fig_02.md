# Review: fig_02

## Who and what
A small red bird (robin-like, cream belly, yellow beak) leaning forward with its head and beak down, pecking at the ground. Both versions read the same; in b the legs are spread so it looks more like it is stepping forward while pecking, in a it is standing still.

## Faults in a
- Legs: only one leg reads clearly, a single straight vertical orange stick under the rear of the belly; the second leg is hidden or merged, so the bird looks one-legged.
- Feet: the toes are tiny short stubs at the bottom of the leg, barely readable as feet.
- Tail: a long flat rectangle sticking straight out up-left, stiff and plank-like rather than feathered.
- Back outline: a small jagged notch in the black outline where the tail meets the back.
- Wing: the dark oval wing covers most of the body and sits on it like a pasted shape, with no join to the shoulder.
- Head: no neck or separation line on the outer contour; the head bulges out of the front of the body.
- Eye: a plain black dot with no highlight, slightly dead-looking.

## Faults in b
- Rear leg: angles out backwards from behind the belly at a sharp slant, ending in a flat foot well behind the body; it looks like it sticks out of the back of the belly rather than from under it.
- Front leg: very short and mostly hidden under the belly, so the two legs are very unequal in length.
- Feet: small and blunt, hard to read as toes.
- Tail: same long flat stiff rectangle sticking straight out up-left.
- Wing outline: a few scratchy, broken dark marks on the outline near the top of the wing where it meets the head.
- Wing: same large pasted-on oval covering most of the body.
- Eye: plain black dot with no highlight.

## Differences
- Legs: a has one straight vertical leg under the rear belly; b has one leg slanting back behind the body and a short front leg under the belly.
- Stance: a stands still; b looks like it is mid-step.
- Body: b is drawn slightly longer and lower, with the body more horizontal; a is a little taller and rounder.
- Head: in b the head is slightly lower and the beak points more steeply down-right.
- Outline: a has a notch at the tail join; b has scratchy marks at the top of the wing near the head.
- Belly: the cream belly patch in b is a little flatter and wider.

## Anatomy
- a: Head-to-body proportion is fine for a cartoon bird; the head joins the body with no neck, acceptable. Wing oversized but readable. Legs read as only one visible leg, thin and straight, feet too small. Tail too long and flat, like a board.
- b: Head and body similar to a. Rear leg too long relative to the front one and joined at the back of the belly, oddly placed; front leg too short. Feet too small. Tail too long and flat, like a board.

## Pose
- a: Reads clearly as a bird bending to peck; natural enough, but stiff, because the single straight leg makes it look propped on a post.
- b: Reads as a bird walking and pecking; livelier, but the rear leg kicked out behind looks awkward, as if it were slipping or about to fall forward.

## Better drawn
a, narrowly. Its legs sit under the body in a stable, believable stance, whereas b's rear leg juts out behind the belly at an odd angle; the other faults (stiff tail, pasted-on wing) are shared.
