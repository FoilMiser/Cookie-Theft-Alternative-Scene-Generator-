# Pair 05

## Who and what
An orange cat walking to the right with its tail held straight up. Both versions read the same way; b has a white chest.

## Faults in a
- Head: a perfect circle placed on the front corner of the body with no neck; looks stuck on.
- Ears: only one pointed ear is drawn, on top of the head.
- Front leg: the near front leg has a kink/notch at the top where it meets the body, like a broken joint.
- Legs: all four legs are long and thin, like stilts, making the cat look tall and lanky.
- Body: the body is a long, flat-topped rectangle.
- Tail: straight thin tube, stiff.

## Faults in b
- Face: the eye is cut by the edge of the head outline, and the nose/muzzle is a black blob sticking out of the head edge; the face looks broken.
- Head: the same perfect circle with no neck as in a.
- Ears: only one ear.
- Front leg: the same kink/notch at the top of the near front leg.
- Legs: the same long, thin, stilt-like legs.
- Chest: the white chest patch is a separate cream shape that bulges out beyond the body outline.

## Differences
- Eye: in a it is fully inside the head; in b it is cut off by the edge of the head.
- Muzzle: in a it is a small white muzzle with whiskers; in b it is a black blob at the edge of the head.
- Chest: b has a white/cream chest patch; a is plain orange.
- Otherwise body, legs and tail look the same.

## Anatomy
- a: head round and joined without a neck; body long and boxy; legs too long and thin, near front leg kinked at the shoulder; feet rounded pads, fine; tail long and stiff.
- b: same as a, plus a broken-looking face with the eye cut off and a dark muzzle blob, and a chest patch bulging past the outline.

## Pose
- a: reads as a natural walking stride with the tail up; a little stiff because the legs are straight and the body is flat.
- b: the same pose and the same stiffness.

## Better drawn
a. The pose and body are the same, but b's face is broken (eye cut off, black muzzle blob), while a's face is clear.
