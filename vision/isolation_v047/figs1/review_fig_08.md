# Pair 08

## 1. Who and what
A brown dog with darker brown ear, cream muzzle and red collar, walking to the right with tail up. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Hip/rear leg: an extra crease line on the haunch above the near back leg makes the thigh join look like a fold or patch, not a smooth hip.
- Near front leg: the leg goes straight down from the chest like a post, with no elbow or wrist bend, and its top edge cuts across the body as a visible line.
- Legs/feet: the feet are round blobs, with no paws or toes drawn.
- Tail: a long, thin, stiff spike pointing straight up and back.

## 3. Faults in b
- Chest: a cream/white patch hangs below the collar and sticks out past the chest outline, so it looks like a bib or tag attached to the collar rather than fur on the chest.
- Eye: the eye sits right on the edge of the head outline at the front of the face and is partly cut off by it.
- Hip/rear leg: the same extra crease line on the haunch as in a.
- Near front leg: the same straight, post-like leg with no bend.
- Feet: the same round blobs; the far back foot is a little larger and flatter.
- Tail: the same stiff thin spike.

## 4. Differences
- b has a cream patch under the collar on the chest; a has none.
- In b the eye is further forward and touches or crosses the head outline; in a it sits fully inside the head.
- The far back foot in b is slightly larger and longer than in a.
- Otherwise the body, legs, tail, ear and collar look the same.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head, ear and muzzle are in proportion. The neck under the collar is fine. The body is long and deep. The legs are thick enough but tube-like, with no joints. There is an odd crease at the hip. The tail is long and very thin. Nothing is detached.
- b: the same as a, plus the chest patch that hangs out of the body outline like a separate piece, and the eye pressed against the edge of the head.

## 6. Pose
- a: reads as a natural walk. The legs are in a diagonal stepping pattern and the head is up. It is slightly stiff because the legs do not bend.
- b: the same walking pose and the same stiffness in the legs.

## 7. Better drawn
a. It is the same dog, but b's chest patch looks like something hanging from the collar and its eye is cut by the head outline.
