# Review: fig 10

## 1. Who and what
A man with short dark hair and a beard, in a green T-shirt, khaki trousers and brown shoes, running to the right. Version b reads more clearly as running, because the front arm is bent with a fist. In a the front arm is held out straight with an open hand, so it reads partly as reaching or pointing.

## 2. Faults in a
- Front arm: held out stiff and straight, level with the shoulder. The flat open hand looks like reaching, not running.
- Back foot: the brown shoe at the far left hangs at the end of the trouser leg and is only loosely joined to it. It looks half detached.
- Back shoulder: the green sleeve makes a lumpy bulge where the bent back arm leaves the torso.
- Face: the mouth sits at the very edge of the head outline, so the profile looks squashed.
- Torso: short and narrow next to the very long trouser legs.

## 3. Faults in b
- Face: the eyebrows are angled down, so he looks cross or frowning rather than neutral.
- Mouth: again pushed to the edge of the head outline.
- Torso: short compared with the long legs, though less so than in a.
- Back arm: the hand rests against the hip and the arm hugs the body, which is a little tight for a run.

## 4. Differences
- Front arm: straight with an open flat hand in a; bent at the elbow with a closed fist in b.
- Eyebrows: a single raised brow in a; angled, frowning brows in b.
- Back foot: the shoe is loosely attached and points down-left in a; it is attached cleanly to the trouser end in b.
- Body lean: the upper body leans forward more in a; it is more upright in b.
- Legs: the stride is slightly wider and lower in a; b's legs are a little shorter, with a clearer bend at the front knee.
- Head size: the head looks slightly larger relative to the body in b.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: The head is a large ball on a thin neck, which is acceptable for the style. The torso is short and the legs are very long. The front arm is long and rigid. The back-shoulder sleeve bulges. The back shoe is poorly joined to the leg, and the front shoe is fine.
- b: The head and neck are fine. The shoulders and arms are better proportioned, with a fist on the front hand. The legs are still long against the short torso, but both shoes are joined properly to the trouser ends.

## 6. Pose
- a: The legs read as a running stride. The straight forward arm makes the upper body stiff, more like reaching for something than running.
- b: Reads as a natural running pose: arms bent and swinging in opposition, the front fist forward, a long stride.

## 7. Better drawn
b. Its bent arms and fist make a convincing running action, and both feet are cleanly attached. The only drawback is the frowning brows.
