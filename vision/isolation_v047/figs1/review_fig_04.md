# Pair 04

## Who and what
An older man (grey hair, grey moustache, blue flat cap, brown waistcoat over a cream shirt, dark trousers, brown shoes) walking to the right carrying a cardboard box in front of him with both hands under it. Both versions read the same way.

## Faults in a
- Face: the body is in side view but the face shows two eyes, as if turned towards the viewer, which looks slightly mismatched with the body.
- Legs: the trousers are very long and wide; the legs look too long for the short upper body.
- Waist: the trouser waistband is a thick dark band that looks like a separate tube around the waist.
- Hands: only one hand is clearly visible under the box; the other arm disappears behind the box.

## Faults in b
- Face: the eye, moustache and mouth sit on the very edge of the head; the moustache runs over the face outline and looks stuck on.
- Legs: as in a, the trousers are very long and wide compared with the upper body.
- Waist: the waistband is an even more prominent separate tube/belt shape with a visible gap above it.
- Hands: as in a, only one hand is clearly shown under the box.
- Feet: the shoes are flat ovals lying on the ground, the back one looks detached from the trouser leg.

## Differences
- Face: a shows two eyes and a three-quarter face; b shows one eye in profile.
- Moustache: in b it is on the edge of the head; in a it is inside the face.
- Head: a's head is a little larger and rounder.
- Waistband: more pronounced and tube-like in b.
- Box: in b the box sits slightly lower and closer to the chest.
- Shoes: a's back shoe is angled up on its toe; b's shoes lie flatter.
- Figure: b is a little narrower overall.

## Anatomy
- a: head and neck fine; shoulders and arms fine (sleeve shape good); hand under box acceptable; hips marked by a thick waistband; legs long and heavy; feet reasonable.
- b: head in profile with features crowded on the edge; arms fine; hips with a thick separate-looking waistband; legs long and heavy; feet flat, back foot loosely joined.

## Pose
- a: reads naturally as walking while carrying a box; arms bent under the box look right.
- b: also reads as walking with a box; equally natural, though flatter feet make the step look a little less grounded.

## Better drawn
a, slightly. The face is clearer and the feet look more grounded; b's features are crowded on the edge of the head and its waistband looks more like a separate tube.
