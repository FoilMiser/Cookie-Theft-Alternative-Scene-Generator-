# Review: fig 07

## 1. Who and what
A child (probably a girl) with short dark curly hair, in a yellow sleeveless top, orange shorts and blue shoes, walking to the right and pointing ahead with an outstretched arm. Both versions read the same. In a the face is more in profile; in b it is turned toward the viewer.

## 2. Faults in a
- Shorts: very puffy and balloon-like, almost like bloomers, and the leg below the hem is much thinner.
- Pointing hand: the hand tapers to a single point with no fingers, so the "point" is vague.
- Back arm: short, ending in a small stubby hand.
- Face: only one eye shows and the mouth sits at the very edge of the head, so the profile looks squashed.
- Neck: thin, with the large head sitting on it like a ball.

## 3. Faults in b
- Head: turned almost to face the viewer while the body walks in profile, which is a slightly odd twist.
- Pointing hand: again a tapered point without fingers.
- Back arm: short, with a small stubby hand.
- Neck: thin.

## 4. Differences
- Face: in a it is in profile, with one eye and the mouth at the edge; in b it is three-quarter or near frontal, with two eyes, eyebrows and a central mouth.
- Shorts: puffy and rounded in a; squarer and less puffy in b.
- Hair: the curls sit slightly differently, a little larger on top in b.
- Back arm: very slightly longer and more visible in b.
- Feet: the back shoe in b is angled more steeply (toe down).
- Legs: in b they look slightly thicker at the calves.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: The head is large on a thin neck, which is acceptable for a child. The shoulder joint for the raised arm is fine. The back arm is short. The shorts are over-puffed, so the legs look thin below them. The feet are flat ovals and attached.
- b: The head and neck are the same. The shoulders and arms are similar, with a slightly better back arm. The hips and shorts are better proportioned. The legs and feet are fine.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads naturally as walking while pointing ahead. The stride and the raised arm work.
- b: Also reads as walking and pointing. The face turned to the viewer makes the head look a little twisted against the body, but it is still natural.

## 7. Better drawn
b. The shorts are better shaped and the face is clearer and more expressive, though the head is turned somewhat toward the viewer.
