# Review: fig 09

## 1. Who and what
A woman (dark hair in a low ponytail, yellow short-sleeved dress, red flat shoes) sitting with her knees bent, reading an open red book held in both hands. Both versions read the same; in b she seems to wear small round glasses.

## 2. Faults in a
- Dress/lap: the skirt is a tight tube over the thighs with a turned-up cuff at the knee, so it reads more like shorts or rolled trousers than a dress hem.
- Hips/seat: the back of the seat is a long flat diagonal with no sitting surface, so she looks as if she floats on air (no chair is expected here, but the body does not suggest one either).
- Lower legs: the shins are very long and plain tubes compared with the short thighs.
- Near arm: the upper arm at the shoulder is a round ball shape, joined like a puppet's.

## 3. Faults in b
- Dress/lap: the skirt hangs down below the thighs as a rounded sack behind the knee, while the thighs come out from under it as separate tubes; the skirt and the legs do not seem to belong together.
- Knees: the knees are drawn as round bumps sitting on top of the skirt edge, slightly detached-looking.
- Lower legs: same very long, plain shins as in a.
- Near arm: same ball-shaped shoulder joint as in a.
- Face: the round glasses lens is small and sits at the very edge of the face, easy to read as a mark rather than glasses.

## 4. Differences
- Skirt: a is narrow and fitted over the thighs, ending in a cuffed hem at the knee; b is fuller, with a bulge hanging below the thighs and the hem shown behind the knees.
- Dress body: a has a fold line across the hip; b has a waist line under the bust and a plain bodice.
- Face: b shows a small round glasses lens at the eye; a has no glasses, just the eye with a lash.
- Knees: in b the knees are separate round shapes over the skirt edge; in a they are covered by the cuffed hem.
- Thighs: in b more bare thigh is visible between skirt and knee.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head, neck and shoulders fine; arms and hands holding the book join well apart from the ball-like near shoulder; hips flat-backed; shins long relative to thighs; feet fine.
- b: head, neck, shoulders and hands as a; hips and thighs oddly joined because the skirt bulges below the thigh line; knees look stuck on; shins long; feet fine.

## 6. Pose
- a: reads naturally as sitting and reading, head bent toward the book, both hands holding it; slightly stiff because the shins are vertical and parallel.
- b: same posture and reads as sitting and reading, but the hanging skirt makes the lap look heavier and the seated line less clear.

## 7. Better drawn
a. The lap and legs read more cleanly as one seated body in a; in b the sagging skirt and stuck-on knees make the lower half look confused.
