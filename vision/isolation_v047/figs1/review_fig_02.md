# Review: fig 02

## 1. Who and what
A young girl (very large head, short dark hair, pink sleeveless dress, yellow shoes) standing and holding a yellow balloon on a string up in one hand. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Head/neck: there is no neck; the large head sits directly on the shoulders.
- Hair: a small dark dot on the side of the head near the ear reads as a stray mark.
- Holding hand: the hand is a round blob with no fingers around the string.
- Far arm: the hanging arm is very thin and partly hidden behind the dress, with the hand barely visible at the hip.
- Legs: very thin and straight, like sticks under the dress.

## 3. Faults in b
- Head/neck: no neck, same as a.
- Holding hand: round blob gripping the string, same as a.
- Face: the nose sticks out past the face outline as a bump, giving a half-profile nose on a front-facing face.
- Far arm: thin, hanging down beside the dress with a small fist at the hem.
- Legs: thin, though a little fuller than in a.

## 4. Differences
- Dress: a has a waist line across the dress; b has no waist line and a slightly wider skirt.
- Face: in b the nose pokes out past the edge of the face and the mouth is an open red smile; in a the nose is inside the face and the mouth is drawn as red lips.
- Far arm/hand: in a the hand is at hip level partly behind the dress; in b the arm is more visible and the fist sits lower, near the hem.
- Hair: a has a dot mark near the ear; b has a curled lock line on top instead.
- Legs: b's legs are slightly thicker; b's stance is slightly wider.
- Overall: b is drawn slightly wider/bigger in the frame.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head very large for a child style (acceptable) but no neck; shoulders and raised arm join fine; hand a blob; hips hidden by dress; legs too thin; feet fine.
- b: same head and missing neck; arm joins fine; hand a blob; legs thin but less stick-like; feet fine; nose protrudes oddly from the face outline.

## 6. Pose
- a: natural way to hold a balloon, arm bent across the chest with the string going up; a little stiff, body upright and legs straight.
- b: the same natural pose, equally slightly stiff.

## 7. Better drawn
b, narrowly. The legs and hanging arm are less stick-thin and there is no stray mark on the hair, though the protruding nose is a small new oddity.
