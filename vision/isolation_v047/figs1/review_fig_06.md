# Review: fig 06

## 1. Who and what
An older man (grey hair, grey moustache, blue flat cap, brown waistcoat over a cream long-sleeved shirt, dark trousers, brown shoes) standing and raking with a long wooden rake held in both hands, rake head at his feet. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Waistcoat front: the shirt shows as a narrow cream strip down the middle with no collar or V, so the waistcoat looks like one solid block with a stripe.
- Waist: a dark band sits between waistcoat and trousers, reading as a separate belt piece.
- Trousers: very wide and baggy, legs long; the figure is long below the waist.
- Neck: short stub under a large head.

## 3. Faults in b
- Trousers: same very wide, long trousers.
- Neck: short stub, mostly hidden under the shirt collar area.
- Waistcoat hem: ends in points over the trousers; the join is fine but the trousers start abruptly.

## 4. Differences
- Waistcoat: a has a cream strip down the front with no neckline; b has a V-neck waistcoat showing the cream shirt, with buttons down the front.
- Waist: a has a dark band/belt between waistcoat and trousers; b's waistcoat comes down lower with pointed ends and no separate band.
- Neck: a shows more of a neck stub; b's shirt rises higher around the neck.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head large, neck short; shoulders and arms join well; both hands grip the handle believably; hips hidden by the belt band; legs long and thick in baggy trousers; feet fine.
- b: same as a, with a cleaner torso-to-trouser join because there is no separate band.

## 6. Pose
- a: reads naturally as raking, slightly bent, head down looking at the rake, one hand high and one low on the handle; the legs are straight and a little stiff.
- b: the same natural raking pose with the same slightly stiff legs.

## 7. Better drawn
b. The V-neck waistcoat with buttons reads as real clothing and the waist join is cleaner; in a the front strip and dark band make the torso look blocky.
