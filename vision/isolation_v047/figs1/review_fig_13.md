# Pair 13

## Who and what
A small red bird (like a robin or cardinal: red body, darker red wing, white belly, yellow beak, orange legs) facing right. In a it is bent forward with its head down, pecking at the ground and stepping; in b it stands on one visible leg with its head only slightly lowered, looking down rather than pecking.

## Faults in a
- Eye: the black eye sits on the very bottom edge of the head, right on the outline next to the beak, so it looks misplaced.
- Head: the head is very large compared with the body.
- Tail: the tail is a long, straight, flat rectangle sticking out behind, longer than looks natural.
- Wing: the wing is a large oval laid over the whole body, with a second darker shape above it; looks layered rather than joined.
- Legs: two thin legs angled back with small flat feet; the feet are blunt and look like little sticks rather than toes.
- Edges: the outlines look soft and blurry.

## Faults in b
- Legs: only one leg is shown, set far back under the body, so the bird seems to balance on one leg behind its centre.
- Tail: the same long, flat rectangular tail as in a.
- Wing: the same large oval wing over the body.
- Head: the head is large for the body.
- Edges: the outlines look soft and blurry.

## Differences
- Head position: in a the head is lower and tipped down towards the ground; in b it is higher and level with the body.
- Eye: in a it is at the bottom edge of the head next to the beak; in b it is in the middle of the face, where it belongs.
- Beak: in a it points straight down; in b it points down and forward.
- Legs: a shows two legs in a walking step; b shows one leg standing straight.
- Body: a's body is tilted forward more; b's is more level.

## Anatomy
- a: head too large and pushed down, eye misplaced at the lower edge; body round; wing an oval laid on top; two legs thin with blunt feet; tail too long and stiff.
- b: head large but eye placed properly; body round; wing an oval laid on top; only one leg, set too far back; tail too long and stiff.

## Pose
- a: reads as a bird pecking or searching the ground while walking; the action is clear, though the head looks squashed down.
- b: reads as a bird standing and looking down; calmer, but the single leg set far back makes it look off balance.

## Better drawn
b. Its eye is in the right place and the head reads clearly, while a's eye sits on the outline at the bottom of the head; however a shows the pecking action more clearly.
