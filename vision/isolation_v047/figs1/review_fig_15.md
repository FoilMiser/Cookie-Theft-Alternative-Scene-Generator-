# Pair 15

## 1. Who and what
A white dog with brown spots and brown ears and a blue collar, standing still facing right with its tail straight up. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Back/rump: the brown spots are separate outlined blobs that bulge out past the body outline (on the back and on the rump), so they look like lumps stuck on rather than markings.
- Belly/back-leg join: a small brown shape with white tooth-like marks hangs under the belly next to the far back leg. It looks like a detached object or a mistake.
- Far legs: the far legs are flat mid-grey, so they read as separate grey posts rather than the same dog's legs in shadow.
- Rump: a short stray vertical line on the rump.
- Front legs: straight, stiff tubes with no elbow or wrist.
- Tail: a very long, thin, rigid spike pointing straight up.
- Face: a small mark at the right edge of the face (a second eye peeking out) looks like a stray stroke.

## 3. Faults in b
- Rump: the same short stray vertical line on the rump.
- Front legs: the same straight, stiff tubes.
- Tail: the same long, thin, rigid tail.
- Face: the same small mark at the right edge of the face.
- Far legs: the pale grey shading is soft; they still look a little like separate posts but much less than in a.

## 4. Differences
- In a the brown spots are outlined and bulge out of the body edge; in b they are flat patches inside the body.
- a has a brown, tooth-marked shape under the belly; b has none.
- The far legs are a darker, flatter grey in a and a lighter grey in b.
- The spot shapes differ a little (b's rump spot is smaller and lies inside the outline).
- The head, collar, ears, tail and leg positions are the same.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: the head is large and round, and the ears and collar are fine. The body is short and boxy. The legs are long, straight columns of even thickness with no joints. The tail is long and too thin. The spots look like lumps added onto the body, and the shape under the belly looks detached or oddly joined.
- b: the same head, body, legs and tail, with the spots properly inside the body. The legs are still jointless columns and the tail is too thin, but nothing looks detached.

## 6. Pose
- a: a plain standing pose, stiff because all four legs are straight posts. The lumps and the belly shape add clutter.
- b: the same standing pose, stiff for the same reason, but it reads cleanly as a dog standing.

## 7. Better drawn
b. The spots are proper markings rather than lumps, and the odd brown shape under the belly is gone.
