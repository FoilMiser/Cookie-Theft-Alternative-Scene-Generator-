# Pair 03

## Who and what
A young woman (dark hair to the shoulders, yellow short-sleeved dress, red flat shoes) walking to the right with both forearms held forward at waist height and the hands together, as if holding something small in front of her. Both versions read the same way.

## Faults in a
- Hands: the two hands are bunched into one lump at the front with no clear fingers, so it is hard to tell whether she is holding something or clasping her hands.
- Arms: both forearms come out of the dress at the same point and overlap, so the near and far arm are hard to tell apart.
- Face: the eye and the open red mouth sit on the right edge of the head, and the mouth runs over the outline of the face, which looks slightly unfinished.
- Back foot: the back shoe is tipped steeply onto its toe and looks small and pointed compared with the front shoe.

## Faults in b
- Neck and chin: under the face there is an extra rounded skin bump between the chin and the dress collar; the neck looks like a separate lump stuck under the head.
- Face: the face is very narrow; the eye, nose and small red mouth are crowded onto the right edge of the head and the mouth sits partly on the outline.
- Hands: as in a, the hands are one lump in front with no clear fingers.
- Dress hem: the lower edge of the dress at the front has a small notch or step, which looks like a drawing error.
- Shoes: both shoes are flat, wide ovals lying almost flat on the ground, which looks more like slippers than shoes on a walking foot.

## Differences
- Head: a's head is wider and rounder with the face turned a little more towards the viewer; b's head is narrower, more in profile.
- Mouth: a has a larger, open, smiling red mouth; b has a smaller red mouth.
- Neck: a shows a short neck hidden mostly by the collar; b shows a visible rounded neck/chin bump.
- Ear: a shows a small ear in the hair; b shows a similar ear slightly lower.
- Dress: b's hem has a small notch at the front; a's hem is smooth.
- Legs: b's legs are a little longer and the stride a little wider.
- Shoes: a's back shoe is tipped up on its toe; in b both shoes lie flatter and look wider.
- Figure: b is slightly narrower overall.

## Anatomy
- a: head and neck join reasonably; shoulders and upper arms fine; forearms short and hands merged into one blob; hips hidden by dress; legs of plausible length and thickness; back foot small and pointed.
- b: head narrow, with an oddly joined neck bump under the chin; arms similar to a with merged hands; legs slightly long; feet flat and wide like ovals.

## Pose
- a: reads as a natural walk with a clear step; the arms held forward are a bit stiff (both arms at the same height, no swing), but fit carrying something small.
- b: also reads as walking, with a slightly wider stride; arms equally stiff; the flat-lying shoes make the step look less grounded than in a.

## Better drawn
a. Its head and neck join cleanly and the face is clearer, whereas b has an odd neck bump, a crowded narrow face and a notched hem.
