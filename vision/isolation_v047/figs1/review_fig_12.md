# Pair 12

## 1. Who and what
A man with short brown hair and a small brown moustache, in a red T-shirt and blue trousers, sitting (seat not shown) and reading an open red book held in both hands. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Face: a near-profile face with one large eye and a sliver of a second eye. The moustache sits low at the side of the face and looks stuck on.
- Arms: the short sleeves are puffy, and the forearms come out of them thin and flat, crossing in front of the body to the book like straps.
- Hands: the hands are small rounded blobs on the book.
- Legs: the shins are long and hang straight down. The knees sit low, so the lower legs look longer than the thighs.
- Feet: the shoes are flat dark ovals, with little sense of a foot shape.
- Torso: short and wide compared with the long legs.

## 3. Faults in b
- Face: the moustache is the same low, stuck-on shape.
- Arms: the same puffy sleeves and thin, strap-like forearms.
- Hands: the same small blobs.
- Legs: the same long shins and low knees.
- Feet: the same flat ovals, slightly more shoe-shaped.
- Torso: the same short, wide torso.

## 4. Differences
- a shows the face nearly in profile with one large eye and a part eye. b shows a three-quarter face with two full eyes, each with a brow line.
- The head in b is turned slightly more toward the viewer.
- The shoes in b are a little more shoe-shaped (flatter soles) than a's ovals.
- The body, arms, book and legs are otherwise the same.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: the head is large, with a short neck that is fine. The shoulders are puffy. The forearms are thin, and the hands are small blobs. The hips are fine for sitting. The lower legs are too long for the thighs. The feet are ovals. Nothing is detached.
- b: the same as a: thin forearms, long lower legs and simple feet. Nothing is detached.

## 6. Pose
- a: sitting and reading reads clearly, with the head bent toward the book. It is a little stiff because the legs hang straight down together like a seated doll.
- b: the same pose and the same stiffness. The two eyes looking down make the reading a bit clearer.

## 7. Better drawn
b, by a small margin. The two-eyed face reads more clearly as looking at the book, and the shoes are slightly better shaped. Otherwise there is no difference.
