# Review: fig 16

## 1. Who and what
An older woman (grey hair in a bun, round dark glasses, green cardigan, grey-blue skirt, brown shoes) standing and waving with one arm raised high. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Cardigan front: the opening is a single cream strip with buttons, set off to one side and stopping halfway down, like a stripe stuck on rather than a garment opening.
- Neck/collar: there is no collar or neckline; the neck stub goes straight into a high round top.
- Glasses: the far lens sticks out past the edge of the face.
- Legs: long and thin compared with the short skirt and body.

## 3. Faults in b
- Glasses: the far lens also extends to the face edge, slightly outside it.
- Legs: long and thin, same as a.
- Raised hand: a mitten shape with a thumb, slightly crude (also true in a).

## 4. Differences
- Cardigan: a has a high round neck with an off-centre cream button strip; b has a V-neck cardigan over a cream blouse, with a button line down the middle.
- Neck: in a a short neck is visible; in b the neck is mostly covered by the blouse collar area.
- Mouth: b's mouth is a wider open smile; a's is smaller.
- Glasses: in a the glasses sit a little higher and the far lens sticks out further past the face.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head and neck fine; raised arm joins well at the shoulder; lowered arm with a fist fine; hands simple; hips and skirt fine; legs long and thin; feet fine.
- b: same as a; head and neck join more naturally because of the V-neck; legs long and thin.

## 6. Pose
- a: reads naturally as a cheerful wave with the arm straight up; the rest of the body is upright and a little stiff.
- b: the same natural wave, equally slightly stiff in the body.

## 7. Better drawn
b. The V-neck cardigan over a blouse reads as real clothing, while a's off-centre cream strip looks like a pasted-on stripe.
