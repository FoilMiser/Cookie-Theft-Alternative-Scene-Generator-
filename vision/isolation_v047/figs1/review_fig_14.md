# Review: fig 14

## 1. Who and what
A boy or young man in a red cap, a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes, standing on one leg and kicking the other leg forward to the right (as if kicking a ball, which is not shown), arms out for balance. Both versions read the same. In a he looks happy, with an open smiling mouth; in b the face is a plain profile.

## 2. Faults in a
- Back arm: it comes out from behind the torso at chest height with no visible shoulder, so it looks stuck onto the back.
- Hood: it shows as a round lump behind the neck that looks like a separate ball.
- Kicking leg: very long compared with the short torso.
- Hands: simple mitten shapes.
- Standing foot: the white shoe is small and flat under a long bent leg.

## 3. Faults in b
- Back arm: the same; it comes out from the back of the hoodie with no shoulder.
- Hood: the same round lump behind the neck.
- Kicking leg: very long compared with the torso.
- Shoes: both white shoes are flat, puffy blobs with little shoe shape.
- Face: one eye and a tiny red mouth crammed at the edge of the head, so the expression is hard to read.

## 4. Differences
- Face: two eyes and a wide open smiling mouth (three-quarter view) in a; one eye and a small mouth at the edge (profile) in b.
- Shoes: in a they have a visible sole line and look like trainers; in b they are flatter, rounder blobs.
- Hoodie front: a shows the drawstrings and a zip line more clearly; in b only a faint line shows.
- Cap: the brim angle differs slightly and is a little more raised in b.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: The head and neck are fine. The far shoulder is missing, so the back arm looks attached to the back. The near arm is bent and fine. The hands are mittens. The hips are fine. The legs are long, the kicking leg especially. The feet are attached and shaped like shoes.
- b: The same as a for the head, arms and legs. The feet are less well shaped.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads as a natural, lively kick, with the arms out for balance and the standing knee bent. The smiling face adds to it.
- b: Reads as the same kick. The blank profile face makes it look less lively but not stiff.

## 7. Better drawn
a. The face is clearer and more expressive and the shoes look like real trainers. The pose and anatomy are otherwise the same in both.
