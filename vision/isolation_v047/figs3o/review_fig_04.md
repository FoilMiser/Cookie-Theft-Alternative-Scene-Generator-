# Review: fig 04

## Who and what
A small red bird (robin-like, cream belly, yellow beak) bending forward as if pecking at the ground. In a it is only slightly tipped, so it reads more as standing and looking down; in b it is tipped steeply head-down, so it reads clearly as pecking.

## Faults in a
- Head: there are two dark dots on the side of the head, a larger grey oval and a smaller black dot just behind the beak; it reads as two eyes on one side, or an eye plus a stray mark.
- Feet: the toes point backwards (towards the tail), opposite to the way the bird faces.
- Legs: very thin straight sticks, set close together, with blunt flat feet that look like little blocks.
- Far wing: shows only as a small dark-red cap on top of the back, which looks like a patch rather than a wing.

## Faults in b
- Legs: the near leg is almost hidden behind the belly and only a short stub and foot show; the far leg is short, so the bird looks like it has barely any legs.
- Feet: the toes point backwards (towards the tail), and are small flat stubs.
- Far wing: rises high behind the head as a big dark-red shape, almost like a hood or ear behind the head.
- Body: tilted so far forward that the beak hangs nearly as low as the feet; the bird looks close to toppling over its beak.

## Differences
- Body tilt: a is tipped only slightly forward; b is tipped steeply, head down.
- Head position: in a the head is level with the body; in b it is low and in front of the body.
- Eyes: a has two dark dots (grey oval plus black dot); b has one black eye with a white highlight.
- Beak: in a it points down and forward; in b it points almost straight down.
- Far wing: in a a small cap on the back; in b a large shape rising behind the head.
- Near wing: in a a wide, near-horizontal oval; in b a taller, steeply slanted oval.
- Legs: in a both legs are clearly visible and fairly long; in b they are short and mostly hidden under the belly.
- Belly: in a a broad cream patch; in b a narrow cream sliver.
- Tail: in a it sticks up to the upper left at a gentle slope; in b it sticks up more steeply.

## Anatomy
- a: Head, body and tail join well; wing sits sensibly on the body. Legs are thin sticks, a bit long and stiff, with backward-pointing block feet. Head has an extra dark dot that confuses the face.
- b: Head, body and tail join well, though the head is pushed far down and forward. Legs are too short and largely hidden; feet are stubs pointing backwards. Far wing is oversized and sits behind the head.

## Pose
- a: Reads as a bird standing and leaning a little to look down; a mild, natural pose, but not clearly pecking.
- b: Reads clearly as a bird pecking at the ground, which is natural for a bird, though the extreme tilt and missing legs make it look top-heavy.

## Better drawn
b. It reads more clearly as pecking and has a single clean eye, whereas a has a confusing second dot on the face; b's short, hidden legs are its main weakness.
