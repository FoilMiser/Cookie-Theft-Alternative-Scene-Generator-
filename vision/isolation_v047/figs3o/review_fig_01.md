# Review: fig 01

## Who and what
A small blue bird (white belly, yellow beak, orange legs) bending down as if pecking at the ground. In a it is tipped steeply head-down; in b it is more upright, leaning forward with the head lowered.

## Faults in a
- Legs: both legs are short and come out at the back/rear of the body, so the bird looks as if it would fall forward onto its head.
- Feet: small flat stubs, barely shaped.
- Wing: the near wing is a big dark oval standing almost vertical, covering much of the body; it looks pasted on.
- Far wing: a dark band shows above and behind the head and reads like a hood or separate shape.
- Head: very large and round, bigger than half the body, placed low at the front.
- Tail: a flat, square-ended slab pointing up-left, stiff.

## Faults in b
- Eye: there are two dark dots on the side of the head, a larger grey one and a smaller black one next to it; it looks like two eyes on one side or an eye plus a stray spot.
- Legs: long, thin and straight, placed close together, a bit stick-like.
- Feet: small flat stubs.
- Wing: large dark oval, pasted-on look.
- Back: a narrow dark strip on top (far wing) looks like a stray band.
- Tail: flat, square-ended slab, stiff.

## Differences
- Body angle: a is tilted steeply forward (head at the bottom, tail at the top); b is tilted only moderately.
- Head position: in a the head is low, at the bottom right; in b it is at the front, level with the body's top half.
- Eyes: a has one black eye with a highlight; b has two dots (grey and black).
- Beak: in a it points straight down; in b it points down-right.
- Legs: in a they are short and at the rear; in b they are longer, thinner and directly under the belly.
- Wing: in a the wing oval is near-vertical and narrow; in b it is horizontal and wider.
- Far wing: in a it shows as a large dark band behind the head; in b as a small strip on the back.
- White belly: larger and more visible in b.

## Anatomy
- a: head oversized and set low; body and tail joined plausibly; legs too short and too far back for the tilt; wing oversized.
- b: head and body in reasonable proportion; legs somewhat too long and thin but correctly under the body; the doubled eye marking on the head is wrong.

## Pose
- a: reads strongly as pecking, but awkward, as the legs are behind the body's weight and the bird looks like it is toppling forward.
- b: balanced and natural for a bird leaning forward to look at or peck the ground, though a little less obviously pecking than a.

## Better drawn
b, because the body is balanced over its legs and the proportions are better; its one clear fault is the double dot on the head, while a looks off balance with stubby rear legs.
