# Review: fig 02

## Who and what
A small blue bird (cream belly, yellow beak) leaning forward with its beak down, as if pecking or looking at the ground. In a it is nearly level and reads more as standing; in b it is tipped head-down and reads more clearly as pecking.

## Faults in a
- Legs: only one leg is shown; the bird looks like it is standing on a single leg.
- Foot: the toes point backwards (towards the tail), and are a flat block.
- Wing: a very large dark-blue oval covering most of the body, sitting like a lid or shell on the back.
- Tail: a long, straight flat paddle that looks stiff and slightly too long.

## Faults in b
- Legs: only one short leg shows, partly hidden under the belly; the bird looks almost legless.
- Foot: small flat stub with toes pointing backwards.
- Wing: the dark-blue oval is huge, tilted, and covers most of the body up to the back of the head.
- Back: a small notch or kink in the outline where the tail meets the back.
- Tail: long, straight and stiff, sticking out horizontally while the body is tipped down, so it looks bent at the join.

## Differences
- Body tilt: a is nearly horizontal; b is tipped forward with the head low.
- Head position: in a the head is at the front, level with the back; in b it is lower and further forward.
- Eye: in a a grey dot; in b a black dot set lower, nearer the beak.
- Beak: in a it points forward and slightly down; in b it points further down.
- Wing: in a a horizontal oval; in b a larger, steeply tilted oval reaching towards the head.
- Leg: in a one fairly long leg is visible; in b one shorter leg, partly hidden.
- Belly: in a a medium cream patch; in b a similar patch set lower and further back.
- Back outline: in b there is a small notch near the tail join that a does not have.

## Anatomy
- a: Head, body and tail join smoothly. Wing is oversized but sits on the body. Only one leg, thin, with a backward-pointing block foot.
- b: Head joins well, but the tail meets the body with a slight kink. Wing is oversized and tilted. Only one leg, too short and partly hidden; foot is a stub pointing backwards.

## Pose
- a: Reads as a bird standing and leaning slightly to look down; fairly natural but stiff, and the single leg makes it look balanced oddly.
- b: Reads more clearly as a bird pecking the ground, but it looks top-heavy and close to tipping over because the leg is short and under the back half of the body.

## Better drawn
a. It is cleaner, with a visible leg and smooth outlines; b reads more strongly as pecking but has a stubby leg and a kink at the tail join.
