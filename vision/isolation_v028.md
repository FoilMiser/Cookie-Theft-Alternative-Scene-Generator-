# v0.28 isolation bench

Each visual was forced alone into a picture, cut out with little else around it and read blind by fresh readers (the method of `isolation_v023.md`). Round 1 was eleven readers and 29 cut-outs, up to three to a reader, no reader seeing two versions of one thing (`isolation_v028/round1`; `map.json` says which cut-out is which case). Rounds 2 and 3 were three readers each, on five and on three cut-outs (`isolation_v028/round2`, `round3`, named by case). The variants are behind `TPDB_BROW`, `TPDB_BLUSH` and `TPDB_BEARD` in the cast and `draw.SPLAT28`, `draw.SEESAW_IN28` and `library.SHADOW28` in the scene; `vision/isolation_bench.py` has the cases.

## The shadow (`library.SHADOW28`)

The commonest named oddity that is plain to fix: the oval came from the figure's box, which takes in arms, tools and the depth of the body, and it was centred on the ground point, not the feet. Now a person on their feet casts it from the feet, and anything else from the columns the body fills, under its lowest part. Drawn at the old centre, the shadow vanished under the sleeping dog, which reaches below its ground point: looked at and fixed before any reader saw it.

| Case | Drawing | What the readers said about the shadow |
|---|---|---|
| `shdO_1` to `_4` | From the box | "The oval shadow is much wider than the dog and looks like a separate disc or a flat patch on the grass"; "a large flat oval lying to his right and crossing onto the path"; "sits below and slightly apart from the figure"; "a shadow that does not line up tightly under him" |
| `shdB_1` to `_4` | From the footprint | Nothing on any of the four |

**Chosen: the footprint.** Four remarks on four cut-outs against none on four.

"A pink hat or a pink frisbee", "a flying saucer" in v0.27. A flat blob had read as "a pink bag or a shoe" in v0.10, so these have lobes, flecks thrown clear of them and the sprinkles still on.

|---|---|---|
| `splO_1`, `_2` | The scoop in a puddle | "A hat, a flying-saucer shape or a small pink sombrero"; "a small flying saucer or a hat lying on the ground" |
| `splA_1`, `_2` | **A**: a flat splat, no dome | "A pink, splattered puddle with coloured sprinkles"; "a pink splat with coloured sprinkles and a few small pink droplets around it, which looks like the dropped ice cream" |

Round 2 (`dogB_1`, `_2`): "a pink, splattered puddle with coloured sprinkles" and "apparently licking it up", though "the mouth is not touching the splat" once.

## The ball and its branch

"A blue planet-like sphere", with "a small bare branch drawn on top of the crown". Two white bands across the ball looked like a target, and were dropped before any reader saw them. The ball is now a beach ball of blue and white panels.

| Case | Drawing | What the readers said |
|---|---|---|
| `balB_1`, `_2`, `brbB_1` (round 1) | The beach ball; leaves over both ends of the branch | "A blue-and-white striped beach ball". But the branch: "no visible connection to the trunk", "does not visibly connect to the trunk", "looks a little like it is floating inside the foliage", three of three |
| `lmbB_1`, `_2`, `_4` (round 2) | A limb from the top of the trunk to the branch | "Angles out from the top of the trunk"; "sticks out to the left from a tree trunk": connected. New: "a sharp, almost right-angle join", "a single bent pipe" |

**Chosen: the beach ball, on a limb.** A second arm then makes the top of the trunk a fork, its tip under leaves, for the bent pipe. The fork was not read on the bench and goes to the design tests. A bird on a branch gets the same limb.

## The faces (`TPDB_BROW`, `TPDB_BLUSH`)

"Dark marks around her nose and mouth", "a pink smudge on the cheek, which could be blush or ice cream". Closed eyes drew two dark lines to each eye, one of them a brow. No brows over closed eyes now, and a smaller blush nearer the eye.

| Case | Drawing | What the readers said about the face |
|---|---|---|
| `facO_1`, `_2` | As they were | "Pink marks near them, which could be blush on the cheeks or smile marks"; "some dark marks around the mouth and chin that could be a moustache, stubble, or just the eating expression" |
| `facB_1` to `_3` | No brows, a smaller blush | No marks and no blush remarked on, though "the face is cluttered around the mouth", where the hand and the scoop meet it |

**Chosen: no brows and the smaller blush**, on a small sample. Closed eyes are still sometimes "squinting or wincing", which they were before.

## The beard (`TPDB_BEARD`)

"A thin dark strap along the jaw", "a chin strap" in v0.27, on a beard that had already been cut back from the ear.

| Case | Drawing | What the readers said about it |
|---|---|---|
| `brdO_1` | v0.27 | "A dark band that could also be read as a scarf or strap across the chin" |
| `brdB_1`, `_2` | **C**: fuller on the cheek | "A dark band ... could read as a mask or scarf covering his mouth", and in the design tests "a dark band or patch across his lower face that could also be a mask" |
| `brdD_1` to `_3` (round 3) | **D**: a goatee and moustache | "A small moustache"; nothing; "a small beard". No strap and no mask |

**Chosen: D.** A fuller beard made it worse, so the answer was less of it. He will be called bearded less often, and no item depends on it.
