# iso_04

## What I see
An elderly woman stands on green grass in front of a wooden plank fence that has a horizontal rail. She has gray hair in a bun and glasses, and she wears a teal long-sleeved top, a knee-length gray skirt and brown shoes. She stretches one arm out to the left and holds a round, dark gray object by a short handle. A blue stream of water pours from its rim, with droplets falling, onto an oval bed of brown soil full of colorful flowers (red, yellow, purple, pink and orange) on green stems with leaves. She seems to be watering the flowers.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The object she holds is the main ambiguity. It looks like a frying pan or saucepan: a round dark pan with a short side handle, with the water pouring over its edge as if from a pot. It could also be read as the sprinkler head (rose) of a watering can, or a shower-type nozzle, but there is no can body or hose attached to it. So reading it as "watering with a pan" is just as plausible as "watering can."
- The water comes out as a single curved stream from the side or bottom of the rim, not as a spray. This fits pouring from a pan better than a watering-can rose.
- There is a white vertical strip down the front of her teal top. It could be a white undershirt or collar showing, a scarf, or an apron strap.
- Only one of her arms is visible, the outstretched one. The other arm is hidden or not drawn, which looks slightly odd for a side view.
- The water stream ends among the flower heads in the middle of the bed. It seems to pass in front of some flowers rather than land on top of them, which is a minor layering quirk.
- The woman's body, legs and feet look properly joined. She stands on a small ground shadow and does not appear to float.
