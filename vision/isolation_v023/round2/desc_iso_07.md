# iso_07

## What I see
An older man in a blue-gray flat cap, white long-sleeved shirt, brown vest and dark trousers sits on a wooden bench on a tiled sidewalk. With his eyes closed and pink cheeks, he looks happy as he eats or licks a pink ice cream cone. A tan-and-white dog with a red bandana sits upright in front of the bench, raising a front paw and looking up at the man, apparently begging for the ice cream. Behind them are a large shop window or glass wall and a window box of yellow and pink flowers. In the bottom-right corner, a gray bike rack and part of a red bicycle are cut off by the frame.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The dog's body is oddly proportioned. It is upright like a begging or sitting dog, but its lower body stretches long and flat to the right, ending in a white hind foot far from the rest of the body. This makes it look more like a kangaroo, a stretched-out figure, or a dog half-lying and half-sitting. Its legs and paws (one raised, one on the ground at the left, one at the far right) are hard to match to the body.
- Two readings of the dog: it is begging for the ice cream, or it is just sitting and looking at the man.
- The man's eyes are closed, so he could be savoring the ice cream or simply smiling. He holds the cone near his mouth, and it is unclear whether he is licking it or about to.
- The man's free arm (white sleeve) hangs down toward his lap, and his hand there is not clearly visible.
- The bike and rack in the corner are cropped heavily. Only a gray loop, a black tire edge and a red frame show, so they could be read as a bike rack alone or as a parked bicycle.
- The flower box seems to float at the bottom of the window, with no clear support or connection to the ground.
