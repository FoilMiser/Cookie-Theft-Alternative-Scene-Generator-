# iso_03

## What I see
A girl or young woman with brown hair in a ponytail (with a red hair tie) stands on green grass in front of a tan wooden plank fence. She wears a blue hooded top, dark gray trousers and white sneakers. She faces right with one arm stretched out, holding a dark handle attached to a round gray head. A blue stream of water arcs out of the gray head and down into an oval bed of brown soil. Small water droplets land among the flowers. The bed is full of flowers in yellow, orange, purple, red and pink, so she is watering the flowers.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The object she holds is only a round gray disc on a short dark handle. There is no can body and no hose. It could be read as the sprinkler head (rose) of a watering can with the can itself missing, as a hose spray nozzle with no hose, or even as a frying pan or magnifying glass that water is somehow pouring from. The water clearly says watering, but the tool itself looks incomplete or odd.
- The water comes out of the side or edge of the disc as one solid stream, not a spray from the face of the rose, which looks a little unnatural.
- Only one arm is visible. The other arm is not drawn or is hidden behind her body, so the figure looks one-armed at a glance.
- Her legs are long and thin compared with a short torso. This could be stylized or could look slightly stretched.
- The water stream passes over or in front of the pink flower in the middle of the bed. It reads as being in front of the flower, but it could look like it passes through it.
- Her face is mostly hidden by her hair, and her expression is hard to read. She seems to be looking toward the flowers.
