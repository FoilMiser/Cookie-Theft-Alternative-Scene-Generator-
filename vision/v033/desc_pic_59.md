# pic_59

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house (red roof, blue door) on the left, a wooden fence along the back and a gate and path on the right. A young woman with long dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers paints the fence blue with a roller on a long pole. One section is already blue, and her paint can is on the grass behind her. In the middle of the lawn a hose feeds a sprinkler that sprays arcs of water straight onto a cardboard box left on the grass. The box is getting soaked and is sitting in a puddle. On the path at the right, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and round glasses holds a giant silver fork, the size of a rake or broom, like a dinner fork. She uses it to sweep a pile of dirt towards a red dustpan, raising a small dust cloud. The giant fork is strange, and the box being soaked by the sprinkler looks like a mishap.
