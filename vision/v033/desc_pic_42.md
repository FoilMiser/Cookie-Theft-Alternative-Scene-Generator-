## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a path to a gate on the right. At the front left, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is watering the flower bed. Strangely, she is pouring the water out of a grey frying pan or saucepan with a long handle, not a watering can. Water streams from the pan in an arc onto the flowers. At the back, near the fence, a small girl in a pink dress flies a yellow kite with a tail of little bows high in the sky. On the right, a man with dark skin, a green shirt and khaki trousers is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. The dog walks ahead of him to the left. Two potted plants stand on the lawn near the gate, and an empty grey wheelbarrow is at the front. The odd thing is the woman using a frying pan to water the plants.
