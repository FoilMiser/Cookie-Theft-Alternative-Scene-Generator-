# Picture 06

## Free description
This is a sunny park or playground with a grey metal railing fence and hedges along the back. On the left stands a slide with a ladder, a red frame and a yellow-and-orange chute, with a small bush beside it and a grey rock in the front-left grass. A green litter bin stands near the fence in the middle. On the right is a red seesaw. An old man in a grey cap, white shirt and brown vest sits on the low end reading an open book. A small girl in a pink dress sits on the high end, up in the air, holding the handle. The man's end rests on the ground and his feet are planted, so the girl is stuck up high. She has a surprised or worried "o" mouth. The man is absorbed in his book and doesn't seem to notice her. A tree stands at the far right.
