A boy in a red cap throws a red ball, shown by a dotted arc, to a man in a red shirt who holds his hands out to catch it. Her paint can and a paint tray of blue paint sit on the grass next to her. A white dog with brown spots has walked through the blue paint: a trail of blue paw prints runs from the paint tray to the dog, which now stands on the path with blue feet. A bearded man in a green shirt points up and to the left, probably at the storm clouds, as though warning that rain is coming. In the front a girl in a purple top digs a hole with a spade, with a heap of soil beside her. A brown dog sits by the house door holding its red lead in its mouth, as if it wants a walk. An orange cat walks along the top of the fence by the tree. The paint is likely to get rained on, and the dog is spreading paint around.
