# Picture 05

## Free description
This is a quiet, sunny back garden with a wooden fence and gate at the back, a tree behind the fence, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a paved path on the left. There is only one person: an old woman with grey hair and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt. She is drinking from a red cup through a straw. She is in a sitting position, with knees bent as if on a chair, but there is no chair, bench or anything else under her. She appears to be sitting on thin air, which is impossible. Around her are two potted plants near the fence, two small bushes, a round flower bed full of colourful flowers, a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow at the front. Nothing else is happening; the garden is otherwise empty and calm.
