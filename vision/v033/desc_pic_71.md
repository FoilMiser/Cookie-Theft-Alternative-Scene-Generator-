# pic_71

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate on the far left, a paved path in front of the gate, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the far left, a person with dark skin and hair in a bun, wearing a green top and tan trousers, holds a giant silver dinner fork. They are using it like a broom to sweep a little pile of dirt into a red dustpan, and a puff of dust rises. Sweeping with a huge fork is strange and impossible. In the centre back, a young man in a red shirt and blue trousers is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle. A rectangular section of the fence is already blue, and a small tin of blue paint sits on the grass behind him. On the right, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers waters a round flower bed with a garden hose. The hose runs back to a hose reel by the house. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands in the front centre, and two potted plants stand near the fence.
