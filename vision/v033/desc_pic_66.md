## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, and a tall wooden fence along the back. There is a wooden gate in the fence on the right, with a paved path leading to it. A woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers stands next to a round flower bed and waters the flowers with a teal watering can. Water sprinkles down onto the flowers. In the tree behind the house, an orange fish sits on a branch, which is impossible. In the front centre, an orange cat is curled up on the grass with its eyes closed, apparently asleep. Near the cat is a red bowl with cat food. There are two potted plants and two round bushes along the fence.
