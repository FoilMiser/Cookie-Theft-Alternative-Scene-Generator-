Inside the pram a baby sleeps with its eyes closed, tucked under a pink spotted blanket. Directly in front of the pram, on the right, is a flower bed of colourful flowers. The pram is very close to it, and if she keeps going she may push into the flowers. She looks ahead with a small smile and seems calm. A grey rock sits on the grass at the bottom near the path. No one else is around.
