## Free description
This is a busy, sunny town square in front of a row of colourful shops with striped awnings. On the left, a blue-and-white beach ball is stuck in a small tree in a round planter. A little girl in a pink dress points up at it. Behind them at the far left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping a pile of autumn leaves with a broom. An older man in a grey cap and brown vest walks past carrying a yellow shopping bag. In the middle, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts jumps in the air with his arms up, smiling. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a bench reading a red book. A sandwich lies next to her on the bench, and a blue bird has landed right beside it, as if about to steal it. At the front, a brown dog with a red neckerchief sits up holding an open newspaper as if reading it, which is impossible. An older woman with grey hair and glasses is watering a box of flowers, but the stream from her watering can curves upward into the air instead of falling onto the flowers, which looks wrong. On the right, a man in a green shirt walks along reading an open book and doesn't seem to notice a cardboard box on the ground in front of him, so he may trip over it. There is also a fruit stall at the right, a lamppost, potted plants in the front corners, and a red bird flying at top left.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Dog with a newspaper: it clearly holds an open newspaper as if reading, which is impossible for a real dog. Maybe the newspaper was dropped and the dog is just pawing at it, but its pose looks like reading.
- Watering stream: the water arcs upward from the can, with drops at the top, rather than falling onto the flowers. One reading is that the water defies gravity (impossible). Another is that it was simply meant to show her watering, drawn oddly. Either way, it does not land on the flowers.
- Blue bird and sandwich: it looks about to steal the sandwich while the woman reads. It could also just be perched there.
- Reading man and box: the box is right in front of him and he is looking at his book, so he looks likely to trip. He might also just walk around it.
- The jumping boy's reason is unclear (playing, cheering, excited).
- The beach ball is wedged in the tree, which is plausible. Apart from the water stream, nothing else is floating or passing through anything.
