## Free description
A sunny park with a pond on the left, a winding sandy path on the right, a tall black lamp post, a bench, trees, a flower bed, a green bin and a grey rock. A man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands at the edge of the pond holding a paper bag. He throws bread crumbs onto the water for a white duck, which swims towards the crumbs, its motion shown by little lines. Further back on the path, an older man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat pours seeds from a paper bag onto the ground. A red bird and a blue bird peck at the seeds. On the right a garden sprinkler fed by a hose sprays arcs of water across the path. One arc lands on a cardboard box sitting on the grass beside the bench, and the box is getting wet, with a puddle underneath. The soaked box looks like a mishap. Otherwise it is a calm scene of people feeding birds.
