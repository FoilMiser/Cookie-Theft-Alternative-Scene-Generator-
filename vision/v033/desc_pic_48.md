## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a blue door and red roof on the left, a tall wooden fence with a gate on the right and a paved path leading to the gate. A woman with dark hair in a ponytail, wearing a purple top and teal trousers, stands near a flower bed on the left. She holds a teal watering can low at her side and seems to be watering the flowers. The water looks strange: it comes out of the spout as a thick curved stream that rises up and to the left over the flowers, with drops at the top, as if the water were shooting upwards rather than pouring down. A clothesline between two wooden posts runs across the middle with a pair of blue jeans and a yellow striped towel or top pegged on it. Two potted plants stand by the fence and an empty grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the front. She is the only person and there are no animals.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water looks unintentionally strange. It flows up from the spout like a fountain or a jet instead of falling down onto the flowers, and the drops sit at the top of the arc. You could read it as water shooting upwards, which is impossible from a tipped watering can. Or you could read it as a stream coming up out of the flower bed towards the can.
- The can is held quite low and close to her body, so it isn't clearly tipped for pouring.
