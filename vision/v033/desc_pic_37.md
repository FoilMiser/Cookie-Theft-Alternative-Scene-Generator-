## Free description
This is a sunny but very windy back garden. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, a wooden fence runs across the back, and a path leads to a wooden gate on the right. White wind lines streak across the sky, and leaves are being blown off the tree behind the house. On a clothesline in the middle, a pair of blue trousers and a yellow-and-red striped towel are blowing out sideways. On the right, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands near the gate with both arms stretched up, grabbing at a red cap that the wind is carrying away above his head. Under a swing frame, a small child in pink sits on a red swing and smiles, with motion lines that show the swing moving. In the front middle, an older man in a grey flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat sits reading a red book. The odd part is that there is nothing under him: no chair, bench or stool, so he seems to be sitting on thin air. A wheelbarrow and a teal watering can sit on the grass nearby.
