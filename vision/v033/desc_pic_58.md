A man in a green shirt and khaki trousers sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket with his legs stretched out. He has a picnic basket beside him and is smiling with his eyes closed. A boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts walks towards the blanket holding a yellow balloon on a string. A wooden ladder stands upright on its own on the grass next to the path, leaning slightly but resting against nothing. That is odd, because a ladder could not stand up like that by itself, and nobody is using it. Otherwise it looks like a relaxed family picnic.
