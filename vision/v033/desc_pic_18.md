# pic_18

## Free description
A sunny but windy back garden. White wind lines streak across the sky and over the fence, and a few leaves are blowing off the tree behind the house. The cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, a wooden fence runs across the back, and a wooden gate with a paved path is on the right. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, stands next to the flower bed pouring water onto the flowers from a frying pan, which is odd because a frying pan is not a watering can. On the right, a young woman or older girl with dark hair, in a purple top and teal trousers, stretches both arms up with a surprised face. Just above her, a red cap is tumbling through the air on the wind, along with a few leaves. It looks as if her cap has just blown off and she is trying to catch it. In the front centre, a small orange cat lies curled up asleep on the grass with its eyes closed, ignoring everything.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The pan could be read as a frying pan or a saucepan. Either way, watering flowers with a pan is odd, and it may be the intended "wrong" element.
- The cap may have blown off the purple-shirted person's head, or it may have come from elsewhere and she is just trying to catch it. Her bare head and raised arms suggest it is hers.
- The person in purple could be an adult woman or a teenage girl.
- Despite the strong wind, the water from the pan falls fairly straight and the elderly woman's hair and skirt are not blown, which is slightly inconsistent.
- The cat could be asleep or just lying still; its closed eyes suggest sleeping.
