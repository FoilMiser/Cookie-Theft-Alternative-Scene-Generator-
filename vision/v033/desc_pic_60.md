# pic_60

## Free description
A back garden under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds. The house (red roof, blue door) is on the right and the gate and path are on the left. Washing hangs on a clothesline between two posts in the middle: a yellow top with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans. With a storm coming, the washing is likely to get wet. A girl in a purple top and teal trousers stands near the path pointing up at the sky with her mouth open, as if noticing the clouds or warning of rain. A girl in a yellow dress stands on the path holding a giant silver fork like a broom, sweeping a small pile of dirt towards a red dustpan. The dustpan seems to be under the pointing girl's feet. A brown dog with a red collar sits by the blue door holding its red lead in its mouth, as if waiting for a walk. An empty wheelbarrow and a hose reel with its hose and yellow nozzle lie on the lawn. There is also a flower bed by the house.
