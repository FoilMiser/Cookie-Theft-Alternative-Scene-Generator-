This is a sunny but windy back garden. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, and a long wooden fence runs across the back with a wooden gate on the right. A paved path leads off towards the bottom right. White wavy lines in the sky and across the middle of the picture show that the wind is blowing. Just above her hands and to the left, a red hat is flying through the air, carried along by the wind lines, with a few small leaves (and a small brown bit) tumbling with it. She looks up with her mouth open, surprised, as if she is grabbing for her hat after the wind blew it off her head. The hat looks like it is about to blow out of her reach. A teal watering can sits on the grass near the path, and there is a colourful flower bed near the house.
