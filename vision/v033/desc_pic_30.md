## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the cream house (red roof, blue door) on the right, a wooden fence along the back, and a gate and paved path on the left. An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, stands in front of the fence painting it blue with a paint roller on a long handle. She has already painted a short section of three boards blue, and she is facing the fence, concentrating on her work. On the ground behind her is a paint tray with blue paint in it and an open tin of blue paint. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is walking away to the left, and its paws are blue. A trail of blue paw prints runs across the lawn from the paint tray to the dog, so it looks as if the dog stepped in the paint tray and is now tracking paint across the grass. The woman does not seem to have noticed. There is a bird house on a tall pole on the left, a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow at the front, and a flower bed by the house.
