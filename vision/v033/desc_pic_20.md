## Free description
A sunny back garden, with the house (red roof, blue door) on the right and a wooden fence with a gate on the left. On the left, a person with short dark hair in a yellow dress and red shoes stands beside the paved path and sweeps a small pile of autumn leaves with a broom. In the centre, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers holds the nozzle of a garden hose up in front of his face with both hands, as if looking at it or waiting for water. No water is coming out. The hose loops across the lawn back to a hose reel next to the house. A white goat with small grey horns is standing right on top of the hose, between the boy and the reel. The hose under and beyond the goat's feet looks squashed flat, so the goat seems to be blocking the water. If the goat steps off, the water might suddenly spray out, maybe into the boy's face. A clothesline with a yellow striped top and blue jeans hangs near the fence. Having a goat in a home garden is unusual.
