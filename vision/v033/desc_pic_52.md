A brown dog sits by the blue door holding a red leash in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. On the right an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, sweeps a pile of autumn leaves on the path with a yellow broom. A teal watering can sits on the grass nearby.
