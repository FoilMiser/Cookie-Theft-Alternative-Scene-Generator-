# Picture 03

## Free description
This is the same kind of sunny shopping street: pink, pale green, peach and cream shops with striped awnings, a street lamp with a red bird on top, a fruit stall and a tree in a planter. On the left, an old man in a grey cap, white shirt and brown vest walks along reading an open book. A cardboard box is on the pavement just ahead of him, so he may trip over it. Behind him, a person in a yellow dress sweeps a pile of orange and red leaves with a broom. In the middle at the back, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers waves one hand in the air. Further right, a man or boy in a red shirt and blue jeans walks along licking a pink ice cream. A man in a green shirt sits on a bench reading a red book, while a blue bird on the bench beside him eats his sandwich. In front of the bench is a red bicycle with square wheels, which is impossible. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of the tree. Most impossibly, a brown dog sits on the ground near the right edge holding open a newspaper as if reading it.
