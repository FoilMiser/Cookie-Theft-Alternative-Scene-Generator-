# Picture 07

## Free description
This is a sunny shopping street with cream, peach, pale green and pink shops behind, striped awnings, a street lamp and a fruit stall on the right. The sun is in the top right, and a blue bird flies across the sky at the top left. On the left, a street sweeper in an orange cap, orange high-visibility vest and blue trousers sweeps a pile of autumn leaves with a yellow broom, next to a tree in a round planter. In the middle, a girl in a pink dress sits on a bench eating a pink ice cream. A small white dog with brown ears and a blue collar sits up on its hind legs in front of the bench, begging. Behind them, a child in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts holds the strings of a blue and a red balloon in one hand. The child reaches up with the other hand toward a purple balloon floating just above, as if it has just slipped away or is being grabbed. A red bird is perched on top of the street lamp. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie walks while reading an open book, heading toward a cardboard box on the pavement, so he may trip over it. There is another box beside the fruit stall and a potted plant in the bottom-right corner.
