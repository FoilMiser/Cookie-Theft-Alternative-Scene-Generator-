## Free description
This is a sunny park with rolling green hills, trees and bushes. On the left is an oval pond with reeds at its edges, and a white duck is swimming on it. An old man in a flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands on the grass at the right-hand edge of the pond, holding a fishing rod; his line runs down to a red-and-white float bobbing in the water with a little ripple around it. In the middle stands a tall black street lamp with a green litter bin next to it. On the right, a winding sandy path leads off into the distance. Next to the path, a wooden ladder stands upright on the grass with nothing to lean against - it is not touching the tree, a wall or anything else - which looks impossible. A grey rock sits on the grass at the front.
