# Picture 01

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence along the back, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a paved path running down the left side. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands on the path by the garden gate, raking a small pile of leaves, and a brown-and-white puppy lies asleep on the path below him. In the middle, an old woman with grey hair and glasses stands with her hands on her hips beside a swing set, and she is wearing a white bucket upside down on her head like a hat. Behind her, a small girl in a pink dress holds a kite string, but the purple kite is caught in the tree behind the fence. In front, a person in a yellow dress holds the nozzle of a green garden hose with both hands. Only a single drop of water comes out, and the hose runs back along the grass to a hose reel next to the bench. A white dog with brown spots stands right next to the hose, and may be standing on it. On the right, a man in a red shirt sits on a bench reading a red book, while a blue bird on the bench beside him pecks at his sandwich. An orange cat walks along the top of the fence. The wheelbarrow at the bottom has a square wheel, which is impossible.

- The kite is clearly stuck in the tree. The girl could be pulling to free it, or still "flying" it without realising.
