## Free description
A sunny back garden. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, with a flower bed and a bush in front of it. A tall wooden fence runs across the back, and there is a wooden gate on the right with a paved path leading up to it. A man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands at the fence holding a long-handled paint roller and is painting the fence blue; a few planks to his left are already blue, and a small paint bucket stands on the grass beside him. In the front right, a boy in a red shirt and blue trousers is sweeping a small pile of dead leaves with a yellow broom, at the edge of the path. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits on the lawn at the front centre. Nothing looks impossible; the scene is a calm day of garden chores.
