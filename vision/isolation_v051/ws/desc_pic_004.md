## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and path on the left, and a cream house with a blue door on the right. On the left, a man with a moustache, in a red shirt and blue trousers, is sweeping a pile of brown leaves with a yellow broom. In the centre, an older grey-haired woman with glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, holds a garden hose and looks at its nozzle, which seems to have no water coming out. The hose runs across the lawn to a hose reel by the house. A white dog with brown spots is standing right on the hose, which bulges where its paw is, so the dog is probably blocking the water. It looks as if water could suddenly spurt out when the dog steps off, perhaps over the woman. A striped yellow towel and blue jeans hang on a washing line, and there are two potted plants, a flower bed and a bench.
