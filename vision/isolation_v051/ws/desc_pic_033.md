# pic_033

## 1. Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left, a wooden fence along the back and a gate and path on the right. A woman in a yellow dress has just thrown a red ball; a dashed line shows its arc travelling from her hands over to a small girl in a pink dress, who reaches out to catch it. On the left, a girl with dark skin in a yellow top and orange shorts sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. Near the gate on the right, a white spotted dog is playing with a yellow-green tennis ball, wagging its tail. In the front right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is kicking an orange ball on the path, with motion lines under his foot. In the front centre, a garden sprinkler attached to a hose is spraying arcs of water straight onto a cardboard box, which is sitting in a puddle and getting soaked. An empty wheelbarrow and a hose reel are at the front left, near a flower bed. The wet box looks like something that should not be there — it is being ruined by the sprinkler.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous
- The red ball is drawn quite close to the woman's head, slightly ahead of her hands; it reads as just thrown by her, though it could also be read as coming toward her. The dashed line toward the girl suggests it is going to the girl.
- The box being sprayed is the oddest thing: either it was left there by mistake and is getting wet, or the sprinkler was set up badly.
- The hose reel is partly hidden behind the wheelbarrow, which makes the two a little hard to separate.
