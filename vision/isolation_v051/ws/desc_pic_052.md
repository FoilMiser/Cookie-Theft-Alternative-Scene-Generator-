## Free description
A sunny playground with a metal railing fence across the back, a tree on the left and a slide on the right. Two children are on a red seesaw at the front left. A boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on the left end, which is slightly lower, and a girl in a pink dress sits on the right end, which is slightly higher. Both are holding the handles. His eyes are squeezed shut and his face looks scrunched, as if the ice cream is too cold (brain freeze) or he has pushed it into his face. His other hand is pressed to his chest. The slide on the right is empty. A green bin stands by the fence, there is a bush on the right and a grey rock in the front right.
