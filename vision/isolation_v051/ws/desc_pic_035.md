## Free description
A sunny but windy back garden: white wind lines blow across the sky and along the left side. A man with brown hair and a moustache, in a red T-shirt and blue trousers, stands on a paved path on the left, looking up with a surprised face and reaching up toward his blue cap, which the wind has blown off and carries away along with a few leaves. A grey cat walks along the top of the wooden fence. A small birdhouse sits on a tall pole. A washing line between two wooden posts holds a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue trousers, pegged on and swinging in the wind. In the tree on the right there is an orange fish sitting among the branches, which is impossible. On the right there is a cream house with a red roof and blue door, and a flower bed in front of it. A wooden gate is in the fence at the far left.
