## Free description
A sunny day in a park playground with a dark railing fence, bushes and a big tree on the right. In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress stands with both hands raised in front of her, ready to catch a red ball that is in the air just in front of her; a dashed line shows the ball was thrown up by a small girl in a pink dress standing further back to the right, who still has her arm out from the throw. In the front centre, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is drinking from a red cup with a straw while in a sitting pose, but there is nothing under him, so he seems to be sitting on thin air. To the right, a brown dog sits up on its hind legs, begging, facing a child in a yellow top on a bench who is eating a pink ice cream cone; the dog seems to want the ice cream. In front of them a small white puppy with brown spots lies curled up on the grass, apparently asleep. At the bottom right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is running fast towards the right edge (speed lines behind her). A red bird flies in the sky at the top right. On the left there is a red slide with a yellow chute, and at the bottom a sandbox with a red bucket and a blue spade, plus a grey rock.
