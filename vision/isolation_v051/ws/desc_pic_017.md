## Free description

In the middle back, a woman in a yellow dress holds her hands out in front of her while a red ball flies through the air near her hands. A dashed line runs from a small girl in a pink dress up to the ball, so the girl seems to have thrown it and the woman is about to catch it. On the right, a child in a yellow top sits on a bench eating a pink ice cream, and a brown dog sits up on its back legs next to the bench, as if begging for the ice cream. A small white puppy is lying down asleep on the grass. At the bottom right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is running fast (speed lines behind her). A red bird flies in the sky at the top right. The sandbox at the bottom left has a red bucket and blue spade in it.
