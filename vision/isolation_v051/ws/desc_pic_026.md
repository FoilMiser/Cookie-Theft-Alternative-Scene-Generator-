## Free description

A girl in a pink dress sits on the left end and a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on the right end, and both hold the handles. The seesaw is nearly level, with the right end a little lower. A man with brown hair and a moustache, in a red T-shirt and blue jeans, walks to the right across the middle of the picture. He holds a pink ice-cream cone up close to his face, as if he is about to lick it or is eating it while he walks. A red ladder leads up to a yellow and orange slide on the right, but nobody is on it. A green rubbish bin stands by the fence, there is a bush at the right and a grey rock in the front right. Nothing looks clearly impossible.
