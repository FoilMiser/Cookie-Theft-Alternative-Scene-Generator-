# pic_027

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a house on the left, a wooden fence along the back and a gate on the right. At the bottom left, an old man in a grey cap, glasses and brown waistcoat sits on a bench reading a red book. Next to him on the bench is a sandwich, and a blue bird has landed beside it and is pecking at it, with crumbs falling; the man is absorbed in his book and does not notice the bird stealing his food. On the right, a man in a red T-shirt and blue trousers stands on the path near the gate sweeping a pile of leaves with a yellow broom. A green hose reel with a hose and yellow nozzle lies on the grass beside the bench. A grey wheelbarrow is parked at the bottom centre, and there is a flowerbed with colourful flowers in front of the house.
