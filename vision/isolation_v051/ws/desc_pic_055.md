## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden, with the house (blue door) on the left and a wooden fence with a gate and path on the right. On the far left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a bench reading a red book. In front of the flower bed, a man with a moustache, in a red shirt and blue trousers, holds a green watering can, but the water is coming out of the spout and going upward into the air above the flowers instead of pouring down, which is impossible. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands on the path flying an orange kite with a ribboned tail high in the sky. A washing line holds blue jeans and a yellow striped towel. In the middle there is a swing set with one empty swing and an empty wheelbarrow on the grass.
