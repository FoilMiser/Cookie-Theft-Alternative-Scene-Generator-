## Free description

A child with dark skin in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on a wooden bench in front of the house reading a red book. Near a bed of colourful flowers, a woman in a yellow dress stands with her arms out, and a red ball flies along a dashed arc down to a girl in a pink dress who runs toward it with her arm out: they are playing catch. Near the gate, a white dog with brown spots crouches playfully next to a green tennis ball, tail wagging. On the right, on a paved path, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is kicking an orange ball. In the front centre, a garden hose runs along the grass to a sprinkler spraying arcs of water, and the water lands on a cardboard box sitting in a puddle, so the box is getting soaked. A grey wheelbarrow stands in front of the flower bed.
