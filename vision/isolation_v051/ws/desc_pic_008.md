On the left by the path a man with a moustache, red T-shirt and blue jeans is sweeping a pile of dry leaves with a yellow broom. Beside him on the grass are two potted plants. The hose runs along the lawn to a hose reel near the house. A white dog with brown spots is standing right on the hose, stepping on it, which would squash it and stop the water. Behind them a clothesline holds a yellow striped towel and a pair of blue jeans. Near the house there is a flower bed and an empty wooden bench.
