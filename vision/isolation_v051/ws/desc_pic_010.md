## Free description
A sunny back garden with the house on the left (cream walls, red roof, blue door) and a wooden fence with a gate on the right. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a bench by the house reading a red book. In front of a flower bed a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans tips a teal watering can, but the stream of water goes up out of the spout in a tall arc, upwards and away from the flowers, with drops at the top, which is impossible. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands on the path near the gate flying an orange kite with a bow-tie tail high in the sky. A clothesline in the middle holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow striped towel. A swing set stands empty with its red seat hanging still. A grey wheelbarrow is parked on the grass at the front.
