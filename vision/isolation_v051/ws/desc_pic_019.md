# pic_019

## 1. Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a house on the right and a paved path on the left leading to a gate. A woman in a yellow dress is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; a few fence planks are already blue. On the ground near her is a paint tray with blue paint and a paint tin. A small white dog with brown spots is on the path at the far left, and its paws are blue; a trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the grass to the dog, so the dog has clearly walked through the paint. In the middle, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is flying an orange kite high in the sky. On the right, a man in a red shirt is watering a flower bed with a garden hose that runs from a hose reel. An empty wheelbarrow sits in the front of the garden. Nobody seems to have noticed the dog's paint prints yet.
