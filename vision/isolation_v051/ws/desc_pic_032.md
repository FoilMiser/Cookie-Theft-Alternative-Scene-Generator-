## Free description
A windy, sunny day in a back garden with a tall wooden fence, a gate on the left and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. Wind lines blow across the sky and across the left of the picture. A man with brown hair and a moustache, in a red T-shirt and blue jeans, stands on the paved path at the front left. He reaches up with one arm and looks surprised, because his blue cap is being blown off and away, along with a few leaves. A grey cat walks along the top of the fence. In the middle, a clothesline between two poles holds a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans pegged on and flapping. A bird house sits on a tall pole on the left. Oddly, an orange goldfish is sitting in the branches of the tree behind the fence on the right, which is impossible. A flower bed is at the front right, near the house.
