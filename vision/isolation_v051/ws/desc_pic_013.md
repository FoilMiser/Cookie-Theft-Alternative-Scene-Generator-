## Free description
A sunny, windy day in a park with rolling hills, trees and a winding path. On the left a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers stands at the edge of a pond fishing with a long rod; his float bobs in the water with a small ripple around it. On a slope at the back right a woman in a yellow dress reaches up with one arm as her orange cap flies off into the air, blown by strong wind lines along with a few green leaves; she looks surprised. In the front right a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans holds a camera to his face and takes a photo, pointing it left towards the pond / fishing man. There is a tall street lamp with a green litter bin next to it, an empty wooden bench by the path, a flower bed in the front left and a grey rock at the front.
