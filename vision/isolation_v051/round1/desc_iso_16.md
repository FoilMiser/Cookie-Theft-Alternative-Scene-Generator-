## What I see
In a back garden with a wooden fence, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers holds the end of a dark teal garden hose and points its nozzle up and forward. The hose runs from a hose reel by the house wall on the left, along the grass, and up to the boy. A brown dog with a red collar stands in the middle of the lawn on top of the hose, apparently stepping on it. No water comes out of the nozzle. On the left are a flower bed with colorful flowers, a bush, a blue door, and part of a wooden bench.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Next to the dog's front paws the hose ends in a wide flattened bulge, like a nozzle or a kink. A separate stretch of hose then starts from under the dog's feet and runs to the boy. It looks like two hose pieces, or a hose that breaks and passes under or through the dog's paws, rather than one continuous hose being stepped on.
- No water comes out. This could mean the dog's paw is blocking the flow, or the water simply isn't on, or the boy is just inspecting the nozzle.
- The hose loops up from near the boy's feet to his hands in an odd tall curve, and the part he holds looks stiff and rod-like.
- The bulge on the ground could be read as a sprinkler, a second nozzle, or a crushed section of hose.
- The bench at the left edge is cut off and looks oddly thin.
