## What I see

He holds a brown paper bag in one hand and a small light-coloured piece of something (looks like bread) in the other, raised slightly. On the pond a white duck swims toward a line of small round beige pieces floating on the water, its beak right at the first piece, as if eating them. There are two cattails at the pond edge and green hedges/trees behind.

- The beige pieces on the water could be bread crumbs or pebbles/stepping stones; they look like food the duck is eating.
- The white lines on the water behind the duck read as motion/ripple lines, but they look a bit like detached sticks.
- The bread in his hand sits a little above his fingers rather than clearly gripped. The floating pieces run in a neat line right up to the cattails, which looks slightly arranged rather than thrown.
