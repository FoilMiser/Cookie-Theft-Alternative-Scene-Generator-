## What I see

Several fence boards to his right have already been painted blue, while the rest are still plain orange-brown wood, so he is painting the fence blue. A grey paint can with blue paint on it sits on the grass by his feet. Colourful flowers (yellow, orange, purple, red) grow in the bottom-right corner, and bushes and a tree are behind the fence.
