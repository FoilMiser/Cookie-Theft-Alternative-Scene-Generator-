## What I see

A man in a red shirt and blue trousers stands on the right holding a garden hose with a yellow nozzle up near his face, frowning at it as if no water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel by the house across the lawn to him. A white dog with brown spots stands on the hose in the middle of the lawn, apparently squashing it and blocking the water.
