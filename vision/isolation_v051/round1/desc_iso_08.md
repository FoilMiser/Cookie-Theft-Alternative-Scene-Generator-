# iso_08

## What I see
A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands on the grass at the edge of a blue pond. He holds a brown paper bag at his waist with one hand and stretches the other hand forward, open, toward the water, looking down. In the pond a white duck swims toward a short trail of small pale round pieces (bread or crumbs) floating on the water, so the boy seems to be feeding the duck. Two cattails stand at the pond's edge, and a tall dark lamp post rises behind the boy on the right; green bushes and hills form the background.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The pale round pieces could be bread crumbs or seeds; they sit in a neat line from the duck's beak toward the cattails, which reads a little like the duck is spitting them out rather than eating them.
- The boy's outstretched hand is well away from the crumbs and holds nothing, so it is unclear whether he has just thrown them or is only gesturing; nothing shows them in the air.
- The white lines on the water by the duck read as a wake or motion lines, but they sit apart from the duck, behind and below it, and could look like stray marks.
- One crumb sits right against a cattail stem, almost touching it.
- The lamp post is cut off at the top and stands on a small square base on the grass; nothing odd beyond that.
