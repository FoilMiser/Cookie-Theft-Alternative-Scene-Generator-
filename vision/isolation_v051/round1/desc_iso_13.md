# iso_13

## What I see
A back garden: a house wall with a blue door on the left, a wooden fence behind, a bush and a round bed of colourful flowers. A man in a red shirt and blue trousers stands on the right holding a garden hose nozzle up in front of his face with both hands, frowning at it as if puzzled that nothing is coming out. The dark teal hose runs from a hose reel by the house wall across the lawn to him. A white dog with brown spots stands on the hose midway, smiling with eyes closed, and the hose has a flattened bulge right under the dog's front, so the dog appears to be standing on the hose and blocking the water.

- The dog's paws do not clearly press on the bulge; it sits slightly in front of / under the front legs, so "standing on the hose" is implied more than shown.
- Past the dog the hose runs along the ground to the man's feet and then rises up beside and slightly behind his leg to his hands; it is hard to tell whether it goes behind his leg or passes through it.
- No water is shown anywhere, which supports the "blocked hose" reading, but the man could also just be inspecting the nozzle.
