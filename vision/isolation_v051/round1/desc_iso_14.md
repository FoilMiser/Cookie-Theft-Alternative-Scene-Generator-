## What I see

A dashed line traces the ball's arc from her hand up to the ball, which is at the top of the arc near the wall. A man in a green shirt and beige trousers stands on the right with both arms held out in front of him, hands together, ready to catch it. A beige paved area shows at the bottom-right corner.

- He is reaching forward at chest height while the ball is still high and well to his left, so his hands are not yet lined up with the ball's path; reads fine as "about to catch" but slightly off.
- The ball at the top of the arc sits right against the bottom edge of the brick wall, so it could look like it is touching or stuck on the wall.
- The girl's pose could read as running forward or as having just thrown; both fit.
