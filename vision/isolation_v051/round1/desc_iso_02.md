# iso_02

## What I see
A man with a beard, wearing a green shirt, tan trousers and brown shoes, stands on grass at the edge of a blue pond. He holds a brown paper bag in one hand and with the other drops small pale crumbs (bread) toward the water. A white duck is swimming in the pond with its orange beak dipped down at the crumbs floating on the water, so he is feeding the duck. There are reeds or cattails at the left edge of the pond and a purple flower in a brown pot (or on a mound of soil) in the bottom-right corner.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The pale item in his raised hand is small and boxy; it reads as a piece of bread, but it could also be a cup or small container.
- The brown thing he holds in front of him looks like a paper bag, but its flat rectangular shape could also read as a cardboard box.
- The crumbs sit on the water as flat oval discs; they could be read as bread pieces or as pebbles/stepping stones.
- The man stands right at the pond's edge; his body overlaps the pond outline, so it is slightly unclear whether he is on the bank or standing in front of the water.
- The duck's beak is a long thin orange stick shape, a bit odd-looking, and could read as the duck holding a stick.
- The brown shape under the purple flower in the corner is cut off; it could be a flowerpot or a dirt mound.
- He looks down with a frowning or neutral expression rather than an obviously pleased one.
