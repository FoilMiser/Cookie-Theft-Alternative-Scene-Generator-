# iso_07

## What I see
A bearded man in a green shirt, tan trousers and brown shoes stands on grass in front of a wooden picket fence. He holds a long-handled paint roller against the fence and is painting it blue; a couple of panels are already blue while the rest are still natural orange-brown wood. A small grey paint bucket with blue paint on it sits on the grass behind him. Colourful flowers (yellow, orange, purple, red) grow in the bottom-right corner, and bushes and a tree are visible above the fence.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The roller head is pressed flat against the fence and drawn as an upright pale-blue cylinder; it reads clearly as a roller, though at first glance it could look like a small lantern or a can stuck to the fence.
- The painted blue section is a perfectly neat rectangle of two whole panels with hard edges, which looks a bit too clean for work in progress, but it reads as "partly painted".
- The roller is on the blue part rather than at the boundary with the unpainted wood, so it is not obvious which direction he is working.
- The man is standing some distance from the fence, with the long handle bridging the gap; nothing looks detached.
- The paint bucket is fairly far from him, behind his legs.
