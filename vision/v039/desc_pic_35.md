## Free description
A sunny back garden seen the other way round: the cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the left, and a wooden fence runs across the back with a gate at the far right and a pale path leading from it to the bottom right. A man in a green shirt and khaki trousers sits on a wooden bench at the lower left, reading a red book. Next to the bench is a teal hose reel, with the hose lying loose on the grass and a yellow nozzle at its end. In the centre-left a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes kneels at a round flower bed, reaching in to tend the flowers. A small pot with a pink tulip-like flower stands at the edge of the bed in front of her, as if she is about to plant it. The strange thing is a closed cardboard box hanging in the sky above the fence in the middle-right. It is tilted, and nobody is near it or throwing it, so it is either floating or falling. A tree stands behind the house and a bush grows by the house wall.
