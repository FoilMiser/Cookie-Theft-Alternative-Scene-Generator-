# Picture 80

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, and a paved path on the right leading to a wooden gate. In the middle, a man with brown hair and a moustache, in a red short-sleeved shirt and blue trousers, is hanging washing on a clothesline strung between two wooden posts. He is reaching up with both hands, pegging a white T-shirt onto the line; a pair of blue jeans is already pegged up to his left. Both the jeans and the T-shirt are dripping water. At his feet is a laundry basket with more clothes in it (blue, white and pink), with a blue item hanging over the edge and dripping too. Around the garden there is a hose reel with its yellow-tipped hose lying on the grass, an empty bench at the front left, a flower bed, an empty wheelbarrow, a teal watering can and two potted plants. Nothing looks wrong or impossible.
