# pic_24

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with no people or ordinary animals in it. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door. A tall wooden fence runs across the back with a wooden gate on the right, and a pale paved path leads from the gate down to the bottom right. A wooden birdhouse sits on a tall post in front of the fence, and two potted plants stand on the grass near it. The odd and impossible thing is an orange goldfish sitting on a branch of the tree behind the house, where you would expect a bird. In the left foreground there is a bed of colourful flowers. At the bottom centre an empty grey wheelbarrow rests on the grass, and beside it on the right is a teal watering can. There are a few round bushes, two clouds and a sun at top right. Otherwise everything looks still and normal.
