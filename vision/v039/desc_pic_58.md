## Free description
This is a busy, sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a house with a blue door on the right and a paved path on the left. On the path near the gate, an old woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and grey skirt is raking up a small pile of leaves, and a little brown puppy with a red collar lies asleep on the path below her. In the middle stands a dark metal swing set with one empty red seat, and next to it a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands with his hands on his hips wearing an upside-down white bucket on his head instead of a hat. A grey cat walks along the top of the fence. A small girl in a pink dress holds the string of a purple kite that is stuck in the leaves of the big tree on the right. A girl in a yellow dress points a garden hose nozzle and looks at it, but only a single drip is coming out, because a white dog with brown spots is standing right on the hose further along. On the bench at the right, a man in a red shirt and jeans is reading a red book while a blue bird pecks at the sandwich lying next to him on the bench, which he has not noticed. In the front there is a grey wheelbarrow whose wheel is drawn as a square. It looks as though the bird is about to steal the sandwich, and the hose may spray suddenly when the dog steps off.
