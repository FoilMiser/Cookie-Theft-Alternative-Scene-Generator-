## Free description
This is a sunny park with a winding sandy path on the left, a big tree, a tall black lamp post, and a round pond with bulrushes on the right, with green hills behind. At the bottom left, a boy in a red baseball cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is running fast towards the left edge of the picture, with speed lines behind him. At the pond's edge, an old man with grey hair, a grey flat cap, a white shirt and a brown waistcoat holds a brown paper bag and is throwing bread crumbs onto the water for a white duck, which is swimming up and pecking at the crumbs. In the middle of the grass beside the path, a wooden ladder is standing upright all on its own with nothing to lean against, which looks impossible. There is also a grey rock in the front and a green rubbish bin in the bottom right corner. No one is near the ladder or using it.
