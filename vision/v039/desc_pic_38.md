## Free description
A paved town square in front of a row of colourful shops (pink, pale green, peach and cream buildings with striped awnings) under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds. In the front centre a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie, black trousers and white shoes walks along reading an open book. A cardboard box lies on the ground just ahead of him, so he looks about to trip over it. Behind him a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers sits on a bench reading a red book. A sandwich lies on the bench beside him, and a blue bird has landed next to it, apparently about to steal it while he isn't looking. At the left an older man in a grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands eating a pink ice cream. Behind the lamp post an older woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt stands pointing upward. A red bird is perched on top of the lamp post. At the right a girl in a pink dress jumps in the air with both arms raised, looking happy. There is also a fruit and vegetable stall at the left, a teal bicycle in a bike rack, a tree in a planter, a green bin, potted plants, a flower planter and a second cardboard box near the stall.
