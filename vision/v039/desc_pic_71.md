## Free description
This is a back garden on a sunny but windy day: there are white wind lines in the sky and three long white gust lines streaking across in front of the fence. The cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, the wooden fence runs across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path leads to the gate. In the middle, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan, grey skirt and brown shoes is standing with both arms raised high and her mouth open in surprise. Just above her and to the left, a blue cap is tumbling through the air along with a few small leaves, carried by the wind. It looks like the wind has just blown her hat off and she is trying to grab it. Two potted plants stand on the grass to the right of centre, a teal watering can sits near the path at the bottom right, and a flower bed is at the left by the house. Apart from the hat and leaves, nothing else in the garden (trees, flowers, plants) looks affected by the wind.
