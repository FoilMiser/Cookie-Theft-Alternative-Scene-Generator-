# pic_69

## Free description
This is a sunny playground with a dark metal railing fence across the back, a tree on the left, a bush on the right, and the sun at the top right. On the left is a red seesaw. A little girl in a pink dress is sitting on the high end, holding the handle, with her feet dangling in the air; her mouth is open and she looks surprised or worried. On the low end, which is touching the ground, a man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers is sitting and reading an open book, apparently not paying attention to her. Because he is heavier and not moving, the girl seems to be stuck up in the air. In the middle of the picture a boy (or teenager) in a red cap, blue hoodie, black trousers and white shoes is standing and eating a pink ice cream, with his eyes closed as if enjoying it. On the right is a slide with a red frame, an orange-and-yellow chute and a ladder, which nobody is using. There is also a green rubbish bin by the fence and a grey rock in the bottom right corner.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The girl's open mouth could mean she is scared, calling to the man, or just surprised.
- An adult reading a book on a seesaw is a bit odd but not impossible.
