# pic_33

## Free description
A sunny playground in a park, with an iron railing fence and hedges at the back, a tree on the left, and a sun and two clouds in a blue sky. On the left is a red seesaw tipped steeply: the left end is up in the air and the right end rests on the ground. A small girl in a pink dress sits on the raised left end, holding the handle, with her mouth open in a round "o" as if surprised or calling out. An older man with grey hair and a grey cap, in a white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers, sits on the low right end. He is calmly reading an open book and is not paying attention to the girl or the seesaw. Because he is heavier and not pushing up, the girl seems stuck in the air. In the middle-right a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands eating a pink ice-cream cone, with his eyes closed as if enjoying it. On the right is a slide with a red frame, an orange-and-yellow chute and a grey ladder, and nobody is on it. A bush and a grey rock are on the right side of the grass.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl's open mouth could mean surprise or fright at being stuck high up, or she could be calling to the man to push off. It could also just be a neutral "oh".
- It is a little odd for a grown man to sit on a seesaw and read instead of playing. It may be the "joke" of the scene, or he may simply be acting as a counterweight.
- The older man's legs look crossed or tucked while he sits on the plank, and it is slightly hard to see how he is balanced. Nothing seems to pass through anything, though.
- The green-shirted man's closed eyes could be read as enjoying the ice cream or as squinting in the sun.
