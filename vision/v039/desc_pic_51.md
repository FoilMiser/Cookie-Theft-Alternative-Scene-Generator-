# pic_51

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden seen the other way round: the cream house with red roof and blue door is on the left, and a gate with a paved path is on the right. In the centre, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, a teal top and a grey skirt is holding the nozzle of a garden hose up in front of her face with both hands and peering into it; only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose runs from a reel at the front left, across the grass, and loops round to her. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is standing right on top of the hose, and the hose under its feet looks squashed and bulging, so the dog is blocking the water. It looks like something is about to happen: when the dog steps off, the water will rush out and spray the woman in the face. Behind her, a washing line between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. There is also a flower bed and bench on the left, two potted plants and a birdhouse on a pole near the gate, and an empty grey wheelbarrow on the grass at the front right.
