## Free description
This is a sunny back garden seen the other way round: the house (cream walls, red roof, blue door) is on the right, the wooden gate and a paved path are on the left, and the sun is top left. A man with brown hair and a moustache, in a red T-shirt and blue jeans, is digging a hole in the lawn with a shovel, and a small pile of dug-up soil sits beside the hole. On top of the fence a grey cat is creeping low along the pickets, stalking a blue bird with a white belly that is perched on the fence a little further to the right; the cat looks about to pounce on the bird. At the bottom centre, a lawn sprinkler connected to a long hose is spraying arcs of water to the left, and the water is landing on a cardboard box sitting on the grass. The box is standing in a puddle with water dripping down its sides, so it is getting soaked. Meanwhile the flower bed near the house, on the other side of the sprinkler, is not getting any water. The scene suggests two things about to go wrong: the bird may be caught by the cat, and the box (perhaps a delivered parcel) is being ruined by the water.
