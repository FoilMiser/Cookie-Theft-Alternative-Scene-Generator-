# pic_21

## Free description
This is a windy day in a sunny park. White swirly wind lines are drawn in the sky and across the pond, and small green and brown leaves are being blown off the tree on the left towards the right. On the right is a blue pond with bulrushes. Above its right end a red baseball cap is tumbling through the air, clearly blown away by the wind. In the front right a young woman in a yellow dress and red shoes has both arms thrown up in the air. Her eyes are wide and her mouth is open in surprise, so it looks like her cap has just blown off and she is reacting or trying to grab it. In the centre an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, stands near a lamp post and a green bin and licks a pink ice-cream cone. She seems unbothered. On the left a winding sandy path runs past an empty wooden bench, and there is a grey rock at the bottom centre.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Cap ownership: the cap is most likely the young woman's, blown off her head, and she is reaching for it. Another reading is that she is just throwing up her arms in shock or cheering, and the cap belongs to no one in view. She is not wearing a hat now, and the cap's red matches her shoes.
- The young woman's hands are below and to the left of the cap, so she is not touching it. She is reaching or reacting, not catching.
- The cap is about to land in the pond, or to be carried further away.
- It is mildly odd that the elderly woman is calmly eating ice cream in strong wind, but nothing is impossible.
