# Picture 03

## Free description
This is a sunny park with hills in the background, a big tree on the left, a lamp post in the middle, a pond on the right and a flower bed in the front-right corner. On the path at the back left, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. In front of her on the path, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is holding an empty ice cream cone and has a hand up to their face, as if crying or rubbing their eyes. Just to the child's left, a pink ice cream has splattered on the grass, and a brown dog with a red collar is sniffing or licking it up. So the child seems to have dropped their ice cream and the dog is eating it. In the middle-right, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers stands in front of the pond taking a photo with a camera pointed to the left. In the front centre, a lawn sprinkler connected to a long hose is spraying arcs of water onto a cardboard box, which is wet and has a puddle around it. The box is getting soaked, which looks like a small accident.
