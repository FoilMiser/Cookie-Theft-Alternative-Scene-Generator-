# pic_09

## Free description
It is a sunny day on a town street. A row of four colourful shop buildings (pink, pale green, peach and cream) with striped awnings stands behind a wide paved pavement, and a red bird flies across the sky at top right. On the left a man in a green shirt carrying a yellow shopping bag walks past a fruit stall that has crates of yellow, orange and red fruit. In front of him a small girl in a pink dress jumps in the air with both arms up. Beside her a woman in a yellow dress walks to the right reading an open book, heading straight for a closed cardboard box on the ground, so she looks about to trip over it. In the middle a woman in a purple top waters some flowers by a shop door with a teal watering can, but the stream of water curves up into the air to the left of the flowers instead of pouring down onto them. On a wooden bench an old woman with grey hair and glasses reads a red book. A blue bird has landed on the bench next to her sandwich and looks about to take it without her noticing, while behind her a street worker in an orange cap and hi-vis vest sweeps a pile of leaves. To the right a brown dog sits up holding an open newspaper with its paw as if reading it, which is impossible, and on the far right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in a tree.

- The dog "reading" a newspaper is impossible. The newspaper also seems to stand up on its own on the ground.
- The jumping girl's shadow is separated from her feet. This reads as her being mid-jump, but it could also look like she is floating.

- The blue bird could just be sitting next to the sandwich, or it could be about to steal it.
