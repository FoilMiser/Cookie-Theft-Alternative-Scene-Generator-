This is a back garden on a sunny day, with a tall wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate in the fence on the left, and a paved path running down from the gate to the front-left. On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue front door, with a wooden bench in front of it. A girl or young woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers stands in the middle of the lawn with her hands on her hips. She is wearing a white bucket upside down on her head like a hat, which is the odd thing in the picture. She looks towards the right, at the flower bed or the house. On the ground in front there is a grey wheelbarrow, which looks empty, and a teal watering can. Near the fence there are two potted plants and a birdhouse on a tall wooden pole. A flower bed full of coloured flowers sits to the right of the girl. No one else is in the picture and nothing seems about to happen.
