# pic_70

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house (red roof, blue door) on the left, a wooden fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path leading to the gate. An old man in a grey cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers is painting the wooden fence blue with a long-handled paint roller; a section of about three boards to his right is already blue. Behind him on the grass is a paint tray with blue paint and a paint tin. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has clearly stepped in the paint: its paws are blue and it has left a trail of blue paw prints across the lawn from the paint tray, and it is walking toward the left, toward the flower bed and the house, so it is about to spread paint further. On the right, a girl (or young woman) in a yellow dress and red shoes, with a dark ponytail, is sweeping a small pile of leaves with a yellow broom at the edge of the path. A hose reel with its hose and nozzle lies on the grass at the bottom left, and an empty grey wheelbarrow stands at the bottom centre. Neither the man nor the girl seems to have noticed the dog.
