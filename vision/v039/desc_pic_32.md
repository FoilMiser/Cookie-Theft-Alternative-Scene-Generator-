## Free description
A sunny back garden with the house and blue door on the right, a wooden gate and stone path on the left, a metal swing set with an empty red swing seat on the left of the lawn, and a clothesline in the centre back with a yellow-and-red striped towel and blue jeans pegged on it. On the left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands on the path sweeping a small pile of leaves with a yellow broom. In the centre, an older man in a grey flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is holding the end of a garden hose up in front of his face and peering into the nozzle; only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel at the front right across the grass to his hands, and a white goat with small horns and a little beard is standing right on top of the hose with its feet. The part of the hose between the goat and the reel looks fatter, as if water is building up, so it seems the goat is blocking the water and the man is puzzled why nothing comes out. If the goat steps off, the water would probably spray straight into the man's face. The goat is next to the flower bed and is looking toward the man.
