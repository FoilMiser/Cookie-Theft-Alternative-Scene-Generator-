## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the cream house, red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. In the centre front, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is flying a yellow kite: he holds the string up with one hand and the kite is high in the sky with a tail of little bows. On the left, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is kneeling beside the flower bed, handling a small pot with a pink flower in it, as if planting or placing it. On the right, on the path, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is holding what looks like a very large silver fork, like a giant dinner fork, and pushing it along the path; a little pile of dirt with a puff of dust sits in front of a red dustpan next to it. So he seems to be sweeping dirt into the dustpan with a fork instead of a broom, which is odd. There is also an empty swing set on the right, an empty grey wheelbarrow at the front centre, a hose reel with a hose at the front left, and a birdhouse on a pole by the gate.
