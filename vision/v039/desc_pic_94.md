## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate at the right and a paved path running down the right side. In front of the house a woman/girl with long dark hair, yellow dress and red shoes is watering a flower bed with a teal watering can. Oddly, the stream of water does not fall down onto the flowers: it arcs upward into the air from the flowers/spout, with drops flying up at the top, like a fountain going the wrong way. In the middle a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and black trousers has just thrown a red ball with his arm stretched out; a dotted line shows the ball flying up toward a man in a green polo shirt and beige trousers, who has his hands up ready to catch it. On the right a small child with dark curly hair, yellow T-shirt and orange shorts sits on a red swing on a dark swing frame, smiling, with motion lines showing the swing moving. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands at the front. The man is about to catch the ball.
