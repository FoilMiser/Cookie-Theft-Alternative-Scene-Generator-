In the bottom right, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, is pushing a teal pram with a sleeping baby tucked under a pink spotted blanket, near the winding sandy path. There is also an empty wooden bench, a grey rock on the grass at the bottom centre, a flowerbed and a green rubbish bin in the bottom left. Everything looks peaceful and nothing looks impossible.
