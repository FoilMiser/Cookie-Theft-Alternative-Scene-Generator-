## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house on the left, a wooden fence and gate at the back and a paved path on the right; a washing line between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow striped towel. In the middle, an elderly man in a grey flat cap, white long-sleeved shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is holding the nozzle of a green-blue garden hose up close to his face and peering into it, with only a single drop of water coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel at the bottom left, across the grass, and back up to his hands. A brown-and-white dog with a red collar is standing right on top of the hose, and the hose looks squashed/kinked under its paws, which is why no water is coming out. It looks as if, once the dog steps off, water will spray straight into the man's face. On the right, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, is sweeping a small pile of leaves with a yellow broom at the edge of the path. There is also a flowerbed near the house and two potted plants by the fence.
