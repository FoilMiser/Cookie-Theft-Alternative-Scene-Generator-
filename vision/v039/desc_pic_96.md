## Free description
A sunny playground with a dark railing fence at the back, a red-framed slide on the left and an A-frame swing set with one empty red swing in the middle. On the right is a red seesaw. An old man with grey hair and a moustache, wearing a grey-blue flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers, sits on the low end of the seesaw, which is resting on the ground, calmly reading an open book. On the high end, up in the air, sits a small girl with dark hair in a bun, pink dress and yellow shoes, holding the handle, with her mouth open in an "o" as if surprised or worried. Because the man is so much heavier and is not paying attention, the girl is stuck up in the air and cannot come down. There is a green bin by the fence, a bush at the left, a tall tree at the right and a grey rock at the bottom left. It is odd for a grown man to be sitting on a seesaw reading a book, ignoring the child at the other end.
