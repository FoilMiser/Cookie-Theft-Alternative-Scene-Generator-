## Free description
It is a sunny back garden behind a house. A cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue back door stands on the left. A tall wooden fence runs across the back of the lawn with a wooden gate on the right, and a pale paved path leads from the gate down to the bottom-right corner. A woman with a dark ponytail, a yellow dress and red shoes stands beside a round flower bed of red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers, holding a teal watering can in both hands. A thin stream of water comes from the can, but it curves upwards and back over the flowers with drops at its top, as if the water is shooting up into the air rather than pouring down onto the plants. Near the fence there is a round green bush and two terracotta pots of green plants. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands on the lawn at the bottom centre, and a tree behind the house and a line of trees or hedge show above the fence. Nobody else is in the garden, and nothing else seems to be happening.
