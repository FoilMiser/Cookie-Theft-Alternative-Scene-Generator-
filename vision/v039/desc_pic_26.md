## Free description
A back garden on a grey, overcast day: the sky is pale grey with three big dark storm clouds and no sun. On the left a woman with dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers stands on the stone path, looking up with wide eyes and a small round mouth and pointing at the dark clouds, as if she has just noticed rain is coming. In the middle of the lawn a girl in a yellow dress with red shoes is reading a red book, calmly, not looking up. The girl is in a sitting position, but there is no chair, bench or anything under her; she seems to be sitting on thin air. On the right, a brown dog with a red collar sits next to the blue door of the cream house, with a red lead that goes from its collar or mouth down to a loop lying on the ground, as if it wants to go for a walk or wants to go inside. There is a flower bed and a hose reel near the house, two potted plants and a bush by the fence, a wooden gate at the far left end of the path, and a tree behind the house. It looks like rain is about to start and the girl hasn't noticed.
