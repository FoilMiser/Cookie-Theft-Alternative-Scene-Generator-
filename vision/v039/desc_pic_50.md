## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence along the back, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the middle front, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is walking to the right carrying a cardboard box in both hands. That is the strange thing in the picture: a frying pan is the wrong tool for watering, and it seems to be pouring a steady stream as if it were a watering can. At the front right, an elderly man with grey hair, a grey flat cap, a moustache, a white shirt and brown waistcoat is sitting on a wooden bench drinking from a red cup through a straw. Two potted plants and a round bush stand near the fence. The scene is otherwise calm and ordinary.
