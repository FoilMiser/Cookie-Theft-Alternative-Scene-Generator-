# Picture 05

## Free description
This is a back garden on a sunny day, with a tall wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A woman with long dark hair in a yellow dress is painting the fence blue with a paint roller. She has painted a section about three boards wide, and an open tin of blue paint sits on the grass by her feet. On top of the fence a grey cat is creeping along towards a small blue bird perched on the fence a little to the right, as if about to pounce on it. A red bird flies in the sky near the sun. In the middle, an older man with grey hair, a grey cap, a white shirt, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers holds the nozzle of a garden hose up and peers at it, because only a drop of water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel by the house across the lawn, and a white dog with brown spots is standing on it. The dog is probably squashing the hose and stopping the water, which explains why the man is puzzled. At the bottom left, a small girl in a pink dress holds a blue and a purple balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other towards a yellow balloon floating away from her. She seems to have let go of it by accident.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- I can't be sure the dog standing on the hose is the reason the water has stopped. The man might instead just be checking or fiddling with the nozzle, but the dog's feet are definitely on the hose.
- The yellow balloon might have slipped from the girl's hand, or she might be trying to catch a balloon that came from elsewhere. Her raised arm and the other two balloons suggest she let it go.
- The man's arms look slightly odd: his white sleeve seems stretched straight across his body to the nozzle.
- The cat looks about to pounce on the blue bird, but nothing has happened yet.
- It's unclear whether painting only part of the fence blue is unfinished work or a design.
