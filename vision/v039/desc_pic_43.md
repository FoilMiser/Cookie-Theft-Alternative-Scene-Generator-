## Free description
This is a sunny back garden. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue front door; a tall wooden fence runs across the back with a wooden gate on the right, and a paved path leads from the gate down to the bottom right. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and round glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, stands in the middle of the lawn raking a small pile of green and brown leaves with a long garden rake. In front of her near the bottom of the picture, a small orange cat is curled up asleep on the grass. A round flowerbed with colourful flowers is near the house, two terracotta pots with green plants stand by the fence, and a teal watering can sits on the grass near the path. The odd and impossible thing is in the tree behind the house: an orange goldfish is sitting on a branch of the tree, out of water, as if it were a bird. Everything else looks like a normal, calm gardening scene.
