# pic_39

## Free description
The same sunny park as before (hills, hedges, winding path, lamp post, pond), this time with people. At the lower left a small girl in a pink dress stands on the path holding a kite string and looking up. Her purple kite is stuck in the top of the big tree on the left, caught among the leaves, with its bow tail hanging out to the side. In the centre-left a young woman in a yellow dress and red shoes sits on a bench reading a red book. A sandwich lies beside her on the bench, and a blue bird has landed right next to it, apparently about to steal it while she reads. In the middle-right, in front of the pond, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie, black trousers and white shoes walks while reading an open book. A cardboard box sits on the grass just ahead of him, so he may trip over it. At the lower right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers strides along carrying a yellow shopping bag. A small white puppy with brown spots lies asleep on the grass at the bottom centre, and a red bird flies in the sky at the upper right. There is a green bin at the bottom right, and the pond has bulrushes at each end but no duck.
