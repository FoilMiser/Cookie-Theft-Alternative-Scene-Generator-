## Free description
This is a sunny back garden seen the other way round: the cream house with the red roof and blue door is on the right, the wooden fence runs across the back, and a gate and paved path are on the left. On the path at the front left, a little girl with dark hair in a bun, in a pink dress and yellow shoes, is flying an orange diamond kite. It is high in the sky, with a tail of little bows, and she holds the string up with both hands. In the middle right, a man with dark skin and a short beard, in a green shirt and tan trousers, is drinking from a red cup through a straw. He is in a sitting position with knees bent, but there is no chair, bench or anything under him. He is sitting on thin air, which looks impossible. On the grass in the middle left, an orange cat is curled up asleep. At the front centre there is a red pet bowl with food in it. There are two potted plants and a bush by the fence, and a flower bed and bush near the house. The main odd thing is the man sitting on nothing.
