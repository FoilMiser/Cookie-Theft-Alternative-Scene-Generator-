# Picture 04

## Free description
This is a quiet sunny park with hills behind, a big tree on the left, a winding sandy path, a black lamp post in the middle, and a pond on the right with bulrushes. A small girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes stands near the lamp post, between it and the pond. She is holding something small and pink up to her mouth with both hands, probably eating a small ice cream or sweet. In the front centre a white goat with dark curved horns has its head down and is eating a tuft of grass. A grey rock sits on the grass just in front of the goat. An empty wooden bench stands beside the path on the left. A flower bed with colourful flowers is at the front right, and a green rubbish bin is in the bottom-right corner. Nothing seems to be about to happen. It is a calm scene.
