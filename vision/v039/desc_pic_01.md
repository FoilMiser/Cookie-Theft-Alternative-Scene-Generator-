# Picture 01

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park, with green hills and a row of bushes in the distance, two white clouds, and a sun in the top-right corner. On the left there is an oval blue pond with bulrushes at both ends, and a tall black lamp post stands in the middle. A sandy path winds from the bottom right up towards the back, with a big tree beside it on the right. A girl or young woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is walking near the path, partly on the grass. She is holding a pink ice cream cone up to her mouth and eating it. In the front centre a white goat with small dark horns has its head down and is eating a tuft of grass. A green rubbish bin stands in the bottom-left corner. Nothing dramatic seems about to happen. It is a calm scene of someone eating ice cream while a goat grazes nearby.
