## Free description
This is a sunny but very windy back garden with a tall wooden fence, a house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a paved path running down from a gate on the left. White wind lines streak across the sky and across the left side of the picture. On the path at the left, a man with a moustache, a red T-shirt and blue jeans has both arms stretched high above his head and looks up in surprise, because his orange cap has blown off and is flying away up and to the right, past the bird-house pole, along with a couple of leaves. In the middle of the lawn, an old man in a grey flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat sits calmly reading a red book, but there is no chair, bench or anything else under him; he is sitting in mid-air. Behind him a washing line between two posts holds a red-and-yellow striped towel and a pair of blue trousers, both blown out sideways by the wind. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is digging with a spade next to a small pile of earth, looking down at her work. In front of the house there is a round flower bed full of colourful flowers. The cap looks like it is about to be lost over the fence unless the man catches it.
