## Free description
A sunny back garden with the cream house (blue door, red roof, window) on the right, a wooden fence across the back with a gate at the far left, and a pale path running from the gate down to the bottom-left corner. On the path at the left a woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds a giant silver fork shaped like a dinner fork. She is using it like a broom to sweep a small pile of dirt or crumbs towards a red dustpan, and a little dust cloud rises from the pile. Using an oversized eating fork to sweep is clearly the wrong tool and the odd thing in the scene. In the centre-left is a grey swing set with one empty red swing hanging still. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands at the front centre. On the right, near the house, is a round flower bed of colourful flowers. Beside it a teal hose reel has its hose lying on the grass with a yellow nozzle. There is a bush by the house and a tree behind the fence, and nobody else is in the garden.
