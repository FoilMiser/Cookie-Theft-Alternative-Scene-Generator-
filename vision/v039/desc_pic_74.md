## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence along the back, and a paved path on the left leading to a wooden gate. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and round glasses, wearing a teal top and a grey skirt, is digging in the lawn with a long-handled spade. She has dug a small hole and there is a heap of brown soil beside it; the spade blade is in or just above the hole with soil on it. Her face looks stern or concentrated. Behind her is a swing set with a single red seat, which is empty and hanging still. A teal watering can sits on the grass near the path, and there is a birdhouse with a red roof on top of a tall pole near the gate. A flower bed with colourful flowers is in front of the house. No one else and no animals are in the picture.
