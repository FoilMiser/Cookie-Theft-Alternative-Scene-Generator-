# Picture 73

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence along the back, and a paved path on the right that leads to a wooden gate in the fence. In the middle of the garden a man in a red shirt and blue trousers is holding the nozzle of a garden hose up in front of him with both hands, pointing it to the left; only a single drop of water is coming out of the nozzle, and he seems to be looking at it. The hose runs from a hose reel at the front left, across the grass, and up to the man. A small white dog with brown patches and a blue collar is standing right on top of the hose, between the reel and the man, and the hose looks squashed/flattened under its feet. So it looks like the dog is blocking the water, and the man may be about to get a burst of water (possibly in his face) when the dog steps off. On the right, by the path, a boy in a red cap and a blue hoodie is sweeping a small pile of leaves with a broom. An empty wheelbarrow stands on the grass in front, and there is a flower bed with colourful flowers by the house and two potted plants by the fence.
