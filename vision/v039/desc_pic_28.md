# pic_28

## Free description
A sunny back garden, this time with the house on the right and a wooden gate and stone path on the left. In the centre back, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is hanging washing on a clothesline strung between two wooden posts: he is reaching up to peg a white T-shirt, and a pair of blue jeans already hangs further along. Both the T-shirt and the jeans are dripping water, so the washing is freshly washed and wet. A laundry basket with more clothes (pink, white, blue) sits on the grass at his feet. At the front left, an older man with a grey moustache, a flat cap, a white shirt and a brown waistcoat is sweeping a small pile of leaves at the edge of the path with a yellow broom. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits in the front centre, a teal watering can beside it, and a hose reel lies near the flower bed in front of the house. Everything looks normal and nothing seems about to go wrong.
