# Picture 08

## Free description
This is a busy sunny park with a pond on the left, a lamp post in the middle, a winding path on the right, and hills in the background. On the far left, a white dog with brown spots is sitting up and holding open a newspaper as if reading it, which is impossible and clearly odd. Next to the dog, an older man in a grey cap, a white shirt, a brown waistcoat and dark trousers is watering a flower bed by pouring water out of a black frying pan, another strange thing because a frying pan isn't a watering can. In the pond a white duck swims, with little pieces of bread floating around it. A girl in a pink dress standing at the pond's edge seems to be feeding it, holding something small and brown. In the front middle, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice cream cone and has a hand to their eyes, as if crying, while a brown dog with a red collar licks up a pink ice cream splattered on the ground. In the back right a white goat with dark horns grazes on a tuft of grass. On the path an elderly woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt sweeps leaves with a yellow broom. At the far right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie holds a kite string, but his purple kite is caught in the branches of a big tree. A blue bird flies in the sky above.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The dog reading a newspaper is impossible, and it looks deliberately so.
- The man's container is black, round and has a long handle. It looks like a frying pan, which makes watering with it odd, though it could be read as a saucepan or scoop.
- The small brown item in the girl's hand could be a bag of bread, a piece of bread, or a cup. The bread pieces around the duck make feeding the most likely reading.
- The child's gesture again reads as crying over the dropped ice cream, but it could be rubbing an eye.
- The kite clearly seems caught in the tree, but the boy could also be trying to pull it free or just flying it close to the tree.
- The goat is near the path behind the bench and partly blends with the grass tuft under its head.
- Nothing appears to float or pass through anything, apart from the bird and kite, which are meant to be in the air.
