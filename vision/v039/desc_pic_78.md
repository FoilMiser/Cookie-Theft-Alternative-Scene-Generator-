# Picture 78

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence along the back, and a paved path on the left leading to a wooden gate. A man with brown hair and a moustache, in a red shirt and blue trousers, is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; a section of fence boards behind and to the left of him is already blue, and a small paint tin stands on the grass at his feet. At the front, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is walking towards the left carrying a cardboard box in both hands. The strange thing is that there is an orange goldfish sitting in the tree behind the fence, perched among the branches near the top of the trunk, which is impossible. There are two potted plants and a teal watering can on the grass on the left, a flower bed in front of the house, and an empty wooden bench at the bottom right.
