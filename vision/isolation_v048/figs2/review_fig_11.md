# Review fig 11

## 1. Who and what
A man (short brown hair, moustache, red T-shirt, blue trousers, dark shoes) walking to the viewer's right; in a his face is in profile, in b his face is turned three-quarters toward the viewer with an open mouth.

## 2. Faults in a
- Shoulder/arm: the arm on the viewer's left comes out of the sleeve with a bulge at the back of the shoulder and swings back stiffly; the upper arm looks detached from the sleeve.
- Arms: both arms are thin, even tubes with no elbow shape.
- Torso: short compared with the long legs.
- Legs: the back leg is straight and very long, stretching far behind; the stride looks overextended.
- Face: only one eye and a moustache; the face is very small for the head.

## 3. Faults in b
- Head: the face is turned toward the viewer while the body walks in profile, so the head looks twisted on the body.
- Shoulder/arm: same bulge where the back arm leaves the sleeve.
- Arms: same thin tube arms.
- Torso/legs: same short torso and overlong, overextended back leg.
- Mouth: open red mouth under the moustache looks slightly odd.

## 4. Differences
- Head turn: a in profile with one eye; b three-quarters with two eyes.
- Mouth: a shows only a moustache; b shows an open mouth under the moustache.
- Ear: a shows an ear on the side of the head; b shows none.
- Hair: b has a fringe cut across the forehead; a's hair sits back on the head.
- b's head is slightly bigger and the body slightly shorter in the frame.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head and neck join fine; back shoulder has a bulge and the back arm looks oddly joined; arms thin; hands small fists; torso short; hips fine; back leg too long; feet fine.
- b: head twisted relative to the profile body; otherwise the same shoulder bulge, thin arms, short torso and long back leg.

## 6. Pose
- a: reads as walking with arms swinging, though the stride is very long and the arms stiff.
- b: same walk, but the turned face makes it read as walking while looking at the viewer, which is less natural.

## 7. Better drawn
a — the head and body face the same way so the walk reads coherently; b's frontal face on a profile walking body looks twisted.
