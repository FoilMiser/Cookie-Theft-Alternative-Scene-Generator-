# Review fig 05

## 1. Who and what
A young woman with long dark hair tied back, in a short-sleeved yellow dress and maroon flat shoes, standing still with her arms at her sides, head turned to the viewer's right. In a her mouth is open (talking or surprised); in b she has a closed smile and reads calmer.

## 2. Faults in a
- Neck: very wide and short, almost as wide as the lower face, so the head looks set straight onto a block.
- Face: the open red mouth sits low at the very edge of the face and looks like a small pout or grimace; a shading line along the jaw makes the chin look doubled.
- Shoulders / sleeves: the near shoulder has a sleeve, but the far shoulder is a square corner of the dress with the far arm coming out from under it without a visible sleeve.
- Hands: both are rounded lumps with a short thumb line, no clear fingers.
- Far arm: partly hidden behind the dress, so it looks shorter and thinner than the near arm.
- Waist: a gap between the near arm and the waist shows a small notch of background, slightly odd.

## 3. Faults in b
- Neck: still thick, though a little narrower and longer than in a.
- Face: nearly in profile with a nose bump and a thin smile line, but the eye is placed far toward the front, so the face reads as flat.
- Shoulders / sleeves: same square far shoulder with no visible sleeve as in a.
- Hands: same rounded lumps with no clear fingers.
- Far arm: same partly hidden, shorter-looking far arm.

## 4. Differences
- Mouth: a has an open red mouth; b has a closed smile line.
- Face turn: b is turned further toward profile, with a nose bump at the front edge; a is more three-quarter with no nose shown.
- Jaw: a has a shading line under the jaw; b's chin line is cleaner.
- Neck: b's neck is slightly narrower and reads a little longer.
- Hair: in b the hair falls a little further down the back of the neck and shoulder.
- Overall size: b is drawn very slightly larger in the frame.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head a reasonable size, but the neck is too thick and short. Shoulders broad and square; arms of reasonable length, the far one looking short because it is half hidden. Hands are blobs. Hips and legs straight and of believable length; feet in small flat shoes look fine.
- b: Same body. Neck slightly better proportioned than in a but still thick. Arms, hands, hips, legs and feet as in a: arms acceptable, hands blob-like, legs and feet fine.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads as a natural relaxed stand with the head turned to look at something; slightly stiff because both arms hang straight and the body faces front while the head turns.
- b: Same natural standing pose; the stronger head turn makes it read a little more clearly as looking at something to her side. Equally slightly stiff in the arms.

## 7. Better drawn
b. The face reads more cleanly (clear profile, calm smile, no doubled jaw line) and the neck is a little less blocky; the body is otherwise the same.
