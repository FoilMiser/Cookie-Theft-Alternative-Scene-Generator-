# Review fig 12

## 1. Who and what
A child (a boy) with short curly dark hair, in a yellow T-shirt, orange shorts and blue shoes, standing still with arms at his sides and holding nothing; in a he has a small smile, in b a small open mouth as if surprised or speaking.

## 2. Faults in a
- Hands: both hands are small rounded lumps with no fingers shown.
- Arms: rather thin and long, hanging to the bottom of the shorts.
- Legs: thin, especially the shins, and the knees are straight.
- Neck: short and thick, joining the head forward of the ear.
- Hair: the curls are drawn as rows of bumps on top only; acceptable.

## 3. Faults in b
- Hands: small rounded lumps, as in a.
- Head: a little larger and rounder than in a, with a faint darker shading on the lower face, which looks slightly smudgy.
- Arms and legs: thin, as in a.
- Mouth: small pink open mouth at the face edge; readable.

## 4. Differences
- Mouth: a has a small closed smile; b has a small open pink mouth.
- Eyebrows: b has a raised, more marked eyebrow; a's is smaller.
- Head: b's head is slightly larger and rounder, with a smaller ear and a faint darker tone on the lower face.
- Shirt: b's T-shirt ends a little higher, so the torso is slightly shorter.
- Shorts: b's shorts are slightly longer/lower and a touch wider.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head large (fine for a child cartoon); neck short; shoulders fine; arms thin and long; hands small lumps; hips fine; legs thin; feet fine. Nothing detached.
- b: Same as a, with a slightly bigger head and shorter torso; arms and legs thin; hands small lumps; feet fine. Nothing detached.

## 6. Pose
- a: A plain standing pose, a little stiff (straight arms and legs) but natural for a child standing and looking to the side.
- b: The same pose, equally a little stiff; the open mouth makes him look like he is reacting to something.

## 7. Better drawn
no difference: the body is the same in both; only the expression differs, and the slight smudgy shading on b's face is balanced by its clearer expression.
