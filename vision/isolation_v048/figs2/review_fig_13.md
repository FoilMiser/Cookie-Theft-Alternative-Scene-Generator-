# Review fig 13

## 1. Who and what
A young man or teenage boy in a red cap, blue hooded top, dark grey trousers and white shoes, walking forward with one arm stretched out pointing ahead; in a he is turned more toward the viewer and talking (open mouth), in b he is in profile with no mouth visible.

## 2. Faults in a
- Pointing hand: a flat paddle shape with no clear finger, so the "pointing" is only implied by the arm.
- Pointing arm: starts from the front of the chest (sleeve cuff shape mid-chest) rather than from the shoulder.
- Rear arm: short, sticking out behind from low on the back, with a plain lump fist.
- Torso: short compared with the legs.
- Ear: placed far back on the head, behind the cap brim, which looks a little off with the face turned toward us.
- Hoodie front: a small dark strip (zip) shows between the arm and body, slightly unclear.

## 3. Faults in b
- Eye: the single eye sits on the very front edge of the face outline, partly cut off.
- Mouth: none shown, so the face is blank.
- Neck: a longer bare neck strip shows between hood and head, making the neck look long and thin.
- Pointing hand and arm: same paddle hand and arm starting mid-chest as in a.
- Rear arm: same short arm with lump fist.
- Torso: short, as in a.

## 4. Differences
- Face: a is three-quarter view with two eyes, eyebrow, nose and open talking mouth; b is a profile with one eye on the face edge, nose, and no mouth.
- Ear: b's ear is larger and placed lower/further forward on the side of the head.
- Neck: b shows more neck between hood and head than a.
- Cap: b's cap brim points more forward and is a little larger.
- Shoes: b's front shoe is slightly further forward and the rear shoe sits slightly lower.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head and neck fine; pointing arm oddly joined at the front of the chest rather than the shoulder; rear arm short; pointing hand has no finger; torso short; hips and legs fine with a good stride; feet fine.
- b: Neck looks long and thin; same chest-joined pointing arm, short rear arm and fingerless hand; torso short; hips, legs and feet fine.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads naturally as walking while pointing ahead and saying something; the stride is good, though the arm out of the chest is slightly awkward.
- b: Same walking-and-pointing pose, readable, but the blank profile face makes it less lively.

## 7. Better drawn
a: the face is complete and expressive and the neck is in proportion, while b has a cut-off eye, no mouth and a long thin neck; the body and arms are the same in both.
