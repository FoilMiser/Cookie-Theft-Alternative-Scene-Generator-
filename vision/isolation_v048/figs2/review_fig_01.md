# Review fig 01

## 1. Who and what
A woman with short dark hair in a pink dress and yellow shoes, walking to the right with a long stride and holding a pink ice-cream cone up near her face. In a she is seen side-on with her face turned toward the viewer. In b the head is seen almost from behind, so her face is barely visible.

## 2. Faults in a
- Hair: a darker round circle on the back of the head reads as an odd bun or a hole rather than hair.
- Face: the features (eyes, nose, small red mouth) are squeezed onto the right edge of a very large head. The mouth sits under a hooked nose and looks small and pursed.
- Arms: only one arm is visible (the one holding the cone). The other arm is missing entirely, with nothing swinging behind the body.
- Hand: the hand grips the cone with a dark area, and a dark stalk or drip hangs below the hand, which looks like a smudge.
- Legs: the stride is very wide, so the back leg stretches far behind and looks long.
- Head: very large relative to the body, on a thin neck.

## 3. Faults in b
- Head and face: the head is turned so far that we see mostly the back of it plus an ear. The face shows only as a dark edge mark (eye and nose), so she seems to look away and her expression and eating cannot be read.
- Hair: the same odd round circle on the back of the head.
- Arms: again only one arm is visible; the other is missing.
- Hand: the same dark drip or stalk under the cone hand.
- Legs: the same very wide stride with a long back leg.
- Head: very large on a thin neck.

## 4. Differences
- Head turn: a shows the face in three-quarter view (two eyes, nose, red mouth); b shows the back or side of the head with an ear and almost no face.
- Ear: visible in b, not visible in a.
- Expression: readable in a (a slight pursed smile); not readable in b.
- Head position: b's head sits slightly further back over the shoulders, and its hair covers more of the head.
- Cone: in b the cone sits slightly further from the face and a little higher on the hand.
- Size: the body and stride look the same; the figure scale is very slightly different.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: The head is very large on a thin, short neck. The shoulder is hidden in the puffed sleeve. The visible arm is a reasonable length and bends naturally at the elbow; the second arm is absent. The hand is a mitt around the cone. The hips are hidden under the dress. The legs are fairly thin and the back leg looks long in the wide stride. The feet are yellow shoes at a reasonable angle.
- b: The same body as a. The head is very large and, because it is turned away, looks like it sits on the neck backwards relative to the forward-walking body. The single arm is fine and the second arm is missing. The legs and feet are the same as a.

## 6. Pose
- a: It reads as walking along while eating an ice cream: the body leans forward slightly and the cone is held up to the mouth. The stride is overlong and the missing second arm makes it a bit stiff, but it is mostly natural.
- b: The body reads as walking, but the head seems to face away from the direction of walking and away from the cone, so it does not read clearly as eating. It looks awkward.

## 7. Better drawn
a. The face is visible and turned toward the ice cream, so the action reads clearly. In b the head looks turned away, the face is lost, and she does not look like she is eating.
