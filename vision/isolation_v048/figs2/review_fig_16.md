# Review fig 16

## 1. Who and what
A man (a workman) in an orange cap, orange high-visibility vest with white stripes, blue trousers and dark shoes, standing and holding a teal hose with a yellow nozzle up in front of him with both hands, as if spraying or checking it; in a he seems to look calmly ahead, in b he looks toward the viewer with a frown and open mouth.

## 2. Faults in a
- Eye: the single eye sits right on the front edge of the face outline, partly cut by it, so the profile looks odd.
- Shoulder/sleeve: the blue-grey T-shirt sleeve is a flat patch laid over the vest, looking pasted on rather than part of the shoulder.
- Near arm: the arm reaching to the nozzle starts from that sleeve patch in the middle of the chest, not from a shoulder.
- Torso: very short compared with the legs.
- Legs: very long and thin, especially the rear leg, which narrows to a thin strip.
- Hose: a straight stiff tube; fine for a hose seen at this size.

## 3. Faults in b
- Shoulder/sleeve: same flat blue-grey sleeve patch over the vest, with the arm starting mid-chest.
- Torso: very short; legs very long and thin as in a.
- Face: the frown and open mouth make him look annoyed or puzzled, which may not suit what he is doing.
- Lower hand: the hand gripping the hose low is a small blob with little hand shape.

## 4. Differences
- Face: a is a near-profile with one eye on the face edge and a small nose; b is turned more toward the viewer with two eyes, an eyebrow, a nose and an open frowning mouth.
- Head turn: in b the head looks out toward the viewer instead of at the nozzle.
- Hair/ear: b shows a smaller ear and less hair at the back than a.
- Hose: in b the hose is held slightly lower and tilted a little differently.
- Body size: b's figure is drawn very slightly narrower overall.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head and neck fine; shoulder unclear because of the pasted-on sleeve; near arm springs from the chest; hands small but readable; torso very short; legs too long and thin; feet fine.
- b: Same body as a: sleeve patch and chest-sprung arm, very short torso, overlong thin legs, small blob lower hand; head and neck fine; feet fine.

## 6. Pose
- a: Holding the hose up with both hands while looking at the nozzle reads as a natural working action, though the straight stiff legs make the stance rigid.
- b: The arms read the same, but the head turned to the viewer with a frown makes it look as if he is not paying attention to the hose; legs equally stiff.

## 7. Better drawn
a, slightly: he looks at what he is doing, so the action reads more clearly, though his eye on the face edge is a flaw; b's face is better drawn but distracts from the action. The body faults are the same in both.
