# Review fig 07

## 1. Who and what
A woman (dark hair tied back, yellow dress, red flat shoes) sitting with knees bent, reading an open red book held in both hands; both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Arms: the arm on the viewer's left is a short, even-width tube from the sleeve to the book, with no visible elbow bend; it looks stiff.
- Hands: the hands are small rounded blobs on the book edges; no fingers or grip are drawn.
- Eyes: drawn as two short dark dashes, so she reads as eyes closed or squinting rather than looking at the book.
- Dress: the skirt hem stops above the knees in a straight band, and the bulge at the hip/bottom makes the dress look stuffed.
- Legs: the shins are long and the thighs very foreshortened, so the lower legs dominate.

## 3. Faults in b
- Arms: same short, stiff tube arm on the viewer's left with no clear elbow.
- Hands: same blob hands with no fingers.
- Eyes: same dash eyes, reading as closed.
- Dress: the waist-to-hip area is fuller and rounder than in a, making the torso look padded and the hips heavy.
- Legs: same long shins and foreshortened thighs.

## 4. Differences
- The torso in b is slightly longer and the waist/hip outline is rounder and fuller.
- The book and hands sit a little lower in b relative to the face.
- The whole figure in b is slightly taller; the legs and feet are placed a little lower.
- Otherwise head, face, hair and shoes look the same.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head and neck join reasonably; shoulders fine; the near-left arm is short and tubular; hands are undetailed blobs; hips and seated thighs are foreshortened but plausible; shins look long; feet/shoes are fine.
- b: as a, but the torso is a touch longer and the hips/waist look thicker; same short tube arm, blob hands and long shins.

## 6. Pose
- a: reads as a natural seated reading pose (head bowed toward the book, book held in front), slightly stiff in the arms.
- b: same natural reading pose; the fuller torso makes it look a little heavier but no less natural.

## 7. Better drawn
a — the two are almost identical, but a's torso and hips are slightly trimmer and better proportioned, while b's waist looks padded.
