# Review fig 02

## 1. Who and what
A child with dark skin and dark curly or braided hair, in a yellow T-shirt, orange shorts and blue shoes, kicking one leg forward high (as if kicking a ball or stepping in a march), with one arm swung back and the other bent forward. In a the face is turned toward the viewer with frowning eyebrows and reads as cross or straining; in b the face is in profile with no eyes visible and reads as blank.

## 2. Faults in a
- Face: the eyes and eyebrows are squeezed to the far right edge of the face; the slanted brows make the child look angry.
- Mouth: a small open mouth with a pink line, reading as a grimace.
- Ear: placed high and far back on the head, half in the hair.
- Back arm: held straight out behind, stiff, ending in a lump hand with no fingers.
- Front arm: forearm and fist overlap the shorts awkwardly; hand is a lump.
- Kicking leg: the thigh comes out of the front of the shorts very low and the leg looks long and straight.
- Head: large compared with the body (acceptable for a child, but heavy).

## 3. Faults in b
- Face: in profile with only a nose and lip bump; no eye is visible, so the face looks empty and could be read as the back of the head.
- Ear: a large plain ear placed in the middle of the side of the head.
- Back arm, front arm, hands: same as in a (stiff straight back arm, lump hands, fist overlapping shorts).
- Kicking leg: same long, low-set thigh as in a.
- Head: same large head.

## 4. Differences
- Face direction: a is turned three-quarter toward the viewer with eyes, brows and mouth; b is in profile with no eyes.
- Expression: a looks cross or straining; b has no readable expression.
- Ear: in a it is small and high on the back of the head; in b it is larger and in the middle of the side.
- Hair: in b the hair comes further down the back of the head and the hairline differs.
- Head position: b's head sits very slightly further back over the shoulders.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head large, neck short and thin but joined. Shoulders fine; back arm a little long and stiff, front arm fine. Hands blob-like. Hips fine; the kicking leg's thigh is set low and the leg looks long; standing leg bent at the knee and looks right. Feet in blue shoes fine.
- b: Same body as a: large head, thin short neck, stiff long back arm, blob hands, long kicking leg, standing leg and feet fine.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads as a lively kick or big step with arms swinging in opposition, which is natural; the straight, rigid back arm makes it a little stiff.
- b: The same pose with the same stiff back arm; the faceless profile makes the action less readable as a child playing.

## 7. Better drawn
a. Its face has eyes and an expression, so the child reads clearly as a child doing the action, although the frown is a little harsh; b's face is blank with no eyes.
