# Review fig 04

## 1. Who and what
A man with short dark hair and a short beard, in a green T-shirt, khaki trousers and brown shoes, standing still with his arms hanging at his sides, holding nothing; both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Beard/jaw: the beard is a soft darker-brown patch on the cheek and jaw with no edge, so it reads more like a shadow or dirt than a beard.
- Mouth: there is almost no mouth; only a tiny mark at the edge of the beard patch, so the face looks blank.
- Arms: both arms are long, hanging to mid-thigh, and the fists are small, plain lumps.
- Neck: short, thick stub that joins the head well forward of the ear.
- Near hand (right side of picture): the hand is drawn as a small curl tucked against the trouser leg, hard to read as a hand.

## 3. Faults in b
- Beard: drawn as a dark outline band along the jaw; reads as a beard but heavy and a bit like a chinstrap.
- Mouth: small open red mouth sits right at the face edge, partly into the beard line; slightly odd but readable.
- Trousers: a faint light-brown smudge/patch on the front of the trousers near the hip and at the crotch, like a stray shading mark.
- Arms: same long arms and small lump fists as a; near hand again a small curl against the leg.
- Neck: short thick stub as in a.

## 4. Differences
- Beard: a is a soft-edged shading patch on cheek and jaw; b is a dark outlined band along the jaw line.
- Mouth: a has essentially no visible mouth; b has a small open red mouth.
- Face: b shows a little more of the face turned toward the viewer (eye and ear slightly differently placed, head marginally larger).
- Shirt: the T-shirt in b ends a little higher, so the torso is slightly shorter and the legs slightly longer than in a.
- Trousers: b has a faint brown smudge on the front of the trousers; a has clean trousers.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head large for the body (cartoon norm); neck short and thick; shoulders fine; arms on the long side with small fists; hips and legs fine, legs fairly long and straight; feet fine. Nothing detached.
- b: Same as a, with a slightly shorter torso and longer legs; neck short and thick; arms long; hands small; feet fine. Nothing detached.

## 6. Pose
- a: A plain standing pose, a little stiff (arms straight down, legs straight), but natural enough for a man standing and looking to the side.
- b: The same standing pose, equally a little stiff but natural; the open mouth makes him look as if he is about to speak.

## 7. Better drawn
b, slightly: the beard reads as a beard and the face has a mouth, whereas in a the beard looks like a shadow and the face is blank; b's only extra flaw is the faint smudge on the trousers.
