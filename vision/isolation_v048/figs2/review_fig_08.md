# Review fig 08

## 1. Who and what
A young woman or teenage girl (long dark hair in a ponytail, purple T-shirt, teal trousers, dark shoes) standing still with her hands in loose fists at her sides, head turned toward her left; in a she has a closed slight smile, in b her mouth is open as if talking or surprised.

## 2. Faults in a
- Arms: the arms hang from low on the shoulders and look stuck on the outside of the body; the arm on the viewer's left is thick and straight with no elbow.
- Hands: small fists with little detail, slightly too small for the arms.
- Neck: wide and short, almost as wide as the jaw.
- Shirt/trousers: the shirt is short and boxy; the trouser crotch line is a short slit high between the legs.
- Hips: wide and square relative to the shoulders.

## 3. Faults in b
- Arms: same stiff, low-set arms with no elbow bend.
- Hands: same small fists.
- Mouth: open red mouth placed at the edge of the face profile, which looks odd.
- Torso: the shirt is shorter and the trouser waist sits higher than in a, so the torso looks short and the legs very long.
- Hips: same wide, square hips.

## 4. Differences
- Mouth: a has a closed smile; b has an open red mouth.
- Ear: b shows an ear on the side of the head; a does not.
- Hair: in a the hair frames the face further forward with a fringe curve; in b the face is more exposed and the hairline sits further back.
- Torso: b's shirt is shorter and the trouser waist is higher.
- Overall height: b's figure is slightly shorter in the frame.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head and neck acceptable but neck wide; shoulders square with low-set arms; hands small fists; hips wide; legs straight and long; feet fine.
- b: as a, but torso shorter and legs relatively longer; ear adds a little realism to the head.

## 6. Pose
- a: reads as standing and looking aside; stiff, because the arms hang rigidly with fists and the body is straight.
- b: same stiff standing pose; the open mouth suggests speaking but the body does not take part.

## 7. Better drawn
a — the torso-to-leg proportion is better and the closed-smile face looks more finished than b's edge-of-face open mouth; the bodies are otherwise the same.
