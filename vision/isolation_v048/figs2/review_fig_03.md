# Review fig 03

## 1. Who and what
A man with short dark hair and dark skin, in a green T-shirt, khaki trousers and brown shoes, standing and raking with a long-handled rake held in both hands, head bent down toward the rake. In a he has a short beard (dark lower face) and a smile; in b he has a moustache and a slightly more turned face.

## 2. Faults in a
- Top hand: a rounded lump wrapped over the top of the handle near the shoulder, no fingers around the handle.
- Lower hand: also a lump stuck onto the side of the handle, not clearly gripping it.
- Near arm: the upper arm drops from the sleeve and the forearm turns sharply across the body; the elbow joint is vague.
- Beard: the dark beard shape covers most of the lower face and reads as a shadow or dirt rather than a beard.
- Torso: short; the shirt ends high and the trousers start close to the chest, so the legs look long.
- Rake head: drawn over the front shoe, so the foot and rake overlap confusingly.

## 3. Faults in b
- Top hand and lower hand: the same lumps not clearly wrapping the handle.
- Near arm: the same vague elbow and sharp turn of the forearm.
- Moustache: a dark smudge under the nose that runs down toward the chin, also reading partly as a shadow.
- Trousers: a faint lighter rectangular patch on the thigh next to the handle, like an unfinished or stray mark.
- Torso: same short torso and long legs.
- Rake head: same overlap with the front shoe.

## 4. Differences
- Facial hair: a has a broad dark beard on the lower face; b has a narrower moustache-like dark shape.
- Face: in b the head is turned a little more toward the viewer's right, with a small nose bump and the eye nearer the front; in a there is a visible smile line.
- Head size: b's head is slightly larger.
- Trousers: b has a faint light patch on the thigh that a does not have.
- Overall: b's figure sits slightly lower and the rake handle runs at a very slightly different angle.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head of reasonable size on a short neck. Shoulders fine; arms of believable length, elbow of the near arm unclear. Hands blob-like. Hips high, torso short, legs long but acceptable for a cartoon. Feet in brown shoes fine, partly covered by the rake head.
- b: Same as a, with the head a little larger. Same unclear elbow, blob hands, short torso and long legs; feet fine.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads naturally as raking: knees slightly bent, head and shoulders leaning toward the work, hands spread along the handle. The only awkward part is the top hand perched up by the shoulder.
- b: The same natural raking pose with the same awkward top hand.

## 7. Better drawn
a, narrowly. The two are almost the same, but b has a stray light patch on the trousers; a's beard is heavier but the face reads as smiling and finished.
