# Review fig 14

## 1. Who and what
An older man with grey hair, a blue flat cap and a white moustache, wearing a brown waistcoat over a cream shirt and dark trousers, standing side-on facing right and holding a long fishing rod angled up and forward with both hands. Both versions read as fishing. In b his eyes are closed, so he may read as dozing or calm.

## 2. Faults in a
- Face: seen in strict profile, the eye, nose and moustache merge into one dark shape on the front edge of the face, so the features are hard to read.
- Moustache: drawn dark and merged with the nose, so it does not read as white or grey like his hair.
- Legs: long, dead straight and stiff, with no knee bend; they look long compared with the torso.
- Feet: both shoes point the same way, one just behind the other, which looks flat and stiff.
- Arms: the near upper arm (cream sleeve) is long and runs diagonally across the waistcoat; the join at the shoulder is hard to see.
- Torso: it is short compared with the legs, and the trousers come up high.

## 3. Faults in b
- Eyes: closed (curved lids), so he could read as asleep, which may confuse what he is doing.
- Legs: the same long, straight, stiff legs with no knee bend.
- Feet: the same stacked shoes pointing the same way.
- Arms: the same long diagonal near arm with an unclear shoulder join.
- Torso: the same short torso and high trousers.

## 4. Differences
- Head angle: a is a strict side profile with an ear showing; b is turned more toward the viewer in three-quarter view, with no ear visible.
- Eyes: a has a single dark eye merged with the nose; b has two closed eyes with eyebrows.
- Moustache: dark in a; white or grey and clearly readable in b.
- Face: b's face is rounder and shows more cheek; a shows more of the back of the head and hair.
- Cap: b's cap sits slightly flatter and further forward over the face.
- Size: the body, rod and hands look the same; b's figure is very slightly larger.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: The head is large on a short neck and looks fine. The shoulders are narrow. The arms are long, the near arm especially, but both hands meet the rod plausibly (one at the reel, one higher up). The hands are mitts. The torso is short and the hips sit high. The legs are long, straight and slightly thin. The feet are both side-on and overlapping.
- b: The same body as a. The head is in three-quarter view on a profile body, which joins acceptably. The arms, hands, hips, legs and feet are the same as a.

## 6. Pose
- a: It reads as standing and fishing, with the rod held out in both hands. The body is very upright and the legs are locked straight, so it is stiff but understandable.
- b: The same stiff, upright fishing stance. The closed eyes make him look relaxed or sleepy, which still fits fishing, but he could also read as dozing.

## 7. Better drawn
b. The face is more readable (clear white moustache, eyebrows, rounder face), while a's profile features merge into a dark blob. The closed eyes in b are a minor drawback.
