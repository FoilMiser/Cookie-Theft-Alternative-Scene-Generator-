# Review fig 10

## 1. Who and what
A young or middle-aged woman with dark shoulder-length hair, yellow short-sleeved dress and dark red shoes, standing facing the viewer with arms at her sides, doing nothing in particular. Both versions read the same, except that a's mouth looks odd (see below) while b clearly smiles.

## 2. Faults in a
- Face: the mouth reads poorly. A dark squiggle sits above small red lips, so it looks like a moustache or a pursed, unhappy mouth rather than a clear expression.
- Face: the eyes are large dark ovals set fairly wide, which gives a startled, staring look.
- Arms: they hang dead straight and come out from under the puffed sleeve caps like separate tubes, so the shoulder join looks pasted on.
- Hands: closed lumps with no fingers, slightly large for the wrists.
- Legs: long, straight and parallel with no knee shape, so they look stiff.

## 3. Faults in b
- Arms: the same straight, tube-like arms coming out from under the sleeve caps, with a pasted-on shoulder join.
- Hands: the same closed lumps with no fingers.
- Legs: the same long, straight legs with no knee shape.
- Face: no clear faults. The smile is a simple brown curve and reads well.

## 4. Differences
- Mouth: a has a dark squiggle over red lips (it reads as a moustache or a pout); b has a simple brown smile.
- Expression: a looks neutral to displeased or startled; b looks friendly.
- Hair: b's hair looks slightly fuller at the top and parted, with the face a slightly longer oval; a's fringe is cut straighter across.
- Eyes: b's eyes look a little smaller and have small highlights, so they read softer than a's.
- Height: b's figure is very slightly taller, and its dress hem sits a little lower.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: The head is large, as cartoons usually draw it, on a short neck, which is fine. The shoulders are square, and the arms are slightly long, reaching mid-thigh, and look joined under the sleeves rather than at the shoulder. The hands are mitts. The hips are hidden by the dress and the waist is pinched, which reads fine. The legs are long, thin, straight and evenly spaced. The feet are front-facing shoes and look fine.
- b: The proportions and joins are the same as a: long tube arms joined under the sleeves, mitt hands, long straight legs, fine feet. The head and neck look fine.

## 6. Pose
- a: A stiff, symmetrical "standing to attention" pose with arms rigid at the sides. It is acceptable for someone just standing, but wooden, and the odd mouth makes her look uneasy.
- b: The same stiff, symmetrical pose. The friendly smile makes her read as calmly standing, but the body is still wooden.

## 7. Better drawn
b. The body is the same in both, but b's face reads clearly as a pleasant smile, while a's mouth looks like a moustache or a grimace.
