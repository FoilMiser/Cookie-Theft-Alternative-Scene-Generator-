# Review fig 15

## 1. Who and what
An older woman with grey hair in a bun and round glasses, wearing a teal buttoned cardigan over a cream top, a blue-grey skirt and brown shoes, standing facing the viewer with arms at her sides. Both versions read as the same grandmother figure. a looks stern or grimacing; b looks smiling.

## 2. Faults in a
- Face: the glasses are very large and thick-rimmed, taking up most of the face, and the eyes inside look like staring dots.
- Mouth: a wide red-and-dark open mouth reads as a grimace or a frown rather than a pleasant expression.
- Neck: almost no neck shows; the head sits straight down on the collar.
- Arms: straight tubes hanging rigidly, with the shoulders boxy and square.
- Hands: small mitts with no fingers.
- Legs: long, straight and parallel with no knee shape.

## 3. Faults in b
- Arms: the same straight, rigid tube arms with boxy shoulders.
- Hands: the same small mitts.
- Legs: the same long, straight legs with no knee shape; they look slightly long for an older woman.
- Face: no clear faults. The smile, smaller glasses and eyebrows read well.

## 4. Differences
- Glasses: a's are large and heavy-rimmed; b's are smaller, thinner-rimmed and sit lower on the face.
- Mouth: a has a red-and-dark open grimace; b has a simple brown smile.
- Eyebrows: visible in b, not clearly visible in a.
- Ears: visible at the sides in b; hardly visible in a.
- Hair: in b a grey fringe sweeps across the forehead; a's hair is a smooth grey cap.
- Neck: b shows a short neck; a shows almost none.
- Height: b's figure is slightly taller overall, and the skirt hem sits slightly lower.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: The head is large and sits almost directly on the collar, with very little neck. The shoulders are square and boxy, and the arms are tube-like, slightly long and joined fine at the shoulder. The hands are small mitts. The hips are under the skirt and look fine. The legs are long, straight and fairly thin. The feet are brown shoes and look fine.
- b: The head is large on a short neck, which looks right. The shoulders and arms are the same boxy tubes. The hands are small mitts. The hips look fine. The legs are long and straight. The feet look fine.

## 6. Pose
- a: A stiff, symmetrical standing pose with arms rigid. Acceptable for standing, but wooden, and the grimace makes her look cross or uncomfortable.
- b: The same stiff, symmetrical pose. The smile makes it read as calmly standing, but the body is still wooden.

## 7. Better drawn
b. The bodies are the same, but b's face (smaller glasses, eyebrows, smile, visible neck) reads as a friendly older woman, while a's oversized glasses and grimace look odd.
