# Review fig 09

## 1. Who and what
An older woman (grey hair in a bun, round glasses, teal jumper, grey-blue skirt, brown shoes) standing side-on, holding a brown box/parcel out in front of her as if handing it over; in a she faces partly toward the viewer and smiles with an open mouth, in b her face is in strict profile with no mouth visible.

## 2. Faults in a
- Head: the face is turned three-quarters toward the viewer while the body is in profile, so the head looks twisted on the neck.
- Glasses: both lenses are drawn as large heavy rings that dominate the face.
- Arms/hands: only one arm is visible; the hand sticks out past the box instead of gripping it, so the box seems to hang under the arm rather than be held.
- Box: the box floats below the forearm with no second hand supporting it.
- Legs: the legs are thin and the back leg crosses behind the front one at the shins, so the feet overlap awkwardly.
- Neck: long and thin between the collar and the jaw.

## 3. Faults in b
- Glasses: in profile the glasses jut out in front of the face like goggles, and the nose and mouth are hidden, so the face reads as blank.
- Face: no mouth drawn; the face is mostly ear and glasses.
- Arms/hands: same single arm with the hand past the box rather than holding it.
- Box: same floating box with no supporting hand.
- Legs: same thin legs crossing at the shins with overlapping feet.

## 4. Differences
- Head turn: a shows the face three-quarters toward the viewer; b shows it in profile.
- Glasses: a shows two full round lenses; b shows the glasses side-on, projecting forward.
- Mouth: a has an open smiling mouth (red inside); b has no visible mouth.
- Ear: in a the ear is small and further back; in b the ear is larger and more central on the head.
- Hair: the grey hair covers more of the head side in b.
- The figure in b is placed slightly lower in the frame; body, box, arms, skirt and legs otherwise look the same.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head too twisted for the profile body; neck long and thin; shoulders and arm acceptable; hand small and not gripping; hips under the skirt fine; legs thin, crossing at the shins; feet overlap.
- b: head sits naturally on the profile body; neck fine; same arm, hand, thin legs and overlapping feet as a.

## 6. Pose
- a: reads as offering a box, but the turned face and the box hanging below the arm make it look stiff and staged.
- b: the head, body and arm all face the same way, so the offering action reads more coherently; still stiff because the box is not visibly held.

## 7. Better drawn
b — the head matches the profile body and the pose reads more naturally, though a's visible smile gives a more readable face; b's goggle-like glasses and missing mouth are its weak points.
