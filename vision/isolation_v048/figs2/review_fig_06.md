# Review fig 06

## 1. Who and what
A man with short brown hair and a moustache, in a red T-shirt, blue trousers and dark shoes, standing facing front with his arms at his sides, holding nothing. Both versions read the same; b looks a little more cheerful because the mouth is shown.

## 2. Faults in a
- Arms: hang straight down and slightly apart from the body, like a mannequin; the upper arms bulge at the sleeve edge.
- Hands: small rounded lumps with a thumb line, no fingers.
- Shirt: boxy, with squared shoulders; the hem is cut straight across.
- Trousers: legs very long relative to the short torso; the gap between the legs is a narrow slit that starts high.
- Feet: small dark shoes, small for the length of the legs.
- Face: no mouth under the moustache, so the face is a little blank.

## 3. Faults in b
- Neck: almost none; the chin nearly touches the collar and the head looks pressed down onto the shoulders.
- Head: larger and wider than in a, slightly too big for the body.
- Ears: hardly visible, hidden in the hair line.
- Arms, hands: same stiff straight arms and lump hands as in a.
- Trousers: start higher than in a, so the legs look even longer and the torso shorter.
- Feet: same small dark shoes.

## 4. Differences
- Head size: b's head is larger and wider.
- Neck: a has a visible short neck; b has almost no neck.
- Mouth: b shows a red open smile under the moustache; a shows only the moustache.
- Ears: a shows an ear on each side; in b they are barely visible.
- Hair: in b the hair comes further down the sides of the head.
- Torso: the shirt is shorter in b and the waistband sits higher.
- Shading: b has a shaded band under the chin at the collar.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head of reasonable size on a short but present neck. Shoulders broad and square; arms of believable length but held away from the body. Hands too small and blob-like. Torso short and legs too long; hips narrow. Feet small.
- b: Head too large, with almost no neck. Shoulders and arms as in a. Hands too small. Torso shorter still and legs too long. Feet small.

## 6. Pose
- a: Stiff: a fully symmetrical front-on stance with both arms hanging straight down, like a standing diagram rather than a person standing naturally.
- b: Equally stiff for the same reasons; the sunken head adds to a hunched look.

## 7. Better drawn
a. The head sits on a visible neck and the body proportions are less extreme; b's large head pressed onto the shoulders looks more awkward, although its visible mouth is a small plus.
