# Review: fig 11

## 1. Who and what
A tan/orange dog with a dark brown floppy ear, cream muzzle and red collar, standing in side view facing right, tail raised; it holds nothing. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Hip/haunch: a stray, unclosed contour line with a small jagged notch sits where the hind leg meets the body, so the thigh join looks unfinished.
- Hind legs: drawn as long, thin, uniform tubes with little thigh mass; the far hind leg is a thin sliver behind the near one.
- Front legs: very straight, uniform-width posts; the far front leg is a narrow wedge that looks pasted on.
- Head: the head is a round ball with the muzzle a small cream patch stuck to the front, giving little snout; looks slightly like a separate disc on top of the collar.
- Feet: paws are simple blobs; the far hind paw is a small stub poking out behind.
- Tail: thick at the base and quite long and stiffly upright.

## 3. Faults in b
- Hind legs: long and fairly thin below the knee; the far hind leg is drawn darker and the near hind paw overlaps it a little awkwardly at the ground.
- Neck/chest: a small pale patch shows under the collar at the chest, slightly odd.
- Front legs: long, straight posts, though less extreme than in a.
- Head: still a round head, but the muzzle is a longer cream snout with a mouth line, so it joins better.
- Minor: the far front leg is again a narrow sliver.

## 4. Differences
- Muzzle: a has a short cream patch; b has a longer, more defined snout with a mouth line.
- Haunch: a has a broken, notched contour at the hip; b has a clean, rounded thigh with a proper curve.
- Hind legs: b's hind legs are thicker at the thigh and more shaped, with a visible hock; a's are thin tubes.
- Tail: b's tail is thinner and more tapered and curved; a's is thicker at the base.
- Ear: a's ear is larger and longer; b's is smaller.
- Collar: b's collar is slightly thinner and set at more of an angle; b shows a pale chest patch beneath it.
- Body: b's back is slightly more sloped and the belly line more shaped; a's body is more of a plain rectangle.
- Head: a's head is set a little higher and more separate from the body; b's head joins the neck more smoothly.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head reads as a ball with a weak snout; neck hidden by the collar. Body is a plain box. Legs are too long and too thin, especially the hind legs, with an unfinished, oddly joined hip contour. Feet are simple blobs. Tail is long and thick at the root.
- b: Head with a proper snout joins the neck well. Body has a more natural chest and belly. Hind legs have a shaped thigh and hock, still a bit long and thin below. Front legs long but acceptable. Feet fine. Tail tapered and natural.

## 6. Pose
- a: A standing dog, readable, but stiff: legs are straight posts and the hind legs look like stilts, so it reads as rigid.
- b: A natural standing pose with a slight forward lean, bent hind legs and alert raised tail; reads as a dog standing or about to walk.

## 7. Better drawn
b. It has a clean, well-shaped haunch and hind legs, a proper snout and a tapered tail, while a has an unfinished hip contour and stiff, thin stilt-like legs.
