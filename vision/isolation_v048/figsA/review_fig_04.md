# Review fig 04

## Who and what
A grey-blue cat standing still on all fours, side view facing right, tail raised. Both versions read as a standing cat; a reads more like a toy or block figure, b more like a real cat.

## Faults in a
- Head: a plain circle set directly on the front of the body with no neck at all.
- Front leg: a single straight cylinder whose top shows as a separate rounded end overlapping the belly, so it looks pasted on rather than growing from the shoulder.
- Front legs: only one front leg is seen; the cat looks three-legged from the front.
- Hind legs: thick and bent, but the two hind legs overlap almost into one shape.
- Tail: a straight stiff stick pointing up and back.
- Ear: only one small triangle, set on top of the circle; second ear missing.
- Face: whiskers are only two short strokes and the white muzzle is a small patch stuck on the edge of the circle.
- Body: boxy and flat-topped.

## Faults in b
- Legs: all four legs are long and thin, so the cat stands high like on stilts.
- Front legs: a thin white sliver shows between the two front legs at the bottom, looking like a gap or seam.
- Head: an extra curved line across the head below the ear, where head meets neck, looks like a leftover seam.
- Ear: only one large ear seen, with a line across its base.
- Chest: white chest patch is a narrow sliver that runs oddly up the front of the neck.

## Differences
- a has no neck; b has a clear neck with the head raised on it.
- a shows one front leg and two overlapping hind legs; b shows four legs.
- a's front leg is a separate cylinder with a visible top end; b's front legs grow from the chest.
- b's legs are longer and thinner than a's.
- a's tail is a straight upright stick; b's tail curves in an S.
- a's eye is a plain black dot; b's eye is yellow with a slit pupil.
- b has a white chest patch; a has a small white muzzle patch only.
- b's ear is larger and taller than a's.
- b has more whisker strokes than a.
- b's body is longer and slimmer; a's is boxy.

## Anatomy
- a: head detached-looking with no neck; body boxy; front leg oddly joined (pasted cylinder) and only one front leg; hind legs thick and merged; tail stiff stick; second ear missing.
- b: head and neck join well; body fine but slim; legs too long and thin; small seams at the head and between the front legs; tail fine.

## Pose
- a: stiff, like a toy on posts; the missing neck and pasted front leg make it look assembled rather than standing.
- b: reads as a natural alert cat standing and looking ahead, though the long legs make it slightly leggy.

## Better drawn
b. It has a neck, four properly joined legs and a natural tail, while a's head sits on the body with no neck and its single front leg looks stuck on.
