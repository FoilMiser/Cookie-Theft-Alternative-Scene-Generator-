# Review fig 15

## 1. Who and what
An orange cat walking to the right with eyes closed (content look), tail raised. Both read the same; a has one front paw lifted mid-step, b looks more like standing stiffly.

## 2. Faults in a
- Legs: long and thin, giving a slightly stilted look.
- Front far leg: the lifted paw is small and bent sharply; a little hard to read.
- Ears: the two ears are drawn as overlapping black spikes at the top of the head; slightly messy.
- Head: tilted up so the face points upward, which is a little odd for walking.

## 3. Faults in b
- Head: a plain circle sitting on top of the body with almost no neck.
- Ears: only one small ear.
- Front near leg: at the top where it joins the chest there are small knobbly bumps, like a separate piece stuck on.
- Body: a straight-edged box shape, no chest curve.
- Tail: straight thick stick going up, with no curve.
- Legs: straight even-width tubes.

## 4. Differences
- a's head joins the body through a neck; b's head is a circle sitting on the body.
- a has two pointed ears; b has one small ear.
- a has a white chest patch; b has none, but has a white muzzle.
- a's tail curves up with a hooked tip; b's tail is a straight stick.
- a has one front paw raised; b has all four feet down.
- b has knobbly bumps at the top of the front near leg; a's leg joins smoothly.
- a's body has a curved chest and belly; b's body is a box.
- a's head is tilted upward; b's faces forward.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head and neck joined well; shoulders and hips shaped; legs a little too long and thin; feet small; tail fine.
- b: no real neck, head looks stuck on; one ear; knobby join at front leg; body boxy; legs tube-like; tail too stiff and straight.

## 6. Pose
- a: reads as a natural cat walk with a lifted paw and raised tail; slightly high-stepping but cat-like.
- b: reads as stiff, like a toy cat standing, because of the boxy body, straight tail and tube legs.

## 7. Better drawn
a. Its head, neck, chest and curved tail look like a real cat in motion, while b is boxy with a stuck-on head and a knobbly leg join.
