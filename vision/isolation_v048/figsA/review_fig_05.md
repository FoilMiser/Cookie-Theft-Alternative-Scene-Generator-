# Review fig 05

## 1. Who and what
A brown dog with a red collar/bandana, sitting (in b clearly sitting on its haunches; in a it reads more as a half-standing, leaning dog with no clear sit), eyes closed, tongue slightly out.

## 2. Faults in a
- Body: the back slopes in one long straight ramp from neck to tail, like a seal or dinosaur; there is no hip or haunch shape.
- Hind legs: only a stubby hind foot pokes out under the body; no thigh or folded hind leg, so it is unclear whether the dog sits or stands.
- Tail: merges into the body as a tapering continuation of the back rather than a separate tail.
- Front legs: very long, thin and straight, like stilts.
- Collar: drawn as a large angular red bib/bandana that sits oddly behind the ear.
- Neck: very long and thick, giraffe-like.
- Muzzle: small, pinched and pointed upward.

## 3. Faults in b
- Hind leg: the haunch is drawn but the thigh line and the lower hind paw are a little muddled where they overlap the front paw.
- Far hind leg: a dark brown shape at the back of the front leg reads as an extra leg, slightly confusing.
- Muzzle: long, boxy snout block, slightly mechanical.
- Tail: thin and flat on the ground, a bit stiff.

## 4. Differences
- Body posture: a has a long ramp-like back with no haunch; b has a proper sitting dog with a rounded haunch and folded hind leg.
- Neck: a has a much longer neck; b's neck is shorter and more natural.
- Collar: a has a large angular bandana/bib; b has a narrow band collar.
- Muzzle: a has a short, pointed, upturned muzzle; b has a longer, squarer muzzle.
- Ear: a's ear is larger and placed lower on the head; b's ear is smaller.
- Eye/brow: a shows a closed-eye arc with lashes; b shows a heavier brow line.
- Tail: a's tail tapers out of the back as one shape; b's tail is a separate thin tail lying on the ground.
- Front legs: a's are longer and thinner; b's are thicker with visible paws.
- Hind paws: a shows only a small stub paw; b shows a full folded hind leg and paw.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head acceptable but muzzle small; neck too long; body lacks any hip or haunch; hind leg essentially missing; front legs too long and thin; tail fused into body. Overall proportions wrong.
- b: Head, neck, body and front legs in reasonable proportion; haunch and hind leg present though the overlap is slightly muddled; tail joined properly. Largely right.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads as awkward and unclear: not convincingly sitting or standing, the body slumps diagonally like a seal.
- b: Reads naturally as a dog sitting upright, front legs straight and hind leg folded.

## 7. Better drawn
b — it reads clearly as a sitting dog with a proper haunch, hind leg and separate tail, whereas a has a ramp-shaped body with no hind quarters and an over-long neck.
