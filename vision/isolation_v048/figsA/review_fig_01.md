# Review fig 01

## Who and what
A white goat with one dark curved horn, a grey beard and a red collar, standing still in side view facing right. Both versions read the same way.

## Faults in a
- Beard: a grey pointed triangle hanging from the jaw, looking like a stuck-on spike or tooth.
- Horn: only one horn seen, a thick dark arc.
- Neck: thick, almost as wide as the head.
- Legs: rather long and thin, especially the far grey legs.
- Hind leg: the near hind leg is slightly kinked at the hock and thin at the bottom.

## Faults in b
- Legs: only two legs are shown, one front and one hind; the far legs are missing, so the goat looks two-legged.
- Hind leg: thin, bent with an awkward kink and a white sliver inside the outline (doubled line).
- Body: a long flat-topped block with a squared rump.
- Neck: longer and narrower; the collar sits low and tilted on it.
- Beard: same stuck-on grey triangle.
- Hooves: small and stubby compared with a.

## Differences
- a shows four legs (two near white, two far grey); b shows only two.
- b's hind leg is thinner and more kinked, with a doubled inner line; a's hind leg is cleaner.
- b's body is longer, flatter on top and squarer at the rump; a's body is rounder.
- b's neck is longer and thinner; a's is thicker.
- b's collar sits lower and more tilted on the neck.
- a's hooves are larger and darker; b's are smaller.
- a's tail sticks up as a small flag; b's tail is a short stub at the rump corner.
- The head and beard are drawn almost the same in both.

## Anatomy
- a: head and horn fine; neck a little thick; body well proportioned; four legs joined properly, slightly long; hooves fine; tail fine; beard looks stuck on.
- b: head fine; neck long; body blocky; only two legs, so the far legs are missing; hind leg too thin and oddly kinked; hooves small; tail stub; beard looks stuck on.

## Pose
- a: reads as a natural goat standing on four legs.
- b: stiff and odd; with only two legs it looks like a cut-out, and the kinked hind leg looks weak.

## Better drawn
a. It shows all four legs and a better-shaped body, while b is missing the far legs and has a thin, kinked hind leg.
