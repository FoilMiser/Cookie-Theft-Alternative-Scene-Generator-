# Review fig 16

## 1. Who and what
A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar doing a play bow or stretch: front end low, rear up, tail high, eyes closed, tongue tip out. Both read the same; b reads more clearly as a play bow with elbows on the ground.

## 2. Faults in a
- Front legs: one thick leg stretches straight forward along the ground with a grey far leg showing above it as a flat strip; the two read as one fat leg.
- Front leg join: a crease line at the chest where the leg starts looks unfinished.
- Head: round with a very short muzzle; reads more as a puppy or a bear.
- Collar: the blue collar shows as a tilted patch rather than a ring around the neck.
- Tail: very thick at the base, almost as wide as the rump.

## 3. Faults in b
- Hind legs: the near hind leg is fairly thin and straight compared with the body.
- Hind far leg: only a small grey stub with a foot shows.
- Front paws: the two front feet overlap at the tips, slightly muddled.

## 4. Differences
- b's head has a longer muzzle; a's muzzle is short and round.
- b shows two front legs lying along the ground with elbows down; a shows one thick forward leg with a grey strip above it.
- b's collar reads as a ring round the neck; a's is a tilted patch.
- a's tail is thicker at the base; b's is thinner and curved.
- b's back slopes more steeply down to the head (deeper bow).
- a's ear is bigger and rounder; b's ear is narrower.
- b's tongue sits under the muzzle tip; a's tongue is at the front of a short snout.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head round, muzzle too short; collar patch odd; front legs too thick and merged, with an unfinished crease at the join; hind legs reasonable; tail too thick at base.
- b: head and muzzle well shaped; neck and collar fine; front legs and elbows read correctly; hind legs a little thin; tail fine.

## 6. Pose
- a: reads as a stretch, but awkward: the front looks like it is sliding forward on one thick leg.
- b: reads naturally as a dog's play bow, elbows down and rear raised.

## 7. Better drawn
b. The head, collar and front legs are clearer and the play bow reads naturally, while a's front legs merge into one thick leg and its muzzle is stubby.
