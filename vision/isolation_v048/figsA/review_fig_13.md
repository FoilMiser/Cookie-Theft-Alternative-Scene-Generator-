# Review fig 13

## Who and what
A white dog with a brown ear and brown patches and a blue collar, sitting on its haunches and looking up and to the right. a reads clearly as sitting; b reads less clearly, the hind leg looking almost as if standing.

## Faults in a
- Front legs: long and thin, so the chest stands very tall.
- Far front leg: shaded grey and a little detached from the body outline at the top.
- Tail: small curl lying on the ground at the back, somewhat lost under the haunch.
- Collar: a flat band that ends at the back of the neck under the ear rather than going round.
- Haunch: the brown patch on the haunch sits near the bottom edge and the hind foot is just a lump in front of it.

## Faults in b
- Tail base: a messy black scribble and brown smear near where the tail joins the rump, looking unfinished.
- Hind leg: drawn as a long, nearly straight leg reaching down to the ground, so the haunch does not fold and the dog looks half-standing.
- Far front leg: grey shading spreads up into the chest in a large grey wedge.
- Collar: very large and blocky, more like a scarf than a collar.
- Neck: longer and thinner than in a.
- Chin: a brown patch under the chin looks like a stray blot.
- Feet: four separate feet in a row at the bottom, hard to tell which is front or back.

## Differences
- b's neck is longer and the head is set higher and further back.
- b's collar is much larger and blockier than a's thin band.
- a's tail is a short curl on the ground; b's tail is longer and stretches out to the left.
- b has a black scribble and brown smear at the tail base; a has a clean brown spot on the haunch.
- a's haunch is folded with the foot tucked forward; b's hind leg is long and straight down.
- b's grey shading on the far front leg spreads up into the chest; in a it stays on the leg.
- a has a smile line on the muzzle; b has a small mouth and a brown patch under the chin.
- b's ear is slightly larger and hangs further back.

## Anatomy
- a: head and neck fine; front legs a little long and thin; far front leg slightly detached at top; haunch folded and readable; tail small but fine.
- b: neck too long; front legs long; hind leg too long and straight for a sitting dog; grey shading confuses the chest and far leg; tail base messy and oddly joined.

## Pose
- a: reads as a natural dog sitting up attentively, if a little tall in the chest.
- b: awkward; the straight hind leg and stretched tail make it unclear whether the dog is sitting or getting up.

## Better drawn
a. It reads clearly as a sitting dog with clean lines, while b has a scribbled tail base, a confusing straight hind leg and spreading grey shading.
