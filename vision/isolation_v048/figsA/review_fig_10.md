# Review fig 10

## 1. Who and what
A dark blue-grey cat with pink inner ears, eyes closed, lying down asleep; in a it lies stretched low with its face toward the viewer, in b it is curled into a ball with the head tilted and resting on the body.

## 2. Faults in a
- Face: the face is turned to the viewer but the features are tilted and spread out: the two closed eyes sit at different heights and far apart, and the muzzle is low and off to the left of the face's centre.
- Muzzle: the white muzzle patch with pink nose is small and sits almost at the chin line, so the face looks lopsided.
- Body: the body behind the head is a lumpy mass with crumpled interior lines; it is hard to tell haunch from back.
- Hind leg/tail: a pointed shape at the far left reads as either a hind leg or tail, unclear.
- Front paws: two flat paws stick out under the head, one either side, placed like pads rather than legs.
- Head/body join: a jagged dark line where the head overlaps the shoulder looks scribbled.

## 3. Faults in b
- Head: tilted about 30 degrees; the closed eyes are a short vertical dash and a horizontal dash, so the face reads rotated and unclear.
- Muzzle: the white muzzle with two puffed cheeks sits at the very bottom edge of the head, below the eyes, and looks stuck on.
- Ears: small and squashed, the near ear partly hidden in the body outline.
- Body: the curled body is a heavy blob; the paw/tail shape at the top left reads like a sausage or a folded arm.
- Middle: tangled, sketchy lines between the body and head (around the front paws) look unfinished.
- Whiskers: a stray long whisker line runs off below the face.

## 4. Differences
- Pose: a lies stretched low and flat; b is curled into a ball.
- Head angle: a faces the viewer fairly upright; b's head is tilted and rotated.
- Ears: a has large upright pink ears; b has small, partly hidden ears.
- Muzzle: a has a single small round white muzzle; b has a larger two-lobed white muzzle with puffed cheeks.
- Eyes: a has two horizontal closed-eye lines; b has one vertical and one horizontal dash.
- Paws: a shows two front paws on the ground under the head; b shows tucked paws hidden in tangled lines.
- Tail/hind leg: a has a pointed shape at the rear left; b has a rounded sausage shape over the top left of the body.
- Line quality: b has more scribbly, unfinished lines in the middle.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head large relative to body (cartoon-acceptable); facial features misplaced and lopsided; body lumpy with unclear haunch; paws flat pads; tail/hind leg ambiguous.
- b: Head large and rotated; muzzle stuck at the lower edge; ears too small; body a shapeless blob; paws lost in tangled lines; tail/leg shape ambiguous.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads reasonably as a cat lying down asleep, though the face toward the viewer with misplaced features is a bit odd.
- b: Reads as a curled sleeping cat, a natural sleeping pose in idea, but the tilted head and tangled body make it harder to read.

## 7. Better drawn
a — its large upright ears and lying shape make it read more clearly as a sleeping cat, while b's tilted face, squashed ears and tangled middle lines are harder to make out; both have lopsided faces.
