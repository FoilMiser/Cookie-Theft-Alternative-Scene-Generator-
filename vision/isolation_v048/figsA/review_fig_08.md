# Review fig 08

## 1. Who and what
A white goat with dark blue-grey horns and hooves and a red collar, head lowered to the ground grazing or eating; both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Neck: there is almost no neck; the head hangs directly off the front of the chest, below the shoulder line.
- Far front leg: a pale grey leg emerges from behind the collar/head and runs down past the face, oddly placed.
- Beard/chin: a small grey wedge under the chin is detached-looking and unclear.
- Tail: a short upright stub that looks like a thumb sticking up from the rump.
- Body: blocky and slab-like with faint stray interior lines on the shoulder.

## 3. Faults in b
- Neck: same lack of a neck; head tucked under the shoulder.
- Body: very boxy, with a sharp right-angle step under the belly where the hind leg meets the body.
- Hind leg: the near hind leg has an odd bulge at the haunch with a kink; reads lumpy.
- Front legs: straight, parallel, post-like tubes.
- Shoulder: stray scribble/hatching marks at the top of the shoulder and a loose line on the chest look unfinished.
- Beard/chin: same small grey wedge under the chin.
- Tail: upright stub, slightly broader.

## 4. Differences
- Body shape: a is slightly more rounded and leaner; b is boxier with a sharp step under the belly.
- Legs: a's legs are a little longer and slimmer, with a slight angle; b's front legs are straighter and more post-like.
- Hind leg: b's near hind leg has a lumpier haunch outline than a's.
- Shoulder lines: b has stray scribble marks at the top of the shoulder; a has only faint interior lines.
- Head size: b's head is slightly larger relative to the body.
- Collar: b's collar is a thinner, more ring-like band; a's is a wider red band.
- Overall stance: a stands a little taller and longer; b is more compact.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head fine, horns fine, but neck essentially absent; body reasonable; legs plausible lengths; far front leg placement near the face odd; tail stub-like.
- b: Same missing neck; body too boxy with a hard angle under the belly; hind haunch lumpy; front legs stiff tubes; unfinished marks on shoulder.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads acceptably as grazing, though the head looks dropped from the chest rather than reached down on a neck.
- b: Reads as grazing too, but stiffer and more blocky; the straight front legs and boxy body make it look rigid.

## 7. Better drawn
a — both share the missing neck, but a has a cleaner, slightly more natural body and legs, while b has a boxier body, lumpier hind leg and stray unfinished marks on the shoulder.
