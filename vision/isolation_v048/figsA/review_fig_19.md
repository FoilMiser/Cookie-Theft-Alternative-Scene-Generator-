# Review fig 19

## 1. Who and what
A white goat with dark grey-blue curved horns, a small grey beard, a red collar and grey hooves, standing/walking in three-quarter view toward the right with eyes closed, smiling. In a it reads as standing; in b as walking with a front hoof lifted.

## 2. Faults in a
- Legs: four long straight stick legs, the near front leg in the middle of the body looks like a post; legs too long for the body.
- Front legs: the far front leg (grey, right) starts at the chest edge and looks attached to the outline rather than the shoulder.
- Hind quarter: the rear outline has a lumpy bump above the hind leg; the hind leg bends forward awkwardly.
- Body: body is a squarish box with a flat back and a stubby upright tail.
- Neck: long, thin neck, head small for the body.
- Beard: tiny grey triangle tucked under the chin, hard to read.

## 3. Faults in b
- Front legs: the far front leg is lifted with the hoof curled back and the near front leg crosses in front of it; slightly tangled.
- Legs: legs still long and thin, though less stiff.
- Body: rounder and more barrel-like, but the belly line is low and the hind leg join is a smooth bulge without a clear thigh.
- Collar: wide collar sits high, almost under the jaw, partly hiding the neck.

## 4. Differences
- a's legs are all straight and planted; b has one front hoof lifted in a step and the near hind leg stretched back.
- b's body is rounder and fuller; a's is boxier with a lumpy rear outline.
- b's neck is shorter and thicker; a's neck is longer and thinner.
- b's beard is larger and clearly visible; a's beard is tiny.
- b's tail is thinner and more pointed; a's tail is a blunt stub.
- b's eyebrow line is heavier and darker.
- b's collar sits higher on the neck.
- b's horns are slightly thicker.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head small and neck long and thin; body boxy; legs too long and too thin, stick-like; far front leg oddly attached at the chest; hind leg join lumpy; hooves fine; tail stubby.
- b: head and neck in better proportion; body round and solid; legs long-ish but joined more plausibly; lifted front leg tangled with near leg; hooves fine; tail fine.

## 6. Pose
- a: stiff, like a table on four posts; standing but unnatural.
- b: reads as a natural walking step with weight shifting, though the crossing front legs are a little busy.

## 7. Better drawn
b — its body, neck and beard are better proportioned and the walking pose looks natural, whereas a looks stiff and stilt-legged.
