# Review fig 09

## 1. Who and what
A tan/brown dog with a red collar and dark brown ear, walking to the right, tail raised, small red tongue at the mouth. Both versions read the same; a looks more like it is striding forward with head stretched out, b more like a calm, upright walk.

## 2. Faults in a
- Muzzle: long, boxy pale snout reads like a muzzle guard or a held object rather than a dog's nose and jaw.
- Eye: drawn as a closed arc with a heavy brow line above it; reads as squinting or scowling.
- Tongue: small red drop hanging below the snout tip looks detached from the mouth.
- Rear legs: hind leg drawn as a long, sweeping almost straight line with a very long lower section; the far hind leg kinks sharply.
- Far front leg: lifted paw bent back under the body at an odd angle, small and pinched.
- Chest/collar: small white patch under the collar is a loose fragment, not clearly part of the chest.
- Body: belly line sags in a deep curve between front and rear legs; the back is long and flat.

## 3. Faults in b
- Hip: a small notch/fold line on the rear hip (where the near hind leg joins) looks like an unfinished or broken outline.
- Ear: very large dark ear almost covers the side of the head and sits over the collar.
- Tongue: small red tongue at the tip of the snout is barely visible and looks like a smudge.
- Legs: legs are fairly straight and tube-like, with little sign of knee or hock joints on the front legs.
- Paws: rounded paws all look the same, like flat blobs.

## 4. Differences
- Head: a has a longer, boxier, stretched-forward muzzle; b has a short rounded snout.
- Eye: a has a closed eye with a heavy brow line; b has a simple closed curved eye with a lighter brow.
- Ear: b's ear is larger relative to the head.
- Collar: a's collar sits lower and is wider; b's sits higher on the neck.
- Neck/chest: a shows a small white chest patch below the collar; b does not.
- Body: a's body is longer with a deeper sagging belly curve; b's body is more compact and the belly line is straighter.
- Tail: a's tail is thinner and curves more; b's is thicker at the base and straighter.
- Hind legs: a's rear legs are longer with a wide stance and a more angled hock; b's are shorter and more upright.
- Front legs: in a the far front leg is lifted and folded back; in b the far front leg is planted, angled back on the ground.
- Hip outline: b has a notch line on the rear hip that a does not.
- Overall proportions: a looks lankier and taller; b looks shorter-legged and stockier.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head on a short neck is fine in size, but the muzzle is overlong and box-shaped. Body long, belly sagging. Hind legs too long and spindly toward the bottom, far hind leg oddly kinked; far front leg folded back awkwardly with a small pinched paw. Tail thin but well joined. Chest patch loosely attached.
- b: Head, neck and body in reasonable proportion; ear somewhat too large. Legs a little thick and straight but joined to the body sensibly, apart from the notch at the rear hip. Tail thick at the base and well joined. Paws simple blobs.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads as walking, with a lifted front paw that gives motion, but the stretched head, wide rear stance and folded front leg make it look strained, like straining on a lead or stalking.
- b: Reads as a natural, relaxed walk: diagonal legs forward/back, head up, tail up. Slightly stiff because the legs are straight with little joint bending.

## 7. Better drawn
b. Its proportions are more natural and the dog reads clearly as a dog walking; a's boxy muzzle, sagging long body and awkward folded front leg look odder, though b has a small notch fault on the hip.
