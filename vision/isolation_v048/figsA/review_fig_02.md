# Review fig 02

## Who and what
A brown dog with a darker brown ear, round black glasses and a red collar, sitting up on its haunches and holding an open newspaper in both front paws, reading it. Both versions read the same way.

## Faults in a
- Body: the trunk is very long and narrow, like a tall tube from collar to haunch, so the dog looks stretched upward.
- Near front leg: one long unbroken tube from shoulder to paw with no elbow, reaching down to the bottom corner of the paper.
- Far front paw: a small round blob sitting on the top edge of the paper, not clearly joined to any arm.
- Collar: stops at the front of the neck and at the back edge; it reads as a short red strip rather than a band round the neck.
- Tail: long and thin, sweeping out low behind and then up, looking rather like a separate piece stuck to the rump.
- Hind leg and foot: a small lump at the bottom with the foot tucked under; the haunch is hard to read.

## Faults in b
- Back: strongly hunched and rounded, so the dog looks a little stooped.
- Far front paw: still a round blob on the top edge of the paper with no visible arm.
- Belly: a small stray tick mark on the belly below the arm, and a white tick on the near paw, which look like leftover marks.
- Tail: short and pressed flat against the rump, partly hidden; reads as a curl of outline rather than a tail.
- Near front leg: still long and tube-like, without a clear elbow.

## Differences
- a has a long upright, narrow trunk; b has a broader, rounder, hunched back.
- a's tail is long and sweeps out behind; b's tail is short and lies against the rump.
- b's collar is thicker and wraps round the neck more fully; a's collar is a short strip.
- b's head is pushed further forward over the paper, with a larger ear and slightly larger glasses.
- b's hind foot shows two toe lumps more clearly at the bottom; a's hind foot is a single small lump.
- b has small stray marks on the belly and a white tick on the near paw; a has none.
- The newspaper is tilted slightly differently and sits a little lower in b.

## Anatomy
- a: head and neck fine; trunk too long and thin; near arm too long with no elbow; far paw detached-looking; hips and hind leg small and unclear; tail long and somewhat stuck on.
- b: head and neck fine, head well forward; trunk in better proportion but back over-rounded; near arm still long without elbow; far paw detached-looking; haunch and hind foot read better; tail too short and flattened.

## Pose
- a: reads as a dog sitting up reading, but the stretched upright trunk makes it stiff and odd, more like a standing tube than a sitting dog.
- b: reads more naturally as a dog sitting hunched over a paper reading it; the stoop suits the action, though the back is slightly exaggerated.

## Better drawn
b. Its body proportions and sitting posture read more naturally as a dog reading, while a's body is stretched too tall and thin; b's only extras are small stray marks and a squashed tail.
