# Review of pair 12

## 1. Who and what
A brown dog with a red collar, sitting up on its haunches and "begging" with both front paws raised, head tilted up, tongue out, eyes shut. Both versions read the same way, though a reads much less clearly as a sitting dog.

## 2. Faults in a
- Body/torso: drawn as a very long, thin, tube-like column, like a sausage; far too narrow and tall for a dog sitting up.
- Neck: the head sits on top of the thin column with almost no neck; head looks perched on a pole.
- Collar: the red collar is drawn as a flat, jagged, cape-like shape on one side rather than a band around the neck; it has a white gap/notch where it meets the paw.
- Front legs: the raised forelegs come out of the side of the column as a bent tube with a lumpy, mitten-like paw; the shoulder join is unclear and a second leg is barely visible behind (a brown patch).
- Hindquarters: the base is a shapeless blob; the hind legs and feet are not readable, with a small white triangle gap and stray marks at the bottom left.
- Head: the ear is a large dark oval that dominates the head side; overall acceptable but the head looks small against the long body.
- Overall: looks unfinished/distorted, more like a stretched rubber toy than a dog.

## 3. Faults in b
- Front paws: the two raised paws are small, stubby and a little lumpy; the near foreleg is only suggested by lines on the chest rather than drawn as a clear leg.
- Chest/body: a few loose interior strokes on the chest/foreleg area look sketchy.
- Hind feet: the back foot is a flat darker brown blob at the base, slightly disconnected-looking from the haunch.
- Neck: slightly long and straight under the head, but acceptable.
- Otherwise no serious faults.

## 4. Differences
- Body shape: a is a long thin tube; b is a full, rounded, believable sitting-dog body.
- Proportion of head to body: in a the head is small on a long column; in b the head and body are balanced.
- Collar: in a a jagged flat red shape like a cape; in b a proper red band around the neck.
- Front legs: in a a single bent tube with a mitten paw from the side of the column; in b two short paws held together in front of the chest.
- Hindquarters: in a a shapeless blob with a white gap; in b a clear haunch with a hind foot on the ground.
- Ear: in a a larger, darker rounded oval; in b a smaller, more teardrop-shaped ear.
- Muzzle: in b the white muzzle is slightly longer and more open; tongue similar in both.
- Overall height/width: a is narrower and more stretched vertically; b is wider and more compact.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head reasonable but small for the body; neck essentially missing; torso far too long and too thin; shoulders not defined, foreleg oddly joined to the side of the column; second foreleg only a hint; hips and hind legs not readable, feet missing/blob-like. Proportions look wrong overall.
- b: Head, neck and body look right for a cartoon dog sitting up; shoulders and forelegs a little vague but paws sit in a believable place; haunch and hind leg read correctly; hind foot slightly flat and blob-like. Proportions look right.

## 6. Pose
- a: Stiff and awkward; the dog seems to be standing on a stretched tube rather than sitting on its haunches, so the begging pose does not read naturally.
- b: Natural; it reads clearly as a dog sitting back on its haunches with front paws raised and nose up, a familiar begging pose.

## 7. Better drawn
b. It has a believable dog body with clear haunches, collar and raised paws, whereas a is stretched into a thin tube with an unreadable base and a cape-like collar.
