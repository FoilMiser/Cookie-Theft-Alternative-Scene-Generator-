# Review fig 06

## 1. Who and what
A brown dog with floppy dark-brown ear and red collar, standing still in side view facing right, tail up. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Legs: all four legs are very long and thin for the body, giving a stilt-like, spidery look.
- Hind legs: the near hind leg bends forward strongly at the hock, so the dog looks crouched or sagging at the back.
- Belly: a dark brown shape (far hind leg) shows between near hind leg and belly and reads as a patch rather than a leg.
- Head: muzzle is a pale box stuck on the front of a round head; mouth line is a plain gap.
- Collar: very wide and tilted, sits low like a band around the chest.

## 3. Faults in b
- Hind quarters: a small broken/jagged notch in the outline where the hind leg meets the body (above the knee), looks like a drawing glitch.
- Far legs: the far front and far hind legs are almost hidden; only small brown toe shapes peek out behind the near feet, so the dog looks close to two-legged.
- Hind leg: thigh is thin and starts low, so the back end looks weak compared with the deep body.
- Body: body is long and boxy with a straight belly line; little shape at chest or waist.

## 4. Differences
- Legs in a are much longer; b's body sits lower and is deeper relative to the legs.
- In a the far legs are clearly visible as darker brown legs; in b they show only as small toe tips.
- In a the hind leg has a strong crouching bend; in b the hind leg is straighter with a milder bend.
- a's ear is smaller and placed lower on the head; b's ear is larger and covers more of the head.
- a's muzzle is a larger, squarer pale block; b's muzzle is smaller and rounder.
- a's eye is larger; b's eye is smaller.
- a's collar is wider and lower; b's collar is narrower and higher on the neck.
- b has a jagged notch at the top of the hind leg; a has a smooth haunch with a curved thigh line.
- a's tail is thinner and curls further back; b's tail is thicker at the base.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head and neck join fine; body reasonable; legs too long and too thin for the body; hind leg oddly bent forward; feet large blobs but acceptable. Tail fine.
- b: head, ear and neck look right; body long and deep, legs shorter and more dog-like in length; hind thigh too thin and with a broken outline at its join to the body; far legs nearly missing; feet fine. Tail fine.

## 6. Pose
- a: reads as standing, but stiff and slightly odd — the long legs and crouched hind leg make it look like it is stalking or sinking at the rear.
- b: reads as a natural standing dog, somewhat stiff (front leg dead straight) but plausible.

## 7. Better drawn
b — its proportions read as a normal dog and the pose is a natural stand, though the notch at the hip and the hidden far legs should be fixed; a's stilt legs look more wrong.
