# Review fig 20

## 1. Who and what
A white dog with brown spots, a brown ear and a blue collar, walking to the right with eyes closed and a small pink tongue tip showing, looking content. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Head: muzzle is short and rounded; nose sits on the top-front with the mouth/tongue tucked under, slightly squashed.
- Ear: very large brown ear covers much of the side of the head and overlaps the collar.
- Legs: legs are fairly straight and tube-like; the near front leg is stiff.
- Hind leg: the near hind leg joins the body with a sharp corner under the big spot.
- Tail: straight and stick-like, pointing up and slightly back.

## 3. Faults in b
- Front legs: the far front leg is lifted with the paw curled back and sits high, close to the near leg; reads slightly tangled.
- Hind leg: near hind leg is long and stretched far back, making the back end look drawn out.
- Head: long narrow muzzle with a thin mouth line; head looks a bit flat on top-front.
- Outline: rear haunch outline around the big spot has a small kink where leg meets body.

## 4. Differences
- b has a longer, pointed muzzle with a visible mouth line; a has a short rounded muzzle.
- b's ear is smaller and narrower; a's ear is bigger.
- b's stride is longer: far front paw lifted, near hind leg stretched back; a's legs are more upright and shorter in stride.
- b's tail curves forward at the tip; a's tail is straight.
- b's body is slightly longer and the head is set a little higher.
- The spot pattern is placed slightly differently (b's small rear spot is separate from the big haunch spot; in a the two rear spots nearly touch).
- b's collar is set further down the neck and shows on both sides of the ear.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head round with short muzzle, ear oversized; neck and body fine; legs a bit straight and tube-like but correct length; hind leg join sharp; feet fine; tail stiff.
- b: head and muzzle more dog-like; body fine; legs reasonable length though the stretched hind leg looks long; lifted front paw joins awkwardly close to the near leg; feet fine; tail natural.

## 6. Pose
- a: reads as walking but stiff, like a slow shuffle with posts for legs.
- b: reads clearly as a natural trotting/walking step with a lifted paw and swinging stride, though the leg overlap is a little busy.

## 7. Better drawn
b — the stride, muzzle and curved tail read as a natural walking dog, while a is stiffer and its ear is oversized.
