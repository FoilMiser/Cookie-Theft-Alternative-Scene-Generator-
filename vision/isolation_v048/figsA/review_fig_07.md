# Review fig 07

## 1. Who and what
A grey-blue cat walking or creeping to the right, low to the ground, tail held out behind. b reads more as stalking (lower, front leg reaching far forward).

## 2. Faults in a
- Head: a plain circle stuck onto the front of the body with no neck; looks like a separate ball.
- Ears: only one thin ear shows, standing at the top-back of the head.
- Front legs: the near front leg has a small knob/lump at the chest where it joins, like a separate piece.
- Front far leg: starts from mid-belly rather than from the shoulder.
- Muzzle: tiny white patch, very small whiskers; face hard to read.
- Legs: all four are even-width tubes with blob feet.

## 3. Faults in b
- Front near leg: very long and thin, stretched almost flat forward; looks overextended.
- Front far leg: short and tucked under the chest, partly hidden.
- Ears: the far ear is just a second black spike merging with the near ear.
- Hind legs: thin and angular, with sharp kinks at the hocks.

## 4. Differences
- a's head is a separate circle with no neck; b's head flows into a neck.
- a has one ear; b has two pointed ears, one with pink inside.
- a's eye is a large black oval; b's eye is yellow with a dark pupil.
- b has a white chest patch; a has none.
- b's whiskers are longer and more visible; a has a tiny white muzzle.
- a's body is higher off the ground; b's is lower and more crouched.
- a's tail is thicker and runs straight back; b's is thinner and joins higher.
- a's front near leg has a knob at the chest; b's front near leg is stretched far forward.
- a's legs are thicker tubes; b's are thinner and more angled.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head detached-looking, no neck; one ear; knob at front leg join; far front leg joins too far back on belly; legs tube-like but reasonable length; tail thick, fine.
- b: head and neck joined well; ears and eye good; near front leg too long and thin; far front leg too short; hind legs thin with sharp kinks; tail fine.

## 6. Pose
- a: reads as a walking cat, but stiff, like a toy on stiff legs, because of the ball head and tube legs.
- b: reads as a cat stalking or creeping, more natural and cat-like overall, though the overstretched front leg is awkward.

## 7. Better drawn
b. The head, neck, ears and face are far better and the body moves like a cat; its overlong front leg is a lesser fault than a's detached ball head and knobbed leg join.
