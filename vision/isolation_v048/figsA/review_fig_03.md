# Review fig 03

## 1. Who and what
A brown dog with a red collar and white muzzle, lying on its belly asleep (eyes shut), head resting on its front paws. Both versions read the same; b looks longer and flatter.

## 2. Faults in a
- Body: a few small stray dark dashes on the flank and belly look like leftover marks.
- Hip / hind leg: the haunch is outlined by loose crease lines that do not clearly close into a leg shape.
- Ear: the dark brown ear sits low on the neck, behind the head, more like a patch than an ear attached to the skull.
- Face: a second eye line pokes out at the far right edge of the head, which looks odd on a side view.
- Head: very large and round compared to the body.

## 3. Faults in b
- Hind legs / belly: the underside is a jumble of short lines and a small lighter-brown wedge; the hind legs cannot be read as legs.
- Front legs: only one front paw is clearly shown, and it is hard to tell where it joins the body.
- Collar: almost hidden, only a small red triangle shows behind the ear.
- Face: a second eye line again pokes out at the right edge of the head.
- Body: long, flat, tube-like, with little shape at shoulder or hip.

## 4. Differences
- b's body is longer and lower; a's is shorter and humped higher at the shoulders.
- a shows two front paws (one lighter, one darker brown); b shows one clear front paw.
- a's red collar is clearly visible under the chin; in b it is mostly hidden behind the ear.
- b's near ear is a big brown flap hanging over the neck, and a second ear shows at the back of the head; a shows one ear placed low on the neck.
- a's hind foot shows as a round paw at the rear; b's hindquarters are a rounded lump with muddled lines underneath.
- a has small stray dashes on the body; b has fewer but has the jumbled underside.
- The head in b sits lower, resting more directly on the paw.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head too large and round; neck hidden by collar (fine); ear placed too low, on the neck; front paws read clearly; hind leg is drawn but its outline is loose; body thick but plausible for a puppy.
- b: head large but better connected through the ear; body overlong and tube-like; hind legs unreadable (muddled lines); only one forepaw; no clear shoulder or hip.

## 6. Pose
- a: reads naturally as a dog sleeping with chin on its paws; slightly hunched but believable.
- b: reads as a lying, sleeping dog, but flatter and less defined; the muddled underside makes it look like a stuffed toy rather than a resting body.

## 7. Better drawn
a. Its legs and paws can actually be read and the collar is clear; b's hindquarters and underside are muddled, though a has stray marks and a low ear to fix.
