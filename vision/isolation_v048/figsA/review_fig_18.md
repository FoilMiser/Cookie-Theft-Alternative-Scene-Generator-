# Review fig 18

## 1. Who and what
An orange cat, eyes closed, sitting upright with its head raised; a reads clearly as a sitting cat, b reads more as a cat half-standing or leaning with no clear sit.

## 2. Faults in a
- Head: the head is small and tilted sharply upward; the muzzle points to the sky and the face is squeezed into the top-right corner of the head.
- Muzzle/whiskers: the black nose and whiskers form a small tangle at the tip, hard to read.
- Ear: only one ear clearly visible, tall and narrow.
- Hind leg: the haunch is drawn but the hind paw line under it overlaps the front paws in a slightly muddled way.
- Neck: long and slender, slightly giraffe-like but acceptable for a cat.

## 3. Faults in b
- Head/neck: the head is a separate round ball sitting on top of the body with a visible gap/notch; no neck join, it looks detached.
- Body: the back runs as one long straight ramp from shoulder to tail with no haunch or hip shape.
- Hind legs: only a stubby hind foot pokes out under the body; no thigh or folded hind leg, so it is unclear whether the cat sits or stands.
- Tail: fused into the body as a tapering continuation of the back rather than a separate tail.
- Front legs: long, straight and post-like.
- Chest: a small stray curved mark on the chest looks unfinished.
- Ear: small, only one visible.

## 4. Differences
- Body posture: a has a proper sitting cat with a rounded haunch; b has a long ramp-shaped body without a haunch.
- Head: a's head is smaller, tilted upward and joined by a neck; b's head is a larger round ball, level, sitting detached on the body.
- Neck: a has a long slender neck; b has none.
- Chest: a has a white chest patch; b has none (only a stray mark).
- Muzzle: a's muzzle points upward with a black nose; b's has a small white muzzle patch pointing forward.
- Tail: a's tail is separate, lying on the ground; b's tail tapers out of the back as one shape.
- Hind legs: a shows a folded hind leg and paw; b shows only a stub paw.
- Ears: a's ear is tall and large; b's is smaller.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Body, legs, haunch and tail in reasonable proportion; neck long but plausible; head a little small and very upturned; hind paw overlap slightly muddled. Largely right.
- b: Head detached from body with no neck; body lacks hip or haunch; hind leg essentially missing; front legs long and stiff; tail fused into body. Proportions wrong.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads naturally as a cat sitting up, though the sharply upturned head is a little odd.
- b: Reads as awkward: neither clearly sitting nor standing, the body slopes diagonally like a seal and the head floats on top.

## 7. Better drawn
a — it reads as a proper sitting cat with a haunch, folded hind leg, neck and separate tail, whereas b has a detached ball head, no hind quarters and a tail fused into a ramp-shaped body.
