# Review fig 09

## 1. Who and what
A light-skinned adult man (workman) in an orange cap, orange high-visibility vest with white stripes, blue trousers and dark shoes, standing and holding a teal hose or pipe diagonally in both hands with a yellow nozzle/tip at the top. In a he is seen in profile looking at the hose; in b his face is turned more towards the viewer with an open mouth, as if speaking.

## 2. Faults in a
- Near arm: the grey-blue sleeve is a flat blob stuck on the vest at chest height; the upper arm comes straight out of the chest rather than from the shoulder.
- Far arm: only a thin forearm appears from behind the vest; no visible upper arm or shoulder, so the lower hand looks attached to the hip.
- Upper hand: wraps the hose top awkwardly; the yellow nozzle pokes out above the hand like a separate piece.
- Torso: vest is short and boxy; body looks thin compared with the head and cap.
- Legs: very long, thin and stiff, almost parallel straight tubes; trousers out of proportion to the short torso.
- Feet: flat dark slabs, the back foot overlapping the front.

## 3. Faults in b
- Near arm: same flat sleeve blob on the chest, arm starts from the chest front not the shoulder.
- Far arm: same thin forearm appearing from behind the vest with no upper arm.
- Face: the face is turned toward the viewer while the hands work the hose to the side, so he is not looking at what he is holding; open mouth looks a little surprised.
- Legs: same long stiff tubes; front knee slightly bent but still wooden.
- Feet: flat slabs.

## 4. Differences
- Face: a is a profile with one eye and a closed mouth; b shows two eyes and an open mouth, facing more frontally.
- Eyebrow/expression: a has a slight frown looking at the hose; b looks up/out with a surprised or talking look.
- Cap brim: a's brim sticks out a little further to the right.
- Vest: b's vest is slightly wider and longer.
- Legs: b's front leg shows a slight bend; a's legs are straighter.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head large but acceptable for cartoon style; neck short; near arm oddly joined to the front of the chest; far arm detached-looking (no upper arm); hands simple but readable; hips high, legs too long and thin; feet simple.
- b: Same arm joins (near arm from the chest, far upper arm missing); head and neck fine; legs too long and thin; feet simple.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads as a man examining or operating a hose, eyes on the nozzle, which is natural; but the body is upright and stiff with locked legs.
- b: Same holding action, but looking away from the hose makes it read less naturally; legs a touch less rigid.

## 7. Better drawn
a — the head turned towards the hose makes the action read clearly; the body and arm faults are the same in both.
