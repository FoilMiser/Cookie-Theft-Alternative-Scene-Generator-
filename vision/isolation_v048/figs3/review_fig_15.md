# Review fig 15

## 1. Who and what
A man with short brown hair and a small moustache, in a red T-shirt, blue trousers and dark shoes, walking to the right with arms swinging; in a the head is in side view, in b the face is turned towards the viewer with an open mouth.

## 2. Faults in a
- Near arm and sleeve: the near sleeve bulges forward at the shoulder and the arm seems to come out of the front of the chest, making the shoulder look oddly joined.
- Hands: both hands are small lumps without fingers.
- Moustache: the moustache is a small brown smudge below the nose and could read as dirt.
- Legs: the legs are long and thin, and the back leg is stretched far behind.
- Shoes: the shoes are dark flat wedges.

## 3. Faults in b
- Near arm and sleeve: same bulging sleeve and odd shoulder join as a.
- Hands: same fingerless lumps.
- Head: the face is turned to the viewer on a side-view walking body, so the head looks twisted round.
- Legs: same long, thin legs with the back leg far behind.
- Shoes: same dark wedges.

## 4. Differences
- Head turn: a is in side view (one eye, ear shown); b is turned towards the viewer (two eyes, no ear seen).
- Eyebrows: b has eyebrows; a has none.
- Moustache: in b the moustache is larger and clearly a moustache; in a it is a small smudge.
- Mouth: b has an open red mouth; a has no clear mouth.
- Hair: in b the hair is drawn from the front with a straight fringe; in a it is seen from the side with the ear below it.
- Body, arms, trousers and shoes look the same.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head and neck fine; near shoulder oddly joined because of the bulging sleeve; arms fine in length; hands fingerless; hips fine; legs long and thin; feet fine.
- b: Head twisted towards viewer on a side-view neck; same odd near-shoulder join; hands fingerless; legs long and thin; feet fine.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads clearly as walking, with arms and legs swinging in opposition; the head in side view matches the body, so it looks natural apart from the shoulder.
- b: Also reads as walking; turning the face to the viewer and opening the mouth makes him look as if he is talking or greeting someone, but the head looks forced round compared with the body.

## 7. Better drawn
b, narrowly: the face with two eyes, eyebrows and a clear moustache is easier to read as a man, whereas a's moustache looks like a smudge; a's head matches the walking body better, so the gain is small.
