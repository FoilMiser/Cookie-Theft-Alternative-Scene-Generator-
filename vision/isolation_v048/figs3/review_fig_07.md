# Review fig 07

## 1. Who and what
A light-skinned young man or teenager with brown hair, in a red cap, blue hooded jacket, dark grey trousers and white trainers, walking forward and pointing ahead and upward with one straight arm. In a he is in profile with a closed mouth; in b his face is turned more towards the viewer with an open mouth, as if calling out about what he is pointing at.

## 2. Faults in a
- Pointing arm: starts from the front of the chest, not the shoulder; the sleeve seems to come out of the middle of the jacket.
- Pointing hand: a flat, thin wedge or paddle shape, with no clear pointing finger.
- Back arm: thin, comes from behind the lower back at waist height, too low for a shoulder join; hand is a blob.
- Ear: large and set far back on the head.
- Neck: thin and fairly long between the head and the hood.
- Legs: long; the rear foot is turned and the back leg looks a little twisted.
- Jacket: short and boxy, waist sits high.

## 3. Faults in b
- Pointing arm: same join at the front of the chest rather than the shoulder.
- Pointing hand: same flat paddle wedge, no clear finger.
- Back arm: same low join behind the waist, blob hand.
- Face: turned to the viewer while the arm points to the side, so the head and the pointing direction disagree somewhat.
- Legs: long; same slightly twisted back leg and turned rear foot.

## 4. Differences
- Face: a is a profile with one eye and a small closed mouth; b shows two eyes and an open, talking mouth in three-quarter view.
- Ear: a's ear is large and prominent; b's is smaller and partly hidden under the cap.
- Cap: b's brim is a little shorter and the cap sits slightly differently.
- Neck: a's neck is thinner and longer; b's is shorter.
- Body, arms, legs and clothes: no other difference seen.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head and cap fine; neck slightly long and thin; pointing arm oddly joined at the chest; back arm joined too low; hands crude (paddle, blob); hips high, legs long; feet fine.
- b: Head and neck better proportioned; same oddly joined arms and crude hands; legs long; feet fine.

## 6. Pose
- a: Walking and pointing reads clearly; the profile looks where he points, which is natural, though the straight arm from the chest is stiff.
- b: Walking and pointing reads clearly, and the open mouth adds "look over there"; but the face turned away from the pointing direction is a little less natural.

## 7. Better drawn
no difference — a's gaze matches the pointing direction while b has better neck and ear proportions; the body and arm faults are the same in both.
