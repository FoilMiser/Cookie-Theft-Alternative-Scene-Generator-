# Review fig 02

## 1. Who and what
A young woman (or teenage girl) with long dark hair, in a purple T-shirt, teal trousers and dark shoes, standing still with arms hanging and looking to the side; in a she has a slight smile, in b her mouth is open as if speaking or surprised.

## 2. Faults in a
- Neck: the neck is fairly long and thick and the head sits forward on it.
- Hands: both hands are closed lumps with no fingers drawn; they look like fists.
- Arms: the arms hang straight and stiff down the sides.
- Legs: the legs are long and the trousers make them look like straight tubes, with no knees shown.
- Body: the shoulders are narrow and the torso is short compared with the long legs.
- Hair: the long hair falls in one dark mass down the back, merging with the shoulder.

## 3. Faults in b
- Hands: same fingerless fist hands.
- Arms: same stiff straight arms.
- Legs: legs look even longer, straight tubes with no knees.
- Body: T-shirt shorter, so the torso looks shorter still against the legs.
- Mouth: the open red mouth is small and set to one side of the face, which looks a little odd.
- Neck: short and partly hidden by hair.

## 4. Differences
- Mouth: a has a small closed smile; b has an open red mouth.
- Eyebrows: b has eyebrows drawn; a has none.
- Face: in b the face is turned a bit more to the viewer, with a fuller cheek; in a it is closer to profile.
- Neck: a shows a longer neck; b's neck is shorter.
- T-shirt: in a the T-shirt is longer, reaching lower down the hips; in b it ends higher.
- Legs: in b the trousers start higher, so the legs look longer.
- Hair: in b the hair has a shorter fringe and shows more of the forehead and ear area.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head fine; neck slightly long; shoulders narrow; arms in proportion but stiff; hands are fingerless fists; hips fine; legs long but acceptable; feet fine.
- b: Head fine, neck short; shoulders narrow; arms stiff; hands fingerless fists; torso short and legs long, a bit out of proportion; feet fine.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads as someone standing and glancing to the side; natural enough but stiff, with arms straight down and fists closed.
- b: Same stiff standing pose; the open mouth makes her look as though she is reacting or calling out, which gives a bit more life.

## 7. Better drawn
a: the body proportions are more balanced (longer T-shirt, less stretched legs) and the face is tidy; b's open mouth adds expression but sits awkwardly and the legs look over-long.
