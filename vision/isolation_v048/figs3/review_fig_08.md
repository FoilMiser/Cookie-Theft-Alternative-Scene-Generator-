# Review fig 08

## 1. Who and what
A man (dark skin, short dark hair, moustache and short beard, green T-shirt, khaki trousers, brown shoes) standing turned to the viewer's right, holding a long rake in both hands with the rake head on the ground by his feet, raking. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Trousers: a faint lighter rectangle and a lighter patch appear on the front of the trousers near the hip and thigh, like an unfinished shading mark.
- Head: large and round, sits on a short neck.
- Upper hand: the hand gripping the handle near the shoulder is a bulky blob and the forearm folds back tightly, hard to read.
- Far arm/shoulder: the far arm is hidden; the upper hand appears from behind the near shoulder with no visible arm.
- Rake: the handle is almost vertical, with the head beside the feet rather than reaching out in front, so the raking action is weak.
- Feet: both shoes point the same way and the near foot is hidden behind the rake head.

## 3. Faults in b
- Upper hand: same bulky blob near the shoulder and unclear far arm.
- Rake: same nearly vertical handle with the head beside the feet.
- Eyes: one eye looks half-closed/squinting, giving a sleepy look.
- Feet: as in a.

## 4. Differences
- Trousers: a has the faint lighter rectangle and patch on the trousers; b's trousers are a clean even colour.
- Head: b's head is slightly smaller and more oval, a's rounder.
- Face: b's eyes look more downcast/half-closed and the beard is drawn more like stubble; a's moustache and beard are darker and fuller.
- Neck: b's neck is a little longer.
- Torso: b's T-shirt is slightly longer, the waist sits a little lower.
- Overall height: b fills the frame very slightly more.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head a little large with a short neck; near shoulder and arm join well; upper hand blob with no clear far arm; hips and legs of reasonable length; feet acceptable.
- b: head and neck slightly better proportioned; arms and hands as in a (upper hand unclear); hips, legs and feet fine.

## 6. Pose
- a: leaning slightly forward with both hands on the rake reads as raking, but the handle is held too upright and the rake head sits by the feet, so it looks more like standing holding a rake than pulling it.
- b: the same pose and the same weakness; the downward gaze matches the task a little better.

## 7. Better drawn
b, slightly. It lacks the stray lighter patch on the trousers and has a better head-to-neck proportion; otherwise the two are almost the same.
