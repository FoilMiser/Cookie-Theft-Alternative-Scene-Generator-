# Review: fig 04

## 1. Who and what
A woman (or older girl) with short dark hair in a pink short-sleeved dress and yellow shoes, walking to the right and holding a pink ice-cream cone up near her face. In a she faces the cone and the viewer, in three-quarter view. In b her head is turned so far away that it reads as the back or side of the head, with the face squeezed onto the right edge.

## 2. Faults in a
- Far arm: missing entirely; only the near arm holding the cone is drawn, so the figure looks one-armed.
- Hand on cone: a dark pointed shape sticks down below the cone and hand, which reads as a stray stub or a drip rather than fingers.
- Head: very large compared with the body, and joined to the torso by a narrow neck.
- Hair: a dark circle drawn on the back of the head reads as an odd patch or ear-shaped blob.
- Front leg: the lower leg comes out of the back of the skirt at an angle, so the knee and hip join looks awkward.

## 3. Faults in b
- Face: only one eye, the nose tip and part of the cheek show, crammed against the right edge of the head. There is no clear mouth, so the head reads as turned away from the direction of walking.
- Ear: drawn on the left side of the head, facing the viewer, which together with the face position makes the head look turned backward.
- Hair: the dark circle on the back of the head is still there.
- Far arm: missing, as in a.
- Hand on cone: the same dark stub below the cone.
- Head: large and on a thin neck, as in a.

## 4. Differences
- Head turn: a shows the face in three-quarter view, with two eyes, a nose and a smiling mouth; b shows mostly the back or side of the head, with one eye and the nose at the edge and no visible mouth.
- Ear: b has a clearly drawn ear on the left of the head; a has none visible.
- Expression: a is smiling; b has no readable expression.
- Hair shape: in b the hair covers more of the head.
- Cone: in b it is held a little further out from the face.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: The head is oversized and the neck thin but attached. There is one arm, joined at the shoulder under the sleeve, bent at the elbow, with the hand at the cone; the second arm is absent. The hips are hidden by the dress. The legs are a fair length in a stride, but the front leg's join under the skirt looks odd. The feet are simple yellow shoes, well placed.
- b: The body is the same. The head is oversized and reads as twisted on the neck relative to the body, and the face features are pushed to the edge. Otherwise the same as a, again with one arm missing.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads as a natural walk with an ice cream raised to eat. The stride is clear, and looking at the cone fits the action.
- b: The walk is the same, but because the head seems turned away, she looks as if she is walking while looking back or past the cone. That is awkward for someone eating an ice cream.

## 7. Better drawn
a. The face is readable and turned toward the cone, so "woman eating an ice cream while walking" comes across at once. In b the head reads as turned away and the face is hard to make out.
