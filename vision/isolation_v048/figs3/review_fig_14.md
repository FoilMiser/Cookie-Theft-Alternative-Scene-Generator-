# Review: fig 14

## 1. Who and what
An older woman with grey hair and round glasses, in a teal buttoned cardigan over a cream top, a blue-grey knee-length skirt and brown shoes, standing facing the viewer with arms at her sides, holding nothing. Both versions read the same. b reads a little more clearly as a grandmother because of its larger bun.

## 2. Faults in a
- Hair: the grey hair is a smooth cap, and the bun on top is very small and hard to see, so the hair could read as a cap or helmet.
- Arms: the sleeves hang straight and stiff, with no elbow bend.
- Hands: small, simple mitten shapes.
- Legs: long, thin and straight like posts, with no knee shape, and quite long for an older figure.
- Feet: shoes are small rounded blobs.

## 3. Faults in b
- Mouth: drawn as an open red wedge, like a lipstick shape, which looks a little odd or startled rather than a calm smile.
- Glasses: large and heavy-rimmed; they dominate the face.
- Neck: not visible; the head sits directly on the cardigan collar.
- Arms, hands, legs: the same stiff straight sleeves, mitten hands and post-like legs as in a.

## 4. Differences
- Bun: b has a larger, clearly visible bun on top of the head; a's is very small.
- Hair: in b the hair frames the sides of the face more, with less of the ears showing.
- Mouth: a has a simple dark smile line; b has an open red mouth.
- Glasses: b's look larger and thicker.
- Head: b's head looks slightly larger and rounder.
- Neck: a shows a short neck; b shows almost none.
- Skirt and legs: in b the skirt sits a little higher and the figure is slightly shorter overall.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: The head and neck join correctly. The shoulders are square under the cardigan. The arms are a reasonable length, with no elbow. The hands are small. The hips are hidden by the skirt. The legs are long and thin, and the feet small. Nothing is detached.
- b: The head is slightly large and sits on the collar with no neck. The rest is the same as a: arms straight, legs long and thin, feet small. Nothing is detached.

## 6. Pose
- a: A stiff, symmetrical standing pose. It is fine for "standing", but has no weight shift or relaxed arms.
- b: The same stiff pose. The open red mouth gives her a slightly surprised look.

## 7. Better drawn
a, narrowly. The face is calmer and the head-to-neck join is cleaner, although b's larger bun makes "older woman" a little easier to read.
