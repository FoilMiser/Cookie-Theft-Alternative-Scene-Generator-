# Review fig 12

## 1. Who and what
A man (brown hair, moustache, red T-shirt, blue trousers, dark shoes) standing straight facing the viewer, arms at his sides, holding nothing. In a he has a large moustache over an open red mouth; in b a moustache with a small chin beard and a closed mouth.

## 2. Faults in a
- Ears: none drawn; the hair comes down like a helmet to the cheeks.
- Hair: a flat bowl/cap shape with a hard straight fringe, looks like a hat.
- Mouth: red open mouth under a heavy moustache, reads as grimacing.
- Neck: very short and wide, head sits on the shoulders.
- Torso: the T-shirt is short, the body above the waist is short compared with the legs.
- Legs: trousers very long and straight, legs look too long for the body.
- Sleeves: stick out as stiff boxy blocks on the shoulders.
- Hands: small rounded blobs with no fingers.
- Feet: small dark lumps, rather small for the legs.

## 3. Faults in b
- Sleeves: same boxy blocks on the shoulders.
- Hands: small blobs with no fingers.
- Feet: small dark lumps, though slightly larger than in a.
- Legs: still long and straight like tubes, but less extreme than a.
- Head: hair ends abruptly at the ears in a flat line.

## 4. Differences
- Ears: b has ears; a has none.
- Head shape: a wide and round; b narrower and more oval with a pointed chin.
- Hair: a covers more of the head like a cap; b sits higher with the ears showing.
- Facial hair: a a big moustache over a red open mouth; b a moustache joined to a small chin beard, mouth closed.
- Neck: b's neck is visible and longer; a's is barely there.
- Torso: b's T-shirt is longer, the waist sits lower.
- Legs: b's trousers are shorter, so the legs are less long.
- Shoes: b's shoes are slightly bigger.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head large and wide with almost no neck; shoulders square under boxy sleeves; arms a reasonable length, hands tiny and fingerless; torso too short and legs too long, joined at a high waist; feet too small.
- b: head and neck in better proportion; shoulders and arms as a; hands tiny and fingerless; torso and legs closer to normal proportions; feet small but acceptable.

## 6. Pose
- a: rigid front-on standing pose, arms straight down, feet together; reads as stiff, like a doll or a mannequin.
- b: the same rigid pose; equally stiff, but the more natural face and proportions make it read slightly more like a person standing.

## 7. Better drawn
b. It has ears, a neck and more balanced torso-to-leg proportions; a's capped hair, missing neck, very long legs and grimacing mouth look less right.
