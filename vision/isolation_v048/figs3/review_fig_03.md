# Review fig 03

## 1. Who and what
A young woman with dark hair, in a yellow short-sleeved dress and red flat shoes, sitting (no seat shown) and reading an open red book held in both hands; both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Seat: she sits on nothing, so the thighs and bottom float (expected in isolation, not counted as a fault in the scene).
- Hands: the hands are pale blobs pressed onto the book's lower edge, with no fingers and no clear grip.
- Far arm: only the far hand shows at the right of the book; the far arm is hidden and the hand looks stuck onto the book edge.
- Dress: the hem stops in a straight band across the thighs, and the dress outline at the hip and seat is a blunt lumpy curve.
- Face: the eye is a short dash and the mouth a small dark mark, so the face reads as rather blank.
- Shoes: the shoes are large flat ovals and look a little oversized for the feet.

## 3. Faults in b
- Seat: same floating sit as a.
- Hands: same blob hands on the book edge, no fingers.
- Far arm: same, only the far hand visible.
- Dress: same straight hem and blunt hip outline.
- Face: same dash eye and small mouth.
- Shoes: same large flat shoes.
- Hair: the ponytail is a dark lump at the back of the neck and looks rather stuck on.

## 4. Differences
- Hair: in a the hair comes further down the face and the side of the head; in b the hairline is higher and more of the face and cheek shows.
- Hair: in b the ponytail at the back of the neck is more distinct; in a it blends into the back of the hair.
- Neck: a shows a small stretch of neck under the chin; in b the chin sits lower and almost no neck shows.
- Face: in b the face is a little larger and rounder; in a it is narrower and more covered by hair.
- Book: in b the book is tilted a touch flatter and the lower left corner sits slightly further left.
- Otherwise the body, dress, legs and shoes look the same.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head and neck join plausibly; shoulders and upper arm fine; hands are fingerless blobs; hips and thighs are thick and rounded but plausible; lower legs long and straight down; feet in large shoes. Nothing detached, but the far arm is missing behind the book.
- b: Same as a; the head sits a little lower on the shoulders with very little neck, which makes the neck look short.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads naturally as someone sitting and reading, head bent over the book; the lower legs hang straight and close together, which is a little stiff.
- b: Same pose and the same slight stiffness in the legs; equally readable.

## 7. Better drawn
a, narrowly: the visible neck makes the head sit more naturally on the body, while b's head looks slightly sunk into the shoulders; b shows the face a little more clearly, but the difference is small.
