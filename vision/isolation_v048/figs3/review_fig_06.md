# Review: fig 06

## 1. Who and what
A young or middle-aged woman with long dark hair in a short-sleeved yellow knee-length dress and dark red shoes, standing facing the viewer with arms hanging at her sides, holding nothing. Both versions read the same, except that b's face is harder to read (see below).

## 2. Faults in a
- Arms: hang dead straight and close to the body, with no bend at the elbow.
- Hands: small closed fists with little shape; they read as blobs.
- Face: no nose drawn; the face is only two eyes and a smile line.
- Legs: long, straight and parallel like posts, with no knee shape.
- Dress: the waist pinch is drawn only as small notches at the sides.

## 3. Faults in b
- Mouth: drawn as red lips with a dark hooked mark above them, which looks like a moustache or a sneer rather than a nose and mouth.
- Hair: a straight, flat fringe cut across the forehead and straight sides make the head look boxy and helmet-like.
- Neck: shorter; the chin sits almost directly on the shoulders.
- Arms, hands, legs: the same stiff straight arms, blob fists and post-like legs as in a.

## 4. Differences
- Hair: a has a middle part with hair falling past the shoulders; b has a straight fringe and the hair stops around the shoulders.
- Mouth: a has a simple dark smile; b has red lips with a dark hooked mark above.
- Head: b's head looks a little wider and squarer.
- Neck: a shows more neck; b's is shorter.
- Dress hem: a's hem sits a little lower on the leg.
- Overall: b's figure sits slightly higher and is a touch shorter in the leg.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head and neck join reasonably. Shoulders are rounded under the sleeves. Arms are a bit thin and long, with no elbow. Hands are small fists. Hips are hidden by the dress. Legs are long and thin with no knees. Feet are simple rounded shoes. Nothing is detached.
- b: The same body. The head looks larger and boxier, and the neck is short, so the head looks pushed down onto the shoulders. The arms are thin and straight, and the legs are post-like. Nothing is detached.

## 6. Pose
- a: A stiff "standing to attention" frontal pose. It is acceptable for a woman just standing, but there is no weight shift and no bend in the arms.
- b: The same stiff pose. The odd mouth makes the expression read as cross or unsure rather than neutral.

## 7. Better drawn
a. The body is the same in both, but a's face (a simple smile) and long hair read clearly and pleasantly, while b's hooked mark over red lips looks like a moustache or a grimace.
