# Review fig 05

## 1. Who and what
An older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, in a teal top, blue-grey skirt and brown shoes, standing and holding out a brown cardboard box or parcel in front of her; in a she is turned mostly away with the face hardly seen, in b her face is turned towards the viewer and she is smiling.

## 2. Faults in a
- Face: the face is turned so far away that only the edge of the glasses and a small dark mouth mark show; the face is hard to read and the mouth looks like a smudge.
- Glasses: the glasses hang at the very edge of the head and look partly off the face.
- Ear: the ear is large and placed near the middle of the side of the head, so the head reads as seen from behind.
- Arms and box: only one arm shows; it passes over the top of the box and the hand is open past the far side of it, so the box is not actually held; it floats in front of the waist.
- Near hand: the open hand at the end of the arm grips nothing.
- Legs: the legs are thin and the near leg is set behind the far leg in a way that makes the stance look narrow and wobbly.

## 3. Faults in b
- Arms and box: same as a, one arm over the top of the box, open hand past it, box floating and not gripped.
- Near hand: same open hand holding nothing.
- Face: the face is turned to the viewer while the body is in side view, so the head looks twisted round on the neck.
- Glasses: the round glasses are thick and large, covering most of the upper face.
- Legs: same thin, narrow stance.

## 4. Differences
- Head turn: in a the head faces away (back and side of the head seen, large ear); in b the face is turned towards the viewer.
- Eyes and glasses: in a the glasses are at the edge and no eyes are clearly seen; in b two round lenses with eyes are clearly drawn.
- Mouth: in a a small brown mark; in b an open smiling mouth with red inside.
- Eyebrows: b has eyebrows drawn; a has none visible.
- Ear: a shows a large ear; in b the ear is not seen.
- Hair: in b the hair is drawn round the face from the front; in a it covers the back of the head.
- Body, arm, box, skirt, legs and shoes look the same.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head plausible but reads as the back of the head; neck short; shoulder and arm reach forward fine; hand open; box not held; hips and skirt fine; legs thin but in proportion; feet fine.
- b: Head turned towards viewer on a side-view body, so the head-to-neck join looks twisted; the rest as a: arm fine, hand open and not holding, legs thin, feet fine.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads as reaching or offering something forward, but the box is not held by the hands, so the action looks unfinished; the averted face makes it less clear.
- b: Same arm and box problem; the face turned to the viewer with a smile makes it read more clearly as offering or handing over the box, though the head turn is a little forced.

## 7. Better drawn
b: the face can be seen and read as a pleasant older woman, while in a the face is almost lost and the glasses look as if they are falling off; the box-holding fault is the same in both.
