# Review fig 16

## 1. Who and what
A dark-skinned child (boy or girl) with short curly black hair, in a yellow T-shirt, orange shorts and blue-grey shoes, standing on one bent leg and kicking the other leg forward (as if kicking a ball, which is not shown), arms out for balance. In a the face is turned towards the viewer with frowning brows and a smile; in b the child is in profile looking down at the kicking foot.

## 2. Faults in a
- Head: very large and round, the face is squeezed onto the right side of the head with the ear far to the left, so the face looks pasted on.
- Face: angled, frowning eyebrows with a smiling pink mouth give a mixed, slightly sly expression; eyes look sideways, not at the foot.
- Back arm: comes straight out horizontally from behind the shoulder, thin, with a small blob hand; join to the shoulder is hidden and looks stuck on.
- Front arm: forearm thin, hand a small lump.
- Standing leg: thin, with a sharp kink at the knee; calf and shin look rubbery.
- Kicking leg: long and straight like a stick from the shorts to the shoe.

## 3. Faults in b
- Head: still very large; hair cap sits on top like a helmet.
- Back arm: same horizontal stick arm from behind the shoulder, small blob hand.
- Front arm: thin, small lump hand.
- Standing leg: same thin leg with sharp knee kink.
- Kicking leg: same long straight stick leg.

## 4. Differences
- Face direction: a is turned three-quarters toward the viewer with two eyes; b is a profile with one eye looking down.
- Expression: a has frowning brows and a smile; b has a neutral, focused look with no visible mouth detail.
- Ear: a's ear is near the back-left of the head; b's ear is more central.
- Hair: the curls are arranged slightly differently and b's hairline comes lower over the forehead.
- Body, arms, legs and clothes: no difference seen.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head very big (child-like but extreme) with the face oddly placed; neck short but present; shoulders narrow, back arm oddly joined from behind; hands tiny lumps; hips fine; standing leg thin with a kinked knee, kicking leg long and straight; feet fine.
- b: Same body; head big but face placement consistent with a side view; same arm join, thin standing leg with kinked knee, straight kicking leg; feet fine.

## 6. Pose
- a: The kick with arms out for balance reads naturally, but the face turned away from the foot towards the viewer weakens it.
- b: Reads more naturally: the head bends and looks down at the kicking foot, as someone kicking a ball would; leg and arm stiffness are as in a.

## 7. Better drawn
b — the profile head looking down at the foot fits the kick and the face is placed consistently on the head; a's face looks pasted onto the side of the head.
