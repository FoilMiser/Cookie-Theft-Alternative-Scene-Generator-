# Review fig 01

## 1. Who and what
A woman (young or middle-aged, dark hair tied back, yellow short-sleeved dress, red flat shoes) standing still, turned three-quarters to the viewer's right, hands loosely closed, holding nothing. In a she looks worried or is speaking (open red mouth); in b she looks calm and slightly smiling.

## 2. Faults in a
- Mouth: open red mouth with a bump of lip reads as alarmed or distressed rather than neutral; it sits off toward the cheek edge.
- Ear: no ear is drawn; the hair runs straight into the face side.
- Neck: very short and narrow, the head sits almost directly on the dress collar.
- Head: small for the body compared with the long legs.
- Near (viewer's left) arm: comes out from behind the dress sleeve with no clear shoulder; the sleeve looks pasted over it.
- Far (viewer's right) arm: separated from the dress side by a thin gap of skin colour under the armpit, so it looks loosely attached.
- Hands: plain mitten-like fists with no fingers indicated.
- Dress: back hem sticks out in a sharp corner at the lower left.

## 3. Faults in b
- Ear: no ear drawn; hair covers that side of the face.
- Near arm: same as a, the upper arm starts under the sleeve with no shoulder line.
- Far arm: same thin gap between the arm and the dress side.
- Hands: mitten-like fists, the far hand is a blob with a curl line.
- Neck: wider and more visible than in a, but blends into the face with no jaw line.
- Dress: same sharp corner at the lower back hem.

## 4. Differences
- Mouth: a open, red, worried; b a small closed smile line.
- Hair: in b the hair comes further forward over the side of the face and is fuller at the back.
- Head: b's head is slightly larger and rounder.
- Neck: b's neck is longer and wider than a's.
- Body: b's waist line sits slightly lower and the dress looks a little longer.
- Overall: b is very slightly taller/larger in the frame.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head small with a very short thin neck; shoulders hidden by puffed sleeves; arms of reasonable length but loosely joined (gap at far armpit); hands are fists without fingers; hips and legs long and slim, legs look a little long; feet in flat shoes are fine.
- b: head a better size for the body; neck visible and joins more plausibly; arms and hands the same as a (loose far arm, mitten hands); hips, legs and feet as in a, legs fine.

## 6. Pose
- a: a neutral standing pose with a slight turn; reasonably natural, but the open mouth and fixed stare make her look startled, and both arms hang straight, slightly stiff.
- b: the same standing pose; reads as calm and natural, still a little stiff in the arms.

## 7. Better drawn
b. The head and neck are better proportioned and the calm closed mouth reads as a normal expression, whereas a's tiny neck and open worried mouth look odd.
