# Review fig 13

## 1. Who and what
A dark-skinned adult man with short black hair and a beard, in a green T-shirt, tan trousers and brown shoes, standing still with arms hanging at his sides, head turned to his left (viewer's right); holding nothing. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Head: large and almost perfectly round, sitting straight on the shoulders with very little neck; reads as a ball placed on the body.
- Beard: a dark patch hanging off one side of the chin/jaw, lopsided and not clearly following the jawline; mouth (small red mark) sits awkwardly inside it.
- T-shirt: short, ends high; torso looks short relative to the legs.
- Trousers: a faint darker smudge/patch on the front of the upper thigh, looks like an unfinished shading mark.
- Hands: simple mitten blobs with no fingers; the near hand is a flat lump against the thigh.
- Legs: very long and straight like tubes, little sign of knees.

## 3. Faults in b
- Beard/moustache: a dark moustache and chin tuft drawn as separate blobs, slightly smeared; reads somewhat like a dirty mark on the face.
- Torso: still fairly short versus the long legs, though less so than a.
- Hands: same mitten blobs, no fingers.
- Legs: long tubes; the inner outline narrows above the knee and bulges below, giving a slightly knock-kneed or wobbly look.
- Shoes: flat brown slabs, back foot partly hidden.

## 4. Differences
- Head size: smaller in b relative to the body; a's head is bigger and rounder.
- Neck: b shows a short visible neck; a has almost none.
- Facial hair: a has a chin beard patch; b has a moustache plus a small chin beard.
- Eyes: b's eyes look slightly downward/sideways; a's look straight to the side.
- Ear: placed a little differently (a's ear lower on the head).
- T-shirt: b's shirt is a little longer and wider at the shoulders.
- Trousers: a has a visible smudge on the thigh; b does not.
- Legs: b's legs have the slight narrowing/bulge around the knees; a's are straighter.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head too big and round with almost no neck; shoulders narrow; arms long but plausible, hands lumpy mittens; hips high and legs too long compared with the short torso; feet acceptable as simple shoes.
- b: Head-to-body ratio more natural, neck present; shoulders and arms reasonable; hands still mittens; legs long, with a slightly odd inward curve at the knees; feet acceptable.

## 6. Pose
- a: A plain standing pose, but stiff: arms hang straight down and the head looks bolted onto the shoulders, so the head turn reads awkwardly.
- b: Also a plain standing pose, slightly more relaxed because the neck lets the head turn; still stiff in the arms.

## 7. Better drawn
b — the head and neck proportions are more natural and there is no stray smudge on the trousers, though the facial hair looks a little messy.
