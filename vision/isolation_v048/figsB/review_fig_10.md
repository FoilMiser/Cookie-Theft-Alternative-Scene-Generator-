# Review: fig_10

## 1. Who and what
A white dog with a brown ear and brown patches, wearing a blue collar, sitting up with its head raised facing right. In b it reads clearly as sitting; in a the rear end is confused, so it reads as half-sitting or crouching.

## 2. Faults in a
- Hindquarters and tail base: a muddle of a brown patch and black scribbled marks where the haunch and tail meet, looks unfinished.
- Hind legs: haunch has no clear shape; the hind legs read as long straight legs, so the dog seems partly standing.
- Tail: curls round low at the back but is hard to separate from the leg and the scribbles.
- Collar: very thick band, with a brown patch peeking out below it at the chin.
- Ear: long brown ear hangs off the back of the head and looks only loosely attached.
- Front legs: long, and the far front leg is a grey shape that looks slightly separate.

## 3. Faults in b
- Haunch: a few small nicks and stray marks at the top and back of the haunch.
- Front legs: quite long for a sitting dog; the far front leg is a pale grey shape.
- Tail: a small curl low at the back, a bit stubby.

## 4. Differences
- a's collar is a thick blue band; b's is a thin blue collar.
- a's rear end has scribbled black marks and an unclear haunch; b has a clear rounded haunch with a brown patch on the thigh.
- a's hind legs read as straight and long; b's hind leg is folded under in a sit.
- b has a smiling mouth line; a has only a short mouth mark.
- a's ear hangs further back and lower; b's ear sits closer to the head.
- a's tail curls longer at the back; b's tail is shorter.
- a has a brown patch showing under the chin by the collar; b barely shows it.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head fine; ear loosely joined; neck hidden by a thick collar; front legs long; hips and hind legs unclear and oddly joined with stray marks; tail tangled with the leg. Rear end wrong.
- b: head, ear and neck fine; front legs a little long; haunch and hind leg clear and properly joined; tail small but attached. Mostly right.

## 6. Pose
- a: awkward; the upright front and unclear straight hind legs make it hover between sitting and standing.
- b: natural sitting dog, head up, haunch down, front legs straight.

## 7. Better drawn
b. It has a clean, readable sitting haunch and a neater collar, while a's rear end is scribbled and unclear.
