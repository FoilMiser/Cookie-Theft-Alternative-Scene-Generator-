# Review fig 07

## 1. Who and what
A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar, walking to the right with its eyes closed happily and its tongue just showing, holding nothing; both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Ear: a very large brown oval that covers most of the side of the head and runs down over the collar.
- Collar: drawn at a tilt so it looks more like a band behind the head than around the neck.
- Hindquarters: the large brown spot at the back bulges beyond the body outline, so it looks pasted on.
- Legs: tubular with rounded blob feet; the near front leg is a straight post.
- Back: flat, straight top line from collar to tail.

## 3. Faults in b
- Far front leg (grey): the lifted paw is bent sharply into a hook shape that looks slightly awkward.
- Ear: sits low and overlaps the collar.
- Hindquarters: the large brown spot on the haunch again sits on the edge of the outline.
- Legs: tubular with rounded blob feet.
- Body: long, so the dog looks slightly stretched.

## 4. Differences
- Ear: a's ear is much larger and covers the side of the head; b's is smaller and narrower.
- Far front leg: in a it angles back with the paw on the ground; in b it is lifted with the paw bent, mid-step.
- Tail: a's tail is straight and upright; b's curves forward at the tip.
- Spots: the spots are in slightly different places and sizes (b's back spot is larger and lower, the small spot further from the big one).
- Mouth: b has a longer smile line; a's mouth is shorter.
- Body length: b's body looks a little longer than a's.
- Collar: b's collar is narrower and sits more neatly around the neck.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head big but acceptable; ear oversized; neck/collar join slightly off; legs plain tubes without joints; feet blobs; tail fine; spot bulges out at the rump.
- b: Head and neck join better; ear sized well; legs tubular, lifted far front paw bent at an awkward angle; feet blobs; tail fine; body a bit long.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads as walking, but stiff: all four paws planted and the front leg a straight post.
- b: Reads more naturally as walking because one front paw is lifted mid-step, though the bent paw is a little hooked.

## 7. Better drawn
b. The lifted paw gives a more natural walk and the ear and collar are better proportioned; a's oversized ear and planted legs look stiffer.
