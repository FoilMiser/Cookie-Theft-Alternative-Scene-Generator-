# Review fig 06

## 1. Who and what
A dark grey cat moving to the right, holding nothing; a reads as creeping or stalking low to the ground, b reads more as an ordinary walk with the head held level.

## 2. Faults in a
- Head: rather small for the body, and pushed forward so the neck and head form a narrow wedge.
- Near front leg: very long and stretched almost flat forward, longer than looks natural.
- Ear: only one ear shows, drawn as a tall, sharp spike with a double outline at the tip.
- Muzzle: tiny and hard to read; the mouth and whiskers are a small scribble.

## 3. Faults in b
- Head: a perfect circle stuck onto the front of the body, with a narrow join; it looks like a ball attached to the body rather than a cat's head.
- Near front leg: has an odd round knob or lump where the upper leg meets the chest, like a separate piece.
- Ear: a single small ear sitting on top of the round head.
- Eye: a large plain black oval with no colour, reading slightly blank.
- Legs: tubular, with paws that are rounded blobs.

## 4. Differences
- Head shape: a is a wedge-shaped cat head with a yellow eye; b is a round ball-shaped head with a black eye.
- Head position: a holds the head low and forward; b holds it level with the back.
- Muzzle: b has a clear white muzzle; a has a small white chin area only.
- Chest: a has a white chest patch; b has none.
- Body posture: a is crouched, back sloping down to the head; b is higher off the ground, back more level.
- Front legs: a's near front leg is stretched far forward and low; b's front legs are under the body in a walking step, with a knob at the elbow.
- Ear: a's ear is tall and spiky; b's is smaller.
- Tail: a's tail is thinner and held straight back; b's is thicker at the base and flows from the back line.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head a bit small; neck join narrow but plausible; body good; near front leg too long and stretched; hind legs and tail fine.
- b: Head too round and too separate, joined oddly; near front leg has a lump at the elbow; far front leg thin; hind legs fine; tail fine.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads naturally as a cat stalking or creeping, low and forward; only the over-stretched front leg is a little awkward.
- b: Reads as a walk, but stiff and toy-like because of the ball head and the lumpy front leg join.

## 7. Better drawn
a. The head looks like a real cat's head and the stalking pose reads naturally; b's ball head and knobby front leg look awkward.
