# Review fig 12

## 1. Who and what
A tan/brown dog with a red collar and a dark brown floppy ear, walking to the right with its tail raised and eyes closed (happy look), small red tongue showing; holding nothing. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Head: round ball-shaped skull sits on top of the neck like a separate disc; the muzzle is a small pale wedge stuck on the front, so the head reads more like a bear/ball than a dog.
- Tongue: the red tongue is a small oval hanging off the very tip of the nose rather than out of the mouth.
- Eye: the closed-eye line has an extra stroke above it (looks like a brow and eye doubled), slightly messy.
- Far front leg: the paw is lifted and bent back at a sharp angle, and the foot is drawn as a small blunt stub that looks cut off.
- Front near leg: very thick, column-like, with an odd inward bulge line at the chest/elbow.
- Hind near leg: heavy thigh with a fold line on the haunch; legs overall are long and stilt-like relative to the body.
- Feet: paws are flat oval pads without toes, near hind paw slightly flattened at the bottom like a shoe.

## 3. Faults in b
- Head: same round ball head with small muzzle wedge; the ear is very large, covering much of the head side.
- Tongue: small red mark at the chin/nose tip, barely readable as a tongue.
- Hip/haunch: a jagged, broken contour line where the hind leg meets the belly (a notch/crease), looks like a drawing glitch.
- Hind near leg: joins the body abruptly; the thigh looks pinched at the top.
- Legs: long and thin-ish, tube-like, with little shape at the knees/hocks; all four feet are on or near the ground with similar oval paws, looks a bit stilted.
- Collar: sits as a thick block at an angle, slightly detached-looking from the neck outline.

## 4. Differences
- a has the far front leg lifted and bent back in a step; in b all four feet are down (far front leg is placed forward on the ground).
- b's front near leg is straighter and thinner; a's is thicker with a bulging chest line.
- b has a jagged crease line at the haunch/belly join; a has a smoother fold line on the thigh.
- a's body is deeper and chunkier; b's body is longer and a little slimmer.
- b's ear is larger relative to the head than in a.
- a's head is set slightly higher above the collar; in b the head sits lower and more forward.
- a's eye has a doubled stroke (brow plus eye); b's eye is a single arc.
- a's tongue hangs as a distinct red oval below the nose; in b it is a small red mark at the mouth.
- Collar angle differs: a's is a narrower band, b's a thicker, more tilted band.
- Tail: in b the tail is thicker at the base and blends more into the rump; in a it is a thinner curved stalk.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head round and large, attached to the neck by the collar, reads acceptably; body solid; front near leg too thick; far front leg's lifted paw bent at an awkward sharp angle with a stubby foot; hind legs long but properly joined; tail fine. Legs overall a bit long for the body.
- b: Head and ear fine (ear large); body long and acceptable; hind near leg oddly joined at the haunch with a jagged notch; legs long and tube-like with little joint shape; feet plain ovals; tail fine, slightly thick at the base.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads as a walking dog mid-stride; the lifted far front paw gives clear motion, though the bent-back angle of that paw is a little awkward.
- b: Reads as walking (legs spread fore and aft), but with all four feet planted it looks more like standing stiffly on stilts than stepping.

## 7. Better drawn
a — its lifted paw makes the walk read more naturally and it lacks the glitchy crease at the haunch seen in b, though its front leg is overly thick.
