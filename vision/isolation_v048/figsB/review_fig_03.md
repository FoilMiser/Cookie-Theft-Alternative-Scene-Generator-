# Review fig 03

## Who and what
A white dog with brown spots, a long brown drop ear and a blue collar, standing still in side view facing right. Both versions read the same.

## Faults in a
- Head: rather large for the body.
- Ear: very large, covering most of the side of the head and the collar.
- Hind leg: thin and pinched just below the hip, with a narrow sliver of outline at the front of the leg that looks like a drawing error.
- Far legs: shown only as small grey slivers at the feet.
- Face: no mouth line.

## Faults in b
- Hind leg: the line where the haunch meets the leg is a little uneven.
- Far legs: shown as pale grey shapes, slightly faint.
- Tail: thin and long, curving up like a hook.

## Differences
- a's head is larger relative to the body; b's head is smaller.
- a's ear is much larger and hangs over the collar; b's ear is smaller and shorter.
- b has a smiling mouth line; a has none.
- b's legs are a little thicker and straighter; a's hind leg is thinner and more bent at the hock.
- a has a stray sliver line at the front of the hind leg; b does not.
- Far legs: a shows them as small grey bits at the feet; b shows them as fuller grey legs.
- Spot positions and sizes differ slightly (b's spots are spread a little more).
- b's tail is thinner and more curved at the tip.

## Anatomy
- a: head and ear too large; neck and collar fine; body fine; hind leg too thin under the hip with an odd sliver; feet fine; tail fine.
- b: head, neck, body and legs well proportioned; far legs drawn more completely; tail slightly thin.

## Pose
- a: reads as a dog standing; the bent hind leg gives some life but the pinched leg looks awkward.
- b: reads as a dog standing naturally and steadily, a little stiff but clear.

## Better drawn
b, because the head and ear are in better proportion, the face has a mouth, and the hind leg has no pinched or stray line.
