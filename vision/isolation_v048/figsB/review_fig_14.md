# Review: fig_14

## 1. Who and what
A grey-blue cat standing side-on facing right with its tail held straight up. Both versions read the same; b reads more clearly as a cat.

## 2. Faults in a
- Head: a plain circle stuck on the front of the body with no neck; it looks pasted on.
- Ears: only one ear shown, sitting on top of the circle like a small tab.
- Front leg: a separate tube with a rounded cap at the top, visibly sitting below/in front of the body rather than growing out of the shoulder.
- Body: a boxy shape with a flat back and blunt front end.
- Legs: only one front and one back leg really drawn, the far legs are just small foot bumps.
- Face: muzzle is a small white patch at the very edge of the circle; whiskers barely visible.

## 3. Faults in b
- Head: a curved line runs across the top of the head behind the ear, looks like a seam.
- Legs: long and thin, especially the front legs, a little spindly.
- Rump: a small extra bump and line at the hip where the hind leg joins.

## 4. Differences
- a's head is a separate circle with no neck; b's head joins the body through a neck.
- a shows one ear; b shows two pointed ears.
- a has a plain black eye; b has a large yellow eye with a slit pupil.
- b has a white chest patch; a has none.
- a has a small white muzzle patch; b has a small dark nose and whiskers without a white muzzle.
- a shows one front and one hind leg plus hints of the others; b shows all four legs.
- a's front leg is a capped tube set onto the body; b's legs flow out of the body.
- b's back slopes slightly from shoulders to hips; a's back is flat.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head detached (no neck); front leg oddly joined with a visible cap; body boxy; legs long; tail fine. Joins look wrong.
- b: head, neck and body joined naturally; legs slightly too long and thin; tail fine; hip join has a small extra bump. Mostly right.

## 6. Pose
- a: stiff; it looks like a toy built from a circle, a box and tubes standing still.
- b: natural standing cat with tail up and head raised, legs placed as in a slow walk.

## 7. Better drawn
b. Its head grows out of a neck, it has both ears and all four legs, and the legs join the body properly, whereas a looks assembled from separate pieces.
