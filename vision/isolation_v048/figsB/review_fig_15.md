# Review: fig_15

## 1. Who and what
An orange cat with a white chest patch standing in three-quarter view, tail straight up, looking right. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Far ear: drawn as a black spike behind the head, not orange like the near ear, looks unfinished.
- Head: a round circle sitting on the shoulders with no neck.
- Near front leg: a separate tube with a rounded cap at the top, sitting in front of the chest with a small dark notch/gap where it should join.
- Legs: all four are straight, even-width tubes, stiff.
- Face: tiny white muzzle with a pink dot; fine but small.

## 3. Faults in b
- Neck: a crease line under the head where the head meets the body.
- Legs: long and thin, splayed slightly outward, a little spindly.
- Head: the near ear's base has a fold line on the forehead.
- Face: no white muzzle, just a pink nose and whiskers; fine.

## 4. Differences
- a's far ear is a black spike; b has two pink-lined orange ears.
- a's eye is plain black; b's is large and green with a slit pupil.
- a has a white muzzle; b has none, only a pink nose.
- a's near front leg is a capped tube set onto the chest; b's legs flow out of the body.
- a's legs are straight and parallel; b's are angled and slightly splayed.
- b's head is joined to the body with a neck line; a's head is a circle on top.
- b's body is a little shorter and the chest patch lower.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head detached-looking with no neck; far ear wrong colour; near front leg oddly joined with a visible cap and gap; legs tube-like; tail fine.
- b: head, neck and body joined; legs slightly too long and thin but attached properly; tail fine. Mostly right.

## 6. Pose
- a: stiff; it stands like a toy with straight tube legs.
- b: more natural, an alert cat standing or about to step, though the splayed thin legs are a little awkward.

## 7. Better drawn
b. The ears, eye and leg joins are finished and natural, while a has a black unfinished far ear and a front leg pasted onto the chest.
