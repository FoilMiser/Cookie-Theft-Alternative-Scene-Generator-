# Review: fig_08

## 1. Who and what
A tan/brown dog with a dark brown ear, eyes shut, head tipped up, tongue showing. In a it clearly sits on its haunches wearing a red collar; in b it wears a red neckerchief and reads as standing or half-standing with a sloping body, not clearly sitting.

## 2. Faults in a
- Eye: drawn only as a short curved stroke with a flick, reads as a squint or brow rather than a closed eye; acceptable but unclear.
- Muzzle: small cream block on the front of a round head, looks stuck on rather than grown out of the head.
- Tail: very long and thin, lying flat along the ground out to the far left, a little longer than natural.
- Hind leg: the haunch is a big rounded outline, the hind foot underneath is a small dark lump partly hidden; acceptable.

## 3. Faults in b
- Body: the back runs in one straight slope from the neck down into the tail with no hip or rump, so the dog looks like a seal or a long-necked dinosaur.
- Hind legs: no haunch at all; the hind leg is a short stub coming out of the middle of the underside, not at the back end of the body.
- Tail: not separated from the body, it is just the tapering end of the body.
- Neck: very long and thick, the head sits on top of a tall column.
- Neckerchief: red shape sits partly over and behind the ear, the ear overlaps it oddly.
- Front legs: long thin straight tubes; the near and far front legs barely separate.

## 4. Differences
- a wears a narrow red collar; b wears a red triangular neckerchief.
- a sits with a clear rounded haunch and hind foot on the ground; b has no haunch and a stub hind leg under mid-body.
- a has a separate thin tail along the ground; in b the tail is the tapering end of the body.
- b's neck is longer and the head sits higher above the body.
- b's ear is larger and hangs lower over the neckerchief.
- b's muzzle is a little smaller and more pointed.
- a's front legs are sturdier; b's are thinner.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head and muzzle fine though the muzzle is small; neck and chest believable; front legs right length; haunch and hind foot in the right place; tail long and thin but attached properly. Mostly right.
- b: neck too long and thick; body has no hip, back merges into tail; hind leg too short and in the wrong place (mid-belly, not at the rear); tail not separate; front legs too thin. Clearly wrong.

## 6. Pose
- a: reads as a natural dog sitting upright with head raised, a familiar pose.
- b: awkward; it is neither sitting nor standing, and the sloped body with a stub leg looks like a different animal.

## 7. Better drawn
a. It is a believable sitting dog with a proper haunch and tail, while b has lost the hindquarters and reads as a seal or dinosaur shape.
