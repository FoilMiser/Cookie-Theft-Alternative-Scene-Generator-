# Review fig 13

## 1. Who and what
An orange cat asleep; a is a curled-up ball seen partly from above and is hard to read as a cat, b clearly reads as a cat lying down with its head on its paws, eyes closed.

## 2. Faults in a
- Face: the features are scattered; the closed eyes are lines in widely separated places (one near the middle of the picture, one at lower right) and the white muzzle is down at the bottom edge, so the face does not read as one face.
- Ears: two ears point in different directions on what looks like a large flat disc, rather than a head.
- Left side (body/paws/tail): a jumble of overlapping lobes with stray lines and dashes; it is unclear which part is a paw, a leg or the tail.
- Middle: a small pointed sliver shape with dashed lines between the body and the head looks unfinished.
- Whiskers: a single long whisker line trails off below the muzzle.

## 3. Faults in b
- Head: very large compared with the body.
- Face: a curved line (the outline of the body or shoulder) runs through the left cheek, so the head edge is doubled there.
- Whiskers: the left whiskers run across that line and onto the body.
- Haunch: lumpy outline at the back, with a small stray dot on it.
- Front paw at lower right: filled in a darker orange than the rest, and looks slightly separate from the body.

## 4. Differences
- Overall pose: a is a tight curl seen from above; b is lying on its belly, seen from the front-side.
- Face: in b the eyes, nose and mouth sit together in one face; in a they are scattered and the muzzle is a white blob at the bottom.
- Ears: b's ears are large and pink inside, both on top of the head; a's are smaller and point in different directions.
- Body: b shows a clear haunch and back legs; a's body is a set of overlapping lobes.
- Line quality: a has many stray dashes and short lines; b has fewer.
- Whiskers: b has several whiskers on each side; a has one long trailing whisker line and a few short ones.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head shape unclear and too flat; face features out of place; body, legs and tail hard to separate; a small shape in the middle looks detached.
- b: Head too big but well formed; head-to-body join is muddled by the line through the cheek; haunch and hind leg plausible though lumpy; front paws tucked under the chin, one oddly shaded; tail not clearly visible.

## 6. Pose
- a: Meant as a curled sleeping cat, but it reads as a confused orange blob; awkward because nothing is clearly where it should be.
- b: Reads naturally as a cat sleeping or dozing with its chin on its front paws.

## 7. Better drawn
b. It reads at once as a sleeping cat with a coherent face, while a's face and body are scrambled and hard to recognise.
