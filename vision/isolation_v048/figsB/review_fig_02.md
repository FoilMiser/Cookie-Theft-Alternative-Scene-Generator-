# Review fig 02

## Who and what
A tan/brown dog with a dark brown drop ear, white muzzle and red collar, standing still in side view facing right. Both versions read the same.

## Faults in a
- Legs: all four legs are thick, straight columns, rather heavy and stumpy for the body; the hind leg has almost no hock bend.
- Hip: a short crease line on the haunch looks like a leftover seam.
- Far legs: shown only as dark wedges between the near legs, a little muddy.

## Faults in b
- Hip/haunch: the line where the hind leg meets the body is jagged and broken, like an unfinished join.
- Far front leg: shown as a very thin dark sliver behind the near front leg.
- Collar: drawn as a thick, angled red block that looks stiff rather than wrapping the neck.
- Muzzle: no mouth line, face a little blank.

## Differences
- a has thick, column-like legs; b has slimmer legs with a visible bend at the hind hock.
- a has a smiling mouth line on the muzzle; b has none.
- b's ear is larger and longer than a's.
- b's collar is thicker and more angled; a's is a thinner band.
- b has a jagged join line at the hip; a has a small smooth crease instead.
- b's body is slightly deeper at the chest and the back line slopes a little more.
- Far legs: in a they are dark wedges; in b the far front leg is a thin sliver and the far hind leg a small dark patch.

## Anatomy
- a: head, neck and body join well; legs are too thick and too straight, especially the hind leg; feet are big rounded blobs but acceptable for a cartoon; tail fine.
- b: head and body fine; legs better proportioned with a doglike hind leg bend; the hip join is oddly drawn (broken outline); far front leg too thin; tail fine.

## Pose
- a: reads as a dog standing, but stiff, like a toy on four posts.
- b: reads as a dog standing naturally, with weight on bent hind legs; slightly more alive.

## Better drawn
b, because its legs have believable proportions and a proper hind-leg bend, though the jagged hip join should be cleaned up.
