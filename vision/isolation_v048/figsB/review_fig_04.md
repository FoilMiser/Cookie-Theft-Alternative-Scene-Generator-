# Review fig 04

## 1. Who and what
A tan/brown dog with a red collar, standing still in side-and-three-quarter view, facing right, holding nothing; both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Legs: all four are plain tubes with no visible knee, elbow or hock, so they look like posts.
- Far front leg (dark brown): a straight column that starts abruptly under the chest; its top edge does not blend into the body.
- Near hind leg: thick at the thigh then narrows suddenly to a thin shin, a little like a balloon joined to a stick.
- Body: very boxy, with a flat back and flat belly.

## 3. Faults in b
- Hindquarters: a jagged, broken crease line runs across the haunch, like a seam or a crack in the drawing; it looks unfinished.
- Far front leg (dark brown): a straight tube with a flat, squared top that looks stuck onto the chest rather than growing out of it.
- Legs: all four are long thin tubes with no joints; the front legs in particular look stilt-like.
- Collar: drawn as a thick red band/bib that bulges below the neck, reading more like a scarf than a collar.
- Tail: a straight, stiff spike.

## 4. Differences
- b has a jagged crease line on the haunch; a has none.
- Collar: a is a narrow ring; b is a thicker, bib-like red band.
- Tail: a curves gently up and back; b is straighter and more spike-like.
- Ear: b's ear is larger and darker brown than a's.
- Muzzle: a has a larger white muzzle with a smile line; b's white muzzle patch is smaller.
- Legs: b's legs are thinner and look longer relative to the body; a's legs are slightly thicker.
- Chest patch: a's white patch is a long strip down the chest; b's is a smaller oval.
- Head: b's head is slightly rounder and sits a little lower on the neck.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head and neck join reasonably; body boxy; legs jointless tubes, near hind leg thick-to-thin abruptly; far front leg joins the chest stiffly; feet are rounded blobs but acceptable; tail fine.
- b: Head fine; collar bulky; haunch has a stray crease line; legs too thin and long, with no joints; far front leg looks detached/attached as a separate tube; tail stiff.

## 6. Pose
- a: A plain standing pose; reads as natural enough for a dog standing still, though stiff because the legs are straight posts.
- b: Same standing pose, but stiffer: the thin straight legs make the dog look as if it is on stilts.

## 7. Better drawn
a. It has no stray crease on the haunch, a neater collar and less stilt-like legs; b looks less finished.
