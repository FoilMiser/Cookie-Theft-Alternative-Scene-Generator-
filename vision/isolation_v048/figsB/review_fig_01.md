# Review fig 01

## Who and what
A grey-blue cat facing right. In b it clearly sits upright on its haunches looking upward; in a it is hard to read: the body is a tall slanted wedge with straight front legs and short hind legs, so it could be sitting, standing or stretching up.

## Faults in a
- Head: a plain circle stuck on top of the body with a narrow pinch, looking detached rather than joined by a neck.
- Ears: only one ear drawn, a small triangle on top.
- Eyes: closed arcs, so the face shows little.
- Chest: a small stray curled line mid-chest that looks like a drawing error.
- Body: a stiff cone/wedge shape with no shaped haunch, so it is unclear whether the cat is sitting.
- Hind legs: short, stubby and bunched at the bottom; front legs long straight posts.
- Tail: straight thick stick coming out low from the rear.

## Faults in b
- Muzzle: nose and whiskers form a small cluttered black blob at the front of the face.
- Eye: placed right at the front edge of the head, a little too far forward.
- Tail: very long, straight and thin, lying flat like a stick.
- Front legs: rather thin and straight.

## Differences
- a's eyes are closed arcs; b has an open yellow eye.
- a shows one ear; b shows two ears.
- a's head is a separate circle on a pinched join; b's head is joined to the body by a proper neck and is tilted upward.
- b has a white chest patch; a has none, but has a stray curl line on the chest.
- b has a rounded haunch and folded hind leg of a sitting cat; a has a wedge body with short straight hind legs.
- a's tail is thick and rises slightly; b's tail is thinner and lies along the ground.
- b has visible whiskers and a nose blob; a has a white muzzle patch with short whiskers.

## Anatomy
- a: head looks detached on a pinched neck; body is a cone without a hip shape; front legs too long and straight, hind legs too short; tail thick and stiff; overall proportions wrong for a cat.
- b: head, neck, chest and haunch join naturally; front legs slightly thin; tail too straight and long but attached correctly.

## Pose
- a: stiff and ambiguous; it does not read as a natural sitting cat, more like a ramp with a ball on top.
- b: reads clearly and naturally as a cat sitting and looking up.

## Better drawn
b, because it is unmistakably a sitting cat with a joined head and neck, two ears, an open eye and a proper haunch, while a is stiff and hard to read.
