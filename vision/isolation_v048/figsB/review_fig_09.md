# Review: fig_09

## 1. Who and what
A tan/orange dog with a dark brown floppy ear and a red collar, standing still in side view facing right, tail raised; both versions read the same (dog standing, not holding anything).

## 2. Faults in a
- Legs: all four legs are very long and thick, almost pillar-like, making the dog look tall and stilt-legged for its body.
- Hind leg: the near hind leg has a soft S-shaped curve but no clear hock angle; the thigh bulge is outlined with a small stray inner line at the hip.
- Far legs: the far front and far hind legs are visible only as darker slivers behind the near legs, partly hidden; reads acceptably but a little flat.
- Head/neck: the head sits directly on top of the collar with very little visible neck; the head is large and round relative to the body (cartoon, acceptable).
- Muzzle: the cream muzzle is a flat wedge with a small notch for the mouth; fine.
- Tail: thin, upright, slightly curved; joins the rump cleanly.

## 3. Faults in b
- Hip: a broken, jagged line at the hip/thigh where the near hind leg meets the body, looking like a crease or tear in the outline; unfinished-looking.
- Hind leg: the near hind leg bends forward at the knee in an odd backward-sitting curve, and the far hind leg is a thin dark sliver; the rear end looks wobbly.
- Front legs: the front legs are thin straight tubes with no elbow or wrist shaping, set far apart from the hind legs, making the belly line long and the legs stiff.
- Feet: feet are small rounded blobs, the far feet appear as small dark crescents behind them; slightly unfinished.
- Collar: the collar is a wide, blocky red band tilted at an angle, looking more like a sleeve or cuff than a collar strap.
- Ear: the ear is very large, covering much of the side of the head.

## 4. Differences
- Collar: thin curved strap in a; wider, blockier band tilted forward in b.
- Ear: smaller and set lower in a; larger, taller and more prominent in b.
- Muzzle: longer cream muzzle with a mouth notch in a; shorter, rounder muzzle in b.
- Body: deeper, fuller chest and body in a; slimmer, more tapered body in b, narrowing toward the hips.
- Front legs: thick, slightly shaped legs in a; thin straight tube legs in b.
- Hind legs: thick, curved thigh with a smooth join in a; thinner leg with a jagged crease at the hip and a forward-kinked knee in b.
- Leg spacing: legs set closer under the body in a; front and hind legs spread further apart in b.
- Tail: tail slightly thicker at the base in b, rising from a higher, wedge-like rump.
- Feet: larger, flatter paws in a; smaller rounded paws in b.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head large and round, sits well on the collar; body solid and in proportion; legs too long and too thick (pillar-like) but joined cleanly to the body; tail fine. No detached parts.
- b: Head fine, ear rather big; body slimmer; front legs too thin and straight; near hind leg oddly joined at the hip (jagged crease) with a forward kink at the knee; feet small. Tail fine. Rear end looks weakest.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads as a natural, alert standing dog; somewhat stiff because the legs are straight columns, but balanced and stable.
- b: Reads as standing too, but the rear leg kink and the wide-set thin legs make it look stiffer and slightly unsteady, as if the dog is half-crouching at the back.

## 7. Better drawn
a. Its legs and hip join cleanly and the body reads as solid and balanced, whereas b has a jagged hip line, thin stiff front legs and an awkward hind leg kink.
