# Review fig 05

## Who and what
An orange cat walking to the right in side view, tail up. Both read as a walking cat; b reads more clearly as mid-stride with a front paw lifted.

## Faults in a
- Head: a plain circle pressed onto the front of the body with no neck, looking stuck on.
- Ears: only one ear drawn.
- Eyes: closed arcs; face shows little.
- Front shoulder: a lumpy, knotted outline where the near front leg joins the body, like an unfinished join.
- Body: a stiff rectangle with no chest or haunch shaping.
- Legs: tube-like with blob feet, little joint shape.

## Faults in b
- Muzzle: nose and whiskers form a small cluttered black blob at the front.
- Eye: set at the very front of the head.
- Far hind leg: a little thin under the body.

## Differences
- a's eyes are closed arcs; b has an open green eye.
- a has one ear; b has two ears.
- a's head is a circle without a neck; b's head sits on a neck and is tilted up.
- b has a white chest patch; a has none.
- b's near front paw is lifted and bent mid-step; in a all four feet are on the ground.
- b has a shaped haunch and chest; a's body is a straight rectangle.
- a has a lumpy join at the front shoulder; b's front leg joins smoothly.
- b has whiskers with a black nose blob; a has a white muzzle patch with short whiskers.
- b's tail curves at the tip; a's tail is straighter.

## Anatomy
- a: head detached-looking with no neck; body boxy; front leg oddly joined at the shoulder; legs tube-like but reasonable length; tail fine.
- b: head, neck, body and legs join well; proportions believable; far hind leg slightly thin; tail fine.

## Pose
- a: reads as walking but stiff, like a wooden toy, with all legs planted.
- b: reads naturally as walking, with a lifted, bent front paw and alternating legs.

## Better drawn
b, because its head joins the body with a neck, it has two ears and an open eye, and the lifted paw makes the walk read naturally; a is boxy with a lumpy shoulder join.
