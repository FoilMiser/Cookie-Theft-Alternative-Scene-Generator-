# Review fig 14

## 1. Who and what
A child (boy) with dark brown skin and dark curly hair, in a yellow T-shirt, orange shorts and blue-grey shoes, kicking one leg forward high, as if kicking a ball (the ball is not shown), with one arm flung back. In a he has an open mouth; in b a closed smile.

## 2. Faults in a
- Back arm: held out straight and horizontal behind him, rigid, with a lumpy hand like a mitten.
- Front arm: thin forearm bent forward with a small blob hand; the upper arm is drawn over the shirt and looks pasted on.
- Mouth: the open mouth with a pink tongue sits low and off to the side, and with the angled eyebrows the face reads as a grimace or snarl.
- Head: very large relative to the body (acceptable for a child cartoon, but the neck is thin under it).
- Standing leg: bent at the knee with the foot pointing backward; reads more like stepping than bracing to kick.
- Kicking leg: very long and thin from the knee down.

## 3. Faults in b
- Back arm: the same rigid horizontal arm and mitten hand as a.
- Front arm: the same thin arm with a blob hand.
- Face: the angled eyebrows with a smile give a sly or cross look.
- Neck: thin under the large head, as in a.
- Standing leg and kicking leg: the same as a, the lower kicking leg long and thin.

## 4. Differences
- Mouth: a has an open mouth with pink tongue; b has a closed curved smile.
- Head: in b the head is tilted down a little more, the face looking toward the kicking foot.
- Eyes: in b the eyes sit a little higher on the face, closer to the brows.
- The figure in b sits slightly lower in the frame; the body, arms and legs otherwise look the same.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head big on a thin neck; shoulders fine; back arm straight and stiff, front arm thin; hands are blobs; hips and shorts fine; kicking lower leg long and thin; feet attached and sized fine.
- b: Same as a in every body part; the downward head tilt joins the neck naturally.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads clearly as a kick, but the back arm stuck straight out and the bent standing leg make it look stiff and a bit like a march step; the face looks strained or snarling.
- b: Same body pose and the same stiffness; looking down toward the foot with a smile reads more like a child playfully kicking a ball.

## 7. Better drawn
b, slightly: the body is identical, but the downward look and closed smile read more naturally than a's off-centre open mouth with tongue.
