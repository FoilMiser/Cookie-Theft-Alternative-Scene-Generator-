# Review: fig 12

## 1. Who and what
An older man with grey hair, a moustache and a blue flat cap, in a cream shirt, brown waistcoat, dark trousers and brown shoes, standing and holding a long fishing rod in both hands, angled up and away to the right. His eyes are closed in both versions, so he can read as dozing or calm. Both read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Eyes: drawn as closed curves, so it is unclear whether he is fishing or asleep.
- Rod: there is no line hanging from the rod tip (could be missing at this size); the grey reel or handle at the hands is a small unclear block.
- Arms: the near forearm and the far arm cross in front of the waistcoat, and it is hard to tell which hand belongs to which arm.
- Legs: very long, straight and stiff compared with the short body, with no bend at the knee.
- Neck: short; the head sits low on the shoulders.

## 3. Faults in b
- Eyes: closed, as in a.
- Rod: the same; no line, unclear grey handle block.
- Arms: the same crossing arms.
- Legs: the same long straight legs.
- Cap: the brim sticks out further forward, slightly large for the head.

## 4. Differences
- Head: b's head is slightly larger.
- Cap: b's brim is longer and sits further forward.
- Hair: b shows more grey hair at the side of the head under the cap.
- Moustache: a's moustache is slightly larger and more prominent.
- Otherwise the body, clothes and rod are the same.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head and neck acceptable, neck short; shoulders and arms join, but the crossing arms are muddled; hands fine around the rod; hips and legs too long and straight for the short body; feet fine.
- b: The same, with a slightly larger head and cap.

## 6. Pose
- Both: Holding a rod with both hands up and out reads as fishing, but the very straight, stiff legs and closed eyes make the figure look wooden or asleep rather than actively fishing.

## 7. Better drawn
No difference. The only changes are small details of the head and cap; the body is drawn the same.
