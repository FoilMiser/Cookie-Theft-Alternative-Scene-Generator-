# Review: fig 10

## 1. Who and what
An older woman with grey hair in a bun and round glasses, in a teal sweater with a white collar, blue-grey skirt and brown shoes, standing and holding a brown box (a parcel) out in front of her, as if handing it over. Both read the same; in b her mouth is open as if talking.

## 2. Faults in a
- Glasses: the far lens sticks out past the outline of the face in front of the nose, floating in the air.
- Hands and box: the box hangs under the arms with no hand gripping its sides; the near hand sticks out past the box with an open palm, so she is not clearly holding it.
- Arms: both arms are held straight out and level, like a rod, with no bend at the elbow.
- Back: there is a hump at the upper back where the sweater meets the shoulders.
- Legs: long and thin compared with the short skirt, with knees locked straight.
- Feet: the shoes overlap each other, one on top of the other.

## 3. Faults in b
- Glasses: larger, and again the far lens extends past the face outline.
- Hands and box: the same; the box hangs below the arms and the near hand reaches past it.
- Arms: the same straight, level arms.
- Back: the same hump at the upper back.
- Legs: the same long thin straight legs.
- Feet: the same overlapping shoes.
- Mouth: open and red, slightly large for the small face.

## 4. Differences
- Mouth: a is a closed red smile; b is open, as if speaking.
- Glasses: b's lenses are larger and thicker-rimmed.
- Ear: a shows an ear on the side of the head; b shows the hair covering that area, no ear.
- Bun: a's bun is at the top of the head; b's is a little further back.
- Head: b's head looks slightly larger and the face slightly rounder.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head and neck fine; shoulders hidden under a hunched back; arms straight and level without visible elbows; hands not clearly on the box; hips fine under the skirt; legs a bit long and thin; feet overlap.
- b: The same, with a slightly larger head; same arm, hand, leg and foot issues.

## 6. Pose
- Both: The idea (holding out a parcel) reads, but the pose is stiff: the body stands upright and slightly back while the arms stick straight out, and the box hangs beneath the arms instead of being held between the hands.

## 7. Better drawn
No difference. The only changes are in the face (mouth, glasses size, ear), and the body faults are the same in both.
