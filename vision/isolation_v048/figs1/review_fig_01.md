# Review fig 01

## 1. Who and what
A young woman (dark hair, brown skin, purple short-sleeved T-shirt, teal trousers, dark shoes) standing three-quarter view, looking to the right, arms hanging at her sides, holding nothing; both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Neck: the neck is a short block with a dark outline running across under the jaw, so the head looks pasted onto it.
- Near arm (viewer's left): the arm is thin and hangs as a straight strip from under the sleeve, looking stuck on rather than coming out of the shoulder.
- Hands: both hands are clenched blobs; the near hand looks oversized for the thin arm.
- Torso: the shirt is very short and the trouser waist very high, so the upper body looks short against long legs.
- Trousers: a small stray dark mark on the thigh near the middle.

## 3. Faults in b
- Neck: the neck is wider and plainer; it joins better but looks thick.
- Mouth: the open red mouth is a small offset shape that reads as a pout or speech rather than a calm face.
- Near arm: same thin strip arm hanging from under the sleeve.
- Hands: same clenched blob hands.
- Torso: same short shirt and high trouser waist.

## 4. Differences
- Hair: a has long hair falling down behind the back to the waist; b has hair with a fringe to the ear and a shorter tail at the back.
- Ear: b shows an ear at the side of the head; a hides it under hair.
- Mouth: a has a small closed smile; b has an open red mouth.
- Neck: a has a short outlined neck; b has a wider neck that joins more smoothly.
- Head shape: b's head is slightly larger and rounder at the back.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head big for the body (cartoon, acceptable); neck oddly joined with a line under the jaw; shoulders narrow, near arm too thin and looks detached at the shoulder; hands blob-like and large; hips high, legs long; feet in flat dark shoes fine.
- b: Head and neck join better but the neck is thick; shoulders and arms as in a, near arm too thin; hands same; hips and legs same, legs long; feet fine.

## 6. Pose
- a: Standing at ease, slightly turned, looking aside; reads as natural if a little stiff because both arms hang straight down with fists.
- b: Same pose and same slight stiffness.

## 7. Better drawn
b, narrowly: the head-to-neck join is smoother and the ear helps the head read, though the open mouth is less pleasant; a's outlined neck looks more like a pasted-on head.
