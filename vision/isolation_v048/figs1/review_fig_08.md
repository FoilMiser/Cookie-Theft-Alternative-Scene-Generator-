# Review fig 08

## 1. Who and what
An older woman (grey hair, round glasses, teal buttoned cardigan over a cream top, slate-blue skirt, brown shoes) standing upright facing the viewer, arms at her sides, holding nothing; both versions read the same, though b reads slightly more clearly as an older woman because of the bun.

## 2. Faults in a
- Hair: grey hair is a smooth cap swept to one side with no clear bun, so it reads less clearly as an older woman's hairstyle.
- Skirt hem: the bottom edge of the skirt is ragged and sketchy, like it is unfinished.
- Hands: both hands are mitten blobs hanging below the cuffs with no fingers.
- Legs: legs are straight tubes of equal width from knee to ankle, a little stiff.

## 3. Faults in b
- Neck: no neck is visible; the head sits directly on the cardigan collar.
- Mouth: the open red mouth is a small dark shape that reads more like a grimace or pout than a smile.
- Skirt hem: same ragged, sketchy hem as a.
- Hands: same mitten-blob hands.
- Legs: same straight tube legs.

## 4. Differences
- Hair: a has a side-swept grey cap; b has hair parted in the middle with sides framing the face and a small bun on top.
- Neck: a shows a short neck between chin and collar; b shows none.
- Mouth: a has a closed curved smile; b has an open red mouth.
- Ears: in a the ears stick out clearly at the sides; in b they are smaller and partly behind the hair.
- Head position: in b the head is lower on the body, so the whole figure is slightly shorter.
- Face shape: in b the face is rounder and wider at the cheeks.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head and neck join well, short neck visible; shoulders and arms correct length, cuffs at hip level; hands small blobs; hips and legs fine, legs plain and straight; feet in shoes fine.
- b: Head sits straight on the shoulders with no neck, which looks squashed; shoulders, arms, hands, hips, legs and feet as in a.

## 6. Pose
- a: Plain standing pose, front-on, arms straight down; reads as natural standing but stiff and symmetrical, like a paper doll.
- b: Same stiff symmetrical standing pose; the missing neck makes it look a little more hunched.

## 7. Better drawn
a: the visible neck and closed smile look more correct and friendlier, though b's bun makes the age read more strongly; the missing neck in b is the bigger drawing fault.
