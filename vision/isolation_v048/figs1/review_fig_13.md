# Review: fig 13

## 1. Who and what
A young woman with long dark hair tied back, in a short-sleeved yellow dress and red flat shoes, standing still with her arms at her sides and looking to the viewer's right. Both versions read the same; in a her mouth is slightly open (as if speaking or surprised), in b she has a small closed smile.

## 2. Faults in a
- Hands: both hands are tight fists with no fingers shown, which looks unfinished for a figure at rest.
- Far arm (viewer's right): very thin and set behind the dress edge, so it looks attached to the back of the dress rather than to a shoulder.
- Near arm (viewer's left): the upper arm almost disappears into the sleeve and the arm hangs out of the side of the body without a clear shoulder joint.
- Neck: very short; the chin sits almost on the dress collar.
- Mouth: the small red open mouth sits low and slightly off to the side, giving a puzzled or uneasy look.
- Head: the ear shows as a small bump on the back of the face outline, which reads as a lump rather than an ear.

## 3. Faults in b
- Hands: the same tight fists with no fingers.
- Far arm: the same thin arm attached at the back edge of the dress.
- Near arm: the same weak shoulder join under the sleeve.
- Neck: the neck is visible but a little long and narrow, with a straight-sided column between head and dress.
- Mouth: the smile is very small and pale red, close to the edge of the face.

## 4. Differences
- Mouth: a open and red; b a small closed smile.
- Neck: a very short; b longer and more visible.
- Ear: a shows an ear bump on the side of the face; b shows no ear, the hair covers that area.
- Hair: b's hair is fuller at the top and sides of the head and covers more of the side of the face.
- Head position: b's head sits a little higher above the shoulders.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head large for the body (cartoon style, acceptable); neck too short; shoulders hidden under sleeves so the arm joins are unclear, far arm too thin; hands are fists; hips and legs slim but believable; feet in shoes fine.
- b: Same body; neck now a better length though slightly stiff and tube-like; same unclear shoulder joins and thin far arm; fist hands; legs and feet fine.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads as standing and looking aside; natural enough, but clenched fists and arms held straight down make it a little stiff, and the open mouth adds a tense look.
- b: The same standing pose; the closed smile makes it read as more relaxed; still slightly stiff because of the fists and straight arms.

## 7. Better drawn
b. The neck is a more believable length and the closed smile reads as a calm, natural expression; the arm and hand issues are the same in both.
