# Review fig 03

## 1. Who and what
An adult man (possibly middle-aged or older) with dark skin, short dark hair and a short grey-black beard, in a green T-shirt, khaki trousers and brown shoes, standing in three-quarter view, turned and looking to the viewer's right, hands loosely at his sides, holding nothing. Both versions read the same; the beard is clearer in a.

## 2. Faults in a
- Face: eyes, nose and mouth are crowded onto the right edge of the head; the mouth is a small red open shape that looks a little odd.
- Beard: drawn as a dark smudgy patch along the jaw; reads as stubble or a shadow rather than a clear beard.
- Neck: thick and short, like a block.
- Near (left) arm: thick tube that hangs from the shoulder without a clear shoulder joint; sleeve and arm outline overlap awkwardly.
- Far (right) arm: thinner than the near arm and hangs straight; no elbow.
- Hands: rounded fists/mittens with no fingers.
- Trousers: a faint lighter triangular mark on the far thigh, like a stray patch.
- Legs: long, straight, no knee shape.
- Feet: brown blobs, the near foot sticks out forward.

## 3. Faults in b
- Head: large and very round, a little big for the body.
- Face: features crowded to the right edge; the nose is a pronounced bump on the outline; mouth a small red mark.
- Beard: mostly lost, only a dark band along the jaw, so he looks younger and the beard reads as a shadow.
- Neck: short; head sits down on the shoulders.
- Near (left) arm: thick tube with an awkward join to the sleeve, as in a.
- Far (right) arm: thin, straight, no elbow.
- Hands: rounded fists/mittens, no fingers.
- Trousers: the same faint lighter mark on the far thigh.
- Legs: long and straight, no knees.
- Feet: brown blobs.

## 4. Differences
- Head size: b's head is larger and rounder than a's.
- Beard: clearly shaded over the chin and jaw in a; reduced to a dark jaw band in b.
- Nose: a small mark in a; a bump on the face outline in b.
- Neck: more visible and blocky in a; shorter in b.
- Shirt hem: ends slightly lower in a.
- Figure in b sits slightly higher on the page.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head and neck acceptable but neck blocky; shoulders broad, near arm thick and poorly joined at the sleeve, far arm thinner, no elbows; hands fists; hips fine; legs long with no knees; feet blobs. The near and far arms are of noticeably different thickness.
- b: head too big; neck short; arms and hands as in a, near arm poorly joined; legs long with no knees; feet blobs.

## 6. Pose
- a: a relaxed standing pose turned to look aside; reads as natural, though the arms hang stiffly.
- b: the same relaxed turned stance; equally natural, slightly top-heavy because of the big head.

## 7. Better drawn
a. The head is better sized for the body and the beard reads more clearly, so he looks like an adult man; in b the oversized round head and the loss of the beard make the figure less clear.
