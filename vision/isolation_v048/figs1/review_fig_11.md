# Review fig 11

## 1. Who and what
A man with a moustache (brown hair, red T-shirt, blue trousers, dark shoes) walking to the right in side view, arms swinging, holding nothing; both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Head: the head is very wide and box-like, with the hair a flat bowl cut ending in a straight fringe line, so it looks like a helmet.
- Ear: the ear is mostly hidden by the hair, leaving a small odd notch at the side of the head.
- Mouth: the open red mouth under the moustache looks like it is shouting.
- Neck: very short neck.
- Near arm (swinging back): the upper arm is drawn over the shirt with a bulge at the sleeve, so it looks pasted onto the side of the chest.
- Torso: the shirt is short and the trousers start high, so legs look very long against a short body.
- Back foot: the rear shoe tips steeply down at the far left, a little awkward.

## 3. Faults in b
- Near arm: same upper arm pasted over the shirt with a bulge at the sleeve.
- Torso: same short shirt and high trouser waist with long legs.
- Moustache: long drooping moustache crosses the lower face and partly covers the mouth.
- Back foot: same steeply tipped rear shoe.

## 4. Differences
- Head shape: a's head is wider and flatter; b's is rounder and taller.
- Hair: a has a flat bowl cut with a straight fringe; b has rounder hair that sits higher on the head.
- Ear: b shows a clear ear; a mostly hides it.
- Eyebrows: b shows eyebrows; a has a line on the brow but less clear.
- Moustache and mouth: a has a small moustache over an open red mouth; b has a longer drooping moustache with a small mouth.
- Neck: b's neck is slightly longer and clearer than a's.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head too wide and heavy for the body, neck too short; shoulders narrow, near arm oddly joined over the chest; hands blob fists, fine at this size; hips high, legs long; feet in shoes fine, back foot steeply angled.
- b: Head in better proportion, neck short but visible; arms, hands, hips, legs and feet as in a, with the same near-arm join.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads as a natural walking stride, legs apart, arms swinging opposite; slightly awkward because the near arm sits stuck on the chest and the head leans forward.
- b: Same natural walking stride with the same near-arm awkwardness; the rounder head sits more naturally on the body.

## 7. Better drawn
b: the head is better proportioned, with a visible ear and a clearer neck, whereas a's wide helmet-like head and shouting mouth look odd; the body is the same in both.
