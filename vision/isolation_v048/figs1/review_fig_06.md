# Review fig 06

## 1. Who and what
An adult man with short brown hair and a large brown moustache, in a red short-sleeved T-shirt, blue trousers and dark shoes, standing facing the viewer with arms at his sides, holding nothing. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Head: tall oval with the hair as a flat cap on top; ears stick out at the sides like small tabs.
- Moustache: very large and curled, sitting over an open red mouth; slightly comic.
- Neck: fairly long and thin for a man of this build.
- T-shirt: short, ending high, so the torso looks short.
- Arms: thin straight tubes with no elbows, much thinner than the legs.
- Hands: small mitten blobs, no fingers.
- Trousers/legs: very long, thick, straight tubes; the legs look over-long compared with the short torso.
- Feet: dark rounded blobs, no shape of a shoe.

## 3. Faults in b
- Head: wide and round, a little large for the body; hair a broad cap.
- Moustache: large and curled over an open mouth, as in a.
- Neck: short; head sits low between the shoulders.
- T-shirt: short, as in a.
- Arms: thin straight tubes with no elbows, much thinner than the legs.
- Hands: small mitten blobs, no fingers.
- Trousers/legs: long thick straight tubes; legs look over-long against the torso.
- Feet: dark rounded blobs.

## 4. Differences
- Head shape: a is a taller oval; b is wider and rounder.
- Ears: visible as small tabs in a; not visible (covered by hair/face outline) in b.
- Neck: longer in a, shorter in b.
- Shirt hem: slightly lower in a than in b.
- The whole figure in b sits slightly higher on the page (shoes higher), with the trousers starting a little higher.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head a little narrow and tall with tab ears; neck long; shoulders broad and square; arms too thin compared with the legs, no elbows; hands tiny; torso short and legs too long; feet blobs. Nothing detached.
- b: head round, slightly big; neck short; shoulders broad; arms too thin, no elbows; hands tiny; torso short and legs too long; feet blobs. Nothing detached.

## 6. Pose
- a: stiff, upright, symmetrical, arms straight down; reads as standing, but wooden.
- b: the same stiff symmetrical pose.

## 7. Better drawn
No difference (very slight lean to b). The bodies are the same; b's rounder head and short neck look a touch more natural than a's long neck and tab ears, but the change is small and both share the thin arms and over-long legs.
