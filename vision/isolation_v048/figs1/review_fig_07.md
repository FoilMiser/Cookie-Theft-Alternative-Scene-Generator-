# Review fig 07

## 1. Who and what
A child (could be a boy or a girl) with dark skin and short dark curly hair, in a yellow T-shirt, orange shorts and blue shoes, standing in three-quarter view, turned and looking to the viewer's right, hands loosely at the sides, holding nothing. In a the child smiles slightly; in b the mouth is a small open "o", so b looks surprised or about to speak.

## 2. Faults in a
- Neck: long and thin like a stalk, so the head looks perched on a stick.
- Head: hair is drawn as a cluster of round lumps on top of the head; the face is turned so far that the features sit on the right edge.
- Near (left) arm: thick tube joined awkwardly at the sleeve; no elbow.
- Far (right) arm: thinner and straight; no elbow.
- Hands: rounded fists with no fingers.
- Legs: thin with a visible seam/bump at the knees just below the shorts.
- Feet: blue blobs; the far foot sits further forward than the near one.

## 3. Faults in b
- Head: large and wide, a little big even for a child.
- Mouth: a small pink "o" which gives a slightly blank, surprised look.
- Hair: the same cluster of round lumps.
- Near (left) arm: thick tube joined awkwardly at the sleeve; no elbow.
- Far (right) arm: thin and straight; no elbow.
- Hands: rounded fists, no fingers.
- Legs: thin with a bump at the knees.
- Feet: blue blobs.

## 4. Differences
- Head size: b's head is larger and wider than a's.
- Neck: long and thin in a; shorter and thicker in b.
- Mouth: a small closed smile in a; a small open pink "o" in b.
- Ear: clearly visible on the left of the head in a; smaller/partly visible in b.
- Shirt and shorts sit slightly lower on the body in a (because of the longer neck).

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head reasonable for a child but on a too long, too thin neck; shoulders narrow, near arm thick, far arm thin, no elbows; hands fists; hips and shorts fine; legs thin with knee bumps; feet blobs.
- b: head big but joined by a believable neck; shoulders, arms, hands, legs and feet as in a.

## 6. Pose
- a: a relaxed child's standing pose turned to look aside; natural, though the stalk neck makes it look slightly strained.
- b: the same relaxed turned stance; natural, and the head sits better on the shoulders.

## 7. Better drawn
b. The shorter, thicker neck fixes a's stalk-like neck, and the larger head suits a child, although the open "o" mouth is a little blank compared with a's smile.
