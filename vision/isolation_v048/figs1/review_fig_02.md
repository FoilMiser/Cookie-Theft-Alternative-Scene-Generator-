# Review fig 02

## 1. Who and what
A man (workman) in an orange cap, orange high-visibility vest with white stripes, blue trousers and dark shoes, holding up a long teal tube-like tool with a yellow tip (possibly a torch, sealant gun or marker) in both hands in front of him. In a he looks ahead with an open mouth; in b he looks down at the tool.

## 2. Faults in a
- Far sleeve: the grey-blue shape on the shoulder/upper arm sits like a flat patch on top of the vest, not clearly a sleeve of an arm behind the body.
- Upper hand: the hand at the top of the tool is a rounded lump; the yellow tip sticks out of it with no clear grip.
- Lower hand: the hand lower on the tool is a blob, and the tool appears to pass in front of it.
- Arms: the forearm reaching to the upper hand is long and thin and runs nearly straight, so the elbow is hard to place.
- Eyes and gaze: he looks forward, past the tool, so it is unclear he is using or looking at it.
- Legs: very long relative to the torso; the trousers are a single straight column with little sign of knees.

## 3. Faults in b
- Far sleeve: the same flat grey-blue patch over the vest as in a.
- Upper and lower hands: the same lumpy hands with no visible grip as in a.
- Arms: the same long, thin forearm to the upper hand.
- Mouth: a small red mark low on the face that reads more like a smear than a mouth.
- Legs: the same very long, straight trouser legs.

## 4. Differences
- Head: in b the head is tilted down toward the tool; in a it is level.
- Eyes: in a two eyes looking forward; in b the eyes look down toward the tool.
- Mouth: in a the mouth is open (as if talking or surprised); in b it is a small closed red mark.
- Ear: slightly larger and lower in b.
- Tool: in b the tool sits a little lower and the hands are slightly closer to the body.
- Overall figure is slightly narrower in b.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head and neck fine; shoulders fine, but far sleeve looks pasted on; near forearm long and thin; hands shapeless; hips fine; legs too long for the torso; feet fine and attached.
- b: Same as a: pasted-on far sleeve, long thin forearm, shapeless hands, legs too long; head tilt joins the neck naturally.

## 6. Pose
- a: Stiff: he stands straight with legs together, holding the tool up while looking away from it, which does not read as using it.
- b: Still upright and stiff in the body, but looking down at the tool makes it read as examining or using it, which is more natural.

## 7. Better drawn
b: the downward gaze at the tool makes the action clear, whereas a's forward look with an open mouth disconnects him from what he holds.
