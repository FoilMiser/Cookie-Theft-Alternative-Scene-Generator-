# Review fig 09

## 1. Who and what
A woman (dark hair tied back, yellow short-sleeved dress, red flat shoes) sitting with knees bent, looking down and reading an open red book held in both hands; both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Seat/lap: she sits on nothing here, so the bent-knee pose floats (expected in isolation, not a fault of the figure itself).
- Dress: a small dark stroke on the belly reads as a stray mark or crease rather than a clear fold.
- Dress hem: the hem over the thighs is lumpy and bulges at the knees, like shorts more than a skirt.
- Near hand: the hand holding the book's lower edge is a plain blob with no clear fingers over the cover.
- Shoes: the far foot's shoe sits higher and further right than the near one, so the feet look slightly out of step with the shins.

## 3. Faults in b
- Seat/lap: same floating sit as in a (expected in isolation).
- Dress: same small dark stroke on the belly.
- Dress hem: same lumpy hem bulging at the knees.
- Near hand: same blob hand on the book edge.
- Neck: the neck is very short; the chin almost meets the collar.

## 4. Differences
- Head: in b the head is a little lower and closer to the shoulders, so less neck shows than in a.
- Hair: in b the hairline over the forehead comes a little further down and the bun at the back sits slightly lower.
- Overall height: b is slightly shorter top to bottom; the shins in b look a touch shorter.
- Book: in b the book sits a little lower and slightly more opened toward the viewer.
- Feet: in a the shoes sit slightly lower relative to the knees.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head and neck fine, a short neck visible; shoulders and arms reasonable, short upper arms in sleeves; hands simple blobs; hips and thighs read correctly for a seated figure; shins long but acceptable; feet in flat shoes fine.
- b: Same as a, but the neck is shorter, so the head looks slightly sunk into the shoulders; shins a little shorter, which fits the thighs slightly better.

## 6. Pose
- a: Natural for reading while seated: head bowed, book held up at chest height in both hands, knees bent. Slightly stiff because the shins hang straight down and parallel.
- b: Same natural reading pose, same slightly stiff parallel shins.

## 7. Better drawn
a, narrowly: the visible neck in a gives the bowed head a clearer join to the body, whereas in b the head looks a little sunk into the shoulders; otherwise the two are almost identical.
