# Review: fig 15

## 1. Who and what
A woman (could also read as a teenage girl) with short dark hair, in a pink dress and yellow shoes, walking to the right with a long stride while holding a pink ice-cream cone up near her face. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Far arm: not drawn at all; only the near arm holding the ice cream is shown, so the body looks one-armed.
- Ice cream hand: the hand wraps the cone low, and a dark stub (the cone tip) sticks out below the fist, which reads oddly, like a handle.
- Ice cream: the scoop is large relative to the head.
- Ear: no ear is drawn; the side of the head is a plain shape under the hair.
- Mouth: the red open smile sits low and to the side, near the cheek edge.
- Feet: the shoes are flat yellow wedges with no clear heel or ankle shape; the back foot is angled sharply.

## 3. Faults in b
- Far arm: missing, as in a.
- Ice cream hand: the same dark stub below the fist.
- Feet: the same wedge-shaped shoes with no ankle.
- Neck: very short; the head sits down low on the shoulders, almost on the dress.
- Shoulder: a small line on the sleeve at the shoulder looks like a stray seam mark.

## 4. Differences
- Ear: b has an ear drawn on the side of the head; a has none.
- Ice cream: b's scoop is smaller and the cone is held a little higher; a's scoop is larger.
- Mouth: a is an open red smile; b is a closed, thinner smile.
- Eyebrows: a's are thicker and darker; b's are thinner.
- Neck: a shows a short neck; b's head sits lower, with almost no neck.
- Sleeve: b has an extra short line at the shoulder.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head large (cartoon style); neck short but present; near shoulder and arm join well; hand around the cone fine apart from the stub; far arm missing; hips and legs in a long stride, leg lengths believable; feet flat wedges.
- b: Head sits too low, neck almost gone; near arm and hand fine; far arm missing; legs and stride same as a; feet same wedges.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads clearly as walking along eating an ice cream; the stride is natural, though the missing far arm (no swing) makes it a little stiff.
- b: The same; equally natural, with the same stiffness from the missing far arm.

## 7. Better drawn
a, narrowly. The head sits better on a visible neck; b's ear and smaller ice cream are small improvements, but its sunk-in head is more noticeable.
