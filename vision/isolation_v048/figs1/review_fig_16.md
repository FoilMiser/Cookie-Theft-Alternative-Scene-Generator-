# Review fig 16

## 1. Who and what
A man with short dark hair, dark brown skin and a moustache, in a green T-shirt, beige trousers and brown shoes, raking with a long wooden garden rake held in both hands. Both versions read the same; in b he looks a little more down at the rake.

## 2. Faults in a
- Face: the eyes are small and set close to the nose, and the moustache-and-mouth reads as one dark smudge, so the expression is unclear.
- Upper hand: the hand at the top of the handle is a rounded blob with no clear grip around the pole.
- Lower hand: the hand at the middle of the handle also has no visible fingers wrapping the pole; the pole seems to pass in front of it.
- Shoulders/arms: the near arm hangs straight down from the shoulder and then bends sharply forward; the upper arm looks short.
- Trousers: a faint lighter patch at the crotch/front looks like an unfinished shading mark.
- Feet: the rake head covers the far foot, so the feet are hard to read.

## 3. Faults in b
- Face: the moustache/beard is a larger dark mass that runs from the mouth into the cheek, reading more like a smudge or shadow than facial hair.
- Upper and lower hands: same blob hands as a, no clear grip on the pole.
- Shoulders/arms: same short upper arm and sharp bend at the elbow as a.
- Trousers: the same faint lighter patch at the front.
- Feet: the rake head overlaps the feet as in a.

## 4. Differences
- Head: in b the head is tilted further down and turned more toward the rake; in a he looks more forward.
- Eyes: in a both eyes are visible side by side; in b the eyes sit lower and further round, with the far eye partly hidden.
- Moustache/mouth: in b the dark moustache area is bigger and spreads onto the cheek; in a it is smaller.
- Ear: the ear is slightly larger/more visible in b.
- Rake: in b the rake head sits a little lower, closer to the bottom of the figure.
- Otherwise the body, clothes, arms and legs look the same.

## 5. Anatomy
- a: Head and neck sit fine; shoulders fine; upper arm somewhat short; hands are shapeless blobs; hips and legs long and straight, in proportion for a cartoon; feet partly hidden by the rake head but attached.
- b: Same as a: short upper arm, blob hands, legs and hips fine, feet partly hidden; head angle joins the neck naturally.

## 6. Pose
- a: Reads as raking, but stiff: he stands straight and upright with legs together, and the rake is held to the side rather than pulled through the body.
- b: Same upright, stiff stance; the more downward head makes it read slightly more like he is watching what he rakes, which is a little more natural.

## 7. Better drawn
b, slightly: the head looking down at the rake makes the action read more naturally, although its larger dark moustache patch is less clean than a's.
