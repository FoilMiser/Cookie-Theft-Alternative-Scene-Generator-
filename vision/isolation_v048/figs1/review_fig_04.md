# Review fig 04

## 1. Who and what
A young or middle-aged woman with long dark hair, in a short-sleeved yellow knee-length dress and red flat shoes, standing facing the viewer with arms hanging at her sides, holding nothing. Both versions read the same; b looks a little younger and friendlier because of the smile.

## 2. Faults in a
- Face: the nose and mouth together look like a small loop over an open red mouth, which reads a bit like a moustache or a pout rather than a clear nose and smile.
- Hair: the fringe is a flat straight band across the forehead, like a helmet edge.
- Head: the face is long and narrow, with a flat top.
- Neck: wide and short, like a block under the chin.
- Arms: stiff straight tubes that come out from under the puff sleeves at the same width all the way down; no elbow.
- Hands: small mitten blobs with no fingers or thumb.
- Legs: long, thin and straight, with no knee shape; a little spindly under the full dress.

## 3. Faults in b
- Hair: hangs as two flat dark panels down both sides of the face to the shoulders; reads more as a shape than as hair.
- Neck: wide and short, as in a.
- Arms: stiff straight tubes from under the sleeves, no elbow, same as a.
- Hands: small mitten blobs with no fingers or thumb.
- Legs: thin and straight with no knees, though a little shorter than in a.

## 4. Differences
- Hair: a has a straight-cut fringe across the forehead; b has a centre parting with hair falling down both sides.
- Face shape: a is longer and narrower with a flat top; b is rounder.
- Mouth: a has an open red mouth under a looped nose; b has a simple closed smile with red lips.
- Nose: a's nose is a looped shape joined to the mouth; b's nose is a small separate mark.
- Dress length: b's dress ends a little lower, so b's legs look a little shorter.
- Overall height of the figure is slightly different (b's shoes sit a little higher on the page).

## 5. Anatomy
- a: head a little long for the body; neck wide and blocky; shoulders broad under puff sleeves, arms straight tubes with no elbows; hands tiny mittens; hips hidden in the dress; legs long and thin with no knees; feet small rounded shoes, slightly apart. Nothing detached, but the limbs are thin and featureless.
- b: head round and in proportion; neck wide; shoulders and arms as in a, no elbows; hands tiny mittens; legs thin and straight but a little better in length; feet as in a. Nothing detached.

## 6. Pose
- a: stiff, symmetrical "standing to attention" pose, arms pinned straight down; acceptable for simply standing, but wooden.
- b: the same stiff symmetrical pose; equally wooden, softened only by the smile.

## 7. Better drawn
b. The face reads clearly as a smiling woman with a simple nose and mouth, and the hair falls naturally, whereas a's looped nose and open mouth look odd and the straight fringe is helmet-like; the body is otherwise the same.
