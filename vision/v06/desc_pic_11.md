## Free description

This is a sunny day in a park with a fence and green hedges along the back, a slide on the left and a tree on the right. In the middle, a girl or young woman with a ponytail in a blue hoodie has just thrown a red ball, shown by a dotted line curving up from her hand, and a man in an orange cap and orange safety vest is holding his hands out ready to catch it. The ball is in mid-air between them. On the right, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is flying a purple kite, but the kite is right up against the tree and looks like it might be caught in the branches. In the front, an old woman with grey hair and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, is walking toward us while eating a pink ice cream cone. In the bottom left, a smiling little girl in a pink dress is sitting in a sandpit next to a sandcastle with a little red flag, with a red bucket and blue spade beside her. The slide is empty. There is a grey rock at the bottom left and a yellow ball on the grass at the bottom right. Nothing looks obviously impossible in this picture.
