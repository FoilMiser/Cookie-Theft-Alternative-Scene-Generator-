## Free description
This is a paved town street or shopping square on a sunny day, in front of a row of four two-storey shop buildings: pink with purple trim, pale green-grey, peach with teal trim, and cream with red trim, with striped awnings over the shop windows. On the left there is a fruit and vegetable stall with crates of green, yellow, orange and red produce under a teal-and-white striped canopy, but nobody is minding it; a cardboard box sits beside it and a potted plant stands at the bottom left. A tall black street lamp stands in the left-centre with a red bird perched right on top of the lamp. In the middle, a woman with a ponytail in a blue jacket and dark trousers is taking a photo with a camera pointed to the left. Just behind her, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts stands with both arms raised and a surprised look, while a purple balloon floats up loose in front of the peach building with its string dangling and nobody holding it, as if it has just been let go. On the right, a bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers is running to the right across the square. Further right are a tree in a round planter, a green litter bin, and a small café table with a cup and saucer and two empty chairs; a grey litter bin stands at the front left of centre and a planter of flowers sits in front of the green building. Nothing looks physically impossible; the main "event" is the balloon floating away.
