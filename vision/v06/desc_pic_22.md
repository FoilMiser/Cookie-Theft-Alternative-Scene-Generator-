# Picture 22

## Free description
This is a busy back garden on a sunny day. On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door, with a tree behind it; a tall wooden fence runs across the back with a gate at the left end, and a small birdhouse on a pole sticks up behind the fence. In the sky a red balloon is floating away near the sun with its string dangling and nobody holding it, and a red bird is flying in front of a cloud. At the far left an older man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat stands on the paved path holding a garden fork with its prongs resting on the paving, and just next to him a sprinkler on the path sprays arcs of water to the right, onto a small cardboard box sitting on the lawn, which has water drops landing on it. Near the gate a child in a yellow top and orange shorts has both arms raised and looks surprised, and a white goat with a horn is grazing on the lawn, which is unusual in a back garden. In the middle, an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses holds the nozzle of a garden hose out at arm's length and peers at it, but only a single drop is coming out; the hose runs across the lawn from a reel by the house, and a brown dog with a red collar is standing right on top of the hose, which may be why no water is coming out. Behind her, a man in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest is pegging a white T-shirt onto a washing line where a yellow-and-red striped towel and blue jeans already hang. A person with a ponytail in a blue hoodie walks to the right while reading a small red book, past another cardboard box on the grass by the fence. On the far right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a bench reading a red book, next to a flower bed full of colourful flowers.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The hose: the woman seems puzzled that hardly any water comes out; the dog standing on the hose looks like it could be squashing it and blocking the flow. Alternatively the tap is simply off and the dog is just standing there.
- The child with arms raised: could be reaching for or reacting to the balloon floating away (perhaps it was theirs), reacting to the goat, or simply cheering. The balloon is well out of reach.
- The sprinkler is set on the paved path and is watering the path and a cardboard box rather than the grass or flowers; the box is getting wet. The drops above the box could also be read as water bouncing or splashing up from it.
- The older man is holding a garden fork with its prongs on hard paving rather than soil; he might be about to dig, resting on it, or about to get splashed (though the spray goes away from him).
- The goat in a suburban back garden is unexpected. Its drawing is a little odd: only two legs are clearly shown, its front leg bends at a strange angle, and its eye is a blank white circle.
- The person walking and reading: could be a teenage girl or a young woman; the red object could be a small book, notebook or a phone in a red case. She is not looking where she is going.
- The "birdhouse" has no entrance hole; it could also be a decorative post-top or feeder.
- The red bird looks as if it is in front of the cloud (overlapping its edge), which is fine for a cartoon.
- There are two separate cardboard boxes; it's unclear why either is in the garden.
