# Picture 03

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden picket fence across the back, a gate on the right, a round flower bed and a stone path leading to the gate. A small boy with curly hair, a yellow shirt and orange shorts in the middle has just thrown a red ball, shown by a dotted line curving up from his hand to the ball high in the air near the top of the fence. A man with brown hair, a red shirt and blue jeans stands to the right of him with his arms out and hands together, ready to catch the ball. The ball is still above his head, so it might go over him. At the house door on the left, a brown dog with a white chest is sitting on the doorstep holding its red lead in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. In the front centre, a white dog with brown spots is crouched with its front legs down and tail up in a play bow, facing right towards a red disc on the grass. On the right, a woman with a long dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping the path with a broom. Nothing here looks clearly impossible; it is an ordinary busy garden scene.
