# pic_13

## Free description

This is a sunny day on a paved town street with a row of colourful two-storey shops with striped awnings, and the sun in the top right. In the front left, a girl or young woman with a ponytail in a blue hoodie is walking while reading a red book, and there is a cardboard box on the ground right in front of her, so she looks about to trip over it. Behind her is a tree, and strangely there is an orange fish in the tree's leaves, which is impossible. In the middle, a woman with short dark hair in a yellow dress and red shoes is sitting on a bench with her eyes closed, enjoying a pink ice cream cone, while a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar stands next to her looking up at the ice cream, as if begging for it. In the middle right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is holding a yellow balloon on a string. On the right, a man in an orange cap and orange safety vest is sweeping the pavement with a broom in front of the fruit stall. There is a teal bicycle parked by the shops, a red bicycle in the front, an empty café table with a cup and two chairs, a flower box, a street lamp, and potted plants in both bottom corners.
