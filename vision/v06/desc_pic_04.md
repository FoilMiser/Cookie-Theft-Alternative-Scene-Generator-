# Picture 04

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden, drawn with the house on the right (cream walls, red roof, blue door) and a wooden gate and stone path on the left, with a wooden picket fence across the back and the sun in the top-left corner. In the middle there is a washing line between two wooden posts with a yellow-and-red striped towel or top and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. Something impossible is in the tree behind the fence on the right: a big orange fish sitting in the leaves. A girl with dark hair in a bun and a pink dress is kneeling on the grass on the left with both arms in the air, smiling with her eyes closed. In the middle, a lawn sprinkler is spraying arcs of water onto a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which seems an odd thing to water. A brown dog behind the flower bed on the right is in a play-bow/stretch pose facing left. At the bottom, an orange cat is creeping up behind a blue bird that is pecking at the ground, so the cat looks about to pounce. There is also a teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow at the bottom, a hose reel by the flower bed and an empty wooden bench at the bottom right.
