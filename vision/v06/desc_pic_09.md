# pic_09

## Free description

This is a sunny day on a paved town street lined with a row of two-storey shops in pink, green, peach and cream, with striped awnings. On the left there is a fruit stall with a striped canopy and crates of green, yellow, orange and red fruit. An old man in a grey cap, with a grey beard, white shirt and brown vest, stands at the stall holding something white, and a line of small crumbs or seeds is falling from his hand down to two little birds, one blue and one red, that are pecking on the ground in front of the stall. In the front, a woman with long dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers is taking a photograph with a camera, pointing it to the left toward the old man and the birds. Behind, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands in a dark doorway of the green building with one arm raised, as if waving. In the middle, a girl with a ponytail in a blue hoodie is walking while reading a red book, and a red ball is lying on the pavement just ahead of her, so she might trip on it. Oddly, a cardboard box is hanging in mid-air in the middle-right of the street, tilted, with its shadow on the ground below it, as if it is floating or falling from nowhere. On the right there is a tree in a round planter, a green litter bin, and an empty café table with a cup on it and two chairs. There are potted plants in the bottom-left and bottom-right corners and a street lamp near the fruit stall.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The floating cardboard box has nothing holding it up and nobody near it to have thrown it. It could be read as falling (dropped from somewhere, about to land) or as simply floating, which is impossible. Its shadow on the ground makes it look intentionally in the air.
- The old man and the crumbs: he could be deliberately feeding the birds, or something could be leaking out of his bag without him noticing. He appears to be facing/reaching toward the fruit rather than looking at the birds, which makes the leaking reading possible.
- The white thing in the old man's hand is small; it could be a paper bag, a cup, or a piece of bread.
- The woman with the camera could be photographing the birds, the old man, or the fruit stall; all are to her left.
- The boy in the doorway could be waving at someone, or reaching up to the door/doorframe.
- The red ball is just ahead of the reading girl, so she may be about to trip on it, but it could also be off to the side of her path.
