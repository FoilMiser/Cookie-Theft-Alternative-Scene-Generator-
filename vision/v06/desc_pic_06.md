# Picture 06

## Free description

This is a sunny park with green rolling hills and bushes in the background, a sun in the top-right corner and two white clouds. On the left there is a round blue pond with a few bulrushes at each end and a white duck swimming on it. A woman with a brown ponytail, a blue hoodie and black trousers is standing just to the right of the pond fishing with a rod; her line runs into the water to a red-and-white float with a ripple around it, as if something might be biting. Behind her is a tall black lamp post with a green rubbish bin next to it. In the front left, a brown dog with a red collar or bandana is sitting upright holding an open book in its paws as if reading it, which is impossible; next to it is an empty red-and-white picnic blanket. In the front centre, a black cat with white paws is creeping to the right towards a red bird that is pecking at the ground beside a winding sandy path, so the cat looks about to pounce. A blue bird is perched at the edge of a big tree on the right. There is another green bin cut off at the bottom-left corner and a smaller tree on the far left.
