# Picture 05

## Free description

This is a back garden on a gloomy day: the sky is grey with three dark storm clouds and there is no sun. The house (cream, red roof, blue door) is on the right, a gate and stone path are on the left, and a wooden picket fence runs across the back with a birdhouse on a pole behind it. An older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt is painting the fence blue with a paintbrush; a section of fence next to the gate is already blue. On the grass behind her is a paint tray of blue paint, and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the tray to a brown dog, so the dog seems to have stepped in the paint. The brown dog is walking up to a woman in a yellow dress who is digging a hole with a spade, with a pile of earth beside it. On the left path, a young woman with a brown ponytail and a blue hoodie is carrying a cardboard box towards the gate. In the front left, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is in a sitting position drinking from a white cup, but there is no chair under him, which looks impossible. In the front centre, a small girl in a pink dress is pointing up at the sky with her mouth open, perhaps at the storm clouds, and on the right a white dog with brown spots is lying stretched out on the grass by the flower bed. Rain looks like it might be about to start.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The man is clearly in a sitting position (thighs level, knees bent) but there is no chair, stool or anything under him. This looks impossible; the only other reading is that he is squatting in a very awkward "wall sit" with no wall.
- The grey tray of blue liquid could be a paint tray or a dog water bowl; the blue paw prints and the blue fence make paint the more likely reading.
- It is not certain which animal made the paw prints, but they lead to the brown dog's back feet.
- The girl is pointing up, most likely at the storm clouds, but she could be pointing at something not shown (no birds or rain are drawn).
- The white spotted dog could be asleep on its side or rolling on its back.
- The woman in yellow could be digging to plant something or just digging a hole; nothing is shown waiting to be planted.
- The young woman may be leaving through the gate or just walking along the path.
