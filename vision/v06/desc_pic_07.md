# Picture 07

## Free description

This is a busy, sunny town street or pedestrian square in front of a row of four colourful shops (pink, pale green, peach and cream) with striped awnings, with the sun top left and a blue bird flying near a cloud at the top right. On the left, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt is walking to the right past a fruit and vegetable stall while looking down at a small red book (or phone), and a red ball is lying on the ground right in front of her, so she looks about to trip over it. Behind her, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands holding a garden fork with its prongs on the paving, which is an odd tool for a paved street. In the middle, a woman with a long dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is holding a watering can by a box of flowers, but the stream of water seems to go up from the can towards the shop awning instead of down into the flowers, which looks wrong. A young man in a blue hoodie and black trousers is jumping or cheering with both arms in the air in front of the shops. In the front right, a small boy with curly hair, a yellow shirt and orange shorts is holding an ice-cream cone while his pink sprinkled scoop lies on the ground, where a white dog with brown spots is licking it up. On the far right, a man in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest is taking a photo with a camera pointed to the left. In the front, a black cat is creeping up behind a red bird pecking at the ground, an orange cat is lying stretched out near an empty bench, and a teal bicycle is parked by a tree in a round planter.

- The bearded man's tool could be a garden fork or a pitchfork; using it on a paved street looks odd. He might be leaning on it rather than using it.
- The boy's cone looks empty (the scoop is on the ground), but it could be read as him still having a little ice cream left.
