# Picture 02

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden picket fence across the back, a wooden gate on the far right, and a sun in the top-right corner. A bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands in the middle-left using a garden hose to spray water onto a round bed of colourful flowers; the hose runs back to a hose reel at the bottom left. On the right, a woman with a black bob, a yellow dress and red shoes is raking a small pile of leaves on the grass beside a stone path. At the bottom right, on the path, a small boy with curly hair, a yellow shirt and orange shorts has both arms in the air and his eyes closed, as if he is jumping or cheering. On top of the fence, a blue bird is perched and an orange cat is creeping along the fence towards it, so the cat looks about to pounce on the bird. Strangely, a cardboard box is hanging in mid-air in the middle of the garden, tilted, with its shadow on the grass well below it, as if it has been thrown or is falling, but nobody is near it. There are also two potted plants by the fence, a birdhouse on a pole behind the fence, and a red disc (a frisbee or a pet bowl) on the grass at the bottom.
