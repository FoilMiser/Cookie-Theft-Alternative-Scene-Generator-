## Free description

This is a sunny day at a small playground in a park, with a bright sun in the top-left corner, two white clouds, a long hedge of trees in the background and a dark metal rail fence running across the whole picture. On the left there is a playground slide with a red frame, a dark leaning ladder and a yellow-and-orange chute, but nobody is on it. In the front left, a small boy with curly black hair, a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts is sitting in a wooden sandbox, leaning back on his hands next to a sandcastle with a little red flag, with a red bucket and a blue spade beside him. In the middle front, a woman (or older girl) with a short black bob, a yellow dress and red shoes is running towards the right with her arms pumping. On the right, a woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers is sitting on a wooden bench in front of the fence, drinking from a white cup. In front of her is a red seesaw on a grey triangular stand; nobody is on it, yet the plank is perfectly level. A small yellow ball lies on its own on the grass in the bottom-right corner, and there is a big tree at the far right and a green rubbish bin by the fence. The running woman seems to be heading towards the seesaw or the ball, but it is not clear what she is running for. The thing that looks most wrong is the empty seesaw sitting perfectly level, since an empty seesaw would normally rest with one end down.
