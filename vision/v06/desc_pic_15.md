# Picture 15

## Free description

This is a busy, sunny park with a pond on the left, a lamp post in the middle, a winding path on the right and green hills behind. On the far left a woman in a yellow dress reads a red book in a seated position, but there is nothing under her: no bench, chair or rock. In front of her is a bed of colourful flowers, and a green rubbish bin sits in the bottom-left corner. In the front, a white dog with brown spots licks a pink scoop of ice cream that has fallen and is melting on the grass. A little girl in a pink dress stands next to the dog holding an ice cream cone, so the scoop may be hers. Beside her, a woman in a blue jacket and dark trousers is kicking a blue ball. Near the pond a man in a red shirt and jeans takes a photo with a camera pointed left. Behind the lamp post an older man in a brown vest stands with his hands on his hips and what looks like a blue bucket upside down on his head. A worker in an orange cap and orange safety vest walks along the path carrying a yellow bag. On a bench a woman in a purple top eats a red apple, and a brown dog sits by the bench looking up at her as if begging. On the path an old woman with grey hair and glasses eats a pink ice cream cone. On the far right a boy in a yellow shirt points up at a blue ball stuck in the tree.
