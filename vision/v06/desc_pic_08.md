# pic_08

## Free description

This is a sunny day in a park with a fence and green hedges across the back. There is a playground with a slide on the left, a swing set in the middle, a seesaw at the front right and a bench and a tree on the right. On the far left a woman with a brown ponytail is flying a yellow kite high in the sky. Behind the slide, a bearded man in a green shirt stands with his hands on his hips, and he is wearing an upside-down blue bucket on his head as if it were a hat. In the front left, a dog has its nose in a pink ice cream that has fallen on the ground, while a little girl in a pink dress stands next to it holding what looks like an empty cone, looking at it. In the middle front, a man in an orange cap and orange safety vest is walking along reading a book, and there is a red ball on the grass right in front of his feet, so he looks about to trip over it. Next to him is a bicycle whose wheels are square boxes instead of round wheels, which is impossible to ride. On the right, an old woman with grey hair and glasses is waving, a young man in a red shirt sits on the bench reading a red book, and a blue ball is stuck up in the tree. A boy in a yellow shirt at the far right has both arms up, maybe reaching for the ball in the tree. An orange cat is lying on the grass under the empty seesaw.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The blue thing on the bearded man's head looks most like an upside-down bucket; it could also be read as an odd tall blue hat (like a fez). Either way it looks out of place.
- The boy at the far right with his arms raised could be reaching/jumping for the blue ball stuck in the tree, or simply cheering/waving. He is to the right of the tree, not directly under the ball.
- The little girl's cone: it looks empty, which together with the ice cream on the ground suggests she dropped it and the dog is eating it, but I can't be certain the ice cream on the ground was hers.
- The walking man's red ball: it is right ahead of his feet, so he seems about to trip over it, but it could also just be lying nearby.
- The bicycle with square box-shaped wheels is physically impossible to ride; it looks deliberately wrong rather than a drawing mistake.
- The orange cat is small and partly hidden by the seesaw base area; it reads as lying down asleep, though it could be read as lying injured or playing.
- Nothing looks like it is floating by accident; the kite, ball in tree and swing seat all look intentionally placed.
