# Picture 19

## Free description

This is a sunny park with a pond on the left, a lamp post in the centre, a winding path on the right and green hills behind. On the far left, a woman in a yellow dress waters a flower bed by pouring water out of a black frying pan, which is a strange thing to use instead of a watering can. A white duck swims on the pond. A bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands at the edge of the pond and throws bits of bread from a white container toward it. In the front centre, a worker in an orange cap and orange safety vest sits on a red-and-white picnic blanket with a picnic basket, laughing with his eyes closed. Next to him, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds the string of a yellow kite flying high in the sky in front of a cloud. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sprinkles seeds from a white container onto the path, where a blue bird and a red bird peck at them. Green rubbish bins stand in the bottom-left corner and next to the lamp post.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Watering flowers with a frying pan is the obviously odd thing. The object is clearly a pan with a long handle, not a watering can.
- Two short white lines on the pond, between the duck and the worker's head, could be the duck's wake or ripples, or glints on the water. They sit a little away from the duck and could also look like stray marks.
- The kite string is very long and thin. It runs from the boy's hand up past the man in green and his flying bread toward the kite. It doesn't go through anyone, but it passes close to the man's hand.
- The picnic basket overlaps the worker's lower legs or feet. It could be resting on the blanket in front of him or sitting on his legs.
- I can't tell what the worker is laughing at. It could be the woman with the frying pan, or he could just be happy.
- I can't tell whether the purple woman's seeds are meant for the birds or are just being scattered on the path. The birds are eating them either way.
