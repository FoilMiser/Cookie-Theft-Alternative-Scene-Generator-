# Picture 14

## Free description

This is a sunny day at a playground on a grassy lawn, with a fence and a row of bushes along the back and a big tree on the left. On the left a small girl in a pink dress sits on the raised end of a red seesaw. At the low end a man in a red shirt and blue jeans sits on the board, facing her. Behind them a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks to the right, pushing a teal-and-blue pram with a baby in it. In the middle is a swing set with one empty red seat, and next to it a green rubbish bin by the fence. On the right is a slide with a red frame, an orange-and-yellow chute and a ladder. At the far right a woman in a blue jacket and dark trousers holds a camera to her face and points it left across the playground. In the middle of the grass a red bird pecks at the ground, and an orange cat runs toward it from the right, so the cat may be about to pounce on the bird. In the front a brown dog sits up on its haunches and holds an open book in its front paws as if reading, which a real dog can't do. In the front right is an empty sandbox with a grey rock next to it.
