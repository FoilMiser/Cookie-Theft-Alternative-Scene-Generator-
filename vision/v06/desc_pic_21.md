# Picture 21

## Free description
It is a sunny day at a park playground with a dark metal railing along the back, green bushes behind it, a large tree on the left, the sun in the top right and two clouds. On the left, a little girl in a pink dress sits on the raised end of a red seesaw while a bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers sits on the low end, which is down at the ground, with his legs stretched out in front of him. Behind them an older man in a flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat with braces and dark trousers stands near the swing set waving with one raised hand. In the middle is a dark grey swing set with a single empty swing with a red seat, and a green litter bin stands behind it by the railing. On the right, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts stands on the top platform of a red-framed slide with an orange-and-yellow chute, both arms raised high as if cheering, standing up without holding on. At the far right, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans holds a camera to his eye pointed to the left, apparently taking a photo of the child on the slide. In the front centre, oddly, a brown dog with a red neckerchief sits up on its haunches like a person and holds an open book in its front paws as if it is reading, next to an empty wooden sandbox. A yellow ball lies alone on the grass at the bottom left, and a grey rock sits on the grass at the bottom right.
