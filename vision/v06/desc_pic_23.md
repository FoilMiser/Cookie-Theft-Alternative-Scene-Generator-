# Picture 23

## Free description
This is a back garden on a sunny day, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a tree behind it, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate at the left, and a small birdhouse on a pole behind the fence. At the bottom left, a boy in a blue hoodie and dark trousers is sweeping the paved path with a yellow broom. Next to the path stands a dark grey swing set with one empty swing with a red seat. In the middle, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts has just kicked a blue ball, which is in mid-air heading toward a man in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest, so the ball may be about to hit him. That man is watering the flower bed with a garden hose, and a stream of water arcs from the nozzle onto the flowers; the hose runs back to a reel next to the house. Behind them a washing line between two posts holds a yellow-and-red striped towel and blue jeans. On the far right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a bench reading a red book, and right beside her a blue bird is pecking at a huge sandwich that seems to be resting on or hovering at her lap, while she doesn't notice. In the front, a teal watering can stands on the grass, and next to it is a grey wheelbarrow whose wheel is square instead of round, which could not roll. The sun is in the top left and there are two white clouds.
