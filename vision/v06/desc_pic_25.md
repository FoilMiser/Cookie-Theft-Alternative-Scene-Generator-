# Picture 25

## Free description
This is a paved town street or shopping square on a sunny day, in front of a row of four colourful two-storey shop buildings (pink, pale green-grey, peach and cream) with striped awnings. On the left, an older man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat stands at a fruit and vegetable stall holding a white paper bag, and a trail of small seeds or crumbs is falling from his hand down to the pavement, where a blue bird is pecking them up. In front of him, a woman with long dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers is eating a pink ice-cream cone. In the middle of the foreground a red bicycle stands on its kickstand on the pavement with nobody on it, and a teal bicycle is parked in the same way further back near the tree. Behind, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts sits on a wooden bench happily eating a red apple with eyes closed, while a tan dog with a red neckerchief rears up on its back legs in front of the bench with its tongue out, looking at the child as if begging for food. Oddly, a white dog with brown spots and a blue neckerchief sits up like a person on the pavement holding an open book in its front paws, as if reading it. On the right, a girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes walks along smiling, holding a yellow balloon on a string. There is also a tall black street lamp, a planter of flowers in front of the green building, a tree in a round planter, and a potted plant in each bottom corner; the sun is top left with two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The dog with the book: a dog reading is impossible; it is posed like a person reading. It could alternatively be read as just holding a book, but the pose strongly suggests reading.
- The seeds/crumbs falling from the older man: he could be deliberately scattering birdseed to feed the blue bird, or his bag could be leaking without him noticing (his back is turned to the bird and he is facing the stall). The dots might also be bits of food rather than seeds.
- The older man's face is not visible (no eyes or mouth shown; he is facing away toward the stall), which looks a little odd.
- The tan dog's pose is anatomically strange: it is half upright with its long tail along the ground and its back legs bent like it's kneeling. It could be read as begging for the child's apple, jumping up, or just walking toward the bench.
- The child on the bench could be eating an apple or a red ball-shaped sweet; it looks like an apple. Eyes closed suggests enjoying it rather than sleeping.
- The red bicycle standing alone in the middle of the walkway with no owner nearby is notable; someone could trip over it.
- The woman's mouth area has a dark mark that could be a smear of something or just the drawn mouth.
