- What the man is photographing: his camera points to the right, at about head height. He could be photographing the woman watering the flowers, the street or buildings, or the bird in the sky. It does not clearly point at the girl and dog, which are low down and slightly behind him.
- The girl's hand at her face: it most likely means she is crying because she dropped her ice cream, but it could also be read as her just rubbing her eye or looking on in surprise. I could not see clear tears at this size.
