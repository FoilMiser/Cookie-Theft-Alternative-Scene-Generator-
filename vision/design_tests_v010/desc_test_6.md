## Unsure or ambiguous

- The watering can's water goes upward instead of down onto the flowers. It could be a deliberate "impossible" detail (water flowing uphill/backward), or the arc could be meant as a splash or spray. To me it reads as water flowing upward, which is impossible.
- I can't tell exactly what the man is photographing. He might be taking a picture of the woman watering the flowers, of the dog with the dropped ice cream and the girl, or just of the street in general.
- The girl's hand at her face most likely means she is crying because she dropped her ice cream. It could also just be her rubbing her eye.
- I can't tell whether the dog is just sniffing the ice cream or already licking/eating it. Its mouth is right at the scoop.
- The bicycle looks rather short and compressed, with its two wheels close together. It is hard to tell how it sits in the rack, whether it is properly in the hoops or just drawn in front of them.
- The fruit stall has no stallholder. Possibly the man is the stallholder, but he is standing in front of it facing away, so it reads as unattended.
- The dog's shadow is much larger and darker than the shadows of the people, which looks slightly odd.
- The water drops near the top of the stream sit in front of the grey shop door and could be misread as marks on the door.
