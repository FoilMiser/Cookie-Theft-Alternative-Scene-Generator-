# Picture 13

## Free description
This is a back garden on a sunny day, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence and gate at the back, and a stepping-stone path with potted plants on the right. Near the house, a girl or young woman with a brown ponytail and blue top has just thrown a red-and-white ball; a dotted line shows it flying up toward the tree behind the house, so it may end up stuck in the tree. A bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers stands in the middle with his hands up in front of him, facing the thrower, as if he was expecting to catch it, but the ball has gone up to the left of him. A purple balloon is floating up above the fence with its string hanging loose, and a little girl in a pink dress below it has both arms in the air, looking upset or reaching, as if she has let go of it. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress with red shoes stands by the fence with one hand raised, waving or pointing. In the front, a man in a red T-shirt and jeans is raking a small pile of leaves, with an empty wheelbarrow beside him and a teal watering can nearby. In the bottom left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a bench drinking from a mug with her eyes closed, looking relaxed and not paying attention to anything. There is also a flower bed in front of the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The ball's path heads up into the tree rather than toward the man in green, so it could be a bad throw that is about to get stuck in the tree or go over the fence; or the man may be about to move to catch it.
- The man in green's raised hands could mean he is ready to catch, clapping, or calling out to the thrower.
- The little girl could be reaching for the balloon she has let go of, crying about it, or simply jumping/cheering. It is not certain the balloon was hers, but she is the closest person to it.
- The woman in yellow could be waving to someone, waving at the balloon, or pointing up at it.
- The woman on the bench has her eyes closed and pink cheeks: she could be enjoying her drink, dozing, or the drink could be hot.
- The thrower overlaps the edge of the house wall slightly, which reads as her standing right against the house.
