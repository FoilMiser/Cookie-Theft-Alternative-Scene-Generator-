# Picture 12

## Free description
This is a busy park on a sunny day, with green hills, a pond on the left, a lamp post in the middle and a sandy path curving down on the right. Up on the grass at the back right, a woman with a brown ponytail and blue top is flying an orange kite; the kite string runs across the sky and passes right by the top of the lamp post, so it may be caught on it. At the foot of the lamp post a white spotted dog has its nose down at a small pink object, and a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands nearby with his arm stretched out to the left. On the left, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses is walking left carrying a yellow bag, and an elderly man with a grey beard and cap is watering a flower bed with a hose wand. In the front middle there is an empty red-and-white picnic blanket, an empty wheelbarrow, a grey rock and a cardboard box. A lawn sprinkler on the edge of the path is spraying arcs of water across the path straight onto the cardboard box, which has water drops on it, so the box is getting soaked. On the path near the sprinkler, a little girl in a pink dress is standing with one hand raised, as if waving. On the far right, a man in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest is striding or running to the left in front of a large tree.
