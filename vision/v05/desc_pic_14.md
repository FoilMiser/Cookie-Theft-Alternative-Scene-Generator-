This is a town square or shopping street on a sunny day, with a row of four colourful shop buildings across the back, each with a striped awning (red-and-white, green-and-white, orange-and-white), and a paved plaza in front. In the front left, an elderly man with a grey beard, a blue-grey cap, a white shirt and brown waistcoat is walking along eating a pink ice-cream cone, with his eyes closed and rosy cheeks as if he is really enjoying it. There is also a tree in a round planter on the left, an empty wooden bench or picnic table in the middle, a black lamp post, and a dark rubbish bin in the front right. Only two people are in the picture.
