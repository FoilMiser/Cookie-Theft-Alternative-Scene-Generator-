# Picture 15

## Free description

This is a sunny day in a park, with a bright sun at the top right, two white clouds, green rolling hills and trees in the background. On the left there is an oval blue pond with cattails at each end, and a white duck is swimming on it. In the middle of the picture there is a tall black lamp post. A brown dog with a red collar or bandana is standing up on its hind legs right against the lamp post, with an open newspaper or book standing upright beside it, so it looks as if the dog is reading, or else sniffing the post. On the right an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, is walking along a winding sandy path pushing a teal pram with a baby lying in it under a blue hood. A blue bird is perched in the big tree on the right. In the foreground there is an empty red-and-white checked picnic blanket on the grass. Just below it a red bird is pecking at the ground, and a black cat with white paws is creeping up on it from the right in a low, stalking crouch, so it looks like the cat is about to pounce on the bird. The odd thing is the dog standing upright with an open newspaper, as if it were reading.
