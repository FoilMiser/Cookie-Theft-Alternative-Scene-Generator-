# Picture 19

## Free description

This is the back garden again, but the sky is grey with three big dark storm clouds and no sun, so it looks like it is about to rain. An elderly man in a flat cap sits on the bench by the house with his eyes closed, sniffing a red apple, while a brown dog with a red collar stands up with its front paws on his knee, tongue out, as if it wants the apple. An orange cat is walking along the top of the fence behind the house. In the middle a bearded man in a green shirt and beige trousers is pegging a white T-shirt onto the clothesline, which already holds blue jeans and a yellow towel, with a laundry basket of clothes at his feet. To the right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller, and a section of fence is already blue. Her paint tray sits on the grass, and a line of blue paw prints leads from the tray to a white dog with brown spots standing on the path, so the dog has stepped in the paint. In front a man in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest is digging a hole with a spade, with a pile of earth beside it and a red pet bowl right at the edge of the hole. A little girl in a pink dress stands in the front looking up with a surprised face and pointing at the sky, probably at the storm clouds. It seems odd that people are hanging washing and painting outdoors just as a storm is coming.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The elderly man may be smelling the apple or about to bite it; his eyes are closed and cheeks rosy, so he looks content.
- The dog may be begging for the apple or just greeting him.
- The girl is pointing up; most likely at the storm clouds, but it could also be at the cat on the fence.
- The red bowl at the edge of the hole could be the dog's bowl about to fall in, or just set down there; it partly overlaps the hole.
- The woman's tool could be a paint roller or a large brush on a long handle.
- The grey sky with people doing outdoor chores (hanging washing, painting) seems like a mismatch with the weather, though it is not impossible.
