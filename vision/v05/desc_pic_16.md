# Picture 16

## Free description

This is a sunny playground with a tree on the left, a dark railing fence across the back and a green hedge behind it. On the left a seesaw is tipped down to the right: a small girl in pink sits on the raised left end holding the handle, and a boy in a red T-shirt and blue jeans sits on the low right end with his legs stretched out. A girl in a blue hoodie and black trousers with a ponytail stands right behind the boy, almost back to back with him, and is kicking a blue ball with one foot. In the middle a man in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest is reaching up with both hands as if to catch a red ball in the air. The ball has a dotted line showing it was thrown by a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts on the right, who still has his arm out. Behind the man a brown dog with a red collar is crouched in a play bow, tail up, as if it wants to join in. Next to the slide a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is standing up on its hind legs behind an open book that stands upright on the grass, as if the dog is reading it, which is impossible. On the far right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is walking along carrying a cardboard box. In front there is an empty sandbox, a red bucket with a blue spade, a yellow ball in the bottom-left corner and a grey rock in the bottom-right corner.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The white dog with the book: it looks like it is standing up reading the book, which is impossible; another reading is that it is just standing behind a book that has been left open on the grass. Either way the book stands upright on its own with nothing holding it.
- The ponytail girl and the seated boy overlap: their heads almost touch back to back and her body is drawn right over the end of the seesaw, so it looks as if she is standing inside the seesaw end or pressed against the boy.
- The ponytail girl's arms are confusing: one hand is at her chin and the other sticks out to the left at waist height, which does not match her facing right while kicking.
- The man's hands are near the ball's path but the ball is still above and to the right of him; he could be about to catch it or it could go over him.
- The yellow ball and grey rock sit right on the bottom corners and seem unconnected to anyone.
