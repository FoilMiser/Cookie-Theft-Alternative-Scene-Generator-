# Picture 21

## Free description

This is a sunny playground with a tree on the left, a dark railing fence and hedge at the back, a swing set in the middle, a slide on the right and an empty sandbox in front. A man in a green T-shirt sits on a bench reading a red book, with a small stack of books beside him and a red bird sitting on the bench next to the books. On the seesaw a little girl in pink sits on the raised end, and an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses sits on the low end. In front of the seesaw a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and looks sad, because a white dog with brown spots is eating a pink blob on the ground, which looks like his dropped scoop of ice cream. A black-and-white cat is lying asleep on the grass near the swings, and the single swing hangs empty. An elderly man in a cap and brown waistcoat is walking along reading a red book, heading toward a red bucket on the grass, so he might trip over it. A man in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest is walking a brown dog on a red lead past the slide. A blue bird sits on the fence on the right. On the far right a wooden ladder is standing upright on the grass leaning on nothing, which is impossible.

- The boy's head overlaps the little girl's yellow shoes on the seesaw, so her feet seem to rest on top of his head; this looks like a drawing overlap.
