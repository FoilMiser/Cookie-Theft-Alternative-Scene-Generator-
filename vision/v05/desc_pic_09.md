# Picture 09

## Free description
This is a back garden on a sunny day, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back and a wooden gate in the fence on the right. A clothesline is strung between two posts with a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes pegged on it, and a basket full of laundry sits on the grass under it. A red balloon is floating above the clothesline, and its string hangs straight down to the line, so it looks caught or tied there. A little girl in a pink dress is standing in the middle of the lawn, looking up and pointing at the balloon. A man in a red T-shirt and jeans is walking toward the left along the lawn with a white spotted dog on a red lead; the dog is out in front, heading left toward the girl and the cat. An orange cat is lying on the grass in the middle, apparently asleep or rolling, near the girl. In front of the house there is a bed of colourful flowers and a hose reel, and on the right a stepping-stone path leads to the gate with two potted plants sitting on it. It looks as if the dog might notice the cat and chase it, and the balloon might come loose and float away.
