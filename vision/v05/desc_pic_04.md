# Picture 04

(Left/right are from the viewer's point of view.)

## Free description

This is a sunny park with green hills and trees, a pond with bulrushes on the left, a winding sandy path on the right and a lamp post in the middle. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands at the edge of the pond throwing pieces of bread to a white duck swimming nearby. Front left, an older woman with grey hair and glasses is digging with a spade beside a pile of earth, next to a flower bed. Next to her, an older man in a cap and brown waistcoat sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket with a picnic basket, eyes shut, relaxing or dozing. An orange cat lies asleep at the edge of the blanket. On a bench to the right, a bearded man in a green shirt is reading a red book and doesn't notice that a blue bird on the bench beside him is pecking at his sandwich. Behind the lamp post, a white dog with brown spots has its nose in a pink object on the grass, and further back a boy in yellow and orange points or reaches toward it. At the right, a sprinkler is spraying water onto a cardboard box sitting on the grass. At the front there is a grey rock and a wheelbarrow whose wheel is square, which is impossible. The odd things are the square wheelbarrow wheel, a sprinkler soaking a cardboard box, and the duck's ripple lines being in front of it instead of behind.

- The boy at the back could be pointing at the dog, calling it, or reaching for something. The pink thing might be his.
