This is a quiet park playground on a sunny day, with a fence and a line of trees across the back, a large tree on the left and two clouds and a sun in the sky. On the right is a slide with a red frame, a blue platform, a grey ladder and an orange-and-yellow chute. Next to her in the sandpit is a sandcastle with a little red flag, and at the left corner of the sandpit there is a red bucket and a blue spade. In the middle of the picture there is an empty wooden bench or picnic table and a green rubbish bin by the fence, and a grey rock sits in the bottom right corner. There is nobody else around.
