# Picture 10

## Free description
This is a park on a sunny day, with rolling green hills, trees, a pond on the right and a sandy path winding down from the left into the bottom of the picture. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and grey skirt is standing on the grass near a lamp post, fishing in the pond with a long rod; her red-and-white float is bobbing on the water with little ripples around it. In the front, a man in an orange cap, orange high-visibility vest and jeans is standing beside a wooden bench with his arm stretched over it, holding a small tan object, as if painting, sanding or fixing the bench. On the path in the bottom left a blue bird is pecking at a few seeds or crumbs on the ground. In the middle of the grass there is an empty red-and-white checked picnic blanket. On the right, a woman with a brown ponytail and a blue hoodie is kneeling on one knee beside a flower bed, holding a small tool, with a pot of pink flowers in front of her, as if she is planting it. Reeds grow at both ends of the pond. From where it is drawn, the lamp post appears to stand right on top of the workman's head.
