# Picture 02

(Left/right are from the viewer's point of view.)

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden. On the left is a cream house with a red roof and a blue door, with a tall tree behind it, and a long wooden fence with a gate at the right end runs across the back. The sun is at the top right with two clouds. In the middle, a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts has just thrown a red ball. A dotted line shows the ball climbing toward a man in a red T-shirt and jeans, who stands with his hands together in front of him ready to catch it. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping the garden path, which has stepping stones, with a yellow-bristled broom. A tan-and-white dog sits at the blue door holding its red lead in its mouth, eyes closed and looking happy, as if asking to go for a walk. At the front, a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is in a play bow (front end down, tail up), looking eager to join in. Near the house there is a bed of colourful flowers. At the front there is a grey wheelbarrow and a red bowl on the grass, and a teal watering can stands near the path. The ball is about to be caught. Nothing looks clearly impossible.
