# Picture 17

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue front door, with a tree behind it and a tall wooden fence running across the back of the garden to a wooden gate on the right. A boy with curly black hair, a yellow top and orange shorts is sitting on a wooden bench near the house, drinking from a white cup. In front of the house there is a round flower bed full of red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers. In the middle-right a young man in a blue hoodie and black trousers is raking up a small pile of fallen leaves. An orange cat is lying curled on the grass in the foreground, apparently asleep with its head on its paws. Near it there is an empty red pet bowl on the grass. On the right a sandy path with stepping stones runs from the front of the picture to the gate, and two potted green plants stand on the path. Nothing in the picture looks wrong or impossible; it is a quiet, ordinary scene.
