# Picture 08

## Free description
This is a sunny day at a park playground, with a fence and a line of trees across the back, two clouds and a bright sun in the top right. In the front left there is a red seesaw. A girl or young woman with a brown ponytail and a blue hoodie sits on the left end, and a small girl in a pink dress sits on the right end. A child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is standing right in the middle of the seesaw, over the pivot, holding up a kite string with one hand. The string runs up to a purple kite that sits in the branches of the big tree on the left, so the kite looks stuck in the tree and the child seems to be tugging at it to get it down. Behind them a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a wooden bench or picnic table drinking from a white cup and looking toward the left. On the right there is an empty slide with a red frame and an orange-and-yellow chute, and a green rubbish bin stands by the fence. A yellow ball lies on the grass in the bottom left corner and a grey rock sits in the bottom right. It looks unsafe for the child to stand in the middle of a seesaw while two people are sitting on it.
