# Picture 01

(Left/right are from the viewer's point of view.)

## Free description

It's a sunny day on a shopping street lined with four two-storey buildings (pink, pale green, peach and cream) that have striped shop awnings. The sun is in the top left and there are two small clouds. On the left, a woman in a yellow dress stands by a fruit-and-vegetable stall with both arms thrown up and her mouth open, looking alarmed. A gust of wind, drawn as white lines with leaves in it, is blowing her orange hat off into the air above her. In front of her, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is reading a red book in a sitting position, but there is no chair or bench under her, so she is sitting on nothing. In the middle, a bearded man in a green shirt sits on a wooden bench eating a pink ice-cream cone. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar stands up on its hind legs beside him, apparently begging for the ice cream. A boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts stands in the foreground waving. Behind the bench, a man in an orange cap and orange hi-vis vest is taking a photo with a camera, next to a teal bicycle that stands up on its own. On the right, a little girl in a pink dress reaches up with both arms toward a blue ball stuck in the leaves of a tree. Near her, an older man in a cap and brown waistcoat is licking a pink ice-cream cone, and a young woman with a ponytail in a blue hoodie walks past carrying a yellow shopping bag. At the front, a red bicycle has black square wheels, which is impossible. Next to it is a small café table with a cup on it and two chairs. The two things that look clearly wrong are the woman sitting on thin air and the bicycle with square wheels.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- **Woman in purple sitting on nothing:** she is clearly in a seated pose, but there is no chair, bench, wall or box under her. Either a seat is missing or she is supposed to be sitting on an invisible chair. This looks wrong.
- **Red bicycle wheels:** the wheels are drawn as black squares with lighter inner squares. Either this is an impossible square-wheeled bicycle, or the wheels are hidden behind two black boxes. Either way it looks wrong.
- The teal bicycle stands upright with no visible kickstand or anything holding it up.
- It's unclear what the man with the camera is photographing. He points it left, so it might be the woman losing her hat, or just the street.
- The boy in yellow could be waving at the dog, at the man on the bench, or at someone off to the right.
- The girl in pink is probably reaching for her ball stuck in the tree, since her face looks upset. She could also just be stretching or cheering.
- The flying orange hat could be a baseball cap or a sun hat. It is most likely the woman in yellow's, since she is reaching for it.
