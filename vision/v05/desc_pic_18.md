# Picture 18

## Free description

This is the same kind of sunny back garden, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence and a gate on the right, and a lot going on. A man in a red T-shirt and jeans sits on a bench by the house reading a red book. Next to the house a man in an orange high-visibility vest stands with his hands on his hips, wearing a blue bucket upside down on his head like a hat. In the tree behind him there is an orange fish among the leaves, which is impossible. In the middle a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is holding the end of a green garden hose, but only a single drop is coming out. A white dog with brown spots is standing right on the hose near the flower bed, which seems to be blocking the water. A clothesline behind her holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel. A boy in a yellow top and orange shorts is pointing up at a red balloon floating away with its string loose. A girl in a blue hoodie with a ponytail walks along reading a red book. At the gate a small girl in a pink dress has both arms thrown up in the air and looks happy. In the front a white goat with horns has its head down eating the grass, next to a blue bucket, and a red bird flies across the sky at the top. The odd things are the fish in the tree, the bucket on the man's head and the goat in a back garden.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The bucket on the man's head: it could be a blue bucket-shaped hat, but the handle-less flared shape and the orange cap brim sticking out underneath make it look like a bucket placed over his cap.
- The hose: only one drop comes out. I read this as the dog standing on the hose and blocking it, but it could also be that the tap has just been turned off. The woman seems to be looking at the nozzle.
- The balloon: nobody is holding it and the string hangs loose, so it seems to have escaped; it is not clear whose it was.
- The small girl at the gate: her knees are bent and arms up, which could be a jump of joy, cheering, or reaching for something; she is drawn quite small and sits right on the top edge of the path.
- The goat has a pale cream oval hanging from its mouth; it could be its beard, a leaf, or something it is chewing (a sock or paper).
- The fish in the tree looks like it is floating or sitting in the leaves, which is impossible.
- The goat's presence in a back garden is unusual.
