# Picture 03

(Left/right are from the viewer's point of view.)

## Free description

This is a shopping street with pink, pale-green, peach and cream buildings, but the sky is grey with three dark storm clouds and there is no sun, so it looks like rain is coming. On the left, a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts standing by a street lamp has thrown a red ball. A dotted line shows it going up toward a man in a red T-shirt and jeans, who has his hands out ready to catch it. To the man's right, a young person with brown hair in a blue hoodie and dark trousers is jumping or cheering with both arms in the air and a big grin. In the front centre, a small girl in a pink dress holds a yellow balloon on a string and looks out at the viewer. Front left, a tan dog with a red collar lies flat on the pavement with its head down, maybe asleep. On the right, an older man in a cap and brown waistcoat points upward, toward the tree or the dark sky, with his mouth open in surprise. There is a fruit stall and a cardboard box on the left, a plain cardboard box in the middle, two rubbish bins, a café table with a cup, and potted plants. One odd detail is a grey shadow on the ground at front centre with nothing above it. It is also a little odd that people are playing outdoors under storm clouds.
