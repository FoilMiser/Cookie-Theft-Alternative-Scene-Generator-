# Picture 20

## Free description

This is a sunny town street with a paved pedestrian square in front of a row of colourful two-storey shops (pink, pale green, peach and cream) with striped awnings. On the left there is a fruit stall with a striped canopy and boxes of green, yellow, orange and red fruit, with a cardboard box on the ground beside it and a potted plant in the corner. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. Further back, near a black lamp post, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts stands holding the string of a yellow balloon. In the middle an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses is walking along reading a red book, and there is a cardboard box right in front of her feet, so she looks about to trip over it. On the right a woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds a small box in one hand and has her other hand out, and a little line of small seeds or crumbs lies on the ground near the bottom-right corner, as if she is scattering them. There is also a tree in a round planter and a planter of flowers in front of the shops. The main thing about to happen is the elderly woman tripping over the box.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The woman in purple: she could be scattering birdseed or crumbs from a small box (but there are no birds to eat them), or she could be holding a cup and the dots are something she has dropped or spilled. The dots are a little distance from her, near the corner of the picture.
- The boy's balloon string passes straight down over his face, so the string appears to go through his head; this is probably just because the balloon is directly above him.
- The elderly woman has not seen the box because she is reading; she seems about to trip, though she might just step past it.
- The white strip on the elderly woman's top could be a blouse showing or a folded paper/newspaper tucked under her arm.
- The cardboard box by the stall could be a delivery or stock for the fruit stall.
