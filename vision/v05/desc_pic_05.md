# Picture 05

(Left/right are from the viewer's point of view.)

## Free description

This is a sunny park with a pond on the left, a winding path on the right, trees and hills, and a lamp post in the middle. Front left, a woman in a yellow dress is watering a flower bed with a teal watering can. The stream of water seems to run right through the bed and drip out underneath it onto the grass. In the middle, a young woman with a ponytail in a blue hoodie has just thrown a red ball (shown by a dotted line) toward a man in an orange cap and hi-vis vest, who reaches up to catch it. The lamp post is right between his hands and the ball, so the ball may hit the post. Below them, a white dog with brown spots sits behind a large open book standing upright on the grass, as if the dog is reading it, and there is an empty bench in front. On the path at the right, a man in a red T-shirt and jeans is running, and by the tree a boy in yellow and orange is kicking a blue ball toward him. In the front centre, a blue bird pecks at the ground while a black cat with white paws creeps low toward it, looking ready to pounce. The odd things are the water passing through the flower bed, the dog with an upright book, and the lamp post in the way of the catch.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- **Water:** the stream from the watering can crosses the flowers and seems to come out below the bottom edge of the bed, with drops on the grass. Either it passes through the bed, which is impossible, or she is overshooting and watering the grass.
- **Lamp post in the way:** the man's hands are at or against the lamp post while the ball is on the other side, so the post is between him and the ball. His hands may also be passing behind the post.
- The book stands upright with nothing holding it. The dog seems to be reading it, or may just be sitting behind it. It's not clear whose book it is, since nobody is on the bench.
- The man in red could be running toward the blue ball, toward the boy, or just jogging along the path.
- The cat is most likely stalking the bird and about to pounce, but it could simply be walking.
- Two separate ball games are going on at the same time, one with the red ball and one with the blue ball.
