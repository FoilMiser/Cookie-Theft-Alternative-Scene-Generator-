# Picture 06

(Left/right are from the viewer's point of view.)

## Free description

This is a sunny shopping street of cream, peach, pale-green and pink buildings with striped awnings, with the sun at the top right and two clouds. On the left, an older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, holds out her hand near a red bird that is pecking at seeds or crumbs on the pavement. She seems to be feeding it. In the middle, a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts has thrown a red ball (shown by a dotted line) up toward a man in an orange cap and hi-vis vest. The man stands beside a street lamp with his hands raised, ready to catch it. A yellow balloon floats just above him, and its string hangs down to the top of his cap. In front of him, a small girl in a pink dress points up at the balloon with her mouth open, looking surprised. Perhaps it is her balloon and it got away. Around them are a tree with a green bin, a café table with a cup, a flower planter, a fruit stall and cardboard box on the right, a dark rubbish bin, and potted plants. The oddest thing is the balloon string, which seems to be attached to the man's head.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- **Balloon string:** the string ends at the man's cap. The balloon might be tied to his hat, which would be odd. It might also have escaped with its string happening to drape over his head, or be caught on the lamp post.
- The girl is probably pointing at the balloon, which may be hers and got away. She could also be pointing at the ball.
- The older woman's open hand is held out level, not directly over the bird. She may be scattering seeds or offering bread. The small brown thing in her other hand could be bread, a bag of seed, or a small purse.
- The red bird's seeds seem to be scattered around it rather than exactly under its beak. It is clearly pecking at the ground, though.
