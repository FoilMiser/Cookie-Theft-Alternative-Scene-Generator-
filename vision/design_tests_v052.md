# v0.52 design tests (whole scenes)

v0.52 changes the cast and no placement rule (revision 48 stands), so its whole-scene test is the bench of `isolation_v052.md`: eighteen of v0.51's screened pictures built again from their own records with the new cast, each with the same templates and the same people as v0.51 drew, and read with the screen's own prompt beside v0.51's own pictures (`isolation_v052/who`; the pictures are `blind/pic_NNN.png`, the readings `desc_pic_NNN.md`, and which picture is which is in `isolation_v052/who_map.json`).

What the eighteen pictures of the new cast were read for:

- **Who the grown figures are.** By the first inventory line that names each figure's clothes, a grown figure was called by a young name in 3 of 35 appearances with the new sleeves, for 4 of 35 in v0.51's own pictures read by the same nine readers.
- **Anything new and odd about an arm.** No unsure section, of the new cast's pictures or of v0.51's, has a line on a sleeve, a shoulder or an arm as drawn. Two lines name an arm for another reason: a raised arm that "overlaps the green awning, which is just layering", and a walker whose "head and arm face left" while her stride could be read the other way; both are in pictures of the new cast, and neither is about how an arm is drawn.
- **The upright sweeper** (`TPDB_SWEEP52` B) was read in ten pictures and not taken: the sweeper was grown in all ten, and in all ten with v0.51's pose.

No rule was added after these readings, and no picture was built again for them.
