# Review: pair 07 (playground)

## 1. People and animals in a
- Girl on slide (left): both arms are raised, but the hands are tiny blobs at the ends of thin arms. The neck is barely there, so the head sits on the shoulders.
- Girl on slide: the legs stretch down the slide and the feet are small yellow shapes. She is small overall, and her face details are hard to see.
- Boy with empty cone (centre bottom): one hand is at his eye (crying or rubbing his eye). The arm and hand at the face merge into the head outline and are hard to separate.
- Boy: the legs are long and thin with very small feet. The cone is held out at the side, and the hand round it is small.
- Dog eating the dropped ice cream: the head is a rounded lump with a large brown ear covering most of it and a white muzzle at the bottom. The face is hard to read.
- Dog: the red collar sits where the head joins the body, and the neck join looks a bit abrupt. The body and legs are thin but fine.
- Outlines are dark and fairly even, and the shading is light and flat.

## 2. People and animals in b
- Girl on slide: same as a. The hands are tiny blobs, there is almost no neck, and the face is small.
- Boy with empty cone: same as a. The hand at his eye merges with the head, the legs are thin and the feet small.
- Dog: same as a. The head is a lump with a large ear over it, and the collar join is abrupt.
- I can see no clear difference in proportions, outlines or shading between a and b.

## 3. Belonging
No difference. In both versions the figures use the same dark outlines and flat colours as the slide, swings, seesaw, sandpit and tree.

## 4. Easier to make out
No difference. In both versions the boy (crying, empty cone) and the girl (sliding, arms up) are equally clear. The dog's head and face are equally hard to read, though it is still clear the dog is at the spilled ice cream.

## 5. Choice
Either. The two versions look the same to me; both share the dog's unclear head and the girl's tiny hands.
