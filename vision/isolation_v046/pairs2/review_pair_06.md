# Review, pair 06 (playground)

Seen at normal size, the two versions look almost the same. I could not see any clear difference in line weight, shading or colour between the figures in a and b.

## 1. People and animals in a
- Child in yellow on the left of the seesaw: the legs hang down below the plank. The way the body joins the plank, with no clear seat, is hard to read.
- Child in pink on the right of the seesaw: the knees are pulled up high and the yellow shoe floats a little below the plank. It looks detached from the leg.
- Child in pink: the arms and hands gripping the handle are tucked into the body and hard to make out.
- Man with camera: his raised arm crosses in front of his face, so his face is mostly hidden. The arm and hand make a dark lump against his dark head.
- Boy kicking the ball: his eyes look shut or squinting and his head is turned down. The blue hood sits as a separate lump behind his neck.
- Boy kicking the ball: his arms are very thin and stick straight out, with small pale hands at the ends.
- No animals in the scene.

## 2. People and animals in b
- Child in yellow: the same as in a. The legs hang below the plank and the seat join is unclear.
- Child in pink: the same high knees, floating yellow shoe and tucked-in hands.
- Man with camera: the same. His arm hides his face.
- Boy kicking the ball: the same squinting face, hood lump and thin, stiff arms.
- No animals in the scene.

## 3. Belonging
No difference. In both versions the figures use the same dark outlines and flat colours as the seesaw, slide, fence and tree, and they have similar soft ground shadows.

## 4. Easier to make out
No difference. In both it is equally clear that there are two children on a seesaw, a man taking a photo and a boy in a cap kicking a ball. The man's face is equally hidden in both. The pink child's hands and the seat joins on the seesaw are equally unclear in both.

## 5. Choice
Either. The two versions look the same to me at normal viewing size.
