# Review, pair 02 (park: man feeding birds, woman reading on bench, older man walking dog)

At the size the pictures open, I could not see any real difference between a and b in how the figures are drawn. The only change I could make out is that the older man's white moustache is a little easier to see in b.

## 1. People and animals in a
- Man feeding birds: he leans forward with a long, thin, straight body. His legs are very long against his short upper body, and his head pokes forward on the neck.
- Man feeding birds: the bag in his hand and the falling crumbs are small. The crumbs are a thin dotted line that is easy to miss.
- Woman on bench: she sits on the front edge of the bench and does not look settled on the seat. Her knees jut forward and her lower legs drop steeply, so she looks half-standing.
- Woman on bench: her arms and hands holding the red book are small and bunched together at her chest.
- Older man with dog: his face is small and dark under the cap. The moustache barely shows and the features run together.
- Older man with dog: the arm holding the lead is bent stiffly, with a small, unclear hand.
- Dog: the head is large against the body and the legs are thin and stiff. It reads as a dog, but a slightly awkward one.
- Birds: they are small, but they read as birds pecking crumbs.

## 2. People and animals in b
- Man feeding birds: drawn the same as in a, with the same long thin body and small bag and crumbs.
- Woman on bench: the same perch on the front edge of the seat and bunched hands on the book. Her head looks very slightly higher.
- Older man with dog: the face is still small, but the white moustache shows a little better than in a. The stiff arm and small hand are the same.
- Dog and birds: the same as in a.

## 3. Belonging
No difference. In both, the figures have the same dark outlines and flat colours as the trees, bench, lamp post and pond, with the same soft ground shadows. I could not see any difference in outline or shading.

## 4. Easier to make out
No difference, or b by a very small margin. In both you can tell a man feeding birds from a bag, a woman (dark hair in a bun, yellow dress) reading a red book, and an older man (cap, grey hair) walking a dog on a red lead. In b the older man's moustache is slightly easier to see, which helps a little in telling his age. Everything else looks equally clear.

## 5. Choice
Either. I could not tell the two drawing styles apart at this size, so the choice should rest on other grounds.
