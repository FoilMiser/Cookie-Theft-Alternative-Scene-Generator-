# Review: pair 03 (back garden: woman on bench, kneeling man at flower bed, older man with box)

At normal viewing size I cannot see a reliable difference in how the figures are drawn between a and b. Outlines, flat colours and shading look the same to me. The only differences I can see are tiny shifts in pose and position. The notes below apply equally to both unless I say otherwise.

## People and animals in a
- Woman on bench: her right forearm and hand merge into the red book, so the hand is hard to read. Her legs are short and stubby next to her body.
- Woman on bench: the sandwich, plate and small blue bird sit together on the bench beside her. They form a small cluster that is hard to separate at a glance.
- Kneeling man: his head is large and his neck short. There is a dark brown smudge at his mouth or chin that reads as a moustache or as dirt, and it looks unintended.
- Kneeling man: his right arm reaching to the trowel is thin and straight. The hand is barely visible where it meets the trowel.
- Kneeling man: his back leg stretches flat along the ground and his rear foot ends in a flat dark shape. The pose is stiff.
- Older man with box: his lower arm holding the box is a white sleeve with no clear hand, and the box seems to float against it.
- Older man with box: his walking legs are long and thin compared with his short upper body. His shoes are small brown wedges.
- Older man with box: his grey moustache and side hair are small. The cap sits well.

## People and animals in b
- The same points as in a: the woman's hand merges into the book, the bench items are cluttered, the kneeling man has the brown mouth smudge, a thin trowel arm and a stiff back leg, and the older man has no clear hand on the box and long thin legs.
- Woman on bench: her head sits very slightly higher. It makes no visible difference to how she is drawn.

## Belonging
No difference. In both versions the figures use the same dark outline, flat fill colours and simple shapes as the house, fence, pots and flower bed. They look like part of the same drawing in both.

## Easier to make out
No difference. In both, the woman is reading a book on the bench. The man is kneeling and digging at the flower bed with a trowel. The older man (grey hair, cap, waistcoat) is carrying a box. The same parts are unclear in both: the woman's hand on the book, the bird and sandwich beside her, and the older man's hand on the box.

## Choice
Either. I can see no difference in drawing style between the two at this size, so the choice should rest on other grounds.
