# Review: pair 08 (street with shops)

## 1. People and animals in a
- Older man waving (left, by fruit stall): the raised arm is a thin white strip, and the hand at the top is a small dark blob. The two "( )" motion marks around the hand read more as brackets than as waving.
- Older man: the arm hanging down is very thin next to the brown vest. The neck is short and the head sits straight on the shoulders.
- Delivery man (centre, carrying box): a grey rounded shape sits on the front of his orange vest, near the shoulder. It reads as a patch, not clearly as an arm or sleeve. The box is held off to one side, and only a small hand shows under it.
- Delivery man: the legs are long and thin compared with the upper body. The walking pose is stiff but readable.
- Older woman with dog (bottom right): the body and neck are long and very slim, and the head is small for the height. The glasses are drawn only as a circle at the side of the face.
- Older woman: the arm holding the lead is thin. The hand is a small lump where the red lead starts.
- Dog: no clear problems. It has a clean outline, brown patches and a blue collar; the legs are thin but fine.
- Shading on all figures is light and flat, with fairly thin outlines.

## 2. People and animals in b
- Older man waving: same as a. The arm is a thin white strip, the hand a small blob, and the motion marks look like brackets.
- Delivery man: same grey rounded patch on the front of the vest, and the same box held to the side with a small hand under it. The vest's white stripes look a little more visible, but that is minor.
- Older woman with dog (now centre right): same long, slim body and small head, and the glasses are still just a side circle. She is drawn somewhat larger here, which makes her face a bit easier to see.
- Dog: same as a, with no clear problems.
- Outlines and shading look the same as in a. I see no clear difference in how the figures are drawn.

## 3. Belonging
No difference. The figures in both versions have the same dark outlines, flat colours and soft ground shadows as the buildings, stall, tree and bins. Nothing about the figures themselves makes one version fit in better.

## 4. Easier to make out
No difference in how the figures are drawn. In b the older woman and dog are larger and nearer the middle, so her grey bun, glasses and the lead are a little easier to see. That comes from where they stand, not from how they are drawn. In both versions the delivery man's arm holding the box is the least clear part.

## 5. Choice
Either. The figures look drawn the same way in a and b, with the same small faults: the waving hand, the delivery man's grey arm patch, and the woman's thin, long body.
