# Review, pair 05 (street with shops)

Seen at normal size, the two versions look almost the same. I could not see any clear difference in line weight, shading or colour between the figures in a and b.

## 1. People and animals in a
- Man with camera: his raised arm crosses in front of his face, so most of his face is hidden behind the arm and camera. The arm reads as a dark band.
- Man with camera: his head looks large for his narrow body and legs.
- Woman with ice cream: her arm folds tightly across her chest to reach the cone. The join of her elbow and hand against the yellow dress is hard to follow.
- Woman with ice cream: her neck is very thin, and her head looks big for her slim body.
- Woman sweeping: her head looks large compared with her body, and her neck is thin.
- Woman sweeping: her two hands sit close together on the broom handle, and the near arm's elbow joins the shoulder awkwardly.
- No animals in the scene.

## 2. People and animals in b
- Man with camera: the same as in a. The arm and camera cover most of his face, and his head looks large for his body.
- Woman with ice cream: the same folded arm across her chest. Her eyes look like closed or smiling curves, while in a she seems to look down at the cone.
- Woman with ice cream: the same thin neck and large head.
- Woman sweeping: the same large head, thin neck and crowded hands on the broom.
- No animals in the scene.

## 3. Belonging
No difference. In both versions the figures have the same dark outlines and flat colours as the buildings, tables, stall and tree, and they sit on the pavement with similar soft shadows.

## 4. Easier to make out
No difference. In both you can tell at a glance that there is a man taking a photo, a woman eating an ice cream and a woman sweeping leaves. The camera, cone and broom are equally clear. The man's face is equally hidden in both. The ice-cream woman's eyes are slightly different (looking down in a, closed or smiling in b), but this does not change what she is doing.

## 5. Choice
Either. I could not see any difference large enough to prefer one version over the other.
