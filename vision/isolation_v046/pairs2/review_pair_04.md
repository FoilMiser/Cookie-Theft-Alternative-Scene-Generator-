# Review: pair 04 (park: man on picnic blanket, dog with newspaper, girl flying kite)

The figures look drawn the same way in a and b: same outlines, flat colours and proportions. What changes is position (the dog and newspaper are mirrored, and the girl stands further left and lower in b) and the kite's colour (yellow in a, orange in b).

## People and animals in a
- Man on blanket: his head is large and tilted, with the eyes drawn as small dark marks with a reddish tint. His face is hard to read and could look like he is squinting or wearing small glasses.
- Man on blanket: his arms are not clearly visible. His left arm seems tucked behind his body and no hand is visible. His torso is a plain green block.
- Man on blanket: his legs are folded flat on the blanket in a single beige mass, and the knee and shin are hard to separate.
- Dog: it holds an open newspaper with its front paws and wears round glasses and a red scarf. Its head and paws overlap the paper edge, so the join between paws and paper is muddled.
- Dog: its back legs and tail form a curled shape, and its hind leg is hard to tell from the body.
- Girl with kite: she is very small compared with the man. Her raised arm is a thin line up to the spool, and her hand is not visible.
- Girl with kite: her legs are very thin sticks under the pink dress, and her yellow shoes are flat ovals.

## People and animals in b
- Man on blanket: the same as in a (hard-to-read face, no visible hands, merged folded legs).
- Dog: the same as in a but mirrored. Its paws again run into the newspaper edge, and the glasses are harder to see because the head is turned towards the paper.
- Girl with kite: the same as in a (thin raised arm, no visible hand, stick legs). She is placed lower and a bit larger, closer to the path.

## Belonging
No difference. In both versions the figures use the same dark outlines and flat colours as the bench, lamp post, trees and pond, and fit into the scene equally well.

## Easier to make out
Slightly b, mainly because of placement rather than drawing:
- The girl is a little larger and clearer in b.
- The dog's glasses and face are a little easier to see in a.
- The man is equally unclear in both: you cannot see what his hands are doing, and his face is hard to read.
- The orange kite in b stands out a little more against the sky than the yellow one in a.

## Choice
Either. The figures are drawn the same way in both. If one must be picked, b, for the slightly larger girl and the more visible kite.
