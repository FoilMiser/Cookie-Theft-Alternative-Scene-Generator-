# Review fig 05

## 1. Who and what
A man, a delivery or work person, in an orange cap, an orange high-visibility vest with white reflective stripes and blue trousers, walking while carrying a cardboard box. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Shoulder/sleeve: a grey-blue patch on the shoulder, apparently a sleeve, does not connect clearly to the bare forearm under the box. The arm looks broken into two pieces.
- Arm/box: only one arm can be seen, and the box sits on the flat hand in front of the chest. It looks as if it floats, with no second hand steadying it.
- Nose: a small circle outlined on the edge of the face. It looks stuck on.
- Mouth: a short dark mark and barely visible, so the face shows little expression.
- Body proportions: the torso is short and the legs are very long and thin. The figure looks stretched below the waist.
- Feet: the shoes are dark flat ovals with little shape.

## 3. Faults in b
- Mouth: a small reddish/pink mark. It is a little more visible than in a, but still tiny.
- Shoulder/sleeve, arm/box, nose, body proportions and feet: the same faults as in a.

## 4. Differences
- Mouth: in b the mouth is a small red/pink mark. In a it is a darker brownish short line.
- I saw no other differences in the figure.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: the neck is very short and mostly hidden between the head and the vest, which is acceptable here. The head is fairly large but suits the body, although the long legs make the upper body look top-heavy.
- b: the same as a. The neck is short but acceptable, and the head size suits the body.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: even, dark outlines. The colours are mostly flat, with slight soft shading on the vest and the trousers.
- b: the same as a.
- Which looks more like the background: no difference. The shop front and pavement behind him also have dark outlines and flat colours, and both versions match them equally.

## 7. Better drawn
No difference in practice. The versions differ only in the colour of a tiny mouth mark, which would not be visible at test size. b's pink mouth is very slightly easier to see.
