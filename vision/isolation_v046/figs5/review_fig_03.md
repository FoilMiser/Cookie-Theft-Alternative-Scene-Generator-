# Review of pair 03

## 1. Who and what
An older woman with grey hair in a bun and large round glasses, in a teal long-sleeved top, a blue-grey skirt and brown shoes. She is walking along holding a thin red stick (a cane) out in front of her with both hands at hip height. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Glasses: the front lens sticks out past the outline of the face, so it seems to float in front of the profile.
- Mouth or cheek: there is a red mark under the glasses near the edge of the face. It reads more as a red smudge on the cheek than as a mouth.
- Back of the top: a white strip runs down the back from the shoulder. It is unclear what it is (a bag strap, a collar or a gap) and it looks unfinished.
- Hands: both hands are clasped together as rounded blobs around the cane, and no fingers can be made out.
- Cane: it is only a thin red line coming out of the hands, with no handle, so it is hard to read as a cane.
- Legs: they are very thin, stick-like and straight-edged, with no knees, and the back leg is angled stiffly.
- Shoes: they are large brown flat shapes that look big for such thin legs.

## 3. Faults in b
- Glasses: the front lens sticks out past the outline of the face.
- Mouth or cheek: there is a red mark under the glasses near the edge of the face. It looks a little smaller than in a, but it still reads more as a smudge than as a mouth.
- Back of the top: there is the same unclear white strip down the back.
- Hands: they are clasped blobs around the cane, with no fingers.
- Cane: it is a thin red line with no handle.
- Legs: they are very thin and stick-like, with no knees.
- Shoes: they are large brown shapes for the thin legs.

## 4. Differences
- Mouth or cheek: the red mark under the glasses looks slightly larger and more like a smudge in a, and slightly smaller in b. This difference is small and I am not certain of it.
- Apart from that, none seen.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: the neck looks a little too long and too thin for the body. The head is slightly large for the narrow body but acceptable for a cartoon.
- b: the same as a. The neck is a little too long and thin, and the head is slightly large but acceptable.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: even, dark, fairly thick outlines, and flat colours with no shading.
- b: even, dark, fairly thick outlines, and flat colours with no shading.
- More like the things behind the figure: no difference. The background lines on the floor or wall are drawn in a similar dark line on a flat colour, and this is the same in both versions.

## 7. Better drawn
no difference. The two versions look the same apart from a possibly slightly smaller red mark at the mouth in b, which is too small to make either one better drawn.
