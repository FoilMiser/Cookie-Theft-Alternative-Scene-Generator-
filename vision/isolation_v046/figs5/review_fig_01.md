# Review fig 01

## 1. Who and what
A man with a moustache, in a red T-shirt and blue trousers, kneeling or crouching and digging beside a yellow flower with a garden trowel. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Mouth/moustache: the brown patch under the nose is a blob, so it is unclear whether it is a moustache, a mouth or dirt. You cannot make out a mouth.
- Legs: the pose is hard to read. The front thigh runs flat to the left and the back leg stretches far to the right with a foot at the edge, so it is unclear whether he is kneeling, lunging or squatting.
- Hips: the trousers bulge where the two legs meet, under the shirt hem, and the join looks lumpy.
- Ear: it sits far back at the hairline and looks slightly detached from the face.
- Hair: it looks like a smooth helmet or cap rather than hair.
- Arm: only one arm can be seen. It is long and straight, and the hand holding the trowel is small.
- Front shoe: it is a dark flat lump under the trowel, with no clear shape of a foot.

## 3. Faults in b
- Mouth/moustache: the same brown blob under the nose, now with a reddish tint inside. It reads a little more like an open mouth under a moustache, but is still unclear.
- Legs, hips, ear, hair, arm and front shoe: the same faults as in a.

## 4. Differences
- Mouth: in b the brown patch under the nose has a red/pink area inside it, like lips or an open mouth. In a it is plain brown with a darker line.
- I saw no other differences in the figure.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: the neck is slightly long and fairly thick, but acceptable. The head is large for the body, a usual cartoon proportion, and suits it.
- b: the same as a. The neck is slightly long but acceptable, and the head is large but suits the body.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: even, dark, fairly thick outlines. The colours are flat, with only faint soft darker shading on the shirt and trousers.
- b: the same as a.
- Which looks more like the background: no difference. The background (fence, flower, soil) also has dark outlines and flat colours, and both versions match it equally.

## 7. Better drawn
b, by a small margin. The red inside the mouth area makes it slightly easier to read as a mouth under a moustache. Everything else is identical.
