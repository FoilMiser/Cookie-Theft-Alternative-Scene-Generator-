# Review of pair 04

## 1. Who and what
A young or middle-aged woman with dark shoulder-length hair, in a yellow short-sleeved dress and red flat shoes. She stands holding a pink ice-cream cone up to her face with her right hand, and her eyes look closed as if she is enjoying it. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Mouth: the red lips sit right on the edge of the face outline, under the nose. They look like a red blob stuck on the cheek rather than a mouth.
- Hand holding the cone: the hand is a small skin-coloured blob under the cone. No fingers can be seen gripping it.
- Dress, shoulder on the viewer's right: the sleeve has a sharp, boxy corner sticking up at the shoulder, which looks stiff.
- Hanging arm: it is thin and straight, and ends in a small, vague fist with only a faint line for the fingers.
- Legs: they are long, thin, even tubes with no knees or ankles, and they look stiff.
- Neck: it is a wide, plain column joining the jaw straight to the dress, with no shaping.

## 3. Faults in b
- Mouth: the red lips sit on the edge of the face outline and look like a red blob on the cheek.
- Hand holding the cone: the hand is a blob under the cone, with no visible fingers.
- Dress, shoulder on the viewer's right: the sleeve has a sharp, boxy corner at the shoulder.
- Hanging arm: it is thin and straight, and ends in a small, vague fist.
- Legs: they are long, thin tubes with no knees or ankles.
- Neck: it is a wide, plain column with no shaping.

## 4. Differences
none seen

## 5. Neck and head
- a: the neck looks a little too thick (about as wide as the lower face) and slightly long. The head size suits the body.
- b: the same as a. The neck is a little too thick and slightly long, and the head size suits the body.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: even, dark, fairly thick outlines all round, and flat colours with no shading.
- b: even, dark, fairly thick outlines, and flat colours with no shading.
- More like the things behind the figure: no difference. In both versions the background (window frames, wall and floor) also has dark outlines and flat colours, the same as the figure.

## 7. Better drawn
no difference. At this size the two versions look identical, with the same faults in the mouth, hand, shoulder and legs.
