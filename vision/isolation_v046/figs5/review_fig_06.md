# Review: fig_06

## 1. Who and what
A child (a boy, dark skin, short curly black hair) in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts, holding one hand up to his mouth and an ice-cream cone in the other hand; the cone looks empty, with a small pink blob near its top and a pink drop on the ground. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Hand at the mouth: drawn as a dark brown lump that merges into the face and chin; no fingers can be read, so it is unclear whether he is eating, wiping or coughing.
- Face: the eye, nose and mouth are crowded together behind the hand; the face is hard to read.
- Ice-cream cone: the cone looks empty (dark brown inside) with only a small pink dot, so it does not clearly read as ice cream.
- Hand holding the cone: the hand is hidden behind the cone; the arm seems to end at the cone rim.
- Raised arm: the upper arm is hidden and the forearm seems to come straight out of the shoulder of the T-shirt.
- Legs: long and thin compared with the short shorts and the large head.
- Shoes: flat grey ovals with no shape of a foot.

## 3. Faults in b
- Same as a: the hand at the mouth merges into the face with no fingers.
- Face crowded and hard to read behind the hand.
- Cone looks empty apart from a small pink dot.
- Hand holding the cone is hidden behind it.
- Raised forearm seems to come straight out of the shoulder.
- Legs long and thin.
- Shoes flat grey ovals.

## 4. Differences
none seen

## 5. Neck and head
- a: neck mostly hidden by the raised hand and chin; what shows looks too short. The head is large for the body (a big childlike head), which suits a child but makes the body look small.
- b: same as a: neck too short / hidden; head large for the body.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: thick, even, dark outlines all round the figure; flat colours with very little shading (slight darker tone on the hair curls and a faint shade on the shorts).
- b: the same thick dark outlines and flat colours.
- More like the things behind the figure (the box at the lower left, the item at lower right, which also have thick dark outlines and flat colours): no difference.

## 7. Better drawn
no difference. I could not see any difference between a and b; both share the same faults (hand merging into the face, empty-looking cone).
