# Review: fig_02

## 1. Who and what
A young man (teen or young adult) in a red cap, blue hooded top and dark trousers, kicking an orange ball with his right foot, arms out to the sides. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Mouth: a red smear at the corner of the mouth/cheek that looks like a wound, lipstick or blood rather than a mouth.
- Face: the eye and mouth are small and pushed to the right edge of the face; the face looks flat and squashed.
- Hands: rounded mitten-like blobs with no clear fingers.
- Hood: drawn as a blue lump behind the neck that looks like a growth on the back rather than a hood.
- Kicking leg: very long and straight, longer-looking than the standing leg.
- Shoes: plain white blobs with no shape of a shoe.
- Arms: the arm reaching back is short and stiff.

## 3. Faults in b
- Mouth: a smaller red mark at the mouth, still slightly odd but reads more as a mouth than in a.
- Face: eye and mouth small and pushed to the right edge; face looks flat.
- Hands: mitten-like blobs with no clear fingers.
- Hood: blue lump behind the neck.
- Kicking leg very long and straight.
- Shoes plain white blobs.
- Rear arm short and stiff.

## 4. Differences
- Mouth: in a the red at the mouth is larger and spreads onto the cheek; in b it is smaller and tighter at the mouth. (This is the only difference I could see, and it is slight.)

## 5. Neck and head
- a: neck short and a little thin, partly hidden by the hood and collar; the head is large for the body, a bit too big.
- b: same as a: neck short and a little thin; head a bit large for the body.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: thick, even, dark outlines; flat colours with very little shading (slight darker tone on the hair and trousers).
- b: the same thick dark outlines and flat colours.
- More like the things behind the figure (the orange ball and the yellow shape at the top right, which also have thick dark outlines and flat colours): no difference.

## 7. Better drawn
b, slightly. The two are almost the same, but in b the mouth is a smaller red mark that reads as a mouth, while in a it looks like a red smear or wound on the cheek.
