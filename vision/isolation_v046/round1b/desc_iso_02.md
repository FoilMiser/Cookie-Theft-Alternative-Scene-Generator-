## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a lawn, a tall wooden fence and a cream house with a red roof on the right. Near the fence in the middle, an older man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is raking a small pile of dead leaves. In front of him and to the right, a grey-haired older woman with glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt is watering a round flower bed of bright flowers with a grey watering can, and water is pouring out onto the flowers. In the bottom left, an orange cat is crouched low on the grass, facing left towards a red bird that is pecking at the ground on the paved path. The cat looks as if it is stalking the bird and might be about to pounce. A metal swing set with a single empty red swing stands on the left side of the lawn. Other things in the garden are a wooden gate in the fence, a birdhouse on a tall pole, a wooden bench by the house, a bush and a large tree behind the fence.
