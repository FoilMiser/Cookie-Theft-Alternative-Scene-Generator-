# iso_08

## Free description
A sunny playground on a grassy field with a dark metal railing fence and bushes behind it. On the left two children sit on a red seesaw with a grey triangular pivot. The child on the left end (yellow top, orange shorts, curly hair) is down, and the girl on the right end (pink dress) is up. In the centre a man in a green shirt and beige trousers stands holding a camera up to his eye, pointing it left as if photographing the children on the seesaw. A red camera strap hangs from the camera. On the right a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is kicking an orange ball, with motion lines under his kicking foot. The ball is just leaving his foot and looks about to fly off to the right. Behind him on the right is a slide with a red frame and ladder and a yellow/orange chute. A tree stands on the far left, with the sun top right and two clouds. Nothing looks impossible. The girl on the high end of the seesaw seems to sit a bit oddly: her feet hang below the board and she seems to grip it.
