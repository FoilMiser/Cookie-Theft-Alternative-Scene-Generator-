# Review: fig_07

## 1. Who and what
A child (dark skin, short curly dark hair, yellow T-shirt, orange shorts) standing with one hand up at the face (as if wiping eyes or crying) and holding an ice-cream cone in the other hand; the cone looks almost empty and a small pink blob lies on the ground by the feet, so it reads as having dropped the ice cream. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Face: the raised hand merges with the face; eye, nose, mouth and hand run together into dark marks and the expression is hard to read.
- Raised arm/hand: the arm is not clearly shown; the hand is a brown blob against the cheek.
- Other arm: drawn as a thin line from the sleeve; the hand around the cone is unclear, no fingers seen.
- Cone: held at an odd angle, tilted out and away, looking stuck to the side of the body.
- Ear: placed high and far back on the head.
- Legs: long and thin for a child; feet are flat grey ovals.
- Shorts: a faint darker patch on the front that looks like a stain rather than a fold.

## 3. Faults in b
- Face: same merging of hand and face; the expression is hard to read.
- Raised arm/hand: same unclear arm, hand a blob against the cheek.
- Other arm: same thin line, unclear hand on the cone.
- Cone: same odd tilted angle.
- Ear: high and far back.
- Legs: long and thin; flat grey oval feet.
- Shorts: a faint darker patch on the front, as in a.

## 4. Differences
- none seen

## 5. Neck and head
- a: neck barely visible, a short stub (too short); head large for the body, which suits a child.
- b: same as a.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: thick dark outlines; flat colour fills with only a faint darker patch on the shorts.
- b: the same.
- More like the things behind the figure (grass, box, which also have dark outlines and flat colour): no difference.

## 7. Better drawn
No difference: I could not see any difference between the two drawings of the figure.
