# Review fig 09

## Who and what
A woman (dark hair in a low ponytail, purple T-shirt, teal trousers) sitting on a bench reading an open red book; a sandwich and a blue bird are on the bench beside her. Both versions read the same.

## Faults in a
- Arm nearest the viewer: the upper arm and forearm are a thick, even sausage shape with no elbow, and the hand is a rounded lump at the book.
- Far hand: only a small lump shows at the right edge of the book; it is hard to tell it is a hand.
- Legs/trousers: the thighs barely read as going forward along the seat; the lower legs drop long and straight, so the seated pose looks stiff and the legs look too long.
- Feet: the shoes are two plain dark ovals with no shape of a foot.
- The sandwich is drawn over her knee/thigh area, so it looks as if it is sitting on her lap rather than on the bench.
- Face: just a dot eye, a small nose and a tiny mouth mark; the face is very sparse.

## Faults in b
- Same as a: sausage-like near arm with no elbow and a lump for a hand.
- Same as a: far hand barely visible at the book edge.
- Same as a: stiff seated pose, long straight lower legs.
- Same as a: shoes are plain dark ovals.
- Same as a: sandwich overlaps her knee/thigh.
- Same as a: very sparse face.

## Differences
- none seen

## Neck and head
- a: neck is short and fairly thick, about right for a cartoon; the head is large for the body but suits the cartoon style.
- b: the same as a — short, fairly thick neck, about right; head large but suits the style.

## Outline and shading
- a: thick dark outlines all round; flat colours with no shading.
- b: thick dark outlines all round; flat colours with no shading.
- More like the things behind the figure: no difference (the bench, wall and bird are drawn with the same dark outlines and flat colours in both).

## Better drawn
no difference — I cannot see any difference in how the figure is drawn between a and b.
