# Review: fig 12

## 1. Who and what
A woman (dark hair tied back in a ponytail) in a purple T-shirt and teal trousers, sweeping a pile of brown leaves with a long-handled yellow broom. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Neck: no neck can be seen; the head sits straight on the shoulders.
- Arms: the lower arm reaches across the body and bends at a sharp angle; the two hands on the handle are hard to tell apart from each other.
- Hands: hands are simple blobs wrapped round the handle, with no fingers.
- Legs/feet: only one dark shoe shows, behind the broom head; the other foot is hidden, and the two trouser legs merge into one shape.
- Face: the face is a very small part of the head; most of the head is hair, so the face is hard to read.

## 3. Faults in b
- Neck: the same: no neck visible.
- Arms: the same sharply bent lower arm across the body.
- Hands: the same blob hands on the handle.
- Legs/feet: the same single visible shoe and merged trouser legs.
- Face: the same small face in a large mass of hair.

## 4. Differences
- none seen

## 5. Neck and head
- a: neck too short (none visible). The head is about the right size for the body.
- b: the same: neck too short, head size about right.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: dark, even outlines; flat colours with a faint darker tone on the trousers and shirt.
- b: the same dark outlines and flat colours with faint shading.
- More like the things behind the figure: no difference.

## 7. Better drawn
no difference. The two versions look the same to me; the missing neck and merged legs are in both.
