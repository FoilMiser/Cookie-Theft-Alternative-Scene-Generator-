# Review: fig_03

## 1. Who and what
A man (worker in an orange cap and orange hi-vis vest with white stripes, blue trousers) walking to the right, carrying a cardboard box on one forearm/hand at chest height. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Mouth: only a small dark smudge just under the nose; it does not read clearly as a mouth.
- Arm: the visible arm comes out of the vest with a grey bulge at the upper arm (sleeve) that looks like a separate lump stuck on the shoulder.
- Hand/box: the box sits on a flat open hand with no fingers wrapping it and no second hand visible, so the grip looks unsteady.
- Head: sits straight on the vest with no neck showing.
- Ear: placed far back on the head, almost at the back edge.
- Legs: long and tube-like; the back leg bends sharply, giving a stiff stride.

## 3. Faults in b
- Mouth: small red-pink lips under the nose; readable, though a bit like lipstick on a man.
- Arm: same grey lump at the upper arm/shoulder as in a.
- Hand/box: same flat hand under the box, no visible grip or second hand.
- Head: no neck showing, head sits straight on the vest.
- Ear: placed far back on the head.
- Legs: long and tube-like, stiff stride.

## 4. Differences
- Mouth: a has a small dark/grey mark; b has a small red-pink mouth.
- Otherwise none seen.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: neck not visible at all (too short); head is large for the body, cartoon-style, but does not look out of place in this style.
- b: same as a: no visible neck (too short); head large but acceptable for the style.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: thick dark outlines all round; flat colour fills with little or no shading.
- b: the same thick dark outlines and flat fills.
- More like the things behind the figure (window frames, sign, pavement, which also have dark outlines and flat colour): no difference.

## 7. Better drawn
b, by a small margin: its mouth reads as a mouth, while in a it is a dark smudge. Everything else is the same.
