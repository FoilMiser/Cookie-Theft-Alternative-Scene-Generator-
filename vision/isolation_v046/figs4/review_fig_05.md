# Review fig 05

## Who and what
An older woman (grey hair in a bun, round glasses, teal jumper, blue-grey skirt, brown shoes) walking forward holding a red cane/stick out in front of her with both hands. Both versions read the same, except the mouth (see Differences).

## Faults in a
- Hands: both hands are bunched together as rounded lumps on the stick; the fingers do not read.
- Jumper/back: a white strip runs down her back with no clear meaning (it could be a bag strap or collar but does not read as anything).
- Torso: the jumper is a narrow, tilted tube with no shoulder shape.
- Legs: thin and straight with no knees; the back leg is very thin at the ankle.
- Shoes: large brown flat shapes, rather big for the legs.
- Face: the red lips sit right under the glasses at the edge of the face and look like a red smudge.
- Glasses: the far lens sticks out past the face outline.

## Faults in b
- Hands: same lumpy bunched hands on the stick.
- Jumper/back: same unexplained white strip down the back.
- Torso: same narrow tilted tube.
- Legs: same thin straight legs, very thin back ankle.
- Shoes: same large brown shapes.
- Face: the mouth is a small dark mark with no colour, so it barely shows.
- Glasses: same far lens sticking out past the face outline.

## Differences
- Mouth: in a the lips are red and stand out; in b the mouth is a small dark, uncoloured mark.

## Neck and head
- a: neck is short and fairly narrow, about right; the head is large for the body but suits the cartoon style.
- b: the same as a — neck about right; head large but suits the style.

## Outline and shading
- a: thick dark outlines; flat colours with no shading.
- b: thick dark outlines; flat colours with no shading.
- More like the things behind the figure: no difference (the paved floor is flat colour with dark lines in both).

## Better drawn
a — the figure is otherwise identical, but the red lips in a make the mouth visible, while in b the mouth almost disappears; neither mouth is well placed.
