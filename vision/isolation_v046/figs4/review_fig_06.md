# Review: fig 06

## 1. Who and what
A man (brown hair, brown moustache) in a red T-shirt and blue trousers, kneeling and digging with a garden trowel next to a yellow flower. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Head: the head is large and round compared with the body.
- Face: the moustache is a brown blob with a red mouth inside it, which looks odd and a little like a wound or food.
- Legs: the back leg stretches far out behind along the ground and looks too long; the kneeling pose is hard to read.
- Arms: only one arm is seen; the other arm is missing from view.
- Hand: the hand holding the trowel is a small blob.
- Shirt/trousers: the shirt ends abruptly at the hip with no clear waist or belt.

## 3. Faults in b
- Head: the same large round head.
- Face: the same blob moustache with a red mouth.
- Legs: the same over-long back leg.
- Arms: the same single visible arm.
- Hand: the same blob hand on the trowel.
- Shirt/trousers: the same abrupt shirt end with no waist.

## 4. Differences
- none seen

## 5. Neck and head
- a: the neck is visible and a little long and thin for the large head. The head is too big for the body.
- b: the same: neck a little long and thin, head too big for the body.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: dark, even outlines; flat colours with slight darker-tone shading on shirt and trousers.
- b: the same dark outlines and flat colours with slight shading.
- More like the things behind the figure: no difference.

## 7. Better drawn
no difference. I could see no difference between the two versions; the large head and long back leg appear in both.
