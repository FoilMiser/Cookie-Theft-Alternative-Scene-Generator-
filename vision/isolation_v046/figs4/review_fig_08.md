# Review: fig 08

## 1. Who and what
An older man (grey hair at the sides, grey moustache, blue-grey flat cap, brown vest over a cream long-sleeved shirt, dark trousers, brown shoes) walking and holding a thin red line in both hands, as if walking a dog on a lead. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Neck: there is no visible neck; the head sits straight on the shoulders.
- Hands: both hands are joined into one brown blob at the front; fingers and which hand is which are not clear.
- Arms: the far arm shows only as a thin white strip along the front edge of the vest, which looks like a stray sliver.
- Waist: a dark band at the waist bulges out over the trousers like a lump rather than a belt.
- Legs: the legs are long compared with the upper body, and the trousers are a single dark shape with little sense of the knees.
- Face: the moustache sits as a grey blob with a small red mouth patch; the face is very small inside the cap and hair.

## 3. Faults in b
- Same as a: no neck, merged blob hands, thin white strip for the far arm, bulging waist band, long legs, blob moustache.

## 4. Differences
- Mouth: the red under the moustache may be very slightly more visible in b than in a; I am not certain of this.
- No other differences seen.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: neck too short (not visible at all); head size suits the body, perhaps slightly small against the long legs.
- b: same as a.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: even dark outlines; flat colours with a darker shade on the vest edge and trousers.
- b: the same.
- More like the things behind the figure: no difference.

## 7. Better drawn
no difference. The two versions look the same to me and share the same faults.
