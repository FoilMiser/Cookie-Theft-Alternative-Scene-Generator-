# Review: fig 11

## 1. Who and what
A woman (dark hair tied back, yellow short-sleeved dress, red shoes) sitting on a wooden park bench reading an open red book held in both hands. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Knees: the thighs end in rounded bumps that stick out from the dress like separate lumps rather than bending into the shins.
- Shins: long, straight, tube-like and the same width all the way down, with no ankle or calf shape.
- Arm on her left (viewer's right): the upper arm and forearm meet in a soft bulge with no clear elbow; the forearm looks swollen.
- Hands: hands on the book are small flat blobs; the hand at the top of the book is barely readable.
- Face: the nose is a small round ring that reads like a spectacle lens or a ring, not a nose; no mouth is visible.
- Dress: the skirt has a shaded patch on the lap that looks like a fold or stain rather than a clear seat shape.

## 3. Faults in b
- Same as a: lumpy knees, tube-like shins, soft elbowless forearm, blob hands, ring-like nose with no mouth, unclear lap shading.

## 4. Differences
- none seen

## 5. Neck and head
- a: neck short and fairly thick, about right for a cartoon; head a little large for the body but acceptable.
- b: same as a.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: even dark outlines of steady width; flat colours with a single darker shade on the dress folds, lap and under the arm.
- b: the same.
- More like the things behind the figure: no difference.

## 7. Better drawn
no difference. I could not see any difference between the two versions; they share the same faults.
