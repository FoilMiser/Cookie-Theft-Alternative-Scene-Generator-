# Review: fig_04

## 1. Who and what
A young man (brown hair, red cap, blue hooded top, dark trousers, white shoes) kicking an orange ball with his right foot, arms out for balance, looking down at the ball. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Mouth: a red-pink smudge at the edge of the face; it reads as a mouth but also a little like a red mark or lipstick.
- Face: features (eye, nose, mouth) are squeezed into the front edge of the face, leaving a large empty cheek.
- Hands: both hands are mitten-like blobs with no clear fingers.
- Back arm: held out stiff and straight behind the body.
- Kicking leg: very long and straight; the foot seems to touch/merge into the ball.
- Hood: a lump at the back of the neck that looks stuck on.
- Ear: placed far back on the head.

## 3. Faults in b
- Mouth: a dark grey-brown mark at the edge of the face; it looks more like stubble or dirt than a mouth.
- Face: same squeezed features and large empty cheek.
- Hands: same mitten-like blobs.
- Back arm: same stiff straight arm.
- Kicking leg: same very long leg, foot merging into the ball.
- Hood: same stuck-on lump.
- Ear: placed far back.

## 4. Differences
- Mouth: a has a red-pink mouth; b has a dark grey-brown mouth mark.
- Otherwise none seen.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: neck short and partly hidden by the hood, roughly about right for a hooded top; head somewhat large for the body but suits the cartoon style.
- b: same as a.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: thick dark outlines; flat colour fills, with only simple fold lines on the hoodie and trousers.
- b: the same.
- More like the things behind the figure (ball, sign, which also have dark outlines and flat colour): no difference.

## 7. Better drawn
a, by a small margin: its red mouth reads as a mouth, while the dark mark in b looks like stubble or dirt. Everything else is the same.
