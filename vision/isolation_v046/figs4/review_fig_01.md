# Review fig 01

## Who and what
A woman (dark shoulder-length hair, yellow dress, red shoes) standing and eating a pink ice-cream cone held up to her mouth, eyes closed as if enjoying it. Both versions read the same.

## Faults in a
- Neck: drawn as a wide, tall skin-coloured column that merges into the shoulders, with no clear jaw line; it looks too long and thick.
- Shoulders/dress: the dress shoulders are square and boxy, with a bump on the far shoulder, so the top of the dress looks stiff.
- Arm holding the cone: the hand under the cone is a lumpy shape and it is hard to see fingers wrapping the cone.
- Hanging arm: straight, thin and stick-like, ending in a small blob hand.
- Legs: long, straight and stick-like, set wide apart, with no knees; they look too long for the body.
- Shoes: plain dark-red ovals.
- Face: eyes are short arcs and the mouth is a small red mark at the side, so the face is hard to read.
- Hair: flat dark block at the back of the head.

## Faults in b
- Same as a: long, thick neck merging into the shoulders.
- Same as a: boxy dress shoulders with a bump.
- Same as a: lumpy hand under the cone.
- Same as a: stick-like hanging arm with a blob hand.
- Same as a: long straight stick legs, set wide apart.
- Same as a: plain oval shoes.
- Same as a: hard-to-read face.
- Same as a: flat block of hair.

## Differences
- none seen

## Neck and head
- a: neck looks too long and too thick; the head looks a little small for the long body and legs.
- b: the same as a — neck too long and too thick; head a little small for the body.

## Outline and shading
- a: dark outlines around the figure; flat yellow, skin and red colours with no shading.
- b: dark outlines around the figure; flat colours with no shading.
- More like the things behind the figure: no difference (the shop front and pavement are also flat colours with dark outlines in both).

## Better drawn
no difference — I cannot see any difference in how the figure is drawn between a and b.
