# Review: fig 02

## 1. Who and what
An older man (grey hair, grey moustache, blue cap) waving with his raised right hand; brown vest over a cream long-sleeved shirt, dark trousers, brown shoes. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Head/body: the head is in side view facing right while the chest and legs face the front, so the head looks turned hard on the body.
- Vest: a white vertical stripe runs down the right front of the vest; it reads as a gap or strap and is unclear.
- Waistband: the trouser waistband is a thick dark band with a small button off to the side, sitting a little low and loose under the vest.
- Raised arm: the waving hand is small and mitten-like next to the long, thick sleeve.
- Legs: the trousers are long and tube-like, so the legs look long against the short body.

## 3. Faults in b
- Head/body: the same head turned to the side on a front-facing body.
- Vest: the same unclear white vertical stripe on the right front.
- Waistband: the same thick band and side button.
- Raised arm: the same small mitten-like waving hand.
- Legs: the same long tube-like trousers.

## 4. Differences
- none seen

## 5. Neck and head
- a: a short neck shows between chin and vest; about right. The head is a little large for the body but suits a cartoon figure.
- b: the same: neck about right, head a little large but acceptable.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: dark, even, fairly thick outlines; flat colours with light darker-tone shading (vest, trousers).
- b: the same dark, even outlines and flat colours with light shading.
- More like the things behind the figure: no difference.

## 7. Better drawn
no difference. I could not see any difference between the two versions; they look the same at this size.
