# Review: fig 10

## 1. Who and what
An older woman (grey hair in a bun, round dark glasses, teal top, blue-grey skirt, brown shoes) standing and watering flowers with a grey watering can held in both hands. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Glasses/eye: the glasses are a large black ring with a big round eye inside, so the eye looks like a goggle or a hole rather than an eye behind a lens; a second dark curve sticks out past the edge of the face.
- Mouth/cheek: a small red mark sits low on the cheek, away from where a mouth should be; it reads as a spot or wound rather than a mouth.
- Chest: a thin white strip runs down the front of the top from the collar, which looks like a stray piece of paper or a gap rather than a shirt collar or opening.
- Arms: only one arm clearly shows; the hands on the can handle are small blobs.
- Legs: the legs below the skirt are thin and straight, and the back foot is partly hidden so the stance looks unsteady.
- Hair: the side of the hair cuts down over the ear area as a flat grey shape with a hard edge.

## 3. Faults in b
- Same glasses/eye problem as a.
- Mouth/cheek: the mark on the cheek looks a little darker/browner than in a, but still sits off where a mouth would be and reads as a spot.
- Same white strip on the chest, blob hands, thin legs and flat hair edge as a.

## 4. Differences
- Cheek/mouth mark: in a it looks red; in b it looks slightly darker and browner. This is a very small difference and I am not certain of it.
- No other differences seen.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: neck short and fairly thick, about right; head large for the body (cartoon proportions), and with the big glasses it looks a bit top-heavy.
- b: same as a.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: even dark outlines; flat colours with little shading (slight darker tone on the skirt and arm).
- b: the same.
- More like the things behind the figure: no difference.

## 7. Better drawn
no difference. Apart from a possibly slightly different cheek mark, the two look the same and share the same faults.
