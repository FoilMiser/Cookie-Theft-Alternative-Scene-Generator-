# Review: fig 01

## 1. Who and what
A woman with a dark bob haircut, in a yellow short-sleeved dress and red shoes, standing and eating (licking) a pink ice-cream cone held up to her mouth; both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Raised arm: the arm holding the cone comes from the far shoulder and crosses the whole chest to reach the cone on the near side, which looks twisted and awkward.
- Hand holding the cone: hardly readable as a hand; the cone seems to sit on a blob at the wrist.
- Neck: long and thin, like a stalk between the head and the dress.
- Lowered arm: very long and thin, hangs below the hem with no elbow; the fist at the end is a small lump.
- Legs: straight tubes with no knees, ankles or shape; they look stiff.
- Shoes: flat red ovals, feet not clearly turned in any direction.
- Dress: the sleeve on the far shoulder sticks out squarely like a pad; the shoulders look boxy.
- Face: eye is a closed curve and mouth is small and pushed against the cone; the face is readable but crowded.

## 3. Faults in b
- Raised arm: same crossing-the-chest arm as in a, equally awkward.
- Hand holding the cone: same unclear blob of a hand.
- Neck: same long, thin neck.
- Lowered arm: same long thin arm with no elbow; the fist is a small lump.
- Legs: same straight tubes with no knees or ankles.
- Shoes: same flat red ovals.
- Dress: same square, padded-looking far shoulder; the dress is one flat yellow so its shape (waist, folds) is harder to read.

## 4. Differences
- Dress: a has shading (lighter highlights on the chest and skirt, darker tone at the sides and folds); b is a single flat yellow.
- Skin (arms, legs, face): a has soft shading/rounding; b is flat colour.
- Outlines: b's outlines look slightly thicker and darker than a's, which are a little thinner and softer.
- Ice cream: a's pink scoop has a highlight and shading; b's is flatter.
- Hair: a has a slight sheen; b is flatter dark grey.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: neck too long and too thin; head is fairly large for the body, which suits the cartoon style, but with the long neck it looks perched on top.
- b: neck too long and too thin, same as a; head size the same as a, acceptable for the cartoon style.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: thinner, softer dark outlines; colours softly shaded with highlights, giving a slightly rounded, glossy look.
- b: somewhat thicker, even dark outlines; flat, unshaded colour fills.
- More like the things behind the figure (flat colours, firm dark outlines on the walls, window and floor): b.

## 7. Better drawn
b, narrowly. The pose faults (crossing arm, long neck, stiff legs) are identical in both, but b's flat fills and firmer outlines match the scene and read more clearly, while a's glossy shading looks like it belongs to a different picture; a's shading does show the dress shape slightly better.
