# Review fig 07

## 1. Who and what
An older woman (grey hair in a bun, round glasses) walking, holding a thin red stick or cane out in front of her with both hands; both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Glasses: the front lens sticks out past the outline of the face, so it looks pasted on rather than worn.
- Mouth: a small red mark right at the edge of the face, it reads as a smudge more than a mouth.
- Hands: the two hands are stacked into one lumpy blob at the front; you can't tell which fingers hold the stick.
- Stick: it comes out of the hand blob with no visible grip and only a short piece shows.
- Torso: a white strip runs down the front of the top; it isn't clear if it is a collar, a placket or something carried under the arm.
- Legs: very thin, straight tubes with no knee or ankle shape.
- Shoes: large flat brown ovals that look heavy compared with the thin legs.

## 3. Faults in b
- Glasses: the same fault, the front lens sticks out past the face outline.
- Mouth: the same red mark at the face edge, with a dark spot next to it that makes it look messier.
- Hands: the same stacked blob of hands at the front.
- Stick: the same, no clear grip.
- Torso: the same unclear white strip down the front.
- Legs: the same thin straight tubes, though the shading gives them a little roundness.
- Shoes: the same large flat ovals.

## 4. Differences
- Top: b has light and dark shading on the teal top (a shiny highlight on the shoulder and sleeve); a is one flat teal.
- Skirt: b has a slight gradient from lighter to darker; a is flat.
- Legs and hands: b has soft shading on the skin; a is flat.
- Shoes: b's shoes are shaded and look slightly glossy; a's are flat brown.
- Face: b's mouth area has an extra dark mark next to the red; a has only the red.
- Hair: b's grey hair has a little soft shading; a's is flatter.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: neck short and a bit thick, about right for a cartoon; head is large for the body, but suits the cartoon style.
- b: the same, neck short and a bit thick, about right; head large but suits the style.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: even, dark, fairly thick outlines; flat colours with no shading.
- b: the same dark outlines; colours with soft, slightly glossy shading and highlights.
- More like the things behind the figure: a (the floor behind is flat colour with plain lines).

## 7. Better drawn
b, by a small margin: the shading gives the top, legs and shoes some shape. The faults in the face, hands and legs are the same in both, and the gloss makes b look a little less like the flat background.
