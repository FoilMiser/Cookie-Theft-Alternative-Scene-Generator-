# Review: fig_04

## 1. Who and what
A woman with dark hair tied back, in a yellow short-sleeved dress and red shoes, sitting on a park bench reading an open red book; both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Legs: long, straight, tube-like shins with no ankles; knees are round balls stuck on top, so the legs look like posts.
- Feet: the red shoes are flat ovals that do not read clearly as feet in shoes.
- Arms: upper arms are puffy tubes and the shoulders bulge like pads; the forearms lie as smooth tubes with no wrists.
- Dress: the soft airbrushed shading on the yellow dress makes it look shiny and rubbery, like a plastic toy.
- Hands: hands on the book are vague blobs.
- Face: the nose and mouth are a small brownish smudge that is hard to read.
- Seat: the body sits in front of the bench slats rather than clearly on the seat.

## 3. Faults in b
- Legs: same long tube shins with no ankles and ball-like knees.
- Feet: same flat red oval shoes.
- Arms: same puffy upper arms and shoulder bulges, tube forearms with no wrists.
- Hands: same vague blob hands on the book.
- Face: same small smudge for nose and mouth.
- Seat: same unclear sitting position against the bench.

## 4. Differences
- Shading: a has soft, smooth gradient shading on the dress, arms and legs (glossy, rounded look); b has flatter colour with only a darker yellow area at the lower dress.
- Outlines: b has clearer, darker outlines round the arms, dress and legs; in a the outlines are softer and partly blend into the shading.
- Dress: the dress in a looks more inflated and rounded at the chest and hips; in b it looks flatter.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: neck about right, perhaps slightly long; head size suits the body.
- b: neck about right; head size suits the body.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: thin, soft outlines; smooth airbrushed gradients giving a glossy, rounded 3D look.
- b: firmer dark outlines; mostly flat colours with a simple darker shade area.
- More like the things behind (bench and grass, flat colours with dark outlines): b.

## 7. Better drawn
b. Its firmer outlines and flatter colours match the bench and the rest of the scene and avoid the shiny plastic look of a; the body shape faults are the same in both.
