# Review: fig 06

## 1. Who and what
A man (delivery or work man) in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest, walking and carrying a cardboard box in front of his chest; both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Upper arm: the grey-blue sleeve is a separate blob stuck on the side of the vest, so it does not clearly join the shoulder or the forearm.
- Hands: only one hand is visible, flat under the box, and it is a simple mitten shape; the other arm is missing, so the box looks held by one hand.
- Legs: very long and thin compared with the short torso, and there is no knee shape where the legs bend.
- Mouth: the mouth is a small mark pushed to the edge of the cheek and is hard to read.
- Feet: the shoes are plain dark blobs.

## 3. Faults in b
- Vest stripes: the reflective bands are ragged and broken, a mix of grey, white and dark patches, and they stick out past the back edge of the vest, so they look torn or unfinished.
- Upper arm: the same detached grey-blue sleeve blob as in a.
- Hands: the same single mitten hand under the box, with no second arm.
- Legs: the same very long, thin legs with no knee shape.
- Mouth: the same small mark at the edge of the cheek.
- Feet: the same plain dark blobs.

## 4. Differences
- Vest stripes: in a they are clean, even white bands inside the vest outline; in b they are jagged, patchy grey-white and stick out beyond the vest at the back.
- Sleeve: in b the grey-blue sleeve looks a little larger and rounder.
- Otherwise the head, cap, face, box, trousers and shoes look the same.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: the neck is too short, barely seen, with the head sitting almost on the vest; the head is large for the body, but suits the cartoon style, and the legs make the body look long.
- b: the same; the neck is too short, and the head is large but acceptable for the style.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: even dark outlines; flat colours with almost no shading; clean white stripes.
- b: the same dark outlines and flat colours, but the stripes have a rough, broken texture.
- More like the background: a (the background uses clean dark outlines and flat colours, and the rough stripes in b do not match it).

## 7. Better drawn
a. The two versions are almost the same, but a's vest stripes are clean and stay inside the vest, while b's look torn and spill past the outline.
