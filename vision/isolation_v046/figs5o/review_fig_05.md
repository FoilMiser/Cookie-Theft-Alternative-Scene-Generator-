# Review: fig 05

## 1. Who and what
A child (a boy with dark curly hair, yellow T-shirt and orange shorts) holding an empty ice-cream cone in one hand and the other hand up at his face, as if crying or rubbing his eye after dropping the ice cream (a pink blob lies on the ground); both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Hand at the face: the raised hand merges into the face as a dark lump, so it is hard to tell whether he is rubbing his eye or touching his mouth.
- Arm holding the cone: there is no visible arm, and the hand with the cone seems to come out of the side of the shirt.
- Legs: long, thin and straight with no knees, almost stick-like under the shorts.
- Ear: it sits high on the side of the head near the hairline.
- Face: the eye and mouth are small and crowded under the hand, so the expression is unclear.

## 3. Faults in b
- Hand at the face: the same dark lump merging with the face, so the action is unclear.
- Arm holding the cone: the same missing arm, with the hand coming out of the shirt side.
- Legs: the same long, thin legs with no knees.
- Ear: the same high position.
- Face: the same crowded, unclear expression.
- Shirt and shorts: the soft shading makes the clothes look a little puffy or blurry next to the flat ground.

## 4. Differences
- Outlines: in a they are thicker and darker; in b they are thinner and lighter.
- Shirt shading: in a it is flat yellow; in b it has a soft lighter-to-darker gradient.
- Shorts shading: in a they are mostly flat orange; in b they have visible soft highlights and shadows.
- Hair: in b the curls look a little more blended and less outlined.
- Body shape: in b the body is very slightly narrower and smoother in outline.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: the neck is short, but just visible between chin and shirt, so about right to slightly short; the head is large for the body, but suits a child.
- b: the same; the neck is short, but acceptable, and the head size suits a child.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: thick dark outlines; flat, solid colours with little shading.
- b: thinner, lighter outlines; soft gradient shading on the shirt, shorts and skin.
- More like the background: a (the box and objects behind have thick dark outlines and flat colours).

## 7. Better drawn
a. It has the same pose faults as b, but its bold outlines and flat colours match the rest of the scene and read more clearly at small size, while b's thin lines and soft shading look blurrier and out of place.
