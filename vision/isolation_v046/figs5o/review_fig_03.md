# Review: fig 03

## 1. Who and what
A woman (dark hair in a ponytail, purple T-shirt, teal trousers) sweeping a small pile of brown leaves with a long-handled yellow broom; both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Hands on the broom: both hands are rounded lumps on the handle with no visible fingers; the upper hand is merged with the top of the handle.
- Near arm: the forearm crossing to the lower hand looks short and thick, with the elbow bent sharply.
- Neck: hardly any neck shown; the head sits almost straight on the shoulders.
- Legs/feet: the legs are wide straight tubes; only one dark shoe shows and the other foot is hidden or missing behind the broom, so the stance is unclear.
- Trousers: the highlight on the thigh is a pale blotch that looks a little like a stain.
- Face: small dot eye and tiny nose/mouth; expression is hard to read.

## 3. Faults in b
- Hands on the broom: the same lumpy hands with no fingers.
- Near arm: same short, thick, sharply bent forearm.
- Neck: same very short neck.
- Legs/feet: same tube legs; only one shoe shows, the other foot unclear behind the broom.
- Trousers and shirt: flat colour, so the line between the two legs and the shape of the hips is harder to see.
- Face: same small dot eye and tiny features.

## 4. Differences
- Shirt: a has shading (lighter and darker purple); b is flat purple.
- Trousers: a has a pale highlight on the thigh and darker shading lower down; b is flat teal with only a faint mark.
- Skin (arms, face): a softly shaded; b flat.
- Outlines: b's outlines look a little thicker and more even than a's.
- Hair: a has a slight sheen; b flatter.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: neck too short (almost none); head is large for the body but suits the cartoon style.
- b: neck too short, same as a; head size the same as a, suits the cartoon style.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: thinner dark outlines; soft shading and highlights on the shirt, trousers and skin.
- b: slightly thicker, even dark outlines; flat colour fills.
- More like the things behind the figure (flat floor tiles and wall with plain dark lines, flat-coloured broom and leaves): b.

## 7. Better drawn
b, narrowly. The figure's shape and faults are the same in both, but b's flat colours and firmer outlines match the broom, leaves and background, while a's shading (especially the pale blotch on the trousers) looks out of place.
