# Review fig 02

## 1. Who and what
An older man (grey hair at the sides, grey moustache, blue cap) standing and waving with his right hand raised high (motion marks either side of the hand); he wears a brown vest over a cream shirt and dark trousers. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Raised arm: the sleeve joins the shoulder at an odd angle, it looks as if it comes out of the top of the vest rather than the shoulder.
- Vest: only one white strap or stripe shows on the front, so it isn't clear whether it is braces, a zip or a seam.
- Neck: very wide, nearly as wide as the head, like a block.
- Lower hand: a small fist half hidden behind the trouser leg, looks tucked in and cut off.
- Waist: the trouser waistband is a thick dark band with a single button on the side, it looks bulky.
- Trousers: very long legs in one dark shape, with little to show the knees.
- Mouth: a small red mark under the moustache, at the edge of the face.

## 3. Faults in b
- Raised arm: the same odd join at the shoulder.
- Raised hand: an extra skin-coloured ring at the wrist under the hand, it looks like a bracelet or a stray piece.
- Vest: the same single unclear white stripe.
- Neck: the same very wide, block-like neck.
- Lower hand: bigger and lower than in a, sticking out past the trouser leg; it looks a little large.
- Waist: the same bulky waistband.
- Trousers: the same long dark legs, though the shading shows some folds.
- Mouth: the same small red mark.

## 4. Differences
- Shirt sleeves: b has soft shading on the cream sleeves; a is flat.
- Vest: b has shading and a slight gloss on the brown; a is flat.
- Trousers: b has shading and fold marks; a is flat dark grey.
- Skin: b's face, hands and neck have soft shading; a's are flat.
- Raised wrist: b has a skin-coloured ring at the wrist; a has none.
- Lower hand: in b it is larger, lower and more visible; in a it is a smaller fist mostly behind the leg.
- Shoes and cap: b's are lightly shaded; a's are flat.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: neck too thick (very wide, block-like), length about right; the head size suits the body.
- b: the same, neck too thick, length about right; the head size suits the body.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: even, dark, fairly thick outlines; flat colours with no shading.
- b: the same dark outlines; colours with soft shading, folds and a slight gloss.
- More like the things behind the figure: a (the wall panels behind are flat colour blocks).

## 7. Better drawn
a, by a small margin: b's shading adds shape to the clothes, but it also adds the stray ring at the raised wrist and an oversized lower hand. The other faults are the same in both.
