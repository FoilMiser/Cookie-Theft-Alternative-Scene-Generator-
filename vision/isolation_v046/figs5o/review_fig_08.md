# Review: fig_08

## 1. Who and what
A young man (teen or young adult) in a red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes, kicking an orange ball with his right foot; both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Hands: both hands are small rounded mitten shapes with a stubby thumb bump and no fingers; the rear hand looks like a blob.
- Mouth: a small red smear low on the face reads more like a wound or lipstick mark than a mouth.
- Hood: the hood behind the neck is a round blue ball that sticks out like a lump on the back of the shoulder.
- Legs: the legs are very long and tube-like compared with the short torso; the standing leg has no visible knee shape.
- Kicking foot: the shoe is a flat white sliver that barely meets the ball.
- Face: nose is a faint bump and the face is very flat; head turned down makes the eye look like a dot under the brim.

## 3. Faults in b
- Hands: same mitten-like hands with no fingers.
- Mouth: the same small red mark low on the face, slightly smaller than in a, still not clearly a mouth.
- Hood: same round lump behind the neck.
- Legs: same very long tube legs relative to the torso.
- Kicking foot: same flat white sliver shoe.
- Face: same flat face with a faint nose.

## 4. Differences
- Mouth: the red mouth mark looks a little larger and more open in a, smaller in b.
- Cap: the shading on the cap crown looks slightly stronger (darker red patch) in a than in b.
- Outlines: the dark outlines look very slightly heavier in a; otherwise the drawings are practically identical.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: neck short and fairly thick, mostly hidden by the hood; acceptable. Head is a little large for the body but suits a cartoon style.
- b: same: neck short, about right for the hooded look; head slightly large but acceptable.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: dark, even outlines round every part; flat colours with mild darker bands (sleeve folds, cap crown).
- b: the same dark even outlines and flat colours with mild darker bands.
- More like the things behind (ball, sign, grass marks, which are flat with dark outlines): no difference.

## 7. Better drawn
No difference. The two versions are almost identical; the only changes (mouth size, a touch of cap shading) are too small to matter at test size.
