## Free description
A sunny day on a paved town square lined with four colourful two-storey shop buildings with striped awnings. In the middle-left, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers holds a camera up to his face, pointing it to the right, toward the woman behind him and to his right, so he seems to be taking her photo. That woman, further back near the shop fronts, wears a yellow dress and is eating a pink ice-cream cone with a happy, eyes-closed smile. In the front-centre-right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sweeps a small pile of brown leaves with a yellow broom. On the right there is a market stall with a green-and-white striped canopy and four crates of fruit or vegetables (red, orange, yellow, green). Around the square are a tree in a round planter, two small round café tables each with two chairs, a black street lamp, a dark grey bin, a cardboard/wooden box and a potted plant. Nothing looks clearly impossible; the scene reads as an ordinary busy morning.
