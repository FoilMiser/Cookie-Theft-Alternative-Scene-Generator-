## Free description
It's a sunny day in a back garden with a tall wooden fence, trees behind it, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a paved path on the left. Near the fence an older man in a grey cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat rakes a small pile of leaves. In front of him to the right, an older grey-haired woman with glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt waters a round flower bed of colourful flowers with a grey watering can. Water pours from the can onto the flowers. On the left there is an empty swing set with a red seat, a wooden gate in the fence, and a birdhouse on a tall pole. At the bottom an orange cat crouches low and stretched out, as if creeping or about to pounce on a red bird pecking at the ground just in front of it. The cat looks like it is about to pounce. A wooden bench stands empty in front of the house.
