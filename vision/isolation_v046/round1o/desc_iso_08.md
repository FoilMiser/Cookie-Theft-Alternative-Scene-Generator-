A swing set in the middle has one empty red swing, a red seesaw on the right is tilted and empty, and a sandbox in the front left is empty, with a grey rock beside it. A green bin stands near the fence. Nothing looks impossible; the main event is the boy's dropped ice cream being eaten by the dog.
