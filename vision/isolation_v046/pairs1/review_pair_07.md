# Review: pair 07 (park picnic, kite, dog with newspaper)

## People and animals in a
- Man on blanket: his near arm sinks behind the basket and the hand is not visible, so it is unclear what he is doing with it.
- Man on blanket: the folded legs read as one beige lump; the knee and the lower leg are hard to separate.
- Man on blanket: the face is small and dark, with eyes and mouth hard to make out at normal viewing size.
- Dog: it sits upright holding an open newspaper in its front paws like a person, and its head is turned back toward the viewer, away from the paper, so it is not clearly "reading".
- Dog: the dark ring round one eye looks like a monocle or glasses, which is odd and hard to read.
- Dog: the back legs and tail are tucked tightly under it; the body is somewhat twisted where the front half meets the back half.
- Girl with kite: both arms go straight up to the reel above her head; the arms are very thin and the hands are tiny dots.
- Girl with kite: the head is large for the thin body and legs (fits a child, but the neck is barely there).

## People and animals in b
- Man on blanket: same as in a; near arm and hand lost behind the basket, folded legs read as one lump, face details small.
- Dog: it sits on its haunches holding the newspaper and faces the paper, which reads more naturally; the red neckerchief is clearer.
- Dog: the tail sticks out stiffly to the left and the back leg is a small curl; the join between body and back leg is a little vague.
- Girl with kite: one arm raised to the reel above her head; arms still thin and hands tiny, but she is drawn slightly larger than in a.
- Girl with kite: the head again sits almost directly on the shoulders with little neck.

## Belonging
No difference. At the size the picture opens, the figures in a and b are drawn with the same dark outlines, flat colours and soft shadows as the bench, trees, pond and flower bed. I could not see a change in line or shading on the figures between the two versions.

## Easier to make out
b, slightly. The dog in b faces the newspaper, so "dog holding/reading a newspaper" is clearer than in a, where it looks away from the paper and has a ring round its eye. The girl is a little larger in b, so it is easier to see she is a child holding the kite string. The man (sitting at a picnic) is equally clear, and equally unclear about his hidden arm, in both. The kite is yellow in a and orange in b; both are easy to see.

## Choice
b, because the dog clearly faces the newspaper it holds and the girl is slightly larger; otherwise the two look the same.
