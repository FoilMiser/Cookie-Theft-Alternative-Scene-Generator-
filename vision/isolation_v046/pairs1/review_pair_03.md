# Review: pair 03 (street scene)

The figures are drawn in almost the same way in a and b. The only differences I could see are small ones on the delivery man and the older woman.

## People and animals in a
- Waving older man (cap, brown vest): his raised arm is a thin white sleeve with a small hand. The motion marks next to the hand are faint, so the wave is readable but slight.
- Waving older man: his lower arm hangs stiffly against his side.
- Delivery man (orange cap and vest): a grey rounded shape sits on his near shoulder or upper arm. It does not clearly read as a sleeve or a strap and looks like a stray or unfinished piece.
- Delivery man: the box is held at his side, and his hands are small and hard to see against it.
- Older woman with dog (teal top): her neck is long and thin.
- Older woman with dog: her glasses are thin, light lines on a small face.
- Older woman with dog: her bare legs below the skirt are thin, pale strokes with little outline weight.
- Dog: drawn cleanly. The lead runs from her hand to the dog's collar without a break.

## People and animals in b
- Waving older man: the same thin raised arm, small hand and stiff lower arm as in a.
- Delivery man: the grey shape on his shoulder is larger and more noticeable than in a. It still looks like an unexplained patch, partly covering the vest stripes.
- Older woman with dog: her glasses are drawn darker and bolder than in a.
- Older woman with dog: her neck is still long and thin, and her legs are still thin, pale strokes.
- Dog: drawn cleanly, the same as in a.

## Belonging
No difference. In both versions the figures share the dark outlines, flat colours and light shading of the shops, lamp post, stall, tree and furniture.

## Easier to make out
Slightly b, mainly because of the older woman:
- Older woman: in b her bolder glasses and larger size make her easier to identify as an older woman at a glance.
- Waving older man: the same in both.
- Delivery man carrying a box: the same in both, though in b the grey patch on his shoulder is more distracting.
- Dog on a lead: the same in both.

## Choice
I would use b, because the older woman reads more clearly. The grey patch on the delivery man's shoulder should be checked in both versions, and it is more noticeable in b.
