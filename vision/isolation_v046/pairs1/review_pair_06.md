# Review: pair 06 (playground, dropped ice cream)

At normal viewing size, a and b look almost the same. The notes below apply to both unless a line says otherwise.

## People and animals in a
- Girl on slide: she seems to sit slightly above the slide surface rather than on it. Her legs are short, and the feet barely touch the chute.
- Girl on slide: both arms are raised in thin lines with small hands, and the join at the shoulders is not clear.
- Boy by the sandpit: his legs are very long and thin compared with his short orange shorts and small body, and his feet are small grey blobs.
- Boy: the hand at his face (rubbing his eye or crying) blends into his dark face, so the gesture is hard to read.
- Boy: the empty cone in his other hand reads well, but the hand holding it is a small blob.
- Dog: the head is pushed down into the pink spill. With its large dark-brown ear patch, the head looks oversized and slightly detached from the neck.
- Dog: the red collar is a stiff rectangle sitting on the neck rather than wrapping around it.

## People and animals in b
- Same figures, poses and issues as in a: the girl floating over the slide, the boy's long thin legs and hidden face gesture, and the dog's large head and boxy collar.
- The dog's body may have very slightly more light-and-dark shading than in a, but I'm not sure of it at this size. Otherwise the outlines and fills look the same.

## Belonging
No difference. In both versions the figures use the same thick dark outline and flat fills as the slide, swings, seesaw, bin and tree.

## Easier to make out
No difference. In both, the girl on the slide, the dog eating the spilled ice cream and the boy holding the empty cone are readable. The boy's hand-to-face (upset) gesture is the least clear detail in both.

## Choice
Either. I can't see a difference that matters at normal viewing size.
