# Review: pair 08 (playground, seesaw, man with camera, boy kicking ball)

## People and animals in a
- Girl in pink on seesaw (right): she seems to hover just above the seesaw end; her seat does not clearly touch the plank.
- Girl in pink on seesaw: her arms are bent into her lap in a tight bundle; it is hard to see whether she is holding a handle.
- Child in yellow on seesaw (left): the lower legs hang down and cross in front of the plank, so it is unclear which side of the seesaw the legs are on.
- Child in yellow: arms are very thin and the hands are hard to see.
- Man with camera: both arms are raised to the camera and fold over each other in front of his face; the near forearm hides most of the face.
- Man with camera: the legs are long and thin and the trousers are very plain; the body is narrow for an adult man.
- Boy kicking ball: the hood at the back of his neck shows as a separate blue bulge behind the head.
- Boy kicking ball: the hands are pale white blobs that look like gloves rather than hands.
- Boy kicking ball: the kicking leg is very long and straight compared with the rest of the body.

## People and animals in b
- Girl in pink on seesaw: same as in a; she seems to hover just above the plank, arms bundled in her lap.
- Child in yellow on seesaw: same as in a; lower legs hang and cross in front of the plank, thin arms.
- Man with camera: same as in a; arms folded up in front of his face, thin long legs.
- Boy kicking ball: same as in a; separate hood bulge, white blob hands, very long kicking leg.

## Belonging
No difference. At the size the pictures open, I cannot see any difference between a and b in how the figures are drawn: same outlines, same flat colours, same small ground shadows. In both, the figures fit with the seesaw, slide, fence and tree.

## Easier to make out
No difference. In both, the man taking a photo, the boy in a cap kicking a ball, and the two children on the seesaw are equally easy to tell apart. In both, the man's face is partly hidden by his arms and camera, and the girl in pink sitting on the seesaw is the least clear because she does not seem to touch the plank.

## Choice
Either; I could not see a difference between the two versions at normal viewing size.
