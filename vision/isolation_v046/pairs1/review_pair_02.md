# Review: pair 02 (back garden)

## 1. People and animals in a
- Older man with rake (by the fence): he faces left and the rake is slanted to the left. His white-sleeved arm crosses his chest and the hands on the handle are small and hard to make out, so the grip looks stiff.
- Older man: the cap brim and moustache area are a dark small cluster; the face is hard to read at this size.
- Older woman watering: neck is thin and long between head and teal top; the arm holding the can is straight and stiff. Otherwise clean outlines, glasses visible.
- Cat (bottom middle): legs bunched under the body, front legs tangled together and hard to count. The head is turned so the face looks pinched. Reads as a crouching or pouncing shape rather than a clear walk or run.
- Bird (red, on grass): fine, simple; reads as a bird pecking.

## 2. People and animals in b
- Older man with rake: he faces right, rake nearly upright, close against his body. His arms bunch together at the chest and the white sleeve folds sharply. The rake head sits by his feet with the leaves to the right. The pose looks a little cramped but more like raking than in a.
- Older man: face small and dark under the cap, as in a.
- Older woman watering: drawn the same as in a (thin neck, stiff arm).
- Cat (bottom left, on the edge of the path): faces left, low to the ground, front legs folded under in a knot. As in a the legs are hard to read, and the head is squashed down.
- Bird (on the path): fine; reads as a bird.

## 3. Belonging
No difference. In both, figures and animals have the same dark outlines, flat colour and soft ground shadows as the swing, fence, house and bench. The drawing style of the figures does not change between a and b; only the poses and positions differ.

## 4. Easier to make out
No difference overall. The older woman watering flowers is equally clear in both. The man raking is slightly clearer in b, where the rake is upright beside him with the leaves at its foot. In a the long slanted rake and the arm across the chest are a little harder to sort out. The cat is unclear in both (tangled front legs); the bird is clear in both.

## 5. Choice
Either. The two versions are drawn the same way; b has a marginally easier-to-read raking pose, but the cat needs fixing in both.
