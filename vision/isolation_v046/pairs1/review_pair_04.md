# Review: pair 04 (park with path, bench, pond)

At normal viewing size, a and b look almost the same. The notes below apply to both unless a line says otherwise.

## People and animals in a
- Man feeding birds (top left): his head looks large for his thin body, and his neck is a thin stalk.
- Man feeding birds: the brown bag is pressed against his chest, and the throwing hand is a small point. It is hard to see which hand holds the bag and which throws.
- Woman on bench: her knees and lower legs stick forward past the front of the seat, so she seems to perch on the edge or float a little in front of the bench.
- Woman on bench: her hands on the red book are small. The book reads clearly, but it looks pasted onto her chest.
- Older man with dog: only one arm shows (cream sleeve, hand at hip holding the lead). The far arm is missing or hidden, which looks slightly unfinished.
- Older man: the brown waistcoat over the cream sleeve is a hard-edged block, and the join at the shoulder looks stiff.
- Dog: the legs are thin, and the near front leg is grey-white with little outline contrast. The head is large and the ear patch is very dark.
- Birds (blue and red): small but readable. The red bird's outline merges with the grain dots.

## People and animals in b
- Same figures, poses and issues as in a: the man's big head and thin neck, the bag/hand confusion, the woman's legs in front of the seat, the older man's single arm, and the dog's thin legs.
- The outlines and fills look the same as in a. If b has softer shading, I can't see it at this size.

## Belonging
No difference. In both versions the figures have the same dark outline and flat colour fills as the trees, bench, lamp post and bin, so they sit in the scene the same way.

## Easier to make out
No difference. In both, the older man (grey hair, moustache, cap, walking the dog on a red lead) and the woman reading a red book are clear. The man feeding birds is the least clear figure in both, because his hands and bag are small and bunched together.

## Choice
Either. I can't see a difference between a and b at normal viewing size that would affect the test.
