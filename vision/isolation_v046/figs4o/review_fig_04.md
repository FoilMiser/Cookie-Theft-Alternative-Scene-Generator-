# Review of figure 04

## 1. Who and what
A woman with dark hair tied back, in a short-sleeved yellow dress and red flat shoes, sitting on a park bench reading an open red book held in both hands; both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Face: the mouth is a small brown smudge right under the nose, more like a mark or moustache than a mouth.
- Knees: drawn as two separate round balls stuck on the ends of the thighs, with a visible join to the shins.
- Lower legs: long, straight tubes of even width with no calf or ankle, and they look too long for the body.
- Feet/shoes: the red shoes are flat ovals with no shape of a foot inside them.
- Right arm (her left, nearer the viewer's right): the upper arm is a thick sausage bent at a hard angle, the elbow a rounded lump.
- Hands: the hands on the book are blobs with no fingers clearly shown.
- Dress: the bodice has glossy, plastic-looking highlights that make the cloth look shiny and inflated.

## 3. Faults in b
- Face: the same small brown smudge under the nose for a mouth.
- Knees: the same ball-like knees with a visible join to the shins.
- Lower legs: the same long, even tubes with no calf or ankle.
- Feet/shoes: the same flat red ovals.
- Right arm: the same thick sausage-like upper arm with a lumpy elbow.
- Hands: the same blob hands without clear fingers.

## 4. Differences
- Dress colour: a is a brighter, lemon yellow; b is a slightly deeper, more golden yellow.
- Dress shading: a has strong light highlights and darker shadow patches (glossy look); b is flatter, with only soft darker shading low on the skirt and under the arm.
- Skin: a has a little more shading on the arms and legs; b is flatter.
- Hair: a has a slightly stronger highlight on the top of the head; b is flatter dark grey.
- Overall the figure in b is very slightly larger in the frame; shape and pose otherwise the same.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: neck short-to-about-right in length and about right in thickness; head a little large for the body, as cartoons often are, but acceptable.
- b: the same; neck about right, head slightly large but acceptable.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: dark, even outlines; colours shaded with glossy highlights and soft gradients, giving a rounded, shiny look.
- b: the same dark, even outlines; colours mostly flat with light shading.
- More like the things behind the figure (flat bench and grass with dark outlines): b.

## 7. Better drawn
b. The pose and shapes are the same, but b's flatter colours sit better with the scene and avoid a's shiny, plastic look on the dress; the faults in the legs, knees and mouth are shared.
