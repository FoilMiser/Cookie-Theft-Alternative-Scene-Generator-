# Review of pair 02

## 1. Who and what
A young man or teenage boy in a red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes kicking an orange ball; both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Mouth: a red smudge on the cheek beside the nose, too large and off to the side, which can read as a cut or blood rather than a mouth.
- Hair at the cap: a brown jagged band shows under the cap at the front, which looks like a torn patch on the cap.
- Arm held behind: straight and stiff, sticking out flat with no bend at the elbow.
- Hands: small fists with stubby thumbs, the front hand looks like a lump.
- Hoodie: the drawstring is a single thin line, hard to read.
- Kicking foot: the shoe is a flat white shape touching the ball with no clear toe or sole.

## 3. Faults in b
- Mouth: smaller and darker than in a, sitting under the nose, but still slightly off to the side.
- Hair at the cap: same brown band under the front of the cap, slightly less jagged.
- Arm held behind: same stiff straight arm.
- Hands: same small fists.
- Hoodie: same thin drawstring line.
- Kicking foot: same flat white shoe.

## 4. Differences
- b has shading on the cap (darker red underneath and at the back), a's cap is flat red.
- b has shading on the hoodie and trousers; a's are flat.
- The mouth in b is smaller and less red than in a.
- The outlines in b are slightly thinner and softer.
- The hood behind the neck in b is shaded and looks rounder.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: neck is a fairly long, thick skin-coloured column between head and hoodie; a little long but acceptable. Head is large for the body, cartoon-style, and suits it.
- b: neck looks about the same, slightly long; head size the same as in a and suits the body.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: thick, dark, even outlines; flat colours.
- b: slightly thinner, softer outlines; colours shaded, with darker undersides.
- More like the things behind the figure (flat green grass, flat shadow, a thick-outlined yellow shape at top right): a.

## 7. Better drawn
b: the mouth no longer reads as a red mark on the cheek and the shading gives the cap and hoodie some shape; the other faults are shared.
