# Review of figure 03

## 1. Who and what
An older woman with grey hair in a bun and large round dark glasses, in a teal jumper, blue-grey skirt and brown shoes, walking and holding a thin red stick or cane out in front of her with both hands; both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Mouth: a small dark red mark at the very edge of the face, partly outside the outline, so it reads as a smudge more than lips.
- Front of the jumper: a narrow white strip runs down the chest edge; it is unclear what it is (collar, paper, strap).
- Hands: both hands are bunched into one lumpy shape with no clear fingers or grip on the stick.
- Legs: long, thin, straight tubes with no knee or ankle shaping; the back leg bends only slightly.
- Shoes: large brown flat ovals, rather big for the thin legs.
- Skirt: a stiff box shape with no sign of the walking legs moving it.
- Jumper: glossy highlights on the shoulder and back make the wool look shiny.
- Outline: thinner and softer than in b, so the edges of the figure look a little blurry.

## 3. Faults in b
- Mouth: a red mark at the edge of the face, again partly outside the outline; it reads more like lipstick than in a, but still looks stuck on.
- Front of the jumper: the same unclear white strip, a little more visible.
- Hands: the same lumpy bunched hands with no clear grip.
- Legs: the same long, thin tubes without knees or ankles.
- Shoes: the same large brown ovals.
- Skirt: the same stiff box shape.

## 4. Differences
- Outline: b has thicker, darker black outlines; a has thinner, slightly softer ones.
- Jumper shading: a has highlights and darker shadow areas (glossy); b is a flat teal.
- Skirt: a has slight shading; b is flat blue-grey.
- Hair: a has a little more shading; b is flatter grey.
- Mouth: b's mouth is a brighter red; a's is darker and smaller.
- White strip on the chest: slightly clearer in b.
- Shape and pose otherwise the same.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: neck about right in length, a little thick; head somewhat large for the body but acceptable for a cartoon.
- b: the same; neck about right and slightly thick, head somewhat large but acceptable.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: thin, soft dark outlines; colours shaded with highlights and gradients.
- b: bold, even black outlines; flat colours.
- More like the things behind the figure (flat grey paving with thin lines): b for the flat colours, though a's thinner outline is closer in weight to the paving lines; overall b.

## 7. Better drawn
b. The shapes are identical, but b's firm outlines and flat colours make the figure crisper and easier to read at small size, while a's soft outline and shiny shading look slightly blurred; the hands, legs and mouth faults are shared.
