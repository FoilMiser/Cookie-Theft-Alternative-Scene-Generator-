# Review of pair 01

## 1. Who and what
A woman (adult, dark hair in a ponytail) in a purple T-shirt and teal trousers sweeping a pile of brown leaves with a long-handled yellow broom; both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Lower hand (her right hand): the broom handle passes over the fist so it reads more as the hand lying on the stick than gripping it.
- Arm nearer the viewer: the forearm reaching down to the lower hand is long and comes across the body at a stiff angle.
- Feet: only one dark shoe is visible, the other foot is lost behind the broom head, so the legs end vaguely.
- Face: the ear is set low and far back on the side of the head; the small red mouth sits almost under the nose and reads as a dot.
- Trousers: flat teal with no fold or knee, so the two legs merge into one shape above the knee.

## 3. Faults in b
- Lower hand: same as a, the handle sits over the fist rather than through the grip.
- Arm nearer the viewer: same long, stiff forearm as in a.
- Feet: same, only one shoe visible.
- Face: same low-set ear and dot-like mouth as in a.
- Trousers: the light patch on the upper thigh looks a little like a stain rather than light falling on the leg.

## 4. Differences
- b has soft shading on the trousers (a lighter patch on the thigh and darker parts lower down); a's trousers are one flat colour.
- b has light shading on the T-shirt and arms; a's are flat.
- The outline in b looks a touch thinner and softer than in a.
- Otherwise pose, hair, face and broom look the same.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: neck is short, barely visible between the jaw and the T-shirt; head is large for the body, in a cartoon way, but acceptable.
- b: same as a, short neck, large cartoon head.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: thick, dark, even outlines; flat colours with no shading.
- b: slightly thinner, softer dark outlines; colours lightly shaded.
- More like the things behind the figure (flat floor, dark-outlined edge at the top, outlined leaves): a.

## 7. Better drawn
b, slightly: the shading gives the trousers and shirt some form while the figure stays just as readable; the drawing faults (hand on the broom, hidden foot) are the same in both.
