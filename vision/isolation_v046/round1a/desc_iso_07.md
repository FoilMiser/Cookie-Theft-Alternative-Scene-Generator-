# iso_07

## Free description

A sunny park scene with a blue pond, green hills, trees, bushes, a tall black lamp post, a wooden bench and a winding sandy path. A man in a green shirt and khaki trousers sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket in front of the pond. Beside him is a wicker basket holding a baguette and a green bottle, and a slice of watermelon on a plate. His eyes look closed or squinting, as if he is relaxing or dozing. In front of him, a brown dog with a red collar or scarf sits up on its hind legs holding an open newspaper with its front paws, as if reading it. That is odd and humorous, since dogs don't read. On the right, a girl in a pink dress stands at the edge of the path, holding a kite string reel up high. Her orange kite flies high in the sky with a bow-tied tail, and the string runs right past the tree next to her. On the left there is a flower bed with red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers, and a green rubbish bin in the bottom-left corner.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The dog reading a newspaper looks impossible. It might be meant as a joke, or the dog might just be holding or playing with the paper. The man's newspaper may also have been taken.
- The man's face: eyes closed (dozing or contented) or squinting/smiling. It is not clear whether he notices the dog.
- The kite string seems to pass in front of or through the tree trunk and leaves on the right instead of going around it, which looks physically odd.
- The kite's tail bows look slightly detached or floating near the kite.
- The girl holds the reel up at arm's length with nothing else in that hand. That is fine, but her arm and the string meet at a small red-and-white reel that is hard to make out.
