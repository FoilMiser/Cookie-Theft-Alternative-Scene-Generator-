## Free description
A sunny back garden next to a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the left. A tall wooden fence runs across the back, with a wooden gate on the right and a pale paved path leading from the gate down to the bottom of the picture. On the left, in front of the house, a woman with dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. A sandwich lies on the bench beside her, and a small blue bird is standing right next to it, apparently pecking at it or about to take it while she isn't looking. In the middle, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans kneels by a round flower bed full of red, yellow, purple and pink flowers, digging with a small trowel; a small pot with a pink plant sits at the edge of the bed, as if waiting to be planted. On the right of centre, an older man with grey hair and a grey moustache, wearing a grey cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers, walks to the right carrying a cardboard box. Two potted green plants stand on the grass near the gate. A tree is behind the house, a bush is beside it, and there are two clouds and a bright sun in the sky.
