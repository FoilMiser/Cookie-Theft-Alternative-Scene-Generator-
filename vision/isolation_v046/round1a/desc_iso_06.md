# iso_06

## Free description
A sunny park with a winding sand-coloured path on the left, a pond on the right, trees, bushes, a tall street lamp and rolling green hills behind. Near the top of the path a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is bending slightly and scattering crumbs or seeds from a paper bag. A trail of little crumbs falls from his hand down to two birds on the ground, a red bird and a blue bird, who are pecking at them. In the middle a woman in a yellow dress sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. In the front right an older man with grey hair, a moustache and a blue cap, wearing a brown waistcoat over a white shirt and dark trousers, is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red leash. The pond has reeds/bulrushes at both ends, and there is a green litter bin in the bottom-right corner. Everything looks calm and normal; nothing seems about to go wrong.
