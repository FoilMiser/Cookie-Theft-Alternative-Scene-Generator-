# Review: fig_11

## 1. Who and what
A child with dark skin and short curly dark hair, in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts. They hold an ice-cream cone (pink inside, apparently empty or with the scoop gone) in one hand and press the other hand to their face by the eye and mouth, as if crying or rubbing their eye. A pink drop lies on the ground. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Face: the hand at the face is a shapeless brown blob merging into the cheek and covering the nose and mouth, so it's hard to tell whether the child is crying, eating or wiping.
- Arm holding the cone: hardly any arm shows. The hand seems to come straight off the shirt's edge with a thin line, so the arm looks missing.
- Cone: very large for a child's hand.
- Legs: long and thin, like sticks, compared with the wide shorts.
- Neck: none. The head sits straight on the shirt.
- Arm at the face: the raised arm shows as one bent shape with no clear elbow or sleeve.

## 3. Faults in b
- Face: the same blob-like hand merging with the cheek and covering the nose and mouth.
- Arm holding the cone: the same, almost no arm visible.
- Cone: very large for the hand.
- Legs: long and stick-thin under wide shorts.
- Neck: none. The head sits on the shirt.

## 4. Differences
- Outlines: b's are thicker and darker all around the figure (hair, shirt, shorts, legs, shoes).
- Shading: a has a soft lighter highlight on the shirt and shorts; b's shirt and shorts look flatter, with only a small darker patch on the shorts.
- The face and hand area reads slightly clearer in b because of the stronger outline separating the hand from the cheek.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: no neck shows, so it reads as too short. The head is big for the body, which suits a child.
- b: the same, no neck (too short), with a big head that suits a child.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: medium-thin dark outlines with soft airbrush-like highlights on the clothes.
- b: thicker, bolder dark outlines with mostly flat colours.
- More like the things behind the figure: b (the box at the lower left has a thick dark outline and flat colour, like b).

## 7. Better drawn
b. Its bolder outlines separate the hand from the face and make the figure read more clearly, though both share the missing cone arm and the unclear hand at the face.
