# Review: fig_12

## 1. Who and what
An older man with grey hair and a grey moustache, in a blue cap, brown waistcoat over a cream long-sleeved shirt and dark trousers, waving with one arm raised high (motion marks by the hand) and his mouth open as if calling out. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Raised arm: very long and stiff, with no clear elbow; it seems to start from behind the shoulder.
- Waistcoat: a cream stripe runs down the front that looks like a gap or a misplaced strap.
- Free hand (viewer's right): a small fist that sits a little detached from the sleeve end.
- Trousers: long, tube-like legs with no knees.
- Shoes: flat brown ovals.
- Waistband: a lone button at the hip that looks out of place.

## 3. Faults in b
- Raised arm: the same long, stiff arm with no clear elbow; an orange band at the wrist looks like a stray mark.
- Waistcoat: the same cream stripe down the front.
- Free hand (viewer's right): hangs open and droops, looking a bit limp and loose at the wrist.
- Trousers: the same tube legs, with shiny highlight streaks.
- Shoes: the same flat brown ovals.
- Waistband: the same lone button at the hip.

## 4. Differences
- Raised hand: b has an orange band at the wrist; a does not.
- Free hand: a is a closed fist; b is a more open, drooping hand with a cuff line.
- Trousers: b has glossier shading and highlight streaks; a is flatter.
- Waistcoat and sleeves: b shows slightly stronger rounded shading than a.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: neck short and fairly thick, about right for an older man; head size suits the body.
- b: same as a, neck short and fairly thick, about right; head size suits the body.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: heavy dark outlines, mostly flat colours with light shading.
- b: similar dark outlines, glossier rounded shading with highlights.
- More like the things behind the figure: a (the background is flat colour with heavy dark lines).

## 7. Better drawn
a. Its flat shading matches the scene and its fist looks more natural than b's drooping hand and stray wrist band; the arm and waistcoat faults are in both.
