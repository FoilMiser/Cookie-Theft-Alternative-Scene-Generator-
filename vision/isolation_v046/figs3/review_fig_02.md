# Review: fig_02

## 1. Who and what
An older woman with grey hair in a bun and round dark glasses, in a teal top and grey-blue skirt, standing and watering flowers with a grey watering can. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Glasses: they are large and stick out past the outline of the face at the front, so they look as if they are floating off the face.
- Face: the mouth is a small red mark low on the chin, beside the glasses, and is easy to miss.
- Chest: a narrow white vertical strip down the front of the top looks like a stray gap or an unfinished collar.
- Legs: the bare legs are thin, straight tubes, and the back leg is hidden behind the front one, so she looks as if she stands on one leg.
- Feet: the brown shoes are flat blobs.
- Hands: the hands on the can's handle are small rounded blobs without fingers.

## 3. Faults in b
- The same faults as a: the glasses stick out past the face, the mouth mark is low on the chin, there is a white strip down the chest, the tube legs overlap, and the shoes and hands are blobs.

## 4. Differences
- None seen. The two versions look the same to me, apart from perhaps a very slight difference in how dark the shading is on the sleeve, which I cannot be sure of.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: the neck is short but visible, and about right in thickness. The head is a little large for the slim body, but acceptable for a cartoon.
- b: the same as a.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: firm dark outlines; flat colours with light soft shading on the top and skirt.
- b: the same firm dark outlines and light soft shading.
- More like the things behind the figure (the brick wall, grass and flowers, with dark outlines and flat colour): no difference.

## 7. Better drawn
No difference. I cannot see any real difference between a and b in how the figure is drawn.
