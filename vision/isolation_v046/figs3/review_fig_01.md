# Review: fig_01

## Who and what
An older man (grey hair, grey moustache, cap) walking to the left while holding a thin red cord or lead in both hands; both versions read the same.

## Faults in a
- Hands: both hands are rounded orange blobs bunched together, with no clear fingers or grip on the red cord.
- Neck: there's no neck. The head sits straight on top of the vest.
- Waist: the dark band at the waist bulges like a separate lump between the vest and trousers.
- Shirt front: the narrow white strip of shirt showing at the vest opening is hard to read.
- Legs: the legs are long compared with the short torso, and the rear leg bends at an awkward angle.
- Shoes: both shoes are flat brown slabs with no shape of a foot.
- Mouth: a small red mouth shows under the moustache and looks like a mark on the face.

## Faults in b
- Hands: the same blob-like bunched hands with no clear grip on the cord.
- Neck: no neck. The head sits on the vest.
- Waist: the same lumpy dark waistband.
- Shirt front: the same unclear white strip at the vest opening.
- Legs: the same long legs and awkward rear-leg bend.
- Shoes: the same flat brown slabs.
- Mouth: the same small red mark under the moustache.

## Differences
- The sleeves: in a the white sleeve has a soft grey shaded band and a highlight. In b the sleeve is flatter, with less shading.
- The vest and trousers: a shows soft gradient shading. b's colours are more even and flat.
- The outlines: in a they're thinner and softer. In b they're a bolder, more even black.

## Neck and head
- a: The neck is too short (none shows). The head is large for the body, in a cartoon way, and doesn't look out of place.
- b: The neck is too short (none shows). The head size is the same as in a and suits the body in the same cartoon way.

## Outline and shading
- a: Thinner, softer dark outlines. Colours have soft gradient shading and highlights.
- b: Bolder, even black outlines. Colours are mostly flat, with little shading.
- More like the things behind the figure: b (the background has a bold black horizon line and flat colours).

## Better drawn
b. Its bold outlines and flat colours match the scene, so the figure sits in it more naturally. The figure's faults are the same in both versions.
