# Review: fig_04

## 1. Who and what
A woman with dark hair tied back, in a short-sleeved yellow dress, sitting on a park bench reading a red book held in both hands. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Knees: each knee is drawn as a separate round ball with a visible seam above and below it, so the legs look like a jointed doll's or a puppet's.
- Legs: the bare lower legs are long, straight, even tubes with no calves or ankles.
- Feet: the red shoes are flat ovals with no shape of a foot.
- Arms: the far upper arm is a thick puffy tube, and the elbow joint shows as a bulge where the forearm crosses the body.
- Hands: the hands on the book are rounded blobs without fingers.
- Dress: the skirt's lower edge sags in a soft lump over the knees, so the hem is hard to read.
- Face: the mouth is a small mark at the very bottom of the chin.

## 3. Faults in b
- Knees: the same round ball knees with seams, again looking like doll joints; the far knee shows a lighter ring around it.
- Legs and feet: the same straight tube shins and flat oval red shoes.
- Arms and hands: the same puffy far upper arm, elbow bulge and fingerless hands.
- Dress: the lower part of the skirt has a darker, dull yellow band that looks like a stain or a shadow pasted on.
- Face: the same mouth mark at the bottom of the chin.

## 4. Differences
- The skirt in b has a darker, browner yellow area across its lower part and back; in a the dress shading is lighter, with paler highlights on the bodice and lap.
- The neck in a shows a little more length between the jaw and the collar than in b.
- The far knee in b has a slightly lighter ring around the joint than in a.
- The head in b looks very slightly smaller in relation to the body than in a.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: the neck is about right in length, perhaps a little thin. The head is large for the body, but within a cartoon style.
- b: the neck looks a little short and about right in thickness. The head suits the body slightly better than in a.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: thin dark outlines that fade in places on the dress; soft, smooth shading on the dress with pale highlights.
- b: similar thin dark outlines; soft shading with a heavier, darker lower skirt.
- More like the things behind the figure (the bench, drawn with dark outlines and flat brown, and the flat green grass): no difference. Both figures look softer and more rounded than the flat background.

## 7. Better drawn
a, by a small margin. Its dress shading is cleaner, while b's darker lower skirt looks stained. Both share the same doll-joint knees and tube legs, which are the main fault.
