# Review: fig_06

## 1. Who and what
A woman with black hair in a short-sleeved yellow dress, eyes closed and smiling, holding a pink ice-cream cone up to her mouth. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Shoulder (viewer's right): the sleeve comes to a hard, squared-off point, like a shoulder pad.
- Arm holding the cone: the forearm crosses the chest at a stiff angle and the hand is a small lump under the cone, so the grip is hard to read.
- Free hand (viewer's left): a rounded blob with no fingers, hanging slightly away from the leg.
- Legs: plain straight tubes with no knees or ankles, very long compared with the dress.
- Shoes: dark red flat ovals that look stuck on below the legs.
- Neck: a long, plain column that looks a little stiff.

## 3. Faults in b
- Shoulder (viewer's right): the same squared-off, pointed sleeve.
- Arm holding the cone: the same stiff crossing forearm and small lumpy hand.
- Free hand (viewer's left): a blob fist with a dark band at the wrist that looks like a stray line rather than a cuff or watch.
- Legs: the same tube legs with no knees or ankles.
- Shoes: the same flat dark red ovals.
- Dress: shiny highlight streaks make the cloth look plastic rather than fabric.

## 4. Differences
- Dress: b has stronger light-to-dark shading and shiny highlight streaks; a is flatter with softer shading.
- Outlines: a has heavier, darker outlines; b's are slightly thinner and softer.
- Free hand: b has a dark band at the wrist that a does not have.
- Skin: b's arms and legs show a little more rounded shading than a's.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: neck a little too long and rather thick; head size suits the body.
- b: neck a little too long and rather thick, much the same as a; head size suits the body.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: heavy dark outlines, mostly flat colours with gentle shading.
- b: slightly thinner outlines, glossier rounded shading with highlights.
- More like the things behind the figure: a (the background has heavy dark outlines and flat colours).

## 7. Better drawn
a. It sits more naturally with the scene and its flatter shading reads as cloth, while b's shiny dress and the stray wrist band look less finished; the body faults are shared.
