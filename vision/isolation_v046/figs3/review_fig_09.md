# Review: fig_09

## Who and what
A man (brown hair, brown moustache, red T-shirt, blue trousers) kneeling on one knee and digging with a small trowel beside a yellow flower; both versions read the same.

## Faults in a
- Arm: the reaching arm is a long straight tube with no elbow, and it looks too long.
- Hand: the hand holding the trowel is a small blob, with an orange patch at the wrist that looks like a cuff or a stray mark.
- Front leg and foot: the front shoe is a flat dark oval on the ground that looks more like a stone than a foot. It's not clearly joined to the front leg.
- Hip: the trousers bulge in a rounded lump at the front of the hip.
- Back foot: the rear shoe is a small dark wedge at the end of the long flat shin.
- Other arm: only one arm shows, so the figure looks one-armed.
- T-shirt: the sleeve and shoulder blend into one shape.

## Faults in b
- Arm: the same long straight tube arm with no elbow.
- Hand: a small plain blob of a hand on the trowel.
- Front leg and foot: the same stone-like dark oval shoe, not clearly joined to the leg.
- Hip: the same bulge at the front of the hip.
- Back foot: the same small dark wedge of a shoe.
- Other arm: only one arm shows.

## Differences
- The wrist: a has an orange patch at the wrist and hand. b's hand is plain skin colour.
- The T-shirt: a has more visible darker red shading on the back and under the sleeve. b's red is flatter.
- The trousers: a has lighter blue highlights. b's blue is more even.
- The outlines: a's look slightly thinner and softer. b's are slightly bolder black.

## Neck and head
- a: The neck is about right in length, and a little thick. The head is large for the body, in a cartoon way, and suits the style.
- b: The neck is about right in length and thickness, as in a. The head size is the same and suits the body in the same cartoon way.

## Outline and shading
- a: Thinner, softer dark outlines. Soft gradient shading on the shirt and trousers, plus the orange wrist patch.
- b: Bolder black outlines. Flatter colours with less shading.
- More like the things behind the figure: b (the fence and flower have bold black outlines and flat colours).

## Better drawn
b. It doesn't have the stray orange patch on the wrist, and its bolder outlines and flatter colours match the fence and flower better. The pose faults are shared by both versions.
