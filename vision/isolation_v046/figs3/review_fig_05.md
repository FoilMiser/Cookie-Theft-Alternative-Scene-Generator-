# Review: fig_05

## 1. Who and what
A young man in a red cap, blue hooded top and dark trousers, kicking an orange ball with his right foot, arms swinging. In a he seems to smile with an open mouth; in b the mouth reads as a small dark mark, so the expression is more neutral or unclear.

## 2. Faults in a
- Kicking leg: very long, straight and stiff, longer than the standing leg, like a plank.
- Face: the open red mouth sits low on the side of the jaw, well away from the nose and eye, so it looks misplaced.
- Back arm: the arm behind the body is short and stubby, with the hand bunched into a lump.
- Hands: small mitten-like fists with little shape.
- Hood: it bulges out behind the neck as a separate blue lump.
- Hoodie drawstring: a faint pale line that is hard to read.

## 3. Faults in b
- Kicking leg: the same very long, stiff, straight leg.
- Face: the mouth is a small dark mark with dots around the chin that look like dirt or stubble rather than a mouth.
- Back arm: short and stubby, as in a.
- Hands: small mitten-like fists.
- Hood: it bulges behind the neck as a separate lump.

## 4. Differences
- Mouth: a has an open red smiling mouth; b has a small dark mark with dark speckles on the chin.
- Hoodie drawstring: pale or white-ish in a, darker in b.
- Shading: a has slightly more soft shading and highlights on the hood and hoodie; b's blue looks a little flatter.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: the neck is short but visible, about right to slightly short. The head is large but suits the cartoon body.
- b: the same short neck, about right to slightly short, with a large head that suits the body.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: dark, even outlines with soft light shading on the hoodie and trousers.
- b: the same dark, even outlines with slightly flatter colours.
- More like the things behind the figure: no difference (the ball and sign have similar dark outlines and flat colours, and both versions match them about equally).

## 7. Better drawn
a. The open smiling mouth gives a clear, readable expression, while b's mouth and speckled chin look smudged. Otherwise the two are nearly the same.
