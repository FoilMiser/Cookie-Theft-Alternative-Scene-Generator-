# Review: fig_10

## Who and what
A woman (dark hair in a ponytail, purple T-shirt, teal trousers) sweeping a pile of brown leaves with a long-handled yellow broom; both versions read the same.

## Faults in a
- Neck: there's no neck. The head sits directly on the shoulders.
- Hair: the ponytail is a large dark lump hanging down the back of the head.
- Arms: the arm on the viewer's left juts out sideways from the sleeve like a stub and bends sharply. It's hard to tell which hand goes with which arm.
- Hands: the hands on the broom handle are rounded blobs with no fingers.
- Legs and feet: the legs merge into one teal shape, and only one dark shoe shows, behind the broom head.
- Trousers: dark shading patches on the trousers look like stains rather than folds.

## Faults in b
- Neck: no neck. The head sits on the shoulders.
- Hair: the same large ponytail lump.
- Arms: the same stub-like, sharply bent arm on the viewer's left.
- Hands: the same blob hands on the handle.
- Legs and feet: the same merged legs, with only one shoe visible.
- Trousers: a pale highlight patch at the hip looks a little like a light stain.

## Differences
- The trousers: a has darker shadow patches. b has a lighter highlight at the hip and thigh instead.
- The T-shirt: a has slightly more visible darker shading on the lower part. b's T-shirt is a little flatter.
- The skin: a has slightly more shading on the forearms.
- The outlines: they look very similar. b's may be slightly thinner.

## Neck and head
- a: The neck is too short (none shows). The head is large for the body, in a cartoon way. It's acceptable, but it looks top-heavy.
- b: The neck is too short (none shows). The head size is the same as in a.

## Outline and shading
- a: Dark grey-black outlines of medium weight. Soft shading, with darker shadow patches on the trousers and T-shirt.
- b: Similar dark outlines. Softer shading, with a pale highlight on the trousers.
- More like the things behind the figure: no difference (the floor tiles are flat, with thin grey lines, and both figures are shaded and outlined more heavily than the floor).

## Better drawn
No difference. The shape and pose are the same, and the faults are shared. The only change is small shading detail, and neither version is clearly better.
