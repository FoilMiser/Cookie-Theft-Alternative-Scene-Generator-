# Review: fig_07

## 1. Who and what
A woman with dark hair in a low ponytail, sitting on a park bench reading a red book, with a sandwich beside her on the bench and a blue bird pecking at it. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Hands: the near hand holding the book is a shapeless round blob with no fingers, and the far hand is a small blob at the book's edge.
- Arm: the near forearm is a long, even sausage with no wrist, and it bends at an odd flat angle under the book.
- Legs: the trousers form straight tubes from knee to ankle, with no shape to the calves, so the lower legs look stiff.
- Feet: the shoes are flat dark ovals that look pasted onto the trouser ends.
- Face: the mouth is a small mark low on the chin, almost below the face, which looks odd.
- Sandwich: it overlaps the figure's lap and thigh, so the figure looks partly hidden behind it (a matter of the scene rather than the figure).

## 3. Faults in b
- Hands: the same blob hands with no fingers, near and far.
- Arm: the same tube forearm with no wrist.
- Trousers: blotchy darker teal patches on the thigh and knee look like stains rather than folds.
- Legs and feet: the same stiff straight lower legs and flat oval shoes.
- Face: the same mouth mark low on the chin.

## 4. Differences
- The trousers in b have soft, darker teal shading patches on the thigh and knee; in a they are a flatter, more even teal.
- The shirt in b has a little more soft darker shading under the arm and at the sides; in a it is flatter purple.
- The neck in b shows slightly more skin between the jaw and the collar than in a.
- The outlines in b look a little softer and slightly thinner on the body than in a.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: the neck looks a little short but acceptable; its thickness is about right. The head is slightly large for the body, as in a cartoon, but it suits the figure.
- b: the neck is about right in length and thickness. The head suits the body about as well as in a.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: firm dark outlines all round; mostly flat colour fills with very little shading.
- b: dark outlines that are slightly softer; soft graded shading on the trousers and shirt.
- More like the things behind the figure (the bench, window and grass, drawn with thick dark outlines and flat colour): a.

## 7. Better drawn
a. Its flat colours and firm outlines match the rest of the scene, and it avoids the blotchy patches on b's trousers; the two are otherwise drawn the same.
