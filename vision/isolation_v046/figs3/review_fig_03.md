# Review: fig_03

## 1. Who and what
A man (delivery or road worker) in an orange cap, orange high-visibility vest and blue trousers, walking while carrying a cardboard box in front of him. Both versions read the same.

## 2. Faults in a
- Vest: the reflective stripes look scratchy and broken, white with grey zigzag hatching, like an unfinished texture.
- Vest: the stripes stick out past the left edge of the torso, so they look like separate straps rather than part of the vest.
- Arm: the upper sleeve is a dull grey-blue blob that doesn't match the vest or trousers; it's hard to read as a sleeve.
- Arms: only one arm shows and the hand under the box is a flat pale shape with no fingers, so the grip on the box is unclear.
- Neck: there is none. The head sits straight on the vest collar.
- Face: the mouth is a tiny mark right under the nose, almost touching it.
- Ear: it sits far back on the head, close to the hairline at the back.

## 3. Faults in b
- Arm: the same grey-blue upper sleeve blob, unclear as a sleeve.
- Arms: the same single flat hand under the box, with no clear grip.
- Neck: none visible. The head sits straight on the vest.
- Face: the same tiny mouth jammed under the nose.
- Ear: placed far back on the head, as in a.
- Vest: the stripes still reach slightly past the torso's left edge, though less obviously than in a.

## 4. Differences
- Vest stripes: in a they are rough, hatched white-and-grey zigzags; in b they are smooth, even pale bands.
- Vest stripes: in a they stick out further beyond the body outline on the left than in b.
- The vest's orange looks slightly flatter and more even in b.

## 5. Neck and head
- a: no neck shows, so it reads as too short. The head is large for the body (cartoon-like) but acceptable.
- b: the same, no neck (too short), with a large but acceptable head.

## 6. Outline and shading
- a: dark, fairly even outlines; flat colours with very light shading; the stripes have busy, sketchy texture.
- b: the same dark, even outlines and flat colours, with clean, untextured stripes.
- More like the things behind the figure: b (the flat, cleanly outlined window frames and sign match b's smooth stripes better than a's hatching).

## 7. Better drawn
b. The vest stripes are clean and sit within the body, while in a they look scratchy and stick out like loose straps.
