## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills, trees, bushes, a winding sandy path on the left, a tall black lamp post in the middle and a blue pond with reeds/cattails on the right. In the back left, a man in a green shirt and tan trousers stands on the path holding a paper bag and scattering seeds or crumbs, which fall in a dotted trail down to the grass. Two small birds, one blue and one red, are on the ground by the path pecking at the seeds. In the centre, a woman in a yellow dress sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. In the front right, an older man with grey hair and a moustache, wearing a blue cap, brown vest, white shirt and dark trousers, walks to the left holding a red leash attached to a white dog with brown patches, which walks ahead of him. A green rubbish bin stands in the bottom right corner. Nothing looks clearly impossible; it is a calm everyday park scene.
