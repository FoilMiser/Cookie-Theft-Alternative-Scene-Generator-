## Free description
This is a sunny back garden next to a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door. A wooden fence runs across the back, and there is a wooden gate in it on the right with a paved path running from the gate down to the front right corner. On the left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. A sandwich lies on the bench beside her, and a small blue bird is perched right next to it, apparently pecking at it or about to take it while she is not looking. In the middle, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans kneels in a lunge at the edge of a flower bed full of colourful flowers, digging in the soil with a small trowel; a small pot with a pink flowering plant sits at the edge of the bed in front of him, as if about to be planted. On the right of centre, an older man with grey hair and a moustache, wearing a blue cap, brown vest, white shirt and dark trousers, walks towards the left carrying a cardboard box. Two potted green plants stand on the grass near the fence. Nothing looks clearly impossible; the main "about to happen" moment is the bird getting the woman's sandwich.
