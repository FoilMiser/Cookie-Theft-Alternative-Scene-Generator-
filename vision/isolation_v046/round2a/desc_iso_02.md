## Free description
It is a sunny day in a park with a pond, rolling green hills, trees and a winding path. A man in a green shirt and beige trousers is sitting on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket in front of the pond; beside him is a wicker picnic basket with a baguette and a green bottle sticking out, and a plate with a slice of watermelon is on the blanket. In front of him, a brown dog is sitting up on its hind legs holding an open newspaper with its front paws, as if reading it, which is odd and impossible for a real dog. On the right, by the path, a girl in a pink dress is flying an orange kite; a long thin string runs from her raised hand up to the kite high in the sky, which has a tail with small bows. The man seems to be looking toward the dog, possibly smiling. A tall black lamp post stands by the pond, an empty wooden bench sits beside the path, there is a flower bed at front left and a green rubbish bin in the bottom-left corner.
