## Free description
A sunny back garden with no people or animals in it. There is a wooden fence along the back with a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A garden hose runs in from the right edge of the picture to a small lawn sprinkler sitting on the grass right of centre. The sprinkler is shooting three arcs of water to the left, and the highest arc lands right on top of a cardboard box that is sitting on the lawn. The box looks wet: water is dripping down its sides and there is a small puddle around its base. The other two arcs land on the grass just in front of the box. The flower bed by the house is not being watered at all, as the sprinkler points away from it. Two potted plants stand near the fence on the left and there are two bushes.
