At the back left is a tall red-framed slide with a ladder and a yellow-and-orange chute, and a green rubbish bin stands near it. There are bushes on both sides, a tall tree on the right, and a grey rock by the sandpit. Nothing looks wrong or impossible. It is a quiet scene.
