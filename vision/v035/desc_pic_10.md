# Picture 10

## Free description

This is a sunny playground with a metal railing fence and hedges at the back. A slide, a green bin and a swing set with one empty seat are at the back left and centre. A girl in a pink dress stands by a tree on the right, flying a yellow kite with a bow-tie tail high in the sky, and a blue bird flies nearby. An older woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a bench sipping a red drink through a straw. In the front, a child with braided hair, a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and puts a hand to their head, looking upset. Their pink ice cream has fallen on the ground in a splat. A brown dog with a red collar is eating the fallen ice cream. Strangely, a white dog with a brown ear and a blue collar sits up on the grass holding an open newspaper and appears to be reading it. There is a sandbox with a red bucket and a blue spade, and a grey rock, at the front left.
