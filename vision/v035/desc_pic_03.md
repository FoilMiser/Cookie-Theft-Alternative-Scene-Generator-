## Free description

This is a sunny playground with a metal railing fence and hedges at the back. On the left there is a red slide with a ladder and a yellow-orange chute, and next to it a green bush. A green bin and a swing set with one empty red seat are in the middle. At the front left there is a sandbox with a red bucket and a blue spade in it, and a grey rock beside it, but no one is playing there. A man in a green shirt and tan trousers walks across the front while eating a pink ice-cream cone. On the right, two children are on a red seesaw. A girl in a pink dress sits on the left end and a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on the right end. The seesaw is almost level, tipped slightly down on the right. A tall tree stands on the far right.
