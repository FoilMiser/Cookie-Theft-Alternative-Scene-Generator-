# pic_77

## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, a blue pond with bulrushes on the left, a tall black lamp post and a green litter bin in the middle, a winding sandy path on the right and a large tree at the far right. An elderly man with grey hair, a blue-grey flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands on the grass at the edge of the pond, holding a fishing rod. His line goes down into the water, where a red-and-white float bobs with a little ripple around it. A white duck swims on the left side of the pond. On the right, beside the path, a wooden ladder stands upright on the grass with its top leaning against nothing at all, only the sky and hills. A grey rock sits at the front, and there are a few bushes and a smaller tree on the left. The free-standing ladder is the odd thing: it should fall over because there is nothing holding it up.
