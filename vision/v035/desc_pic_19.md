## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence and trees behind it. A cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the right, and the sun is in the top left. On the right a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers stands beside a colourful flower bed, holding a green watering can tipped towards the flowers. The water does not pour down onto the flowers. The stream comes out of the spout and curves upwards into the air, with drops flying off the top, which water from a watering can could not do. In the centre-left a metal swing set has one empty red seat hanging still. On the far left a paved path leads to a wooden gate, and next to it a small birdhouse with a red roof sits on top of a tall pole. A grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the front and a wooden bench stands at the front right by the house. Apart from the upward water, the scene is calm and ordinary.
