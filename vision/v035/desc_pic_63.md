# pic_63

## Free description
A back garden under a grey, overcast sky with three dark storm clouds. It looks as if rain is about to start. A wooden fence runs across the back with a gate on the left and a paved path down the left side. On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door. In the middle, a washing line strung between two wooden posts has a yellow top with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. Below it, a woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers looks up with a surprised or worried face and points up toward the sky or the washing. That suggests she has noticed the coming rain and the laundry needs bringing in. On the path at the left, a girl or woman with dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes is sweeping dirt toward a red dustpan using a giant silver dinner fork as a broom, which is odd. A small puff of dust rises from the dirt. A brown dog sits beside the house door holding a red lead in its mouth, as if waiting for a walk. In the front there is an empty wheelbarrow, and on the right a hose reel with a hose and a yellow nozzle lies on the grass next to a flower bed. Two potted plants stand by the fence on the left.
