# pic_73

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden picket fence, a gate on the left, a paved path, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. Three people are doing chores. At the front left on the path, a man or boy in a green shirt and khaki trousers is sweeping dirt into a red dustpan, raising a small cloud of dust. Oddly, he is using a huge silver eating fork instead of a broom. In the middle, a man or boy in a red shirt and blue trousers is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a handle. A few fence boards are already blue, and an open tin of blue paint sits on the grass behind him. On the right, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is watering the flower bed with a hose that runs back to a hose reel by the house. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands on the grass at the front, and two potted plants sit by the fence. The giant fork used as a broom is the thing that looks wrong.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The sweeping tool reads to me as a giant dinner fork, because it is all silver with four thin tines and no wooden handle. Another reading is a garden fork or rake, but it does not look like one, and using either to sweep into a dustpan is odd.
- There is a small dark round shape at the top left of the sweeper's head. It could be a hair bun, or it could be the gate's knob showing behind his head. I could not tell which.
- The sweeper could be a man or a teenage boy, and so could the painter.
- The painter stands a little in front of the fence and reaches with the long roller. That looks plausible, not strange.
