# Picture 44

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back, a gate at the far left and a paved path running down the left side. A washing line in the middle holds a yellow-and-red striped item and blue jeans. On the left path, an older man with grey hair, a blue flat cap, a brown waistcoat and white shirt is sweeping a pile of autumn leaves with a yellow broom. In the centre, a man with dark skin, a green shirt and khaki trousers is holding the nozzle of a long green garden hose out in front of him and peering at it, because only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel next to the house, along the ground, round to the man - and a white goat with dark horns is standing right on the hose, squashing it, which is why the water is not coming through. It looks like when the goat steps off, the water may suddenly spray out, possibly into the man's face since he is looking at the nozzle. There are two potted plants near the fence and a flower bed next to the house.
