# Picture 39

## Free description
A windy day in a back garden: white wind lines streak across the sky and a few green leaves are blowing off the tree. A cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, with a flower bed in front of it, and a wooden fence and gate run across the back with a paved path on the right. On the washing line in the middle, the blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped item are blown sideways, slanting to the right. On the right near the gate, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans has both arms up and a surprised look, because his orange cap has just been blown off his head and is flying away up and to the right. In the middle of the garden, a little girl in pink is happily swinging on a swing set, eyes closed and smiling, with motion lines showing she is moving. At the front centre, an older man with grey hair, a blue flat cap, a brown waistcoat and white shirt is sitting reading a red book - but there is no chair, bench or anything under him, so he appears to be sitting on thin air. A teal watering can stands on the grass near the path at the front right.
