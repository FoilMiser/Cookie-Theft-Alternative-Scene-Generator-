# Picture 08

## Free description

This is a back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a wooden fence, a gate and a path on the right. The sun is out, and white wind lines in the sky and in front of the fence show that it is windy. An older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, stands in the middle of the lawn with both arms raised and her mouth open in surprise. Just above and to the left of her hands, a blue hat is flying through the air along with a few leaves, so the wind seems to have blown her hat off and she is reaching for it. Two potted plants stand on the lawn, a flower bed is in front of the house, and a teal watering can sits at the front right by the path. Nobody else is in the picture.

- Older woman, centre: grey hair, round glasses, teal top, grey skirt, brown shoes; arms up, mouth open, looking surprised and reaching for her flying hat.
- Hat: blue, in mid-air above the woman, tumbling in the wind.
- Leaves: two or three small leaves flying near the hat.
- Wind lines: white wavy lines in the sky (top left and right) and in front of the fence beside the hat.
- Potted plants: two terracotta pots with green plants, on the lawn at centre-right.
- Flower bed: colourful flowers in front of the house, left.
- Watering can: teal, front right near the path.
- House: cream walls, red roof, blue door, one window, left.
- Gate and path: wooden gate at the right, stone path.
- Tree: behind the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The wind direction is unclear. The wind lines start next to the hat and run to the right, but the hat sits at their left end, so it could be moving either left or right.
- Two readings: the wind blew her hat off and she is grabbing at it, which is most likely given her surprised face. Or she threw her hat up in the air.
- It is odd that only the hat and a few leaves are affected by the wind. The tree, flowers, potted plants and her hair are not moving.
- The hat looks like a blue cap or a small sun hat. I cannot tell which.
