## Free description
A sunny back garden with the cream, red-roofed house on the left, a wooden fence along the back and a gate and paved path on the right. On the left an older man with grey hair, a grey flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands at the flower bed holding a teal watering can. Strangely, the water coming from the can goes upward: a blue stream rises from the spout in an arc over the flowers with drops at the top, instead of pouring down onto them. On the right a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes is jumping in the air with both arms raised and a big smile, eyes squeezed shut; his shadow is on the grass below him. Along the top of the fence an orange cat is walking with its tail up. Behind the man a washing line holds blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. A grey wheelbarrow stands in the front and a wooden bench sits on the left in front of the house. The boy looks like he is cheering or celebrating something.
