# Picture 05

## Free description

It is a sunny day in a park with green hills, trees and bushes in the background. On the left there is a pond with reeds at both ends and a white duck swimming in it. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie holds a paper bag and is sprinkling seeds or crumbs onto the grass. A blue bird and a red bird are on the ground in front of him, pecking at the food. In the middle there is a tall street lamp, a green bin and an empty wooden bench. A winding path runs from the back to the front right. An older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, walks along pushing a teal pram. In the pram, a baby is asleep under a pink blanket. There is a flower bed with colourful flowers at the front left.
