# Picture 02

## Free description

This is a sunny town street with a row of shops in four colours (pink, pale green, peach and cream), each with a striped awning. On the left there is a market stall with crates of green, yellow, orange and red fruit, with a cardboard box next to it. An older woman with grey hair in a bun and dark glasses is walking along while reading an open book, and she is not looking where she is going. A cardboard box sits on the pavement right in front of her, so she looks about to trip over it. A woman in a yellow dress is watering flowers in a planter box with a teal watering can, and water is pouring out. An older man in a grey cap and brown vest sits on a bench eating a pink ice cream. A small white dog with a brown ear and a blue collar sits up on its back legs next to him, begging. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is taking a photo with a camera, pointing it to the left towards the man, the dog or the woman. A red bird sits on top of a tall street lamp. A teal bicycle is parked in a bike rack. On the right there is a tree in a round planter, a green bin and a small café table with two chairs.
