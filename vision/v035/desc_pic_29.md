## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and paved path at the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door at the right. On the left a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is sitting on a red swing on a metal swing set; motion lines behind him show he is swinging. In the middle a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is raking a small pile of leaves on the grass, standing in front of a washing line that holds a yellow towel with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans. In the front, right of centre, a small girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes is holding a kite string up with both hands and looking up. Her string goes up to a purple kite that is caught in the tree beside the house: some of the leaves are drawn in front of the kite, so it looks stuck in the branches rather than flying. She seems to be trying to pull it free, or about to find out it won't come loose. There is a flower bed with mixed colourful flowers in front of the house. Nobody else seems to have noticed the stuck kite.
