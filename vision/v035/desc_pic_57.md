## Free description
A busy back garden under a grey, overcast sky with three dark storm clouds. It looks as if rain is coming. On the left is a cream house with a red roof and a blue door. A brown dog sits beside the door holding a red lead in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. An orange cat walks along the top of the wooden fence near a tree at the back left. Left of centre, a boy in a red cap and a blue hoodie has just thrown a red ball, shown flying along a dotted arc. It is heading toward a young man in a red shirt and blue jeans, who holds his hands out to catch it. A section in the middle of the back fence has been painted blue. A woman in a yellow dress is painting it with a paint roller on a long handle, and a paint tin and a tray of blue paint sit on the grass near her feet. A trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the grass to a white dog with brown spots on the path at the right. The dog's feet are blue, so it has clearly stepped in the paint. A bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands near the painter, looking up and pointing toward the dark clouds. In the foreground, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is digging with a spade next to a pile of earth. Nearby are an empty wheelbarrow, a teal watering can and a colourful flower bed on the left. Several things seem about to happen: the ball is about to be caught, rain may fall on the fresh paint, and the spotted dog is spreading blue paint around.
