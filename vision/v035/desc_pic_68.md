## Free description
This is a sunny back garden. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, a wooden picket fence runs across the back, and a gate with a paved path is on the right. A woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers stands next to a round flower bed near the house. She is watering the flowers with a teal watering can, and water is pouring out onto them. At the front centre, an orange cat is curled up on the grass with its eyes closed, apparently asleep. Nearby on the grass is a red bowl of cat food. The strange thing is an orange fish sitting in the branches of the tree behind the house. A fish up in a tree is impossible or at least very odd. Two potted plants stand by the fence on the right, and there are bushes along the fence.
