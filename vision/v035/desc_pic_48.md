## Free description
This is a sunny town street with a row of four coloured shop buildings with striped awnings and a wide paved pavement in front. On the far left, a street worker in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest is sweeping a pile of autumn leaves with a broom. In the middle, a girl in a pink dress sits on a bench eating something pink, probably an ice cream, and a white dog with brown ears sits up on its back legs in front of her, begging. A child in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts stands near the lamp post holding a red and a blue balloon in one hand, and reaching up with the other hand towards a purple balloon whose string is no longer in their hand; the purple balloon seems to be floating away. A red bird is perched on top of the street lamp, and a blue bird is flying in the sky at the top left. On the right there is a market stall with crates of red, orange, yellow and green fruit. In front of it, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is walking while reading an open book, and a cardboard box is lying on the ground just ahead of him, so he looks about to trip over it. A second wooden crate sits by the stall and a potted plant is in the bottom right corner.
