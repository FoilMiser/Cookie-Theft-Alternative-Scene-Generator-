## Free description
A sunny day in a park playground with a railing fence and a row of bushes behind it, a sun in the top right and two clouds. On the left a red seesaw is tipped: a small girl in a pink dress sits on the raised left end, holding the handle, her legs dangling in the air and her mouth open in a small "o", as if calling out or surprised. A man in a green shirt and khaki trousers sits on the low right end, which is down near the ground, with his feet on the grass. He is reading an open book and not looking at the girl, so his weight keeps her stuck up in the air. In the middle a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands eating a pink ice-cream cone, eyes closed as if enjoying it. A green litter bin stands by the fence behind him. On the right is an empty slide with a ladder, a bush, and a grey rock in the front right corner. A tree stands on the far left. Nothing looks physically impossible. The odd thing is the situation: the girl is left stranded at the top while the man reads.
