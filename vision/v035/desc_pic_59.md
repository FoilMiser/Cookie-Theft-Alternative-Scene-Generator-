## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, a sun in the top-left corner and two white clouds. There is a tree on the left, a sandy path winding down from the back toward the front, a tall black lamp post in the middle, and a pond on the right with reeds at both ends. In front of him, a woman with long dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes waters a flower bed with a teal watering can, and water pours onto the flowers. There is a grey rock at the front and a green bin in the bottom-right corner.
