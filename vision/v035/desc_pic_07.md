# Picture 07

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and a path on the left, a swing set, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle. Several boards to his left are already blue, and a small paint tin with blue paint stands on the grass by his feet. In the front, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is digging with a shovel, and a pile of brown soil is next to her. A teal watering can and an empty wheelbarrow are on the grass at the front left. The swing has one empty red seat. A flower bed with colourful flowers is in front of the house. Strangely, an orange fish is sitting up in the branches of the tree behind the fence on the right.

- Boy, centre back by the fence: red cap, blue hoodie, dark trousers, white shoes; painting the fence blue with a roller.
- Woman, centre front: dark hair in a ponytail, purple top, teal trousers; digging a hole with a shovel.
- Fish, upper right, in the tree: orange; lying among the branches and leaves.
- Painted fence boards: a section of about five boards painted blue, left of the boy and partly behind the swing set.
- Paint roller: blue roller head pressed on the fence, in the boy's hands.
- Paint tin: small grey tin with blue paint, on the grass near the boy.
- Shovel and soil pile: the woman's shovel has soil on its blade, and a mound of soil sits next to her.
- Watering can: teal, front left.
- Wheelbarrow: grey with a round black wheel and wooden handles, empty, front centre-left.
- Swing set: dark grey frame, one empty red seat.
- Flower bed: colourful flowers in front of the house.
- House: cream walls, red roof, blue door, one window, right.
- Gate and path: wooden gate in the fence at the left, stone path leading from it.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The fish in the tree is impossible or out of place. Fish do not sit in trees, and there is no water or person nearby to explain it.
- The woman could be digging a hole to plant something, or she could be moving soil into the wheelbarrow. The pile suggests she is digging a hole.
- The blue colour on the fence is very bright and even. It clearly reads as freshly painted.
