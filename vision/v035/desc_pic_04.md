# Picture 04

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, a gate and a path on the right. A child with braided hair, a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on a bench by the house reading a red book. Next to the bench there is a hose reel, with the hose lying on the grass and a yellow nozzle at its end. In the middle there is a washing line between two posts with a pair of blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped shirt or towel pegged on it. A cardboard box is floating in the sky above the washing line, tilted, with nothing holding it up and nobody nearby who could have thrown it. On the right, a girl in pink is swinging happily on a swing set, and there are motion lines behind her. A teal watering can stands on the grass at the front right. There is also a flower bed, a bush and a tree behind the house.
