A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a closed wooden gate at the far left, a paved path running down the left side, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A small child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands on the path and has just thrown a red ball; a dashed line shows the ball arcing up and across toward a woman. The woman, in a yellow dress with long dark hair, faces the child with both arms held out, ready to catch the ball. In the middle at the front, a boy in a white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands with his hands on his hips and has an upside-down metal bucket on his head like a hat. Behind them, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is reaching up to a washing line, pegging up a white T-shirt; a pair of blue jeans already hangs beside it. A teal watering can sits on the grass near the path, and a flower bed with colourful flowers is in front of the house. The odd thing is the boy wearing a bucket on his head; everything else looks like an ordinary garden afternoon.
