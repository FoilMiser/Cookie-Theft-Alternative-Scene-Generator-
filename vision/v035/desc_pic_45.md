# Picture 45

## Free description
A busy, sunny park with green hills behind, the sun at the top right, two clouds and a blue bird flying in the sky. On the left is a pond with bulrushes, where a white duck is swimming quickly (motion lines behind it) toward pieces of bread floating on the water; a small girl in a pink dress standing at the edge of the pond is holding a little brown bag, apparently feeding it. At the far left, a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is sitting up on its back legs holding an open newspaper and seemingly reading it, which is impossible. Next to the flower bed, an older man with grey hair, a blue flat cap, brown waistcoat and white shirt is watering the flowers by pouring water out of a black frying pan instead of a watering can, which is odd. In the front centre, a girl with braided hair, a yellow top and orange shorts is holding an empty ice-cream cone and clutching her head in dismay, because her pink ice cream has fallen on the ground; a brown dog with a red collar is licking up the splat. On the right, an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, is sweeping leaves on the path, and a white goat is grazing on the grass behind her. At the far right, a boy in a red cap and blue jacket is pulling on a kite string, but his purple kite is stuck in the branches of a big tree. There is also an empty bench, a tall lamp post and a green litter bin.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The dog reading a newspaper is impossible in real life (deliberately silly or a mistake).
- Watering flowers with a frying pan is very odd - it could be read as the man confusing a pan for a watering can.
- The girl by the pond may be throwing bread from the bag, or just holding it; the crumbs suggest she has been feeding the duck.
- The brown dog may have caused the ice cream to fall, or just be eating it after she dropped it - not clear which.
- The goat being loose in a public park is a little unusual.
