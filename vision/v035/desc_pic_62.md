# pic_62

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a blue sky, two white clouds and a sun in the top-right corner. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door, with a tree behind it, a bush, and a colourful flower bed in front. A wooden fence runs across the back with a wooden gate on the right and a paved path going down the right side. In the middle of the back, a woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers is painting a section of the fence blue with a roller on a long handle. A paint tin with blue paint is on the grass by her feet. In the front centre, a garden hose runs in from the left edge to a sprinkler. The sprinkler sprays arcs of water to the right straight onto a closed cardboard box sitting on the lawn, which is getting soaked and has a puddle forming under it. On the path at the right, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt is sweeping dirt toward a red dustpan. She is using a giant silver dinner fork as if it were a broom, which is odd. A little puff of dust rises from the dirt. Two potted plants stand on the grass near the fence on the right.
