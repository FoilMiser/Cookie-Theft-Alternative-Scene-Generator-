# Picture 06

## Free description

This is a sunny playground with a metal railing fence and hedges at the back, a tree on the left and a slide on the right. On the left, a red seesaw is tipped steeply. A small girl in a pink dress is stuck up high on the raised end, and she looks surprised or worried, with her mouth open. On the low end sits an older man in a grey cap, brown vest and white shirt, calmly reading an open book. He is not paying attention to the girl, and his weight is keeping her stuck up in the air. In the middle front, a child with braided hair, a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands licking a pink ice-cream cone with their eyes closed. On the right there is a sandbox with a red bucket and a blue spade, a grey rock, a green bin, a red slide with a ladder and a green bush. There are no swings in this playground.
