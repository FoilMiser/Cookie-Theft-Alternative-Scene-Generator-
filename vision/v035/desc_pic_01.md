# Picture 01

## Free description

It is a sunny day in a back garden. The garden has a wooden fence, a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right, a path on the left, and a swing set in the middle. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is raking a small pile of leaves on the path by the gate, and a brown-and-white puppy is asleep on the path below him. An older woman with grey hair and glasses stands near the swing set with her hands on her hips, and she is wearing a white bucket upside down on her head like a hat. A small girl in a pink dress at the back is pulling on a kite string, but her purple kite is caught in the tree behind the fence. A woman in a yellow dress is holding a garden hose out in front of her and looking at the nozzle, which only has a single drop coming out. A white dog with brown spots is standing on or right beside the hose, so the dog may be blocking the water. On the right, a man in a red shirt sits on a bench reading a red book, and a blue bird on the bench next to him is right beside his sandwich, as if about to take it. An orange cat is walking along the top of the fence. In the front there is a wheelbarrow whose wheel looks square instead of round.
