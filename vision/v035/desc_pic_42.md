# Picture 42

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with red roof and blue door on the left, a flower bed in front of it, a wooden fence across the back and a gate with a paved path on the right. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and black trousers is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; a section of the fence to his left is already blue. Behind him on the grass are a grey paint tin with blue paint and a paint tray of blue paint. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has clearly stepped in the paint tray: a trail of blue paw prints leads from the tray to the dog, whose paws are blue, and it is walking off to the right across the lawn. Neither the boy nor anyone else seems to have noticed the dog. On the right, a man with dark skin in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers is sweeping a pile of autumn leaves on the path with a yellow broom. There are two potted plants and a birdhouse on a tall pole near the fence, and an empty wheelbarrow and a teal watering can at the front. It looks like the dog is about to spread blue paint all over the garden.
