## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with no people or animals in it. A tall wooden picket fence runs across the back, with a wooden gate at the far left, and a paved path runs from that gate down to the front-left corner. On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door, with a tree and a bush behind and beside it. In front of the house is a round flower bed with mixed colourful flowers, and in front of that, at the bottom right, is an empty wooden bench. Two potted plants stand on the grass near the fence, left of centre, and a teal watering can sits on the grass near the path at the front. The odd thing is a closed cardboard box hanging tilted in the sky above the fence, near the centre. Nothing is holding it, nobody is there who could have thrown it, and it looks like it is floating or flying in mid-air.
