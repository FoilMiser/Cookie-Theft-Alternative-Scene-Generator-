# pic_49

## Free description
This is a sunny park playground with a railing fence along the back. At the front left, a child in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts holds a red and a yellow balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other hand towards a blue balloon that is drifting away; the child looks upset. Behind them, a tree has a blue bird sitting on one of its branches. An old man with grey hair, a flat cap, a white shirt and a brown waistcoat sits on a bench drinking from a red cup through a straw, with his eyes closed. In the middle there is a swing set with one empty red swing, and a green bin next to it. On the right is a red-and-yellow slide with a ladder, and beside it a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is flying an orange kite with a bow tail high in the sky near the sun. At the front centre, an orange cat is crouched low and creeping towards a red bird on the grass, as if about to pounce on it. At the front right there is an empty sandpit and a grey rock.
