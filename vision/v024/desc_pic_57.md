# Picture 57

## Free description
A sunny town street or pedestrian square with a row of four colourful shops behind a paved area. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands in the centre foreground, pouring or scattering seeds from a paper bag, and a trail of seeds falls to the ground where a small blue bird is pecking at them. On the left, a small girl in a pink dress points up at a large blue ball with a white stripe that is stuck in the branches of a tree. On the right of centre, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a red and a yellow balloon on strings in one hand, and reaches up with the other hand towards a purple balloon that has got away and is floating up by the shop awning; he looks upset or worried. A red bird is perched on top of a street lamp. Far right, there is a fruit and vegetable stall with crates of produce under a striped awning, and nobody is minding it. The ball stuck in the tree and the escaping balloon are the two little mishaps in the scene. Nothing looks impossible, though the ball is rather large for that branch.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The purple balloon's string ends in mid-air just above the boy's raised hand. It could be read as a balloon that has just slipped away, or as one he is still almost holding. His face and raised arm suggest it has escaped.
- The girl's raised hand could be pointing at the ball or simply waving; the direction fits pointing at the ball.
- The blue ball sits on a branch that is drawn as a separate stick lying across the canopy, with small leaf clumps around it. The ball looks large and a bit pasted on, though it reads as stuck in the tree.
- The red bird's position overlaps the window of the green building behind it, so it can at first look like it is at the window rather than on the lamp.
- The stall has no seller, which may just be how the scene is, not something wrong.
- The tan shop has a teal door and window trim but no visible striped awning, unlike the others; the tree hides part of the red awning on the cream shop.
