# Picture 45

## Free description

This is a sunny shopping street paved with grey slabs, with a row of colourful shops with striped awnings at the back. In the foreground, a small child in a yellow top and orange shorts has dropped an ice cream: the child holds an empty cone in one hand and rubs an eye with the other, as if crying. The pink ice cream with sprinkles lies splatted on the ground, and a brown dog with a red neckerchief has its head down over it with its tongue out, about to lick it up. In the middle, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers has both arms in the air and his mouth open, looking excited or surprised; his shadow is below him and separate from his feet, so he seems to be jumping. There is a small yellow object in the air by his raised hand. On the left, a bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers walks to the right with a long yellow bag hanging from his hand, passing a fruit stall with crates of green, yellow, orange and red fruit. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes pushes a teal-and-navy pram with a sleeping baby tucked under a pink spotted blanket. A tree in a round planter, a green bin, a lamp post, a cardboard box and a potted plant fill out the street.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The boy's raised arms could mean he is celebrating, is surprised, is tossing the little yellow object up, or is trying to catch something. The small yellow object is too tiny to identify: it could be a coin, a ball, or even a detail of the shop behind him.
- His oval shadow is well below his feet with a gap, so he seems to be jumping, though it could also look as if he is floating a bit.
- The man's yellow item is very long and thin for a shopping bag; it could also be a large tag, a folded paper or a baguette bag.
- The child's gesture could be crying and rubbing an eye, or just scratching their head; the dropped ice cream makes crying the more likely reading.
- The fruit stall's far support is hidden behind the man, and the man's head covers some of the fruit, so the stall can look as if it is partly unsupported.
- The boy's face looks slightly awkward, with his features pushed to one side of his head under the cap.
