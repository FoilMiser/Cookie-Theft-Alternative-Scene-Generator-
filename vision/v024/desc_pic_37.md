# Picture 37

## Free description
This is a sunny park with a large pond, green hills, a tall lamp post, a winding path and several trees. In the middle, an older man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers stands at the right-hand end of the pond, throwing bits of food to a white duck. The duck swims on the pond with white lines behind it showing it is moving, and it is heading towards the small orange-brown pieces that are scattered around it on the water, with one or two pieces still in the air and some near the bulrushes. He is holding what looks like a white paper bag or cup in one hand and reaching out with the other. On the right a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is walking and pushing a teal pram or pushchair along the path, with a sleeping baby tucked in under a pink polka-dot blanket. A wooden ladder stands upright near the path with nothing to lean against, which looks odd. A bed of colourful flowers and a green bin are at the front left, and a grey rock is at the front centre. Nothing seems about to go wrong; it is a calm scene, apart from the unexplained ladder.
