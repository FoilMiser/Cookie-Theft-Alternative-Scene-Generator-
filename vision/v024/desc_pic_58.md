# Picture 58

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence along the back, a cream house with a red roof on the right, and a path running from a gate at the left towards the front. A woman with a long dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence blue with a paint roller; a section of fence is already blue and the rest is still brown. On the ground near her are a paint tin and a paint tray with blue paint. A white spotted dog with a blue collar is walking along the path to the left, and it has blue paint on its paws; a trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray to the dog, so it has clearly walked through the tray. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress sits on a wooden bench drinking from a red cup with a straw, near a flower bed of mixed flowers. Oddly, there is an orange fish in the tree at the back right, which is impossible and looks out of place. A teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow are on the grass in the foreground, not being used. The painter does not seem to have noticed the dog.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The paw prints are spaced in pairs and stop short of the dog's feet by a small gap, but they clearly read as the dog's trail from the tray.
- The fish could be an intentional absurdity or a decoration hanging in the tree; there is nothing holding it, it just sits drawn over the leaves. I read it as something that should not be there.
- The roller head is up near the top of the fence but hardly seems to be touching it, and it sits at the top right edge of the blue area; she could be starting a stroke or just finishing one.
- The dog's face is turned so it could be looking back towards the viewer or sniffing to the left.
- The seated woman's dress and legs look a bit awkward, with long thin legs and the skirt bunched up on the bench, but nothing is really wrong.
- The dog's shadow is a big oval that extends past the path edge, slightly bigger than expected.
