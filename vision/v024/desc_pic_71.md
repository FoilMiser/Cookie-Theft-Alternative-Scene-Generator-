## Free description
This is a peaceful, sunny day in a park with green hills, a pond on the left, a winding path on the right, and a tall black street lamp in the middle. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is walking to the right, pushing a teal and dark blue pram in which a baby lies asleep under a pink spotted blanket. At the front, a child with dark curly or braided hair, a yellow top and orange shorts is running or striding to the left, holding a yellow balloon up on a string. On the right, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers stands on the path, leaning forward and scattering seeds or crumbs from her hand, which fall in a little arc down to two birds, a red one and a blue one, pecking at them on the grass. She seems to be holding a small bag or packet of feed in her other hand. An empty bench, a grey rock, a flower bed and a green bin are dotted around the scene. Nothing looks dangerous or impossible; everyone seems to be calmly busy with their own activity.
