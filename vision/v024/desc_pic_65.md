# Picture 65

## Free description
This is a sunny, windy back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left and a tall wooden fence with a gate across the back. A strong wind seems to be blowing, shown by white streak lines in the sky and near the fence, and the washing on the line (blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes) is blown out sideways at a slant. In the middle-right of the garden a man with a beard, in a green shirt and khaki trousers, stretches both arms straight up towards an orange cap that is flying through the air just above his hands, along with a couple of leaves; it looks as if the wind has just taken his cap and he is trying to catch it. In the front left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands at a flower bed holding a dark frying pan, tipping it so that water pours out onto the flowers. Using a frying pan instead of a watering can looks wrong or silly. On the paved path on the right, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt is sweeping with a broom. An empty wheelbarrow sits on the lawn at the front and a bench stands empty at the front left. The boy and the woman do not seem to be paying attention to the man or his cap.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The dark round thing the boy holds reads best as a frying pan, but it could be a shallow bowl or wok. There is no watering can anywhere in the picture.
- The cap could have been blown off the man's head, or the man could have tossed it and be about to catch it. The wind makes the first reading more likely. It could also be someone else's cap.
- The wind affects the washing, cap and leaves, but the tree, flowers, broom, the woman's skirt and the boy's clothes do not move or bend at all.
- The wind direction is slightly hard to read. The washing slants down and to the left, as if the wind comes from the right.
- The man's upturned face looks a little odd, with the beard and hair making patchy dark shapes. There is also a short thin dark line between his head and the leaves that could be a leaf stem.
- The water drops land at the very front edge of the bed, so the water seems to miss most of the flowers.
- The soft shadow under the woman looks like a large oval spreading over the path. The wheelbarrow's shadow is also a large green oval.
