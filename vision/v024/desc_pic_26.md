# Picture 26

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left and a wooden picket fence running across the back. A wooden gate is at the right end of the fence, with a paved path running from it down to the bottom of the picture.

There is only one person: a girl or young woman with dark hair in a ponytail, wearing a purple T-shirt and teal trousers, in the front centre of the picture. She is reading a red book, holding it open in front of her and looking down at it.

The odd thing is that she is posed as if sitting, with her knees bent and her legs coming down at the front, but there is nothing under her at all: no chair, bench or stool. She seems to be sitting on thin air, or on an invisible chair, with her shoes resting on the grass.

Right next to her is an empty grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles, and further right a teal watering can. Two potted plants stand by the fence, and a flowerbed with colourful flowers is by the house. No animals are visible, and nothing appears to be about to happen.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Nothing supports the seated girl. This could be read two ways: as something deliberately wrong (sitting on thin air), or as a drawing mistake where a chair or bench has been left out. There is a faint shadow on the grass only near her feet, and none underneath a seat.
- A second reading is that she is perched on the wheelbarrow, since it is close by, but she is not touching it and it is clearly to one side of her.
- The grey wheelbarrow has a shadow on the grass around it that is rather large, and it appears to be separate from anything else.
