# Picture 64

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof on the right, a wooden fence along the back and a paved path at the left leading to a gate. In the centre, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers stands holding the nozzle end of a green hose with both hands and looking into it; only a drop comes out and no water is flowing. The hose runs along the ground, and to the right a white goat is standing on the hose with its back hoof, which is pinching it: the hose bulges up behind the goat, on the reel side, so the goat is blocking the water. The woman does not seem to realise what is wrong, and if the goat steps off, the water might suddenly spray her in the face. At the left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is kicking an orange ball with a white stripe to the left, on the path. At the back left, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is painting the fence blue with a roller, with a paint tin next to him. The goat in a garden is a little unexpected, but it looks calm.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The bulge in the hose could be read as a hose swollen with water behind a blockage (most likely) or as a kink or fat fitting, or even a snake-like shape on the ground.
- It is not perfectly clear whether the goat's hoof is actually on the hose or just next to it, but both hooves meet the hose line at the ground and the bulge starts right there.
- The woman could be looking at the nozzle in puzzlement or she could be drinking from it; her hands and the hose end are very close to her face.
- The hose rises straight up to the nozzle, stiffly standing at the woman's hands as if it were a pole, which looks a little odd for a floppy hose with no water flowing.
- The man's face is hidden, as he has his back to the viewer; his head is near the top of the fence and he looks a little floaty because of his long legs.
- The ball is at the boy's foot, so it could be just about to be kicked or just kicked. It sits right where his foot ends and seems to touch the shoe.
- The goat's head has a pale shape and a slightly odd pose. Its legs look stiff and its body is rather boxy and square.
