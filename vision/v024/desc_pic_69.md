# Picture 69

## Free description
This is a sunny, quiet town street seen from a paved square, with a row of four colourful two-storey shop buildings (pink, pale green, peach and cream) with striped awnings across the back. In the middle, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is using a teal watering can to water a box of flowers on the pavement; water sprays and drips from the long spout onto the flowers. On the right, a woman with dark hair in a yellow dress and red shoes stands holding a camera up to her face, taking a photo towards the left of the scene. On the left, a market stall with a striped canopy shows crates of green, yellow, orange and red fruit, with no one serving or buying. There are also a closed cardboard box beside the stall, a potted plant and a dark bin at the front, and a tall black street lamp left of centre. At the front, a red bicycle is parked against a grey metal bike rack, and a tree in a round planter stands on the right. Nothing looks dangerous or impossible; both women are just getting on with what they are doing and neither seems to interact with the other.
