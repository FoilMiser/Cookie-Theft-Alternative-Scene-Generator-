# Picture 53

## Free description
This is a sunny park with a round blue pond at the left, rolling green hills and trees in the distance, and a winding path at the right. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands at the pond's edge in the middle of the picture, seen from the side and behind, holding a fishing rod out over the water. The line drops from the tip of the rod to a red-and-white float, with a small ripple ring around it. A white duck with an orange bill is sitting on the water at the left of the pond, not far from the float and heading away from or looking past it. The float is between the boy and the duck, so nothing has been caught, but the duck is close enough that it could become tangled up. Around the scene are ordinary park things: a tall black lamp post, two green bins, an empty wooden bench by the path, a grey rock, a flower bed, some bushes and two big trees. Nothing looks dangerous and the scene seems calm. The boy appears to be simply waiting for a bite.

- The boy's face is turned away, showing mostly his cheek and the back of his head, so his expression can't be made out. It could be read as him looking at the float or at the duck.
- The duck might be about to swim towards the float or be swimming away. It faces away from the float, so it does not look interested in it.
- The rod's tip and the line form a sharp upside-down V, and the line curves a little oddly rather than hanging straight down, which could read as a slight breeze or a tug.
- The bulrushes at the right are partly standing in the water and partly on the bank, so they could be growing in the pond or next to it.
- There are two green bins, one near the lamp post and one in the corner, which seems like more than the scene needs, but they are not strange as such.
