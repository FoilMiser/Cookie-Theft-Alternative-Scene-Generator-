# Picture 06

## Free description
This is a sunny playground with a dark rail fence, bushes behind it, a red-framed slide on the left and a big tree on the right. A small girl in a pink dress is sliding down the slide with both arms raised, apparently enjoying herself. In the middle, a boy in a backwards red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands with one arm stretched out in front of him, having just thrown a ball. A dashed curved line traces the ball's path from his hand up over a small arc and down to a red ball with a white stripe, which is in the air to the right of him. To the right, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands facing the boy with both arms out in front, ready to catch it. The ball looks about to reach the child. An empty bench, a bin, a bush and a rock are in the background and foreground. Nothing looks wrong or impossible here; it looks like an ordinary game of catch.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The ball is well above the height of the child's hands, so it could be read as about to drop into the hands, or as possibly going over the child's head.
- The boy's pose could be read as just having thrown the ball, or as pointing at it. The dashed line makes throwing the more likely reading.
- It is unclear whether the girl on the slide has anything to do with the game. She seems separate from it.
- Slight drawing oddity: the boy's raised throwing hand and fingers are very thin and pointed. Otherwise I see nothing floating unintentionally or passing through anything, as the ball is clearly meant to be in mid-air.
