# Picture 25

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right and a tall wooden picket fence running across the back. At the far left of the fence is a wooden gate, with a paved path coming down from it to the bottom-left corner.

An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt stands at the fence holding a long brush up against it. About three planks to her left are already bright blue while the rest of the fence is still bare wood, and a bucket of blue paint is on the grass by her feet, so she is painting the fence and has only just started.

An orange cat is walking along the very top of the fence, to the right of the woman. At the front centre, a white dog with brown spots has its front end down and its rear up, tail wagging (small motion lines), with its nose right at a yellow-green tennis ball, as if it wants to play. At the bottom right, a girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench holding something pink to her mouth, which looks like an ice cream. A brown dog with a red neckerchief sits up on its haunches in front of the bench with its front paws raised, facing the girl, as if begging for her ice cream.

Nothing looks impossible. The most likely thing about to happen is the brown dog getting (or being refused) a bite of the ice cream.

## Inventory

People and animals:
- Centre-left, at the back by the fence: elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, teal top, grey skirt, brown shoes. Standing and holding a long-handled brush against the fence, painting it blue.
- Centre, on top of the fence at the back: orange cat. Walking along the top of the fence, head to the left, tail up.
- Front centre: white dog with brown spots and a blue collar. Play-bow pose, tail wagging, nose at the tennis ball.
- Front right, in front of the bench: brown dog with long darker ears and a red neckerchief. Sitting up on its haunches with front paws raised, facing right towards the girl.
- Front right, on the bench: girl with dark hair in a pink dress and yellow shoes. Sitting with feet off the ground, holding a pink thing to her mouth, apparently eating an ice cream.

Objects:
- Blue section of fence, left of the woman: about three planks and the rails between them, freshly painted.
- Paint bucket, on the grass by the woman's feet: grey bucket with blue paint inside and a drip on the side.

- Paved path, front left: leads from the gate to the bottom edge of the picture.

- Yellow-green tennis ball, front centre: on the ground at the spotted dog's nose.

- Sun at top left with orange rays, and two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The pink thing the girl holds: most likely an ice cream, but it is small and her hand and face overlap it, so it could be some other snack, or she could be smelling something like a pink flower.
- The brown dog could be begging from the girl, or just sitting up. The direction of its head suggests begging. Its paws reach close to the bench, and it overlaps the bench leg and the hose reel, which makes this corner a little crowded.
- The dark teal round object beside the flowerbed reads as a hose reel, but it could also be read as a wheel or some sort of machine, since it is partly hidden.
- At the brush tip there is a small pale oval outline on the plank, which could be the brush head or a blob of paint; it does not look very much like a brush.
- The third blue plank has a flat top with bare wood showing above it, unlike the other two, whose pointed tops are painted. It looks like the plank is only partly done, or a slightly odd overlay.
- The woman stands to the right of the painted area and only just reaches it with her brush, with the bucket on her other side, so her position is slightly awkward.
