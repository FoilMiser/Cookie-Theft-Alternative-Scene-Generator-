# Picture 59

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof on the left, a wooden fence along the back and a paved path at the right leading to a gate. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands in the middle-left, holding a green hose with a yellow nozzle and spraying an arc of water on to a flower bed. The hose runs from a reel on the ground at the left. A small girl in a pink dress sits on a red swing on a dark metal swing frame on the right, smiling, with small motion lines suggesting she is swinging. At the far right on the path, an older man with a flat cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers is using something to sweep up a pile of dust and debris into a red dustpan. The odd thing is that what he is holding is a giant silver fork instead of a broom, which is very strange; a broom would be expected there. A grey wheelbarrow stands on the grass in the front centre, unused. A birdhouse on a tall post stands at the right near the gate.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fork: it is drawn as a very large table fork, as tall as a broom, which looks impossible as a real tool. It could possibly be read as a garden fork or rake, but the shape and silver colour look like a dinner fork.
- The man's hands and the fork handle are drawn in a slightly confusing way, with the top of the handle bulging near his chest (the wide end looks like a spoon-shaped grip), so it is unclear how he is holding it.
- The dustpan's handle and the debris pile look a bit disconnected; the debris pile sits just right of the pan's mouth rather than inside it.
- The dustpan could also be read as a red hand-shovel or just a red wedge shape.
- The swing's ropes come down in front of the fence and the girl faces straight out, while the motion lines to the right suggest sideways movement; she might be moving, or the lines may just be decoration.
- The hose passes across the front of the flower bed. Part of it looks as if it runs through or right along the edge of the soil, but this seems fine.
