# Picture 47

## Free description

This is a busy back garden on a sunny day, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. A purple kite with little bows on its tail is stuck in the leaves of a tree behind the fence, and a boy in a red cap, blue top and dark trousers is standing with his arm up, holding the kite string that runs up to it, trying to tug it free. On top of the fence, a black cat with white paws walks to the right, towards a red bird perched further along the fence, so the cat might be stalking it. In the middle-left, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is spraying water from a hose nozzle onto a round flower bed; the teal hose runs back along the ground to a hose reel. At bottom left, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts sits on a wooden bench eating a pink ice cream in a cone, while a white dog with brown patches and a blue collar sits up on its hind legs beside the bench, begging for it. On the right, an elderly woman with a grey bun, teal cardigan and grey skirt walks to the left with her eyes on an open book. A red-and-white ball lies on the grass right in her path, so she looks about to trip over it because she is not looking where she is going.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The kite overlaps the leaves, and clumps of leaves sit in front of it, so it clearly looks stuck, but it could also be read as flying just in front of the tree. The boy could be trying to free it or could still be flying it.
- The string passes in front of the fence and the sagging curve means the kite is not pulling tight, which fits the stuck reading.
- The cat could be stalking the bird, or just walking along the fence. The bird does not seem to have noticed it, or else it is watching the cat, since it faces towards it.
- The hose rises in a stiff big arch from the ground to the woman's hand, and the upper part passes behind her arm; it looks rather rigid and not quite like a hose lying naturally.
- The elderly woman's face is hard to see because her head is turned away and mostly shows her hair, so her glasses or expression cannot be made out.
- It is not clear who the ball belongs to, or whether she will actually step on it, as it is a little to the left of her front foot.
- The dog is begging with its front paws up, but it is hard to say if it is about to snatch the ice cream.
