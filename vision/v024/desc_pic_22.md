# Picture 22

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof on the right, and a wooden fence and gate across the back. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands on the left of the lawn holding the nozzle of a garden hose up in front of her face and looking into it; only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose runs along the ground from a reel on the right, by the flower bed, and a white goat is standing with its hoof on the hose in the middle, so a bulge has built up in the hose just by its foot. It looks as if water is blocked by the goat and that when the goat moves or the pressure builds up, the water will spray into the woman's face. In the back, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is hanging washing on a line, pegging up a white T-shirt, with blue jeans already hanging there. The odd thing is a cardboard box floating in the air in the upper left of the sky, tilted, with nothing holding it up, which looks impossible. The goat has something white hanging from its mouth, perhaps a beard or something it is chewing.
