# Picture 32

## Free description

This is a sunny playground on grass with a dark metal railing along the back and a line of bushes beyond it. There is only one person in it: a small girl with dark hair in a pink dress and yellow shoes, at the front left of the picture.

She is kneeling or crouching at the right-hand side of a wooden sandpit, reaching forward with her hands on a sandcastle she has built or is building. The castle has a square base, a tall tower and a smaller tower, with a little red flag on top.

Behind her on the left is a tall slide with a red frame, a dark grey ladder on its left and an orange and yellow chute coming down to the right, with nobody on it. A green bin stands near the railing, a grey rock is beside the sandpit, and there are two bushes. A large tree stands on the right, and the rest of the right side of the picture is just open grass.

Nothing looks impossible, and there are no animals or other children. She looks calmly absorbed in her game.
