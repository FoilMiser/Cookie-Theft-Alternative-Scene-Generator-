# Picture 33

## Free description
This is a back garden on a grey, overcast day, with three very dark storm clouds in the sky at top centre. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door stands on the right, a tall wooden fence runs across the back with a gate on the left, and a pale paving path comes down from that gate at the bottom left. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands near the fence just right of the swing set, looking up and pointing at the dark clouds with a worried or surprised expression. An older man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands at the right using a garden hose to water a bed of colourful flowers, with his back half-turned to the woman and apparently paying no attention to the sky. In the front centre a boy in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is drinking through a straw from a red and white cup; his pose is that of someone sitting down with his knees bent, but there is no chair, bench or swing under him. The swing set on the left is empty, with its red seat hanging still. It seems about to rain, which makes watering the flowers look rather pointless, and the woman seems to be the only one who has noticed. The oddest thing is the boy sitting on nothing in mid-air.

## Unsure or ambiguous

As drawn, he looks like he is sitting on empty air, with his body tilted and his feet on the grass. His shadow is on the ground beneath and to the left of him, with nothing to explain it.
- The man's stream of water is drawn as a thick pale-blue curved band, which looks partly like a solid strip or a second hose as much as a jet of water. It seems to end among the flowers with a few drops.
- The woman's expression could be worry about the rain or simple surprise, or she could be pointing something out to the others; nobody responds.
- The woman's pointing finger reaches roughly to the top of the fence line, and she stands so close to the swing frame that her arm and body sit just beside the frame's right leg; this looks fine, but she is quite close.
- It is unclear whether the man is unaware of the coming storm or just carrying on regardless.
