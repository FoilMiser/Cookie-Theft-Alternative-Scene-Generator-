## Free description
A sunny high street with four coloured shop buildings with striped awnings, a fruit and vegetable stall on the left, and a paved pavement in front. In the front centre a small dark-skinned child in a yellow top and orange shorts is standing with one hand up to their face, seemingly crying, holding an empty ice cream cone in the other hand. On the ground at the child's feet is a fallen pink ice cream that has splatted into a pink puddle. A tan dog with a red collar is bending down right next to it, sniffing or licking the dropped ice cream. On the left, a woman in a yellow dress with red shoes is holding up a camera and taking a photo, pointing to the right. In the back centre, a woman with a dark ponytail in a purple top and teal trousers walks to the left carrying a yellow shopping bag, with her back partly to the scene. A red bird flies in the sky at top right. Nothing looks impossible, and the sad moment is the main event.
