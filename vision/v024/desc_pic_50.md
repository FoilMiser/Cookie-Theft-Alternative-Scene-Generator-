# Picture 50

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate at the far right, and a paved path running from the gate down to the bottom right. One person is present: a woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers standing at the back centre with her back partly to us, reaching up with both hands to peg a white T-shirt onto a washing line. A pair of blue jeans is already pegged on the line to her left. In the front of the picture a dark hose runs along the grass from the left edge, past and under a grey wheelbarrow, to a sprinkler in the lawn. The sprinkler is spraying three blue arcs of water, and the farthest arc comes down right on a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which has water running down it and a puddle around its base. So the odd thing is that a cardboard box has been left out and is getting soaked. The woman doesn't seem to have noticed, and if nobody moves it, the box will probably go soggy. Two potted plants stand on the grass near the fence, and a colourful flower bed is by the house.
