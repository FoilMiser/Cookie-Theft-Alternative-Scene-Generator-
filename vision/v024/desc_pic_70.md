# Picture 70

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny back garden with a cream house on the right and a tall wooden fence across the back, and several odd things are going on at once. In the middle, an older woman with grey hair in a bun is painting part of the fence bright blue with a long brush, with a paint tin and tray on the grass behind her. A white dog with brown spots has clearly walked through the paint: its feet are blue and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the tray across the lawn towards the path, without the woman noticing. At the front, a brown dog is sitting up like a person, holding open a newspaper in its paws and apparently reading it, which is impossible. In the tree on the right there is an orange goldfish, which is also impossible, since a fish should not be in a tree. On top of the fence, a black cat with white paws creeps along towards a small red bird perched a little further along, so the cat looks about to pounce on it. A little girl in a pink dress holds a yellow and a purple balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other towards a red balloon that has slipped away and is floating off. An orange cat is asleep on the path at the front left, a man with a beard sits on the bench at the front right reading a red book, and a blue bird flies across the sky.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The goldfish could be an actual fish impossibly in the tree, or a hanging toy or ornament. It is drawn flat over the leaves, as if stuck on, with nothing holding it.
- The brown dog may just be holding the paper up, but it looks like it is reading. Its pose is very human and its body shape looks a bit stretched and twisted, with white paws gripping the paper.
- The red balloon's string ends in mid-air, close to but not touching the girl's raised hand, so she may have just let it go or may be about to catch it.
- The black cat could be stalking the bird or just walking along the fence; its low head and body make stalking look more likely.
- The woman has her back to the paw prints, so she does not appear to know about them. The prints begin very close to the tray, but it is not clearly shown that the dog stepped in the tray rather than the tin or a spill.
- The man does not react to anything going on around him.
- The woman's brush seems to touch just the top-left corner of the blue section, with a blue blob at the tip; it looks slightly small for the job.
- The large grey oval shadows under the orange cat and the white dog are wider than the animals themselves.
