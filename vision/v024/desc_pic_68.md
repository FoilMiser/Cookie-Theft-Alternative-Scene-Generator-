# Picture 68

## Free description
This is a sunny playground with a dark metal railing fence along the back, a tall slide on the left, a sandpit at the front left and a red seesaw on the right. An older man with grey hair, a grey cap, a brown waistcoat with white sleeves and dark trousers is sitting on the low end of the seesaw, reading an open book. His weight holds his end down near the ground with his feet on the grass. At the other end, up in the air, sits a little girl with dark hair in a pink dress and yellow shoes, her legs dangling with small motion lines under her feet, as if she is swinging or kicking them. She is stuck up high because the man is not bouncing the seesaw, and he seems not to notice her, keeping his eyes on his book. It looks funny that a grown man is sitting on a seesaw to read. No one is using the slide or the sandpit, where a red bucket and blue spade have been left.
