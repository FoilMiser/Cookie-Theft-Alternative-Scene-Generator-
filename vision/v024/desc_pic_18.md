# Picture 18

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a long wooden fence with a gate running across the back. There are no people, only animals. On top of the fence a black-and-white cat is creeping low to the left, stalking a small blue bird perched on the fence just in front of it, so it looks as if the cat is about to pounce and the bird may be caught. Down on the lawn in the front centre a white goat with its head lowered is nibbling a small tuft of grass. At the right, in front of the blue door, a brown-and-white dog sits upright holding a red lead in its mouth, as if asking to be taken for a walk. There is also a flower bed, two potted plants, a teal watering can and a paved path on the left. Nothing looks impossible, although a goat in a home garden is a little unusual.

## Inventory
- Blue bird, centre, back, on top of the fence: small, blue with a yellow beak; perched and facing right, towards the cat.
- Cat, centre-right, back, on top of the fence: black with white paws/chest; crouched and creeping left towards the bird, tail stretched out behind.
- Goat, centre, front: white with small dark horns and dark hooves; head down, eating a tuft of grass or a small plant.
- Dog, right, in front of the blue door: tan/brown with white chest and paws, floppy ears; sitting upright facing the viewer with a red lead in its mouth that trails to the ground.
- Red lead, right: held in the dog's mouth, loops on the grass near its feet.
- Wooden fence and gate, across the back: fence with pointed slats; gate at far left.
- House, right: cream walls, red roof, one window, blue door with a grey step.
- Flower bed, centre-right: brown soil with colourful flowers.
- Two terracotta potted plants, left of centre near the fence.
- Teal watering can, front left, standing on the grass.
- Paved path, front left, leading from the gate to the front edge.
- Bushes along the fence; tree behind the fence near the house; sun top left, two clouds at top.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The goat's head is seen from a strange angle and its head markings are hard to read; its muzzle overlaps with the grass tuft. It could also be read as a white dog or lamb, but the horns suggest a goat.
- The dog is drawn standing/sitting very upright with its body narrow, so it could be read as sitting or standing on its hind legs. It is not clear whether it is standing on the door step or just in front of it.
- The cat could be read as simply walking along the fence or as actively stalking the bird; its low crouch suggests stalking.
- The bird faces the cat and does not seem to have noticed the danger, or perhaps it has.
