# Picture 28

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left and a wooden picket fence across the back. There is a wooden gate at the right end of the fence, with a paved path running down from it to the bottom of the picture.

Three people are in the garden. At the front, left of centre, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, is walking to the right carrying a cardboard box in both arms.

To the right of her, a younger woman with a dark ponytail, in a purple T-shirt and teal trousers, is standing and facing left, with one arm raised and her hand waving (little motion marks by the hand). She seems to be waving at the woman with the box, who is coming towards her, or greeting someone beyond her.

At the back, by the fence, an old man in a blue-grey cap, brown waistcoat, white sleeves and dark trousers is standing holding a long rake, with a small pile of leaves at his feet, so he is raking leaves. Nothing looks wrong or impossible, and no animals are visible. It looks like an ordinary friendly moment, perhaps someone bringing a parcel.

- Who the younger woman is waving at: it could be the elderly woman walking towards her, or it could be the old man at the back. She is facing left and slightly towards the viewer, so the elderly woman seems more likely. The wave could mean hello or goodbye.
- Whether the elderly woman is bringing the box to the younger woman or taking it elsewhere: she is walking in the younger woman's direction but does not clearly look at her, and could be heading for the gate and path.
- The old man's face is partly hidden by the rake handle and the cap peak, and it is hard to tell which way he is looking.
- The rake handle passes in front of his body and his hands grip it awkwardly, with the leaves scattered around the rake head; it reads as raking but the rake head is small and hard to make out.
