# Picture 30

## Free description

This is a sunny playground on a grassy area with a dark metal railing along the back and a line of bushes beyond it. On the left is a large tree, and in front of it a red seesaw on a grey triangular support.

Two children are playing on the seesaw, facing each other. A boy in a yellow top and orange shorts sits on the left end, which is down, with his feet on the ground. A girl in a pink dress sits on the right end, which is up, with her yellow shoes dangling in the air. Both are holding the handles in front of them.

On the right, behind them, is a tall slide with a red frame, a dark grey ladder and an orange and yellow chute, with nobody on it. At the front right is a wooden sandpit with a red bucket and blue spade left in the sand and nobody playing in it, and next to it a grey rock. A green bin stands near the railing in the middle, and there are two bushes.

Nothing looks wrong. The seesaw will presumably tip the other way when the boy pushes off.
