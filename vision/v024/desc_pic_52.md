# Picture 52

## Free description
This is a sunny but windy back garden, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate at the back right and a paved path running down the right side. In the centre a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands on the lawn with both arms stretched above her head and her mouth open in surprise, looking up and to the right. A blue cap is flying through the air just above and to her right, and white wind lines and a few small leaves are blowing past her, so it looks as though the wind has just snatched her hat and she's trying to grab it. More wind lines appear in the sky at the right, and leaves are being blown off the big tree at the back left. On the left, in front of the door, a brown dog with a red collar sits with its eyes closed, holding a red lead in its mouth, as if waiting to be taken for a walk. The dog seems calm and is not reacting to the wind. A grey wheelbarrow is on the grass at the front centre, a teal watering can stands at the front right, and a colourful flower bed is between the dog and the woman. The hat will probably get away from her over the fence.

## Inventory
- Woman, centre: dark short hair, yellow dress, bare legs, red shoes, mouth open; arms raised up towards the flying cap, possibly just out of reach.
- Dog, left, in front of the blue door: brown and white, red collar, sitting upright with eyes closed; holding its red lead in its mouth, with the lead trailing down to the ground in a loop.
- Blue cap, centre right: in mid-air above the woman's hands, tilted, blown by the wind.
- Wind lines: three white wavy lines going across behind the woman to the cap; two more in the sky at the right; one at the tree.
- Small leaves: a few around the cap and a few blowing away from the tree at the back left.
- Wheelbarrow, front centre: grey tray, wooden handles pointing right, dark square-framed wheel at the left; empty and standing still.
- Watering can, front right: teal can with spout pointing left, on the grass by the path.
- Flower bed, left of centre: oval of dark soil with red, yellow, orange, purple and pink flowers.
- House, left: cream wall, red roof, blue door with grey step, one window.
- Bush and tree, back left: small bush by the house and a large round tree behind it.
- Gate, back right: brown wooden gate with diagonal brace and round knob.
- Paved path, right: pale stone path from the gate to the bottom edge.
- Sun, top right, and two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The woman could be reaching to catch the cap, or could have just thrown it up in the air herself or be throwing up her hands in surprise. Her open mouth and the wind lines suggest it was blown off.
- The cap might be hers or someone else's; no one else is around, and she has no hat on, so it is probably hers.
- The dog appears to be holding its own lead, which could mean "take me for a walk", or the lead could look like a long red tongue running down its chest. The red line starts at the mouth and runs down the body to a loop by the step, so it could be read either way at a glance.
- The sun is bright and the sky clear, which is a little at odds with the strong wind, but not impossible.
- The wheelbarrow wheel looks like a square dark frame with a lattice, not a round wheel, which is odd.
- The woman's face is drawn turned to the right while her body faces front, making her head look a little twisted.
- The tree shows no sign of bending in the wind, though leaves are flying from it.
