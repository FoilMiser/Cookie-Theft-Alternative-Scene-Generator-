# Picture 56

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate at the back right and a paved path running down the right side. In the front centre right an older woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal top, grey skirt and brown shoes stands with her hands on her hips, and she has a silver metal bucket upside down on her head like a hat. That is the odd thing, and she seems to be standing quite calmly as though nothing is wrong, though she could be cross. To the right on the path a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is leaping in the air with both arms up, laughing, and there is a shadow on the ground beneath him showing he has left the ground. He looks like he's cheering or jumping for joy, maybe at the woman's bucket. At the back left a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is digging a hole in the lawn with a spade, with a mound of earth beside it, leaning into the work and not paying attention to the others. A washing line behind her holds blue jeans and a yellow striped towel or top. A teal watering can stands on the grass at the front right, a flower bed is by the house, and an empty wooden bench is at the bottom left.
