# Picture 29

## Free description

This is a quiet sunny park with green hills and a line of bushes in the distance. A sandy path winds from the middle distance down to the bottom of the picture on the left side, past a big tree. A round blue pond with reeds at both ends is on the right, with a smaller tree behind it.

There is only one person: a young woman with long dark hair, in a purple T-shirt and teal trousers, sitting on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket at the front right, just in front of the pond. She is leaning back on one arm with her knees up, with a wicker picnic basket in front of her on the blanket. Her eyes look closed or squeezed in a smile and her cheeks are pink, so she looks happy and relaxed, or possibly is laughing.

Nothing much is happening and nothing seems about to happen. There are no animals or other people. A tall black street lamp and a green bin stand in the middle of the grass, another green bin is in the bottom-right corner, and there is a grey rock near the front. Nothing looks impossible.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Her expression: the arcs for eyes and pink cheeks could mean she is happily smiling with eyes closed, enjoying the sun, or laughing. Her face is small and the features are a little crowded, so it could also be read as squinting or grimacing.
- The basket overlaps her waist and hip, so it looks slightly as if it sits against or in front of her body rather than clearly next to her.
- Her legs and feet extend off the end of the blanket on to the grass, which is natural, but the far part of the blanket seems to go under the basket and her legs, making it hard to see how she sits on it.
- It is unclear whether she is waiting for someone or having a picnic alone; there is only one person and no food is out.
