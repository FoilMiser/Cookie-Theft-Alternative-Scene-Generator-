# Picture 31

## Free description

This is a sunny playground on grass with a dark metal railing along the back and a line of bushes beyond it. There is a large tree on the left and, in front of it, a red seesaw on a grey triangular support.

Two children are on the seesaw, facing each other. A girl in a pink dress sits on the left end, which is up, with her yellow shoes off the ground. A boy in a yellow top and orange shorts sits on the right end, which is down, with his feet close to or on the grass. Both are holding handles.

In the middle of the picture is a grey A-frame swing set with one red seat hanging from two ropes; it is empty and not moving. To the right is a slide with a red frame, a dark grey ladder and an orange and yellow chute, also empty. A green bin stands by the railing, a bush is on the far right and a grey rock is at the front right.

Nothing looks impossible. The children are simply seesawing and the rest of the playground is unused.
