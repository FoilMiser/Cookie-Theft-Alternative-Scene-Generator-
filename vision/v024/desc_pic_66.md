# Picture 66

## Free description
This is a sunny day in a park with green hills, a pond on the right, a winding path on the left, an empty bench and a tall street lamp in the middle. A boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands on the grass in front of the pond flying a yellow diamond-shaped kite high in the sky, holding the string up in one hand. At the front, a child with dark curly or braided hair, a yellow top and orange shorts holds an empty ice cream cone and has a hand up to the side of their head. The pink ice cream scoop has fallen and is lying in a melting pink puddle on the grass by the child's feet. A brown dog with a red collar or neckerchief is bending down right next to the fallen ice cream, sniffing it or about to lick it. On the path at the bottom left, a small white puppy with brown ears is curled up, apparently asleep with its eyes shut. At the upper left, a woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers walks along beside the top of the path carrying a cardboard box. Nobody else appears to have noticed the dropped ice cream.
