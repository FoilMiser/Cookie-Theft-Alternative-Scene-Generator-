# Picture 10

## Free description
This is a garden on a windy, sunny day, with a cream house with a red roof at the left and a wooden fence and gate at the back. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands in the centre-left with both arms raised, looking up, with her mouth open, reaching for an orange cap that is blowing through the air above her along with a couple of small leaves. White wind lines stream across the picture from the right toward the cap. An older man in a blue-grey flat cap and brown waistcoat walks to the right holding a red lead attached to a white dog with brown spots. The dog is walking toward the path on the right, and a red object on the ground, shaped like a dustpan or the head of a shovel, appears to be lying right beside its feet. Next to that object there is a small pile of dirt or rubble with puffs of dust rising. On the path at the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is holding a gigantic fork, as tall as his legs, and pushing its tines down at the ground, as if using it as a shovel or broom. The fork is far too big, so that looks like the odd or impossible part of the picture. A bench, a flower bed and two pots of plants are also in the garden.

- The boy could be digging up the dirt pile, or sweeping it toward the red dustpan. The dust and small scrape marks suggest he is scraping or digging.
- The orange cap's owner is unclear. The woman has no hat and reaches for it, so it may be hers, but it could belong to someone else. It also looks partly like an open shell or a slice of fruit because of the way it is angled.
