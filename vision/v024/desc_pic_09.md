A wooden picket fence runs across the back with a gate at the left, a pale paved path comes forward from the gate, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door stands on the right. A dark grey or black cat with white paws is walking along the top of the fence, heading right. A closed cardboard box is hanging in mid-air in the sky above the fence, tilted at an angle, with nothing holding it up. A tall wooden pole with a small birdhouse on top stands near the gate. A flower bed with mixed colourful flowers sits in front of the house. The odd things are the box floating in the air and a cardboard box left out under the sprinkler.
