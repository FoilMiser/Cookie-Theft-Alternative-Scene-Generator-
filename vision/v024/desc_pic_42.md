## Free description

This is a back garden on a sunny day, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left and a tall wooden fence along the back with a gate at the right. A woman with a long dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is kneeling on one knee on the lawn at the edge of a round flower bed full of red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers. She is leaning forward and working low down with her hands, next to a small terracotta pot holding a pink flower, so she appears to be planting or tending it. Behind her a washing line strung between two wooden posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow item with red stripes, both held by pegs. A grey wheelbarrow stands empty on the grass at the bottom of the picture and a teal watering can stands to its right. Two more pots of green plants sit by the fence, and a paved path runs from the gate down the right-hand side. She is the only person there and no animals are visible. Nothing looks wrong or impossible; it is a calm gardening scene.
