# Picture ctl_07

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills, a curving path, a blue pond with reeds, a lamp post and a big tree on the right. At the edge of the pond, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is throwing crumbs to a white duck, which is swimming towards a scatter of crumbs on the water. At the front left, a small girl in a pink dress holds a blue and a red balloon in one hand and reaches with the other towards a yellow balloon that has got away and is drifting over the pond, its string hanging free. A brown dog with a red collar or bandana sits on the grass holding an open newspaper with its paws as if reading it, which is odd and impossible. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers sits on a bench reading a red book, and a blue bird on the bench beside her is pecking at a sandwich without her noticing. An old man with a cap and brown waistcoat walks along the path carrying a yellow shopping bag, while an orange cat is curled up asleep on the path near his feet. On the far right, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts points up at a blue ball that is stuck on a branch in the big tree. In the back, a woman in a blue hoodie walks across the grass holding a pink ice cream cone. Flowers grow in a bed at the front left, with a bin in the corner and a rock in the middle.
