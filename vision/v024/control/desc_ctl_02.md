# Picture ctl_02

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a paved path and gate on the left. An old man in a cap and brown waistcoat is painting the fence blue with a brush; a few planks are already blue, and a paint can and paint tray sit on the grass behind him. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is walking away from the tray with blue paws, leaving a trail of blue paw prints across the lawn. An orange cat is creeping along the top of the fence towards a small blue bird perched there, so it looks as if the cat is about to pounce. A woman in a blue hoodie stands on the lawn looking up and holding a string, and it leads to a purple kite caught in the tree behind the fence. On the right, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans kneels at a flower bed, tending the flowers next to a small pot with a pink plant. An empty wheelbarrow stands in the foreground, and a red bird flies in the sky at top left. The odd things are the dog's blue paw prints, which the painter has not noticed, and the stuck kite.
