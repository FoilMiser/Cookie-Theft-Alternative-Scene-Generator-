# Picture ctl_04

## Free description
This is a busy, windy town square with four coloured shop buildings, a paved pavement, a lamp post, a tree in a planter and a market stall on a sunny day. On the left, a bearded man in a green shirt reaches up with both arms for his red cap, which has blown off and is flying away with some leaves along white wind lines. Next to him a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes walks left carrying a yellow shopping bag. A man in a red shirt sits on a bench reading a red book, while a blue bird on the bench next to him pecks at his sandwich without him noticing. In the front centre, a white dog with brown ears is sitting up holding an open newspaper in its paws as if reading it, which is impossible and odd. On the right, a little girl in a pink dress holds an empty ice cream cone and rubs her eye as if crying, because her ice cream has fallen on the ground, where a brown dog with a red collar is eating or licking it. A boy in a yellow top and orange shorts jumps nearby with both arms in the air. At far right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands with her hands on her hips and a metal bucket upside down on her head, which is also strange. A red bird flies at top left, and a teal bicycle is parked in a rack behind the bench.
