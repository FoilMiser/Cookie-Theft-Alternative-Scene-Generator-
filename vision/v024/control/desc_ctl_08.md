# Picture ctl_08

## Free description
This is a sunny playground with a dark railing fence, a big tree on the left, a swing set in the middle and a red-and-yellow slide on the right. A woman in a blue hoodie stands by the tree, pointing up with her mouth open at a blue ball stuck on a branch among the leaves. At the far left in front, an old man in a cap and brown waistcoat is drinking from a red cup with a straw, in a sitting pose but with no seat or bench under him, which looks strange or impossible. A woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench reading a red book, while a blue bird on the bench beside her pecks at a sandwich she has not noticed. Just behind the bench, an elderly woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt stands licking a pink ice cream. A small girl in a pink dress is jumping with both arms in the air, and her shadow is below her on the grass. A brown dog with a red collar or bandana sits up holding an open newspaper as if reading it, which is odd. On the right, beyond the slide, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts holds a red and a yellow balloon and reaches towards a purple balloon that is floating away. A white puppy sleeps curled up on the grass near a sandpit, which has a red bucket and blue spade in it but no one playing.
