# Picture 40

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden, with a bright sun at top left and two white clouds, a tall wooden fence across the back with a gate at far left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is walking to the left across the grass, carrying a cardboard box with a white label on it, as if delivering or moving something. At the back, an older man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers stands in front of the fence with one hand raised, waving, with little motion lines around the hand; he seems to be greeting the woman with the box or someone else. At the front left, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a green cardigan and grey skirt stands on the paved path sweeping with a broom. A wooden post with a little red-roofed birdhouse on top stands at the left near the fence. A teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow are left on the grass at the front, and a bed of colourful flowers sits in front of the house. Everything looks calm and friendly, and nothing seems about to go wrong.
