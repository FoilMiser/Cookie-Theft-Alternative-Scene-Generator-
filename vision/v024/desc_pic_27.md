# Picture 27

## Free description

This is a paved town square or shopping street in front of a row of colourful shop buildings with striped awnings, under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds. It looks as if it might rain, but no rain is falling yet.

On the left, a boy in a red cap and blue top is running to the left with speed lines behind him. In the front, left of centre, an old man in a blue-grey cap and brown waistcoat is walking to the right while reading an open book or paper, and he is not looking where he is going. A cardboard box is on the ground just ahead of him, so he looks about to trip over it.

In the middle, a bearded man in a green shirt sits on a bench reading a red book. Beside him on the bench is a big sandwich, and a blue bird is right next to it with its head at the food, apparently stealing or pecking at the sandwich while the man does not notice. Just to the right, a small girl in a pink dress is pointing with her arm stretched up and to the left, seemingly at the bird or at the man.

Behind her, near a tall street lamp, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts stands holding the string of a yellow balloon. On the right, a man in a red T-shirt and blue trousers walks to the left holding a red lead attached to a white dog with brown spots. Behind him is a market stall with a striped awning and crates of fruit or vegetables.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The small girl's pointing: it could be at the bird stealing the sandwich, or at the bearded man, or up at the sky or clouds. Her arm does not point exactly at the bird, but that is the most sensible reading.
- The boy on the left could be running away from something, running to catch something, or trying to get out of the coming rain. Nothing shows why he is running. He is also heading away from everything else.
- What the old man is holding could be a book, a newspaper or a magazine. The white sleeves of his arms lie across it in a stiff block, which looks a bit odd.
- Whether the old man will actually hit the box: it is slightly to the right of his next step, so he may trip or may just miss it.
- The bird overlaps the plate and the end of the bench, and the sandwich, bird and man's leg bunch up at the same spot, which makes it hard to see exactly what the bird is doing. It could be pecking at the sandwich or just standing next to it.
- The balloon string runs in front of the shopfront, and the balloon is close to the lamp post, so it can look at first as if it is tied to or caught on the post, but the string goes to the boy's hand.
- The dark cloud at the right sits partly behind the roof of the pale green building, which looks a little as if the cloud is resting on the roof.
- The man walking the dog has his face mostly turned away, so it is hard to see where he is looking.
