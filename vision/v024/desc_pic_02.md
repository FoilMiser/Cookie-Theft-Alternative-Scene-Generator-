# Picture 02

## Free description
This is a sunny park with a blue pond, rolling green hills, a winding sandy path, a tall black lamp post and two trees, with many separate small things going on. At the front left, a small girl in a pink dress holds the strings of a yellow and a purple balloon in one hand and reaches up with a worried face towards a red balloon that has got away and is drifting over the pond with its string trailing free. In the front middle, an old man in a cap and brown waistcoat walks to the right carrying something yellow, not paying attention to anything around him. Right beside him, a brown dog with a red neckerchief sits on the grass holding an open newspaper in its front paws as if reading it, which is impossible in real life. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands at the edge of the pond throwing crumbs from a small cup or carton towards a white duck, which is swimming towards the crumbs with wake lines behind it. On a bench at centre right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers reads a red book and does not notice a blue bird with its head down in the big sandwich next to her, apparently stealing it. Further back, a boy in a backwards red cap and blue hoodie walks near the path, about to lick a pink ice-cream cone. At the far right, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands pointing up, looking unhappy, at a blue ball stuck on a branch high in the tree. An orange cat lies asleep on the grass at the front, near the middle of the picture. A colourful flower bed sits next to the girl, and reeds grow at both ends of the pond.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The yellow item the old man carries is very narrow. It could be a shopping bag seen edge-on, a folded newspaper, or a large tag.
- The sandwich overlaps the woman's knee area, so it could be on the bench beside her or resting partly on her lap.
- The small object in the yellow-dress woman's hand could be a cup, a carton or a paper bag.
- The red balloon's string ends near the girl's raised hand with a gap, so she has either just let it go or is just failing to reach it. It is drawn in the air but overlaps the pond, so it looks at first as if it might be standing on the water's edge.
- Things that look strange: the dog reading a newspaper is plainly impossible, and its paws look like white gloved hands, which is odd. The blue bird is about as big as the sandwich and oversized for a bird of that kind.
- The cat's curled pose is a little hard to read. It could be asleep on its side or lying partly upside down.
- The boy's feet are at the very edge of the path, so it is unclear whether he is on the path or on the grass.
