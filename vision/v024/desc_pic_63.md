# Picture 63

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof on the left, a wooden fence, a swing frame and a paved path on the right. An older woman with grey hair in a bun, a teal top and a grey skirt stands at the fence, painting it blue with a roller; a section to the left of her is already blue, and a paint tin is on the grass beside her. In the front centre, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is running forward and kicking an orange ball with a white stripe, which is just leaving his foot and is a little way off the ground. He is kicking towards the right of the picture, towards the swing frame and the path. At the far left, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts sits on a wooden bench, drinking from a red cup with a straw. The swing is empty and hanging still. Nothing is about to hit anyone directly, though the ball is moving fast and may go on towards the swing. Nothing looks impossible here.
