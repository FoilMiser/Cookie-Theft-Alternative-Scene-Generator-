# Picture 17

## Free description
A sunny day in a park, with a sun at top left, two white clouds, and green rolling hills and a tree line behind. A woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers stands in the middle of the picture at the left edge of a blue pond. She is holding a small white paper bag or cup in one hand and tossing little orange-brown pellets or crumbs towards the water with the other. A white duck with an orange bill swims in the pond close to her, facing left towards the food, leaving white wake lines behind it. Several of the crumbs are scattered in the air and on the water near the duck. Around her are ordinary park things: a wooden bench, a sandy winding path, a tall black lamp post right behind her, a green bin, a flower bed and a rock. Nothing dramatic is about to happen; the duck looks about to eat the food. Nothing looks impossible, though some crumbs appear to hang in the air between her hand and the pond.

- The lamp post stands very close behind the woman, so it looks almost as if it touches her head and shoulder area, though it seems to be just an overlap.
