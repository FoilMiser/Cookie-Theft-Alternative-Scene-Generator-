An older man in a blue-grey cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers stands at the near edge of the pond, fishing. He holds a long rod out over the water, and the line drops to a red-and-white float sitting in a small ripple ring. A white duck is swimming on the pond just to the right of the float, quite close to it. An empty wooden bench sits beside the path, and a tall black lamp post stands in the middle of the scene. Nobody is interacting with anybody else: the woman is running past behind the fisherman and he is watching his line. The duck might swim into the float or line next, but nothing dramatic is happening. Nothing looks clearly impossible.
