# Picture 13

## Free description
This is a sunny garden with a cream house with a red roof at the left, and a wooden fence with a gate at the back right. An older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal jacket and grey skirt, stands at the flower bed pouring water over the flowers from a teal watering can, with streams of water falling on the flowers. In the centre, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands with his hands on his hips, and he is wearing a large silver metal bucket upside down on his head like a hat; he looks calm or pleased, and this is the silly thing in the picture. Right beside him at the front there is a grey wheelbarrow, standing empty. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie stands at the top of the path, looking up and holding the string of an orange kite with two small bows on its tail, which is flying high in the sky. A swing set with a red seat stands empty to the right of centre. A bench sits at the bottom left. The scene looks peaceful, apart from the bucket on the man's head.

- The man's small smile and the way he looks to the side could suggest he is pleased or just waiting; he is not doing anything else.
- The wheelbarrow looks tilted backward with the wheel at the left and its handles to the right, and it has no one holding it; it may simply be parked.
- The water from the can looks like two colours (blue with red bits where it passes over a red flower); it overlaps the flowers, so I could not separate it clearly from them.
- The boy's kite string passes in front of the fence and the gate, and the boy's hand seems to meet it cleanly, so this looks fine.
- The swing's rope and seat overlap the fence, which makes the ropes slightly hard to see, but it does not look wrong.
