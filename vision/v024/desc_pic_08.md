# Picture 08

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. There are no people in the picture. The only animal is a white goat in the middle of the lawn, with a small dark curved horn, its head bent right down to a small tuft of grass, which it seems to be eating or sniffing. Around it the garden is tidy and still: a teal watering can sits on the ground at the edge of the path, a green hose reel stands by the flower bed, and two potted plants stand near a birdhouse on a tall pole. A colourful flower bed sits by the house and a wooden bench at the right is empty. A goat loose in an ordinary back garden is a little unexpected, and it could go on to eat the plants, but nothing is happening apart from the grazing.
