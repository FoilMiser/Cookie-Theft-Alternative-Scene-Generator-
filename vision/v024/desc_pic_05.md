# Picture 05

## Free description
This is a sunny playground with a dark rail fence, bushes behind it, a red-framed slide on the left and a big tree on the right. At the front left, a small girl in a pink dress kneels in a wooden sandpit, building a sandcastle with a little red flag on top. In the middle front, an orange cat is crouched low with its tail stretched out behind it, creeping or about to pounce on a red bird that is pecking at the ground just in front of it, apparently unaware. It looks as if the cat is about to catch the bird. Further back, a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has its chest low and its rear end up in a play bow, next to a yellow-green tennis ball. An empty wooden bench stands to the right of the dog. At the front right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers with a long dark ponytail walks along holding a pink ice-cream cone up near her mouth. Nobody is on the slide, and no one seems to be looking at the cat and bird.

## Inventory
People and animals:
- Front left, in the sandpit: small girl with dark hair, pink dress, yellow shoes. She kneels and works on a sandcastle.
- Front, centre: orange cat with white paws and a long tail. It crouches low, stalking or about to pounce towards the right.
- Front, right of centre: red bird with a yellow bill, right next to the cat. It stands with its head down and tail up, pecking at the ground.
- Back, right of centre: white dog with brown spots and ear and a blue collar. It is in a play bow, facing the tennis ball.
- Front right: woman with a long dark ponytail, purple top, teal trousers, dark shoes. She walks, holding a pink ice-cream cone up to her mouth.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The cat could be stalking the bird and about to pounce, or could just be walking or stretching low to the ground. The closeness to the bird makes the first reading more likely.
- The bird's body is tipped over, with its head low and its tail up, so at first it could be read as eating, falling over or being upside down.
- The woman's gaze could be towards the cat and bird, or she may just be looking ahead. She seems to be walking left, in their general direction.
- The dog could be playing with the ball, or sniffing it, or crouching to pick it up.
- Drawing oddities: the cat's legs are bent at strange angles and overlap under its body, and the girl's lower leg and foot seem partly hidden behind or merged with the sandcastle base. The castle's base also sits right on the front edge of the sandpit.
