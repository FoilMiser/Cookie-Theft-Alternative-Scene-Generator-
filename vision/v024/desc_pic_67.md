# Picture 67

## Free description
This is a calm, sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, and a tall wooden fence with a gate at the back. There is only one person, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt. She stands at the washing line in the middle of the garden with her arms raised, pegging up a white T-shirt; a pair of blue jeans is already hanging next to it. Around the garden are the usual tools and plants, none being used: a hose reel by the flower bed, a wheelbarrow and a watering can at the front, two potted plants on the lawn, and an empty bench at the front left. A paved path runs from the gate down the right side. Nothing looks wrong or unusual, and nothing dramatic seems about to happen; it looks like an ordinary chore on a dry day.
