# Picture 01

## Free description
This is a sunny playground with a dark metal rail fence, a row of bushes behind it, a big tree on the right and a tall red-framed slide on the left. Nobody is using the slide, and the only two people are on a red seesaw at the lower right. An older man in a blue-grey cap, brown waistcoat, white sleeves and dark trousers sits on the low left end with his feet on the ground. He is absorbed in an open book he holds in front of him and does not look up. A small girl in a pink dress sits on the high right end, up in the air, holding the handle in front of her, with her feet dangling and small curved marks under them. The seesaw looks stuck in this position, because the man is heavy and reading rather than pushing off, so she seems stranded at the top. She appears to be facing towards him, perhaps waiting for him to notice. Nothing is physically impossible, but a grown man quietly reading on a seesaw with a child stuck in the air is a funny, slightly odd situation.
