# Picture 49

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a wooden gate at the back left where a paved path begins. In the left foreground a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is standing on the path and using a giant dinner fork, held like a broom, to sweep a small pile of dirt and grit towards a red dustpan on the ground. That is the odd thing: he is sweeping with a huge table fork rather than a broom, and little dust puffs and motion lines rise around the pile. In the centre an older man in a blue-grey cap, brown waistcoat, white sleeves and dark trousers is digging a hole in the lawn with a spade; a mound of earth is beside the hole and the spade carries a clump of soil. Just left of him a small smiling girl in a pink outfit sits on a red swing on a dark blue-grey swing frame, with small motion lines suggesting she is swinging. Behind the digging man a washing line between two posts holds a yellow striped towel or top and a pair of blue jeans. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands on the grass at the front centre, a colourful flower bed is by the house, a wooden bench is at the far right, and a birdhouse on a tall post stands near the gate. Nobody seems to be in danger, and the people are not paying attention to one another.
