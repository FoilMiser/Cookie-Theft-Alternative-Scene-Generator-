# Picture 04

## Free description
This is a sunny town street or pedestrian square paved with grey slabs, in front of a row of four coloured two-storey shops with striped awnings, and there are many separate small things going on. At the left, an old man in a cap and brown waistcoat walks along carrying something yellow, near a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts who frowns and points up at a blue ball stuck on a branch in a street tree. In the centre, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans sits on a bench eating a pink ice cream while a brown dog with a red neckerchief sits up beside him, front paws raised, begging for it. On the ground in front of them, a white dog with brown patches and a blue neckerchief sits holding an open newspaper in its paws as if reading it, which is impossible. To the right of centre, a boy in a backwards red cap and blue hoodie holds a green watering can by a planter of flowers, but the water arcs upwards in a curve and droplets fly off at the top rather than falling on the flowers, which looks wrong. Behind him, a street sweeper in an orange cap and hi-vis vest sweeps with a broom next to a lamp post, and a blue bird perches on top of the lamp. At the right, a market stall displays crates of red, orange, yellow and green fruit, with a cardboard box next to it. In the front right, an older woman with a grey bun, teal top and grey skirt walks along reading an open book, and a red bucket of water stands on the ground close to her path, so she might walk into it. A red bird flies in the sky at the upper left, and a teal bicycle stands in a rack against the shops.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water could be read two ways: the boy is watering badly and the stream defies gravity, or he is tipping the can and flicking water upwards. Either way it arcs up in a way that looks impossible.
- The narrow yellow item the old man carries could be a shopping bag seen edge-on, a folded newspaper, or a tag.
- The older woman's head is hard to read: it mostly shows grey hair and a dark oval at the side, which could be glasses, an ear or a shadow on her face.
- It is unclear if she is about to walk into the bucket or will just miss it. Her feet and direction of travel seem to point different ways, so she could be walking left towards it or walking past it.
- Things that look strange: the white dog reading a newspaper is plainly impossible, and its paws look like white gloved hands. The brown dog is sitting up unusually upright.
- The lamp post appears to stand in front of a window on the green shop, so the bird may at first look as if it is in that window.
- The sweeper and the lamp post are close together and it is not obvious who is nearer to us, but no part of him clearly passes through the post.
