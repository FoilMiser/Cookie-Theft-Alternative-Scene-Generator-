# Picture 07

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. At the front left, an old man in a cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers walks to the right while reading an open book, and a red ball with a white stripe lies on the grass just ahead of his feet, so he looks about to trip over it. In the middle back, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands with arms raised holding a kite string, which sags and then rises up to a purple kite tangled in the leaves of a tree at the upper right. The kite appears to be stuck. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes kneels on the grass at a flower bed, next to a terracotta pot holding a pink flower, seemingly planting or tending the garden. At the far right, a boy in a backwards red cap and blue hoodie sits on a bench reading a red book and does not notice a blue bird at the end of the bench with its head down in his big sandwich, apparently stealing it. A small brown and white dog is curled up asleep on the grass in the front, right of centre. A red bird flies in the sky at the upper left.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The kite could be read two ways: stuck in the tree (leaf clumps overlap it and the string is slack, which supports this), or still flying just in front of the tree.
- The woman could be about to plant the potted flower, or could have just dug it up. The pot may also just be standing near her.
- The old man's feet straddle the edge of the path and his shadow falls across both path and grass, so it is unclear if he is on the path or the grass. He could trip on the ball, or just miss it.
- The sandwich overlaps the boy's lap area, so it could be on the bench beside him or partly on his knees.
- Things that look slightly strange: the blue bird's body and tail stick out past the end of the bench, so it looks partly unsupported. The shadow under the sleeping dog is much larger than the dog.
- The dog's pose is hard to read, with its head towards us and its body curled behind. It could be asleep, or sniffing the ground.
