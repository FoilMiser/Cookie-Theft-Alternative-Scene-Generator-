# Picture 20

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof on the left, and a wooden fence and gate across the back. A woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers stands at the right by the fence, painting it blue with a long-handled roller or brush; a section of fence next to the gate is already blue. On the ground beside her are a paint tin and a paint tray with blue paint. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is walking away to the left from the paint tray, and it has left a trail of blue paw prints across the lawn, so its feet are covered in blue paint. In the front centre, a small brown and white puppy lies curled up asleep on the grass, near a grey wheelbarrow, and a teal watering can stands to the right. The odd thing is that an orange fish is in the tree near the house, where a fish should not be, which looks impossible. The woman has her back to the dog and does not seem to have noticed the paw prints.
