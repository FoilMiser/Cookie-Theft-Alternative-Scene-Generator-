# Picture 24

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof on the left, a wooden fence and a gate across the back. Three people are each doing their own activity. On the far left, a boy wearing a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. In the middle, a man with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands next to the flower bed, pouring water from a teal watering can over the flowers. Towards the front centre, a child with dark curly hair, yellow top and orange shorts is standing with their back partly to us, looking up and holding the string of an orange kite that flies high in the sky at top right. The kite string stretches a long way across the picture, passing in front of the fence and the swing frame area. On the right there's a dark metal swing set with an empty red seat, and in the front a grey wheelbarrow stands on the grass. Nothing looks about to go wrong and nothing looks impossible; it's a calm, ordinary garden scene.
