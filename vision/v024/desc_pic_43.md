# Picture 43

## Free description

This is a sunny park with green hills and bushes in the distance, a winding sandy path on the left and a blue pond on the right. A man with a dark beard, a green T-shirt, tan trousers and brown shoes is sitting on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket in the foreground, right of centre. He leans back on one arm with his knees bent up, smiling and looking off to the right. A wicker picnic basket with a handle sits on the blanket right in front of him. A white duck swims at the back of the pond, facing left. The odd thing is a tall wooden ladder standing by itself on the grass near the path, left of centre: it tilts as if leaning on something, but there is nothing there for it to lean on, and nobody is using it. It seems to balance on its own, which looks impossible or at least very out of place. A tall black lamp post stands in the middle of the picture, but it is not near the ladder. Everything else is peaceful, and nothing else seems about to happen.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The ladder is the main puzzle. It could be a deliberate oddity (a ladder leaning against nothing), or it could just have been left there and be meant to be leaning against something that is not shown. Either way it does not visibly touch anything.
- The basket sits so close to the man that it overlaps his lap and legs, so it could be read as on the blanket in front of him or resting against his legs.
- One of his hands is partly hidden near his thigh, so it is not clear whether he is reaching towards the basket or just resting his hand.
- His head overlaps the near edge of the pond, which is only a matter of drawing depth; he is sitting on the grass, not in the water.
