# Picture 44

## Free description

This is a back garden on a sunny day, with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right and a tall wooden fence along the back with a gate at the far left. A woman with short dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes stands on the lawn next to a round flower bed by the house. She holds out a dark frying pan by its handle, and a stream of water is pouring out of the pan onto the flowers, with drops splashing at the bottom. So she is watering the flowers with a frying pan instead of a watering can, which looks strange and is the main odd thing in the picture. She looks calm, as if this were normal. A grey wheelbarrow stands empty on the grass at the bottom, and two pots of green plants and a bush sit near the fence. A paved path runs from the gate to the bottom left corner. There is nobody else in the picture and there are no animals.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The object could be read as a frying pan, a saucepan, or possibly a watering-can rose or big ladle. It looks most like a frying pan, and it has no spout or water can body.
- A pan this shallow holding enough water for such a steady stream seems unlikely, and the pan is only barely tipped, so the water looks as if it is pouring by itself.
- She could be emptying dirty water from the pan onto the flowers rather than deliberately watering them, but the result is the same in the picture.
- She has only one arm clearly visible; the other is hidden behind her body or not drawn clearly.
