# Picture 11

## Free description
This is a sunny park with a round blue pond on the left, green hills and trees in the distance, and a winding pale path on the right. A single man with brown hair, wearing a red T-shirt and blue jeans, stands near the middle of the picture at the edge of the pond, facing left. He is holding a long fishing rod with both hands, angled up and to the left, and the line drops down from the rod tip to a red-and-white float bobbing in the water, with a small ripple around it. Nothing has been caught yet; he seems to be waiting for a bite. There is nobody else in the picture and no animals. A tall black lamp post, an empty bench and a couple of green bins stand around the park, and a flower bed and a grey rock are in the foreground. Nothing looks particularly wrong; it is a calm, ordinary scene.
