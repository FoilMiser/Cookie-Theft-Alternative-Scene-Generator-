# Picture 23

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof on the left and a wooden fence and gate across the back. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands to the right of centre holding a hose nozzle up in front of her face and looking at it, with just a single drop of water coming out. The hose runs along the ground from a reel on the left, near the flower bed, and across the lawn to her. A tan dog with a red collar is standing on the hose in the middle, and a large bulge has formed in the hose right by its back foot. So it looks as if the dog's paw is blocking the water, and that the woman may get sprayed in the face when the dog steps off. The dog is looking to the right towards the woman. Two potted plants and a gate are behind her, and a paved path is on the right. Nothing looks impossible.

- The hose's nozzle end at her hands is slightly separated with a gap/overlap near the top, so it isn't completely clear how the nozzle joins the hose.
- The woman's arms stick out straight from close to her neck, and her pose looks a little stiff; the hand holding the nozzle is small and hard to read.
- The dog might not know it is standing on the hose; its attention appears aimed at the woman.
