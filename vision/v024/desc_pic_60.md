# Picture 60

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof on the left, a wooden fence, a washing line and a swing frame. In the centre-left, a man with dark skin, a green shirt and beige trousers is standing with his back partly to the viewer, holding a teal watering can and watering a flower bed. The water, however, does not fall downward: it shoots up in an arc from the spout, curving upward away from the flowers and ending in droplets in the air above the flower bed, which looks wrong or impossible. To the right of him, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is raking a small pile of leaves on the grass. Behind them, a pair of blue jeans and a yellow striped towel or top hang on a washing line between two posts. On the right, a small girl in a pink dress is on the path, jumping in the air with both arms up, looking happy. The swing is empty and still. Nobody appears to be reacting to the odd water.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water could be read two ways: as a jet shooting upward from the can (impossible for a watering can, looks like an error or a deliberate oddity), or as a stream coming down from above into the can (water flowing the wrong way). Either way it does not look like normal watering; the drops at the top look like a fountain.
- The man's head is drawn tilted right down so his face is hard to see; he has a dark beard and it is not obvious if he is looking at the can or the flowers.
- The left washing-line post stands in front of the fence and the line sags more to the right; the jeans and striped item hang close together, as if one peg is shared. This looks normal.
- The girl's jump: she might be jumping for joy or could be reaching for something, but there is nothing above her. The shadow well below her shows she is off the ground.
- The rake teeth overlap the leaf pile and are small and hard to make out.
