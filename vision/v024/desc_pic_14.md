# Picture 14

## Free description
This is a sunny park with a blue pond at the back left, green hills, a winding pale path at the right and a tall black lamp post in the middle. An older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal jacket and grey skirt, kneels on the grass at the front left beside a flower bed, holding a small thin plant or stick toward a terracotta pot with a pink tulip-like plant in it; she seems to be planting or gardening. In the centre front, a white goat with a dark curved horn has its head bent down and is eating a small tuft of grass. On the right, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands by the path holding a white container and scattering small seeds or crumbs, which fall in a line down to the ground. Two small birds on the path, one red and one blue, are pecking at the seeds. The bench, pond and lamp post are empty. It looks like a calm, ordinary scene, and nothing looks impossible.
