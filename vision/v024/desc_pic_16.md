# Picture 16

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny town square paved with grey slabs, with a row of shops with coloured awnings behind it. At the back left a bearded man in a green T-shirt and tan trousers walks left carrying a cardboard box. Near the centre-back, a woman in a yellow dress stands sweeping with a broom, and a small girl in a pink dress stands next to her holding a yellow balloon on a string. In the middle, a man in a red T-shirt and jeans sits on a bench reading a red book, and he does not seem to notice that a blue bird is at his side pecking at or stealing his big sandwich, which is on the bench next to him. To the right of him, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds a teal watering can over a planter, but the water is shooting out in a big curved stream and splashing up rather than down. At the right, a girl with braided hair in a yellow top and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone, with her hand on her head looking upset, because her pink ice cream has fallen on the ground; a brown dog with a red collar is licking it up. In the front left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie walks along reading an open book, and he is about to walk into a cardboard box on the ground in front of him. In the front right, a small white dog with brown patches is sitting up on its hind legs holding a newspaper open with its paws, as if reading it, which is impossible. A red bird flies at the top right near the sun, and there is a fruit stall at the right behind the girl.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The watering can's water curves upward and away rather than down into the planter. It could be meant as water going the wrong way (impossible), or it may be a splash that missed the flowers. It looks like a thick band or hose rather than a stream of drops, and it ends in drops above.
- The man on the bench may be unaware of the bird, or could be feeding it; nothing shows him reacting.
- The blue bird's head appears close to the sandwich but also overlaps it. The bird is large compared with the man and sandwich; it could be read as a big bird or a blue kettle/jug at first glance.
- The boy walking with the book may trip on the box, but the box is a little to the side of his path and there is some gap, so it may be just near him.
- The girl's hand on her head could mean she is upset, or could be her scratching her head. Her face is hard to read because her hair and hand overlap it.
- The white dog reading a paper is clearly impossible in real life; it could be read as a cartoon joke, or the dog might have just picked the paper up. It sits with its back turned slightly away from the paper's front.
- The pink ice cream on the ground looks almost like a flat pink hat or a dropped plate at first glance, with a domed top and a wide brim.
- The tree grows out of a raised planter on the pavement and hides part of the buildings; nothing about it looks wrong, though its leaves cover the left shop's awning.
- The girl with the balloon is standing very near the broom's path and the woman in yellow, but neither reacts; they could be together as a family.
