# Picture 38

## Free description
This is a back garden on a grey day, with three very dark storm clouds at the top of the sky. A cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, with a flower bed in front of it, and a tall wooden fence runs across the back, with a gate at far right and a pale path coming down from it. A clothes line stretched between two wooden posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow and red striped towel or shirt, pegged out to dry. A woman or teenager with short dark hair in a yellow dress and red shoes walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead, heading towards the right. At the left of her, a person seen from behind in a green top and tan trousers is watering the flowers with a teal watering can, but the water is going up and over in a strange big arc rather than simply falling on the plants. Far away at the right, by the gate, a small girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes is pointing up at the sky, seemingly at the dark clouds, with a worried or surprised expression. A swing set stands empty on the right and a red bowl of dog food is on the grass at the bottom. It looks as though it is going to rain, and the washing is about to get soaked; watering the flowers seems pointless too, and only the little girl seems to have noticed.
