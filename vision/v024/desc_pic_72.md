# Picture 72

## Free description
This is a calm, sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, and a tall wooden fence with a gate across the back. There is only one person, a woman with short or tied-back dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes, who is standing on the lawn digging. She holds a long-handled spade with both hands, and its blade is loaded with brown soil, lifted just beside a small dark hole in the grass. A small mound of brown soil lies next to the hole, on the left, so she seems to be piling up what she digs out. Behind her, blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes hang still on a washing line. A wheelbarrow and a watering can stand unused at the front, two potted plants sit on the lawn on the right, and a flower bed is on the left. Nothing looks wrong or impossible; digging in a dress and flat red shoes is the only slightly unusual thing, and she may be about to plant something.
