## 1. Free description

It is a sunny day in a back garden with a lawn. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door. A tall wooden fence with a gate runs across the back, and there is a clothesline hung with jeans and a striped towel. In the centre a woman in a yellow dress holds a green garden hose up near her face and looks into the nozzle. Only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose runs back across the lawn to a hose reel at the front left, and a brown dog with a red collar is standing on or right beside the hose. It looks as if the dog is standing on the hose and stopping the water. If the dog steps off, the water will probably spray straight into the woman's face. On the right a smiling boy in a yellow shirt is swinging on a grey metal swing set; motion lines show the swing is moving. At the far right an older woman with grey hair and glasses is raking a small pile of brown leaves on the paved path that leads to the gate. An empty wheelbarrow stands on the lawn between the woman with the hose and the swings.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous

- The dark blue patch on the hose under the dog's hind paw could be the hose squashed flat under the dog's foot or a small puddle of water. Either way, the dog seems to be standing on the hose, but I can't be completely sure that its paw is on the hose rather than just beside it.
- The woman with the hose: she is probably checking why no water is coming out (the drop and her pose suggest this). Another reading is that she is about to drink from the hose. Either way she is not pointing it at the flowers.
- The woman is turned mostly away from the viewer, so her face is shown only in profile and her facial expression is hard to read.
- The hose goes from the ground up past the front of the woman's dress to her hands. It looks plausible, but it is hard to tell whether it passes in front of or behind her legs.
- The leaves are only at the older woman's feet. There is no tree near her that would have dropped them; the only tree is far away behind the house. This is minor and not clearly wrong.
- The wheelbarrow is empty and doesn't seem to be in use; it could be meant for the leaves.
- Nothing appears to be floating or passing through anything else.
