## 3. Unsure or ambiguous

- Water stream: this is the most noticeable thing that looks wrong. It curves upward from near the can's spout to a high point with drops, as if the water is defying gravity or spraying upward like a fountain. Reading A: it is meant as water pouring from the can, drawn going the wrong way (impossible). Reading B: the stream's lower end sits just above the flowers rather than clearly at the spout, so it could be read as water rising out of the flower bed. Either way it does not look like normal watering.
- Cat and bird: the cat is clearly crouched and stalking, so it seems about to pounce on the bird. But the bird faces the cat and looks calm, so it could be read either as the bird not having noticed the danger or as the two just looking at each other.
- Dog: it is clear it holds a lead in its mouth. It is not clear whether it is waiting at the door to be let in or asking the boy for a walk. It faces right, roughly towards the door and the boy.
- The cat's body is long and low with bent legs. It reads as creeping or crawling, but the shape is a little awkward.
- The wheelbarrow is empty and alone in the front of the lawn. It does not seem connected to anything else in the scene.
