- The ice-cream man's eyes are squeezed shut. This could mean he is **enjoying the taste**, or he may have **brain freeze** from the cold ice cream.
- It is not clear what the photographer is aiming at: the **goat** (which is roughly in line with the camera) or the **man eating ice cream** further right. His camera points between and toward both.
- The goat's eyes look closed or half-closed, which reads as contented grazing. Its head is close to the ground, so the face is a little hard to read.
- The ice-cream man's lower hand, at his side, looks like a small pale fist. He might be holding something small or nothing at all; I can't tell.
- Nothing looks clearly floating or passing through anything. The man in red stands just in front of the pond's near edge, so the pond outline runs behind his chest. That reads as depth (he is in front of the pond), not as him standing in the water.
- A goat on a rope in a public park is a little unexpected, but it does not look impossible. It is clearly tied to a stake rather than running loose.
