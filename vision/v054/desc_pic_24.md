## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left, a wooden fence, a gate at right and a paved path. A woman in a yellow dress with dark hair stands between two clothesline posts and reaches up to peg a white T-shirt onto the line. A pair of blue jeans already hangs there. Both garments are dripping water, so they are freshly washed. A wicker laundry basket full of more clothes (blue, white, pink) sits on the ground beside her. On the right are two potted plants and a birdhouse on a tall pole. In the front are a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can lying on the grass. A flower bed grows in front of the house. No one else is around, and nothing looks wrong.
