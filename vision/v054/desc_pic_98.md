# pic_98

## Free description
A back garden on a sunny day, with a wooden fence, a gate and a paved path on the left, and a house with a blue door on the right. On the path at the bottom left, an old woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt is using a giant silver dinner fork like a broom or rake. She is pushing a small pile of grey dirt or gravel into a red dustpan, and little puffs of dust rise. This is odd: a table fork is the wrong tool for sweeping, and it is much bigger than a real fork. Nearby on the lawn stand a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow, both unused. There are two potted plants near the fence, a couple of bushes, and a flower bed in front of the house. Nobody else is in the garden.
