# pic_37

## Free description
A busy, sunny park full of people and animals, with several odd things going on. At front left, a brown dog wearing glasses sits up holding and reading a newspaper, which is impossible. Next to it, an older man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat is watering the flower bed by pouring water out of a frying pan instead of a watering can. On the pond, a white duck swims after pieces of bread, and a girl in a pink dress stands at the pond's edge holding a slice of bread, apparently feeding it. Up the middle, a girl in a yellow top and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and rubs her eye, crying, because her ice cream has fallen on the ground where a white spotted dog is licking it up. Behind the lamp post, a white goat on a tether is eating grass, and an elderly woman in a teal cardigan sweeps leaves with a broom at the start of the path. On the right, a boy in a red cap holds a kite string but his purple kite is stuck in the tree. A blue bird flies across the sky.
