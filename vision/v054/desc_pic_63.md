# pic_63

## Free description
A busy, sunny shopping street with four colourful shop buildings. On the left, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and rubs an eye as if crying, while a brown dog licks the pink ice cream spilled on the ground. An older woman with grey hair walks past the shops carrying a yellow shopping bag. In the front centre, a man in a green shirt sits reading a red book, but there is no chair or bench under him, so he is sitting on thin air. On a bench behind him, a woman in a purple top reads a red book, and next to her on the bench a blue bird is pecking at her sandwich, which she has not noticed. An older man in a cap and brown waistcoat scatters seeds from a bag to a red bird on the ground. On the right, a blue-and-white beach ball is stuck in a tree and a girl in a pink dress points up at it, and a boy in a red cap sweeps up leaves with a broom. In the front left is a red bicycle with square wheels, and in the front right a small café table with two chairs; there are also a lamp post, a fruit stall, a flower planter and a bin.
