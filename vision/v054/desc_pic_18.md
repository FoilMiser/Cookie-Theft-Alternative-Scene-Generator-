In the middle distance, a white goat with a red collar grazes on a tuft of grass. It is tied by a rope to a short wooden stake. A boy in a yellow top and orange shorts stands on the path holding a red balloon on a string and looking toward the bench and goat area. A green bin is at bottom left and a grey rock at bottom centre. Nothing looks impossible.
