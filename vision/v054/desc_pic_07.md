# Picture 07

## Free description
A sunny playground with a railing fence and bushes behind it. On the left a red seesaw is tilted: a young girl in a pink outfit sits high up on the raised end holding the handle, with her feet dangling. An older man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat sits on the low end, reading an open newspaper or book and paying no attention to her. Because he is heavy and sitting still, the girl is stuck up in the air. She has a hand at her mouth as if calling out to him, or looking worried. In the centre is a swing frame with one empty red swing seat, and a green bin is behind it by the fence. On the right a red slide stands empty. In the front right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts kneels in a sandpit building a sandcastle with a red spade. A grey rock sits beside the sandpit and a tree stands on the far left.

- Boy (front right, in the sandpit): short dark hair, yellow shirt, orange shorts; kneeling and building a sandcastle with a red spade.

- Bin (centre-right, back): green bin by the fence.
- Slide (right): red ladder and frame with a yellow/orange chute, empty.

- Rock (bottom right): grey rock beside the sandpit.
- Tree (far left) and bush (far right).
- Sun (top right) and two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl's hand at her mouth could be read as calling out, whistling or shouting to the man, or as nervousness, nail-biting or surprise. Her expression is hard to read.
- The seesaw's balance is physically plausible, since the heavier adult holds his end down, but an adult reading on a seesaw while a child is stuck up high is odd and seems to be the point of tension.
- It is unclear whether the man is reading a newspaper or a book.
- Nothing looks floating or passing through anything.
