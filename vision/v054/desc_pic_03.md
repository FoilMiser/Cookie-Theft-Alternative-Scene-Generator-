# Picture 03

## Free description
A sunny back garden. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, and a wooden fence with a gate on the right runs across the back. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt stands in the middle-left, watering a flower bed of red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers with a grey watering can. Oddly, a thin curved blue stream rises upward out of the spout like a hose or fountain, with water drops above it. Water would normally pour down from a watering can. Behind her a washing line between two posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow striped top, pegged out to dry. On the right there is a birdhouse on a tall pole, two potted plants and a paved path leading to the garden gate. A grey wheelbarrow stands empty in the front centre, and a wooden bench sits in the front left by the house. A tree shows above the fence behind the house.
