A sunny playground with a dark railing fence, a tree on the left, a swing frame in the middle, a slide on the right and a sandpit at the bottom right. On the left, a small girl in a pink dress is sitting on the raised end of a red seesaw, holding its handle. Her mouth is open and her hand is up as if she is calling out or upset. An older man with grey hair, a blue cap, a white shirt and a brown waistcoat sits on the low end of the same seesaw, reading an open book or newspaper. He doesn't seem to notice her, and his weight keeps her stuck up in the air. On the right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is sliding down the slide with one arm raised. The swing frame has a single empty red seat. The sandpit holds a red bucket and a blue spade, and nobody is in it. There is a green bin by the fence and a grey rock at the bottom right.
