# Picture 08

## Free description
A sunny playground with a railing fence and bushes behind it. In the middle a boy in a red cap, blue top and dark trousers has just thrown a red ball, which is shown in mid-air with a dotted line marking its path. A woman in a yellow dress with dark hair stands to the right with her arms raised, ready to catch it. In the front left a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a kite reel and string up towards the sky. His kite, a purple one with bows on its tail, is caught in the leaves of the tree on the left, so he seems to be trying to pull it free. In the centre front a white dog with brown spots crouches in a playful bow with a yellow-green tennis ball in front of it, wagging its tail. At the front right a young girl in pink kneels in a sandpit building a sandcastle with a red spade. An empty bench, a red slide, a grey rock and a bush complete the scene.

- Woman (centre, back): long dark hair, yellow dress, red shoes; arms raised to catch the ball.
- Kite boy (front left): curly dark hair, yellow shirt, orange shorts; holding a kite reel up, with string running to the kite in the tree.
- Dog (front centre): white with brown patches and a blue collar; crouched in a play bow with a tennis ball by its front paws, tail wagging.
- Girl (front right, in the sandpit): dark hair, pink outfit, yellow shoes; kneeling, building a sandcastle with a red spade.
- Red ball (centre, in the air): thrown ball travelling from the boy to the woman, with a dotted trajectory line.
- Kite (upper left): purple diamond kite with bow tails, tangled in the tree's leaves.
- Kite string: long line from the reel to the kite.
- Tennis ball (front centre): yellow-green ball in front of the dog.
- Sandpit and sandcastle (front right): wooden sandpit with a three-towered castle and a small red flag.
- Bench (left-centre, back): empty wooden bench.
- Slide (right): red ladder and frame with a yellow/orange chute, empty.
- Rock (bottom right): grey rock.
- Tree (far left), bush (far right), sun (top right) and two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The kite is stuck in the tree rather than flying. The boy could be trying to tug it loose, or he may not have noticed yet.
- The woman's arms are raised. This could be ready to catch, or waving or clapping, but the ball is heading her way, so catching fits best.
- The dog could be inviting someone to throw its tennis ball, or about to chase the red ball.
- The red ball in mid-air with a dotted path is meant to show motion, not a floating object.
