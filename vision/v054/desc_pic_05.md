# Picture 05

## Free description
A sunny park with rolling green hills, a winding sandy path on the left, trees, bushes, a tall lamp post and an oval pond on the right. An older man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands at the edge of the pond holding a brown paper bag. He is throwing small pieces of bread into the water for a white duck, which is swimming towards the crumbs and pecking at them. In the front centre-left a white goat with horns and a red collar is tied by a rope to a short wooden stake, and it is grazing on a tuft of grass beside the path. A young girl in a pink dress walks or skips across the front right, eating a pink ice cream cone. There is a grey rock in the front centre and a green bin in the bottom right corner. A goat in a town park is a bit unusual, but it is tethered, so it seems to be meant to be there.

- Duck (right, in the pond): white duck swimming towards the bread pieces and pecking at them, with ripple lines behind it.
- Bread pieces (in the pond): several small pale crumbs floating on the water between the man and the duck.
- Paper bag: brown bag held by the man.
- Goat (front centre-left): white goat with dark horns and a red collar; head down eating grass, tethered.
- Rope and stake (front centre-left): rope from the goat's collar to a short wooden stake in the ground.
- Grass tuft (front left): small clump of grass the goat is eating.
- Girl (front right): dark hair, pink dress, yellow shoes; walking or skipping and eating a pink ice cream cone.
- Pond (right): oval blue pond with bulrushes at each end.
- Path (left): winding sandy path.
- Lamp post (centre, back): tall black street lamp.
- Rock (front centre): grey boulder.
- Bin (bottom right): green bin.
- Trees: large tree on the left, smaller tree on the right; several bushes.
- Sun (top left) and two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The man's right hand is held out over the water with a small pale piece in it, and the crumbs sit in a line towards the duck, so he is throwing bread. The duck's beak touches a crumb.
- The goat might be a sheep or a kid goat, but the horns and shape say goat. A goat in a public park with a pond is slightly unexpected.
- The girl could be walking or running. Her stride looks lively.
- Nothing looks floating or impossible.
