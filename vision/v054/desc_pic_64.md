# pic_64

## Free description
A sunny park with hills, a pond with reeds on the left, a lamp post and bin in the middle, and a winding sandy path and big tree on the right. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands at the edge of the pond holding a paper bag and tossing pieces of bread to a white duck, which is swimming toward the bread and eating it (there are bread pieces on the water and a wake behind the duck). On a bench to the right, an older woman with grey hair and glasses in a green cardigan eats a pink ice-cream cone. A small white dog with brown spots sits up on its back legs in front of her, looking up and begging for the ice cream. There is a colourful flower bed in the front left. Nothing looks impossible.
