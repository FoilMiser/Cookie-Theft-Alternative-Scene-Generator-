## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house on the left, wooden fence, gate and stone path on the right. An older man with grey hair, a cap, a brown vest and dark trousers is painting the fence blue with a roller; a few boards are blue and a paint tin sits on the grass behind him. In the centre of the lawn, a brown dog is sitting upright holding a newspaper in its front paws as if reading it, which is impossible/silly. On the left, a girl in a pink dress sits on a bench eating a pink ice cream, while a white spotted dog sits up begging beside her, looking up at the ice cream. A flower bed, a wheelbarrow and a watering can are also there.
