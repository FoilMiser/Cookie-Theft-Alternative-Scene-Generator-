# pic_38

## Free description
A sunny back garden with the house on the left. At the front left, an elderly woman in a teal cardigan sits on a bench reading a red book; on the bench beside her is a sandwich, and a small blue bird is perched next to it pecking at / about to steal the sandwich, which she has not noticed. In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress pegs dripping-wet laundry (blue jeans and a white T-shirt) onto a washing line, with a laundry basket of clothes at her feet. On the right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands on the path holding the strings of a red and a blue balloon, looking up unhappily at a yellow balloon that has escaped and is floating away into the sky. A hose reel, a grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can lie on the lawn. At the back are the fence, a birdhouse on a pole and the gate.
