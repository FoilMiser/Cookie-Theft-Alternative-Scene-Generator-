# pic_72

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a stone path running down the right side to a wooden gate. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; a few planks behind and to the left of her are already blue. Her paint tin and a paint tray of blue paint sit on the grass by her feet. A white dog with brown spots is on the path at the right, and its paws are blue: a trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the grass to the dog, so it seems to have stepped in the paint. A man in a red shirt near the gate is sweeping a small pile of leaves with a broom. In the centre front a man in a green shirt is walking towards the viewer carrying a cardboard box, and a little girl in a pink dress stands nearby waving. By the blue house door on the left, a brown dog sits holding a lead in its mouth, as if asking to go for a walk. A flower bed, a bush, a tree and a teal watering can complete the scene.
