# Picture 02

## Free description
A sunny playground on a grassy lawn, with a dark metal railing fence and a line of bushes behind it. At the front left a young girl in a pink outfit kneels in a wooden sandpit, building a sandcastle with a red spade. The castle has three towers and a small red flag on top. Towards the front centre a small white puppy with brown patches lies on the grass with its head down. It looks asleep, or perhaps sniffing the ground. Behind them is a tall red slide with a yellow and orange chute, on the left. On the right are an empty wooden bench and a large tree. The playground is otherwise empty and quiet, and there is a lot of open grass and sky. Nothing looks wrong or impossible.

- Bushes: two small round bushes, one at the far left and one near the bench.

- Sun (top left) and two clouds.
