## Free description
A sunny back garden with a gate and paved path on the left and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the path at the left, a man in a green shirt walks quickly while reading an open book, and a cardboard box sits on the path right in front of his feet, so he looks about to trip over it. Behind him, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie walks a white dog with brown spots on a red lead. In the centre, an older woman with grey hair and glasses holds the nozzle of a garden hose and looks at it, but no water comes out. The hose runs back to a hose reel near the house, and a brown dog is standing right on the hose, which seems to be blocking the water. Further back, a girl in a pink dress reaches up toward a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of a tree. On the right, a man in a red shirt sits on a bench reading a red book.
