## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden gate and paved path on the left and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the wooden fence blue with a paint roller; one section of the fence is already blue. Behind her on the grass are an open paint tin and a paint tray with blue paint. A white dog with brown spots is walking away from the paint tray toward the left, and a line of blue paw prints runs from the tray to the dog, so the dog has stepped in the paint and is tracking it across the lawn. The woman does not seem to have noticed. On the left, an older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a green cardigan, sweeps a pile of dead leaves on the path with a broom. A teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow stand on the grass at the front, and there is a flower bed by the house.
