# Picture 04

## Free description
A sunny back garden with no people or ordinary animals in it. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the right, and a wooden fence with a garden gate on the far left runs across the back. A paved path runs from the gate down the left side. A washing line between two posts holds a yellow red-striped top and a pair of blue jeans. There is a flower bed of colourful flowers near the house, with a wooden bench in front of the house and a bush beside it. Two potted plants stand near the fence on the left. In the front, a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow sit on the grass, unused. The strange thing is an orange fish sitting in the tree behind the house, among the leaves near a branch, as if it were a bird. A fish in a tree is impossible. Nothing seems to be about to happen, apart from the oddity of the fish.
