## Free description
A sunny but windy back garden: a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, a gate and stone path on the right. White wind lines streak across the sky and across the fence. In the centre a bearded man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers is reaching up with one arm, looking surprised, as a red cap blows off and flies away in the wind just above his hand, along with a few small leaves. It looks like the wind has just blown his cap off his head and he is trying to grab it. There are two potted plants by the fence on the right, a teal watering can in front, and a flower bed on the left. Nothing else seems strange.
