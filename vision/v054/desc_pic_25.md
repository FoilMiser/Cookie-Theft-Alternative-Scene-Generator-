## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a stone path running down the left from a wooden gate. In the middle, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence blue with a long-handled paint roller; a few fence boards are already blue. At her feet are a paint tin and a paint tray with blue paint in it. A white dog with brown spots is walking away from the tray toward the path on the left, and it has left a trail of blue paw prints across the grass; its paws look blue. The woman has her back to the dog and does not seem to have noticed. The dog looks pleased with itself. There are two potted plants, a flower bed by the house, a watering can and a wheelbarrow in the foreground.
