This is a busy sunny park with a pond, a lamp post, a winding path, a bench and trees. At front left, a woman in a yellow dress is pouring water onto a colourful flower bed, but she is using what looks like a grey cooking pot or saucepan instead of a watering can. Next to her, a man in a green shirt and beige trousers holds a paper bag and scatters seeds on the ground. A red bird stands on the grass pecking at the seeds. A child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts runs past the bird while eating a pink ice cream cone, and the child is very close to the bird. In the middle distance, a girl in a pink dress holds a red and a purple balloon and reaches up after a yellow balloon that is floating away into the sky. On the path at right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks along carrying a yellow shopping bag. The bench is empty.
