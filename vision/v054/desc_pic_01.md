# Picture 01

## Free description
A sunny town street or shopping square with a row of colourful two-storey shops, each with a striped awning. In the middle a young girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench eating a pink ice cream cone. A small white dog with brown spots and a blue collar sits up on its hind legs in front of the bench, begging and looking up at her ice cream. In the front centre an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses and a teal top walks towards the right carrying a yellow shopping bag. On the right an older man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers sweeps a small pile of dry leaves with a broom. Behind him is an outdoor fruit and vegetable stall with crates of red, orange, yellow and green produce under a teal-and-white awning. Other things on the street are a green bicycle in a bike rack, a tree in a round planter, a green bin, a small café table with two chairs, a street lamp, two window boxes of flowers, a wooden crate and a potted plant. Nothing looks wrong. The dog might be about to get some ice cream, or the girl might drop it.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The pink thing in the girl's hand reads as an ice cream cone. It could also be a small pink sweet or toy, but ice cream fits best with the begging dog.
- The man's broom ends in a pile of brownish shapes. I read them as dry leaves, but they could be rubbish or twigs.
- Nobody is minding the fruit stall. The sweeping man could be the stallholder.
- Nothing looks floating or physically impossible.
