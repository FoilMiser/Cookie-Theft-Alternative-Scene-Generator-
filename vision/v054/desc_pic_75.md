## Free description
A sunny, windy back garden seen with the house on the right (cream walls, red roof, blue door) and the gate and stone path on the left. Wind lines cross the sky, and leaves blow off the tree by the house. At the left, by the gate, an older woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal cardigan and grey skirt reaches up in surprise as her blue hat/cap blows away over the fence in the wind. In the middle a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts is happily swinging on a swing set, with motion lines behind him. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller; the planks near the house are already blue, and his paint tin and paint tray sit on the grass. A white dog with brown spots in the centre has blue paws and has left a trail of blue paw prints leading from the paint tray, so it seems to have stepped in the paint. In front there is a teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow, and a flower bed by the house.
