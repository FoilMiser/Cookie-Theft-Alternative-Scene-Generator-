# pic_69

## Free description
A sunny back garden seen from the other side: the house is on the right, and a gate and paved path are on the left. An elderly woman with grey hair, glasses and a teal cardigan paints the fence blue with a long-handled roller, with a paint tin at her feet. Several boards are already blue. A little girl in a pink dress stands in the middle and flies a yellow kite high in the sky; its string runs from her hands up to the kite. In the front left, a woman in a yellow dress sweeps dust and dirt on the path into a red dustpan using a huge silver dinner fork as if it were a broom. That is odd and silly. A grey wheelbarrow sits on the grass at the front, and there are two potted plants, a birdhouse on a tall pole and a flower bed by the house.
