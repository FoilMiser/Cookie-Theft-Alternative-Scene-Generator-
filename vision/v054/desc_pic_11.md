## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left with a paved path, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A man in a green shirt and beige trousers stands in the centre holding the nozzle of a green hose and peering into it, frowning, as if wondering why no water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel by the house across the lawn, and a brown dog with a red collar is standing with its back paw on the hose, which seems to be blocking the water. At the back, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red leash. On the left, a girl in a yellow dress is drinking from a red cup with a straw while sitting with her knees bent, but there is no chair or seat under her. A flower bed with colourful flowers sits in front of the house.
