# pic_13

## Free description
This is a sunny playground with a metal railing fence, a tree on the right, a bush, and a grey rock in the front left. On the left, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is sliding down a red-and-yellow slide with one arm up, looking happy. On the right is a red seesaw. An older man with grey hair, a cap and a brown vest is sitting on the low end of the seesaw, reading an open newspaper or book and not paying attention. On the high end, a small girl in a pink dress is stuck up in the air, holding the handle with one hand and her other hand near her mouth, looking worried or surprised. Because the man is so much heavier and isn't pushing up, the girl can't come down. A green rubbish bin stands near the fence behind the slide.
