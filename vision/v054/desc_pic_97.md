## Free description
A busy back garden on a sunny day, with a wooden fence, a house with a blue door on the right, a gate and a paved path on the left, and lots of people doing chores. On the path at the bottom left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps a pile of dry leaves with a broom. Next to the gate, a man in a red shirt and jeans paints the fence blue with a paint roller. Part of the fence is already blue, and a paint tin sits at his feet. In the middle, an old woman with glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt holds the end of a garden hose and looks at the nozzle as if puzzled. The hose runs across the lawn from a hose reel near the bench, and a brown dog stands with a back paw on the hose, which may be blocking the water. A man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands with his hands on his hips with an upturned metal bucket on his head, which is odd. A small girl in a pink dress points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the tree beside the house. A grey cat walks along the top of the fence and a blue bird flies in the sky. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench drinking from a cup with a straw. There is a flower bed in front of the house. The wheelbarrow in the middle has a square wheel, which wouldn't roll.
