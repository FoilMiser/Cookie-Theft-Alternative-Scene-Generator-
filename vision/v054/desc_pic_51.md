## Free description
A busy, sunny park with a railing fence and hedges behind. A small girl in a pink dress holds red and purple balloons and reaches up after a yellow balloon that has floated away into the sky. In the front, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is crying, holding an empty ice-cream cone, while a brown dog licks the pink ice cream that has fallen on the grass. An elderly woman in a teal top and grey skirt walks along carrying a cardboard box. An older man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat sits on a bench reading a red book, while a blue bird pecks at a sandwich lying on the bench beside him, which he hasn't noticed. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of a tree. A red bicycle lies on its side on the grass. On the left are a red slide, a sandpit with a bucket and spade, a white spotted dog playing with a tennis ball, a grey rock, and a red bird perched on the fence.
