## Free description
This is a back garden on a grey, overcast day with three dark storm clouds overhead and no sun. There is a wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and grey skirt is sweeping a pile of dead leaves with a broom by the path. In the centre, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is pointing up at the dark clouds with a worried expression, as if warning that it is about to rain. A grey wheelbarrow sits on the lawn, and a hose reel with its hose and yellow nozzle lies by the house. There are also two potted plants by the fence and a flower bed by the house. It looks like rain is coming and the woman hasn't noticed yet.
