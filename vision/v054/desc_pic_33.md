An elderly woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal cardigan and grey skirt, stands on the lawn right of centre, looking up and pointing at the dark clouds, as if she thinks it is about to rain. Nobody else is in the picture. A teal watering can sits on the grass beside her, which is a little ironic if rain is coming. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door; in front of it is a round bed of colourful flowers. At the back is a wooden fence with a gate on the right, a tall pole with a birdhouse on top, and two potted plants by the pole. A paved path runs from the front right up to the gate. Nothing looks clearly impossible; the scene suggests rain is about to start.
