# Picture 06

## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, a winding sandy path on the left, trees, bushes, a tall lamp post and an oval pond on the right. A young girl in a pink dress sits on a wooden bench beside the path, eating a pink ice cream cone. A brown dog with a red collar sits up on its hind legs next to the bench, begging and looking up at her. On the right, in front of the pond, an older man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands eating a pink ice cream cone too. Next to him is a round flower bed of colourful flowers. There is a green bin in the bottom right corner. No one is in the pond and nothing seems to be going wrong. The dog may be hoping for some of the girl's ice cream.

## Inventory
- Girl (centre-left, middle): dark hair, pink dress, yellow shoes; sitting on the bench eating a pink ice cream cone.
- Dog (centre-left, beside the bench): brown dog with a red collar; sitting up on its hind legs begging, facing the girl.
- Older man (right, middle-front): grey hair, blue-grey flat cap, brown waistcoat over a white shirt, dark trousers; standing and eating a pink ice cream cone.
- Bench (centre-left): wooden park bench.
- Pond (right, back): oval blue pond with bulrushes at each end; empty.
- Flower bed (right, front): round soil bed with red, orange, yellow, pink and purple flowers.
- Path (left): winding sandy path.
- Lamp post (centre, back): tall black street lamp.
- Bin (bottom right): green bin.
- Trees and bushes: large tree on the left, smaller tree on the right, several bushes.
- Sun (top left) and two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The man holds his ice cream at his mouth. It is the same pink as the girl's, so both are eating ice cream.
- The dog looks at the girl rather than the man, so it is presumably begging from her.
- Nothing looks floating or impossible.
