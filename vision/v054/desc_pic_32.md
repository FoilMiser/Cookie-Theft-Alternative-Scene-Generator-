# pic_32

## Free description
A windy, sunny back garden: white wind lines streak across the sky and over the fence. In the centre a bearded man in a green shirt and beige trousers is reaching up with one arm, looking surprised, as his red cap is blown off his head and flies up and away to the right along with a few leaves. On the right, a brown dog sits by the blue door of the cream house holding a yellow lead in its mouth, as if asking for a walk. Front centre-right, a small white dog is curled up asleep on the grass. Two potted plants, a bush, a flower bed, a watering can and a wheelbarrow are in the garden; a gate and stone path are on the left.
