## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a paved path leading to a wooden gate on the right. A brown dog sits by the blue door holding a yellow leash in its mouth, as if it wants to go for a walk. In the middle, a man in a red shirt and blue trousers stands by a round flower bed holding a green watering can. The water does not pour down onto the flowers: a blue stream comes out of the spout and arcs upward and back toward the left, ending in drops in the air above the flowers, which looks impossible. In front, a grey-haired older woman with glasses and a green cardigan is digging with a spade, with a pile of soil beside her. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands to her right. On the right are an empty swing set with a red seat and a birdhouse on a tall pole.
