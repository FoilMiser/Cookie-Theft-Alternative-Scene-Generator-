## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. An older man with grey hair, a cap and a brown vest is painting the fence blue with a paint roller; a section of the fence on the right is already blue. On the ground near him are a paint tray with blue paint and a paint tin. A white dog with brown spots is walking away from the paint tray toward the left, and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the tray to the dog, so the dog has stepped in the paint and is tracking it across the lawn. On the left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping a pile of dead leaves with a broom beside the path. A green watering can, a grey wheelbarrow, two potted plants and a flower bed are also in the garden. The man and boy don't seem to have noticed the dog's paint trail.
