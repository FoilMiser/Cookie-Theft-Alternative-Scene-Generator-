## 1. Free description

This is a cartoon of a sunny park with a blue sky, two clouds, a bright sun at top right, green hills and a line of bushes in the distance. A woman or girl in a yellow dress and red shoes stands at the near edge of a pond in the centre-left, holding a brown paper bag in one hand and reaching her other hand out over the water. A white duck on the pond has its head down and is pecking at small pale pieces of food (they look like breadcrumbs or seeds) floating in front of it, so she seems to be feeding the duck. On the right, a boy or man in a red shirt and blue trousers is walking along a sandy path with his head bent down, reading an open book held in front of him. A cardboard box sits in the middle of the path just ahead of or beside him, lower left of his feet, and because he is looking at his book and not the ground, it looks as if he may be about to trip over it or bump into it. There is an empty wooden bench beside the path, a tall black lamp post, and a green rubbish bin next to it. The one obviously wrong thing is an orange fish on the tree at the far right: it sits on the end of a bare branch, up among the leaves, like a bird perched in the tree, which is impossible since fish live in water. A bed of colourful flowers is in the front left.
