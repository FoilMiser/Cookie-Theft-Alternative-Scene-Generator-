# Description: test_1.png

## Free description

This is a cartoon of a back garden on a grey day. A house with a red roof stands on the left, and a wooden plank fence runs across the back with a hedge and a tree behind it. Three dark storm clouds hang in the grey sky, so it looks like it is about to rain, but nothing is falling yet. An older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses is watering a round bed of colourful flowers with a green watering can, even though rain is coming. Further back, a girl with a brown ponytail in a blue hoodie is painting the fence blue with a long-handled brush or roller. A few boards are already bright blue and a paint can sits on the grass near her. Rain would wash off or spoil the wet paint, and the watering is pointless if it is about to rain. On the right, a bald man with a black beard in a green T-shirt stands on a stone path near a wooden gate. He points up at the dark clouds with his mouth open, as if warning the others. In the front centre, a white dog with brown spots is in a play bow next to a yellow tennis ball, with its tail up and wagging. A red bowl of dog food sits on the grass to its right. Nobody except the man seems to have noticed the storm.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The girl's tool could be a paint roller on a stick or a long-handled paint brush. The tip is small and pale blue, so I can't tell which.
- The girl stands a little in front of the fence, so her brush could be read as touching the fence or as reaching out toward it. In the drawing the tip sits over the top of the blue boards.
- The man's pointing arm aims up and to the left. It most likely points at the storm clouds, but it could also be read as pointing toward the girl and the freshly painted fence, or the top of the fence or gate.
- The man's expression could be alarm or worry about the storm, or he could just be calling out to the others. His open mouth and upward gaze fit either reading.
- The watering can is almost the same teal/green as the woman's cardigan, so at a glance the can and her arm blend together.
- The water spray is drawn over the front of some flowers, so in places it looks like it passes in front of or through the blooms rather than landing on them.
- The potted plants and the paint can have no shadows, while the people and the dog do. The pots look a little pasted-on but not clearly floating.
- The dog is clearly in a play pose, but it's unclear whether it wants someone to throw the ball or is about to grab it. The food bowl nearby doesn't seem to be part of the action.
- The tree's crown sits partly behind the house roof. That reads as normal depth (tree behind the house), not an error.
- Nothing looks impossible. The odd parts are the situation itself: watering flowers and painting a fence as a rainstorm approaches.
