This is a sunny day in a park with green hills, trees, a pond, a bench, a tall black lamp post and a flower bed. On the left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers sweeps a small pile of brown leaves on a curving path with a yellow broom. In the centre, a small child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds the strings of two balloons, yellow and purple, in one hand. The child's other arm reaches up and to the left, toward a blue balloon. The blue balloon floats high in the air next to the lamp post, and its string hangs loose, ending in mid-air. The child looks worried or upset. It looks as if the child has just let go of the blue balloon and it is floating away. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is eating or licking a pink ice-cream cone. Nobody else is helping the child or seems to notice the balloon.
