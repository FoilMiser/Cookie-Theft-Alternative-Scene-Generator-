# Description of test_5.png

## 1. Free description

A sunny day in a park with green hills, a few trees, a lamppost, a pond and a winding sand-coloured path. On the right, a small girl in a pink dress has just thrown a red ball (a dashed line shows its flight from her hand) towards a man in a red shirt and blue trousers, who stands on the path with both arms held out as if to catch it; his mouth is open and he looks a bit surprised. In the middle background a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts stands at the edge of the pond holding a slice of bread, apparently feeding a white duck that is swimming towards crumbs floating on the water. In the front centre a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket, leaning back and smiling, next to a wicker basket with a bottle and a baguette in it. On the lower left is a round bed of colourful flowers and a green rubbish bin. The ball is about to reach the man. Nothing looks clearly impossible; the oddest points are that the ball's path seems to come from the girl and travel up-left towards the man even though the girl is facing/reaching right-to-left from behind the tree area, and that the bread crumbs look rather large.
