# Description of test_7.png

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden beside a cream house with a red roof, a blue door and a window, under a blue sky with two clouds and a bright sun. Behind the garden runs a tall wooden fence with a gate in the far right corner, and a big tree and green hedges show above it. Near the house, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is painting the fence blue with a paint roller. Several boards behind and just to the right of him are already blue, and a paint tin stands on the grass at his feet. In the middle, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is holding a green garden hose and looking at its yellow nozzle, which she points up at about head height. The hose runs from a reel next to the house, across the lawn and around to her. A white dog with brown spots stands on the hose near the flower bed, apparently on the hose's join or kink, looking back toward the house. That may explain why the woman is peering at the nozzle: the water may not be coming out. On the right is a metal swing set with a red swing seat, an empty grey wheelbarrow in front of it, and a paving-stone path running from the gate to the front right corner. A wooden bench and a round flower bed full of colourful flowers sit on the left near the house.
