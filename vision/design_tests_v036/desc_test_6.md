## Free description

It is a sunny day in a back garden. A wooden fence with a gate runs across the back, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door stands on the right. A paved path runs from the gate down to the front left corner. In the front left, a woman with dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers stands on the path holding a very large silver fork, and she is looking down at it. The fork is shaped like a dinner fork, not a garden fork. Its prongs rest on the path next to a small pile of dirt with a puff of grey dust, and a red dustpan lies beside the pile. It looks as if she is trying to sweep dirt into the dustpan with the fork, which is the wrong tool. Just behind her, on the grass beside the path, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is sweeping a pile of orange and red autumn leaves with a proper broom. The rest of the garden is calm: washing hangs on a line, flowers grow in a bed, and an empty wheelbarrow and a hose reel sit on the lawn. The giant table fork is the one thing that looks wrong.
