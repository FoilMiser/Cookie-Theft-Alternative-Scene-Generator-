On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands holding a spade with its blade in the ground beside a mound of brown soil, looking down as if digging. There is also a flower bed, a couple of bushes, an empty wheelbarrow, a red dish on the grass, and two potted plants on the path.
