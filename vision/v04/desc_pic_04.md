# Picture 04

## Free description

This is a busy, sunny back garden, with a wooden fence and gate at the back left and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. Near the gate, a man in a red shirt sweeps the path with a broom. A black-and-white cat walks along the top of the fence toward a small birdhouse on a pole. Left of centre, a woman in a yellow dress throws up both arms with her mouth open as the wind blows a blue cap away into the air. An old woman with glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt stands at the front with her hands on her hips. She is wearing an upside-down blue bucket on her head like a hat. A delivery man in an orange cap and high-visibility vest walks left while looking at his phone, straight toward a cardboard box on the grass, so he seems about to trip over it. By the house, a woman with a ponytail flies an orange kite whose string passes across the tree. At the front, a lawn sprinkler sprays water onto another cardboard box with drops on it, so that box is getting wet. On the right, a bearded man in a green shirt sits on a bench eating a red apple, while a brown dog with a red bandana pushes its nose at his knee, perhaps begging for food. An empty swing frame with no swings stands on the left, and a watering can, a red dish, an empty wheelbarrow and two potted plants are scattered over the lawn and path.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The blue thing on the old woman's head looks like an upside-down bucket, but it could be a tall blue hat.
- It is unclear whose cap is blowing away. It seems to come from the woman in yellow, but it could belong to someone else.
- The swing frame has no swings. Either the swings are missing, or it is a different kind of frame.
- The teal round object by the flower bed could be a garden hose reel (though no hose is visible), a fan, or something else.
- The kite string is drawn across the lower part of the tree. It could be passing in front of the tree or caught in it.
- The cat on the fence could be heading for the birdhouse to hunt, or just walking along.
- The dog could be begging for the apple, sniffing the man, or nuzzling his knee.
- The broom man's eyes look closed. He could be sweeping or just leaning on the broom.
- The box in front of the delivery man could be one he dropped or one left there. He looks likely to trip on it.
- The woman in yellow stands just behind and beside the swing frame. At a glance she can look as if she is inside or on the frame, though her feet are further back.
- The wind is blowing a cap away, but the woman's dress and hair and the other people show no wind.
