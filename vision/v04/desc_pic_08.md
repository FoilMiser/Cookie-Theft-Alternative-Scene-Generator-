## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate on the right, and a sandy path with stepping stones in the front right. In the front left, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands with her hands on her hips and an upside-down blue bucket on her head like a hat. Her eyes are closed and she looks pleased with herself. Behind her, a bearded man in a green shirt and beige trousers kneels at a round flower bed, reaching towards a potted pink flower as if planting it. At the back by the fence, a small girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes is flying an orange kite with a bow tail, high in the sky next to a cloud. In the middle of the lawn a sprinkler is spraying arcs of water towards the left, and one arc is landing on a cardboard box sitting on the grass, which has water drops on top of it. An older man in a blue cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is walking left while looking down at a phone, heading towards a red ball on the grass and the sprinkler, so he might trip over the ball or walk into the spray. At the back right, a man in an orange cap, orange high-visibility vest and blue trousers stands in front of the fence with one fist raised and a big smile. The front of the garden also has a wheelbarrow, a red dish or frisbee on the grass, a teal watering can, two potted plants on the path, and an empty bench. The strangest things are the woman wearing a bucket on her head and the sprinkler watering a cardboard box instead of plants.
