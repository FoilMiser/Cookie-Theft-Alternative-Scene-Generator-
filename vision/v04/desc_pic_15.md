# Picture 15 — description

## Free description

It is a sunny day in a park. A winding sandy path runs on the left, a pond with bulrushes is on the right, and trees, bushes and green hills are behind. On the path at the front left, a woman in a yellow dress is sweeping with a long-handled broom while looking towards the viewer. Further back, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt stands next to a lamppost holding a small grey object up to her face, right at the lamp. It looks as if she is photographing it with a camera or looking at it through binoculars. In the middle, an elderly man in a blue cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat walks to the right while reading a small book or phone. He seems to have a white band or bandage across his forehead. He is heading towards a red ball sitting on the corner of an empty red-and-white picnic blanket, and looks likely to trip over it. On the right, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans holds a green watering can beside a flower bed, but the water curves upwards, away from the flowers and towards the pond. A white duck floats on the pond right where that stream of water ends, with drops falling beside it. There is also a grey stone on the grass, two green litter bins and a flowerpot on the lawn. The strange things are the water from the watering can flowing upwards, the lamppost being barely taller than the old woman, and the white band under the old man's cap.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- **Old woman at the lamp:** She could be taking a photo of the lamp with a camera (the white strap down her front could be a camera strap). She could be looking at it through binoculars, or doing something to the lamp, such as fixing it or changing the bulb.
- **Height:** The lamppost is drawn barely taller than her, so either she is unusually tall or the lamppost is unusually short.
- **White band on the old man's head:** It could be a bandage wrapped round his forehead, white hair showing under the cap, or part of the cap.
- **What the old man is reading:** It could be a small book or a phone.
- **Watering-can stream:** It is drawn as a thick pale-blue tube with white dashes, so it could be read as a hose rather than water. Either way, it rises upwards and away from the flowers, which is impossible for water poured from a can.
- **Drops near the duck:** They could be spray from the top of the water stream or water dripping off the duck.
- **Woman sweeping:** She looks at the viewer rather than at what she is sweeping.
- **Lamp:** The lamp is yellow, so it could be switched on in daytime or just painted yellow.
