# Picture 03

## Free description

This is a park on a grey, overcast day. Three dark storm clouds hang overhead as if rain is coming, and it seems windy. In the centre, a man in a red shirt and blue trousers throws his hands up with his mouth open in surprise, because his blue cap has blown off and is flying away, with white wind lines beside him. On the left, a girl in a yellow top and orange skirt seems to have thrown a red-and-white ball into the pond. A dotted line arcs from her direction over a white duck to the ball floating beside it, and a little girl in a pink dress at the pond's edge reaches toward the water. A man in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest walks through the middle staring at his phone. He passes the corner of a red-and-white picnic blanket and is about to walk into a blue bucket right in front of his feet. Behind him, a black-and-white cat creeps toward a blue bird pecking the ground near a lamp post, as if about to pounce, and a brown dog with a red collar crouches low on the grass in the bottom left. On the right, a woman with a ponytail stands by a winding path taking a photo, and an old man in a cap and brown waistcoat stands by a tree with one arm raised to his forehead. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands right of centre, and oddly its wheel is square. There is also a rubbish bin in the bottom-left corner and a potted plant at the right edge.
