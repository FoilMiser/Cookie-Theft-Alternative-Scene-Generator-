# Picture 14 — description

## Free description

It is a sunny day in a park. A winding sandy path runs on the left, a pond with bulrushes is behind, and there is a lamppost, trees, bushes and green hills in the distance. A purple diamond-shaped kite is caught in the leaves of the big tree on the left. A child with curly dark hair, a yellow top and orange shorts stands just behind the park bench beside the path, holding the kite string up with one arm as if trying to tug the kite free. A bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers sits on the bench reading a blue book, and he isn't looking at the child. In the middle, a woman with long dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers walks to the left, leading a tan dog with floppy ears on a red lead. On the right, a person with a brown ponytail, a blue hoodie and dark trousers is jogging to the left past a bed of colourful flowers. The jogger and the dog walker are both heading left, with the jogger coming up behind. The main problem in the scene is the kite stuck in the tree. Nothing else looks clearly impossible.
