# Picture 05

## Free description

This is a busy, sunny town street with a row of four coloured shop buildings behind a paved square. On the left, a man in a red shirt and blue trousers walks along carrying a cardboard box in front of a tree. A bare branch of the tree sticks out right behind his neck, so it looks as if it goes through him or he is carrying it on his shoulder. Behind him, by a teal door, a man in a blue hoodie throws up his arms with his mouth open as the wind blows his red cap off. Beside him, a little girl in a pink dress stands next to a parked teal bicycle and reaches toward the door. In the middle, a girl in a yellow top and orange skirt has just thrown a red-and-white ball, which flies left along a dotted line toward the peach shop's window. A woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench nearby, talking on a red phone with her eyes closed. To the right, an old woman with grey hair stands behind the street lamp pouring from a watering can, but the stream of water curves up into the air instead of down onto the flowers below. A bearded man in a green shirt walks past the fruit stall looking at his phone, heading toward a cardboard box on the ground. In the foreground, a black-and-white cat creeps toward a red bird pecking the ground, as if about to pounce, and a blue bird flies near the sun at top right.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The water from the watering can flows upward, which is impossible. Another reading is that the stream is pouring from above down into the can. Either way, the water does not go onto the flowers below.
- The lamp post runs vertically in front of the old woman, so she looks cut in half by it, or as if the post passes through her.
- The tree branch runs behind the neck and shoulders of the man carrying the box. It can look as if the branch goes through his neck, or as if he is carrying a log on his shoulder.
- The red object at the yellow girl's head could be a hair bow, or a second ball held in her raised hand.
- It is unclear where the ball will land: the peach shop's window, the man in the blue hoodie, or the little girl in pink.
- The little girl in pink could be reaching for the door handle (opening the door), or reaching toward the ball or the bicycle.
- The bicycle stands upright by itself with no visible kickstand.
- The bearded man looking at his phone could walk into the box on the ground.
- The blue bird overlaps the edge of the sun's rays.
- The seated woman's eyes are closed. She could be laughing, enjoying the call, or dozing.
