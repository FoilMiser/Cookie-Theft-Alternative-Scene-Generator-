# Picture 07

## Free description

This is a sunny day on a paved town street or square, with a row of colourful two-storey shops behind: a pink one with an orange-and-white awning, a pale green one with a green-and-white awning, a peach one with a teal door, and a cream one with a red-and-white awning. In the front left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is walking a brown dog on a red lead. Behind them, a woman in a yellow dress is carrying a cardboard box in both hands and walking towards the right. In the front centre, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, wearing a teal top and a dark skirt, is walking while looking down at a small blue phone (or little book) in her hands. A cardboard box is sitting on the pavement just in front of her and also directly ahead of the dog, so it looks like the elderly woman, or the dog, might bump into or trip over it. Further back, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is walking right, carrying a yellow shopping bag, and passing behind a wooden bench. There is an unattended fruit stall on the left with crates of green, yellow, orange and red fruit. Also on the street are a black lamp post, a planter box of flowers, a teal bicycle parked by itself, a tree growing out of the pavement, a green rubbish bin, and two small round white tables, each with a red object on it and no chairs. Nothing looks clearly impossible, but the box lying in the walkway looks like a hazard.
