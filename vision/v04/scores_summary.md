# v04 picture-description scoring summary

Scored: 15 pictures, 206 key items. Source: `desc_pic_01..15.md` scored against `keys.json`. Per-item evidence is in `scores.json`.

How the scores were given:
- **1**: the right meaning is stated, including with "as if" or "seems" when no competing reading is given.
- **0.5**: the element was seen but the action or meaning is only half right, or the target is offered as one of two or more equal readings ("could be X, Y or Z", "perhaps").
- **misread**: the element was seen but read as something else. Misreads count as **0** in the means.
- Wrong age words were not penalised. Teenager characters were often called "woman", "man" or "boy".

## Common failure patterns

1. **Inferences stated only as possibilities (7 items).** Intentions and causes are listed as one of several readings. The dog-begging inference got 0.5 in all three bench-and-dog pictures (04, 10, 13). The others are the kite stuck in the tree, the balloon let go, the cat stalking and the bucket trip.
2. **Throw/catch and spatial links lost (7 items).** No catcher was identified in any of the three catch scenes (03, 05, 13). The ball's path was read as ending in the pond, at a shop window or over the fence. Related links were also lost: the kite in front of the tree rather than caught in it, the bucket not placed in the walker's path, and the balloon hovering low. In four pictures (01, 03, 05, 13) the thrower also seems to hold a second red ball.
3. **Gestures misread (5 items).** A wave drawn as a raised fist reads as cheering or flexing (08, 11), and a waving hand read as gripping a swing frame (13). Pointing at the sky read as shading the eyes (03). Reaching up for the balloon read as surprise or cheering (10).
4. **Animal posture or state (4 items).** Sleeping animals are read as crouching or creeping (dog in 03, cat in 09). The begging dog reads as standing and sniffing at the knee rather than sitting and looking up (04, 10).
5. **Small handheld props default to "phone" (4 items, plus credited cases).** The cup became a phone (05), and the drink (09), the apple (10) and the camera (15) were all ambiguous. Every reading-while-walking figure (7 of 7) was said to hold a phone, and three were described with a phone only. Those items were still credited, because the distracted-walker meaning came through.
