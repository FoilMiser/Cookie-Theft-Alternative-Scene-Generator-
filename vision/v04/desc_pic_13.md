## Free description

A bearded man in a green shirt stands holding one leg of a swing set that has no swings, while a brown dog bows down in front of him with its nose at his hand. On the right, a child in yellow and orange jumps with both arms raised near the gate. The odd or impossible things are the watering-can water flowing upwards, the sprinkler soaking a cardboard box, the swing frame with no swings, and the clothesline passing through the man's head.
