# Picture 06

## Free description

This is a sunny day in a park, with a bright sun in the top left, two small white clouds, green hills and hedges in the background, a pond on the right and a winding sandy footpath on the left. In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands on the grass holding a long fishing rod. Her line runs out over the pond to a red-and-white float sitting in the water with a little ripple ring around it. Directly in front of her a boy in a blue hoodie and dark trousers has both fists raised above his head, eyes squeezed shut and mouth open in a big grin, knees bent as if he is jumping or cheering. Because the float has ripples around it, he could be cheering because a fish is biting, but no fish is visible. In the front right, a bearded man in a green shirt and beige trousers kneels on one knee beside a round flower bed of colourful flowers. He holds a small grey hand tool, maybe a trowel, next to a pot with a pink flower in it, as if he is about to plant it. A red-and-white checked picnic blanket lies empty on the grass between the boy and the man, with no food or people on it. A wooden bench by the path is also empty, and there is a black lamp post with a yellow lamp behind the boy. Nothing looks clearly impossible. The only odd-looking thing is the tree on the left, which has a single bare branch sticking straight out sideways from the trunk below the leaves.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The boy's pose: he could be jumping for joy, cheering (perhaps at the woman's fishing, if a fish is biting), dancing, or stretching. His eyes are closed, so it isn't clear what he is reacting to.
- The ripple around the float could mean a fish is biting, or it could just be how a floating bobber is drawn.
- The object in the man's hand is small and grey, most likely a trowel or small garden fork. He may be about to dig a hole for the potted flower, or tending the flower bed in general.
- The woman stands a few steps back from the pond on dry grass rather than at the water's edge. With her long rod this works, but it looks a bit far.
- The boy is drawn right in front of the woman, and his raised hand and head overlap her legs and shoes. They look very close together, almost touching.
- The tree on the left has one bare stub branch sticking straight out sideways from the trunk below the leaves, which looks a little unnatural.
- The lamp at the top of the post is yellow. It may simply be the colour of the glass, or the lamp may be switched on in daylight.
- The empty picnic blanket could mean someone is about to have a picnic, or that someone has left it there.
