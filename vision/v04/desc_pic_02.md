# Picture 02

## Free description

It is a sunny day in a back garden. A wooden fence runs across the back with a wooden gate at the far left, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door stands on the right. Two children are flying kites. A small girl in a pink dress near the back fence holds the string of a purple kite flying near the tree beside the house. A girl in a yellow top and orange skirt, standing in front of a flower bed, holds the string of an orange kite flying high in the middle of the sky. The two kite strings cross in mid-air, so it looks as if they may be about to tangle. The purple kite also overlaps the tree, so it may get caught in the branches. In the foreground, a man in an orange cap, an orange high-visibility vest and blue trousers walks across the lawn carrying a cardboard parcel, like a delivery person. He faces left, toward the gate and the path, rather than toward the house door. A path with oval stepping stones runs from the gate down the left side, with two potted plants on it. A teal watering can and a red round dish (a frisbee or a pet bowl) lie on the grass, but no dog or other animal is visible.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The strings cross, so it is hard to be sure which string belongs to which child. My reading is pink girl to purple kite and yellow girl to orange kite. At the crossing point they could be read the other way round.
- The purple kite overlaps the tree's leaves. It could be flying in front of the tree or already caught in it.
- The girl in yellow overlaps the flower bed. Her feet seem to be on the grass in front of it, but she could also be read as standing in the flowers.
- The man walks leftward, away from the house door. It is unclear whether he is arriving with the parcel or leaving with it.
- The red dish could be a frisbee or a dog's bowl, although there is no dog.
- The girl in pink is drawn much smaller and thinner than the others. She reads either as a very young child or as someone far away.
- Left of the gate, the fence has only horizontal rails and no boards.
