# Picture 01

## Free description

It is a sunny day on a town shopping street. Four two-storey shop buildings run across the back (cream, peach, pale green and pink), most with striped awnings, and in front of them is a wide paved square. In the middle of the square, a woman with a brown ponytail, blue top and dark trousers stands in a throwing pose. Her right arm is raised behind her head holding a red ball, and her left arm points forward. A red-and-white ball is in the air to her right, and a dotted line shows it travelling from her hand toward a man. The man stands right of centre in an orange cap, an orange high-visibility vest over a grey shirt and blue trousers. He holds both arms out toward her with open hands, as if about to catch the ball. A red bird is perched on top of a black street lamp on the right, and an unattended fruit and vegetable stall with a striped canopy stands at the far right, with a cardboard box beside it. On the left there is a tree, a green rubbish bin, a small round café table with a red cup on it, and a potted plant in the bottom-left corner. Several things look odd. The woman still seems to be holding a ball even though one is already flying. A planter behind the man's head makes it look as if flowers are sprouting from his cap. The bird on the lamp has a grey shadow drawn on the wall behind it.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Two balls, or one ball shown twice? The woman holds a plain red ball overhead while a red-and-white ball is already in flight. Either there are two balls, or the same ball is drawn twice (before and during the throw).
- The flying ball sits right at the bottom edge of the green shop's window, where the building meets the pavement. So it could also be read as a ball resting on the window ledge or on the ground against the wall, rather than flying through the air.
- The flower planter is directly behind the man's head, so the flowers look as if they are growing out of his cap.
- A grey oval shadow is drawn on the green wall just under the bird on the lamp. It floats in mid-air on the wall instead of lying on the ground.
- The tree has a bare branch sticking straight out sideways from the trunk below the leaves. It looks like a stick poking out, or an arm.
- I can't tell whether the man's cap brim faces forward or backward.
- The red item on the café table is probably a cup, but it could be a small box or can.
- The lamp post stands very close to the green building, right in front of its awning.
