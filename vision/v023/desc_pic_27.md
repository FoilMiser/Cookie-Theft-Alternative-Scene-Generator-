# Picture 27

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny playground behind a long railing fence, with a swing set in the middle, a slide on the right and a sandbox in the front right. In the tree on the left, a blue ball is stuck on a branch. A young person in a blue hoodie stands below it, pointing up at it with an open mouth. A red bird flies across the sky above. A woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench reading a red book. Next to her on the bench is a sandwich, and a blue bird at the end of the bench is pecking at it or about to steal it, while she doesn't notice. An older woman with grey hair and glasses stands near the fence licking a pink ice-cream cone. A girl in a pink dress is jumping in the air with both arms up, her shadow on the ground below her. On the right, a boy in a yellow shirt holds a red and a yellow balloon and reaches up after a purple balloon that is floating away. A brown dog is sitting up holding open a newspaper as if reading it, which is impossible, and a small white puppy is curled up asleep near the sandbox. In the bottom left, an older man in a cap sips a red drink through a straw while sitting in a sitting position with no chair or seat under him, so he is sitting on thin air.
