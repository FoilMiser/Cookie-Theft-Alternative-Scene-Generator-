# Picture 11

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence along the back, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, and a gate and paved path on the right. In the middle, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is holding the end of a green garden hose out in front of her with both hands. Only a single drop of water is coming out, and she is looking at the nozzle as if puzzled about why it isn't working. The reason is visible: a white goat with small horns is standing on the hose between her and the hose reel by the house. On the reel side of the goat the hose is swollen and bulging, as if water is building up behind the goat's hoof. It looks as if water will burst out and maybe spray the woman when the goat steps off. In the bottom-right corner a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is crouched down on the path in a playful pose next to a yellow tennis ball, wagging its tail. A grey wheelbarrow stands at the back of the lawn, and its wheel looks square instead of round. There is also a flower bed with colourful flowers by the house, a bush, a tree behind the house, a sun at top right and two clouds. A goat standing in an ordinary back garden is itself unusual.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The wheelbarrow's wheel is a square (a dark square frame with green inside) rather than a circle. It could be a deliberately wrong detail, or the square might be meant as a box or crate next to the wheelbarrow, but it seems to be joined to the wheelbarrow's axle like a wheel.
- The bulge in the hose reads as trapped water pressure from the goat standing on it. It could also just be a thicker section or a fitting in the hose.
- I can't tell whether the nozzle is pointed at the woman's face or just at the ground in front of her. Her face is turned toward it, so a spray could hit her.
- It is unclear whether the goat is standing on the hose on purpose or by accident; it seems to be looking at the woman.
