# Picture 59

## Free description

This is a busy, sunny day in a park with green hills, a pond on the left, a winding sandy path on the right, a lamp post in the middle and an empty wooden bench. At the front left, an older man in a cap, glasses, white shirt and brown vest is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red leash. Behind him, a white duck is swimming on the pond toward some small crumbs floating in the water, and a bearded man in a green shirt at the edge of the pond is holding a small white bag or cup and throwing the crumbs to the duck. In the middle at the back, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is walking to the right carrying a cardboard box. At the front, a little boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is holding an empty ice-cream cone and has his hand to his head, looking upset, because his pink scoop of ice cream has fallen on the grass and melted into a puddle. A brown dog with a red collar, with no owner or leash in sight, has its nose down at the dropped ice cream and looks about to eat or lick it. On the right, a blue ball is stuck up in the branches of a tree, and a little girl in a pink dress is pointing up at it, as if she wants it back. At the far right front, an older woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal cardigan and grey skirt is walking to the right carrying a yellow shopping bag.
