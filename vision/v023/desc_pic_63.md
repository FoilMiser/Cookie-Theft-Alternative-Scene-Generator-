# Picture 63

## Free description

This is a sunny playground surrounded by a grey metal railing fence, with a slide on the left, a tree on the right and hedges behind the fence. On the right side there is a red seesaw on a grey triangular stand. A bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers sits on the low left end of the seesaw with his feet on the ground, reading an open book. On the high right end, a little girl in a pink dress is sitting up in the air holding the handle, her feet dangling with small motion lines under them as if she is kicking her legs. Because the man is heavy and busy reading, the girl seems stuck up in the air and cannot come down. No one else is in the playground; the slide, a trash can, a bush and a grey rock are unused.
