# Picture 44

## Free description
This is a sunny backyard with a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the left, a tree behind it, a wooden picket fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a pale path running down the right side. A man with dark skin and a beard, wearing a green shirt and khaki trousers, stands near the house watering a round flower bed with a garden hose; an arc of water falls on the flowers, and the hose runs back across the grass to a hose reel on the lawn at the left. On top of the fence, a small red bird is perched, and an orange cat is crouched low and creeping along the fence toward it, as if about to pounce. On the right side of the yard is a grey metal swing set with one red swing seat. The strange thing is a brown cardboard box hanging in the sky above the fence on the right, tilted, with nothing holding it and nobody nearby who could have thrown it. The sun is in the upper-right corner with two white clouds.
