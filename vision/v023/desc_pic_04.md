# pic_04

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream-coloured house with red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, and a wooden gate at the far right with a paved path. On the left, a man with dark skin, a dark beard and a green shirt with khaki trousers is watering a round flowerbed with a teal watering can; strangely, the water comes out of the spout as a blue stream that curves upward into the air with drops at the top, rather than pouring down onto the flowers. In the middle, under a clothesline holding a pair of blue jeans and a yellow-and-red striped towel, a white goat with small horns is bending down eating a tuft of grass. On the right is a swing set with one red seat hanging empty. At the far right, an old man with a grey moustache wearing a grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is sweeping the path near the gate with a broom with yellow bristles. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands at the bottom centre. A goat in a back garden is unusual, and it is standing right under the washing, so it might go for the laundry next.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The white animal is most likely a goat (it has small horns); someone could also read it as a white dog or lamb.
- The goat could simply be grazing, or it could be read as about to eat the laundry hanging just above it; it is not touching the laundry.
- The man watering is drawn facing away/three-quarter rear, so his face is only partly visible.
- Whether the goat belongs there or has wandered in (e.g. through the gate) is not shown.
