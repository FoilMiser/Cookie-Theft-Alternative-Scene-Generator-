# Picture 42

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills, trees and bushes, a blue pond with reeds on the left, a tall black lamp post in the centre, a winding sandy path on the right and an empty wooden bench beside it. At the front left, a woman with a long dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers is watering a round flower bed with a teal watering can. In the middle, a little girl in a pink dress holds a red balloon and a purple balloon by their strings in one hand, and reaches up with her other arm toward a yellow balloon that has escaped and is floating away just out of her reach. Behind her, a young woman with a brown ponytail, a blue hoodie and dark trousers is running fast toward the right across the grass, with speed lines behind her. In the bottom-right corner, a small brown-and-white dog (it looks like a puppy) is curled up asleep on the grass. A green rubbish bin stands in the bottom-left corner. It looks like the yellow balloon is about to float away into the sky. The sun is in the upper-right corner with two white clouds.
