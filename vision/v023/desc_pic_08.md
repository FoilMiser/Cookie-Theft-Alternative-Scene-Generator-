## Free description
This is a busy, sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a wooden gate and paved path at the far left, and a cream house with red roof and blue door on the right. On the left, an old man in a grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is painting the fence blue with a paintbrush; a section of the fence beside the gate is already blue, and a paint tin sits on a paint tray on the grass at his feet. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has stepped in the blue paint and is walking away across the lawn, leaving a trail of blue paw prints from the paint tray to where it stands, its paws blue. In the centre, a woman (or girl) with a brown ponytail in a blue top and black trousers is holding a kite string and looking up, but her purple kite is caught in the tree beside the house, with its bow tail dangling. On top of the fence, an orange cat is creeping along toward a small blue bird perched on the fence, as if about to pounce on it, and a red bird is flying in the sky near the sun. On the right, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is kneeling by a flowerbed, placing or planting a pot with a pink flower. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits at the bottom of the picture. Several little mishaps are happening at once: the dog spreading paint, the kite stuck in the tree, and the cat stalking the bird.
