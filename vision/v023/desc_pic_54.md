# pic_54

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and a blue door, with a wooden fence across the back and a gate at the right. In the middle of the lawn, a girl in a yellow dress and red shoes is reading a red book. She is in a sitting position, with her knees bent as if on a chair, but there is no chair, bench or anything else under her, so she seems to be sitting on thin air. There is a shadow on the grass beneath her. Around her are a bed of colourful flowers by the house, two potted plants near the fence, an empty wheelbarrow in front and a teal watering can on the grass at the right. A paved path leads from the gate down the right side. The sun is shining in the top right with two white clouds. The missing seat under the girl is the one thing that looks impossible.
