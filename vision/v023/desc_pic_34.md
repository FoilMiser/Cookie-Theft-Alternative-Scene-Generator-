# Picture 34

## Free description
This is a quiet, sunny park with rolling green hills in the background, a blue pond on the left, a winding sandy path on the right, and trees and bushes around the edges. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers with long dark hair is standing at the right-hand edge of the pond, tossing small crumbs of bread onto the water with one hand while holding a small white bag in the other. A white duck with an orange beak is swimming quickly towards the crumbs, with wake lines on the water behind it. Some of the crumbs are on the water near the duck and a few seem to be still in the air between her hand and the pond. Behind the woman there is a tall black lamp post and a green rubbish bin. To the right there is an empty wooden bench beside the path, and there are reeds/bulrushes at both ends of the pond. In the bottom left corner there is another green rubbish bin and a round bed of colourful flowers, and there is a grey rock on the grass at the bottom centre. Nothing in the scene looks wrong or impossible; it is just one person and one duck.
