# Picture 28

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a house on the left (red roof, blue door, window), a tall wooden fence with a gate on the right, and a paved path leading to the gate. Near the house is a bed of colourful flowers and a hose reel. The green-blue hose runs from the reel across the grass to an older woman with grey hair in a bun, who wears a teal top and grey skirt. She is holding the end of the hose right up to her face and peering into it, with just a small drop of water at the tip. That is because a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is standing on the hose. Behind its feet the hose has swollen into a big bulge where the water is trapped. It looks as if the woman is about to get sprayed in the face when the dog steps off. On the right, a small girl in a pink dress sits on a swing and is swinging, with motion lines beside her. In the bottom right is a grey wheelbarrow whose wheel is square instead of round, which would not roll.

- The bulge in the hose could be water building up behind the dog's paw, or just a thicker part of the hose. Next to the dog, the first reading seems intended.
- I can't tell whether the dog is deliberately standing on the hose or just happens to be there. It is looking toward the woman.
- The woman might be looking into the nozzle, or she might be about to drink from it. Her face is very close to the tip either way.
