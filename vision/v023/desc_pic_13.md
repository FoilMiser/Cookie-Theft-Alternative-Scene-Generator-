# Picture 13

## Free description
This is a sunny playground surrounded by a dark metal railing fence, with green bushes and hills behind it. On the right, two children are playing on a red seesaw on a grey triangular stand. On the left end, which is down on the ground, sits a child with short dark curly hair in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts, with feet touching the ground. On the right end, which is up in the air, sits a girl with dark hair in a pink dress, holding the handle, with her feet dangling. They seem to be taking turns going up and down. On the left there is a red-framed slide with a grey metal ladder up one side and a yellow-and-orange chute sloping down to the grass. In the front left is a wooden sandpit with a red bucket and a blue spade in it, and a grey rock beside it. There is also a green rubbish bin by the fence, a bush on the left, a large tree on the right, and a sun at top left with two clouds. Nobody is using the slide or the sandpit. Nothing looks wrong or impossible to me.
