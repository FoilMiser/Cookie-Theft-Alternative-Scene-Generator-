# Picture 64

## Free description

This is a calm, sunny park with rolling green hills, a winding sandy path on the left, an empty wooden bench, a tall lamp post, trees and bushes, and a blue pond with cattails on the right. An older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and grey skirt is standing on the grass in front of the pond, holding a fishing rod with both hands. Her fishing line hangs from the tip of the rod down into the pond, where a red-and-white float (bobber) sits on the water with a small ripple ring around it. She seems to be watching the float, waiting for a fish to bite. She is the only person in the picture. There is a flower bed with colourful flowers at the front right, a grey rock at the front, and two green trash cans.
