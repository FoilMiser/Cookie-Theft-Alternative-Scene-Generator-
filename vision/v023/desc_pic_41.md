# Picture 41

## Free description
This is a sunny backyard with a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the left, a tree behind it, a wooden picket fence across the back with a gate on the right, and a pale path leading from the gate down the right side. A brown-and-white dog with a red collar sits next to the blue door holding a red leash in its mouth, as if it wants to go for a walk. In front of the house, a woman (or older girl) with short dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes is watering a round flower bed, but she is pouring the water out of a frying pan rather than a watering can, which is odd. Further back by the fence, an older man in a blue cap, brown vest, white shirt and dark trousers is digging a hole in the lawn with a shovel, with a mound of dirt next to it and a clump of dirt on his shovel. On the right stands a grey metal swing set with one red swing seat hanging still. At the bottom centre is an empty grey wheelbarrow with a wooden handle. The sun is in the upper-right corner with two white clouds.
