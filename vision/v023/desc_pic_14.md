# Picture 14

## Free description
This is a sunny park with a pond on the right, a winding sandy path on the left, hills and trees behind and a tall black lamp post in the middle. In the background, a woman with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal leggings is jogging or running to the left across the grass, with speed lines behind her. In the front right, an older man in a blue-grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is standing by the pond scattering seeds or crumbs from a small white bag or carton. The seeds fall in a trail down to two small birds on the grass by his feet, a red one and a blue one, which are both pecking at them. On the pond a white duck is swimming. There is an empty wooden bench in the middle, a grey rock at the front, a green rubbish bin at the bottom right, reeds at both ends of the pond, and bushes and trees around the edges. The sky has a sun at top left and two clouds. It is a peaceful scene and I did not notice anything impossible.
