# Picture 31

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate on the far left, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. On the left, a girl in a yellow dress is standing on a paved path, sweeping it with a broom. In the middle of the lawn, an older man in a grey cap, brown vest, white long sleeves and dark trousers is walking along with his eyes on an open book he is holding out in front of him. A red bucket with blue water in it is sitting on the grass just ahead of him, and because he is reading and not looking where he is going, it looks like he could walk into it or trip over it. At the back on the right, a woman in a blue hoodie with a brown ponytail is painting the fence blue with a brush. A section of the fence boards behind her has already been painted blue, and a paint tin with blue paint is on the grass near her feet. There is a birdhouse on a tall pole, two potted plants, some bushes and a round flower bed full of colourful flowers near the house. Nothing in the scene looks impossible.
