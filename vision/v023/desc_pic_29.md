# Picture 29

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a mostly empty playground behind a long dark railing fence. On the left is a big tree, with a small bush near it. In the middle is a swing set with one empty red swing hanging still. On the right is a red slide with orange and yellow chute, and one child is on it. The child has dark skin and dark curly or braided hair and wears a yellow shirt and orange shorts. The child is going down the slide feet first with their hands up near their head. A green rubbish bin stands by the fence between the swings and the slide, there is a bush on the far right, and a grey rock sits in the front right. The whole front half of the picture is empty grass. Nothing looks odd or impossible.
