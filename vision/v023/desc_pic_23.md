# Picture 23

## Free description
This is a sunny park with rolling green hills, a pond on the left, a curving path on the right, a tall lamp post and several trees. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands at the edge of the pond tossing crumbs to a white duck, which is swimming toward the crumbs floating on the water. In the front left, a little girl in a pink dress holds a blue and a red balloon. With her mouth open, she reaches up toward a yellow balloon that has escaped and is drifting away over the pond. In the middle front, a brown dog with a red collar sits on the grass holding open a newspaper with its paws as if reading it, which is impossible. On a bench, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers reads a red book while a blue bird pecks at the sandwich beside her without her noticing. An older man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat walks along the path carrying a yellow shopping bag. An orange cat is curled up asleep on the path right by his feet, so he could trip over it or step on it. On the far right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts points up at a blue ball stuck in the branches of a tree. In the background, a woman in a blue top with a ponytail strolls along eating a pink ice-cream cone, and a green rubbish bin, a flower bed and a grey rock sit at the front.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The dog reading a newspaper is impossible.
- The woman feeding the duck holds a small white item in her other hand. It could be a bag of bread or a cup.
- It is not completely clear which way the old man is walking along the path, but the cat lies right beside his front foot and he is not looking down, so he might step on it or trip.
- The yellow balloon has escaped. It was probably the girl's, since she is reaching for it, but it could also have come from somewhere else.
- The woman eating ice cream in the background is about the same size as the people in the foreground even though she is further away. This makes her look slightly large, or as if she is walking along the top of the hill.
- The blue thing on the bench reads as a blue bird with its tail raised, eating the sandwich. It could possibly be read as a blue object lying on the bench.
- The ball is resting on a branch among the leaves, so it reads as stuck in the tree rather than floating.
