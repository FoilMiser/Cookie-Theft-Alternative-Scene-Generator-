# Picture 61

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a gate on the left, a birdhouse on a pole, a house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and a colourful flower bed. In the middle, an older woman with grey hair, glasses, a teal cardigan and grey skirt is painting the fence blue with a paintbrush; she has already painted a few planks, and a paint tray and a paint can of blue paint sit on the grass beside her. A white dog with brown spots has stepped in the blue paint: its paws are blue and a trail of blue paw prints leads across the grass from the paint tray to where the dog now stands on the paved path on the left. Along the top of the fence a black-and-white cat is creeping toward a red bird perched on the fence a little further along, as if about to pounce. A little girl in a pink dress is holding a yellow and a purple balloon, while a red balloon has escaped and is floating away; she is reaching up for it. At the front, a brown dog with a red collar is sitting up and reading a newspaper held in its paws, next to a red bowl of dog food. On the path at the bottom left, an orange cat is curled up asleep, and on the right a man in a green shirt sits on a bench reading a red book. Two things look impossible: there is an orange fish in the tree behind the fence, and the brown dog is reading a newspaper like a person.
