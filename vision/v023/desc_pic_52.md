# pic_52

## Free description
This is a back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and a blue front door, under a grey sky with three dark storm clouds overhead. Near the back fence, an older woman with grey hair in a bun is painting the wooden fence blue with a paintbrush; a section of the fence to her left is already blue, and a tin of blue paint sits on the grass by her feet. In the middle of the lawn, a bearded man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers is pointing up toward the sky, looking wide-eyed, as if warning that rain is coming. On the paved path at the right, a girl in a yellow dress is sweeping a little pile of dirt toward a red dustpan, but she is using a giant silver fork as a broom instead of a real broom. There is a swing set with one empty red seat, an empty wheelbarrow in the front, and a bed of colourful flowers by the house. It looks like it is about to rain on the fresh paint. The giant fork used for sweeping is clearly odd.
