## Free description
This is a back garden on a sunny but windy day: white wind streaks are drawn in the sky and across the fence. The cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, with an empty wooden bench in front of it and a round flowerbed next to it. In the centre, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and round glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, has both arms thrown up and her mouth open in surprise, because an orange cap is being blown off into the air above her head along with a few leaves. On the right is a swing set whose single red seat is hanging crooked and tilted, as if being pushed by the wind, with nobody on it. At the bottom there is an empty grey wheelbarrow and a teal watering can on the grass, and the paved path leads to a wooden gate at the far right. The sun is at the top right with two white clouds. Nobody else is in the garden.
