# Picture 16

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence across the back, a gate and paved path on the left and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the middle, a girl or young woman with short dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes is raking a small pile of leaves on the lawn with a long wooden rake. Behind her, a clothesline between two wooden posts has a yellow towel with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. On the right, an orange cat is walking along the very top of the fence, near the tree behind the house. In the front centre is a grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles, and its wheel is drawn as a square instead of a round wheel, which looks wrong because it couldn't roll properly. There are also two potted plants by the fence, a teal watering can on the left, a flower bed with colourful flowers on the right and a hose reel next to it. A bush stands by the house, and the sky has a sun at top left and two clouds. Otherwise it looks like an ordinary garden chore day.
