# Picture 32

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a long wooden fence across the back, and a wooden gate and paved path on the right. A boy in a yellow top and orange shorts near the back fence has just thrown a red ball; a dotted line shows its arc through the air towards a woman in a purple top and teal trousers. The woman has her arms stretched out in front of her and is about to catch the ball. By the blue front door, a brown dog is sitting holding a red lead in its mouth, as if it wants to be taken for a walk. In the front middle of the lawn, a white dog with brown spots is crouched down with its front end low and its tail up and wagging, next to a yellow-green tennis ball, as if it wants someone to play. On the right, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and a grey skirt is sweeping the paved path with a broom. There is a teal watering can on the grass near her, two potted plants, a colourful flower bed and a tree behind the house. Nothing in the scene looks impossible.
