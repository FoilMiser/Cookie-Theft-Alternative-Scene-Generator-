## Free description

This is a back garden on a sunny day, with the sun in the top left and two clouds. A wooden fence runs across the back with a wooden gate on the left, and a paved path leads from that gate down to the bottom left corner. On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door, with a tree behind it. In the centre-left foreground, a boy with brown hair, a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes is holding a long rake and raking a small pile of green and brown leaves at his feet. In the centre-right, an older man with grey hair, a blue cap, a white shirt, a brown vest and dark trousers is holding a teal watering can next to a round bed of colourful flowers. Oddly, the water from his watering can is shooting upward in a curved arc into the air, with drops at the top, instead of pouring down onto the flowers, which is not how a watering can works. On the far right, a brown-and-white dog is sitting in front of the blue door holding a red leash in its mouth, with the rest of the leash trailing on the ground, as if it wants to go for a walk. Two potted plants sit near the fence on the left.
