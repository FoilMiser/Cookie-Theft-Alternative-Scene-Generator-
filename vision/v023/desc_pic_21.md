# Picture 21

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the middle, a washing line holds a yellow striped towel and blue jeans. On the left path, a little girl in a pink dress has just thrown a red-and-white ball. A dotted line traces its path through the air toward a woman in a blue top with a brown ponytail, who stands by the fence with her hands out ready to catch it. At the front left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks across the lawn carrying a cardboard box in both hands. Near the house, an older man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat kneels by the flower bed with a small pot of pink flowers, as if he is planting. A garden sprinkler on a long hose is spraying arcs of water across the lawn, and the spray lands right on a cardboard box sitting on the grass in a puddle, soaking it. The woman carrying the other box is walking in the direction of the sprinkler and might walk into the spray. The thing that looks wrong is the cardboard box left under the sprinkler and getting wet.
