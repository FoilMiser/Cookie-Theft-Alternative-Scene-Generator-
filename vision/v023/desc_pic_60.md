# Picture 60

## Free description

This is a sunny playground surrounded by a grey metal railing fence, with bushes behind it, a tree on the left and a slide on the right. In the left-centre there is a red seesaw on a grey triangular stand. A little girl in a pink dress is sitting on the left end of the seesaw, which is high up in the air; she holds the handle and her feet are dangling, with little motion lines under them as if she is kicking her legs. On the other, low end of the seesaw sits an older man in a cap, glasses, white shirt, brown vest and dark trousers, with his feet on the ground, calmly reading an open book or newspaper. Because he is much heavier and is not paying attention, the girl seems to be stuck up in the air and cannot come down. Nobody else is in the playground. The slide on the right, a trash can by the fence, a bush and a grey rock are all unused.
