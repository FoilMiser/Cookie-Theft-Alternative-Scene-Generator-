# Picture 20

## Free description
This is a back garden on a dull, overcast day. The sky is grey and three dark storm clouds hang overhead, so it looks as though it is about to rain. A cream house with a red roof and blue door stands on the left, a wooden fence runs across the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path leads from the gate toward the front. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers kneels on one knee beside a round bed of colourful flowers, gardening, with a small pot of pink flowers in front of her. On the path on the right, a man in a red T-shirt and jeans points up at the dark clouds. His mouth is open in surprise or worry, as if he is warning that rain is coming. The woman is facing the flowers and doesn't seem to have noticed the clouds. A small brown-and-white puppy is curled up asleep on the lawn at the front, and a teal watering can stands on the grass nearby. Two potted plants sit near the fence, and there are a couple of green bushes. Nothing looks impossible.
