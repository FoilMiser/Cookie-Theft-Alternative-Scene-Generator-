## Free description
This is a sunny back garden behind a cream-coloured house with a red roof and a blue door on the right, and a tall wooden fence across the back with a wooden gate at the far left and a paved path leading from it. On the left, a person with short dark hair in a yellow dress is digging a hole in the lawn with a spade; there is a mound of dug-up earth beside the hole and a clump of dirt on the spade. In the middle, an older grey-haired woman in a teal top and grey skirt is reaching up to hang washing on a clothesline strung between two wooden posts; a white T-shirt and a pair of blue jeans are already pegged on the line. On the right, a man (or older boy) in a red shirt and blue jeans is watering a round flowerbed full of colourful flowers with a teal watering can. The odd thing is the water: it comes out of the spout as a curved blue stream that rises upward over the flowers, with drops at the top, instead of pouring down onto them. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands in the foreground at the bottom. The sky has a bright sun at the top left and two white clouds, and a tree is visible behind the fence near the house.
