It is a sunny day in a fenced playground. A tree stands on the left, and a slide, a sandbox and a bush are on the right. On the left a red seesaw is tipped down to the right. A small girl in a pink dress sits on the high left end, holding the handle. Her legs dangle and there are little motion lines under her feet, as if she is kicking them. On the low right end sits an older man in a grey cap, white long-sleeved shirt, brown vest and dark trousers. He is reading an open book or paper and not paying attention to the girl. Because he is so much heavier and is reading, the girl looks stuck up in the air and the seesaw is not going up and down. Nobody is using the slide or the sandbox. A red bucket and a blue spade sit in the sandbox. Nothing looks physically impossible. The odd part is a grown man sitting on a seesaw reading while a child waits at the other end.
