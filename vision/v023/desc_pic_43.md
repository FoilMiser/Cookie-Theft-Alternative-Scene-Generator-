# Picture 43

## Free description
This is a sunny backyard with a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the right, a tree behind it, and a wooden picket fence across the back with a gate and a pale path on the left. In the middle, a man with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans is painting the fence blue with a long paintbrush; a strip of about three fence boards is already blue, and a paint tray and an open paint can sit on the grass by his feet. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has walked through the blue paint: its paws are blue and it has left a trail of blue paw prints across the lawn from the paint tray, and it is still walking away toward the path. A black cat with white paws is walking along the top of the fence just next to the freshly painted section. On the right, a woman (or older girl) with short dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes is watering the flower bed by the house by pouring water from a frying pan, which is odd. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits at the front centre, and a wooden bench sits in the bottom-right corner. The sun is in the upper-left corner with two white clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The dog's trail suggests it stepped in the paint tray and is about to track paint further (possibly onto the path); the painter does not seem to have noticed.
