# Picture 22

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with no people in it. A cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, a wooden fence runs across the back with a gate on the right, a paved path leads from the gate, and the sun is in the top right corner. In the middle of the lawn, a white goat with small dark curved horns has its head down and is nibbling a tuft of grass, right next to a round bed of colourful flowers. A sprinkler on a long hose is spraying arcs of water to the right, and the water lands on a cardboard box sitting in a puddle on the grass, so the box is getting soaked. An empty wooden bench stands at the front left, and there are two potted plants near the fence and a couple of green bushes. Very oddly, an orange fish is sitting in the leafy top of the tree behind the house, and fish don't live in trees. A goat wandering loose in a garden is also unusual, and it looks as though it could move on to eat the flowers.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The orange shape in the tree is drawn as a simple fish (body, eye, tail fin). It could be a real fish in an impossible place, or a fish-shaped toy, kite or decoration caught in the tree. Either way it is out of place.
- I read the white animal as a goat because of its horns and hooves. At a glance it could be mistaken for a lamb or a white dog. Its pose is a little awkward, with the head tucked down low near its front legs.
- The box under the sprinkler could be a parcel left outside and being ruined, or something put there deliberately.
- The scene has no people at all. The bench is empty.
