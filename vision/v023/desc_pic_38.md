# Picture 38

## Free description
This is a sunny backyard scene with a cream-coloured house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the right, and a tall wooden picket fence running across the back. A woman (or older girl) with short dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes stands on the lawn in the centre-right, holding a garden hose and spraying an arc of water onto a round bed of colourful flowers. The hose runs from her hands down across the grass to a hose reel that sits at the edge of the flower bed near the house. An orange cat is walking along the top of the fence, in front of a tree that rises behind the fence. On the left there is a wooden gate in the fence with a pale concrete path leading from it down to the front of the picture, and a small birdhouse with a red roof on top of a tall wooden pole. The odd thing is a brown cardboard box hanging in the sky above the fence, tilted at an angle, with nobody nearby who could have thrown it and nothing holding it up. The sun is in the upper-left corner and there are two white clouds.
