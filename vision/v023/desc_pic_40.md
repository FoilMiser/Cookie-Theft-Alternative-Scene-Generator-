# Picture 40

## Free description
This is a busy, windy day in a park, with green hills, trees, bushes, a winding sandy path, a black lamp post and a blue pond with reeds on the right; white wind lines streak across the sky. On the hill at the back left, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers has his arms up because the wind has blown his red cap off his head, along with some green leaves. At the front left, a little girl in a pink dress is crying, holding an empty ice-cream cone, because her pink scoop of ice cream has fallen on the ground — and a brown dog with a red collar is sniffing at it, about to eat it. On a bench, a grey-haired old woman with glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt is reading a red book and does not notice that a blue bird is pecking at the long sandwich sitting next to her on the bench. In the middle front, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes walks along carrying a cardboard box. An older man in a blue cap and brown vest sits on a red-and-white checked picnic blanket beside a picnic basket with his eyes closed, as if dozing or enjoying the sun. On the right, a woman with a long dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers is watering a flower bed by pouring water out of a frying pan, which is a strange thing to water with. Most impossibly, a white dog with a brown ear patch is sitting up at the far right holding open a newspaper and reading it. A red bird is perched on top of the lamp post, a green rubbish bin stands in the bottom-right corner, and the sun is in the upper left with two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The man on the picnic blanket has his eyes closed and pink cheeks: he could be asleep/dozing, or awake and enjoying the sunshine (or even pursing his lips); I can't tell which.
- The man whose cap is blowing away is drawn about the same size as the foreground people even though he is standing far back near the hills, so he looks slightly too large for the distance.
- The pink thing on the ground could be read as a dropped ice-cream scoop (most likely, given the girl's empty cone) or as a fallen doughnut.
- The blue bird could be eating from the sandwich or about to carry part of it off.
- A dog reading a newspaper is impossible; watering flowers with a frying pan is odd.
- The woman in yellow carrying a box doesn't seem connected to anything else in the scene; she may just be walking through.
