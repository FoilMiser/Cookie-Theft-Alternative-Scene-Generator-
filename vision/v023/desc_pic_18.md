# Picture 18

## Free description
This is a busy, windy shopping street on a sunny day, lined with colourful two-storey shops (pink, pale green, peach and cream) under striped awnings, and white wind lines and blowing leaves show a strong breeze. On the left, a bearded man in a green shirt standing at a fruit and vegetable stall reaches up with both arms as his red baseball cap is blown away into the air. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes walks past carrying a yellow shopping bag. In the middle, a man in a red shirt and jeans sits on a bench reading a red book. Next to him, without his noticing, a blue bird is pecking at his sandwich. In front, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts jumps with both arms in the air. To the right, a little girl in a pink dress is crying and holding an empty ice-cream cone, because her pink ice cream has fallen on the pavement, and a brown dog with a red collar is bending down to lick it up. On the far right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands with her hands on her hips and a metal bucket turned upside down on her head. In the front centre, a white dog sits on the ground holding open a newspaper as if reading it, which dogs cannot do. A red bird flies in the sky near the sun, and a teal bicycle is parked in a bike rack.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The blue thing on the bench reads as a blue bird with its tail up, eating the sandwich. It could possibly be read as a blue object such as a bag, glove or hat lying there.
- The jumping boy could be cheering or playing, or he could be jumping to catch the flying cap or leaves. He is not close to the cap.
- The man at the stall is most likely reaching for his blown-off cap (he isn't wearing one), but his pose could also be read as cheering or as having just thrown the cap.
- The thing on the far-right woman's head looks like a metal pail, so it reads as a bucket worn as a hat. It could also be seen as a strange grey hat.
- The white dog reading a newspaper is impossible. Its head is just above the paper.
- The wind lines are strong, yet the woman in yellow's dress and hair and the other people's clothes are not blowing. This is a small inconsistency.
- The man reading does not seem to notice the bird at his sandwich.
- The girl is probably crying because she dropped her ice cream, though this is inferred.
