# Picture 65

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof, window and blue door on the left, a tree behind the house, and a long wooden fence with a gate at the right end. A paved path leads from the front right up to the gate. An older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal cardigan and grey skirt is standing next to a round flower bed by the house, watering the colourful flowers with a teal watering can; streams of water are falling onto the flowers. Near the fence there is a bush and two terracotta pots with green leafy plants. At the front of the garden there is an empty grey wheelbarrow. She is the only person in the picture, and the scene is calm and ordinary.
