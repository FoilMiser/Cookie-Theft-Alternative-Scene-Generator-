# Picture 33

## Free description
This is a sunny park or playground with a dark metal railing fence and hedges at the back, a tree on the left, a swing set in the middle, and a slide on the right. A girl in a blue top with a brown ponytail, standing just to the right of the slide, is flying a yellow kite high in the sky on a long string. In the bottom right, a small girl in a pink dress is kneeling in a sandpit building a sandcastle with a little red flag on top, and there is a grey rock beside the sandpit. On the left, an older woman with grey hair, glasses and a teal cardigan is sitting on a park bench licking a pink ice-cream cone, and a brown dog with a red neckerchief is sitting up on its hind legs with a paw raised next to her, begging for the ice cream. An older man in a grey cap, brown vest and white sleeves is standing nearby taking a photo with a camera, pointed towards the right. A bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red lead in the middle of the lawn. Odd thing: in the bottom left corner, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is reading a red book in a seated position, but there is no bench, chair or anything underneath her, so she appears to be sitting on thin air. The swing is empty and hangs still, and there is a green rubbish bin behind the swings.
