# Picture 36

## Free description
It is a sunny day in a back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a long wooden fence at the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers with a dark ponytail is holding a garden hose nozzle up close to her face and peering into it, with just a single drop of water coming out. The reason is visible on the ground: a white goat with small horns is standing on the hose, which runs from a hose reel by the flower bed, and the hose has a big swollen bulge just behind where the goat is standing. It looks like the water is blocked, and if the goat steps off, water could suddenly spray out into the woman's face. On the right, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts is sitting on a swing, smiling with his eyes closed, swinging (there are motion lines beside him). A grey wheelbarrow is standing on the grass in the front centre-right. Odd thing: a cardboard box is hanging in the sky just below the sun on the right, with nothing holding it up.
