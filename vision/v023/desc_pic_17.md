# Picture 17

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden. A tall wooden fence runs across the back with a gate on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door stands on the right. On the left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is sweeping dirt into a red dustpan. Instead of a broom she is using a giant silver dinner fork, and a small puff of dust is rising. In the middle-left, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is swinging on the red seat of a dark grey swing set, and motion lines show he is moving. Behind him, a washing line between two wooden posts holds a yellow towel with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans. On the right, an older man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat sits on a wooden bench with his eyes closed, sipping through a straw from a red-and-white cup and looking content. Beside him are a round bed of colourful flowers and a teal hose reel, and an empty grey wheelbarrow stands on the lawn at the front. The odd thing is the woman sweeping with an oversized fork, which is not something you could really sweep with.
