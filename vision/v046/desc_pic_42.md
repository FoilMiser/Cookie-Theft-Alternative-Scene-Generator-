## Free description
A sunny park scene with a path, a pond, a lamppost, trees and a flowerbed. A girl with dark hair, wearing a yellow dress and red shoes, walks along the path at the front left. She is eating a pink ice-cream cone. An empty wooden bench stands just to the right of her. In the middle foreground, a white goat with small dark horns and a red collar bends its head down to eat a tuft of grass. The goat has a rope attached to its collar, and the rope is tied to a short wooden stake in the ground, so it is tethered. A green rubbish bin stands next to the lamppost. Nobody else is around. A goat in a public park is a little unusual, but it is clearly tied up.
