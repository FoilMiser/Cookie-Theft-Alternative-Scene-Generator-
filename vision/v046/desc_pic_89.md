# pic_89

## Free description
A sunny back garden with the same cream house, blue door, flower bed, tall wooden fence, gate and paved path. A woman with dark hair in a yellow dress is digging a hole in the middle of the lawn with a spade. She is bent over and lifting a spadeful of brown soil, and a mound of dug-up soil sits next to the hole. Behind her on the grass are two small potted plants in terracotta pots, apparently waiting to be planted. A teal watering can sits on the grass to the right, near the path. Nothing looks clearly impossible. It seems she is about to plant the potted plants and water them.
