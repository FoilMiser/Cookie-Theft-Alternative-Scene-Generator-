## Free description
A sunny park with green hills behind. There is a big tree and winding sandy path on the left, a tall lamp post in the centre, and an empty pond with bulrushes on the right. In the front centre a white goat with small dark horns and a red collar is eating grass. A rope runs from its collar to a small wooden stake in the ground, so it is tied up. Just to its right is a flower bed with colourful flowers, and the goat seems near enough that it might reach them. A grey rock lies at the front and a green litter bin stands in the bottom right corner. There are no people.
