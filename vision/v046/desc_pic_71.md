# pic_71

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, and a gate at the back right. In the centre an older man in a grey cap, white shirt, brown vest and dark trousers holds a garden hose nozzle up close to his face and peers into it; a single drop of water is next to the nozzle. The hose runs from a hose reel at the front left, along the grass, past a white-and-brown dog, and up to the man. The dog is standing right on the hose, and the hose looks squashed or bulged where it passes under the dog, as if the dog is blocking the water. The man seems to be checking why no water is coming out, so he looks about to get sprayed in the face when the dog steps off. On the right an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and grey skirt sweeps a pile of leaves on the paved path with a broom. A clothesline in the middle holds blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes, and there is an empty swing set with a red seat. There is a flower bed with colourful flowers, a bush, a tree behind the house, two clouds and a sun.
