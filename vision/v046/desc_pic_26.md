# pic_26

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back, a gate at the right and a paved path on the right. A man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands next to a flower bed by the house and pours water from a teal watering can onto the flowers; streams of water are falling on them. In the front centre, a brown dog with a red collar is crouched in a playful bow with its nose near a yellow-green tennis ball, tail up and wagging (motion marks). Just to its right on the grass is a red dog bowl with food in it. Odd thing: an orange fish is sitting on a branch of the tree behind the fence, high up, which is impossible. Two potted plants and a bush are by the fence.
