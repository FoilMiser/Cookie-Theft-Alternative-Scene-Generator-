## Free description
A sunny park with a pond on the left, a tall lamp post in the centre, a winding path on the right, and trees and bushes around. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal cardigan and grey skirt, stands near the front of the pond eating a pink ice cream in a cone. On the right an elderly man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat and white shirt sits on a bench reading a red book. A sandwich lies on the bench next to him, and a small blue bird stands beside it pecking at it. The man has not noticed because he is reading, so it looks as if the bird is about to steal his sandwich. A grey rock sits at the front and a green litter bin stands in the bottom left corner.
