## Free description
A sunny back garden with a wooden fence, a gate on the left, a paved path, a house with a blue door on the right, a flower bed and a bench. In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress is pegging wet laundry onto a washing line strung between two posts. A white T-shirt and blue jeans are hanging and dripping water, and a basket of more clothes sits on the grass below. In the front left, a girl in a purple top and teal trousers is holding a garden hose with a yellow nozzle that is dripping a little water. The hose runs across the lawn to a hose reel by the house. A brown dog with a red collar is standing in the middle of the lawn with its back feet on the hose. Oddly, an orange fish sits in the branches of the tree behind the fence on the right, which is impossible. There is a birdhouse on a pole on the left.
