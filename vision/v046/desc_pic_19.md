# pic_19

## Free description
A back garden on a gloomy day: the sky is grey with three dark storm clouds, as if it is about to rain. On the left is a cream house with a blue door and a window, a tree behind it, and a bed of colourful flowers. A woman in a yellow dress holds a garden hose and looks at the nozzle, from which only a single drop of water comes out. The hose runs back along the grass to a hose reel by the house, and a brown dog with a red collar is standing right on the hose, which looks like the reason no water is coming out; the hose looks swollen on the reel side of the dog. In the front right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is in a sitting pose reading a red book, but there is no chair, bench or anything under him, so he is sitting on thin air. On the right, on a paved path leading to a wooden gate, a girl in a pink dress points or reaches up at the sky. There is also a birdhouse on a tall pole, two potted plants and some bushes in front of a wooden fence.
