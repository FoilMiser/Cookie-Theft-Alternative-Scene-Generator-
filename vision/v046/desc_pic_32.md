## Free description
A sunny back garden with a gate and paved path on the left, a wooden fence across the back, a washing line in the middle, and a house with a blue door on the right. Centre left, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds the end of a garden hose up near her face and looks into the nozzle; only a single drop is coming out, so the water is not flowing. The hose runs along the grass from her to a hose reel by the house, and a brown dog is standing right on top of it, where the hose is squashed flat under its paws, so the dog is blocking the water. The dog faces away, towards the flower bed. Behind, near the gate, an old man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat is sweeping leaves with a yellow broom. The washing line holds a yellow striped towel and blue jeans. If the dog steps off, the water may suddenly spray the woman in the face.
