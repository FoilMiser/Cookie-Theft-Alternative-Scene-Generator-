# pic_03

## Free description
A sunny park with hills, trees, a winding sand path on the left, a pond with reeds on the right and a tall black lamp post in the middle. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands just to the right of the lamp post, eating a pink ice-cream cone. In the front centre a white goat with a red collar bends down to eat a small tuft of grass. The goat is on a lead that runs from its collar to a small wooden stake in the ground, so it is tethered. A green bin stands in the bottom-right corner. The scene is calm; nothing looks impossible.
