# pic_05

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A woman in a yellow dress stands near the gate raking a small pile of leaves. On top of the fence a grey cat creeps along toward a blue bird perched a little further along, as if about to pounce; a red bird sits further right on the fence. On the lawn an orange cat is curled up asleep, with a red food bowl nearby. A lawn sprinkler connected to a long hose sprays arcs of water to the left, and the water lands on a cardboard box, which is wet and has water dripping off it into a puddle. A white dog with brown patches lies asleep by the hose on the right. The cat stalking the bird and the box getting soaked are the main things that seem to be happening.
