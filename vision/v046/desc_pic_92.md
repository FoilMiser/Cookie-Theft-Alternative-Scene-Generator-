A blue baseball cap is flying through the air above the edge of the pond, and a few leaves are tumbling along with it in the wind. A man in a green shirt and beige trousers, in the centre-right foreground, reaches up with one arm towards the cap and looks surprised. It looks as if the wind has just blown his hat off, and it may land in the pond. In the front left, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is walking a white dog with brown patches on a red lead along the path. The wooden bench in the middle is empty, and a green litter bin stands in the bottom-right corner.
