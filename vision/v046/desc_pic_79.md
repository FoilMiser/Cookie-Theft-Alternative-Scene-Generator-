On the right, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts stands on the path flying an orange diamond kite with a bow tail, holding the string reel up high. Something odd: an orange goldfish is sitting on a branch of the tree behind the house, which is impossible. In the front are a grey wheelbarrow, a teal watering can and two potted plants.
