## Free description
This is a playground on a sunny day, with a metal railing fence and bushes behind it. On the left, a red seesaw is tilted down to the right. An old man with a grey beard, a blue flat cap, a white shirt and a brown waistcoat sits on the low end with his feet on the ground. He is calmly reading an open book. A young girl in a pink dress sits on the high end, up in the air, holding the handle. Her mouth is open as if she is calling out or complaining, perhaps because she is stuck up there while he reads. On the right, a boy in a yellow top and orange shorts is sliding down a red-framed yellow and orange slide with one arm raised in the air. There is a tree on the far left, a green bin by the fence, a bush on the right and a grey rock at the front right.
