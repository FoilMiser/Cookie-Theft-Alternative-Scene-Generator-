# pic_43

## Free description
A back garden on a sunny day. There is a cream house with a blue door and a window on the left, a wooden fence across the back, a wooden gate on the right and a paved path in the right corner. An elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, stands by a flowerbed holding a grey watering can. A thin blue stream of water comes from the watering can, but it arcs upward and backward over the flowers, with water drops above it, rather than pouring down. On a clothesline between two posts hang a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. Two potted plants sit on the grass near the fence, an empty wheelbarrow is in the front middle, and an empty wooden bench is at the front left. No one else is in the garden.
