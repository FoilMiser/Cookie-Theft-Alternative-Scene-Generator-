# pic_74

## Free description
A sunny back garden seen from the other side: the cream house with red roof and blue door is on the right, the gate and paved path on the left, and a wooden fence across the back. In the centre-right a woman with dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers paints the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; a few fence boards near her are already blue. On the ground beside her are a paint can and a paint tray with blue paint. A white-and-brown dog walks away from the paint tray toward the left, and its paws are blue; a trail of blue paw prints leads from the tray to the dog, so the dog has stepped in the paint and is tracking it across the lawn. The woman hasn't noticed. On the left an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and grey skirt sweeps leaves on the path. A teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow sit on the grass at the front, and there is a flower bed by the house, a tree, a bush, two clouds and a sun.
