# pic_30

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a gate and paved path on the left, a wooden fence across the back and a house with a blue door on the right. On the right, a man in a green T-shirt and beige trousers sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. Beside him on the bench is a sandwich on a plate, and a small blue bird is at the end of the bench right next to it, apparently pecking at or about to take the sandwich while the man keeps reading. On the left of the lawn is a swing frame with one empty red seat. A teal watering can and a grey wheelbarrow sit on the grass at the front. A flower bed is in front of the house.
