## Free description
A sunny park with a pond on the left, a lamppost in the middle, an empty bench and a winding path on the right, trees, bushes and a flowerbed. At the front left, an elderly man in a grey cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is in a seated position, drinking from a red cup through a straw. There is nothing under him: no bench or chair, so he appears to be sitting on thin air. In the middle foreground, a small blue bird stands on the grass, and an orange cat behind it is crouched low as if stalking it and about to pounce. In the back right, a woman with dark hair, a purple top and teal trousers walks beside the path eating a pink ice-cream cone. A grey rock sits on the grass at the bottom centre. The empty bench is to the right, far from the man.
