## Free description
A sunny playground with a dark railing fence across the back, a tree on the left and bushes behind. On the left is a red seesaw: a girl in a pink dress sits on the high end, which is raised in the air. An old man with grey hair, a cap, a white shirt and brown vest sits on the low end reading an open book, apparently not paying attention to the girl. In the centre a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands with her hand on her hip, wearing a white bucket upside down on her head like a hat. On the right a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is sliding down a yellow/orange slide with an arm raised. In the front right is a sandbox with a red bucket and blue spade in it, and a grey rock beside it. A green bin stands by the fence. The odd thing is the bucket on the woman's head; also the seesaw is stuck with the girl up high because the heavy man is sitting reading on the other end.
