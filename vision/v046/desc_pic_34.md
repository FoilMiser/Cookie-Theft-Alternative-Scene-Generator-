White wind lines blow across the sky and leaves are flying. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands near the fence reaching up as her blue cap is blown off her head into the air. A swing set stands empty on the right with its red seat. A wheelbarrow and a teal watering can are on the grass in front. Strangely, an orange fish is sitting in the tree branches on the left, which is impossible.
