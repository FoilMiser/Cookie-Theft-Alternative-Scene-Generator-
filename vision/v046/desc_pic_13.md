## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a gate at the right, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left. A boy in a red baseball cap, a blue hoodie and dark trousers stands on the paved path at the right, sweeping a small pile of brown leaves with a broom. In the middle of the lawn, a garden hose leads to a sprinkler that is spraying arcs of water. The water lands on a cardboard box sitting on the grass, and the box is getting soaked: there are drips on it and a puddle around it. Nobody seems to have noticed the box getting wet. There is also a flower bed with colourful flowers near the house, two potted plants by the fence, a birdhouse on a tall pole, a tree and some bushes.
