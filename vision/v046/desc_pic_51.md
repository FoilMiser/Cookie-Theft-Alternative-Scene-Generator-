# pic_51

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house on the right, a wooden fence along the back, a gate at the far left and a paved path on the left. On the left, an old man with grey hair, a moustache, a flat cap and a brown waistcoat is sweeping a pile of brown leaves with a yellow broom. In the middle of the garden there is a washing line between two posts with a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is holding a teal watering can over a flower bed; a stream of water comes out of it and curves up into the flowers with drops at the top. There are also two potted plants, an empty wheelbarrow at the front and a wooden bench by the house. The water stream rising upward from the can is the only thing that looks a bit strange.
