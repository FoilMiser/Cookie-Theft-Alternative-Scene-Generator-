## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, and a gate at the back right beside a paved path. In the centre a woman with dark hair in a yellow dress paints the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle; a section of boards to her left is already blue. On the grass by her feet are a paint can and a paint tray with blue paint. A white-and-brown dog on the right is walking away from the tray toward the path, its paws are blue, and a trail of blue paw prints runs from the tray to the dog, so it has stepped in the paint and is tracking it across the lawn. The woman is facing the fence and hasn't noticed. At the front left a hose reel has its hose lying on the grass with a yellow nozzle; a grey wheelbarrow and teal watering can are at the front, and two potted plants are near the fence. There is a flower bed, a bush, a tree, two clouds and a sun.
