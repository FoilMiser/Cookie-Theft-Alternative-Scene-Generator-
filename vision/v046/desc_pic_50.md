## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house on the right, a wooden fence at the back and a paved path on the left leading to a wooden gate. On the left path, a woman in a yellow dress is "sweeping" a small pile of dirt into a red dustpan, but she is using a huge silver dinner fork instead of a broom, which looks wrong and silly. In the middle, a smiling girl in a pink dress is swinging on a swing set, with motion lines behind her. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is painting the fence blue with a roller; the boards near the house are already blue. A white dog with brown spots is walking away from a paint tray full of blue paint, leaving a trail of blue paw prints on the grass, so it has obviously stepped in the paint. There is also a paint tin, a flower bed, a wooden bench, an empty wheelbarrow and a tree behind the house.
