# pic_38

## Free description
A sunny park with hills, a winding path on the left, a lamp post, trees, bushes and a pond on the right. A boy in a red cap, blue top and dark trousers stands at the front of the pond holding a paper bag and scattering seeds or crumbs on the ground. Two small birds, one red and one blue, are pecking at the food on the grass. A white duck swims on the pond. Oddly, a wooden ladder stands upright on its own next to the path, leaning against nothing, which looks impossible. A grey rock and a green bin are at the front.
