# pic_08

## Free description
A busy sunny park with a pond, lamp post, bench, trees and a winding path. On the left an older man in a flat cap, glasses and a brown waistcoat waters a flower bed, but he is pouring the water out of a frying pan rather than a watering can. On the pond a white duck swims toward floating bits of bread, and a girl in a pink dress at the pond's edge holds a bag, apparently feeding it. Behind, a white goat on a lead tied to a stake grazes, and an older woman with grey hair and glasses sweeps leaves into a pile with a broom. In the front centre a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds an empty cone and covers their eyes, crying, because the pink ice cream has fallen on the ground; a white dog with brown patches is licking up the spilled ice cream. On the right a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie holds a kite reel and looks up at his purple kite, which is caught in the tree. In the bottom-right a brown dog with a red collar sits upright holding open a newspaper, as if reading it, which is impossible. A blue bird flies in the sky.
