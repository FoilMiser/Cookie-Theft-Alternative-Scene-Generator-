# pic_55

## Free description
A windy, sunny day in a park with a pond, trees, a lamp post and a winding path. In the centre a woman with long dark hair in a purple top and teal trousers stretches one arm up toward a red cap that is being blown through the air above the pond, with white wind lines and a couple of leaves flying with it. She looks surprised, as if her cap has just blown off and she is trying to grab it. The cap seems about to land in the pond. On the right, an old man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands near the path eating a pink ice cream cone. There are also wind lines near the sun, reeds at the pond edges, bushes and a green bin at the bottom left.
