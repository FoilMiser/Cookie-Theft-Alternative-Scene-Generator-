## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a wooden gate on the right. A paved path runs from the gate down the right side. In the middle of the lawn, a boy in a red cap and blue top has just thrown a red ball. A dotted line shows its arc toward a woman in a purple top and teal trousers, who has her hands out ready to catch it. An orange cat walks along the top of the fence. On the right, a man in a red shirt and blue trousers is digging at the edge of the path with what looks like a huge silver dinner fork rather than a garden fork. A small puff of dust and a little pile of dirt come up where the fork hits the ground, and a red trowel lies on the ground beside it. Odd things: he seems to be digging into the paved path or its edge, and the tool looks like a giant piece of cutlery.
