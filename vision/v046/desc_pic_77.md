# pic_77

## Free description
A sunny park with green hills, a pond with reeds on the left, a winding sandy path on the right, trees, a street lamp and a green bin. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands by the pond flying a yellow diamond kite with a bow tail, holding the string handle up. Behind him near the lamp post a white goat with horns grazes on a tuft of grass; a rope from its collar runs down to a small stake in the ground, so it seems tethered. Right of centre a child in a yellow top and orange shorts holds a yellow and a blue balloon in one hand and reaches up with the other toward a purple balloon floating away above them, its string dangling, so the purple balloon seems to have just escaped. On the far right an older man in a grey cap, white shirt and brown vest walks along carrying a yellow shopping bag. In the middle foreground is a grey wheelbarrow, but its wheel is a square black frame rather than a round wheel, which looks wrong. There is a flower bed with colourful flowers at the front left.
