# Description: test_4.png

## Free description

This is a back garden on a sunny but windy day. A cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door is on the left, and a tall wooden fence with green trees behind it runs across the back. In the middle of the garden, an older woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, has both arms raised and is looking up at a blue cap. The cap is flying through the air above her and to her left, along with a few leaves, and there are wind lines around it, so it looks like the wind has just blown it away and she is trying to catch it. Behind her, a black-and-white cat is creeping along the top of the fence towards a red bird sitting on the fence near the gate on the right, so the cat looks about to pounce on the bird. A clothesline between two posts holds a pair of blue trousers and a yellow towel with red stripes, and both are blowing sideways in the wind. In the front right, an older man in a blue cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers is sweeping the paved path with a broom and does not seem to notice anything that is happening. A small orange cat is curled up asleep on the lawn in the front centre, and a red bowl of pet food sits on the grass near the bottom of the picture. There is also a swing set with one red seat, a round bed of colourful flowers, and an empty wooden bench by the house.

- The black-and-white cat could be stalking the bird or just walking along the fence. Its low, stretched body suggests it is sneaking up to pounce. The bird is facing the cat, so it may have spotted it.
