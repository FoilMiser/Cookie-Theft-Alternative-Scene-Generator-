## Free description

The shops are cream, peach, pale green and pink, and three of them have striped awnings. In the front left, a woman with a short dark bob, a yellow dress and red shoes walks to the right carrying a yellow shopping bag. She looks toward the middle of the square. In the centre, an orange cat crouches low and creeps or runs toward a blue bird that has its head down, pecking at the ground, so it looks like the cat is about to pounce on the bird. On the right, an older man with a grey beard, a cap and a brown vest walks to the left. He holds a taut red leash attached to a white dog with brown spots, which is heading left toward the cat and bird and could lunge at them. Behind him, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands beside a fruit and vegetable stall with her hand raised in a wave (small motion lines by her hand), facing right toward the man. Other street details are a tree in a round planter, a green bin, a flower box, a tall black street lamp, a potted plant in the corner, two clouds and a bright sun. Nothing looks clearly impossible. The main tension is the cat stalking the bird, with the leashed dog possibly about to join in.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Who the woman in purple is waving at. She faces right, so she seems to be greeting the older man. She might also be waving at someone off-picture to the right, or (less likely) at the woman in yellow.

- The cat's action. It could be stalking slowly (crouched and sneaky) or running fast toward the bird. Either way it reads as going after the bird.
- The bird's action. It looks like it is pecking at the ground for food. Its pose could also read as a bird bowing or ducking.
- The dog and the man. The taut, straight leash and the man's forward lean could mean the dog is pulling him toward the cat and bird. It could also just be a normal walk with a straight-drawn leash.
- The woman in yellow's gaze. She could be watching the cat and bird ahead of her, or just looking where she is walking.
- The produce. The red items could be apples or tomatoes, the orange items oranges, the yellow items lemons, and the green items limes or green apples.
- The yellow shopping bag is almost the same yellow as the dress, so it blends in and is easy to miss.
- The woman in yellow's red shoes seem to point in slightly different directions, which looks a little awkward for a walking stride. This may just be the drawing style.
- The older man is drawn overlapping the front of the fruit stall, with his cap level with the produce crates. He reads as standing in front of it, but he is close enough that his head slightly blends with the crates.
- The sun is high on the upper right, but every shadow is a small oval directly under each figure. That is a little inconsistent but typical of cartoons. The tree, lamp post, bin and potted plant have no shadows.
