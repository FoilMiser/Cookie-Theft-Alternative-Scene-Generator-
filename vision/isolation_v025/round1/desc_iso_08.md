## What I see

She wears a teal top, a grey skirt and brown shoes, and she holds a long-handled paint roller up against the fence. A section of the fence has been painted bright blue, while the rest is still natural orange-brown wood, so she appears to be painting the fence blue. A paint can or bucket with a wire handle and a blue drip on its side sits on the grass near her feet. Behind the fence are rounded green hedges or trees and a bit of pale blue sky, and a round green bush is partly visible at the left edge.
