# iso_13

## What I see
A young man in a red cap, a blue hoodie, dark trousers and white shoes sits on a brown wooden bench on a paved pavement, in front of shop windows. He is looking down at an open red book held in his hands, reading. On the bench to his left is a large sandwich or burger with a bun, green lettuce and red filling. A small blue bird with an orange-yellow foot or legs is right beside the sandwich, at its left end, and seems to be pecking at it or stealing from it. At the far left is part of a dark blue planter with red and yellow flowers, and a dark curved edge of something shows at the bottom left corner.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The blue bird is hard to read. Its head and body are dark and bunched up against the sandwich, and the shape could be read as a bird pecking at the food or as a blue object such as a glove or a bag lying on the bench.
- The bird's orange part looks like a small box or square shape under the body, so it could be feet or a separate small item, like a tiny container.
- The sandwich is very large compared with the man's body and with the bird, nearly as long as his thigh, and looks more like a burger drawn side-on than a sandwich. It sits against his hip and seems to overlap him slightly.
- The bench back rails pass behind the man and his body sits well in front of them, which looks fine, but the seat and backrest are close together, so the bench looks thin and flat.
- His shadow lies on the ground to the right of his feet only, rather than under the bench, which makes the bench and man look a little disconnected from the ground; the bench legs have no shadow.
- His feet look slightly small and sit close together, with the white shoes overlapping one another.
- The dark curved shape at the bottom left corner is too cut off to identify.
