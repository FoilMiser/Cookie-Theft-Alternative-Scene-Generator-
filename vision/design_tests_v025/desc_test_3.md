# Picture 3

## Free description

This is a cartoon of a sunny garden, with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left, and a long wooden fence across the back with trees and hedges behind it. At the back in the middle, a woman in a yellow dress stands at the fence using a long-handled paint roller to paint it blue; a section of the fence is already blue while the rest is still brown, and a small pot of blue paint stands on the grass behind her. In the front centre, an older man in a blue cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers is raking a small pile of green and brown leaves. Just to his right is an empty grey wheelbarrow, which looks ready to take the leaves away. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks towards the right, holding a red lead attached to a white dog with brown spots, which is ahead of her on a paved path. The path runs up to a closed wooden gate in the fence. There is also a bed of colourful flowers, an empty bench, a teal watering can, two potted plants, and a bird house on a tall post. Everyone seems calmly busy with their own task and nobody is interacting with anyone else. Nothing looks wrong or impossible, and nothing seems about to go wrong.
