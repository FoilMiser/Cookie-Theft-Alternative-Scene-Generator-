# Test 6 description

## 1. Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a green lawn, a tall wooden fence along the back, and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. In the middle of the back, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands facing the fence, painting it blue with a roller or wide brush on a long handle. Part of the fence behind her has already been painted bright blue, and a grey paint tin with blue paint is on the grass near her feet. On the right, a boy or man in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers is kneeling on one knee beside a round flower bed full of multicoloured flowers. He seems to be planting or tending, and a terracotta pot with a pink plant stands on the ground right next to his hand and the bed. A grey wheelbarrow stands on the lawn at the front centre, with no one using it, and a teal watering can sits on the grass towards the front left. An empty swing set with a single red seat stands on the left, in front of the painted section of fence. A wooden gate in the fence at the far left leads onto a pale paved path that runs to the bottom of the picture. Nothing dramatic seems to be about to happen, and the two people are each busy with their own job, but the wheelbarrow's wheel looks wrong because it is drawn as a square.
