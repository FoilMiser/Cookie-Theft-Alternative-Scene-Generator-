# Picture 2

## Free description

This is a cartoon of a sunny shopping street or town square, with a row of four coloured buildings with striped shop awnings along the back and a wide paved area in front. In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress sits on a wooden bench reading a red book, with her head bent down towards it. Right beside her on the bench is a large sandwich, and a blue bird is next to it with its beak at the sandwich, so it looks as if the bird is pecking at or about to steal her lunch, and she has not noticed. On the left, an older man in a blue cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers walks along carrying a cardboard box. To the right of the bench, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, a teal cardigan and a blue skirt stands holding a camera up to her eye, taking a picture towards the right, with her back to the bench. On the right, a man with a dark beard, a green shirt and beige trousers walks past carrying a yellow shopping bag, in front of a fruit stall with crates of red, orange, yellow and green fruit under a teal and white awning. Nobody appears to be serving at the stall. There is also a tree in a raised planter, a green bin, an empty cafe table with two chairs, and a tall street lamp. The only thing that seems wrong or about to go wrong is the bird at the sandwich; everyone else is just getting on with what they are doing.
