# Test 5 description

## 1. Free description

This is a sunny day on a paved town square or shopping street, with a row of four colourful two-storey shops along the back, each with a striped awning. In the middle, a man with a dark beard and a green shirt sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. Beside him on the bench is a sandwich, and a blue bird is standing right next to it, pecking at or about to steal the sandwich while the man keeps reading and does not notice. To the right of centre, a brown dog with a red collar has its head down and is sniffing or eating a pink ice cream that has fallen on the ground and melted into a puddle. A small girl in a pink dress stands just beside it holding an empty ice cream cone and rubbing her eye, so she appears to be crying over her dropped ice cream. On the far right, a man in a red shirt and blue trousers walks towards the left carrying a yellow shopping bag, looking back or ahead without paying attention to the girl. At the front in the centre there is an odd white figure, like a white dog or a person drawn all in white, sitting on the ground with an open newspaper next to it; it has no normal colouring, and it looks as though it was left uncoloured or is a ghostly character reading the paper. There are also empty café tables and chairs on the left, a tree, a green bin, a bicycle in a rack, a flower planter, a lamp post and a fruit stall at the back right.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous

- The white figure at the front centre is the hardest thing to read. It could be a white dog sitting up beside the newspaper, a person drawn with no colour filled in, or a ghost-like figure. Its body is hard to separate into head, arms and legs. It may be holding the newspaper as if reading it, or the newspaper may simply be standing beside it.
- The white figure's lack of colour looks unintentional, as though its fill is missing, since everything else in the picture is fully coloured.
- The newspaper appears to stand upright on the ground without being clearly held, which looks odd unless the white figure is holding it.
- The bird could be pecking at the sandwich, or it could be just standing on the bench next to the sandwich. It also overlaps the man's leg and the bench closely, so it takes a moment to see.
- The girl could be crying and wiping her eye, or she could be eating or touching her face. The fallen ice cream and empty cone make crying the more likely reading.
- The dog could be eating the ice cream or only sniffing it.
- The girl's arm and the cone are small and hard to make out, so the cone could be read as something else she is holding.
- The man on the right has his face and body turned somewhat differently; he could be walking to the left past the scene or looking off to the left. His legs are in a wide stride that looks a little stretched.
- The fruit stall is unattended, and the café tables are empty, which may or may not mean anything.
- The tree's trunk rises from a round planter at the front, and the tree crown overlaps the shop awning, so the left-hand shop is partly hidden.
- The lamp post overlaps the green building's upper window and awning, but this looks like ordinary depth rather than an error.
