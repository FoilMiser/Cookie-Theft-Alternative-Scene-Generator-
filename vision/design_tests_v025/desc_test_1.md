# Picture 1

## Free description

This is a sunny day in a park, with a bright sun at the top left, two white clouds, and green hills and a line of trees in the distance. On the left, an older man in a grey-blue cap, a brown waistcoat over a white shirt and dark trousers is walking to the right beside a winding sand-coloured path, carrying a yellow shopping bag. In the middle, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. Next to her on the bench is a long sandwich, and a blue bird is right beside it with its beak at the food; she is looking at her book and does not seem to notice, so the bird looks about to eat or take her lunch. Behind her, next to a tall black lamp post, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands at the edge of a pond, holding a white container in one hand and tossing small brown crumbs with the other. The crumbs are spread through the air and on the water, and a white duck is on the pond near them, facing them, with white lines trailing behind it as if it is swimming over. In the front, a little girl in a pink dress and yellow shoes is walking or running to the right, holding up an ice cream cone with a pink scoop. To her right, a tan-coloured dog with a dark floppy ear, something red at its neck and white paws sits up on the grass like a person, holding an open newspaper in its paws as if reading it, which is the plainly impossible thing in the picture. Further right is a round bed of colourful flowers, and in the bottom right corner stands a green rubbish bin.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The dog's body is hard to read. It could be a dog sitting up like a person or, less likely, a person in a tan outfit, because the head is bent forward and I mostly see the top of it rather than a clear face. The legs and tail run together into a lump on the ground.
- The older man's head looks odd: the grey patch could be hair or a beard, and what looks like his ear sits close to the middle of his face. His torso also looks narrow and slightly twisted, and he walks half on the grass and half at the path's edge.
- The blue bird and the sandwich are crowded together on the bench, and there is a small white shape under the bird that could be its legs, a cup or a napkin. The sandwich could also be read as a burger.
- The white object in the yellow-dress woman's hand could be a cup, a tub or a small bag, and it seems to hang below her forearm rather than being clearly gripped.
- The crumbs hang in mid-air between her and the pond. They could be flying through the air as she throws them, or look as though they are floating, and some sit on the grass side of the pond edge while others are on the water.
- The white lines by the duck read as a wake, but they are quite straight and stick-like and could be taken for sticks or scratches on the water.
- The girl's treat is small and could be an ice cream or a lollipop.
- The lamp post is very tall compared with everyone else, and the woman in yellow stands so close to it that the two nearly touch.
