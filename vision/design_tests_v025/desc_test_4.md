# Picture 4

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden, with a bright sun at the top right and two white clouds, a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, and a tall wooden fence running across the back. Near the middle, a man with dark hair and a dark beard, in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers, is bent over digging with a long-handled shovel that has a load of soil on it, next to a hole and a small mound of earth. Just to his right, an older woman with grey hair, a teal top and a grey skirt holds a long-handled roller or brush up against the fence, and a section of the fence beside her has been painted bright blue. On the ground by her feet are a paint tin and a shallow tray of blue paint. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is walking away from the paint tray to the right, and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the tray to the dog, whose paws are blue, so it seems to have stepped in the paint and is tracking it across the lawn. Nobody appears to have noticed the dog, as the woman is facing the fence and the man is looking at his digging. On the right, a boy in a red cap, a blue hoodie and dark trousers is sweeping a paved path with a yellow-bristled broom. The path leads back to a brown gate in the fence. Around the garden are a tall bird box on a post, a round flower bed, an empty bench, an empty wheelbarrow and a teal watering can, none of them being used.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The woman's tool could be a paint roller or a wide paintbrush. The blue head stands upright near the top of the fence, at the edge of the painted block, and looks a little like a blue paddle rather than a tool clearly touching the wood.
- The painted block is very neat with straight edges from top to bottom and no drips, so it could be read as freshly painted fence or simply as a separate blue section of fence or a blue gate. The tray and tin suggest paint, but the block alone looks like a panel.
- The earth by the man could be read two ways: he is digging a hole, or he is shovelling soil onto a mound. The man's dark beard also looks a bit like a band or mask across the lower part of his face.
- The man's shadow is large and sits off to his right, bigger than the shadows of the other figures.
- The boy's cap brim points left while his face looks right. He could be wearing the cap backwards, or the cap may simply look wrongly placed on his head.
- The paw prints look like small clusters of blue dots. They read as paw prints because they line up between the tray and the dog, but on their own they could be blobs or spots of paint.
- The bird box post has a small green oval at its base that looks slightly odd, as if the post is standing on a little patch rather than going into the ground, and it stands in front of the fence.
- The bench, flower bed, wheelbarrow and watering can are scattered around with nobody near them, and the bench has no shadow although the people do. They could be things waiting to be used or just garden furniture.
- The tree on the left is cut off by the house and appears to grow behind the fence, so I cannot tell whether it is in this garden or the next one.
