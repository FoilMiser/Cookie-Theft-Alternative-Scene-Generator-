# iso_04

## What I see
A woman (or older girl) with a short dark bob, wearing a yellow dress and red flat shoes, is walking along a paved grey-tiled sidewalk while reading an open book with a red cover held out in front of her; her face is turned down toward the book, not toward where she is going. A red ball with a white curved stripe sits on the pavement just ahead of her and slightly to her right, in her walking path. Behind her is a building front with a teal door (yellow knob) and a window with a teal frame, a grey planter box of red, yellow, pink and orange flowers along the base of the building, a tree with a brown trunk growing out of a round tree pit/planter on the left, and part of a wooden bench cut off at the right edge.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- Her arm and hand look oddly joined to the book: one arm runs from her shoulder straight toward the book, and a tan hand pokes out past the lower-right corner of the book, as if the arm passes behind the book and comes out the far side. Her second hand is not clearly visible (possibly hidden behind the book).
- Her body looks somewhat blocky and stacked: the short yellow top and the fuller yellow skirt read almost as two separate pieces joined at the waist, her neck is a thin stalk, and the upper body seems to lean back slightly over the striding legs. Her legs are thin with no visible knees.
- Her head is mostly dark hair with only a small part of her face visible on the right, so it could be read either as her looking down at the book or as her head turned partly away from the viewer.
- The red ball with a white curved band reads as a ball, but the white curve also looks like a smile, so it could be read as a red smiley-face object. It does not appear to have its own shadow, though it sits at roughly the same ground level as her feet.
- The grey box of flowers could be a window box fixed under the window or a planter standing on the pavement against the wall.
- The wooden object at the right edge is cut off; it reads as the end of a bench, but could also be a chair, a railing or a wooden sign post.
- The tree's top and the door/window are cut off by the top edge, so their full shapes are not visible.
