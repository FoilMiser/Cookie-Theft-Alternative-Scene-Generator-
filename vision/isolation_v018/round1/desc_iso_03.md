# iso_03

## What I see
A sunny back garden scene: a cream house with a red roof, a four-pane window and a blue door with a step on the left, a tall wooden plank fence across the back with a wooden gate on the right, and a pale paved path leading from the bottom right up to that gate. An older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, stands in the middle of the lawn holding a long-handled rake and appears to be raking a small pile of leaves by her feet. Behind her is a clothesline strung between two wooden posts, with blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes pegged on it. On the lawn are a round flower bed with mixed red, yellow, purple and pink flowers, a teal hose reel next to the flower bed, a small orange cat curled up asleep on the grass, a grey A-frame swing set with a red seat, and a teal watering can near the path. Above, there is a yellow sun with rays, two white clouds, and green trees/hedges behind the fence and behind the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The tool in the woman's hands is small at this size: the head at the bottom reads as a rake (grey tines next to the leaves), but it could also be read as a hoe or a broom. Exactly where her two hands grip the handle is hard to make out.
- The pile at her feet reads as fallen leaves (brown, green and cream bits), but could also be read as small stones or general garden debris.
- The orange animal on the grass is very small; it reads as a sleeping cat (closed eyes, pointed ears, white tail tip), but it could also be read as a fox or a small orange toy. Its body shape is hard to read at this size.
- The teal round object with a dark base next to the flower bed reads as a garden hose reel, but it could also be read as a fan or a speaker. The short hose loop coming out of it ends without connecting to anything.
- The white vertical line on the front of the woman's top could be an undershirt/collar opening or a necklace/strap.
- The yellow item with red stripes on the clothesline reads as a towel, but could also be a shirt or blanket.
- Nothing looked clearly broken: the clothesline passes just above the woman's head rather than through it, the posts, cat, swing and woman all sit on the ground with shadows where present, and the watering can has no visible shadow but does not look like it is floating. The woman is drawn about as tall as the fence, which reads as a perspective effect since she stands in front of it.
