# iso_06

## What I see
An orange cat with white paws is crouched low on green grass in a stalking or about-to-pounce pose. Its body is stretched forward, its tail extends out behind it to the left, and its head is lowered toward a small red bird just in front of its face. The bird stands on thin yellow legs and has a dark red/maroon wing patch and a white patch underneath. Both animals have soft oval shadows under them. The cat looks like it is sneaking up on, or about to catch, the bird.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The bird's orientation can be read two ways. Reading A: its tail is the pointed red shape sticking up toward the upper left, and its round red head is on the right, tipped down as if pecking the ground with its back to the cat. Reading B: the pointed shape on the left is a crest or head facing the cat, and the round end on the right is its rear. Reading A seems a bit more likely, but the tilted, lopsided body makes it hard to be sure. The bird could be a cardinal or just a generic red bird.
- The bird's body is tipped steeply forward, so it looks oddly unbalanced on its legs.
- The cat's front leg looks oddly joined. It seems tucked or bent up under the chest and chin with a white paw near its face, so it is unclear whether the leg is raised to swipe or folded under the body. Only one front paw and one back paw are clearly visible.
- The cat's body is very long and thin compared with its fairly large head, and the back half (haunches and hind leg) is hard to make out.
- It is unclear whether the cat's face is touching the bird or just very close to it.
