# iso_04

## What I see
A sunny backyard. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door with a grey doorstep; a tall wooden fence runs across the back with a wooden gate on the right, and a pale paved path leads from the gate toward the viewer. A girl in a pink dress with dark hair and yellow shoes sits on a wooden bench in front of the house, reading a red book. In the middle of the lawn is an oval flower bed with red, yellow, orange, purple and pink flowers; there are also round bushes, two terracotta pots with green plants, and a birdhouse on a tall pole by the fence. A small orange cat lies curled up asleep on the grass, and a teal watering can (for watering the plants) sits on the grass near the path. Behind the fence are a tree, a line of green treetops, two clouds and a sun.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The cat is very small, and it's hard to tell its pose. It reads as a curled-up sleeping cat, with closed eyes and its head at the lower right. The small white patch on its left could be a paw or the tip of its tail. It could also be read as a cat lying on its side or back.
- The girl's legs slant down and forward from the seat to yellow shoes resting on the shadow just in front of the bench. The pose reads as sitting, but her lower legs look a bit long and thin, and she seems to perch on the front edge of the seat.
- The two plant pots and the watering can have no shadows, unlike the girl and the cat, so they look a little pasted on, though they don't clearly float.
- The birdhouse pole stands in front of the fence with a small green oval at its base (grass or a shadow). The pole is very thin for how tall it is.
- The bush to the left of the flower bed is partly hidden behind the house's corner. This reads as normal overlap (bush behind the house) and isn't a real problem.
- Nothing obviously passes through anything else.
