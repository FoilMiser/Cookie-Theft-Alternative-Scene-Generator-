- The clothesline man: he could be hanging the T-shirt up or taking it down; his hands are on the peg either way.
- The woman's hose: she could be pointing the nozzle toward the man/laundry, or looking at the nozzle wondering why no water is coming out. Because she is seen from behind, I can't see where she is looking, and the nozzle's direction is hard to make out at her hands.
- The bulge in the hose: I read it as a cartoon pressure bulge from the dog standing on the hose. It could also be read as a lump, a hose connector or sprayer attachment lying in the line, or even as a snake-like swelling. It is on the reel side of the dog's paw, which supports the "blocked water" reading.
- Which of the dog's paws is on the hose: the back paw is clearly on it right next to the bulge; the front paws are also very close to or on the hose further left.
- The man in the orange vest: his role is unclear (road/utility worker, delivery person, neighbour, crossing guard). He carries nothing. He seems to be waving at the family, but no one is looking back at him. The gate behind him is closed, so it is unclear whether he has just come through it.
- The dog's open mouth could be panting or barking; it seems to be facing toward the woman/swing rather than at the hose.
- The swing has motion lines, but the chains are drawn straight up and down, so it looks only mildly in motion.
- The hose reel stands on its own by the flower bed and isn't visibly connected to a tap on the house. That is slightly odd if water is supposed to be flowing through the hose.
- Nothing appears to float or pass through anyone. The raised arm of the man in orange overlaps the edge of the gate/fence, and the clothesline man's head overlaps the yellow cloth on the line. Both look like normal front/behind layering.
