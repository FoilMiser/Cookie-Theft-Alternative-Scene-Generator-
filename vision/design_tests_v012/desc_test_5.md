## Free description

It is a sunny day in a park. The sun is in the top left, there are two white clouds, and green hills and bushes run along the back. A beige path winds from the bottom left toward the back. Near the back left, a man in a blue hoodie is digging with a shovel beside a pile of dirt and a hole. Below him is a grey wheelbarrow whose wheel is drawn as a square box with an X on it instead of a round wheel, which looks wrong or impossible. An empty wooden bench is in front of it. In the middle, under a tall black lamp post, a grey-haired older woman holding a white bag throws bread crumbs toward a pond. A white duck is swimming toward the crumbs floating on the water. In front of her, a white dog with brown spots is in a play bow, wagging its tail, next to a yellow-green tennis ball. On the left, a child in a yellow-orange outfit is leaning back with one leg kicking toward an orange ball. He might be kicking it, or he might be about to fall. In the front centre-right, a small girl in a pink dress holds an empty ice-cream cone and rubs her eye as if crying, because her pink scoop with sprinkles has fallen and is melting on the grass. A brown dog with a red collar is putting its nose down to the dropped ice cream, as if it is about to eat it. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks to the left while reading an open book, heading toward a cardboard box on the ground, so she looks about to trip over it. A green trash can stands at the far right edge.
