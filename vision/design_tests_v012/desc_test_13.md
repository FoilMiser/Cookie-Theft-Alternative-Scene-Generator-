Behind them is an empty wooden bench, and to the left a black street lamp. In the foreground a red bicycle stands upright on its own with nobody holding or riding it. A woman in a yellow dress with black bobbed hair and red shoes walks just to the right of it, carrying a yellow shopping bag. On the far right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers holds a camera to her face and points it left, toward the people in the middle. A big round green tree in a raised planter stands between her and the rest of the scene. The strange thing is the bicycle: its wheels look like rounded squares rather than circles, and it stays upright with nobody holding it.

## 3. Unsure or ambiguous

- Bicycle wheels: they look like rounded squares, not circles. This could be a deliberate "something is wrong" detail (square wheels can't roll), or it could just be the drawing style. Either way, it is the clearest oddity in the picture.
- How the bicycle stays up: it stands upright with no rider and no lean. A small grey piece near the pedals might be a kickstand, or it might only be the pedal or crank. I can't tell.
- Who owns the bicycle: it doesn't obviously belong to anyone. It could be parked or abandoned in the middle of the walkway. It is close to the woman in yellow and could be read as a trip hazard, but she doesn't seem to be on a collision course with it.
- Which way the woman in yellow is walking: her face points right, but her feet are spread so that it isn't fully clear whether she is stepping right or left. My best reading is that she is walking right.
- What the photographer is photographing: the camera points left. It could be aimed at the woman in yellow, at the man and dog, or at the street and buildings in general.
- The fruit in the crates is drawn as simple coloured balls. Green could be limes or apples, yellow could be lemons, orange could be oranges, and red could be apples or tomatoes.
- The lamp post's lantern overlaps the lower edge of an upper window on the green building. This reads as normal depth (the lamp is in front), not an error.
- The tree canopy covers part of the cream building's awning and window. This also reads as normal depth.
- Nothing appears to be floating or passing through anyone.
