In the middle of the lawn, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands under a clothesline between two wooden posts, reaching up to peg a white T-shirt. A yellow cloth with a red stripe and a pair of blue jeans already hang on the line. To the right, a bearded man in a green shirt and tan trousers digs with a shovel next to the right-hand clothesline post, where there is a small hole and a mound of dirt. Strangely, a cardboard box hangs tilted in mid-air above the fence on the left, with a dark shadow on the grass well below it, so it looks like it is flying or falling into the yard. Near the house there is a flower bed with bright red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers and a teal garden hose on a reel beside it.
