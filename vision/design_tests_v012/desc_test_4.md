## Free description

This is a sunny day on a paved pedestrian shopping street. A row of four colourful two-storey shops runs across the back: pink, pale green, peach and cream, with striped awnings. The sun is in the top-left corner and two small white clouds are in a light blue sky. On the left is a fruit stall with a teal-and-white striped canopy. It has four crates of green, yellow, orange and red fruit, and nobody is minding it. In the middle, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers walks to the left with a white, brown-spotted dog on a red lead. The dog walks ahead of him and seems to be leading. To the right of centre, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes walks toward the right, carrying a yellow shopping bag. At the far right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands holding a camera to her face and points it left, toward the people and the tree in the middle of the street. There is an empty wooden bench, a black street lamp, a rubbish bin, a potted plant, a cardboard box and a tree in a round planter. In the front centre is a red bicycle with nobody on it, standing upright on its own. Its wheels are square, which is impossible: a bicycle with square wheels could not roll.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Square bicycle wheels: these are clearly impossible and look deliberate. It is also odd that the bicycle stands upright with no rider, stand or support. I could not tell for sure which end is the handlebars and which is the seat, because both are small black shapes on top of the frame.
- Where the lead joins the dog: the lead seems to reach the dog around the middle of its back (near a brown spot), not the blue collar at its neck. It could be clipped to a harness, or it could be a small drawing slip.
- What the photographer is photographing: it could be the woman in yellow, who is the nearest person in the camera's direction, or more generally the tree, the man and dog, or the street. The camera points left toward the centre, but the target is not clear.
- Which way the woman in yellow is going: her head and front foot point right, so I read her as walking right, toward the tree and photographer. She might instead be stopping or turning to look at the photographer.
- Her shopping bag is nearly the same yellow as her dress, so it blends in and is easy to miss.
- The unattended fruit stall: no seller is visible. It could just be empty for the moment, or it could be meant to be noticed.
- The dog's mouth: the small red mark could be a tongue sticking out, or it could just be the nose or mouth drawing.
- Small drawing quirks: the top of the street lamp overlaps one of the green building's windows. The bench, bin, potted plant and bicycle seem to have no ground shadows, while the people, the dog, the box and the tree planter do. Neither of these looks like part of the scene's story.
