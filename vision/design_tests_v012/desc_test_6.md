## Free description

It's a sunny day in a back garden. A yellow sun is in the top-left corner and two white clouds are in a blue sky. A tall wooden fence runs across the back with a wooden gate at the far left, and green hedges and a large tree show over the top of it. On the right is the side of a house with a red roof, a window and a blue door. On the left, a small girl in a pink dress sits on a red swing hanging from a grey A-frame swing set. Her eyes are closed and she is smiling, and motion lines behind her show she is swinging. In the middle, near the fence, a white dog with brown spots crouches in a "play bow" over a yellow-green tennis ball, wagging its tail, as if it is about to grab the ball or wants someone to throw it. On the right, a bearded man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers stands at the edge of a round flower bed and pours water from a teal watering can onto bright yellow, orange, purple, pink and red flowers. A grey wheelbarrow stands empty at the front of the lawn, and a red dog bowl full of food sits on the grass near the paving path at the bottom. The strangest thing is the wheelbarrow's wheel, which is drawn as a square box with an X in it rather than a round wheel.

One reading is that it is meant to be an impossible square wheel (an intentional "what's wrong" item). The other is that it is a crate or box standing in front of the wheelbarrow, hiding the real wheel. To me it looks most like a square wheel.
- Wheelbarrow support: only one dark leg is visible under the tray, so it looks slightly unbalanced. This could just be the angle.
- Girl's hands: her arms stick straight out to the sides, roughly level with the chains. It's unclear whether she is holding the chains or swinging with her arms out and not holding on.
- Man's gaze: his head is turned to the right and his face is drawn in rough profile. It's hard to tell whether he is looking at the flowers or straight ahead past them.
- Water stream: the water comes out as a thick, smooth, rope-like curve that bends down into the flowers. It reads as water, but its shape is a little hose-like.
- Dog's pose: it could be a play bow (inviting play) or the dog about to pick up or pounce on the ball. Either way, it is playful.
