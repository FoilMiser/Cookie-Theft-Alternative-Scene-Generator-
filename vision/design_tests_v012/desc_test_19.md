# Description: test_19.png

## Free description

This cartoon shows a sunny day in a park. There is a bright sun in the top left, two white clouds, green hills in the background, and a pond on the right with reeds at its edges. A sandy path curves from the back left down to the bottom of the picture. Near the back, a man in a blue hoodie is digging a hole with a shovel and has made a pile of dirt next to it. In the middle, an older woman with grey hair in a bun is holding a white bag and throwing crumbs toward the pond, where a white duck is swimming toward the crumbs and leaving a wake behind it. On the left, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is running and kicking an orange ball. Below him on the path, a white dog with brown spots is crouched down in a play bow in front of a yellow-green tennis ball, with its tail wagging. In the front centre, a little girl in a pink dress is holding an empty ice-cream cone and rubbing her eye as if she is crying, because her pink ice cream has fallen on the ground and a brown dog with a red collar is sniffing or eating it. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is walking while reading an open book and not looking where she is going. There is a wooden box on the ground right in front of her, so she looks about to trip over it. A grey wheelbarrow stands behind her, and a green trash can is partly visible in the bottom right corner.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Girl: she could be crying and wiping tears (most likely, given the dropped ice cream), or just rubbing a tired or itchy eye.
- Brown dog: could be eating/licking the ice cream or only sniffing it.
- Man digging: it is not clear why he is digging (planting something? burying something?). He could be a gardener or a park worker. The hole and mound read as a hole in the lawn.
- Reading woman and box: the box is in front of her as she walks left, so she seems about to trip. She might also step around it. It is also unclear who the box belongs to or why it is on the grass.
- Wheelbarrow: it looks a bit odd. The wheel is drawn as a square frame with spokes rather than a round wheel, and the whole thing looks slightly tilted, as if it is tipped or resting on its leg. It could be a normal parked wheelbarrow or one that has been tipped over. It seems to have nothing to do with anyone in particular (it could go with the digging man, but it is far from him).
- Boy and ball: he could be kicking the ball deliberately (playing), or running and about to trip over it. The ball's direction is toward the path and bench.
- Crumbs: a few crumbs look like they are hanging in mid-air between the woman and the pond. That reads as "being thrown", but they also look a bit like they are floating. Some crumbs at the pond's edge could be on the grass or in the water.
- Spotted dog: the crouched pose could be a play bow (wanting to play with the tennis ball) or a stretch. The motion lines are behind its tail, so it is probably wagging.
- Orange ball: it could be a basketball or a small play ball.
- Trash can: it is cut off at the edge of the picture, and nothing seems to be going on with it.
