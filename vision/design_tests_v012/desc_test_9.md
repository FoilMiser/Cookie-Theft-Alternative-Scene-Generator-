# Description of test_9.png

## Free description

This is a cartoon of a sunny day in a park. There is a bright sun in the top left, two white clouds, green rolling hills, and a beige path winding from the back down to the front. In the back left, a man in a blue hoodie is digging a hole with a shovel. Next to him is a pile of dirt, and a grey wheelbarrow stands near the path in front of him. In the middle, a grey-haired older woman with glasses stands by a tall black lamp post and throws bits of bread toward a pond. A white duck swims toward the crumbs, and some crumbs are in the air or lying at the edge of the water. At the front, a little girl in a pink dress holds an empty ice-cream cone and rubs her eye as if she is crying. Her pink ice cream with sprinkles has fallen on the grass and is melting, and a brown dog with a red collar has its head down eating or sniffing it. On the left, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is kicking an orange-and-white ball. On the path, a white dog with brown spots is crouched in a play bow in front of a yellow-green tennis ball, wagging its tail. On the right, a woman in a purple top is walking while reading an open book, and she looks about to walk into or trip over a wooden box on the ground in front of her. The oddest detail is the wheelbarrow's wheel, which looks square instead of round.
