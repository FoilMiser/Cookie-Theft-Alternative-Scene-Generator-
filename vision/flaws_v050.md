**The game of catch.** In both pictures a small girl has thrown to the woman in the yellow dress, and the ball is drawn at the end of its path, by the woman's hands, with the dashed line running back down to the girl. One reader had the woman throwing: "A woman in a yellow dress has just thrown a red ball (a dashed line shows its arc) toward a girl in a pink dress, who runs with her arms out to catch it." (79). The other three pictures read right: the boy throwing to the girl, twice (66, 82), and the teenager throwing to the same woman (15). The template was v0.49's R3 on one reader's doubt, and v0.50 looked at it and left it.
