## Free description

This is a sunny but windy day in a back garden. A tall wooden picket fence runs across the back, with a hedge behind it, and a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue front door is on the right. White wind lines in the sky, leaves blowing off a tree behind the house, and clothes swinging on a washing line all show that the wind is strong. On the left a young man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands on a paved path next to a wooden gate. He has both arms raised and looks surprised, because his orange baseball cap has just blown off his head and is flying up and to the left, above the fence. He seems about to lose the cap over the fence unless he catches it. In the middle of the garden is an empty swing set with one red seat. Behind it a washing line between two wooden posts holds a striped yellow-and-red towel and a pair of blue jeans, which are blowing sideways and look as if they might come off. On the right a girl in a yellow dress and red shoes is watering a colourful flower bed with a green garden hose; the hose runs back to a hose reel at the edge of the bed. In the front of the scene are a teal watering can, an empty wheelbarrow, and a wooden bench by the house. Nothing looks impossible; the main event is the cap blowing away in the wind.
