## Free description

This is a cartoon of a sunny park with a yellow sun at the top left, two white clouds, and rolling green hills and a line of bushes in the background. In the middle of the picture a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers stands on the grass and fishes in a blue pond on the right. Her line runs down to a red-and-white float in the water, with small ripple rings around it. In the front, a little girl in a pink dress holds an empty ice-cream cone in one hand and rubs her eye with the other, so she seems to be crying. Her pink scoop of ice cream with sprinkles has fallen and splattered on the grass at her feet. A brown dog with a red collar or bandana is right next to the dropped ice cream with its head down, and it looks about to lick or eat it. On the left there is a winding sandy path, a big tree, an empty wooden park bench, and a tall black lamppost. The odd thing is a wooden ladder that stands upright behind the bench, beside the path, leaning slightly but resting against nothing. It just stands up into the air with nothing holding it. In the bottom right corner there is a bed of colourful flowers and a green trash can.
