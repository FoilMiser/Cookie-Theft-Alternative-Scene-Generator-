# Picture 23

## Free description
This is a back garden on a bright sunny day, with a yellow sun in the top left and two small white clouds. A cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the right, and a wooden fence with a gate on the left runs along the back. A woman (or older girl) in a purple T-shirt and teal trousers, with dark hair in a ponytail, stands on the paved path on the left sweeping it with a broom. She glances off to the left. In the middle of the lawn a white dog with brown spots is crouched in a "play bow" with its tail wagging, and a yellow-green tennis ball sits in front of its nose, as if it wants someone to throw it. At the bottom right a black-and-white cat is creeping low towards a red bird that is pecking at the grass, so the cat looks about to pounce. There are two potted plants near the fence, a teal watering can and an empty grey wheelbarrow on the grass at the front, and a bed of colourful flowers in front of the house. Nothing looks clearly impossible. The main bits of action are the dog wanting to play and the cat stalking the bird.
