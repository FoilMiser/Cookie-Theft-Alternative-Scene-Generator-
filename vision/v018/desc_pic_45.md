# Picture 45

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and a blue door, with a wooden fence, a gate on the right and a stone path. A brown-and-white dog sits by the blue back door holding a red lead in its mouth, as if it is asking to go for a walk. A black-and-white cat is walking along the top of the back fence. In the centre-right there is a dark grey swing frame with a single red swing seat, which is empty. On the path at the right, a girl or young woman in a purple top and teal trousers, with dark hair in a ponytail, is sweeping dirt into a red dustpan. She isn't using a broom: she is pushing the dirt with a giant white dinner fork, which is impossible or absurd. A little cloud of dust rises where she is sweeping. Next to the flowerbed of colourful flowers there is a teal hose reel, and an empty grey wheelbarrow stands on the lawn at the bottom. Nobody is using the swing, the hose or the wheelbarrow.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The giant fork used as a broom is clearly wrong or impossible: a dinner fork that big doesn't exist, and a fork wouldn't sweep dirt well. It looks intentional rather than a drawing error, but I'm reporting what I see.
- The dog with the lead in its mouth could be read as "wants to go for a walk", or it could simply be carrying/chewing the lead.
- The cat on the fence top is plausible, since cats do walk along fences. It is only notable.
- The sweeper could be a teenage girl or a young woman.
- The swing frame's right legs stand in front of the gate area. It is drawn in front and doesn't look like it passes through anything.
