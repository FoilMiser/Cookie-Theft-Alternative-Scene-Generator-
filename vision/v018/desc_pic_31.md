# Picture 31

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a sun in the top left, two white clouds, a wooden fence and gate across the back on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A paved path runs from the gate down the left edge. On the left a smiling boy with curly dark hair, a yellow shirt and orange shorts is swinging on a red swing on a grey swing frame; small lines beside him show movement. In the middle a man in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest with reflective stripes, blue trousers and black shoes is raking a small pile of leaves on the grass. Behind him a clothesline between two wooden posts holds a yellow towel with red stripes and a pair of blue jeans. A blue ball with a white stripe is stuck up in the branches of the tree behind the house. A girl in a pink dress stands at the back by the fence with both arms raised, looking toward the tree/ball, as if she wants it back or is cheering; she is far too low and too far away to reach it. A teal watering can sits on the grass near the path, and a colourful flower bed and a small bush are in front of the house.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl's raised arms can be read two ways: she is reaching up / asking for help to get the stuck ball, or she is waving or cheering. She is nowhere near the ball, so she cannot be literally reaching it.
- The tree with the ball is behind the fence (its trunk is on the far side), so the ball may actually be in the neighbour's tree.
- The ball could be a beach ball or a football; it has a single white curved stripe.
- The boy's eyes are closed and he is smiling; he seems to be enjoying the swing.
- Nothing appears to float or pass through other things; the ball sitting in the tree is the main "something's wrong" element.
