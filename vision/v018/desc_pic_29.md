## Free description
This is a sunny back garden: a sun in the top left, two small white clouds, a tall wooden fence across the back with a wooden gate on the left, and a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue front door on the right. A pale paved path runs from the gate down to the bottom left corner. On the left, a girl in a yellow dress and red shoes stands on the grass with one arm raised high, waving; small curved lines by her hand show the wave. In the middle, a man in a blue-grey cap, brown vest, white shirt and dark trousers is bent over digging a hole with a shovel; there is a mound of dirt beside the hole and a clump of soil on the shovel blade. On the right, an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, is watering a round flower bed full of colourful flowers in front of the house. The odd thing is that she is not using a watering can: she is holding out a dark frying pan (or saucepan) by its handle, and a stream of water pours from its rim onto the flowers. A tree with a brown trunk rises behind the fence near the house, with a row of green hedges or trees along the back.
