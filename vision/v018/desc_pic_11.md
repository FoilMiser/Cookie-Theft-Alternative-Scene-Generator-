## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. In the tree behind the house sits an orange fish, which is impossible. In the centre, a man in an orange cap, an orange high-visibility vest, and dark blue trousers is painting the fence blue with a long-handled brush. A section of boards to his right is already blue. A paint can and a paint tray with blue paint sit on the grass by his feet. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has walked through the paint and is leaving blue paw prints as it walks left toward the flower bed. On the path at the bottom right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is flying a yellow kite. He holds the string up with one hand while the kite, with bow-tie tail decorations, flies high near the sun. Two potted plants stand by the fence, a teal watering can sits on the grass near the path, and an empty grey wheelbarrow is at the front.
