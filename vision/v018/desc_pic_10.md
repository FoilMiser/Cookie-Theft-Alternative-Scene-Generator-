# pic_10

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence along the back, and a gate and paved path on the left. Near the gate, a woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is painting the fence blue with a long-handled brush. A few boards next to the gate are already blue. On the grass at her feet are a can of blue paint and a paint tray. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar has walked through the paint tray. It is leaving a trail of blue paw prints across the lawn, and its feet are blue. On the right, an older man with a grey beard and a grey cap, wearing a white shirt and brown waistcoat, is watering the flower bed with a hose that comes from a reel. At the front, there is a grey wheelbarrow with wooden handles, but its wheel is square instead of round. An empty wooden bench stands at the bottom right.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The wheelbarrow's wheel is drawn as a square with spokes. That is impossible for a working wheel. It looks deliberate, but it could also be read as a drawing glitch or a box-shaped wheel guard.
- The dog seems unaware that it is spreading paint. The prints start at the tray, so it probably stepped in it, although it might have stepped in the can.
- The woman's brush has a long handle, so it could be a paint brush on a pole or a small roller.
- The man's cap could be a flat cap or a baseball cap.
- The woman is painting right next to the gate, and the gate itself is still unpainted.
