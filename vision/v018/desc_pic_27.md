# Picture 27

## Free description
This is a busy back garden on a sunny day, with the sun in the top right and two white clouds. A cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, and a wooden fence with a gate and a paved path is on the right. Near the house, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is painting the fence blue with a paintbrush, and a section of the fence beside her is already blue. In the middle, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is reaching up to peg a white T-shirt onto the washing line, next to some blue jeans and a yellow item that are already hanging. In front of him, a woman in a yellow dress holds the end of a garden hose up close to her face and looks into the nozzle, but only a single drop of water is coming out. The reason is that a brown dog with a red collar is standing on the hose, and the hose has swollen into a big bulge just behind the dog's paws where the water is backed up. When the dog steps off, the water will probably burst out and spray the woman in the face. There is also a wooden bench, a flower bed, a hose reel, an empty swing set, a birdhouse on a pole, a wheelbarrow and a watering can.
