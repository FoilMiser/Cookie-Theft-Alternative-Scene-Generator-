## Free description
This is a back garden on a sunny day, with a sun in the upper left and two clouds. A tall wooden fence runs across the back with a wooden gate on the far left, and a paved path leads from the bottom left up to the gate. On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door, with a tree behind it. A woman with a brown ponytail, a blue hoodie and black trousers is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller or brush; a few boards to her right are already blue, and a paint tin sits on the grass by her feet. Near the house, a woman with short dark hair, a yellow dress and red shoes is watering a round flower bed with a teal watering can. In the front centre, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is drinking from a white mug in a seated position — but there is no chair or seat under him at all, so he is sitting on thin air, which is impossible. Next to him on the ground, at the bottom, is an empty grey wheelbarrow. There are also two potted plants and a round bush near the fence.
