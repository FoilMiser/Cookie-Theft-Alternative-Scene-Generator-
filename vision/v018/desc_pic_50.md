# Picture 50 - Description

## Free description
This is a sunny park with rolling green hills, a pond, trees, a lamp post, a bench and a winding sandy path. On the left, an older woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses, wearing a teal top and grey skirt, is kneeling on one knee beside a flower bed and digging or planting with a small hand tool. Next to her is a terracotta pot with a pink flower in it, as if she is about to plant it. In the centre foreground a white goat with a curved horn has its head down and is eating a tuft of grass. On the right, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands next to the path and pours seed or crumbs from a small white box, and a trail of seeds falls to the ground. Two small birds, one red and one blue, are pecking at the seeds on the path near his feet. A wooden bench and a tall black lamp post stand in the middle of the grass, and the pond with bulrushes at each end is behind the woman. The most unusual thing is the goat grazing loose in an ordinary park.

## Inventory
- Older woman, left front: grey hair in a bun, glasses, teal top, grey skirt, brown shoes; kneeling by the flower bed, using a small trowel or similar hand tool in the soil.
- Man, right middle: short brown hair, red T-shirt, blue jeans, dark shoes; standing beside the path, tipping seeds or crumbs out of a small white box.
- Goat, centre front: white with a grey curved horn; head down, eating a tuft of grass.
- Red bird, right front on the path: pecking at the seeds.
- Blue bird, right front on the path next to the red bird: pecking at the seeds.
- Seeds/crumbs: a dotted trail falling from the man's box down to the path where the birds are; food for the birds.
- Potted plant: terracotta pot with a pink tulip-like flower, beside the kneeling woman; waiting to be planted.
- Flower bed: left front; red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers in brown soil.
- Pond: centre-left background, blue water with bulrushes (cattails) at both ends.
- Lamp post: tall black lamp post, centre.
- Bench: wooden park bench, centre-right, empty.
- Path: sandy winding path from the background down to the bottom right.
- Trees: one small tree on the far left, one large tree on the right.
- Sun (top right) and two clouds (top centre).

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The tool in the woman's hand is very small; it could be a trowel, a hand fork, or she might just be pressing soil with her fingers.
- The man's container could be a box of birdseed or a bag/carton of bread crumbs.
- The goat's head is drawn a little oddly (round, with one curved horn showing), but it reads as a goat. A goat in a public park is unusual; it could be read as a loose/escaped animal or as a park animal.
- The man's face is partly hidden by his hair as he looks down, so his expression is hard to read.
- Nothing appears to be floating apart from the seeds, which seem to be falling naturally.
