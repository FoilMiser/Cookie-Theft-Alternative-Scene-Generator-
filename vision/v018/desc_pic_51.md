# Picture 51 - Description

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a tall wooden fence, a house with a red roof and blue door on the right, and the sun in the top left. On the far left, a small girl in a pink dress stands on the paved path by a wooden gate and flies an orange diamond-shaped kite high in the sky; its long string runs from her hand up to the kite, which has a tail with little bows. In the middle, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is digging a hole in the lawn with a shovel, with a pile of earth beside her. Behind her is a clothesline between two wooden posts, with a yellow-and-red striped towel and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. On the right, an older man in a grey cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers is watering a flower bed with a teal watering can. The water coming out of the can curves upward into the air instead of pouring down onto the flowers, with drops flying up above it, which is impossible. There is also an empty swing set with a red seat on the left, an empty wheelbarrow at the bottom, and a wooden bench near the house.
