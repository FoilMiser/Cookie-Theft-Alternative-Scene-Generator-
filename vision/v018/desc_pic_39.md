# Picture 39

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. In the middle at the back, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is painting the fence blue with a long-handled roller or brush. A few fence boards are already blue, and a paint tray and a can of blue paint sit on the grass near him. A white dog with brown spots has walked through the paint tray and is leaving a trail of blue paw prints across the grass toward the path on the left. In the middle right, a grey-haired woman in a teal top and grey skirt is watering a flower bed with a teal watering can. Strangely, the stream of water curves upward into the air, with drops at the top, instead of pouring down onto the flowers. At the front right, an older man in a cap, brown vest and white shirt sits on a bench reading a red book. Next to him on the bench, a blue bird is pecking at his sandwich, and he hasn't noticed. An empty grey wheelbarrow sits at the bottom centre. The funny or wrong things are the dog tracking paint, the bird stealing the sandwich, and the water going up out of the watering can.
