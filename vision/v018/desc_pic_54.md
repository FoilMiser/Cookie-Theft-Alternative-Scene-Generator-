# Picture 54 - Description

## Free description
This is a sunny town square or high street, paved with grey slabs, with a row of colourful shops behind (cream, peach, green and pink buildings with striped awnings). On the left a tree grows from a round planter, and a blue ball is stuck in its branches. A man in a blue hoodie and black trousers stands next to the tree with both arms raised and his mouth open in shock or dismay. In the middle-left, a little girl in a pink dress is holding the strings of a purple and a blue balloon, while a yellow balloon floats away above her; she is reaching up for it. In the centre, an older woman with grey hair and glasses sits on a bench eating a pink ice cream cone, and a brown dog with a red collar is leaning up toward her, as if trying to get the ice cream. A red bird is perched on top of a lamp post and a blue bird flies in the sky near the sun. On the right there is an unattended fruit and vegetable stall with crates of red, orange, yellow and green produce under a striped canopy, with a cardboard box beside it. In the front there is a red bicycle parked in a bike rack, a grey litter bin, and potted plants in both bottom corners.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The man's reaction could be read two ways: (a) he has thrown or kicked the ball into the tree and is upset it is stuck, or (b) he is reacting to the girl's yellow balloon floating away; he faces toward the right, more toward the girl than the ball.
- It is unclear whether the girl has just let go of the yellow balloon or whether it belonged to someone else; it seems to be hers.
- The dog's body is drawn long and stretched out low with its back legs trailing, which looks slightly odd; it could be read as jumping up at the woman or as stretching toward the ice cream.
- The fruit stall has no seller, which may just be an unattended stall.
- The bicycle and rack overlap closely; the rack loops appear to sit behind the wheels, which looks normal.
