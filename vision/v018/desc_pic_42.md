# Picture 42

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. On the left, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans is walking a white dog with brown spots on a red leash along the path toward the gate. In the middle of the lawn stands a clothesline between two wooden posts, with a yellow striped towel or top and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. At the bottom centre, a white goat with small dark horns has its head down, eating a little tuft of grass. A red bowl full of pet food sits on the grass near the path, but no animal is eating from it. The strangest thing is an orange fish sitting in the leaves of the tree behind the house, which is impossible since fish don't live in trees. There is also a flower bed and a bush next to the house.
