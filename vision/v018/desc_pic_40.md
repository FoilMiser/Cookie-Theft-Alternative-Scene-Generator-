# Picture 40

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a gate and paved path on the left, and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A black-and-white cat is walking along the very top of the fence on the left. In the middle, a little girl in a pink dress has both arms raised and a happy face, and her feet are off the ground with her shadow on the grass below her, so she seems to be jumping for joy. On the right, a woman with a brown ponytail, blue hoodie and black trousers is watering a flower bed with a spray nozzle, and water is pouring down onto the flowers. Next to the flower bed, near the house, is a hose reel. There are two potted plants and a bush near the fence, a teal watering can on the grass at the front left, and an empty grey wheelbarrow at the bottom centre. One odd thing is that the woman's spray nozzle doesn't seem to be attached to any hose. Otherwise it is a cheerful, ordinary garden scene.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl's feet are clearly above the ground with her shadow separate below. Most likely she is jumping, but it could also be read as her floating or hovering in the air.
- The woman's spray nozzle has no visible hose connecting it to the hose reel. It could be (a) a disconnected nozzle that somehow sprays water, which would be impossible, or (b) a hose hidden behind her body or simply not drawn.
- A cat walking on top of a fence is normal cat behaviour, though it could be read as a cat about to jump down into the garden.
- The watering can and wheelbarrow lie about unused. Nothing odd about them, just props.
