# Picture 04

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills, a pond on the left, a lamp post in the middle and a winding sandy path on the right. At the left edge of the pond, a little girl in a pink dress is holding something white and scattering crumbs or seeds. Small brown pieces lie on the grass at her feet and some float on the water, and a white duck is swimming toward them, leaving a little wake. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands at the right side of the pond fishing with a long rod, and her red-and-white float is bobbing in the water. Behind her, a bearded man in a green shirt is digging a hole in the grass with a shovel beside a mound of dirt. On the path at the right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is holding a kite string with his arm up, but his purple kite is caught in the branches of a big tree. At the bottom, a garden hose runs from the left behind a flower bed to a sprinkler. The sprinkler is spraying its water in arcs onto a wooden crate, not onto the flowers, so the crate is wet and sitting in a puddle while the flower bed gets nothing. There is also an empty wooden bench beside the path.
