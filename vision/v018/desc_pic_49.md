# Picture 49 - Description

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden behind a house, enclosed by a tall wooden picket fence, with trees and hedges behind it and a sun and two clouds in the sky. On the left is a cream-coloured house with a red roof, a window and a blue door, with a bed of colourful flowers and a hose reel in front of it. In the middle-left foreground an older man in a white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers stands with his hands on his hips, and he is wearing an upside-down blue bucket on his head like a hat. Behind him, near the fence, a woman in a blue hoodie with a ponytail is painting the fence blue with a roller on a long handle; a few fence boards are already blue, and a paint tin with blue paint sits on the grass behind her. In the centre-right there is an empty swing set with a single red swing. On the far right, next to a paved path and a wooden gate in the fence, a woman in a yellow dress is sweeping with a yellow broom. A grey wheelbarrow lies empty at the bottom centre and a teal watering can stands on the grass near the path. The odd thing is the man wearing a bucket on his head; it is the same blue as the paint, so it may be a paint bucket.
