# pic_14

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence along the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. A woman in a yellow dress and red shoes stands beside the flower bed holding a teal watering can. The water is not pouring down onto the flowers. It arcs upward out of the spout into the air, with drops flying above it, which is impossible. In front of the blue door, a brown-and-white dog with a red collar sits holding a red leash in its mouth, as if asking to be taken for a walk. In the front middle of the lawn, a white goat with small dark horns bends its head down to eat a tuft of grass. Nearby, on the grass, there is a red pet bowl full of food. On the right is a tall wooden pole with a little birdhouse on top. Two potted plants and a bush stand near the fence.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The water going upward from the watering can is impossible and looks deliberate. Another reading is that she is flinging water up into the air, but the can is tilted as if pouring normally, so the water defies gravity.
- The dog holding its own leash could mean it wants a walk. The leash might also be attached to its collar and simply picked up in its mouth. It's hard to tell where the leash attaches.
- The goat's head is turned at a slightly awkward, twisted angle while eating, but it reads as a goat grazing.
- It's unclear whom the pet bowl is for (the dog, presumably).
- A goat in a back garden is unusual but not impossible.
