# Picture 28

## Free description
This is a town shopping street or pedestrian square on a sunny but windy day. There is a sun in the top right, white wind lines in the sky, and a red bird flying in the top left. A row of colourful shops with striped awnings runs along the back. Near the middle, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers has both arms thrown up as her orange cap is blown off her head by the wind, along with some leaves. In the front centre, a little girl in a pink dress is holding an empty ice-cream cone and rubbing her eye as if crying, because her pink ice-cream scoop is lying melted on the pavement and a brown dog with a red collar is sniffing or licking it up. A woman in a yellow dress is walking to the left with her nose in an open book, heading towards a blue bucket on the ground that she might trip over. Near her, a bearded man in a green T-shirt is sweeping the pavement with a broom. On the far right, a man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands with his hands on his hips in front of a fruit stall, wearing an upside-down blue bucket on his head, which is odd. On the left, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses walks to the right carrying a yellow shopping bag, and there is also a tree, a bicycle in a bike rack, a bench, a street lamp, a bin, a wooden crate and two potted plants.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The blue thing on the right-hand man's head looks like an upside-down bucket worn as a hat. It could possibly be a tall blue hat, but its shape matches the blue bucket on the ground.
- The woman in yellow reading while walking: she is heading towards the blue bucket and might trip over it. The lamp post is right behind her, so one could also read it as her having just narrowly missed the lamp post. She is walking away from it, not into it.
- The girl's ice cream: most likely she dropped it and the dog is eating it. Another reading is that the dog knocked it out of her hand.
- The dog's head is drawn at an awkward angle, twisted and looking almost upside down, so it is a little hard to read.
- The flying cap and the leaves near it are in mid-air on purpose, because of the wind. Nothing else seems to be floating.
- The fruit stall's back legs and the man in red overlap a little, but he looks to be standing in front of it.
