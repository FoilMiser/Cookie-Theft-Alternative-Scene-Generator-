# Picture 34

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the cream red-roofed house on the left, the sun in the top right, two white clouds, a wooden fence across the back with a gate at the far right, and a paved path down the right side. A person with dark hair (and possibly a beard), wearing a green T-shirt and beige trousers, stands in the middle watering a colourful flower bed with a garden hose; the water arcs in a dashed stream from the nozzle down onto the flowers. The dark hose runs back along the grass to a hose reel sitting on the ground at the left end of the flower bed. Behind the person, a clothesline between two wooden posts holds a pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes. A black-and-white cat is walking along the top of the fence near the tree on the left. On the right is a grey swing frame with one empty red swing seat. The strange thing is a closed cardboard box hanging in mid-air in the sky above the fence and trees on the right, with nothing holding it up; it looks as if it is flying or falling, which seems impossible, and no one in the picture is reacting to it.
