# Picture 03

## Free description
This is a sunny town square paved with grey tiles, in front of a row of four colourful two-storey shops with striped awnings. On the left a blue ball with a white stripe is stuck in the branches of a tree. A young man in a blue hoodie stands next to the tree with both arms up and a worried, unhappy face, as if he can't reach the ball. In the middle back, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts has thrown a red ball (a dotted line shows its path) to a woman in a purple top and teal trousers, who has her hands out ready to catch it. Next to a lamp post, an older man in a cap and brown waistcoat is holding a camera to his eye and taking a picture toward the left. In the front centre, a little girl in a pink dress is holding a blue and a red balloon in one hand and reaching up with the other toward a purple balloon that has come loose and is floating away. She looks upset. On the right, a man in a red shirt and jeans is pushing a teal pram with a sleeping baby in it, heading left. Behind him is a market stall with crates of red, orange, yellow and green fruit, with a cardboard box beside it. There are potted plants in both bottom corners.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The young man in blue: his raised arms and upset face fit "can't reach the ball stuck in the tree". It could also be read as alarm, or as shouting/waving at someone. He is standing beside the trunk rather than directly under the ball.
- Who owns the ball in the tree is unclear. It might be his, or it might belong to the children playing catch.
- The older man's camera points left. He could be photographing the woman and boy playing catch, the young man, or just the shops.
- The pram is heading left in the general direction of the little girl. It is not clear whether it will reach her; it is probably just passing.
- The purple balloon is clearly loose (its string ends in mid-air, not in her hand), so she has lost it. The girl's hand reaches toward it without touching it.
- The market stall has no one tending it.
- Nothing looks physically impossible. The blue ball sits on a branch among the leaves, which reads as stuck rather than floating.
