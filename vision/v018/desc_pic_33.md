This back garden is under a grey, overcast sky with three dark grey storm clouds and no sun. The cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, a wooden fence runs across the back with a gate at the far right, and a paved path runs down the right side. A man with dark skin, short dark hair and a black beard, wearing a green T-shirt, belt and beige trousers, stands in the front centre-right looking up with wide eyes and pointing up at the dark clouds with one arm. His expression looks surprised or worried, as if he thinks it is about to rain. Behind him by the fence is a small red-roofed birdhouse on top of a tall wooden pole, and two potted green plants. A colourful flower bed sits in front of the house, a teal watering can is on the grass near the path, and there is a round bush beside the house and a tree behind it. No rain is falling yet.
