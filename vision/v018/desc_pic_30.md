# Picture 30

## Free description
This is a sunny playground with a sun in the top left, two white clouds, a dark metal railing fence across the back and a row of green bushes behind it. On the left is a slide with a red frame and ladder and a yellow-and-orange chute; a child with curly dark hair, a yellow top and orange shorts is sliding down it with arms up. In the middle is a tall swing frame with a single red seat that is empty and still. A green bin stands between the slide and the swings, and a wooden park bench sits at the back on the right next to a large tree. In the front right is a red seesaw on a grey triangular stand: an older man with grey hair, a blue cap, brown vest and white shirt is sitting at the low end reading an open book, while a girl in a pink dress sits at the high end up in the air, holding the handle, with little motion lines under her dangling feet. It looks as if the man is not paying attention to the seesaw at all, so the girl is stuck up high. In the front left is a sandpit with a red bucket and a blue spade in it, and a grey rock lies on the grass beside it.
