## Free description
This is a sunny playground surrounded by a dark metal railing fence, with hedges behind it. On the left there is a red seesaw. A small girl in a pink dress sits on the high end, up in the air, holding the handle, with her feet dangling. Little curved lines by her feet suggest she is kicking or wriggling. On the low end, resting on the ground, sits an older man with grey hair and a grey beard. He wears a blue cap, a white shirt, a brown vest and dark trousers. He is reading an open newspaper or book and pays no attention to the girl. Because he is much heavier and his end is down, the girl looks stuck up in the air. On the right, a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts kneels in a wooden sandpit, building a sandcastle with a little red flag on top. Behind him there is a tall red-framed slide with an orange/yellow chute and a ladder, but nobody is on it. There is also a green litter bin by the fence, a tree on the left, a bush on the right and a grey rock beside the sandpit. The odd part is the grown man sitting on a child's seesaw reading, which leaves the girl stranded at the top.
