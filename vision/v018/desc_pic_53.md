# Picture 53 - Description

## Free description
This is a busy, sunny and windy park with a pond, a winding path, a bench, a lamp post and trees; wind lines are drawn in the sky. At the top left, a red cap is being blown away by the wind along with a few leaves, and a woman in a yellow dress below it has both arms raised and her mouth open in surprise, as if it is her hat. Next to her, a woman in a blue hoodie walks along reading or holding an open newspaper, and a small blue bucket sits on the grass beside her. A red bird is perched on top of the lamp post. In the centre, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is throwing bread crumbs into the pond, where a white duck is swimming toward them. On the right, a child in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is jumping in the air with arms up in front of the pond. On the bench at left-centre, a woman in a purple top is absorbed in a red book while a blue bird pecks at the sandwich next to her, as if it is about to steal it. At the bottom left a park worker in an orange cap and orange high-visibility vest is raking leaves on the path. At the bottom centre an older man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat is reading a red book in a sitting position, but there is no bench or chair under him, so he is sitting on nothing.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The older man at the bottom is in a sitting position with knees bent but there is no seat under him; he could be read as sitting on an invisible chair or, less likely, crouching/squatting while reading. Either way it looks wrong.
- It is not certain the red cap belongs to the woman in yellow; she is reaching for it and looks surprised, so it seems to be hers.
- The child's shadow is drawn below and apart from the feet, so the child appears to be in mid-jump; the child's upper body overlaps the pond in the picture, but the child is standing on the grass in front of it.
- The woman in the blue hoodie could be reading a newspaper, a map, or struggling to hold papers in the wind.
- The blue bucket next to her has no obvious owner or purpose.
- There is a small white object near the blue bird on the bench that could be a cup or part of the plate.
