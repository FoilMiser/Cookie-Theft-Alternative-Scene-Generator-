# Picture 46

## Free description

This is a sunny town square or shopping street with a row of four two-storey shop buildings in pink, pale green, peach and cream. The shops have striped awnings over their windows and coloured doors. In the centre of the paved square, a young person in a blue hoodie and black trousers stands with both arms raised high. Their eyes are closed, and they are smiling and blushing, as if cheering, celebrating or about to be hugged. On the right, a man or boy in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is running or striding to the left towards them, with one arm reaching forward. It looks as if he is about to reach the cheering person, perhaps to hug them or greet them. Because the cheering person's eyes are closed, they might bump into each other. On the left there is a fruit stall with a teal-and-white awning and crates of green, yellow, orange and red fruit, but nobody is serving at it. A cardboard box sits on the ground beside the stall. A red bird flies in the sky near the top right, and the sun is in the top left. Around the square there is a black street lamp, a tree in a round planter, a planter box of flowers, two litter bins and two potted plants.
