# Picture 47

## Free description

This is a sunny playground in a park with a dark metal railing fence and hedges behind it. In the front left, two girls are riding a red see-saw together, facing each other and holding the handles. On the left end sits a girl in a pink dress with dark hair in a bun and yellow shoes. Her end is slightly raised, and her feet are off the ground. On the right end sits a girl in a yellow top and orange shorts with dark curly/braided hair. Her end is slightly lower, and her feet touch the grass, as if she is about to push off. The rest of the playground is empty: a swing frame with one red swing seat hangs still in the middle, and a yellow-and-orange slide with a red frame and ladder stands unused on the right. There is an empty wooden bench near the big tree on the left, a green litter bin by the fence, and a round bush on the right. In the bottom right there is a sandpit with a red bucket and a blue spade, with a grey rock beside it. The scene is calm and ordinary, and nothing looks wrong.
