# Picture 07

## Free description
This is a sunny town street paved with grey tiles, in front of four colourful two-storey shops (pink, pale green, peach and cream) with striped awnings. On the left is a market stall with crates of green, yellow, orange and red fruit, but nobody is tending it. A red bird is perched on top of a tall black lamp post. In front of the green shop, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers is watering a planter box of flowers with a teal watering can. In front of the peach shop, a young man in a blue hoodie and black trousers is facing left and waving one raised hand, with little motion marks around it. In the front centre, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts is holding an empty ice-cream cone and has his other hand at his head/eye, looking upset. His pink ice cream has fallen onto the ground and splattered. A brown dog with a red collar has its head down at the fallen ice cream, sniffing or eating it. On the right there is a tree in a round planter and a small café table with two black chairs, and a grey rubbish bin stands at the front left.

- The dog has no leash and no owner nearby. It might belong to the boy or be a stray. It is not clear whether it caused the ice cream to fall or just found it.
- It is unclear who the young man in blue is waving at. He faces left, so it could be the bearded man, someone off-picture, or the viewer. The raised hand with motion marks could also be read as hailing someone or calling for attention.
- The red bird is quite large for the top of the lamp. It reads as perched there, not floating.
