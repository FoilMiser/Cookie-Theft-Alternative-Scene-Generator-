# Picture 22

## Free description
This is a back garden on a gloomy day: the sky is grey and three big dark storm clouds hang overhead. A cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, and a wooden fence with a gate runs along the back. A pair of blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes are pegged on a washing line in the middle of the garden, so they look likely to get rained on. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress stands on the garden path near the gate, looking up with her mouth open and pointing at the dark clouds, as if warning that rain is coming. Below her, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun and glasses sits reading a red book, but there is no chair, bench or seat under her: she is sitting on thin air above the path. In the middle of the lawn a black-and-white cat is creeping or running low towards a blue bird that is pecking at the grass, so the cat looks about to pounce on it. There is an empty swing set with one red seat, a bed of colourful flowers in front of the house, a hose reel, and a teal watering can on the grass.
