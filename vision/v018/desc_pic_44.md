# Picture 44

## Free description

This is a sunny playground in a park with a dark metal railing fence and green hedges behind it. In the middle, a girl in a pink dress with dark hair in a bun stands looking up and holds a kite string with her arms raised. The string runs in a long sagging curve up to a purple kite, which is stuck in the leaves of a big tree on the left. The kite's tail with little bows hangs down below it. It looks like her kite has got caught in the tree and she is trying to pull it free. On the right, a second girl in a yellow top and orange shorts, with dark curly or braided hair, is sliding down a yellow-and-orange slide with a red frame and a ladder, and her hands are up near her head. A blue bird flies across the sky on the upper left. On the left there is an empty red see-saw, tipped with its left end down on the ground and its right end up. In the bottom right there is a sandpit with a red bucket and a blue spade in it, with a grey rock next to it. There is also a green litter bin by the fence and a round bush on the right.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The kite: I think it is stuck in the tree because it sits among the leaves and partly behind them. Another reading is that it is flying just in front of the tree. The leaves overlapping it suggest it is caught.
- The girl on the slide: her hands are raised near her head. She could be cheering ("whee") or holding her hair/head. Her exact position on the slide is a little hard to read. She seems to be near the top, partway down.
- The girl in pink might be pulling on the string to free the kite, or she might just be holding it while looking up at it.
- The see-saw is tilted with nobody on it, which is normal for an empty see-saw.
- Nothing appears to float unnaturally apart from the bird, which is flying.
