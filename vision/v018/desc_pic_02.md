# Picture 02

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence across the back, a gate on the far left and a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the right. At the back of the garden a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts has just thrown a red ball. A dotted line shows its path through the air toward a man in a red shirt and jeans, who is holding his hands out to catch it. On the left a small girl in pink is on a swing, eyes closed and smiling, with motion lines showing she is swinging. In the middle front a bearded man in a green shirt is walking while reading an open book, and a blue bucket is right in front of his foot, so he looks about to trip over it or kick it. Next to him a brown-and-white puppy is curled up asleep on the grass. On the right a woman in a yellow dress sits on a bench happily licking a pink ice-cream cone with her eyes closed. A white spotted dog with a blue collar is jumping up at her legs, apparently trying to get the ice cream, and she has not noticed. There is also a flower bed, a garden hose reel, a birdhouse on a pole and a wheelbarrow at the front left. The wheelbarrow's wheel looks square instead of round, which is impossible for a working wheel.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Bearded man and bucket: it reads as if he is about to trip over or kick the bucket because he is reading. It could also simply be standing near him.
- The spotted dog could be trying to grab the ice cream, or it could just be jumping up to greet or beg from the woman. She has her eyes closed and doesn't seem to notice either way.
- The man in red's pose (hands held together in front) is read as catching the ball. It could also be read as clapping.
- No one is pushing the toddler on the swing, yet she is shown in motion. Slightly odd for such a small child, but not impossible.
- The dog's paws/body overlap the woman's legs and the bench area. It looks like it is pressing against her legs rather than passing through them, but the overlap is a little cramped.
- The brown-and-white puppy and the spotted dog are two different animals. The puppy is asleep and unaffected by the action.
