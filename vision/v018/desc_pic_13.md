## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence along the back, and a gate and paved path on the left. On the path at the far left, a boy in a red shirt and blue trousers is sweeping dirt into a red dustpan. He is using a giant silver dinner fork as a broom, which is odd, and little dust clouds are rising from the pile. In the middle-right, a woman with a dark ponytail in a purple top and teal trousers kneels by the flower bed with a small pot holding a pink flower, as if she is about to plant it. On the far right, a boy or young man in a blue hoodie sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. While he reads, a blue bird on the bench next to him is pecking at his sandwich. He hasn't noticed. Two potted plants stand by the fence, there are a couple of bushes, and an empty grey wheelbarrow sits in the foreground.
