## Free description

This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right, a wooden fence across the back, and a gate and paved path on the left. In the middle, a man in an orange cap, an orange high-visibility vest, and dark blue trousers holds the end of a garden hose up in front of his face. He is peering at the nozzle, which is only letting out a single drop of water. The reason is a white goat standing on the hose. Between the goat and the hose reel by the flower bed, the hose has a big swollen bulge where water is backed up. It looks as if the water will burst out into the man's face when the goat steps off. Behind him, a clothesline strung between two wooden posts holds a yellow-and-red striped towel or shirt and a pair of blue jeans pegged up to dry. Two potted plants stand by the fence on the left, and a teal watering can sits on the grass near the path. A colourful flower bed and a bush are next to the house.
