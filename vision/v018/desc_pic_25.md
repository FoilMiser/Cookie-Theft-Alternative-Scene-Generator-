# Picture 25

## Free description
This is a back garden on a sunny day, with the sun in the top right and two white clouds. A cream house with a red roof and blue door is on the left, and a wooden fence runs along the back, with a gate and a paved path on the right. Blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes hang on the washing line, and a small birdhouse with a red roof stands on a tall pole. A tan-and-white dog with a red collar sits on the doorstep holding its red lead in its mouth, as if asking to be taken for a walk. In the middle front of the lawn, a white dog with a brown ear is sitting up on its back legs, holding an open newspaper in its front paws and "reading" it like a person. This is clearly impossible or comic. On the right, a person in a blue hoodie with a brown ponytail is mid-kick next to an orange ball on the grass, with their foot swung out over or just past the ball. A red bowl full of dog food sits on the grass near the front.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The dog reading the newspaper is the main impossible thing. Dogs can't hold or read a paper. It is drawn deliberately, sitting upright with the paper held open in both paws.
- The kick could be read as just about to hit the ball or as a miss, since the foot has swung past or above the ball, which is still on the ground. The kick is heading left, roughly towards the newspaper dog and the bowl.
- The dog at the door could be read as wanting a walk (most likely) or simply carrying its lead.
