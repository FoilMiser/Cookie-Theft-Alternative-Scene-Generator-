# Picture 48

## Free description

This is a sunny back garden behind a cream house with a red roof and a blue door, with a wooden fence, a gate and a stone path on the right. On the bench at the left, an old man in a flat cap, white shirt, brown waistcoat and dark trousers sits drinking from a cup. In the middle, a man in an orange cap and an orange high-visibility vest with reflective stripes holds the end of a long teal garden hose up in front of him. He is peering into the nozzle, and only a single drop of water is coming out. The hose runs from a hose reel by the flowerbed, across the lawn, to him. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar is standing on the hose, and just behind the dog's feet the hose has a big swollen bulge. So the dog is blocking the water. When the dog steps off, the water will probably burst out and spray the man in the face. On the path at the right, an old woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt is sweeping dirt into a red dustpan. She is using a giant white dinner fork instead of a broom, which is absurd and impossible. A washing line in the back holds blue jeans and a yellow towel with red stripes, and an empty grey wheelbarrow stands on the lawn at the bottom.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The bulge in the hose: I read it as water building up because the dog is standing on the hose. The bulge is on the reel side of the dog, which fits. It could also be read as a hose nozzle or a connector piece lying on the ground, but it looks like a swelling of the hose itself.
- The man in the hi-vis vest and cap looks like a workman or utility worker rather than a gardener. That is slightly unusual for a home garden, but not impossible.
- The droplet at the nozzle could be read as "only a trickle is coming out" or "water is just starting to come out".
- The giant fork used as a broom is clearly impossible/absurd: a dinner fork that size doesn't exist and wouldn't sweep well.
- What the old man is drinking is unclear (tea/coffee from a cup or mug).
- Nothing else floats or passes through anything.
