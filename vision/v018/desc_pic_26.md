# Picture 26

## Free description
This is a playground in a park on a gloomy day: the sky is grey and three dark storm clouds hang overhead. Along the back there is a dark metal railing fence with hedges behind it. On the far left, a bearded man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers stands with his hands on his hips, wearing an upside-down blue bucket on his head like a hat, which is odd. Next to him, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts is sliding down the yellow-and-orange slide. Behind the slide, an elderly woman with grey hair and glasses, in a teal top and grey skirt, stands looking and pointing up at the dark clouds, as if warning that rain is coming. In the centre there is a swing set with one empty red seat. At the front, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers walks to the right, holding a red lead attached to a white dog with brown spots. On the right, an older man in a grey cap and brown waistcoat sits on the low end of a red seesaw reading a book, while a little girl in a pink dress sits up in the air on the high end, kicking her legs. She looks stuck up there because he is sitting and not paying attention. The sandpit at the front left is empty except for a red bucket and a blue spade, and a small brown-and-white puppy is curled up asleep on the grass in the bottom right corner.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The blue object on the bearded man's head looks like an upside-down bucket worn as a hat. It could also be read as an odd tall blue hat, but its shape matches a bucket.
- The seesaw: the man appears to be sitting on the low end of the plank (his bottom is at the plank and his feet are on the ground). One reading is that he sat on it to read and has unknowingly trapped the girl up high. Another is that he is a bystander sitting close to, but not on, the seesaw, in which case the plank would have no reason to be tilted with the girl up.
- The girl's leg motion lines could mean she is happily swinging her legs or kicking to try to get down. Her face looks fairly neutral.
- The child on the slide: we seem to see mostly the back or side of the head, so it is hard to tell which way they are facing or what their expression is. The raised arm could be "wheee" or holding the head.
- The dog on the lead faces left while the woman walks right, so they look as if they are going in opposite directions, or the dog is pulling back.
- The elderly woman stands right up against the fence, behind the slide, and is quite small, so she reads as being in the background.
