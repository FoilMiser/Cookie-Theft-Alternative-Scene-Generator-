# Picture 37

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence across the back, and a gate and paved path on the right. In the middle, a bearded man in a green shirt and khaki trousers stands with his hands up to catch a red-and-white ball. A dotted line shows the ball has been thrown in an arc by a little girl in a pink dress on the right, who is leaning forward with her arms out as if she has just thrown it. At the front, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds the strings of a purple balloon and a red balloon in one hand. His other arm reaches up toward a yellow balloon whose string is loose, so it seems to have just slipped away and is floating off. He looks up at it with an open, surprised mouth. Lower right, a lawn sprinkler on a blue hose is spraying arcs of water to the left, right onto a cardboard box, which is wet and sitting in a small puddle. A red bird sits on top of the fence, a blue bird flies through the sky, and a birdhouse on a tall pole stands near the girl. There is also a flower bed and a hose reel near the house. The things that seem about to go wrong are the yellow balloon escaping and the box getting soaked.
