## What I see
An older woman with grey hair in a bun and dark glasses walks to the left across green grass, reading an open book that she holds out in front of her. She wears a teal top, a slate-blue skirt and brown shoes. A closed cardboard box sits on the grass just ahead of her, and a dark metal railing fence runs along the top of the picture behind her. She is looking at the book and not at the box, so she seems to be walking towards it.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The glasses are drawn oddly: a dark round lens sits on the face with a dark shape at the side, so they could be read as sunglasses or as ordinary reading glasses seen side-on. The eye and the lens are hard to separate.
- Only one hand is clearly visible, under the near edge of the book. Something pale at the far side of the book, next to her body, may be her other hand or arm, or it may be part of the book's pages.
- The book's lines of text are drawn as thick dashes, as a symbol for writing. The book seems to tilt away from her face rather than towards it, so she could be reading it or just carrying it open.
- The legs are thin and tube-like compared with the upper body, and they come out of the skirt close together in a wide stride. It looks a little stiff but not broken.
- The box could be either a parcel that was left there or just a trip hazard; nothing shows who it belongs to. It is close to her but not touching her.
