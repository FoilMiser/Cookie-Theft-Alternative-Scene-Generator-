## What I see

No person or animal is in this cut-out. A round flower bed of brown soil holds tall, colourful flowers in orange, red, pink, purple and yellow, with a green bush behind it. Next to the bed, on the grass, is a garden hose wound on a dark teal reel mounted on a dark box-like base. A short length of hose comes off the reel and ends in a yellow, pointed nozzle lying on the grass, which is for watering. Above and to the right is part of a cream house wall with a blue door, a gold round doorknob and a grey doorstep.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The yellow pointed tip could be a hose nozzle or a small cone or funnel; its shape is simple, and I read it as a nozzle because it is joined to the hose.
- The hose is very short here: it curves down from the reel and ends almost straight away, so it looks more like a stub or a hook than a usable length of hose. It could also read as a handle attached to the reel.
- The dark base could be a stand for the reel or a small box or bucket behind it.
- No water is coming from the nozzle, and nobody is holding it, so it appears put down and not in use.
- The flowers on the left are cut off by the edge of the picture.
- The bush at the top is cut off and appears to sit right behind the house wall corner.
