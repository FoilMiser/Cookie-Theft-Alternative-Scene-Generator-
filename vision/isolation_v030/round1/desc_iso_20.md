## What I see

A girl or woman with short dark hair, wearing a yellow short-sleeved dress and red shoes, stands on green grass on the right. She holds a yellow hose nozzle out towards a round brown flower bed, and a blue arc of water falls from it onto the mixed red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers, with a few droplets near the soil, so she is watering the flowers. A dark teal garden hose runs from her hand down along the ground and around the bed to a round hose reel on a dark stand at the lower left. Behind her is a wooden plank fence and a green bush, and at the left is part of a cream house wall with a blue door that has a round yellow knob and a grey step.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- Near her hand the hose goes straight down in a stiff vertical section, ending in a squared-off shape by her hip, before continuing along the ground. It could be the hose hanging down or a separate handle or post, and it looks slightly disconnected from the nozzle.
- The water stream looks like a solid, thick pale-blue ribbon rather than a spray, and it starts abruptly at the nozzle.
- Her legs are thin and slightly crossed, and her feet overlap in a way that makes the red shoes look stacked. The torso and skirt join at a narrow belt, giving an oddly stiff, tube-like body.
- The hose reel could be read as a reel on a stand or as a wheel or spool-shaped machine. The hose passes in front of the flower bed edge and loops close to it, though it does not seem to go through anything.
- The flowers are very large relative to the bed and to the person, and they stand on straight stalks with small leaves that look a little stuck on.
