## What I see

A brown-haired man in a red short-sleeved shirt, blue jeans with a brown belt and dark shoes stands on a lawn watering a flower bed with a garden hose. He holds a yellow nozzle out in front of him, and a pale blue arc of water sprays from it down onto the colourful flowers, with drops falling around where it lands. The dark teal hose runs from the nozzle down along his side, along the ground around the front of the bed, and back to a round hose reel on a dark base beside the house. Behind him are a tall wooden fence and a green bush, and on the left is part of a cream house wall with a blue door, a gold doorknob and a grey doorstep.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The water lands in the middle of the flowers and the arc passes in front of some of them, so it is hard to tell whether it is hitting the blooms or the soil behind them.
- The hose near the man rises as a stiff, nearly straight vertical section just below the nozzle and then loops sharply, so it looks more like a rigid pipe or stand standing beside his leg than a hose he is holding. It is not clear how the nozzle connects to it, since the hose seems to go up behind his hand.
- The man's far arm is not clearly visible; only one arm is seen reaching for the nozzle.
- His face has a small pinkish mark near the mouth and nose, which could be his mouth or a blemish; his expression is hard to read.
- His legs are long, thin and close together and his feet overlap, which makes the stance look a bit stiff and narrow.
- The hose reel looks very large compared with the door and the man's distance from it, though this may just be because it is closer to the viewer.
