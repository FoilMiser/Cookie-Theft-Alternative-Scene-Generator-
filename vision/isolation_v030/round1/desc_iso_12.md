# iso_12

## What I see
A garden corner with a large green tree whose brown trunk forks into two branches, with a wooden picket fence in front of it and a low green hedge behind the fence. An orange fish, with a dark eye, a curved gill line and a forked tail, sits in the middle of the tree at the end of the right-hand branch. At the left edge is part of a cream house wall with a red roof, and at the top edge are the bottoms of white clouds in a blue sky. No person is visible; the fish is the only animal, and it is not doing anything other than resting on the branch.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fish is the strange part: a fish in a tree makes no sense as a real scene. It could be read as a fish perched on or stuck to the branch end, or as a decoration or toy hung in the tree. It could also be read as an orange leaf or fruit shape, but the eye and tail make it look like a fish.
- It looks like the branch runs right up into the fish, so the fish may be growing out of or joined to the branch rather than sitting on it.
- The fish is large compared with the branch and is drawn flat on top of the foliage, so it looks pasted on rather than among the leaves.
- The left-hand branch partly disappears behind a small leaf clump, and a sliver of green appears along the trunk near the fork; I cannot tell if that is foliage in front of the trunk or a drawing overlap.
- The lighter leaf clumps inside the tree look like bushes floating in the foliage, though they most likely just stand for leaf texture.
