## What I see
A woman with dark hair tied back, a purple top and teal trousers stands at the edge of a blue pond, leaning forward with one hand held out over the water. A white duck on the water bends its head down, pecking at a few small pale oval pieces floating in front of it. She seems to be feeding the duck with bits of bread or grain, taken from a brown paper bag that she is holding against her body. Green reeds with brown tops stand at the left end of the pond, and part of a purple flower growing from a brown pot or mound shows in the bottom-right corner.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The brown rectangular object at her front could be a paper bag or a small cardboard box. It seems to sit in front of or behind her arm at the waist, and I cannot clearly see which hand holds it, so it could almost be floating there beside her.
- The pale ovals could be bread pieces, seeds or pebbles. They appear to sit on the water; one lies apart from the others, nearer to her.
- The white lines near the duck's tail could be ripples on the water or motion marks, and they look a little like loose sticks.
- Her feet look bunched together, with one shoe on top of the other, and the legs are long and rather tube-like, so the stance is slightly stiff. She seems to stand on the grass with her body overlapping the pond edge.
- There is a pale patch on the duck's back, which reads as its folded wing but could also be a separate object resting on it.
- The object at the bottom right is cut off: it could be a flower growing out of a pot, or a flower beside a rock or mound of soil.
