- The right branch curves out nearly horizontally and ends abruptly under the fish, looking more like a perch or a bent pipe than a natural limb.
- The left branch ends in a blunt, cut-off shape against the foliage, and there is a small bit of green showing between the two branches at the fork that looks a little untidy.
- The lighter leaf clumps at upper left overlap one another, with a darker clump partly behind the lower one, which looks slightly doubled up.
