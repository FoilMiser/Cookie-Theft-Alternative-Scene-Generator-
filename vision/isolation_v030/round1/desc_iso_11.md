## What I see
A large round-topped tree with a brown trunk stands on green grass, with a pale blue sky and rolling green hills behind it. A bright orange fish sits on the end of a curved branch that sweeps out to the right from the top of the trunk, in front of the leafy crown. A small dark green bush is at the lower right, and a sliver of a pale sand-coloured shape shows at the bottom-left corner. There are no people or animals other than the fish, which is not doing anything except sitting there.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- A fish in a tree is the strange thing here. It could be read as a real fish wrongly placed on a branch, or as a piece of fruit or a toy or ornament shaped like a fish hung on the tree. It looks like a whole fish lying sideways with an eye, a gill line and a tail, and it does not appear to be hanging from anything.
- The fish seems to balance on, or be stuck to, the tip of the branch, with the tail touching where the branch ends. The join between branch and fish is abrupt: it could look as if the branch grows into the fish.
- The branch is a single smooth curve, thick at the trunk and tapering, and it is the only branch visible. It is drawn on top of the crown rather than being hidden by the leaves, which looks slightly pasted-on.
- The fish is large compared with the bush, so the scale is unclear.
- The pale shape in the bottom-left corner is cut off and I can't tell what it is; it could be a path or a patch of sand.
