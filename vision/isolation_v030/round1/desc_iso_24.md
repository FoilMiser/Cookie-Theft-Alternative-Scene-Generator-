## What I see

A large cartoon tree with a brown trunk and a big, dark green, cloud-shaped crown stands on green grass, with rolling hills and pale blue sky behind it. An orange fish with a dark eye, a line near its gills and a triangular tail is lying on top of a branch that curves out to the right from the trunk. A second, shorter branch goes up and to the left, ending in a small tuft of lighter green leaves. A rounded dark green bush sits on the grass at the lower right, and a pale tan shape, possibly a path, shows in the bottom-left corner.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- A fish in a tree is strange: it could be a deliberate absurdity (the fish simply does not belong there), or the orange shape could be meant as a fruit or a bird, though it reads clearly as a fish to me.
- The fish sits on the end of the branch as if balanced or stuck to it, with no sign of how it got there; it could also be read as growing from the branch like a fruit.
- The right-hand branch is drawn in front of the leaf mass rather than among the leaves, and it stops abruptly under the fish, so it looks pasted on top of the crown.
- The left-hand branch with its small leaf tuft also overlaps the crown oddly: the tuft is a smaller clump drawn over the big crown, and the branch has a blunt, slightly lumpy join where it meets the trunk.
- The pale shape at the bottom-left is cut off, so I cannot tell whether it is a path, a patch of sand or something else.
