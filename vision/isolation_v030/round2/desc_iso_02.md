## What I see

A woman or girl with short dark hair, wearing a yellow sleeveless dress and red shoes, stands on a green lawn holding a garden hose. She is watering a round brown flower bed full of red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers; a pale blue arc of water runs from the yellow nozzle down onto the flowers, with droplets around where it lands. The dark teal hose runs from her hand down to the ground, across the grass, and back to a round hose reel on a dark stand at lower left. Behind are a cream house wall with a blue door and a grey step, a green bush, and a brown wooden fence.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- The person's age is hard to tell: could be a girl or a slim adult woman.
- The hose rises up alongside her body in a stiff loop near the hand, and it seems to stand up by itself before dropping to the ground; it could be read as held or as an oddly rigid hose.
- The hand holding the nozzle is small and simplified, so the grip is not very clear, and the nozzle seems to sit at the end of the water arc as much as in the hand.
- Her face has a small dark mark or marks near the nose and mouth that are hard to read at this size (could be the mouth and nose, or a smudge).
- The legs look thin and a little stiff under the dress, and the feet overlap each other so they read as a jumble of red shoes.
- The flowers have long stiff stems that look like they stand separately rather than growing as natural plants, though this reads as a cartoon style.
