## What I see

A brown-haired man or boy in a red short-sleeved shirt, blue trousers with a brown belt, and dark shoes stands on a green lawn, watering a flower bed with a garden hose. He holds a yellow nozzle out in front of him at waist height, and a pale blue arc of water with droplets falls onto a round bed of brown soil full of red, yellow, orange, pink and purple flowers. The dark teal hose runs from a reel on a small stand at lower left, along the grass under the front of the flower bed, and up to his hand. Behind him is a wooden plank fence, and at the left is part of a cream house wall with a blue door, a round yellow door knob and a grey step, with a green bush beside the wall.

## Unsure or ambiguous

- His face has a small dark mark or shape around the mouth and nose, which is hard to read at this size. It could be a small open mouth, or a nose and mouth run together into a blob.
- The hose climbs up alongside his body and makes a tight bend at about waist height before reaching the nozzle. It looks a little stiff, as though it is standing up on its own in the air rather than hanging from his hand, and it passes close to his hip.
- The water arc starts thick and solid-looking at the nozzle, and it ends as a broad band landing among the flower stems. It could read as water or as a pale blue tube or ribbon.
- His arm looks short and comes straight forward from the shoulder, and his hand is small and hard to make out where it grips the nozzle.
- His two feet are close together and overlap, angled oddly so that it is hard to tell which shoe belongs to which leg. His legs also look long and thin compared with his upper body.
- The bush appears to sit partly behind the house wall and is cut off by it, which seems fine, but it is not clear how far back it stands.
