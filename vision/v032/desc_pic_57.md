# Picture 57

## Free description
It is a calm, sunny day in a back garden. On the left, in front of the house's blue door, a brown dog with floppy ears and a red collar sits holding a red leash in its mouth. The leash trails down to a loop on the ground, as if the dog wants to be taken for a walk. Left of centre, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt kneels on the grass beside the flower bed, reaching toward the flowers. A terracotta pot with a pink flower stands on the grass in front of the bed, perhaps waiting to be planted. Behind her, a clothesline holds blue trousers and a yellow towel with red stripes, hanging straight down. On the right, a man with dark skin, short black hair, a green T-shirt and beige trousers is walking to the right holding a red leash. On the other end of the leash, a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar walks ahead of him on the path toward the gate. A grey wheelbarrow stands empty on the grass at the front. The brown dog by the door seems to have been left behind while the other dog goes for a walk. Nothing looks physically impossible.
