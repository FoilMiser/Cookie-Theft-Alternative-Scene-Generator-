## Free description
This is a busy sunny park with a pond on the left, green hills behind, a lamp post in the middle and a curved path on the right. A white duck swims on the pond towards some small pieces of bread floating on the water. A girl in a pink dress stands at the pond edge holding a small brown bag, so she seems to be feeding it. In the front an old man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat is watering a flower bed, but he is pouring the water from a frying pan, not a watering can. On the far left a white dog in a blue scarf sits up holding an open newspaper as if reading it, which is impossible. In the centre front a child in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts holds an empty ice-cream cone and has a hand to their head, looking upset. Their pink ice cream has fallen on the ground, and a brown dog with a red collar is sniffing or licking it. On the right a boy in a red cap is pulling on the string of a purple kite that is stuck in a tree. An older woman with glasses sweeps leaves on the path near an empty bench, a white goat eats grass at the back, and a blue bird flies overhead.
