# Picture 50

## Free description
A sunny playground with a black metal railing fence across the back, hedges behind it, a big tree on the left and a sun at top right. Two children play on a red seesaw on the left. The child on the left end, a girl with dark curly or braided hair, a yellow top and orange shorts, is on the lower end, and a girl in a pink dress with her hair in a bun sits on the raised right end. In the middle, a man with dark skin, a green T-shirt and khaki trousers stands eating or licking a pink ice-cream cone, looking at it. On the right is a slide with a red frame, a ladder at the back and a yellow-and-orange chute, with nobody on it. At the bottom right is a wooden sandpit with a red bucket and a blue spade in it, and a grey rock next to it. A green rubbish bin stands by the fence and there is a bush on the far right. Everything looks normal and calm.
