# pic_22

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a wooden fence across the back and a wooden gate on the right. A paved path runs from the gate down the right side of the picture. On the path, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie, black trousers and white trainers is sweeping a small pile of dirt towards a red dustpan. Strangely, he is not using a broom but a giant silver fork, like an enormous table fork almost as tall as him. A small grey dust cloud rises from the pile, and motion lines under the fork show he is pushing it along. That is an odd, unsuitable tool, because the dirt would slip through the prongs. The rest of the garden is quiet: an empty wooden bench at the front left, a flower bed in front of the house, an empty wheelbarrow at the front centre, two potted plants near the fence, and a birdhouse on a tall pole. There are no other people or animals. Nothing else looks wrong.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fork could be read two ways: (a) a giant table fork used by mistake as a broom, or (b) some kind of garden fork. Its rounded shape and four curved tines look much more like cutlery than a garden tool, so it reads as the wrong tool for sweeping.
- The boy's body and the fork handle overlap a bit around his hands, but it reads as him holding it.
