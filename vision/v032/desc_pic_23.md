# pic_23

## Free description
A busy, sunny town street with a row of colourful shops (pink, pale green, peach and cream) behind a wide paved pavement. On the left, by a fruit and vegetable stall, an old man in a cap and brown waistcoat walks to the right with his nose in an open book. A cardboard box sits on the ground right in his path, so he looks about to trip over it. Behind him, a girl or young woman in a yellow dress sweeps a pile of autumn leaves with a broom beside a black lamp post, and a red bird perches on top of the lamp. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers stands in front of a shop door, waving. In the centre, a man in a green T-shirt sits on a bench reading a red book. A blue bird on the bench beside him is pecking at his sandwich and seems about to steal it while he isn't looking. A young man in a red T-shirt and jeans walks to the right eating a pink ice cream. A blue-and-white beach ball is stuck in the branches of a tree on the right, and a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie points up at it. Two impossible things stand out: a red bicycle at the front has square wheels, and a brown dog on the right sits up reading a newspaper held in its paws like a person.
