## Free description
This is a busy, sunny town street or square with four coloured shop buildings with striped awnings, a street lamp, a tree in a round planter and a fruit and vegetable stall. On the far left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie sweeps a pile of autumn leaves with a broom, while a little girl in a pink dress points up at a blue-and-white beach ball stuck in the branches of the tree. An old man with a blue cap, a brown waistcoat and a white shirt walks along the front carrying a yellow shopping bag. In the middle, a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts jumps in the air with both arms up, smiling. Behind him, a woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers sits on a bench reading a red book. A blue bird is pecking at the sandwich lying next to her, so it is about to steal her lunch. An older woman with glasses, grey hair in a bun, a teal top and a grey skirt is watering a box of flowers by the green shop. Oddly, the stream of water rises upward from her watering can into the air instead of pouring down onto the flowers. At the front centre, a brown dog with a red neckerchief sits holding an open newspaper with its paws as if reading it, which is impossible. On the right, a man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers walks while reading an open book and is about to trip over a cardboard box on the ground in front of him. A red bird flies across the sky at the top left.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The watering can's water could be read two ways: (a) water spurting upward out of the can into the air like a fountain, the wrong way, or (b) an arc that rises and then falls onto the flowers. The drawn stream ends in droplets at the top, above the flowers, so it looks like the water is going up rather than down. It reads as unintentionally or deliberately strange.
- The watering can is hard to see because it is the same teal as the woman's top.
- The jumping boy could be jumping for joy or trying to reach the ball, but he is not under the tree, so I read it as jumping for joy.
- The item on the bench could be a sandwich or a burger.
- A dog reading a newspaper is clearly impossible, a deliberate-looking oddity.
