# pic_06

## Free description
A sunny park playground on green grass, with a dark metal railing fence and hedges across the back. There is a sun in the top right and two clouds. In the front left, a child in a yellow top and orange shorts holds the strings of a red and a yellow balloon in one hand. Their other arm is raised towards a blue balloon that is floating just out of reach, its string dangling right above the hand. The child looks worried, as if the blue balloon has just slipped away. A blue bird is perched on a branch in the tree above. Further back, an older man in a cap and brown vest sits on a bench sipping a red drink through a straw with his eyes closed. In the middle stands a swing set with one empty red swing. On the far right, past the slide, a boy in a red cap and blue top is flying an orange diamond-shaped kite high in the sky, with a bow-tied tail. In the front centre, an orange cat is crouched low and creeping towards a small red bird pecking on the grass, so the cat looks about to pounce. There is also an empty sandbox, a green bin and a grey rock.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The blue balloon can be read two ways. Most likely it has just escaped and the child is reaching to catch it. It could also be read as the child about to grab or hand over the balloon, or as the balloon caught near the tree.
- The cat and bird face each other. The cat is clearly stalking, but the bird could be about to fly off or not yet aware of it.
- The kite-flying boy stands right by the fence beyond the slide, quite far back. He reads as behind the slide rather than on it.
