# Picture 58

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with no people in it, only animals. The strangest thing is an orange goldfish up in the tree behind the house, sitting at the end of a branch as if it were a bird. A fish in a tree is impossible. In the front centre, a white goat with small dark curved horns has its head down and is eating a small tuft of grass. On the right at the front, a brown-and-white puppy lies curled up asleep on the grass with its eyes closed. On the left, a dark teal garden hose is wound on a reel, and its yellow nozzle lies on the grass. The hose is not in use. There is a flower bed with colourful flowers, a clothesline with blue trousers and a yellow striped towel hanging still, and an empty swing set with a red seat. A paved path leads to a closed gate on the right, and the house is on the left. A goat in an ordinary back garden is also a little unusual, but it looks calm.

## Inventory
- White goat: centre front. White with a small dark curved horn, head lowered. Eating a small tuft of grass.
- Puppy: right front, on the grass just left of the path. Brown with white muzzle and paws, eyes closed, lying curled up. Sleeping.
- Orange fish: upper left, in the tree behind the house. An orange goldfish with faint stripes, sitting at the end of a branch.

Objects:
- Hose reel and hose, front left: a dark teal reel with the hose coming off it and a yellow nozzle lying on the grass. No water is running.
- Tuft of grass, under the goat's mouth: what the goat is eating.
- Flower bed, centre-left: an oval patch of soil with red, yellow, orange, purple and pink flowers.
- Clothesline, centre back: blue trousers and a yellow towel with red stripes, hanging still.
- Swing set, right of centre: dark grey frame with one empty red seat hanging still.
- House on the left with a blue door. A closed wooden gate on the right, at the end of the paved path.
- Wooden fence, a bush by the house, the tree behind the house, the sun at top right and two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fish in the tree is the clearly impossible thing. It could be read as a real goldfish sitting on a branch, or as a fish-shaped ornament hanging in the tree. A branch leads straight to it and it is drawn like a real goldfish, so it looks like a real fish in the tree.
- The goat's legs look a little bent and awkward, but it still reads as a goat grazing.
- I can only see one horn clearly on the goat.
- The puppy has its eyes closed and is curled up. I read it as asleep, but it could just be resting.
- Apart from the fish, nothing looks like it is floating or passing through anything.
