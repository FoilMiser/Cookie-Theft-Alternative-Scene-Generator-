## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the cream house, red roof and blue door on the left and a wooden fence and gate at the back. A girl or young woman in a yellow dress holds the end of a garden hose up in front of her face and peers into the nozzle. Only a single drop is coming out. The reason seems to be a brown dog with a red collar standing right on the hose in the middle of the lawn. The hose looks squashed under it and swollen on the reel side, as if water is building up behind the dog. It looks as if, when the dog steps off, the water will spray straight into the girl's face. At the back right an old man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat is painting part of the fence blue with a long-handled roller, with a paint pot by his feet. In the right foreground, on the path, an older woman with glasses and grey hair stands with her hands on her hips wearing an upturned white bucket on her head. An empty wheelbarrow stands on the grass in the front centre.
