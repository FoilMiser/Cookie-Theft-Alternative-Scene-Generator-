The line runs from the rod tip down into the pond, ending in a red-and-white float that sits in a ring of ripples. He is looking at the float, waiting for a bite. There are cattail reeds at both ends of the pond. There are trees at the left and right, a few bushes, a grey rock in the front middle and a green rubbish bin in the bottom right corner. Nobody else is in the park, and nothing looks wrong or about to go wrong.
