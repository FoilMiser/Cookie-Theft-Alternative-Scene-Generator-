# pic_31

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with a wooden fence and gate, a cream house with a blue door on the right, and a paved path down the left side. A man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers stands in the middle holding a garden hose nozzle out in front of him. Only a single drop of water hangs from it, and he seems to be looking down at it as if wondering why no water is coming out. The hose runs from a reel near the house, across the lawn, and up to his hands. A white goat with small horns stands on the lawn between him and the flower bed, and its hooves appear to be standing right on the hose, which could be blocking the water. A goat in an ordinary back garden is itself a little unusual. A washing line with a yellow striped towel and blue jeans hangs behind them, and there is a birdhouse on a pole, two potted plants, an empty bench and a wheelbarrow.
