# pic_18

## Free description
A sunny playground on grass, with a dark metal railing fence across the back and trees and bushes behind it. On the left, a red seesaw is tipped right down on one side. An old man in a flat cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat sits on the low end of it. He is reading an open book and seems to have no idea anyone else is on the seesaw. A young girl in a pink dress sits on the high end, up in the air and holding the handle. Her mouth is open in an "o" as if she is surprised or calling out, and small curved lines under her dangling feet suggest she is wobbling, kicking or bouncing. She seems stuck up there because the man is heavier and not paying attention. In the middle distance, an elderly woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey skirt walks to the right in front of the fence, eating a pink ice cream cone. On the right there is a red-framed slide with a yellow-and-orange chute and a ladder at the back, nobody on it. In the bottom right is a wooden sandpit with a red bucket and a blue spade in it, and a grey rock next to it. A tree stands on the far left, a green bin sits by the fence, and a bush is on the far right.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The girl's situation could be read two ways: (a) she is stuck high up and alarmed because the reading man is weighing his end down, or (b) she is happily bouncing on the seesaw. Her open mouth and the marks under her feet make (a) more likely to me.
- It is unclear whether the man knows the girl is there. He looks only at his book. It is also odd for an adult to sit on a seesaw just to read.
- The motion arcs under the girl's feet make her look as though she is hovering slightly above the plank.
- The seesaw plank seems to pass behind or among the old man's legs. It mostly reads as him sitting on it, but the overlap is a little cramped.
