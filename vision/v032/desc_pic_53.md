# Picture 53

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof, window and blue door on the right, a wooden fence along the back with a gate at the far left, and a paved path leading to that gate. In the middle, a clothesline is strung between two wooden posts, with a white T-shirt and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. A man with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands under the line, reaching up with both hands to peg up (or unpeg) the washing. In the front left, a man with dark skin, a green T-shirt and khaki trousers is digging with a long-handled shovel, and there is a heap of brown soil next to the hole. An orange fish appears up in the tree behind the fence, on the right, as if sitting in the branches, which is impossible. A teal garden hose on a reel with a yellow nozzle lies on the grass at the bottom right, near a colourful flower bed and a bush. The sun is at top left with two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fish in the tree is clearly out of place; it could be read as resting on a branch or as floating in the air in front of the tree.
- The man at the clothesline could be hanging the washing up or taking it down; his hands are at the line near the T-shirt.
- The digger could be digging a hole or filling one in; the soil on his shovel and the heap next to him make digging the more likely reading.
