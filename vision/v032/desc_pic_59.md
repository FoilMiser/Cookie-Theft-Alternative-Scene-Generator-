# Picture 59

## Free description
It is a sunny day at a park playground with a dark metal railing fence and hedges behind it. On the left, a girl with dark hair in a pink dress slides down a yellow slide with her arms raised. In the centre, a man in an orange cap, an orange high-visibility vest with grey reflective stripes and blue trousers walks to the right carrying a cardboard box. He looks like a delivery worker. On the right, a boy with dark skin and curly hair, wearing a yellow top and orange shorts, holds the strings of a yellow balloon and a red balloon in one hand. With his other hand he is reaching up toward a purple balloon that floats above him and slightly to the left. The purple balloon's string is hanging loose just out of his reach, so it looks like it has just escaped and is drifting away. The boy looks up at it with his mouth open. In the front left is a sandpit with a red bucket and a blue spade, with a grey rock beside it. On the right there is an empty wooden bench, a tree and some bushes. The sun is at the top left. What seems about to happen is that the purple balloon floats away out of reach.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The end of the purple balloon's string is close to the boy's fingertips but just above them. I read it as just escaped and floating away. It could also be read as him about to catch it, or as holding it very loosely.
- The man's vest and cap could make him a construction or road worker rather than a delivery worker. The box makes "delivery" the stronger reading.
- The delivery man is looking ahead and does not seem to notice the balloon.
- I can't tell whether the girl on the slide notices the balloon.
- Nothing looks like it is passing through anything. The free-floating purple balloon is the only thing in the air, and a balloon can float.
