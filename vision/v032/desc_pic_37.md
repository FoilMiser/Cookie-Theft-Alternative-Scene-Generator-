## Free description

In the middle at the back, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers is digging with a shovel, with a pile of soil beside her. Next to the lamp post, an older man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat stands eating a pink ice cream cone. On the right, a girl in a pink dress holds a purple balloon and a blue balloon, while a red balloon with a loose string floats away over the pond; she is reaching up toward it, so she seems to have just let it go. At the far right, a boy in a yellow shirt and orange shorts holds a yellow balloon. In the front-centre, a black-and-white cat is crouching low and creeping toward a small red bird on the grass, as if about to pounce on it. There is an empty bench by the path and a round flowerbed at the bottom right.
