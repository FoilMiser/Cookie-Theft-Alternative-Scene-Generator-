# Picture 61

## Free description
It is a sunny day at a park playground with a dark metal railing fence. On the right is a red seesaw. An older man with grey hair, a blue-grey flat cap, a brown waistcoat over a white shirt and dark trousers sits on its low end, reading an open book with his feet on the ground. On the high end, a small girl with dark hair in a pink dress is stuck up in the air. She is holding the handle with her legs dangling, and there are small motion lines under her feet as if she is kicking them. Her mouth is open and she looks surprised or upset. The man is heavier and is paying attention to his book, not to her, so she can't come down. In the front centre, a child with dark skin and curly hair, wearing a yellow top and orange shorts, walks or runs to the right toward the seesaw. The child is holding a small pink ice cream cone or lollipop. On the left there is an empty slide, a green bin, a bush, and a sandpit with a red bucket and blue spade, with a grey rock beside it. There is a tree on the right, the sun at the top left and two clouds.
