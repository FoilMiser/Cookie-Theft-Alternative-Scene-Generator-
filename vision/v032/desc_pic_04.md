# pic_04

## Free description
A sunny back garden with green grass and a tall wooden fence across the back. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door, with a tree and a bush beside it. On the right, a wooden gate opens onto a paved path running down to the bottom right. On the left, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers waters a round flower bed with a teal watering can, and the water pours down onto the flowers. In the back, right of centre, a boy in a red T-shirt and blue jeans is painting the fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle. Several fence boards to his left are already blue. On the grass beside him are a paint tin and a paint tray of blue paint. A trail of blue paw prints leads from the paint tray across the grass to a white dog with brown spots on the path, and the dog's feet are blue. So the dog has clearly stepped in the paint and is walking away towards the gate, leaving prints. Oddly, a cardboard box is hanging in mid-air in the sky above the fence, tilted at an angle, with nobody near it.
