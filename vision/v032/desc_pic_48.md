# Picture 48

## Free description
A sunny day in a park with green hills and a line of hedges in the background, a bright sun at top left and two white clouds. A young man (or older boy) with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue trousers stands on the grass just in front of a pond on the right and is fishing. He holds a fishing rod in both hands, and the line runs down to a small red-and-white float bobbing in the water, with a ripple ring around it. A white duck swims on the right side of the pond, near some reeds, not far from the float. A sandy path winds from the bottom left of the picture up into the background. A tall wooden ladder stands upright on the grass beside the path, leaning slightly to the left, but there is nothing for it to lean against, so it seems to stand by itself. In the middle is a tall black lamp post, and there is a big tree on the left, a smaller tree on the right, a few bushes, a grey rock at the bottom centre and a green rubbish bin in the bottom right corner. Nothing else is happening; the fisherman seems to be waiting for a bite.
