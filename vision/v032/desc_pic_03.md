## Free description
A sunny back garden with green grass, a tall wooden fence across the back and a wooden gate in the fence on the left. A paved path runs from the gate down to the bottom-left corner. On the right is a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door, and there is a tree behind it. On the left, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie is kicking an orange ball. His body and face are turned to the right, but his kicking leg swings back to the left, where the ball is, and there are motion lines under the ball. In the middle, an older man in a grey cap and brown vest walks along carrying a cardboard box in both arms. Behind him, a washing line hangs between two posts with a yellow-and-red striped shirt or towel and a pair of blue jeans pegged on it. On the right, a woman in a purple top and teal trousers, with a dark ponytail, waters a round flower bed with a teal watering can. The water from her can looks odd: instead of pouring down onto the flowers, it shoots up in a curved stream above the flowers, with drops at the top, like a fountain.
