# pic_14

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the house on the right (red roof, blue door), a wooden fence with a gate on the left, a paved path from the gate down the left side, a washing line in the middle and a flower bed by the house. At the front left, a man in a green T-shirt and khaki trousers stands on the path holding a huge silver fork, shaped like a dinner fork but as big as a broom. He is scraping a small pile of dirt or gravel toward a red dustpan, raising a little puff of dust. He seems to be using the giant fork as if it were a broom, which looks wrong. In the middle, a boy in a yellow T-shirt and orange shorts has just thrown a red ball, and its dashed path arcs through the air toward a girl in a pink dress. The girl holds out her arms to catch it, so she is about to catch the ball. At the bottom centre, a lawn sprinkler connected to a green hose is spraying arcs of water onto a cardboard box sitting on the grass. The box is getting soaked and has a puddle under it, so it will probably be ruined.
