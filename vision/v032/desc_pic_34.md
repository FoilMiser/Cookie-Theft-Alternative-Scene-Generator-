# pic_34

## Free description
This is a sunny park with green hills, trees, a winding sandy path on the left and a blue pond on the right. In the front-centre, an older man in a flat cap, brown waistcoat, white shirt and dark trousers is eating a pink ice cream cone with his eyes closed, as if enjoying it. Just behind him and to the right, a girl or young woman in a yellow dress and red shoes is standing at the edge of the pond fishing with a rod. Her line goes down into the water, where a red-and-white float sits with a small ripple around it. A tall black lamp post stands in the middle of the park. On the left, beside the path, a wooden ladder stands on the grass leaning at an angle, but there is nothing for it to lean against, so it looks impossible. A green rubbish bin is in the bottom-right corner. Nothing seems about to happen, apart from perhaps a fish biting.
