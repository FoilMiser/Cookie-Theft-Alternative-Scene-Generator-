## Free description
This is a sunny park with a pond with reeds on the left, a tall black street lamp in the middle, a wooden bench and a winding sandy path on the right, and trees at both sides. At the front left, an older woman with grey hair in a bun, glasses, a teal top and a grey-blue skirt kneels on one knee beside a flower bed. She seems to be planting or tending a pink flower in a small terracotta pot, holding a little tool or stick down by the pot. In the front centre, a white goat with a dark curved horn has its head down, eating a tuft of grass. On the right, a man with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans stands next to the path holding a paper bag and sprinkling seeds. A dotted trail of seeds falls from his hand to two small birds, one red and one blue, which are pecking at the seeds on the path. Everyone seems busy with a calm, ordinary activity. The only slightly unexpected thing is a goat wandering loose in a park.
