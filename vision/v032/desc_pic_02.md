## Free description
A sunny town street with a row of colourful two-storey shop buildings along the back (cream, peach, pale green and pink), each with a striped awning. The sun is in the top right and there are two clouds. In the middle, a boy in a red T-shirt and blue jeans sits on a wooden bench happily licking a pink ice-cream cone, with his eyes closed. To his left, a brown dog with a red bandana sits up on its hind legs with its front paws raised, begging towards the boy and his ice cream. On the right, a man in a green shirt and khaki trousers walks to the right, leading a white dog with brown spots on a red leash. They are passing in front of a fruit and vegetable stall that has nobody serving at it. At the front centre, a red bicycle is parked in a bike rack. The scene also has a tree with a green bin beside it on the left, a planter of flowers, a tall street lamp, and potted plants in both bottom corners. Nothing in it looks dangerous or about to go wrong, except that the dog may be about to get (or try to get) some of the ice cream.
