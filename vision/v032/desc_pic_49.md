# Picture 49

## Free description
A sunny back garden, with a cream house with a red roof, a window and a blue door on the left, and a tall wooden fence with a gate across the back and right. A boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers sits on a wooden bench at the bottom left, drinking from a red cup through a straw. Next to him is an oval flower bed full of red, yellow, purple, pink and orange flowers. On the right, a man with dark skin, a green T-shirt and khaki trousers stands on a paved path and rakes up a small pile of leaves with a long rake. An orange cat lies curled up asleep on the grass at the bottom centre, with its eyes closed. Two potted plants stand on the lawn near the fence, and a teal watering can sits on the grass near the path. A tree shows behind the house, and the sun is at top right with two clouds. It looks like a calm, ordinary afternoon of gardening and relaxing; nothing seems about to happen.
