# pic_64

## Free description
A sunny back garden with a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the left, a tall wooden fence along the back with a gate on the right, and a paved path leading to the gate. An orange cat is walking along the top of the fence. A woman in a purple top and teal trousers is watering a flower bed with a teal watering can. The water looks wrong: it arcs upwards into the air from the can, with drops at the top, instead of pouring down onto the flowers. In the middle, a small girl in a pink dress holds her arms out in front of her, ready to catch. On the right, a boy in a red cap and blue hoodie has just thrown a red-and-white ball towards her. A dotted line shows its path, and the ball is in mid-air between them. It looks like the girl is about to catch, or try to catch, the ball. An empty grey wheelbarrow stands at the front.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The water from the watering can goes upwards, which is impossible. One reading is that the stream was drawn in reverse and was meant to pour down onto the flowers. Literally, it rises from the spout and ends in drops up in the air.
- The ball is somewhat higher than the girl's hands. She might catch it, or it might go over her head.
- The ball could be a cricket ball or a small red-and-white toy ball.
