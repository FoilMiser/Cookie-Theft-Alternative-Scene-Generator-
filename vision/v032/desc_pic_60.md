# Picture 60

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with the house on the right and a wooden gate on the left. Left of centre, a man with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans is painting the wooden fence blue with a paint roller on a long handle. One section of the fence is already painted blue, and a small tin of blue paint stands on the grass near his feet. At the front, a lawn sprinkler fed by a long green hose (coming in from the right edge) is spraying arcs of water to the left. The water lands on a cardboard box left on the grass. The box is wet, water is running down its sides and it is sitting in a puddle, so it is getting soaked. Nobody seems to have noticed. Something impossible is also going on: up in the tree behind the house on the right, an orange goldfish sits at the end of a branch. On the left there is a birdhouse on a tall pole and two potted green plants. There is also a flower bed in front of the house, a bush, and a paved path leading to the gate. The sun is at the top left.

## Inventory
- Man: left of centre, standing in front of the fence. Brown hair, red T-shirt, blue jeans, dark shoes. Holding a long-handled paint roller against the fence, painting it blue and looking at the roller.
- Orange fish: upper right, in the tree behind the house. An orange goldfish sitting at the end of a branch.

Objects:

- Paint tin, centre near the man's feet: a grey tin with blue paint inside and a handle.
- Sprinkler, front, right of centre: a dark sprinkler head on the end of the hose, spraying three arcs of water to the left.
- Green hose: runs from the sprinkler off the right edge of the picture.
- Cardboard box, front, left of centre: a taped box sitting in a puddle on the grass and being soaked by the sprinkler.
- Birdhouse on a pole, left: red roof, round entrance hole, no bird visible.
- Two potted plants, left of centre: terracotta pots with green leafy plants.
- Flower bed, right, in front of the house: red, yellow, orange, purple and pink flowers.
- House on the right with a blue door and a window. A closed wooden gate on the left, with the paved path leading to it.
- Wooden fence, a bush by the house, the tree behind the house, the sun at top left and two clouds.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- The fish in the tree is impossible. It could be read as a fish-shaped ornament, but it is drawn like a real goldfish sitting on a branch.
- The box could be a delivered parcel that was left on the lawn, or something put there on purpose. Since it is getting soaked, it reads as an accident.
- Of the three water arcs, one lands right on the box and the others land on the grass just beside it. The sprinkler seems to be pointing straight at the box.
- I can't tell whether the man is supposed to be painting the whole fence or just that section.
- Nothing looks like it is passing through anything.
