## Free description
This is a sunny back garden. A cream house with a red roof and a blue door is on the left, and a tall wooden fence with a gate runs along the back. A white dog with brown spots is near the fence in the middle. It is crouched with its front low, in a playful bow, next to a yellow-green tennis ball. A garden hose runs across the lawn from the left to a sprinkler in the front middle. The sprinkler's water arcs land right on a cardboard box sitting in a puddle, so the box is getting soaked instead of the plants. On the right, a woman (or older girl) with a dark ponytail, purple top and teal trousers stands on the paved path. She is holding a huge silver fork like a broom, pushing a little pile of dirt that is puffing up dust towards a red dustpan on the ground. Using a giant fork to sweep looks wrong; you would expect a broom. There is a flower bed of mixed colourful flowers at the front left, two bushes, and two potted plants near the back right.
