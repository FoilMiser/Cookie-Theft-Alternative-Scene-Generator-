## Free description
A sunny back garden with a tall wooden picket fence across the back, a wooden gate in the fence on the left and a cream house with a red roof and blue door on the right. A girl in a yellow dress stands by the fence, painting it blue with a paint roller on a long handle. Several fence boards to her left are already blue. On the grass behind her are a paint tin and a roller tray of blue paint. A white dog with brown spots and a blue collar walks to the right away from the tray, and a trail of blue paw prints leads from the tray to the dog. It has clearly stepped in the paint and is tracking it across the lawn, and the girl hasn't noticed. Further right, an old man in a cap, white shirt and brown waistcoat digs with a spade next to a mound of soil. A bed of bright flowers sits in front of the house. At the front there is an empty wheelbarrow and a hose reel with a hose and yellow nozzle, and a paved path runs along the left edge. Oddly, a cardboard box floats in the sky in the upper left, near the sun, with nothing holding it up and nobody near it.
