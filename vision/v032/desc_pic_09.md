# pic_09

## Free description
This is a sunny back garden with nobody in it, no people and no animals. On the left is a cream house with a red roof, a window with four panes and a blue front door with a grey step. A tall wooden fence runs across the back of the garden, with a wooden gate on the right, and a pale paved path leads from the gate down to the bottom right corner. In front of the house there is a round bed of colourful flowers, with two potted plants near the fence and a couple of green bushes. A teal watering can sits on the grass beside the path. The strange thing is a cardboard box hanging in mid-air in the sky above the fence, below the clouds and tilted at an angle, as if it were flying or tumbling through the air. Nobody is anywhere near it to have thrown it, so it looks like it is floating or falling out of the sky. It looks as if the box is about to drop into the garden.
