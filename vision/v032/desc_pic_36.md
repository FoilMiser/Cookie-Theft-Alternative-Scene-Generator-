## Free description

His pink ice cream has fallen onto the grass and made a splat, and a brown dog with a red collar is sniffing or licking it up. Further right, a girl in a pink dress is flying a yellow kite; its string runs up from her hands to the kite in the sky, passing just above the old man's head. A blue bird flies in the top right. The swing set has one empty red swing. The sandpit has a red bucket and a blue spade in it, and a grey rock lies beside it.
