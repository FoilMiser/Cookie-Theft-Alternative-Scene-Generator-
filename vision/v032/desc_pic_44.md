## Free description
It is a sunny but windy day in a back garden, with a cream house with a red roof and a blue door on the left and a wooden fence across the back. An old man in a flat cap and brown waistcoat waters the flower bed with a green garden hose that runs from a hose reel by the house. Further back, a young woman in a yellow dress is painting the fence blue with a long-handled paint roller. One section is already blue, and a pot of blue paint stands on the grass beside her. A metal A-frame swing set with an empty red seat stands in the middle-right. On the path at the right, a young man in a red T-shirt and blue jeans has both arms in the air and a startled face. His orange cap is flying away in the wind just above and to his left, with some leaves. White wind lines blow in from the right edge, and there are more wind lines in the sky near the clouds. It looks as if he is about to try to grab his hat before it blows further away.
