# Picture 51

## Free description
A back garden on a grey, gloomy day: the sky is overcast, with three dark storm clouds, so it looks like it might rain soon. A man with brown hair, a red T-shirt and blue jeans is painting the wooden fence blue with a paint roller on a stick; a section of the fence to his left is already blue, and a paint tin and paint tray sit on the grass by his feet. A trail of blue paw prints runs from the paint tray to a white dog with brown spots and a blue collar, whose paws are blue, so the dog has stepped in the paint and is walking off across the lawn. On the right, a woman in a yellow dress points up in surprise at an orange cat walking along the top of the fence, and a small blue bird sits on top of the fence near the painter. A little girl in a pink dress jumps in the air near the flower bed with both arms raised. In the front, a man with dark skin, a green T-shirt and khaki trousers is in a sitting position drinking from a red cup with a straw, but there is no chair, bench or anything under him, so he appears to be sitting on thin air. A wheelbarrow and a teal watering can are on the lawn, the house is on the left, and a paved path leads to the gate on the right.

- The dark clouds could just be a dull day or could mean rain is about to fall on the fresh paint.
- The woman's gesture reads as pointing at the cat on the fence, but she might be pointing at the bird; her arm is aimed toward the cat.
- The little girl could be jumping for joy or jumping/reaching toward something; she seems to be in mid-air because of the gap above her shadow.
