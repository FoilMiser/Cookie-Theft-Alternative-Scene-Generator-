# Picture 55

## Free description
It is a sunny but very windy day in a back garden. White wind lines are drawn across the sky and to the right of the picture. On the left is a cream house with a red roof and a blue door. In front of it, a young woman with a dark ponytail, a purple top and teal trousers sits on a wooden bench reading a red book. To the left of centre, a boy in a red cap, blue hoodie and dark trousers stands beside a flower bed. He holds a frying pan out by its handle and water pours out of it, so he seems to be using the frying pan as a watering can. The water drips onto the ground at the front edge of the flower bed. Behind him, a clothesline between two posts holds a pair of blue trousers and a yellow towel with red stripes, both blown out sideways to the left by the wind. To the right is a swing set with an empty red seat, hanging slightly tilted. On the far right, a man in a red shirt and blue jeans stands on the paved path with both arms up and his mouth open. He is looking up at his blue cap, which has been blown off his head and is flying away with a few leaves. It looks like he is about to lose the cap. A grey wheelbarrow stands empty on the grass at the front. The odd thing in the picture is the boy watering the flowers with a frying pan.

## Unsure or ambiguous
- To me the boy's object looks like a frying pan, but it could be a saucepan. Either way it is a cooking pan being used as a watering can, which looks odd or comic.
- The water from the pan falls on the front edge of the bed and the grass rather than clearly on the flowers. He could be missing the flowers, or just watering the bed.
- The man's raised arms and open mouth suggest the wind took his cap by surprise and he is grabbing for it. Another reading is that he threw it up himself, but the wind lines and leaves next to the cap make "blown off" the stronger reading.
- The swing seat is slightly tilted. This could be the wind moving it, or it could just be drawn at an angle.
- I can't tell whether the person on the bench is a teenage girl or an adult woman.
- Nothing seems to float or pass through anything apart from the cap, and the wind explains the cap.
