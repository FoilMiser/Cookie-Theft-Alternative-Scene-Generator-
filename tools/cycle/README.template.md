# TPDB v0.57: the list of v0.56's screen read on two blind benches; the game of catch, the frying pan and the heavy seesaw mended; all 64 templates through the gate, read a fifty-fourth time

v0.56 ended with 63 of its 64 templates through the gate. The game of catch failed where the ball stood at a garden fence's top, the frying pan was a watering can first to two readers of five, and two readers had the heavy seesaw's man ignoring the child. Sam's brief for v0.57: "Go for v0.57", the list of `vision/flaws_v056.md`.

Every new drawing and rule was put behind a switch whose other value is v0.56's, and was read blind in whole pictures before it went into scenes; what the readers did not take is not in the release.
- **The game of catch** has a flat flight toward the catcher's hands, kept off the top line of a fence or railing and away from a cat on the fence, and its catcher is a child or a teenager, the taller of the two throwing.
- **The frying pan** is hollow and dark inside on a wooden handle, the water runs over its rim as a sheet, and an unused watering can stands on the grass behind the holder.
- **The heavy seesaw's** man has no book: he sits with a hand on each knee and looks up at the child. The seesaw has a yellow seat with a back at each end and rubber buffers under the plank's ends.
- **Smaller rows:** the string of the kite in the tree lies on the grass; the bread thrower and the angler stand lower in front of the pond; in a park a blown cap and the wind's lines are off the pond; the paint is a deeper pink, the box in a walker's way darker, an orange cap purple; and the build now refuses a layout in which a scored thing does not stand off in grey.
- **The keys:** each action item lists the names its figure may be called by, and the scorers flag their judgement calls beside each score.
- **Read and not taken:** the pan's pale inside (a grey watering can to four readers of ten). **Looked at and left:** the other templates that the strict count marks down, the grown figures called young first, the thief bird's size.
- **Two benches judged it:** 125 readings of 27 of v0.56's pictures in up to four arms and of five new pictures built two ways, and 50 more in a second round.

**The release is like for like.** The planner's priors were left at v0.56's and every record of v0.56 was built again under revision 51 from its own regeneration data. Of v0.56's 126 pictures, 65 came back to the pixel and 17 more within 24 pixels; 41 were drawn again or moved, each with its own templates; three were replaced; and ten probes were added. So this screen is a second reading of 82 pictures and a first of the mended ones, picture for picture.

__INTRO_RESULT__

Everything is built by code and reproducible: the cast is modeled and posed in Blender (Blender 5.2.3 LTS), and the scenes are composed in SVG by the generator in `scenegen/`. From v0.24 the whole cycle also runs on Windows with an installed Blender (`RUNBOOK.md`, Setup).

Source and release history: https://github.com/FoilMiser/Cookie-Theft-Alternative-Scene-Generator- (each release is one commit; `RUNBOOK.md` there says how a release is made).

## The benches

Every reader was a fresh subagent given the screen's own reader prompt and its pictures and nothing else. The record is `vision/isolation_v057.md`.
- **Round 1.** Twenty-seven of v0.56's pictures were built again on their own records' plans with only the new drawings changed. Each was read as v0.56 screened it (one fresh reader, beside the screen's own reading), with the new drawing (two readers) and, for the pan and the seesaw, with an alternative (two); v0.55's deep pan came back in three pictures (three readers each); and five new pictures of the game of catch were built on one seed with the flat flight (two readers) and with the old arc (one). 125 readings by 16 readers, no reader with a scene twice. Five analysts who never saw a picture counted each row from the readers' own lines.
- **A reader's habit.** Counted by arm, the dark pan was a watering can first in 1 reading of 10 and the pale one in 4 of 10. Counted by reader, all ten such readings in the round came from four readers, each in every pan picture it had: "- Watering can, in the man's hand: grey, pan-shaped with a long handle; pouring water." The dark inside was taken, and the habit went to round 2.
- **What the new pictures showed.** The three old pictures of the game of catch all had a child catching. In the new ones, where a grown figure with raised hands caught a child's flat throw, who had thrown was asked: "- The ball could be thrown by the child to the woman, or thrown by the woman to the child; the dashed line from the child's hand suggests the child threw it."
- **Round 2.** Twelve readers, 50 readings. The pan with an unused watering can beside its holder (eight readers, 20 readings) and alone (four readers, 10), a reader with pan pictures of one arm only: "The strange thing is that he waters the flowers with a frying pan while the watering can is right beside him." ## Key words, frequency and age of acquisition


Unchanged from v0.20: every item record carries its key word's Zipf frequency (f, for things and actions only) and its
age of acquisition (a, from the bundled excerpt of Kuperman, Stadthagen-Gonzalez and Brysbaert, 2012) at weight 0;

## How to run

```bash
python -m venv .venv && source tools/env.sh      # from Git Bash on Windows too
pip install -r requirements.txt                  # the cast needs Blender 5.2.3 LTS: installed, or pip install bpy==5.2.3 on Python 3.13

python assets/cast/build_cast.py assets/cast/out057                 # build the cast (under a minute)
cd assets/cast && python gapcheck.py out057 person.kai && cd ../..  # check one character's poses for parts that come apart
python -m scenegen.generate --tier 3 --setting playground --seed 7 --out scenes
python -m scenegen.generate --from-record __REBUILD_EXAMPLE__ --out tpdb_work/check   # exact rebuild
python -m scenegen.reach --out reach.json                          # the poses the templates can ask for

python -m scenegen.batch showcase --out scenes
python -m scenegen.calibrate --out calibration.json
python -m scenegen.difficulty --check-aoa <the original Kuperman et al. file>   # compare the bundled AoA rows
python -m scenegen.combos --out combos.json
TPDB_TRIP48=O python vision/isolation_bench.py tpdb_work/bench tpwN_1 tpwN_2   # one template alone, cropped (here with revision 44's trip)
python vision/prepare_screen.py scenes,probes vision/v057   # then describe and score: vision/screen_prompts.md
bash tools/cycle/summarize.sh                               # add the screen to vision/screen_summary.json
python vision/gate.py vision/v057 scenes,probes
python -m scenegen.site --scenes scenes --gallery assets/cast/gallery057 --old-gallery <v0.56 renders of the same variants> \
    --combos combos.json --screen vision/screen_summary.json --out site
python3 package_release.py 0.57 --single   # the release as one zip, tpdb_v0.57.zip
```

The release is one zip, `tpdb_v0.57.zip`, about __ZIP_MB__ MB. Google Drive does not virus-scan a file over 100 MB, so it asks to confirm the download ("can't scan this file for viruses"); the file downloads as normal after that. `package_release.py` checks, file by file, that the zip holds every file of the release. Without `--single` it writes the same release as zip parts under 50 MB each, with the README and the set's pictures beside them, for browsing in Drive.

Exact rebuilds hold within a release: a v0.56 record rebuilds with the v0.56 package. They also depend on Pillow, pinned in `requirements.txt`: Pillow 13 resamples RGBA differently from 12, and every embedded asset changes. A different cairo moves a few pixels by one level and leaves the SVG identical. Renders from an installed Blender differ from the Linux module's only in sub-pixel anti-aliasing on the outlines, with identical anchors and geometry, so the library mixes the two.

## Known limits

- The weights are still the spec's starting values: every predicted completeness is a placeholder
  until the pilot fit.
- The blind screen used AI readers. It is a filter for illegible drawing; the pilot with people
  remains the test of difficulty.
- __LIMITS__
- The cast is a stand-in. Final character art would replace the models; rigs, poses, faces,
  anchors and manifests carry over.
