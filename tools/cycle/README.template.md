# TPDB v0.47: the cast's anatomy drawn again from a study of one figure, the weak actions redrawn on a blind bench, read a forty-fourth time

v0.46 ended with 60 of the 64 templates through the gate. Sam's brief for v0.47: "Go ahead and work on v0.47, addressing the flaws, and maybe focusing on one character, finding general anatomy flaws and defects and shortcomings, fix those up and make the character art cleaner and better, and apply that to the cast and animals, and refine weak actions too, to hopefully fix the screening". The work list came from two places:
- v0.46's list (`vision/flaws_v046.md`): the four templates that failed its gate (`dog_reading`, `about_to_trip`, `sweep_fork`, `blocked_hose`), the four under them (`bird_steals`, `cat_stalks_bird`, `water_upward`, `lost_balloon`) and seven oddities, O1 to O7;
- a study of v0.46's cast as drawings. The one character is the woman in the yellow dress, whose neck was v0.46's brief. She was rendered in 52 poses and angles, and 24 of those figures went to three fresh reviewers, one figure to a picture on a plain ground, each reviewer listing whatever in the drawing looks wrong, awkward or unfinished. Two more reviewers read the other nine people the same way, and two read eight animal figures each. A fault named by two reviews or more is the cast's and not one figure's: "Square coat-hanger shoulders", arms and legs "straight tubes of one width", hands "mitten blobs with one curl and no thumb", shoes "flat ovals or paddles with no toe, sole, heel or ankle", the nose "an outlined ring", in a turned view "the far eye is clipped by the head outline", and of the animals "Two eyes are drawn side by side on a head in side view".

The work is in the cast (builder 0.47.0) and in the placement rules (revision 44). Each change is behind a switch whose letter O gives v0.46's drawing, so the old and the new could go to a reviewer as a pair. A bench of five blind rounds (`vision/isolation_v047.md`) took these:
- **Every person:** the shoulders slope from the base of the neck; arms and legs taper, with an elbow, a slim wrist, a knee, a calf and an ankle, and a trouser leg narrows to a cuff; the hand is a mitten with its thumb standing clear; the shoe is a wedge with a rounded toe on a dark sole; a skirt's lower half turns with the thighs, and seated it wraps them; the nose has no line round it; the head is fuller between front and side, and a side view turns 48 degrees toward the viewer, a three-quarter face with both eyes whole.
- **Clothes:** the cardigan shows the top under it in a V and closes on a line of buttons; a garment made over another (the vest, the cardigan) rides on the finished surface of the one under it, so nothing comes through at a bent elbow; each rim of the glasses is a flat ring standing off the face, and the eyes are drawn smaller behind the lens, so skin shows round each eye.
- **Every animal:** the eyes of the dogs and cats sit further round the head, so a side view has one whole eye inside the outline; a coat's markings are patches of the body's own surface with no line of their own; a white coat's far legs are a light shade of the near ones; the dog's tail is shorter, tapered and curved; the paws are shorter and higher.
- **The weak actions:** the walker's trailing foot is up on its toe and the leading leg reaches forward to a flat foot, so the feet show which way a walker goes; the runner's front knee is bent and forward; a figure sitting on the ground has both legs out straight. The reading dog sits up with an open newspaper held in both forepaws below its muzzle (a pose of its own, drawn four times over the first four rounds). The dog on the hose stands still and square with its near forepaw flat on the hose. The pecking bird is tipped far over about its hips. The stalking cat's four paws are apart along the ground.
- **Two oddities of v0.46's cast:** the moustache is wider than the mouth, in two halves parted under the nose and a shade darker than the hair (O1); the trunk's cloth keeps the shading it has standing upright whatever the arms do, so no lighter patch lies where the arms cross (O2).
- **Placement revision 44, thirteen rules**, one or more for each template of the list, two that came of the design tests (below) and one found when no tier-4 isomorph would build: the dropped ice cream could be drawn with one child of the two, and had been since v0.28.
- **Put to readers and not taken:** three earlier drawings of the reading dog, two of the pecking bird, one of the stalking cat, the dog striding on the hose with a long low swelling, and the sweeper's heap with two leaves in it, which all three of its readers took for an animal.
- **Not changed:** the riders above their seat (O3) were put on the bench and left; the figures over the pond (O4) were not worked. Both are carried to `vision/flaws_v047.md`.

The set was regenerated and screened a forty-fourth time. Every person and every animal is drawn anew, so no picture is identical to v0.46's.

__INTRO_RESULT__

Everything is built by code and reproducible: the cast is modeled and posed in Blender (Blender 5.2.3 LTS), and the scenes are composed in SVG by the generator in `scenegen/`. From v0.24 the whole cycle also runs on Windows with an installed Blender (`RUNBOOK.md`, Setup).

Source and release history: https://github.com/FoilMiser/Cookie-Theft-Alternative-Scene-Generator- (each release is one commit; `RUNBOOK.md` there says how a release is made).

## The isolation bench

A case of the bench is one event forced alone on one seed in one setting: the picture is built whole and the event cut out of it. Each thing was built in v0.46's drawing and in v0.47's on the same seeds, and a reader wrote what it saw and what it was unsure of. Beside the cut-outs, figure reviewers had one figure of the library in two drawings as "a" and "b", which is which drawn at random, and said which is the better drawn and why. Every reader and reviewer was a fresh subagent given its prompt and its pictures and nothing else. The record is `vision/isolation_v047.md`; there were five rounds:
- **Round 1: fifteen readers of 58 cut-outs, four readers of eight whole tier-3 pictures, and four reviewers of sixteen figure pairs.** The new figure was the better drawn in fourteen pairs of sixteen: "The front foot is a proper shoe on the leg rather than a loose oval, and the face is clearer."; "The V-neck cardigan over a blouse reads as real clothing, while a's off-centre cream strip looks like a pasted-on stripe."; "The spots are proper markings rather than lumps, and the odd brown shape under the belly is gone." Every forced event of the eight whole pictures was read. The two pairs that went to v0.46 named the worker's vest at the shoulder and the pecking bird's eye. Of the list's things:
  - the dog on the hose did not read, 0 of 3, as v0.46's did not: drawn in a stride it was a dog walking by, "A white dog with brown patches and a blue collar walks over the hose in the foreground.", and the long low swelling was a puddle;
  - the fork sweeper read in 2 cut-outs of 3 for v0.46's 1, and all three readers took its heap, a round one with a leaf at each end, for an animal: "It could be misread as a dead bird or small animal.";
  - the reading dog read in 3 of 3, as v0.46's did, with its lower body "a rounded lump with bent hind legs and no clear tail" and its collar a flap behind the neck;
  - the stalking cat read in 3 of 3 in both drawings, with its forelegs a knot in both: "It looks as if the cat is holding something or hugging itself, not standing on four legs.";
  - the walker was read as about to trip in 1 cut-out of 3 in both drawings. In all three of the new ones the box is ahead of the walker, which is what the walk was redrawn for; a cut-out of one event cannot show the rule about what stands behind him, so both went on to the design tests;
  - the upward water was read as rising from "What I see" alone in 1 of 3 for 0 of 3, and all three readers also offered a hose or a wire. It was left as drawn;
  - the balloon, the fence painter, the seesaw and the slide read in both drawings, and nothing of theirs was changed after it.
- **Round 2: three readers of fifteen cut-outs, six pairs.** The fork sweeper read in 3 of 3 and its heap, now low, ragged and of one grey, was dust to every reader: "using it like a broom to sweep a small pile of grey dirt/dust (with a dust cloud) toward a red dustpan on the path". The dog stood on the hose in all three cut-outs and walked in none; the blocking was read outright in one and offered in the other two, and the short round swelling was a connector to two readers. It is the fourth drawing of that swelling since revision 27, and each was read as a thing of its own beside the hose, so it was left there. The pecking bird read in the scene, 3 of 3, and lost both its pairs as a drawing, "a looks like it is falling forward on stubs": tipped about its feet, a body drops its hips. The reading dog's hindquarters were still a blob, and the stalking cat's legs still tangled.
- **Round 3: three readers of nine cut-outs, eight pairs.** Each of round 2's three changes was the better drawing than what it replaced, 4 pairs of 4. The cat stalked and the bird pecked in 3 of 3. The reading dog read in 3 of 3, and all three readers offered another animal: "almost like a weasel or otter when it sits up", "more like a meerkat". Round 2 had taken its collar off, and with it what made a thin upright body a dog. Against v0.46's level bird the pecking bird lost three pairs of four on its balance, and in all four the reviewer said the new one reads more strongly as pecking: "a conveys pecking more strongly but the rear-placed leg makes it look as if it is falling over."
- **Round 4: three readers of the reading dog, one pair.** With a collar fitted as a band to the body's own section: a dog reading a newspaper in three cut-outs of three, a red collar named in each and no other animal offered. "A brown and tan cartoon dog with dark brown floppy ears and a red collar sits upright on a tiled pavement, holding an open newspaper in its front paws and wearing round dark glasses, as if reading." As a drawing the pair was "No difference".
- **Round 5, after the set was built: three readers of the younger child's dropped ice cream.** `CONE47` (below) lets the generator draw the dropped cone with either child, and she had held it in no picture since v0.27. Three cut-outs of three were read as built: "The scene reads as: the girl dropped her ice cream and the dog is eating it." Two readers of three offered her hand round the top of the cone as a scoop still in it.

**The trade.** The pecking bird is kept though it loses to v0.46's as a drawing. v0.46's level bird was read as standing beside the sandwich or the seeds, which is what two rows of the list were; the new one is read as pecking in six cut-outs of six and as the thief in three of three. Its balance is on the watch list.

**The yields.** A new pose changes what an old placement rule draws, so each template the release touched was planned and laid out again on 24 seeds a setting. With the new cat and bird, revision 43's rule for the stalked bird placed in 0, 0, 9 and 0 of 24: it drew a distance and then tested a gap, and the new figures reach a few pixels further. The rule now draws the gap itself (`GAP47`), 24, 24, 23 and 24 of 24. Two things do not place and are on the list for v0.48: the fork sweeper in the park and the lost balloon in the street.

**The blind rule.** Seven of round 1's subagents listed their round folder after their files were written and so saw the names of the other files in it; the names are numbers, and no description or review names a case, a switch or a version. From round 2 the prompts say to write with the Write tool and not to list the folder, and none did.

## Placement revision 44

Every record stores the placement revision it was made with. v0.47 pictures use revision 44. Its thirteen rules, each behind a switch whose letter O draws revision 43 (`scenegen/templates.py` unless another file is named):
- **The reading dog** (`READ47` B): the cast's new pose, sitting up with the paper in both forepaws. The paper is part of the dog's own drawing and is measured as a part of the dog.
- **The dog on the hose** (`HOSE47` D): the dog stands on the hose by its near forepaw; the hose is squashed to a flat band under that paw and swells against it on the tap's side, short and round, and no drop hangs at the nozzle.
- **The walker about to trip** (`TRIP47` C): nothing upright of the setting, a post, a pole, a trunk or a frame, stands from a body's width behind the walker to a body's width past the obstacle, over the walker's whole height. In v0.46's screen a bird table's post stood right behind the walker's back and the reader had him heading the other way; in a design test of this release a tree just past the open book was a second thing he might walk into.
- **The fork sweeper** (`FORK47` D): a low, wide, ragged heap of pale dust with grit in it and an open dustpan lying with its mouth to the heap, the fork's head to the pan about 100 px for 158, so the three fit a path.
- **The bird at the sandwich** (`STEAL47` B): the bird is stood by its beak, the tip in a bite a quarter of the sandwich deep, with crumbs on the seat under its head; the reader sits a little further along and turned away, so the sandwich lies behind the reader's back and clear of the legs; both of the bird's feet are on the seat.
- **The stalked bird** (`SEED47` B, `GAP47` B, `POST47` B): a few seeds on the ground at its beak; the gap between the cat's front and the bird is itself drawn, 50 to 80 px at full size; and nothing upright of the setting stands in the span of the cat and its bird.
- **The lost balloon** (`BAL47` B): the string's end is 42 to 60 px above the reaching hand and 8 to 22 ahead of it, for 62 to 80 and 16 to 30.
- **The fence painter** (`PAINT47` B): the painter stands at most half a board to one side, so that the roller's middle lies on the middle of its board, above that board's wet paint.
- **The upward water** (`UPW47` B, `scenegen/draw.py`): one stream of a pour's width, 10 px narrowing to 7, its top round, with no drops beside it.
- **A figure that holds a wide thing** (`SHADOW47` B, `scenegen/library.py`): its ground shadow is taken from the filled columns that reach the lowest eighth of the figure, so the reading dog's shadow lies under the dog and not under its paper.
- **The dropped ice cream** (`CONE47` B): revision 8 lays the scoop a fixed 10 px clear of the child's near foot and then asks that the cone be above the scoop, within 0.9 of the splat's half width of its middle. The older child passed that by a tenth of a pixel on the middle knob and failed it on the easy one; the younger child failed it on every knob, so every dropped cone of the sets and probes from v0.28 to v0.46 is his. Where the fixed clearance would put the cone outside the splat, the scoop is now laid by the cone, and the splat's rim stays 5 px clear of the near foot. Alone at tier 4 the event placed on 12, 13 and 11 seeds of 24 in the park, the street and the playground, and places on 24 of 24 in each. It was found late, after the set was built, when no tier-4 isomorph would build: the event is alone in its isomorph class, so every isomorph of a picture that holds it must draw it again.

A v0.46 record rebuilds with the v0.46 package, and a v0.47 record with v0.47's.

## Key words, frequency and age of acquisition


Unchanged from v0.20: every item record carries its key word's Zipf frequency (f, for things and actions only) and its
age of acquisition (a, from the bundled excerpt of Kuperman, Stadthagen-Gonzalez and Brysbaert, 2012) at weight 0;
`scenegen/norms/aoa_kuperman2012.tsv` keeps the citation and source in its header and every record stores its sha256.
The licence note and what the pilot should test are in the v0.20 README section of the same name (the CC BY-NC-ND 3.0
question about shipping an excerpt; the model with AoA against the model without, pre-registered; AoA kept varying apart
from frequency when choosing the pilot's pictures). Before the pilot, check the bundled rows against the original file
(`python -m scenegen.difficulty --check-aoa <the original .xlsx or .csv>`).

## How to run

```bash
python -m venv .venv && source tools/env.sh      # from Git Bash on Windows too
pip install -r requirements.txt                  # the cast needs Blender 5.2.3 LTS: installed, or pip install bpy==5.2.3 on Python 3.13

python assets/cast/build_cast.py assets/cast/out047                 # build the cast (under a minute)
cd assets/cast && python gapcheck.py out047 person.kai && cd ../..  # check one character's poses for parts that come apart
python -m scenegen.generate --tier 3 --setting playground --seed 7 --out scenes
python -m scenegen.generate --from-record __REBUILD_EXAMPLE__ --out tpdb_work/check   # exact rebuild
python -m scenegen.reach --out reach.json                          # the poses the templates can ask for

python -m scenegen.batch showcase --out scenes
python -m scenegen.calibrate --out calibration.json
python -m scenegen.difficulty --check-aoa <the original Kuperman et al. file>   # compare the bundled AoA rows
python -m scenegen.combos --out combos.json
BENCH_CAST=<a cast built with TPDB_PROFILE=0> python vision/isolation_bench.py tpdb_work/bench proO_1 proO_2   # one template alone, cropped
python vision/prepare_screen.py scenes,probes vision/v047   # then describe and score: vision/screen_prompts.md
bash tools/cycle/summarize.sh                               # add the screen to vision/screen_summary.json
python vision/gate.py vision/v047 scenes,probes
python -m scenegen.site --scenes scenes --gallery assets/cast/gallery047 --old-gallery <v0.46 renders of the same variants> \
    --combos combos.json --screen vision/screen_summary.json --out site
python3 package_release.py 0.47 --single   # the release as one zip, tpdb_v0.47.zip
```

The release is one zip, `tpdb_v0.47.zip`, about __ZIP_MB__ MB. Google Drive does not virus-scan a file over 100 MB, so it asks to confirm the download ("can't scan this file for viruses"); the file downloads as normal after that. `package_release.py` checks, file by file, that the zip holds every file of the release. Without `--single` it writes the same release as zip parts under 50 MB each, with the README and the set's pictures beside them, for browsing in Drive.

Exact rebuilds hold within a release: a v0.46 record rebuilds with the v0.46 package. They also depend on Pillow, pinned in `requirements.txt`: Pillow 13 resamples RGBA differently from 12, and every embedded asset changes. A different cairo moves a few pixels by one level and leaves the SVG identical. Renders from an installed Blender differ from the Linux module's only in sub-pixel anti-aliasing on the outlines, with identical anchors and geometry, so the library mixes the two.

## Known limits

- The weights are still the spec's starting values: every predicted completeness is a placeholder
  until the pilot fit.
- The blind screen used AI readers. It is a filter for illegible drawing; the pilot with people
  remains the test of difficulty.
- __LIMITS__
- The cast is a stand-in. Final character art would replace the models; rigs, poses, faces,
  anchors and manifests carry over.
