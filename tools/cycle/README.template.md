# TPDB v0.51: a thrown ball read the right way round, no hose by the wheelbarrow, bread in the feeder's hand; all 64 templates through the gate, read a forty-eighth time

v0.50 ended with two templates under the gate and a list of what read least well. Sam's brief for v0.51: "go for v0.51!" It names nothing in the pictures, so the work list is v0.50's own (`vision/flaws_v050.md`):
- its two failures: the game of catch, read the wrong way round when a small child threw to a grown catcher (R1), and the square wheel on the wheelbarrow, taken for a hose reel with a hose behind it (R2);
- the templates that read least well beside them: the thief bird (R3), the stopped hose (R4), the bread for the ducks (R5) and the hedged full marks (R6);
- the oddities: the lost balloon's string ending by a fence or a pond (O1), the man in the red shirt called a boy (O2), the fence painter's roller and tin (O3), and O4 to O9.

The work is in the placement rules (revision 48) and in three poses of the cast (builder 0.51.0). Each change is behind a switch whose letter O gives v0.50's drawing, and each was read blind against v0.50's drawing before it went in (`vision/isolation_v051.md`):
- **The game of catch.** The ball is drawn a third of the way along its path, at the end of a short dashed line from the thrower's hand, where it was drawn late on its arc, by the catcher's hands. The catcher reaches for it with two open hands, one above the other, where the hands were together; the thrower stands on two planted feet. The ball's place was chosen on v0.50's own two failing pictures, built again and read in whole (below).
- **The square wheel.** No hose lies behind, under or beside the square-wheeled barrow or bicycle: the template is placed after every template that lays a hose and keeps off each one's run, and off the garden's reel. The wheel's drawing is as it was.
- **The bread for the ducks.** A slice of bread stands in the feeder's forward hand.
- **The lost balloon's string** ends on no rail of the garden's fence, by no gate, on no line of the backdrop and over no pond; the child stands where that is so.
- **The fence painter's tin** stands at the heel, and the roller's end caps are a dark blue, where they were cream.
- **The stopped hose** is no longer planned under storm clouds.
- **Put to readers and not taken:** the stopped hose's swelling ending short of the paw (R4), and a moustache as wide as it was on v0.49's head for the man in the red shirt (O2). Each read no better than v0.50's drawing, or worse.
- **Looked at and left:** the thief bird (R3), the hedged full marks (R6), the slider (O4), the heavy seesaw's rider (O5), figures at the pond's rim (O6) and the swing's one seat (O8).

The set was regenerated and screened a forty-eighth time. Seven of the sixteen cells have v0.50's templates, the tier-4 park, street and playground on v0.50's seeds among them; the other nine are new sets. Six of the 102 screened pictures are v0.50's to the pixel.

## The isolation bench

Every reader was a fresh subagent given its prompt and its pictures and nothing else. The record is `vision/isolation_v051.md`:
- **One round of cut-outs,** thirty-seven of them and thirteen readers; no reader saw two takes of one thing.
  - The game of catch, a child throwing to a grown catcher, drawn three ways. All nine readers had the child throwing, v0.50's drawing included, so the cut-outs do not show the screen's fault: a pair cut out of its picture is easy. What they did say is that v0.50's catcher has the hands together, in six cut-outs of six, "clasped together rather than cupped open, which could read as clapping or praying instead of catching"; that three solid lines behind the ball, as behind a runner, are no clearer than the dashed arc, "the lines are short and ambiguous in direction"; and that a first new catcher, the upper hand raised palm out, was "waving or fending the ball off". The catcher was drawn a third time, both arms reaching toward the ball.
  - The barrow under the rule, with a sprinkler or a watering hose in the picture: a wheel that is square in three of three, and no reel. With the hose behind it, on the same seed without the rule: "so it could look like it is standing on the hose."
  - The bread. With the slice, a piece of bread is in the hand in three of three. Of v0.50's: "holds nothing, so it is unclear whether he has just thrown them or is only gesturing".
  - The lost balloon. The string's end on the fence's plain boards is "not attached to anything"; in the park the rule had sent it to the hedge's line, "tied to or growing out of the bushes", and the rule then took the backdrop's lines in.
  - The stopped hose with the swelling short of the paw: "It looks like two hose pieces", and the stopped water read by one of three, for three of three with v0.50's. Not taken.
  - The painter. The tin is "by his feet" with the rule and "fairly far from him, behind his legs" without it; the cream caps were "a small lantern or a can stuck to the fence".
- **A bench in whole scenes, for the game of catch.** v0.50's two failing pictures were built again from their own records, with the same people in the same places, in three arms: as screened; with the cast's new poses and v0.50's ball; and with those poses and the ball early. Twelve fresh readers each read one picture of each scene, from two arms, with the screen's own prompt. The girl was read as the thrower in **3 readings of 8 as screened, 4 of 8 with the poses alone, and 7 of 8 with the ball early**; the woman was the thrower in 3, 3 and none. As screened: "In the middle, a woman in a yellow dress has just thrown a red ball, and a dashed line shows it arcing toward a small girl in a pink dress, who runs forward with her arm out to catch it." With the ball early: "In the middle of the lawn a girl in a pink dress has just thrown a red ball (a dashed line shows its path from her hand) toward a woman in a yellow dress, who stands with her hands raised ready to catch it."
- **The same readers, for the moustache.** Sixteen of v0.50's pictures of the man in the red shirt were built again with the wider moustache and read beside v0.50's own, two of each to a reader. He is a boy first in 2 of 16 with each, and the moustache is in his line in 9 with the wider one and in 7 with v0.50's. Not taken.

**What the bench leaves open.** The two arms with v0.50's ball cannot be told apart. Each reader had both scenes, with the same girl and the same woman, and a reader who had seen the ball early read the other picture right as well: the four readers who had no early ball read neither picture right, none of 8, and the eight who had one read their other picture right in 7 of 8. So the poses were taken for what the cut-outs said of the catcher's hands, and not for the direction. And the man in the red shirt is a boy when he sweeps, in three readings of eight on the bench: the pose is on the next list.

Every record stores the placement revision it was made with. v0.51 pictures use revision 48: six rules and a rank, each rule behind a switch whose letter O draws revision 47:

- **No hose by the square wheel** (`BARROW51` B): `square_wheel` is placed after every template that lays a hose (`planner.realize`), and its box keeps off each hose's run on the ground, off the ground nobody may stand on (a sprinkler's spray, a tether) and off the garden's reel.

- **The stopped hose under a clear sky only** (`BlockedHose.sky`), as the watering templates since revision 30.

age of acquisition (a, from the bundled excerpt of Kuperman, Stadthagen-Gonzalez and Brysbaert, 2012) at weight 0;

## How to run

```bash
python -m venv .venv && source tools/env.sh      # from Git Bash on Windows too
pip install -r requirements.txt                  # the cast needs Blender 5.2.3 LTS: installed, or pip install bpy==5.2.3 on Python 3.13

python assets/cast/build_cast.py assets/cast/out051                 # build the cast (under a minute)
cd assets/cast && python gapcheck.py out051 person.kai && cd ../..  # check one character's poses for parts that come apart
python -m scenegen.generate --tier 3 --setting playground --seed 7 --out scenes
python -m scenegen.generate --from-record __REBUILD_EXAMPLE__ --out tpdb_work/check   # exact rebuild
python -m scenegen.reach --out reach.json                          # the poses the templates can ask for

python -m scenegen.batch showcase --out scenes
python -m scenegen.calibrate --out calibration.json
python -m scenegen.difficulty --check-aoa <the original Kuperman et al. file>   # compare the bundled AoA rows
python -m scenegen.combos --out combos.json
TPDB_TRIP48=O python vision/isolation_bench.py tpdb_work/bench tpwN_1 tpwN_2   # one template alone, cropped (here with revision 44's trip)
python vision/prepare_screen.py scenes,probes vision/v051   # then describe and score: vision/screen_prompts.md
bash tools/cycle/summarize.sh                               # add the screen to vision/screen_summary.json
python vision/gate.py vision/v051 scenes,probes
python -m scenegen.site --scenes scenes --gallery assets/cast/gallery051 --old-gallery <v0.50 renders of the same variants> \
    --combos combos.json --screen vision/screen_summary.json --out site
python3 package_release.py 0.51 --single   # the release as one zip, tpdb_v0.51.zip
```

The release is one zip, `tpdb_v0.51.zip`, about __ZIP_MB__ MB. Google Drive does not virus-scan a file over 100 MB, so it asks to confirm the download ("can't scan this file for viruses"); the file downloads as normal after that. `package_release.py` checks, file by file, that the zip holds every file of the release. Without `--single` it writes the same release as zip parts under 50 MB each, with the README and the set's pictures beside them, for browsing in Drive.

Exact rebuilds hold within a release: a v0.50 record rebuilds with the v0.50 package. They also depend on Pillow, pinned in `requirements.txt`: Pillow 13 resamples RGBA differently from 12, and every embedded asset changes. A different cairo moves a few pixels by one level and leaves the SVG identical. Renders from an installed Blender differ from the Linux module's only in sub-pixel anti-aliasing on the outlines, with identical anchors and geometry, so the library mixes the two.

## Known limits

- The weights are still the spec's starting values: every predicted completeness is a placeholder
  until the pilot fit.
- The blind screen used AI readers. It is a filter for illegible drawing; the pilot with people
  remains the test of difficulty.
- __LIMITS__
- The cast is a stand-in. Final character art would replace the models; rigs, poses, faces,
  anchors and manifests carry over.
